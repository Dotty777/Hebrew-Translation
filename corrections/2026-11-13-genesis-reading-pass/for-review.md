# Genesis 6–50 · points left for a specialist (13 November 2026)

Not changed in this pass. Each needs an editorial or specialist decision.

## Changes that alter how a verse reads (please confirm)

- **27:39** reverses the sense of Isaac’s word to Esau (grant, not deprivation). Rashi and the 27:28 parallel support it.
- **28:21–22** moves the start of the vow’s “then” clause to 28:22, as Rashi reads it.
- **15:9** “three heifers, three female goats, three rams” (Onkelos, Rashi) departs from most English Bibles’ “three years old”.
- **22:8** “God will see for Himself the sheep” replaces “God will provide”, to keep the echo of 22:14.

## Rabbinic Priority decisions not yet taken

- 6:2, 6:4 “sons of God” — Rashi: sons of the princes or judges.
- 9:20 “began and planted” — Rashi reads the verb as “made himself profane”.
- 9:24 “younger son” — literal “youngest”; Rashi reads “the despised one”.
- 16:13 — hard to follow aloud; Rashi reads it differently.
- 18:3, 19:18 — *Adonai* addressed, then lowercase “your”; choose God or the visitors consistently.
- 18:21 — Rashi reads *kalah* as destruction.
- 22:13 — Rashi reads *achar* as “afterward”.
- 24:55 — Rashi: “a year or ten months”.
- 33:20 — Rashi does not read El-Elohe-Israel as the altar’s name.
- 37:28 — who drew Joseph out (Rashi: the brothers).
- 38:2 — Rashi and Onkelos: “merchant” for Canaanite.
- 38:14, 38:21 — Rashi: “Enaim” as the crossroads.
- 41:40 — Rashi: “by your word all my people shall be provided for”.
- 49:5 — disputed hapax for “weapons”.
- 49:22–24 — Rashi reads the Joseph blessing very differently; too large a rework of the poetry without a specialist.
- 49:25 — bare *Shaddai* as “the Almighty” vs the column’s “El Shaddai” elsewhere.
