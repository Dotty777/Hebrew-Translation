# Genesis 6–50 Hebrew–English reading pass · 13 November 2026

Continues the [Genesis 1–5 pass](../2026-11-10-genesis-reading-pass/README.md). All 1,396 rows of Genesis 6–50 were read against the Westminster Leningrad Codex for fidelity first and clarity when read aloud second, following the edition's Literal-Smooth, Rabbinic Priority approach. 87 English readings were revised: 3 major, 66 minor and 18 for spoken flow. The other 1,309 readings were left as they are.

**Major corrections** (the English said something the Hebrew does not):

- 14:4 — “for thirteen years they rebelled”, not “in the thirteenth year”; Rashi counts 14:5 as the fourteenth year of the rebellion.
- 27:39 — Isaac’s word to Esau is a grant, “of the richness of the earth shall your dwelling be”, matching 27:28 and Rashi. The previous “away from” reading made it a deprivation.
- 34:27 — the brothers plundered the city “because they had defiled their sister”; the Hebrew gives the reason, not the place.

**Rabbinic readings adopted** include 6:16 (“a light”), 11:28 (Haran died during his father’s lifetime), 15:9 (three of each animal, with Onkelos), 18:10 and 18:14 (“at this time next year”), 19:14 (sons-in-law who were to marry his daughters), 21:9 (“mocking”), 22:8 (“God will see for Himself the sheep”, keeping the link to 22:14), 28:21–22 (the vow’s “then” begins in 22), 36:24 (“mules”, not the Vulgate’s hot springs), 40:16 (openwork baskets), 48:14 (“he guided his hands knowingly”) and 49:4, 49:12.

Other revisions restore repetitions the Hebrew makes on purpose (7:23, 26:13, 37:7, 47:9), keep idioms an English listener can follow (“lifted up his eyes”, “lifted up his feet”, “Laban’s face”), align repeated phrases across a story (33:8–15, 37:20/33, 43:9/44:32), and break sentences that were hard to follow aloud.

The review was AI-assisted (five parallel readers working from a shared brief, then checked together). Every changed reading was matched against its previous English in `genesis/verses.json` and `genesis/Genesis.txt`; card IDs, verse references and Masoretic references are unchanged. Independent specialist review remains open, and the points below were deliberately left for it.
