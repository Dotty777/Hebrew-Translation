# Genesis 1–5 Hebrew–English reading pass · 10 November 2026

Reviewed all 138 verses in Genesis 1–5 against the Westminster Leningrad Codex. Revised 29 English readings for fidelity and phrasing that is easier to follow aloud. The five chapters range from narrative and dialogue to poetry and genealogy. Repeated words, genealogical numbers, and the edition’s use of YHWH were checked during the review.

- Genesis 1:16 makes the stars a separate object of God’s making. Genesis 1:20 represents the Hebrew verb and its cognate noun with “teem with swarms.”
- Genesis 2:17 and 3:4 retain an audible echo between the warning and the serpent’s denial.
- Genesis 4:7 retains the lifting image and reads the final verb as ability, following Rashi. Genesis 5:29 restores the two parallel phrases of relief “from” work and painful toil.

The main verse text follows the existing Literal-Smooth, Rabbinic Priority approach. The review keeps the edition’s “Spirit of God” in Genesis 1:2 and “rib” in Genesis 2:21. Other renderings of the Hebrew are possible. Independent specialist review and a full-edition read-aloud pass remain open.
