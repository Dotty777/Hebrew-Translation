# Masoretic coverage and English revisions · 9 November 2026

This GitHub revision adds 185 English readings keyed to the Masoretic Text and revises 10 existing readings in Genesis, Daniel, and Ezekiel. It builds on the [accuracy audit across all 39 books](../2026-11-09-full-review/README.md)
and the [first editorial revision](../2026-11-09-hebrew-editorial/README.md).

The additions resolve the 169 source references listed in the first editorial revision.
A further 16 readings came from a second check against the complete witness index.

The English follows the [pinned Westminster Leningrad Codex](https://github.com/embabel-worlds/realm-tanakh/tree/73eed523b5349ee21f19cfe6061725168ebe827e/reference), with the JPS 1917 rendering used as a comparison. For difficult readings, the source file records the choice where it matters. For example, 1 Samuel 13:1 leaves Saul’s accession age unresolved and retains the two years given by the Masoretic Text. The new Daniel readings follow Biblical Aramaic. English wording preserves numbers, repetitions and contrasts while making clauses easier to follow aloud.

The additional readings appear in each book’s structured source file and its plain-text export. They use Masoretic verse numbers. Existing reader references and card IDs are preserved. The English revisions appear in both representations of the aligned readings. Two Masoretic reference annotations were corrected in Psalms and Ezekiel, where separate verses have identical Hebrew.

This is a GitHub archive update. The earlier 39-book accuracy review was AI-assisted, and this pass concentrates on the source gaps and selected passages in three books. Independent human Hebrew and Aramaic review and a full read-aloud pass remain pending.
