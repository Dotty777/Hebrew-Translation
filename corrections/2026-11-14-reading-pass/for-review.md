## Leviticus 10–24

# hebrew-004 notes (Leviticus 10–24)

## Not changed — needs a specialist or an editorial decision
- **Lev 19:20** “there shall be an inquiry” for בִּקֹּרֶת תִּהְיֶה: Rashi (following Sifra and Keritot 11a) reads this as a flogging of the woman, not an inquiry. Editors should decide whether to follow Rashi here.
- **Lev 21:20** “a hunchback, one who is wasted”: Rashi reads גִבֵּן as long, drooping eyebrows and דַק as a film over the eye, so both are eye defects in his reading. The current wording is the usual modern one; this needs a specialist’s call.
- **Lev 10:1** “unauthorized fire” for אֵשׁ זָרָה (literally “strange” or “foreign” fire). Kept because the column uses the same words in Num 3:4 and 26:61. Any change should cover all three verses.
- **Lev 22:4–5** “Nor shall one who touches …” runs across two rows and stops at the end of 22:5 without its own verb (the reader has to carry over “eat the holy things”). It reads acceptably, but editors could smooth it.
- **Lev 21:23** “shall not enter toward the curtain” sounds a little awkward aloud (לֹא יָבֹא means “shall not come”). Left as it is.

## Data and conventions
- In Leviticus (here and in hebrew-003) direct speech has a colon and no quotation marks. Exodus, Numbers and Deuteronomy use curly quotes. I kept the Leviticus style; the column may want one convention.
- The SRC for 18:17 and 19:1 has no sof pasuq (׃). The SRC for 21:10 has a stray zero-width joiner in אֲ‍שֶׁר. The English is not affected.

## Leviticus 25–27; Numbers 1–7

# hebrew-005 notes (Leviticus 25–27; Numbers 1–7)

## For a specialist or editorial decision (not changed)

- Leviticus 25:5, 25:11: נְזִירֶךָ / נְזִרֶיהָ is rendered “untended vine(s)”. That is the plain sense (unpruned, like a Nazirite’s hair). Rashi, following Sifra, reads it as grapes you guarded and kept from others (did not make ownerless). This is an editorial choice.
- Leviticus 25:33: this is a hard verse. The English says “the city of their possession”, but the Hebrew has אֲחֻזָּתוֹ (singular). The meaning of יִגְאַל here (“redeem” or “buy”, as some commentators read it) is worth a specialist look.
- Numbers 3:9: נְתוּנִם נְתוּנִם is rendered “wholly given”. If the column wants doubled words kept as they are (“given, given”), this verse and Numbers 8:16 should be changed together.
- Numbers 6:26: “set peace for you” is kept as the literal rendering of וְיָשֵׂם; “give you peace” would be smoother if the editors want that.

## Data problems

- Numbers 6:27 (card v-6-27, placed after 6:23 in the Greek order) has the reference “Numbers 6:23”, the same as card v-6-23. validate.py looks rows up by reference, so the second row overwrites the first. A change to the real 6:23 (v-6-23) would fail validation. Neither row was changed.
- The formula וַיֹּאמֶר יְהוָה אֶל־מֹשֶׁה לֵּאמֹר is rendered several ways across the column: “YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,” (hebrew-002), “YHWH said to Moses, saying,” (hebrew-006, line 769), and “YHWH said to Moses:” (hebrew-007). This chunk’s Numbers 7:4 now uses “YHWH said to Moses:”. hebrew-006 may want the same change.
