# Hebrew–English reading pass · 14 November 2026

Continues the Genesis pass of 13 November across the rest of the Hebrew Bible. Every row was read against the Westminster Leningrad Codex under the edition’s Literal-Smooth, Rabbinic Priority approach, for fidelity first and clarity when read aloud second. Readings were changed only where the English said something the source does not (major), lost something the source does deliberately (minor), or was hard to follow aloud (spoken).

In all: 38 books, 21,841 rows reviewed, 344 readings revised (66 major, 167 minor, 111 spoken).

Rows that could not be revised safely were shown to the reviewers for context but locked: rows with no source text or no English, placeholder rows (“No MT counterpart …”), rows sharing one site card, and grouped Vulgate verses. Bracketed tags inside readings (“[BHS 3:1]”, “[Aramaic]”) were kept exactly.

The review was AI-assisted: parallel readers worked from a shared brief, each change was validated against the archive text, and the results were merged into `verses.json` and the book text exports with card IDs and references unchanged. Independent specialist review remains open.

## On staging

Every revised reading was written into its book’s comparison pages on the Saints.Bible staging site (the live site is unchanged), and each saved page was checked against its expected fingerprint. Afterwards every published reading was audited card by card. Card IDs such as `verse-10` repeat in every chapter, and an early version of the publishing script wrote 16 readings into the card with the same ID in another chapter (Ezekiel 4, Isaiah 1, Jeremiah 2, Psalms 9). Those cards were restored to their own readings, the intended readings were confirmed in their own cards, and a second audit found no card holding another verse’s reading. The highlighted copy of Job 42:10 was also brought into line with its plain copy.

- Comparison notes beside a revised reading were not rewritten, so a few may still quote the older English (for example Proverbs 11:30).
- Revised readings show without their Rainbow colour highlighting until those colour plans are rebuilt for the new text, as with the earlier passes.
