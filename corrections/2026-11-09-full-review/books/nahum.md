## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Nahum 1:2** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: אֵל קַנּוֹא וְנֹקֵם יְהוָה נֹקֵם יְהוָה וּבַעַל חֵמָה נֹקֵם יְהוָה לְצָרָיו וְנוֹטֵר הוּא לְאֹיְבָיו׃
- Now: YHWH is a jealous and avenging God; YHWH avenges and is master of wrath. YHWH avenges His adversaries, and He reserves wrath for His enemies.
- Proposed: YHWH is a jealous and avenging God; YHWH avenges and is master of wrath. YHWH takes vengeance on His adversaries, and He reserves wrath for His enemies.
- Why: נֹקֵם יְהוָה לְצָרָיו: vengeance is taken on the adversaries (לְ of the object of vengeance). English "avenges His adversaries" says He takes vengeance on their behalf, the reverse sense.
