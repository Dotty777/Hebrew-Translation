## Proposed English corrections (6)

**Psalms 31:5 (Masoretic 32:5)** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: חַטָּאתִי אוֹדִיעֲךָ וַעֲוֺנִי לֹא־כִסִּיתִי אָמַרְתִּי אוֹדֶה עֲלֵי פְשָׁעַי לַיהוָה וְאַתָּה נָשָׂאתָ עֲוֺן חַטָּאתִי סֶלָה׃
- Now: I will make my sin known to You; I have not covered my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my rebellions against myself to YHWH,” and You bore away the iniquity of my sin. Selah.
- Proposed: I will make my sin known to You; I have not covered my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my rebellions to YHWH,” and You bore away the iniquity of my sin. Selah.
- Why: MT עֲלֵי פְשָׁעַי is the poetic preposition 'concerning my rebellions'; 'against myself' reads it as עָלַי, the Septuagint/Vulgate reading (kat' emou, adversum me), which the Hebrew pointing does not give.

**Psalms 49:18 (Masoretic 50:18)** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: אִם־רָאִיתָ גַנָּב וַתִּרֶץ עִמּוֹ וְעִם מְנָאֲפִים חֶלְקֶךָ׃
- Now: If you see a thief, you run with him; your portion is with adulterers.
- Proposed: If you see a thief, you are pleased with him; your portion is with adulterers.
- Why: MT points וַתִּרֶץ, from רצה, 'you are pleased/consent with him'. 'You run with him' renders וַתָּרָץ (from רוץ), the vocalization behind the Septuagint's 'you ran along with him'.

**Psalms 57:8 (Masoretic 58:8)** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: יִמָּאֲסוּ כְמוֹ־מַיִם יִתְהַלְּכוּ־לָמוֹ יִדְרֹךְ חצו [חִצָּיו] כְּמוֹ יִתְמֹלָלוּ׃
- Now: Let them be despised; let them walk as through water. He will aim His arrows, so that they are cut down.
- Proposed: Let them be despised; let them go their way like water. He will aim His arrows, so that they are cut down.
- Why: כְמוֹ־מַיִם יִתְהַלְּכוּ־לָמוֹ is 'like water they go their way' (they themselves are like water running off); 'walk as through water' turns the simile into a medium they walk through.

**Psalms 58:13 (Masoretic 59:13)** · meaning · major

- Hebrew: חַטַּאת־פִּימוֹ דְּבַר־שְׂפָתֵימוֹ וְיִלָּכְדוּ בִגְאוֹנָם וּמֵאָלָה וּמִכַּחַשׁ יְסַפֵּרוּ׃
- Now: The sin of their mouth is the word of their lips; the poor are trapped through their arrogance, and through the curse and falsehood they recount.
- Proposed: The sin of their mouth is the word of their lips; let them be trapped through their arrogance, and through the curse and falsehood they recount.
- Why: וְיִלָּכְדוּ בִגְאוֹנָם: 'let them (the enemies just described) be caught in their pride'. 'The poor' has no counterpart in the Hebrew and changes who is trapped.

**Psalms 59:6 (Masoretic 60:6)** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: נָתַתָּה לִּירֵאֶיךָ נֵּס לְהִתְנוֹסֵס מִפְּנֵי קֹשֶׁט סֶלָה׃
- Now: You gave those who fear You a trial to be tested, for the sake of beauty. Selah.
- Proposed: You gave those who fear You a trial to be tested, for the sake of truth. Selah.
- Why: קֹשֶׁט means 'truth' (cf. Prov 22:21 קֹשְׁטְ אִמְרֵי אֱמֶת; so Targum and Rashi), not 'beauty'.

**Psalms 59:7 (Masoretic 60:7)** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: לְמַעַן יֵחָלְצוּן יְדִידֶיךָ הוֹשִׁיעָה יְמִינְךָ ועננו [וַעֲנֵנִי׃]
- Now: So that Your beloved may be delivered, save Your right hand and answer me.
- Proposed: So that Your beloved may be delivered, save with Your right hand and answer me.
- Why: הוֹשִׁיעָה יְמִינְךָ: יְמִינְךָ is the instrument, 'save with Your right hand' (parallel to delivering the beloved); 'save Your right hand' makes the hand the thing saved.
