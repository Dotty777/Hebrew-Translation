## Proposed English corrections (4)

**Deuteronomy 5:15** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וְזָכַרְתָּ כִּי־עֶבֶד הָיִיתָ בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם וַיֹּצִאֲךָ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ מִשָּׁם בְּיָד חֲזָקָה וּבִזְרֹעַ נְטוּיָה עַל־כֵּן צִוְּךָ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת־יוֹם הַשַׁבָּת׃
- Now: Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt and that YHWH your God brought you out with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore YHWH your God commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.
- Proposed: Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt and that YHWH your God brought you out from there with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore YHWH your God commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.
- Why: מִשָּׁם (mi-sham, 'from there') after וַיֹּצִאֲךָ is not rendered.

**Deuteronomy 6:11** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וּבָתִּים מְלֵאִים כָּל־טוּב אֲשֶׁר לֹא־מִלֵּאתָ וּבֹרֹת חֲצוּבִים אֲשֶׁר לֹא־חָצַבְתָּ כְּרָמִים וְזֵיתִים אֲשֶׁר לֹא־נָטָעְתָּ וְאָכַלְתָּ וְשָׂבָעְתָּ׃
- Now: houses full of good things you did not fill, hewn cisterns you did not hew, vineyards and olive groves you did not plant—and you eat and are satisfied,
- Proposed: houses full of all good things you did not fill, hewn cisterns you did not hew, vineyards and olive groves you did not plant—and you eat and are satisfied,
- Why: כָּל־טוּב (kol tuv) is 'every good thing'; the English drops כָּל ('all').

**Deuteronomy 12:26** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: רַק קָדָשֶׁיךָ אֲשֶׁר־יִהְיוּ לְךָ וּנְדָרֶיךָ תִּשָּׂא וּבָאתָ אֶל־הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר־יִבְחַר יְהוָה׃
- Now: Take your sacred things and your vowed offerings and go to the place YHWH will choose.
- Proposed: But take your sacred things that you have and your vowed offerings and go to the place YHWH will choose.
- Why: Restrictive רַק ('only/but', the exception to local slaughter) and אֲשֶׁר־יִהְיוּ לְךָ ('that you have') are not rendered.

**Deuteronomy 32:43** · other-text · major

- Hebrew: הַרְנִינוּ גוֹיִם עַמּוֹ כִּי דַם־עֲבָדָיו יִקּוֹם וְנָקָם יָשִׁיב לְצָרָיו וְכִפֶּר אַדְמָתוֹ עַמּוֹ׃
- Now: Rejoice, nations, with His people, for He avenges His servants’ blood, repays His adversaries, and makes atonement for His land and people.
- Proposed: Acclaim His people, nations, for He avenges His servants’ blood, repays His adversaries, and makes atonement for His land and people.
- Why: MT הַרְנִינוּ גוֹיִם עַמּוֹ has no עִם ('with'); עַמּוֹ is the object: 'nations, acclaim His people' (Onkelos, Rashi). 'Rejoice ... with His people' is the Septuagint reading (meta tou laou autou), kept by the KJV.

## Withdrawn on second reading (2)

- **Deuteronomy 1:27**: מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם as 'out of Egypt' names the same place in idiomatic English; dropping אֶרֶץ changes nothing the verse says.
- **Deuteronomy 8:14**: מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם as 'out of Egypt' names the same place in idiomatic English; dropping אֶרֶץ changes nothing the verse says.
