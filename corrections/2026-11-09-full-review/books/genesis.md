## Proposed English corrections (11)

**Genesis 22:7** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיֹּאמֶר יִצְחָק אֶל־אַבְרָהָם אָבִיו וַיֹּאמֶר אָבִי וַיֹּאמֶר הִנֶּנִּי בְנִי וַיֹּאמֶר הִנֵּה הָאֵשׁ וְהָעֵצִים וְאַיֵּה הַשֶּׂה לְעֹלָה׃
- Now: Isaac said to his father Abraham, “My father!” He answered, “Here I am, my son.” Isaac said, “Here are the fire and the wood, but where is the animal for the burnt offering?”
- Proposed: Isaac said to his father Abraham, “My father!” He answered, “Here I am, my son.” Isaac said, “Here are the fire and the wood, but where is the sheep for the burnt offering?”
- Why: הַשֶּׂה (ha-seh) is specifically a sheep or lamb, one of the flock; 'the animal' is generic and loses the word's sense.

**Genesis 22:8** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיֹּאמֶר אַבְרָהָם אֱלֹהִים יִרְאֶה־לּוֹ הַשֶּׂה לְעֹלָה בְּנִי וַיֵּלְכוּ שְׁנֵיהֶם יַחְדָּו׃
- Now: Abraham said, “God will provide for Himself the animal for the burnt offering, my son.” The two went together.
- Proposed: Abraham said, “God will provide for Himself the sheep for the burnt offering, my son.” The two went together.
- Why: הַשֶּׂה (ha-seh) is specifically a sheep or lamb, one of the flock; 'the animal' is generic and loses the word's sense.

**Genesis 24:46** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַתְּמַהֵר וַתּוֹרֶד כַּדָּהּ מֵעָלֶיהָ וַתֹּאמֶר שְׁתֵה וְגַם־גְּמַלֶּיךָ אַשְׁקֶה וָאֵשְׁתְּ וְגַם הַגְּמַלִּים הִשְׁקָתָה׃
- Now: “And she quickly lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, ‘Drink, and I will water your camels.’ So I drank, and she watered my camels.”
- Proposed: “And she quickly lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, ‘Drink, and I will also water your camels.’ So I drank, and she watered the camels also.”
- Why: וְגַם־גְּמַלֶּיךָ אַשְׁקֶה and וְגַם הַגְּמַלִּים הִשְׁקָתָה: וְגַם 'also' (the point of the sign, kept in 24:14) is dropped twice, and הַגְּמַלִּים 'the camels' becomes 'my camels'.

**Genesis 27:5** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וְרִבְקָה שֹׁמַעַת בְּדַבֵּר יִצְחָק אֶל־עֵשָׂו בְּנוֹ וַיֵּלֶךְ עֵשָׂו הַשָּׂדֶה לָצוּד צַיִד לְהָבִיא׃
- Now: Now Rebekah heard Isaac speaking to Esau, his son; and Esau went out into the plain to hunt game and bring it.
- Proposed: Now Rebekah heard Isaac speaking to Esau, his son; and Esau went out into the field to hunt game and bring it.
- Why: הַשָּׂדֶה is 'the field' (open country), rendered 'field' in 27:3 for the same word; 'plain' is not its sense and echoes the Greek column's wording.

**Genesis 32:32 (Masoretic 32:33)** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: עַל־כֵּן לֹא־יֹאכְלוּ בְנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל אֶת־גִּיד הַנָּשֶׁה אֲשֶׁר עַל־כַּף הַיָּרֵךְ עַד הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה כִּי נָגַע בְּכַף־יֶרֶךְ יַעֲקֹב בְּגִיד הַנָּשֶׁה׃
- Now: Therefore the Israelites do not eat the sinew at the hip socket to this day, because he touched Jacob’s hip socket at that sinew.
- Proposed: Therefore the Israelites do not eat the sinew of the thigh that is at the hip socket to this day, because he touched Jacob’s hip socket at the sinew of the thigh.
- Why: גִּיד הַנָּשֶׁה (twice) is 'the sinew of the thigh/sciatic sinew'; the English renders only גִּיד 'the sinew' and leaves הַנָּשֶׁה untranslated both times.

**Genesis 36:35** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיָּמָת חֻשָׁם וַיִּמְלֹךְ תַּחְתָּיו הֲדַד בֶּן־בְּדַד הַמַּכֶּה אֶת־מִדְיָן בִּשְׂדֵה מוֹאָב וְשֵׁם עִירוֹ עֲוִית׃
- Now: And Husham died, and Hadad, son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the plain of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith.
- Proposed: And Husham died, and Hadad, son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the country of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith.
- Why: בִּשְׂדֵה מוֹאָב is 'in the field/country of Moab' (cf. שְׂדֵה אֱדוֹם rendered 'country of Edom' at 32:3); 'plain' is not the sense of שָׂדֶה and echoes the Greek column's wording.

**Genesis 38:22** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיָּשָׁב אֶל־יְהוּדָה וַיֹּאמֶר לֹא מְצָאתִיהָ וְגַם אַנְשֵׁי הַמָּקוֹם אָמְרוּ לֹא־הָיְתָה בָזֶה קְדֵשָׁה׃
- Now: So he returned to Judah and said, “I did not find her; and moreover, the men of the place said, ‘There is no prostitute here.’”
- Proposed: So he returned to Judah and said, “I did not find her; and moreover, the men of the place said, ‘There has been no prostitute here.’”
- Why: לֹא־הָיְתָה בָזֶה קְדֵשָׁה has perfect הָיְתָה, 'there was/has been no prostitute here' (so rendered in 38:21); the English shifts it to present 'There is no'.

**Genesis 38:24** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיְהִי כְּמִשְׁלֹשׁ חֳדָשִׁים וַיֻּגַּד לִיהוּדָה לֵאמֹר זָנְתָה תָּמָר כַּלָּתֶךָ וְגַם הִנֵּה הָרָה לִזְנוּנִים וַיֹּאמֶר יְהוּדָה הוֹצִיאוּהָ וְתִשָּׂרֵף׃
- Now: And it came to pass after three months that it was reported to Judah, saying, “Tamar your daughter-in-law has played the prostitute, and behold, she is with child from prostitution.” Then Judah said, “Bring her out and let her be burned.”
- Proposed: And it came to pass after about three months that it was reported to Judah, saying, “Tamar your daughter-in-law has played the prostitute, and behold, she is with child from prostitution.” Then Judah said, “Bring her out and let her be burned.”
- Why: כְּמִשְׁלֹשׁ חֳדָשִׁים has approximating כְּ, 'about three months'; the English gives an exact 'after three months'.

**Genesis 41:21** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַתָּבֹאנָה אֶל־קִרְבֶּנָה וְלֹא נוֹדַע כִּי־בָאוּ אֶל־קִרְבֶּנָה וּמַרְאֵיהֶן רַע כַּאֲשֶׁר בַּתְּחִלָּה וָאִיקָץ׃
- Now: “And they went into their bellies; yet it was completely imperceptible that they had gone into their bellies, for their appearance remained as ugly as at the beginning, then I awoke.”
- Proposed: “And they went into their bellies; yet it was imperceptible that they had gone into their bellies, for their appearance remained as ugly as at the beginning, then I awoke.”
- Why: וְלֹא נוֹדַע is simply 'and it was not known'; the intensifier 'completely' has no counterpart in the Hebrew.

**Genesis 41:56** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: וְהָרָעָב הָיָה עַל כָּל־פְּנֵי הָאָרֶץ וַיִּפְתַּח יוֹסֵף אֶת־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר בָּהֶם וַיִּשְׁבֹּר לְמִצְרַיִם וַיֶּחֱזַק הָרָעָב בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם׃
- Now: And the famine was upon the face of all the earth. Then Joseph opened all the granaries and sold to all the Egyptians; and the famine prevailed in the land of Egypt.
- Proposed: And the famine was upon the face of all the earth. Then Joseph opened all the granaries and sold to the Egyptians; and the famine prevailed in the land of Egypt.
- Why: וַיִּשְׁבֹּר לְמִצְרַיִם is 'sold to Egypt/the Egyptians'; 'all' has no Hebrew basis and matches the Septuagint's 'to all the Egyptians' ('granaries' for כָּל־אֲשֶׁר בָּהֶם is defensible via Targum/Rashi).

**Genesis 47:8** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיֹּאמֶר פַּרְעֹה אֶל־יַעֲקֹב כַּמָּה יְמֵי שְׁנֵי חַיֶּיךָ׃
- Now: And Pharaoh asked Jacob, “How many are the years of the days of your life?”
- Proposed: And Pharaoh asked Jacob, “How many are the days of the years of your life?”
- Why: כַּמָּה יְמֵי שְׁנֵי חַיֶּיךָ is 'the days of the years of your life'; the English inverts the construct chain to 'the years of the days'.
