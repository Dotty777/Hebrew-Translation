## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Daniel 11:26** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וְאֹכְלֵי פַת־בָּגוֹ יִשְׁבְּרוּהוּ וְחֵילוֹ יִשְׁטוֹף וְנָפְלוּ חֲלָלִים רַבִּים׃
- Now: Those who eat his choice food will break him. His army will be swept away, and many will fall slain.
- Proposed: Those who eat his choice food will break him. His army will overflow, and many will fall slain.
- Why: וְחֵילוֹ יִשְׁטוֹף is qal active, 'his army will overflow/sweep on'; 'will be swept away' renders a passive (the conjectured niphal יִשָּׁטֵף), not the pointed MT form.
