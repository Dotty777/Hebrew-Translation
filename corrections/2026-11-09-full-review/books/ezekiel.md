## Proposed English corrections (3)

**Ezekiel 31:3** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: הִנֵּה אַשּׁוּר אֶרֶז בַּלְּבָנוֹן יְפֵה עָנָף וְחֹרֶשׁ מֵצַל וּגְבַהּ קוֹמָה וּבֵין עֲבֹתִים הָיְתָה צַמַּרְתּוֹ׃
- Now: Behold, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, beautiful in branches, a shady forest, tall in stature; its top was among the clouds.
- Proposed: Behold, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, beautiful in branches, a shady forest, tall in stature; its top was among the thick boughs.
- Why: MT עֲבֹתִים (plural of עֲבֹת) means interwoven foliage, thick boughs (so Rashi). 'Clouds' follows the Septuagint (nephelon) / the emendation to עָבוֹת, not the pointed Hebrew.

**Ezekiel 31:10** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: לָכֵן כֹּה אָמַר אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה יַעַן אֲשֶׁר גָּבַהְתָּ בְּקוֹמָה וַיִּתֵּן צַמַּרְתּוֹ אֶל־בֵּין עֲבוֹתִים וְרָם לְבָבוֹ בְּגָבְהוֹ׃
- Now: Therefore thus says Lord YHWH: Because you grew tall in stature, and it set its top among the clouds and its heart rose high in its height,
- Proposed: Therefore thus says Lord YHWH: Because you grew tall in stature, and it set its top among the thick boughs and its heart rose high in its height,
- Why: MT אֶל־בֵּין עֲבוֹתִים is 'among the thick boughs' (עֲבֹת, interwoven foliage). 'Clouds' follows the Septuagint / the emendation to עָבוֹת rather than the pointed Hebrew, as in 31:3.

**Ezekiel 31:14** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יִגְבְּהוּ בְקוֹמָתָם כָּל־עֲצֵי־מַיִם וְלֹא־יִתְּנוּ אֶת־צַמַּרְתָּם אֶל־בֵּין עֲבֹתִים וְלֹא־יַעַמְדוּ אֵלֵיהֶם בְּגָבְהָם כָּל־שֹׁתֵי מָיִם כִּי־כֻלָּם נִתְּנוּ לַמָּוֶת אֶל־אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית בְּתוֹךְ בְּנֵי אָדָם אֶל־יוֹרְדֵי בוֹר׃
- Now: so that none of the water-fed trees will rise high in stature or set their tops among the clouds, and none of those drinking water will stand toward them in their height. For all of them are given to death, to the lower land, among human beings, with those descending to the pit.
- Proposed: so that none of the water-fed trees will rise high in stature or set their tops among the thick boughs, and none of those drinking water will stand toward them in their height. For all of them are given to death, to the lower land, among human beings, with those descending to the pit.
- Why: MT אֶל־בֵּין עֲבֹתִים is 'among the thick boughs' (עֲבֹת, interwoven foliage). 'Clouds' follows the Septuagint / the emendation to עָבוֹת rather than the pointed Hebrew, as in 31:3 and 31:10.
