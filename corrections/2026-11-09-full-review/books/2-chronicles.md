## Proposed English corrections (1)

**2 Chronicles 23:14** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיּוֹצֵא יְהוֹיָדָע הַכֹּהֵן אֶת־שָׂרֵי הַמֵּאוֹת פְּקוּדֵי הַחַיִל וַיֹּאמֶר אֲלֵהֶם הוֹצִיאוּהָ אֶל־מִבֵּית הַשְּׂדֵרוֹת וְהַבָּא אַחֲרֶיהָ יוּמַת בֶּחָרֶב כִּי אָמַר הַכֹּהֵן לֹא תְמִיתוּהָ בֵּית יְהוָה׃
- Now: [BHS 23:14] Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders of hundreds appointed over the force and said to them, “Take her outside the ranks, and anyone following her shall be put to death with the sword.” For the priest had said, “Do not put her to death in YHWH's house.”
- Proposed: [BHS 23:14] Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders of hundreds appointed over the force and said to them, “Take her out between the ranks, and anyone following her shall be put to death with the sword.” For the priest had said, “Do not put her to death in YHWH's house.”
- Why: אֶל־מִבֵּית הַשְּׂדֵרוֹת means 'to within/between the ranks' (מִבֵּית = inside); 'outside the ranks' reverses the spatial sense.
