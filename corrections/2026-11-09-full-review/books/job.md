## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Job 5:5** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: אֲשֶׁר קְצִירוֹ רָעֵב יֹאכֵל וְאֶל־מִצִּנִּים יִקָּחֵהוּ וְשָׁאַף צַמִּים חֵילָם׃
- Now: The hungry eat his harvest and take it even from among thorns, and the thirsty pant after their wealth.
- Proposed: The hungry eat his harvest and take it even from among thorns, and robbers pant after their wealth.
- Why: 'the thirsty' reads צְמֵאִים with Aquila, Symmachus, Syriac and Vulgate; MT צַמִּים is 'snare' (so rendered at 18:9) or, with Targum and Rashi, 'robbers'.
