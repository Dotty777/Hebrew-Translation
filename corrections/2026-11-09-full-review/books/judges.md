## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Judges 5:13** · other-text · major

- Hebrew: אָז יְרַד שָׂרִיד לְאַדִּירִים עָם יְהוָה יְרַד־לִי בַּגִּבּוֹרִים׃
- Now: Then a remnant went down to the nobles; YHWH’s people went down for me among the warriors.
- Proposed: Then a remnant went down to the nobles of the people; YHWH went down for me among the warriors.
- Why: MT accents and pausal עָם close the first colon (לְאַדִּירִים עָם); יְהוָה is subject of the second יְרַד. English joins them as 'YHWH's people', the Septuagint's division (λαὸς κυρίου), not the pointed text's.
