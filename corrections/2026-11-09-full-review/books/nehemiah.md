**Nehemiah 10:34** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: לְלֶחֶם הַמַּעֲרֶכֶת וּמִנְחַת הַתָּמִיד וּלְעוֹלַת הַתָּמִיד הַשַּׁבָּתוֹת הֶחֳדָשִׁים לַמּוֹעֲדִים וְלַקֳּדָשִׁים וְלַחַטָּאוֹת לְכַפֵּר עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל וְכֹל מְלֶאכֶת בֵּית־אֱלֹהֵינוּ׃
- Now: \| conventional 10:33 for the bread arranged before God, the regular grain offering and the regular burnt offering, the Sabbaths, new moons and appointed feasts, the holy offerings and the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of the house of our God.
- Proposed: \| conventional 10:33 for the bread of the arrangement, the regular grain offering and the regular burnt offering, the Sabbaths, new moons and appointed feasts, the holy offerings and the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of the house of our God.
- Why: לֶחֶם הַמַּעֲרֶכֶת is 'the bread of the arrangement/row'; the English adds 'before God', which is not in the Hebrew.
