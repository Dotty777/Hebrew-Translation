## Proposed English corrections (2)

**Leviticus 13:55** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וְרָאָה הַכֹּהֵן אַחֲרֵי הֻכַּבֵּס אֶת־הַנֶּגַע וְהִנֵּה לֹא־הָפַךְ הַנֶּגַע אֶת־עֵינוֹ וְהַנֶּגַע לֹא־פָשָׂה טָמֵא הוּא בָּאֵשׁ תִּשְׂרְפֶנּוּ פְּחֶתֶת הִוא בְּקָרַחְתּוֹ אוֹ בְגַבַּחְתּוֹ׃
- Now: After it has been washed, the priest shall examine the lesion. If it has not changed its appearance, though it has not spread, it is unclean. Burn it with fire; it is a corrosive condition on its bare side or its hairy side.
- Proposed: After it has been washed, the priest shall examine the lesion. If it has not changed its appearance, though it has not spread, it is unclean. Burn it with fire; it is a corrosive condition on its back side or its front side.
- Why: בְגַבַּחְתּוֹ is 'its frontal baldness', the bare spot on the front/outer side (paired with בְקָרַחְתּוֹ, back/inner side); neither word means 'hairy'. The contrast is back versus front, not bare versus hairy.

**Leviticus 26:39** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וְהַנִּשְׁאָרִים בָּכֶם יִמַּקּוּ בַּעֲוֺנָם בְּאַרְצֹת אֹיְבֵיכֶם וְאַף בַּעֲוֺנֹת אֲבֹתָם אִתָּם יִמָּקּוּ׃
- Now: Those of you who remain shall waste away in their guilt in their enemies’ lands; also in the guilt of their fathers they shall waste away with them.
- Proposed: Those of you who remain shall waste away in their guilt in your enemies’ lands; also in the guilt of their fathers they shall waste away with them.
- Why: בְּאַרְצֹת אֹיְבֵיכֶם has a second-person plural suffix, 'your enemies' lands'; the English gives 'their enemies' lands' (the Septuagint's third person).
