## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Ezra 8:17** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: ואוצאה [וָאֲצַוֶּה] אוֹתָם עַל־אִדּוֹ הָרֹאשׁ בְּכָסִפְיָא הַמָּקוֹם וָאָשִׂימָה בְּפִיהֶם דְּבָרִים לְדַבֵּר אֶל־אִדּוֹ אָחִיו הנתונים [הַנְּתִינִים] בְּכָסִפְיָא הַמָּקוֹם לְהָבִיא־לָנוּ מְשָׁרְתִים לְבֵית אֱלֹהֵינוּ׃
- Now: I directed them to Iddo, the head at the place Casiphia, and put words in their mouths to speak to Iddo and his brothers the Nethinim at the place Casiphia, to bring us ministers for the house of our God.
- Proposed: I directed them to Iddo, the head at the place Casiphia, and put words in their mouths to speak to Iddo and his brother, the Nethinim at the place Casiphia, to bring us ministers for the house of our God.
- Why: MT אֶל־אִדּוֹ אָחִיו is pointed singular, 'Iddo his brother', with no conjunction; English 'and his brothers' is the plural reading of the versions (Vulgate, 1 Esdras) and modern emendation.
