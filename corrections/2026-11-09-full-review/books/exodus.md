## Proposed English corrections (3)

**Exodus 11:1** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיֹּאמֶר יְהוָה אֶל־מֹשֶׁה עוֹד נֶגַע אֶחָד אָבִיא עַל־פַּרְעֹה וְעַל־מִצְרַיִם אַחֲרֵי־כֵן יְשַׁלַּח אֶתְכֶם מִזֶּה כְּשַׁלְּחוֹ כָּלָה גָּרֵשׁ יְגָרֵשׁ אֶתְכֶם מִזֶּה׃
- Now: YHWH said to Moses, “I will bring one more plague upon Pharaoh and Egypt. Afterward he will let you go from here. When he lets you go, he will drive you out completely.
- Proposed: YHWH said to Moses, “I will bring one more plague upon Pharaoh and Egypt. Afterward he will let you go from here. When he lets you go, he will drive you out from here completely.
- Why: The second מִזֶּה ("from here") in גָּרֵשׁ יְגָרֵשׁ אֶתְכֶם מִזֶּה is left untranslated; the Hebrew says he will drive you out "from here".

**Exodus 29:28** · meaning · major

- Hebrew: וְהָיָה לְאַהֲרֹן וּלְבָנָיו לְחָק־עוֹלָם מֵאֵת בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל כִּי תְרוּמָה הוּא וּתְרוּמָה יִהְיֶה מֵאֵת בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל מִזִּבְחֵי שַׁלְמֵיהֶם תְּרוּמָתָם לַיהוָה׃
- Now: These shall belong to Aaron and his sons by a perpetual statute from the sons of Israel. It is a contribution, taken from the sacrifices of their peace offerings as their contribution to YHWH.
- Proposed: These shall belong to Aaron and his sons by a perpetual statute from the sons of Israel. It is a contribution, and it shall be a contribution from the sons of Israel, taken from the sacrifices of their peace offerings as their contribution to YHWH.
- Why: The clause וּתְרוּמָה יִהְיֶה מֵאֵת בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל ("and it shall be a contribution from the sons of Israel") is collapsed into "taken from"; the second statement and its "from the sons of Israel" are not rendered.

**Exodus 40:38** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: כִּי עֲנַן יְהוָה עַל־הַמִּשְׁכָּן יוֹמָם וְאֵשׁ תִּהְיֶה לַיְלָה בּוֹ לְעֵינֵי כָל־בֵּית־יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּכָל־מַסְעֵיהֶם׃
- Now: For the cloud of YHWH was over the tabernacle by day, and fire was over it by night, in the sight of the whole house of Israel throughout all their journeys.
- Proposed: For the cloud of YHWH was over the tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night, in the sight of the whole house of Israel throughout all their journeys.
- Why: וְאֵשׁ תִּהְיֶה לַיְלָה בּוֹ: bo means 'in it' (the fire was in the cloud), not 'over it'; the English repeats the preposition of עַל־הַמִּשְׁכָּן from the first clause.

## Withdrawn on second reading (1)

- **Exodus 29:46**: אֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם and 'Egypt' name the same place; 'out of Egypt' for מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם is idiomatic compression that loses no meaning.
