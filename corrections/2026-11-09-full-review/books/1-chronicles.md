## Proposed English corrections (3)

**1 Chronicles 2:18** · meaning · major

- Hebrew: וְכָלֵב בֶּן־חֶצְרוֹן הוֹלִיד אֶת־עֲזוּבָה אִשָּׁה וְאֶת־יְרִיעוֹת וְאֵלֶּה בָנֶיהָ יֵשֶׁר וְשׁוֹבָב וְאַרְדּוֹן׃
- Now: Caleb son of Hezron fathered Azubah-ishah and Jerioth. These were her sons: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.
- Proposed: Caleb son of Hezron fathered children by Azubah his wife and by Jerioth. These were her sons: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.
- Why: אִשָּׁה ('wife, woman') is left untranslated and fused into an invented name 'Azubah-ishah', making Azubah Caleb's child; עֲזוּבָה אִשָּׁה is 'Azubah, [his] wife' (cf. 2:19, where she dies and he takes Ephrath).

**1 Chronicles 9:17** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וְהַשֹּׁעֲרִים שַׁלּוּם וְעַקּוּב וְטַלְמֹן וַאֲחִימָן וַאֲחִיהֶם שַׁלּוּם הָרֹאשׁ׃
- Now: The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their brothers; Shallum was the head.
- Proposed: The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, and Ahiman; their brother Shallum was the head.
- Why: וַאֲחִיהֶם is pointed singular, 'and their brother', in apposition to Shallum ('their brother Shallum was the head'); the English makes it a plural 'their brothers' added to the list.

**1 Chronicles 29:15** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: כִּי־גֵרִים אֲנַחְנוּ לְפָנֶיךָ וְתוֹשָׁבִים כְּכָל־אֲבֹתֵינוּ כַּצֵּל יָמֵינוּ עַל־הָאָרֶץ וְאֵין מִקְוֶה׃
- Now: We are strangers before You and resident sojourners, like all our fathers. Our days upon the earth are like a shadow, and there is no abiding hope.
- Proposed: We are strangers before You and resident sojourners, like all our fathers. Our days upon the earth are like a shadow, and there is no hope.
- Why: וְאֵין מִקְוֶה is simply 'and there is no hope'; 'abiding' has no Hebrew counterpart and echoes the KJV's 'none abiding'.
