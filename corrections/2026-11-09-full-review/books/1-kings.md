## Proposed English corrections (2)

**1 Kings 11:23 (additional source reading)** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: וַיָּקֶם אֱלֹהִים לוֹ שָׂטָן אֶת־רְזוֹן בֶּן־אֶלְיָדָע אֲשֶׁר בָּרַח מֵאֵת הֲדַדְעֶזֶר מֶלֶךְ־צוֹבָה אֲדֹנָיו׃
- Now: God raised up another adversary against him: Rezon son of Eliada, who had fled from his lord, Hadadezer king of Zobah.
- Proposed: God raised up an adversary against him: Rezon son of Eliada, who had fled from his lord, Hadadezer king of Zobah.
- Why: Hebrew has only שָׂטָן, "an adversary" (וַיָּקֶם אֱלֹהִים לוֹ שָׂטָן); "another" is not in the text and matches the KJV tradition's addition.

**1 Kings 14:5 (additional source reading)** · other-text · minor

- Hebrew: וַיהוָה אָמַר אֶל־אֲחִיָּהוּ הִנֵּה אֵשֶׁת יָרָבְעָם בָּאָה לִדְרֹשׁ דָּבָר מֵעִמְּךָ אֶל־בְּנָהּ כִּי־חֹלֶה הוּא כָּזֹה וְכָזֶה תְּדַבֵּר אֵלֶיהָ וִיהִי כְבֹאָהּ וְהִיא מִתְנַכֵּרָה׃
- Now: YHWH had said to Ahijah, “Look, Jeroboam’s wife is coming to seek a word from you about her son, because he is ill. Thus and thus shall you speak to her.” When she came, she was disguised.
- Proposed: YHWH had said to Ahijah, “Look, Jeroboam’s wife is coming to seek a word from you about her son, because he is ill. Thus and thus shall you speak to her. When she comes, she will be disguised.”
- Why: MT points וִיהִי (jussive/future, "and it shall be"), so וִיהִי כְבֹאָהּ וְהִיא מִתְנַכֵּרָה continues YHWH's speech as prediction; the English renders the emended narrative וַיְהִי, "When she came, she was disguised", outside the speech.
