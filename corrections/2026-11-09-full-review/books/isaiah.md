## Proposed English corrections (5)

**Isaiah 38:12** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: דּוֹרִי נִסַּע וְנִגְלָה מִנִּי כְּאֹהֶל רֹעִי קִפַּדְתִּי כָאֹרֵג חַיַּי מִדַּלָּה יְבַצְּעֵנִי מִיּוֹם עַד־לַיְלָה תַּשְׁלִימֵנִי׃
- Now: My generation is uprooted and taken away from me like a shepherd's tent. Like a weaver I have cut off my life; He will cut me off from my glory. You will finish me from days and nights.
- Proposed: My generation is uprooted and taken away from me like a shepherd's tent. Like a weaver I have cut off my life; He will cut me off from my glory. From day until night You will finish me.
- Why: מִיּוֹם עַד־לַיְלָה is singular 'from day until night' (עַד = until); 'from days and nights' makes both nouns plural and turns 'until' into 'and', echoing the Targum paraphrase rather than the Hebrew.

**Isaiah 38:13** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: שִׁוִּיתִי עַד־בֹּקֶר כָּאֲרִי כֵּן יְשַׁבֵּר כָּל־עַצְמוֹתָי מִיּוֹם עַד־לַיְלָה תַּשְׁלִימֵנִי׃
- Now: I braced myself until morning like a lion; so it would break all my bones. You will finish me from days and nights.
- Proposed: I braced myself until morning like a lion; so it would break all my bones. From day until night You will finish me.
- Why: מִיּוֹם עַד־לַיְלָה תַּשְׁלִימֵנִי = 'from day until night You make an end of me'; 'from days and nights' pluralises the nouns and renders עַד 'until' as 'and'.

**Isaiah 45:9** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: הוֹי רָב אֶת־יֹצְרוֹ חֶרֶשׂ אֶת־חַרְשֵׂי אֲדָמָה הֲיֹאמַר חֹמֶר לְיֹצְרוֹ מַה־תַּעֲשֶׂה וּפָעָלְךָ אֵין־יָדַיִם לוֹ׃
- Now: Woe to one contending with his Former, a potsherd among earth's potsherds! Does clay say to its potter, ‘What are you making?’ or your work say, ‘He has no place’?
- Proposed: Woe to one contending with his Former, a potsherd among earth's potsherds! Does clay say to its potter, ‘What are you making?’ or your work say, ‘He has no hands’?
- Why: אֵין־יָדַיִם לוֹ is literally 'he has no hands' (dual יָדַיִם, i.e. no skill); 'He has no place' does not render יָדַיִם.

**Isaiah 48:7** · meaning · major

- Hebrew: עַתָּה נִבְרְאוּ וְלֹא מֵאָז וְלִפְנֵי־יוֹם וְלֹא שְׁמַעְתָּם פֶּן־תֹּאמַר הִנֵּה יְדַעְתִּין׃
- Now: They are created now, not long ago; I let you hear before their day, and you had not heard them before, lest you say, ‘Look, I knew them.’
- Proposed: They are created now, not long ago; before their day you had not heard them, lest you say, ‘Look, I knew them.’
- Why: וְלִפְנֵי־יוֹם וְלֹא שְׁמַעְתָּם is one statement, 'and before the day you had not heard them'; the English adds a clause 'I let you hear' with no Hebrew verb behind it and doubles 'before'.

**Isaiah 53:12** · meaning · minor

- Hebrew: לָכֵן אֲחַלֶּק־לוֹ בָרַבִּים וְאֶת־עֲצוּמִים יְחַלֵּק שָׁלָל תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר הֶעֱרָה לַמָּוֶת נַפְשׁוֹ וְאֶת־פֹּשְׁעִים נִמְנָה וְהוּא חֵטְא־רַבִּים נָשָׂא וְלַפֹּשְׁעִים יַפְגִּיעַ׃
- Now: Therefore I will allot him a share in public, and he will divide spoil with the strong, because he poured out his life to death and was counted with transgressors. He bore the sin of many and intercedes for transgressors.
- Proposed: Therefore I will allot him a share among the many, and he will divide spoil with the strong, because he poured out his life to death and was counted with transgressors. He bore the sin of many and intercedes for transgressors.
- Why: בָרַבִּים here is 'among the many/great', parallel to אֶת־עֲצוּמִים 'with the strong' and to רַבִּים 'many' in the same verse; 'in public' is a later idiom, not the sense of the word here.
