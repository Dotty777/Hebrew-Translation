# Hebrew–English editorial revision · 9 November 2026

This revision follows the Westminster Leningrad Codex and the edition’s Literal-Smooth, Rabbinic Priority approach. Accuracy comes first, with attention to phrasing and rhythm when spoken. It builds on the [full 39-book review](../2026-11-09-full-review/README.md).

The English pass revised 43 readings from the audit and 63 further readings selected for spoken flow. The four retained readings have direct support in Rashi. Judges 5:13 follows the Masoretic clause division and the traditional sense of dominion. In Isaiah 38:12–13 the wording is smoother and retains the traditional sense of the repeated line. The spoken-English pass focused on selected passages.

The source pass addressed all 79 pairing findings. Some findings cover grouped rows, so the total is 81 rows. The changes include removing unrelated Hebrew from 12 placeholders that had no equivalent at those positions. It restores 56 missing references on aligned rows. Seven previously absent readings from Joshua and Lamentations are now in additional source files, numbered by the Masoretic Text. Two further source readings preserve verses displaced by pairing repairs in Proverbs and Jeremiah.

The Hebrew and Aramaic were checked against the [pinned Westminster Leningrad Codex dataset](https://github.com/embabel-worlds/realm-tanakh/tree/73eed523b5349ee21f19cfe6061725168ebe827e/reference). The 23,375 existing aligned rows retain their reader references, card IDs, and staging classifications. Structured readings and book text exports were updated together. The remaining coverage list describes the GitHub archive. Staging may already display some readings separately. An independent human language review remains pending.
