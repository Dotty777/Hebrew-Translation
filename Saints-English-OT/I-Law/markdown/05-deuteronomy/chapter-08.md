## Text

**8:1** Every commandment that I command you this day you shall be careful to observe, that you may live and multiply, and enter and possess the land that the LORD swore to your fathers.

**8:2** And you shall remember all the way by which the LORD your God has led you these forty years in the wilderness, to humble you and to test you, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep His commandments or not.

**8:3** And He humbled you, caused you to hunger, and fed you with manna, which you did not know, nor did your fathers know, that He might make you know that man does not live by bread alone, but by everything that goes forth from the mouth of the LORD does man live.

**8:4** Your garment did not wear out upon you, nor did your foot swell, these forty years.

**8:5** Therefore you shall know in your heart that just as a man chastens his son, so the LORD your God chastens you.

**8:6** And you shall keep the commandments of the LORD your God, to walk in His ways and to fear Him.

**8:7** For the LORD your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of brooks of water, of fountains and deep springs issuing forth in the valley and in the hill country;

**8:8** a land of wheat and barley, of vines, fig trees, and pomegranates, a land of olive oil and honey;

**8:9** a land where you shall eat bread without scarcity, where you shall lack nothing; a land whose stones are iron, and out of whose mountains you may hew copper.

**8:10** And you shall eat and be satisfied, and you shall bless the LORD your God for the good land that He has given you.

**8:11** Take heed to yourself, lest you forget the LORD your God by failing to keep His commandments, His judgments, and His statutes that I command you this day,

**8:12** lest, when you have eaten and are satisfied, and have built fine houses and dwell in them,

**8:13** and when your herds and your flocks multiply, and your silver and gold increase for you, and all that belongs to you increases,

**8:14** then your heart be lifted up, and you forget the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage;

**8:15** who led you through the great and dreadful wilderness, with fiery serpents and scorpions and thirsty ground where there was no water; who brought forth water for you out of the flinty rock;

**8:16** who fed you with manna in the wilderness, which your fathers did not know, to humble you and to test you, to do you good in your latter end;

**8:17** and you say in your heart, "My own strength and the might of my hand have acquired this wealth for me."

**8:18** Rather, you shall remember the LORD your God, for it is He who gives you strength to acquire wealth, that He may establish His covenant that He swore to your fathers, as it is this day.

**8:19** And it shall come to pass, if you indeed forget the LORD your God, and walk after other gods, and serve them and bow down to them, I solemnly testify against you this day that you shall surely perish.

**8:20** Like the nations that the LORD causes to perish before you, so shall you perish, because you would not hearken to the voice of the LORD your God.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — Literally 'everything that goes forth from the mouth of the LORD,' denoting every sustaining decree and provision ordained by God rather than bread alone.
- **8:9** — The Hebrew noun miskenut ('scarcity' or 'poverty') appears only here in the Hebrew Bible, and nechoshet refers to copper ore mined from the hills.
- **8:15** — The Hebrew phrase nachash saraf ('fiery serpent') refers to venomous desert snakes whose bite produces a burning pain, recalling Numbers 21:6.
- **8:18** — Verses 17 and 18 deliberately repeat the Hebrew idiom for acquiring wealth by strength, contrasting human pride with the LORD's covenant empowerment.
