## Text

**28:1** And it shall come to pass, if you faithfully heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep and to do all His commandments that I command you this day, that the LORD your God will set you supreme above all the nations of the earth.

**28:2** And all these blessings shall come upon you and overtake you, because you heed the voice of the LORD your God.

**28:3** Blessed shall you be in the city, and blessed shall you be in the field.

**28:4** Blessed shall be the fruit of your womb, and the fruit of your ground, and the fruit of your livestock—the offspring of your herds and the young of your flocks.

**28:5** Blessed shall be your basket and your kneading trough.

**28:6** Blessed shall you be in your coming in, and blessed shall you be in your going out.

**28:7** The LORD will cause your enemies who rise up against you to be struck down before you; they shall come out against you by one way, and flee before you by seven ways.

**28:8** The LORD will command the blessing upon you in your granaries and in all to which you put your hand; and He will bless you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

**28:9** The LORD will establish you as a holy people for Himself, just as He swore to you, if you keep the commandments of the LORD your God and walk in His ways.

**28:10** Then all the peoples of the earth shall see that the name of the LORD is called upon you, and they shall stand in fear of you.

**28:11** And the LORD will make you abound in good—in the fruit of your womb, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground—upon the land that the LORD swore to your fathers to give you.

**28:12** The LORD will open to you His good treasury, the heavens, to give the rain of your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hand. And you shall lend to many nations, yet you yourself shall not borrow.

**28:13** And the LORD will make you the head and not the tail, and you shall be only above, and you shall not be beneath, if you heed the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you this day, to keep and to do them.

**28:14** And you shall not turn aside from any of the words that I command you this day, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

**28:15** But it shall come to pass, if you do not heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep and to do all His commandments and His statutes that I command you this day, that all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you.

**28:16** Cursed shall you be in the city, and cursed shall you be in the field.

**28:17** Cursed shall be your basket and your kneading trough.

**28:18** Cursed shall be the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground, the offspring of your herds and the young of your flocks.

**28:19** Cursed shall you be in your coming in, and cursed shall you be in your going out.

**28:20** The LORD will send upon you cursing, confusion, and rebuke in all to which you put your hand to do, until you are destroyed and until you perish swiftly, because of the wickedness of your deeds by which you have forsaken Me.

**28:21** The LORD will make the pestilence cling to you until He has consumed you from off the land that you are entering to possess.

**28:22** The LORD will strike you with wasting disease, with fever, with inflammation, with fiery heat, with the sword, with blight, and with mildew; and they shall pursue you until you perish.

**28:23** And your heavens that are over your head shall be bronze, and the earth that is beneath you shall be iron.

**28:24** The LORD will turn the rain of your land into powder and dust; from the heavens it shall come down upon you until you are destroyed.

**28:25** The LORD will cause you to be struck down before your enemies; you shall go out against them by one way, and flee before them by seven ways; and you shall become an object of horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**28:26** And your carcass shall be food for every bird of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, and there shall be no one to frighten them away.

**28:27** The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors, with scab, and with the itch, from which you cannot be healed.

**28:28** The LORD will strike you with madness, and with blindness, and with bewilderment of heart.

**28:29** And you shall grope at noonday, just as the blind man gropes in the darkness, and you shall not prosper in your ways; and you shall be only oppressed and plundered continually, and there shall be no one to save you.

**28:30** You shall betroth a wife, yet another man shall lie with her; you shall build a house, yet you shall not dwell in it; you shall plant a vineyard, yet you shall not enjoy its fruit.

**28:31** Your ox shall be slaughtered before your eyes, yet you shall not eat of it; your donkey shall be violently taken away from before your face, and shall not be restored to you; your sheep shall be given to your enemies, and you shall have no one to save you.

**28:32** Your sons and your daughters shall be given to another people, and your eyes shall look on and fail with longing for them all the day long, and there shall be no strength in your hand.

**28:33** A nation whom you have not known shall eat the fruit of your ground and all your labor, and you shall be only oppressed and crushed continually.

**28:34** So you shall be driven mad by the sight of your eyes that you shall behold.

**28:35** The LORD will strike you with grievous boils on the knees and on the legs, from which you cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot even to the crown of your head.

**28:36** The LORD will lead you and your king whom you set over yourself to a nation that neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you shall serve other gods—wood and stone.

**28:37** And you shall become a horror, a proverb, and a taunt among all the peoples where the LORD will lead you away.

**28:38** Much seed you shall carry out to the field, yet little shall you gather in, for the locust shall devour it.

**28:39** You shall plant vineyards and till them, yet you shall neither drink the wine nor gather it in, for the worm shall devour them.

**28:40** You shall have olive trees throughout all your territory, yet you shall not anoint yourself with oil, for your olives shall drop off.

**28:41** You shall father sons and daughters, yet they shall not be yours, for they shall go into captivity.

**28:42** The buzzing locust shall possess all your trees and the fruit of your ground.

**28:43** The sojourner who is in your midst shall rise above you higher and higher, and you shall sink down lower and lower.

**28:44** He shall lend to you, and you shall not lend to him; he shall be the head, and you shall be the tail.

**28:45** Moreover, all these curses shall come upon you and pursue you and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commandments and His statutes that He commanded you.

**28:46** And they shall be upon you as a sign and a wonder, and upon your descendants forever.

**28:47** Because you did not serve the LORD your God with gladness and with goodness of heart for the abundance of all things,

**28:48** therefore you shall serve your enemies whom the LORD will send against you, in hunger, in thirst, in nakedness, and in want of all things; and He will put a yoke of iron upon your neck until He has destroyed you.

**28:49** The LORD will bring a nation against you from afar, from the end of the earth, as the eagle swoops down—a nation whose tongue you will not understand,

**28:50** a nation of fierce countenance, which will neither show regard for the aged nor show favor to the young.

**28:51** And it shall eat the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your ground until you are destroyed; it shall not leave you grain, new wine, or fresh oil, the offspring of your herds or the young of your flocks, until it has caused you to perish.

**28:52** And it shall besiege you within all your gates until your high and fortified walls, in which you trust, come down throughout all your land; indeed, it shall besiege you within all your gates throughout all your land that the LORD your God has given you.

**28:53** And you shall eat the fruit of your own womb, the flesh of your sons and of your daughters whom the LORD your God has given you, in the siege and distress with which your enemy shall distress you.

**28:54** The man among you who is tender and exceedingly delicate—his eye shall be evil toward his brother, and toward the wife of his bosom, and toward the remnant of his children whom he has left,

**28:55** so that he will not give to any of them any of the flesh of his children whom he is eating, because nothing at all is left to him in the siege and distress with which your enemy shall distress you within all your gates.

**28:56** The tender and delicate woman among you, who would not venture to set the sole of her foot upon the ground for delicateness and tenderness—her eye shall be evil toward the husband of her bosom, and toward her son, and toward her daughter,

**28:57** and toward her afterbirth that comes out from between her feet, and toward her children whom she bears; for she shall eat them in secret for lack of all things, in the siege and distress with which your enemy shall distress you within your gates.

**28:58** If you are not careful to do all the words of this law that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and awesome name, the LORD your God,

**28:59** then the LORD will make your plagues and the plagues of your descendants extraordinary—great and enduring plagues, and grievous and enduring sicknesses.

**28:60** And He will bring back upon you all the diseases of Egypt, of which you were in dread, and they shall cling to you.

**28:61** Also every sickness and every plague that is not written in the book of this law, the LORD will bring upon you until you are destroyed.

**28:62** And you shall be left few in number, whereas you had been as the stars of the heavens for multitude, because you did not heed the voice of the LORD your God.

**28:63** And it shall come to pass, that just as the LORD rejoiced over you to do you good and to multiply you, so the LORD will rejoice over you to cause you to perish and to destroy you; and you shall be uprooted from off the land that you are entering to possess.

**28:64** And the LORD will scatter you among all the peoples, from one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth; and there you shall serve other gods, which neither you nor your fathers have known—wood and stone.

**28:65** And among those nations you shall find no ease, nor shall there be a resting place for the sole of your foot; but there the LORD will give you a trembling heart, failing of eyes, and anguish of soul.

**28:66** And your life shall hang in doubt before you; you shall be in dread night and day, and have no assurance of your life.

**28:67** In the morning you shall say, "Oh, that it were evening!" and in the evening you shall say, "Oh, that it were morning!" because of the dread of your heart that you shall dread, and because of the sight of your eyes that you shall behold.

**28:68** And the LORD will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I said to you, "You shall never see it again." And there you shall offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female servants, yet there shall be no buyer.

**28:69** These are the words of the covenant that the LORD commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant that He made with them at Horeb.

## Translator Notes

- **28:22** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes this word as "sword"; the same consonants with different vowel points mean "drought," which some interpreters associate with the surrounding terms for heat and crop blight.
- **28:27** — Translates the Masoretic Qere ("tumors" or "hemorrhoids"); the Ketiv reads "swellings."
- **28:30** — Translates the Masoretic Qere ("shall lie with her"), a euphemistic reading for the Ketiv ("shall violate her").
- **28:32** — Literally "and there is not to the power of your hand," a Hebrew idiom expressing complete helplessness.
- **28:54** — "His eye shall be evil" is a Hebrew idiom meaning to look begrudgingly or stingily upon someone, refusing to share.
- **28:67** — Literally "Who will give evening?" and "Who will give morning?", a Hebrew idiom expressing desperate longing.
- **28:69** — Follows the Masoretic Text versification; most English Bibles number this verse as Deuteronomy 29:1.
