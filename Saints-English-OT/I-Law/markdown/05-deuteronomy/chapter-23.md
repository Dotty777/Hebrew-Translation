## Text

**23:1** A man shall not take his father's wife, nor shall he uncover his father's skirt.

**23:2** One who is wounded by crushing or whose private member is cut off shall not enter the assembly of the LORD.

**23:3** One born of an unlawful union shall not enter the assembly of the LORD; even to the tenth generation, none of his descendants shall enter the assembly of the LORD.

**23:4** No Ammonite or Moabite shall enter the assembly of the LORD; even to the tenth generation, none belonging to them shall enter the assembly of the LORD forever,

**23:5** because they did not meet you with bread and water along the way when you came forth from Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam the son of Beor from Pethor of Mesopotamia to curse you.

**23:6** Yet the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam, but the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loved you.

**23:7** You shall not seek their peace or their welfare all your days forever.

**23:8** You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother; you shall not abhor an Egyptian, because you were a sojourner in his land.

**23:9** The children who are born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of the LORD.

**23:10** When you go forth in encampment against your enemies, then you shall keep yourself from every evil thing.

**23:11** If there is among you any man who is not clean because of an occurrence by night, then he shall go outside the camp; he shall not come within the camp.

**23:12** And it shall be, as evening approaches, he shall wash himself with water; and when the sun sets, he may come into the midst of the camp.

**23:13** Also you shall have an appointed place outside the camp, and you shall go out there.

**23:14** And you shall have a trowel among your gear; and it shall be, when you sit down outside, that you shall dig with it and turn back and cover your excrement.

**23:15** For the LORD your God walks in the midst of your camp to deliver you and to give your enemies over before you; therefore your camp shall be holy, lest He see any indecent thing among you and turn away from you.

**23:16** You shall not surrender to his master a servant who has escaped to you from his master.

**23:17** He shall dwell with you in your midst, in the place that he chooses within any of your gates, wherever it is good for him; you shall not oppress him.

**23:18** There shall be no cult prostitute among the daughters of Israel, nor shall there be a male cult prostitute among the sons of Israel.

**23:19** You shall not bring the wages of a harlot or the price of a dog into the house of the LORD your God for any vow, for both of these are an abomination to the LORD your God.

**23:20** You shall not charge interest to your brother—interest on money, interest on food, or interest on anything that is lent at interest.

**23:21** To a foreigner you may charge interest, but to your brother you shall not charge interest, so that the LORD your God may bless you in every undertaking of your hand in the land that you are entering to possess.

**23:22** When you make a vow to the LORD your God, you shall not delay to fulfill it; for the LORD your God will surely require it of you, and it would be sin in you.

**23:23** But if you refrain from making a vow, there shall be no sin in you.

**23:24** What has gone forth from your lips you shall keep and perform, just as you have vowed as a freewill offering to the LORD your God, which you have spoken with your mouth.

**23:25** When you enter your neighbor's standing grain, you may pluck the heads of grain with your hand, but you shall not swing a sickle upon your neighbor's standing grain.

**23:26** When you enter your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes according to your desire to your fill, but you shall not put any into your vessel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — Hebrew 23:1 corresponds to 22:30 in most English versions, shifting the verse numbering of chapter 23 by one. To 'uncover his father's skirt' is an idiom for violating his father's marital rights.
- **23:3** — The Hebrew term 'mamzer' denotes the child of a strictly forbidden or incestuous union.
- **23:5** — 'Mesopotamia' translates the Hebrew 'Aram-naharaim,' literally 'Aram of the Two Rivers,' referring to the upper Euphrates region.
- **23:19** — 'The price of a dog' is a biblical idiom for the earnings of a male cult prostitute, echoing the prohibition in verse 18.
- **23:25** — In the Saints Hebrew Critical Text, the provision regarding a neighbor's standing grain stands in verse 25 and that regarding a neighbor's vineyard in verse 26, reversing the order in standard printed Masoretic editions.
