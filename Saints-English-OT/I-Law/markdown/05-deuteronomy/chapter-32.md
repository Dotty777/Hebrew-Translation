## Text

**32:1** Give ear, O heavens, that I may speak; and let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

**32:2** May my teaching drop like the rain, may my speech distill like the dew, like gentle rain upon the tender grass, and like abundant showers upon the green herb.

**32:3** For I will proclaim the name of the LORD; ascribe greatness to our God.

**32:4** The Rock—His work is perfect, for all His ways are justice; a God of faithfulness and without iniquity, righteous and upright is He.

**32:5** They have acted corruptly toward Him; they are not His children, because of their blemish—a crooked and twisted generation.

**32:6** Is this how you repay the LORD, O foolish and unwise people? Is He not your Father who acquired you? He has made you and established you.

**32:7** Remember the days of old; consider the years of generation after generation. Ask your father, and he will declare it to you; ask your elders, and they will tell you.

**32:8** When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance, when He divided the sons of mankind, He fixed the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the children of Israel.

**32:9** For the LORD's portion is His people; Jacob is the allotted measure of His inheritance.

**32:10** He found him in a desert land, and in the howling waste of the wilderness; He encircled him, He cared for him, He guarded him as the apple of His eye.

**32:11** Like an eagle that stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, He spread out His wings, He took him, He bore him upon His pinions.

**32:12** The LORD alone guided him, and no foreign god was with Him.

**32:13** He made him ride upon the heights of the earth, and he ate the produce of the field; He nourished him with honey from the crag, and oil from the flinty rock,

**32:14** curds of the herd and milk of the flock, with the fat of lambs and rams of the breed of Bashan, and male goats, with the choicest kernels of wheat; and of the blood of the grape you drank foaming wine.

**32:15** But Jeshurun grew fat and kicked; you grew fat, you grew thick, you became gorged. Then he forsook God who made him, and scorned the Rock of his salvation.

**32:16** They stirred Him to jealousy with foreign gods; with abominations they provoked Him to anger.

**32:17** They sacrificed to demons that were not God, to gods they had never known, new gods that had recently appeared, whom your fathers did not dread.

**32:18** You were unmindful of the Rock who fathered you, and you forgot the God who brought you forth.

**32:19** And the LORD saw it and spurned them, because of the provocation of His sons and His daughters.

**32:20** And He said, "I will hide My face from them; I will see what their end shall be, for they are a perverse generation, children in whom there is no faithfulness.

**32:21** They have stirred Me to jealousy with what is not God; they have provoked Me to anger with their vanities. So I will stir them to jealousy with those who are not a people; with a foolish nation I will provoke them to anger.

**32:22** For a fire is kindled in My wrath, and burns to the lowest Sheol; it devours the earth and its produce, and sets ablaze the foundations of the mountains.

**32:23** I will heap calamities upon them; I will exhaust My arrows against them.

**32:24** They shall be wasted with famine, devoured by burning pestilence and bitter destruction; I will also send the teeth of beasts against them, with the venom of creatures crawling in the dust.

**32:25** Outside, the sword shall bereave, and within the chambers, terror—both young man and virgin, the nursing infant together with the man of gray hair.

**32:26** I said, 'I will scatter them afar; I will make the remembrance of them cease from among mankind,'

**32:27** had I not feared the taunt of the enemy, lest their adversaries should misjudge, lest they should say, 'Our hand is triumphant, and it was not the LORD who has done all this.'"

**32:28** For they are a nation void of counsel, and there is no understanding in them.

**32:29** Oh, that they were wise, that they would comprehend this, that they would discern their latter end!

**32:30** How could one pursue a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them, and the LORD had surrendered them?

**32:31** For their rock is not like our Rock, our enemies themselves being judges.

**32:32** For their vine is from the vine of Sodom and from the fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are grapes of poison, and their clusters are bitter.

**32:33** Their wine is the venom of serpents, and the cruel poison of cobras.

**32:34** "Is this not laid up in store with Me, sealed up within My treasuries?

**32:35** Vengeance is Mine, and recompense, for the time when their foot shall slip; for the day of their calamity is near, and the things destined for them hasten swiftly."

**32:36** For the LORD will vindicate His people and have compassion on His servants, when He sees that their strength is gone, and there is no one remaining, bond or free.

**32:37** And He will say, "Where are their gods, the rock in which they sought refuge,

**32:38** who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their drink offerings? Let them rise up and help you; let them be a shelter over you!

**32:39** See now that I, even I, am He, and there is no God beside Me; I put to death and I make alive; I have wounded and I will heal; and there is none who can deliver out of My hand.

**32:40** For I lift up My hand to heaven and say, 'As I live forever,

**32:41** if I sharpen My flashing sword, and My hand takes hold on judgment, I will render vengeance to My adversaries, and I will repay those who hate Me.

**32:42** I will make My arrows drunk with blood, and My sword shall devour flesh—with the blood of the slain and the captives, from the long-haired heads of the enemy.'"

**32:43** Rejoice, O nations, with His people; for He will avenge the blood of His servants, and render vengeance to His adversaries, and make atonement for His land and His people.

**32:44** So Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Hoshea the son of Nun.

**32:45** And Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel.

**32:46** And he said to them, "Set your hearts upon all the words which I solemnly witness to you today, which you shall command your children to observe carefully—all the words of this law.

**32:47** For it is not an empty word for you, because it is your very life; and by this word you shall prolong your days upon the land which you are crossing over the Jordan to possess."

**32:48** Then the LORD spoke to Moses that very same day, saying,

**32:49** "Go up this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, opposite Jericho, and behold the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the children of Israel as a possession.

**32:50** And die on the mountain to which you ascend, and be gathered to your people, just as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people,

**32:51** because you broke faith with Me in the midst of the children of Israel at the waters of Meribah-Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not hallow Me in the midst of the children of Israel.

**32:52** For you shall see the land from afar, yet you shall not enter there, into the land which I am giving to the children of Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **32:5** — The Masoretic wording is compact and poignant, literally indicating that Israel's moral blemish disqualifies them from being counted as His true children, in sharp contrast to the Rock's perfection in verse 4.
- **32:13** — The Hebrew text has a minor orthographic ketiv/qere variant for "the heights," with the ketiv written plene and the qere vocalized defectively; both carry the same sense.
- **32:42** — The Hebrew term rendered "long-haired" can refer to warriors with unbound hair vowed for battle, or alternatively to the enemy's "leaders" or "princes."
- **32:44** — The Masoretic Text preserves "Hoshea," Joshua's original name prior to its change by Moses in Numbers 13:16.
