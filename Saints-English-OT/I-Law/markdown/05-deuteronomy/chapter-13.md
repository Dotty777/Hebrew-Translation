## Text

**13:1** Every word that I am commanding you, you shall take care to do it; you shall not add to it, nor diminish from it.

**13:2** If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises in your midst, and gives you a sign or a wonder,

**13:3** and the sign or the wonder of which he spoke to you comes to pass, saying, "Let us go after other gods"—which you have not known—"and let us serve them,"

**13:4** you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or to that dreamer of dreams; for the LORD your God is testing you, to know whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**13:5** You shall walk after the LORD your God and fear Him, and keep His commandments and heed His voice; you shall serve Him and cleave to Him.

**13:6** And that prophet or that dreamer of dreams shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of bondage, to turn you astray from the way in which the LORD your God commanded you to walk. So you shall purge the evil from your midst.

**13:7** If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or your daughter, or the wife of your bosom, or your friend who is as your own soul, secretly entices you, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," which neither you nor your fathers have known,

**13:8** of the gods of the peoples who are all around you, near to you or far off from you, from one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth,

**13:9** you shall not consent to him nor listen to him, nor shall your eye pity him, nor shall you spare him nor conceal him;

**13:10** but you shall surely slay him; your hand shall be first against him to put him to death, and afterward the hand of all the people.

**13:11** And you shall stone him with stones so that he dies, because he sought to turn you astray from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

**13:12** Then all Israel shall hear and fear, and shall not again do such an evil thing as this in your midst.

**13:13** If you hear in one of your cities, which the LORD your God is giving you to dwell there, saying,

**13:14** "Worthless men have gone out from your midst and have led astray the inhabitants of their city, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods,'" which you have not known,

**13:15** then you shall inquire, and search out, and ask diligently. And behold, if it is true and the matter is established, that this abomination has been committed in your midst,

**13:16** you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, devoting it to destruction, and all that is in it and its livestock, with the edge of the sword.

**13:17** And you shall gather all its spoil into the midst of its open square, and burn with fire the city and all its spoil wholly to the LORD your God; and it shall be a perpetual heap; it shall not be built again.

**13:18** And nothing of the things devoted to destruction shall cling to your hand, that the LORD may turn from the fierceness of His anger, and grant you mercy, and have compassion on you and multiply you, just as He swore to your fathers,

**13:19** when you heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep all His commandments which I command you this day, to do what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, this verse opens chapter 13; many English Bibles place it as 12:32, shifting the subsequent verses of chapter 13 by one.
- **13:14** — Hebrew literally reads men, sons of worthlessness (or sons of Belial), an idiom for lawless, corrupt, and destructive persons.
- **13:16** — The Hebrew root behind devoting to destruction (and things devoted to destruction in verse 18) denotes persons or property irrevocably placed under the ban and given over wholly to the LORD for total destruction.
- **13:17** — The Hebrew term rendered wholly is also a sacrificial term denoting a whole burnt offering consumed completely by fire.
