## Text

**30:1** And it shall come to pass, when all these things come upon you—the blessing and the curse which I have set before you—and you recall them to your heart among all the nations to which the LORD your God has banished you,

**30:2** and you return to the LORD your God and heed His voice, according to all that I command you this day, you and your children, with all your heart and with all your soul,

**30:3** then the LORD your God shall restore your fortunes and have compassion upon you, and He shall gather you again from all the peoples where the LORD your God has scattered you.

**30:4** Even if your banished ones are at the uttermost end of the heavens, from there the LORD your God shall gather you, and from there He shall take you.

**30:5** And the LORD your God shall bring you into the land which your fathers possessed, and you shall possess it; and He shall do good to you and multiply you beyond your fathers.

**30:6** Moreover, the LORD your God shall circumcise your heart and the heart of your offspring, to love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, that you may live.

**30:7** And the LORD your God shall lay all these curses upon your enemies and upon those who hate you, who persecuted you.

**30:8** And you shall again heed the voice of the LORD, and perform all His commandments which I command you this day.

**30:9** Then the LORD your God shall cause you to abound in all the work of your hand—in the fruit of your womb, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground—for good; for the LORD shall again rejoice over you for good, just as He rejoiced over your fathers,

**30:10** when you heed the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commandments and His statutes written in this Book of the Law, and when you turn to the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**30:11** For this commandment which I command you this day is not too difficult for you, nor is it far away.

**30:12** It is not in heaven, that you should say, "Who shall ascend into heaven for us and fetch it for us, and make us hear it, that we may perform it?"

**30:13** Nor is it beyond the sea, that you should say, "Who shall cross over to the far side of the sea for us and fetch it for us, and make us hear it, that we may perform it?"

**30:14** Rather, the word is exceedingly near to you, in your mouth and in your heart, so that you may perform it.

**30:15** Behold, I have placed before you this day life and good, and death and evil,

**30:16** in that I command you this day to love the LORD your God, to walk in His ways, and to keep His commandments, His statutes, and His judgments, so that you may live and multiply; and the LORD your God shall bless you in the land which you are entering to possess.

**30:17** But if your heart turns away and you will not listen, and you are drawn astray to bow down to other gods and serve them,

**30:18** I declare to you this day that you shall surely perish; you shall not prolong your days upon the land which you are crossing over the Jordan to enter and possess.

**30:19** I summon the heavens and the earth as witnesses against you this day: life and death I have placed before you, the blessing and the curse. Therefore choose life, so that you and your offspring may live,

**30:20** by loving the LORD your God, by heeding His voice, and by clinging fast to Him; for He is your life and the length of your days, so that you may dwell upon the land which the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them.

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — "Restore your fortunes" renders the Hebrew idiom shuv shevut, literally "turn back your captivity," signifying full restoration from exile and affliction.
- **30:6** — "Circumcise your heart" recalls Deuteronomy 10:16, depicting inward spiritual renewal in which the LORD removes stubbornness and enables wholehearted love.
- **30:11** — "Too difficult" translates the Niphal of pala, referring to something too wondrous, cryptic, or beyond human reach and understanding.
