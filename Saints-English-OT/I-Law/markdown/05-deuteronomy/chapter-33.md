## Text

**33:1** And this is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the children of Israel before his death.

**33:2** And he said, "The LORD came from Sinai, and dawned upon them from Seir; He shone forth from Mount Paran, and He came from among the myriads of holy ones; from His right hand went a fiery law for them.

**33:3** Indeed, He loves the peoples; all His holy ones are in Your hand; they bow down at Your feet, and each one receives from Your words.

**33:4** Moses commanded a law for us, a heritage for the congregation of Jacob.

**33:5** And He became King in Jeshurun, when the heads of the people were gathered, the tribes of Israel together.

**33:6** Let Reuben live, and not die, nor let his men be few."

**33:7** And this is for Judah, and he said, "Hear, O LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people; let his hands be sufficient for him, and may You be a help to him against his adversaries."

**33:8** And of Levi he said, "Let Your Thummim and Your Urim belong to Your godly one, whom You tested at Massah, and with whom You contended at the waters of Meribah;

**33:9** who said of his father and of his mother, 'I have not seen him,' nor did he acknowledge his brethren, nor did he know his own children; for they have kept Your word, and they guard Your covenant.

**33:10** They shall teach Your ordinances to Jacob, and Your law to Israel; they shall place incense before You, and whole burnt offerings upon Your altar.

**33:11** Bless his substance, O LORD, and accept the work of his hands; strike through the loins of those who rise against him, and of those who hate him, so that they may not rise again."

**33:12** Of Benjamin he said, "The beloved of the LORD shall dwell securely by Him; He shelters him all the day long, and He dwells between his shoulders."

**33:13** And of Joseph he said, "Blessed of the LORD be his land, with the choice gifts of heaven, with the dew, and with the deep that couches beneath,

**33:14** with the choice harvest of the sun, and with the choice produce of the months,

**33:15** with the finest things of the ancient mountains, and with the choice bounty of the everlasting hills,

**33:16** with the choice gifts of the earth and its fullness, and the favor of Him who dwelt in the bush. Let them come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the head of him who was set apart from his brethren.

**33:17** A firstborn bull—majesty is his, and his horns are the horns of a wild ox; with them he shall gore the peoples together, even to the ends of the earth. Such are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and such are the thousands of Manasseh."

**33:18** And of Zebulun he said, "Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents!

**33:19** They shall summon peoples to the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness; for they shall draw from the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand."

**33:20** And of Gad he said, "Blessed is He who enlarges Gad; he dwells like a lion, and tears away the arm, yes, even the crown of the head.

**33:21** He chose the first part for himself, for there a commander's portion was reserved; he came with the heads of the people; he carried out the righteousness of the LORD, and His judgments with Israel."

**33:22** And of Dan he said, "Dan is a lion's whelp; he shall leap forth from Bashan."

**33:23** And of Naphtali he said, "O Naphtali, satisfied with favor, and full of the blessing of the LORD, possess the west and the south."

**33:24** And of Asher he said, "Blessed above sons be Asher; let him be favored by his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil.

**33:25** Your bolts shall be iron and bronze; and as your days are, so shall your strength be."

**33:26** "There is none like God, O Jeshurun, who rides across the heavens to your help, and on the clouds in His majesty.

**33:27** The eternal God is a dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms; He drove out the enemy from before you, and said, 'Destroy!'

**33:28** So Israel dwelt in safety, the fountain of Jacob alone, in a land of grain and new wine; indeed, his heavens drop down dew.

**33:29** Blessed are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD, the shield of your help and the sword of your majesty! Your enemies shall cringe before you, and you shall tread upon their high places."

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — The Ketiv (written text) reads a single word, eshdat, while the Qere (traditional read text, translated here) divides it into two words, esh dat, meaning "a fiery law."
- **33:5** — "Jeshurun" is a poetic and affectionate title for Israel meaning "the upright one."
- **33:23** — "The west" translates the Hebrew yam (literally "sea"), which may refer either to the western region or specifically to the Sea of Chinnereth (Galilee).
- **33:25** — The Hebrew term rendered "bolts" can also be understood as "sandals," and the rare word translated "strength" occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible.
