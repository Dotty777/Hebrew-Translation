## Text

**22:1** You shall not see your brother's ox or his sheep straying and hide yourself from them; you shall surely return them to your brother.

**22:2** And if your brother is not near to you, or if you do not know him, then you shall gather it into your house, and it shall remain with you until your brother seeks after it; then you shall restore it to him.

**22:3** In like manner you shall do with his donkey, and so you shall do with his garment, and so you shall do with every lost thing of your brother's that is lost from him and you have found; you may not hide yourself.

**22:4** You shall not see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen down along the way and hide yourself from them; you shall surely lift them up with him.

**22:5** A man's apparel shall not be upon a woman, nor shall a man wear a woman's garment, for everyone who does these things is an abomination to the LORD your God.

**22:6** If a bird's nest happens to lie before you along the way, in any tree or on the ground, with young birds or eggs, and the mother is sitting upon the young birds or upon the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the young.

**22:7** You shall surely send the mother away, and the young you may take for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may prolong your days.

**22:8** When you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, so that you do not bring bloodguilt upon your house if anyone falls from it.

**22:9** You shall not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, lest the entire yield become forfeited—the seed that you sow and the produce of the vineyard.

**22:10** You shall not plow with an ox and a donkey together.

**22:11** You shall not wear a mingled fabric, wool and linen together.

**22:12** You shall make tassels for yourself on the four corners of the garment with which you cover yourself.

**22:13** If a man takes a wife, and goes in to her, and hates her,

**22:14** and lays shameful charges against her, and brings out an evil reputation against her, saying, "I took this woman, and when I drew near to her I found no tokens of virginity in her,"

**22:15** then the father and the mother of the young woman shall take and bring forth the tokens of the young woman's virginity to the elders of the city at the gate.

**22:16** And the father of the young woman shall say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as a wife, and he hates her.

**22:17** And behold, he has laid shameful charges, saying, 'I found no tokens of virginity in your daughter.' Yet these are the tokens of my daughter's virginity." Then they shall spread out the garment before the elders of the city.

**22:18** Then the elders of that city shall take the man and chastise him.

**22:19** And they shall fine him one hundred shekels of silver and give them to the father of the young woman, because he has brought an evil reputation upon a virgin of Israel. She shall remain his wife; he may not send her away all his days.

**22:20** But if this matter is true, and tokens of virginity were not found for the young woman,

**22:21** then they shall bring out the young woman to the entrance of her father's house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones until she dies, because she has committed a disgraceful folly in Israel by playing the harlot in her father's house. Thus you shall purge the evil from your midst.

**22:22** If a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, then both of them shall die—the man who lay with the woman, and the woman also. Thus you shall purge the evil from Israel.

**22:23** If there is a young woman, a virgin betrothed to a man, and a man finds her in the city and lies with her,

**22:24** then you shall bring both of them out to the gate of that city, and you shall stone them with stones until they die—the young woman because she did not cry out in the city, and the man because he violated his neighbor's wife. Thus you shall purge the evil from your midst.

**22:25** But if in the field the man finds the betrothed young woman, and the man seizes her and lies with her, then only the man who lay with her shall die.

**22:26** To the young woman you shall do nothing; there is in the young woman no sin worthy of death, for just as when a man rises up against his neighbor and murders him, so is this case.

**22:27** For he found her in the field; the betrothed young woman cried out, yet there was no one to rescue her.

**22:28** If a man finds a young woman, a virgin who is not betrothed, and takes hold of her and lies with her, and they are found out,

**22:29** then the man who lay with her shall give to the father of the young woman fifty shekels of silver, and she shall become his wife because he has violated her; he may not send her away all his days.

## Translator Notes

- **22:5** — The Hebrew phrase rendered 'a man's apparel' literally means 'gear' or 'an article of a man,' encompassing male garments as well as items associated with men such as weapons or equipment.
- **22:9** — The verb translated 'become forfeited' literally means 'become holy' or 'consecrated,' signifying that the mixed yield is withdrawn from ordinary human use and forfeited to the sanctuary.
- **22:15** — Throughout verses 15 through 29, except in verse 19, the Hebrew employs the archaic Pentateuchal spelling of 'young woman' lacking the final feminine letter, while vocalizing it as feminine.
- **22:19** — Hebrew literally reads 'one hundred of silver' here and 'fifty of silver' in verse 29, with the standard weight of a shekel implied.
