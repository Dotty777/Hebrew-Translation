## Text

**6:1** Now this is the commandment—the statutes and the judgments—which the LORD your God has commanded to teach you, that you may perform them in the land into which you are crossing over to possess it,

**6:2** that you may fear the LORD your God, to keep all His statutes and His commandments which I command you—you and your son and your son's son, all the days of your life—and that your days may be prolonged.

**6:3** Hear therefore, O Israel, and take care to perform them, that it may go well with you and that you may multiply exceedingly, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you—a land flowing with milk and honey.

**6:4** Hear, O Israel: the LORD is our God; the LORD is one.

**6:5** And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your might.

**6:6** And these words which I command you this day shall be upon your heart.

**6:7** You shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall speak of them when you sit in your house, when you walk along the way, when you lie down, and when you rise up.

**6:8** And you shall bind them as a sign upon your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

**6:9** And you shall write them upon the doorposts of your house and upon your gates.

**6:10** And it shall come to pass, when the LORD your God brings you into the land which He swore to your fathers—to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob—to give you, with great and fine cities which you did not build,

**6:11** and houses full of all good things which you did not fill, and hewn cisterns which you did not hew, vineyards and olive trees which you did not plant, and you eat and are satisfied,

**6:12** then take heed to yourself, lest you forget the LORD, who brought you forth from the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

**6:13** You shall fear the LORD your God, and Him you shall serve, and by His name you shall swear.

**6:14** You shall not walk after other gods, the gods of the peoples who are all around you,

**6:15** for the LORD your God in your midst is a jealous God—lest the wrath of the LORD your God be kindled against you, and He destroy you from the face of the earth.

**6:16** You shall not test the LORD your God, as you tested Him at Massah.

**6:17** You shall diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and His testimonies and His statutes which He has commanded you.

**6:18** And you shall do what is right and good in the sight of the LORD, that it may go well with you, and that you may enter and possess the good land which the LORD swore to your fathers,

**6:19** by driving out all your enemies from before you, just as the LORD has spoken.

**6:20** When your son asks you in time to come, saying, "What are the testimonies, the statutes, and the judgments which the LORD our God has commanded you?"

**6:21** then you shall say to your son, "We were slaves to Pharaoh in Egypt, and the LORD brought us forth from Egypt with a mighty hand.

**6:22** And the LORD displayed signs and wonders, great and grievous, against Egypt, against Pharaoh, and against all his household, before our eyes.

**6:23** And He brought us out from there, that He might bring us in, to give us the land which He swore to our fathers.

**6:24** And the LORD commanded us to perform all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, for our good always, that He might preserve us alive, as it is this day.

**6:25** And it shall be righteousness for us if we take care to perform all this commandment before the LORD our God, just as He has commanded us."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The compact Hebrew declaration of the Shema may also be rendered "the LORD our God, the LORD is one" or "the LORD our God is one LORD."
- **6:7** — The Hebrew verb rendered "teach them diligently" comes from a root meaning to sharpen, conveying repeated and incisive instruction.
- **6:8** — The Hebrew term "frontlets" refers to memorial bands or phylacteries bound upon the forehead as a visible sign of devotion to the LORD.
