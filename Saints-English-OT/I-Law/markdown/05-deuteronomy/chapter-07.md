## Text

**7:1** When the LORD your God brings you into the land that you are entering to possess, and drives away many nations from before you—the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, seven nations more numerous and mightier than you,

**7:2** and when the LORD your God delivers them before you and you strike them down, you shall utterly devote them to destruction; you shall make no covenant with them, nor show mercy to them.

**7:3** And you shall not intermarry with them; you shall not give your daughter to their son, nor shall you take their daughter for your son.

**7:4** For they will turn your son away from following Me, and they will serve other gods; and the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and He will destroy you swiftly.

**7:5** Rather, thus you shall deal with them: you shall tear down their altars, shatter their sacred pillars, hew down their Asherah poles, and burn their carved images with fire.

**7:6** For you are a holy people to the LORD your God; the LORD your God has chosen you to be for Himself a people of treasured possession, above all the peoples who are upon the face of the earth.

**7:7** It was not because you were more numerous than all the peoples that the LORD set His affection upon you and chose you, for you were the fewest of all the peoples.

**7:8** Rather, because the LORD loved you and because He kept the oath that He swore to your fathers, the LORD brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of bondage, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

**7:9** Therefore know that the LORD your God, He is God, the faithful God, who keeps the covenant and lovingkindness with those who love Him and keep His commandments to a thousand generations,

**7:10** and repays those who hate Him to their face, to destroy them; He will not delay with the one who hates Him, but to his face He will repay him.

**7:11** Therefore you shall keep the commandment, the statutes, and the judgments that I command you this day, to do them.

**7:12** And it shall come to pass, because you hearken to these judgments, and keep and do them, that the LORD your God will keep with you the covenant and the lovingkindness that He swore to your fathers.

**7:13** And He will love you, bless you, and multiply you; He will also bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground—your grain, your new wine, and your fresh oil, the increase of your herds and the young of your flock—upon the land that He swore to your fathers to give you.

**7:14** You shall be blessed above all peoples; there shall not be a barren male or female among you, nor among your livestock.

**7:15** And the LORD will remove from you every sickness; none of the evil diseases of Egypt that you have known will He lay upon you, but He will bring them upon all who hate you.

**7:16** And you shall consume all the peoples whom the LORD your God gives over to you; your eye shall have no pity on them, nor shall you serve their gods, for that would be a snare to you.

**7:17** If you say in your heart, "These nations are more numerous than I; how can I dispossess them?"

**7:18** you shall not fear them; you shall surely remember what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt:

**7:19** the great trials that your eyes beheld, the signs and the wonders, the mighty hand and the outstretched arm, by which the LORD your God brought you out—so shall the LORD your God do to all the peoples of whom you are afraid.

**7:20** Moreover, the LORD your God will send the hornet among them, until those who remain and those who hide themselves from before you have perished.

**7:21** You shall not be terrified before them, for the LORD your God is in your midst, a great and awesome God.

**7:22** And the LORD your God will drive away these nations from before you little by little; you will not be able to make an end of them swiftly, lest the beasts of the field multiply against you.

**7:23** But the LORD your God will deliver them before you, and will throw them into a great panic until they are destroyed.

**7:24** And He will deliver their kings into your hand, and you shall cause their name to perish from under heaven; no man shall stand before you until you have destroyed them.

**7:25** The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire; you shall not covet the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it for yourself, lest you be ensnared by it, for it is an abomination to the LORD your God.

**7:26** Nor shall you bring an abomination into your house, and become devoted to destruction like it; you shall utterly detest it and utterly abhor it, for it is a thing devoted to destruction.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Hebrew infinitive absolute construction from the root charam expresses total devotion under the ban, irrevocably setting the Canaanite nations apart for destruction.
- **7:9** — The Masoretic text writes 'His commandments' in a defective spelling without the plural yod, read as the plural 'His commandments' here and again in 8:2.
- **7:13** — The Hebrew terms shegar alafeka and ashterot tsoneka refer respectively to the calving increase of cattle and the lambing offspring of flocks, underscoring covenant fertility.
- **7:26** — The noun cherem ('a thing devoted to destruction') echoes the command of verse 2, warning that bringing forbidden idolatrous objects into a home brings the ban upon the household itself.
