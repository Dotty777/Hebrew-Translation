## Text

**26:1** And it shall be, when you have come into the land which the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you possess it and dwell in it,

**26:2** that you shall take some of the first of all the fruit of the ground, which you shall bring in from your land that the LORD your God is giving you, and put it in a basket, and go to the place which the LORD your God chooses to make His name dwell there.

**26:3** And you shall go to the priest who is in office in those days and say to him, "I declare today to the LORD your God that I have come to the land which the LORD swore to our fathers to give us."

**26:4** Then the priest shall take the basket from your hand and set it down before the altar of the LORD your God.

**26:5** And you shall answer and say before the LORD your God, "A wandering Aramean was my father; and he went down to Egypt and sojourned there, few in number, and there he became a nation, great, mighty, and populous.

**26:6** But the Egyptians mistreated us, afflicted us, and laid hard bondage upon us.

**26:7** Then we cried out to the LORD, the God of our fathers, and the LORD heard our voice and looked upon our affliction and our labor and our oppression.

**26:8** And the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand and with an outstretched arm, with great terror and with signs and wonders.

**26:9** He has brought us to this place and has given us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**26:10** And now, behold, I have brought the first of the fruit of the ground which You, O LORD, have given me." Then you shall set it before the LORD your God, and worship before the LORD your God.

**26:11** And you shall rejoice in every good thing which the LORD your God has given to you and to your house, you and the Levite and the sojourner who is in your midst.

**26:12** When you have finished tithing all the tithe of your increase in the third year, the year of tithing, and have given it to the Levite, to the sojourner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, that they may eat within your gates and be satisfied,

**26:13** then you shall say before the LORD your God, "I have removed the sacred portion from the house, and also have given it to the Levite and to the sojourner, to the fatherless and to the widow, according to all Your commandment which You have commanded me; I have not transgressed Your commandments, nor have I forgotten them.

**26:14** I have not eaten of it in my mourning, nor have I removed any of it while unclean, nor have I given any of it for the dead; I have obeyed the voice of the LORD my God, and have done according to all that You have commanded me.

**26:15** Look down from Your holy habitation, from heaven, and bless Your people Israel and the ground which You have given us, just as You swore to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey."

**26:16** This day the LORD your God commands you to perform these statutes and judgments; therefore you shall keep and do them with all your heart and with all your soul.

**26:17** Today you have declared the LORD to be your God, and that you will walk in His ways and keep His statutes, His commandments, and His judgments, and that you will heed His voice.

**26:18** And today the LORD has declared you to be His treasured people, just as He promised you, and that you shall keep all His commandments,

**26:19** and that He will set you high above all the nations which He has made, in praise, in name, and in honor, and that you may be a holy people to the LORD your God, just as He has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **26:5** — "A wandering Aramean" may also be translated "a perishing Aramean," referring to the patriarch Jacob during his precarious years in Aram and his later descent into Egypt.
- **26:14** — "In my mourning" refers to bereavement over the dead, which rendered a person ritually unfit to eat consecrated portions; "for the dead" alludes to ancient Near Eastern funerary food offerings.
- **26:17** — The rare Hebrew causative verb used here and reciprocally in verse 18 signifies a solemn, binding covenant declaration between Israel and the LORD.
