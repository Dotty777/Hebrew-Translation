## Text

**29:1** And Moses called to all Israel and said to them, "You yourselves have seen all that the LORD did before your eyes in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land—

**29:2** the great testings which your eyes saw, those great signs and wonders.

**29:3** Yet the LORD has not given you a heart to know, nor eyes to see, nor ears to hear, until this day.

**29:4** And I led you forty years in the wilderness; your garments did not wear out upon you, and your sandal did not wear out upon your foot.

**29:5** You did not eat bread, nor did you drink wine or strong drink, so that you might know that I am the LORD your God.

**29:6** And when you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us in battle, and we struck them down.

**29:7** And we took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, to the Gadites, and to the half-tribe of the Manassites.

**29:8** Therefore you shall keep the words of this covenant and perform them, so that you may prosper in all that you do.

**29:9** All of you are standing this day before the LORD your God: your heads, your tribes, your elders, and your officers, every man of Israel,

**29:10** your little ones, your wives, and the sojourner who is in the midst of your camps, from the cutter of your wood to the drawer of your water,

**29:11** that you may enter into the covenant of the LORD your God, and into His sworn oath, which the LORD your God makes with you this day,

**29:12** in order that He may establish you this day as a people for Himself, and that He may be God to you, just as He spoke to you, and just as He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

**29:13** Nor is it with you alone that I am making this covenant and this sworn oath,

**29:14** but both with the one who stands here with us this day before the LORD our God, and with the one who is not here with us this day.

**29:15** For you yourselves know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, and how we passed through the midst of the nations through which you passed.

**29:16** And you saw their detestable things and their idols—wood and stone, silver and gold—which were among them.

**29:17** Beware lest there be among you a man or woman, or family or tribe, whose heart turns away this day from the LORD our God to go and serve the gods of those nations—lest there be among you a root bearing gall and wormwood;

**29:18** and it shall come to pass, when he hears the words of this sworn curse, that he blesses himself in his heart, saying, 'Peace shall be mine, even though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart'—to sweep away the watered along with the dry.

**29:19** The LORD will not be willing to forgive him; rather, the anger of the LORD and His jealousy shall smoke against that man, and every curse written in this book shall settle upon him, and the LORD shall blot out his name from under heaven.

**29:20** And the LORD shall separate him for calamity from all the tribes of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant written in this Book of the Law.

**29:21** Then the coming generation—your children who shall rise up after you, and the foreigner who shall come from a distant land—shall speak, when they see the plagues of that land and the sicknesses with which the LORD has afflicted it,

**29:22** seeing that all its land is brimstone and salt, a burning waste, neither sown nor bringing forth growth, nor does any grass spring up in it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in His anger and in His wrath—

**29:23** all the nations shall say, 'Why has the LORD done thus to this land? What does the heat of this great anger mean?'

**29:24** Then men shall answer, 'Because they forsook the covenant of the LORD, the God of their fathers, which He made with them when He brought them out of the land of Egypt;

**29:25** for they went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods whom they had not known and whom He had not allotted to them.

**29:26** Therefore the anger of the LORD burned against that land, to bring upon it every curse written in this book;

**29:27** and the LORD uprooted them from their soil in anger, in wrath, and in great indignation, and cast them into another land, as it is this day.'

**29:28** The hidden things belong to the LORD our God, but the things revealed are for us and for our children forever, that we may perform all the words of this law."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — In the Masoretic Text, Deuteronomy 29:1-28 corresponds to 29:2-29 in most English Bibles because the covenant superscription appears as 28:69.
- **29:4** — In verses 4-5 Moses shifts directly into first-person divine speech, proclaiming the LORD's wilderness provision as His covenant mediator.
- **29:9** — Literally "your heads, your tribes"; the Masoretic Text juxtaposes the two nouns in apposition to address both the tribal leaders and the tribes as a whole.
- **29:18** — The Hebrew idiom "to sweep away the watered along with the dry" conveys sweeping, indiscriminate ruin, sparing neither the sated nor the craving.
- **29:28** — The Masoretic Text marks the Hebrew words for "for us and for our children forever" with extraordinary dots (puncta extraordinaria), traditionally highlighting Israel's enduring responsibility to obey the revealed law.
