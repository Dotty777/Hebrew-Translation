## Text

**18:1** The Levitical priests—all the tribe of Levi—shall have no portion nor inheritance with Israel; they shall eat the offerings made by fire to the LORD, and His inheritance.

**18:2** They shall have no inheritance in the midst of their brethren; the LORD is their inheritance, as He spoke to them.

**18:3** Now this shall be the priests' due from the people, from those who offer a sacrifice, whether an ox or a sheep: one shall give to the priest the shoulder, the two cheeks, and the stomach.

**18:4** The firstfruits of your grain, of your new wine, and of your fresh oil, and the first of the fleece of your sheep, you shall give to him.

**18:5** For from among all your tribes the LORD your God has chosen him to stand and minister in the name of the LORD, both he and his sons forever.

**18:6** And if a Levite comes from any of your gates out of all Israel where he sojourns, and comes with all the desire of his soul to the place that the LORD shall choose,

**18:7** then he shall minister in the name of the LORD his God, like all his brethren the Levites who stand there before the LORD.

**18:8** They shall eat equal portions, apart from what he receives from the sale of his ancestral property.

**18:9** When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, you shall not learn to follow the abominations of those nations.

**18:10** There shall not be found among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, or one who practices divination, or a soothsayer, or one who interprets omens, or a sorcerer,

**18:11** or one who casts spells, or one who consults a medium or a spiritist, or one who inquires of the dead.

**18:12** For whoever does these things is an abomination to the LORD, and on account of these abominations the LORD your God is driving them out from before you.

**18:13** You shall be blameless with the LORD your God.

**18:14** For these nations, which you are about to dispossess, listen to soothsayers and diviners; but as for you, the LORD your God has not permitted you to do so.

**18:15** A prophet from your midst, from among your brethren, like me, the LORD your God shall raise up for you; to him you shall listen,

**18:16** according to all that you asked of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, "May I not hear again the voice of the LORD my God, nor see this great fire any more, lest I die."

**18:17** And the LORD said to me, "They have done well in what they have spoken.

**18:18** I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brethren, and I will put My words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them everything that I command him.

**18:19** And it shall come to pass that whoever will not heed My words, which he shall speak in My name, I Myself will require it of him.

**18:20** Yet the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods—that prophet shall die."

**18:21** And if you say in your heart, "How may we know the word that the LORD has not spoken?"

**18:22** When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD, if the thing does not happen nor come to pass, that is the word which the LORD has not spoken; the prophet has spoken it presumptuously—you shall not stand in dread of him.

## Translator Notes

- **18:8** — The terse Hebrew phrase levad mimkarav al-ha'avot indicates that any income a Levite retains from selling ancestral family property does not reduce his equal share in sanctuary provisions.
- **18:10** — Making a son or daughter pass through the fire refers to pagan child sacrifice, listed together with divination, sorcery, and necromancy as forbidden practices of the surrounding nations.
- **18:15** — Moses promises that the LORD will raise up a prophet like himself from among Israel to mediate God's word, a promise fulfilled both in Israel's true prophets and ultimately in the Messiah.
