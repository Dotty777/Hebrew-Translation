## Text

**4:1** And now, O Israel, listen to the statutes and the judgments which I am teaching you to perform, that you may live, and enter in and possess the land which the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving you.

**4:2** You shall not add to the word which I command you, nor take away from it, that you may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I am commanding you.

**4:3** Your own eyes have seen what the LORD did at Baal Peor; for every man who went after Baal Peor, the LORD your God destroyed him from your midst.

**4:4** But you who held fast to the LORD your God are alive, all of you, this day.

**4:5** Behold, I have taught you statutes and judgments, just as the LORD my God commanded me, so that you may do accordingly in the midst of the land which you are entering to possess.

**4:6** Observe them therefore and perform them, for that is your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the peoples, who shall hear all these statutes and say, "Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people."

**4:7** For what great nation is there that has a god so near to it as the LORD our God is, whenever we call upon Him?

**4:8** And what great nation is there that has statutes and judgments so righteous as all this law which I set before you this day?

**4:9** Only take heed to yourself, and guard your soul diligently, lest you forget the things which your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life; and make them known to your children and to your children's children—

**4:10** the day when you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb, when the LORD said to me, "Assemble the people to Me, and I will cause them to hear My words, that they may learn to fear Me all the days that they live upon the earth, and that they may teach their children."

**4:11** Then you drew near and stood at the foot of the mountain, while the mountain burned with fire to the very heart of heaven, with darkness, cloud, and thick gloom.

**4:12** And the LORD spoke to you from the midst of the fire. You heard the sound of words, yet saw no form; there was only a voice.

**4:13** So He declared to you His covenant which He commanded you to perform, the Ten Commandments; and He wrote them upon two tablets of stone.

**4:14** And the LORD commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and judgments, that you might perform them in the land into which you are crossing over to possess.

**4:15** Therefore guard your souls very carefully, for you saw no form on the day when the LORD spoke to you at Horeb from the midst of the fire,

**4:16** lest you act corruptly and make for yourselves a carved image in the form of any figure: the likeness of male or female,

**4:17** the likeness of any beast that is upon the earth, or the likeness of any winged bird that flies in the heavens,

**4:18** the likeness of anything that creeps upon the ground, or the likeness of any fish that is in the waters beneath the earth;

**4:19** and lest you lift up your eyes toward heaven, and when you see the sun, the moon, and the stars, even all the host of heaven, you be led astray and bow down to them and serve them—which the LORD your God has allotted to all the peoples under the whole heaven.

**4:20** But the LORD took you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be a people of His own inheritance, as you are this day.

**4:21** Furthermore, the LORD was angry with me on your account, and swore that I should not cross over the Jordan, and that I should not enter that good land which the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.

**4:22** For I must die in this land; I am not crossing over the Jordan. But you shall cross over and take possession of this good land.

**4:23** Take heed to yourselves, lest you forget the covenant of the LORD your God which He made with you, and make for yourselves a carved image in the form of anything which the LORD your God has forbidden you.

**4:24** For the LORD your God is a consuming fire—a jealous God.

**4:25** When you father children and grandchildren, and have grown old in the land, and act corruptly and make a carved image in the form of anything, and do evil in the sight of the LORD your God to provoke Him to anger,

**4:26** I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day that you shall surely and swiftly perish from the land which you are crossing the Jordan to possess; you shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall be utterly destroyed.

**4:27** And the LORD shall scatter you among the peoples, and you shall remain few in number among the nations where the LORD will drive you.

**4:28** And there you shall serve gods, the work of men's hands, wood and stone, which neither see nor hear nor eat nor smell.

**4:29** Yet from there you will seek the LORD your God, and you shall find Him, if you search for Him with all your heart and with all your soul.

**4:30** When you are in distress, and all these things come upon you in the latter days, you shall return to the LORD your God and obey His voice.

**4:31** For the LORD your God is a merciful God; He will not forsake you nor destroy you, nor forget the covenant of your fathers which He swore to them.

**4:32** For ask now concerning the former days that were before you, since the day God created man upon the earth, and from one end of heaven to the other: has anything so great as this ever come to pass, or has anything like it ever been heard?

**4:33** Has any people ever heard the voice of God speaking from the midst of the fire, as you yourself have heard, and lived?

**4:34** Or has any god ever attempted to go and take for himself a nation from the midst of another nation, by trials, by signs, by wonders, by war, by a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, and by great terrors, according to all that the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

**4:35** To you it was shown, that you might know that the LORD, He is God; there is no other besides Him.

**4:36** Out of heaven He caused you to hear His voice, that He might discipline you; and upon the earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words from the midst of the fire.

**4:37** And because He loved your fathers, He chose their descendants after them; and He brought you out of Egypt with His own presence, by His great power,

**4:38** to drive out from before you nations greater and mightier than you, to bring you in, and to give you their land for an inheritance, as it is this day.

**4:39** Know therefore this day, and lay it to your heart, that the LORD, He is God in heaven above and on the earth beneath; there is no other.

**4:40** Therefore you shall keep His statutes and His commandments which I command you this day, that it may go well with you and with your children after you, and that you may prolong your days upon the land which the LORD your God is giving you for all time.

**4:41** Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun,

**4:42** that the manslayer might flee there, who kills his neighbor unintentionally, without having hated him in times past, and that fleeing to one of these cities he might live:

**4:43** Bezer in the wilderness, in the tableland, for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.

**4:44** Now this is the law which Moses set before the children of Israel.

**4:45** These are the testimonies, the statutes, and the judgments which Moses spoke to the children of Israel after they came out of Egypt,

**4:46** beyond the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt at Heshbon, whom Moses and the children of Israel struck down after they came out of Egypt.

**4:47** And they took possession of his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun,

**4:48** from Aroer, which is on the bank of the Valley of the Arnon, even to Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,

**4:49** and all the Arabah beyond the Jordan eastward, even to the Sea of the Arabah, beneath the slopes of Pisgah.

## Translator Notes

- **4:13** — Hebrew reads literally 'the ten words,' the traditional designation for the Ten Commandments.
- **4:23** — The Hebrew verb meaning 'commanded you' here carries the contextual sense 'forbidden you,' referring back to the prohibition against making graven images.
- **4:37** — Hebrew uses singular collective pronouns ('his descendants after him'), referring to the patriarchs as a whole.
- **4:48** — Mount Sion (Hebrew Si'on) is an alternate name for Mount Hermon and is distinct from Mount Zion (Tsiyyon) in Jerusalem.
