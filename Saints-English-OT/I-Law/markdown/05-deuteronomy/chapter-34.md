## Text

**34:1** Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the summit of Pisgah, which is opposite Jericho. And the LORD showed him all the land—Gilead as far as Dan,

**34:2** all of Naphtali, the territory of Ephraim and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah to the Western Sea,

**34:3** and the Negev, and the plain—the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees—as far as Zoar.

**34:4** Then the LORD said to him, "This is the land of which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you shall not cross over there."

**34:5** Thus Moses, the servant of the LORD, died there in the land of Moab at the command of the LORD.

**34:6** And He buried him in the valley in the land of Moab, facing Beth Peor; and to this day no one knows his burial place.

**34:7** Now Moses was one hundred and twenty years old when he died; his eye had not grown dim, nor had his vigor departed.

**34:8** And the children of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days; then the days of weeping and mourning for Moses came to an end.

**34:9** Now Joshua the son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom, because Moses had laid his hands upon him; so the children of Israel listened to him, and did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**34:10** And no prophet has arisen since in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face,

**34:11** in all the signs and wonders that the LORD sent him to perform in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh, to all his servants, and to all his land,

**34:12** and in all the mighty power and all the great and awesome deeds that Moses performed in the sight of all Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — "The Western Sea" literally translates "the hinder sea," denoting the Mediterranean Sea.
- **34:5** — Lit. at the mouth of the LORD
- **34:6** — The Hebrew verb "buried" leaves its singular subject implicit, traditionally understood as the LORD Himself.
