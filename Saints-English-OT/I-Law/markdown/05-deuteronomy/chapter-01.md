## Text

**1:1** These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel across the Jordan, in the wilderness, in the Arabah facing Suph, between Paran and Tophel, and Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab.

**1:2** It is an eleven-day journey from Horeb along the way of Mount Seir to Kadesh Barnea.

**1:3** It came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first of the month, that Moses spoke to the children of Israel according to everything the LORD had commanded him concerning them,

**1:4** after he had struck down Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who dwelt in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.

**1:5** Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses undertook to expound this law, saying:

**1:6** The LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb, saying, "You have dwelt at this mountain long enough.

**1:7** Turn and set out on your journey, and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all its neighboring regions, in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the lowland, in the Negev, and along the seacoast—the land of the Canaanites and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the River Euphrates.

**1:8** Behold, I have set the land before you; go in and take possession of the land which the LORD swore to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—to give to them and to their seed after them."

**1:9** And I spoke to you at that time, saying, "I am not able to bear you by myself.

**1:10** The LORD your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are this day as the stars of heaven in multitude.

**1:11** May the LORD, the God of your fathers, increase you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you just as He has promised you!

**1:12** How can I by myself bear your trouble, your burden, and your strife?

**1:13** Choose for yourselves wise, discerning, and well-known men from your tribes, and I will appoint them as heads over you."

**1:14** And you answered me and said, "The thing that you have spoken is good to do."

**1:15** So I took the heads of your tribes, wise and well-known men, and appointed them as heads over you: commanders of thousands, commanders of hundreds, commanders of fifties, commanders of tens, and officers for your tribes.

**1:16** And I charged your judges at that time, saying, "Hear the cases between your brethren, and judge righteously between a man and his brother, or the sojourner who is with him.

**1:17** You shall not show partiality in judgment; you shall hear the small as well as the great. You shall not be afraid of the face of any man, for the judgment belongs to God. And the case that is too hard for you, you shall bring to me, and I will hear it."

**1:18** And I commanded you at that time all the things that you should do.

**1:19** Then we set out from Horeb and journeyed through all that great and fearsome wilderness which you saw, by way of the hill country of the Amorites, as the LORD our God had commanded us; and we came to Kadesh Barnea.

**1:20** And I said to you, "You have come to the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God is giving us.

**1:21** Behold, the LORD your God has set the land before you; go up and take possession of it, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you. Do not fear, and do not be dismayed."

**1:22** Then all of you came near to me and said, "Let us send men before us, that they may search out the land for us and bring back word to us concerning the way by which we should go up, and the cities to which we shall come."

**1:23** And the word seemed good in my eyes, so I took twelve men from among you, one man from each tribe.

**1:24** And they turned and went up into the hill country, and came to the Valley of Eshcol, and spied it out.

**1:25** They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us; and they brought back word to us and said, "It is a good land that the LORD our God is giving us."

**1:26** Yet you were not willing to go up, but rebelled against the command of the LORD your God.

**1:27** And you murmured in your tents and said, "Because the LORD hated us, He has brought us out of the land of Egypt to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us.

**1:28** Where are we going up? Our brethren have made our hearts melt, saying, 'The people are greater and taller than we; the cities are great and fortified up to heaven; moreover we have seen the sons of the Anakim there.'"

**1:29** Then I said to you, "Do not be terrified, nor be afraid of them.

**1:30** The LORD your God, who goes before you, He Himself will fight for you, according to all that He did for you in Egypt before your eyes,

**1:31** and in the wilderness where you saw how the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his own son, through all the way that you walked until you came to this place."

**1:32** Yet in this matter you did not trust in the LORD your God,

**1:33** who went before you along the way to seek out a place for you to encamp, in fire by night to show you the way in which you should walk, and in a cloud by day.

**1:34** And the LORD heard the sound of your words, and was angry and swore, saying,

**1:35** "Surely not one of these men, this evil generation, shall see the good land that I swore to give to your fathers,

**1:36** except Caleb the son of Jephunneh; he shall see it, and to him and to his children I will give the land on which he has walked, because he has wholly followed the LORD."

**1:37** The LORD was also angry with me on your account, saying, "You also shall not enter there.

**1:38** Joshua the son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall go in there. Strengthen him, for he shall cause Israel to inherit it.

**1:39** Moreover your little ones, whom you said would become plunder, and your children, who this day do not know good from evil, they shall enter there; to them I will give it, and they shall possess it.

**1:40** But as for you, turn back and journey into the wilderness by way of the Red Sea."

**1:41** Then you answered and said to me, "We have sinned against the LORD; we ourselves will go up and fight, according to all that the LORD our God commanded us." And every man of you girded on his weapons of war, and you deemed it an easy thing to go up into the hill country.

**1:42** And the LORD said to me, "Say to them, 'Do not go up, nor fight, for I am not in your midst, lest you be struck down before your enemies.'"

**1:43** So I spoke to you, yet you would not listen; rather you rebelled against the command of the LORD, and acted presumptuously and went up into the hill country.

**1:44** Then the Amorites who dwelt in that hill country came out against you and pursued you as bees do, and beat you down in Seir as far as Hormah.

**1:45** And you returned and wept before the LORD, but the LORD would not listen to your voice, nor give ear to you.

**1:46** So you remained in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you dwelt there.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — "Beyond the Jordan" renders the Hebrew expression for the territory across the Jordan River, here referring to the eastern side in the plains of Moab. "Arabah" denotes the Jordan rift valley and its southern arid continuation.
- **1:17** — "Show partiality" translates the Hebrew idiom literally meaning "recognize faces," forbidding judges from favoring any person in court.
- **1:40** — "Red Sea" is the traditional rendering of Hebrew Yam Suph, literally "Sea of Reeds," here pointing toward the northeastern arm of the Red Sea.
- **1:41** — "Deemed it an easy thing" translates a rare Hebrew verb meaning to regard a task as light or to venture forth rashly and presumptuously.
