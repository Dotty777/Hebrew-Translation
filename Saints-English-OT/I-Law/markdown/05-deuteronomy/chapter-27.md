## Text

**27:1** Then Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Observe the whole commandment that I am commanding you today.

**27:2** And it shall be, on the day when you cross over the Jordan into the land which the LORD your God is giving you, that you shall set up for yourself large stones and coat them with plaster.

**27:3** You shall write upon them all the words of this law when you have crossed over, so that you may enter into the land which the LORD your God is giving you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you.

**27:4** And it shall be, when you have crossed over the Jordan, that you shall set up these stones, which I am commanding you today, on Mount Ebal, and you shall coat them with plaster.

**27:5** And there you shall build an altar to the LORD your God, an altar of stones; you shall not wield iron upon them.

**27:6** With whole stones you shall build the altar of the LORD your God, and upon it you shall offer up burnt offerings to the LORD your God.

**27:7** And you shall sacrifice peace offerings, and eat there, and rejoice before the LORD your God.

**27:8** And you shall write upon the stones all the words of this law very clearly."

**27:9** Then Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Keep silence and hear, O Israel: this very day you have become a people for the LORD your God.

**27:10** You shall therefore heed the voice of the LORD your God, and perform His commandments and His statutes which I am commanding you today."

**27:11** And Moses charged the people on that day, saying,

**27:12** "These shall stand to bless the people on Mount Gerizim when you cross over the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.

**27:13** And these shall stand for the curse on Mount Ebal: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.

**27:14** Then the Levites shall declare and say to every man of Israel with a loud voice:

**27:15** 'Cursed is the man who makes a carved or molten image, an abomination to the LORD, the work of the hands of a craftsman, and sets it up in secret.' And all the people shall answer and say, 'Amen.'

**27:16** 'Cursed is the one who dishonors his father or his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:17** 'Cursed is the one who shifts the landmark of his neighbor.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:18** 'Cursed is the one who misleads a blind man along the way.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:19** 'Cursed is the one who perverts the justice of the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:20** 'Cursed is the one who lies with the wife of his father, for he has uncovered the covering of his father.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:21** 'Cursed is the one who lies with any beast.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:22** 'Cursed is the one who lies with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:23** 'Cursed is the one who lies with his mother-in-law.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:24** 'Cursed is the one who strikes down his neighbor in secret.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:25** 'Cursed is the one who accepts a bribe to strike down a life of innocent blood.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:26** 'Cursed is the one who does not uphold the words of this law to perform them.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'"

## Translator Notes

- **27:6** — "Whole stones" translates Hebrew shlemot, denoting complete, unhewn natural stones untouched by iron tools (compare verse 5).
- **27:9** — "Keep silence" renders a rare Hebrew imperative occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible, calling Israel to hushed, attentive reverence.
- **27:20** — "The covering of his father" literally reads "the skirt" or "edge of his father," a Hebrew idiom for the garment or blanket symbolizing marital union.
- **27:26** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "the words of this law" without "all," whereas some ancient versions and Galatians 3:10 include "all."
