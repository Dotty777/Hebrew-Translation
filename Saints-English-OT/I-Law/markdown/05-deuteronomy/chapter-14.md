## Text

**14:1** You are children of the LORD your God; you shall not cut yourselves nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.

**14:2** For you are a holy people to the LORD your God, and the LORD has chosen you to be for Him a people of treasured possession, above all the peoples who are on the face of the earth.

**14:3** You shall not eat any abominable thing.

**14:4** These are the beasts which you may eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat,

**14:5** the hart, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountain sheep.

**14:6** And every beast that parts the hoof, having the hoof cloven into two, and chews the cud among the beasts, that you may eat.

**14:7** Nevertheless, of those that chew the cud or of those that have the cloven hoof, you shall not eat these: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger; for they chew the cud, yet do not part the hoof; they are unclean to you.

**14:8** And the swine, because it parts the hoof yet does not chew the cud, it is unclean to you. You shall not eat of their flesh, nor shall you touch their dead carcass.

**14:9** These you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales you may eat.

**14:10** And whatever does not have fins and scales you shall not eat; it is unclean to you.

**14:11** Every clean bird you may eat.

**14:12** But these are the ones of which you shall not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, and the osprey;

**14:13** the buzzard, the falcon, and the kite after its kind;

**14:14** and every raven after its kind;

**14:15** the ostrich, the nighthawk, the sea gull, and the hawk after its kind;

**14:16** the little owl, the great owl, and the white owl;

**14:17** the pelican, the carrion vulture, and the cormorant;

**14:18** the stork, the heron after its kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

**14:19** And every winged swarming thing is unclean to you; they shall not be eaten.

**14:20** Every clean winged creature you may eat.

**14:21** You shall not eat anything that dies of itself; you may give it to the sojourner who is within your gates, that he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner; for you are a holy people to the LORD your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

**14:22** You shall surely tithe all the yield of your seed, which the field brings forth year by year.

**14:23** And you shall eat before the LORD your God, in the place which He chooses to make His name dwell there, the tithe of your grain, of your new wine, and of your oil, and the firstborn of your herds and of your flocks, that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always.

**14:24** And if the journey is too long for you, so that you are not able to carry it, because the place which the LORD your God chooses to set His name there is too far from you, when the LORD your God blesses you,

**14:25** then you shall exchange it for silver, and bind up the silver in your hand, and go to the place which the LORD your God chooses.

**14:26** And you shall spend the silver for whatever your soul desires: for oxen, or for sheep, or for wine, or for strong drink, or for whatever your soul asks of you; and you shall eat there before the LORD your God, and you shall rejoice, you and your household.

**14:27** And the Levite who is within your gates, you shall not forsake him, for he has no portion nor inheritance with you.

**14:28** At the end of every three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your produce in that year, and lay it up within your gates.

**14:29** And the Levite—because he has no portion nor inheritance with you—and the sojourner and the fatherless and the widow who are within your gates shall come and eat and be satisfied, that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hand which you do.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The exact modern species identification of several Hebrew wild-animal names in verse 5 and unclean-bird names in verses 12 through 18 remains uncertain.
- **14:21** — Deuteronomy distinguishes the sojourner (a resident alien dwelling within Israelite towns) from the foreigner (a non-resident outsider).
- **14:28** — Unlike the annual festival tithe brought to the central sanctuary, this third-year tithe was stored locally within each town to support the Levites and the vulnerable.
