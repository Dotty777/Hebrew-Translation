## Text

**2:1** Then we turned and journeyed into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea, as the LORD spoke to me, and we skirted Mount Seir for many days.

**2:2** And the LORD spoke to me, saying,

**2:3** "You have skirted this mountain long enough; turn yourselves northward.

**2:4** And command the people, saying, 'You are about to pass through the territory of your brethren, the sons of Esau, who dwell in Seir, and they will be afraid of you; therefore guard yourselves carefully.

**2:5** Do not contend with them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even so much as the tread of the sole of a foot, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

**2:6** You shall buy food from them with silver, that you may eat; and you shall also purchase water from them with silver, that you may drink.'"

**2:7** For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hand; He knows your journeying through this great wilderness. These forty years the LORD your God has been with you; you have lacked nothing.

**2:8** So we passed beyond our brethren, the sons of Esau, who dwell in Seir, away from the road of the Arabah, from Elath and from Ezion Geber; and we turned and passed along the way of the Wilderness of Moab.

**2:9** Then the LORD said to me, "Do not harass Moab, nor contend with them in battle, for I will not give you any of their land as a possession, because I have given Ar to the sons of Lot as a possession."

**2:10** The Emim dwelt there formerly, a people great and numerous and tall as the Anakim.

**2:11** They also were reckoned as Rephaim, like the Anakim, but the Moabites call them Emim.

**2:12** The Horites also dwelt in Seir formerly, but the sons of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them from before them, and dwelt in their place, just as Israel did to the land of their possession which the LORD gave to them.

**2:13** "Now arise and cross over the Brook Zered." So we crossed over the Brook Zered.

**2:14** And the time that we journeyed from Kadesh Barnea until we crossed over the Brook Zered was thirty-eight years, until all the generation of the men of war were consumed from the midst of the camp, just as the LORD had sworn to them.

**2:15** Moreover, the hand of the LORD was against them, to destroy them from the midst of the camp until they were wholly consumed.

**2:16** So it came to pass, when all the men of war had finished dying from the midst of the people,

**2:17** that the LORD spoke to me, saying,

**2:18** "This day you are to pass over the border of Moab at Ar.

**2:19** And when you draw near opposite the sons of Ammon, do not harass them nor contend with them, for I will not give you any of the land of the sons of Ammon as a possession, because I have given it to the sons of Lot as a possession."

**2:20** That also was reckoned a land of Rephaim; Rephaim dwelt there formerly, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,

**2:21** a people great and numerous and tall as the Anakim. Yet the LORD destroyed them from before them, and they dispossessed them and dwelt in their place,

**2:22** just as He did for the sons of Esau, who dwell in Seir, when He destroyed the Horites from before them, and they dispossessed them and dwelt in their place to this day.

**2:23** And as for the Avvim, who dwelt in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came forth from Caphtor, destroyed them and dwelt in their place.

**2:24** "Arise, set out, and cross over the Valley of the Arnon. Behold, I have given into your hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to possess it, and contend with him in battle.

**2:25** This day I will begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the peoples under all the heavens, who shall hear the report of you, and shall tremble and be in anguish because of you."

**2:26** And I sent messengers from the Wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,

**2:27** "Let me pass through your land; I will keep strictly to the highway, and I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**2:28** You shall sell me food for silver, that I may eat, and give me water for silver, that I may drink; only let me pass through on foot,

**2:29** just as the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir and the Moabites who dwell in Ar did for me, until I cross over the Jordan into the land which the LORD our God is giving us."

**2:30** But Sihon king of Heshbon was not willing to let us pass through his land, for the LORD your God hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate, that He might deliver him into your hand, as it is this day.

**2:31** And the LORD said to me, "Behold, I have begun to deliver Sihon and his land before you; begin to possess it, that you may inherit his land."

**2:32** Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, to battle at Jahaz.

**2:33** And the LORD our God delivered him over before us, and we struck down him and his sons and all his people.

**2:34** We captured all his cities at that time and utterly destroyed every city—men, women, and little ones; we left no survivor.

**2:35** Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, along with the spoil of the cities which we had captured.

**2:36** From Aroer, which is on the rim of the Valley of the Arnon, and from the city that is in the valley, even as far as Gilead, there was not a single town that was too high for us; the LORD our God delivered them all before us.

**2:37** Only you did not draw near to the land of the sons of Ammon, nor to any part along the side of the Brook Jabbok, nor to the cities of the hill country, nor to anything which the LORD our God had commanded.

## Translator Notes

- **2:11** — The Rephaim were an ancient people of great stature who inhabited regions of Transjordan; the Moabite name Emim means "terrors."
- **2:20** — Zamzummim was the Ammonite name for the Rephaim who formerly occupied Ammon, often linked with the Zuzim of Genesis 14:5.
- **2:24** — Hebrew nachal refers to a seasonal stream bed or deep wadi gorge, which also accounts for the reference to a city situated "in the valley" in verse 36.
- **2:33** — The Masoretic text spells "his sons" defectively without the plural yod, though the consonantal and contextual usage represents the plural "his sons."
