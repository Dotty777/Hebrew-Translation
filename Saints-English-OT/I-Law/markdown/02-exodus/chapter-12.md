## Text

**12:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,

**12:2** "This month shall be to you the beginning of months; it shall be to you the first among the months of the year.

**12:3** Speak to all the congregation of Israel, saying, 'On the tenth day of this month, each man shall take for himself a lamb for a father's house, a lamb for each household.

**12:4** And if the household is too small for a lamb, then he and his neighbor nearest to his house shall take one according to the number of persons; according to what each man can eat, you shall reckon your count for the lamb.

**12:5** Your lamb shall be without blemish, a male of the first year; you may take it from the sheep or from the goats.

**12:6** And you shall keep it until the fourteenth day of this month; then the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel shall slaughter it at twilight.

**12:7** And they shall take some of the blood and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel of the houses in which they shall eat it.

**12:8** And they shall eat the flesh that night, roasted in fire; with unleavened bread and bitter herbs they shall eat it.

**12:9** Do not eat any of it raw, nor boiled at all in water, but roasted in fire—its head with its legs and its inward parts.

**12:10** And you shall leave none of it until morning; and what remains of it until morning you shall burn in the fire.

**12:11** And in this manner you shall eat it: with your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand; and you shall eat it in haste. It is the LORD's Passover.

**12:12** And I will pass through the land of Egypt on that night, and I will strike every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from man to beast; and against all the gods of Egypt I will execute judgments: I am the LORD.

**12:13** And the blood shall be a sign for you upon the houses where you are; and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you when I strike the land of Egypt.

**12:14** And this day shall be a memorial to you, and you shall observe it as a feast to the LORD; throughout your generations you shall observe it as a perpetual statute.

**12:15** Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread; surely on the first day you shall put away leaven from your houses. For whoever eats anything leavened from the first day until the seventh day, that soul shall be cut off from Israel.

**12:16** On the first day there shall be a holy convocation, and on the seventh day there shall be a holy convocation for you. No manner of work shall be done on them; only that which every soul must eat, that alone may be prepared by you.

**12:17** And you shall observe the Feast of Unleavened Bread, for on this very day I have brought your hosts out of the land of Egypt; therefore you shall keep this day throughout your generations as an everlasting statute.

**12:18** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at evening, you shall eat unleavened bread, until the twenty-first day of the month at evening.

**12:19** For seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses, for whoever eats anything leavened, that soul shall be cut off from the congregation of Israel, whether a sojourner or a native of the land.

**12:20** You shall eat nothing leavened; in all your dwellings you shall eat unleavened bread.'"

**12:21** Then Moses summoned all the elders of Israel and said to them, "Draw out and take for yourselves lambs according to your families, and slaughter the Passover lamb.

**12:22** And you shall take a bunch of hyssop, dip it in the blood that is in the basin, and touch the lintel and the two doorposts with the blood that is in the basin. And none of you shall go out from the doorway of his house until morning.

**12:23** For the LORD will pass through to strike the Egyptians; and when He sees the blood upon the lintel and upon the two doorposts, the LORD will pass over the doorway and will not allow the destroyer to come into your houses to strike you.

**12:24** And you shall observe this thing as a statute for you and for your children forever.

**12:25** And it shall come to pass, when you enter the land which the LORD will give you, just as He has spoken, that you shall keep this service.

**12:26** And it shall come to pass, when your children say to you, 'What is this service to you?'

**12:27** that you shall say, 'It is the Passover sacrifice to the LORD, who passed over the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt when He struck the Egyptians and delivered our households.'" Then the people bowed down and worshiped.

**12:28** And the children of Israel went and did it; just as the LORD had commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.

**12:29** And it came to pass at midnight that the LORD struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sat upon his throne to the firstborn of the captive who was in the dungeon, and every firstborn of the livestock.

**12:30** Then Pharaoh arose in the night, he, all his servants, and all the Egyptians; and there was a great outcry in Egypt, for there was not a house in which there was not one dead.

**12:31** And he summoned Moses and Aaron by night and said, "Arise, go forth from among my people, both you and the children of Israel; and go, serve the LORD as you have spoken.

**12:32** Take also your flocks and your herds, as you have said, and go; and bless me also."

**12:33** And the Egyptians pressed urgently upon the people to send them out of the land in haste, for they said, "We are all dead!"

**12:34** So the people took up their dough before it was leavened, with their kneading bowls bound up in their cloaks upon their shoulders.

**12:35** Now the children of Israel had done according to the word of Moses, for they had asked of the Egyptians articles of silver, articles of gold, and garments.

**12:36** And the LORD had given the people favor in the sight of the Egyptians, so that they granted them what they asked; and thus they plundered the Egyptians.

**12:37** Then the children of Israel journeyed from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, besides little ones.

**12:38** A mixed multitude also went up with them, and flocks and herds, a very great abundance of livestock.

**12:39** And they baked unleavened cakes from the dough which they had brought out of Egypt, for it was not leavened, because they were driven out of Egypt and could not delay, nor had they prepared any provisions for themselves.

**12:40** Now the sojourning of the children of Israel who dwelt in Egypt was four hundred and thirty years.

**12:41** And it came to pass at the end of four hundred and thirty years—on that very day—all the hosts of the LORD went out from the land of Egypt.

**12:42** It is a night of solemn watchfulness to the LORD for bringing them out of the land of Egypt; this is that night of the LORD, to be solemnly kept by all the children of Israel throughout their generations.

**12:43** And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "This is the ordinance of the Passover: No foreigner shall eat of it.

**12:44** But every man's servant who is bought with money, once you have circumcised him, then he may eat of it.

**12:45** A temporary resident and a hired servant shall not eat of it.

**12:46** It shall be eaten in one house; you shall not carry any of the flesh outside the house, nor shall you break a bone of it.

**12:47** All the congregation of Israel shall keep it.

**12:48** And when a sojourner dwells with you and would keep the Passover to the LORD, let every male belonging to him be circumcised, and then let him draw near to keep it; and he shall be as a native of the land. But no uncircumcised person shall eat of it.

**12:49** One law shall be for the native-born and for the sojourner who dwells among you."

**12:50** Thus all the children of Israel did; just as the LORD had commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.

**12:51** And it came to pass on that very day that the LORD brought the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their hosts.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Hebrew seh denotes a young animal from the flock, either a sheep or a goat, as clarified in verse 5.
- **12:6** — Literally "between the two evenings," denoting the twilight period between sunset and nightfall.
- **12:22** — The Hebrew saph is rendered "basin" here as the vessel holding the sacrificial blood, though the same term can also mean "threshold."
- **12:40** — The Masoretic Text states directly that the sojourn in Egypt lasted four hundred and thirty years, without the addition of Canaan found in the Samaritan Pentateuch and Septuagint.
- **12:42** — The Hebrew leil shimmurim expresses both the LORD's watchful vigilance in delivering Israel and the people's perpetual night of solemn observance.
