## Text

**24:1** Now He said to Moses, "Come up to the LORD, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel, and worship from afar.

**24:2** And Moses alone shall draw near to the LORD, yet they shall not draw near, nor shall the people come up with him."

**24:3** Then Moses came and recounted to the people all the words of the LORD and all the judgments. And all the people answered with one voice and said, "All the words which the LORD has spoken we will do."

**24:4** And Moses wrote down all the words of the LORD. And he rose early in the morning and built an altar at the foot of the mountain, and twelve pillars for the twelve tribes of Israel.

**24:5** Then he sent the young men of the children of Israel, who offered up burnt offerings and sacrificed bulls as peace offerings to the LORD.

**24:6** And Moses took half of the blood and put it in the basins, and half of the blood he sprinkled upon the altar.

**24:7** Then he took the Book of the Covenant and read it in the hearing of the people. And they said, "All that the LORD has spoken we will do, and we will obey."

**24:8** And Moses took the blood and sprinkled it upon the people, and said, "Behold, the blood of the covenant which the LORD has made with you concerning all these words."

**24:9** Then Moses went up, together with Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel.

**24:10** And they saw the God of Israel. And beneath His feet there was as it were a pavement of sapphire stone, like the very heavens in clarity.

**24:11** Yet against the nobles of the children of Israel He did not stretch out His hand; indeed, they beheld God, and they ate and drank.

**24:12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Come up to Me on the mountain and remain there, and I will give you the tablets of stone, and the law and the commandment which I have written, to instruct them."

**24:13** So Moses arose with Joshua his attendant, and Moses went up to the mountain of God.

**24:14** And to the elders he said, "Wait for us in this place until we return to you. And behold, Aaron and Hur are with you; whoever has a dispute, let him draw near to them."

**24:15** Then Moses went up into the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

**24:16** Now the glory of the LORD dwelt upon Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days. And on the seventh day He called to Moses from the midst of the cloud.

**24:17** And the appearance of the glory of the LORD was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain in the sight of the children of Israel.

**24:18** So Moses entered into the midst of the cloud and went up into the mountain. And Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights.

## Translator Notes

- **24:8** — Hebrew karat literally means "cut," reflecting the ancient Near Eastern idiom of "cutting a covenant" ratified by sacrificial blood.
- **24:10** — Hebrew livnat hassappir denotes a paved work or tiled surface of blue sapphire stone, likely lapis lazuli in ancient usage.
- **24:11** — Hebrew atzilei is a rare term meaning "nobles" or "chief men," and the verb chazah ("beheld") distinguishes solemn or visionary sight from ra'ah ("saw") in verse ten.
