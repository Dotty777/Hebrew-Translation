## Text

**29:1** And this is the thing that you shall do for them to consecrate them, that they may serve as priests to Me: take one young bull of the herd and two rams without blemish,

**29:2** and unleavened bread, and unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil; you shall make them of fine wheat flour.

**29:3** And you shall place them in one basket and bring them near in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

**29:4** And you shall bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and wash them with water.

**29:5** Then you shall take the garments and clothe Aaron with the tunic, and the robe of the ephod, and the ephod, and the breastplate, and gird him with the woven band of the ephod.

**29:6** And you shall set the turban upon his head, and put the holy crown upon the turban.

**29:7** Then you shall take the anointing oil, pour it upon his head, and anoint him.

**29:8** And you shall bring his sons near and clothe them with tunics.

**29:9** And you shall gird them with sashes, Aaron and his sons, and bind caps upon them; and the priesthood shall be theirs by a perpetual statute. Thus you shall ordain Aaron and his sons.

**29:10** And you shall bring the bull before the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the bull.

**29:11** Then you shall slaughter the bull before the LORD, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**29:12** And you shall take some of the blood of the bull and apply it to the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour out all the blood at the base of the altar.

**29:13** And you shall take all the fat that covers the inward parts, and the lobe upon the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is upon them, and offer them up in smoke upon the altar.

**29:14** But the flesh of the bull, and its hide, and its dung, you shall burn with fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.

**29:15** And you shall take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the ram.

**29:16** Then you shall slaughter the ram, and take its blood and dash it against the altar all around.

**29:17** And you shall cut the ram into its pieces, and wash its inward parts and its legs, and place them with its pieces and with its head.

**29:18** And you shall offer up the entire ram in smoke upon the altar; it is a burnt offering to the LORD, a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**29:19** Then you shall take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands upon the head of the ram.

**29:20** And you shall slaughter the ram, and take some of its blood and apply it to the lobe of the right ear of Aaron, and to the lobe of the right ear of his sons, and to the thumb of their right hand, and to the great toe of their right foot, and dash the blood against the altar all around.

**29:21** And you shall take some of the blood that is upon the altar, and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it upon Aaron and upon his garments, and upon his sons and upon the garments of his sons with him; so he and his garments shall be consecrated, and his sons and the garments of his sons with him.

**29:22** And you shall take from the ram the fat and the fat tail, the fat that covers the inward parts, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys and the fat that is upon them, and the right thigh, for it is a ram of ordination;

**29:23** and one loaf of bread, one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD.

**29:24** And you shall place all of these upon the palms of Aaron and upon the palms of his sons, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

**29:25** Then you shall take them from their hands and offer them up in smoke upon the altar on top of the burnt offering, for a soothing aroma before the LORD; it is an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**29:26** And you shall take the breast from the ram of ordination that is for Aaron, and wave it as a wave offering before the LORD; and it shall be your portion.

**29:27** And you shall consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the heave offering, which was waved and which was lifted up from the ram of ordination, from that which is for Aaron and from that which is for his sons.

**29:28** And it shall belong to Aaron and to his sons by a perpetual statute from the children of Israel, for it is a heave offering; and it shall be a heave offering from the children of Israel out of the sacrifices of their peace offerings, their heave offering to the LORD.

**29:29** And the holy garments that belong to Aaron shall be his sons' after him, to be anointed in them and to be ordained in them.

**29:30** Seven days shall the priest from among his sons who succeeds him wear them, when he enters the tent of meeting to minister in the holy place.

**29:31** And you shall take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place.

**29:32** And Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the bread that is in the basket, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**29:33** They shall eat those things by which atonement was made to ordain them and to consecrate them; but an outsider shall not eat of them, for they are holy.

**29:34** And if any of the flesh of ordination or of the bread remains until the morning, then you shall burn the remainder with fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is holy.

**29:35** Thus you shall do to Aaron and to his sons, according to all that I have commanded you; seven days you shall ordain them.

**29:36** And you shall offer a bull each day as a sin offering for atonement; and you shall purify the altar when you make atonement for it, and you shall anoint it to consecrate it.

**29:37** Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and consecrate it; and the altar shall be most holy. Whatever touches the altar shall become holy.

**29:38** Now this is what you shall offer upon the altar: two lambs of the first year each day continually.

**29:39** The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer at twilight.

**29:40** And a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a drink offering of a fourth of a hin of wine, shall be for the one lamb.

**29:41** And the second lamb you shall offer at twilight; you shall do with it according to the grain offering of the morning and according to its drink offering, for a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**29:42** This shall be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak to you there.

**29:43** And there I will meet with the children of Israel, and it shall be sanctified by My glory.

**29:44** And I will sanctify the tent of meeting and the altar; Aaron also and his sons I will sanctify to serve as priests to Me.

**29:45** And I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel, and I will be their God.

**29:46** And they shall know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt that I might dwell in their midst; I am the LORD their God.

## Translator Notes

- **29:9** — Hebrew literally reads "fill the hand of Aaron and the hand of his sons," the standard priestly idiom for ordination and installation into office (used also in vv. 29, 33, and 35).
- **29:13** — The Hebrew verb here denotes turning a sacrifice into ascending smoke upon the altar, distinguishing sacred offering from ordinary burning with fire outside the camp in v. 14.
- **29:39** — "At twilight" translates the Hebrew idiom literally meaning "between the two evenings," designating late afternoon toward dusk (also in v. 41).
- **29:40** — "A tenth of an ephah" translates the Hebrew dry measure issaron, roughly two quarts; a hin was a liquid measure of about one gallon, making a fourth of a hin roughly one quart.
