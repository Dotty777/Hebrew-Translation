## Text

**5:1** And afterward Moses and Aaron went in and said to Pharaoh, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'Let My people go, that they may hold a feast to Me in the wilderness.'"

**5:2** And Pharaoh said, "Who is the LORD, that I should heed His voice to let Israel go? I do not know the LORD, nor will I let Israel go."

**5:3** So they said, "The God of the Hebrews has met with us. Please let us go a three days' journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to the LORD our God, lest He fall upon us with pestilence or with the sword."

**5:4** Then the king of Egypt said to them, "Moses and Aaron, why do you draw the people away from their labor? Go back to your burdens!"

**5:5** And Pharaoh said, "Behold, the people of the land are now many, and you make them rest from their burdens!"

**5:6** So on that same day Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters of the people and their officers, saying,

**5:7** "You shall no longer give the people straw to make bricks as before; let them go and gather straw for themselves.

**5:8** Yet the quota of bricks which they made previously you shall lay upon them; you shall not reduce it at all. For they are idle; therefore they cry out, saying, 'Let us go and sacrifice to our God.'

**5:9** Let the labor be made heavier upon the men, that they may toil at it, and let them not pay regard to lying words."

**5:10** So the taskmasters of the people and their officers went out and spoke to the people, saying, "Thus says Pharaoh: 'I will not give you straw.

**5:11** Go yourselves, get straw for yourselves wherever you can find it; for nothing at all shall be reduced from your labor.'"

**5:12** So the people scattered throughout all the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.

**5:13** And the taskmasters pressed them, saying, "Complete your work, your daily portion, just as when there was straw."

**5:14** And the officers of the children of Israel, whom Pharaoh's taskmasters had set over them, were beaten and asked, "Why have you not completed your allotted task in making bricks as before, both yesterday and today?"

**5:15** Then the officers of the children of Israel came and cried out to Pharaoh, saying, "Why do you deal thus with your servants?

**5:16** No straw is given to your servants, yet they say to us, 'Make bricks!' And behold, your servants are beaten, though the fault is with your own people."

**5:17** But he said, "You are idle, idle! Therefore you say, 'Let us go and sacrifice to the LORD.'

**5:18** Go now and work; for no straw shall be given to you, yet you shall deliver the full tally of bricks."

**5:19** And the officers of the children of Israel saw that they were in distress when it was said, "You shall not reduce anything from your bricks, your daily portion."

**5:20** Then they came upon Moses and Aaron standing to meet them as they came out from Pharaoh.

**5:21** And they said to them, "May the LORD look upon you and judge, because you have made our odor foul in the sight of Pharaoh and in the sight of his servants, to put a sword in their hand to slay us."

**5:22** So Moses returned to the LORD and said, "O Lord, why have You brought harm upon this people? Why is it that You have sent me?

**5:23** For ever since I came to Pharaoh to speak in Your name, he has done harm to this people; and You have not delivered Your people at all."

## Translator Notes

- **5:16** — The final clause is terse in Hebrew, literally "and the sin of your people," conveying that the blame for the brick shortfall belongs to Pharaoh's own officials rather than the Israelites.
- **5:21** — Literally "made our odor stink in the eyes of Pharaoh," a vivid Hebrew idiom for rendering someone utterly disfavored and detested.
