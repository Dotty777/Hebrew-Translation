## Text

**30:1** "And you shall make an altar for the burning of incense; of acacia wood you shall make it.

**30:2** One cubit shall be its length and one cubit its breadth; foursquare it shall be, and two cubits shall be its height. Its horns shall be of one piece with it.

**30:3** You shall overlay it with pure gold, its top and its walls all around and its horns, and you shall make for it a molding of gold all around.

**30:4** And two rings of gold you shall make for it beneath its molding; on its two corners, upon its two sides, you shall make them, and they shall serve as holders for poles with which to bear it.

**30:5** And you shall make the poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold.

**30:6** And you shall place it before the veil that is before the ark of the Testimony, before the mercy seat that is over the Testimony, where I will meet with you.

**30:7** Aaron shall burn fragrant incense upon it; morning by morning, when he tends the lamps, he shall burn it.

**30:8** And when Aaron lights the lamps at twilight, he shall burn it, a perpetual incense before the LORD throughout your generations.

**30:9** You shall not offer upon it strange incense, nor a burnt offering, nor a grain offering; neither shall you pour a drink offering upon it.

**30:10** And Aaron shall make atonement upon its horns once a year; with the blood of the sin offering of atonement he shall make atonement upon it once a year throughout your generations. It is most holy to the LORD."

**30:11** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:12** "When you take the census of the children of Israel according to their number, each man shall give a ransom for his life to the LORD when you number them, that there may be no plague among them when you number them.

**30:13** This is what everyone who passes over among those who are numbered shall give: half a shekel according to the shekel of the sanctuary—the shekel is twenty gerahs—half a shekel as an offering to the LORD.

**30:14** Everyone who passes over among those who are numbered, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the offering to the LORD.

**30:15** The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less, than half a shekel, when presenting the offering to the LORD to make atonement for your souls.

**30:16** And you shall receive the atonement silver from the children of Israel and assign it for the service of the tent of meeting, that it may be a memorial for the children of Israel before the LORD, to make atonement for your souls."

**30:17** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:18** "You shall also make a laver of bronze, and its base of bronze, for washing; and you shall set it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water into it.

**30:19** And Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it.

**30:20** When they enter the tent of meeting, they shall wash with water, lest they die; or when they draw near to the altar to minister, to burn an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**30:21** So they shall wash their hands and their feet, lest they die. And it shall be a perpetual statute for them—for him and for his descendants throughout their generations."

**30:22** Moreover the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:23** "Take also for yourself the choicest spices: five hundred shekels of liquid myrrh, half as much—two hundred and fifty shekels—of fragrant cinnamon, two hundred and fifty shekels of aromatic cane,

**30:24** and five hundred shekels of cassia, measured by the shekel of the sanctuary, and a hin of olive oil.

**30:25** And you shall make of these a holy anointing oil, a fragrant blend, the work of a perfumer; a holy anointing oil it shall be.

**30:26** With it you shall anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the Testimony,

**30:27** the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, and the altar of incense,

**30:28** the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the laver and its base.

**30:29** You shall consecrate them, so that they shall be most holy; whatever touches them shall become holy.

**30:30** And you shall anoint Aaron and his sons and consecrate them, that they may serve Me as priests.

**30:31** And you shall speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'This shall be a holy anointing oil to Me throughout your generations.

**30:32** It shall not be poured upon the flesh of man, nor shall you make any other like it according to its composition. It is holy, and it shall be holy to you.

**30:33** Whoever blends any like it, or whoever puts any of it upon an outsider, shall be cut off from his people.'"

**30:34** And the LORD said to Moses, "Take for yourself fragrant spices—stacte, onycha, and galbanum—fragrant spices with pure frankincense; of each there shall be an equal portion.

**30:35** And you shall make of these an incense, a blend after the art of the perfumer, seasoned with salt, pure and holy.

**30:36** And you shall grind some of it very fine, and place some of it before the Testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you. It shall be most holy to you.

**30:37** And as for the incense that you shall make, you shall not make any for yourselves according to its composition; it shall be to you holy for the LORD.

**30:38** Whoever makes any like it to smell its fragrance shall be cut off from his people."

## Translator Notes

- **30:8** — "At twilight" translates the Hebrew idiom meaning "between the two evenings," referring to the period between sunset and full dusk.
- **30:12** — "Take the census" translates a Hebrew idiom literally meaning "lift up the head," and "for his life" renders the Hebrew word denoting both life and soul.
- **30:24** — A hin was a Hebrew liquid measure of approximately one gallon, or nearly four liters.
- **30:34** — Stacte, onycha, and galbanum were rare aromatic resins and spice ingredients compounded in equal portions with pure frankincense for sacred use alone.
