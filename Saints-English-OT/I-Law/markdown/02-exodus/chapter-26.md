## Text

**26:1** Moreover, you shall make the tabernacle of ten curtains of fine twisted linen, and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; with cherubim, the work of a skillful designer, you shall make them.

**26:2** The length of each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; all the curtains shall be of one measure.

**26:3** Five of the curtains shall be coupled one to another, and the other five curtains shall be coupled one to another.

**26:4** And you shall make loops of blue upon the edge of the one curtain, at the end of the first set; and likewise you shall make them upon the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.

**26:5** Fifty loops you shall make on the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make on the end of the curtain that is in the second set, the loops standing directly opposite one another.

**26:6** And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and couple the curtains one to another with the clasps, and the tabernacle shall be one.

**26:7** You shall also make curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle; eleven curtains you shall make them.

**26:8** The length of each curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; the eleven curtains shall all be of one measure.

**26:9** And you shall couple five of the curtains by themselves and six of the curtains by themselves, and you shall fold back the sixth curtain at the front of the tent.

**26:10** And you shall make fifty loops upon the edge of the one outermost curtain in the first set, and fifty loops upon the edge of the curtain of the second set.

**26:11** And you shall make fifty clasps of bronze, and put the clasps into the loops, and couple the tent together, that it may be one.

**26:12** And as for the overhanging remnant of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that remains shall hang over the rear of the tabernacle.

**26:13** And the cubit on the one side and the cubit on the other side, of that which remains in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall hang down over the sides of the tabernacle, on this side and on that side, to cover it.

**26:14** You shall also make a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of fine leather above that.

**26:15** And for the tabernacle you shall make the boards of acacia wood, standing upright.

**26:16** Ten cubits shall be the length of each board, and a cubit and a half shall be the width of each board.

**26:17** Two tenons shall be in each board, fitted one to another; thus you shall make for all the boards of the tabernacle.

**26:18** And you shall make the boards for the tabernacle, twenty boards for the south side, southward.

**26:19** And you shall make forty sockets of silver beneath the twenty boards: two sockets beneath one board for its two tenons, and two sockets beneath the next board for its two tenons.

**26:20** And for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, there shall be twenty boards,

**26:21** and their forty sockets of silver: two sockets beneath one board, and two sockets beneath the next board.

**26:22** And for the far end of the tabernacle, westward, you shall make six boards.

**26:23** And two boards you shall make for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear.

**26:24** They shall be coupled together from beneath, and in the same way they shall be completely joined at the top into a single ring; thus shall it be for both of them—they shall be for the two corners.

**26:25** So there shall be eight boards, with their silver sockets, sixteen sockets in all: two sockets beneath one board, and two sockets beneath the next board.

**26:26** And you shall make bars of acacia wood: five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,

**26:27** five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the side of the tabernacle at the rear, westward.

**26:28** And the middle bar shall pass through the center of the boards from end to end.

**26:29** You shall overlay the boards with gold, make their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and overlay the bars with gold.

**26:30** And you shall erect the tabernacle according to the plan of it that was shown to you on the mountain.

**26:31** You shall also make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen; it shall be made with cherubim, the work of a skillful designer.

**26:32** And you shall hang it upon four pillars of acacia wood overlaid with gold, their hooks being of gold, set upon four sockets of silver.

**26:33** And you shall hang the veil beneath the clasps; then you shall bring the ark of the Testimony in there, behind the veil. And the veil shall make a separation for you between the Holy Place and the Most Holy Place.

**26:34** You shall set the mercy seat upon the ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place.

**26:35** And you shall place the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle; and the table you shall put on the north side.

**26:36** You shall also make a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer.

**26:37** And you shall make for the screen five pillars of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold; their hooks shall be of gold, and you shall cast five sockets of bronze for them.

## Translator Notes

- **26:3** — The phrase "one to another" renders a feminine Hebrew idiom literally meaning "a woman to her sister," used throughout this chapter for paired curtains, loops, and tenons.
- **26:14** — "Fine leather" translates a Hebrew phrase whose precise meaning is uncertain, variously understood as fine tanned hides, sea-cow or dugong skins, or traditionally badger skins.
- **26:24** — "Completely joined" follows the Masoretic reading meaning "complete" or "whole," complementing the opening term meaning "coupled" or "twinned" to describe the reinforcement of the two rear corners.
- **26:31** — The Hebrew distinguishes the "skillful designer" (vv. 1, 31), who wove cherubim into the inner tabernacle curtains and the veil, from the "embroiderer" (v. 36; 27:16), who fashioned the outer screens.
