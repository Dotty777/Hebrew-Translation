## Text

**7:1** And the LORD said to Moses, "See, I have made you as God to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother shall be your prophet.

**7:2** You shall speak all that I command you, and Aaron your brother shall speak to Pharaoh, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.

**7:3** And I will harden Pharaoh's heart, and multiply My signs and My wonders in the land of Egypt.

**7:4** Yet Pharaoh will not listen to you, so that I may lay My hand upon Egypt and bring out My hosts, My people the children of Israel, from the land of Egypt by great judgments.

**7:5** And the Egyptians shall know that I am the LORD, when I stretch out My hand upon Egypt and bring out the children of Israel from among them."

**7:6** So Moses and Aaron did; just as the LORD commanded them, so they did.

**7:7** Now Moses was eighty years old and Aaron was eighty-three years old when they spoke to Pharaoh.

**7:8** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**7:9** "When Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, 'Show a wonder for yourselves,' then you shall say to Aaron, 'Take your rod and cast it before Pharaoh, and it shall become a serpent.'"

**7:10** So Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh, and they did so, just as the LORD had commanded. And Aaron cast down his rod before Pharaoh and before his servants, and it became a serpent.

**7:11** Then Pharaoh also summoned the wise men and the sorcerers; and they also, the magicians of Egypt, did in like manner with their enchantments.

**7:12** For each man cast down his rod, and they became serpents; yet Aaron's rod swallowed up their rods.

**7:13** And Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**7:14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Pharaoh's heart is stubborn; he refuses to let the people go.

**7:15** Go to Pharaoh in the morning—behold, he is going out to the water—and you shall stand by the bank of the Nile to meet him; and the rod that was turned into a serpent you shall take in your hand.

**7:16** And you shall say to him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you, saying, "Let My people go, that they may serve Me in the wilderness," yet behold, until now you have not listened.

**7:17** Thus says the LORD: "By this you shall know that I am the LORD. Behold, I will strike the waters that are in the Nile with the rod that is in My hand, and they shall be turned to blood.

**7:18** And the fish that are in the Nile shall die, and the Nile shall become foul, and the Egyptians will loathe to drink water from the Nile."'"

**7:19** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Take your rod and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, over their rivers, over their canals, over their ponds, and over all their pools of water, that they may become blood; and there shall be blood throughout all the land of Egypt, both in wood and in stone.'"

**7:20** And Moses and Aaron did so, just as the LORD commanded. He lifted up the rod and struck the waters that were in the Nile, in the sight of Pharaoh and in the sight of his servants; and all the waters that were in the Nile were turned to blood.

**7:21** And the fish that were in the Nile died, and the Nile became foul, so that the Egyptians could not drink water from the Nile; and there was blood throughout all the land of Egypt.

**7:22** Then the magicians of Egypt did so with their secret arts; and Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**7:23** And Pharaoh turned and went into his house, and he did not take even this to heart.

**7:24** So all the Egyptians dug all around the Nile for water to drink, because they could not drink of the water of the Nile.

**7:25** And seven full days passed after the LORD had struck the Nile.

**7:26** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh and say to him, 'Thus says the LORD: "Let My people go, that they may serve Me.

**7:27** But if you refuse to let them go, behold, I will smite all your territory with frogs.

**7:28** So the Nile shall swarm with frogs, which shall go up and come into your house, and into your bedchamber, and upon your bed, and into the house of your servants, and upon your people, and into your ovens, and into your kneading bowls.

**7:29** And the frogs shall come up upon you, and upon your people, and upon all your servants."'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Literally "I have made you God to Pharaoh," indicating that Moses would act as God's authoritative representative while Aaron declared his words as his prophet.
- **7:9** — The Hebrew term tannin denotes a large serpent or reptile, distinct from nachash ("serpent") used in 7:15 and 4:3.
- **7:14** — "Stubborn" translates Hebrew kaved (literally "heavy"), one of three verbs describing Pharaoh's hardened heart in this chapter alongside qashah in 7:3 and chazaq in 7:13 and 7:22.
- **7:19** — "Both in wood and in stone" refers to water held in wooden and stone vessels throughout the land of Egypt.
- **7:26** — In the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the announcement of the plague of frogs appears at 7:26-29, corresponding to 8:1-4 in most English versions.
