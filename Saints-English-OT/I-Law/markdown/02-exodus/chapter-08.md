## Text

**8:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your hand with your rod over the streams, over the rivers, and over the pools, and bring up the frogs upon the land of Egypt.'"

**8:2** So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

**8:3** And the magicians did likewise with their secret arts, and brought up frogs upon the land of Egypt.

**8:4** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Plead with the LORD, that He may take away the frogs from me and from my people; and I will send away the people, that they may sacrifice to the LORD."

**8:5** And Moses said to Pharaoh, "Accept the honor of telling me: for what time shall I plead for you, for your servants, and for your people, to cut off the frogs from you and from your houses, that they may remain only in the River?"

**8:6** And he said, "For tomorrow." And he said, "Let it be according to your word, that you may know that there is none like the LORD our God.

**8:7** And the frogs shall depart from you, from your houses, from your servants, and from your people; they shall remain only in the River."

**8:8** Then Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to the LORD concerning the frogs which He had set upon Pharaoh.

**8:9** And the LORD did according to the word of Moses. So the frogs died out of the houses, out of the courtyards, and out of the fields.

**8:10** And they gathered them together in heaps upon heaps, and the land stank.

**8:11** But when Pharaoh saw that there was relief, he hardened his heart and did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**8:12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your rod and strike the dust of the land, that it may become gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.'"

**8:13** And they did so. For Aaron stretched out his hand with his rod and struck the dust of the earth, and there were gnats on man and on beast. All the dust of the land became gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.

**8:14** Now the magicians tried likewise with their secret arts to bring forth gnats, but they could not. So the gnats were on man and on beast.

**8:15** Then the magicians said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." Yet Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**8:16** And the LORD said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh—behold, he is going out to the water—and say to him, 'Thus says the LORD: Send away My people, that they may serve Me.

**8:17** For if you will not send away My people, behold, I am sending swarms of flies upon you, upon your servants, upon your people, and into your houses; and the houses of the Egyptians shall be filled with the swarms of flies, and also the ground on which they stand.

**8:18** And in that day I will set apart the land of Goshen, in which My people dwell, that no swarms of flies shall be there, in order that you may know that I am the LORD in the midst of the land.

**8:19** And I will set a distinction between My people and your people. Tomorrow this sign shall come to pass.'"

**8:20** And the LORD did so. A heavy swarm of flies came into the house of Pharaoh and into the houses of his servants; and throughout all the land of Egypt, the land was ruined because of the swarms of flies.

**8:21** Then Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron and said, "Go, sacrifice to your God within the land."

**8:22** And Moses said, "It is not right to do so, for we would be sacrificing what is an abomination to the Egyptians to the LORD our God. Behold, if we sacrifice what is an abomination to the Egyptians before their eyes, will they not stone us?

**8:23** We will go a journey of three days into the wilderness and sacrifice to the LORD our God, as He shall say to us."

**8:24** So Pharaoh said, "I will send you away, that you may sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness; only you shall not go very far away. Plead for me."

**8:25** Then Moses said, "Behold, I am going out from you, and I will plead with the LORD that the swarms of flies may depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people tomorrow. Only let Pharaoh not deal deceitfully again by refusing to send the people away to sacrifice to the LORD."

**8:26** So Moses went out from Pharaoh and pleaded with the LORD.

**8:27** And the LORD did according to the word of Moses, and removed the swarms of flies from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; not one remained.

**8:28** Yet Pharaoh hardened his heart this time also, and did not send the people away.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Hebrew uses the collective singular hatzfardea ("the frog") to depict the overwhelming mass of frogs covering the land.
- **8:5** — Hebrew hitpaer alai, literally "glory over me," is a polite idiom inviting Pharaoh to take the honor of appointing the exact time.
- **8:11** — Hebrew kaved literally means "made heavy" or dull, distinguishing this hardening from chazaq ("grew strong" or stubborn) in 8:15.
- **8:17** — Hebrew arov denotes a dense, mixed swarm of biting insects, traditionally rendered as swarms of flies.
- **8:19** — Hebrew pedut literally means "redemption" or "deliverance," here denoting a separating distinction that spares Israel.
