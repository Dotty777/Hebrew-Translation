## Text

**33:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, "Depart and go up from here, you and the people whom you brought up out of the land of Egypt, to the land of which I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'To your seed I will give it.'

**33:2** And I will send an angel before you, and I will drive out the Canaanite, the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite.

**33:3** Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey; for I will not go up in your midst, because you are a stiff-necked people, lest I consume you along the way."

**33:4** When the people heard this grievous word, they mourned, and no man put his ornaments upon himself.

**33:5** And the LORD said to Moses, "Say to the children of Israel, 'You are a stiff-necked people. If for a single moment I should go up in your midst, I would consume you. Now therefore, take off your ornaments, that I may know what to do with you.'"

**33:6** So the children of Israel stripped themselves of their ornaments from Mount Horeb onward.

**33:7** Now Moses used to take the tent and pitch it outside the camp, far off from the camp, and he called it the Tent of Meeting. And it came to pass that everyone who sought the LORD would go out to the Tent of Meeting that was outside the camp.

**33:8** And it came to pass, whenever Moses went out to the tent, that all the people would rise, and each man would stand at the entrance of his tent and gaze after Moses until he entered the tent.

**33:9** And it came to pass, when Moses entered the tent, that the pillar of cloud would descend and stand at the entrance of the tent, and He would speak with Moses.

**33:10** All the people would see the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, and all the people would rise and worship, each man at the entrance of his tent.

**33:11** Thus the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his attendant Joshua the son of Nun, a young man, would not depart from within the tent.

**33:12** Then Moses said to the LORD, "See, You say to me, 'Bring up this people,' yet You have not made known to me whom You will send with me. Yet You have said, 'I know you by name, and you have also found favor in My sight.'

**33:13** Now therefore, if indeed I have found favor in Your sight, make known to me, I pray, Your ways, that I may know You, so that I may find favor in Your sight. And consider that this nation is Your people."

**33:14** And He said, "My Presence shall go with you, and I will give you rest."

**33:15** Then he said to Him, "If Your Presence does not go with us, do not bring us up from here.

**33:16** For how then shall it be known that I have found favor in Your sight, I and Your people? Is it not in Your going with us? So shall we be set apart, I and Your people, from every people that is upon the face of the earth."

**33:17** And the LORD said to Moses, "This very thing also that you have spoken I will do, for you have found favor in My sight, and I know you by name."

**33:18** And he said, "Show me, I pray, Your glory."

**33:19** Then He said, "I Myself will cause all My goodness to pass before your face, and I will proclaim the name of the LORD before you; and I will show grace to whom I will show grace, and I will show mercy to whom I will show mercy."

**33:20** And He said, "You are not able to see My face, for no man may see Me and live."

**33:21** And the LORD said, "Behold, there is a place beside Me, and you shall take your stand upon the rock.

**33:22** And it shall come to pass, as My glory passes by, that I will place you in the cleft of the rock, and I will cover you with My hand until I have passed by.

**33:23** Then I will remove My hand, and you shall see My back; but My face shall not be seen."

## Translator Notes

- **33:6** — The phrase "from Mount Horeb" indicates that the Israelites laid aside their ornaments beginning at Horeb and continuing onward as a mark of contrition.
- **33:14** — Literally "My face shall go," using "face" as the idiomatic expression for God's personal, manifest Presence accompanying His people.
- **33:19** — The repeated verbal clauses ("I will show grace to whom I will show grace") employ a Hebrew idiom declaring the sovereign freedom of God's favor and mercy.
