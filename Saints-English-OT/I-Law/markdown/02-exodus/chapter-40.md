## Text

**40:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**40:2** "On the day of the first month, on the first of the month, you shall raise up the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.

**40:3** You shall place there the ark of the Testimony, and you shall screen the ark with the veil.

**40:4** And you shall bring in the table and set its arrangement in order, and bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.

**40:5** You shall place the altar of gold for incense before the ark of the Testimony, and set the screen for the entrance of the tabernacle.

**40:6** Then you shall place the altar of burnt offering before the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.

**40:8** And you shall set up the court all around, and put up the screen of the gate of the court.

**40:9** Then you shall take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it; you shall consecrate it and all its vessels, and it shall be holy.

**40:10** And you shall anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, and consecrate the altar; and the altar shall be most holy.

**40:12** Then you shall bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and wash them with water.

**40:13** And you shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and anoint him and consecrate him, that he may minister to Me as priest.

**40:14** And you shall bring his sons near and clothe them with tunics.

**40:15** You shall anoint them just as you anointed their father, that they may minister to Me as priests; and their anointing shall surely be for them an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations."

**40:16** And Moses did so; according to all that the LORD commanded him, so he did.

**40:17** And it came to pass in the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, that the tabernacle was raised up.

**40:18** Moses raised up the tabernacle, laid its sockets, placed its boards, put in its bars, and raised up its pillars.

**40:19** He spread out the tent over the tabernacle and placed the covering of the tent above it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**40:20** He took the Testimony and placed it into the ark, set the poles on the ark, and placed the mercy seat on top of the ark.

**40:21** Then he brought the ark into the tabernacle, hung the veil of the screen, and screened the ark of the Testimony, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**40:22** He placed the table in the tent of meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil.

**40:23** And he set the bread in order upon it before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**40:24** He placed the lampstand in the tent of meeting, opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.

**40:25** And he lighted the lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**40:26** He placed the altar of gold in the tent of meeting before the veil.

**40:27** And he burned fragrant incense upon it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**40:29** And he placed the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting, and offered upon it the burnt offering and the grain offering, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**40:33** Then he raised up the court all around the tabernacle and the altar, and put up the screen of the gate of the court. Thus Moses completed the work.

**40:34** And the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

**40:35** And Moses could not enter the tent of meeting, because the cloud abode upon it, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

**40:36** And whenever the cloud was lifted up from above the tabernacle, the children of Israel would set out in all their journeys.

**40:37** But if the cloud was not lifted up, then they would not set out until the day it was lifted up.

**40:38** For the cloud of the LORD was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.

## Translator Notes

- **40:2** — The Hebrew reads literally 'on the day of the first month, on the first of the month,' marking the opening day of the new year for the inauguration of the sanctuary.
- **40:4** — The phrase 'set its arrangement in order' reflects the cognate Hebrew expression for arranging the bread of the Presence and its appointed vessels upon the table.
- **40:15** — The Hebrew doubles the verb 'to be' to emphasize the enduring certainty of the Aaronic priesthood conferred by their anointing.
- **40:35** — The verb rendered 'abode' shares the same Hebrew root as 'tabernacle,' underscoring that the sanctuary was now inhabited by the abiding presence of the LORD.
