## Text

**38:1** Then Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood; two cubits and a half was its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height.

**38:2** He overlaid it with pure gold within and without, and made for it a gold molding all around.

**38:3** And he cast for it four rings of gold upon its four feet—two rings on its one side, and two rings on its other side.

**38:4** He inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to bear the ark.

**38:5** And he made a mercy seat of pure gold; two cubits and a half was its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth.

**38:6** He made two cherubim of gold; of hammered work he fashioned them at the two ends of the mercy seat:

**38:7** one cherub at the end on the one side, and one cherub at the end on the other side; of one piece with the mercy seat he made the cherubim at its two ends.

**38:8** And the cherubim stretched out their wings upward, overshadowing the mercy seat with their wings, with their faces turned toward one another; toward the mercy seat were the faces of the cherubim.

**38:9** Then he made the table of acacia wood; two cubits was its length, one cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height.

**38:10** And he cast for it four rings of gold, and set the rings upon the four corners that belonged to its four legs. Close beside the frame were the rings, as holders for the poles to bear the table.

**38:11** He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold. And he made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold, to bear the table.

**38:12** He also made the vessels that were upon the table—its dishes, its pans, its bowls, and the pitchers with which drink offerings are poured—of pure gold.

**38:13** Then he made the lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work he fashioned the lampstand. Its base and its shaft, its cups, its knobs, and its blossoms were of one piece with it.

**38:15** And six branches extended from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side, and three branches of the lampstand from its other side.

**38:16** Three cups shaped like almond blossoms were on the one branch, with a knob and a blossom, and three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the other branch, with a knob and a blossom—so for the six branches extending from the lampstand. Their knobs and their branches were of one piece with it; all of it was one hammered piece of pure gold.

**38:17** And he made its seven lamps, its wick-trimmers, and its trays of pure gold.

**38:18** And he overlaid the boards with gold, and made their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and overlaid the bars with gold.

**38:22** Then he made the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood; five cubits was its length and five cubits its breadth—it was square—and three cubits was its height.

**38:23** He made all the utensils of the altar—the pots, the shovels, the basins, the forks, and the firepans; all of its utensils he made of bronze.

**38:24** And he made for the altar a grate of bronze network under its ledge, reaching from below to its middle. He also cast four rings at the four corners of the bronze grate, as holders for the poles.

**38:25** He also made the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of sweet spices, the work of a perfumer.

**38:26** And he made the laver of bronze and its base of bronze, from the mirrors of the serving women who ministered at the doorway of the tent of meeting.

**38:27** From it Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and their feet. Whenever they went into the tent of meeting, and whenever they drew near to the altar, they washed, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **38:7** — Ketiv reads qetsoto (an orthographic spelling lacking the plural yod), while Qere reads qetsotav ("its ends"), which is followed here as "its two ends."
- **38:11** — This verse preserves a textual doublet recording both the making of gold-overlaid acacia poles and the making of the poles specifically to bear the table.
- **38:26** — The "serving women" (hatsobe'ot) were women who performed organized service at the entrance of the tent of meeting; their polished metal mirrors supplied bronze for the laver.
