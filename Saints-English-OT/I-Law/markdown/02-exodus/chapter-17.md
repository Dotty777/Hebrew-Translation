## Text

**17:1** Then the whole congregation of the children of Israel journeyed from the Wilderness of Sin by their stages, according to the command of the LORD, and encamped at Rephidim; and there was no water for the people to drink.

**17:2** Therefore the people contended with Moses and said, "Give us water, that we may drink." And Moses said to them, "Why do you contend with me? Why do you test the LORD?"

**17:3** And the people thirsted there for water, and the people murmured against Moses and said, "Why have you brought us up out of Egypt, to kill me and my children and my livestock with thirst?"

**17:4** So Moses cried out to the LORD, saying, "What shall I do with this people? Yet a little while, and they will stone me!"

**17:5** And the LORD said to Moses, "Pass on before the people, and take with you some of the elders of Israel. Take also in your hand your rod, with which you struck the Nile, and go.

**17:6** Behold, I will stand before you there upon the rock in Horeb; and you shall strike the rock, and water shall come out of it, that the people may drink." And Moses did so in the sight of the elders of Israel.

**17:7** And he called the name of that place Massah and Meribah, because of the contention of the children of Israel, and because they tested the LORD, saying, "Is the LORD in our midst, or not?"

**17:8** Then Amalek came and fought against Israel at Rephidim.

**17:9** And Moses said to Joshua, "Choose men for us, and go out and fight against Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand upon the summit of the hill with the rod of God in my hand."

**17:10** So Joshua did as Moses had spoken to him, to fight against Amalek. And Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the summit of the hill.

**17:11** And it came to pass, whenever Moses lifted up his hand, Israel prevailed; and whenever he lowered his hand, Amalek prevailed.

**17:12** Now the hands of Moses grew heavy; so they took a stone and placed it beneath him, and he sat upon it. And Aaron and Hur upheld his hands, one on this side and one on the other side; thus his hands remained steady until the going down of the sun.

**17:13** And Joshua overwhelmed Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.

**17:14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Write this as a memorial in the book and rehearse it in the hearing of Joshua, that I will utterly blot out the remembrance of Amalek from beneath heaven."

**17:15** And Moses built an altar and called its name The-LORD-Is-My-Banner;

**17:16** for he said, "Because a hand is upon the throne of the LORD, the LORD shall have war against Amalek from generation to generation."

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Hebrew uses first-person singular suffixes ("me and my children and my livestock"), portraying the complaining congregation as speaking with a single collective voice.
- **17:7** — Massah means "Testing" and Meribah means "Contention" or "Quarreling," commemorating both Israel's dispute with Moses and their testing of the LORD.
- **17:15** — The Hebrew name of the altar is Yahweh-Nissi, literally "The LORD Is My Banner," signifying the LORD as Israel's rallying standard in battle.
- **17:16** — The Hebrew phrase "a hand upon the throne of the LORD" employs a rare shortened form for "throne" and is variously understood as a solemn oath sworn by the divine throne or as Amalek's hostile hand lifted against the LORD's rule.
