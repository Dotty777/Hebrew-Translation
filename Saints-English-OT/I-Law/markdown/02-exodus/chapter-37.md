## Text

**37:1** Then all the wise-hearted men among those performing the work made the tabernacle of ten curtains: of finely twisted linen, and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; with cherubim, the work of a skilled designer, he made them.

**37:2** The length of each curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain was four cubits; all the curtains had one measurement.

**37:3** And he made the veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen; with the work of a skilled designer he made it with cherubim.

**37:4** And he made for it four pillars of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold, with their hooks of gold; and he cast for them four sockets of silver.

**37:5** He also made a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer;

**37:6** and its five pillars with their hooks; and he overlaid their capitals and their bands with gold, and their five sockets were of bronze.

**37:7** Then he made the court: for the south side, southward, the hangings of the court were of finely twisted linen, one hundred cubits;

**37:8** their pillars were twenty and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

**37:9** And for the north side, one hundred cubits; their pillars were twenty and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

**37:10** And for the west side, hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars were ten and their sockets ten; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

**37:11** And for the east side, eastward, fifty cubits.

**37:12** The hangings for one side were fifteen cubits; their pillars were three and their sockets three.

**37:13** And for the other side—on this side and on that side of the gate of the court—hangings of fifteen cubits; their pillars were three and their sockets three.

**37:14** All the hangings of the court all around were of finely twisted linen.

**37:15** And the sockets for the pillars were of bronze, the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver, and the overlaying of their capitals was of silver; and all the pillars of the court were banded with silver.

**37:16** And the screen for the gate of the court was the work of an embroiderer, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen; its length was twenty cubits, and its height in breadth was five cubits, corresponding to the hangings of the court.

**37:17** And their pillars were four, and their sockets four, of bronze; their hooks were of silver, and the overlaying of their capitals and their bands were of silver.

**37:18** And all the pegs for the tabernacle and for the court all around were of bronze.

**37:19** These are the inventories of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the testimony, which were tallied at the command of Moses, as the service of the Levites, under the hand of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest.

**37:20** And Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

**37:21** And with him was Oholiab the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver and skilled designer, and an embroiderer in blue, in purple, in scarlet yarn, and in fine linen.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The Hebrew shifts from plural ("made") to singular ("he made them"), reflecting Bezalel's master supervision over the artisans. In this critical text, the curtains, veil, entrance screen, and court are grouped together in chapter 37.
- **37:6** — Hebrew rashim (literally "heads") refers to the tops or capitals of the pillars, and chashuqim to their decorative bands or connecting rods.
- **37:19** — Hebrew pequdei denotes the official accounting or inventory of the tabernacle's materials, compiled under the supervision of Ithamar.
