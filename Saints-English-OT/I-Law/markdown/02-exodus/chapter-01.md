## Text

**1:1** Now these are the names of the sons of Israel who entered Egypt with Jacob; each man and his household came:

**1:2** Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah;

**1:3** Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin;

**1:4** Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

**1:5** Now all the souls who came forth from the loins of Jacob were seventy in number, for Joseph was already in Egypt.

**1:6** Then Joseph died, along with all his brethren and all that generation.

**1:7** But the sons of Israel were fruitful and teemed abundantly, and they multiplied and grew exceedingly mighty, so that the land was filled with them.

**1:8** Then a new king arose over Egypt, who did not know Joseph.

**1:9** And he said to his people, "Behold, the people of the sons of Israel are more numerous and mightier than we.

**1:10** Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, lest they multiply, and it come to pass that when war breaks out, they also join themselves to those who hate us and fight against us, and go up out of the land."

**1:11** So they appointed taskmasters over them to afflict them with their burdens; and they built store cities for Pharaoh, Pithom and Raamses.

**1:12** Yet the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and spread abroad, so that the Egyptians came to dread the sons of Israel.

**1:13** And the Egyptians compelled the sons of Israel to serve with rigor.

**1:14** And they made their lives bitter with harsh bondage, in mortar and in brick, and in all manner of labor in the field; with rigor they imposed all their service upon them.

**1:15** Then the king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives, the name of one being Shiphrah and the name of the second Puah,

**1:16** and he said, "When you attend the Hebrew women in childbirth and look upon the birthstool, if it is a son, then you shall put him to death; but if it is a daughter, then she shall live."

**1:17** But the midwives feared God and did not do as the king of Egypt had spoken to them, but kept the male children alive.

**1:18** So the king of Egypt summoned the midwives and said to them, "Why have you done this thing, and kept the male children alive?"

**1:19** And the midwives answered Pharaoh, "Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women; for they are vigorous, and they give birth before the midwife comes to them."

**1:20** Therefore God dealt well with the midwives, and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly mighty.

**1:21** And it came to pass, because the midwives feared God, that He established households for them.

**1:22** Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, "Every son who is born you shall cast into the Nile, and every daughter you shall keep alive."

## Translator Notes

- **1:7** — The Hebrew verb rendered "teemed abundantly" echoes the creation blessing of Genesis 1:20-21, portraying Israel's growth in Egypt as a fulfillment of God's covenant promise.
- **1:16** — "Birthstool" translates a Hebrew dual noun meaning literally "the two stones," referring to the pair of stones or bricks on which a woman crouched during childbirth.
- **1:21** — "Households" is literally "houses" in Hebrew, indicating that God rewarded the midwives by granting them enduring families and descendants.
