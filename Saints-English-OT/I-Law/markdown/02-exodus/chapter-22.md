## Text

**22:1** If a thief is found breaking in, and he is struck so that he dies, there shall be no bloodguilt on his account.

**22:2** If the sun has risen upon him, there shall be bloodguilt on his account. He shall surely make restitution; if he has nothing, then he shall be sold for his theft.

**22:3** If the stolen property is indeed found alive in his hand, whether ox or donkey or sheep, he shall repay double.

**22:4** If a man causes a field or vineyard to be grazed over, and lets his livestock loose so that it grazes in the field of another, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field and from the best of his own vineyard.

**22:5** If fire breaks out and catches in thorns, so that stacked grain, or standing grain, or the field is consumed, the one who kindled the fire shall surely make restitution.

**22:6** If a man entrusts money or goods to his neighbor for safekeeping, and it is stolen from the man's house, if the thief is found, he shall repay double.

**22:7** If the thief is not found, then the owner of the house shall come near before God, to determine whether he has laid his hand upon his neighbor's property.

**22:8** For every matter of transgression—whether concerning an ox, a donkey, a sheep, a garment, or any lost thing of which one says, "This is it"—the case of both parties shall come before God; the one whom God condemns shall repay double to his neighbor.

**22:9** If a man entrusts to his neighbor a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any beast for safekeeping, and it dies, or is injured, or is driven away with no one seeing it,

**22:10** then an oath of the LORD shall be between them both that he has not laid his hand upon his neighbor's property; and its owner shall accept it, and he shall not make restitution.

**22:11** But if it is indeed stolen from him, he shall make restitution to its owner.

**22:12** If it is indeed torn to pieces, let him bring it as evidence; he shall not make restitution for what was torn.

**22:13** And if a man borrows anything from his neighbor, and it is injured or dies while its owner is not with it, he shall surely make restitution.

**22:14** If its owner is with it, he shall not make restitution; if it was hired, it came for its hire.

**22:15** And if a man entices a virgin who is not betrothed, and lies with her, he shall surely pay the bride-price for her to be his wife.

**22:16** If her father utterly refuses to give her to him, he shall weigh out silver equal to the bride-price of virgins.

**22:17** You shall not permit a sorceress to live.

**22:18** Whoever lies with a beast shall surely be put to death.

**22:19** Whoever sacrifices to any god, except to the LORD alone, shall be devoted to destruction.

**22:20** You shall neither wrong a sojourner nor oppress him, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

**22:21** You shall not afflict any widow or fatherless child.

**22:22** If you afflict them in any way, and they cry out at all to Me, I will surely hear their cry;

**22:23** and My wrath shall burn hot, and I will slay you with the sword; and your wives shall become widows, and your children fatherless.

**22:24** If you lend money to any of My people who are poor among you, you shall not act toward him like a creditor, nor shall you impose interest upon him.

**22:25** If you ever take your neighbor's cloak as a pledge, you shall return it to him before the sun goes down.

**22:26** For that is his only covering; it is his garment for his skin. In what shall he sleep? And it shall come to pass, when he cries out to Me, that I will hear, for I am gracious.

**22:27** You shall not revile God, nor curse a ruler among your people.

**22:28** You shall not delay to offer the fullness of your harvest and the outflow of your presses; the firstborn of your sons you shall give to Me.

**22:29** Likewise you shall do with your oxen and your sheep: seven days it shall remain with its mother; on the eighth day you shall give it to Me.

**22:30** And you shall be holy men to Me; you shall not eat any flesh torn by beasts in the field, but you shall cast it to the dogs.

## Translator Notes

- **22:1** — In Masoretic versification, Exodus 22:1 corresponds to 22:2 in most English Bibles, as the Masoretic Text places the preceding verse at 21:37.
- **22:7** — The Hebrew word elohim here, in 22:8 (where it takes a plural verb), and in 22:27 literally means "God," though traditional interpretation also understands it in judicial contexts as "the judges."
- **22:28** — Literally "your fullness and your tear," poetic expressions for the abundance of the grain harvest and the dripping outflow of wine and olive presses.
