## Text

**28:1** Now you, bring near to yourself Aaron your brother, and his sons with him, from the midst of the children of Israel, that he may serve as priest to Me—Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron.

**28:2** And you shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for splendor.

**28:3** And you shall speak to all who are wise of heart, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, that they may make the garments of Aaron to consecrate him, that he may serve as priest to Me.

**28:4** And these are the garments which they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a checkered tunic, a turban, and a sash. Thus they shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons, that he may serve as priest to Me.

**28:5** And they shall take the gold, the blue, the purple, the scarlet yarn, and the fine linen.

**28:6** And they shall make the ephod of gold, of blue and purple, of scarlet yarn, and of finely twisted linen, the work of a skillful designer.

**28:7** It shall have two shoulder pieces attached to it at its two edges, so that it may be joined together.

**28:8** And the woven waistband of the ephod, which is upon it, shall be of the same workmanship and of one piece with it: of gold, blue, purple, scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen.

**28:9** And you shall take two onyx stones, and engrave upon them the names of the sons of Israel:

**28:10** six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the remaining six on the second stone, according to the order of their birth.

**28:11** With the work of a stone engraver, like the engravings of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel; you shall mount them enclosed in settings of gold.

**28:12** And you shall place the two stones upon the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of memorial for the sons of Israel; and Aaron shall bear their names before the LORD upon his two shoulders for a memorial.

**28:13** And you shall make settings of gold.

**28:14** And you shall make two chains of pure gold, twisted like cords of braided work, and you shall fasten the braided chains to the settings.

**28:15** And you shall make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a skillful designer; like the workmanship of the ephod you shall make it: of gold, blue, purple, scarlet yarn, and finely twisted linen you shall make it.

**28:16** It shall be square, folded double: a span shall be its length, and a span shall be its width.

**28:17** And you shall fill it with settings of stones, four rows of stones: a row of sardius, topaz, and emerald shall be the first row;

**28:18** and the second row shall be carbuncle, sapphire, and diamond;

**28:19** and the third row shall be jacinth, agate, and amethyst;

**28:20** and the fourth row shall be beryl, onyx, and jasper. They shall be enclosed in gold in their settings.

**28:21** And the stones shall bear the names of the sons of Israel, twelve according to their names; like the engravings of a signet, each one with its own name, they shall be for the twelve tribes.

**28:22** And you shall make upon the breastpiece twisted chains of braided work, of pure gold.

**28:29** And you shall put the two braided cords of gold on the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece. And the two ends of the two braided cords you shall fasten to the two settings, and place them upon the shoulder pieces of the ephod at its front. So Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel in the breastpiece of judgment upon his heart, when he enters the holy place, for a memorial before the LORD continually.

**28:30** And you shall put in the breastpiece of judgment the Urim and the Thummim, and they shall be upon the heart of Aaron when he goes in before the LORD; thus Aaron shall bear the judgment of the children of Israel upon his heart before the LORD continually.

**28:31** And you shall make the robe of the ephod wholly of blue.

**28:32** And there shall be an opening for the head in the midst of it; it shall have a woven border around its opening, the work of a weaver, like the opening of a coat of mail, so that it shall not be torn.

**28:33** And upon its hem you shall make pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, around its hem on every side, and bells of gold between them all around:

**28:34** a golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate, upon the hem of the robe all around.

**28:35** And it shall be upon Aaron when he ministers, and its sound shall be heard when he enters the holy place before the LORD and when he comes out, so that he may not die.

**28:36** And you shall make a plate of pure gold, and engrave upon it, like the engravings of a signet, "Holiness to the LORD."

**28:37** And you shall fasten it on a cord of blue, that it may be upon the turban; upon the front of the turban it shall be.

**28:38** And it shall be upon the forehead of Aaron, that Aaron may bear the iniquity of the holy things which the children of Israel consecrate in all their holy gifts; and it shall be upon his forehead continually, that they may find acceptance before the LORD.

**28:39** And you shall weave the tunic in checkered work of fine linen, and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and a sash you shall make as the work of an embroiderer.

**28:40** And for the sons of Aaron you shall make tunics, and you shall make sashes for them, and headdresses you shall make for them, for glory and for splendor.

**28:41** And you shall clothe Aaron your brother with them, and his sons with him; and you shall anoint them, and ordain them, and consecrate them, that they may serve as priests to Me.

**28:42** And make for them linen undergarments to cover their nakedness; from the waist to the thighs they shall reach.

**28:43** And they shall be upon Aaron and upon his sons when they enter the tent of meeting, or when they approach the altar to minister in the holy place, lest they bear iniquity and die. It shall be an everlasting statute for him and for his descendants after him.

## Translator Notes

- **28:17** — The exact modern mineralogical identifications of several gemstones in the breastpiece remain uncertain; traditional English equivalents are used here.
- **28:29** — In this Hebrew critical text, verse 29 directly follows verse 22 and combines the fastening of the braided gold cords to the breastpiece and ephod with Aaron bearing the names of the sons of Israel before the LORD.
- **28:30** — Urim and Thummim literally mean Lights and Perfections, sacred objects placed within the breastpiece of judgment by which the high priest sought divine guidance.
- **28:41** — The Hebrew idiom rendered ordain them literally reads fill their hand, signifying formal installation into priestly service.
