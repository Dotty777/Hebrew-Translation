## Text

**20:1** Now God spoke all these words, saying:

**20:2** "I am the LORD your God, who brought you forth from the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.

**20:3** You shall not have other gods before My presence.

**20:4** You shall not make for yourself a graven image, nor any likeness of what is in the heavens above, or in the earth beneath, or in the waters under the earth.

**20:5** You shall not bow down before them nor serve them, for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of fathers upon children, to the third and the fourth generation of those who hate Me,

**20:6** yet showing lovingkindness to thousands, to those who love Me and keep My commandments.

**20:7** You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not acquit the one who takes His name in vain.

**20:8** Remember the Sabbath day, to sanctify it.

**20:9** Six days you shall labor and perform all your work,

**20:10** but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God; you shall not do any work—neither you, nor your son, nor your daughter, nor your manservant, nor your maidservant, nor your livestock, nor your sojourner who is within your gates.

**20:11** For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and everything that is in them, and He rested on the seventh day; therefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and sanctified it.

**20:12** Honor your father and your mother, so that your days may be prolonged upon the land which the LORD your God gives you.

**20:13** You shall not commit adultery.

**20:14** You shall not steal.

**20:15** You shall not murder.

**20:16** You shall not testify as a false witness against your neighbor.

**20:17** You shall not covet your neighbor's house; you shall not covet your neighbor's wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his donkey, nor anything that belongs to your neighbor."

**20:18** And all the people perceived the thunderings and the lightning flashes, and the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; and when the people saw it, they trembled and stood far off.

**20:19** Then they said to Moses, "You yourself speak with us, and we will hear; but do not let God speak with us, lest we die."

**20:20** And Moses said to the people, "Do not fear, for God has come in order to test you, and so that the fear of Him may be before you, that you may not sin."

**20:21** So the people remained standing at a distance, while Moses approached the thick darkness where God was.

**20:22** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the children of Israel: 'You yourselves have seen that from heaven I have spoken with you.

**20:23** You shall not make gods beside Me—gods of silver and gods of gold you shall not make for yourselves.

**20:24** An altar of earth you shall make for Me, and upon it you shall sacrifice your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your flocks and your herds. In every place where I cause My name to be remembered, I will come to you and bless you.

**20:25** And if you make for Me an altar of stones, you shall not build them of hewn stone; for if you lift your chisel upon it, you will profane it.

**20:26** Nor shall you ascend by steps to My altar, so that your nakedness may not be uncovered upon it.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:13** — In this Hebrew text, verses 13 through 15 order these three commandments as adultery, theft, and murder, matching the sequence found in the Nash Papyrus and Codex Vaticanus of the Septuagint, whereas the standard Masoretic order is murder, adultery, and theft.
- **20:18** — The Hebrew participle literally means seeing, expressing the people's unified sensory perception of both the audible thunder and trumpet and the visible lightning and smoke.
- **20:25** — The Hebrew noun literally denotes a sword or sharp cutting instrument, referring here to an iron chisel used to shape building stones.
