## Text

**3:1** Now Moses was tending the flock of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and he led the flock to the far side of the wilderness and came to Horeb, the mountain of God.

**3:2** Then the Angel of the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush; and as he looked, behold, the bush burned with fire, yet the bush was not being consumed.

**3:3** And Moses said, "Let me turn aside now and behold this great sight, why the bush does not burn up."

**3:4** When the LORD saw that he had turned aside to look, God called to him out of the midst of the bush and said, "Moses, Moses!" And he answered, "Here I am."

**3:5** Then He said, "Do not come near here; remove your sandals from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground."

**3:6** And He said, "I am the God of your father—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." Then Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to gaze upon God.

**3:7** And the LORD said, "I have surely beheld the affliction of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their outcry because of their taskmasters, for I know their sorrows.

**3:8** Therefore I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring them up from that land to a good and spacious land, to a land flowing with milk and honey, to the place of the Canaanites and the Hittites and the Amorites and the Perizzites and the Hivites and the Jebusites.

**3:9** And now, behold, the outcry of the sons of Israel has come to Me, and I have also seen the oppression with which the Egyptians are oppressing them.

**3:10** Now therefore, come, and I will send you to Pharaoh, that you may bring My people, the sons of Israel, out of Egypt."

**3:11** But Moses said to God, "Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh, and that I should bring the sons of Israel out of Egypt?"

**3:12** And He said, "Assuredly I will be with you; and this shall be the sign to you that I Myself have sent you: when you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall serve God upon this mountain."

**3:13** Then Moses said to God, "Behold, when I come to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'The God of your fathers has sent me to you,' and they say to me, 'What is His name?'—what shall I answer them?"

**3:14** Then God answered Moses, "I AM WHO I AM." And He said, "This you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'I AM has sent me to you.'"

**3:15** Moreover God said to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'The LORD, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.' This is My name forever, and this is My memorial from generation to generation.

**3:16** Go and gather the elders of Israel together, and say to them, 'The LORD, the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—has appeared to me, saying, "I have surely visited you and observed what has been done to you in Egypt,

**3:17** and I have said that I will bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt to the land of the Canaanites and the Hittites and the Amorites and the Perizzites and the Hivites and the Jebusites, to a land flowing with milk and honey."'

**3:18** Then they will heed your voice; and you shall go, you and the elders of Israel, to the king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us; and now, please, let us go a journey of three days into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to the LORD our God.'

**3:19** Yet I Myself know that the king of Egypt will not permit you to go, not even by a mighty hand.

**3:20** So I will stretch forth My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders which I will perform in its midst; and after that he will send you away.

**3:21** And I will grant this people favor in the sight of the Egyptians; and it shall come to pass, when you go, that you shall not go empty-handed.

**3:22** Rather, every woman shall ask of her neighbor, and of her who sojourns in her house, articles of silver, articles of gold, and garments; and you shall put them upon your sons and upon your daughters. Thus you shall plunder the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **3:12** — "Serve" uses the same Hebrew root as the "bondage" of chapters 1-2, underscoring that Israel is liberated from serving Pharaoh in order to serve God.
- **3:14** — The Hebrew phrase rendered "I AM WHO I AM" uses an imperfect verb that can also mean "I WILL BE WHO I WILL BE," affirming God's eternal, active presence and linking to the covenant name "the LORD" in verse 15.
- **3:19** — Literally "and not by a mighty hand," meaning Pharaoh will stubbornly resist even strong compulsion until God strikes Egypt with His wonders in verse 20.
