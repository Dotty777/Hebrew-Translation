## Text

**9:1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh and speak to him, 'Thus says the LORD, the God of the Hebrews: Send away My people, that they may serve Me.

**9:2** For if you refuse to send them away, and still hold them fast,

**9:3** behold, the hand of the LORD shall be upon your livestock which are in the field, upon the horses, upon the donkeys, upon the camels, upon the herds, and upon the flocks—a very grievous pestilence.

**9:4** And the LORD shall make a distinction between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt, so that nothing shall die of all that belongs to the children of Israel.'"

**9:5** And the LORD appointed a set time, saying, "Tomorrow the LORD shall do this thing in the land."

**9:6** So the LORD did this thing on the next day, and all the livestock of Egypt died; but of the livestock of the children of Israel, not one died.

**9:7** And Pharaoh sent, and behold, not even one of the livestock of Israel had died. Yet the heart of Pharaoh became hardened, and he did not send the people away.

**9:8** Then the LORD said to Moses and to Aaron, "Take for yourselves handfuls of soot from a kiln, and let Moses scatter it toward the heavens in the sight of Pharaoh.

**9:9** And it shall become fine dust over all the land of Egypt, and it shall become boils breaking out with blisters on man and on beast throughout all the land of Egypt."

**9:10** So they took soot from the kiln and stood before Pharaoh, and Moses scattered it toward the heavens; and it became boils breaking out with blisters on man and on beast.

**9:11** And the magicians could not stand before Moses because of the boils, for the boils were upon the magicians and upon all the Egyptians.

**9:12** But the LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken to Moses.

**9:13** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh, and say to him, 'Thus says the LORD, the God of the Hebrews: Send away My people, that they may serve Me.

**9:14** For at this time I am sending all My plagues against your heart, and upon your servants and upon your people, in order that you may know that there is none like Me in all the earth.

**9:15** For by now I could have stretched out My hand and struck you and your people with pestilence, and you would have been cut off from the earth.

**9:16** Indeed, for this very purpose I have caused you to stand: to show you My power, and that My name may be declared in all the earth.

**9:17** You still exalt yourself against My people, so as not to send them away.

**9:18** Behold, about this time tomorrow I will rain down a very heavy hail, such as has not been in Egypt from the day it was founded even until now.

**9:19** Now therefore send, bring your livestock and all that you have in the field to safety; every man and beast that is found in the field and is not gathered home—the hail shall come down upon them, and they shall die.'"

**9:20** Whoever feared the word of the LORD among the servants of Pharaoh made his servants and his livestock flee into the houses.

**9:21** But whoever did not set his heart to the word of the LORD left his servants and his livestock in the field.

**9:22** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the heavens, that there may be hail throughout all the land of Egypt—upon man, upon beast, and upon every herb of the field in the land of Egypt."

**9:23** So Moses stretched out his rod toward the heavens; and the LORD sent thunder and hail, and fire ran down to the earth. And the LORD rained hail upon the land of Egypt.

**9:24** Thus there was hail, and fire flashing continually in the midst of the hail, exceedingly heavy, such as had never been in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation.

**9:25** And the hail struck throughout all the land of Egypt all that was in the field, both man and beast; and the hail struck every herb of the field and shattered every tree of the field.

**9:26** Only in the land of Goshen, where the children of Israel were, there was no hail.

**9:27** Then Pharaoh sent and summoned Moses and Aaron, and said to them, "I have sinned this time. The LORD is righteous, and I and my people are wicked.

**9:28** Plead with the LORD, for there has been enough of the thunderings of God and hail; and I will send you away, and you shall stay no longer."

**9:29** And Moses said to him, "As soon as I have gone out of the city, I will spread out my hands to the LORD; the thunder shall cease, and there shall be hail no more, in order that you may know that the earth belongs to the LORD.

**9:30** But as for you and your servants, I know that you do not yet fear the LORD God."

**9:31** Now the flax and the barley were struck down, for the barley was in the ear and the flax was in bud.

**9:32** But the wheat and the spelt were not struck down, for they ripen late.

**9:33** So Moses went out of the city from Pharaoh and spread out his hands to the LORD; and the thunder and the hail ceased, and the rain no longer poured down upon the earth.

**9:34** And when Pharaoh saw that the rain, the hail, and the thunder had ceased, he sinned yet again; and he hardened his heart, both he and his servants.

**9:35** So the heart of Pharaoh grew hard, and he did not send away the children of Israel, just as the LORD had spoken by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **9:12** — This is the first time in the plague narratives that the text explicitly states that the LORD Himself hardened Pharaoh's heart.
- **9:16** — Hebrew heemadtikha literally means "I have caused you to stand," conveying that God preserved Pharaoh on the throne to display His power.
- **9:28** — Hebrew qolot Elohim, literally "voices of God," is an idiom for awe-inspiring, mighty thunder.
- **9:31** — Hebrew aviv designates barley that has already formed green ears on the stalk, while givol indicates flax in bud or bloom.
