## Text

**21:1** Now these are the ordinances that you shall set before them:

**21:2** When you buy a Hebrew servant, six years he shall serve, and in the seventh he shall go forth free, without payment.

**21:3** If he enters alone, he shall depart alone; if he is the husband of a wife, then his wife shall go out with him.

**21:4** If his master gives him a wife, and she bears him sons or daughters, the woman and her children shall belong to her master, and he shall depart alone.

**21:5** But if the servant solemnly declares, "I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free,"

**21:6** then his master shall bring him before God, and bring him to the door or to the doorpost; and his master shall pierce his ear with an awl, and he shall serve him forever.

**21:7** And if a man sells his daughter as a maidservant, she shall not depart as the menservants depart.

**21:8** If she is displeasing in the eyes of her master, who has designated her for himself, then he shall allow her to be redeemed; he shall have no authority to sell her to a foreign people, since he has dealt faithlessly with her.

**21:9** And if he designates her for his son, he shall deal with her according to the rights of daughters.

**21:10** If he takes another woman for himself, he shall not diminish her sustenance, her clothing, or her marital rights.

**21:11** And if he does not perform these three things for her, then she shall go out free, without payment of money.

**21:12** Whoever strikes a man so that he dies shall surely be put to death.

**21:13** Yet if he did not lie in wait, but God delivered him into his hand, then I will appoint for you a place to which he may flee.

**21:14** But if a man acts willfully against his neighbor to slay him by treachery, you shall take him from My altar, that he may die.

**21:15** And whoever strikes his father or his mother shall surely be put to death.

**21:16** And whoever curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death.

**21:17** And whoever steals a man and sells him, or if he is found in his hand, shall surely be put to death.

**21:18** And when men contend with one another, and one strikes his neighbor with a stone or with his fist, and he does not die but takes to his bed,

**21:19** if he rises again and walks outdoors leaning on his staff, then the one who struck him shall be cleared of guilt; only he shall compensate for his lost time and ensure that he is thoroughly healed.

**21:20** And if a man strikes his manservant or his maidservant with a rod, and the servant dies under his hand, he shall surely be avenged.

**21:21** Nevertheless, if he endures a day or two, he shall not be avenged, for he is his property.

**21:22** And when men strive together and strike a pregnant woman, so that her children come forth, yet no harm ensues, he shall surely be fined according to what the woman's husband lays upon him, and he shall pay as the judges determine.

**21:23** But if harm ensues, then you shall give life for life,

**21:24** eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,

**21:25** burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.

**21:26** And if a man strikes the eye of his manservant or the eye of his maidservant and destroys it, he shall release him free on account of his eye.

**21:27** And if he knocks out the tooth of his manservant or the tooth of his maidservant, he shall release him free on account of his tooth.

**21:28** And if an ox gores a man or a woman to death, the ox shall surely be stoned, and its flesh shall not be eaten; yet the owner of the ox shall be held blameless.

**21:29** But if the ox was prone to gore in times past, and its owner had been warned, yet he did not restrain it, and it puts to death a man or a woman, the ox shall be stoned, and its owner also shall be put to death.

**21:30** If a ransom is laid upon him, then he shall pay for the redemption of his life whatever is laid upon him.

**21:31** Whether it gores a son or gores a daughter, according to this same ordinance it shall be done to him.

**21:32** If the ox gores a manservant or a maidservant, he shall pay thirty shekels of silver to his master, and the ox shall be stoned.

**21:33** And if a man opens a pit, or if a man digs a pit and does not cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it,

**21:34** the owner of the pit shall make restitution; he shall repay silver to its owner, and the dead animal shall belong to him.

**21:35** And if a man's ox strikes his neighbor's ox so that it dies, then they shall sell the living ox and divide its price, and they shall also divide the dead one.

**21:36** Or if it was known that the ox was prone to gore in times past, and its owner did not restrain it, he shall surely make restitution, ox for ox, and the dead animal shall belong to him.

**21:37** If a man steals an ox or a sheep and slaughters it or sells it, he shall restore five oxen for the ox and four sheep for the sheep.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — The Hebrew phrase literally reads God; many traditional interpreters understand it as referring to the judges who officiated and rendered legal decisions before God.
- **21:8** — Translates the read form (qere), for himself; the written consonantal form (ketiv) reads not, which would yield who has not designated her.
- **21:16** — In this Hebrew text, the statute against cursing parents stands before the statute against stealing a person in verse 17, matching the sequence in the Septuagint, whereas the standard Masoretic text reverses these two verses.
- **21:37** — Verse numbering follows the Masoretic Hebrew division, in which this verse concludes chapter 21; most English versions number it as Exodus 22:1.
