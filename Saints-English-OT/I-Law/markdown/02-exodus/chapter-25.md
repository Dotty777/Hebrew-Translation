## Text

**25:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, that they bring Me an offering. From every man whose heart prompts him willingly you shall receive My offering.

**25:3** And this is the offering which you shall receive from them: gold, silver, and bronze;

**25:4** blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, fine linen, and goat hair;

**25:5** ram skins dyed red, fine leather, and acacia wood;

**25:7** onyx stones, and stones for setting in the ephod and in the breastplate.

**25:8** And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell in their midst.

**25:9** According to all that I am showing you—the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its furnishings—just so you shall make it.

**25:10** And they shall make an ark of acacia wood; two and a half cubits shall be its length, a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

**25:11** And you shall overlay it with pure gold; inside and outside you shall overlay it, and you shall make upon it a molding of gold all around.

**25:12** And you shall cast four rings of gold for it and set them upon its four feet—two rings on its one side, and two rings on its other side.

**25:13** And you shall make poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold.

**25:14** And you shall insert the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark by them.

**25:15** The poles shall remain in the rings of the ark; they shall not be removed from it.

**25:16** And you shall place into the ark the Testimony which I will give you.

**25:17** You shall also make a mercy seat of pure gold; two and a half cubits shall be its length, and a cubit and a half its width.

**25:18** And you shall make two cherubim of gold; of hammered work you shall make them at the two ends of the mercy seat.

**25:19** Make one cherub at the one end and one cherub at the other end; of one piece with the mercy seat you shall make the cherubim at its two ends.

**25:20** And the cherubim shall spread out their wings upward, overshadowing the mercy seat with their wings, with their faces one toward the other; toward the mercy seat shall the faces of the cherubim be.

**25:21** And you shall set the mercy seat upon the top of the ark, and in the ark you shall place the Testimony which I will give you.

**25:22** And there I will meet with you, and I will speak with you from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubim which are upon the ark of the Testimony, of all that I will command you for the children of Israel.

**25:23** You shall also make a table of acacia wood; two cubits shall be its length, a cubit its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

**25:24** And you shall overlay it with pure gold, and make for it a molding of gold all around.

**25:25** And you shall make for it a frame of a handbreadth all around, and you shall make a molding of gold for its frame all around.

**25:26** And you shall make for it four rings of gold, and attach the rings to the four corners that are on its four legs.

**25:27** Close to the frame the rings shall be, as holders for the poles to carry the table.

**25:28** And you shall make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, that the table may be carried with them.

**25:29** You shall also make its plates, its dishes, its pitchers, and its bowls with which drink offerings are poured; of pure gold you shall make them.

**25:30** And you shall set the bread of the Presence upon the table before Me continually.

**25:31** And you shall make a lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work shall the lampstand be made. Its base and its shaft, its cups, its calyxes, and its blossoms shall be of one piece with it.

**25:32** And six branches shall extend from its sides: three branches of the lampstand out of its one side, and three branches of the lampstand out of its other side.

**25:33** Three cups shaped like almond blossoms shall be on the one branch, with a calyx and a blossom, and three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the next branch, with a calyx and a blossom—so for the six branches extending from the lampstand.

**25:34** And on the lampstand itself there shall be four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with its calyxes and its blossoms.

**25:35** And there shall be a calyx beneath two of the branches of one piece with it, and a calyx beneath two of the branches of one piece with it, and a calyx beneath two of the branches of one piece with it—according to the six branches extending from the lampstand.

**25:36** Their calyxes and their branches shall be of one piece with it; the whole of it shall be one hammered piece of pure gold.

**25:37** You shall also make seven lamps for it, and they shall mount its lamps so that they shed light upon the space in front of it.

**25:38** And its tongs and its trays shall be of pure gold.

**25:39** Of a talent of pure gold it shall be made, along with all these utensils.

**25:40** And see that you make them according to their pattern which is being shown to you on the mountain."

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — The exact identity of Hebrew techashim is uncertain; scholars variously understand it as fine tanned leather or the hides of marine animals such as dugongs or dolphins.
- **25:17** — Hebrew kapporet derives from a root associated with covering and atonement, traditionally rendered "mercy seat" as the golden lid over the ark of the Testimony.
- **25:30** — Hebrew lechem panim literally means "bread of the face" or "bread of the Presence" (traditionally "showbread"), echoing "before Me" (literally "before My face") in the same clause.
- **25:31** — Hebrew kaftor denotes the rounded ornamental bulb or calyx positioned beneath each almond blossom along the lampstand's central shaft and branches.
- **25:39** — A biblical talent (Hebrew kikkar) was a large standard of weight equal to roughly seventy-five pounds.
