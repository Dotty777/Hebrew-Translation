## Text

**4:1** Then Moses answered and said, "But behold, they will not believe me, nor will they listen to my voice; for they will say, 'The LORD has not appeared to you.'"

**4:2** So the LORD said to him, "What is this in your hand?" And he said, "A staff."

**4:3** And He said, "Cast it to the ground." So he cast it to the ground, and it became a serpent; and Moses fled from before it.

**4:4** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand and take hold of its tail"—and he stretched out his hand and grasped it, and it became a staff in his palm—

**4:5** "so that they may believe that the LORD, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has appeared to you."

**4:6** And the LORD spoke to him again, "Put your hand now into your bosom." So he put his hand into his bosom; and he brought it out, and behold, his hand was leprous, like snow.

**4:7** Then He said, "Put your hand back into your bosom." So he put his hand back into his bosom, and brought it out from his bosom, and behold, it was restored like his own flesh.

**4:8** "And it shall be, if they do not believe you nor heed the voice of the first sign, that they will believe the voice of the latter sign.

**4:9** And it shall be, if they do not believe even these two signs nor listen to your voice, that you shall take some of the water of the Nile and pour it on the dry ground; and the water that you take from the Nile shall become blood on the dry ground."

**4:10** Then Moses said to the LORD, "O Lord, I am not a man of words, neither in time past nor since You have spoken to Your servant; for I am heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue."

**4:11** And the LORD said to him, "Who has made the mouth of man? Or who makes the mute, or the deaf, or the seeing, or the blind? Is it not I, the LORD?

**4:12** Now therefore, go, and I will be with your mouth and teach you what you shall speak."

**4:13** But he said, "O Lord, please send by the hand of whomever You will send."

**4:14** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Moses, and He said, "Is there not Aaron your brother, the Levite? I know that he can indeed speak well. And behold, he is also coming out to meet you; and when he sees you, he will rejoice in his heart.

**4:15** You shall speak to him and put the words in his mouth; and I will be with your mouth and with his mouth, and I will teach you what you shall do.

**4:16** And he shall speak for you to the people; and it shall be that he himself shall be a mouth for you, and you shall be to him as God.

**4:17** And you shall take this staff in your hand, with which you shall perform the signs."

**4:18** So Moses went and returned to Jether his father-in-law, and said to him, "Please let me go and return to my brethren who are in Egypt, and see whether they are still alive." And Jethro said to Moses, "Go in peace."

**4:19** Now the LORD said to Moses in Midian, "Go, return to Egypt; for all the men who were seeking your life are dead."

**4:20** So Moses took his wife and his sons and set them on a donkey, and he returned to the land of Egypt. And Moses took the staff of God in his hand.

**4:21** And the LORD said to Moses, "When you go back to Egypt, see that you perform before Pharaoh all the wonders which I have put in your hand; yet I will harden his heart, so that he will not let the people go.

**4:22** Then you shall say to Pharaoh, 'Thus says the LORD: Israel is My son, My firstborn.

**4:23** And I have said to you, "Let My son go that he may serve Me," yet you have refused to let him go; behold, I will slay your son, your firstborn.'"

**4:24** Now it came to pass on the way, at the lodging place, that the LORD met him and sought to put him to death.

**4:25** Then Zipporah took a flint and cut off the foreskin of her son, and touched it to his feet, and said, "Surely you are a bridegroom of blood to me!"

**4:26** So He let him alone. Then she said, "A bridegroom of blood, because of the circumcision."

**4:27** And the LORD said to Aaron, "Go into the wilderness to meet Moses." So he went and met him at the mountain of God, and kissed him.

**4:28** Then Moses told Aaron all the words of the LORD with which He had sent him, and all the signs which He had commanded him.

**4:29** So Moses and Aaron went and gathered together all the elders of the children of Israel.

**4:30** And Aaron spoke all the words which the LORD had spoken to Moses, and he performed the signs in the sight of the people.

**4:31** So the people believed; and when they heard that the LORD had visited the children of Israel and that He had looked upon their affliction, they bowed their heads and worshiped.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Masoretic Ketiv writes "what is this" as a single contracted word, while the Qere divides it into two words ("what is this"), which is followed in the translation.
- **4:10** — Literally "a man of words" and "heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue," Hebrew idioms expressing a lack of fluency or ease in speaking.
- **4:18** — The Masoretic Hebrew uses the shorter name form Jether in the first clause and the fuller form Jethro in the second clause for Moses' father-in-law.
- **4:21** — The Hebrew verb rendered "harden" literally means to strengthen or make firm, indicating the stiffening of Pharaoh's resolve.
- **4:25** — Zipporah's phrase "bridegroom of blood" is a compressed Hebrew expression connecting her husband's sparing with the blood of her son's circumcision.
