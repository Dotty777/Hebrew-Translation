## Text

**31:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "See, I have called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.

**31:3** And I have filled him with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, in knowledge, and in every kind of craftsmanship,

**31:4** to devise skilled designs, to work in gold, in silver, and in bronze,

**31:5** in the cutting of stones for setting, and in the carving of wood, to labor in every manner of workmanship.

**31:6** And behold, I Myself have appointed with him Oholiab the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and into the heart of all who are wise of heart I have placed wisdom, that they may make all that I have commanded you:

**31:7** the tent of meeting, the ark for the Testimony, the mercy seat that is upon it, and all the furnishings of the tent,

**31:8** the table and its utensils, the pure lampstand with all its utensils, and the altar of incense,

**31:9** the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the laver and its base,

**31:10** the finely woven garments, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests,

**31:11** and the anointing oil and the fragrant incense for the holy place. According to all that I have commanded you, they shall make them."

**31:12** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:13** "And you, speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'Surely you shall keep My Sabbaths, for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

**31:14** Therefore you shall keep the Sabbath, for it is holy to you. Everyone who profanes it shall surely be put to death; for whoever does any work on it, that soul shall be cut off from the midst of his people.

**31:15** For six days work may be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD. Whoever does work on the Sabbath day shall surely be put to death.

**31:16** Therefore the children of Israel shall keep the Sabbath, observing the Sabbath throughout their generations as an everlasting covenant.

**31:17** Between Me and the children of Israel it is a sign forever; for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.'"

**31:18** And He gave to Moses, when He had finished speaking with him upon Mount Sinai, the two tablets of the Testimony, tablets of stone, written with the finger of God.

## Translator Notes

- **31:10** — "Finely woven garments" renders a Hebrew phrase referring either to intricately braided priestly vestments or to the woven cloths used to cover the sanctuary furnishings in transit.
- **31:13** — "It is a sign" translates the feminine pronoun referring to the Sabbath, written here and in verses 14 and 17 with the standard Pentateuchal spelling read as the feminine form.
- **31:17** — "Was refreshed" translates a Hebrew verb linked to the root for breath or soul, an anthropomorphic expression for God's restful delight upon the completion of creation.
