## Text

**39:1** All the gold that was used for the work in all the labor of the sanctuary—the gold of the wave offering—was twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and thirty shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary.

**39:2** And the silver from those who were numbered of the congregation was one hundred talents and one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary:

**39:3** a beka for each head, half a shekel according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for everyone who passed over to those who were numbered, from twenty years old and above, for six hundred and three thousand, five hundred and fifty men.

**39:4** And the hundred talents of silver were for casting the sockets of the sanctuary and the sockets of the veil: one hundred sockets from the hundred talents, one talent for each socket.

**39:5** And from the one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels he made hooks for the pillars, overlaid their capitals, and joined them with bands.

**39:6** And the bronze of the wave offering was seventy talents and two thousand four hundred shekels.

**39:8** And the sockets of the court all around, and the sockets of the gate of the court, and all the pegs of the tabernacle, and all the pegs of the court all around.

**39:9** With it he made the sockets for the doorway of the tent of meeting, the bronze altar, the bronze grate that belonged to it, and all the utensils of the altar.

**39:10** Thus all the work of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was completed. And the children of Israel did according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses; so they did.

**39:13** And they brought the tabernacle to Moses—the tent and all its furnishings, its clasps, its boards, its bars, its pillars, and its sockets;

**39:14** the ark of the Testimony with its poles, and the mercy seat;

**39:15** the golden altar, the anointing oil, the sweet incense, and the screen for the doorway of the tent;

**39:16** the pure lampstand with its lamps, the lamps set in order, all its utensils, and the oil for the light;

**39:17** the table, all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;

**39:18** the finely woven garments for ministering in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons to serve as priests;

**39:19** the hangings of the court, its pillars and its sockets, the screen for the gate of the court, its cords and its pegs, and all the utensils for the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;

**39:20** and the covering of ram skins dyed red, and the covering of fine leather, and the veil of the screen.

**39:22** According to all that the LORD had commanded Moses, so the children of Israel performed all the work.

**39:23** Then Moses looked upon all the work, and behold, they had done it; just as the LORD had commanded, so they had done. And Moses blessed them.

## Translator Notes

- **39:3** — A beka was an ancient weight equal to half a sanctuary shekel, approximately five to six grams of silver.
- **39:8** — The arrangement of verses 8 and 9 follows this edition's order of the bronze inventory, placing the court sockets and pegs before the governing verb in verse 9.
- **39:17** — "Bread of the Presence" (lehem hapanim) is traditionally known as showbread, the twelve loaves set continually upon the golden table before the LORD.
- **39:20** — The Hebrew term rendered "fine leather" (tehashim) is of uncertain identification, variously understood as fine tanned leather, dyed goatskins, or the hides of marine animals such as dugongs.
