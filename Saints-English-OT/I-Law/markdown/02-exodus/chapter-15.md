## Text

**15:1** Then Moses and the children of Israel sang this song to the LORD, and they spoke, saying, "I will sing to the LORD, for He is gloriously exalted; the horse and its rider He has cast into the sea.

**15:2** My strength and my song is the LORD, and He has become salvation to me; this is my God, and I will extol Him, the God of my father, and I will exalt Him.

**15:3** The LORD is a man of battle; the LORD is His name.

**15:4** The chariots of Pharaoh and his host He has hurled into the sea, and the choicest of his captains were sunk in the Red Sea.

**15:5** The deeps have covered them; they went down into the depths like a stone.

**15:6** Your right hand, O LORD, is glorious in might; Your right hand, O LORD, shatters the foe.

**15:7** And in the abundance of Your majesty You tear down those who rise against You; You send forth Your fierce wrath, and it devours them like stubble.

**15:8** And by the blast of Your nostrils the waters were heaped up; the flowing floods stood firm like a wall, and the deeps congealed in the heart of the sea.

**15:9** The enemy said, 'I will pursue, I will overtake, I will apportion the spoil; my soul shall have its fill of them; I will unsheathe my sword, and my hand shall dispossess them.'

**15:10** You blew with Your wind, and the sea covered them; they plunged like lead into the mighty waters.

**15:11** Who is like You among the gods, O LORD? Who is like You, majestic in holiness, awesome in praises, working wonders?

**15:12** You stretched forth Your right hand, and the earth swallowed them up.

**15:13** In Your lovingkindness You have led forth the people whom You redeemed; in Your strength You have guided them to the abode of Your holiness.

**15:14** The peoples have heard, and they tremble; pangs of anguish have seized the inhabitants of Philistia.

**15:15** Then the chieftains of Edom were terrified; trembling lays hold on the mighty men of Moab; all the inhabitants of Canaan have melted away.

**15:16** Terror and dread shall fall upon them; by the greatness of Your arm they shall be still as stone, until Your people pass over, O LORD, until the people whom You have acquired pass over.

**15:17** You will bring them in and plant them upon the mountain of Your inheritance, the established place You have made for Your dwelling, O LORD, the sanctuary, O Lord, which Your hands have founded.

**15:18** The LORD shall reign as King forever and ever."

**15:19** For Pharaoh's horses went into the sea with his chariots and his horsemen, and the LORD turned back the waters of the sea upon them; yet the children of Israel walked upon dry ground in the midst of the sea.

**15:20** Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron's sister, took the timbrel in her hand, and all the women went forth after her with timbrels and with dances.

**15:21** And Miriam answered them in song, "Sing to the LORD, for He is gloriously exalted; the horse and its rider He has cast into the sea."

**15:22** Then Moses led Israel onward from the Red Sea, and they went out into the Wilderness of Shur; and they traveled three days in the wilderness and found no water.

**15:23** And they came to Marah, yet they could not drink the waters of Marah, for they were bitter; therefore its name was called Marah.

**15:24** And the people murmured against Moses, saying, "What shall we drink?"

**15:25** So he cried out to the LORD, and the LORD showed him a tree; and he cast it into the waters, and the waters became sweet. There He appointed for them a statute and an ordinance, and there He tested them,

**15:26** and He said, "If you will diligently listen to the voice of the LORD your God, and do what is right in His eyes, and give ear to His commandments, and keep all His statutes, none of the diseases that I laid upon Egypt will I lay upon you; for I am the LORD who heals you."

**15:27** Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees; and they encamped there beside the waters.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Hebrew text here uses Yah, a poetic shortened form of the divine name, rendered "the LORD." The verb rendered "I will extol Him" is a rare poetic form meaning to adorn with praise or to glorify.
- **15:4** — "The Red Sea" renders the Hebrew yam-suph, literally "the Sea of Reeds," following traditional English usage.
- **15:17** — The verse pairs the Tetragrammaton ("the LORD") in the second clause with the title Adonai ("the Lord") in the final clause.
