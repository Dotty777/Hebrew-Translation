## Text

**22:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:2** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, that they keep themselves separate from the holy things of the children of Israel, so that they do not profane My holy name in what they consecrate to Me: I am the LORD.

**22:3** Say to them, 'Throughout your generations, any man of all your offspring who draws near to the holy things which the children of Israel consecrate to the LORD while his uncleanness is upon him—that soul shall be cut off from My presence: I am the LORD.

**22:4** No man of the offspring of Aaron who is a leper or has a bodily discharge shall eat of the holy things until he is clean. And whoever touches anything defiled by the dead, or a man who has an emission of seed,

**22:5** or a man who touches any creeping thing by which he may be made unclean, or any person by whom he may be made unclean, whatever his uncleanness may be—

**22:6** the person who touches any such thing shall be unclean until evening, and shall not eat from the holy things unless he has bathed his body in water.

**22:7** When the sun has set, he shall be clean; and afterward he may eat of the holy things, because it is his food.

**22:8** What dies of itself or is torn by beasts he shall not eat, lest he defile himself with it: I am the LORD.

**22:9** They shall therefore keep My charge, lest they incur sin because of it and die for it when they profane it: I am the LORD who sanctifies them.

**22:10** No outsider shall eat any holy thing; neither a guest residing with a priest nor a hired servant shall eat any holy thing.

**22:11** But if a priest purchases a person with his own money, that one may eat of it; and those born in his house may also eat of his food.

**22:12** If a priest's daughter marries an outsider, she shall not eat of the heave offering of the holy things.

**22:13** Yet if a priest's daughter becomes a widow or is divorced, and has no offspring, and returns to her father's house as in her youth, she may eat of her father's food; but no outsider shall eat of it.

**22:14** And if a man eats a holy thing unintentionally, he shall add one-fifth to it and repay the holy thing to the priest.

**22:15** And they shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel which they offer up to the LORD,

**22:16** nor cause them to bear the iniquity of guilt when they eat their holy things; for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.'"

**22:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:18** "Speak to Aaron, to his sons, and to all the children of Israel, and declare to them, 'Any man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourners in Israel, who brings his offering for any of their vows or for any of their freewill offerings which they present to the LORD as a burnt offering,

**22:19** that it may be accepted on your behalf, it shall be a male without blemish, from the cattle, from the sheep, or from the goats.

**22:20** Whatever has a blemish you shall not present, for it shall not be accepted on your behalf.

**22:21** And whoever presents a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD to fulfill a special vow, or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock—it must be without blemish to be accepted; no blemish at all shall be in it.

**22:22** Any animal that is blind, or fractured, or maimed, or has a running sore, or an itching disease, or scabs—these you shall not offer to the LORD, nor place any of them as a fire offering upon the altar to the LORD.

**22:23** An ox or a sheep that has a limb too long or too short you may present as a freewill offering, but for a vow it shall not be accepted.

**22:24** Any animal that is bruised, or crushed, or torn, or cut you shall not offer to the LORD, nor shall you do such things in your land.

**22:25** Neither from the hand of a foreigner shall you present any of these as the bread of your God, because their deformity is in them and a blemish is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.'"

**22:26** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:27** "When an ox, or a sheep, or a goat is born, it shall remain seven days with its mother; and from the eighth day onward it shall be accepted as a fire offering to the LORD.

**22:28** Whether an ox or a sheep, you shall not slaughter both it and its young on the same day.

**22:29** And when you offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving to the LORD, you shall offer it so that it may be accepted on your behalf.

**22:30** On that same day it shall be eaten; you shall leave none of it until the morning: I am the LORD.

**22:31** Therefore you shall keep My commandments and perform them: I am the LORD.

**22:32** And you shall not profane My holy name, but I will be hallowed in the midst of the children of Israel; I am the LORD who sanctifies you,

**22:33** who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God: I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — Keep themselves separate translates a Hebrew verb meaning to abstain or hold aloof, directing the priests to refrain from handling or eating sacred offerings while ritually unclean.
- **22:10** — Outsider (Hebrew zar) refers here not necessarily to a foreigner, but to any unauthorized person outside the priestly household.
- **22:24** — The four Hebrew participles—bruised, crushed, torn, and cut—refer to forms of castration or injury to an animal's reproductive organs, forbidden both in sacrifices and as a practice within Israel.
- **22:27** — With its mother is literally under its mother, referring to the seven-day period in which the newborn animal remains nursing with its mother before becoming acceptable for sacrifice.
