## Text

**21:1** And the LORD said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and declare to them, 'None of them shall defile himself for a dead body among his people,

**21:2** except for his blood relative who is nearest to him: for his mother, for his father, for his son, for his daughter, and for his brother;

**21:3** and for his virgin sister who is near to him, who has not belonged to a husband—for her he may defile himself.

**21:4** He shall not defile himself as a husband among his people, so as to profane himself.

**21:5** They shall not make any bald patch upon their head, nor shave off the edges of their beard, nor cut any gashes in their flesh.

**21:6** They shall be holy to their God and shall not profane the name of their God, for they present the fire offerings of the LORD, the bread of their God; therefore they shall be holy.

**21:7** They shall not take in marriage a woman who is a harlot or one who has been defiled, nor shall they take a woman divorced from her husband, for he is holy to his God.

**21:8** Therefore you shall regard him as holy, for he offers the bread of your God; he shall be holy to you, because I, the LORD who sanctifies you, am holy.

**21:9** And if the daughter of any priest profanes herself by committing harlotry, she profanes her father; she shall be burned with fire.

**21:10** Now the priest who is chief among his brethren, upon whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been consecrated to wear the garments, shall not let the hair of his head hang loose, nor tear his garments.

**21:11** Neither shall he go near any dead body, nor defile himself for his father or for his mother.

**21:12** And he shall not depart from the sanctuary, nor profane the sanctuary of his God, for the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is upon him: I am the LORD.

**21:13** Moreover, he shall take a wife in her virginity.

**21:14** A widow, or a divorced woman, or one who has been defiled, or a harlot—these he shall not marry; rather, he shall take a virgin of his own people as his wife.

**21:15** Thus he shall not profane his offspring among his people, for I am the LORD who sanctifies him.'"

**21:16** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**21:17** "Speak to Aaron, saying, 'No man of your offspring throughout their generations who has any blemish shall draw near to offer the bread of his God.

**21:18** For no man in whom there is a blemish shall draw near: a man who is blind, or lame, or has a disfigured face, or a limb too long,

**21:19** or a man who has a broken foot or a broken hand,

**21:20** or is a hunchback, or a dwarf, or has a clouded spot in his eye, or an itching disease, or scabs, or crushed testicles.

**21:21** No man of the offspring of Aaron the priest who has a blemish shall come near to present the fire offerings of the LORD; he has a blemish, so he shall not come near to present the bread of his God.

**21:22** He may eat the bread of his God, both from the most holy things and from the holy things;

**21:23** only he shall not come near the veil nor approach the altar, because he has a blemish, lest he profane My sanctuaries; for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.'"

**21:24** Thus Moses spoke to Aaron and to his sons, and to all the children of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **21:4** — The Hebrew word baal here can mean either a husband (forbidding defilement for relatives by marriage) or a chief man and leader among his people.
- **21:5** — Ketiv/qere variant: the written consonantal text (ketiv) has an extra initial yod before the noun, whereas the read form (qere) gives the standard noun for a bald patch or baldness, translated here.
- **21:10** — Consecrated is literally whose hand has been filled; leaving the hair unbound and tearing garments were customary signs of mourning forbidden to the high priest.
- **21:20** — Several of the Hebrew terms for physical blemishes and skin afflictions in this verse are rare, so their exact medical equivalents remain somewhat uncertain.
