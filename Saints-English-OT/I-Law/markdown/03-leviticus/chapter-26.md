## Text

**26:1** You shall not make idols for yourselves, nor shall you erect for yourselves a carved image or a sacred pillar, nor shall you place a figured stone in your land to bow down before it; for I am the LORD your God.

**26:2** My Sabbaths you shall keep, and My sanctuary you shall revere; I am the LORD.

**26:3** If you walk in My statutes, and observe My commandments and perform them,

**26:4** then I will grant your rains in their season, and the land shall yield its produce, and the trees of the field shall give their fruit.

**26:5** Threshing shall overtake the vintage for you, and the vintage shall overtake the sowing; you shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell securely in your land.

**26:6** I will grant peace in the land, and you shall lie down with none to make you afraid; I will remove fierce beasts from the land, and no sword shall pass through your land.

**26:7** You shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.

**26:8** Five of you shall pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you shall pursue ten thousand; and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

**26:9** I will turn toward you with favor, and make you fruitful and multiply you, and uphold My covenant with you.

**26:10** You shall eat the old harvest long stored, and clear out the old because of the new.

**26:11** I will set My dwelling place among you, and My soul shall not abhor you.

**26:12** I will walk in your midst, and I will be your God, and you shall be My people.

**26:13** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that you should not be their slaves; I broke the bars of your yoke and led you forth upright.

**26:14** But if you do not listen to Me, and do not perform all these commandments,

**26:15** and if you spurn My statutes, or if your soul abhors My judgments, so that you do not perform all My commandments, so as to break My covenant,

**26:16** then I also will do this to you: I will appoint sudden terror over you, wasting disease and burning fever that consume the eyes and cause the soul to languish; and you shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies shall eat it.

**26:17** I will set My face against you, and you shall be struck down before your enemies; those who hate you shall rule over you, and you shall flee when no one pursues you.

**26:18** And if after all these things you still do not listen to Me, then I will chasten you yet seven times more for your sins.

**26:19** I will break the pride of your might; I will make your heavens like iron and your earth like bronze.

**26:20** Your strength shall be spent in vain; your land shall not yield its produce, nor shall the trees of the land give their fruit.

**26:21** And if you walk contrary to Me and are unwilling to listen to Me, then I will bring upon you sevenfold more plagues, according to your sins.

**26:22** I will send the wild beasts of the field among you, which shall bereave you of your children, cut off your livestock, and make you few in number; and your roads shall lie desolate.

**26:23** And if by these things you are not disciplined by Me, but walk contrary to Me,

**26:24** then I also will walk contrary to you, and I Myself will strike you sevenfold for your sins.

**26:25** I will bring upon you a sword that shall execute the vengeance of the covenant; and when you gather within your cities, I will send pestilence into your midst, and you shall be given into the hand of the enemy.

**26:26** When I break your staff of bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and they shall return your bread by weight; and you shall eat, yet not be satisfied.

**26:27** And if in spite of this you do not listen to Me, but walk contrary to Me,

**26:28** then I will walk contrary to you in fury, and I Myself will chasten you sevenfold for your sins.

**26:29** You shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you shall eat.

**26:30** I will destroy your high places, cut down your incense altars, and cast your carcasses upon the carcasses of your idols; and My soul shall abhor you.

**26:31** I will lay your cities waste and bring your sanctuaries to desolation, and I will not smell the fragrance of your soothing aromas.

**26:32** I Myself will make the land desolate, so that your enemies who dwell in it shall be appalled at it.

**26:33** And you I will scatter among the nations, and I will unsheathe a sword after you; your land shall become a desolation, and your cities shall be a waste.

**26:34** Then the land shall enjoy its sabbaths all the days of its desolation, while you are in the land of your enemies; then the land shall rest and enjoy its sabbaths.

**26:35** All the days of its desolation it shall have rest, the rest that it did not have during your sabbaths when you dwelt upon it.

**26:36** And as for those of you who are left, I will send faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies; the sound of a driven leaf shall pursue them, and they shall flee as one flees from the sword, and fall though no one pursues.

**26:37** They shall stumble over one another as if before a sword, when no one pursues; and you shall have no power to stand before your enemies.

**26:38** You shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall devour you.

**26:39** And those of you who are left shall waste away in their iniquity in the lands of your enemies; and also because of the iniquities of their fathers with them they shall waste away.

**26:40** But if they confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, in their unfaithfulness with which they acted unfaithfully against Me, and also that they walked contrary to Me,

**26:41** so that I also walked contrary to them and brought them into the land of their enemies—if then their uncircumcised heart is humbled, and then they make amends for their iniquity,

**26:42** then I will remember My covenant with Jacob, and also My covenant with Isaac, and also My covenant with Abraham I will remember; and the land I will remember.

**26:43** For the land shall be forsaken by them, and shall enjoy its sabbaths while it lies desolate without them; and they shall make amends for their iniquity, because, even because they spurned My judgments and their soul abhorred My statutes.

**26:44** Yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not spurn them, nor will I abhor them so as to destroy them utterly and break My covenant with them; for I am the LORD their God.

**26:45** But for their sake I will remember the covenant of their forefathers, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, that I might be their God; I am the LORD.

**26:46** These are the statutes and judgments and laws which the LORD established between Himself and the children of Israel on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The phrase figured stone translates a Hebrew expression for a carved or engraved stone set in place for idolatrous prostration.
- **26:21** — The idiom walk contrary to Me renders a Hebrew term found only in this chapter, denoting stubborn opposition, hostility, or willful disregard of God.
- **26:34** — The Hebrew verb rendered enjoy also carries the legal sense of paying off or making restitution, linking the land's forced sabbatical rest with the people making amends for their iniquity in verses 41 and 43.
- **26:42** — The patriarchs are named in reverse chronological order, tracing the covenant back to its origin; in the Masoretic text the name Jacob is written here in its rare full spelling.
