## Text

**3:1** Now if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, if he presents it from the herd, whether male or female, he shall present it without blemish before the LORD.

**3:2** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall dash the blood all around against the altar.

**3:3** Then he shall present from the sacrifice of the peace offerings, as an offering by fire to the LORD, the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is upon the entrails,

**3:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them by the loins, and the lobe upon the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

**3:5** And the sons of Aaron shall burn it on the altar, upon the burnt offering that is on the wood upon the fire; it is an offering by fire of a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**3:6** And if his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD is from the flock, male or female, he shall present it without blemish.

**3:7** If he presents a lamb as his offering, then he shall present it before the LORD.

**3:8** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his offering and slaughter it before the tent of meeting; and the sons of Aaron shall dash its blood all around against the altar.

**3:9** Then he shall present from the sacrifice of the peace offerings, as an offering by fire to the LORD, its fat—the entire fat tail, which he shall remove close to the backbone—and the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is upon the entrails,

**3:10** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them by the loins, and the lobe upon the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

**3:11** And the priest shall burn it on the altar as food, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**3:12** And if his offering is a goat, then he shall present it before the LORD.

**3:13** And he shall lay his hand upon its head and slaughter it before the tent of meeting; and the sons of Aaron shall dash its blood all around against the altar.

**3:14** Then he shall present from it his offering, as an offering by fire to the LORD, the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is upon the entrails,

**3:15** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them by the loins, and the lobe upon the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

**3:16** And the priest shall burn them on the altar as food, an offering by fire for a pleasing aroma. All fat belongs to the LORD.

**3:17** It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations, in all your dwellings: you shall not eat any fat or any blood.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Peace offerings, from a Hebrew root denoting wholeness and well-being, were fellowship sacrifices whose meat was shared between the altar, the priests, and the worshiper.
- **3:9** — Broad-tailed sheep common in the ancient Near East have a heavy, fatty tail considered a choice portion reserved for the altar.
- **3:11** — The Hebrew word literally meaning bread or food is used figuratively for the choice sacrificial portions presented to the LORD upon the altar.
