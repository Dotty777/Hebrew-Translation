## Text

**8:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and the bull for the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread;

**8:3** and assemble all the congregation at the entrance of the tent of meeting."

**8:4** So Moses did just as the LORD commanded him, and the congregation assembled at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**8:5** And Moses said to the congregation, "This is the thing which the LORD has commanded to be done."

**8:6** Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.

**8:7** He put the tunic on him, girded him with the sash, clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him; and he girded him with the woven band of the ephod, and fastened the ephod upon him with it.

**8:8** Then he placed the breastplate on him, and into the breastplate he put the Urim and the Thummim.

**8:9** And he set the turban upon his head; and upon the turban, at the front of it, he set the golden plate, the holy crown, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:10** Then Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and all that was in it, and consecrated them.

**8:11** He sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its utensils, and the laver and its base, to consecrate them.

**8:12** And he poured some of the anointing oil upon Aaron's head and anointed him, to consecrate him.

**8:13** Then Moses brought Aaron's sons forward and clothed them with tunics, and girded them with sashes, and bound caps upon them, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:14** And he brought forward the bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the bull for the sin offering.

**8:15** And he slaughtered it, and Moses took the blood and put it on the horns of the altar all around with his finger, and purified the altar; and the rest of the blood he poured out at the base of the altar, and consecrated it to make atonement for it.

**8:16** Then he took all the fat that was on the entrails, and the fatty lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses burned them upon the altar.

**8:17** But the bull, its hide, its flesh, and its offal he burned with fire outside the camp, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:18** Then he brought forward the ram for the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the ram.

**8:19** And he slaughtered it, and Moses dashed the blood against the altar all around.

**8:20** Then he cut the ram into its pieces, and Moses burned the head, the pieces, and the suet.

**8:21** And he washed the entrails and the legs with water, and Moses burned the entire ram upon the altar; it was a burnt offering for a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:22** Then he brought forward the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the ram.

**8:23** And he slaughtered it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

**8:24** Then he brought Aaron's sons forward, and Moses put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, and on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet; and Moses dashed the blood against the altar all around.

**8:25** And he took the fat, and the fat tail, and all the fat that was on the entrails, and the fatty lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and the right thigh.

**8:26** And from the basket of unleavened bread that was before the LORD he took one unleavened cake, and one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer, and placed them upon the fat portions and upon the right thigh.

**8:27** Then he placed all these upon the palms of Aaron and upon the palms of his sons, and waved them as a wave offering before the LORD.

**8:28** Then Moses took them from their palms and burned them upon the altar on top of the burnt offering; they were an ordination offering for a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**8:29** And Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD; from the ram of ordination it was Moses' portion, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:30** Then Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar, and sprinkled it upon Aaron, upon his garments, and upon his sons, and upon the garments of his sons with him; and he consecrated Aaron, his garments, and his sons, and the garments of his sons with him.

**8:31** And Moses said to Aaron and to his sons, "Boil the flesh at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and there you shall eat it along with the bread that is in the basket of ordination, just as I have commanded, saying, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat it.'

**8:32** And whatever remains of the flesh and of the bread you shall burn with fire.

**8:33** And you shall not go out from the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until the day when the days of your ordination are fulfilled; for seven days it shall take to ordain you.

**8:34** As has been done this day, so the LORD has commanded to be done, to make atonement for you.

**8:35** Therefore you shall abide at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days, and keep the charge of the LORD, so that you may not die; for thus I have been commanded."

**8:36** So Aaron and his sons did all the things which the LORD had commanded by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **8:16** — Hebrew distinguishes between hiqtir, used here for turning sacrificial portions into smoke upon the altar, and saraf in verse 17, used for burning the remainder with fire outside the camp.
- **8:31** — The Masoretic vocalization reads the active form tsivviti, meaning I have commanded, whereas the parallel expression in verse 35 is pointed passively as tsuvveti, meaning I have been commanded.
- **8:33** — Literally, for seven days one will fill your hand; filling the hand is the standard Hebrew idiom for ordaining or installing priests into office, corresponding to the noun ordination (milluim).
