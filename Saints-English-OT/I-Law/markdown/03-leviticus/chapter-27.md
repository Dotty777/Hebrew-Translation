## Text

**27:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**27:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, 'When a man makes a special vow of persons to the LORD according to your valuation,

**27:3** then your valuation of a male from twenty years old up to sixty years old—your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary.

**27:4** And if it is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.

**27:5** And if from five years old up to twenty years old, then your valuation of the male shall be twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

**27:6** And if from one month old up to five years old, then your valuation of the male shall be five shekels of silver, and for the female your valuation shall be three shekels of silver.

**27:7** And if from sixty years old and upward, if a male, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

**27:8** But if he is too poor for your valuation, then he shall present him before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to the means of him who made the vow, the priest shall value him.

**27:9** And if it is an animal from which men bring an offering to the LORD, whatever one gives of such to the LORD shall be holy.

**27:10** He shall not substitute for it nor exchange it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good; and if he exchanges animal for animal at all, then both it and its substitute shall be holy.

**27:11** And if it is any unclean animal, from which men do not bring an offering to the LORD, then he shall present the animal before the priest.

**27:12** And the priest shall value it, whether it is good or bad; according to your valuation, O priest, so it shall be.

**27:13** But if he will indeed redeem it, then he shall add one-fifth to your valuation.

**27:14** And when a man consecrates his house to be holy to the LORD, then the priest shall value it, whether it is good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall stand.

**27:15** And if he who consecrated it redeems his house, then he shall add one-fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be his.

**27:16** And if a man consecrates to the LORD part of a field of his possession, then your valuation shall be according to its seed: a homer of barley seed at fifty shekels of silver.

**27:17** If he consecrates his field from the Year of Jubilee, according to your valuation it shall stand.

**27:18** But if he consecrates his field after the Jubilee, then the priest shall reckon to him the money according to the years that remain until the Year of Jubilee, and a deduction shall be made from your valuation.

**27:19** And if he who consecrated the field will indeed redeem it, then he shall add one-fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be confirmed to him.

**27:20** But if he does not redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed anymore.

**27:21** Rather, when the field is released in the Jubilee, it shall be holy to the LORD, as a devoted field; it shall be the possession of the priest.

**27:22** And if he consecrates to the LORD a field of his purchase, which is not part of the field of his possession,

**27:23** then the priest shall reckon to him the amount of the valuation up to the Year of Jubilee, and he shall give the valuation on that day, as a holy thing to the LORD.

**27:24** In the Year of Jubilee the field shall return to him from whom he bought it, to him to whom the possession of the land belongs.

**27:25** And every valuation of yours shall be according to the shekel of the sanctuary; the shekel shall be twenty gerahs.

**27:26** However, a firstborn of the animals, which as a firstborn belongs to the LORD, no man shall consecrate it; whether it is an ox or a sheep, it belongs to the LORD.

**27:27** And if it is among the unclean animals, then he shall ransom it according to your valuation, and shall add its fifth part to it; or if it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.

**27:28** Nevertheless, no devoted thing that a man devotes to the LORD of all that he has, whether of man or animal, or of the field of his possession, shall be sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to the LORD.

**27:29** No one devoted, who is devoted from among men, shall be ransomed; he shall surely be put to death.

**27:30** And all the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land or of the fruit of the tree, belongs to the LORD; it is holy to the LORD.

**27:31** And if a man will indeed redeem any of his tithe, he shall add its fifth part to it.

**27:32** And concerning every tithe of the herd and of the flock, whatever passes under the rod, the tenth one shall be holy to the LORD.

**27:33** He shall not examine whether it is good or bad, nor shall he exchange it; and if he exchanges it at all, then both it and its substitute shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.'"

**27:34** These are the commandments which the LORD commanded Moses for the children of Israel on Mount Sinai.

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — The Hebrew expression for your valuation carries a fossilized second-person singular suffix and functions throughout this chapter as a technical legal term for the fixed priestly assessment scale.
- **27:16** — A homer was a large dry measure of roughly six bushels; agricultural land was assessed by the quantity of barley seed needed to sow it.
- **27:23** — Here the Masoretic text attaches the definite article to the suffixed form for your valuation, confirming its idiomatic use as a fixed noun meaning the valuation.
- **27:25** — The shekel of the sanctuary was the standard temple weight of silver, fixed at twenty gerahs.
- **27:28** — A devoted thing translates Hebrew cherem, denoting persons, animals, or property irrevocably dedicated to the LORD that could never be sold or redeemed.
