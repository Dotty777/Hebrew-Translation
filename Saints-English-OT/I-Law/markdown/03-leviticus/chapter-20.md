## Text

**20:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:2** "Moreover, you shall say to the children of Israel, 'Any man of the children of Israel, or of the sojourners who dwell in Israel, who gives any of his offspring to Molech, shall surely be put to death; the people of the land shall stone him with stones.

**20:3** And I Myself will set My face against that man and cut him off from the midst of his people, because he has given of his offspring to Molech, defiling My sanctuary and profaning My holy name.

**20:4** And if the people of the land indeed hide their eyes from that man when he gives of his offspring to Molech, and do not put him to death,

**20:5** then I Myself will set My face against that man and against his family, and I will cut off him and all who turn after him to play the harlot after Molech from the midst of their people.

**20:6** And as for the soul who turns to mediums and to spiritists, to play the harlot after them, I will set My face against that soul and cut him off from the midst of his people.

**20:7** Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be holy, for I am the LORD your God.

**20:8** And you shall observe My statutes and perform them; I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

**20:9** For anyone who curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death. He has cursed his father or his mother; his blood shall be upon him.

**20:10** And the man who commits adultery with another man's wife, even he who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife—the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

**20:11** The man who lies with his father's wife has uncovered his father's nakedness; both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

**20:12** And if a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall surely be put to death. They have committed perversion; their blood shall be upon them.

**20:13** And if a man lies with a male as with a woman, both of them have done an abomination; they shall surely be put to death, and their blood shall be upon them.

**20:14** And if a man takes a woman and her mother, it is depravity; they shall burn both him and them with fire, so that there may be no depravity in your midst.

**20:15** And if a man lies with a beast, he shall surely be put to death, and you shall slay the beast.

**20:16** And if a woman approaches any beast to mate with it, you shall slay both the woman and the beast; they shall surely be put to death, and their blood shall be upon them.

**20:17** And if a man takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness and she sees his nakedness, it is a disgrace; and they shall be cut off in the sight of the children of their people. He has uncovered his sister's nakedness; he shall bear his iniquity.

**20:18** And if a man lies with a woman during her monthly impurity and uncovers her nakedness, he has laid bare her fountain, and she has uncovered the fountain of her blood; both of them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.

**20:19** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister nor of your father's sister, for whoever does so lays bare his own near kin; they shall bear their iniquity.

**20:20** And if a man lies with his uncle's wife, he has uncovered his uncle's nakedness; they shall bear their sin, and they shall die childless.

**20:21** And if a man takes his brother's wife, it is an impure thing; he has uncovered his brother's nakedness, and they shall be childless.

**20:22** You shall therefore observe all My statutes and all My judgments, and perform them, lest the land into which I am bringing you to dwell vomit you out.

**20:23** And you shall not walk in the customs of the nation which I am driving out before you, for they practiced all these things, and I abhorred them.

**20:24** But I have said to you, "You shall inherit their land, and I Myself will give it to you to possess, a land flowing with milk and honey." I am the LORD your God, who has separated you from the peoples.

**20:25** You shall therefore distinguish between the clean beast and the unclean, and between the unclean bird and the clean; and you shall not make your souls detestable by beast, or by bird, or by anything that creeps upon the ground, which I have separated for you to regard as unclean.

**20:26** Thus you shall be holy to Me, for I, the LORD, am holy, and I have set you apart from the peoples so that you should belong to Me.

**20:27** And a man or a woman in whom there is a spirit of a medium or a spiritist shall surely be put to death; they shall stone them with stones, and their blood shall be upon them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — "Molech" was an ancient Near Eastern deity linked with child sacrifice, strictly forbidden under penalty of death in Israel.
- **20:9** — "His blood shall be upon him" is a legal declaration indicating that the offender alone bears guilt for his execution.
- **20:17** — The Hebrew term rendered "disgrace" is a rare homonym of the common word for "lovingkindness," signifying shameful reproach.
- **20:25** — The Hebrew orders the pairs chiastically—clean beast to unclean, then unclean bird to clean—highlighting the call to maintain strict ritual separation.
