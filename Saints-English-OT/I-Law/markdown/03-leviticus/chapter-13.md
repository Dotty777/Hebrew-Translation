## Text

**13:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying:

**13:2** "When a man has on the skin of his flesh a swelling, or a scab, or a bright spot, and it becomes on the skin of his flesh a plague of leprosy, then he shall be brought to Aaron the priest, or to one of his sons the priests.

**13:3** And the priest shall examine the plague on the skin of the flesh; and if the hair in the plague has turned white, and the appearance of the plague is deeper than the skin of his flesh, it is a plague of leprosy. Then the priest shall look upon him and declare him unclean.

**13:4** But if the bright spot is white on the skin of his flesh, and its appearance is not deeper than the skin, and its hair has not turned white, then the priest shall confine the one who has the plague for seven days.

**13:5** And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day; and behold, if the plague has remained unchanged in his sight, and the plague has not spread on the skin, then the priest shall confine him for another seven days.

**13:6** Then the priest shall examine him a second time on the seventh day; and behold, if the plague has faded, and the plague has not spread on the skin, then the priest shall declare him clean; it is a scab. And he shall wash his garments and be clean.

**13:7** But if the scab spreads further on the skin after he has shown himself to the priest for his cleansing, he shall show himself a second time to the priest.

**13:8** And the priest shall examine him, and behold, if the scab has spread on the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:9** When a plague of leprosy is on a man, then he shall be brought to the priest.

**13:10** And the priest shall examine him, and behold, there is a white swelling on the skin, and it has turned the hair white, and there is a patch of raw flesh in the swelling.

**13:11** It is a chronic leprosy on the skin of his flesh, and the priest shall declare him unclean; he shall not confine him, for he is unclean.

**13:12** And if the leprosy breaks out extensively on the skin, and the leprosy covers all the skin of the one who has the plague, from his head to his feet, as far as the eyes of the priest can see,

**13:13** then the priest shall examine him; and behold, if the leprosy has covered all his flesh, he shall declare the one who has the plague clean. It has all turned white; he is clean.

**13:14** But on the day raw flesh appears on him, he shall be unclean.

**13:15** And the priest shall examine the raw flesh and declare him unclean; the raw flesh is unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:16** Or if the raw flesh changes again and turns white, then he shall come to the priest.

**13:17** And the priest shall examine him, and behold, if the plague has turned white, then the priest shall declare the one who has the plague clean; he is clean.

**13:18** And when the body has a boil on its skin, and it is healed,

**13:19** and in the place of the boil there is a white swelling or a reddish-white bright spot, then it shall be shown to the priest.

**13:20** And the priest shall examine it, and behold, if its appearance is lower than the skin, and its hair has turned white, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a plague of leprosy; it has broken out in the boil.

**13:21** But if the priest examines it, and behold, there is no white hair in it, and it is not lower than the skin, and it has faded, then the priest shall confine him for seven days.

**13:22** And if it spreads further on the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a plague.

**13:23** But if the bright spot remains in its place and has not spread, it is the scar of the boil; and the priest shall declare him clean.

**13:24** Or when the body has a burn by fire on its skin, and the raw flesh of the burn becomes a bright spot, reddish-white or white,

**13:25** then the priest shall examine it; and behold, if the hair in the bright spot has turned white, and its appearance is deeper than the skin, it is leprosy; it has broken out in the burn. Therefore the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a plague of leprosy.

**13:26** But if the priest examines it, and behold, there is no white hair in the bright spot, and it is not lower than the skin, and it has faded, then the priest shall confine him for seven days.

**13:27** And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day. If it spreads further on the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a plague of leprosy.

**13:28** But if the bright spot remains in its place, and has not spread on the skin, and it has faded, it is a swelling from the burn; and the priest shall declare him clean, for it is the scar of the burn.

**13:29** And when a man or a woman has a plague on the head or on the beard,

**13:30** then the priest shall examine the plague; and behold, if its appearance is deeper than the skin, and there is thin yellow hair in it, then the priest shall declare him unclean. It is a scaly eruption; it is leprosy of the head or of the beard.

**13:31** And when the priest examines the plague of the scaly eruption, and behold, its appearance is not deeper than the skin, and there is no black hair in it, then the priest shall confine the one who has the plague of the scaly eruption for seven days.

**13:32** And the priest shall examine the plague on the seventh day; and behold, if the scaly eruption has not spread, and there is no yellow hair in it, and the appearance of the scaly eruption is not deeper than the skin,

**13:33** then he shall shave himself, but he shall not shave the scaly eruption; and the priest shall confine the one who has the scaly eruption for another seven days.

**13:34** And the priest shall examine the scaly eruption on the seventh day; and behold, if the scaly eruption has not spread on the skin, and its appearance is not deeper than the skin, then the priest shall declare him clean. And he shall wash his garments and be clean.

**13:35** But if the scaly eruption spreads further on the skin after his cleansing,

**13:36** then the priest shall examine him; and behold, if the scaly eruption has spread on the skin, the priest need not search for yellow hair; he is unclean.

**13:37** But if the scaly eruption has remained unchanged in his sight, and black hair has grown in it, the scaly eruption is healed; he is clean, and the priest shall declare him clean.

**13:38** And when a man or a woman has bright spots on the skin of their flesh, white bright spots,

**13:39** then the priest shall examine them; and behold, if on the skin of their flesh there are dull white bright spots, it is a white rash that has broken out on the skin; he is clean.

**13:40** And when a man loses the hair of his head, he is bald; he is clean.

**13:41** And if he loses the hair from the front of his head, he has a bald forehead; he is clean.

**13:42** But if there is on the bald head or on the bald forehead a reddish-white plague, it is leprosy breaking out on his bald head or on his bald forehead.

**13:43** Then the priest shall examine him; and behold, the swelling of the plague is reddish-white on his bald head or on his bald forehead, like the appearance of leprosy on the skin of the flesh.

**13:44** He is a leprous man; he is unclean. The priest shall surely declare him unclean; his plague is on his head.

**13:45** And as for the leper in whom the plague is, his garments shall be torn and his head shall be left unkempt, and he shall cover his upper lip, and cry out, 'Unclean! Unclean!'

**13:46** All the days that the plague is in him he shall be unclean. He is unclean; he shall dwell alone; his dwelling shall be outside the camp.

**13:47** Also, when a garment has a plague of leprosy in it, whether in a garment of wool or in a garment of linen,

**13:48** whether in the warp or in the woof of linen or of wool, or in leather, or in anything made of leather,

**13:49** and if the plague is greenish or reddish in the garment or in the leather, or in the warp or in the woof, or in any article of leather, it is a plague of leprosy, and it shall be shown to the priest.

**13:50** And the priest shall examine the plague, and confine that which has the plague for seven days.

**13:51** And he shall examine the plague on the seventh day. If the plague has spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in the leather, for whatever use the leather is made, the plague is a destructive leprosy; it is unclean.

**13:52** Therefore he shall burn the garment, or the warp or the woof, whether of wool or of linen, or any article of leather in which the plague is; for it is a destructive leprosy; it shall be burned in the fire.

**13:53** But if the priest examines it, and behold, the plague has not spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any article of leather,

**13:54** then the priest shall command that they wash the thing in which the plague is, and he shall confine it for another seven days.

**13:55** And the priest shall examine the plague after it has been washed; and behold, if the plague has not changed its appearance, even though the plague has not spread, it is unclean; you shall burn it in the fire. It is a corrosion, whether on its back side or on its front side.

**13:56** And if the priest examines it, and behold, the plague has faded after it has been washed, then he shall tear it out of the garment, or out of the leather, or out of the warp, or out of the woof.

**13:57** And if it appears again in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any article of leather, it is breaking out; you shall burn in the fire that in which the plague is.

**13:58** But as for the garment, or the warp, or the woof, or any article of leather which you wash, if the plague departs from them, then it shall be washed a second time, and it shall be clean.

**13:59** This is the law of the plague of leprosy in a garment of wool or of linen, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any article of leather, to declare it clean or to declare it unclean."

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Hebrew tsara'at, traditionally rendered "leprosy," encompasses a range of scaly or disfiguring skin afflictions as well as contaminating growths on garments and walls (cf. 13:47; 14:34), rather than solely modern Hansen's disease.
- **13:4** — Literally "confine the plague," using the affliction by metonymy for the afflicted person (similarly in verses 12, 13, and 17).
- **13:5** — The Hebrew idiom "stood in his eyes" denotes that the affliction remained unchanged in appearance as observed by the priest.
- **13:30** — Hebrew neteq denotes a scaly eruption of the scalp or beard affecting the hair, traditionally rendered "scall" or "scale."
- **13:39** — Hebrew bohaq refers to a benign, dull white skin eruption or discoloration that does not render a person ceremonially unclean.
- **13:45** — Tearing the garments, leaving the hair unkempt, and covering the upper lip were traditional Israelite signs of mourning (cf. Leviticus 10:6; Ezekiel 24:17), marking the afflicted person's separation from the community.
- **13:55** — Literally "on its back baldness or on its front baldness," reusing the terms from verses 42-43 metaphorically for the worn back or front side of the fabric.
