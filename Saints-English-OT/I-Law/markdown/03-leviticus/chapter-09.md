## Text

**9:1** Now it came to pass on the eighth day that Moses called Aaron and his sons and the elders of Israel.

**9:2** And he said to Aaron, "Take for yourself a calf from the herd for a sin offering, and a ram for a burnt offering, without blemish, and offer them before the LORD.

**9:3** And to the children of Israel you shall speak, saying, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering, and a calf and a lamb, both a year old, without blemish, for a burnt offering;

**9:4** also an ox and a ram for peace offerings, to sacrifice before the LORD, and a grain offering mixed with oil; for today the LORD will appear to you.'"

**9:5** So they brought what Moses had commanded before the tent of meeting, and all the congregation drew near and stood before the LORD.

**9:6** Then Moses said, "This is the thing which the LORD has commanded that you should do, and the glory of the LORD shall appear to you."

**9:7** And Moses said to Aaron, "Draw near to the altar, offer your sin offering and your burnt offering, and make atonement for yourself and for the people; and offer the offering of the people, and make atonement for them, just as the LORD has commanded."

**9:8** So Aaron drew near to the altar and slaughtered the calf of the sin offering which was for himself.

**9:9** Then the sons of Aaron brought the blood to him, and he dipped his finger in the blood and put it on the horns of the altar, and poured out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.

**9:10** But the fat, the kidneys, and the fatty lobe from the liver of the sin offering he burned upon the altar, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**9:11** And the flesh and the hide he burned with fire outside the camp.

**9:12** Then he slaughtered the burnt offering, and Aaron's sons presented the blood to him, and he dashed it against the altar all around.

**9:13** And they presented the burnt offering to him, piece by piece, along with the head, and he burned them upon the altar.

**9:14** And he washed the entrails and the legs, and burned them upon the burnt offering on the altar.

**9:15** Then he brought forward the people's offering, and took the male goat of the sin offering which was for the people, and slaughtered it and offered it for sin, like the first.

**9:16** And he brought forward the burnt offering and offered it according to the ordinance.

**9:17** Then he brought forward the grain offering, filled his palm from it, and burned it upon the altar, besides the burnt offering of the morning.

**9:18** He also slaughtered the ox and the ram as the sacrifice of peace offerings which was for the people; and Aaron's sons presented the blood to him, and he dashed it against the altar all around.

**9:19** As for the fat portions from the ox and from the ram—the fat tail, the covering fat, the kidneys, and the fatty lobe of the liver—

**9:20** they placed the fat portions upon the breasts, and he burned the fat portions upon the altar.

**9:21** And the breasts and the right thigh Aaron waved as a wave offering before the LORD, just as Moses had commanded.

**9:22** Then Aaron lifted up his hands toward the people and blessed them, and came down from offering the sin offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings.

**9:23** And Moses and Aaron went into the tent of meeting, and came out and blessed the people; and the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people.

**9:24** And fire came forth from before the LORD and consumed upon the altar the burnt offering and the fat portions; and when all the people saw it, they shouted for joy and fell upon their faces.

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — The Masoretic Hebrew text explicitly reads for yourself and for the people, translated directly without harmonizing to ancient versions that read for your household.
- **9:19** — Hebrew introduces the fat portions as an anticipatory clause resumed by the verb in verse 20; the covering fat refers to the layer of fat covering the entrails.
- **9:22** — The consonantal text spells yadav defectively without a yod, resembling the singular his hand, while the Masoretic vocalization reads the plural his hands.
