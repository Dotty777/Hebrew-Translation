## Text

**23:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: 'The appointed times of the LORD, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations—these are My appointed times.

**23:3** Six days work shall be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, a holy convocation. You shall not do any work; it is a Sabbath to the LORD in all your dwellings.

**23:4** These are the appointed times of the LORD, the holy convocations which you shall proclaim at their appointed times:

**23:5** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at twilight, is the Passover to the LORD.

**23:6** And on the fifteenth day of this month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the LORD; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**23:7** On the first day you shall have a holy convocation; you shall not do any laborious work.

**23:8** And you shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD for seven days. On the seventh day is a holy convocation; you shall not do any laborious work.'"

**23:9** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:10** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: 'When you enter the land which I am giving to you, and reap its harvest, then you shall bring a sheaf of the firstfruits of your harvest to the priest.

**23:11** He shall wave the sheaf before the LORD for your acceptance; on the day after the Sabbath the priest shall wave it.

**23:12** And on the day when you wave the sheaf, you shall offer a male lamb without blemish, a year old, as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**23:13** Its grain offering shall be two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, an offering made by fire to the LORD for a soothing aroma; and its drink offering shall be of wine, a fourth of a hin.

**23:14** You shall eat neither bread, nor roasted grain, nor fresh grain until this very day, until you have brought the offering of your God; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

**23:15** And you shall count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day that you brought the sheaf of the wave offering: seven complete Sabbaths they shall be.

**23:16** Until the day after the seventh Sabbath you shall count fifty days; then you shall present a new grain offering to the LORD.

**23:17** From your dwellings you shall bring two wave loaves made of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour; they shall be baked with leaven, as firstfruits to the LORD.

**23:18** And with the bread you shall present seven male lambs without blemish, a year old, one young bull, and two rams. They shall be a burnt offering to the LORD, along with their grain offering and their drink offerings, an offering made by fire of a soothing aroma to the LORD.

**23:19** Then you shall offer one male goat as a sin offering, and two male lambs a year old as a sacrifice of peace offerings.

**23:20** And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits as a wave offering before the LORD, together with the two lambs; they shall be holy to the LORD for the priest.

**23:21** And you shall make proclamation on this very day; there shall be a holy convocation for you. You shall not do any laborious work; it shall be a perpetual statute in all your dwellings throughout your generations.

**23:22** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not wholly reap the corner of your field when you reap, nor shall you gather the gleaning of your harvest; you shall leave them for the poor and for the sojourner. I am the LORD your God.'"

**23:23** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:24** "Speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a solemn rest, a memorial of trumpet blasts, a holy convocation.

**23:25** You shall not do any laborious work, and you shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD.'"

**23:26** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:27** "Moreover, on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement. It shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall afflict your souls, and present an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**23:28** And you shall not do any work on this very day, for it is a Day of Atonement, to make atonement for you before the LORD your God.

**23:29** For any person who is not afflicted on this very day shall be cut off from his people.

**23:30** And any person who does any work on this very day, that very person I will destroy from the midst of his people.

**23:31** You shall not do any work; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

**23:32** It shall be to you a Sabbath of solemn rest, and you shall afflict your souls; on the ninth day of the month at evening, from evening to evening, you shall observe your Sabbath."

**23:33** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:34** "Speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month shall be the Feast of Booths for seven days to the LORD.

**23:35** On the first day there shall be a holy convocation; you shall not do any laborious work.

**23:36** For seven days you shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD. On the eighth day you shall have a holy convocation, and you shall present an offering made by fire to the LORD. It is a solemn assembly; you shall not do any laborious work.

**23:37** These are the appointed times of the LORD, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations, to present an offering made by fire to the LORD—a burnt offering and a grain offering, a sacrifice and drink offerings, each on its own day—

**23:38** besides the Sabbaths of the LORD, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your freewill offerings which you give to the LORD.

**23:39** Moreover, on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you shall celebrate the feast of the LORD for seven days; on the first day there shall be a solemn rest, and on the eighth day there shall be a solemn rest.

**23:40** And on the first day you shall take for yourselves the fruit of majestic trees, branches of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook; and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God for seven days.

**23:41** You shall celebrate it as a feast to the LORD for seven days in the year. It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations; you shall celebrate it in the seventh month.

**23:42** You shall dwell in booths for seven days; every native-born in Israel shall dwell in booths,

**23:43** that your generations may know that I made the children of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'"

**23:44** So Moses declared the appointed times of the LORD to the children of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — Hebrew mo'adim means appointed times or appointed seasons, a broader category encompassing the weekly Sabbath, the Day of Atonement, and the annual pilgrim feasts.
- **23:5** — Literally between the two evenings, referring to the twilight period between late afternoon or sunset and full darkness.
- **23:13** — Hebrew literally reads two tenths, understood as tenths of an ephah; soothing aroma reflects Hebrew reach nichoach, signifying an offering acceptable and pleasing to the LORD.
- **23:15** — Literally seven complete Sabbaths, denoting seven full weeks leading to the fiftieth day, the Feast of Weeks.
- **23:27** — Afflict your souls is a Hebrew idiom for humble self-denial, especially fasting, practiced on the Day of Atonement.
- **23:34** — Hebrew Sukkot, traditionally also called the Feast of Tabernacles, commemorating the temporary shelters in which Israel dwelt during the wilderness journey.
