## Text

**4:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'If a person sins unintentionally against any of the commandments of the LORD concerning things which ought not to be done, and does any one of them:

**4:3** If the anointed priest sins, bringing guilt upon the people, then he shall offer to the LORD for his sin which he has sinned a young bull without blemish as a sin offering.

**4:4** And he shall bring the bull to the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, and lay his hand upon the head of the bull, and slay the bull before the LORD.

**4:5** Then the anointed priest shall take some of the blood of the bull and bring it to the tent of meeting.

**4:6** And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.

**4:7** And the priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense before the LORD, which is in the tent of meeting; and all the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**4:8** And he shall lift off from it all the fat of the bull of the sin offering: the fat that covers the inward parts and all the fat that is upon the inward parts,

**4:9** and the two kidneys and the fat that is upon them, which is by the loins, and the lobe upon the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys,

**4:10** just as it is lifted off from the ox of the sacrifice of peace offerings; and the priest shall offer them up in smoke upon the altar of burnt offering.

**4:11** But the hide of the bull and all its flesh, with its head and with its legs, its inward parts and its dung,

**4:12** the whole bull he shall carry outside the camp to a clean place, where the ashes are poured out, and burn it on wood with fire; where the ashes are poured out it shall be burned.

**4:13** Now if the whole congregation of Israel errs unintentionally, and the matter is hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they have done any one of all the commandments of the LORD which ought not to be done, and have become guilty;

**4:14** when the sin which they have sinned concerning it becomes known, then the assembly shall offer a young bull for a sin offering, and bring it before the tent of meeting.

**4:15** And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands upon the head of the bull before the LORD, and the bull shall be slain before the LORD.

**4:16** Then the anointed priest shall bring some of the blood of the bull to the tent of meeting.

**4:17** And the priest shall dip his finger in some of the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil.

**4:18** And some of the blood he shall put on the horns of the altar which is before the LORD, which is in the tent of meeting; and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**4:19** And he shall lift off all its fat from it and offer it up in smoke upon the altar.

**4:20** Thus he shall do with the bull; just as he did with the bull of the sin offering, so shall he do with this one. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and it shall be forgiven them.

**4:21** Then he shall carry the bull outside the camp and burn it just as he burned the first bull; it is a sin offering for the assembly.

**4:22** When a ruler sins, and does unintentionally any one of all the commandments of the LORD his God which ought not to be done, and becomes guilty,

**4:23** or if his sin which he has committed is made known to him, then he shall bring as his offering a male goat without blemish.

**4:24** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the goat and slay it in the place where they slay the burnt offering before the LORD; it is a sin offering.

**4:25** Then the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out its blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering.

**4:26** And all its fat he shall offer up in smoke upon the altar, like the fat of the sacrifice of peace offerings; so the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin, and it shall be forgiven him.

**4:27** Now if any one person of the people of the land sins unintentionally by doing one of the commandments of the LORD which ought not to be done, and becomes guilty,

**4:28** or if his sin which he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring as his offering a female goat without blemish for his sin which he has sinned.

**4:29** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering and slay the sin offering in the place of the burnt offering.

**4:30** Then the priest shall take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out all its blood at the base of the altar.

**4:31** And he shall remove all its fat, just as fat is removed from the sacrifice of peace offerings; and the priest shall offer it up in smoke upon the altar for a soothing aroma to the LORD. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him, and it shall be forgiven him.

**4:32** And if he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he shall bring it a female without blemish.

**4:33** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering and slay it for a sin offering in the place where they slay the burnt offering.

**4:34** Then the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out all its blood at the base of the altar.

**4:35** And he shall remove all its fat, just as the fat of the lamb is removed from the sacrifice of peace offerings; and the priest shall offer them up in smoke upon the altar, upon the offerings made by fire to the LORD. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin which he has sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.'"

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The phrase "anointed priest" translates the Hebrew hakohen hamashiach, referring specifically to the high priest consecrated with the sacred anointing oil.
- **4:10** — "Offer them up in smoke" translates the Hebrew verb hiqtir, denoting altar burning as aromatic smoke, distinct from saraf ("burn" in 4:12 and 4:21), used for consuming the remainder outside the camp.
- **4:22** — The opening "When" translates the Hebrew relative particle asher, used here with conditional force in place of the usual im ("if") to introduce the case of a ruler (nasi).
- **4:27** — "The people of the land" translates the Hebrew am ha-arets, referring here to ordinary Israelite laypersons as distinct from the high priest, the full assembly, and a ruler.
