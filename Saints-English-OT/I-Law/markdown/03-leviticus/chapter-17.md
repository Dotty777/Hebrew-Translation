## Text

**17:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Speak to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the children of Israel, and say to them, 'This is the thing which the LORD has commanded, saying:

**17:3** "Any man of the house of Israel who slaughters an ox or a lamb or a goat in the camp, or who slaughters it outside the camp,

**17:4** and has not brought it to the doorway of the tent of meeting to present an offering to the LORD before the tabernacle of the LORD, bloodguilt shall be reckoned to that man—he has shed blood—and that man shall be cut off from the midst of his people;

**17:5** so that the children of Israel may bring their sacrifices which they sacrifice in the open field, and bring them to the LORD, to the doorway of the tent of meeting, to the priest, and sacrifice them as peace offerings to the LORD.

**17:6** And the priest shall sprinkle the blood upon the altar of the LORD at the doorway of the tent of meeting, and burn the fat as a soothing aroma to the LORD.

**17:7** And they shall no more sacrifice their sacrifices to the goat demons, after whom they play the harlot. This shall be a perpetual statute to them throughout their generations."'

**17:8** And you shall say to them, 'Any man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, who offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice,

**17:9** and does not bring it to the doorway of the tent of meeting to offer it to the LORD, that man shall be cut off from his people.

**17:10** And any man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, who eats any blood, I will set My face against the soul who eats blood, and I will cut him off from the midst of his people.

**17:11** For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you upon the altar to make atonement for your souls; for the blood itself makes atonement by the life.

**17:12** Therefore I have said to the children of Israel, "No soul among you shall eat blood, nor shall the sojourner who sojourns in your midst eat blood."

**17:13** And any man of the children of Israel, or of the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, who hunts and catches any wild beast or bird that may be eaten, shall pour out its blood and cover it with dust.

**17:14** For as for the life of all flesh, its blood is one with its life; therefore I said to the children of Israel, "You shall not eat the blood of any flesh, for the life of all flesh is its blood; whoever eats it shall be cut off."

**17:15** And every soul who eats that which dies of itself, or that which is torn by beasts, whether native-born or sojourner, shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until the evening; then he shall be clean.

**17:16** But if he does not wash them nor bathe his flesh, then he shall bear his iniquity.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — Hebrew literally reads "blood shall be reckoned to that man," an idiom denoting bloodguilt incurred by unauthorized slaughter.
- **17:7** — Hebrew se'irim literally means "hairy ones" or "goats," referring to goat-shaped demon idols worshipped in the open field.
- **17:11** — Hebrew nephesh ("life" or "soul") occurs three times in this verse; the final phrase, literally "by the life," indicates that blood makes atonement because it bears the life of the sacrifice.
- **17:14** — Hebrew literally reads "its blood is in its life," expressing the complete identification between the blood and the life of every living creature.
