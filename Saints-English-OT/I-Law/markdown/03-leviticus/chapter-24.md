## Text

**24:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:2** "Command the children of Israel that they bring to you pure oil of beaten olives for the light, to keep a lamp burning continually.

**24:3** Outside the veil of the Testimony, in the Tent of Meeting, Aaron shall keep it in order from evening until morning before the LORD continually; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations.

**24:4** He shall keep the lamps in order on the pure lampstand before the LORD continually.

**24:5** And you shall take fine flour and bake twelve loaves with it; two tenths of an ephah shall be in each loaf.

**24:6** You shall set them in two rows, six in a row, upon the pure table before the LORD.

**24:7** And you shall put pure frankincense upon each row, that it may be with the bread as a memorial portion, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

**24:8** Every Sabbath day he shall set it in order before the LORD continually, taken from the children of Israel as an everlasting covenant.

**24:9** And it shall belong to Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place; for it is most holy to him from the offerings made by fire to the LORD, as a perpetual due."

**24:10** Now the son of an Israelite woman, who was the son of an Egyptian man, went out among the children of Israel; and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought together in the camp.

**24:11** And the son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name and cursed; so they brought him to Moses. Now his mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**24:12** And they put him in custody, that it might be declared clearly to them by the word of the LORD.

**24:13** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:14** "Bring the one who cursed outside the camp, and let all who heard him lay their hands on his head, and let all the congregation stone him.

**24:15** And you shall speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'Whoever curses his God shall bear his sin.

**24:16** And whoever blasphemes the name of the LORD shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall surely stone him. Both the sojourner and the native-born, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death.

**24:17** And whoever strikes down any human life shall surely be put to death.

**24:18** And whoever strikes down the life of an animal shall make restitution for it, life for life.

**24:19** If a man causes an injury to his neighbor, just as he has done, so it shall be done to him:

**24:20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; just as he has caused an injury to a person, so it shall be inflicted on him.

**24:21** Whoever strikes down an animal shall make restitution for it, and whoever strikes down a man shall be put to death.

**24:22** You shall have one law; it shall be for the sojourner as well as for the native-born, for I am the LORD your God.'"

**24:23** Then Moses spoke to the children of Israel; and they brought the one who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones. Thus the children of Israel did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — The twelve loaves of the bread of the Presence correspond to the twelve tribes of Israel, set continually before the LORD on the golden table.
- **24:11** — The Name (Hebrew ha-Shem) refers to the sacred covenant name of the LORD; the verb rendered blasphemed carries the sense of piercing or uttering profanely.
- **24:19** — This statute of proportional restitution and justice limits retaliation to the measure of the injury and applies equally to both native-born Israelites and sojourners.
