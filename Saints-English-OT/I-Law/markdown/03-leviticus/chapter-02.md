## Text

**2:1** Now when any person presents a grain offering to the LORD, his offering shall be of fine flour; and he shall pour oil upon it and put frankincense upon it.

**2:2** He shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests, and one of them shall take from it a full handful of its fine flour and of its oil, together with all its frankincense; and the priest shall burn its memorial portion on the altar, an offering by fire of a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**2:3** And the remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and to his sons; it is most holy among the offerings by fire to the LORD.

**2:4** And when you present a grain offering baked in an oven, it shall be of fine flour—unleavened cakes mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers anointed with oil.

**2:5** And if your offering is a grain offering prepared on a griddle, it shall be of fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened.

**2:6** You shall break it into pieces and pour oil upon it; it is a grain offering.

**2:7** And if your offering is a grain offering prepared in a deep pan, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.

**2:8** And you shall bring to the LORD the grain offering that is made of these things; it shall be presented to the priest, and he shall bring it near to the altar.

**2:9** Then the priest shall take up from the grain offering its memorial portion and burn it on the altar, an offering by fire of a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**2:10** And the remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and to his sons; it is most holy among the offerings by fire to the LORD.

**2:11** No grain offering that you present to the LORD shall be made with leaven, for you shall burn neither any leaven nor any honey as an offering by fire to the LORD.

**2:12** As an offering of firstfruits you may present them to the LORD, yet they shall not ascend upon the altar for a pleasing aroma.

**2:13** And every grain offering of yours you shall season with salt; you shall not allow the salt of the covenant of your God to be lacking from your grain offering. With every offering of yours you shall present salt.

**2:14** And if you present a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you shall present fresh heads of grain parched with fire, crushed grain of the new harvest, as the grain offering of your firstfruits.

**2:15** And you shall put oil upon it and lay frankincense upon it; it is a grain offering.

**2:16** Then the priest shall burn its memorial portion—part of its crushed grain and part of its oil, together with all its frankincense—as an offering by fire to the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The memorial portion is the representative token share of the grain offering burned on the altar to bring the worshiper into remembrance before the LORD.
- **2:11** — The Hebrew word for honey includes both bee honey and sweet fruit syrups, such as date syrup, which were subject to fermentation.
- **2:13** — The salt of the covenant reflects salt as an ancient emblem of permanence, purity, and an enduring covenant bond.
