## Text

**11:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying to them:

**11:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'These are the living creatures which you may eat among all the beasts that are upon the earth.

**11:3** Whatever parts the hoof, having cloven hooves and chewing the cud, among the beasts—that you may eat.

**11:4** Nevertheless, these you shall not eat among those that chew the cud or those that part the hoof: the camel, because it chews the cud, yet does not part the hoof; it is unclean to you;

**11:5** and the hare, because it chews the cud, yet does not part the hoof; it is unclean to you;

**11:6** and the rock badger, because it chews the cud, yet does not part the hoof; it is unclean to you;

**11:7** and the swine, because it parts the hoof and has cloven hooves, yet does not chew the cud; it is unclean to you.

**11:8** Of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall not touch; they are unclean to you.

**11:9** These you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas or in the streams—those you may eat.

**11:10** But whatever does not have fins and scales in the seas and in the streams, of all the swarming things in the waters and of every living creature that is in the waters, they are detestable to you.

**11:11** They shall remain detestable to you; of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall detest.

**11:12** Whatever does not have fins and scales in the waters is detestable to you.

**11:13** And these you shall detest among the birds; they shall not be eaten, for they are detestable: the eagle, the bearded vulture, and the black vulture;

**11:14** the kite, and the falcon according to its kind;

**11:15** every raven according to its kind;

**11:16** the ostrich, the nighthawk, the seagull, and the hawk according to its kind;

**11:17** the little owl, the cormorant, and the great owl;

**11:18** the barn owl, the desert owl, and the carrion vulture;

**11:19** the stork, the heron according to its kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

**11:20** Every winged swarming creature that walks on all fours is detestable to you.

**11:21** Yet these you may eat among all the winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours: those that have jointed legs above their feet with which to leap upon the earth.

**11:22** These of them you may eat: the locust according to its kind, the bald locust according to its kind, the cricket according to its kind, and the grasshopper according to its kind.

**11:23** But every other winged swarming creature that has four feet is detestable to you.

**11:24** And by these you shall become unclean: whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until the evening,

**11:25** and whoever carries any part of their carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening.

**11:26** As for every beast that parts the hoof, yet is not cloven-footed and does not chew the cud, they are unclean to you; everyone who touches them shall be unclean.

**11:27** And whatever walks on its paws, among all the living creatures that walk on all fours, they are unclean to you; whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until the evening.

**11:28** And he who carries their carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening; they are unclean to you.

**11:29** And these shall be unclean to you among the swarming creatures that swarm upon the earth: the mole rat, the mouse, and the great lizard according to its kind;

**11:30** the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the sand lizard, and the chameleon.

**11:31** These are the ones that are unclean to you among all the swarming creatures; whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until the evening.

**11:32** And anything upon which any of them falls when they are dead shall be unclean, whether it is any article of wood, or garment, or skin, or sackcloth—any article with which work is done—it shall be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the evening; then it shall be clean.

**11:33** And every earthen vessel into which any of them falls—whatever is in it shall be unclean, and you shall break it.

**11:34** Any food that may be eaten, upon which water comes, shall be unclean, and any drink that may be drunk in any vessel shall be unclean.

**11:35** And everything upon which any part of their carcasses falls shall be unclean; whether an oven or a hearth for pots, it shall be broken down; they are unclean, and shall be unclean to you.

**11:36** Nevertheless, a spring or a cistern containing a gathering of water shall be clean, yet whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean.

**11:37** And if any part of their carcasses falls upon any sowing seed that is to be sown, it is clean.

**11:38** But if water is put upon the seed, and any part of their carcasses falls upon it, it is unclean to you.

**11:39** And if any beast dies which is yours for food, he who touches its carcass shall be unclean until the evening.

**11:40** And he who eats of its carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening; and he who carries its carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening.

**11:41** And every swarming creature that swarms upon the earth is detestable; it shall not be eaten.

**11:42** Whatever goes on its belly, and whatever walks on all fours, or whatever has many feet among all the swarming creatures that swarm upon the earth—you shall not eat them, for they are detestable.

**11:43** Do not make yourselves detestable with any swarming creature that swarms, nor shall you make yourselves unclean with them, lest you be defiled by them.

**11:44** For I am the LORD your God. You shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and you shall be holy, for I am holy; neither shall you defile yourselves with any swarming creature that creeps upon the earth.

**11:45** For I am the LORD who brings you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God; you shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.'"

**11:46** This is the law of the beasts and the birds and every living creature that moves in the waters, and of every creature that swarms upon the earth,

**11:47** to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the living creature that may be eaten and the living creature that may not be eaten.

## Translator Notes

- **11:5** — The Hebrew source text lists the hare ('arnevet) in verse 5 and the rock badger (shaphan, rock hyrax) in verse 6; both perform chewing motions resembling rumination though they lack divided hooves.
- **11:13** — Several Hebrew bird names in verses 13-19 and small-creature names in verses 29-30 have uncertain exact zoological identifications and are rendered according to standard lexicographical consensus.
- **11:21** — Translated according to the Masoretic Qere lo ('that have'); the Ketiv reads lo' ('not'), a common homophonous orthographic variant in Hebrew manuscripts.
- **11:43** — The Hebrew text pairs the Hithpael and Niphal stems of tame' ('make yourselves unclean' and 'be defiled') to underscore the spiritual gravity of maintaining ritual purity.
