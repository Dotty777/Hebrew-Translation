## Text

**15:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: 'When any man has a discharge from his flesh, his discharge is unclean.

**15:3** And this shall be his uncleanness in his discharge: whether his flesh flows with his discharge, or his flesh is stopped up from his discharge, it is his uncleanness.

**15:4** Every bed on which the one who has the discharge lies shall be unclean, and every article on which he sits shall be unclean.

**15:5** And anyone who touches his bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:6** And he who sits on the article on which the one with the discharge has sat shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:7** And he who touches the flesh of the one with the discharge shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:8** And if the one with the discharge spits on one who is clean, then he shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:9** And every saddle on which the one with the discharge rides shall be unclean.

**15:10** And whoever touches anything that was beneath him shall be unclean until evening; and he who carries those things shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:11** And anyone whom the one with the discharge touches without having rinsed his hands in water shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:12** And an earthen vessel that the one with the discharge touches shall be broken, and every wooden vessel shall be rinsed in water.

**15:13** And when the one with the discharge is cleansed of his discharge, he shall count for himself seven days for his cleansing, wash his garments, and bathe his flesh in running water; and he shall be clean.

**15:14** And on the eighth day he shall take for himself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and come before the LORD to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and give them to the priest.

**15:15** And the priest shall offer them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD because of his discharge.

**15:16** And if a man has an emission of semen, then he shall bathe all his flesh in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:17** And every garment and every piece of leather on which there is semen shall be washed with water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:18** Also, when a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, both shall bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:19** And when a woman has a discharge, and the discharge in her flesh is blood, she shall remain in her menstrual impurity seven days; and whoever touches her shall be unclean until evening.

**15:20** And everything on which she lies during her menstrual impurity shall be unclean; and everything on which she sits shall be unclean.

**15:21** And whoever touches her bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:22** And whoever touches any article on which she sits shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:23** And if anything is on the bed or on the article on which she sits, when he touches it, he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:24** And if a man indeed lies with her, so that her menstrual impurity comes upon him, he shall be unclean seven days; and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.

**15:25** And if a woman has a discharge of blood for many days outside the time of her menstrual impurity, or if it continues beyond her menstrual impurity, all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness she shall be as in the days of her menstrual impurity; she is unclean.

**15:26** Every bed on which she lies all the days of her discharge shall be to her like the bed of her menstrual impurity; and every article on which she sits shall be unclean, as with the uncleanness of her menstrual impurity.

**15:27** And whoever touches those things shall be unclean; he shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.

**15:28** But if she is cleansed of her discharge, then she shall count for herself seven days, and afterward she shall be clean.

**15:29** And on the eighth day she shall take for herself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and bring them to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**15:30** And the priest shall offer the one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for her before the LORD for the discharge of her uncleanness.'

**15:31** Thus you shall separate the children of Israel from their uncleanness, lest they die in their uncleanness when they defile My tabernacle which is in their midst.

**15:32** This is the law for him who has a discharge, and for him from whom an emission of semen goes out, by which he becomes unclean,

**15:33** and for her who is unwell in her menstrual impurity, and for anyone who has a discharge, whether male or female, and for a man who lies with an unclean woman."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Hebrew basar, translated flesh, is used throughout this chapter for the body and euphemistically for the genital organ.
- **15:13** — Running water renders Hebrew mayim chayyim, literally living water, referring to fresh spring or flowing water.
- **15:31** — Tabernacle renders Hebrew mishkan, dwelling place, distinguishing it from tent of meeting, Hebrew ohel moed, in verses fourteen and twenty-nine.
