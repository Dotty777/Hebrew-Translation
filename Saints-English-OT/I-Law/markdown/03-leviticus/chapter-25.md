## Text

**25:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: 'When you enter the land which I am giving to you, then the land shall keep a sabbath to the LORD.

**25:3** Six years you shall sow your field, and six years you shall prune your vineyard, and gather in its produce;

**25:4** but in the seventh year there shall be a sabbath of solemn rest for the land, a sabbath to the LORD. You shall not sow your field, nor shall you prune your vineyard.

**25:5** What grows of its own accord from your harvest you shall not reap, and the grapes of your untrimmed vine you shall not gather; it shall be a year of solemn rest for the land.

**25:6** And the sabbath yield of the land shall be food for you: for you, for your manservant and your maidservant, and for your hired servant and your sojourner who dwell with you,

**25:7** and for your livestock and the wild beasts that are in your land; all its produce shall be for food.

**25:8** And you shall count for yourself seven sabbaths of years, seven years seven times; and the period of the seven sabbaths of years shall be to you forty-nine years.

**25:9** Then you shall cause the trumpet of loud blast to sound in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month; on the Day of Atonement you shall cause the trumpet to sound throughout all your land.

**25:10** And you shall consecrate the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty in the land to all its inhabitants. It shall be a Jubilee for you; and each of you shall return to his possession, and each of you shall return to his family.

**25:11** That fiftieth year shall be a Jubilee for you; you shall not sow, nor reap what grows of its own accord, nor gather from its untrimmed vines.

**25:12** For it is the Jubilee; it shall be holy to you; from the field you shall eat its produce.

**25:13** In this Year of Jubilee, each of you shall return to his possession.

**25:14** And if you sell anything to your neighbor or buy from the hand of your neighbor, you shall not wrong one another.

**25:15** According to the number of years after the Jubilee you shall buy from your neighbor, and according to the number of years of crops he shall sell to you.

**25:16** According to the multitude of the years you shall increase its purchase price, and according to the fewness of the years you shall diminish its purchase price; for it is the number of crops that he is selling to you.

**25:17** And you shall not wrong one another, but you shall fear your God; for I am the LORD your God.

**25:18** So you shall carry out My statutes, and keep My judgments and perform them; and you shall dwell in the land in safety.

**25:19** Then the land shall yield its fruit, and you shall eat to the full, and dwell upon it in safety.

**25:20** And if you say, "What shall we eat in the seventh year? Behold, we shall not sow nor gather in our produce,"

**25:21** then I will command My blessing upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth produce for three years.

**25:22** And you shall sow in the eighth year, and eat from the old produce; until the ninth year, until its produce comes in, you shall eat of the old supply.

**25:23** The land shall not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is Mine; for you are strangers and sojourners with Me.

**25:24** And throughout all the land of your possession, you shall grant redemption for the land.

**25:25** If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his possession, then his redeemer who is nearest to him shall come and redeem what his brother has sold.

**25:26** Or if a man has no redeemer, yet he himself prospers and finds sufficient means to redeem it,

**25:27** then he shall reckon the years since its sale, and restore the balance to the man to whom he sold it, and return to his possession.

**25:28** But if he does not have sufficient means to repay him, then what was sold shall remain in the hand of him who bought it until the Year of Jubilee; and in the Jubilee it shall be released, and he shall return to his possession.

**25:29** If a man sells a dwelling house in a walled city, then he may redeem it until the completion of a full year from its sale; for a full year he shall have the right of redemption.

**25:30** But if it is not redeemed before a full year is completed, then the house that is in the city which has a wall shall be established in perpetuity to him who bought it, throughout his generations; it shall not be released in the Jubilee.

**25:31** However, the houses of villages which have no wall around them shall be reckoned as the fields of the country; they may be redeemed, and they shall be released in the Jubilee.

**25:32** As for the cities of the Levites, the houses in the cities of their possession, the Levites shall have a perpetual right of redemption.

**25:33** And if one redeems property from the Levites, then the house that was sold in the city of his possession shall be released in the Jubilee; for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the children of Israel.

**25:34** But the field of the pastureland of their cities shall not be sold, for it is their perpetual possession.

**25:35** And if your brother becomes poor and his hand falters with you, then you shall uphold him; like a stranger and a sojourner, he shall live with you.

**25:36** Do not take interest or increase from him, but fear your God, that your brother may live with you.

**25:37** You shall not lend him your money at interest, nor lend your food for profit.

**25:38** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God.

**25:39** And if your brother who is with you becomes poor and sells himself to you, you shall not compel him to do the work of a bondservant.

**25:40** As a hired servant and as a sojourner he shall be with you; he shall serve with you until the Year of Jubilee.

**25:41** Then he shall go out from you—he and his children with him—and shall return to his own family, and to the possession of his fathers he shall return.

**25:42** For they are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt; they shall not be sold as bondservants.

**25:43** You shall not rule over him with rigor, but you shall fear your God.

**25:44** And as for your male and female servants whom you may have—from the nations that are around you, from them you may buy male and female servants.

**25:45** Moreover, from the children of the sojourners who dwell among you, from them you may buy, and from their families who are with you, whom they have fathered in your land; and they shall become your possession.

**25:46** And you may bequeath them as an inheritance to your children after you, to inherit as a possession; you may hold them in service forever; but over your brethren, the children of Israel, you shall not rule over one another with rigor.

**25:47** Now if a stranger or sojourner with you becomes prosperous, and your brother beside him becomes poor and sells himself to the stranger or sojourner with you, or to an offshoot of the family of a stranger,

**25:48** after he is sold he shall have the right of redemption; one of his brothers may redeem him,

**25:49** or his uncle or the son of his uncle may redeem him, or anyone of his near kin from his family may redeem him; or if he prospers, he may redeem himself.

**25:50** Then he shall reckon with him who bought him from the year that he sold himself to him until the Year of Jubilee; and the price of his sale shall be according to the number of years, reckoned with him according to the days of a hired servant.

**25:51** If there are still many years remaining, in proportion to them he shall repay the price of his redemption from the money with which he was bought.

**25:52** And if only a few years remain until the Year of Jubilee, then he shall reckon with him; according to his years he shall repay the price of his redemption.

**25:53** As a servant hired year by year he shall be with him; he shall not rule over him with rigor in your sight.

**25:54** And if he is not redeemed by these means, then he shall be released in the Year of Jubilee—he and his children with him.

**25:55** For to Me the children of Israel are servants; they are My servants whom I brought out of the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — The Hebrew term for untrimmed vine literally denotes a consecrated or untended vine, employing the same root used for the uncut hair of a Nazirite.
- **25:10** — The word Jubilee derives from the Hebrew yovel, denoting the ram's horn blown to proclaim the fiftieth year of liberty and restoration.
- **25:30** — Following the Qere reading ("which has a wall"), which accords with verse 29; the Ketiv reads the homophonous negative particle ("not").
- **25:33** — The Masoretic Hebrew states concisely that when Levitical house property is acquired or redeemed from the Levites, it still reverts in the Jubilee, since these houses constitute their sole hereditary possession in Israel.
