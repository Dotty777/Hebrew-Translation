## Text

**1:1** And the LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent of meeting, saying,

**1:2** "Speak to the children of Israel and say to them, 'When any man among you presents an offering to the LORD, from the livestock—from the herd and from the flock—you shall present your offering.

**1:3** If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall present a male without blemish; at the entrance of the tent of meeting he shall present it, for his acceptance before the LORD.

**1:4** And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the burnt offering, and it shall be accepted on his behalf to make atonement for him.

**1:5** Then he shall slaughter the young bull before the LORD; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall present the blood and dash the blood all around against the altar that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**1:6** Then he shall skin the burnt offering and divide it into its pieces.

**1:7** And the sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire upon the altar and arrange wood in order upon the fire.

**1:8** Then the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall arrange the pieces, the head, and the fat upon the wood that is on the fire upon the altar;

**1:9** but its entrails and its legs he shall wash with water. And the priest shall burn all of it on the altar—a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**1:10** And if his offering is from the flock—from the sheep or from the goats—for a burnt offering, he shall present a male without blemish.

**1:11** He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall dash its blood all around against the altar.

**1:12** And he shall cut it into its pieces, along with its head and its fat, and the priest shall arrange them upon the wood that is on the fire upon the altar;

**1:13** but the entrails and the legs he shall wash with water. And the priest shall present all of it and burn it on the altar; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**1:14** And if his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, then he shall present his offering from the turtledoves or from the young pigeons.

**1:15** The priest shall bring it to the altar, wring off its head, and burn it on the altar; and its blood shall be drained out against the wall of the altar.

**1:16** And he shall remove its crop with its feathers and cast it beside the altar on the east side, at the place of the ashes.

**1:17** Then he shall tear it open by its wings, but shall not divide it completely; and the priest shall burn it on the altar, upon the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a pleasing aroma to the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Hebrew phrase rendered for his acceptance may alternatively be translated of his own free will; the sense of acceptance corresponds closely to the verb accepted in verse four.
- **1:5** — The Hebrew verb rendered dash denotes tossing or throwing blood from a basin against the sides of the altar, distinct from sprinkling with the finger.
- **1:9** — The Hebrew verb rendered burn literally means to turn into smoke, used specifically for burning sacrificial portions upon the altar.
- **1:16** — The Hebrew term rendered feathers traditionally denotes plumage, though some ancient versions and interpreters understand it as the contents or filth of the crop.
