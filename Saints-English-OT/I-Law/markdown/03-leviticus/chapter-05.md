## Text

**5:1** "And if a person sins in hearing the voice of a solemn adjuration, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or known of the matter—if he does not tell it, then he shall bear his iniquity.

**5:2** Or if a person touches any unclean thing, whether the carcass of an unclean wild beast, or the carcass of an unclean domestic animal, or the carcass of an unclean creeping thing, and it is hidden from him, yet he is unclean, then he becomes guilty.

**5:3** Or if he touches human uncleanness, whatever his uncleanness may be by which a person becomes unclean, and it is hidden from him—when he comes to know it, then he becomes guilty.

**5:4** Or if a person swears, speaking rashly with the lips to do evil or to do good, whatever a man may speak rashly with an oath, and it is hidden from him—when he comes to know it, then he shall be guilty in any one of these matters.

**5:5** And it shall be, when he is guilty in any one of these matters, that he shall confess that which he has sinned concerning it,

**5:6** and he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD for his sin which he has sinned, a female from the flock, a lamb or a female goat, as a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin.

**5:7** And if his means are not sufficient for a lamb, then he shall bring to the LORD, as his guilt offering for that which he has sinned, two turtledoves or two young pigeons: one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering.

**5:8** And he shall bring them to the priest, who shall offer first that which is for the sin offering, and pinch its head at the back of its neck, yet shall not sever it completely.

**5:9** And he shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering upon the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar; it is a sin offering.

**5:10** And the second he shall prepare as a burnt offering according to the ordinance; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin which he has sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.

**5:11** But if his means cannot reach two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he who has sinned shall bring as his offering one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a sin offering; he shall put no oil upon it, nor shall he place frankincense upon it, for it is a sin offering.

**5:12** And he shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it as its memorial portion and offer it up in smoke upon the altar, upon the offerings made by fire to the LORD; it is a sin offering.

**5:13** Thus the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin which he has sinned in any one of these matters, and it shall be forgiven him; and it shall belong to the priest, like the grain offering."

**5:14** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:15** "If a person commits a trespass and sins unintentionally regarding the holy things of the LORD, then he shall bring to the LORD as his guilt offering a ram without blemish from the flock, according to your valuation in shekels of silver, by the shekel of the sanctuary, for a guilt offering.

**5:16** And he shall make restitution for that in which he has sinned regarding the holy thing, and shall add one-fifth to it and give it to the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and it shall be forgiven him.

**5:17** And if a person sins and does any one of all the commandments of the LORD which ought not to be done, though he does not know it, yet he is guilty and shall bear his iniquity.

**5:18** And he shall bring to the priest a ram without blemish from the flock, according to your valuation, as a guilt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his unintentional error in which he erred, though he did not know it, and it shall be forgiven him.

**5:19** It is a guilt offering; he has surely incurred guilt before the LORD."

**5:20** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:21** "If a person sins and commits a trespass against the LORD, and deals falsely with his neighbor concerning a deposit, or a pledge placed in his hand, or robbery, or has extorted from his neighbor,

**5:22** or has found what was lost and dealt falsely concerning it, and sworn falsely—in any one of all the things that a man may do to sin in them:

**5:23** then it shall be, because he has sinned and incurred guilt, that he shall restore what he took by robbery, or what he gained by extortion, or the deposit which was entrusted to him, or the lost thing which he found,

**5:24** or anything about which he has sworn falsely; he shall restore it in full, and shall add one-fifth more to it, and give it to the one to whom it belongs on the day of his guilt offering.

**5:25** And he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD, a ram without blemish from the flock, according to your valuation, as a guilt offering, to the priest.

**5:26** And the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD, and it shall be forgiven him for any one of all the things he may have done to incur guilt in it."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — "The voice of a solemn adjuration" translates the Hebrew qol alah, a public imprecatory oath pronounced to summon anyone with knowledge of a wrongdoing to testify.
- **5:6** — "Guilt offering" translates the Hebrew asham (also rendered "trespass offering"), which in verses 6 and 7 refers to a reparation penalty brought as a sin offering, while verses 15 through 25 prescribe the distinct ram of the guilt offering.
- **5:20** — In the Masoretic Hebrew text, Leviticus 5:20 through 5:26 concludes chapter 5; many English versions number these seven verses as Leviticus 6:1 through 6:7.
- **5:21** — "A pledge placed in his hand" translates the Hebrew tesumet yad (literally "a placing of the hand"), denoting money, security, or property entrusted to another in a business or personal agreement.
