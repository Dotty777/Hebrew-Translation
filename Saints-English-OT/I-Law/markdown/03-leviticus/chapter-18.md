## Text

**18:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, 'I am the LORD your God.

**18:3** According to the deeds of the land of Egypt, where you dwelt, you shall not do; and according to the deeds of the land of Canaan, to which I am bringing you, you shall not do; nor shall you walk in their statutes.

**18:4** You shall perform My judgments and keep My statutes, to walk in them: I am the LORD your God.

**18:5** And you shall keep My statutes and My judgments; the man who does them shall live by them. I am the LORD.

**18:6** No man among you shall draw near to any close relative of his flesh to uncover nakedness. I am the LORD.

**18:7** The nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother you shall not uncover; she is your mother—you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:8** The nakedness of the wife of your father you shall not uncover; it is the nakedness of your father.

**18:9** The nakedness of your sister, the daughter of your father or the daughter of your mother, whether born at home or born outside—their nakedness you shall not uncover.

**18:10** The nakedness of the daughter of your son or of the daughter of your daughter—their nakedness you shall not uncover, for theirs is your own nakedness.

**18:11** The nakedness of the daughter of the wife of your father, born to your father—she is your sister—you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:12** The nakedness of the sister of your father you shall not uncover; she is the near kinswoman of your father.

**18:13** The nakedness of the sister of your mother you shall not uncover, for she is the near kinswoman of your mother.

**18:14** The nakedness of the brother of your father you shall not uncover; you shall not draw near to his wife—she is your aunt.

**18:15** The nakedness of your daughter-in-law you shall not uncover; she is the wife of your son—you shall not uncover her nakedness.

**18:16** The nakedness of the wife of your brother you shall not uncover; it is the nakedness of your brother.

**18:17** The nakedness of a woman and her daughter you shall not uncover, nor shall you take the daughter of her son or the daughter of her daughter to uncover her nakedness; they are her near kinswomen; it is depravity.

**18:18** Nor shall you take a woman in addition to her sister, as a rival, to uncover her nakedness beside her during her lifetime.

**18:19** And you shall not draw near to a woman in her menstrual uncleanness to uncover her nakedness.

**18:20** And you shall not lie carnally with the wife of your neighbor, to defile yourself with her.

**18:21** And you shall not give any of your offspring to pass over to Molech, nor shall you profane the name of your God: I am the LORD.

**18:22** You shall not lie with a male as with a woman; it is an abomination.

**18:23** Nor shall you lie with any beast to defile yourself with it; nor shall any woman stand before a beast to mate with it: it is perversion.

**18:24** Do not defile yourselves with any of these things; for by all these the nations are defiled, which I am driving out before you.

**18:25** And the land became defiled, so I visited its iniquity upon it, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.

**18:26** But you yourselves shall keep My statutes and My judgments, and shall not commit any of these abominations, neither the native-born nor the sojourner who sojourns in your midst,

**18:27** for all these abominations the men of the land who were before you committed, and the land became defiled,

**18:28** lest the land vomit you out when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.

**18:29** For whoever commits any of these abominations, the souls who commit them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.

**18:30** Therefore you shall keep My charge, so that you do not practice any of the abominable customs which were practiced before you, and that you do not defile yourselves by them: I am the LORD your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — Hebrew literally reads "flesh of his flesh," an idiom denoting close blood relatives.
- **18:7** — The phrase "uncover nakedness" is a standard Hebrew idiom throughout this chapter for unlawful sexual relations.
- **18:21** — Hebrew "to pass over to Molech" is an idiom for the pagan rite of child sacrifice by fire.
- **18:23** — Hebrew tebel denotes a grave perversion or unnatural violation of the created order.
