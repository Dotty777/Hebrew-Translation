## Text

**14:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**14:2** "This shall be the law of the leper in the day of his cleansing: he shall be brought to the priest.

**14:3** And the priest shall go outside the camp, and the priest shall look; and behold, if the plague of leprosy has been healed in the leper,

**14:4** then the priest shall command to take for the one to be cleansed two living clean birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop.

**14:5** And the priest shall command to slaughter the one bird in an earthen vessel over running water.

**14:6** As for the living bird, he shall take it, and the cedar wood, and the scarlet yarn, and the hyssop, and shall dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird that was slaughtered over the running water.

**14:7** And he shall sprinkle seven times upon the one to be cleansed from the leprosy, and shall pronounce him clean, and shall let the living bird go free into the open field.

**14:8** Then the one to be cleansed shall wash his garments, shave off all his hair, and bathe in water, and he shall be clean; and afterward he may enter the camp, yet he shall dwell outside his tent seven days.

**14:9** And it shall be on the seventh day that he shall shave off all his hair—his head, his beard, and his eyebrows, indeed all his hair he shall shave off; and he shall wash his garments and bathe his flesh in water, and he shall be clean.

**14:10** And on the eighth day he shall take two male lambs without blemish, and one ewe lamb of the first year without blemish, and three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mingled with oil, and one log of oil.

**14:11** Then the priest who performs the cleansing shall present the man who is to be cleansed, together with those things, before the LORD at the doorway of the tent of meeting.

**14:12** And the priest shall take the one male lamb and offer it as a trespass offering, along with the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

**14:13** Then he shall slaughter the male lamb in the place where he slaughters the sin offering and the burnt offering, in the holy place; for like the sin offering, the trespass offering belongs to the priest. It is most holy.

**14:14** And the priest shall take some of the blood of the trespass offering, and the priest shall put it upon the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot.

**14:15** And the priest shall take some of the log of oil and pour it into the palm of his own left hand.

**14:16** Then the priest shall dip his right finger in the oil that is in his left palm, and with his finger he shall sprinkle some of the oil seven times before the LORD.

**14:17** And of the remainder of the oil that is in his palm, the priest shall put some upon the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot, over the blood of the trespass offering.

**14:18** And what remains of the oil that is in the palm of the priest he shall put upon the head of the one to be cleansed; and the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD.

**14:19** Then the priest shall offer the sin offering and make atonement for the one to be cleansed from his uncleanness; and afterward he shall slaughter the burnt offering.

**14:20** And the priest shall offer up the burnt offering and the grain offering upon the altar; thus the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be clean.

**14:21** Yet if he is poor and cannot afford so much, then he shall take one male lamb as a trespass offering to be waved, to make atonement for him, and one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mingled with oil as a grain offering, and a log of oil,

**14:22** and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, such as he can afford; and one shall be a sin offering, and the other a burnt offering.

**14:23** And he shall bring them on the eighth day for his cleansing to the priest, to the doorway of the tent of meeting, before the LORD.

**14:24** Then the priest shall take the lamb of the trespass offering and the log of oil, and the priest shall wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

**14:25** And he shall slaughter the lamb of the trespass offering, and the priest shall take some of the blood of the trespass offering and put it upon the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot.

**14:26** And the priest shall pour some of the oil into the palm of his own left hand.

**14:27** Then the priest shall sprinkle with his right finger some of the oil that is in his left palm seven times before the LORD.

**14:28** And the priest shall put some of the oil that is in his palm upon the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot, upon the place of the blood of the trespass offering.

**14:29** And what remains of the oil that is in the palm of the priest he shall put upon the head of the one to be cleansed, to make atonement for him before the LORD.

**14:30** And he shall offer one of the turtledoves or of the young pigeons, such as he can afford,

**14:31** even such as he is able to afford, the one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, together with the grain offering; and the priest shall make atonement for the one to be cleansed before the LORD.

**14:32** This is the law for him who has a plague of leprosy, who cannot afford what pertains to his cleansing."

**14:33** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**14:34** "When you enter the land of Canaan, which I give to you as a possession, and I put a plague of leprosy in a house of the land of your possession,

**14:35** then he who owns the house shall come and tell the priest, saying, 'Something like a plague has appeared to me in the house.'

**14:36** Then the priest shall command that they empty the house before the priest goes in to examine the plague, so that all that is in the house may not become unclean; and afterward the priest shall go in to examine the house.

**14:37** And he shall examine the plague; and behold, if the plague is in the walls of the house with greenish or reddish depressions, and their appearance is deeper than the wall,

**14:38** then the priest shall go out of the house to the doorway of the house and shut up the house seven days.

**14:39** And the priest shall return on the seventh day and look; and behold, if the plague has spread in the walls of the house,

**14:40** then the priest shall command that they pull out the stones in which the plague is, and cast them outside the city into an unclean place.

**14:41** And he shall have the house scraped inside all around, and they shall pour out the plaster that they scrape off outside the city into an unclean place.

**14:42** Then they shall take other stones and put them in the place of those stones, and he shall take other mortar and plaster the house.

**14:43** Now if the plague returns and breaks out in the house after the stones have been pulled out, after the house has been scraped, and after it has been plastered,

**14:44** then the priest shall come and look; and behold, if the plague has spread in the house, it is a malignant leprosy in the house. It is unclean.

**14:45** And he shall tear down the house—its stones, its timber, and all the plaster of the house—and carry them outside the city to an unclean place.

**14:46** Moreover, whoever enters the house during all the days that he has shut it up shall be unclean until the evening.

**14:47** And he who lies down in the house shall wash his garments, and he who eats in the house shall wash his garments.

**14:48** But if the priest indeed comes in and looks, and behold, the plague has not spread in the house after the house was plastered, then the priest shall pronounce the house clean, because the plague is healed.

**14:49** And to cleanse the house he shall take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop.

**14:50** And he shall slaughter the one bird in an earthen vessel over running water.

**14:51** Then he shall take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the scarlet yarn, and the living bird, and dip them in the blood of the slaughtered bird and in the running water, and sprinkle the house seven times.

**14:52** Thus he shall cleanse the house with the blood of the bird, and with the running water, and with the living bird, and with the cedar wood, and with the hyssop, and with the scarlet yarn.

**14:53** Then he shall let the living bird go free outside the city into the open field, and make atonement for the house, and it shall be clean.

**14:54** This is the law for every plague of leprosy and for a scale,

**14:55** for leprosy of a garment and of a house,

**14:56** for a swelling, for a scab, and for a bright spot,

**14:57** to teach when a thing is unclean and when it is clean. This is the law of leprosy."

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Hebrew term rendered leprosy encompasses a range of serious skin afflictions as well as fungal mildew or mold in garments and houses.
- **14:5** — Literally living water, referring to fresh running water drawn from a spring or flowing stream rather than standing water.
- **14:10** — A tenth of an ephah was a dry measure of roughly two quarts, and a log was a liquid measure of approximately one-third of a quart.
- **14:37** — The Hebrew word for depressions occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes sunken or hollow streaks in the plastered wall.
- **14:57** — Literally in the day of the unclean and in the day of the clean.
