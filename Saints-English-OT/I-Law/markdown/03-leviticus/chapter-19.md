## Text

**19:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**19:2** "Speak to the entire congregation of the children of Israel, and say to them, 'You shall be holy, for I, the LORD your God, am holy.

**19:3** Each one of you shall revere his mother and his father, and you shall keep My Sabbaths; I am the LORD your God.

**19:4** Do not turn to idols, nor make for yourselves molten gods; I am the LORD your God.

**19:5** And when you offer a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD, you shall offer it so that you may be accepted.

**19:6** It shall be eaten on the day you sacrifice it and on the next day; and whatever remains until the third day shall be burned with fire.

**19:7** And if it is eaten at all on the third day, it is an abomination; it shall not be accepted.

**19:8** Therefore whoever eats it shall bear his iniquity, because he has profaned the holy thing of the LORD; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

**19:9** When you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not reap to the very edges of your field, nor shall you gather the gleanings of your harvest.

**19:10** You shall not strip your vineyard bare, nor shall you gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard; you shall leave them for the poor and for the sojourner; I am the LORD your God.

**19:11** You shall not steal, nor deceive, nor lie one to another.

**19:12** And you shall not swear falsely by My name, and so profane the name of your God; I am the LORD.

**19:13** You shall not oppress your neighbor, nor rob him. The wages of a hired laborer shall not remain with you overnight until morning.

**19:14** You shall not curse the deaf, nor place a stumbling block before the blind, but you shall fear your God; I am the LORD.

**19:15** You shall commit no injustice in judgment; you shall neither show partiality to the poor nor defer to the mighty, but in righteousness you shall judge your neighbor.

**19:16** You shall not go about as a talebearer among your people, nor shall you stand against the blood of your neighbor; I am the LORD.

**19:17** You shall not hate your brother in your heart; you shall surely reprove your neighbor, and not bear sin because of him.

**19:18** You shall not avenge yourself, nor harbor a grudge against the children of your people, but you shall love your neighbor as yourself; I am the LORD.

**19:19** You shall keep My statutes. You shall not breed your livestock with another kind; you shall not sow your field with mixed seed; nor shall a garment of mixed woven fabric come upon you.

**19:20** And if a man lies carnally with a woman who is a female servant designated for another man, and she has not been redeemed at all nor has freedom been granted to her, there shall be an inquiry; they shall not be put to death, because she was not freed.

**19:21** And he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD, to the entrance of the tabernacle of meeting, a ram as a guilt offering.

**19:22** And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering before the LORD for the sin which he has committed, and he shall be forgiven for the sin which he has committed.

**19:23** And when you enter the land and plant any kind of tree for food, you shall regard its fruit as uncircumcised; for three years it shall be as uncircumcised to you, and it shall not be eaten.

**19:24** But in the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy, a tribute of praise to the LORD.

**19:25** And in the fifth year you may eat its fruit, so that its yield may be increased for you; I am the LORD your God.

**19:26** You shall not eat anything with its blood; you shall not practice divination, nor soothsaying.

**19:27** You shall not round off the hair at the sides of your head, nor shall you mar the edges of your beard.

**19:28** You shall not make any gashes in your flesh for the dead, nor imprint any tattoo marks upon yourselves; I am the LORD.

**19:29** Do not profane your daughter by making her a harlot, lest the land fall into harlotry and the land be filled with wickedness.

**19:30** You shall observe My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuary; I am the LORD.

**19:31** Do not turn to mediums or to spiritists; do not seek them out to be defiled by them; I am the LORD your God.

**19:32** You shall rise before the gray-headed and honor the presence of an aged man, and fear your God; I am the LORD.

**19:33** And if a sojourner dwells with you in your land, you shall not mistreat him.

**19:34** The sojourner who dwells with you shall be to you as one native-born among you, and you shall love him as yourself, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt; I am the LORD your God.

**19:35** You shall commit no injustice in judgment, in measurement of length, in weight, or in volume.

**19:36** Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin you shall have; I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt.

**19:37** Therefore you shall keep all My statutes and all My judgments, and perform them; I am the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — The Hebrew phrase rendered "so that you may be accepted" denotes offering the peace sacrifice in the prescribed manner to secure divine favor, though some render it "of your own free will."
- **19:16** — "Stand against the blood of your neighbor" is a Hebrew idiom forbidding both plotting against another's life and standing idly by when his life is in danger.
- **19:20** — The Hebrew noun rendered "inquiry" occurs only here in Scripture and refers to a formal judicial investigation or penalty short of the death sentence.
- **19:36** — An "ephah" was a dry measure of roughly three-fifths of a bushel, and a "hin" was a liquid measure of about one gallon.
