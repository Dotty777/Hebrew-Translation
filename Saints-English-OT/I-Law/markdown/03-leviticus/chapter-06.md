## Text

**6:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:2** "Command Aaron and his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the burnt offering: The burnt offering shall remain on the hearth upon the altar all night until the morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it.

**6:3** And the priest shall put on his linen garment, and he shall put linen undergarments over his flesh, and take up the ashes of the burnt offering which the fire has consumed on the altar, and place them beside the altar.

**6:4** Then he shall take off his garments, put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place.

**6:5** And the fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not go out. And the priest shall burn wood on it morning by morning, and arrange the burnt offering in order on it, and burn on it the fat portions of the peace offerings.

**6:6** A continual fire shall be kept burning upon the altar; it shall not go out.

**6:7** Now this is the law of the grain offering: The sons of Aaron shall present it before the LORD, in front of the altar.

**6:8** And one shall take up from it a handful of the fine flour of the grain offering and of its oil, together with all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and burn it on the altar for a sweet aroma, as its memorial portion to the LORD.

**6:9** And the remainder of it Aaron and his sons shall eat; it shall be eaten unleavened in a holy place; they shall eat it in the court of the tent of meeting.

**6:10** It shall not be baked with leaven. I have given it as their portion from My offerings made by fire; it is most holy, like the sin offering and like the guilt offering.

**6:11** Every male among the sons of Aaron may eat it. It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations from the offerings made by fire to the LORD. Whatever touches them shall become holy.'"

**6:12** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:13** "This is the offering of Aaron and his sons, which they shall present to the LORD on the day when he is anointed: one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a continual grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

**6:14** It shall be prepared on a griddle with oil; well-mixed you shall bring it. You shall present the baked pieces of the grain offering in broken fragments as a sweet aroma to the LORD.

**6:15** And the priest from among his sons who is anointed in his place shall prepare it. It is a perpetual statute to the LORD; it shall be wholly burned.

**6:16** For every grain offering of a priest shall be wholly burned; it shall not be eaten."

**6:17** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:18** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the sin offering: In the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the sin offering shall be slaughtered before the LORD. It is most holy.

**6:19** The priest who offers it for sin shall eat it. It shall be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the tent of meeting.

**6:20** Whatever touches its flesh shall become holy. And if any of its blood is spattered upon a garment, you shall wash what it was spattered upon in a holy place.

**6:21** But the earthen vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; and if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, it shall be both scoured and rinsed with water.

**6:22** Every male among the priests may eat it; it is most holy.

**6:23** Yet no sin offering from which any of the blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the holy place shall be eaten; it shall be burned with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew text, chapter six begins here, whereas most English translations number verses 6:1 through 6:23 as 6:8 through 6:30.
- **6:2** — The Hebrew term mokedah, translated hearth, refers to the burning surface of the altar and is traditionally written in Masoretic manuscripts with a diminutive letter mem.
- **6:14** — The Hebrew word tuphine, translated baked pieces, occurs only here in the Old Testament and denotes baked or fried portions of the high priest's daily grain offering.
- **6:19** — The Hebrew verb form hamechatte, translated offers it for sin, is a ritual Piel form denoting the priest who performs the purification or sin offering.
