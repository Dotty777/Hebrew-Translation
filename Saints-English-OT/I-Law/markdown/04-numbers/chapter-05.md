## Text

**5:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:2** "Command the children of Israel that they send out from the camp every leper, everyone who has a discharge, and everyone who is defiled by a dead body.

**5:3** Both male and female you shall send out; outside the camp you shall send them, so that they do not defile their camps, in the midst of which I dwell."

**5:4** And the children of Israel did so, and sent them outside the camp; just as the LORD had spoken to Moses, so the children of Israel did.

**5:5** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:6** "Speak to the children of Israel: When a man or a woman commits any of the sins of mankind, committing an act of unfaithfulness against the LORD, and that person becomes guilty,

**5:7** then they shall confess their sin which they have committed; and he shall make restitution for his guilt in full, add one-fifth to it, and give it to the one whom he has wronged.

**5:8** But if the man has no near kinsman to whom restitution may be restored, the restitution owed to the LORD shall belong to the priest, besides the ram of atonement with which atonement is made for him.

**5:9** And every heave offering from all the holy things of the children of Israel, which they present to the priest, shall belong to him.

**5:10** And each man's holy things shall be his own; whatever a man gives to the priest shall belong to him."

**5:11** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:12** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: If any man's wife goes astray and acts unfaithfully against him,

**5:13** and a man lies with her carnally, and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband, and it is kept secret though she has defiled herself, and there is no witness against her, nor was she caught in the act,

**5:14** and a spirit of jealousy comes upon him and he becomes jealous of his wife, when she has defiled herself, or if a spirit of jealousy comes upon him and he becomes jealous of his wife, though she has not defiled herself,

**5:15** then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and he shall bring her offering on her behalf: one-tenth of an ephah of barley meal. He shall pour no oil upon it and put no frankincense upon it, for it is a grain offering of jealousy, an offering of remembrance, bringing iniquity to remembrance.

**5:16** And the priest shall bring her near and set her before the LORD.

**5:17** Then the priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel, and the priest shall take some of the dust that is on the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water.

**5:18** And the priest shall stand the woman before the LORD, uncover the woman's head, and place in her hands the grain offering of remembrance, which is the grain offering of jealousy; and in the hand of the priest shall be the bitter water that brings a curse.

**5:19** And the priest shall put her under oath and say to the woman, 'If no man has lain with you, and if you have not turned aside to uncleanness while under your husband's authority, be free from this bitter water that brings a curse.

**5:20** But if you have turned aside while under your husband's authority, and if you have defiled yourself, and some man other than your husband has lain with you'—

**5:21** then the priest shall put the woman under the oath of the curse, and the priest shall say to the woman—'may the LORD make you a curse and an oath in the midst of your people, when the LORD causes your thigh to waste away and your belly to swell;

**5:22** and may this water that brings a curse enter into your inward parts, to make the belly swell and the thigh waste away.' And the woman shall say, 'Amen, amen.'

**5:23** Then the priest shall write these curses on a scroll, and he shall wash them off into the bitter water.

**5:24** And he shall make the woman drink the bitter water that brings a curse, and the water that brings a curse shall enter into her to produce bitterness.

**5:25** Then the priest shall take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman's hand, wave the grain offering before the LORD, and bring it to the altar.

**5:26** And the priest shall take a handful from the grain offering as its memorial portion and burn it upon the altar, and afterward he shall make the woman drink the water.

**5:27** When he has made her drink the water, then it shall be, if she has defiled herself and has acted unfaithfully against her husband, that the water that brings a curse shall enter into her to produce bitterness, and her belly shall swell, and her thigh shall waste away, and the woman shall become a curse in the midst of her people.

**5:28** But if the woman has not defiled herself, and she is clean, then she shall be free and shall be able to conceive children.

**5:29** This is the law of jealousy, when a wife turns aside while under her husband's authority and defiles herself,

**5:30** or when a spirit of jealousy comes upon a man and he becomes jealous of his wife; then he shall stand the woman before the LORD, and the priest shall carry out upon her all this law.

**5:31** Then the man shall be free from iniquity, but that woman shall bear her iniquity."

## Translator Notes

- **5:18** — The Hebrew verb translated 'uncover' denotes loosening or unbinding a woman's hair as a sign of solemn exposure or distress.
- **5:21** — Literally, 'causes your thigh to fall and your belly to swell,' an expression describing severe physical affliction and loss of fertility.
- **5:28** — Literally, 'and shall be sown with seed,' an idiom meaning that she shall be able to conceive children.
