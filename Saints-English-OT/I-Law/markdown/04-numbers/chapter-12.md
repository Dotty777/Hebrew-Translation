## Text

**12:1** Then Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses on account of the Cushite woman whom he had married, for he had married a Cushite woman.

**12:2** And they said, "Has the LORD truly spoken only through Moses? Has He not spoken through us as well?" And the LORD heard it.

**12:3** Now the man Moses was exceedingly humble, more than all men who were on the face of the earth.

**12:4** Suddenly the LORD said to Moses, to Aaron, and to Miriam, "Come out, the three of you, to the tent of meeting." So the three of them came out.

**12:5** Then the LORD descended in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent, and He called Aaron and Miriam, and the two of them stepped forward.

**12:6** And He said, "Hear now My words: If there is a prophet among you, I, the LORD, make Myself known to him in a vision; in a dream I speak with him.

**12:7** Not so with My servant Moses; in all My house he is faithful.

**12:8** Mouth to mouth I speak with him, clearly and not in riddles, and he beholds the likeness of the LORD. Why then were you not afraid to speak against My servant, against Moses?"

**12:9** So the anger of the LORD burned against them, and He departed.

**12:10** And the cloud withdrew from above the tent, and behold, Miriam was leprous as snow. Then Aaron turned toward Miriam, and behold, she was leprous.

**12:11** So Aaron said to Moses, "Oh, my lord, please do not lay sin upon us, for we have acted foolishly and we have sinned.

**12:12** Please do not let her be like one dead, whose flesh is already half consumed when he comes out of his mother's womb!"

**12:13** So Moses cried out to the LORD, saying, "Please, O God, please heal her!"

**12:14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "If her father had indeed spit in her face, would she not bear her shame for seven days? Let her be shut outside the camp for seven days, and afterward she shall be received back."

**12:15** So Miriam was shut outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was received back.

**12:16** Afterward the people journeyed from Hazeroth and encamped in the wilderness of Paran.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Hebrew verb "spoke" is feminine singular, indicating that Miriam took the lead in the complaint against Moses.
- **12:6** — Following the Masoretic accentuation, "the LORD" introduces the main clause rather than modifying "prophet," yielding "I, the LORD, make Myself known to him in a vision."
- **12:10** — The Hebrew term tzaraat denotes a serious skin affliction traditionally rendered "leprosy," which rendered a person ceremonially unclean.
- **12:13** — Moses' prayer consists of only five Hebrew words with a doubled particle of entreaty, conveying immediate urgency and compassion.
