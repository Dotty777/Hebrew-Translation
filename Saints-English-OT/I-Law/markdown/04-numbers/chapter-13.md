## Text

**13:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Send men for yourself to spy out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the children of Israel; from each tribe of their fathers you shall send one man, every one a leader among them."

**13:3** So Moses sent them from the wilderness of Paran at the command of the LORD; all of them were men who were heads of the children of Israel.

**13:4** Now these were their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua the son of Zaccur;

**13:5** from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat the son of Hori;

**13:6** from the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephunneh;

**13:7** from the tribe of Issachar, Igal the son of Joseph;

**13:8** from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea the son of Nun;

**13:9** from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti the son of Raphu;

**13:10** from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel the son of Sodi;

**13:11** from the tribe of Joseph, namely from the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi the son of Susi;

**13:12** from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel the son of Gemalli;

**13:13** from the tribe of Asher, Sethur the son of Michael;

**13:14** from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi the son of Vophsi;

**13:15** from the tribe of Gad, Geuel the son of Machi.

**13:16** These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses gave Hoshea the son of Nun the name Joshua.

**13:17** Then Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, and said to them, "Go up this way into the Negev, and ascend into the hill country,

**13:18** and observe what the land is like, and whether the people who dwell in it are strong or weak, whether they are few or many;

**13:19** what the land is like in which they dwell, whether it is good or bad; and what the cities are like in which they dwell, whether in open camps or in strongholds;

**13:20** and what the land is like, whether it is rich or lean, and whether there are trees in it or not. Take courage, and bring back some of the fruit of the land." Now the time was the season of the first ripe grapes.

**13:21** So they went up and spied out the land from the wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, near the entrance of Hamath.

**13:22** And they went up into the Negev and came to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, were. Now Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.

**13:23** Then they came to the Valley of Eshcol, and cut down from there a branch with a single cluster of grapes, and they bore it on a pole between two of them; they also brought some of the pomegranates and the figs.

**13:24** That place was named the Valley of Eshcol on account of the cluster which the children of Israel cut down from there.

**13:25** And they returned from spying out the land at the end of forty days.

**13:26** So they went and came to Moses and Aaron and all the congregation of the children of Israel in the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh; they brought back word to them and to all the congregation, and showed them the fruit of the land.

**13:27** Then they recounted to him and said, "We went to the land where you sent us, and indeed it flows with milk and honey, and this is its fruit.

**13:28** Nevertheless, the people who dwell in the land are strong, and the cities are fortified and exceedingly great; moreover, we saw the descendants of Anak there.

**13:29** Amalek dwells in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, the Jebusites, and the Amorites dwell in the hill country; and the Canaanites dwell by the sea and along the banks of the Jordan."

**13:30** Then Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, "Let us surely go up and possess it, for we are well able to overcome it."

**13:31** But the men who had gone up with him said, "We are not able to go up against the people, for they are stronger than we."

**13:32** And they spread an evil report of the land which they had spied out to the children of Israel, saying, "The land through which we have passed to spy it out is a land that devours its inhabitants, and all the people whom we saw in its midst are men of great stature.

**13:33** There we saw the Nephilim, the sons of Anak, who come from the Nephilim; and we were like grasshoppers in our own sight, and so we were in their sight."

## Translator Notes

- **13:16** — Hoshea means "salvation," whereas Joshua incorporates the divine name and means "the LORD is salvation."
- **13:22** — The Hebrew verb "came" shifts to the singular, which traditional commentators associate specifically with Caleb, who later received Hebron as his inheritance.
- **13:24** — The name Eshcol means "cluster" in Hebrew, commemorating the large cluster of grapes cut from that valley.
- **13:33** — The Hebrew term "Nephilim" denotes towering figures or giants of renown, also mentioned in Genesis 6:4.
