## Text

**10:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**10:2** "Make for yourself two silver trumpets; of hammered work you shall make them, and they shall be for you for summoning the congregation and for moving the camps.

**10:3** When they blow both of them, all the congregation shall assemble to you at the doorway of the tent of meeting.

**10:4** And if they blow only one, then the leaders, the heads of the thousands of Israel, shall assemble to you.

**10:5** When you sound an alarm, the camps that are encamped on the east shall set out.

**10:6** When you sound an alarm a second time, the camps that are encamped on the south shall set out; they shall sound an alarm for their departures.

**10:7** Yet when gathering the assembly together, you shall blow the trumpets, but you shall not sound an alarm.

**10:8** The sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets; and these shall be to you as a perpetual statute throughout your generations.

**10:9** And when you go to war in your land against the adversary who oppresses you, then you shall sound an alarm with the trumpets, and you shall be remembered before the LORD your God, and you shall be saved from your enemies.

**10:10** Also in the day of your gladness, in your appointed feasts, and at the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and over the sacrifices of your peace offerings; and they shall be a memorial for you before your God: I am the LORD your God."

**10:11** Now it came to pass in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, that the cloud was lifted up from above the tabernacle of the Testimony.

**10:12** And the children of Israel set out on their journeys from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.

**10:13** Thus they set out for the first time according to the command of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**10:14** The standard of the camp of the children of Judah set out first according to their hosts; and over its host was Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

**10:15** Over the host of the tribe of the children of Issachar was Nethanel the son of Zuar.

**10:16** And over the host of the tribe of the children of Zebulun was Eliab the son of Helon.

**10:17** Then the tabernacle was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari set out, carrying the tabernacle.

**10:18** Next the standard of the camp of Reuben set out according to their hosts; and over its host was Elizur the son of Shedeur.

**10:19** Over the host of the tribe of the children of Simeon was Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

**10:20** And over the host of the tribe of the children of Gad was Eliasaph the son of Deuel.

**10:21** Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the sacred things, while the tabernacle was set up before their arrival.

**10:22** Then the standard of the camp of the children of Ephraim set out according to their hosts; and over its host was Elishama the son of Ammihud.

**10:23** Over the host of the tribe of the children of Manasseh was Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

**10:24** And over the host of the tribe of the children of Benjamin was Abidan the son of Gideoni.

**10:25** Then the standard of the camp of the children of Dan, the rear guard of all the camps, set out according to their hosts; and over its host was Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

**10:26** Over the host of the tribe of the children of Asher was Pagiel the son of Ocran.

**10:27** And over the host of the tribe of the children of Naphtali was Ahira the son of Enan.

**10:28** Such was the order of march of the children of Israel according to their hosts, when they set out.

**10:29** Now Moses said to Hobab the son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law, "We are setting out for the place of which the LORD said, 'I will give it to you.' Come with us, and we will deal well with you, for the LORD has spoken good concerning Israel."

**10:30** And he said to him, "I will not go, but I will depart to my own land and to my kindred."

**10:31** And he said, "Please do not leave us, since you know how we are to encamp in the wilderness, and you shall be to us as eyes.

**10:32** And it shall be, if you go with us—indeed it shall be—that whatever good the LORD bestows upon us, that very good we will do to you."

**10:33** So they departed from the mountain of the LORD on a journey of three days; and the ark of the covenant of the LORD went before them for the three days' journey, to seek out a resting place for them.

**10:34** And it came to pass whenever the ark set out, that Moses said, "Arise, O LORD! Let Your enemies be scattered, and let those who hate You flee from before You."

**10:35** And when it rested, he said, "Return, O LORD, to the ten thousands of the thousands of Israel."

**10:36** And the cloud of the LORD was above them by day whenever they set out from the camp.

## Translator Notes

- **10:29** — The Hebrew noun hoten, translated father-in-law, may modify either Reuel or Hobab and can also denote a male relative by marriage.
- **10:34** — In this critical Hebrew text, Moses' words concerning the ark appear in verses 34-35 before the notice of the cloud in verse 36, whereas traditional Masoretic versification places the cloud at verse 34 and the ark sayings at verses 35-36.
