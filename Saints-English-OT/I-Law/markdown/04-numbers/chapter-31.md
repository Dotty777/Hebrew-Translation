## Text

**31:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "Take vengeance for the children of Israel on the Midianites; afterward you shall be gathered to your people."

**31:3** Then Moses spoke to the people, saying, "Arm men from among you for war, and let them go against Midian to execute the vengeance of the LORD on Midian.

**31:4** A thousand from each tribe, throughout all the tribes of Israel, you shall send to war."

**31:5** So there were supplied from the thousands of Israel a thousand from each tribe, twelve thousand armed for war.

**31:6** And Moses sent them, a thousand from each tribe, to the war—them and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest to the war, with the holy vessels and the trumpets for sounding the alarm in his hand.

**31:7** And they warred against Midian, just as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they slew every male.

**31:8** They slew the kings of Midian along with the rest of their slain—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian; and Balaam the son of Beor they also slew with the sword.

**31:9** And the children of Israel took captive the women of Midian and their little ones, and plundered all their cattle, all their flocks, and all their goods.

**31:10** And all their cities in their dwelling places, and all their encampments, they burned with fire.

**31:11** And they took all the spoil and all the plunder, both of man and of beast.

**31:12** Then they brought the captives, the plunder, and the spoil to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the congregation of the children of Israel, at the camp in the plains of Moab, which are by the Jordan at Jericho.

**31:13** And Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp.

**31:14** But Moses was angry with the officers of the army—the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds—who had come from the service of the war.

**31:15** And Moses said to them, "Have you kept all the women alive?

**31:16** Behold, it was these who, through the counsel of Balaam, caused the children of Israel to commit trespass against the LORD in the matter of Peor, so that the plague came upon the congregation of the LORD.

**31:17** Now therefore, slay every male among the little ones, and slay every woman who has known a man through lying with a male.

**31:18** But all the young girls who have not known lying with a male, keep alive for yourselves.

**31:19** And as for you, encamp outside the camp seven days; whoever has slain a person, and whoever has touched anyone slain, purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, both you and your captives.

**31:20** And every garment, every article of leather, everything made of goats' hair, and every wooden article you shall purify."

**31:21** Then Eleazar the priest said to the men of war who had gone to the battle, "This is the statute of the law which the LORD has commanded Moses:

**31:22** Only the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead,

**31:23** everything that can withstand the fire, you shall pass through the fire, and it shall be clean; nevertheless, it shall also be purified with the water of purification. And all that cannot withstand the fire you shall pass through water.

**31:24** And you shall wash your garments on the seventh day and be clean, and afterward you may come into the camp."

**31:25** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:26** "Take a count of the plunder that was captured, both of man and of beast—you, Eleazar the priest, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the congregation;

**31:27** and divide the plunder into two halves, between those who waged the war, who went out to battle, and all the congregation.

**31:28** And levy a tribute for the LORD from the men of war who went out to battle: one soul out of five hundred, of the people, of the cattle, of the donkeys, and of the sheep.

**31:29** Take it from their half, and give it to Eleazar the priest as a heave offering to the LORD.

**31:30** And from the half belonging to the children of Israel you shall take one drawn out of every fifty—of the people, of the cattle, of the donkeys, and of the sheep, of all the livestock—and give them to the Levites who keep charge of the tabernacle of the LORD."

**31:31** So Moses and Eleazar the priest did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**31:32** Now the plunder, the remainder of the booty which the men of the army had seized, was six hundred seventy-five thousand sheep,

**31:33** seventy-two thousand cattle,

**31:34** sixty-one thousand donkeys,

**31:35** and human persons, of the women who had not known lying with a male, thirty-two thousand persons in all.

**31:36** And the half, the portion for those who had gone out to war, was in number three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep;

**31:37** and the LORD's tribute of the sheep was six hundred seventy-five.

**31:38** The cattle were thirty-six thousand, of which the LORD's tribute was seventy-two.

**31:39** The donkeys were thirty thousand five hundred, of which the LORD's tribute was sixty-one.

**31:40** The human persons were sixteen thousand, of which the LORD's tribute was thirty-two persons.

**31:41** So Moses gave the tribute, which was the LORD's heave offering, to Eleazar the priest, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**31:42** And as for the half belonging to the children of Israel, which Moses separated from the men who went to war:

**31:43** the congregation's half was three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep,

**31:44** thirty-six thousand cattle,

**31:45** thirty thousand five hundred donkeys,

**31:46** and sixteen thousand human persons.

**31:47** From the half belonging to the children of Israel, Moses took one drawn out of every fifty, both of man and of beast, and gave them to the Levites who kept charge of the tabernacle of the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**31:48** Then the officers who were over the thousands of the army, the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds, came near to Moses;

**31:49** and they said to Moses, "Your servants have taken a count of the men of war who are under our command, and not a man of us is missing.

**31:50** Therefore we have brought the LORD's offering, what every man found of articles of gold—armlets and bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and necklaces—to make atonement for our souls before the LORD."

**31:51** So Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from them, all wrought articles.

**31:52** And all the gold of the heave offering that they presented to the LORD was sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty shekels, from the captains of thousands and from the captains of hundreds.

**31:53** The men of the army had taken plunder, every man for himself.

**31:54** And Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and brought it into the tent of meeting as a memorial for the children of Israel before the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **31:5** — Hebrew 'thousands of Israel' denotes Israel's traditional tribal clans or military divisions. The rare verb rendered 'supplied' also appears in verse 16 in the idiom 'commit trespass.'
- **31:10** — Hebrew 'encampments' refers to circular or walled nomadic settlements characteristic of pastoral peoples such as the Midianites.
- **31:23** — Literally 'water for impurity,' referring to the ceremonial cleansing water mixed with the ashes of the red heifer prescribed in Numbers 19.
- **31:28** — The Hebrew noun rendered 'tribute' appears in the Hebrew Bible exclusively in this chapter, designating the fixed levy reserved for the LORD from the spoils of war.
