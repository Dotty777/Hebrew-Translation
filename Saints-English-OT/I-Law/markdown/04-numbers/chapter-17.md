## Text

**17:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Speak to Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, that he lift up the censers from the midst of the burning, and scatter the fire far away, for they are holy.

**17:3** As for the censers of these men who sinned at the cost of their own lives, let them make them into hammered plates as a covering for the altar, for they offered them before the LORD, and they became holy; and they shall be a sign to the children of Israel."

**17:4** So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers which those who were burned had offered, and they hammered them out as a covering for the altar,

**17:5** to be a memorial to the children of Israel, so that no outsider who is not of the seed of Aaron should draw near to burn incense before the LORD, and that he might not become like Korah and his company—just as the LORD had spoken to him by the hand of Moses.

**17:6** But on the next day all the congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and against Aaron, saying, "You have killed the people of the LORD!"

**17:7** Now it came to pass, when the congregation gathered against Moses and against Aaron, that they turned toward the tent of meeting; and behold, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the LORD appeared.

**17:8** Then Moses and Aaron came before the tent of meeting.

**17:9** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:10** "Get away from the midst of this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment." And they fell upon their faces.

**17:11** So Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, and put fire in it from off the altar, and lay incense on it, and carry it swiftly to the congregation and make atonement for them; for wrath has gone forth from before the LORD. The plague has begun!"

**17:12** Then Aaron took it just as Moses had spoken, and ran into the midst of the assembly; and behold, the plague had already begun among the people. So he put on the incense and made atonement for the people.

**17:13** And he stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was stayed.

**17:14** Now those who died in the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died in the matter of Korah.

**17:15** So Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tent of meeting, for the plague had been stayed.

**17:16** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:17** "Speak to the children of Israel, and take from them a rod for each father's house, from all their leaders according to their fathers' houses, twelve rods. You shall write each man's name upon his rod.

**17:18** And you shall write Aaron's name upon the rod of Levi, for there shall be one rod for the head of each of their fathers' houses.

**17:19** Then you shall place them in the tent of meeting before the testimony, where I meet with you.

**17:20** And it shall be that the rod of the man whom I choose will blossom; thus I will rid Myself of the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmur against you."

**17:21** So Moses spoke to the children of Israel, and all their leaders gave him a rod apiece, for each leader one rod according to their fathers' houses, twelve rods; and the rod of Aaron was among their rods.

**17:22** And Moses placed the rods before the LORD in the tent of the testimony.

**17:23** Now it came to pass on the next day that Moses went into the tent of the testimony, and behold, the rod of Aaron, of the house of Levi, had sprouted and put forth buds, produced blossoms, and borne ripe almonds.

**17:24** Then Moses brought out all the rods from before the LORD to all the children of Israel; and they looked, and each man took his rod.

**17:25** And the LORD said to Moses, "Bring Aaron's rod back before the testimony, to be kept as a sign for the sons of rebellion, that you may put an end to their murmurings against Me, lest they die."

**17:26** Thus Moses did; just as the LORD had commanded him, so he did.

**17:27** Then the children of Israel spoke to Moses, saying, "Behold, we die! We perish! We all perish!

**17:28** Everyone who comes near—everyone who comes near to the tabernacle of the LORD—shall die! Shall we all perish completely?"

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, chapter 17 begins with the aftermath of Korah's rebellion (verses 1-15), whereas most English Bibles place these verses at the end of chapter 16 (16:36-50) and number 17:16-28 as 17:1-13.
- **17:17** — The Hebrew noun matteh carries a double meaning of both "rod" and "tribe," creating an intentional wordplay between the wooden staffs and the twelve tribes they represent.
- **17:25** — "Sons of rebellion" is a literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom bene-meri, denoting persistently rebellious people.
