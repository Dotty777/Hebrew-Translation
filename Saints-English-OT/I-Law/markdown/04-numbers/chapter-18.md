## Text

**18:1** Then the LORD said to Aaron: "You and your sons and your father's house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood.

**18:2** Also bring near with you your brethren of the tribe of Levi, your father's tribe, that they may be joined to you and minister to you, while you and your sons with you are before the tent of the Testimony.

**18:3** They shall keep your charge and the charge of all the tent; only they shall not draw near to the vessels of the sanctuary or to the altar, lest both they and you die.

**18:4** They shall be joined to you and keep the charge of the tent of meeting, for all the service of the tent; and no outsider shall draw near to you.

**18:5** And you shall keep the charge of the sanctuary and the charge of the altar, so that there may be no more wrath upon the children of Israel.

**18:6** Behold, I Myself have taken your brethren the Levites from among the children of Israel; they are a gift to you, given to the LORD, to perform the service of the tent of meeting.

**18:7** Therefore you and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for every matter of the altar and for what is within the veil, and you shall serve. I give your priesthood as a service of gift, and the outsider who draws near shall be put to death."

**18:8** And the LORD spoke to Aaron: "Behold, I Myself have given you the charge of My heave offerings, even all the holy things of the children of Israel; to you I have given them by virtue of the anointing, and to your sons as a perpetual statute.

**18:9** This shall be yours of the most holy things kept from the fire: every offering of theirs—every grain offering of theirs, every sin offering of theirs, and every trespass offering of theirs, which they render to Me—shall be most holy for you and for your sons.

**18:10** In a most holy place you shall eat it; every male may eat it; it shall be holy to you.

**18:11** This also is yours: the heave offering of their gift, along with all the wave offerings of the children of Israel; I have given them to you, and to your sons and your daughters with you, as a perpetual statute. Everyone who is clean in your house may eat it.

**18:12** All the choicest of the oil and all the choicest of the new wine and the grain, their firstfruits which they give to the LORD, I have given to you.

**18:13** The first ripe produce of all that is in their land, which they bring to the LORD, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house may eat it.

**18:14** Every devoted thing in Israel shall be yours.

**18:15** Everything that first opens the womb of all flesh, which they present to the LORD, whether of man or of beast, shall be yours; nevertheless, you shall surely redeem the firstborn of man, and the firstborn of an unclean beast you shall redeem.

**18:16** And those to be redeemed among them you shall redeem from a month old, according to your valuation, for five shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs.

**18:17** But the firstborn of an ox, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat, you shall not redeem; they are holy. You shall sprinkle their blood upon the altar, and their fat you shall burn as an offering made by fire for a sweet aroma to the LORD.

**18:18** And their flesh shall be yours; just as the wave breast and the right thigh, it shall be yours.

**18:19** All the heave offerings of the holy things, which the children of Israel present to the LORD, I have given to you and to your sons and your daughters with you as a perpetual statute; it is an everlasting covenant of salt before the LORD for you and for your descendants with you."

**18:20** Then the LORD said to Aaron: "In their land you shall have no inheritance, nor shall you have any portion among them; I am your portion and your inheritance among the children of Israel.

**18:21** Behold, to the sons of Levi I have given every tithe in Israel for an inheritance, in return for their service which they perform, the service of the tent of meeting.

**18:22** And the children of Israel shall no longer draw near to the tent of meeting, lest they bear sin and die.

**18:23** But the Levites themselves shall perform the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall bear their iniquity; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations, that among the children of Israel they shall possess no inheritance.

**18:24** For the tithe of the children of Israel, which they lift up as a heave offering to the LORD, I have given to the Levites for an inheritance; therefore I have said to them, 'Among the children of Israel they shall possess no inheritance.'"

**18:25** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:26** "Speak also to the Levites, and say to them: 'When you receive from the children of Israel the tithe which I have given you from them as your inheritance, then you shall lift up from it a heave offering to the LORD, a tithe of the tithe.

**18:27** And your heave offering shall be reckoned to you as the grain from the threshing floor and as the fullness from the winepress.

**18:28** Thus you also shall lift up a heave offering to the LORD from all your tithes which you receive from the children of Israel, and from it you shall give the LORD's heave offering to Aaron the priest.

**18:29** Out of all your gifts you shall lift up every heave offering of the LORD, from all the best of it, even the consecrated part of it.'

**18:30** And you shall say to them: 'When you have lifted up the best of it from it, then it shall be reckoned to the Levites as the produce of the threshing floor and as the produce of the winepress.

**18:31** And you may eat it in any place, you and your household, for it is your wages in return for your service in the tent of meeting.

**18:32** And you shall bear no sin because of it when you have lifted up the best of it from it; neither shall you profane the holy things of the children of Israel, lest you die.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — "Bear the iniquity of" denotes assuming legal accountability and liability for offenses connected with the sanctuary and the priestly office.
- **18:2** — The Hebrew text plays on the name Levi and the verb rendered "be joined," highlighting the Levites' assigned role as assistants attached to the Aaronic priests.
- **18:8** — The Hebrew noun rendered "by virtue of the anointing" can also signify an allotted share or perquisite granted to the priests.
- **18:19** — A "covenant of salt" signifies an enduring, incorruptible pledge, reflecting salt's ancient role as a preservative and emblem of lasting fellowship.
