## Text

**8:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Speak to Aaron and say to him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall shine toward the front of the lampstand.'"

**8:3** And Aaron did so; he set up its lamps toward the front of the lampstand, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:4** Now this was the workmanship of the lampstand: hammered work of gold; from its base to its flowers, it was hammered work. According to the pattern that the LORD had shown Moses, so he made the lampstand.

**8:5** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:6** "Take the Levites from among the children of Israel and cleanse them.

**8:7** Thus you shall do to them to cleanse them: sprinkle water of purification upon them, and let them pass a razor over all their flesh, and wash their garments, and cleanse themselves.

**8:8** Then let them take a young bull with its grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil, and a second young bull you shall take for a sin offering.

**8:9** And you shall bring the Levites before the tent of meeting, and you shall assemble the entire congregation of the children of Israel.

**8:10** You shall bring the Levites before the LORD, and the children of Israel shall lay their hands upon the Levites.

**8:11** Then Aaron shall present the Levites as a wave offering before the LORD from the children of Israel, that they may perform the service of the LORD.

**8:12** And the Levites shall lay their hands upon the heads of the bulls; then offer the one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD, to make atonement for the Levites.

**8:13** And you shall set the Levites before Aaron and before his sons, and present them as a wave offering to the LORD.

**8:14** Thus you shall separate the Levites from among the children of Israel, and the Levites shall be Mine.

**8:15** After that the Levites shall enter to serve the tent of meeting; so you shall cleanse them and present them as a wave offering.

**8:16** For they are wholly given to Me from among the children of Israel; in place of all who open the womb, the firstborn of all from the children of Israel, I have taken them for Myself.

**8:17** For every firstborn among the children of Israel is Mine, both of man and of beast; on the day that I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated them to Myself.

**8:18** And I have taken the Levites in place of every firstborn among the children of Israel.

**8:19** And I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and to his sons from among the children of Israel, to perform the service of the children of Israel in the tent of meeting, and to make atonement for the children of Israel, that there may be no plague among the children of Israel when the children of Israel draw near to the sanctuary."

**8:20** Thus Moses and Aaron and all the congregation of the children of Israel did to the Levites; according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so the children of Israel did to them.

**8:21** And the Levites purified themselves from sin and washed their garments; then Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD, and Aaron made atonement for them to cleanse them.

**8:22** And after that the Levites went in to perform their service in the tent of meeting before Aaron and before his sons; just as the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so they did to them.

**8:23** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:24** "This is what pertains to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward he shall enter to perform duty in the work of the tent of meeting;

**8:25** and from fifty years old he shall withdraw from the duty of the work and shall labor no more.

**8:26** He may assist his brethren in the tent of meeting to keep the charge, yet he shall do no labor. Thus you shall do with the Levites concerning their duties."

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — Hebrew "mei chattat" literally means "water of sin offering," referring to the water used for ritual purification from sin.
- **8:11** — Hebrew "tenufah" denotes a ceremonial presentation or "wave offering" by which the Levites were dedicated to the service of the LORD.
- **8:16** — Hebrew "netunim netunim" is an emphatic doubling literally meaning "given, given," rendered here as "wholly given."
- **8:24** — Hebrew "litzvo tzava" literally means "to serve a tour of duty," using terminology of organized muster; the starting age of twenty-five here contrasts with thirty in Numbers 4:3, often understood as a five-year period of initial training.
