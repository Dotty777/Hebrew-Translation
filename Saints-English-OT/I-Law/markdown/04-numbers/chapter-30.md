## Text

**30:1** So Moses told the children of Israel according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

**30:2** And Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the children of Israel, saying, "This is the word which the LORD has commanded:

**30:3** If a man vows a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath to bind himself by a pledge, he shall not profane his word; he shall do according to all that proceeds out of his mouth.

**30:4** And if a woman vows a vow to the LORD, and binds herself by a pledge in her father's house in her youth,

**30:5** and her father hears her vow and her pledge by which she has bound herself, and her father keeps silent toward her, then all her vows shall stand, and every pledge by which she has bound herself shall stand.

**30:6** But if her father forbids her on the day he hears of it, none of her vows or her pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand; and the LORD will forgive her, because her father forbade her.

**30:7** And if she indeed marries a husband while her vows are upon her, or a rash utterance of her lips by which she has bound herself,

**30:8** and her husband hears of it, and on the day he hears it keeps silent toward her, then her vows shall stand, and her pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand.

**30:9** But if on the day her husband hears of it he forbids her, then he shall annul her vow which is upon her and the rash utterance of her lips by which she has bound herself; and the LORD will forgive her.

**30:10** But as for the vow of a widow or of a divorced woman, everything by which she has bound herself shall stand against her.

**30:11** And if she vowed in her husband's house, or bound herself by a pledge with an oath,

**30:12** and her husband heard of it, and kept silent toward her, and did not forbid her, then all her vows shall stand, and every pledge by which she bound herself shall stand.

**30:13** But if her husband utterly annuls them on the day he hears of them, nothing that proceeded from her lips concerning her vows or concerning the pledge upon her soul shall stand; her husband has annulled them, and the LORD will forgive her.

**30:14** Every vow and every binding oath to afflict the soul, her husband may confirm it, or her husband may annul it.

**30:15** Yet if her husband remains altogether silent toward her from day to day, then he confirms all her vows or all her pledges which are upon her; he has confirmed them, because he kept silent toward her on the day he heard of them.

**30:16** But if he indeed annuls them after he has heard of them, then he shall bear her iniquity."

**30:17** These are the statutes which the LORD commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, and between a father and his daughter in her youth in her father's house.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — In the Masoretic Text this verse begins chapter 30, whereas many English Bibles place it as 29:40, shifting the verse numbering of the rest of chapter 30 by one.
- **30:3** — Hebrew issar refers to a binding pledge or self-imposed obligation of restraint; the verb rendered profane literally means to treat as common or violate.
- **30:14** — The idiom to afflict the soul refers to acts of self-denial such as fasting, using the same Hebrew expression found in 29:7 for the Day of Atonement.
