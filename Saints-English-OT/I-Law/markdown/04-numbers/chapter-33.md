## Text

**33:1** These are the journeys of the children of Israel, who came forth from the land of Egypt according to their hosts, by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

**33:2** And Moses wrote down their departures according to their journeys at the command of the LORD; and these are their journeys according to their departures:

**33:3** They set out from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the day after the Passover the children of Israel went forth with an uplifted hand before the eyes of all the Egyptians.

**33:4** Now the Egyptians were burying every firstborn whom the LORD had struck down among them; upon their gods also the LORD had executed judgments.

**33:5** Then the children of Israel journeyed from Rameses and encamped at Succoth.

**33:6** They journeyed from Succoth and encamped at Etham, which is on the edge of the wilderness.

**33:7** They journeyed from Etham and turned back to Pi Hahiroth, which faces Baal Zephon; and they encamped before Migdol.

**33:8** They journeyed from before Hahiroth and passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness; and they went a journey of three days in the Wilderness of Etham and encamped at Marah.

**33:9** They journeyed from Marah and came to Elim. At Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they encamped there.

**33:10** They journeyed from Elim and encamped by the Red Sea.

**33:11** They journeyed from the Red Sea and encamped in the Wilderness of Sin.

**33:12** They journeyed from the Wilderness of Sin and encamped at Dophkah.

**33:13** They journeyed from Dophkah and encamped at Alush.

**33:14** They journeyed from Alush and encamped at Rephidim, where there was no water for the people to drink.

**33:15** They journeyed from Rephidim and encamped in the Wilderness of Sinai.

**33:16** They journeyed from the Wilderness of Sinai and encamped at Kibroth Hattaavah.

**33:17** They journeyed from Kibroth Hattaavah and encamped at Hazeroth.

**33:18** They journeyed from Hazeroth and encamped at Rithmah.

**33:19** They journeyed from Rithmah and encamped at Rimmon Perez.

**33:20** They journeyed from Rimmon Perez and encamped at Libnah.

**33:21** They journeyed from Libnah and encamped at Rissah.

**33:22** They journeyed from Rissah and encamped at Kehelathah.

**33:23** They journeyed from Kehelathah and encamped at Mount Shepher.

**33:24** They journeyed from Mount Shepher and encamped at Haradah.

**33:25** They journeyed from Haradah and encamped at Makheloth.

**33:26** They journeyed from Makheloth and encamped at Tahath.

**33:27** They journeyed from Tahath and encamped at Terah.

**33:28** They journeyed from Terah and encamped at Mithkah.

**33:29** They journeyed from Mithkah and encamped at Hashmonah.

**33:30** They journeyed from Hashmonah and encamped at Moseroth.

**33:31** They journeyed from Moseroth and encamped at Bene Jaakan.

**33:32** They journeyed from Bene Jaakan and encamped at Hor Haggidgad.

**33:33** They journeyed from Hor Haggidgad and encamped at Jotbathah.

**33:34** They journeyed from Jotbathah and encamped at Abronah.

**33:35** They journeyed from Abronah and encamped at Ezion Geber.

**33:36** They journeyed from Ezion Geber and encamped in the Wilderness of Zin, which is Kadesh.

**33:37** They journeyed from Kadesh and encamped at Mount Hor, on the edge of the land of Edom.

**33:38** Then Aaron the priest ascended Mount Hor at the command of the LORD and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel came forth from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month.

**33:39** And Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.

**33:40** Now the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who dwelt in the Negev in the land of Canaan, heard of the coming of the children of Israel.

**33:41** They journeyed from Mount Hor and encamped at Zalmonah.

**33:42** They journeyed from Zalmonah and encamped at Punon.

**33:43** They journeyed from Punon and encamped at Oboth.

**33:44** They journeyed from Oboth and encamped at Iye Abarim, on the border of Moab.

**33:45** They journeyed from Iyim and encamped at Dibon Gad.

**33:46** They journeyed from Dibon Gad and encamped at Almon Diblathaim.

**33:47** They journeyed from Almon Diblathaim and encamped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.

**33:48** They journeyed from the mountains of Abarim and encamped in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

**33:49** They encamped by the Jordan, from Beth Jeshimoth as far as Abel Shittim, in the plains of Moab.

**33:50** Now the LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**33:51** "Speak to the children of Israel and say to them, 'When you cross over the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**33:52** then you shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, and you shall destroy all their carved stones, and destroy all their molten images, and demolish all their high places.

**33:53** And you shall take possession of the land and dwell in it, for to you I have given the land to possess it.

**33:54** And you shall inherit the land by lot according to your families: to the larger you shall give a greater inheritance, and to the smaller you shall give a lesser inheritance; wherever the lot falls for each one, that shall belong to him. According to the tribes of your fathers you shall receive your inheritance.

**33:55** But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you, then it shall come to pass that those of them whom you leave behind shall be barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides, and they shall afflict you in the land in which you dwell.

**33:56** Moreover, it shall come to pass that as I purposed to do to them, so I will do to you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **33:3** — "With an uplifted hand" translates a Hebrew idiom expressing a bold, triumphant, and unopposed departure.
- **33:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "from before Hahiroth" here without the initial "Pi" found in verse 7 ("Pi Hahiroth").
- **33:48** — "By the Jordan at Jericho" is literally "by the Jordan of Jericho," referring to the stretch of the Jordan River opposite Jericho.
- **33:52** — "Carved stones" translates a Hebrew term for stones engraved or figured with pagan religious imagery used in idolatrous worship.
