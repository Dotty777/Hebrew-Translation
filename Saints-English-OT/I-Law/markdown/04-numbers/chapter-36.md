## Text

**36:1** Then the heads of the fathers' houses of the family of the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, from the families of the sons of Joseph, drew near and spoke before Moses and before the princes, the heads of the fathers' houses of the children of Israel.

**36:2** And they said, "The LORD commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the children of Israel, and my lord was commanded by the LORD to give the inheritance of our brother Zelophehad to his daughters.

**36:3** Now if they become wives to any of the sons of the other tribes of the children of Israel, then their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our fathers and added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they belong; thus it will be taken away from the lot of our inheritance.

**36:4** And when the Jubilee arrives for the children of Israel, their inheritance will be joined to the inheritance of the tribe to which they belong, and from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers their inheritance will be removed."

**36:5** Then Moses commanded the children of Israel according to the word of the LORD, saying, "The tribe of the sons of Joseph speaks what is right.

**36:6** This is the word which the LORD has commanded concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, 'Let them become wives to whoever is good in their eyes; only within the family of the tribe of their father shall they become wives.'

**36:7** Thus no inheritance of the children of Israel shall pass from tribe to tribe, for every one of the children of Israel shall hold fast to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers.

**36:8** And every daughter who possesses an inheritance in any of the tribes of the children of Israel shall become the wife of one from the family of the tribe of her father, that the children of Israel may each possess the inheritance of his fathers.

**36:9** So no inheritance shall pass from one tribe to another tribe, for each of the tribes of the children of Israel shall hold fast to its own inheritance."

**36:10** Just as the LORD commanded Moses, so did the daughters of Zelophehad.

**36:11** For Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, became wives to the sons of their uncles.

**36:12** They became wives within the families of the sons of Manasseh the son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of the family of their father.

**36:13** These are the commandments and the judgments which the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses to the children of Israel in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

## Translator Notes

- **36:4** — Land sold during hardship returned to its ancestral family in the Year of Jubilee, as ordained in Leviticus 25, but land transferred permanently through marriage inheritance would not revert and would remain attached to the husband's tribe.
- **36:5** — Literally "at the mouth of the LORD," a Hebrew idiom denoting direct divine instruction spoken through Moses.
- **36:11** — The Hebrew noun translated "uncles" refers specifically to paternal uncles, the brothers of their father Zelophehad, ensuring the estate stayed within their paternal clan.
- **36:13** — Literally "by the Jordan of Jericho," referring to the stretch of the Jordan River opposite the city of Jericho on the Moabite side.
