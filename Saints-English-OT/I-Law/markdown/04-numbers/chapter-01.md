## Text

**1:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after their departure from the land of Egypt, saying,

**1:2** "Take the sum of the whole congregation of the children of Israel, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, every male, head by head.

**1:3** From twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war in Israel, you and Aaron shall number them by their hosts.

**1:4** And with you there shall be one man for each tribe, each man being the head of the house of his fathers.

**1:5** Now these are the names of the men who shall stand with you: for Reuben, Elizur the son of Shedeur.

**1:6** For Simeon, Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

**1:7** For Judah, Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

**1:8** For Issachar, Nethanel the son of Zuar.

**1:9** For Zebulun, Eliab the son of Helon.

**1:10** For the sons of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama the son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

**1:11** For Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gideoni.

**1:12** For Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

**1:13** For Asher, Pagiel the son of Ocran.

**1:14** For Gad, Eliasaph the son of Deuel.

**1:15** For Naphtali, Ahira the son of Enan."

**1:16** These were those summoned from the congregation, the chieftains of the tribes of their fathers; they were the heads of the thousands of Israel.

**1:17** Then Moses and Aaron took these men who had been designated by name.

**1:18** And they assembled the whole congregation on the first day of the second month, and they declared their lineage according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, head by head.

**1:19** Just as the LORD had commanded Moses, so he numbered them in the wilderness of Sinai.

**1:20** Now the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:21** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Reuben, were forty-six thousand five hundred.

**1:22** Of the sons of Simeon, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, those who were numbered of him, according to the count of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:23** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Simeon, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**1:24** Of the sons of Judah, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:25** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Judah, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**1:26** Of the sons of Issachar, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:27** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Issachar, were fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**1:28** Of the sons of Zebulun, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:29** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Zebulun, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**1:30** Of the sons of Joseph, namely, of the sons of Ephraim, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:31** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Ephraim, were forty thousand five hundred.

**1:32** Of the sons of Manasseh, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:33** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Manasseh, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**1:34** Of the sons of Benjamin, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:35** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Benjamin, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**1:36** Of the sons of Gad, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:37** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Gad, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

**1:38** Of the sons of Dan, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:39** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Dan, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**1:40** Of the sons of Asher, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:41** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Asher, were forty-one thousand five hundred.

**1:42** The sons of Naphtali, their generations, according to their clans, by the house of their fathers, according to the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war:

**1:43** those who were numbered of them, for the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**1:44** These are those who were numbered, whom Moses and Aaron numbered, with the chieftains of Israel, twelve men; each one was for the house of his fathers.

**1:45** So all those who were numbered of the children of Israel, by the house of their fathers, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes forth to war in Israel:

**1:46** all those who were numbered were six hundred three thousand five hundred and fifty.

**1:47** But the Levites, according to the tribe of their fathers, were not numbered in their midst.

**1:48** For the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**1:49** "Only the tribe of Levi you shall not number, nor shall you take their sum in the midst of the children of Israel.

**1:50** Rather, you shall appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its furnishings, and over all that belongs to it; they shall bear the tabernacle and all its furnishings, and they shall minister to it, and around the tabernacle they shall encamp.

**1:51** And when the tabernacle is to set forward, the Levites shall take it down, and when the tabernacle is to encamp, the Levites shall raise it up; and the outsider who draws near shall be put to death.

**1:52** And the children of Israel shall encamp, every man by his own camp, and every man by his own standard, according to their hosts.

**1:53** But the Levites shall encamp around the tabernacle of the testimony, lest wrath come upon the congregation of the children of Israel; and the Levites shall keep the charge of the tabernacle of the testimony."

**1:54** Thus the children of Israel did; according to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so they did.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — The Hebrew phrase rendered 'Take the sum' is literally 'lift up the head,' an idiom for conducting a census; 'head by head' is literally 'by their skulls.'
- **1:16** — Follows the Qere reading qeru'ei ('those summoned,' Qal passive participle of qara') rather than the Ketiv qeri'ei ('called' or 'renowned ones'), which conveys essentially the same sense of duly appointed representatives.
- **1:18** — The Hebrew Hithpael verb rendered 'declared their lineage' occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes formally enrolling oneself by ancestral descent.
- **1:51** — In priestly legislation, 'outsider' (zar) designates any unauthorized person outside the appointed Levitical or priestly order who encroaches upon the sanctuary or its furnishings.
