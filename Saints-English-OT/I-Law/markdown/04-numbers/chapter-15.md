## Text

**15:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, 'When you come into the land of your dwellings, which I am giving to you,

**15:3** and you prepare an offering made by fire to the LORD, a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a special vow or as a freewill offering or at your appointed feasts, to make a pleasing aroma to the LORD from the herd or from the flock,

**15:4** then the one who presents his offering to the LORD shall bring a grain offering of one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour, mingled with one-fourth of a hin of oil;

**15:5** and one-fourth of a hin of wine for a drink offering you shall prepare with the burnt offering or for the sacrifice, for each lamb.

**15:6** Or for a ram you shall prepare as a grain offering two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour, mingled with one-third of a hin of oil;

**15:7** and for a drink offering you shall present one-third of a hin of wine, as a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**15:8** And when you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a special vow or as peace offerings to the LORD,

**15:9** then he shall present with the young bull a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour, mingled with half a hin of oil;

**15:10** and you shall present half a hin of wine for the drink offering, as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**15:11** Thus it shall be done for each ox, or for each ram, or for each young one among the sheep or among the goats.

**15:12** According to the number that you prepare, so you shall do for each one according to their number.

**15:13** Every native-born person shall do these things in this manner, in presenting an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**15:14** And if a stranger sojourns with you, or whoever is in your midst throughout your generations, and would prepare an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, just as you do, so he shall do.

**15:15** As for the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the sojourning stranger, a perpetual statute throughout your generations; as you are, so shall the stranger be before the LORD.

**15:16** One law and one ordinance shall be for you and for the stranger who sojourns with you.'"

**15:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:18** "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, 'When you come into the land to which I am bringing you,

**15:19** then it shall be, when you eat of the bread of the land, that you shall lift up a heave offering to the LORD.

**15:20** Of the first of your dough you shall lift up a cake as a heave offering; as the heave offering of the threshing floor, so you shall lift it up.

**15:21** From the first of your dough you shall give to the LORD a heave offering throughout your generations.

**15:22** Now if you err unintentionally, and do not observe all these commandments which the LORD has spoken to Moses—

**15:23** all that the LORD has commanded you by the hand of Moses, from the day that the LORD gave commandment and onward throughout your generations—

**15:24** then it shall be, if it was committed unintentionally, hidden from the eyes of the congregation, that all the congregation shall offer one young bull for a burnt offering, as a pleasing aroma to the LORD, with its grain offering and its drink offering, according to the ordinance, and one male goat for a sin offering.

**15:25** And the priest shall make atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them, for it was an unintentional error; and they have brought their offering, an offering made by fire to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD, for their unintentional error.

**15:26** It shall be forgiven all the congregation of the children of Israel and the stranger who sojourns in their midst, because all the people acted in unintentional error.

**15:27** And if one person sins unintentionally, then he shall present a female goat of the first year for a sin offering.

**15:28** So the priest shall make atonement for the person who erred, when he sins unintentionally before the LORD, to make atonement for him; and it shall be forgiven him.

**15:29** For the native-born among the children of Israel and for the stranger who sojourns in their midst, you shall have one law for him who acts unintentionally.

**15:30** But the person who acts with a high hand, whether native-born or stranger, he blasphemes the LORD, and that person shall be cut off from among his people.

**15:31** Because he has despised the word of the LORD, and has broken His commandment, that person shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him.'"

**15:32** Now while the children of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day.

**15:33** And those who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses and Aaron, and to all the congregation.

**15:34** They placed him under guard, because it had not been declared what should be done to him.

**15:35** Then the LORD said to Moses, "The man shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall stone him with stones outside the camp."

**15:36** So all the congregation brought him outside the camp and stoned him with stones, and he died, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**15:37** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:38** "Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them to make tassels for themselves on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and to put a cord of blue upon the tassel of each corner.

**15:39** And it shall be a tassel for you, that you may look upon it and remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them, and not go seeking after your own heart and your own eyes, after which you play the harlot;

**15:40** so that you may remember and do all My commandments, and be holy to your God.

**15:41** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God; I am the LORD your God."

## Translator Notes

- **15:4** — The Hebrew dry measure issaron denotes one-tenth of an ephah (roughly two quarts), and the liquid measure hin equals roughly one gallon.
- **15:20** — The Hebrew term rendered dough refers specifically to coarse meal or a freshly mixed batch of dough, from which a cake was set apart as a sacred heave offering.
- **15:30** — The Hebrew idiom with a high hand signifies deliberate, defiant rebellion against the LORD, standing in sharp contrast to the unintentional errors addressed in verses twenty-two through twenty-nine.
- **15:38** — The tassels with a cord of blue-violet dye served as a visible reminder of covenant obedience; in verse thirty-nine, the verb rendered go seeking echoes the same Hebrew root used for spying out the land in chapters thirteen and fourteen.
