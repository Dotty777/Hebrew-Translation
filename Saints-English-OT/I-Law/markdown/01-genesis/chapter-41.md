## Text

**41:1** And it came to pass, at the end of two full years, that Pharaoh was dreaming, and behold, he stood by the Nile.

**41:2** And behold, seven cows came up out of the Nile, fair in appearance and plump in flesh, and they grazed in the reed grass.

**41:3** And behold, seven other cows came up after them out of the Nile, ugly in appearance and gaunt in flesh, and stood beside the cows on the bank of the Nile.

**41:4** And the cows that were ugly in appearance and gaunt in flesh devoured the seven cows that were fair in appearance and plump. Then Pharaoh awoke.

**41:5** And he slept and dreamed a second time; and behold, seven heads of grain were growing on one stalk, plump and good.

**41:6** And behold, seven heads of grain, thin and scorched by the east wind, sprouted up after them.

**41:7** And the thin heads of grain swallowed up the seven plump and full heads. Then Pharaoh awoke, and behold, it was a dream.

**41:8** And it came to pass in the morning that his spirit was troubled, so he sent and summoned all the magicians of Egypt and all its wise men. And Pharaoh recounted his dream to them, but there was no one who could interpret them to Pharaoh.

**41:9** Then the chief cupbearer spoke to Pharaoh, saying, "I call to mind my offenses this day.

**41:10** Pharaoh was angry with his servants, and he placed me in custody in the house of the captain of the guard, both me and the chief baker.

**41:11** And we dreamed a dream on one night, I and he; each of us dreamed according to the interpretation of his own dream.

**41:12** Now a young Hebrew man was there with us, a servant of the captain of the guard. And we told him, and he interpreted our dreams for us; to each man he interpreted according to his dream.

**41:13** And it came to pass, just as he interpreted to us, so it happened: me he restored to my office, and him he hanged."

**41:14** Then Pharaoh sent and summoned Joseph, and they brought him swiftly out of the dungeon; and he shaved, changed his garments, and came to Pharaoh.

**41:15** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have dreamed a dream, and there is no one who can interpret it. Yet I have heard it said of you that when you hear a dream, you can interpret it."

**41:16** And Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, "It is not in me; God will give Pharaoh an answer of peace."

**41:17** Then Pharaoh spoke to Joseph: "In my dream, behold, I was standing on the bank of the Nile.

**41:18** And behold, seven cows came up out of the Nile, plump in flesh and fair in form, and they grazed in the reed grass.

**41:19** And behold, seven other cows came up after them, poor, exceedingly ugly in form, and lean in flesh; I have never seen the like of them in all the land of Egypt for ugliness.

**41:20** And the lean and ugly cows devoured the first seven plump cows.

**41:21** Yet when they had gone into their inward parts, no one could tell that they had gone into them, for their appearance was just as ugly as at the beginning. Then I awoke.

**41:22** And I saw in my dream, and behold, seven heads of grain were growing on one stalk, full and good.

**41:23** And behold, seven heads of grain, withered, thin, and scorched by the east wind, sprouted up after them.

**41:24** And the thin heads of grain swallowed up the seven good heads. So I told this to the magicians, but there was no one who could declare it to me."

**41:25** Then Joseph said to Pharaoh, "The dream of Pharaoh is one; God has declared to Pharaoh what He is about to do.

**41:26** The seven good cows are seven years, and the seven good heads of grain are seven years; the dream is one.

**41:27** And the seven lean and ugly cows that came up after them are seven years, and the seven empty heads of grain scorched by the east wind shall be seven years of famine.

**41:28** This is the word which I have spoken to Pharaoh: what God is about to do, He has shown to Pharaoh.

**41:29** Behold, seven years of great abundance are coming throughout all the land of Egypt,

**41:30** and after them seven years of famine shall arise, and all the abundance shall be forgotten in the land of Egypt; and the famine shall consume the land.

**41:31** So the abundance shall not be known in the land because of that famine which follows, for it shall be exceedingly severe.

**41:32** And as for the repeating of the dream to Pharaoh twice, it is because the matter is firmly established by God, and God will swiftly bring it to pass.

**41:33** Now therefore, let Pharaoh seek out a discerning and wise man, and set him over the land of Egypt.

**41:34** Let Pharaoh act and appoint overseers over the land, and collect a fifth part of the land of Egypt during the seven years of abundance.

**41:35** And let them gather all the food of these good years that are coming, and store up grain under the hand of Pharaoh for food in the cities, and guard it.

**41:36** Then that food shall be a reserve for the land for the seven years of famine which shall be in the land of Egypt, that the land may not be cut off by the famine."

**41:37** And the counsel was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of all his servants.

**41:38** And Pharaoh said to his servants, "Can we find a man like this, in whom is the Spirit of God?"

**41:39** Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has made all this known to you, there is no one as discerning and wise as you.

**41:40** You shall be over my house, and by your command all my people shall be governed; only in the throne shall I be greater than you."

**41:41** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "See, I have set you over all the land of Egypt."

**41:42** Then Pharaoh removed his signet ring from his hand and put it on Joseph's hand; and he clothed him in garments of fine linen and placed a chain of gold around his neck.

**41:43** And he had him ride in his second chariot, and they cried out before him, "Bow the knee!" Thus he set him over all the land of Egypt.

**41:44** Pharaoh also said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh, and without you no man shall lift his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt."

**41:45** And Pharaoh called Joseph's name Zaphenath-Paneah. And he gave him Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of On, as a wife. So Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.

**41:46** Now Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh and passed through all the land of Egypt.

**41:47** During the seven years of abundance, the earth brought forth by handfuls.

**41:48** So he gathered up all the food of the seven years which were in the land of Egypt and laid up the food in the cities; the food from the fields surrounding each city he stored within it.

**41:49** And Joseph heaped up grain in great abundance, like the sand of the sea, until he ceased counting it, for it was without number.

**41:50** And to Joseph were born two sons before the year of famine arrived, whom Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of On, bore to him.

**41:51** Joseph called the name of the firstborn Manasseh: "For God has made me forget all my toil and all my father's house."

**41:52** And the name of the second he called Ephraim: "For God has made me fruitful in the land of my affliction."

**41:53** Then the seven years of abundance which had been in the land of Egypt came to an end,

**41:54** and the seven years of famine began to come, just as Joseph had said. There was famine in all the lands, but in all the land of Egypt there was bread.

**41:55** Then all the land of Egypt was famished, and the people cried out to Pharaoh for bread. And Pharaoh said to all the Egyptians, "Go to Joseph; whatever he says to you, do."

**41:56** And the famine was over all the face of the earth, and Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold grain to the Egyptians. And the famine grew severe in the land of Egypt.

**41:57** And all the earth came to Egypt to Joseph to buy grain, because the famine was severe in all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **41:1** — The Hebrew term ye'or is an Egyptian loanword referring specifically to the Nile River.
- **41:40** — The Hebrew verb yishaq is understood either as being ordered or governed by Joseph's command or as paying homage at his word.
- **41:43** — The exclamation abrek is an Egyptian acclamation of homage, traditionally associated in Hebrew with the root meaning to kneel or bow the knee.
- **41:45** — Zaphenath-Paneah is an Egyptian name commonly understood to mean 'God speaks and he lives.'
- **41:56** — Hebrew literally reads 'all that was in them,' referring to all the storehouses in the cities where the grain had been laid up.
