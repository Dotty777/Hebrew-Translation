## Text

**36:1** Now these are the generations of Esau, that is, Edom.

**36:2** Esau took his wives from among the daughters of Canaan: Adah the daughter of Elon the Hittite, Oholibamah the daughter of Anah, the daughter of Zibeon the Hivite,

**36:3** and Basemath the daughter of Ishmael, the sister of Nebaioth.

**36:4** And Adah bore Eliphaz to Esau, and Basemath bore Reuel.

**36:5** And Oholibamah bore Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These were the sons of Esau who were born to him in the land of Canaan.

**36:6** Then Esau took his wives, his sons, his daughters, and all the souls of his household, together with his livestock, all his beasts, and all his possessions that he had acquired in the land of Canaan, and departed to a land away from his brother Jacob.

**36:7** For their substance was too great for them to dwell together, and the land of their sojourning could not sustain them on account of their livestock.

**36:8** So Esau dwelt in Mount Seir—Esau is Edom.

**36:9** And these are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom, in Mount Seir.

**36:10** These are the names of the sons of Esau: Eliphaz the son of Adah the wife of Esau, and Reuel the son of Basemath the wife of Esau.

**36:11** And the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.

**36:12** Now Timna was concubine to Eliphaz the son of Esau, and she bore Amalek to Eliphaz. These were the sons of Adah the wife of Esau.

**36:13** And these were the sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These were the sons of Basemath the wife of Esau.

**36:14** And these were the sons of Oholibamah the daughter of Anah, the daughter of Zibeon, the wife of Esau; she bore to Esau Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

**36:15** These were the chiefs of the sons of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz, the firstborn of Esau: Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zepho, Chief Kenaz,

**36:16** Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, and Chief Amalek. These were the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Edom; these were the sons of Adah.

**36:17** And these were the sons of Reuel the son of Esau: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Shammah, and Chief Mizzah. These were the chiefs of Reuel in the land of Edom; these were the sons of Basemath the wife of Esau.

**36:18** And these were the sons of Oholibamah the wife of Esau: Chief Jeush, Chief Jalam, and Chief Korah. These were the chiefs of Oholibamah the daughter of Anah, the wife of Esau.

**36:19** These were the sons of Esau, and these were their chiefs—that is, Edom.

**36:20** These were the sons of Seir the Horite, the inhabitants of the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

**36:21** Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These were the chiefs of the Horites, the sons of Seir, in the land of Edom.

**36:22** And the sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam; and Lotan's sister was Timna.

**36:23** And these were the sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.

**36:24** And these were the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. This was the Anah who found the hot springs in the wilderness as he tended the donkeys of his father Zibeon.

**36:25** And these were the children of Anah: Dishon, and Oholibamah the daughter of Anah.

**36:26** And these were the sons of Dishan: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**36:27** These were the sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.

**36:28** These were the sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

**36:29** These were the chiefs of the Horites: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah,

**36:30** Chief Dishon, Chief Ezer, and Chief Dishan. These were the chiefs of the Horites, according to their chiefs, in the land of Seir.

**36:31** Now these were the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the children of Israel.

**36:32** Bela the son of Beor reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**36:33** When Bela died, Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his place.

**36:34** When Jobab died, Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

**36:35** When Husham died, Hadad the son of Bedad, who struck down Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith.

**36:36** When Hadad died, Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his place.

**36:37** When Samlah died, Shaul of Rehoboth by the River reigned in his place.

**36:38** When Shaul died, Baal-Hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his place.

**36:39** When Baal-Hanan the son of Achbor died, Hadar reigned in his place; the name of his city was Pau, and the name of his wife was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab.

**36:40** Now these are the names of the chiefs of Esau, according to their clans, according to their places, by their names: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,

**36:41** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,

**36:42** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,

**36:43** Chief Magdiel, and Chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom, according to their dwelling places in the land of their possession. He is Esau, the father of Edom.

## Translator Notes

- **36:5** — The Hebrew text preserves a consonantal variant (Ketiv: Jeish), while the traditional reading (Qere) is Jeush; the same variant recurs in verse 14.
- **36:15** — The Hebrew term alluph designates a tribal chief or clan leader among the Edomites and Horites.
- **36:24** — The Hebrew word hayyemim occurs only here in Scripture; it is widely understood to mean hot springs, though some older versions rendered it mules.
- **36:26** — The Masoretic Hebrew text reads Dishan here, though this line corresponds to Anah's son Dishon in verse 25 and 1 Chronicles 1:41.
- **36:39** — The Masoretic Hebrew text reads Hadar here, whereas 1 Chronicles 1:50 and several ancient witnesses read Hadad.
