## Text

**43:1** Now the famine was severe in the land.

**43:2** And it came to pass, when they had finished eating the grain which they had brought from Egypt, that their father said to them, "Return, buy for us a little food."

**43:3** Then Judah spoke to him, saying, "The man solemnly warned us, saying, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'

**43:4** If you will send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you.

**43:5** But if you will not send him, we will not go down; for the man said to us, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"

**43:6** And Israel said, "Why have you dealt so ill with me as to tell the man whether you still had a brother?"

**43:7** And they said, "The man inquired closely concerning us and our kindred, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Have you another brother?' And we answered him according to the tenor of these words. Could we in any way know that he would say, 'Bring your brother down'?"

**43:8** Then Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the lad with me, and let us arise and go, that we may live and not die—both we and you, and also our little ones.

**43:9** I myself will be surety for him; from my hand you shall require him. If I do not bring him back to you and set him before you, then let me bear the blame before you forever.

**43:10** For if we had not delayed, surely by now we would have returned this second time."

**43:11** And Israel their father said to them, "If it must be so, then do this: take some of the choice produce of the land in your vessels and carry down a present for the man—a little balm and a little honey, spices and myrrh, pistachio nuts and almonds.

**43:12** And take double the silver in your hand, and the silver that was returned in the mouth of your bags carry back in your hand; perhaps it was an oversight.

**43:13** Take your brother also, and arise, return to the man.

**43:14** And may God Almighty grant you mercy before the man, that he may release to you your other brother and Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved."

**43:15** So the men took that present, and they took double the silver in their hand, along with Benjamin; and they arose and went down to Egypt, and stood before Joseph.

**43:16** When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the steward of his house, "Bring the men into the house, and slaughter an animal and make preparations, for the men shall eat with me at noon."

**43:17** And the man did just as Joseph had spoken, and the man brought the men into Joseph's house.

**43:18** Now the men were afraid because they were brought into Joseph's house, and they said, "It is on account of the silver that was returned in our bags the first time that we are being brought in, so that he may seek an occasion against us and fall upon us, to take us as slaves, together with our donkeys."

**43:19** So they drew near to the steward of Joseph's house and spoke to him at the doorway of the house,

**43:20** and said, "O my lord, we indeed came down the first time to buy food;

**43:21** and it came to pass, when we arrived at the lodging place and opened our bags, that behold, every man's silver was in the mouth of his bag, our silver in its full weight; so we have brought it back in our hand.

**43:22** And we have brought down other silver in our hand to buy food. We do not know who placed our silver in our bags."

**43:23** But he said, "Peace be to you, do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has given you treasure in your bags; your silver came to me." Then he brought Simeon out to them.

**43:24** So the man brought the men into Joseph's house and gave them water, and they washed their feet; and he gave fodder to their donkeys.

**43:25** And they made ready the present for Joseph's coming at noon, for they had heard that they were to eat bread there.

**43:26** When Joseph came into the house, they brought to him the present that was in their hand into the house, and bowed down before him to the earth.

**43:27** Then he asked them about their well-being and said, "Is your father well, the old man of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?"

**43:28** And they said, "Your servant our father is well; he is still alive." And they bowed their heads and prostrated themselves.

**43:29** Then he lifted up his eyes and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother's son, and said, "Is this your youngest brother, of whom you spoke to me?" And he said, "May God be gracious to you, my son."

**43:30** And Joseph hurried out, for his heart yearned deeply for his brother, and he sought a place to weep; so he entered his inner chamber and wept there.

**43:31** Then he washed his face and came out, and he restrained himself and said, "Serve the meal."

**43:32** So they served him by himself, and them by themselves, and the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves; because the Egyptians could not eat bread with the Hebrews, for that is an abomination to the Egyptians.

**43:33** And they were seated before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth; and the men looked in astonishment one at another.

**43:34** And he sent portions to them from before him, yet Benjamin's portion was five times as great as the portion of any of them. So they drank and made merry with him.

## Translator Notes

- **43:11** — "Choice produce" translates a Hebrew phrase literally meaning "the song" or "the prized products of the land."
- **43:14** — "God Almighty" translates the Hebrew divine title El Shaddai.
- **43:28** — The Masoretic Ketiv reads a singular form, whereas the Qere followed here reads the plural ("and prostrated themselves"), agreeing with the preceding plural verb.
- **43:34** — "Five times as great" renders the Hebrew idiom literally meaning "five hands," denoting a fivefold portion.
