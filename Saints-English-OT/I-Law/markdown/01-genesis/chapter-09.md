## Text

**9:1** And God blessed Noah and his sons, and He said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.

**9:2** And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth and upon every bird of the heavens; with all that creeps upon the ground and with all the fish of the sea, into your hand they are delivered.

**9:3** Every moving creature that is alive shall be food for you; even as the green plants, I have given all things to you.

**9:4** Yet flesh with its life—its blood—you shall not eat.

**9:5** And surely for your lifeblood I will demand a reckoning; from the hand of every beast I will require it, and from the hand of man—from the hand of each man for his brother—I will require the life of man.

**9:6** He who sheds the blood of man, by man his blood shall be shed; for in the image of God He made man.

**9:7** And as for you, be fruitful and multiply; teem upon the earth and multiply in it."

**9:8** Then God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him, saying,

**9:9** "And as for Me, behold, I establish My covenant with you and with your seed after you,

**9:10** and with every living creature that is with you—the birds, the livestock, and every beast of the earth with you—from all that came out of the ark to every beast of the earth.

**9:11** I establish My covenant with you, that never again shall all flesh be cut off by the waters of the flood, and never again shall there be a flood to destroy the earth."

**9:12** And God said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I give between Me and you, and between every living creature that is with you, for perpetual generations:

**9:13** My bow I have placed in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between Me and the earth.

**9:14** And it shall come to pass, when I bring a cloud over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud,

**9:15** and I will remember My covenant that is between Me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall never again become a flood to destroy all flesh.

**9:16** When the bow is in the cloud, I will look upon it to remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:17** And God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant that I have established between Me and all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:18** Now the sons of Noah who went forth from the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham was the father of Canaan.

**9:19** These three were the sons of Noah, and from these the whole earth was dispersed.

**9:20** And Noah began to be a man of the soil, and he planted a vineyard.

**9:21** And he drank of the wine and became drunk, and he uncovered himself within his tent.

**9:22** And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and he told his two brothers outside.

**9:23** Then Shem and Japheth took the garment, laid it upon the shoulders of both of them, and walked backward and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were turned backward, so they did not see their father's nakedness.

**9:24** Then Noah awoke from his wine, and he knew what his youngest son had done to him.

**9:25** And he said, "Cursed be Canaan; a servant of servants he shall be to his brethren."

**9:26** He also said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Shem, and let Canaan be a servant to him.

**9:27** May God enlarge Japheth, and may he dwell in the tents of Shem; and let Canaan be a servant to him."

**9:28** And Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.

**9:29** So all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **9:13** — The Hebrew noun qeshet literally means 'bow' (both a warrior's bow and a rainbow), set here in the cloud as a sign of peace between God and the earth.
- **9:21** — The Masoretic text preserves the archaic orthographic form oholoh ('his tent') written with a final heh rather than the usual vav suffix.
- **9:26** — The poetic pronominal suffix lamo ('to him' or 'to them') appears here and in verse 27, signifying Canaan's servitude.
- **9:27** — The blessing plays on the name Japheth with the Hebrew verb yaft ('may He enlarge'). While 'he' traditionally refers to Japheth dwelling in Shem's tents, some interpreters take God as the subject.
