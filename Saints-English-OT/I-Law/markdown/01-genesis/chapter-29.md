## Text

**29:1** Then Jacob lifted up his feet and went to the land of the sons of the East.

**29:2** And he looked, and behold, a well was in the field; and behold, three flocks of sheep were lying there beside it, for from that well they watered the flocks, and the stone upon the mouth of the well was great.

**29:3** Now all the flocks would be gathered there, and they would roll the stone from the mouth of the well and water the sheep, and restore the stone to its place upon the mouth of the well.

**29:4** And Jacob said to them, "My brethren, from where are you?" And they replied, "We are from Haran."

**29:5** Then he asked them, "Do you know Laban the son of Nahor?" And they said, "We know him."

**29:6** And he said to them, "Is it well with him?" And they answered, "It is well; and behold, Rachel his daughter is coming with the sheep."

**29:7** Then he said, "Behold, it is still high day; it is not the time for the livestock to be gathered. Water the sheep, and go and pasture them."

**29:8** But they replied, "We cannot, until all the flocks are gathered together and they roll the stone from the mouth of the well; then we will water the sheep."

**29:9** While he was still speaking with them, Rachel came with the sheep of her father, for she was a shepherdess.

**29:10** And it came to pass, when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and the sheep of Laban his mother's brother, that Jacob drew near and rolled the stone from the mouth of the well, and watered the sheep of Laban his mother's brother.

**29:11** Then Jacob kissed Rachel, and he raised his voice and wept.

**29:12** And Jacob declared to Rachel that he was her father's kinsman, and that he was the son of Rebekah; and she ran and told her father.

**29:13** And it came to pass, when Laban heard the tidings concerning Jacob his sister's son, that he ran to meet him, embraced him, and kissed him, and brought him into his house. And he recounted to Laban all these things.

**29:14** And Laban said to him, "Truly, you are my bone and my flesh!" So he dwelt with him for a full month.

**29:15** Then Laban said to Jacob, "Just because you are my kinsman, should you serve me for nothing? Tell me, what shall your wages be?"

**29:16** Now Laban had two daughters: the elder's name was Leah, and the younger's name was Rachel.

**29:17** Leah's eyes were tender, while Rachel was lovely in form and lovely in appearance.

**29:18** And Jacob loved Rachel; therefore he said, "I will serve you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel."

**29:19** And Laban replied, "It is better for me to give her to you than to give her to another man; abide with me."

**29:20** Thus Jacob served seven years for Rachel, yet in his eyes they were like only a few days because of his love for her.

**29:21** Then Jacob spoke to Laban, "Give me my wife, for my days are completed, so that I may go in to her."

**29:22** So Laban assembled all the men of the place and held a feast.

**29:23** And when evening came, he took his daughter Leah and brought her to Jacob; and he went in to her.

**29:24** Moreover, Laban gave Zilpah his maidservant to Leah his daughter as her maidservant.

**29:25** And when morning came, behold, it was Leah! And he said to Laban, "What is this that you have done to me? Did I not serve with you for Rachel? Why then have you deceived me?"

**29:26** And Laban answered, "It is not done thus in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn.

**29:27** Complete the week of this one, and we will give you this other one also, in return for the service that you shall serve with me seven more years."

**29:28** And Jacob did so, and completed her week; then Laban gave him Rachel his daughter to be his wife.

**29:29** And Laban gave Bilhah his maidservant to Rachel his daughter as her maidservant.

**29:30** So he went in to Rachel also, and indeed he loved Rachel more than Leah; and he served with him seven more years.

**29:31** And the LORD saw that Leah was unloved, and He opened her womb; but Rachel was barren.

**29:32** And Leah conceived and bore a son, and she called his name Reuben; for she said, "Because the LORD has looked upon my affliction; surely now my husband will love me."

**29:33** And she conceived again and bore a son, and she said, "Because the LORD has heard that I am unloved, He has given me this one also." So she named him Simeon.

**29:34** And she conceived yet again and bore a son, and she said, "Now this time my husband will be joined to me, for I have borne him three sons." Therefore his name was called Levi.

**29:35** And she conceived again and bore a son, and she said, "This time I will praise the LORD." Therefore she named him Judah; and she ceased bearing.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — "Lifted up his feet" is a literal Hebrew idiom conveying that Jacob set out briskly on his journey.
- **29:17** — The Hebrew adjective rendered "tender" can also mean "delicate" or "weak," contrasting with the description of Rachel's beauty.
- **29:27** — "The week of this one" refers to the customary seven-day bridal celebration for Leah before Rachel would also be given to Jacob.
- **29:31** — Literally "hated," a Hebrew idiom used in comparative family contexts to indicate being less favored or unloved.
- **29:34** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads an impersonal or masculine verb ("his name was called"), unlike the feminine forms in verses 32, 33, and 35. The names Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah each play in Hebrew on Leah's words ("looked," "heard," "joined," and "praise").
