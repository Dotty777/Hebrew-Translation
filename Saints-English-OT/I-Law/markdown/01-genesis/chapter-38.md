## Text

**38:1** Now it came to pass at that time that Judah went down from his brethren and turned aside to a certain Adullamite man, whose name was Hirah.

**38:2** And Judah saw there a daughter of a certain Canaanite man, whose name was Shua; and he took her and went in to her.

**38:3** So she conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Er.

**38:4** And she conceived again and bore a son, and she called his name Onan.

**38:5** And she yet again bore a son, and she called his name Shelah. Now he was at Chezib when she bore him.

**38:6** Then Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.

**38:7** But Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the LORD; and the LORD put him to death.

**38:8** And Judah said to Onan, "Go in to your brother's wife and perform the duty of a brother-in-law to her, and raise up offspring for your brother."

**38:9** But Onan knew that the offspring would not be his; and it came to pass, whenever he went in to his brother's wife, that he wasted it on the ground, so as not to give offspring to his brother.

**38:10** And what he did was evil in the sight of the LORD; therefore He put him to death also.

**38:11** Then Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Remain a widow in your father's house until Shelah my son grows up." For he said, "Lest he also die like his brethren." So Tamar went and dwelt in her father's house.

**38:12** Now in the course of many days, the daughter of Shua, Judah's wife, died. And when Judah was comforted, he went up to the shearers of his flock at Timnah, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite.

**38:13** And it was told to Tamar, saying, "Behold, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his flock."

**38:14** So she put off her widow's garments from herself, covered herself with a veil, and wrapped herself, and sat at the entrance of Enaim, which is by the way to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah had grown up, and she had not been given to him as a wife.

**38:15** When Judah saw her, he supposed her to be a harlot, because she had covered her face.

**38:16** Then he turned aside to her by the way and said, "Come now, let me come in to you"—for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, "What will you give me, that you may come in to me?"

**38:17** And he said, "I will send a young goat from the flock." And she said, "Only if you give a pledge until you send it."

**38:18** Then he said, "What pledge shall I give you?" So she said, "Your signet and your cord, and your staff that is in your hand." And he gave them to her and went in to her, and she conceived by him.

**38:19** Then she arose and departed, and laid aside her veil from herself, and put on her widow's garments.

**38:20** And Judah sent the young goat by the hand of his friend the Adullamite, to receive the pledge from the woman's hand; but he did not find her.

**38:21** Then he asked the men of her place, saying, "Where is the temple harlot who was at Enaim by the wayside?" And they said, "There has been no temple harlot in this place."

**38:22** So he returned to Judah and said, "I did not find her; and also the men of the place said, 'There has been no temple harlot in this place.'"

**38:23** And Judah said, "Let her keep them for herself, lest we become an object of scorn; behold, I sent this young goat, and you did not find her."

**38:24** And it came to pass, about three months later, that it was told to Judah, saying, "Tamar your daughter-in-law has played the harlot; and moreover, behold, she is with child by harlotry." So Judah said, "Bring her out and let her be burned!"

**38:25** As she was being brought out, she sent word to her father-in-law, saying, "By the man to whom these belong, I am with child." And she said, "Please identify to whom these belong—the signet, the cords, and the staff."

**38:26** Then Judah recognized them and said, "She is more righteous than I, because I did not give her to Shelah my son." And he did not know her again.

**38:27** Now it came to pass, at the time of her giving birth, that behold, twins were in her womb.

**38:28** And it came to pass, as she was giving birth, that one put out a hand; and the midwife took a scarlet thread and bound it upon his hand, saying, "This one came out first."

**38:29** Then it came to pass, as he drew back his hand, that behold, his brother came out; and she said, "What a breach you have made for yourself!" Therefore his name was called Perez.

**38:30** And afterward his brother came out, upon whose hand was the scarlet thread; and his name was called Zerah.

## Translator Notes

- **38:14** — Enaim (literally "Two Springs" or "Opening of Eyes") refers to a town or landmark situated along the road leading up to Timnah (cf. verse 21).
- **38:21** — Whereas verse 15 uses the ordinary word for a harlot, Hirah and the men of the place use the Hebrew term for a cult or temple harlot connected with Canaanite shrine practices.
- **38:25** — Tamar's appeal "Please identify" repeats the exact Hebrew phrase Judah and his brothers used when presenting Joseph's bloodstained tunic to Jacob in 37:32.
- **38:29** — The name Perez resembles the Hebrew word for "breach" or "breaking through," while Zerah in verse 30 suggests "dawning" or "shining," recalling the scarlet thread.
