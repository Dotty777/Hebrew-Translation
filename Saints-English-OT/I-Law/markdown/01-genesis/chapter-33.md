## Text

**33:1** Now Jacob lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, Esau was coming, and with him four hundred men. So he divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two maidservants.

**33:2** And he placed the maidservants and their children in front, Leah and her children after them, and Rachel and Joseph last.

**33:3** Then he himself went on before them and bowed to the ground seven times, until he drew near to his brother.

**33:4** But Esau ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell upon his neck and kissed him; and they wept.

**33:5** And he lifted up his eyes and saw the women and the children, and said, "Who are these with you?" And he said, "The children whom God has graciously given your servant."

**33:6** Then the maidservants drew near, they and their children, and bowed down.

**33:7** Leah also drew near with her children, and they bowed down. Afterward Joseph and Rachel drew near, and they bowed down.

**33:8** Then he said, "What do you mean by all this company which I encountered?" And he answered, "To find favor in the sight of my lord."

**33:9** But Esau said, "I have plenty, my brother; keep what is yours for yourself."

**33:10** And Jacob said, "No, I pray, if indeed I have found favor in your sight, then accept my present from my hand; for I have seen your face as though seeing the face of God, and you have received me favorably.

**33:11** Take, I pray, my blessing that has been brought to you, because God has dealt graciously with me, and because I have everything." So he urged him, and he accepted it.

**33:12** Then he said, "Let us set out and go on our way, and I will go before you."

**33:13** But he said to him, "My lord knows that the children are tender, and the nursing flocks and herds are a care to me; if they are driven hard for one day, all the flocks will die.

**33:14** Let my lord, I pray, pass on ahead of his servant, and I will move along gently at the pace of the livestock before me and at the pace of the children, until I come to my lord in Seir."

**33:15** And Esau said, "Let me leave with you some of the people who are with me." But he said, "What need is there? Only let me find favor in the sight of my lord."

**33:16** So Esau returned that day on his way to Seir.

**33:17** And Jacob journeyed to Succoth, and built himself a house and made booths for his livestock. Therefore he called the name of the place Succoth.

**33:18** Then Jacob arrived safely at the city of Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, when he came from Paddan Aram; and he pitched his camp before the city.

**33:19** And he purchased the parcel of the field where he had pitched his tent from the hand of the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred pieces of money.

**33:20** Then he erected an altar there and called it El Elohe Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **33:4** — In the Masoretic Text, the Hebrew word for "and kissed him" bears extraordinary points above each letter, an ancient scribal marking traditionally prompting reflection on the emotion or sincerity of Esau's kiss.
- **33:11** — Jacob's expression "my blessing" echoes the blessing taken from Esau in Genesis 27, and his words "I have everything" contrast with Esau's "I have plenty" in verse 9.
- **33:18** — The Hebrew word shalem is rendered here as "safely" in the sense of wholeness and peace, though some ancient interpreters take it as a proper name, Shalem, a city of Shechem.
- **33:19** — The Hebrew qesitah refers to an ancient unit of weight or currency of uncertain value; in verse 20, the altar name El Elohe Israel means God, the God of Israel.
