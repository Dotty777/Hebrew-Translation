## Text

**16:1** Now Sarai, Abram's wife, had borne him no children. And she had an Egyptian maidservant whose name was Hagar.

**16:2** So Sarai said to Abram, "Behold now, the LORD has restrained me from bearing children. Please go in to my maidservant; perhaps I shall be built up through her." And Abram heeded the voice of Sarai.

**16:3** Then Sarai, Abram's wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her maidservant, after Abram had dwelt ten years in the land of Canaan, and gave her to her husband Abram to be his wife.

**16:4** So he went in to Hagar, and she conceived. And when she saw that she had conceived, her mistress became lightly esteemed in her eyes.

**16:5** Then Sarai said to Abram, "May the wrong done to me be upon you! I gave my maidservant into your embrace; and when she saw that she had conceived, I became lightly esteemed in her eyes. May the LORD judge between me and you!"

**16:6** So Abram said to Sarai, "Behold, your maidservant is in your hand; do to her what is good in your eyes." And Sarai afflicted her, and she fled from her presence.

**16:7** Now the Angel of the LORD found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the way to Shur.

**16:8** And He said, "Hagar, Sarai's maidservant, where have you come from, and where are you going?" And she said, "I am fleeing from the presence of my mistress Sarai."

**16:9** Then the Angel of the LORD said to her, "Return to your mistress, and submit yourself under her hand."

**16:10** And the Angel of the LORD said to her, "I will greatly multiply your offspring, so that they cannot be numbered for multitude."

**16:11** And the Angel of the LORD said to her, "Behold, you are with child and shall bear a son; you shall name him Ishmael, for the LORD has heard your affliction.

**16:12** He shall be a wild donkey of a man; his hand shall be against every man, and every man's hand against him, and he shall dwell over against all his brethren."

**16:13** Then she called the name of the LORD who spoke to her, "You are the God who sees," for she said, "Have I indeed here looked after Him who sees me?"

**16:14** Therefore the well was called Beer Lahai Roi; behold, it lies between Kadesh and Bered.

**16:15** So Hagar bore Abram a son; and Abram called the name of his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael.

**16:16** Now Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.

## Translator Notes

- **16:2** — The Hebrew verb translated "be built up" plays on the word for "son," signifying establishing a family through offspring.
- **16:5** — The Masoretic Text marks the Hebrew word for "and you" with an extraordinary scribal dot above one of its letters.
- **16:11** — The name "Ishmael" means "God hears," matching the declaration that the LORD has heard Hagar's affliction.
- **16:12** — The Hebrew expression rendered "over against" can signify dwelling to the east of his kinsmen as well as living in hostility toward them.
- **16:13** — The Hebrew title "El Roi" means "God of seeing" or "God who sees me," and "Beer Lahai Roi" in verse 14 means "Well of the Living One who sees me."
