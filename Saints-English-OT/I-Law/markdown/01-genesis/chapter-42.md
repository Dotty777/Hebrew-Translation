## Text

**42:1** And Jacob saw that there was grain in Egypt, and Jacob said to his sons, "Why do you gaze at one another?"

**42:2** And he said, "Behold, I have heard that there is grain in Egypt. Go down there and buy grain for us from that place, that we may live and not die."

**42:3** So ten of Joseph's brethren went down to buy grain from Egypt.

**42:4** But Jacob did not send Benjamin, Joseph's brother, with his brethren, for he said, "Lest calamity befall him."

**42:5** And the sons of Israel came to buy grain among those who were coming, for the famine was in the land of Canaan.

**42:6** Now Joseph was the ruler over the land; it was he who sold grain to all the people of the land. And Joseph's brethren came and bowed down before him with their faces to the earth.

**42:7** And Joseph saw his brethren and recognized them, yet he acted as a stranger toward them and spoke harsh words with them. And he said to them, "From where have you come?" And they said, "From the land of Canaan, to buy food."

**42:8** Thus Joseph recognized his brethren, but they did not recognize him.

**42:9** And Joseph remembered the dreams which he had dreamed concerning them, and he said to them, "You are spies! You have come to see the nakedness of the land."

**42:10** And they said to him, "No, my lord; rather, your servants have come to buy food.

**42:11** All of us are sons of one man. We are honest men; your servants have not been spies."

**42:12** And he said to them, "No, but you have come to see the nakedness of the land."

**42:13** And they said, "Your servants are twelve brethren, the sons of one man in the land of Canaan; and behold, the youngest is this day with our father, and one is no more."

**42:14** And Joseph said to them, "It is just as I spoke to you, saying, 'You are spies!'

**42:15** By this you shall be tested: by the life of Pharaoh, you shall not go out from this place unless your youngest brother comes here.

**42:16** Send one of you, and let him bring your brother, while you shall be kept in custody, that your words may be tested to see whether truth is with you; and if not, by the life of Pharaoh, surely you are spies."

**42:17** So he put them all together into custody for three days.

**42:18** And Joseph said to them on the third day, "Do this and live, for I fear God:

**42:19** If you are honest men, let one of your brethren be bound in your prison house, and you go, carry grain for the famine of your households.

**42:20** And bring your youngest brother to me, so that your words may be verified, and you shall not die." And they did so.

**42:21** Then they said one to another, "Truly we are guilty concerning our brother, because we saw the distress of his soul when he pleaded with us, and we did not listen; therefore this distress has come upon us."

**42:22** And Reuben answered them, saying, "Did I not speak to you, saying, 'Do not sin against the boy,' and you did not listen? Therefore behold, his blood also is now required."

**42:23** Now they did not know that Joseph understood them, for there was an interpreter between them.

**42:24** And he turned away from them and wept. Then he returned to them and spoke with them, and he took Simeon from them and bound him before their eyes.

**42:25** Then Joseph gave a command to fill their vessels with grain, and to restore every man's silver to his sack, and to give them provisions for the journey. And thus it was done for them.

**42:26** So they loaded their grain upon their donkeys and departed from there.

**42:27** And as one of them opened his sack to give fodder to his donkey at the lodging place, he saw his silver; and behold, it was in the mouth of his bag.

**42:28** And he said to his brethren, "My silver has been returned, and behold, it is right here in my bag!" Then their hearts failed them, and they turned trembling one to another, saying, "What is this that God has done to us?"

**42:29** Then they came to Jacob their father in the land of Canaan and told him all that had befallen them, saying,

**42:30** "The man, the lord of the land, spoke harshly with us and treated us as spies of the land.

**42:31** And we said to him, 'We are honest men; we have not been spies.

**42:32** We are twelve brethren, sons of our father; one is no more, and the youngest is this day with our father in the land of Canaan.'

**42:33** Then the man, the lord of the land, said to us, 'By this I shall know that you are honest men: leave one of your brethren with me, and take grain for the famine of your households, and go.

**42:34** And bring your youngest brother to me, so that I may know that you are not spies, but that you are honest men. Then I will deliver your brother to you, and you may trade in the land.'"

**42:35** And it came to pass, as they were emptying their sacks, that behold, every man's bundle of silver was in his sack; and when they and their father saw the bundles of their silver, they were afraid.

**42:36** And Jacob their father said to them, "You have bereaved me! Joseph is no more, Simeon is no more, and you would take Benjamin; all these things are against me."

**42:37** Then Reuben spoke to his father, saying, "You may slay my two sons if I do not bring him back to you; entrust him into my hand, and I will restore him to you."

**42:38** But he said, "My son shall not go down with you, for his brother is dead, and he alone remains. If calamity should befall him on the way in which you go, then you would bring down my gray hair in sorrow to Sheol."

## Translator Notes

- **42:7** — The Hebrew creates a striking wordplay between "recognized them" and "acted as a stranger toward them," both formed from the same verbal root.
- **42:9** — "The nakedness of the land" is a Hebrew idiom denoting the land's exposed, undefended, or vulnerable points.
- **42:25** — Here and throughout chapters forty-two and forty-three, "silver" renders the Hebrew term used for weighed silver serving as money (see 43:21).
- **42:38** — "Sheol" is the Hebrew name for the realm of the dead or the grave.
