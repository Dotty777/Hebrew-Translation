## Text

**39:1** Now Joseph had been brought down to Egypt, and Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, an Egyptian, purchased him from the hand of the Ishmaelites who had brought him down there.

**39:2** And the LORD was with Joseph, and he became a prosperous man; and he was in the house of his master the Egyptian.

**39:3** And his master saw that the LORD was with him, and that the LORD caused all that he did to prosper in his hand.

**39:4** So Joseph found favor in his sight and attended him; and he appointed him over his house, and all that he possessed he placed in his hand.

**39:5** And it came to pass, from the time that he appointed him in his house and over all that he possessed, that the LORD blessed the house of the Egyptian on account of Joseph; and the blessing of the LORD was upon all that he had, both in the house and in the field.

**39:6** Thus he left all that he had in the hand of Joseph, and with him he did not concern himself with anything except the bread which he ate. Now Joseph was handsome in form and fair in appearance.

**39:7** And it came to pass after these things that his master's wife lifted up her eyes toward Joseph, and she said, "Lie with me."

**39:8** But he refused and said to his master's wife, "Behold, with me here my master does not know what is in the house, and he has committed all that he possesses into my hand.

**39:9** He is no greater in this house than I, nor has he withheld anything from me except you, because you are his wife. How then can I commit this great wickedness, and sin against God?"

**39:10** And it came to pass, as she spoke to Joseph day by day, that he did not heed her, to lie beside her or to be with her.

**39:11** And it happened on a certain day that he came into the house to perform his work, and not one of the men of the house was there inside the house.

**39:12** And she caught hold of him by his garment, saying, "Lie with me!" But he left his garment in her hand, and fled, and went outside.

**39:13** And it came to pass, when she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and had fled outside,

**39:14** that she called to the men of her house and spoke to them, saying, "See, he has brought in to us a Hebrew man to mock us! He came in to me to lie with me, and I cried out with a loud voice.

**39:15** And it came to pass, when he heard that I lifted up my voice and cried out, that he left his garment beside me, and fled, and went outside."

**39:16** And she laid his garment beside her until his master came to his house.

**39:17** And she spoke to him with words like these, saying, "The Hebrew servant whom you brought to us came in to me to mock me;

**39:18** and it came to pass, as I lifted up my voice and cried out, that he left his garment beside me and fled outside."

**39:19** And it came to pass, when his master heard the words of his wife which she spoke to him, saying, "In this manner your servant dealt with me," that his wrath was kindled.

**39:20** Then Joseph's master took him and put him into the prison, the place where the king's prisoners were confined; and he was there in the prison.

**39:21** But the LORD was with Joseph and extended lovingkindness to him, and He granted him favor in the sight of the keeper of the prison.

**39:22** And the keeper of the prison committed into Joseph's hand all the prisoners who were in the prison; and whatever was done there, he was the one who did it.

**39:23** The keeper of the prison did not inspect anything that was in his hand, because the LORD was with him; and whatever he did, the LORD caused to prosper.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — Potiphar's title "officer" renders Hebrew saris, denoting a high royal court official; "captain of the guard" is literally "chief of the bodyguards."
- **39:20** — The Masoretic Ketiv reads asurei ("those bound of the king"), while the Qere reads asirei ("the prisoners of the king"), which is followed here. "Prison" renders Hebrew bet hassohar, literally "house of confinement."
- **39:21** — Hebrew chesed ("lovingkindness") denotes the LORD's steadfast covenant mercy and faithful devotion toward Joseph even in confinement.
