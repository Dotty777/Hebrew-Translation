## Text

**6:1** And it came to pass, when mankind began to multiply upon the face of the ground, and daughters were born to them,

**6:2** that the sons of God beheld the daughters of men, that they were fair; and they took for themselves wives from among all whom they chose.

**6:3** And the LORD said, "My Spirit shall not abide in man forever, since he indeed is flesh; and his days shall be one hundred and twenty years."

**6:4** The Nephilim were upon the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God went in to the daughters of men and they bore offspring to them; those were the mighty men from of old, the men of renown.

**6:5** And the LORD saw that the wickedness of man was multiplied upon the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil all the day long.

**6:6** And the LORD regretted that He had made man upon the earth, and He was pained in His heart.

**6:7** And the LORD said, "I will blot out man whom I have created from off the face of the ground—from man to beast, to creeping thing, and to the birds of the heavens—for I regret that I have made them."

**6:8** Yet Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.

**6:9** These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a righteous man, blameless among his generations; with God Noah walked.

**6:10** And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**6:11** Now the earth became corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with violence.

**6:12** And God saw the earth, and behold, it was corrupted; for all flesh had corrupted its way upon the earth.

**6:13** And God said to Noah, "The end of all flesh has come before Me, for the earth is filled with violence because of them; and behold, I am about to destroy them together with the earth.

**6:14** Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood; you shall make compartments in the ark, and you shall coat it within and without with pitch.

**6:15** And this is the manner in which you shall make it: three hundred cubits shall be the length of the ark, fifty cubits its breadth, and thirty cubits its height.

**6:16** You shall make a window for the ark, and finish it to a cubit from above; and the doorway of the ark you shall set in its side. With lower, second, and third stories you shall make it.

**6:17** And as for Me, behold, I am bringing the flood of waters upon the earth to destroy all flesh in which is the breath of life from under the heavens; all that is upon the earth shall expire.

**6:18** Yet I will establish My covenant with you; and you shall enter the ark—you, your sons, your wife, and the wives of your sons with you.

**6:19** And of every living thing of all flesh, two of every kind you shall bring into the ark, to preserve them alive with you; male and female they shall be.

**6:20** Of the birds according to their kind, of the cattle according to their kind, and of every creeping thing of the ground according to its kind, two of every kind shall come to you to be preserved alive.

**6:21** And as for you, take for yourself of all food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself; and it shall be for food to you and to them."

**6:22** So Noah did; according to everything that God commanded him, thus he did.

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The rare Hebrew verb yadon occurs only here in Scripture and is understood either as "abide" (following ancient versions) or "contend/strive" (from the judicial root meaning to judge or plead).
- **6:4** — "Nephilim" transliterates the Masoretic Hebrew term, which is traditionally associated with the root meaning "to fall" and was rendered "giants" in the Septuagint and Vulgate.
- **6:14** — "Gopher wood" renders an unidentified Hebrew timber name occurring only here; the verse also features a deliberate wordplay between the verb "coat" (kaphar) and the noun "pitch" (kopher).
- **6:16** — The Hebrew noun tsohar appears in this architectural sense only here and denotes a window, skylight, or elevated light-opening beneath the roofline.
