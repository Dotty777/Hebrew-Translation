## Text

**13:1** Then Abram went up out of Egypt, he and his wife and all that belonged to him, and Lot with him, into the Negev.

**13:2** Now Abram was exceedingly wealthy in livestock, in silver, and in gold.

**13:3** And he went on his journeys from the Negev as far as Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Bethel and Ai,

**13:4** to the place of the altar that he had made there at the first; and there Abram called upon the name of the LORD.

**13:5** Lot also, who went with Abram, had flocks and herds and tents.

**13:6** And the land could not support them to dwell together, for their possessions were so great that they were not able to dwell together.

**13:7** And there arose strife between the herdsmen of Abram's livestock and the herdsmen of Lot's livestock. At that time the Canaanites and the Perizzites were dwelling in the land.

**13:8** So Abram said to Lot, "Please let there be no strife between me and you, and between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are brethren.

**13:9** Is not the whole land before you? Please separate yourself from me: if you go to the left, then I will go to the right; and if you go to the right, then I will go to the left."

**13:10** Then Lot lifted up his eyes and beheld all the plain of the Jordan, that it was everywhere well watered—before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah—like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, as you go toward Zoar.

**13:11** So Lot chose for himself all the plain of the Jordan, and Lot journeyed eastward. Thus they separated one from the other.

**13:12** Abram dwelt in the land of Canaan, while Lot dwelt among the cities of the plain and moved his tents as far as Sodom.

**13:13** Now the men of Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful against the LORD.

**13:14** And the LORD said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, "Lift up your eyes now and look from the place where you are—northward, and southward, and eastward, and westward;

**13:15** for all the land that you see I will give to you and to your seed forever.

**13:16** And I will make your seed like the dust of the earth, so that if a man can number the dust of the earth, then your seed also shall be numbered.

**13:17** Arise, walk through the land in its length and in its breadth, for to you I will give it."

**13:18** Then Abram moved his tents, and came and dwelt by the terebinths of Mamre, which are in Hebron, and there he built an altar to the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Hebrew adjective kaved literally means 'heavy,' an idiom denoting abundant wealth in livestock and precious metals.
- **13:8** — Literally 'men, brothers,' a Hebrew idiom for close kinsmen—here uncle and nephew.
- **13:10** — Hebrew kikkar ('circle' or 'basin') denotes the broad, fertile alluvial plain of the lower Jordan valley.
