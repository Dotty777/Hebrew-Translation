## Text

**11:1** Now all the earth had one language and common words.

**11:2** And it happened, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a broad plain in the land of Shinar, and they settled there.

**11:3** Then they said one to another, "Come, let us mold bricks and burn them thoroughly." So brick served them for stone, and bitumen served them for mortar.

**11:4** And they said, "Come, let us build for ourselves a city and a tower with its summit in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, lest we be dispersed over the face of all the earth."

**11:5** Then the LORD descended to see the city and the tower that the sons of men had built.

**11:6** And the LORD said, "Behold, they are one people and all of them have one language, and this is what they have begun to do; and now nothing that they purpose to do will be withheld from them.

**11:7** Come, let Us go down and confound their language there, so that one may not understand the speech of another."

**11:8** So the LORD dispersed them from there over the face of all the earth, and they ceased building the city.

**11:9** Therefore its name was called Babel, because there the LORD confounded the language of all the earth; and from there the LORD dispersed them over the face of all the earth.

**11:10** These are the generations of Shem: Shem was one hundred years old, and he fathered Arpachshad two years after the flood.

**11:11** And Shem lived five hundred years after he fathered Arpachshad, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:12** Now Arpachshad lived thirty-five years, and he fathered Shelah.

**11:13** And Arpachshad lived four hundred and three years after he fathered Shelah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:14** Now Shelah lived thirty years, and he fathered Eber.

**11:15** And Shelah lived four hundred and three years after he fathered Eber, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:16** And Eber lived thirty-four years, and he fathered Peleg.

**11:17** And Eber lived four hundred and thirty years after he fathered Peleg, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:18** And Peleg lived thirty years, and he fathered Reu.

**11:19** And Peleg lived two hundred and nine years after he fathered Reu, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:20** And Reu lived thirty-two years, and he fathered Serug.

**11:21** And Reu lived two hundred and seven years after he fathered Serug, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:22** And Serug lived thirty years, and he fathered Nahor.

**11:23** And Serug lived two hundred years after he fathered Nahor, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:24** And Nahor lived twenty-nine years, and he fathered Terah.

**11:25** And Nahor lived one hundred and nineteen years after he fathered Terah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:26** And Terah lived seventy years, and he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

**11:27** Now these are the generations of Terah: Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran fathered Lot.

**11:28** And Haran died in the presence of his father Terah in the land of his nativity, in Ur of the Chaldeans.

**11:29** Then Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves: the name of Abram's wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah and the father of Iscah.

**11:30** Now Sarai was barren; she had no child.

**11:31** And Terah took his son Abram, and Lot the son of Haran, his son's son, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, the wife of his son Abram; and they set out with them from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan, and they came to Haran and settled there.

**11:32** And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years; and Terah died in Haran.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Hebrew miqqedem can mean either 'from the east' or 'eastward'; here it introduces the migration into the Mesopotamian plain of Shinar.
- **11:9** — The name Babel (Babylon) is linked by wordplay to the Hebrew verb balal, meaning 'to confuse' or 'to confound.'
- **11:28** — Literally 'upon the face of Terah his father,' a Hebrew idiom meaning 'in the presence of' or 'during the lifetime of' his father.
- **11:31** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads 'they set out with them,' indicating that Terah and Abram departed together with Lot and Sarai as one company.
