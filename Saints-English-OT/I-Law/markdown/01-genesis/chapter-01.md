## Text

**1:1** In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

**1:2** Now the earth was unformed and empty, and darkness lay over the face of the deep; and the Spirit of God hovered over the face of the waters.

**1:3** And God said, "Let there be light"; and light came to be.

**1:4** And God saw the light, that it was good; and God divided between the light and the darkness.

**1:5** And God named the light Day, and the darkness He named Night. And evening came to be, and morning came to be: one day.

**1:6** Then God said, "Let a firmament come to be in the midst of the waters, and let it divide between waters and waters."

**1:7** So God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were beneath the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament; and so it came to be.

**1:8** And God named the firmament Heavens. And evening came to be, and morning came to be: a second day.

**1:9** Then God said, "Let the waters beneath the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the dry ground appear"; and so it came to be.

**1:10** And God named the dry ground Earth, and the gathering together of the waters He named Seas; and God saw that it was good.

**1:11** Then God said, "Let the earth sprout forth tender vegetation: seed-bearing herbs, and fruit trees yielding fruit according to their kind, whose seed is in itself upon the earth"; and so it came to be.

**1:12** So the earth brought forth tender vegetation, herbs yielding seed according to their kind, and trees yielding fruit whose seed is in itself according to their kind; and God saw that it was good.

**1:13** And evening came to be, and morning came to be: a third day.

**1:14** Then God said, "Let luminaries come to be in the firmament of the heavens to divide between the day and the night; and let them serve for signs, and for appointed seasons, and for days and years;

**1:15** and let them be for luminaries in the firmament of the heavens to shed light upon the earth"; and so it came to be.

**1:16** So God made the two great luminaries: the greater luminary to hold dominion over the day, and the lesser luminary to hold dominion over the night, and the stars also.

**1:17** And God set them in the firmament of the heavens to shed light upon the earth,

**1:18** and to hold dominion over the day and over the night, and to divide between the light and the darkness; and God saw that it was good.

**1:19** And evening came to be, and morning came to be: a fourth day.

**1:20** Then God said, "Let the waters swarm with swarms of living creatures, and let winged fowl fly above the earth across the face of the firmament of the heavens."

**1:21** So God created the great sea monsters, and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarmed according to their kind, and every winged bird according to its kind; and God saw that it was good.

**1:22** And God blessed them, saying, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas; and let the fowl multiply in the earth."

**1:23** And evening came to be, and morning came to be: a fifth day.

**1:24** Then God said, "Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kind: livestock, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth according to their kind"; and so it came to be.

**1:25** So God made the beasts of the earth according to their kind, and the livestock according to their kind, and every creeping thing of the ground according to its kind; and God saw that it was good.

**1:26** Then God said, "Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness; and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the heavens, and over the livestock, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth."

**1:27** And God created the man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.

**1:28** And God blessed them, and God said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it; and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the heavens, and over every living creature that moves upon the earth."

**1:29** And God said, "Behold, I have given to you every seed-bearing herb which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree in which is the fruit of a seed-bearing tree; to you it shall be for food.

**1:30** And to every beast of the earth, and to every fowl of the heavens, and to everything that creeps upon the earth in which is a living soul, I have given every green herb for food"; and so it came to be.

**1:31** And God beheld everything which He had made, and behold, it was exceedingly good. And evening came to be, and morning came to be: the sixth day.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Masoretic Hebrew reads plural hashshamayim ('the heavens') with the verb bara ('created').
- **1:2** — Hebrew tohu wavohu ('unformed and empty') is a rhyming pair describing uninhabitable emptiness; ruach Elohim ('Spirit of God,' or 'mighty wind from God') is paired with merachephet ('hovered,' cf. Deut 32:11).
- **1:5** — Hebrew yom echad uses the cardinal numeral ('one day'), whereas days two through five use anarthrous ordinals ('a second day,' etc.) and day six adds the definite article ('the sixth day,' v. 31).
- **1:6** — Hebrew raqia ('firmament') denotes a beaten-out vault or expanse dividing the waters.
- **1:26** — Hebrew adam ('man' or 'mankind') is paired with the plural verb 'let them have dominion,' marking humanity collectively as male and female (v. 27).
