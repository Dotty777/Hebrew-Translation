## Text

**30:1** And Rachel saw that she bore no children to Jacob, so Rachel grew envious of her sister and said to Jacob, "Give me children, or else I will die!"

**30:2** Then the anger of Jacob was kindled against Rachel, and he said, "Am I in God's place, who has withheld the fruit of the womb from you?"

**30:3** And she said, "Behold, my maidservant Bilhah! Go in to her, that she may bear upon my knees, and that I also may be built up through her."

**30:4** So she gave him Bilhah her handmaid as a wife, and Jacob went in to her.

**30:5** And Bilhah conceived and bore a son to Jacob.

**30:6** Then Rachel said, "God has vindicated me, and He has also heard my voice and given me a son." Therefore she called his name Dan.

**30:7** And Bilhah, Rachel's handmaid, conceived again and bore a second son to Jacob.

**30:8** And Rachel said, "With fierce wrestlings I have wrestled with my sister, and I have also prevailed." So she called his name Naphtali.

**30:9** When Leah saw that she had ceased from bearing, she took Zilpah her handmaid and gave her to Jacob as a wife.

**30:10** And Zilpah, Leah's handmaid, bore a son to Jacob.

**30:11** Then Leah said, "Good fortune has come!" So she called his name Gad.

**30:12** And Zilpah, Leah's handmaid, bore a second son to Jacob.

**30:13** Then Leah said, "What happiness is mine! For the daughters will call me blessed." So she called his name Asher.

**30:14** Now Reuben went out in the days of the wheat harvest and found mandrakes in the field, and brought them to Leah his mother. Then Rachel said to Leah, "Please give me some of your son's mandrakes."

**30:15** But she said to her, "Is it too small a thing that you have taken my husband, that you would also take my son's mandrakes?" And Rachel said, "Therefore he shall lie with you tonight in exchange for your son's mandrakes."

**30:16** When Jacob came in from the field in the evening, Leah went out to meet him and said, "You must come in to me, for I have surely hired you with my son's mandrakes." So he lay with her that night.

**30:17** And God heard Leah, and she conceived and bore a fifth son to Jacob.

**30:18** Then Leah said, "God has given me my reward, because I gave my handmaid to my husband." So she called his name Issachar.

**30:19** And Leah conceived again and bore a sixth son to Jacob.

**30:20** And Leah said, "God has endowed me with a good gift; this time my husband will dwell with me, for I have borne him six sons." So she called his name Zebulun.

**30:21** Afterward she bore a daughter, and called her name Dinah.

**30:22** Then God remembered Rachel, and God heard her and opened her womb.

**30:23** And she conceived and bore a son, and said, "God has taken away my reproach."

**30:24** So she called his name Joseph, saying, "May the LORD add to me another son."

**30:25** And it came to pass, when Rachel had borne Joseph, that Jacob said to Laban, "Send me away, that I may go to my own place and to my own land.

**30:26** Give me my wives and my children for whom I have served you, and let me depart; for you know the service with which I have served you."

**30:27** And Laban said to him, "If now I have found favor in your sight—I have learned by divination that the LORD has blessed me because of you."

**30:28** And he said, "Name your wages to me, and I will pay them."

**30:29** So he said to him, "You know how I have served you, and how your livestock has fared with me.

**30:30** For what you had before my coming was little, and it has increased into a multitude, and the LORD has blessed you wherever I turned. And now, when shall I also provide for my own house?"

**30:31** And he said, "What shall I give you?" And Jacob said, "You shall give me nothing at all. If you will do this thing for me, I will again tend and keep your flock:

**30:32** Let me pass through all your flock today, removing from there every speckled and spotted one, and every dark one among the sheep, and the spotted and speckled among the goats; and such shall be my wages.

**30:33** So my righteousness will answer for me in time to come, when you come concerning my wages in your presence: every one that is not speckled and spotted among the goats, and dark among the sheep, shall be reckoned as stolen with me."

**30:34** And Laban said, "Behold, let it be according to your word!"

**30:35** So he removed on that day the male goats that were striped and spotted, and all the female goats that were speckled and spotted—every one that had white on it—and every dark one among the sheep, and put them into the hand of his sons.

**30:36** And he set a three days' journey between himself and Jacob, while Jacob pastured the remainder of Laban's flocks.

**30:37** Then Jacob took for himself fresh rods of poplar, almond, and plane tree, and peeled white strips in them, exposing the white that was in the rods.

**30:38** And he set the rods which he had peeled in the gutters, in the watering troughs where the flocks came to drink, directly in front of the flocks; and they mated when they came to drink.

**30:39** So the flocks mated before the rods, and the flocks brought forth young that were striped, speckled, and spotted.

**30:40** And Jacob separated the lambs, and set the faces of the flocks toward the striped and all the dark ones in the flock of Laban; and he set apart his own droves for himself, and did not put them with Laban's flock.

**30:41** And it came to pass, whenever the stronger of the flock were mating, that Jacob laid the rods before the eyes of the flock in the gutters, so that they might mate among the rods.

**30:42** But when the flock was feeble, he did not place them there; so the feebler became Laban's, and the stronger became Jacob's.

**30:43** Thus the man prospered exceedingly, and had abundant flocks, handmaids and menservants, and camels and donkeys.

## Translator Notes

- **30:8** — The Hebrew expression translated 'fierce wrestlings' is literally 'wrestlings of God,' an idiom expressing great intensity.
- **30:11** — Following the Masoretic qere, 'Good fortune has come' (ba gad); the ketiv reads 'With good fortune' (begad).
- **30:20** — Leah's words play on the name Zebulun through two similar-sounding Hebrew roots meaning 'to endow' and 'to dwell with' (or 'to honor').
- **30:27** — The Hebrew verb rendered 'I have learned by divination' literally denotes observing or interpreting omens; Laban's opening 'If' clause breaks off in polite entreaty.
