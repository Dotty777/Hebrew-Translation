## Text

**8:1** Then God remembered Noah, and every wild beast and all the livestock that were with him in the ark; and God caused a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters subsided.

**8:2** And the fountains of the deep and the floodgates of the heavens were stopped up, and the rain from the heavens was held back.

**8:3** And the waters receded steadily from off the earth; and at the end of one hundred and fifty days the waters had diminished.

**8:4** And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.

**8:5** And the waters continued to diminish until the tenth month; in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the summits of the mountains became visible.

**8:6** And it came to pass, at the close of forty days, that Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made.

**8:7** And he sent forth a raven, and it went out, going to and fro until the waters had dried up from off the earth.

**8:8** Then he sent forth a dove from himself to see whether the waters had abated from the face of the ground.

**8:9** But the dove found no resting place for the sole of her foot, so she returned to him into the ark, for waters were upon the face of the whole earth. Then he reached out his hand, took her, and brought her to himself into the ark.

**8:10** Then he waited still another seven days, and once more he sent forth the dove out of the ark.

**8:11** And the dove came to him toward evening, and behold, a freshly plucked olive leaf was in her beak; thus Noah knew that the waters had abated from the earth.

**8:12** And he waited still another seven days and sent forth the dove, and she did not return to him anymore.

**8:13** Now it came to pass in the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the waters had dried up from off the earth. Then Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and behold, the face of the ground was dry.

**8:14** And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth had become dry.

**8:15** And God spoke to Noah, saying,

**8:16** "Come forth from the ark, you and your wife, and your sons and the wives of your sons with you.

**8:17** Bring forth with you every living creature of all flesh that is with you—birds, livestock, and every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth—that they may teem in the earth, and be fruitful and multiply upon the earth."

**8:18** So Noah came forth, and his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons with him.

**8:19** Every wild beast, every creeping thing, and every bird—everything that moves upon the earth—according to their families, came forth from the ark.

**8:20** And Noah built an altar to the LORD, and he took from every clean beast and from every clean bird, and offered up burnt offerings upon the altar.

**8:21** And the LORD smelled the soothing aroma, and the LORD said in His heart, "I will never again curse the ground on account of man, for the inclination of the heart of man is evil from his youth; nor will I again strike down every living thing as I have done.

**8:22** For all the remaining days of the earth, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease."

## Translator Notes

- **8:13** — Hebrew distinguishes the verb here (charvu, indicating that standing surface water had drained away) from the verb in verse 14 (yavshah, indicating that the ground itself had dried thoroughly).
- **8:17** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text reads hotse and the read text (Qere) reads haytse; both are Hiphil imperatives meaning "bring forth," with the Qere preserving an older orthographic form retaining the root yod.
- **8:21** — "The soothing aroma" (reach hannichoach) echoes the sound of Noah's name (Noach), recalling the hope of rest and relief expressed at his birth in Genesis 5:29.
