## Text

**45:1** Then Joseph could no longer restrain himself before all those who stood attending him, and he cried out, "Send every man out from before me!" So no one stood with him when Joseph made himself known to his brothers.

**45:2** And he lifted up his voice in weeping, so that the Egyptians heard it, and the household of Pharaoh heard it.

**45:3** And Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?" But his brothers could not answer him, for they were dismayed before his presence.

**45:4** Then Joseph said to his brothers, "Please draw near to me." So they drew near. And he said, "I am Joseph your brother, whom you sold into Egypt.

**45:5** Now therefore, do not be grieved nor angry with yourselves because you sold me here; for God sent me ahead of you to preserve life.

**45:6** For these two years the famine has been in the midst of the land, and there remain five years in which there shall be neither plowing nor harvest.

**45:7** And God sent me before you to preserve for you a remnant in the earth, and to keep you alive through a great deliverance.

**45:8** So now, it was not you who sent me here, but God; and He has appointed me a father to Pharaoh, and lord over all his house, and ruler throughout all the land of Egypt.

**45:9** Hasten and go up to my father, and say to him, 'Thus says your son Joseph: God has made me lord of all Egypt; come down to me, do not delay.

**45:10** And you shall dwell in the land of Goshen, and you shall be near to me, you and your sons and your sons' sons, your flocks and your herds, and all that you possess.

**45:11** And there I will sustain you, for there are yet five years of famine, lest you and your household and all that you have come to poverty.'

**45:12** And behold, your own eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my own mouth speaking to you.

**45:13** So you shall declare to my father all my glory in Egypt, and all that you have seen; and you shall hasten and bring my father down here."

**45:14** Then he fell upon his brother Benjamin's neck and wept, and Benjamin wept upon his neck.

**45:15** Moreover he kissed all his brothers and wept over them; and after that his brothers spoke with him.

**45:16** Now the report was heard in Pharaoh's house, saying, "Joseph's brothers have come." And it was pleasing in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of his servants.

**45:17** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Say to your brothers, 'Do this: load your beasts and depart, go to the land of Canaan,

**45:18** and take your father and your households and come to me; and I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and you shall eat the fat of the land.'

**45:19** Now you are commanded, 'Do this: take for yourselves wagons from the land of Egypt for your little ones and for your wives, and bring your father and come.

**45:20** And do not let your eyes look with regret upon your goods, for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.'"

**45:21** And the sons of Israel did so; and Joseph gave them wagons, according to the command of Pharaoh, and he gave them provisions for the journey.

**45:22** To all of them he gave, to each man, changes of garments; but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of garments.

**45:23** And to his father he sent as follows: ten donkeys laden with the good things of Egypt, and ten female donkeys laden with grain, bread, and sustenance for his father for the journey.

**45:24** So he sent his brothers away, and they departed; and he said to them, "Do not quarrel along the way."

**45:25** Then they went up out of Egypt, and came to the land of Canaan, to Jacob their father.

**45:26** And they told him, saying, "Joseph is still alive, and indeed he is ruler over all the land of Egypt." And his heart grew numb, for he did not believe them.

**45:27** Yet when they spoke to him all the words of Joseph which he had spoken to them, and when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him, the spirit of Jacob their father revived.

**45:28** And Israel said, "It is enough! Joseph my son is still alive. I will go and see him before I die."

## Translator Notes

- **45:8** — The title 'a father to Pharaoh' reflects an ancient Egyptian court designation for a trusted senior counselor and vizier.
- **45:17** — The Hebrew verb translated 'load' appears only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **45:24** — The Hebrew verb translated 'quarrel' broadly denotes agitation, anger, or trembling, cautioning the brothers against mutual recrimination or turmoil on the journey home.
- **45:26** — The Hebrew idiom 'his heart grew numb' literally describes the heart growing cold or faint in stunned disbelief.
