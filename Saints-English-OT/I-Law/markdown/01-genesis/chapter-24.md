## Text

**24:1** Now Abraham was old, advanced in days; and the LORD had blessed Abraham in everything.

**24:2** And Abraham said to his servant, the elder of his house, who ruled over all that belonged to him, "Please place your hand under my thigh,

**24:3** and I will have you swear by the LORD, the God of heaven and the God of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose midst I dwell;

**24:4** but you shall go to my land and to my kindred, and take a wife for my son Isaac."

**24:5** And the servant said to him, "Perhaps the woman will not be willing to follow me to this land. Must I indeed bring your son back to the land from which you came out?"

**24:6** And Abraham said to him, "Take heed to yourself, lest you bring my son back there.

**24:7** The LORD, the God of heaven, who took me from my father's house and from the land of my kindred, and who spoke to me and swore to me, saying, 'To your seed I will give this land,' He will send His angel before you, and you shall take a wife for my son from there.

**24:8** And if the woman is not willing to follow you, then you shall be clear of this oath of mine; only you shall not bring my son back there."

**24:9** So the servant placed his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master, and swore to him concerning this matter.

**24:10** Then the servant took ten camels from his master's camels and departed, and all the good things of his master were in his hand; and he arose and journeyed to Aram Naharaim, to the city of Nahor.

**24:11** And he made the camels kneel down outside the city by the well of water at the time of evening, the time when the women go out to draw water.

**24:12** And he said, "O LORD, God of my master Abraham, please grant me success today, and show lovingkindness to my master Abraham.

**24:13** Behold, I am standing by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water.

**24:14** Now let it come to pass that the maiden to whom I say, 'Please lower your pitcher that I may drink,' and she says, 'Drink, and I will give your camels water as well'—let her be the one You have appointed for Your servant Isaac; and by this I shall know that You have shown lovingkindness to my master."

**24:15** And it came to pass, before he had finished speaking, that behold, Rebekah came out, who was born to Bethuel the son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother, with her pitcher upon her shoulder.

**24:16** Now the maiden was exceedingly fair to look upon, a virgin, and no man had known her. And she went down to the spring, filled her pitcher, and came up.

**24:17** And the servant ran to meet her and said, "Please let me sip a little water from your pitcher."

**24:18** And she said, "Drink, my lord." Then she quickly lowered her pitcher upon her hand and gave him a drink.

**24:19** And when she had finished giving him a drink, she said, "For your camels also I will draw water, until they have finished drinking."

**24:20** So she hastened and emptied her pitcher into the trough, and ran again to the well to draw water, and drew for all his camels.

**24:21** And the man gazed upon her in wonder, keeping silent to know whether the LORD had made his journey prosperous or not.

**24:22** And it came to pass, when the camels had finished drinking, that the man took a gold nose ring weighing half a shekel, and two bracelets for her wrists weighing ten shekels of gold.

**24:23** And he said, "Whose daughter are you? Please tell me, is there room in your father's house for us to lodge?"

**24:24** And she said to him, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Milcah, whom she bore to Nahor."

**24:25** Moreover she said to him, "We have both straw and fodder in abundance, and room to lodge as well."

**24:26** Then the man bowed his head and worshiped the LORD.

**24:27** And he said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who has not forsaken His lovingkindness and His truth toward my master. As for me, while I was on the way, the LORD led me to the house of my master's brethren."

**24:28** So the maiden ran and told her mother's household concerning these things.

**24:29** Now Rebekah had a brother whose name was Laban, and Laban ran outside to the man, to the spring.

**24:30** And it came to pass, when he saw the nose ring and the bracelets upon his sister's wrists, and when he heard the words of his sister Rebekah, saying, "Thus the man spoke to me," that he came to the man; and behold, he was standing by the camels at the spring.

**24:31** And he said, "Come in, O blessed of the LORD! Why do you stand outside? For I have prepared the house, and a place for the camels."

**24:32** So the man came into the house. And he unloaded the camels, and provided straw and fodder for the camels, and water to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him.

**24:33** Then food was set before him to eat, but he said, "I will not eat until I have spoken my words." And he said, "Speak on."

**24:34** And he said, "I am Abraham's servant.

**24:35** And the LORD has blessed my master exceedingly, and he has become great; and He has given him flocks and herds, silver and gold, menservants and maidservants, and camels and donkeys.

**24:36** And Sarah my master's wife bore a son to my master after she had grown old, and he has given to him all that he possesses.

**24:37** Now my master made me swear, saying, 'You shall not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I dwell;

**24:38** but you shall go to my father's house and to my family, and take a wife for my son.'

**24:39** And I said to my master, 'Perhaps the woman will not follow me.'

**24:40** And he said to me, 'The LORD, before whom I walk, will send His angel with you and prosper your way; and you shall take a wife for my son from my family and from my father's house.

**24:41** Then you shall be clear of my oath when you come to my family; and if they do not give her to you, then you shall be free from my oath.'

**24:42** And I came today to the spring and said, 'O LORD, God of my master Abraham, if You will indeed prosper the way upon which I go,

**24:43** behold, I am standing by the spring of water; and let it come to pass that the young woman who comes out to draw water, to whom I say, "Please give me a little water from your pitcher to drink,"

**24:44** and she says to me, "Drink yourself, and I will draw for your camels also"—let her be the woman whom the LORD has appointed for my master's son.'

**24:45** Before I had finished speaking in my heart, behold, Rebekah came out with her pitcher upon her shoulder, and she went down to the spring and drew water. And I said to her, 'Please give me a drink.'

**24:46** And she quickly lowered her pitcher from her shoulder and said, 'Drink, and I will give your camels water as well.' So I drank, and she watered the camels also.

**24:47** Then I asked her and said, 'Whose daughter are you?' And she said, 'The daughter of Bethuel, Nahor's son, whom Milcah bore to him.' So I placed the nose ring on her nose and the bracelets upon her wrists.

**24:48** And I bowed my head and worshiped the LORD, and blessed the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who had led me in the way of truth to take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.

**24:49** Now therefore, if you will show lovingkindness and truth to my master, tell me; and if not, tell me, that I may turn to the right or to the left."

**24:50** Then Laban and Bethuel answered and said, "This matter has come forth from the LORD; we cannot speak to you either bad or good.

**24:51** Behold, Rebekah is before you; take her and go, and let her become the wife of your master's son, as the LORD has spoken."

**24:52** And it came to pass, when Abraham's servant heard their words, that he bowed down to the earth before the LORD.

**24:53** Then the servant brought forth ornaments of silver, ornaments of gold, and garments, and gave them to Rebekah; he also gave precious gifts to her brother and to her mother.

**24:54** And they ate and drank, he and the men who were with him, and lodged the night. And they arose in the morning, and he said, "Send me away to my master."

**24:55** But her brother and her mother said, "Let the maiden abide with us a few days, at least ten; afterward she may go."

**24:56** And he said to them, "Do not delay me, seeing that the LORD has prospered my way; send me away, that I may go to my master."

**24:57** So they said, "Let us call the maiden and ask her directly."

**24:58** Then they called Rebekah and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" And she said, "I will go."

**24:59** So they sent away Rebekah their sister and her nurse, and Abraham's servant and his men.

**24:60** And they blessed Rebekah and said to her, "Our sister, may you become thousands of ten thousands! And may your seed possess the gate of those who hate them!"

**24:61** Then Rebekah and her maidens arose, and rode upon the camels, and followed the man. So the servant took Rebekah and departed.

**24:62** Now Isaac had come from the way of Beer Lahai Roi, for he was dwelling in the land of the Negev.

**24:63** And Isaac went out to meditate in the field toward evening; and he lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, camels were approaching.

**24:64** And Rebekah lifted up her eyes, and when she saw Isaac, she alighted from the camel.

**24:65** And she said to the servant, "Who is this man walking in the field to meet us?" And the servant said, "He is my master." So she took her veil and covered herself.

**24:66** And the servant recounted to Isaac all the things that he had done.

**24:67** Then Isaac brought her into the tent of his mother Sarah; and he took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her. So Isaac was comforted after his mother's death.

## Translator Notes

- **24:10** — Aram Naharaim means Aram of the Two Rivers, denoting the region of northern Mesopotamia where Nahor's city of Haran lay.
- **24:22** — The weight of the nose ring is Hebrew beka, a half-shekel, and the bracelets are literally ten of gold, referring to ten shekels in weight.
- **24:33** — The translation follows the Masoretic Qere passive reading, food was set, where the consonantal Ketiv may reflect an older passive form or active he set.
- **24:43** — Hebrew almah denotes a young woman of marriageable age, varying the term from naarah, maiden, used earlier in the chapter.
- **24:63** — The Hebrew verb translated to meditate occurs only here in Scripture and has traditionally been understood as meditating or praying, though some take it to mean strolling through the field.
