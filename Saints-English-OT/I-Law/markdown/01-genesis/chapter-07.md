## Text

**7:1** And the LORD said to Noah, "Enter the ark, you and all your household, for you I have seen to be righteous before Me in this generation.

**7:2** Of every clean beast you shall take for yourself seven each, a male and his mate; and of the beasts that are not clean, two, a male and his mate;

**7:3** also of the birds of the heavens, seven each, male and female, to preserve offspring alive upon the face of all the earth.

**7:4** For after seven days more, I will send rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights, and I will blot out every living thing that I have made from the face of the ground."

**7:5** So Noah did according to everything that the LORD commanded him.

**7:6** Now Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters came upon the earth.

**7:7** And Noah went into the ark, together with his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons, because of the waters of the flood.

**7:8** Of the clean beasts, and of the beasts that are not clean, and of the birds, and of everything that creeps upon the ground,

**7:9** two by two they came to Noah into the ark, male and female, just as God had commanded Noah.

**7:10** And it came to pass at the end of the seven days that the waters of the flood came upon the earth.

**7:11** In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that day all the fountains of the great deep burst open, and the floodgates of the heavens were opened.

**7:12** And the rain fell upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.

**7:13** On that very day Noah entered the ark, together with Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and the wife of Noah and the three wives of his sons with them—

**7:14** they, and every wild beast according to its kind, and all the livestock according to their kind, and every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth according to its kind, and every bird according to its kind, every bird of every wing.

**7:15** And they came to Noah into the ark, two by two of all flesh in which was the breath of life.

**7:16** And those that came in entered, male and female of all flesh, just as God had commanded him; and the LORD shut him in.

**7:17** And the flood was forty days upon the earth; and the waters multiplied and bore up the ark, so that it rose above the earth.

**7:18** And the waters prevailed and multiplied exceedingly upon the earth, and the ark floated upon the face of the waters.

**7:19** And the waters prevailed most exceedingly upon the earth, and all the high mountains beneath all the heavens were covered.

**7:20** Fifteen cubits higher the waters prevailed, so that the mountains were covered.

**7:21** And all flesh that moved upon the earth perished—birds, livestock, wild beasts, and every swarming thing that swarms upon the earth, and all mankind.

**7:22** Every creature that had the breath of the spirit of life in its nostrils, of all that were on the dry land, died.

**7:23** And He blotted out every living thing that was upon the face of the ground—from man to beast, to creeping thing, and to the birds of the heavens—and they were blotted out from the earth; and only Noah remained, and those who were with him in the ark.

**7:24** And the waters prevailed upon the earth for one hundred and fifty days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The doubled numeral "seven seven" may denote either seven pairs or seven individuals of each clean kind, and "a male and his mate" renders the vivid Hebrew idiom literally meaning "a man and his wife."
- **7:11** — "Floodgates of the heavens" translates Hebrew arubbot hashamayim, literally "windows" or "lattices of the heavens," picturing the heavenly sluices releasing waters from above.
- **7:23** — The Masoretic vocalization of the opening verb is active (Qal, "and He blotted out"), taking God as the implicit subject before the direct object marker, followed by the passive Niphal ("and they were blotted out").
