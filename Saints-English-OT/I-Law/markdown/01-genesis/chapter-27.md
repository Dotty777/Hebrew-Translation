## Text

**27:1** Now it came to pass, when Isaac had grown old and his eyes were too dim to see, that he called Esau his elder son and said to him, "My son." And he said to him, "Here I am."

**27:2** Then he said, "Behold now, I have grown old; I do not know the day of my death.

**27:3** Now therefore, take your weapons, your quiver and your bow, and go out into the field and hunt wild game for me.

**27:4** And prepare savory food for me, such as I love, and bring it to me that I may eat, so that my soul may bless you before I die."

**27:5** Now Rebekah was listening as Isaac spoke to Esau his son. And Esau went into the field to hunt wild game to bring back.

**27:6** Then Rebekah spoke to Jacob her son, saying, "Behold, I heard your father speaking to Esau your brother, saying,

**27:7** 'Bring me wild game and prepare savory food for me, that I may eat and bless you in the presence of the LORD before my death.'

**27:8** Now therefore, my son, heed my voice in what I command you.

**27:9** Go now to the flock and fetch for me from there two choice young goats, and I will prepare savory food from them for your father, such as he loves.

**27:10** Then you shall bring it to your father so that he may eat, in order that he may bless you before his death."

**27:11** And Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Behold, Esau my brother is a hairy man, whereas I am a smooth-skinned man.

**27:12** Perhaps my father will feel me, and I shall appear in his eyes as a deceiver; and I shall bring a curse upon myself and not a blessing."

**27:13** But his mother said to him, "Let your curse be upon me, my son; only heed my voice, and go, fetch them for me."

**27:14** So he went and fetched them and brought them to his mother, and his mother prepared savory food, such as his father loved.

**27:15** Then Rebekah took the finest garments of Esau her elder son, which were with her in the house, and put them on Jacob her younger son.

**27:16** And she placed the skins of the young goats upon his hands and upon the smooth part of his neck.

**27:17** Then she put the savory food and the bread, which she had prepared, into the hand of Jacob her son.

**27:18** So he went in to his father and said, "My father." And he said, "Here I am. Who are you, my son?"

**27:19** And Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau your firstborn; I have done just as you told me. Arise, I pray, sit up and eat of my wild game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27:20** But Isaac said to his son, "How is it that you have found it so quickly, my son?" And he said, "Because the LORD your God brought it before me."

**27:21** Then Isaac said to Jacob, "Draw near, I pray, that I may feel you, my son, whether you are indeed my son Esau or not."

**27:22** So Jacob drew near to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said, "The voice is the voice of Jacob, yet the hands are the hands of Esau."

**27:23** And he did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like the hands of his brother Esau; so he blessed him.

**27:24** Then he said, "Are you truly my son Esau?" And he said, "I am."

**27:25** He said, "Bring it near to me, and I will eat of my son's wild game, so that my soul may bless you." So he brought it near to him, and he ate; and he brought him wine, and he drank.

**27:26** Then Isaac his father said to him, "Draw near now and kiss me, my son."

**27:27** So he drew near and kissed him; and he smelled the fragrance of his garments and blessed him, and said, "See, the fragrance of my son is like the fragrance of a field which the LORD has blessed.

**27:28** May God grant you of the dew of heaven, and of the richness of the earth, and an abundance of grain and new wine.

**27:29** Let peoples serve you, and nations bow down to you; be master over your brethren, and let your mother's sons bow down to you. Cursed be everyone who curses you, and blessed be everyone who blesses you."

**27:30** Now it came to pass, as soon as Isaac had finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had scarcely gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, that Esau his brother came in from his hunting.

**27:31** He also had prepared savory food and brought it to his father, and he said to his father, "Let my father arise and eat of his son's wild game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27:32** And Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" And he said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau."

**27:33** Then Isaac trembled with an exceedingly great trembling and said, "Who then was he who hunted wild game and brought it to me? I ate of it all before you came, and I have blessed him—and indeed he shall be blessed."

**27:34** When Esau heard the words of his father, he cried out with an exceedingly great and bitter cry, and said to his father, "Bless me, even me also, O my father!"

**27:35** And he said, "Your brother came deceitfully and has taken your blessing."

**27:36** And he said, "Is he not rightly named Jacob? For he has supplanted me these two times: he took away my birthright, and behold, now he has taken away my blessing." And he said, "Have you not reserved a blessing for me?"

**27:37** Then Isaac answered and said to Esau, "Behold, I have appointed him master over you, and all his brethren I have given to him as servants; and with grain and new wine I have sustained him. What then can I do for you, my son?"

**27:38** Then Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me, me also, my father!" And Esau raised his voice and wept.

**27:39** Then Isaac his father answered and said to him, "Behold, your dwelling shall be of the richness of the earth, and of the dew of heaven from above.

**27:40** By your sword you shall live, and you shall serve your brother; yet it shall come to pass, when you grow restless, that you shall break his yoke from off your neck."

**27:41** So Esau bore hatred against Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him, and Esau said in his heart, "The days of mourning for my father are drawing near; then I will slay my brother Jacob."

**27:42** Now the words of Esau her elder son were told to Rebekah. So she sent and called Jacob her younger son, and said to him, "Behold, your brother Esau comforts himself concerning you by intending to slay you.

**27:43** Now therefore, my son, heed my voice: arise and flee to my brother Laban in Haran,

**27:44** and dwell with him a few days, until your brother's fury turns away,

**27:45** until your brother's wrath turns away from you and he forgets what you have done to him; then I will send and bring you back from there. Why should I be bereaved of you both in one day?"

**27:46** Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am weary of my life because of the daughters of Heth. If Jacob takes a wife from the daughters of Heth, like these who are of the daughters of the land, what good will my life be to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Qere (read form) has the masculine noun for wild game, whereas the Ketiv (written form) carries an additional final letter often denoting provisions or game.
- **27:29** — The Qere (read form) reads the plural verb 'bow down' in agreement with 'nations,' whereas the Ketiv (written form) is written in the singular.
- **27:36** — Esau plays on the Hebrew name Jacob and the related verb meaning to supplant, deceive, or grasp by the heel.
- **27:39** — The Hebrew preposition rendered 'of' may also carry a privative sense, 'away from,' contrasting Esau's lot with Jacob's blessing in verse 28.
