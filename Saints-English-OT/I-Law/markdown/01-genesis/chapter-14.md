## Text

**14:1** Now it came to pass in the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim,

**14:2** that they waged war against Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela—that is, Zoar.

**14:3** All these joined forces at the Valley of Siddim—that is, the Salt Sea.

**14:4** For twelve years they served Chedorlaomer, but in the thirteenth year they rebelled.

**14:5** And in the fourteenth year Chedorlaomer came, along with the kings who were with him, and they struck down the Rephaim in Ashteroth Karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, the Emim in Shaveh Kiriathaim,

**14:6** and the Horites in their hill country of Seir, as far as El Paran, which is beside the wilderness.

**14:7** Then they turned back and came to En Mishpat—that is, Kadesh—and struck all the territory of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites who dwelt in Hazazon Tamar.

**14:8** Then the king of Sodom marched out, with the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela—that is, Zoar—and they arrayed themselves for battle against them in the Valley of Siddim:

**14:9** against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar—four kings against the five.

**14:10** Now the Valley of Siddim was filled with bitumen pits; and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled and fell into them, while those who remained fled to the mountains.

**14:11** So they seized all the possessions of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all their provisions, and departed.

**14:12** They also took Lot, the son of Abram's brother, along with his possessions, and departed, for he was dwelling in Sodom.

**14:13** Then one who had escaped came and reported it to Abram the Hebrew, for he was dwelling by the terebinths of Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshcol and brother of Aner; and they were allies of Abram by covenant.

**14:14** When Abram heard that his kinsman had been taken captive, he mustered his trained men, born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued as far as Dan.

**14:15** He divided his men against them by night, he and his servants, and struck them down, and pursued them as far as Hobah, which lies north of Damascus.

**14:16** So he brought back all the possessions, and also brought back his kinsman Lot and his possessions, as well as the women and the people.

**14:17** And after his return from striking down Chedorlaomer and the kings who were with him, the king of Sodom went out to meet him at the Valley of Shaveh—that is, the King's Valley.

**14:18** And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine; now he was priest of God Most High.

**14:19** And he blessed him and said, "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth;

**14:20** and blessed be God Most High, who has delivered your adversaries into your hand." And he gave him a tenth of everything.

**14:21** Now the king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people, and take the possessions for yourself."

**14:22** But Abram replied to the king of Sodom, "I have lifted up my hand to the LORD, God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth,

**14:23** that I will not take even a thread or a sandal strap, nor anything that belongs to you, lest you say, 'I have made Abram rich'—

**14:24** nothing for myself, except only what the young men have eaten, and the share of the men who went with me—Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre; let them take their share."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — Hebrew "Goiim" literally means "nations" and here designates a region or coalition of peoples.
- **14:15** — Hebrew literally reads "to the left of Damascus," denoting north from an eastward orientation.
- **14:19** — The Hebrew term translated "Possessor" carries the dual sense of "Owner" and "Creator."
- **14:22** — Lifting up the hand to the LORD is a traditional Hebrew idiom for swearing a solemn oath.
