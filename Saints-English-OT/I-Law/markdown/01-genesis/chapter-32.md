## Text

**32:1** And Laban rose early in the morning, and kissed his sons and his daughters and blessed them. Then Laban departed and returned to his place.

**32:2** Now Jacob went on his way, and the angels of God met him.

**32:3** When Jacob saw them, he said, "This is the camp of God." Therefore he called the name of that place Mahanaim.

**32:4** Then Jacob sent messengers before him to Esau his brother in the land of Seir, the country of Edom.

**32:5** And he commanded them, saying, "Thus you shall say to my lord Esau: 'Thus says your servant Jacob: I have sojourned with Laban and tarried until now.

**32:6** I have oxen and donkeys, flocks, and male and female servants; and I have sent to inform my lord, that I may find favor in your sight.'"

**32:7** Then the messengers returned to Jacob, saying, "We came to your brother Esau, and indeed he is coming to meet you, and four hundred men are with him."

**32:8** So Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed; and he divided the people who were with him, along with the flocks, the herds, and the camels, into two camps.

**32:9** And he said, "If Esau comes against the one camp and strikes it, then the remaining camp shall escape."

**32:10** Then Jacob said, "O God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, O LORD who said to me, 'Return to your land and to your kindred, and I will deal well with you,'

**32:11** I am unworthy of all the lovingkindnesses and all the faithfulness which You have shown to Your servant; for with only my staff I crossed this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.

**32:12** Deliver me, I pray, from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau; for I fear him, lest he come and strike me—mother with children.

**32:13** Yet You said, 'I will surely do good to you, and make your descendants as the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered for multitude.'"

**32:14** So he lodged there that night, and took from what he had at hand a present for Esau his brother:

**32:15** two hundred female goats and twenty male goats, two hundred ewes and twenty rams,

**32:16** thirty nursing camels with their colts, forty cows and ten bulls, twenty female donkeys and ten donkey colts.

**32:17** And he delivered them into the hand of his servants, each drove by itself, and said to his servants, "Cross over ahead of me, and put a space between drove and drove."

**32:18** And he commanded the first one, saying, "When Esau my brother meets you and asks you, saying, 'To whom do you belong, and where are you going, and whose are these ahead of you?'

**32:19** then you shall say, 'They belong to your servant Jacob; it is a present sent to my lord Esau, and behold, he also is behind us.'"

**32:20** And he likewise commanded the second, and the third, and all who followed the droves, saying, "After this manner you shall speak to Esau when you find him;

**32:21** and you shall say, 'Behold, your servant Jacob is also behind us.'" For he said, "I will appease him with the present that goes ahead of me, and afterward I will see his face; perhaps he will accept me."

**32:22** So the present crossed over before him, while he himself stayed that night in the camp.

**32:23** And he arose during that night and took his two wives, his two maidservants, and his eleven sons, and crossed the ford of the Jabbok.

**32:24** He took them and brought them across the stream, and sent across all that belonged to him.

**32:25** Then Jacob was left alone; and a Man wrestled with him until the breaking of the dawn.

**32:26** Now when He saw that He did not prevail against him, He touched the socket of his hip; and the socket of Jacob's hip was put out of joint as he wrestled with Him.

**32:27** And He said, "Let Me go, for the dawn is breaking." But he said, "I will not let You go unless You bless me."

**32:28** So He said to him, "What is your name?" And he answered, "Jacob."

**32:29** And He said, "No longer shall your name be called Jacob, but Israel; for you have contended with God and with men, and have prevailed."

**32:30** Then Jacob asked Him, saying, "Tell me, I pray, Your name." And He said, "Why is it that you ask for My name?" And He blessed him there.

**32:31** So Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: "For I have seen God face to face, and yet my life has been delivered."

**32:32** And the sun rose upon him as he passed beyond Penuel, and he was limping because of his hip.

**32:33** Therefore the children of Israel do not eat the sinew of the thigh which is upon the socket of the hip, to this day, because He touched the socket of Jacob's hip at the sinew of the thigh.

## Translator Notes

- **32:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, Genesis 32:1 corresponds to 31:55 in most English Bibles, shifting the verse numbering of chapter 32 forward by one.
- **32:3** — Mahanaim is a Hebrew dual form meaning Two Camps, reflecting both the camp of God's angels and Jacob's subsequent division of his own company into two camps.
- **32:21** — The Hebrew text weaves a fourfold play on the word panim, meaning face or presence, across this verse, foreshadowing Jacob's naming of Peniel, Face of God, in verse 31.
- **32:33** — The Hebrew phrase gid hannasheh denotes the sciatic sinew or tendon along the hip socket; note also the Hebrew spelling variation between Peniel in verse 31 and Penuel in verse 32.
