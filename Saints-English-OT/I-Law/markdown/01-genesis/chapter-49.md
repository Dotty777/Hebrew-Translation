## Text

**49:1** And Jacob called his sons and said, "Gather yourselves together, that I may declare to you what shall befall you in the latter days:

**49:2** Assemble and hear, you sons of Jacob, and listen to Israel your father.

**49:3** Reuben, you are my firstborn, my might and the firstfruits of my vigor, preeminent in dignity and preeminent in power.

**49:4** Turbulent as water, you shall not have preeminence, because you went up to your father's bed; then you defiled it—he went up to my couch!

**49:5** Simeon and Levi are brothers; weapons of violence are their swords.

**49:6** May my soul not enter their council, and may my glory not be joined to their assembly; for in their anger they slew a man, and in their self-will they hamstrung an ox.

**49:7** Cursed be their anger, for it is fierce, and their wrath, for it is cruel! I will divide them in Jacob and scatter them in Israel.

**49:8** Judah, you are the one whom your brethren shall praise; your hand shall be upon the neck of your enemies; your father's sons shall bow down before you.

**49:9** Judah is a lion's cub; from the prey, my son, you have gone up. He crouches, he lies down like a lion, and like a lioness—who shall rouse him?

**49:10** The scepter shall not depart from Judah, nor the ruler's staff from between his feet, until Shiloh comes; and to him shall be the obedience of the peoples.

**49:11** Tying his foal to the vine, and his donkey's colt to the choice vine, he washes his garments in wine, and his robe in the blood of grapes.

**49:12** His eyes are darker than wine, and his teeth are whiter than milk.

**49:13** Zebulun shall dwell at the shore of the seas; he shall become a haven for ships, and his border shall be toward Sidon.

**49:14** Issachar is a sturdy donkey, lying down between the sheepfolds.

**49:15** He saw that rest was good and that the land was pleasant; so he bowed his shoulder to bear burdens and became subject to forced labor.

**49:16** Dan shall judge his people as one of the tribes of Israel.

**49:17** Dan shall be a serpent by the way, a viper by the path, that bites the heels of the horse so that its rider falls backward.

**49:18** I have waited for Your salvation, O LORD!

**49:19** Gad—raiders shall raid him, yet he shall raid at their heel.

**49:20** From Asher his bread shall be rich, and he shall yield royal delicacies.

**49:21** Naphtali is a doe let loose; he utters words of beauty.

**49:22** Joseph is a fruitful bough, a fruitful bough beside a spring; his branches climb over the wall.

**49:23** The archers bitterly assailed him; they shot at him and harried him;

**49:24** yet his bow remained steadfast, and the arms of his hands were made agile by the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob—from there is the Shepherd, the Stone of Israel—

**49:25** by the God of your father, who will help you, and by the Almighty, who will bless you with blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lies beneath, blessings of the breasts and of the womb.

**49:26** The blessings of your father have surpassed the blessings of my forefathers, up to the utmost bound of the everlasting hills; may they be upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the head of the one set apart among his brethren.

**49:27** Benjamin is a ravenous wolf: in the morning he devours the prey, and at evening he divides the spoil."

**49:28** All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father spoke to them as he blessed them; he blessed each one according to his own blessing.

**49:29** Then he charged them and said to them, "I am about to be gathered to my people; bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,

**49:30** in the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, which is before Mamre in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought along with the field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial possession.

**49:31** There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife, there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife, and there I buried Leah.

**49:32** The purchase of the field and of the cave that is in it was from the sons of Heth."

**49:33** And when Jacob had finished commanding his sons, he drew his feet up into the bed, breathed his last, and was gathered to his people.

## Translator Notes

- **49:5** — The rare Hebrew noun mekherotehem is most widely understood as their swords or knives, though some ancient interpreters took it as their dwellings or compacts.
- **49:10** — Shiloh is traditionally understood as a messianic title; alternatively, the Hebrew form has been interpreted as the one to whom it belongs or as tribute brought to him.
- **49:19** — The Hebrew verse weaves a fourfold soundplay around the name Gad with the words for raiders, shall raid him, and shall raid.
- **49:26** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads horay, meaning my forefathers or progenitors, whereas some ancient versions read a similar consonantal form meaning ancient mountains.
