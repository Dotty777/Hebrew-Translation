## Text

**20:1** And Abraham journeyed from there toward the land of the Negev, and dwelt between Kadesh and Shur, and sojourned in Gerar.

**20:2** And Abraham said concerning Sarah his wife, "She is my sister." So Abimelech king of Gerar sent and took Sarah.

**20:3** But God came to Abimelech in a dream by night and said to him, "Behold, you are a dead man because of the woman whom you have taken, for she is a man's wife."

**20:4** Now Abimelech had not come near her; and he said, "Lord, will You slay even a righteous nation?

**20:5** Did he not say to me, 'She is my sister'? And she, even she herself, said, 'He is my brother.' In the integrity of my heart and the innocence of my hands I have done this."

**20:6** And God said to him in the dream, "Yes, I too know that in the integrity of your heart you did this, and I Myself also kept you from sinning against Me; therefore I did not permit you to touch her.

**20:7** Now therefore, restore the man's wife, for he is a prophet, and he will pray for you and you shall live. But if you do not restore her, know that you shall surely die, you and all who belong to you."

**20:8** So Abimelech rose early in the morning, and called all his servants, and spoke all these words in their hearing; and the men were exceedingly afraid.

**20:9** Then Abimelech called Abraham and said to him, "What have you done to us? And how have I sinned against you, that you have brought upon me and upon my kingdom a great sin? You have done deeds to me that ought not to be done."

**20:10** And Abimelech said to Abraham, "What did you see, that you have done this thing?"

**20:11** And Abraham said, "Because I thought, 'Surely there is no fear of God in this place, and they will slay me on account of my wife.'

**20:12** And moreover, indeed she is my sister, the daughter of my father, yet not the daughter of my mother; and she became my wife.

**20:13** And it came to pass, when God caused me to wander from my father's house, that I said to her, 'This is the kindness which you shall show to me: at every place where we come, say of me, "He is my brother."'"

**20:14** Then Abimelech took flocks and herds, and male and female servants, and gave them to Abraham; and he restored Sarah his wife to him.

**20:15** And Abimelech said, "Behold, my land is before you; dwell wherever it seems good in your eyes."

**20:16** And to Sarah he said, "Behold, I have given one thousand pieces of silver to your brother; behold, it is a covering of the eyes for you before all who are with you and before all others; thus you are vindicated."

**20:17** So Abraham prayed to God; and God healed Abimelech, and his wife, and his maidservants, so that they bore children.

**20:18** For the LORD had completely closed every womb of the house of Abimelech, on account of Sarah, Abraham's wife.

## Translator Notes

- **20:13** — The Hebrew verb "caused to wander" is grammatically plural with Elohim ("God"), a rare construction when referring to the one true God, possibly reflecting Abraham's manner of speaking before Abimelech.
- **20:16** — "A covering of the eyes" is a Hebrew idiom for a public compensatory gift that removes reproach and silences suspicion; the closing verb denotes being set right or publicly vindicated.
- **20:18** — The Hebrew uses an emphatic infinitive absolute construction (literally "closing, He had closed") to underscore that the LORD had completely shut every womb in Abimelech's household.
