## Text

**10:1** And King Ahasuerus levied a tribute upon the land and the coastlands of the sea.

**10:2** And all the deeds of his authority and his might, and the full account of the greatness of Mordecai, to which the king exalted him, are they not recorded in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia?

**10:3** For Mordecai the Jew was second in rank to King Ahasuerus, great among the Jews and favored by the multitude of his brethren, seeking the good of his people and speaking peace to all his descendants.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The consonantal text writes Ahasuerus defectively without the letter vav, while the traditional reading supplies the standard spelling Ahasuerus. Hebrew mas denotes royal tribute or taxation levied across the mainland and the maritime islands or coastlands.
- **10:2** — The phrase full account renders Hebrew parashah, meaning a detailed statement or exact record, and chronicles renders divrei hayyamim, literally the matters of the days, referring to the official royal annals of Media and Persia.
- **10:3** — Hebrew zera, literally seed, denotes Mordecai's posterity and by extension the whole lineage of his people, while shalom encompasses both peace and enduring well-being.
