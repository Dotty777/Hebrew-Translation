## Text

**6:1** That night sleep fled from the king; so he commanded to bring the book of the records of the chronicles, and they were read before the king.

**6:2** And it was found written that Mordecai had reported concerning Bigthana and Teresh, two of the king's eunuchs, keepers of the threshold, who had sought to lay hands on King Ahasuerus.

**6:3** Then the king asked, "What honor and dignity have been bestowed on Mordecai for this?" And the king's attendants who ministered to him answered, "Nothing has been done for him."

**6:4** And the king asked, "Who is in the court?" Now Haman had come into the outer court of the king's house to speak to the king concerning hanging Mordecai on the gallows which he had prepared for him.

**6:5** The king's attendants said to him, "Behold, Haman is standing in the court." And the king said, "Let him come in."

**6:6** So Haman came in, and the king said to him, "What shall be done for the man whom the king delights to honor?" Now Haman said in his heart, "Whom would the king delight to honor more than myself?"

**6:7** And Haman answered the king, "For the man whom the king delights to honor,

**6:8** let them bring a royal robe which the king has worn, and a horse on which the king has ridden and on whose head a royal crown has been set.

**6:9** And let the robe and the horse be delivered into the hand of one of the king's most noble princes, that they may array the man whom the king delights to honor, and lead him on horseback through the city square, and proclaim before him, 'Thus shall it be done to the man whom the king delights to honor!'"

**6:10** Then the king said to Haman, "Hasten, take the robe and the horse, just as you have spoken, and do so for Mordecai the Jew who sits at the king's gate! Leave nothing undone of all that you have spoken."

**6:11** So Haman took the robe and the horse, arrayed Mordecai, and led him on horseback through the city square, and proclaimed before him, "Thus shall it be done to the man whom the king delights to honor!"

**6:12** Afterward Mordecai returned to the king's gate, while Haman hurried to his house, mourning and with his head covered.

**6:13** When Haman recounted to his wife Zeresh and all his friends everything that had befallen him, his wise men and his wife Zeresh said to him, "If Mordecai, before whom you have begun to fall, is of the seed of the Jews, you shall not prevail against him, but shall surely fall before him."

**6:14** While they were still speaking with him, the king's eunuchs arrived and hastened to bring Haman to the banquet that Esther had prepared.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — "Sleep fled from the king" renders a vivid Hebrew idiom literally meaning "the king's sleep wandered away."
- **6:8** — In the Hebrew syntax, "on whose head a royal crown has been set" modifies the horse, reflecting the ancient Persian custom of adorning the king's steed with a royal crest or diadem.
- **6:13** — "Shall surely fall" reflects the emphatic Hebrew infinitive absolute construction, stressing the certainty of Haman's downfall.
