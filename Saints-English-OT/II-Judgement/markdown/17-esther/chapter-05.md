## Text

**5:1** Now it came to pass on the third day that Esther put on her royal robes and stood in the inner court of the king's palace, facing the king's house, while the king was sitting upon his royal throne in the royal house, facing the entrance of the house.

**5:2** And it came to pass, when the king saw Queen Esther standing in the court, that she obtained favor in his sight; and the king extended to Esther the golden scepter that was in his hand. Then Esther drew near and touched the tip of the scepter.

**5:3** Then the king said to her, "What is your wish, Queen Esther? And what is your request? Up to half the kingdom, it shall be granted to you."

**5:4** And Esther answered, "If it pleases the king, let the king and Haman come today to the banquet that I have prepared for him."

**5:5** Then the king said, "Fetch Haman quickly, to fulfill the word of Esther." So the king and Haman came to the banquet that Esther had prepared.

**5:6** At the banquet of wine the king said to Esther, "What is your petition? It shall be given to you. And what is your request? Even to half the kingdom, it shall be performed."

**5:7** Then Esther answered and said, "My petition and my request is this:

**5:8** If I have found favor in the sight of the king, and if it pleases the king to grant my petition and perform my request, let the king and Haman come to the banquet which I shall prepare for them, and tomorrow I will do according to the king's word."

**5:9** So Haman went out that day joyful and glad of heart; yet when Haman saw Mordecai at the king's gate, and that he neither rose nor stirred before him, Haman was filled with wrath against Mordecai.

**5:10** Nevertheless Haman restrained himself and went to his house; and he sent and brought his friends and his wife Zeresh.

**5:11** Then Haman recounted to them the splendor of his riches, the multitude of his sons, and all the ways in which the king had magnified him and how he had exalted him above the princes and servants of the king.

**5:12** Moreover Haman said, "Even Queen Esther brought no one with the king to the banquet she prepared except myself; and tomorrow also I am invited by her together with the king.

**5:13** Yet all of this avails me nothing, so long as I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king's gate."

**5:14** Then his wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, "Let a gallows be made, fifty cubits high, and in the morning speak to the king that Mordecai may be hanged upon it; then go joyfully with the king to the banquet." And the counsel pleased Haman, so he had the gallows made.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — "Put on her royal robes" translates a concise Hebrew expression literally meaning "put on royalty," referring to royal apparel.
- **5:14** — "Gallows" renders the Hebrew word for "wood" or "tree," denoting in Persian usage a lofty wooden stake or pole for public impalement or hanging, fifty cubits (roughly seventy-five feet) tall.
