## Text

**2:1** After these things, when the wrath of King Ahasuerus had subsided, he remembered Vashti, and what she had done, and what had been decreed against her.

**2:2** Then the king's young men who ministered to him said, "Let fair young virgins be sought for the king.

**2:3** And let the king appoint officers in all the provinces of his kingdom, that they may gather every fair young virgin to Shushan the citadel, to the house of the women, into the custody of Hegai the king's eunuch, keeper of the women; and let their beauty treatments be given them.

**2:4** Then let the maiden who pleases the king reign as queen in place of Vashti." And the thing pleased the king, and he did so.

**2:5** Now there was a certain Jew in Shushan the citadel whose name was Mordecai, the son of Jair, the son of Shimei, the son of Kish, a Benjamite,

**2:6** who had been carried away into exile from Jerusalem with the captives who had been taken into exile with Jeconiah king of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried away.

**2:7** And he had brought up Hadassah, that is, Esther, his uncle's daughter, for she had neither father nor mother. The maiden was lovely in form and fair of countenance; and when her father and mother died, Mordecai took her as his own daughter.

**2:8** So it came to pass, when the king's command and his decree were heard, and when many maidens were gathered to Shushan the citadel into the custody of Hegai, that Esther was taken to the king's palace, into the custody of Hegai, keeper of the women.

**2:9** Now the maiden pleased him, and she won his favor; so he hastened to provide her with her beauty treatments and her portions, and with the seven choice maidservants appointed to be given her from the king's palace; and he moved her and her maidservants to the best place in the house of the women.

**2:10** Esther had not disclosed her people or her kindred, for Mordecai had charged her that she should not disclose it.

**2:11** And every single day Mordecai walked to and fro before the court of the house of the women, to learn of Esther's welfare and what was being done with her.

**2:12** Now when each maiden's turn arrived to go in to King Ahasuerus, after she had been twelve months according to the regulation for the women—for so were the days of their beautification fulfilled: six months with oil of myrrh, and six months with spices and the beauty treatments of the women—

**2:13** in this manner the maiden would go in to the king: whatever she asked for was given her to go with her from the house of the women to the king's palace.

**2:14** In the evening she would go in, and in the morning she would return to the second house of the women, to the custody of Shaashgaz the king's eunuch, keeper of the concubines. She would not go in to the king again unless the king delighted in her and she was summoned by name.

**2:15** Now when the turn came for Esther—the daughter of Abihail, the uncle of Mordecai, who had taken her as his daughter—to go in to the king, she requested nothing except what Hegai the king's eunuch, keeper of the women, advised. And Esther won favor in the sight of all who beheld her.

**2:16** So Esther was taken to King Ahasuerus, to his royal palace, in the tenth month, which is the month of Tebeth, in the seventh year of his reign.

**2:17** And the king loved Esther more than all the other women, and she found grace and favor in his sight more than all the virgins; so he set the royal crown upon her head and made her queen in place of Vashti.

**2:18** Then the king made a great feast for all his princes and his servants, the Feast of Esther; and he proclaimed a holiday for the provinces and gave gifts according to the bounty of the king.

**2:19** And when virgins were gathered a second time, Mordecai was sitting at the king's gate.

**2:20** Now Esther had still not disclosed her kindred or her people, just as Mordecai had charged her; for Esther obeyed the command of Mordecai just as when she was brought up by him.

**2:21** In those days, while Mordecai sat at the king's gate, two of the king's eunuchs, Bigthan and Teresh, keepers of the threshold, became furious and sought to lay hands on King Ahasuerus.

**2:22** And the matter became known to Mordecai, who told it to Queen Esther; and Esther reported it to the king in Mordecai's name.

**2:23** And when the matter was investigated and found to be true, both of them were hanged upon a gallows; and it was written in the book of the chronicles in the presence of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Hebrew text spells this official's name Hege here and Hegai in verses 8 and 15; it is rendered Hegai consistently.
- **2:18** — The Hebrew term hanachah literally denotes a "granting of rest" and may refer either to a public holiday or to a royal remission of taxes and tribute.
- **2:23** — Gallows renders Hebrew ets, literally "tree" or "wooden pole," reflecting the Persian practice of public hanging or impalement on a stake.
