## Text

**4:1** Now Mordecai learned all that had been done, and Mordecai tore his garments and put on sackcloth and ashes; and he went out into the midst of the city and cried out with a great and bitter cry.

**4:2** He went as far as the front of the king's gate, for no one might enter the king's gate clothed in sackcloth.

**4:3** And in every province, wherever the king's command and his decree arrived, there was great mourning among the Jews, with fasting, weeping, and lamentation; sackcloth and ashes were spread as a bed for many.

**4:4** Then Esther's maidens and her eunuchs came and told her, and the queen was deeply distressed; and she sent garments to clothe Mordecai and to remove his sackcloth from him, but he would not accept them.

**4:5** So Esther summoned Hathach, one of the king's eunuchs whom he had appointed to attend her, and gave him a charge to Mordecai, to learn what this was and why it was.

**4:7** And Mordecai told him all that had befallen him, and the exact sum of silver that Haman had promised to weigh out into the king's treasuries for the Jews, to destroy them.

**4:8** He also gave him a copy of the written decree that had been issued in Shushan for their annihilation, to show Esther and explain to her, and to charge her to go in to the king to make supplication to him and to plead before him on behalf of her people.

**4:9** So Hathach came and reported to Esther the words of Mordecai.

**4:10** Then Esther spoke to Hathach and sent him back with a charge to Mordecai:

**4:11** "All the servants of the king and the people of the king's provinces know that for any man or woman who enters the inner court to the king without being summoned, there is but one law—to be put to death—except the one to whom the king holds out the golden scepter, that he may live. Yet as for me, I have not been summoned to come to the king these thirty days."

**4:12** So they reported Esther's words to Mordecai.

**4:13** Then Mordecai told them to reply to Esther: "Do not suppose within yourself that in the king's house you will escape any more than all the other Jews.

**4:14** For if you remain wholly silent at this time, respite and deliverance will arise for the Jews from another quarter, while you and your father's house shall perish. And who knows whether it is for a time such as this that you have attained to royalty?"

**4:15** Then Esther told them to reply to Mordecai:

**4:16** "Go, assemble all the Jews who are found in Shushan, and fast on my behalf; do not eat and do not drink for three days, night or day. I and my maidens also will fast in like manner. Then I will go in to the king, though it is contrary to the law; and if I perish, I perish."

**4:17** So Mordecai departed and did according to all that Esther had commanded him.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The written text (ketiv) spells the verb with an extra vowel letter, while the read form (qere) preserves the standard feminine plural form meaning "and they came."
- **4:7** — The written text (ketiv) uses a double-yod spelling for the gentilic plural, while the read form (qere) gives the standard plural form "for the Jews."
- **4:14** — "Another quarter" translates Hebrew "another place," long recognized as a discreet allusion to divine providence in a book that never explicitly mentions the name of God.
