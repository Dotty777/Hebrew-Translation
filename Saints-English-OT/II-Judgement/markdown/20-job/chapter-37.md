## Text

**37:1** "At this my heart also quakes, and leaps out of its place.

**37:2** Listen closely to the raging of His voice, and the rumbling that goes forth from His mouth.

**37:3** Beneath all the heavens He lets it loose, and His lightning to the corners of the earth.

**37:4** In its wake a voice roars; He thunders with His voice of majesty, and He does not hold them back when His voice is heard.

**37:5** God thunders wondrously with His voice; He does great things that we cannot comprehend.

**37:6** For to the snow He says, 'Fall upon the earth,' and to the shower of rain, even the downpour of His mighty rains.

**37:7** He puts a seal upon the hand of every man, that all mankind may know His work.

**37:8** Then the beasts withdraw into their lairs, and abide in their dens.

**37:9** From its chamber comes the whirlwind, and cold from the scattering winds.

**37:10** By the breath of God He gives ice, and the breadth of the waters is frozen solid.

**37:11** He also loads the thick cloud with moisture; He scatters the cloud of His lightning.

**37:12** And it wheels round and round by His guidance, that they may do all that He commands them upon the face of the habitable earth.

**37:13** Whether for correction, or for His earth, or for lovingkindness, He brings it to pass.

**37:14** Give ear to this, O Job; stand still and contemplate the wondrous works of God.

**37:15** Do you know how God lays His charge upon them, and causes the lightning of His cloud to shine forth?

**37:16** Do you know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of Him who is complete in knowledge,

**37:17** you whose garments are hot when the earth lies still beneath the south wind?

**37:18** Can you, with Him, spread out the skies, firm as a cast mirror?

**37:19** Make known to us what we shall say to Him; we cannot draw up our case because of darkness.

**37:20** Shall it be told Him that I wish to speak? Would any man ask to be swallowed up?

**37:21** And now men cannot look upon the light when it shines brightly in the skies, after a wind has passed by and cleared them.

**37:22** From the north comes golden splendor; upon God is awesome majesty.

**37:23** As for the Almighty, we cannot find Him out; He is exalted in power, and He does not violate justice and abundant righteousness.

**37:24** Therefore men fear Him; He has no regard for any who are wise of heart."

## Translator Notes

- **37:6** — Literally "Be upon the earth," using an Aramaizing imperative form of the verb "to be" to command the snow to fall.
- **37:9** — "Its chamber" refers to the storehouse of the southern tempest (compare 9:9), and "the scattering winds" (mezarim) denote the cold northern winds that disperse clouds.
- **37:20** — The Hebrew forms a double rhetorical question underscoring the danger of demanding an audience with God; the second clause may also be read conditionally, "If a man speaks, surely he will be swallowed up."
- **37:24** — The verse closes Elihu's speech with a Hebrew wordplay between "fear Him" (yere'uhu) and "has no regard" (lo-yir'eh, literally "does not look upon").
