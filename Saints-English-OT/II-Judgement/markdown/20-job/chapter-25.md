## Text

**25:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**25:2** "Dominion and dread rest with Him; He establishes peace in His lofty heights.

**25:3** Is there any count of His hosts? And upon whom does His light not arise?

**25:4** How then can mortal man be justified with God, and how can one born of a woman be pure?

**25:5** Behold, even the moon does not give brightness, and the stars are not pure in His sight;

**25:6** how much less mortal man, a maggot, and the son of man, a worm!"

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — "Hosts" renders Hebrew gedudim, denoting military troops or heavenly armies marshaled under God's command.
- **25:5** — The rare Hebrew verb ya'ahil means "to shine" or "to give brightness," emphasizing that even the most luminous heavenly bodies appear dim before God's purity.
