## Text

**10:1** My soul abhors my life; I will let loose my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.

**10:2** I will say to God, "Do not condemn me; make known to me why You contend against me."

**10:3** Is it good to You that You should oppress, that You should reject the labor of Your hands, and shine forth upon the counsel of the wicked?

**10:4** Do You have eyes of flesh, or do You see as mortal man sees?

**10:5** Are Your days like the days of a mortal, or Your years like the days of a man,

**10:6** that You seek out my iniquity and search after my sin,

**10:7** though You know that I am not wicked, and there is none who can deliver out of Your hand?

**10:8** Your hands shaped me and fashioned me altogether round about, yet You destroy me.

**10:9** Remember, I pray, that You formed me like clay; and will You turn me back to dust?

**10:10** Have You not poured me out like milk, and congealed me like cheese?

**10:11** You clothed me with skin and flesh, and wove me together with bones and sinews.

**10:12** You have granted me life and lovingkindness, and Your watchful care has guarded my spirit.

**10:13** Yet these things You concealed in Your heart; I know that this was with You.

**10:14** If I sin, You keep watch over me, and You will not acquit me of my iniquity.

**10:15** If I am wicked, woe to me; and even if I am righteous, I cannot lift up my head, being sated with disgrace and beholding my affliction.

**10:16** Should my head lift itself up, You hunt me like a fierce lion, and again You display Your wondrous power against me.

**10:17** You renew Your witnesses against me and multiply Your indignation toward me; relays and a warring host are against me.

**10:18** Why then did You bring me forth from the womb? Would that I had breathed my last, and no eye had ever seen me!

**10:19** I would have been as though I had never existed, carried from the womb to the grave.

**10:20** Are not my days few? Cease then, and turn away from me, that I may take a little comfort,

**10:21** before I depart and do not return, to the land of darkness and the shadow of death,

**10:22** a land of deep gloom like thick darkness, of the shadow of death and without any order, where even the shining is like thick darkness.

## Translator Notes

- **10:15** — The Hebrew form rendered 'beholding' may be read either as a verbal adjective parallel to 'sated' or as an imperative addressed to God, 'behold my affliction.'
- **10:17** — The phrase 'relays and a warring host' reflects the Hebrew hendiadys for waves of fresh troops arrayed in succession against Job.
- **10:20** — Follows the qere imperatives 'cease' and 'turn away'; the ketiv reads third-person verb forms, 'let it cease' and 'let Him turn away.'
- **10:22** — The Hebrew term translated 'the shadow of death' denotes deepest gloom, and the final clause literally reads 'and it shines forth like thick darkness.'
