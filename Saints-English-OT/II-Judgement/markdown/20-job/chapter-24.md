## Text

**24:1** "Why are times not laid up by the Almighty, and those who know Him do not behold His days?

**24:2** Men remove landmarks; they seize flocks by force and pasture them.

**24:3** They drive away the donkey of the fatherless; they take the widow's ox in pledge.

**24:4** They thrust the needy from the roadway; all the poor of the land hide themselves together.

**24:5** Behold, like wild donkeys in the wilderness, they go forth to their toil, seeking diligently for food; the desert plain yields them bread for their children.

**24:6** They reap his fodder in the field, and they glean the vineyard of the wicked.

**24:7** They spend the night naked, without clothing, and have no covering in the cold.

**24:8** They are drenched by the mountain downpour, and for want of shelter they embrace the rock.

**24:9** Some snatch the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge against the poor.

**24:10** Naked they go about without clothing, and though hungry they carry the sheaves.

**24:11** Between their walls they press out oil; they tread the winepresses, yet they thirst.

**24:12** From out of the city men groan, and the soul of the wounded cries out; yet God does not impute wrong.

**24:13** They are among those who rebel against the light; they do not know its ways, nor abide in its paths.

**24:14** At the break of light the murderer rises; he slays the poor and needy, and in the night he becomes like a thief.

**24:15** The eye of the adulterer also watches for the twilight, saying, 'No eye shall behold me,' and he sets a veil over his face.

**24:16** In the darkness they dig through houses; by day they shut themselves in; they do not know the light.

**24:17** For to all of them alike, morning is as the shadow of death, for each knows well the terrors of the shadow of death.

**24:18** Swift is he upon the face of the waters; their portion is accursed in the land; he does not turn toward the way of the vineyards.

**24:19** Drought and heat snatch away the snow waters; so does Sheol those who have sinned.

**24:20** The womb forgets him; the worm feasts sweetly on him; he shall be remembered no more, and unrighteousness shall be broken like a tree.

**24:21** He preys upon the barren woman who bears no child, and he does no good to the widow.

**24:22** Yet God drags away the mighty by His power; when He rises up, none is sure of life.

**24:23** He grants him security, and he leans upon it; yet His eyes are upon their ways.

**24:24** They are exalted for a little while, and then are no more; they are brought low, gathered in like all others, and cut off like the heads of grain.

**24:25** Now if it is not so, who will prove me a liar, and reduce my speech to nothing?"

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — Ketiv/Qere: the written consonantal text (ketiv) reads a form suggesting "they cause to reap," whereas the traditional read text (qere) translated here reads the standard Qal verb "they reap."
- **24:12** — The Masoretic vocalization reads "men" rather than "the dying," and is followed directly here.
- **24:22** — The Hebrew verb rendered "drags away" can also mean "prolongs," and the final word for "life" displays an Aramaizing plural ending characteristic of Job's poetry.
