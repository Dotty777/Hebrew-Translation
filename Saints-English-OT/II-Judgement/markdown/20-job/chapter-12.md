## Text

**12:1** Then Job answered and said:

**12:2** "Truly you are the people, and wisdom shall die with you!

**12:3** Yet I also have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you. Indeed, who does not know such things as these?

**12:4** I have become a laughingstock to my friend—one who called upon God and He answered him; the righteous and blameless man is a laughingstock.

**12:5** In the mind of one who is at ease, a lamp is held in contempt; it awaits those whose feet are slipping.

**12:6** The tents of robbers prosper in peace, and those who provoke God dwell in security—even those who carry their god in their own hand.

**12:7** Yet ask now the beasts, and they shall instruct you; and the birds of the heavens, and they shall tell you;

**12:8** or speak to the earth, and it shall teach you; and the fish of the sea shall declare it to you.

**12:9** Who among all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has wrought this,

**12:10** in whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the breath of all human flesh?

**12:11** Does not the ear try words, just as the palate tastes its food?

**12:12** With the aged is wisdom, and in length of days is understanding.

**12:13** With Him reside wisdom and might; to Him belong counsel and understanding.

**12:14** Behold, He tears down, and it shall not be rebuilt; He shuts a man in, and it shall not be opened.

**12:15** Behold, He holds back the waters, and they dry up; He sends them forth, and they overturn the earth.

**12:16** With Him are strength and sound wisdom; both the one led astray and the one who leads astray belong to Him.

**12:17** He leads counselors away stripped, and turns judges into fools.

**12:18** He loosens the bonds of kings and binds a waistcloth about their loins.

**12:19** He leads priests away stripped, and overthrows the mighty ones.

**12:20** He deprives the trusted of speech, and takes away the discernment of the elders.

**12:21** He pours out contempt upon nobles, and loosens the belt of the strong.

**12:22** He uncovers deep things out of darkness, and brings forth the shadow of death into light.

**12:23** He makes nations great, and destroys them; He spreads nations abroad, and leads them away.

**12:24** He takes away the understanding of the chiefs of the people of the earth, and causes them to wander in a pathless wasteland.

**12:25** They grope in darkness without light, and He makes them stagger like a drunken man."

## Translator Notes

- **12:5** — Translated according to the Masoretic vocalization meaning 'a lamp' or 'torch'; an alternative consonantal division yields 'contempt for misfortune.'
- **12:6** — The final clause is compact in Hebrew and may mean either 'those who carry their god in their own hand' (trusting in their own might) or 'those into whose hand God brings provision.'
- **12:9** — This is the sole occurrence of the covenant name 'the LORD' in the poetic dialogues of Job, where the titles 'God' and 'the Almighty' otherwise predominate.
