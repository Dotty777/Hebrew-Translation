## Text

**31:1** I made a covenant with my eyes; why then should I gaze upon a virgin?

**31:2** For what is the portion allotted by God from above, and the heritage of the Almighty from on high?

**31:3** Is there not calamity for the unjust, and misfortune for those who practice iniquity?

**31:4** Does not He Himself see my ways, and count every one of my steps?

**31:5** If I have walked with falsehood, or my foot has hastened toward deceit,

**31:6** let Him weigh me in just balances, that God may know my integrity.

**31:7** If my step has turned aside from the way, and my heart has walked after my eyes, or if any stain has clung to my hands,

**31:8** then let me sow and another eat, and let my harvest be uprooted.

**31:9** If my heart has been enticed by a woman, or if I have lain in wait at my neighbor's door,

**31:10** then let my wife grind for another, and let other men bow down over her.

**31:11** For that would be a heinous wickedness; indeed, it would be an iniquity deserving of judgment.

**31:12** For that is a fire that consumes down to Abaddon, and would uproot all my increase.

**31:13** If I have rejected the cause of my manservant or my maidservant when they contended with me,

**31:14** what then shall I do when God rises up? And when He calls to account, what shall I answer Him?

**31:15** Did not He who made me in the womb also make him? Did not the same One fashion us in the womb?

**31:16** If I have kept the poor from their desire, or caused the eyes of the widow to fail,

**31:17** or eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless did not eat of it—

**31:18** for from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and from my mother's womb I have guided the widow—

**31:19** if I have seen anyone perishing for lack of clothing, or the needy with no covering,

**31:20** if his loins have not blessed me, and he was not warmed by the fleece of my sheep;

**31:21** if I have raised my hand against the fatherless because I saw support for me in the gate,

**31:22** then let my shoulder fall from my shoulder blade, and let my arm be broken from its socket.

**31:23** For calamity from God was a dread to me, and before His majesty I could not endure.

**31:24** If I have made gold my hope, or said to fine gold, "You are my confidence";

**31:25** if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because my hand had acquired abundance;

**31:26** if I have gazed at the sun when it shone, or the moon advancing in splendor,

**31:27** so that my heart was secretly enticed, and my hand kissed my mouth,

**31:28** this also would be an iniquity deserving of judgment, for I would have denied God above.

**31:29** If I have rejoiced at the calamity of him who hated me, or exulted when evil overtook him—

**31:30** indeed, I have not allowed my mouth to sin by asking for his life with a curse—

**31:31** if the men of my tent have not said, "Who is there who has not been satisfied with his meat?"

**31:32** No sojourner had to lodge in the street; I opened my doors to the traveler.

**31:33** If I have covered my transgressions as Adam did, by hiding my iniquity in my bosom,

**31:34** because I feared the great multitude, and the contempt of the families terrified me, so that I kept silent and did not go out of the door—

**31:35** Oh, that I had one to hear me! Behold, here is my signature—let the Almighty answer me! And oh, that I had the indictment my adversary has written!

**31:36** Surely I would carry it upon my shoulder; I would bind it on myself like a crown.

**31:37** I would declare to Him the number of my steps; like a prince I would approach Him.

**31:38** If my own ground cries out against me, and all its furrows weep together;

**31:39** if I have eaten its yield without payment, or caused the life of its owners to expire,

**31:40** then let thorns spring up instead of wheat, and foul weeds instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.

## Translator Notes

- **31:11** — The read form (Qere) aligns the gender of each pronoun with its predicate noun, "heinous wickedness" and "iniquity," whereas the written form (Ketiv) reverses their genders.
- **31:12** — Hebrew Abaddon means "Destruction" and is used poetically in Job for the realm of ruin and the grave.
- **31:33** — The Hebrew phrase translated "as Adam did" may refer specifically to the first man hiding his sin in Genesis 3, or more generally "as mortals do."
