## Text

**29:1** Then Job once more took up his discourse and said:

**29:2** "Oh, that I were as in the months gone by, as in the days when God kept watch over me,

**29:3** when His lamp shone over my head, and by His light I walked through the darkness;

**29:4** as I was in the days of my prime, when the intimate counsel of God was over my tent,

**29:5** when the Almighty was still with me, and my children were round about me;

**29:6** when my footsteps were washed in cream, and the rock poured out streams of oil beside me!

**29:7** When I went forth to the gate up by the city, and prepared my seat in the open square,

**29:8** the young men saw me and withdrew, and the aged rose up and stood;

**29:9** princes restrained their words, and laid a hand upon their mouth;

**29:10** the voice of nobles was stilled, and their tongue clung to the roof of their mouth.

**29:11** When the ear heard, it called me blessed, and when the eye saw, it gave testimony for me,

**29:12** because I delivered the afflicted who cried for help, and the fatherless who had no helper.

**29:13** The blessing of the one who was perishing came upon me, and I made the heart of the widow sing for joy.

**29:14** Righteousness I put on, and it arrayed me; like a robe and a turban was my justice.

**29:15** I was eyes for the blind, and feet for the lame was I.

**29:16** I was a father to the needy, and the cause of one whom I did not know I searched out.

**29:17** I shattered the fangs of the unrighteous, and forced the prey from his teeth.

**29:18** Then I said, 'I shall expire in my nest, and like the sand I shall multiply my days;

**29:19** my root lies open to the waters, and the dew abides all night upon my bough;

**29:20** my glory remains fresh with me, and my bow is ever renewed in my hand.'

**29:21** To me they listened and waited, and remained silent for my counsel.

**29:22** After my word they did not speak again, and my speech dropped gently upon them.

**29:23** They waited for me as for rain, and opened their mouths wide as for the latter rain.

**29:24** When I smiled upon them, they did not believe it, and the light of my countenance they did not cast down.

**29:25** I chose their way and sat as chief; I dwelt like a king among the troops, as one who comforts the mourners."

## Translator Notes

- **29:4** — "Prime" translates the Hebrew word for "autumn," the harvest season used figuratively for the fruitful maturity of Job's former life; "intimate counsel" denotes close, trusted fellowship.
- **29:18** — The Hebrew word rendered "sand" is a common emblem of vast abundance, though an ancient Jewish tradition also interprets it as the legendary phoenix in light of the parallel mention of a "nest."
- **29:24** — The clause "they did not believe it" may indicate either that they could scarcely believe Job's gracious favor or that he smiled upon them to reassure them when they lacked confidence.
