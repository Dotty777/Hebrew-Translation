## Text

**1:1** There was a man in the land of Uz named Job, and that man was blameless and upright, fearing God and turning away from evil.

**1:2** And seven sons and three daughters were born to him.

**1:3** His livestock numbered seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred female donkeys, along with a very great household of servants; so that this man was the greatest of all the people of the East.

**1:4** Now his sons would go and hold a feast in the house of each one on his own day, and they would send and invite their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.

**1:5** And it came to pass, when the days of feasting had completed their round, that Job would send and consecrate them; and he would rise early in the morning and offer burnt offerings according to the number of them all. For Job said, "Perhaps my sons have sinned and cursed God in their hearts." Thus Job did continually.

**1:6** Now there came a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan also came among them.

**1:7** And the LORD said to Satan, "From where do you come?" Then Satan answered the LORD and said, "From roaming across the earth, and from walking back and forth in it."

**1:8** Then the LORD said to Satan, "Have you considered My servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth, a blameless and upright man, fearing God and turning away from evil?"

**1:9** So Satan answered the LORD and said, "Does Job fear God for nothing?

**1:10** Have You not put a hedge around him, around his household, and around all that belongs to him on every side? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his livestock have spread abroad in the land.

**1:11** Yet stretch out Your hand now and touch all that belongs to him, and surely he will curse You to Your face!"

**1:12** And the LORD said to Satan, "Behold, all that he has is in your hand; only upon him do not stretch out your hand." So Satan went out from the presence of the LORD.

**1:13** Now there came a day when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their firstborn brother.

**1:14** And a messenger came to Job and said, "The oxen were plowing and the female donkeys were grazing beside them,

**1:15** when the Sabeans swept down and carried them off; and they struck down the servants with the edge of the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you."

**1:16** While he was still speaking, another came and said, "The fire of God fell from heaven and burned among the sheep and the servants and consumed them, and I alone have escaped to tell you."

**1:17** While he was still speaking, another came and said, "The Chaldeans formed three companies, raided the camels and carried them off, and struck down the servants with the edge of the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you."

**1:18** While he was still speaking, yet another came and said, "Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their firstborn brother,

**1:19** and behold, a mighty wind came from across the wilderness and struck the four corners of the house, so that it collapsed upon the young people, and they died; and I alone have escaped to tell you."

**1:20** Then Job arose, tore his robe, and shaved his head; and he fell to the ground and worshiped.

**1:21** And he said, "Naked I came forth from my mother's womb, and naked I shall return there. The LORD has given, and the LORD has taken away; may the name of the LORD be blessed."

**1:22** In all this Job did not sin, nor did he ascribe any wrong to God.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — Cursed translates a Hebrew verb literally meaning blessed, a reverent euphemism used here and in 1:11, 2:5, and 2:9 to avoid placing the word curse directly beside God.
- **1:6** — Satan translates the Hebrew title meaning the Adversary or the Accuser, who appears in the heavenly council among the sons of God.
- **1:10** — Translated according to the Masoretic Qere reading the full spelling of the masculine pronoun you; the Ketiv preserves a shorter orthographic spelling with the same meaning.
