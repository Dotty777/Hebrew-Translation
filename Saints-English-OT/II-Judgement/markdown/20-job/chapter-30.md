## Text

**30:1** But now men younger than I in days laugh at me, whose fathers I disdained to put alongside the dogs of my flock.

**30:2** Indeed, of what use to me was the strength of their hands, men in whom mature vigor had perished?

**30:3** Gaunt from want and famine, they gnaw the dry earth by night, in waste and desolation.

**30:4** They pluck saltwort among the bushes, and the roots of the broom tree are their food.

**30:5** They are driven out from the midst of men; people shout after them as after a thief.

**30:6** They must dwell in the clefts of the ravines, in holes of the earth and among the crags.

**30:7** Among the bushes they bray; beneath the nettles they huddle together.

**30:8** Sons of fools, indeed sons of men without a name, they have been scourged out of the land.

**30:9** And now I have become their mocking song; yes, I am a byword to them.

**30:10** They abhor me, they keep far from me, and they do not hold back from spitting in my face.

**30:11** Because He has loosed my bowstring and afflicted me, they have cast off the bridle before my face.

**30:12** At my right hand the rabble rise up; they push away my feet, and build up against me their ways of destruction.

**30:13** They tear up my path; they hasten my ruin, though they have no helper.

**30:14** They come as through a wide breach; amid the ruin they roll on.

**30:15** Terrors are turned upon me; they pursue my honor like the wind, and my prosperity has passed away like a cloud.

**30:16** And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction have seized me.

**30:17** The night pierces my bones within me, and my gnawing pains take no rest.

**30:18** By great force my garment is disfigured; it binds me tightly like the collar of my tunic.

**30:19** He has thrown me down into the mire, and I am likened to dust and ashes.

**30:20** I cry out to You, yet You do not answer me; I stand up, and You only look upon me.

**30:21** You have turned cruel to me; with the might of Your hand You oppose me.

**30:22** You lift me up to the wind and cause me to ride upon it; You melt away my substance.

**30:23** For I know that You will return me to death, to the house of assembly for all the living.

**30:24** Yet does not a man amid a heap of ruins stretch out his hand, or in his calamity therefore cry out for help?

**30:25** Did I not weep for him whose day was hard? Was not my soul grieved for the needy?

**30:26** Yet when I looked for good, evil came; and when I waited for light, thick darkness came.

**30:27** My inward parts boil and are not still; days of affliction have come to meet me.

**30:28** I go about in gloom, without the sun; I rise up in the assembly and cry for help.

**30:29** A brother I have become to jackals, and a companion to ostriches.

**30:30** My skin grows black and peels from me, and my bones burn with parching heat.

**30:31** My lyre has been turned to mourning, and my pipe to the sound of those who weep.

## Translator Notes

- **30:11** — The read form (Qere) has "my bowstring" or "my cord," while the written form (Ketiv) has "His bowstring"; both convey that God has stripped Job of strength and opened him to humiliation.
- **30:22** — The read form (Qere) is tushiyyah, meaning "substance," "security," or "soundness," whereas the written form (Ketiv) is commonly understood as "storm" or "crashing tempest."
- **30:24** — The Hebrew phrasing is terse and difficult; it may depict a ruined person instinctively reaching out for help in disaster, or affirm that one does not strike a heap of ruins when it cries out.
