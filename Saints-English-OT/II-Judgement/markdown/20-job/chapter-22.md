## Text

**22:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

**22:2** "Can a man be of benefit to God? Surely the prudent man is of benefit to himself.

**22:3** Is it a delight to the Almighty that you are righteous, or is it any gain that you make your ways perfect?

**22:4** Is it because of your reverence that He reproves you, and enters into judgment with you?

**22:5** Is not your wickedness abundant, with no end to your iniquities?

**22:6** For you have taken pledges from your brethren without cause, and stripped the garments from the naked.

**22:7** You have not given the weary water to drink, and you have withheld bread from the hungry.

**22:8** As for the man of might, the land belongs to him, and the honored man dwells in it.

**22:9** Widows you have sent away empty-handed, and the arms of the fatherless have been crushed.

**22:10** Therefore snares are all around you, and sudden dread terrifies you,

**22:11** or darkness, so that you cannot see, and a flood of waters covers you.

**22:12** Is not God in the height of heaven? Behold the loftiest stars, how high they are!

**22:13** Yet you say, 'What does God know? Can He judge through the thick darkness?

**22:14** Clouds are a covering for Him, so that He does not see, and He walks about on the vault of heaven.'

**22:15** Will you keep to the ancient path that men of iniquity have trodden,

**22:16** who were snatched away before their time, whose foundation was swept away by a river,

**22:17** who said to God, 'Depart from us!' and, 'What can the Almighty do to them?'

**22:18** Yet He filled their houses with good things; but the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

**22:19** The righteous see it and rejoice, and the innocent deride them:

**22:20** 'Surely our adversaries are cut off, and the fire has consumed their abundance!'

**22:21** Now be reconciled with Him and be at peace; in this way good shall come to you.

**22:22** Accept, please, instruction from His mouth, and set His words in your heart.

**22:23** If you return to the Almighty, you shall be built up, if you remove unrighteousness far from your tent.

**22:24** Lay your precious gold in the dust, and the gold of Ophir among the stones of the brooks;

**22:25** then the Almighty shall be your precious gold, and your choicest silver.

**22:26** For then you shall delight yourself in the Almighty, and lift up your countenance to God.

**22:27** You shall make supplication to Him, and He shall hear you, and you shall fulfill your vows.

**22:28** You shall also decree a matter, and it shall be established for you; and light shall shine upon your ways.

**22:29** When men are brought low, you shall say, 'There is lifting up!' for He saves him whose eyes are downcast.

**22:30** He shall deliver even one who is not innocent; indeed, he shall be delivered by the purity of your hands."

## Translator Notes

- **22:17** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts from first-person plural ("from us") to third-person plural ("to them") within the quoted speech of the wicked.
- **22:25** — The Hebrew noun echoes the word for precious gold or gold ore in verse twenty-four, creating a wordplay that presents the Almighty Himself as Job's true treasure.
- **22:30** — The expression "one who is not innocent" translates a rare Hebrew negative prefix found also in the name Ichabod.
