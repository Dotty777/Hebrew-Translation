## Text

**32:1** Then these three men ceased from answering Job, for he was righteous in his own eyes.

**32:2** Then the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, was kindled; against Job his wrath burned, because he justified himself rather than God.

**32:3** Against his three friends also his wrath burned, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job.

**32:4** Now Elihu had waited to speak with Job, because they were older in days than he.

**32:5** When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, his wrath was kindled.

**32:6** So Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said: "I am young in days, and you are aged; therefore I held back and was afraid to declare my opinion to you.

**32:7** I said, 'Days should speak, and a multitude of years should teach wisdom.'

**32:8** Yet there is a spirit in mortal man, and the breath of the Almighty gives them understanding.

**32:9** It is not the great who are wise, nor do the aged understand justice.

**32:10** Therefore I say, 'Listen to me; I also will declare my opinion.'

**32:11** Behold, I waited for your words; I listened for your reasonings, while you searched out words.

**32:12** Indeed, I gave you my full attention, and behold, there was none among you who refuted Job, or who answered his words.

**32:13** Lest you say, 'We have found wisdom; God will vanquish him, not man.'

**32:14** Now he has not directed his words against me, and I will not reply to him with your speeches.

**32:15** They are dismayed and answer no more; words have departed from them.

**32:16** And shall I wait, because they do not speak, because they stand still and answer no more?

**32:17** I also will answer my part; I too will declare my opinion.

**32:18** For I am full of words; the spirit within me constrains me.

**32:19** Behold, my belly is like wine that has not been opened; like new wineskins, it is about to burst.

**32:20** Let me speak, that I may find relief; I will open my lips and answer.

**32:21** Let me not show partiality to any man, nor give flattering titles to any mortal;

**32:22** for I do not know how to flatter, else my Maker would soon take me away."

## Translator Notes

- **32:9** — Hebrew rabbim can mean either the great or those of many years, standing in parallel with the aged in the second line.
- **32:19** — New wineskins refers to freshly filled skins strained to the bursting point by newly fermenting wine.
- **32:21** — Show partiality translates the Hebrew idiom lift up the face of a man; give flattering titles renders a verb meaning to address with honorific surnames.
