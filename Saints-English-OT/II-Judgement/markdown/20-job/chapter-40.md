## Text

**40:1** And the LORD answered Job and said,

**40:2** "Shall a faultfinder contend with the Almighty? Let him who reproves God answer it."

**40:3** Then Job answered the LORD and said,

**40:4** "Behold, I am of small account; what shall I reply to You? I have laid my hand upon my mouth.

**40:5** Once I have spoken, and I will not answer; yes, twice, and I will add no more."

**40:6** Then the LORD answered Job out of the tempest and said,

**40:7** "Gird up your loins now like a man; I will question you, and you shall make it known to Me.

**40:8** Will you indeed annul My justice? Will you condemn Me that you may be righteous?

**40:9** And do you have an arm like God, and can you thunder with a voice like His?

**40:10** Adorn yourself now with majesty and loftiness, and clothe yourself with glory and splendor.

**40:11** Pour forth the overflowings of your wrath, and look upon every proud one and bring him low.

**40:12** Look upon every proud one and humble him, and tread down the wicked in their place.

**40:13** Hide them in the dust together; bind their faces in the hidden place.

**40:14** Then I also will acknowledge to you that your own right hand can save you.

**40:15** Behold now Behemoth, which I made along with you; he eats grass like an ox.

**40:16** Behold now, his strength is in his loins, and his power is in the muscles of his belly.

**40:17** He stiffens his tail like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are knit tightly together.

**40:18** His bones are tubes of bronze; his limbs are like bars of iron.

**40:19** He is the foremost of the ways of God; let Him who made him bring near His sword.

**40:20** Surely the mountains bring forth produce for him, and all the beasts of the field play there.

**40:21** He lies beneath the lotus trees, in the covert of the reed and the marsh.

**40:22** The lotus trees cover him with shade; the willows of the brook surround him.

**40:23** Behold, though a river surges violently, he is not alarmed; he remains confident though the Jordan rushes against his mouth.

**40:24** Can anyone capture him while he watches, or pierce his nose with snares?

**40:25** Can you draw out Leviathan with a fishhook, or press down his tongue with a cord?

**40:26** Can you put a rush cord through his nose, or pierce his jaw with a hook?

**40:27** Will he make many supplications to you? Will he speak gentle words to you?

**40:28** Will he make a covenant with you? Will you take him for a servant forever?

**40:29** Will you play with him as with a bird, or bind him for your maidens?

**40:30** Will partners bargain over him? Will they divide him among the merchants?

**40:31** Can you fill his skin with harpoons, or his head with fishing spears?

**40:32** Lay your hand upon him; remember the battle—you will not do it again."

## Translator Notes

- **40:6** — The Ketiv writes the preposition and noun as a single word, while the Masoretic Qere divides them into two words, min se'arah, meaning out of the tempest or whirlwind.
- **40:15** — Behemoth is an intensive plural of the Hebrew word for beast, designating a creature of immense size and strength.
- **40:19** — The clause literally reads He who made him brings near His sword, indicating that only God can master Behemoth, though some understand it of God equipping the creature with its own weapon.
- **40:25** — Masoretic verses 40:25 through 40:32 correspond to 41:1 through 41:8 in most English versifications; Leviathan denotes a fearsome sea creature.
