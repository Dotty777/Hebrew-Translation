## Text

**19:1** Then Job answered and said:

**19:2** How long will you torment my soul, and crush me with words?

**19:3** These ten times you have put me to shame; you are not ashamed to deal harshly with me.

**19:4** And even if indeed I have erred, my error remains with me.

**19:5** If indeed you magnify yourselves against me, and plead my reproach against me,

**19:6** know then that God has wronged me, and has cast His net around me.

**19:7** Behold, I cry out, "Violence!" yet I am not answered; I cry for help, yet there is no justice.

**19:8** He has walled up my way so that I cannot pass, and He has set darkness upon my paths.

**19:9** He has stripped my glory from me, and taken the crown from my head.

**19:10** He tears me down on every side, and I am gone; He has uprooted my hope like a tree.

**19:11** He has kindled His wrath against me, and He counts me as one of His adversaries.

**19:12** His troops come on together and build up their way against me; they encamp all around my tent.

**19:13** He has put my brethren far from me, and my acquaintances have become wholly estranged from me.

**19:14** My kinsfolk have failed me, and my close friends have forgotten me.

**19:15** Those who dwell in my house and my maidservants count me as a stranger; I have become a foreigner in their sight.

**19:16** I call to my servant, yet he does not answer; with my own mouth I must implore him.

**19:17** My breath is repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to the children of my own body.

**19:18** Even young children despise me; when I rise up, they speak against me.

**19:19** All the men of my counsel abhor me, and those whom I loved have turned against me.

**19:20** My bone clings to my skin and to my flesh, and I have escaped by the skin of my teeth.

**19:21** Have pity on me, have pity on me, O you my friends, for the hand of God has struck me!

**19:22** Why do you pursue me as God does, and are not satisfied with my flesh?

**19:23** Oh, that my words were written down! Oh, that they were inscribed in a book,

**19:24** with an iron pen and lead, carved into the rock forever!

**19:25** Yet as for me, I know that my Redeemer lives, and at the last He shall rise upon the dust;

**19:26** and after my skin has been thus destroyed, yet from my flesh I shall behold God,

**19:27** Whom I shall behold for myself, and my own eyes shall see, and not another—my heart faints within my bosom!

**19:28** If you say, "How shall we pursue him?" since the root of the matter is found in me,

**19:29** fear the sword for yourselves, for wrath brings the punishments of the sword, that you may know there is a judgment.

## Translator Notes

- **19:17** — "The children of my own body" renders a Hebrew idiom meaning "sons of my womb" or "body," referring here to Job's uterine brothers or close household kin.
- **19:25** — "Redeemer" translates the Hebrew goel, the kinsman-redeemer or legal vindicator who defends the rights and honor of a wronged relative.
- **19:26** — The Hebrew preposition in "from my flesh" can signify either "from within my flesh" or "apart from my flesh"; the opening clause is literally "after my skin, they have struck off this."
- **19:28** — The Masoretic text reads first-person "in me" rather than "in him," reflecting Job's vivid shift from reporting his friends' words into his own perspective.
- **19:29** — The qere reads a rare noun form meaning "that there is a judgment," differing slightly in spelling from the ketiv's more common noun form with the same sense.
