## Text

**17:1** My spirit is shattered, my days are extinguished; the graves await me.

**17:2** Surely mockers are with me, and my eye abides upon their bitter provocation.

**17:3** Lay down now a pledge, stand surety for me with Yourself; who is there who will strike hands with me?

**17:4** For You have hidden their heart from understanding; therefore You will not exalt them.

**17:5** He who informs against his friends for a share—even the eyes of his children shall fail.

**17:6** Yet He has set me as a byword of the peoples, and I have become one in whose face men spit.

**17:7** My eye has grown dim because of grief, and all my members are like a shadow.

**17:8** The upright are astonished at this, and the innocent stirs himself up against the godless.

**17:9** Yet the righteous holds fast to his way, and he who has clean hands increases in strength.

**17:10** But come back again, all of you, come now; for I shall not find one wise man among you.

**17:11** My days have passed by, my plans are torn apart, even the cherished desires of my heart.

**17:12** They turn night into day; light is near in the presence of darkness.

**17:13** If I look to Sheol as my house, and spread my bed in the darkness,

**17:14** if I cry to the pit, "You are my father," and to the worm, "My mother and my sister,"

**17:15** where then is my hope? As for my hope, who shall behold it?

**17:16** Will it go down to the bars of Sheol? Shall we rest together in the dust?

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — Striking hands was the ancient gesture of legally guaranteeing a debt or standing as surety for another.
- **17:5** — This terse proverb speaks literally of informing against friends for a share or portion, warning that such treachery brings ruin upon one's own children.
- **17:16** — The Hebrew word rendered "rest" can also be taken as a verb meaning "descend," yielding "Shall we go down together into the dust?"
