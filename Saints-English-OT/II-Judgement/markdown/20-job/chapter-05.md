## Text

**5:1** "Call now—is there anyone who will answer you? And to whom among the holy ones will you turn?

**5:2** For vexation slays the foolish man, and jealousy puts the simple one to death.

**5:3** I myself have seen a fool taking root, yet suddenly I cursed his habitation.

**5:4** His children are far from safety; they are crushed in the gate, and there is none to rescue them.

**5:5** His harvest the hungry devours, taking it even out of the thorns, and a snare pants after their wealth.

**5:6** For affliction does not come forth from the dust, nor does trouble sprout from the soil;

**5:7** but man is born for trouble, even as the sparks mount upward.

**5:8** Yet as for me, I would seek after God, and to God I would entrust my cause—

**5:9** who performs great things beyond searching out, and wondrous deeds without number.

**5:10** He gives rain upon the face of the earth, and sends waters across the open fields,

**5:11** to set the lowly on high, and those who mourn are raised up to deliverance.

**5:12** He thwarts the schemes of the crafty, so that their hands achieve no success.

**5:13** He ensnares the wise in their own craftiness, and the counsel of the wily is brought to a hasty end.

**5:14** By day they encounter darkness, and they grope at noonday as in the night.

**5:15** But He delivers the needy from the sword of their mouth, and from the hand of the mighty.

**5:16** So there is hope for the poor, and injustice shuts its mouth.

**5:17** Behold, blessed is the man whom God reproves; so do not reject the discipline of the Almighty.

**5:18** For He inflicts pain, yet He binds up; He smites, yet His hands heal.

**5:19** In six troubles He will rescue you, and in seven no calamity shall touch you.

**5:20** In famine He will redeem you from death, and in battle from the grasp of the sword.

**5:21** From the lash of the tongue you shall be hidden, and you shall not fear devastation when it comes.

**5:22** At devastation and famine you shall laugh, and do not fear the wild beasts of the earth.

**5:23** For your covenant shall be with the stones of the field, and the wild beasts of the field shall be at peace with you.

**5:24** You shall know that your tent abides in peace; you shall inspect your habitation and miss nothing.

**5:25** You shall also know that your seed shall be numerous, and your descendants like the grass of the earth.

**5:26** You shall come to the grave in full vigor, like a stack of grain brought in at its season.

**5:27** Behold, this we have searched out, and so it is; hear it, and know it for yourself."

## Translator Notes

- **5:5** — Following the Masoretic vocalization tsammim, the noun signifies "a snare" (cf. Job 18:9); ancient versions and some commentators revocalize it to mean "the thirsty."
- **5:7** — Hebrew bene-resheph, literally "sons of flame," is a poetic idiom for rising sparks.
- **5:24** — Hebrew lo techeta, literally "you shall not miss," expresses here the concrete sense of finding nothing missing when inspecting one's dwelling.
