## Text

**16:1** Then Job answered and said:

**16:2** "I have heard many things such as these; troublesome comforters are all of you!

**16:3** Is there any end to words of wind? Or what goads you that you answer?

**16:4** I also could speak as you do, if your soul were in the place of my soul; I could string words together against you, and shake my head at you.

**16:5** I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips would bring relief.

**16:6** If I speak, my pain is not relieved; and if I refrain, what departs from me?

**16:7** Surely now He has worn me out; You have made desolate all my company.

**16:8** You have shriveled me up, and it has become a witness; my leanness rises up against me, and testifies to my face.

**16:9** His wrath has torn me and harried me with hatred; He has gnashed at me with His teeth; my adversary sharpens His eyes against me.

**16:10** They have gaped at me with their mouths; in reproach they have struck my cheeks; together they band themselves against me.

**16:11** God delivers me over to the ungodly, and casts me into the hands of the wicked.

**16:12** I was at ease, yet He shattered me; He seized me by the neck and dashed me to pieces; He also set me up as His target.

**16:13** His archers encompass me; He splits open my kidneys and does not spare; He pours out my gall upon the ground.

**16:14** He breaks through me with breach upon breach; He charges against me like a mighty warrior.

**16:15** I have sewn sackcloth over my skin, and thrust my horn into the dust.

**16:16** My face is flushed from weeping, and upon my eyelids rests the shadow of death,

**16:17** although there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure.

**16:18** O earth, do not cover my blood, and let there be no resting place for my outcry!

**16:19** Even now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high.

**16:20** My friends are my scorners; my eye pours out tears to God.

**16:21** Oh, that one might plead for a man with God, as a son of man pleads for his neighbor!

**16:22** For when a few years have come, I shall go the way from which I shall not return."

## Translator Notes

- **16:7** — The Hebrew shifts abruptly within the verse from speaking about God in the third person ("He has worn me out") to addressing Him directly in the second person ("You have made desolate").
- **16:16** — Follows the Masoretic Qere (plural verb, agreeing grammatically with the plural noun "face"); the Ketiv reads a feminine singular verb form.
- **16:19** — The word rendered "my advocate" is an Aramaizing term meaning "my witness" (parallel to the standard Hebrew word for witness in the first line).
