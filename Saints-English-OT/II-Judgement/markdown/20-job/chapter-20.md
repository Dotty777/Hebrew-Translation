## Text

**20:1** Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

**20:2** "Therefore my anxious thoughts move me to reply, even because of the haste that is within me.

**20:3** I hear a reproof that brings me reproach, and a spirit from my understanding answers me.

**20:4** Do you know this from of old, ever since man was set upon the earth,

**20:5** that the exulting of the wicked is brief, and the joy of the godless is only for a moment?

**20:6** Though his loftiness ascends to the heavens, and his head reaches to the clouds,

**20:7** he shall perish forever like his own dung; those who saw him shall say, 'Where is he?'

**20:8** He shall fly away like a dream, and they shall not find him; he shall be driven away like a vision of the night.

**20:9** The eye that looked upon him shall see him no more, nor shall his place behold him any longer.

**20:10** His children shall seek the favor of the poor, and his own hands shall give back his wealth.

**20:11** His bones are full of his youthful vigor, yet it shall lie down with him in the dust.

**20:12** Though wickedness tastes sweet in his mouth, and he hides it beneath his tongue,

**20:13** though he spares it and will not let it go, keeping it still within his mouth,

**20:14** yet his food is turned in his stomach; it becomes the venom of asps within him.

**20:15** He swallows down riches, and he shall vomit them up again; God shall drive them out of his belly.

**20:16** He shall suck the poison of asps; the tongue of a viper shall slay him.

**20:17** He shall not look upon the streams, the rivers and brooks of honey and curds.

**20:18** He must give back what he toiled for, and shall not swallow it; according to the wealth of his gain, he shall not rejoice.

**20:19** For he has crushed and forsaken the poor; he has seized a house that he did not build.

**20:20** Because he knew no quietness within him, he shall not save anything that he prized.

**20:21** Nothing remained from his devouring; therefore his prosperity shall not endure.

**20:22** In the fullness of his abundance he shall be in distress; every hand of the afflicted shall come against him.

**20:23** To fill his belly, He shall send upon him the fierceness of His wrath, and shall rain it down on him as his food.

**20:24** He shall flee from the weapon of iron, yet a bow of bronze shall pierce him through.

**20:25** It is drawn forth and comes out of his back, and the glittering blade comes out of his gall; terrors fall upon him.

**20:26** Utter darkness is laid up for his treasures; a fire unfanned shall devour him, and it shall consume whatever remains in his tent.

**20:27** The heavens shall uncover his iniquity, and the earth shall rise up against him.

**20:28** The produce of his house shall depart, flowing away in the day of His wrath.

**20:29** This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed to him by God."

## Translator Notes

- **20:23** — The Hebrew word rendered "as his food" can also mean "into his flesh" or "while he eats."
- **20:26** — The Hebrew verb rendered "shall consume" may also be understood as "it shall go ill with" whoever remains in his tent.
- **20:29** — Literally "the heritage of his word," meaning the destiny decreed or appointed for him by God.
