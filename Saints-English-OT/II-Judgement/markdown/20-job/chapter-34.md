## Text

**34:1** Then Elihu answered and said:

**34:2** "Hear my words, you wise men, and give ear to me, you who have knowledge.

**34:3** For the ear examines words, and the palate tastes its food.

**34:4** Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us discern among ourselves what is good.

**34:5** For Job has said, 'I am righteous, yet God has removed my justice.

**34:6** Should I lie against my own right? My wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.'

**34:7** What man is like Job, who drinks up derision like water,

**34:8** who travels in company with workers of iniquity, and walks with wicked men?

**34:9** For he has said, 'A man gains no profit by taking delight in God.'

**34:10** Therefore listen to me, you men of understanding: far be it from God to do wickedness, and from the Almighty to commit iniquity.

**34:11** For He repays a man for his work, and according to a man's way He brings it upon him.

**34:12** Of a truth, God will not act wickedly, nor will the Almighty pervert justice.

**34:13** Who gave Him charge over the earth? And who laid upon Him the entire world?

**34:14** If He should set His heart upon Himself, and gather to Himself His Spirit and His breath,

**34:15** all flesh would expire together, and mankind would return to dust.

**34:16** If you have understanding, hear this; give ear to the sound of my words.

**34:17** Shall one who hates justice govern? And will you condemn Him who is righteous and mighty?

**34:18** Should one say to a king, 'You are worthless,' or to nobles, 'You are wicked'?

**34:19** How much less to Him who shows no partiality to princes, nor favors the rich above the poor? For they are all the work of His hands.

**34:20** In a moment they die; at midnight the people are shaken and pass away, and the mighty are removed without a human hand.

**34:21** For His eyes are upon the ways of a man, and He beholds all his footsteps.

**34:22** There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where workers of iniquity may conceal themselves.

**34:23** For He has no need to examine a man further, that he should go before God in judgment.

**34:24** He shatters mighty men without inquiry, and sets others in their place.

**34:25** Therefore He knows their works; He overthrows them in the night, and they are crushed.

**34:26** He strikes them down as wicked men in the sight of onlookers,

**34:27** because they turned back from following Him, and had no regard for any of His ways,

**34:28** so that they caused the cry of the poor to come before Him, and He hears the cry of the afflicted.

**34:29** When He grants quietness, who then can condemn? And when He hides His face, who can behold Him—whether concerning a nation or a man alike—

**34:30** so that a godless man should not reign, nor those who lay snares for the people.

**34:31** For has anyone said to God, 'I have borne chastisement; I will offend no more;

**34:32** what I do not see, You teach me; if I have done iniquity, I will do so no more'?

**34:33** Should He repay it according to your terms, because you reject it? For you must choose, and not I; therefore declare what you know.

**34:34** Men of understanding will say to me, and every wise man who hears me will say,

**34:35** 'Job speaks without knowledge, and his words are without insight.'

**34:36** Oh, that Job were tried to the very end, because his answers are like those of men of iniquity!

**34:37** For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us, and multiplies his words against God."

## Translator Notes

- **34:6** — Hebrew literally reads "my arrow," used by metonymy for the wound inflicted by an arrow.
- **34:18** — The Masoretic Text uses an interrogative infinitive ("Should one say"), arguing from the respect owed to earthly rulers to the far greater reverence owed to God.
- **34:23** — The Hebrew reads "further" or "again," indicating that God has no need to prolong His examination before bringing a person to judgment.
- **34:36** — The opening Hebrew word is a rare exclamation meaning "Oh, that!" or "My desire is."
