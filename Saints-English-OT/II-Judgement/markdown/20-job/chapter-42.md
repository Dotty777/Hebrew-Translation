## Text

**42:1** And Job answered the LORD and said,

**42:2** "I know that You are able to do all things, and that no purpose can be restrained from You.

**42:3** 'Who is this who conceals counsel without knowledge?' Therefore I have declared what I did not comprehend, things too wondrous for me, which I did not know.

**42:4** 'Listen now, and I will speak; I will ask you, and you shall inform Me.'

**42:5** By the hearing of the ear I had heard of You, but now my eye has seen You.

**42:6** Therefore I loathe myself, and I repent in dust and ashes."

**42:7** Now it came to pass, after the LORD had spoken these words to Job, that the LORD said to Eliphaz the Temanite, "My wrath is kindled against you and against your two companions, for you have not spoken concerning Me what is right, as My servant Job has.

**42:8** And now, take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams, and go to My servant Job, and offer up a burnt offering on your behalf; and My servant Job shall pray for you, for him I will surely accept, lest I deal with you according to your folly, because you have not spoken concerning Me what is right, as My servant Job has."

**42:9** So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as the LORD had spoken to them; and the LORD accepted Job.

**42:10** And the LORD restored the fortunes of Job when he prayed for his friends, and the LORD increased all that belonged to Job twofold.

**42:11** Then all his brothers, and all his sisters, and all who had known him formerly came to him and ate bread with him in his house; and they grieved with him and comforted him over all the calamity that the LORD had brought upon him. And each one gave him a piece of silver, and each one a ring of gold.

**42:12** And the LORD blessed the latter years of Job more than his beginning, and he had fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, one thousand yoke of oxen, and one thousand female donkeys.

**42:13** And he had seven sons and three daughters.

**42:14** He called the name of the first Jemimah, and the name of the second Keziah, and the name of the third Keren-Happuch.

**42:15** And no women were found so fair as the daughters of Job in all the land; and their father gave them an inheritance among their brothers.

**42:16** And Job lived after this one hundred and forty years, and he saw his children and his children's children, four generations.

**42:17** And Job died, aged and full of days.

## Translator Notes

- **42:2** — The ketiv omits the final yod (potentially a defective spelling or second-person form), whereas the qere reads the first-person form "I know."
- **42:4** — In verses three and four, Job cites the LORD's own earlier summons to him (Job 38:2-3; 40:7) before answering in humility.
- **42:6** — The Hebrew verb translated "loathe" lacks an explicit object; it is traditionally understood reflexively ("loathe myself") or as referring to Job's earlier words ("retract").
- **42:10** — The ketiv reads "shevit" and the qere reads "shevut," variant forms of the noun in the Hebrew idiom meaning "to restore the fortunes" or "turn the captivity."
- **42:16** — The ketiv preserves the shorter verb form and the qere the fuller spelling, both conveying "and he saw."
