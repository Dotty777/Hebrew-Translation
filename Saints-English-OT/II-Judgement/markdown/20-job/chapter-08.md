## Text

**8:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**8:2** "How long will you speak these things, and the words of your mouth be like a mighty wind?

**8:3** Does God pervert justice, or does the Almighty pervert righteousness?

**8:4** If your children have sinned against Him, He has delivered them into the hand of their transgression.

**8:5** If you will earnestly seek God and make supplication to the Almighty,

**8:6** if you are pure and upright, surely even now He would rouse Himself for you and restore the habitation of your righteousness.

**8:7** Though your beginning was small, yet your latter end would increase exceedingly.

**8:8** For inquire, please, of the former generation, and give heed to what their fathers have searched out;

**8:9** for we are but of yesterday and know nothing, because our days upon the earth are a shadow.

**8:10** Will they not teach you and tell you, and bring forth words from their heart?

**8:11** Can the papyrus grow up without a marsh? Can the reed grass flourish without water?

**8:12** While it is still in its greenness and not cut down, it withers before any other grass.

**8:13** So are the paths of all who forget God, and the hope of the godless shall perish,

**8:14** whose confidence is broken off, and whose trust is a spider's web.

**8:15** He leans upon his house, but it will not stand; he holds fast to it, but it will not endure.

**8:16** He is fresh and green before the sun, and his shoots spread forth over his garden.

**8:17** His roots are entwined about the stone heap; he looks upon a place of stones.

**8:18** If he is destroyed from his place, then it will deny him, saying, 'I have not seen you.'

**8:19** Behold, this is the joy of his way, and out of the dust others shall spring forth.

**8:20** Behold, God will not cast away the blameless, nor will He uphold the hand of evildoers.

**8:21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter and your lips with shouts of joy.

**8:22** Those who hate you shall be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked shall be no more."

## Translator Notes

- **8:11** — Hebrew gome denotes the papyrus plant and achu refers to marsh reed grass, both of which depend entirely on standing water to thrive.
- **8:17** — The Hebrew verb translated "looks upon" literally means "sees," picturing the plant spreading across and resting upon a stony foundation.
- **8:18** — The verb "is destroyed" is an indefinite active form in Hebrew, literally "if one swallows him up from his place."
