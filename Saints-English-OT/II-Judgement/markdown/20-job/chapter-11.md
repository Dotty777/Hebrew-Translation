## Text

**11:1** Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

**11:2** "Shall a multitude of words go unanswered, and shall a man full of talk be justified?

**11:3** Shall your empty boasts reduce men to silence? And when you mock, shall no one put you to shame?

**11:4** For you have said, 'My teaching is pure, and I am clean in Your sight.'

**11:5** But oh, that God would speak and open His lips against you,

**11:6** and declare to you the hidden depths of wisdom! For sound wisdom is manifold. Know therefore that God exacts of you less than your iniquity deserves.

**11:7** Can you search out the deep things of God? Can you attain to the utmost limit of the Almighty?

**11:8** It is as high as the heights of heaven—what can you do? Deeper than Sheol—what can you know?

**11:9** Its measure is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.

**11:10** If He passes by, imprisons, and summons an assembly, who can turn Him back?

**11:11** For He knows worthless men; He sees iniquity, and will He not take note of it?

**11:12** An empty-headed man will gain understanding when a wild donkey's colt is born a man.

**11:13** If you prepare your heart and stretch out your hands toward Him,

**11:14** if iniquity is in your hand, put it far away, and do not let unrighteousness dwell in your tents—

**11:15** then surely you shall lift up your face without blemish; you shall be steadfast and shall not fear.

**11:16** For you shall forget your trouble; you shall remember it as waters that have passed away.

**11:17** And your life shall rise brighter than the noonday; even its darkness shall be like the morning.

**11:18** You shall be confident, because there is hope; you shall look around you and lie down in safety.

**11:19** You shall rest, and none shall make you afraid; and many shall seek your favor.

**11:20** But the eyes of the wicked shall fail; refuge shall perish from them, and their hope shall be the breathing out of the soul."

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — The Hebrew verb rendered 'exacts of you less than' comes from a root meaning 'to forget' or 'to remit,' expressing that God overlooks part of Job's iniquity.
- **11:12** — The Hebrew pairs a wordplay between 'empty-headed' (literally 'hollow') and 'gain understanding' (literally 'acquire a heart') with a proverb of impossibility.
- **11:17** — The word translated 'darkness' denotes dense gloom, contrasting sharply with the bright morning promised upon repentance.
