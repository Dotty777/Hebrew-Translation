## Text

**13:1** Behold, all this my eye has beheld; my ear has heard and comprehended it.

**13:2** As you know, so I also know; I do not fall short of you.

**13:3** Yet for my part, I would speak to the Almighty, and to reason with God is my desire.

**13:4** But as for you, you are plasterers of falsehood; physicians of no worth are all of you.

**13:5** Oh, that you would remain altogether silent, and that would become your wisdom!

**13:6** Hear now my plea, and give ear to the pleadings of my lips.

**13:7** Will you speak unrighteousness for God, and for Him will you speak deceit?

**13:8** Will you show partiality toward Him, or will you plead the cause of God?

**13:9** Would it go well if He should search you out? Or as one deceives a mortal man, will you deceive Him?

**13:10** Without fail He will rebuke you if in secret you show partiality.

**13:11** Does not His majesty fill you with terror, and His dread fall upon you?

**13:12** Your cherished maxims are proverbs of ashes; your bulwarks are bulwarks of clay.

**13:13** Keep silent before me, and I will speak; then let pass over me whatever may.

**13:14** Why should I take my flesh in my teeth and place my life in the palm of my hand?

**13:15** Behold, though He should slay me, in Him I will hope; yet I will defend my ways before His face.

**13:16** He indeed shall be my salvation, for a godless man shall not come before Him.

**13:17** Hear my speech diligently, and let my declaration be in your ears.

**13:18** Behold now, I have set forth my case in order; I know that I shall be vindicated.

**13:19** Who is there to contend with me? For then I would be silent and breathe my last.

**13:20** Only do not do two things to me, and then I will not conceal myself from Your presence:

**13:21** Put Your hand far away from me, and do not let Your dread terrify me.

**13:22** Then call out, and I will answer; or let me speak, and return an answer to me.

**13:23** How many iniquities and sins are mine? Cause me to know my transgression and my sin.

**13:24** Why do You conceal Your face and reckon me as an enemy to Yourself?

**13:25** Will You terrify a windblown leaf, and will You pursue dry stubble?

**13:26** For You record bitter things against me, and cause me to inherit the iniquities of my youth.

**13:27** You place my feet in the stocks and keep watch over all my paths; You trace a boundary around the soles of my feet.

**13:28** And he wastes away like something rotten, like a garment that a moth has eaten.

## Translator Notes

- **13:12** — The Hebrew word rendered 'bulwarks' literally denotes mounds or shield-bosses, used figuratively here for fragile arguments or defenses.
- **13:15** — The ketiv reads 'not' ('I have no hope' or 'I will not wait'), whereas the qere, followed here, reads 'in Him' ('in Him I will hope').
- **13:27** — Literally 'the roots of my feet,' referring to the soles or footprints of one closely confined and watched.
