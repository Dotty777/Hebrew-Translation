## Text

**4:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

**4:2** "If one ventures a word to you, will you grow weary? Yet who is able to withhold words?

**4:3** Behold, you have instructed many, and you have strengthened feeble hands.

**4:4** Your words have raised up the one who was stumbling, and you have made firm the buckling knees.

**4:5** But now it comes to you, and you grow weary; it reaches you, and you are dismayed.

**4:6** Is not your reverence your assurance, and the blamelessness of your ways your hope?

**4:7** Remember now: who, being innocent, has ever perished, and where have the upright been destroyed?

**4:8** As I have observed, those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap it.

**4:9** By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of His wrath they are consumed.

**4:10** The roar of the lion, and the cry of the fierce lion—the teeth of the young lions are broken out.

**4:11** The strong lion perishes for want of prey, and the whelps of the lioness are scattered abroad.

**4:12** Now a word was stealthily brought to me, and my ear caught a whisper of it.

**4:13** Amid unsettling thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falls upon men,

**4:14** dread befell me, and trembling, which caused all my bones to shudder.

**4:15** Then a spirit glided past my face; the hair of my flesh stood on end.

**4:16** It stood still, yet I could not discern its appearance; a form was before my eyes. There was stillness, and I heard a voice:

**4:17** 'Can mortal man be righteous before God? Can a man be pure before his Maker?

**4:18** Behold, He does not put His trust in His servants, and He charges His angels with error;

**4:19** how much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation rests in the dust, who are crushed before the moth!

**4:20** From morning to evening they are shattered; with no one taking notice, they perish forever.

**4:21** Is not their tent cord plucked up within them? They die, and without wisdom.'"

## Translator Notes

- **4:17** — The Hebrew preposition min in me-Eloah and me-osehu may be read either as "before God" and "before his Maker" or comparatively as "more righteous than God" and "purer than his Maker."
- **4:18** — The Hebrew noun toholah occurs only here in the Old Testament and denotes "error" or "folly."
- **4:21** — Hebrew yitram means "their tent cord" (matching the verb "plucked up" and the dwelling imagery of verse 19), though it can also be understood as "their excellence" or "abundance."
