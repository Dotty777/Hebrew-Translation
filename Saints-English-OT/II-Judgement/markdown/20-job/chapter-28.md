## Text

**28:1** Truly there is a source for silver, and a place for gold which they refine.

**28:2** Iron is taken from the dust, and copper is poured forth from stone.

**28:3** Man sets a bound to darkness, and to every farthest limit he searches out the stone of gloom and the shadow of death.

**28:4** He breaks open a shaft away from where people dwell; forgotten by the foot, far from mankind they hang down and sway to and fro.

**28:5** As for the earth, bread comes forth from it, yet beneath it is overturned as by fire.

**28:6** Its stones are the place of sapphires, and it holds dust of gold.

**28:7** That path no bird of prey knows, nor has the falcon's eye caught sight of it.

**28:8** The proud beasts have not trodden it, nor has the fierce lion passed along it.

**28:9** He puts forth his hand upon the flinty rock; he overturns mountains from their roots.

**28:10** He hews out channels through the rocks, and his eye beholds every precious thing.

**28:11** He binds up the streams from seeping, and brings what is hidden forth to light.

**28:12** Yet wisdom—where shall it be found? And where is the place of understanding?

**28:13** Mortal man does not know its worth, nor is it found in the land of the living.

**28:14** The deep says, "It is not within me," and the sea says, "It is not with me."

**28:15** Fine gold cannot be given in exchange for it, nor shall silver be weighed out as its price.

**28:16** It cannot be appraised with the gold of Ophir, with precious onyx or sapphire.

**28:17** Gold and glass cannot compare with it, nor can vessels of pure gold be its exchange.

**28:18** Coral and crystal shall not be mentioned, for the acquisition of wisdom surpasses rubies.

**28:19** The topaz of Cush cannot compare with it, nor can it be appraised with pure gold.

**28:20** Wisdom then—from where does it come? And where is the place of understanding?

**28:21** Indeed, it is hidden from the eyes of every living thing, and from the birds of the heavens it is concealed.

**28:22** Abaddon and Death say, "With our ears we have heard a report of it."

**28:23** God discerns the way to it, and He Himself knows its place.

**28:24** For He gazes to the ends of the earth, and sees beneath all the heavens.

**28:25** When He appointed weight for the wind, and meted out the waters by measure,

**28:26** when He made a decree for the rain, and a way for the thunderbolt,

**28:27** then He beheld it and declared it; He established it, and also searched it out.

**28:28** And He said to mankind, "Behold, the fear of the Lord—that is wisdom; and turning away from evil is understanding."

## Translator Notes

- **28:4** — Describes ancient miners descending deep shafts far from settled habitation, hanging suspended on ropes as they work in the dark.
- **28:18** — The Hebrew word rendered "acquisition" literally means a "drawing out" or "pouch," signifying either the gaining of wisdom or its price.
- **28:22** — Abaddon is a Hebrew term meaning "Destruction," used here as a poetic personification of the realm of the dead.
