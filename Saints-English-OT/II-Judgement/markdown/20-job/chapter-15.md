## Text

**15:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

**15:2** "Should a wise man respond with knowledge of wind, and fill his belly with the east wind?

**15:3** Should he argue with talk that does not profit, or with words by which he can accomplish no good?

**15:4** Indeed, you make void reverence, and diminish meditation before God.

**15:5** For your iniquity instructs your mouth, and you choose the tongue of the crafty.

**15:6** Your own mouth declares you guilty, and not I; yes, your own lips testify against you.

**15:7** Were you born as the first of mankind, or were you brought forth before the hills?

**15:8** Have you listened in the secret council of God, and do you limit wisdom to yourself?

**15:9** What do you know that we do not also know? What do you discern that is not with us?

**15:10** Both the gray-haired and the aged are among us, far greater in days than your father.

**15:11** Are the consolations of God too little for you, and the word spoken gently with you?

**15:12** Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash,

**15:13** that you turn your spirit against God, and bring forth such words from your mouth?

**15:14** What is mortal man, that he should be pure, and he who is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?

**15:15** Behold, He puts no trust in His holy ones, and the heavens are not pure in His sight;

**15:16** how much less one who is detestable and corrupt, a man who drinks iniquity like water!

**15:17** I will declare to you; listen to me, and what I have seen I will recount,

**15:18** what wise men have declared from their fathers, and have not concealed,

**15:19** to whom alone the land was given, and no stranger passed through their midst:

**15:20** All his days the wicked man writhes in anguish, and numbered years are stored up for the ruthless.

**15:21** The sound of terrors is in his ears; in peace the destroyer comes upon him.

**15:22** He does not believe that he will return out of darkness, and he is awaited by the sword.

**15:23** He wanders about for bread, saying, 'Where is it?' He knows that a day of darkness is ready at his hand.

**15:24** Distress and anguish terrify him; they overpower him like a king poised for battle.

**15:25** Because he has stretched out his hand against God, and acts defiantly against the Almighty,

**15:26** charging stubbornly against Him with the thick bosses of his shields;

**15:27** because he has covered his face with his fat, and gathered folds of fat upon his loins.

**15:28** He dwells in desolate cities, in houses no one inhabits, which are doomed to become heaps of ruins.

**15:29** He shall not grow rich, nor shall his wealth endure, nor shall their possessions spread over the earth.

**15:30** He shall not escape from darkness; the flame shall dry up his shoots, and he shall vanish by the breath of His mouth.

**15:31** Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself, for emptiness shall be his recompense.

**15:32** Before his time it shall be paid in full, and his branch shall not be green.

**15:33** He shall drop his unripe grapes like a vine, and cast off his blossoms like an olive tree.

**15:34** For the company of the godless is barren, and fire consumes the tents of bribery.

**15:35** They conceive trouble and bring forth iniquity, and their womb prepares deceit."

## Translator Notes

- **15:12** — The Hebrew verb rendered "flash" occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes winking, blinking, or flashing the eyes in indignation or arrogance.
- **15:22** — Follows the Masoretic Qere (passive participle, "watched for" or "awaited"); the Ketiv reflects an active or defective form.
- **15:29** — The rare Hebrew noun rendered "their possessions" is a hapax legomenon whose exact sense is uncertain, traditionally understood as property, wealth, or grain-ears; the third-person plural suffix shifts briefly to the wicked collectively.
- **15:30** — "His mouth" refers to God, whose sovereign breath sweeps away the wicked (compare Isaiah 11:4).
