## Text

**23:1** Then Job answered and said:

**23:2** "Even today my complaint is bitter; my hand lies heavy despite my groaning.

**23:3** Oh, that I knew where to find Him, that I might come to His dwelling place!

**23:4** I would lay out my case before Him, and fill my mouth with arguments.

**23:5** I would learn the words with which He would answer me, and understand what He would say to me.

**23:6** Would He contend with me in the greatness of His strength? No, surely He Himself would give heed to me.

**23:7** There an upright man could reason with Him, and I would be delivered forever from my Judge.

**23:8** Behold, I go forward, and He is not there; and backward, yet I do not perceive Him;

**23:9** on the left where He works, yet I cannot behold Him; He turns to the right, and I cannot see Him.

**23:10** Yet He knows the way that is with me; when He has tried me, I shall come forth as gold.

**23:11** My foot has held closely to His steps; I have kept His way and have not turned aside.

**23:12** I have not departed from the commandment of His lips; I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my allotted portion.

**23:13** Yet He is unchangeable, and who can turn Him back? What His soul desires, He does.

**23:15** Therefore I am dismayed before His presence; when I consider it, I am in dread of Him.

**23:16** For God has made my heart faint, and the Almighty has terrified me;

**23:17** for I have not been silenced before the darkness, nor has He hidden the thick gloom from my face."

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — The Hebrew word rendered "bitter" literally denotes rebellion or defiance, and the Masoretic reading "my hand" is preserved faithfully rather than following the Septuagint's "His hand."
- **23:12** — "More than my allotted portion" translates the Masoretic Hebrew literally, referring to one's prescribed daily portion or necessary food.
- **23:13** — "Unchangeable" translates the Hebrew phrase literally meaning "in one," expressing God's solitary, unalterable singleness of purpose.
