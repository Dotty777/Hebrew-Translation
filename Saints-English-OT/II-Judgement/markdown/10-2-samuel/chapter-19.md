## Text

**19:1** Then the king was deeply shaken, and went up to the upper chamber over the gate, and wept. And as he went, thus he said: "O my son Absalom—my son, my son Absalom! Would that I myself had died in your place! O Absalom, my son, my son!"

**19:2** And it was told to Joab, "Behold, the king is weeping and mourning for Absalom."

**19:3** So the victory on that day was turned into mourning for all the people, because the people heard it said on that day, "The king is grieved for his son."

**19:4** And the people stole into the city on that day, just as people who are put to shame steal away when they flee in battle.

**19:5** Now the king had covered his face, and the king cried out with a loud voice, "O my son Absalom! O Absalom, my son, my son!"

**19:6** Then Joab came into the house to the king and said, "Today you have put to shame the faces of all your servants, who today have delivered your life, and the lives of your sons and your daughters, and the lives of your wives, and the lives of your concubines,

**19:7** by loving those who hate you and hating those who love you. For you have declared today that commanders and servants mean nothing to you; for today I perceive that if Absalom were alive and all of us were dead today, then it would have been pleasing in your sight.

**19:8** Now therefore, arise, go out and speak kindly to your servants. For I swear by the LORD, if you do not go out, not a single man will remain with you tonight; and that will be worse for you than all the calamity that has come upon you from your youth until now."

**19:9** So the king arose and sat in the gate. And they told all the people, saying, "Behold, the king is sitting in the gate." Then all the people came before the king. Now Israel had fled, every man to his tent.

**19:10** And all the people were in dispute throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, "The king delivered us from the hand of our enemies, and he rescued us from the hand of the Philistines; yet now he has fled from the land because of Absalom.

**19:11** And Absalom, whom we anointed over us, has died in battle. Now therefore, why do you remain silent about bringing back the king?"

**19:12** Then King David sent to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, saying, "Speak to the elders of Judah, saying, 'Why should you be the last to bring the king back to his house, since the word of all Israel has come to the king, even to his house?

**19:13** You are my brethren; you are my bone and my flesh. Why then should you be the last to bring back the king?'

**19:14** And say to Amasa, 'Are you not my bone and my flesh? May God do so to me, and more also, if you are not commander of the army before me continually in the place of Joab.'"

**19:15** So he swayed the heart of all the men of Judah as one man, and they sent word to the king: "Return, you and all your servants."

**19:16** Then the king returned and came to the Jordan. And Judah came to Gilgal to go to meet the king, to bring the king across the Jordan.

**19:17** And Shimei the son of Gera, a Benjamite who was from Bahurim, hurried and came down with the men of Judah to meet King David.

**19:18** There were a thousand men of Benjamin with him, and Ziba the servant of the house of Saul, and his fifteen sons and his twenty servants with him; and they rushed across the Jordan before the king.

**19:19** And a ferryboat crossed over to bring over the king's household and to do what was good in his sight. Now Shimei the son of Gera fell down before the king as he was about to cross the Jordan,

**19:20** and said to the king, "Do not let my lord impute iniquity to me, nor remember the wrong your servant committed on the day that my lord the king departed from Jerusalem, that the king should take it to heart.

**19:21** For your servant knows that I have sinned; therefore, behold, I have come today, the first of all the house of Joseph, to go down to meet my lord the king."

**19:22** But Abishai the son of Zeruiah answered and said, "Should not Shimei be put to death for this, because he cursed the LORD's anointed?"

**19:23** And David said, "What have I to do with you, you sons of Zeruiah, that you should become adversaries to me today? Shall any man be put to death today in Israel? For do I not know that today I am king over Israel?"

**19:24** Therefore the king said to Shimei, "You shall not die." And the king swore to him.

**19:25** Now Mephibosheth the son of Saul came down to meet the king. And he had not cared for his feet, nor trimmed his mustache, nor washed his garments, from the day the king departed until the day he came back in peace.

**19:26** And it came to pass, when he came to Jerusalem to meet the king, that the king said to him, "Why did you not go with me, Mephibosheth?"

**19:27** And he answered, "My lord, O king, my servant deceived me. For your servant said, 'I will saddle a donkey for myself, that I may ride on it and go with the king,' because your servant is lame.

**19:28** And he has slandered your servant to my lord the king. Yet my lord the king is like the angel of God; therefore do what is good in your sight.

**19:29** For all my father's house were nothing but dead men before my lord the king, yet you set your servant among those who eat at your own table. What right, therefore, do I still have to cry out any more to the king?"

**19:30** Then the king said to him, "Why do you speak any more of your matters? I have said: you and Ziba shall divide the land."

**19:31** And Mephibosheth said to the king, "Rather, let him take it all, since my lord the king has come back in peace to his own house."

**19:32** Now Barzillai the Gileadite came down from Rogelim, and went across the Jordan with the king, to escort him over the Jordan.

**19:33** Now Barzillai was very aged, eighty years old; and he had sustained the king while he abode at Mahanaim, for he was a very wealthy man.

**19:34** And the king said to Barzillai, "Cross over with me, and I will sustain you with me in Jerusalem."

**19:35** But Barzillai said to the king, "How many are the days of the years of my life, that I should go up with the king to Jerusalem?

**19:36** I am eighty years old today. Can I discern between good and bad? Can your servant taste what I eat or what I drink? Can I still hear the voice of singing men and singing women? Why then should your servant be a burden any longer to my lord the king?

**19:37** Your servant will go a little way across the Jordan with the king; and why should the king repay me with such a reward?

**19:38** Please let your servant turn back, that I may die in my own city, near the grave of my father and my mother. Yet behold, here is your servant Chimham; let him cross over with my lord the king, and do for him what is good in your sight."

**19:39** And the king answered, "Chimham shall cross over with me, and I will do for him what is good in your sight; and whatever you desire of me, I will do for you."

**19:40** Then all the people crossed over the Jordan, and the king crossed over. And the king kissed Barzillai and blessed him, and he returned to his own place.

**19:41** Now the king went on to Gilgal, and Chimham went on with him; and all the people of Judah escorted the king across, and also half the people of Israel.

**19:42** And behold, all the men of Israel came to the king, and said to the king, "Why have our brethren, the men of Judah, stolen you away and brought the king and his household across the Jordan, and all David's men with him?"

**19:43** So all the men of Judah answered the men of Israel, "Because the king is near of kin to us. Why then are you angry over this matter? Have we eaten at all at the king's expense, or has any gift been bestowed upon us?"

**19:44** And the men of Israel answered the men of Judah and said, "We have ten shares in the king, and we also have more right in David than you. Why then did you despise us? Was not our word the first to bring back our king?" Yet the words of the men of Judah were fiercer than the words of the men of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **19:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, 19:1 corresponds to 18:33 in many English Bibles, shifting the numbering of the chapter by one verse.
- **19:7** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("if"); the written Ketiv reads "not."
- **19:32** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("over the Jordan"); the written Ketiv reads "at the Jordan."
- **19:41** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("escorted the king across"); the written Ketiv has a variant verbal form, and Chimham is spelled Chimhan in the Hebrew text of this verse.
