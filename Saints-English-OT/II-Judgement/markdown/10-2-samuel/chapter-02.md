## Text

**2:1** And it came to pass after this that David inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I go up to any of the cities of Judah?" And the LORD said to him, "Go up." And David said, "Where shall I go up?" And He said, "To Hebron."

**2:2** So David went up there, and his two wives also, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**2:3** And David brought up his men who were with him, every man with his household; and they dwelt in the cities of Hebron.

**2:4** Then the men of Judah came, and there they anointed David king over the house of Judah. And they told David, saying, "It was the men of Jabesh Gilead who buried Saul."

**2:5** So David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh Gilead, and said to them, "Blessed are you of the LORD, because you have shown this lovingkindness to your lord, to Saul, and have buried him.

**2:6** And now may the LORD show lovingkindness and truth to you; and I also will show you this goodness, because you have done this thing.

**2:7** Now therefore, let your hands be strengthened, and be men of valor; for your lord Saul is dead, and the house of Judah has also anointed me king over them."

**2:8** But Abner the son of Ner, commander of Saul's army, took Ish-bosheth the son of Saul and brought him over to Mahanaim;

**2:9** and he made him king over Gilead, over the Ashurites, over Jezreel, over Ephraim, over Benjamin, and over all Israel.

**2:10** Ish-bosheth, Saul's son, was forty years old when he began to reign over Israel, and he reigned two years. Only the house of Judah followed David.

**2:11** And the time that David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah was seven years and six months.

**2:12** Now Abner the son of Ner, and the servants of Ish-bosheth the son of Saul, went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.

**2:13** And Joab the son of Zeruiah, and the servants of David, went out and met them together by the pool of Gibeon. So they sat down, these on the one side of the pool, and those on the other side of the pool.

**2:14** Then Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men now arise and hold a contest before us." And Joab said, "Let them arise."

**2:15** So they arose and crossed over by number: twelve for Benjamin and for Ish-bosheth the son of Saul, and twelve of the servants of David.

**2:16** And each one grasped his opponent by the head and thrust his sword into his opponent's side, so that they fell down together. Therefore that place was called Helkath Hazzurim, which is in Gibeon.

**2:17** And the battle was exceedingly fierce on that day, and Abner and the men of Israel were defeated before the servants of David.

**2:18** Now the three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. And Asahel was as swift of foot as one of the gazelles in the open field.

**2:19** And Asahel pursued Abner, and as he went he did not turn aside to the right hand or to the left from following Abner.

**2:20** Then Abner looked behind him and said, "Is it you, Asahel?" And he answered, "It is I."

**2:21** And Abner said to him, "Turn aside to your right hand or to your left, and lay hold of one of the young men, and take his armor for yourself." Yet Asahel would not turn aside from following him.

**2:22** So Abner spoke yet again to Asahel, "Turn aside from following me! Why should I strike you to the ground? How then could I lift up my face to Joab your brother?"

**2:23** However, he refused to turn aside. Therefore Abner struck him in the belly with the butt end of the spear, so that the spear came out behind him; and he fell down there and died on the spot. And it came to pass that all who came to the place where Asahel fell down and died stood still.

**2:24** Joab and Abishai also pursued Abner. And the sun was going down when they came to the hill of Ammah, which lies before Giah on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon.

**2:25** Now the children of Benjamin gathered themselves together behind Abner and formed one band, and they stood upon the top of a hill.

**2:26** Then Abner called to Joab and said, "Shall the sword devour forever? Do you not know that it will be bitter in the end? How long then will it be before you tell the people to turn back from pursuing their brethren?"

**2:27** And Joab said, "As God lives, if you had not spoken, surely even in the morning the people would have withdrawn, every man from following his brother."

**2:28** So Joab blew the trumpet; and all the people stood still and did not pursue Israel any further, nor did they fight anymore.

**2:29** Then Abner and his men marched through the Arabah all that night; and they crossed over the Jordan, and marched through all Bithron, and came to Mahanaim.

**2:30** And Joab returned from pursuing Abner; and when he had gathered all the people together, there were missing of David's servants nineteen men and Asahel.

**2:31** But the servants of David had struck down of Benjamin and of Abner's men, so that three hundred and sixty men died.

**2:32** Then they took up Asahel and buried him in his father's tomb, which was in Bethlehem. And Joab and his men marched all night, and the day dawned upon them at Hebron.

## Translator Notes

- **2:9** — The Masoretic Text reads "the Ashurites," referring to a northern Israelite territory or people, perhaps linked with the tribe of Asher.
- **2:16** — Helkath Hazzurim means "Field of Flints" or "Field of Sword-Edges," commemorating the mutual slaughter of the twenty-four combatants.
- **2:23** — The Hebrew term translated "belly" denotes the lower abdomen or midsection.
- **2:29** — The Arabah is the rift valley along the Jordan River; Bithron denotes a rugged ravine or cleft east of the Jordan.
