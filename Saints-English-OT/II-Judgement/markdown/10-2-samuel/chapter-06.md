## Text

**6:1** And David again gathered all the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand.

**6:2** And David arose and went with all the people who were with him from Baale-judah, to bring up from there the ark of God, which is called by the Name—the Name of the LORD of hosts, who is enthroned above the cherubim.

**6:3** So they set the ark of God upon a new cart and brought it out of the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill; and Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, were driving the new cart.

**6:4** They brought it out of the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill, accompanying the ark of God; and Ahio went before the ark.

**6:5** And David and all the house of Israel were celebrating before the LORD with all manner of instruments of cypress wood, and with lyres, harps, tambourines, castanets, and cymbals.

**6:6** And when they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah reached out to the ark of God and took hold of it, for the oxen stumbled.

**6:7** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Uzzah, and God struck him down there for his irreverence; and he died there beside the ark of God.

**6:8** And David became angry because the LORD had broken out in a breach against Uzzah; and he called that place Perez-uzzah, to this day.

**6:9** And David feared the LORD that day, and he said, "How can the ark of the LORD come to me?"

**6:10** So David was not willing to move the ark of the LORD with him into the City of David; instead, David turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

**6:11** And the ark of the LORD remained in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite three months; and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and all his household.

**6:12** Now it was told to King David, saying, "The LORD has blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that belongs to him, because of the ark of God." So David went and brought up the ark of God from the house of Obed-edom to the City of David with gladness.

**6:13** And it came to pass, when those who bore the ark of the LORD had gone six paces, that he sacrificed an ox and a fatling.

**6:14** Then David danced before the LORD with all his might; and David was girded with a linen ephod.

**6:15** So David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the LORD with shouting and with the sound of the trumpet.

**6:16** Now as the ark of the LORD came into the City of David, Michal the daughter of Saul looked down through a window and saw King David leaping and whirling before the LORD; and she despised him in her heart.

**6:17** So they brought in the ark of the LORD and set it in its place inside the tent that David had pitched for it. And David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

**6:18** And when David had finished offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of hosts.

**6:19** Then he distributed among all the people, among the whole multitude of Israel, both men and women, to each one a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a cake of raisins. So all the people departed, each to his own house.

**6:20** Then David returned to bless his household. And Michal the daughter of Saul came out to meet David and said, "How glorious was the king of Israel today, uncovering himself today in the sight of the maidservants of his servants, as one of the base fellows shamelessly uncovers himself!"

**6:21** So David said to Michal, "It was before the LORD, who chose me above your father and above all his house, to appoint me ruler over the people of the LORD, over Israel. Therefore I will celebrate before the LORD.

**6:22** And I will be even more undignified than this, and will be humble in my own eyes. Yet by those maidservants of whom you have spoken, by them I shall be held in honor."

**6:23** Therefore Michal the daughter of Saul had no child to the day of her death.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "from Baale-judah" (another name for Kiriath-jearim) and emphasizes the ark's holiness by doubling "the Name" before the title "the LORD of hosts."
- **6:7** — The Hebrew word rendered "irreverence" occurs only here in Scripture and denotes a rash act, error, or irreverent offense.
- **6:8** — Perez-uzzah means "Outbreak against Uzzah" or "Breach of Uzzah," echoing the Hebrew verb and noun for the LORD's sudden judgment.
- **6:19** — The Hebrew term rendered "a portion of meat" is rare (occurring only here and in the parallel passage of Chronicles) and has been understood either as a cut of meat or as a date cake.
