## Text

**20:1** Now there happened to be there a worthless man, whose name was Sheba the son of Bichri, a Benjamite. And he blew the trumpet and said, "We have no share in David, nor do we have an inheritance in the son of Jesse; every man to his tents, O Israel!"

**20:2** So all the men of Israel withdrew from following David, and followed Sheba the son of Bichri; but the men of Judah clung steadfastly to their king, from the Jordan even to Jerusalem.

**20:3** Now David came to his house at Jerusalem. And the king took the ten women, his concubines whom he had left to keep the house, and put them in a guarded house and sustained them, but did not go in to them. So they were shut up until the day of their death, living in widowhood.

**20:4** Then the king said to Amasa, "Summon the men of Judah for me within three days, and be present here yourself."

**20:5** So Amasa went to summon Judah; but he delayed longer than the appointed time which he had appointed for him.

**20:6** And David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba the son of Bichri will do us more harm than Absalom. Take your lord's servants and pursue him, lest he find for himself fortified cities and escape from our sight."

**20:7** So Joab's men went out after him, with the Cherethites, the Pelethites, and all the mighty men; and they went out from Jerusalem to pursue Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:8** When they were at the great stone which is in Gibeon, Amasa came to meet them. Now Joab was dressed in his battle garment, and over it was a belt with a sword fastened upon his waist in its sheath; and as he stepped forward, it fell out.

**20:9** Then Joab said to Amasa, "Is it well with you, my brother?" And Joab's right hand took hold of Amasa's beard to kiss him.

**20:10** But Amasa did not guard against the sword that was in Joab's hand; so he struck him with it in the belly and poured out his inward parts to the ground, and did not strike him a second time; and he died. Then Joab and Abishai his brother pursued after Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:11** And one of Joab's young men stood beside him and said, "Whoever favors Joab, and whoever is for David—follow Joab!"

**20:12** Now Amasa wallowed in his blood in the middle of the highway. And when the man saw that all the people stood still, he moved Amasa from the highway into the field and threw a garment over him, when he saw that everyone who came upon him stopped.

**20:13** When he had been removed from the highway, all the men passed on after Joab to pursue Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:14** And he passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel and Beth Maachah, and all the Berites; and they gathered together and also went after him.

**20:15** Then they came and besieged him in Abel of Beth Maachah; and they cast up a siege mound against the city, and it stood against the rampart. And all the people who were with Joab were battering the wall to throw it down.

**20:16** Then a wise woman cried out from the city, "Hear, hear! Please say to Joab, 'Come near here, that I may speak with you.'"

**20:17** When he came near to her, the woman said, "Are you Joab?" And he answered, "I am." Then she said to him, "Hear the words of your maidservant." And he answered, "I am listening."

**20:18** So she spoke, saying, "In former times they used to speak, saying, 'Let them surely seek counsel at Abel,' and thus they would end a matter.

**20:19** I am among the peaceable and faithful in Israel. You seek to destroy a city and a mother in Israel. Why would you swallow up the inheritance of the LORD?"

**20:20** And Joab answered and said, "Far be it, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or destroy!

**20:21** The matter is not so. Rather, a man from the hill country of Ephraim, Sheba the son of Bichri by name, has lifted up his hand against the king, even against David. Deliver him alone, and I will depart from the city." And the woman said to Joab, "Behold, his head shall be thrown to you over the wall."

**20:22** Then the woman went to all the people in her wisdom. And they cut off the head of Sheba the son of Bichri and threw it out to Joab. Then he blew the trumpet, and they dispersed from the city, every man to his tent. And Joab returned to the king at Jerusalem.

**20:23** Now Joab was over all the army of Israel; and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and over the Pelethites;

**20:24** Adoram was over the forced labor; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was the recorder;

**20:25** Sheva was scribe; Zadok and Abiathar were the priests;

**20:26** and Ira the Jairite was also a priest to David.

## Translator Notes

- **20:5** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("and he delayed"); the written Ketiv preserves an orthographic variant of the same verb.
- **20:14** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("and they gathered together"); the written Ketiv transposes two consonants of the root.
- **20:23** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("the Cherethites"); the written Ketiv reads "the Carites."
- **20:25** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("Sheva"); the written Ketiv reads "Sheya."
- **20:26** — The Hebrew word kohen ("priest") may also denote a chief royal counselor or minister in David's court (compare 2 Samuel 8:18).
