## Text

**8:1** Now it came to pass after this that David struck the Philistines and subdued them, and David took Metheg-ammah out of the hand of the Philistines.

**8:2** He also struck Moab, and he measured them with a line, making them lie down on the ground; and he measured two lines to put to death and one full line to keep alive. So Moab became servants to David, bringing tribute.

**8:3** David also struck Hadadezer the son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as he went to restore his dominion at the River Euphrates.

**8:4** And David captured from him one thousand seven hundred horsemen and twenty thousand foot soldiers. David also hamstrung all the chariot horses, except that he reserved enough of them for one hundred chariots.

**8:5** When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men among the Arameans.

**8:6** Then David placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became servants to David, bringing tribute. And the LORD preserved David wherever he went.

**8:7** And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

**8:8** And from Betah and from Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took a very great quantity of bronze.

**8:9** When Toi king of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer,

**8:10** then Toi sent Joram his son to King David to inquire of his welfare and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him—for Hadadezer had been at war with Toi—and in his hand were vessels of silver, vessels of gold, and vessels of bronze.

**8:11** These also King David dedicated to the LORD, along with the silver and the gold that he had dedicated from all the nations which he had subdued:

**8:12** from Aram, from Moab, from the children of Ammon, from the Philistines, from Amalek, and from the spoil of Hadadezer the son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

**8:13** And David made a name for himself when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand of the Arameans in the Valley of Salt.

**8:14** He also placed garrisons in Edom; throughout all Edom he placed garrisons, and all the Edomites became servants to David. And the LORD preserved David wherever he went.

**8:15** So David reigned over all Israel, and David administered justice and righteousness to all his people.

**8:16** Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder.

**8:17** Zadok the son of Ahitub and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar were priests, and Seraiah was scribe.

**8:18** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and the sons of David were priests.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — "Metheg-ammah" literally means "the bridle of the cubit" or "the bridle of the mother-city," likely denoting a Philistine town or figuratively control of the chief Philistine metropolis.
- **8:3** — The Masoretic consonantal text reads simply "the River," while the traditional Masoretic reading supplies "Euphrates."
- **8:4** — The Masoretic Text reads "one thousand seven hundred horsemen," whereas First Chronicles eighteen four and the Septuagint here read "one thousand chariots and seven thousand horsemen."
- **8:13** — The Masoretic Text reads "Aram" (the Arameans), whereas some Hebrew manuscripts, ancient versions, and First Chronicles eighteen twelve read "Edom" (compare verse fourteen).
- **8:18** — Hebrew literally "priests," which in this royal administrative list likely denotes priestly counselors or chief court officials (compare First Chronicles eighteen seventeen).
