## Text

**3:1** Now the war was long between the house of Saul and the house of David; and David grew steadily stronger, while the house of Saul grew steadily weaker.

**3:2** And sons were born to David in Hebron: his firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelitess;

**3:3** and his second, Chileab, by Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite; and the third, Absalom the son of Maacah, the daughter of Talmai, king of Geshur;

**3:4** and the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith; and the fifth, Shephatiah the son of Abital;

**3:5** and the sixth, Ithream, by Eglah, David's wife. These were born to David in Hebron.

**3:6** Now it came to pass, while the war continued between the house of Saul and the house of David, that Abner was strengthening his hold in the house of Saul.

**3:7** Now Saul had a concubine whose name was Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah; and he said to Abner, "Why have you gone in to my father's concubine?"

**3:8** Then Abner became exceedingly angry at the words of Ishbosheth, and said, "Am I a dog's head that belongs to Judah? This day I show lovingkindness to the house of Saul your father, to his brethren and to his friends, and have not delivered you into the hand of David; yet today you charge me with an iniquity concerning this woman!

**3:9** May God do so to Abner, and more also, for just as the LORD has sworn to David, surely so I will do for him:

**3:10** to transfer the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to establish the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, from Dan even to Beersheba."

**3:11** And he could not answer Abner another word, because he feared him.

**3:12** Then Abner sent messengers to David on his behalf, saying, "Whose is the land?" and saying, "Make your covenant with me, and behold, my hand shall be with you to bring all Israel over to you."

**3:13** And he said, "Good; I will make a covenant with you. Yet one thing I ask of you: you shall not see my face unless you first bring Michal, Saul's daughter, when you come to see my face."

**3:14** So David sent messengers to Ishbosheth, Saul's son, saying, "Give me my wife Michal, whom I betrothed to myself for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines."

**3:15** And Ishbosheth sent and took her from her husband, from Paltiel the son of Laish.

**3:16** Then her husband went along with her, weeping as he walked behind her as far as Bahurim. And Abner said to him, "Go, return!" And he returned.

**3:17** Now Abner had conferred with the elders of Israel, saying, "In time past you were seeking David to be king over you.

**3:18** Now therefore do it! For the LORD has spoken concerning David, saying, 'By the hand of My servant David I will save My people Israel from the hand of the Philistines and from the hand of all their enemies.'"

**3:19** And Abner also spoke in the hearing of Benjamin. Then Abner also went to speak in the hearing of David in Hebron all that was good in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of the whole house of Benjamin.

**3:20** So Abner came to David at Hebron, and twenty men with him. And David made a feast for Abner and for the men who were with him.

**3:21** Then Abner said to David, "I will arise and go, and gather all Israel to my lord the king, that they may make a covenant with you, and that you may reign over all that your soul desires." So David sent Abner away, and he departed in peace.

**3:22** And behold, the servants of David and Joab came from a raid and brought much spoil with them. But Abner was no longer with David in Hebron, for he had sent him away, and he had gone in peace.

**3:23** When Joab and all the army that was with him arrived, they told Joab, saying, "Abner the son of Ner came to the king, and he sent him away, and he has gone in peace."

**3:24** Then Joab came to the king and said, "What have you done? Behold, Abner came to you; why is it that you sent him away, and he is altogether gone?

**3:25** You know Abner the son of Ner, that he came to deceive you, and to know your going out and your coming in, and to know all that you are doing."

**3:26** And when Joab had gone out from David's presence, he sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from the well of Sirah; yet David did not know it.

**3:27** Now when Abner returned to Hebron, Joab took him aside inside the gate to speak with him privately, and there struck him in the belly so that he died, for the blood of Asahel his brother.

**3:28** And afterward, when David heard of it, he said, "I and my kingdom are guiltless before the LORD forever of the blood of Abner the son of Ner.

**3:29** Let it fall upon the head of Joab and upon all his father's house; and let there never be cut off from the house of Joab one who has a discharge, or a leper, or one who holds a spindle, or one who falls by the sword, or one who lacks bread."

**3:30** So Joab and Abishai his brother slew Abner, because he had put their brother Asahel to death at Gibeon in the battle.

**3:31** Then David said to Joab and to all the people who were with him, "Tear your garments, gird yourselves with sackcloth, and mourn before Abner." And King David walked behind the bier.

**3:32** So they buried Abner in Hebron; and the king lifted up his voice and wept at the grave of Abner, and all the people wept.

**3:33** And the king sang a lament over Abner and said, "Should Abner die as a fool dies?

**3:34** Your hands were not bound, nor were your feet put into bronze fetters; as a man falls before sons of iniquity, so you fell." And all the people wept over him once more.

**3:35** Then all the people came to urge David to eat bread while it was still day; but David swore, saying, "May God do so to me, and more also, if I taste bread or anything else before the sun goes down!"

**3:36** And all the people took note of it, and it was good in their eyes, as indeed everything the king did was good in the eyes of all the people.

**3:37** So all the people and all Israel understood that day that it had not come from the king to put Abner the son of Ner to death.

**3:38** Then the king said to his servants, "Do you not know that a prince and a great man has fallen this day in Israel?

**3:39** And I am weak today, though anointed king; and these men, the sons of Zeruiah, are too harsh for me. May the LORD repay the evildoer according to his wickedness."

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Qere reads the Niphal passive form wayyiwwaledu ("were born"), whereas the Ketiv has a shorter consonantal spelling; the translation follows the Qere.
- **3:3** — The Ketiv spells the name Abigal, while the Qere reads the standard form Abigail.
- **3:15** — The Ketiv reads Lush, while the Qere reads Laish, followed here.
- **3:18** — The Masoretic Text reads hoshia (a Hiphil infinitive absolute or third-person perfect), rendered "I will save" in keeping with the first-person divine speech ("My servant", "My people").
- **3:25** — The Ketiv (mevoekha) and the Qere (movaekha) are orthographic variants sharing the same meaning, "your coming in."
- **3:29** — The Hebrew word pelek literally means "spindle" (as in Proverbs 31:19), conveying frailty or disgrace, though some understand it as a crutch or staff.
