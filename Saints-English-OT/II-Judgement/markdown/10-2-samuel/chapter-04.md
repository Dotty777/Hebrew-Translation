## Text

**4:1** When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his hands grew feeble, and all Israel was dismayed.

**4:2** Now Saul's son had two men who were captains of raiding bands: the name of the one was Baanah, and the name of the other Rechab, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the sons of Benjamin—for Beeroth also was reckoned to Benjamin,

**4:3** and the Beerothites had fled to Gittaim and have been sojourners there to this day.

**4:4** Now Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was crippled in his feet. He was five years old when the tidings of Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel; and his nurse took him up and fled. And it came to pass, as she made haste to flee, that he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

**4:5** Then the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, set out and came about the heat of the day to the house of Ishbosheth, while he was taking his noonday rest.

**4:6** And they came right into the midst of the house, as though to fetch wheat, and they struck him in the belly; and Rechab and Baanah his brother escaped.

**4:7** For when they had entered the house, while he was lying on his bed in his bedchamber, they struck him and put him to death, and cut off his head; and they took his head and went by way of the Arabah all that night.

**4:8** And they brought the head of Ishbosheth to David at Hebron, and said to the king, "Behold, the head of Ishbosheth, the son of Saul your enemy, who sought your life! And the LORD has given my lord the king vengeance this day upon Saul and upon his offspring."

**4:9** Then David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and said to them, "As the LORD lives, who has redeemed my soul from all adversity,

**4:10** when one told me, saying, 'Behold, Saul is dead,' and thought in his own eyes that he was a bearer of good tidings, I seized him and slew him in Ziklag—which was the reward I gave him for his tidings.

**4:11** How much more, when wicked men have slain a righteous man in his own house upon his bed? Therefore, shall I not now require his blood at your hand and purge you from the earth?"

**4:12** So David commanded the young men, and they slew them, cut off their hands and their feet, and hanged them beside the pool in Hebron. But they took the head of Ishbosheth and buried it in the grave of Abner in Hebron.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — Following the Masoretic Text faithfully, Ishbosheth is referred to simply as "Saul's son" in verses 1 and 2.
- **4:6** — Translating the Masoretic Text directly, "as though to fetch wheat" renders the participle phrase loqchey chittim (literally "takers of wheat"), explaining the assassins' pretext for entering the house.
