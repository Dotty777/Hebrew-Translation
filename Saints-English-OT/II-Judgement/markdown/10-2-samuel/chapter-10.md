## Text

**10:1** Now it came to pass after this that the king of the sons of Ammon died, and Hanun his son reigned in his place.

**10:2** Then David said, "I will show lovingkindness to Hanun the son of Nahash, just as his father showed lovingkindness to me." So David sent by the hand of his servants to comfort him concerning his father; and the servants of David came to the land of the sons of Ammon.

**10:3** And the princes of the sons of Ammon said to Hanun their lord, "Is David honoring your father in your sight, because he has sent comforters to you? Has David not sent his servants to you in order to search out the city, to spy it out, and to overthrow it?"

**10:4** Therefore Hanun seized the servants of David, shaved off half of their beards, cut off their garments at the middle up to their buttocks, and sent them away.

**10:5** When they told David, he sent to meet them, for the men were exceedingly ashamed. And the king said, "Remain in Jericho until your beards have grown, and then return."

**10:6** Now when the sons of Ammon saw that they had become odious to David, the sons of Ammon sent and hired the Arameans of Beth-Rehob and the Arameans of Zobah, twenty thousand foot soldiers, and the king of Maacah with one thousand men, and from the men of Tob twelve thousand men.

**10:7** When David heard of it, he sent Joab and all the host of the mighty men.

**10:8** Then the sons of Ammon came out and drew up in battle array at the entrance of the gate, while the Arameans of Zobah and of Rehob, and the men of Tob and Maacah, were by themselves in the field.

**10:9** When Joab saw that the battle front was against him both before and behind, he chose from all the select men of Israel and arrayed them against the Arameans.

**10:10** And the rest of the people he placed under the hand of Abishai his brother, and he arrayed them against the sons of Ammon.

**10:11** And he said, "If the Arameans are too strong for me, then you shall come to my deliverance; but if the sons of Ammon are too strong for you, then I will go and deliver you.

**10:12** Be strong, and let us show ourselves valiant for our people and for the cities of our God; and may the LORD do what is good in His sight."

**10:13** So Joab and the people who were with him drew near to battle against the Arameans, and they fled before him.

**10:14** And when the sons of Ammon saw that the Arameans had fled, they also fled before Abishai and entered into the city. Then Joab turned back from the sons of Ammon and came to Jerusalem.

**10:15** When the Arameans saw that they had been routed before Israel, they gathered themselves together.

**10:16** And Hadadezer sent and brought out the Arameans who were beyond the River, and they came to Helam, with Shobach the commander of Hadadezer's host going before them.

**10:17** When it was reported to David, he gathered all Israel, crossed over the Jordan, and came to Helam. And the Arameans drew up in battle array against David and fought with him.

**10:18** Then the Arameans fled before Israel, and David slew of the Arameans the men of seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen; and he struck down Shobach the commander of their host, so that he died there.

**10:19** And when all the kings who were servants of Hadadezer saw that they had been routed before Israel, they made peace with Israel and served them. So the Arameans feared to help the sons of Ammon anymore.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — Hebrew Aram is rendered Arameans here and throughout the chapter (traditionally translated Syrians in older English versions). Hebrew ish Tov uses the collective singular ish to denote the men of the region of Tob (cf. Judges 11:3).
- **10:9** — Following the Masoretic Qere, "of Israel"; the Ketiv reads "in Israel."
- **10:18** — Hebrew rekhev ("chariots") is used collectively with the verb "slew" to refer to chariot crews or charioteers.
