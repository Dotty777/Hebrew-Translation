## Text

**22:1** And David spoke to the LORD the words of this song in the day the LORD rescued him from the grasp of all his enemies and from the grasp of Saul.

**22:2** And he said, "The LORD is my crag and my stronghold, and my own deliverer;

**22:3** the God of my rock, in Him I take refuge—my shield and the horn of my salvation, my high tower and my shelter, my Savior; from violence You save me.

**22:4** I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and from my enemies I shall be saved.

**22:5** For the breakers of death encompassed me; the torrents of ruin filled me with dread.

**22:6** The cords of Sheol encircled me; the snares of death closed in upon me.

**22:7** In my distress I called upon the LORD, and to my God I called out; and from His temple He heard my voice, and my cry for help entered His ears.

**22:8** Then the earth reeled and quaked; the foundations of the heavens trembled and shook, because His anger burned.

**22:9** Smoke ascended from His nostrils, and fire from His mouth devoured; burning coals blazed forth from Him.

**22:10** He bent the heavens and descended, and thick darkness lay beneath His feet.

**22:11** He rode upon a cherub and flew, and He was seen upon the wings of the wind.

**22:12** He set darkness around Himself as pavilions—a gathering of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

**22:13** Out of the radiance before Him, coals of fire were kindled.

**22:14** The LORD thundered from the heavens, and the Most High gave forth His voice.

**22:15** He sent forth arrows and scattered them—lightning, and brought confusion.

**22:16** Then the channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were laid bare at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of His nostrils.

**22:17** He reached down from on high and took hold of me; He drew me out of many waters.

**22:18** He delivered me from my mighty enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me.

**22:19** They came against me in the day of my calamity, yet the LORD became my stay.

**22:20** He brought me forth into a spacious place; He rescued me because He delighted in me.

**22:21** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the purity of my hands He repaid me.

**22:22** For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not turned wickedly away from my God.

**22:23** For all His ordinances were before me, and from His statutes I did not turn aside.

**22:24** I was blameless toward Him, and I guarded myself from my iniquity.

**22:25** So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my purity before His eyes.

**22:26** With the faithful You show Yourself faithful; with the blameless mighty man You show Yourself blameless;

**22:27** with the pure You show Yourself pure, and with the crooked You show Yourself shrewd.

**22:28** An afflicted people You save, but Your eyes are upon the haughty, whom You bring low.

**22:29** For You are my lamp, O LORD, and the LORD brightens my darkness.

**22:30** For by You I can charge against a troop; by my God I can leap over a wall.

**22:31** As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is refined; He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

**22:32** For who is God apart from the LORD? And who is a rock apart from our God?

**22:33** God is my mighty stronghold, and He guides my way blamelessly.

**22:34** He makes my feet like the feet of deer, and causes me to stand upon my high places.

**22:35** He trains my hands for battle, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**22:36** You have given me the shield of Your salvation, and Your gentleness has made me great.

**22:37** You widened my stride beneath me, and my ankles did not slip.

**22:38** I pursued my enemies and destroyed them, and I did not turn back until they were consumed.

**22:39** I consumed them and struck them down so that they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet.

**22:40** You girded me with strength for battle; You brought down beneath me those who rose up against me.

**22:41** You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and those who hated me I cut off.

**22:42** They looked around, but there was no one to save—even to the LORD, yet He did not answer them.

**22:43** I ground them fine as the dust of the earth; like the mire of the streets I crushed them and trampled them down.

**22:44** You rescued me from the strivings of my people; You preserved me as the head of the nations; a people I did not know shall serve me.

**22:45** Foreigners cringe in submission to me; at the hearing of the ear they obey me.

**22:46** Foreigners fade away and come limping out of their strongholds.

**22:47** The LORD lives, and blessed be my Rock! May God, the Rock of my salvation, be exalted!

**22:48** He is the God who grants me vengeance and brings down peoples beneath me,

**22:49** who brings me out from my enemies; You also lift me up above those who rise against me, and from the man of violence You rescue me.

**22:50** Therefore I will give thanks to You, O LORD, among the nations, and to Your name I will sing praises.

**22:51** He is a tower of salvation to His king, and shows lovingkindness to His anointed, to David and to his descendants forever."

## Translator Notes

- **22:8** — Qere reads the masculine verb form while Ketiv reads the feminine form agreeing with 'the earth'; both yield the same sense in English.
- **22:15** — Translated from the Qere, which omits the pronominal suffix on the final verb ('and brought confusion'); Ketiv includes the plural suffix ('and routed them').
- **22:33** — Translated from the Qere 'my way'; Ketiv reads 'his way.' The same first-person Qere versus third-person Ketiv variation recurs in verse 34 ('my feet' vs. 'his feet').
- **22:46** — The Masoretic verb here denotes limping or halting, in contrast to the parallel song in Psalm 18, which uses a different verb meaning 'come trembling.'
- **22:51** — Translated from the Qere 'a tower of salvation'; Ketiv reads a participle meaning 'magnifying salvation,' as in the parallel song in Psalm 18.
