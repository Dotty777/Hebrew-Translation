## Text

**23:1** Now these are the last words of David: The oracle of David the son of Jesse, and the oracle of the man raised on high, the anointed of the God of Jacob, and the sweet singer of the psalms of Israel:

**23:2** "The Spirit of the LORD has spoken through me, and His word is on my tongue.

**23:3** The God of Israel spoke; the Rock of Israel said to me, 'He who rules over mankind righteously, who rules in the fear of God,

**23:4** is like the light of morning when the sun rises, a morning without clouds, bringing tender grass from the earth through brightness after rain.'

**23:5** Is not my house indeed thus with God? For He has established with me an everlasting covenant, ordered in all things and secure. For all my salvation and every desire—will He not cause it to spring forth?

**23:6** Yet worthless men are all of them like thorns thrust aside, for they cannot be taken with the hand;

**23:7** rather, the man who touches them must be armed with iron and the shaft of a spear, and they shall be utterly burned with fire right in their place."

**23:8** These are the names of the mighty men who belonged to David: Josheb-basshebeth a Tahchemonite, chief of the captains; he was also called Adino the Eznite, because of eight hundred whom he slew at one time.

**23:9** And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodo, the son of an Ahohite, one of the three mighty men with David when they defied the Philistines who were gathered there for battle, and the men of Israel had withdrawn.

**23:10** He arose and struck down the Philistines until his hand grew weary, and his hand clung to the sword. And the LORD wrought a great deliverance that day; and the people returned after him only to plunder.

**23:11** And after him was Shammah the son of Agee the Hararite. Now the Philistines had gathered together into a troop where there was a plot of ground full of lentils, and the people fled from before the Philistines.

**23:12** But he took his stand in the midst of the plot, defended it, and struck down the Philistines; so the LORD wrought a great deliverance.

**23:13** Then three of the thirty chief men went down and came to David at harvest time at the cave of Adullam, while a troop of the Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.

**23:14** Now David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was then at Bethlehem.

**23:15** And David longed and said, "Oh, that someone would give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem, which is by the gate!"

**23:16** So the three mighty men broke through the camp of the Philistines, drew water from the well of Bethlehem that was by the gate, and carried it and brought it to David. Yet he refused to drink it, and he poured it out to the LORD.

**23:17** And he said, "Far be it from me, O LORD, that I should do this! Is this not the blood of the men who went at the peril of their lives?" Therefore he would not drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**23:18** Now Abishai the brother of Joab, the son of Zeruiah, was chief of the three. He brandished his spear against three hundred and slew them, and he had a name among the three.

**23:19** Was he not the most honored among the three? Therefore he became their commander; yet he did not attain to the three.

**23:20** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a valiant man, great in exploits, from Kabzeel—he struck down the two lionlike heroes of Moab. He also went down and slew the lion in the midst of a pit on a snowy day.

**23:21** And he struck down an Egyptian, a man of formidable appearance. Now the Egyptian had a spear in his hand, yet he went down to him with a staff, snatched the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.

**23:22** These things Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**23:23** He was more honored than the thirty, yet he did not attain to the three. And David appointed him over his bodyguard.

**23:24** Asahel the brother of Joab was among the thirty; Elhanan the son of Dodo of Bethlehem,

**23:25** Shammah the Harodite, Elika the Harodite,

**23:26** Helez the Paltite, Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite,

**23:27** Abiezer the Anathothite, Mebunnai the Hushathite,

**23:28** Zalmon the Ahohite, Maharai the Netophathite,

**23:29** Heleb the son of Baanah the Netophathite, Ittai the son of Ribai from Gibeah of the children of Benjamin,

**23:30** Benaiah a Pirathonite, Hiddai from the brooks of Gaash,

**23:31** Abi-albon the Arbathite, Azmaveth the Barhumite,

**23:32** Eliahba the Shaalbonite, of the sons of Jashen, Jonathan,

**23:33** Shammah the Hararite, Ahiam the son of Sharar the Hararite,

**23:34** Eliphelet the son of Ahasbai, the son of the Maacathite, Eliam the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite,

**23:35** Hezrai the Carmelite, Paarai the Arbite,

**23:36** Igal the son of Nathan of Zobah, Bani the Gadite,

**23:37** Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Beerothite, armor-bearer to Joab the son of Zeruiah,

**23:38** Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite,

**23:39** and Uriah the Hittite—thirty-seven in all.

## Translator Notes

- **23:8** — Qere reads 'the Eznite' and feminine 'one' agreeing with 'time'; ketiv reads a variant form 'ha-ezno' and masculine 'one.'
- **23:9** — Qere reads 'Dodo' and 'the mighty men' with the definite article; ketiv reads 'Dodai' and 'mighty men.'
- **23:13** — Qere reads 'three' of the thirty chief men; ketiv reads 'thirty.'
- **23:18** — Qere reads 'the three' (matching v. 19); ketiv reads 'the captains' or 'officers.'
- **23:20** — Qere reads 'a valiant man' and 'the lion'; ketiv reads 'a living man' and an alternate spelling of 'the lion.' In v. 21, qere reads 'a man' where ketiv reads 'who.'
- **23:35** — Qere reads 'Hezrai' (ketiv: 'Hezro'). In v. 37, qere reads singular 'armor-bearer' (ketiv: plural 'armor-bearers').
