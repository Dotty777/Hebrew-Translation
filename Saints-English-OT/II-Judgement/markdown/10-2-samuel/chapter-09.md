## Text

**9:1** Now David said, "Is there still anyone who is left of the house of Saul, that I may show him lovingkindness for the sake of Jonathan?"

**9:2** Now the house of Saul had a servant whose name was Ziba. When they had called him to David, the king said to him, "Are you Ziba?" And he said, "Your servant."

**9:3** And the king said, "Is there not still someone of the house of Saul, to whom I may show the lovingkindness of God?" And Ziba said to the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan who is lame in his feet."

**9:4** So the king said to him, "Where is he?" And Ziba said to the king, "Behold, he is in the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, in Lo-debar."

**9:5** Then King David sent and brought him out of the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, from Lo-debar.

**9:6** When Mephibosheth the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, came to David, he fell on his face and prostrated himself. And David said, "Mephibosheth." And he answered, "Behold, your servant!"

**9:7** Then David said to him, "Do not fear, for I will surely show you lovingkindness for the sake of Jonathan your father, and will restore to you all the land of Saul your grandfather; and you shall eat bread at my table continually."

**9:8** Then he bowed down and said, "What is your servant, that you should look upon such a dead dog as I?"

**9:9** And the king called Ziba, Saul's servant, and said to him, "All that belonged to Saul and to all his house I have given to your master's son.

**9:10** You therefore, and your sons and your servants, shall till the land for him, and you shall bring in the produce, that your master's son may have bread to eat; yet Mephibosheth your master's son shall eat bread at my table continually." Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

**9:11** Then Ziba said to the king, "According to all that my lord the king commands his servant, so shall your servant do." "And Mephibosheth," replied the king, "shall eat at my table as one of the sons of the king."

**9:12** Now Mephibosheth had a young son whose name was Micha. And all who dwelt in the house of Ziba were servants to Mephibosheth.

**9:13** So Mephibosheth dwelt in Jerusalem, for he ate continually at the table of the king. And he was lame in both his feet.

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — "Grandfather" translates Hebrew "father," which commonly refers to a grandfather or forefather.
- **9:11** — The Masoretic Text literally reads "at my table" without repeating a speaker tag; "replied the king" is supplied to mark the king's concluding declaration resuming verse ten.
