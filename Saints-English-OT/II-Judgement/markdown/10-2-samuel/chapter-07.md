## Text

**7:1** Now it came about, when the king was dwelling in his house and the LORD had given him rest on every side from all his enemies,

**7:2** that the king said to Nathan the prophet, "Look now, I am dwelling in a house of cedar, yet the ark of God dwells within the curtain."

**7:3** And Nathan said to the king, "Go, do all that is in your heart, for the LORD is with you."

**7:4** And it came to pass in that night that the word of the LORD came to Nathan, saying,

**7:5** "Go and say to My servant David, 'Thus says the LORD: Shall you build for Me a house for My dwelling?

**7:6** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought up the children of Israel from Egypt even to this day, but I have been walking about in a tent and in a tabernacle.

**7:7** In every place where I have walked among all the children of Israel, did I speak a word to any of the tribes of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built for Me a house of cedar?"'

**7:8** Now therefore, thus shall you say to My servant David, 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: I took you from the pasture, from following the flock, to be ruler over My people, over Israel.

**7:9** And I have been with you wherever you have gone, and have cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a great name, like the name of the great men who are on the earth.

**7:10** And I will appoint a place for My people Israel and will plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more; nor shall the sons of wickedness afflict them any more, as at the first,

**7:11** even from the day that I commanded judges to be over My people Israel. Moreover, I will give you rest from all your enemies. And the LORD declares to you that the LORD will make a house for you.

**7:12** When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who shall come forth from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom.

**7:13** He shall build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

**7:14** I will be a father to him, and he shall be a son to Me. When he commits iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men and with the stripes of the sons of men.

**7:15** Yet My lovingkindness shall not depart from him, as I removed it from Saul, whom I removed from before you.

**7:16** And your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever before you; your throne shall be established forever.'"

**7:17** According to all these words and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**7:18** Then King David went in and sat before the LORD, and he said, "Who am I, O Lord GOD, and what is my house, that You have brought me thus far?

**7:19** Yet even this was a small thing in Your eyes, O Lord GOD, and You have spoken also concerning the house of Your servant for a great while to come—and this is the law of man, O Lord GOD!

**7:20** And what more can David say to You? For You know Your servant, O Lord GOD.

**7:21** For the sake of Your word, and according to Your own heart, You have done all this greatness, to make it known to Your servant.

**7:22** Therefore You are great, O LORD God; for there is none like You, nor is there any God besides You, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

**7:23** And who is like Your people, like Israel, the one nation on the earth whom God went to redeem for Himself as a people, and to make for Himself a name, and to do for you great and awesome deeds for Your land, before Your people whom You redeemed for Yourself from Egypt, from the nations and their gods?

**7:24** For You have established for Yourself Your people Israel as Your very own people forever; and You, O LORD, have become their God.

**7:25** And now, O LORD God, confirm forever the word which You have spoken concerning Your servant and concerning his house, and do as You have spoken.

**7:26** So let Your name be magnified forever, saying, 'The LORD of hosts is God over Israel.' And let the house of Your servant David be established before You.

**7:27** For You, O LORD of hosts, God of Israel, have revealed to the ear of Your servant, saying, 'I will build a house for you.' Therefore Your servant has found it in his heart to pray this prayer to You.

**7:28** And now, O Lord GOD, You are God, and Your words are truth, and You have spoken this goodness to Your servant.

**7:29** Now therefore, let it please You to bless the house of Your servant, that it may continue forever before You; for You, O Lord GOD, have spoken it, and with Your blessing let the house of Your servant be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **7:7** — The Masoretic Text reads "tribes of Israel," whereas the parallel passage in First Chronicles seventeen six reads "judges of Israel."
- **7:16** — The Masoretic Text reads "before you," addressing David directly, whereas the Septuagint and Syriac read "before Me."
- **7:19** — Hebrew literally "the law of man" or "the instruction for mankind," understood either as David marveling at God's gracious charter for humanity or as a rhetorical exclamation, "Is this the manner of man?"
- **7:23** — The Masoretic Text shifts briefly to the plural "for you" (addressing Israel) and ends literally "from Egypt, nations and its gods."
