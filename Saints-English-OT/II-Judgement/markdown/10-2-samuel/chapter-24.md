## Text

**24:1** Again the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He incited David against them, saying, "Go, number Israel and Judah."

**24:2** And the king said to Joab, the commander of the army who was with him, "Pass now through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan even to Beersheba, and muster the people, so that I may know the number of the people."

**24:3** And Joab said to the king, "May the LORD your God add to the people a hundred times as many as they are, while the eyes of my lord the king look on! Yet why does my lord the king delight in this thing?"

**24:4** Yet the word of the king prevailed over Joab and over the commanders of the army. And Joab and the commanders of the army departed from before the king to muster the people of Israel.

**24:5** And they crossed over the Jordan and encamped at Aroer, on the south side of the city that is in the middle of the ravine of Gad, and on toward Jazer.

**24:6** And they came to Gilead and into the land of Tahtim-hodshi; and they arrived at Dan-jaan and around toward Sidon,

**24:7** and they came to the fortress of Tyre and to all the cities of the Hivites and of the Canaanites; then they went out to the South of Judah, to Beersheba.

**24:8** So when they had gone through all the land, they came to Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days.

**24:9** And Joab gave the sum of the numbering of the people to the king: and there were in Israel eight hundred thousand valiant men who drew the sword, and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand men.

**24:10** And David's heart struck him after he had numbered the people. So David said to the LORD, "I have sinned greatly in what I have done; but now, O LORD, take away, I pray, the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly."

**24:11** Now when David arose in the morning, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Gad, David's seer, saying,

**24:12** "Go and speak to David, 'Thus says the LORD: I hold out three things before you; choose one of them for yourself, that I may do it to you.'"

**24:13** So Gad came to David and told him, and he said to him, "Shall seven years of famine come upon you in your land? Or shall you flee three months before your adversaries while they pursue you? Or shall there be three days of pestilence in your land? Now consider and see what answer I shall return to Him who sent me."

**24:14** And David said to Gad, "I am in great distress. Let us fall, I pray, into the hand of the LORD, for His mercies are very great; but do not let me fall into the hand of man."

**24:15** So the LORD sent a pestilence upon Israel from the morning until the appointed time; and seventy thousand men of the people died from Dan to Beersheba.

**24:16** And when the angel stretched out his hand toward Jerusalem to destroy it, the LORD relented concerning the calamity, and He said to the angel who was bringing destruction among the people, "It is enough! Now stay your hand." And the angel of the LORD was by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

**24:17** Then David spoke to the LORD when he saw the angel who was striking the people, and he said, "Behold, I have sinned, and I have done wickedly; but these sheep, what have they done? Let Your hand, I pray, be against me and against my father's house."

**24:18** And Gad came to David that day and said to him, "Go up, erect an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."

**24:19** So David went up according to the word of Gad, as the LORD had commanded.

**24:20** Now Araunah looked out and saw the king and his servants coming toward him. So Araunah went out and bowed down before the king with his face to the ground.

**24:21** And Araunah said, "Why has my lord the king come to his servant?" And David said, "To buy the threshing floor from you, to build an altar to the LORD, that the plague may be restrained from the people."

**24:22** Then Araunah said to David, "Let my lord the king take and offer up whatever is good in his sight. Behold, here are the oxen for the burnt offering, and the threshing sledges and the yokes of the oxen for wood.

**24:23** All this, O king, Araunah gives to the king." And Araunah said to the king, "May the LORD your God accept you."

**24:24** But the king said to Araunah, "No, for I will surely buy it from you for a price; neither will I offer up to the LORD my God burnt offerings without cost." So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

**24:25** And David built there an altar to the LORD, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. So the LORD heeded the prayers for the land, and the plague was restrained from Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **24:13** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads 'seven years of famine,' in contrast to 'three years' in 1 Chronicles 21:12.
- **24:16** — Qere reads 'Araunah' here and in v. 18; ketiv preserves alternate consonantal spellings of the Jebusite name.
- **24:23** — The Hebrew 'hammelek' may be understood either as a vocative ('O king') within Araunah's address or in apposition ('Araunah the king').
- **24:25** — The Hebrew verb denotes the LORD answering supplication on behalf of the land, bringing the plague to an end.
