## Text

**13:1** Now it came to pass after this that Absalom the son of David had a beautiful sister, whose name was Tamar; and Amnon the son of David loved her.

**13:2** And Amnon was so distressed that he made himself sick on account of Tamar his sister, for she was a virgin, and it seemed impossible in Amnon's eyes to do anything to her.

**13:3** Now Amnon had a companion whose name was Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, David's brother; and Jonadab was a very shrewd man.

**13:4** And he said to him, "Why are you, the king's son, growing so gaunt morning after morning? Will you not tell me?" And Amnon said to him, "I love Tamar, the sister of Absalom my brother."

**13:5** Then Jonadab said to him, "Lie down upon your bed and feign yourself ill; and when your father comes to see you, you shall say to him, 'Please let Tamar my sister come and give me food to eat, and prepare the fare in my sight, that I may see it and eat from her hand.'"

**13:6** So Amnon lay down and feigned himself ill; and when the king came to see him, Amnon said to the king, "Please let Tamar my sister come and make two cakes in my sight, that I may eat from her hand."

**13:7** Then David sent home to Tamar, saying, "Go now to the house of Amnon your brother, and prepare food for him."

**13:8** So Tamar went to the house of Amnon her brother, and he was lying down. And she took dough and kneaded it, and shaped cakes in his sight, and baked the cakes.

**13:9** Then she took the pan and poured them out before him, but he refused to eat. And Amnon said, "Have every man go out from my presence." So every man went out from his presence.

**13:10** Then Amnon said to Tamar, "Bring the food into the inner chamber, that I may eat from your hand." So Tamar took the cakes which she had made, and brought them into the inner chamber to Amnon her brother.

**13:11** Now when she brought them near to him to eat, he took hold of her and said to her, "Come, lie with me, my sister."

**13:12** But she said to him, "No, my brother, do not violate me, for such a thing is not done in Israel! Do not commit this disgraceful folly.

**13:13** And I, where could I carry my reproach? And as for you, you would be like one of the fools in Israel. Now therefore, please speak to the king, for he will not withhold me from you."

**13:14** Yet he would not listen to her voice; and being stronger than she, he violated her and lay with her.

**13:15** Then Amnon hated her with an exceedingly great hatred, so that the hatred with which he hated her was greater than the love with which he had loved her. And Amnon said to her, "Arise, be gone!"

**13:16** And she said to him, "No! For this evil in sending me away is greater than the other that you have done to me." Yet he would not listen to her.

**13:17** Then he called his young man who attended him, and said, "Send this woman out from my presence now, and bolt the door behind her."

**13:18** Now she was wearing a tunic of many colors, for in such robes the virgin daughters of the king were attired. And his attendant brought her outside, and bolted the door behind her.

**13:19** Then Tamar put ashes upon her head, and tore the tunic of many colors that was on her; and she laid her hand upon her head and went away, crying out aloud as she went.

**13:20** And Absalom her brother said to her, "Has Amnon your brother been with you? Now therefore, my sister, hold your peace; he is your brother. Do not take this matter to heart." So Tamar dwelt desolate in the house of Absalom her brother.

**13:21** Now when King David heard of all these things, he was exceedingly angry.

**13:22** And Absalom spoke to Amnon neither bad nor good; for Absalom hated Amnon, because he had violated Tamar his sister.

**13:23** And it came to pass after two full years that Absalom had sheepshearers in Baal-hazor, which is beside Ephraim; and Absalom invited all the king's sons.

**13:24** Then Absalom came to the king and said, "Behold now, your servant has sheepshearers; please let the king and his servants go with your servant."

**13:25** But the king said to Absalom, "No, my son, let us not all go, lest we be a burden upon you." And he urged him, yet he would not go, but blessed him.

**13:26** Then Absalom said, "If not, please let Amnon my brother go with us." And the king said to him, "Why should he go with you?"

**13:27** Yet Absalom urged him, so he sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him.

**13:28** Now Absalom had commanded his young men, saying, "Watch now, when Amnon's heart is merry with wine, and when I say to you, 'Strike Amnon,' then put him to death. Do not fear; have I not commanded you? Be strong and be men of valor!"

**13:29** So the young men of Absalom did to Amnon just as Absalom had commanded. Then all the king's sons arose, and every man mounted his mule and fled.

**13:30** And it came to pass, while they were on the way, that the report came to David, saying, "Absalom has struck down all the king's sons, and not one of them is left!"

**13:31** Then the king arose, tore his garments, and lay upon the ground; and all his servants stood by with their garments torn.

**13:32** Then Jonadab the son of Shimeah, David's brother, answered and said, "Do not let my lord suppose that they have slain all the young men, the king's sons, for Amnon alone is dead; for by the command of Absalom this has been determined from the day he violated Tamar his sister.

**13:33** Now therefore, do not let my lord the king take the matter to heart, thinking, 'All the king's sons are dead,' for Amnon alone is dead."

**13:34** Meanwhile Absalom fled. And the young man who kept watch lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, many people were coming along the road behind him, from the side of the mountain.

**13:35** Then Jonadab said to the king, "Behold, the king's sons have come! According to the word of your servant, so it has come to pass."

**13:36** And it came to pass, as soon as he had finished speaking, that behold, the king's sons arrived, and they lifted up their voice and wept; and the king also and all his servants wept with an exceedingly great weeping.

**13:37** And Absalom fled and went to Talmai the son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. And David mourned for his son every day.

**13:38** So Absalom fled and went to Geshur, and was there three years.

**13:39** And the spirit of King David longed to go out to Absalom, for he was comforted concerning Amnon, since he was dead.

## Translator Notes

- **13:20** — The Hebrew here uses the spelling Aminon, generally understood as a contemptuous diminutive of Amnon.
- **13:33** — The Qere omits the conditional particle written in the Ketiv, reading cleanly 'for Amnon alone is dead.'
- **13:37** — The Qere reads 'Ammihud' (followed here), whereas the Ketiv reads 'Ammihur.' The Hebrew subject of 'mourned' is an unexpressed pronoun referring back to King David.
- **13:39** — The Hebrew verb is feminine singular, implicitly referring to the spirit or soul of King David longing to go out to Absalom.
