## Text

**4:1** The sons of Judah were Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.

**4:2** And Reaiah the son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the families of the Zorathites.

**4:3** And these were from the father of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash; and the name of their sister was Hazzelelponi.

**4:4** And Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.

**4:5** And Ashhur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

**4:6** And Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.

**4:7** And the sons of Helah were Zereth, Zohar, and Ethnan.

**4:8** And Koz fathered Anub, Zobebah, and the families of Aharhel the son of Harum.

**4:9** Now Jabez was more honorable than his brethren; and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, "Because I bore him in pain."

**4:10** And Jabez called upon the God of Israel, saying, "Oh, that You would surely bless me and enlarge my border, and that Your hand would be with me, and that You would keep me from harm, so that it may not bring me pain!" And God brought about what he had asked.

**4:11** Now Chelub the brother of Shuhah fathered Mehir, who was the father of Eshton.

**4:12** And Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah.

**4:13** And the sons of Kenaz were Othniel and Seraiah; and the sons of Othniel were Hathath.

**4:14** And Meonothai fathered Ophrah; and Seraiah fathered Joab, the father of the Valley of Craftsmen, for they were craftsmen.

**4:15** And the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh were Iru, Elah, and Naam; and the sons of Elah: even Kenaz.

**4:16** And the sons of Jehallelel were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.

**4:17** And the sons of Ezrah were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. And she conceived Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.

**4:18** And his Judean wife bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. And these were the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered took.

**4:19** And the sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham, were the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

**4:20** And the sons of Shimon were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

**4:21** The sons of Shelah the son of Judah were Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the families of the house of the linen workers at Beth-ashbea;

**4:22** and Jokim, the men of Cozeba, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. Now these records are ancient.

**4:23** These were the potters and those who dwelt at Netaim and Gederah; there they dwelt with the king for his work.

**4:24** The sons of Simeon were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul;

**4:25** Shallum was his son, Mibsam his son, and Mishma his son.

**4:26** And the sons of Mishma were Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, and Shimei his son.

**4:27** And Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters; but his brethren did not have many children, nor did all their family multiply like the children of Judah.

**4:28** They dwelt at Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual,

**4:29** at Bilhah, at Ezem, at Tolad,

**4:30** at Bethuel, at Hormah, at Ziklag,

**4:31** at Beth-marcaboth, at Hazar-susim, at Beth-biri, and at Shaaraim. These were their cities until the reign of David.

**4:32** And their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five cities—

**4:33** along with all their villages that were around these cities as far as Baal. These were their dwelling places, and they had their genealogical record.

**4:34** Also Meshobab, Jamlech, and Joshah the son of Amaziah,

**4:35** and Joel, and Jehu the son of Joshibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,

**4:36** also Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah,

**4:37** and Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah—

**4:38** these mentioned by name were leaders in their families, and their fathers' houses increased greatly.

**4:39** So they went to the entrance of Gedor, as far as the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.

**4:40** And they found rich and good pasture, and the land was broad, quiet, and peaceful; for some of the descendants of Ham had dwelt there formerly.

**4:41** And these who were recorded by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah; and they struck down their tents and the Meunites who were found there, and utterly destroyed them to this day. Then they dwelt in their place, because there was pasture there for their flocks.

**4:42** And some of them, five hundred men of the sons of Simeon, went to Mount Seir, with Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, at their head.

**4:43** And they struck down the remnant of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have dwelt there to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **4:7** — Translates the Qere reading ve-Tzohar (and Zohar); the Ketiv reads Yitzhar (Izhar).
- **4:9** — In Hebrew, the name Jabez (Yabetz) plays on the word for pain (otsev), a motif echoed in his prayer in verse ten.
- **4:20** — Translates the Qere reading ve-Tilon (and Tilon); the Ketiv reads ve-Tolon (and Tolon).
- **4:41** — Translates the Qere reading ha-Meunim (the Meunites); the Ketiv reads ha-Meinim.
