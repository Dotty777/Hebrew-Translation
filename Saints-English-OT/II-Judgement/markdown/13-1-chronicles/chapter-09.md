## Text

**9:1** So all Israel was enrolled by genealogies, and behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel; and Judah was carried away captive to Babylon for their unfaithfulness.

**9:2** Now the first inhabitants who were in their possession in their cities were Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the Nethinim.

**9:3** And in Jerusalem dwelt some of the sons of Judah, and some of the sons of Benjamin, and some of the sons of Ephraim and Manasseh:

**9:4** Uthai the son of Ammihud, the son of Omri, the son of Imri, the son of Bani, of the sons of Perez the son of Judah.

**9:5** And of the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.

**9:6** And of the sons of Zerah: Jeuel, and their brethren, six hundred and ninety.

**9:7** And of the sons of Benjamin: Sallu the son of Meshullam, the son of Hodaviah, the son of Hassenuah;

**9:8** and Ibneiah the son of Jeroham; and Elah the son of Uzzi, the son of Michri; and Meshullam the son of Shephatiah, the son of Reuel, the son of Ibnijah;

**9:9** and their brethren, according to their generations, nine hundred and fifty-six. All these men were heads of fathers' houses according to the house of their fathers.

**9:10** And of the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin;

**9:11** and Azariah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, the ruler of the house of God;

**9:12** and Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah; and Maasai the son of Adiel, the son of Jahzerah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Meshillemith, the son of Immer;

**9:13** and their brethren, heads of their fathers' house, one thousand seven hundred and sixty—men of great ability for the work of the service of the house of God.

**9:14** And of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari;

**9:15** and Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah the son of Mica, the son of Zichri, the son of Asaph;

**9:16** and Obadiah the son of Shemaiah, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah the son of Asa, the son of Elkanah, who dwelt in the villages of the Netophathites.

**9:17** And the gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their brethren; Shallum was the chief.

**9:18** Until this time they were at the king's gate toward the east; they were the gatekeepers for the camps of the sons of Levi.

**9:19** And Shallum the son of Kore, the son of Ebiasaph, the son of Korah, and his brethren of his father's house, the Korahites, were over the work of the service, keepers of the thresholds of the tent; and their fathers had been over the camp of the LORD, keepers of the entrance.

**9:20** And Phinehas the son of Eleazar was ruler over them in former times; the LORD was with him.

**9:21** Zechariah the son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper of the doorway of the tent of meeting.

**9:22** All those chosen as gatekeepers at the thresholds were two hundred and twelve. They were enrolled by their genealogies in their villages; David and Samuel the seer had appointed them to their office of trust.

**9:23** So they and their sons were over the gates of the house of the LORD—the house of the tent—by watches.

**9:24** On the four sides were the gatekeepers: toward the east, the west, the north, and the south.

**9:25** And their brethren in their villages were to come for seven days from time to time to be with them.

**9:26** For the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were in an office of trust, and they were over the chambers and over the treasuries of the house of God.

**9:27** And they lodged all around the house of God, because the watch was upon them, and they had charge of the key to open it morning by morning.

**9:28** And some of them were in charge of the vessels of service, for they brought them in by count and took them out by count.

**9:29** Some of them also were appointed over the furnishings and over all the sacred vessels, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices.

**9:30** And some of the sons of the priests compounded the ointment of the spices.

**9:31** And Mattithiah of the Levites, who was the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, had the trusted charge over the things baked in flat pans.

**9:32** And some of their brethren of the sons of the Kohathites were in charge of the showbread, to prepare it every Sabbath.

**9:33** Now these are the singers, heads of fathers' houses of the Levites, dwelling in the chambers, free from other duties; for day and night they were engaged in their work.

**9:34** These were the heads of the fathers' houses of the Levites, chief men according to their generations; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

**9:35** Now in Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon, Jeiel, whose wife's name was Maacah.

**9:36** And his firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,

**9:37** Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth.

**9:38** And Mikloth fathered Shimeam. They also dwelt opposite their brethren in Jerusalem, with their brethren.

**9:39** And Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.

**9:40** And the son of Jonathan was Merib-Baal, and Merib-Baal fathered Micah.

**9:41** And the sons of Micah were Pithon, Melech, and Tahrea.

**9:42** And Ahaz fathered Jarah, and Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Moza.

**9:43** And Moza fathered Binea, and Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah his son, and Azel his son.

**9:44** And Azel had six sons, and these are their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Nethinim were hereditary temple servants assigned to assist the Levites in the duties of the sanctuary.
- **9:4** — Translated according to the Masoretic Qere reading, of the sons of; the written Ketiv combines the consonants into a single form resembling Benjamin.
- **9:33** — Translated according to the Masoretic Qere reading, exempt or free from other duties; the written Ketiv presents an orthographic variant with the same meaning.
- **9:35** — Translated according to the Masoretic Qere reading, Jeiel; the written Ketiv reads Jeuel.
