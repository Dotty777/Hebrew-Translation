## Text

**7:1** Now the sons of Issachar were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron—four in all.

**7:2** The sons of Tola were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Jibsam, and Shemuel, heads of their fathers' houses—of Tola—mighty men of valor according to their generations; their number in the days of David was twenty-two thousand six hundred.

**7:3** The son of Uzzi was Izrahiah, and the sons of Izrahiah were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah—five, all of them chiefs.

**7:4** And with them, according to their generations by their fathers' houses, were bands of troops for war, thirty-six thousand, for they had many wives and sons.

**7:5** Now their brethren among all the families of Issachar were mighty men of valor, eighty-seven thousand enrolled by genealogy in all.

**7:6** The sons of Benjamin were Bela, Becher, and Jediael—three in all.

**7:7** The sons of Bela were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri—five in all, heads of fathers' houses, mighty men of valor; and those enrolled by genealogy were twenty-two thousand and thirty-four.

**7:8** The sons of Becher were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth; all these were the sons of Becher.

**7:9** And their enrollment by genealogy according to their generations, heads of their fathers' houses, mighty men of valor, was twenty thousand two hundred.

**7:10** The son of Jediael was Bilhan, and the sons of Bilhan were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

**7:11** All these were the sons of Jediael, according to the heads of their fathers' houses, mighty men of valor, seventeen thousand two hundred going out to war with the army.

**7:12** Also Shuppim and Huppim were the sons of Ir, and Hushim was the son of Aher.

**7:13** The sons of Naphtali were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum, the sons of Bilhah.

**7:14** The sons of Manasseh were Asriel, whom his Aramean concubine bore; she bore Machir, the father of Gilead.

**7:15** And Machir took a wife for Huppim and Shuppim; the name of his sister was Maacah. And the name of the second was Zelophehad, and Zelophehad had daughters.

**7:16** And Maacah the wife of Machir bore a son, and she called his name Peresh; the name of his brother was Sheresh, and his sons were Ulam and Rakem.

**7:17** The son of Ulam was Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh.

**7:18** And his sister Hammoleketh bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah.

**7:19** And the sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

**7:20** Now the sons of Ephraim were Shuthelah, Bered his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, Tahath his son,

**7:21** Zabad his son, Shuthelah his son, and Ezer and Elead. Now the men of Gath who were born in the land slew them, because they came down to take away their livestock.

**7:22** Then Ephraim their father mourned many days, and his brethren came to comfort him.

**7:23** And he went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son; and he called his name Beriah, because calamity had come upon his house.

**7:24** Now his daughter was Sheerah, who built Lower and Upper Beth Horon, as well as Uzzen Sheerah.

**7:25** And Rephah was his son, along with Resheph, Telah his son, Tahan his son,

**7:26** Ladan his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son,

**7:27** Nun his son, and Joshua his son.

**7:28** Now their possessions and dwellings were Bethel and its villages, to the east Naaran, to the west Gezer and its villages, also Shechem and its villages, as far as Ayyah and its villages;

**7:29** and along the borders of the sons of Manasseh were Beth Shean and its villages, Taanach and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, and Dor and its villages. In these dwelt the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel.

**7:30** The sons of Asher were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and Serah their sister.

**7:31** The sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel, who was the father of Birzaith.

**7:32** And Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and Shua their sister.

**7:33** The sons of Japhlet were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were the sons of Japhlet.

**7:34** The sons of Shemer were Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram.

**7:35** And the sons of his brother Helem were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.

**7:36** The sons of Zophah were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah,

**7:37** Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera.

**7:38** The sons of Jether were Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.

**7:39** The sons of Ulla were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia.

**7:40** All these were the sons of Asher, heads of their fathers' houses, choice men, mighty men of valor, chief of the princes. And their number enrolled by genealogy for military service in war was twenty-six thousand men.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Following the Masoretic Qere (read tradition), "Jashub"; the Ketiv (written consonantal text) reads "Jashib."
- **7:10** — Following the Masoretic Qere, "Jeush"; the Ketiv reads "Jeish."
- **7:23** — The name Beriah echoes the Hebrew phrase for "in calamity" or "in evil," recalling the tragic slaughter of Ephraim's earlier sons.
- **7:31** — Following the Masoretic Qere, "Birzaith"; the Ketiv reads "Birzoth."
- **7:34** — Following the Masoretic Qere, "Rohgah" and "Hubbah"; the Ketiv reads "Rohegah" and "Jehubbah."
