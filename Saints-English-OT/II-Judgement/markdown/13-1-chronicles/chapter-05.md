## Text

**5:1** Now the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel—for he was the firstborn, yet because he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph the son of Israel, though he was not to be enrolled in the genealogy according to the birthright.

**5:2** For Judah prevailed over his brethren, and from him came a ruler, yet the birthright belonged to Joseph.

**5:3** The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

**5:4** The sons of Joel were Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

**5:5** Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,

**5:6** and Beerah his son, whom Tilgath-Pilneser king of Assyria carried into exile; he was a prince of the Reubenites.

**5:7** And his brethren by his families, when the genealogy of their generations was enrolled, were Jeiel the chief, and Zechariah,

**5:8** and Bela the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel. He dwelt in Aroer, even as far as Nebo and Baal Meon.

**5:9** Eastward also he dwelt as far as the entrance of the wilderness extending from the River Euphrates, because their livestock had multiplied in the land of Gilead.

**5:10** Now in the days of Saul they waged war with the Hagrites, who fell by their hand; and they dwelt in their tents throughout all the territory east of Gilead.

**5:11** And the sons of Gad dwelt opposite them in the land of Bashan as far as Salecah:

**5:12** Joel was the chief, Shapham the second, and Janai and Shaphat in Bashan.

**5:13** And their brethren, according to their fathers' houses, were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber—seven in all.

**5:14** These were the sons of Abihail the son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz.

**5:15** Ahi the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, was chief of their fathers' house.

**5:16** And they dwelt in Gilead, in Bashan and its villages, and in all the pasturelands of Sharon to their farthest borders.

**5:17** All of these were enrolled by genealogy in the days of Jotham king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

**5:18** The sons of Reuben, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had valiant men—men who bore shield and sword, drew the bow, and were trained in warfare—forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty able to go out to war.

**5:19** They made war against the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab.

**5:20** And they were helped against them, and the Hagrites and all who were with them were delivered into their hand; for they cried out to God in the battle, and He answered their prayer, because they put their trust in Him.

**5:21** So they carried away their livestock—fifty thousand of their camels, two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, and two thousand donkeys—and also one hundred thousand souls.

**5:22** For many fell slain, because the war was of God. And they dwelt in their place until the exile.

**5:23** Now the sons of the half-tribe of Manasseh dwelt in the land; from Bashan as far as Baal Hermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon, they multiplied.

**5:24** And these were the heads of their fathers' houses: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel—mighty men of valor, men of renown, heads of their fathers' houses.

**5:25** Yet they were unfaithful to the God of their fathers, and played the harlot after the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God had destroyed before them.

**5:26** Therefore the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria and the spirit of Tilgath-Pilneser king of Assyria, and he carried the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh into exile, and brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river of Gozan, to this day.

**5:27** The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**5:28** And the sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

**5:29** And the children of Amram were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. And the sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**5:30** Eleazar fathered Phinehas, and Phinehas fathered Abishua.

**5:31** Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi.

**5:32** Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, and Zerahiah fathered Meraioth.

**5:33** Meraioth fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.

**5:34** Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz.

**5:35** Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan.

**5:36** And Johanan fathered Azariah—it was he who ministered as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

**5:37** And Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.

**5:38** Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum.

**5:39** Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah.

**5:40** Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak.

**5:41** And Jehozadak went into exile when the LORD carried away Judah and Jerusalem by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Reuben forfeited the privileges of the firstborn by defiling his father Jacob's bed (Genesis 35:22; 49:3-4), so the birthright passed to the sons of Joseph while preeminence and royal leadership passed to Judah.
- **5:6** — Chronicles spells the Assyrian king's name Tilgath-Pilneser (also in verse 26), corresponding to Tiglath-Pileser in 2 Kings 15:29.
- **5:26** — Pul was the Babylonian throne name of Tilgath-Pilneser III of Assyria; the Hebrew conjunction between the two names may also be understood explicatively as "even".
- **5:27** — In the Masoretic Hebrew text, verses 27 through 41 conclude chapter 5; most English translations place this Levitical genealogy at 1 Chronicles 6:1-15.
