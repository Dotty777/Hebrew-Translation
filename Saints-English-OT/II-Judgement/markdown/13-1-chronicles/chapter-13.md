## Text

**13:1** Then David took counsel with the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and with every leader.

**13:2** And David said to all the assembly of Israel, "If it seems good to you, and if it is from the LORD our God, let us send out far and wide to our brethren who remain in all the lands of Israel, and with them to the priests and the Levites in the cities of their pasturelands, that they may gather together to us;

**13:3** and let us bring back the ark of our God to us, for we did not inquire at it in the days of Saul."

**13:4** Then all the assembly said that they would do so, for the matter was right in the eyes of all the people.

**13:5** So David assembled all Israel together, from Shihor of Egypt to the entrance of Hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim.

**13:6** And David and all Israel went up to Baalah, to Kiriath-jearim, which belongs to Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God the LORD, who sits enthroned above the cherubim, which is called by the Name.

**13:7** So they carried the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Abinadab, and Uzza and Ahio were driving the cart.

**13:8** And David and all Israel were celebrating before God with all their might, with songs, with lyres, with harps, with tambourines, with cymbals, and with trumpets.

**13:9** And when they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, Uzza stretched out his hand to take hold of the ark, for the oxen stumbled.

**13:10** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Uzza, and He struck him because he had put out his hand to the ark; and he died there before God.

**13:11** And David became angry because the LORD had broken out with an outburst against Uzza; and he called that place Perez-uzza, as it is to this day.

**13:12** And David was afraid of God that day, saying, "How shall I bring the ark of God to me?"

**13:13** So David did not move the ark to himself into the City of David, but turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

**13:14** And the ark of God remained with the household of Obed-edom in his house three months; and the LORD blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that belonged to him.

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — The Hebrew phrase literally reads where the Name is called, referring to the LORD's covenant Name proclaimed over the ark.
- **13:11** — Perez-uzza means Outburst against Uzza or Breach of Uzza, echoing the Hebrew verb and noun for the LORD's sudden judgment.
- **13:13** — Obed-edom the Gittite was likely a Levite from Gath-rimmon, a Levitical city in Dan assigned to the Kohathites.
