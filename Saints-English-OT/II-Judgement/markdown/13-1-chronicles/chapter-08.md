## Text

**8:1** Now Benjamin fathered Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third,

**8:2** Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

**8:3** And Bela had sons: Addar, Gera, Abihud,

**8:4** Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah,

**8:5** Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram.

**8:6** Now these were the sons of Ehud—they were the heads of fathers' houses of the inhabitants of Geba, and they carried them into exile to Manahath:

**8:7** Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera—it was he who carried them into exile—and he fathered Uzza and Ahihud.

**8:8** And Shaharaim fathered children in the country of Moab after he had sent them away—Hushim and Baara, his wives.

**8:9** By Hodesh his wife he fathered Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam,

**8:10** Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of fathers' houses.

**8:11** And by Hushim he fathered Abitub and Elpaal.

**8:12** The sons of Elpaal were Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod with its surrounding towns.

**8:13** And Beriah and Shema—they were the heads of the fathers' houses of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who put to flight the inhabitants of Gath.

**8:14** Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth,

**8:15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder,

**8:16** Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.

**8:17** Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber,

**8:18** Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal.

**8:19** Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi,

**8:20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel,

**8:21** Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei.

**8:22** Ishpan, Eber, Eliel,

**8:23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan,

**8:24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah,

**8:26** Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah,

**8:27** Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.

**8:28** These were heads of fathers' houses according to their generations, chief men; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

**8:30** His firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab,

**8:31** Gedor, Ahio, and Zecher.

**8:39** And the sons of Eshek his brother were Ulam his firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third.

**8:40** And the sons of Ulam were mighty men of valor, archers, having many sons and grandsons, one hundred and fifty. All these were of the sons of Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — In verses 6 and 7, the plural verb translated "carried them into exile" is resumed by the singular pronoun pointing to Gera as the one who led the relocation from Geba to Manahath.
- **8:25** — The Masoretic written text (ketiv) reads "Peniel," whereas the traditional read form (qere) followed here is "Penuel."
- **8:29** — The Masoretic Text omits the personal name of the father of Gibeon here; the parallel genealogy in 1 Chronicles 9:35 identifies him as Jeiel.
- **8:33** — Saul's son Eshbaal and Jonathan's son Merib-baal (verse 34) appear in 2 Samuel as Ish-bosheth and Mephibosheth, substituting the element "bosheth" ("shame") for "Baal."
