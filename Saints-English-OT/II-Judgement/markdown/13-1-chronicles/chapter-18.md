## Text

**18:1** Now after this it came to pass that David struck the Philistines and subdued them, and he took Gath and its towns from the hand of the Philistines.

**18:2** Then he struck Moab, and the Moabites became David's servants, bringing tribute.

**18:3** And David struck Hadadezer king of Zobah as far as Hamath, as he went to establish his power by the River Euphrates.

**18:4** David captured from him one thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. Also David hamstrung all the chariot horses, except that he spared enough of them for one hundred chariots.

**18:5** When the Syrians of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men of the Syrians.

**18:6** Then David stationed garrisons in Syria of Damascus, and the Syrians became David's servants, bringing tribute. And the LORD preserved David wherever he went.

**18:7** And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

**18:8** Also from Tibhath and from Chun, cities of Hadadezer, David took a very great quantity of bronze; with it Solomon made the bronze Sea, the pillars, and the vessels of bronze.

**18:9** Now when Tou king of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,

**18:10** he sent Hadoram his son to King David, to greet him and bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him—for Hadadezer had been at war with Tou—and with him he sent all kinds of vessels of gold, silver, and bronze.

**18:11** King David also dedicated these to the LORD, along with the silver and gold that he had brought from all the nations—from Edom, from Moab, from the sons of Ammon, from the Philistines, and from Amalek.

**18:12** Moreover Abishai the son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

**18:13** He also placed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites became servants to David. And the LORD preserved David wherever he went.

**18:14** So David reigned over all Israel, and administered judgment and righteousness to all his people.

**18:15** Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder;

**18:16** Zadok the son of Ahitub and Abimelech the son of Abiathar were the priests; Shavsha was scribe;

**18:17** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and the sons of David were chief officials at the king's side.

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — The object 'garrisons' is implied in the Hebrew verb here, matching the explicit wording of the parallel clause in verse 13.
- **18:10** — The Masoretic Ketiv and Qere differ only in full versus defective spelling of the infinitive 'to ask' (translated 'to greet'), with no difference in sense.
- **18:16** — The Masoretic Text of Chronicles reads 'Abimelech' and 'Shavsha' here, differing in spelling from the parallel names in 2 Samuel 8:17.
