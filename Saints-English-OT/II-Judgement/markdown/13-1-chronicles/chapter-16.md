## Text

**16:1** So they brought the ark of God and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it; and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**16:2** When David had finished offering the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.

**16:3** Then he distributed to everyone of Israel, both man and woman, to each person a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a cake of raisins.

**16:4** And he appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of the LORD, to commemorate, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD God of Israel:

**16:5** Asaph the chief, and second to him Zechariah; then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel with harps and lyres, while Asaph sounded aloud with the cymbals;

**16:6** and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests regularly blew the trumpets before the ark of the covenant of God.

**16:7** On that day David first appointed this thanksgiving to the LORD by the hand of Asaph and his brethren:

**16:8** Give thanks to the LORD, call upon His name; make known His deeds among the peoples.

**16:9** Sing to Him, sing praises to Him; tell of all His wondrous works.

**16:10** Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

**16:11** Seek the LORD and His strength; continually seek His face.

**16:12** Remember His marvelous works which He has done, His wonders, and the judgments of His mouth,

**16:13** O seed of Israel His servant, you sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**16:14** He is the LORD our God; His judgments are throughout all the earth.

**16:15** Remember His covenant forever, the word which He commanded for a thousand generations—

**16:16** the covenant which He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac,

**16:17** which He confirmed to Jacob as a statute, to Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**16:18** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan as the allotted portion of your inheritance,"

**16:19** when you were few in number—very few indeed, and sojourners in it.

**16:20** When they wandered from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people,

**16:21** He permitted no one to oppress them; indeed, He rebuked kings for their sake:

**16:22** "Do not touch My anointed ones, and do My prophets no harm."

**16:23** Sing to the LORD, all the earth; proclaim the good tidings of His salvation from day to day.

**16:25** For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; He is also to be feared above all gods.

**16:26** For all the gods of the peoples are worthless idols, but the LORD made the heavens.

**16:27** Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and gladness are in His place.

**16:28** Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

**16:29** Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; bring an offering and come before Him. Worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness.

**16:30** Tremble before Him, all the earth; indeed, the world is firmly established, it shall not be moved.

**16:31** Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; and let them say among the nations, "The LORD reigns!"

**16:32** Let the sea roar, and all its fullness; let the field exult, and all that is in it.

**16:33** Then the trees of the forest shall sing for joy before the LORD, for He is coming to judge the earth.

**16:34** Oh, give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; for His lovingkindness endures forever.

**16:35** And say, "Save us, O God of our salvation; gather us together and deliver us from the nations, to give thanks to Your holy name, and to glory in Your praise."

**16:36** Blessed be the LORD God of Israel from everlasting to everlasting! And all the people said, "Amen!" and praised the LORD.

**16:37** So he left Asaph and his brethren there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark continually, as every day's work required;

**16:38** and Obed-Edom with their sixty-eight brethren, including Obed-Edom the son of Jeduthun, and Hosah, to be gatekeepers;

**16:39** and he left Zadok the priest and his brethren the priests before the tabernacle of the LORD at the high place that was at Gibeon,

**16:40** to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, and to do according to all that is written in the Law of the LORD which He commanded Israel;

**16:41** and with them Heman and Jeduthun and the rest of the chosen men who were designated by name, to give thanks to the LORD, because His lovingkindness endures forever;

**16:42** and with them Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets and cymbals for those who should sound aloud, and instruments for the songs of God; and the sons of Jeduthun were stationed at the gate.

**16:43** Then all the people departed, every man to his house; and David returned to bless his own household.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The rare Hebrew word eshpar appears only here and in 2 Samuel 6:19; it is traditionally rendered 'a portion of meat,' though some scholars interpret it as a cake of dates.
- **16:15** — The Hebrew text here uses the plural imperative 'Remember' addressed to the congregation, whereas the parallel in Psalm 105:8 reads 'He remembers.'
- **16:23** — David's hymn of thanksgiving in verses 8 through 36 draws together passages from Psalms 105, 96, and 106 adapted for the installation of the ark in Jerusalem.
- **16:27** — The wording 'in His place' suits the tent David prepared for the ark in Jerusalem (15:1, 3), contrasting with 'in His sanctuary' in the parallel line of Psalm 96:6.
