## Text

**29:1** Then King David said to the entire assembly, "Solomon my son, the only one whom God has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great; for the palace is not for man, but for the LORD God.

**29:2** Now with all my might I have made preparation for the house of my God: the gold for the things of gold, and the silver for the things of silver, and the bronze for the things of bronze, the iron for the things of iron, and the wood for the things of wood, onyx stones and stones for setting, stones of dark luster and of variegated colors, and all manner of precious stones, and marble stones in abundance.

**29:3** Moreover, because of my delight in the house of my God, I have a personal treasure of gold and silver, which I have given to the house of my God, over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house:

**29:4** three thousand talents of gold from the gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of purified silver, to coat the walls of the houses,

**29:5** the gold for the things of gold and the silver for the things of silver, and for all the work to be done by the hand of craftsmen. Who then offers willingly to consecrate himself this day to the LORD?"

**29:6** Then the chiefs of the fathers' houses, the rulers of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the overseers of the king's work gave willingly.

**29:7** And they gave for the service of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand darics of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.

**29:8** And whoever had precious stones in his possession gave them to the treasury of the house of the LORD, under the charge of Jehiel the Gershonite.

**29:9** Then the people rejoiced because they had offered so willingly, for with a whole heart they offered freely to the LORD; and King David also rejoiced with great joy.

**29:10** Then David blessed the LORD in the sight of all the assembly, and David said, "Blessed are You, O LORD, God of Israel our father, from everlasting to everlasting.

**29:11** To You, O LORD, belong the greatness and the might, the splendor, the victory, and the majesty; for everything in the heavens and upon the earth is Yours. Yours, O LORD, is the kingdom, and You are lifted high as head over all.

**29:12** Both riches and honor proceed from You, and You hold dominion over all; in Your hand are power and might, and in Your hand it lies to make great and to strengthen all.

**29:13** And now, O our God, we give thanks to You and praise Your glorious name.

**29:14** Yet who am I, and who are my people, that we should possess strength to offer so willingly as this? For all things come from You, and out of Your own hand we have given to You.

**29:15** For we are strangers before You, and sojourners, as all our fathers were; our days upon the earth are like a shadow, and there is no abiding.

**29:16** O LORD our God, all this abundance that we have made ready to build for You a house for Your holy name comes from Your own hand, and all of it is Yours.

**29:17** And I know, O my God, that You examine the heart and take pleasure in uprightness. As for me, in the uprightness of my heart I have freely offered all these things; and now I have beheld with joy Your people, who are present here, offering willingly to You.

**29:18** O LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel our fathers, preserve this forever in the intent of the thoughts of the heart of Your people, and establish their heart toward You.

**29:19** And give to Solomon my son a whole heart to keep Your commandments, Your testimonies, and Your statutes, to perform all things, and to build the palace for which I have made preparation."

**29:20** Then David said to all the assembly, "Now bless the LORD your God." So all the assembly blessed the LORD, the God of their fathers, and bowed low and prostrated themselves before the LORD and the king.

**29:21** And on the next day they offered sacrifices to the LORD and presented burnt offerings to the LORD: one thousand bulls, one thousand rams, and one thousand lambs, together with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**29:22** So they ate and drank before the LORD on that day with great gladness. And they made Solomon the son of David king a second time, and anointed him to the LORD to be ruler, and Zadok to be priest.

**29:23** Then Solomon sat upon the throne of the LORD as king in place of David his father, and he prospered; and all Israel obeyed him.

**29:24** And all the leaders and the mighty men, and also all the sons of King David, pledged their allegiance to King Solomon.

**29:25** And the LORD magnified Solomon exceedingly in the sight of all Israel, and bestowed upon him such royal majesty as had not been upon any king before him over Israel.

**29:26** Now David the son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.

**29:27** And the span of time that he reigned over Israel was forty years: seven years he reigned in Hebron, and thirty-three years he reigned in Jerusalem.

**29:28** Then he died in a good old age, full of days, riches, and honor; and Solomon his son reigned in his place.

**29:29** Now the acts of King David, from first to last, behold, they are written in the records of Samuel the seer, and in the records of Nathan the prophet, and in the records of Gad the seer,

**29:30** together with all his reign and his might, and the times that passed over him, over Israel, and over all the kingdoms of the lands.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Hebrew habbirah ("the palace") is a term used in Late Biblical Hebrew for a grand palace-sanctuary or fortified temple complex; it recurs in 29:19.
- **29:5** — Literally "to fill his hand," a Hebrew idiom denoting consecration and the presentation of a willing offering to the LORD.
- **29:15** — Hebrew miqweh literally means "hope" or "expectation," here conveying the transience of mortal life with no lasting stay on earth.
- **29:16** — Ketiv/Qere: the Ketiv reads the feminine pronoun hi while the Qere reads the masculine pronoun hu to agree with the masculine noun for "abundance"; the translation follows the Qere.
- **29:24** — Literally "gave a hand under King Solomon," a Hebrew idiom expressing formal submission and pledged loyalty.
