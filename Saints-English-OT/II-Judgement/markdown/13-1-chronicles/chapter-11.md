## Text

**11:1** Then all Israel gathered together to David at Hebron, saying, "Behold, we are your bone and your flesh.

**11:2** Also in time past, even when Saul was king, you were the one who led Israel out and brought them in; and the LORD your God said to you, 'You shall shepherd My people Israel, and you shall be ruler over My people Israel.'"

**11:3** So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and David made a covenant with them at Hebron before the LORD. And they anointed David king over Israel, according to the word of the LORD by the hand of Samuel.

**11:4** And David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus, and there were the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land.

**11:5** And the inhabitants of Jebus said to David, "You shall not enter here." Nevertheless David captured the stronghold of Zion, which is the City of David.

**11:6** Now David said, "Whoever strikes the Jebusites first shall become chief and commander." So Joab the son of Zeruiah went up first and became chief.

**11:7** Then David dwelt in the stronghold; therefore they called it the City of David.

**11:8** And he built up the city all around, from the Millo even to the surrounding area; and Joab restored the rest of the city.

**11:9** So David went on and became continually greater, and the LORD of hosts was with him.

**11:10** Now these are the chiefs of the mighty men whom David had, who strongly supported him in his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel.

**11:11** And this is the number of the mighty men whom David had: Jashobeam the son of Hachmoni, chief of the captains; he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain at one time.

**11:12** And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodo, the Ahohite, who was among the three mighty men.

**11:13** He was with David at Pas Dammim, where the Philistines were gathered together for battle; and there was a plot of ground full of barley, and the people fled before the Philistines.

**11:14** But they took their stand in the midst of the plot and defended it, and struck down the Philistines; and the LORD brought about a great deliverance.

**11:15** Now three of the thirty chief men went down to the rock to David, into the cave of Adullam; and the camp of the Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.

**11:16** And David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was then at Bethlehem.

**11:17** And David longed and said, "Oh, that someone would give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem, which is by the gate!"

**11:18** So the three broke through the camp of the Philistines, drew water from the well of Bethlehem, which is by the gate, and carried it and brought it to David. Yet David would not drink it, but poured it out to the LORD.

**11:19** And he said, "Far be it from me, before my God, to do this! Shall I drink the blood of these men who went at the peril of their lives? For at the peril of their lives they brought it." Therefore he refused to drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**11:20** Now Abishai the brother of Joab was chief of the three. And he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain, and he had a name among the three.

**11:21** Of the three he was more honored than the two, and he became their commander; yet he did not attain to the three.

**11:22** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a valiant man of Kabzeel, great in exploits, struck down the two lionlike heroes of Moab; he also went down and slew a lion in the midst of a pit on a snowy day.

**11:23** And he struck down an Egyptian, a man of great stature, five cubits tall. In the Egyptian's hand was a spear like a weaver's beam; and he went down to him with a staff, wrested the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.

**11:24** These things Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**11:25** Behold, he was more honored than the thirty, yet he did not attain to the three; and David appointed him over his bodyguard.

## Translator Notes

- **11:11** — The Masoretic Qere reads "the captains" (or "the officers"), whereas the Ketiv reads "the thirty."
- **11:20** — The Masoretic Qere reads "and to him" ("and he had a name"), whereas the Ketiv reads "and not."
- **11:21** — The Hebrew phrase literally reads "of the three, in the two he was honored," indicating that Abishai was doubly honored or more honored than the other two in his triad, yet did not reach the rank of the premier Three.
- **11:44** — The Masoretic Qere reads "Jeiel," whereas the Ketiv reads "Jeuel."
