## Text

**24:1** Now these were the divisions of the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**24:2** But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and they had no sons; therefore Eleazar and Ithamar ministered as priests.

**24:3** And David, along with Zadok of the sons of Eleazar and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, divided them according to their appointed order in their service.

**24:4** More chief men were found among the sons of Eleazar than among the sons of Ithamar, and thus they divided them: among the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen heads of fathers' houses, and among the sons of Ithamar eight according to their fathers' houses.

**24:5** Thus they divided them by lots, one group alike with the other; for there were officers of the sanctuary and officers of God both of the sons of Eleazar and of the sons of Ithamar.

**24:6** And Shemaiah the son of Nethanel the scribe, one of the Levites, recorded them in the presence of the king, the leaders, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the priests and the Levites—one father's house taken for Eleazar, and one taken in turn for Ithamar.

**24:7** Now the first lot came out for Jehoiarib, the second for Jedaiah,

**24:8** the third for Harim, the fourth for Seorim,

**24:9** the fifth for Malchijah, the sixth for Mijamin,

**24:10** the seventh for Hakkoz, the eighth for Abijah,

**24:11** the ninth for Jeshua, the tenth for Shecaniah,

**24:12** the eleventh for Eliashib, the twelfth for Jakim,

**24:13** the thirteenth for Huppah, the fourteenth for Jeshebeab,

**24:14** the fifteenth for Bilgah, the sixteenth for Immer,

**24:15** the seventeenth for Hezir, the eighteenth for Happizzez,

**24:16** the nineteenth for Pethahiah, the twentieth for Jehezkel,

**24:17** the twenty-first for Jachin, the twenty-second for Gamul,

**24:18** the twenty-third for Delaiah, and the twenty-fourth for Maaziah.

**24:19** This was their appointed order for their service, to come into the house of the LORD according to their regulation by the hand of Aaron their father, just as the LORD God of Israel had commanded him.

**24:20** Now concerning the rest of the sons of Levi: of the sons of Amram, Shubael; of the sons of Shubael, Jehdeiah.

**24:21** Concerning Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, Isshiah was the chief.

**24:22** Of the Izharites, Shelomoth; of the sons of Shelomoth, Jahath.

**24:23** And the sons: Jeriah, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.

**24:24** The sons of Uzziel: Micah; of the sons of Micah, Shamir.

**24:25** The brother of Micah was Isshiah; of the sons of Isshiah, Zechariah.

**24:26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi; the sons of Jaaziah: Beno.

**24:27** The sons of Merari by Jaaziah: Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.

**24:28** Of Mahli: Eleazar, who had no sons.

**24:29** Of Kish: the sons of Kish, Jerahmeel.

**24:30** And the sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites according to their fathers' houses.

**24:31** These also cast lots just as their brethren the sons of Aaron did, in the presence of King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the priests and the Levites—the chief father's house just like his younger brother.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — The Masoretic text repeats the participle ("taken, taken for Ithamar"), conveying the alternating selection of fathers' houses between the lines of Eleazar and Ithamar.
- **24:23** — The Masoretic text omits the name of Hebron—identified as the father of these four sons in 23:19—as well as the designation "the chief" for Jeriah.
- **24:24** — Follows the Masoretic Qere "Shamir"; the written Ketiv reads "Shamur."
- **24:26** — The Hebrew form rendered as the proper name "Beno" may also be understood literally as "his son" (compare 24:27).
