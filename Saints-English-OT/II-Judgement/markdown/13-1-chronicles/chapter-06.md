## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, 6:1 corresponds to 6:16 in most English Bibles, since the earlier Levitical and priestly genealogy is numbered at the end of chapter 5.
- **6:11** — The traditional reading (Qere) has "the sons of" before the second "Elkanah," whereas the written consonantal text (Ketiv) reads "his son."
- **6:13** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "the firstborn Vashni, and Abijah," without the name Joel, who appears as Samuel's son in 6:18 and as his firstborn in 1 Samuel 8:2; many scholars take "Vashni" as reflecting "and the second."
- **6:20** — The traditional reading (Qere) has "Zuph," matching 1 Samuel 1:1 (compare "Zophai" in 6:11), whereas the written consonantal text (Ketiv) reads "Ziph."
- **6:42** — The Masoretic Hebrew uses the plural "cities of refuge" here and in 6:52, though Hebron (6:42) and Shechem (6:52) were the designated cities of refuge within these respective allotments (compare Joshua 21:13, 21).
- **6:43** — The Hebrew text here reads "Hilez," whereas some Hebrew manuscripts read "Hilen" and the parallel passage in Joshua 21:15 reads "Holon."
- **6:46** — The Masoretic Hebrew mentions "the family of the tribe, from the half-tribe, the half of Manasseh," while the fuller parallel in Joshua 21:5 also names Ephraim and Dan (compare 6:51–55).
