## Text

**17:1** Now it came to pass, when David dwelt in his house, that David spoke to Nathan the prophet, "Behold, I dwell in a house of cedars, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD is under curtains."

**17:2** And Nathan said to David, "Do all that is in your heart, for God is with you."

**17:3** And it came to pass that same night that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

**17:4** "Go and speak to My servant David, 'Thus says the LORD: It is not you who shall build Me the house to dwell in.

**17:5** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought Israel up to this day, but I have been from tent to tent, and from tabernacle to tabernacle.

**17:6** In all the places where I have walked among all Israel, have I ever spoken a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people, saying, "Why have you not built Me a house of cedars?"'

**17:7** Now therefore, thus shall you say to My servant David, 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: I took you from the pasture, from following the flock, to be ruler over My people Israel.

**17:8** And I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a name like the name of the great men who are on the earth.

**17:9** Moreover I will appoint a place for My people Israel and will plant them, that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more; nor shall the sons of iniquity wear them out anymore, as at the first,

**17:10** and from the days that I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will subdue all your enemies. Furthermore I declare to you that the LORD will build you a house.

**17:11** And it shall come to pass, when your days are fulfilled that you must go to be with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who shall be of your sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

**17:12** He shall build Me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.

**17:13** I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to Me a son; and My lovingkindness I will not take away from him, as I took it away from him who was before you.

**17:14** And I will confirm him in My house and in My kingdom forever; and his throne shall be established forever.'"

**17:15** According to all these words and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**17:16** Then King David went in and sat before the LORD; and he said, "Who am I, O LORD God, and what is my house, that You have brought me thus far?

**17:17** And yet this was a small thing in Your sight, O God; for You have also spoken of Your servant's house for a great while to come, and have regarded me according to the rank of a man of high degree, O LORD God.

**17:18** What more can David say to You concerning the honor bestowed upon Your servant? For You know Your servant.

**17:19** O LORD, for Your servant's sake, and according to Your own heart, You have done all this greatness, in making known all these great things.

**17:20** O LORD, there is none like You, nor is there any God besides You, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

**17:21** And who is like Your people Israel, the one nation on the earth whom God went to redeem for Himself as a people—to make for Yourself a name of great and awesome deeds, by driving out nations from before Your people whom You redeemed from Egypt?

**17:22** For You have made Your people Israel Your very own people forever; and You, O LORD, have become their God.

**17:23** And now, O LORD, let the word which You have spoken concerning Your servant and concerning his house be established forever, and do as You have spoken.

**17:24** So let it be established, and let Your name be magnified forever, saying, 'The LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, is God to Israel.' And let the house of Your servant David be established before You.

**17:25** For You, O my God, have revealed to Your servant that You will build him a house; therefore Your servant has found courage to pray before You.

**17:26** And now, O LORD, You are God, and You have spoken this goodness concerning Your servant.

**17:27** Now therefore You have been pleased to bless the house of Your servant, that it may continue before You forever; for You have blessed it, O LORD, and it shall be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:5** — The Hebrew concludes elliptically with 'and from tabernacle,' naturally understood as 'from tabernacle to tabernacle' in parallel with 'from tent to tent.'
- **17:17** — The Hebrew phrase 'according to the rank of a man of high degree' is difficult; it expresses David's awe that God treats him with the standing of an exalted person.
- **17:25** — Literally, 'have uncovered the ear of Your servant,' a Hebrew idiom for private, gracious disclosure.
