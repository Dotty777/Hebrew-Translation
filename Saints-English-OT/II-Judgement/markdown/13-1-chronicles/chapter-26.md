## Text

**26:1** Concerning the divisions of the gatekeepers: of the Korahites, Meshelemiah the son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph.

**26:2** And Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,

**26:3** Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, and Eliehoenai the seventh.

**26:4** Moreover, Obed-Edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sacar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

**26:5** Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peullethai the eighth; for God blessed him.

**26:6** Also to Shemaiah his son were born sons who held authority over their father's house, for they were mighty men of valor.

**26:7** The sons of Shemaiah were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad, whose brethren Elihu and Semachiah were men of valor.

**26:8** All these were of the sons of Obed-Edom, they and their sons and their brethren, able men with strength for the service: sixty-two of Obed-Edom.

**26:9** And Meshelemiah had sons and brethren, valiant men, eighteen.

**26:10** Also Hosah, of the sons of Merari, had sons: Shimri the chief—for though he was not the firstborn, his father appointed him chief—

**26:11** Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth; all the sons and brethren of Hosah were thirteen.

**26:12** To these divisions of the gatekeepers, to the chief men, belonged duties corresponding to those of their brethren, to minister in the house of the LORD.

**26:13** And they cast lots, the small as well as the great, according to their fathers' houses, for every gate.

**26:14** And the lot for the east fell to Shelemiah. Then they cast lots for Zechariah his son, a counselor of understanding, and his lot came out for the north;

**26:15** to Obed-Edom, the south; and to his sons, the storehouse.

**26:16** To Shuppim and Hosah, the west, with the Gate of Shallecheth on the ascending highway, watch opposite watch.

**26:17** On the east were six Levites, on the north four each day, on the south four each day, and for the storehouse two by two.

**26:18** As for the colonnade on the west, there were four on the highway and two at the colonnade.

**26:19** These were the divisions of the gatekeepers among the sons of the Korahites and among the sons of Merari.

**26:20** And of the Levites, Ahijah was over the treasuries of the house of God and over the treasuries of the dedicated things.

**26:21** The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonites belonging to Ladan, the heads of the fathers' houses belonging to Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.

**26:22** The sons of Jehieli, Zetham and Joel his brother, were over the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

**26:23** Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:

**26:24** Shebuel the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was chief overseer over the treasuries.

**26:25** And his brethren by Eliezer were Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.

**26:26** This Shelomoth and his brethren were over all the treasuries of the dedicated things which King David, and the heads of the fathers' houses, the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and the commanders of the army, had dedicated.

**26:27** Out of the battles and out of the spoil they dedicated these things to maintain the house of the LORD.

**26:28** And all that Samuel the seer, and Saul the son of Kish, and Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Zeruiah had dedicated—whatever anyone had dedicated—was under the hand of Shelomith and his brethren.

**26:29** Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were appointed for the outside work over Israel, as officers and judges.

**26:30** Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brethren, one thousand seven hundred able men, had the oversight of Israel on the side of the Jordan westward, for all the work of the LORD and for the service of the king.

**26:31** Among the Hebronites, Jerijah was the chief of the Hebronites according to his generations by fathers' houses. In the fortieth year of the reign of David they were sought out, and mighty men of valor were found among them at Jazer of Gilead.

**26:32** And his brethren, valiant men, were two thousand seven hundred, heads of fathers' houses; and King David appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of the Manassites, for every matter pertaining to God and for every matter of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **26:15** — Literally the house of the storehouses or gatherings, a complex of storage chambers associated with the southern area of the temple precinct.
- **26:18** — The Hebrew term parbar denotes an outer colonnade, portico, or western precinct structure adjoining the temple area.
- **26:20** — Following the Masoretic Hebrew text, which names the Levite Ahijah as overseer of the treasuries of the house of God.
- **26:25** — The translation follows the Masoretic Qere (read form), Shelomith, which also appears in verse 28, whereas the Ketiv (written form) reads Shelomoth, matching the spelling in verse 26.
