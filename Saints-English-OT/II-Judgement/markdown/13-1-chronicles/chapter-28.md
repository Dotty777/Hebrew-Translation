## Text

**28:1** Now David assembled at Jerusalem all the officials of Israel: the leaders of the tribes and the commanders of the divisions who served the king, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, and the stewards over all the property and livestock belonging to the king and his sons, together with the court officers, the mighty men, and every valiant warrior.

**28:2** Then King David rose to his feet and said, "Hear me, my brethren and my people: I had it in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the LORD and for the footstool of our God, and I had made preparations to build it.

**28:3** But God said to me, 'You shall not build a house for My name, because you are a man of war and have shed blood.'

**28:4** Yet the LORD God of Israel chose me from all my father's house to be king over Israel forever; for He chose Judah to be the ruler, and of the house of Judah, the house of my father, and among my father's sons He was pleased with me to make me king over all Israel.

**28:5** And of all my sons—for the LORD has given me many sons—He has chosen my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the kingdom of the LORD over Israel.

**28:6** And He said to me, 'Solomon your son, he shall build My house and My courts; for I have chosen him to be My son, and I will be a Father to him.

**28:7** Moreover I will establish his kingdom forever, if he remains steadfast to do My commandments and My judgments, as it is this day.'

**28:8** Now therefore, in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of the LORD, and in the hearing of our God, observe and seek out all the commandments of the LORD your God, that you may possess the good land and leave it as an inheritance to your children after you forever.

**28:9** And you, my son Solomon, know the God of your father, and serve Him with an undivided heart and a willing soul; for the LORD searches all hearts and discerns every intent of the thoughts. If you seek Him, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will cast you off forever.

**28:10** Take heed now, for the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be strong, and do it."

**28:11** Then David gave his son Solomon the pattern for the vestibule, its buildings, its treasuries, its upper chambers, its inner rooms, and the room of the mercy seat;

**28:12** and the pattern of all that he had by the Spirit, for the courts of the house of the LORD, for all the chambers all around, for the treasuries of the house of God, and for the treasuries of the dedicated things;

**28:13** also for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, for all the work of the service of the house of the LORD, and for all the vessels of service in the house of the LORD:

**28:14** he gave gold by weight for the things of gold, for all vessels of every kind of service; and for all vessels of silver by weight, for all vessels of every kind of service;

**28:15** and the weight for the lampstands of gold and their lamps of gold, by weight for each lampstand and its lamps; and for the lampstands of silver by weight, for each lampstand and its lamps, according to the use of each lampstand;

**28:16** and the gold by weight for the tables of the showbread, for each table, and silver for the tables of silver;

**28:17** also pure gold for the forks, the basins, and the pitchers; and for the golden bowls, by weight for each bowl, and for the silver bowls, by weight for each bowl;

**28:18** and refined gold by weight for the altar of incense, and gold for the pattern of the chariot—the cherubim that spread out their wings and overshadowed the ark of the covenant of the LORD.

**28:19** "All this in writing, by the hand of the LORD upon me, He made me understand—all the works of the pattern."

**28:20** And David said to his son Solomon, "Be strong and of good courage, and do it; do not fear nor be dismayed, for the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you, until all the work for the service of the house of the LORD is finished.

**28:21** And behold, the divisions of the priests and the Levites are ready for all the service of the house of God; and with you in all the work will be every willing man of skill for every kind of service; and the officials and all the people will be wholly at your command."

## Translator Notes

- **28:12** — "By the Spirit" renders Hebrew baruach, which can also mean "in his mind" or "in his spirit," understood here in light of verse 19 as divinely imparted revelation for the temple design.
- **28:18** — "The chariot" (merkabah) portrays the golden cherubim with outstretched wings over the ark as the symbolic throne-chariot of the LORD.
- **28:19** — The Hebrew shifts directly into first-person speech ("upon me") without an introductory speech formula, recording David's own attestation that the temple pattern came from the hand of the LORD.
