## Text

**12:1** Then the men of Ephraim were summoned together, and crossed over to Zaphon; and they said to Jephthah, "Why did you cross over to fight against the children of Ammon, and did not call us to go with you? We will burn your house over you with fire."

**12:2** And Jephthah said to them, "I and my people were in fierce contention with the children of Ammon; and when I cried out to you, you did not deliver me from their hand.

**12:3** So when I saw that you would not bring deliverance, I took my life in my own hands and crossed over against the children of Ammon, and the LORD gave them into my hand. Why then have you come up to me this day to fight against me?"

**12:4** Then Jephthah gathered together all the men of Gilead and fought against Ephraim; and the men of Gilead struck down Ephraim, because they had said, "You are fugitives of Ephraim, O Gilead, in the midst of Ephraim and in the midst of Manasseh."

**12:5** And Gilead seized the fords of the Jordan against Ephraim. And it came to pass, whenever any of the fugitives of Ephraim said, "Let me cross over," the men of Gilead would say to him, "Are you an Ephraimite?" And he would say, "No."

**12:6** Then they would say to him, "Say now, 'Shibboleth!'" And he would say, "Sibboleth," for he was not able to pronounce it rightly. Then they would seize him and slay him at the fords of the Jordan. And there fell at that time of Ephraim forty-two thousand.

**12:7** And Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in one of the cities of Gilead.

**12:8** And after him Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel.

**12:9** He had thirty sons; and thirty daughters he sent outside in marriage, and thirty daughters he brought in from outside for his sons. And he judged Israel seven years.

**12:10** Then Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.

**12:11** And after him Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel; and he judged Israel ten years.

**12:12** And Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.

**12:13** And after him Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel.

**12:14** He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, who rode on seventy young donkeys. And he judged Israel eight years.

**12:15** Then Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of the Amalekites.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Hebrew directional form tsaphonah may be rendered either "to Zaphon," a town in Gad in the Jordan Valley (cf. Josh. 13:27), or "northward."
- **12:6** — Shibboleth means "ear of grain" or "flowing stream"; the Ephraimites could not pronounce the initial "sh" sound, betraying their tribal dialect at the Jordan fords.
- **12:7** — Literally "in the cities of Gilead," a Hebrew plural idiom referring to one unspecified city within Gilead.
