## Text

**21:1** Now the men of Israel had sworn in Mizpah, saying, "No man among us shall give his daughter to Benjamin for a wife."

**21:2** And the people came to Bethel and sat there until evening before God, and they lifted up their voice and wept with great weeping.

**21:3** And they said, "O LORD, God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that one tribe should be missing from Israel this day?"

**21:4** And it came to pass on the next day that the people rose early, built an altar there, and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings.

**21:5** And the children of Israel said, "Who among all the tribes of Israel did not come up to the LORD in the assembly?" For a great oath had been taken concerning whoever did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah, saying, "He shall surely be put to death."

**21:6** And the children of Israel were grieved for Benjamin their brother, and said, "One tribe has been cut off from Israel this day.

**21:7** What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since we have sworn by the LORD not to give them any of our daughters as wives?"

**21:8** And they said, "Which one from the tribes of Israel did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah?" And behold, no man had come to the camp from Jabesh Gilead, to the assembly.

**21:9** And the people were mustered, and behold, not one man of the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead was there.

**21:10** So the congregation sent there twelve thousand of the men of valor, and commanded them, saying, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead with the edge of the sword, along with the women and the little ones.

**21:11** And this is the thing that you shall do: every male, and every woman who has known lying with a male, you shall devote to utter destruction."

**21:12** And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead four hundred young virgins who had not known a man by lying with a male; and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

**21:13** Then the whole congregation sent word and spoke to the children of Benjamin who were at the Rock of Rimmon, and proclaimed peace to them.

**21:14** So Benjamin returned at that time, and they gave them the women whom they had spared alive from the women of Jabesh Gilead; yet even so there were not enough for them.

**21:15** And the people grieved for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a breach among the tribes of Israel.

**21:16** Then the elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for wives for those who remain, for the women have been destroyed out of Benjamin?"

**21:17** And they said, "There must be an inheritance for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe may not be blotted out from Israel.

**21:18** Yet we cannot give them wives from our daughters, for the children of Israel have sworn, saying, 'Cursed is he who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"

**21:19** Then they said, "Behold, there is a feast of the LORD in Shiloh from year to year, which is on the north of Bethel, on the east side of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah."

**21:20** So they commanded the children of Benjamin, saying, "Go and lie in wait in the vineyards,

**21:21** and watch; and behold, when the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then come out of the vineyards, and let every man seize his wife for himself from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

**21:22** And it shall be, when their fathers or their brethren come to us to contend, that we will say to them, 'Graciously grant them to us, because we did not take a wife for each man in the war, and because it was not you who gave them to them, otherwise you would now be guilty.'"

**21:23** And the children of Benjamin did so, and took wives according to their number from the dancing women whom they carried off; then they went and returned to their inheritance, and rebuilt the cities and dwelt in them.

**21:24** So the children of Israel departed from there at that time, every man to his tribe and to his family, and they went out from there, every man to his inheritance.

**21:25** In those days there was no king in Israel; each man would do what was right in his eyes.

## Translator Notes

- **21:14** — The Hebrew clause literally reads "and they did not find for them thus," an idiom meaning the four hundred young women from Jabesh Gilead did not suffice for the six hundred surviving Benjaminites.
- **21:20** — Ketiv reads the singular "and he commanded," whereas the Qere followed here reads the plural "and they commanded," agreeing with the elders of the congregation who are speaking.
- **21:22** — Ketiv reads an alternate spelling from the by-form root for contending, while the Qere followed here reads the standard infinitive construct meaning "to contend" or "to complain." The plea "Graciously grant them to us" reflects the Hebrew double-object construction of the verb for showing favor.
- **21:25** — The Hebrew imperfect verb "would do" conveys habitual past action, closing the book of Judges with a refrain highlighting the covenantal disorder before the rise of the monarchy.
