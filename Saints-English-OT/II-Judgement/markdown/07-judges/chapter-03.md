## Text

**3:1** Now these are the nations that the LORD left remaining to test Israel by them—all those who had not known any of the wars of Canaan,

**3:2** only so that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them warfare, at least those who beforehand had not known them:

**3:3** the five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites dwelling in Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal Hermon as far as the entrance of Hamath.

**3:4** And they were there to test Israel by them, to know whether they would heed the commandments of the LORD, which He had commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

**3:5** So the children of Israel dwelt in the midst of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

**3:6** And they took their daughters to themselves for wives, and gave their own daughters to their sons; and they served their gods.

**3:7** Then the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD, and they forgot the LORD their God, and served the Baals and the Asherahs.

**3:8** Therefore the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of Cushan-Rishathaim king of Aram Naharaim; and the children of Israel served Cushan-Rishathaim eight years.

**3:9** When the children of Israel cried out to the LORD, the LORD raised up a deliverer for the children of Israel who delivered them—Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother.

**3:10** And the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel. He went out to war, and the LORD delivered Cushan-Rishathaim king of Aram into his hand, and his hand prevailed against Cushan-Rishathaim.

**3:11** So the land had rest forty years. Then Othniel the son of Kenaz died.

**3:12** And the children of Israel again did evil in the sight of the LORD; and the LORD strengthened Eglon king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the LORD.

**3:13** And he gathered to himself the children of Ammon and Amalek, and went and struck Israel, and they took possession of the City of Palms.

**3:14** So the children of Israel served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years.

**3:15** But when the children of Israel cried out to the LORD, the LORD raised up a deliverer for them: Ehud the son of Gera, the Benjamite, a left-handed man. And by his hand the children of Israel sent tribute to Eglon king of Moab.

**3:16** Now Ehud made for himself a double-edged sword, a cubit in length, and girded it under his garments on his right thigh.

**3:17** And he presented the tribute to Eglon king of Moab. Now Eglon was a very fat man.

**3:18** And it came to pass, when he had finished presenting the tribute, that he sent away the people who had carried the tribute.

**3:19** But he himself turned back from the carved images that were near Gilgal, and said, "I have a secret message for you, O king." And he said, "Keep silence!" So all who attended him went out from his presence.

**3:20** Then Ehud came to him while he was sitting in the cool upper chamber that belonged to him alone. And Ehud said, "I have a message from God for you." So he arose from his seat.

**3:21** And Ehud reached out with his left hand, took the sword from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly.

**3:22** Even the hilt went in after the blade, and the fat closed over the blade, for he did not draw the sword out of his belly; and the dung came out.

**3:23** Then Ehud went out onto the porch, and shut the doors of the upper chamber behind him and locked them.

**3:24** When Ehud had gone out, Eglon's servants came and looked, and behold, the doors of the upper chamber were locked. So they said, "Surely he is covering his feet in the cool chamber."

**3:25** So they waited until they were embarrassed; and behold, he still did not open the doors of the upper chamber. Therefore they took the key and opened them, and behold, their master lay fallen on the floor, dead.

**3:26** Meanwhile Ehud had escaped while they delayed, and he passed beyond the carved images and escaped to Seirah.

**3:27** And it came to pass, when he arrived, that he blew the trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim, and the children of Israel went down with him from the hill country, and he went before them.

**3:28** Then he said to them, "Follow after me, for the LORD has given your enemies, Moab, into your hand." So they went down after him, seized the fords of the Jordan leading to Moab, and did not allow anyone to cross over.

**3:29** And at that time they struck down about ten thousand men of Moab, every robust man and every man of valor; and not a man escaped.

**3:30** So Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel. And the land had rest eighty years.

**3:31** After him was Shamgar the son of Anath, who struck down six hundred men of the Philistines with an oxgoad; and he also delivered Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — Aram Naharaim literally means Aram of the Two Rivers, that is, Mesopotamia; the name Cushan-Rishathaim means Cushan of Double Wickedness.
- **3:15** — The Hebrew expression for left-handed literally means bound or restricted in his right hand, forming wordplay with Benjamite, meaning son of the right hand.
- **3:16** — The Hebrew measure gomed occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes a short cubit, approximately eighteen inches.
- **3:22** — The Hebrew word rendered dung occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; its precise meaning is uncertain, referring either to bodily waste or to the weapon exiting from behind.
- **3:24** — Covering his feet is an idiomatic Hebrew euphemism for relieving oneself.
