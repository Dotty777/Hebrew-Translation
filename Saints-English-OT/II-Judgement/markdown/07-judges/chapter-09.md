## Text

**9:1** Now Abimelech the son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem, to his mother's brothers, and spoke with them and with all the family of the house of his mother's father, saying,

**9:2** "Please speak in the hearing of all the lords of Shechem: 'Which is better for you, that seventy men, all the sons of Jerubbaal, rule over you, or that one man rule over you?' And remember that I am your bone and your flesh."

**9:3** So his mother's brothers spoke all these words concerning him in the hearing of all the lords of Shechem; and their heart inclined to follow Abimelech, for they said, "He is our brother."

**9:4** And they gave him seventy pieces of silver from the house of Baal-Berith, with which Abimelech hired worthless and reckless men, and they followed him.

**9:5** Then he came to his father's house at Ophrah and slew his brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy men, upon one stone. Yet Jotham the youngest son of Jerubbaal was left, for he had hidden himself.

**9:6** And all the lords of Shechem and all Beth-Millo gathered together, and they went and made Abimelech king beside the oak of the pillar that was in Shechem.

**9:7** When they told Jotham, he went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice and cried out, and said to them, "Listen to me, you lords of Shechem, that God may listen to you!

**9:8** Once the trees went forth to anoint a king over themselves, and they said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us!'

**9:9** But the olive tree said to them, 'Shall I leave my rich oil, by which God and men are honored, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:10** Then the trees said to the fig tree, 'You come and reign over us!'

**9:11** But the fig tree said to them, 'Shall I leave my sweetness and my good fruit, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:12** Then the trees said to the vine, 'You come and reign over us!'

**9:13** And the vine said to them, 'Shall I leave my new wine, which cheers God and men, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:14** Then all the trees said to the bramble, 'You come and reign over us!'

**9:15** And the bramble said to the trees, 'If in truth you are anointing me as king over you, then come and take refuge in my shade; but if not, let fire come forth from the bramble and devour the cedars of Lebanon!'

**9:16** Now therefore, if you have acted in truth and integrity in making Abimelech king, and if you have dealt well with Jerubbaal and his house, and have done to him as his hands deserved—

**9:17** for my father fought for you, and risked his life, and delivered you from the hand of Midian;

**9:18** yet you have risen up against my father's house this day and slain his sons, seventy men, upon one stone, and made Abimelech, the son of his maidservant, king over the lords of Shechem, because he is your brother—

**9:19** if then you have acted in truth and integrity with Jerubbaal and with his house this day, then rejoice in Abimelech, and let him also rejoice in you.

**9:20** But if not, let fire come forth from Abimelech and devour the lords of Shechem and Beth-Millo; and let fire come forth from the lords of Shechem and from Beth-Millo and devour Abimelech!"

**9:21** Then Jotham ran away and fled, and went to Beer and dwelt there, because of Abimelech his brother.

**9:22** Now Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.

**9:23** Then God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the lords of Shechem; and the lords of Shechem dealt treacherously with Abimelech,

**9:24** that the violence done to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid upon Abimelech their brother, who slew them, and upon the lords of Shechem, who strengthened his hands to slay his brothers.

**9:25** And the lords of Shechem set men in ambush against him on the tops of the mountains, and they robbed all who passed by them along that way; and it was told to Abimelech.

**9:26** Now Gaal the son of Ebed came with his brothers and crossed over into Shechem; and the lords of Shechem put their confidence in him.

**9:27** So they went out into the fields, and gathered the grapes of their vineyards and trod them, and held a festival; and they went into the house of their god, and ate and drank, and cursed Abimelech.

**9:28** Then Gaal the son of Ebed said, "Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerubbaal, and is Zebul not his officer? Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem! Why then should we serve him?

**9:29** Would that this people were under my hand! Then I would remove Abimelech." So he said to Abimelech, "Increase your army and come out!"

**9:30** When Zebul, the ruler of the city, heard the words of Gaal the son of Ebed, his anger was kindled.

**9:31** And he sent messengers to Abimelech secretly, saying, "Behold, Gaal the son of Ebed and his brothers have come to Shechem, and behold, they are stirring up the city against you.

**9:32** Now therefore, arise by night, you and the people who are with you, and lie in wait in the field.

**9:33** And it shall be in the morning, as soon as the sun rises, that you shall rise early and rush upon the city; and behold, when he and the people who are with him come out against you, you may do to him whatever your hand finds to do."

**9:34** So Abimelech and all the people who were with him arose by night, and they lay in wait against Shechem in four companies.

**9:35** Now Gaal the son of Ebed went out and stood in the entrance of the gate of the city; and Abimelech and the people who were with him rose up from the ambush.

**9:36** When Gaal saw the people, he said to Zebul, "Behold, people are coming down from the tops of the mountains!" But Zebul said to him, "You see the shadow of the mountains as if they were men."

**9:37** And Gaal spoke yet again and said, "Behold, people are coming down from the center of the land, and one company is coming by way of the Oak of the Diviners."

**9:38** Then Zebul said to him, "Where indeed is your mouth now, with which you said, 'Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?' Is this not the people whom you despised? Come, go out now and fight against them!"

**9:39** So Gaal went out before the lords of Shechem and fought against Abimelech.

**9:40** And Abimelech pursued him, and he fled before him; and many fell slain, even up to the entrance of the gate.

**9:41** Then Abimelech dwelt at Arumah, and Zebul drove out Gaal and his brothers, so that they could not dwell in Shechem.

**9:42** And it came to pass on the next day that the people went out into the field, and they told Abimelech.

**9:43** So he took the people, divided them into three companies, and lay in wait in the field; and he looked, and behold, the people were coming out of the city, and he rose up against them and struck them down.

**9:44** And Abimelech and the companies that were with him rushed forward and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city; and the two companies rushed upon all who were in the field and struck them down.

**9:45** So Abimelech fought against the city all that day; and he captured the city and slew the people who were in it, and he tore down the city and sowed it with salt.

**9:46** Now when all the lords of the tower of Shechem heard of it, they entered the stronghold of the house of El-Berith.

**9:47** And it was told to Abimelech that all the lords of the tower of Shechem were gathered together.

**9:48** Then Abimelech went up to Mount Zalmon, he and all the people who were with him; and Abimelech took an axe in his hand and cut down a bough from the trees, and lifted it and laid it on his shoulder, and said to the people who were with him, "What you have seen me do, make haste and do likewise!"

**9:49** So every man among all the people likewise cut down his bough and followed Abimelech, and laid them against the stronghold, and set the stronghold on fire over them; so all the people of the tower of Shechem also died, about a thousand men and women.

**9:50** Then Abimelech went to Thebez, and encamped against Thebez and captured it.

**9:51** But there was a strong tower in the midst of the city, and all the men and women and all the lords of the city fled there and shut themselves in; and they went up to the roof of the tower.

**9:52** So Abimelech came up to the tower and fought against it; and he drew near to the entrance of the tower to burn it with fire.

**9:53** And a certain woman cast an upper millstone upon Abimelech's head and crushed his skull.

**9:54** Then he called hastily to the young man, his armor-bearer, and said to him, "Draw your sword and slay me, lest men say of me, 'A woman slew him.'" So his young man thrust him through, and he died.

**9:55** And when the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, every man departed to his own place.

**9:56** Thus God repaid the wickedness of Abimelech, which he had done to his father in slaying his seventy brothers.

**9:57** And all the wickedness of the men of Shechem God returned upon their own heads, and upon them came the curse of Jotham the son of Jerubbaal.

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — The Ketiv writes the fuller spelling מלוכה while the Qere reads the imperative מׇלְכָה, both meaning "Reign!"; the translation follows the Qere.
- **9:12** — The Ketiv writes מלוכי while the Qere reads the feminine imperative מָלְכִי ("reign"); the translation follows the Qere.
- **9:31** — The Hebrew word בְּתׇרְמָה is translated "secretly" (or "craftily"), though some understand it as a place name, "at Tormah" (compare Arumah in verse 41).
- **9:37** — "The center of the land" renders Hebrew טַבּוּר הָאָרֶץ, literally "the navel of the land"; "the Oak of the Diviners" renders אֵלוֹן מְעוֹנְנִים (also transliterated "the Oak of Meonenim").
- **9:44** — "The companies that were with him" preserves the plural noun הָרָאשִׁים of the Masoretic Text for Abimelech's force alongside the other two companies.
