## Text

**17:1** Now there was a man from the hill country of Ephraim, whose name was Micah.

**17:2** And he said to his mother, "The one thousand one hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, about which you uttered a curse and also spoke in my hearing—behold, the silver is with me; I took it." And his mother said, "Blessed be my son by the LORD!"

**17:3** So he restored the one thousand one hundred pieces of silver to his mother. Then his mother said, "I have wholly consecrated the silver to the LORD from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a molten image; now therefore I will restore it to you."

**17:4** Thus he restored the silver to his mother. And his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave them to the silversmith, and he made of it a carved image and a molten image; and it was in the house of Micah.

**17:5** Now the man Micah had a house of gods, and he made an ephod and household idols, and consecrated one of his sons, who became his priest.

**17:6** In those days there was no king in Israel; each man did that which was right in his own eyes.

**17:7** Now there was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah, of the clan of Judah; he was a Levite, and he sojourned there.

**17:8** And the man departed from the city, from Bethlehem in Judah, to sojourn wherever he might find a place; and as he made his journey, he came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah.

**17:9** And Micah said to him, "Where do you come from?" And he said to him, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to sojourn wherever I may find a place."

**17:10** Then Micah said to him, "Dwell with me, and be to me a father and a priest, and I will give you ten pieces of silver by the year, a suit of apparel, and your sustenance." So the Levite went in.

**17:11** And the Levite was content to dwell with the man; and the young man became to him like one of his own sons.

**17:12** So Micah consecrated the Levite, and the young man became his priest, and he remained in the house of Micah.

**17:13** Then Micah said, "Now I know that the LORD will do good to me, because the Levite has become my priest."

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Hebrew uses the fuller name Micayehu here and in verse four, and the shortened form Micah from verse five onward; both refer to the same man and are rendered uniformly as Micah.
- **17:2** — Follows the Masoretic Qere for the feminine pronoun you; the Ketiv preserves an archaic Hebrew spelling of the same pronoun.
- **17:5** — Household idols renders Hebrew teraphim, and consecrated translates the Hebrew idiom filled the hand of, used for priestly ordination.
- **17:10** — By the year renders the Hebrew idiom for the days, denoting an annual stipend.
