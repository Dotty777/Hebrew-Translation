## Text

**15:1** Now it came to pass after a time, in the days of the wheat harvest, that Samson visited his wife with a young goat, and he said, "I will go in to my wife, into the inner chamber." Yet her father would not permit him to go in.

**15:2** And her father said, "I surely thought that you utterly hated her; therefore I gave her to your companion. Is not her younger sister fairer than she? Please let her be yours instead of her."

**15:3** Then Samson said to them, "This time I am blameless toward the Philistines when I do them harm."

**15:4** So Samson went and caught three hundred foxes; and he took torches, turned them tail to tail, and set one torch in the midst between the two tails.

**15:5** And he kindled fire in the torches and loosed them into the standing grain of the Philistines, and he burned from the stacked sheaves even to the standing grain, and even to the olive groves.

**15:6** Then the Philistines said, "Who has done this?" And they answered, "Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he took his wife and gave her to his companion." So the Philistines went up and burned her and her father with fire.

**15:7** And Samson said to them, "If this is how you act, I will surely take vengeance upon you, and afterward I will cease."

**15:8** So he struck them hip and thigh with a great slaughter; then he went down and dwelt in the cleft of the rock of Etam.

**15:9** Then the Philistines went up and encamped in Judah, and spread themselves out in Lehi.

**15:10** And the men of Judah said, "Why have you come up against us?" So they replied, "We have come up to bind Samson, to do to him as he has done to us."

**15:11** Then three thousand men of Judah went down to the cleft of the rock of Etam, and said to Samson, "Do you not know that the Philistines rule over us? What is this that you have done to us?" And he said to them, "As they did to me, so have I done to them."

**15:12** And they said to him, "We have come down to bind you, that we may deliver you into the hand of the Philistines." And Samson said to them, "Swear to me that you yourselves will not fall upon me."

**15:13** So they spoke to him, saying, "No, for we will surely bind you and deliver you into their hand; but we will certainly not put you to death." And they bound him with two new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

**15:14** When he came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted as they came to meet him. Then the Spirit of the LORD rushed mightily upon him, and the ropes that were on his arms became like flax that has burned with fire, and his bonds melted away from his hands.

**15:15** He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey, stretched out his hand and took it, and struck down one thousand men with it.

**15:16** Then Samson said, "With the jawbone of a donkey, heaps upon heaps, with the jawbone of a donkey I have struck down one thousand men!"

**15:17** And it came to pass, when he had finished speaking, that he cast the jawbone from his hand and named that place Ramath Lehi.

**15:18** Then he became exceedingly thirsty, and he cried out to the LORD and said, "You have granted this great deliverance by the hand of Your servant; and now shall I die of thirst and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?"

**15:19** So God split open the hollow place that is in Lehi, and water came forth from it; and when he drank, his spirit returned and he revived. Therefore he called its name En Hakkore, which is in Lehi to this day.

**15:20** And he judged Israel in the days of the Philistines twenty years.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — Hebrew kerem zayit literally means an olive orchard or plantation, rendered here as olive groves.
- **15:8** — Hip and thigh translates the Hebrew idiom leg upon thigh, denoting a thorough and devastating defeat.
- **15:16** — Samson's victory boast plays on the Hebrew word chamor, which means both donkey and heap.
- **15:19** — En Hakkore means Spring of the Caller, recalling Samson's cry to the LORD in his thirst.
