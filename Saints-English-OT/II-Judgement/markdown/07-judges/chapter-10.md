## Text

**10:1** After Abimelech, there arose to save Israel Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar; and he dwelt in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim.

**10:2** He judged Israel twenty-three years; then he died and was buried in Shamir.

**10:3** And after him arose Jair the Gileadite, and he judged Israel twenty-two years.

**10:4** Now he had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkey colts, and they had thirty towns—which are called Havoth Jair to this day—that are in the land of Gilead.

**10:5** And Jair died and was buried in Kamon.

**10:6** Then the children of Israel again did evil in the sight of the LORD, and served the Baals and the Ashtoreths, and the gods of Aram, the gods of Sidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines; and they forsook the LORD and did not serve Him.

**10:7** So the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of the Philistines and into the hand of the children of Ammon.

**10:8** And they shattered and crushed the children of Israel in that year—for eighteen years—all the children of Israel who were beyond the Jordan, in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead.

**10:9** Moreover, the children of Ammon crossed the Jordan to fight against Judah also, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim, so that Israel was greatly distressed.

**10:10** Then the children of Israel cried out to the LORD, saying, "We have sinned against You, for we have forsaken our God and have served the Baals."

**10:11** And the LORD said to the children of Israel, "Did I not deliver you from Egypt, and from the Amorites, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines?

**10:12** The Sidonians also, and Amalek, and Maon oppressed you; and you cried out to Me, and I saved you out of their hand.

**10:13** Yet you have forsaken Me and served other gods; therefore I will save you no more.

**10:14** Go and cry out to the gods whom you have chosen; let them save you in the time of your distress."

**10:15** And the children of Israel said to the LORD, "We have sinned! Do to us according to all that is good in Your sight; only deliver us, we pray, this day."

**10:16** So they put away the foreign gods from among them and served the LORD; and His soul could no longer endure the misery of Israel.

**10:17** Then the children of Ammon were summoned together and encamped in Gilead; and the children of Israel assembled themselves and encamped in Mizpah.

**10:18** And the people, the leaders of Gilead, said one to another, "Who is the man who will begin to fight against the children of Ammon? He shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — The Hebrew text creates a wordplay between 'donkey colts' and 'towns,' giving both words the same Masoretic vocalization. 'Havoth Jair' means 'the tent-villages of Jair.'
- **10:11** — The Hebrew sentence is elliptical, literally opening 'Not from Egypt...' with the verb 'deliver' implied from the conclusion of the statement in verse twelve.
- **10:12** — The Masoretic Text reads 'Maon,' a people of the southern wilderness; some ancient versions read 'Midian.'
- **10:16** — Literally 'His soul grew short because of the misery of Israel,' a Hebrew idiom expressing deep grief and inability to bear their suffering any longer.
