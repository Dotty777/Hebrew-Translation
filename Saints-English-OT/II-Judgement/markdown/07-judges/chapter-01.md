## Text

**1:1** Now it came to pass after the death of Joshua that the children of Israel inquired of the LORD, saying, "Who shall go up first for us against the Canaanites to fight against them?"

**1:2** And the LORD said, "Judah shall go up. Behold, I have given the land into his hand."

**1:3** Then Judah said to his brother Simeon, "Go up with me into my lot, and let us fight against the Canaanites; and I also will go with you into your lot." So Simeon went with him.

**1:4** And Judah went up, and the LORD delivered the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hand; and they struck them down at Bezek, ten thousand men.

**1:5** And they found Adoni-Bezek in Bezek and fought against him, and they struck down the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

**1:6** But Adoni-Bezek fled, and they pursued him and captured him, and cut off his thumbs and his big toes.

**1:7** And Adoni-Bezek said, "Seventy kings with their thumbs and their big toes cut off used to glean beneath my table; just as I have done, so God has repaid me." And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

**1:8** Now the children of Judah fought against Jerusalem and captured it; they struck it with the edge of the sword and set the city on fire.

**1:9** And afterward the children of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanites who dwelt in the hill country, the Negev, and the lowland.

**1:10** Then Judah marched against the Canaanites who dwelt in Hebron—now the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath Arba—and they struck down Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

**1:11** From there he went against the inhabitants of Debir; now the name of Debir formerly was Kiriath Sepher.

**1:12** And Caleb said, "Whoever strikes Kiriath Sepher and captures it, to him I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife."

**1:13** And Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, captured it; so he gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.

**1:14** Now it came to pass, when she came, that she urged him to ask of her father a field. Then she dismounted from the donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What do you wish?"

**1:15** And she said to him, "Give me a blessing; since you have given me the land of the Negev, give me also springs of water." So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

**1:16** Now the children of the Kenite, Moses' father-in-law, went up from the City of Palms with the children of Judah into the wilderness of Judah, which lies in the Negev of Arad; and they went and dwelt with the people.

**1:17** Then Judah went with his brother Simeon, and they struck the Canaanites who inhabited Zephath and devoted it to destruction. Therefore the name of the city was called Hormah.

**1:18** Also Judah captured Gaza with its territory, Ashkelon with its territory, and Ekron with its territory.

**1:19** And the LORD was with Judah, and he took possession of the hill country; yet he could not drive out the inhabitants of the valley, because they had chariots of iron.

**1:20** And they gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had spoken; and from there he drove out the three sons of Anak.

**1:21** But the children of Benjamin did not drive out the Jebusites who inhabited Jerusalem; so the Jebusites have dwelt with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.

**1:22** And the house of Joseph also went up against Bethel, and the LORD was with them.

**1:23** So the house of Joseph scouted out Bethel; now the name of the city formerly was Luz.

**1:24** And the watchmen saw a man coming out of the city, and they said to him, "Please show us the entrance to the city, and we will show mercy to you."

**1:25** So he showed them the entrance to the city, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword; but the man and all his family they let go.

**1:26** And the man went to the land of the Hittites, built a city, and called its name Luz, which is its name to this day.

**1:27** Manasseh did not dispossess Beth Shean and its villages, or Taanach and its villages, or the inhabitants of Dor and its villages, or the inhabitants of Ibleam and its villages, or the inhabitants of Megiddo and its villages; for the Canaanites persisted in dwelling in that land.

**1:28** And it came to pass, when Israel grew strong, that they put the Canaanites to forced labor, yet did not completely drive them out.

**1:29** Nor did Ephraim drive out the Canaanites who dwelt in Gezer; so the Canaanites dwelt in their midst in Gezer.

**1:30** Zebulun did not drive out the inhabitants of Kitron or the inhabitants of Nahalol; so the Canaanites dwelt in their midst and became subject to forced labor.

**1:31** Asher did not drive out the inhabitants of Acco or the inhabitants of Sidon, nor of Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob.

**1:32** So the Asherites dwelt in the midst of the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land; for they did not drive them out.

**1:33** Naphtali did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth Shemesh or the inhabitants of Beth Anath, but dwelt in the midst of the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land; nevertheless the inhabitants of Beth Shemesh and Beth Anath became subject to forced labor for them.

**1:34** And the Amorites pressed the children of Dan into the hill country, for they would not allow them to come down into the valley.

**1:35** And the Amorites persisted in dwelling in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim; yet the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy, and they became subject to forced labor.

**1:36** Now the border of the Amorites was from the Ascent of Akrabbim, from Sela, and upward.

## Translator Notes

- **1:17** — Hormah means "destruction" or "devoted to destruction," echoing the Hebrew verb translated "devoted it to destruction."
- **1:27** — The Hebrew reading tradition (Qere) reads the plural "the inhabitants of Dor," matching the following clauses, whereas the written consonantal text (Ketiv) has the collective singular "the inhabitant of."
- **1:36** — Sela literally means "the Rock" in Hebrew, denoting a prominent geographical landmark along the southern frontier.
