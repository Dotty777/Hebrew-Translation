## Text

**13:1** Again the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD, and the LORD gave them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.

**13:2** Now there was a certain man of Zorah, of the family of the Danites, whose name was Manoah; and his wife was barren and had borne no children.

**13:3** And the Angel of the LORD appeared to the woman and said to her, "Behold now, you are barren and have borne no children, yet you shall conceive and bear a son.

**13:4** Now therefore, take heed, and do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean.

**13:5** For behold, you shall conceive and bear a son. No razor shall come upon his head, for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb; and he shall begin to deliver Israel from the hand of the Philistines."

**13:6** Then the woman came and spoke to her husband, saying, "A man of God came to me, and His countenance was like the countenance of the Angel of God, exceedingly awesome; I did not ask Him where He was from, nor did He tell me His name.

**13:7** And He said to me, 'Behold, you shall conceive and bear a son. Now therefore, do not drink wine or strong drink, nor eat anything unclean, for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb to the day of his death.'"

**13:8** Then Manoah prayed to the LORD and said, "O my Lord, please let the man of God whom You sent come to us again and teach us what we shall do for the child who will be born."

**13:9** And God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the Angel of God came again to the woman as she sat in the field; but Manoah her husband was not with her.

**13:10** Then the woman made haste and ran and told her husband, and said to him, "Behold, the man who came to me the other day has appeared to me!"

**13:11** So Manoah arose and followed his wife. When he came to the man, he said to Him, "Are You the man who spoke to the woman?" And He said, "I am."

**13:12** And Manoah said, "Now let Your words come to pass! What shall be the rule of life for the child, and what shall be his work?"

**13:13** And the Angel of the LORD said to Manoah, "Of all that I said to the woman let her be careful.

**13:14** She shall not eat anything that comes from the grapevine, nor let her drink wine or strong drink, nor eat anything unclean. All that I commanded her she shall observe."

**13:15** Then Manoah said to the Angel of the LORD, "Please let us detain You, that we may prepare a young goat for You."

**13:16** And the Angel of the LORD said to Manoah, "Though you detain Me, I will not eat of your food; yet if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to the LORD." For Manoah did not know that He was the Angel of the LORD.

**13:17** Then Manoah said to the Angel of the LORD, "What is Your name, so that when Your word comes to pass we may honor You?"

**13:18** And the Angel of the LORD said to him, "Why do you ask My name, seeing it is wonderful?"

**13:19** So Manoah took the young goat together with the grain offering, and offered it upon the rock to the LORD. And He did wondrously while Manoah and his wife looked on.

**13:20** For it came to pass, as the flame went up toward heaven from off the altar, that the Angel of the LORD ascended in the flame of the altar. And Manoah and his wife saw it, and they fell on their faces to the ground.

**13:21** And the Angel of the LORD appeared no more to Manoah and to his wife. Then Manoah knew that He was the Angel of the LORD.

**13:22** And Manoah said to his wife, "We shall surely die, because we have seen God!"

**13:23** But his wife said to him, "If the LORD had desired to put us to death, He would not have received a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hand, nor would He have shown us all these things, nor would He at this time have declared to us such things as these."

**13:24** So the woman bore a son and called his name Samson; and the child grew, and the LORD blessed him.

**13:25** And the Spirit of the LORD began to stir him in Mahaneh Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Translator Notes

- **13:17** — The Masoretic Ketiv (written text) reads plural "your words," while the Qere (read text), translated here, reads singular "your word" in agreement with the singular verb.
- **13:18** — The Hebrew word rendered "wonderful" denotes something marvelous and beyond human comprehension, echoed by "did wondrously" in verse 19.
- **13:25** — Mahaneh Dan means "Camp of Dan" (cf. Judg. 18:12), located in the territory between Zorah and Eshtaol.
