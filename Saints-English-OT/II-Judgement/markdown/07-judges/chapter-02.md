## Text

**2:1** Then the Angel of the LORD went up from Gilgal to Bochim and said, "I brought you up from Egypt and led you into the land which I swore to your fathers; and I said, 'I will never break My covenant with you.

**2:2** And you shall make no covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you shall tear down their altars.' Yet you have not obeyed My voice. What is this that you have done?

**2:3** Therefore I also said, 'I will not drive them out from before you; rather, they shall be adversaries to you, and their gods shall be a snare to you.'"

**2:4** And it came to pass, when the Angel of the LORD spoke these words to all the children of Israel, that the people lifted up their voice and wept.

**2:5** So they called the name of that place Bochim, and they sacrificed there to the LORD.

**2:6** When Joshua dismissed the people, the children of Israel went each man to his inheritance to possess the land.

**2:7** So the people served the LORD all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua, who had seen all the great work of the LORD which He had done for Israel.

**2:8** Now Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died, being one hundred and ten years old.

**2:9** And they buried him within the border of his inheritance at Timnath Heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, on the north side of Mount Gaash.

**2:10** Also all that generation were gathered to their fathers, and another generation arose after them who did not know the LORD, nor the work which He had done for Israel.

**2:11** Then the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD, and served the Baals;

**2:12** and they forsook the LORD, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt; and they went after other gods from among the gods of the peoples who were all around them, and they bowed down to them; and they provoked the LORD to anger.

**2:13** They forsook the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.

**2:14** And the anger of the LORD burned against Israel. So He delivered them into the hand of plunderers who plundered them; and He sold them into the hand of their enemies all around, so that they could no longer stand before their enemies.

**2:15** Wherever they went out, the hand of the LORD was against them for calamity, as the LORD had spoken and as the LORD had sworn to them; and they were greatly distressed.

**2:16** Nevertheless, the LORD raised up judges who delivered them out of the hand of those who plundered them.

**2:17** Yet they would not listen to their judges either, for they played the harlot after other gods and bowed down to them. They turned aside quickly from the way in which their fathers had walked, in obeying the commandments of the LORD; they did not do so.

**2:18** And whenever the LORD raised up judges for them, the LORD was with the judge and delivered them out of the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge; for the LORD was moved to pity by their groaning because of those who oppressed them and afflicted them.

**2:19** And it came to pass, when the judge died, that they would turn back and act more corruptly than their fathers, by going after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them. They did not abandon their practices nor their stubborn way.

**2:20** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He said, "Because this nation has transgressed My covenant which I commanded their fathers, and has not obeyed My voice,

**2:21** I also will no longer drive out before them any of the nations which Joshua left when he died,

**2:22** so that through them I may test Israel, whether they will keep the way of the LORD to walk in it, just as their fathers kept it, or not."

**2:23** Therefore the LORD left those nations, without driving them out hastily; nor did He deliver them into the hand of Joshua.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Bochim means "Weepers" in Hebrew, anticipating the people's weeping at that place in verses 4-5.
- **2:3** — The Hebrew word translated "adversaries" can also mean "sides," which traditional renderings often expand as "thorns in your sides" in light of Numbers 33:55.
- **2:9** — Timnath Heres is an alternate form of Timnath Serah (Joshua 24:30), with the consonants of the second element transposed.
- **2:16** — The Hebrew term translated "judges" denotes divinely appointed deliverers and rulers who rescued and governed Israel rather than solely judicial magistrates.
