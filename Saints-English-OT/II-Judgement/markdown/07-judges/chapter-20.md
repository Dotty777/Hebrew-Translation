## Text

**20:1** Then all the children of Israel went forth, and the congregation assembled as one man, from Dan even to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead, to the LORD at Mizpah.

**20:2** And the leaders of all the people, of all the tribes of Israel, presented themselves in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand men on foot who drew the sword.

**20:3** Now the children of Benjamin heard that the children of Israel had gone up to Mizpah. And the children of Israel said, "Speak, how did this evil come to pass?"

**20:4** So the Levite, the husband of the woman who was murdered, answered and said, "I came to Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin, I and my concubine, to lodge for the night.

**20:5** And the men of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded the house by night because of me; they intended to kill me, and they violated my concubine so that she died.

**20:6** So I took hold of my concubine, cut her into pieces, and sent her throughout all the territory of the inheritance of Israel, for they have committed depravity and outrage in Israel.

**20:7** Behold, all of you are children of Israel; give your word and counsel here."

**20:8** Then all the people arose as one man, saying, "Not one of us will go to his tent, nor will any of us return to his house.

**20:9** But now this is the thing that we will do to Gibeah: we will go up against it by lot.

**20:10** We will take ten men out of a hundred throughout all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, to bring provisions for the people, so that when they come to Geba of Benjamin, they may repay them according to all the outrage that they have committed in Israel."

**20:11** So all the men of Israel gathered against the city, united together as one man.

**20:12** Then the tribes of Israel sent men throughout all the clans of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has been done among you?

**20:13** Now therefore, deliver up the men, the sons of Belial, who are in Gibeah, that we may put them to death and purge evil from Israel." But the children of Benjamin would not listen to the voice of their brethren, the children of Israel.

**20:14** Instead, the children of Benjamin assembled from the cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the children of Israel.

**20:15** And from the cities the children of Benjamin were mustered on that day twenty-six thousand men who drew the sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, of whom seven hundred chosen men were mustered.

**20:16** Among all this people were seven hundred chosen men who were left-handed; every one of them could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

**20:17** Now the men of Israel, apart from Benjamin, were mustered at four hundred thousand men who drew the sword; all of these were men of war.

**20:18** Then they arose, went up to Bethel, and inquired of God. And the children of Israel said, "Who shall go up first for us to battle against the children of Benjamin?" And the LORD said, "Judah first."

**20:19** So the children of Israel arose in the morning and encamped against Gibeah.

**20:20** And the men of Israel marched out to battle against Benjamin, and the men of Israel set themselves in battle array against them at Gibeah.

**20:21** Then the children of Benjamin came forth from Gibeah and struck down to the ground twenty-two thousand men of Israel on that day.

**20:22** Yet the people, the men of Israel, took courage and again drew up in battle array at the place where they had arrayed themselves on the first day.

**20:23** Then the children of Israel went up and wept before the LORD until evening; and they inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I again draw near to battle against the children of my brother Benjamin?" And the LORD said, "Go up against him."

**20:24** So the children of Israel advanced against the children of Benjamin on the second day.

**20:25** And Benjamin came out from Gibeah to meet them on the second day, and struck down to the ground another eighteen thousand men of the children of Israel; all of these drew the sword.

**20:26** Then all the children of Israel, and all the people, went up and came to Bethel; they wept and sat there before the LORD, and fasted on that day until evening, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

**20:27** And the children of Israel inquired of the LORD—for the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days,

**20:28** and Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, stood before it in those days—saying, "Shall I yet again go out to battle against the children of my brother Benjamin, or shall I cease?" And the LORD said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will deliver him into your hand."

**20:29** So Israel set men in ambush all around Gibeah.

**20:30** And the children of Israel went up against the children of Benjamin on the third day and arrayed themselves against Gibeah as at the other times.

**20:31** And the children of Benjamin came out to meet the people and were drawn away from the city. They began to strike down some of the people as at the other times, along the highways—one of which goes up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah—and in the open field, about thirty men of Israel.

**20:32** And the children of Benjamin said, "They are routed before us, as at the first." But the children of Israel had said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways."

**20:33** Then all the men of Israel rose up from their place and drew up in array at Baal Tamar, while the ambush of Israel burst forth from its place, from the open ground of Geba.

**20:34** And ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came opposite Gibeah, and the battle grew fierce; yet they did not know that calamity was closing in upon them.

**20:35** And the LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel; and the children of Israel struck down twenty-five thousand one hundred men of Benjamin on that day; all of these drew the sword.

**20:36** So the children of Benjamin saw that they were defeated. Now the men of Israel had given ground to Benjamin, because they trusted in the ambush which they had set against Gibeah.

**20:37** And the men in ambush hurried and rushed upon Gibeah; the ambush spread out and struck the whole city with the edge of the sword.

**20:38** Now the appointed signal between the men of Israel and the ambush was that they should cause a great cloud of smoke to rise up from the city.

**20:39** Then the men of Israel turned about in the battle. Now Benjamin had begun to strike down and slay about thirty of the men of Israel, for they said, "Surely they are completely defeated before us, as in the first battle."

**20:40** But when the signal began to ascend from the city in a pillar of smoke, Benjamin looked behind him, and behold, the whole city was ascending toward heaven.

**20:41** And the men of Israel turned around, and the men of Benjamin were dismayed, for they saw that calamity had come upon them.

**20:42** Therefore they turned their backs before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness, yet the battle overtook them; and those who came out of the cities were destroying them in their midst.

**20:43** They surrounded Benjamin, pursued them, and trod them down at their resting place, as far as opposite Gibeah toward the rising of the sun.

**20:44** And eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell; all of these were men of valor.

**20:45** Then they turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, yet the men of Israel gleaned five thousand of them along the highways, and pursued closely after them as far as Gidom, and struck down two thousand men of them.

**20:46** So all those of Benjamin who fell on that day were twenty-five thousand men who drew the sword; all of these were men of valor.

**20:47** But six hundred men turned and fled into the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and dwelt at the rock of Rimmon for four months.

**20:48** And the men of Israel turned back against the children of Benjamin and struck them with the edge of the sword, from every entire city to the beasts, even everything that was found; moreover, they set fire to all the cities that were found.

## Translator Notes

- **20:10** — The Masoretic Text reads Geba of Benjamin here, used interchangeably with Gibeah of Benjamin in this narrative.
- **20:13** — The qere reads the children of Benjamin, supplying the word for children or sons, whereas the ketiv written text reads simply Benjamin.
- **20:33** — The phrase open ground of Geba renders Hebrew maareh-Geba, literally the bare or open place of Geba, which may also designate a local place name.
- **20:43** — Hebrew menuhah literally means resting place, though it may also be understood adverbially as with ease or as a place name, Menuhah.
- **20:48** — The phrase every entire city follows the Masoretic vocalization ir metom, literally a city of completeness, emphasizing the total devastation of the Benjamite towns.
