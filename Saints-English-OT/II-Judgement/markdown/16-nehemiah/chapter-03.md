## Text

**3:1** Then Eliashib the high priest arose with his brethren the priests and built the Sheep Gate; they consecrated it and set up its doors; they consecrated it as far as the Tower of the Hundred, and as far as the Tower of Hananel.

**3:2** And next to him the men of Jericho built. And next to him Zaccur the son of Imri built.

**3:3** Also the sons of Hassenaah built the Fish Gate; they laid its beams and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars.

**3:4** And next to them Meremoth the son of Uriah, the son of Hakkoz, made repairs. And next to them Meshullam the son of Berechiah, the son of Meshezabel, made repairs. And next to them Zadok the son of Baana made repairs.

**3:5** And next to them the Tekoites made repairs; but their nobles did not put their necks to the work of their lords.

**3:6** Moreover Joiada the son of Paseah and Meshullam the son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate; they laid its beams and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars.

**3:8** Next to him Uzziel the son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs. And next to him Hananiah, one of the perfumers, made repairs; and they restored Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

**3:9** And next to them Rephaiah the son of Hur, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs.

**3:10** And next to them Jedaiah the son of Harumaph made repairs opposite his house. And next to him Hattush the son of Hashabneiah made repairs.

**3:11** Malchijah the son of Harim and Hashub the son of Pahath-Moab repaired another section, as well as the Tower of the Ovens.

**3:12** And next to him Shallum the son of Hallohesh, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs, he and his daughters.

**3:13** Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate; they built it and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars, and repaired a thousand cubits of the wall as far as the Dung Gate.

**3:14** And Malchijah the son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-Haccerem, repaired the Dung Gate; he built it and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars.

**3:15** And Shallun the son of Col-Hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, repaired the Fountain Gate; he built it, roofed it, and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars, and he also repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah by the King's Garden, as far as the stairs that descend from the City of David.

**3:16** After him Nehemiah the son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth-Zur, made repairs as far as a point opposite the tombs of David, and as far as the artificial pool, and as far as the House of the Mighty Men.

**3:17** After him the Levites made repairs: Rehum the son of Bani. Next to him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, made repairs for his district.

**3:18** After him their brethren made repairs: Bavvai the son of Henadad, ruler of half the district of Keilah.

**3:19** And next to him Ezer the son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section opposite the ascent to the armory at the angle.

**3:20** After him Baruch the son of Zaccai zealously repaired another section, from the angle to the door of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

**3:21** After him Meremoth the son of Uriah, the son of Hakkoz, repaired another section, from the door of the house of Eliashib even to the end of the house of Eliashib.

**3:22** And after him the priests, the men of the plain, made repairs.

**3:23** After him Benjamin and Hashub made repairs opposite their house. After him Azariah the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ananiah, made repairs beside his house.

**3:24** After him Binnui the son of Henadad repaired another section, from the house of Azariah to the angle, and even to the corner.

**3:25** Palal the son of Uzai made repairs opposite the angle, and the tower projecting from the upper house of the king that is by the court of the guard. After him Pedaiah the son of Parosh made repairs.

**3:26** Moreover the temple servants dwelling on Ophel made repairs as far as a point opposite the Water Gate toward the east, and the projecting tower.

**3:27** After him the Tekoites repaired another section, from opposite the great projecting tower even to the wall of Ophel.

**3:28** Above the Horse Gate the priests made repairs, each one opposite his own house.

**3:29** After him Zadok the son of Immer made repairs opposite his own house. And after him Shemaiah the son of Shecaniah, the keeper of the East Gate, made repairs.

**3:30** After him Hananiah the son of Shelemiah, and Hanun the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section. After him Meshullam the son of Berechiah made repairs opposite his chamber.

**3:31** After him Malchijah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs as far as the house of the temple servants and of the merchants, opposite the Inspection Gate, and as far as the upper chamber of the corner.

**3:32** And between the upper chamber of the corner and the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and the merchants made repairs.

**3:33** Now it came to pass, when Sanballat heard that we were rebuilding the wall, that he was furious and greatly indignant, and mocked the Jews.

**3:34** And he spoke before his brethren and the army of Samaria, and said, "What are these feeble Jews doing? Will they restore it for themselves? Will they offer sacrifices? Will they finish it in a day? Will they revive the stones out of the heaps of dust, burned as they are?"

**3:35** Now Tobiah the Ammonite was beside him, and he said, "Even what they are building—if a fox should go up on it, he would break down their stone wall!"

**3:36** Hear, O our God, for we are despised; turn their reproach back upon their own head, and give them over as plunder in a land of captivity.

**3:37** Do not cover their iniquity, and do not let their sin be blotted out from before You; for they have provoked You to anger in the sight of the builders.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — Hebrew adonaihem ("their lords") can refer either to the human supervisors directing the work or, as a plural of majesty, to God ("their Lord").
- **3:15** — The ketiv reads plural "and they set up," while the qere followed here reads singular "and set up" to agree with Shallun; the Pool of Shelah is the Pool of Siloam.
- **3:20** — The ketiv spells the father's name "Zabbai," whereas the qere followed here reads "Zaccai."
- **3:26** — "Temple servants" translates Hebrew Nethinim, literally "the given ones," a guild of dedicated assistants to the Levites who resided on Ophel.
- **3:30** — Here and in verse thirty-one, the ketiv reads "after me," while the qere followed in the translation reads "after him."
- **3:33** — In Masoretic Hebrew versification, verses thirty-three through thirty-seven belong to chapter three rather than opening chapter four.
