## Text

**9:1** Now on the twenty-fourth day of this month, the children of Israel assembled with fasting, clad in sackcloth, and with earth upon them.

**9:2** Then the seed of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners; and they stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.

**9:3** And they stood up in their places and read from the Book of the Law of the LORD their God for one-fourth of the day; and for another fourth they made confession and worshiped the LORD their God.

**9:4** Then Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani stood upon the stairs of the Levites and cried out with a loud voice to the LORD their God.

**9:5** And the Levites—Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah—said, "Arise and bless the LORD your God from everlasting to everlasting! And let them bless Your glorious name, which is exalted above all blessing and praise!

**9:6** You alone are the LORD; You have made the heavens, the heaven of heavens with all their host, the earth and everything upon it, the seas and all that is in them. You preserve them all in life, and the host of heaven worships You.

**9:7** You are the LORD God, who chose Abram, brought him forth from Ur of the Chaldeans, and gave him the name Abraham.

**9:8** You found his heart faithful before You, and You made a covenant with him to give the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, and the Girgashites—to give it to his seed. And You have established Your words, for You are righteous.

**9:9** You saw the affliction of our fathers in Egypt, and You heard their cry by the Red Sea.

**9:10** You performed signs and wonders against Pharaoh, against all his servants, and against all the people of his land, for You knew that they dealt arrogantly with them; and You made a name for Yourself, as it is to this day.

**9:11** You divided the sea before them, so that they passed through the midst of the sea on dry ground; and their pursuers You cast into the depths, like a stone into mighty waters.

**9:12** By a pillar of cloud You led them by day, and by a pillar of fire by night, to light for them the way in which they should walk.

**9:13** You descended also upon Mount Sinai and spoke with them from heaven; and You gave them upright ordinances and laws of truth, good statutes and commandments.

**9:14** You made known to them Your holy Sabbath, and commanded them precepts, statutes, and law by the hand of Your servant Moses.

**9:15** You gave them bread from heaven for their hunger, and brought forth water for them out of the rock for their thirst; and You told them to enter in to possess the land which You had lifted up Your hand to give them.

**9:16** Yet they and our fathers acted arrogantly; they stiffened their necks and did not hearken to Your commandments.

**9:17** They refused to obey and did not remember Your wondrous works that You had performed among them; instead they stiffened their necks, and in their rebellion appointed a leader to return to their bondage. Yet You are a God of forgiveness, gracious and merciful, slow to anger and abundant in lovingkindness, and You did not forsake them.

**9:18** Even when they made a molten calf for themselves and said, 'This is your god who brought you up out of Egypt,' and committed great blasphemies,

**9:19** yet You, in Your abundant mercies, did not forsake them in the wilderness. The pillar of cloud did not depart from over them by day to guide them along the way, nor the pillar of fire by night to give them light and show them the path in which they should walk.

**9:20** You also gave Your good Spirit to instruct them, and You did not withhold Your manna from their mouths, and You gave them water for their thirst.

**9:21** For forty years You sustained them in the wilderness, so that they lacked nothing; their garments did not wear out, and their feet did not swell.

**9:22** You also gave them kingdoms and peoples and allotted them into every district; so they took possession of the land of Sihon, even the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.

**9:23** You multiplied their children like the stars of heaven, and brought them into the land concerning which You had told their fathers to enter and possess it.

**9:24** So the children went in and possessed the land; and You subdued before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and delivered them into their hand, along with their kings and the peoples of the land, to do with them according to their pleasure.

**9:25** They captured fortified cities and fertile soil, and took possession of houses filled with every good thing, hewn cisterns, vineyards, olive groves, and fruit trees in abundance. So they ate, were filled, grew fat, and reveled in Your great goodness.

**9:26** Nevertheless they were defiant and rebelled against You; they cast Your Law behind their backs, slew Your prophets who warned them to turn back to You, and committed great blasphemies.

**9:27** Therefore You gave them into the hand of their adversaries, who afflicted them; yet in the time of their distress they cried out to You, and You heard from heaven, and according to Your abundant mercies You gave them deliverers who saved them out of the hand of their adversaries.

**9:28** Yet as soon as they had rest, they returned to doing evil before You. Therefore You abandoned them into the hand of their enemies, so that they ruled over them; yet when they turned and cried out to You, You heard from heaven, and many times You rescued them according to Your mercies.

**9:29** And You warned them in order to bring them back to Your Law. Yet they acted presumptuously and did not obey Your commandments, but sinned against Your ordinances—by which a man shall live if he does them—and they turned a stubborn shoulder, stiffened their necks, and would not listen.

**9:30** Yet for many years You were patient with them and admonished them by Your Spirit through Your prophets, but they would not give ear. Therefore You delivered them into the hand of the peoples of the lands.

**9:31** Nevertheless, in Your abundant mercies You did not make a full end of them nor forsake them; for You are a gracious and merciful God.

**9:32** Now therefore, our God—the great, the mighty, and the awesome God, who keeps covenant and lovingkindness—do not let all the hardship seem small before You that has come upon us, upon our kings, our princes, our priests, our prophets, our fathers, and all Your people, from the days of the kings of Assyria to this day.

**9:33** Yet You are righteous in all that has befallen us; for You have dealt in truth, while we have acted wickedly.

**9:34** Neither our kings, our princes, our priests, nor our fathers have kept Your Law, nor have they paid heed to Your commandments and Your testimonies with which You warned them.

**9:35** Even in their own kingdom, amid the great goodness that You bestowed upon them, and in the spacious and fertile land that You set before them, they did not serve You, nor did they turn from their evil deeds.

**9:36** Behold, we are servants this day; and as for the land that You gave to our fathers to eat its fruit and its bounty, behold, we are servants upon it.

**9:37** Its rich yield increases for the kings whom You have set over us because of our sins; they also rule over our bodies and over our livestock at their pleasure, and we are in great distress."

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — Following the Qere attah ("You"); the Ketiv writes the shorter defective form at.
- **9:9** — Hebrew yam-suph literally means "Sea of Reeds," traditionally rendered "Red Sea."
- **9:17** — Following the Qere we-rab-hesed ("and abundant in lovingkindness"); the Ketiv reads we-hesed ("and lovingkindness"). The Hebrew text also reads "in their rebellion" rather than "in Egypt."
- **9:22** — Hebrew le-pe'ah literally means "to a corner" or "by border," indicating the allotment of territories into districts or to their farthest bounds.
