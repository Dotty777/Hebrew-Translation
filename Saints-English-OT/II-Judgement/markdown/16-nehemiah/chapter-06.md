## Text

**6:1** Now it came to pass, when it was heard by Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and the rest of our enemies that I had built the wall and that no breach remained in it, though up to that time I had not set up the doors in the gates,

**6:2** that Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, let us meet together in the villages in the plain of Ono." But they were planning to do me harm.

**6:3** So I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am carrying out a great work, and I am not able to come down. Why should the work cease while I leave it and come down to you?"

**6:4** And they sent to me four times in this manner, and I answered them in the same manner.

**6:5** Then Sanballat sent his servant to me in this same manner a fifth time, with an open letter in his hand.

**6:6** In it was written, "It is reported among the nations, and Gashmu also says, that you and the Jews intend to rebel; therefore you are building the wall, and according to these reports you are to become their king.

**6:7** You have also appointed prophets to proclaim concerning you in Jerusalem, saying, 'There is a king in Judah!' Now it will be reported to the king according to these words. Come now, therefore, and let us take counsel together."

**6:8** Then I sent to him, saying, "No such things as you speak of have taken place, for you are inventing them out of your own heart."

**6:9** For all of them were trying to frighten us, saying, "Their hands will drop from the work, and it will not be done." Now therefore, strengthen my hands.

**6:10** Afterward I went to the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah, the son of Mehetabel, who was shut in; and he said, "Let us meet together in the house of God, within the temple, and let us shut the doors of the temple, for they are coming to slay you; indeed, at night they are coming to slay you."

**6:11** And I said, "Should a man such as I flee? And who is there such as I who would go into the temple and live? I will not go in."

**6:12** Then I discerned, and behold, God had not sent him; for he spoke this prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.

**6:13** For this purpose he was hired, that I might be afraid and act accordingly and sin, and so give them occasion for an evil report, that they might reproach me.

**6:14** Remember Tobiah and Sanballat, O my God, according to these works of theirs, and also Noadiah the prophetess and the rest of the prophets who were trying to frighten me.

**6:15** So the wall was finished on the twenty-fifth day of Elul, in fifty-two days.

**6:16** And it came to pass, when all our enemies heard of it and all the nations around us saw it, that they fell greatly in their own eyes; for they perceived that this work had been accomplished by our God.

**6:17** Moreover, in those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and letters from Tobiah came to them.

**6:18** For many in Judah were bound by oath to him, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah the son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah.

**6:19** They also kept speaking of his good deeds before me, and reported my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to frighten me.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Hebrew term translated "in the villages" (bakkefirim) may alternatively represent a specific town name, Chephirim, in the plain of Ono.
- **6:6** — "Gashmu" preserves the Hebrew letter's variant spelling of the name Geshem (compare verse 1), reflecting an Arabic nominative ending.
- **6:9** — "Now therefore, strengthen my hands" is a brief petition directed to God, whose name is implied rather than explicitly stated in the Hebrew text.
