## Text

**4:1** Now it came to pass, when Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites heard that the restoration of the walls of Jerusalem was advancing and that the breaches had begun to be closed, that they became exceedingly angry.

**4:2** And all of them conspired together to come and fight against Jerusalem and to cause confusion in it.

**4:3** But we prayed to our God, and we stationed a watch against them day and night on account of them.

**4:4** Then Judah said, "The strength of the burden bearers is failing, and the rubble is abundant, so we are not able to build on the wall."

**4:5** And our adversaries said, "They will neither know nor see until we come into their midst and slay them, and cause the work to cease."

**4:6** And it came to pass, when the Jews who dwelt near them came, that they said to us ten times, "From every place to which you turn, they will come upon us."

**4:7** Therefore I positioned them in the lowest parts of the space behind the wall, in the exposed places; indeed, I stationed the people according to their families, with their swords, their spears, and their bows.

**4:8** And I looked, and I arose and said to the nobles, to the officials, and to the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord, who is great and awesome, and fight for your brethren, your sons, your daughters, your wives, and your houses."

**4:9** And it came to pass, when our enemies heard that it had become known to us, and that God had brought their counsel to nothing, that all of us returned to the wall, each man to his work.

**4:10** And it came to pass from that day onward that half of my servants labored at the work, while the other half held the spears, the shields, the bows, and the coats of mail; and the leaders stood behind the whole house of Judah.

**4:11** Those who built on the wall and those who carried burdens, loading them—each with one of his hands performed the work, and with the other held a weapon.

**4:12** And the builders each had his sword bound at his side as they built; and the one who sounded the trumpet was beside me.

**4:13** Then I said to the nobles, to the officials, and to the rest of the people, "The work is great and extensive, and we are separated upon the wall, each man far from his brother.

**4:14** In whatever place you hear the sound of the trumpet, gather to us there. Our God will fight for us."

**4:15** So we labored at the work, while half of them held the spears from the rising of the dawn until the stars appeared.

**4:16** At that same time I also said to the people, "Let each man and his servant lodge within Jerusalem, that they may be a watch for us by night, and labor by day."

**4:17** So neither I, nor my brethren, nor my servants, nor the men of the watch who followed me—none of us took off our garments; each man kept his weapon even at the water.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, Nehemiah 4:1–17 corresponds to 4:7–23 in most English Bibles. The Hebrew idiom translated "the restoration ... was advancing" literally reads "healing went up."
- **4:6** — The Hebrew clause is notably terse and elliptical, literally reading "from all the places where you return, against us," conveying that enemies threatened from every direction.
- **4:7** — Translates the Qere ("in the exposed places"); the Ketiv displays a slightly different spelling of the same rare noun.
- **4:9** — Translates the Qere (wayyiqtol "we returned"); the Ketiv writes the full vowel spelling with waw.
- **4:17** — The final Hebrew phrase is extremely compressed, literally "each man his weapon the water," indicating that the men kept their weapons with them even when going for water.
