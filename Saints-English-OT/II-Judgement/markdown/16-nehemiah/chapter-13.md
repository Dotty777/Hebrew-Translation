## Text

**13:1** On that day the Book of Moses was read in the hearing of the people, and it was found written in it that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever enter the assembly of God,

**13:2** for they failed to meet the children of Israel with bread and with water, and hired Balaam against them to invoke a curse on them; yet our God turned the curse into a blessing.

**13:3** Now it came to pass, when they heard the law, that they separated all the mixed multitude from Israel.

**13:4** Now before this, Eliashib the priest, who was appointed over the chamber of the house of our God, was closely related to Tobiah.

**13:5** And he prepared for him a great chamber, where previously they had stored the grain offerings, the frankincense, the vessels, and the tithes of grain, new wine, and oil, which were commanded for the Levites, the singers, and the gatekeepers, as well as the offerings for the priests.

**13:6** But during all this I was not in Jerusalem, for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon I had gone to the king. Then after some time I asked leave of the king.

**13:7** And I came to Jerusalem and perceived the evil that Eliashib had done for Tobiah, in preparing a chamber for him in the courts of the house of God.

**13:8** And it grieved me bitterly; therefore I cast all the household goods of Tobiah out of the chamber.

**13:9** Then I gave command, and they cleansed the chambers; and I brought back there the vessels of the house of God, with the grain offering and the frankincense.

**13:10** I also learned that the portions of the Levites had not been given to them; for the Levites and the singers who performed the service had fled, each to his own field.

**13:11** So I contended with the rulers and said, "Why is the house of God forsaken?" And I gathered them together and set them at their posts.

**13:12** Then all Judah brought the tithe of the grain, the new wine, and the oil into the storehouses.

**13:13** And I appointed as treasurers over the storehouses Shelemiah the priest and Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites; and at their side was Hanan the son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah; for they were esteemed faithful, and their duty was to distribute to their brethren.

**13:14** Remember me, O my God, concerning this, and do not blot out my faithful deeds that I have done for the house of my God and for its services.

**13:15** In those days I saw men in Judah treading winepresses on the Sabbath, bringing in heaps of grain, and loading donkeys with them, as well as wine, grapes, figs, and all manner of burdens, which they brought into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. And I warned them on the day they sold provisions.

**13:16** Men of Tyre also dwelt in it, who brought in fish and all kinds of merchandise, and sold them on the Sabbath to the children of Judah, and in Jerusalem.

**13:17** Then I contended with the nobles of Judah and said to them, "What is this evil thing that you are doing, profaning the Sabbath day?

**13:18** Did not your fathers do thus, and did not our God bring all this calamity upon us and upon this city? Yet you bring added wrath upon Israel by profaning the Sabbath!"

**13:19** And it came to pass, as the shadows fell upon the gates of Jerusalem before the Sabbath, that I commanded the doors to be shut, and gave orders that they should not be opened until after the Sabbath. And I stationed some of my servants at the gates, so that no burden would enter on the Sabbath day.

**13:20** Now the merchants and sellers of all kinds of wares lodged outside Jerusalem once or twice.

**13:21** Then I warned them and said to them, "Why do you lodge in front of the wall? If you do so again, I will lay hands on you!" From that time on they came no more on the Sabbath.

**13:22** And I commanded the Levites that they should purify themselves, and come and guard the gates, to sanctify the Sabbath day. Remember me, O my God, concerning this also, and spare me according to the abundance of Your lovingkindness.

**13:23** In those days I also saw Jews who had married Ashdodite, Ammonite, and Moabite women.

**13:24** And half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod, and could not speak the language of Judah, but spoke according to the language of each respective people.

**13:25** So I contended with them and cursed them, struck some of the men and pulled out their hair, and made them swear by God, saying, "You shall not give your daughters to their sons, nor take of their daughters for your sons or for yourselves.

**13:26** Did not Solomon king of Israel sin by these things? Yet among many nations there was no king like him, who was beloved by his God; and God made him king over all Israel. Nevertheless, foreign women caused even him to sin.

**13:27** Should we then listen to you and do all this great evil, acting unfaithfully against our God by marrying foreign women?"

**13:28** And one of the sons of Joiada, the son of Eliashib the high priest, was a son-in-law of Sanballat the Horonite; therefore I drove him away from me.

**13:29** Remember them, O my God, because they have defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.

**13:30** Thus I cleansed them of everything foreign, and I appointed duties for the priests and the Levites, each in his service,

**13:31** and for the wood offering at appointed times, and for the firstfruits. Remember me, O my God, for good!

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — Artaxerxes I is called king of Babylon as ruler of the Persian Empire, which encompassed Babylon; his thirty-second year corresponds to 433 BC.
- **13:19** — The verb translated as the shadows fell refers to the deepening dusk at the gates of Jerusalem just before the Sabbath began at sundown.
- **13:23** — The Masoretic text notes an orthographic ketiv/qere variation in the spellings of Ashdodite and Ammonite, with the qere using the shorter vocalized forms without changing the meaning.
- **13:25** — Hebrew oath clauses introduced by if express an emphatic prohibition, rendered here as you shall not.
