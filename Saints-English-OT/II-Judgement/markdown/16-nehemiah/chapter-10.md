## Text

**10:1** And because of all this, we make a sure covenant and set it in writing; and upon the sealed document are our leaders, our Levites, and our priests.

**10:2** Now those who placed their seal on the document were Nehemiah the governor, the son of Hachaliah, and Zedekiah;

**10:3** Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah,

**10:4** Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah,

**10:5** Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch,

**10:6** Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

**10:7** Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch,

**10:8** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**10:9** Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah—these were the priests.

**10:10** And the Levites: Jeshua the son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, and Kadmiel;

**10:11** and their brethren: Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan,

**10:12** Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah,

**10:13** Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

**10:14** Hodiah, Bani, and Beninu.

**10:15** The chiefs of the people: Parosh, Pahath-Moab, Elam, Zattu, Bani,

**10:16** Bunni, Azgad, Bebai,

**10:17** Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin,

**10:18** Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur,

**10:19** Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai,

**10:20** Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,

**10:21** Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir,

**10:22** Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua,

**10:23** Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah,

**10:24** Hoshea, Hananiah, Hasshub,

**10:25** Hallohesh, Pilha, Shobek,

**10:26** Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah,

**10:27** Ahijah, Hanan, Anan,

**10:28** Malluch, Harim, and Baanah.

**10:29** Now the rest of the people—the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and all who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the Law of God, with their wives, their sons, and their daughters, everyone who had knowledge and understanding—

**10:30** joined firmly with their brethren, their nobles, and entered into a curse and an oath to walk in the Law of God, which was given by the hand of Moses the servant of God, and to keep and perform all the commandments of the LORD our Lord, and His judgments and His statutes:

**10:31** that we will not give our daughters to the peoples of the land, nor take their daughters for our sons;

**10:32** and if the peoples of the land bring wares or any grain on the Sabbath day to sell, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day; and we will forego the seventh year and the exaction of every debt.

**10:33** Also we have established ordinances upon ourselves to contribute one-third of a shekel yearly for the service of the house of our God:

**10:34** for the showbread, for the continual grain offering, for the continual burnt offering, for the Sabbaths, the New Moons, and the appointed feasts, for the holy things, and for the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and for all the work of the house of our God.

**10:35** We have also cast lots—the priests, the Levites, and the people—for the wood offering, to bring it to the house of our God, according to our fathers' houses, at appointed times year by year, to burn upon the altar of the LORD our God, as it is written in the Law.

**10:36** We also obligate ourselves to bring the firstfruits of our ground and the firstfruits of all fruit of every tree, year by year, to the house of the LORD;

**10:37** also to bring the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the Law, and the firstborn of our herds and our flocks, to the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God;

**10:38** and we will bring the first of our dough, our contributions, the fruit of every tree, the new wine, and the oil to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God, and the tithe of our ground to the Levites; for the Levites themselves are the ones who collect the tithes in all the cities of our labor.

**10:39** And the priest, the son of Aaron, shall be with the Levites when the Levites receive the tithes; and the Levites shall bring up the tithe of the tithe to the house of our God, to the chambers of the storehouse.

**10:40** For the children of Israel and the sons of Levi shall bring the contribution of the grain, the new wine, and the oil to the chambers, where the vessels of the sanctuary are, together with the priests who minister, the gatekeepers, and the singers; thus we will not neglect the house of our God.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Masoretic versification places this verse at the beginning of chapter 10, corresponding to 9:38 in many English Bibles and shifting the remaining verse numbers of chapter 10 by one.
- **10:2** — The Hebrew title hattirshatha is a Persian loanword designating the provincial governor.
- **10:20** — The qere reads Nebai, followed in the translation; the ketiv reads Nobai.
- **10:29** — Hebrew Nethinim denotes dedicated temple servants who assisted the Levites in sanctuary duties.
