## Text

**7:4** Now the city was wide and great, yet the people within it were few, and no houses had been built.

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — The Masoretic Qere reads "And I said" (first person, continuing Nehemiah's memoir), whereas the Ketiv reads "and he said."
- **7:4** — Literally "broad of hands," a Hebrew idiom denoting spaciousness and wide extent.
- **7:6** — This genealogical register parallels the list in Ezra 2, with several minor differences in clan totals and name spellings that are preserved faithfully from the Hebrew text of Nehemiah.
- **7:46** — Hebrew "Nethinim," literally "those given," referring to hereditary attendants appointed to temple service.
- **7:52** — The Masoretic Qere reads "Nephishesim," whereas the Ketiv reads "Nephushesim."
- **7:65** — Hebrew "Tirshatha," a Persian official title for the provincial governor.
- **7:69** — The Masoretic text followed here passes directly from verse 67 to verse 69, without the tally of horses and mules present in Ezra 2:66 and in some manuscripts.
