## Text

**9:1** When these things were finished, the leaders approached me, saying, "The people of Israel and the priests and the Levites have not separated themselves from the peoples of the lands, concerning their abominations—those of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, the Egyptians, and the Amorites.

**9:2** For they have taken some of their daughters for themselves and for their sons, so that the holy seed has mingled with the peoples of the lands. Indeed, the hand of the leaders and the rulers has been foremost in this unfaithfulness."

**9:3** And when I heard this matter, I tore my garment and my robe, and plucked out some of the hair of my head and my beard, and sat down appalled.

**9:4** Then everyone who trembled at the words of the God of Israel assembled to me because of the unfaithfulness of the exiles, while I sat appalled until the evening offering.

**9:5** And at the evening offering I arose from my fasting, with my garment and my robe torn, and I bowed down on my knees and spread out my hands to the LORD my God.

**9:6** And I said, "O my God, I am ashamed and humiliated to lift up my face to You, my God; for our iniquities have risen higher than our heads, and our guilt has grown up to the heavens.

**9:7** From the days of our fathers to this day we have been in great guilt; and because of our iniquities we, our kings, and our priests have been delivered into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, to plunder, and to shame of face, as it is this day.

**9:8** And now for a brief moment favor has been shown by the LORD our God, to leave us a remnant to escape and to give us a peg in His holy place, that our God may enlighten our eyes and grant us a measure of revival in our bondage.

**9:9** For we are bondservants; yet our God has not forsaken us in our bondage, but He has extended lovingkindness to us in the sight of the kings of Persia, to grant us revival, to raise up the house of our God, to repair its ruins, and to give us a wall in Judah and in Jerusalem.

**9:10** And now, O our God, what shall we say after this? For we have forsaken Your commandments,

**9:11** which You commanded by Your servants the prophets, saying, 'The land which you are entering to possess is an unclean land, defiled by the uncleanness of the peoples of the lands, by their abominations with which they have filled it from one end to the other in their impurity.

**9:12** Now therefore, do not give your daughters to their sons, nor take their daughters for your sons; and never seek their peace or their prosperity, that you may be strong and eat the good of the land, and leave it as an inheritance to your children forever.'

**9:13** And after all that has come upon us for our evil deeds and for our great guilt, since You our God have punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and have given us such a remnant as this,

**9:14** should we again break Your commandments, and join in marriage with the peoples of these abominations? Would You not be angry with us until You had consumed us, so that there would be neither remnant nor survivor?

**9:15** O LORD God of Israel, You are righteous, for we are left as a remnant that has escaped, as it is this day. Behold, we are before You in our guilt, for no one can stand before You because of this."

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — Literally "a peg" (tent peg or nail), a biblical image of secure foothold and stability granted by God in His sanctuary.
- **9:11** — Literally "from mouth to mouth," a Hebrew idiom denoting complete extent from one end to the other.
- **9:13** — Literally "You have held back downward from our iniquity," an idiom expressing that God's discipline was far lighter than Israel's sin merited.
