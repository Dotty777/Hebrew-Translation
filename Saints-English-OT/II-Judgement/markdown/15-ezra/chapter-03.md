## Text

**3:1** When the seventh month arrived, and the children of Israel were in the cities, the people assembled as one man to Jerusalem.

**3:2** Then Jeshua the son of Jozadak and his brethren the priests, and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and his brethren, arose and built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings upon it, as is written in the Law of Moses the man of God.

**3:3** And they set the altar firmly upon its bases, for dread was upon them because of the peoples of the lands; and they offered burnt offerings upon it to the LORD, burnt offerings for the morning and for the evening.

**3:4** They also observed the Feast of Tabernacles as is written, with the daily burnt offerings by number, according to the ordinance for the requirement of each day.

**3:5** And afterward they offered the continual burnt offering, both for the New Moons and for all the consecrated appointed feasts of the LORD, as well as for everyone who willingly brought a freewill offering to the LORD.

**3:6** From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, though the foundation of the temple of the LORD had not yet been laid.

**3:7** They also gave silver to the stonecutters and the craftsmen, and food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and the Tyrians, to bring cedar timbers from Lebanon to the sea at Joppa, according to the grant of Cyrus king of Persia to them.

**3:8** Now in the second year after their coming to the house of God at Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak, with the rest of their brethren the priests and the Levites, and all who had returned from the captivity to Jerusalem, began the work and appointed the Levites from twenty years old and upward to oversee the work of the house of the LORD.

**3:9** Then Jeshua, with his sons and his brethren, and Kadmiel with his sons, the sons of Judah, stood together as one to oversee those performing the work in the house of God, along with the sons of Henadad, their sons, and their brethren the Levites.

**3:10** When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, they stationed the priests in their vestments with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise the LORD according to the ordinance of David king of Israel.

**3:11** And they sang responsively, praising and giving thanks to the LORD: "For He is good, for His lovingkindness endures forever toward Israel." And all the people raised a great shout in praising the LORD, because the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid.

**3:12** Yet many of the priests and Levites and heads of the fathers' houses, aged men who had seen the first house, wept with a loud voice when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, while many lifted up their voice with a shout of joy.

**3:13** So the people could not distinguish the sound of the shout of joy from the sound of the weeping of the people, for the people shouted with a great shout, and the sound was heard far away.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The traditional reading (Qere) has the plural verb 'they offered,' whereas the written consonantal text (Ketiv) has the singular 'he offered.'
- **3:10** — Literally 'by the hands of David,' an idiom denoting liturgical directions established by King David.
