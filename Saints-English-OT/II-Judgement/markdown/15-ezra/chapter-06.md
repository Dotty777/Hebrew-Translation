## Text

**6:1** Then King Darius issued a decree, and a search was made in the house of the archives, where the treasures were stored in Babylon.

**6:2** And in Ecbatana, in the citadel that is in the province of Media, a scroll was found, and within it a record was written thus:

**6:3** "In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus issued a decree concerning the house of God in Jerusalem: Let the house be rebuilt, the place where they offer sacrifices, and let its foundations be firmly laid; its height shall be sixty cubits and its width sixty cubits,

**6:4** with three courses of heavy stones and a course of new timber; and let the cost be paid from the king's treasury.

**6:5** And also let the gold and silver vessels of the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar took out of the temple that is in Jerusalem and brought to Babylon, be restored and returned to the temple that is in Jerusalem, each to its place, and you shall deposit them in the house of God."

**6:6** "Now therefore, Tattenai, governor of the region beyond the River, Shethar-Boznai, and your companions, the officials who are beyond the River, keep far away from there.

**6:7** Leave the work on this house of God alone; let the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews rebuild this house of God on its site.

**6:8** Moreover, I issue a decree regarding what you shall do for these elders of the Jews for the rebuilding of this house of God: from the king's resources, out of the tribute of the region beyond the River, the expenses shall be paid in full to these men without interruption.

**6:9** And whatever is needed—young bulls, rams, and lambs for burnt offerings to the God of heaven, as well as wheat, salt, wine, and oil—according to the word of the priests who are in Jerusalem, let it be given to them day by day without fail,

**6:10** that they may offer sacrifices of sweet aroma to the God of heaven, and pray for the life of the king and his sons.

**6:11** Also I issue a decree that if any man alters this edict, let a beam be pulled out from his house, and let him be lifted up and impaled upon it, and let his house be made a refuse heap because of this.

**6:12** And may the God who has caused His name to dwell there overthrow any king or people who puts forth his hand to alter or to destroy this house of God that is in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have issued a decree; let it be carried out with all diligence."

**6:13** Then Tattenai, governor of the region beyond the River, Shethar-Boznai, and their companions did with all diligence according to what King Darius had sent.

**6:14** So the elders of the Jews built and prospered through the prophesying of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo. And they built and finished it, according to the command of the God of Israel and according to the decree of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes king of Persia.

**6:15** Now this house was finished on the third day of the month of Adar, which was in the sixth year of the reign of King Darius.

**6:16** Then the children of Israel, the priests and the Levites and the rest of the children of the captivity, celebrated the dedication of this house of God with joy.

**6:17** And they offered at the dedication of this house of God one hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and as a sin offering for all Israel twelve male goats, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

**6:18** They appointed the priests in their divisions and the Levites in their courses for the service of God in Jerusalem, as it is written in the Book of Moses.

**6:19** And the children of the captivity kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**6:20** For the priests and the Levites had purified themselves together; all of them were ceremonially clean. So they slaughtered the Passover lamb for all the children of the captivity, for their brethren the priests, and for themselves.

**6:21** Then the children of Israel who had returned from the captivity ate it, together with all who had separated themselves from the uncleanness of the nations of the land to join them in seeking the LORD God of Israel.

**6:22** And they kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with joy; for the LORD made them joyful, and turned the heart of the king of Assyria toward them, to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of God, the God of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Aramaic Achmetha, the royal summer capital of Media known in Greek as Ecbatana.
- **6:6** — The Aramaic term apharsekaye, traditionally transliterated Apharsachites, is likely a Persian administrative title meaning investigators or officials.
- **6:14** — Following the qere neviyya, meaning the prophet, which represents standard Aramaic orthography for the ketiv neviyah.
- **6:17** — Following the qere lechattaah, meaning as a sin offering; the ketiv reflects an alternate spelling or plural form.
- **6:22** — Here the king of Assyria refers to Darius, the Persian monarch whose empire absorbed and ruled the former Assyrian territory.
