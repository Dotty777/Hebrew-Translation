## Text

**1:1** Now in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, so that the word of the LORD from the mouth of Jeremiah might be accomplished, the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he sent a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and also in writing, saying,

**1:2** "Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: The LORD, the God of heaven, has given to me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has charged me to build for Him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah.

**1:3** Who among you is of all His people? Let his God be with him, and let him ascend to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel—He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

**1:4** And as for everyone who remains, in any of the places where he sojourns, let the men of his place support him with silver and with gold, with goods and with livestock, together with the freewill offering for the house of God which is in Jerusalem."

**1:5** Then the heads of the fathers' houses of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and the Levites, arose—everyone whose spirit God had stirred up—to go up to build the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem.

**1:6** And all who were around them strengthened their hands with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, with livestock, and with choice gifts, besides all that was freely offered.

**1:7** King Cyrus also brought forth the vessels of the house of the LORD, which Nebuchadnezzar had brought out from Jerusalem and placed in the house of his gods.

**1:8** Cyrus king of Persia brought them forth by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and numbered them to Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah.

**1:9** And this was their number: thirty basins of gold, one thousand basins of silver, twenty-nine knives;

**1:10** thirty bowls of gold, four hundred and ten bowls of silver of a second sort, and one thousand other vessels.

**1:11** All the vessels of gold and of silver were five thousand four hundred. All of these Sheshbazzar brought up when the exiles were brought up from Babylon to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The phrase "sent a proclamation" translates a Hebrew idiom meaning "caused a voice to pass through." Jeremiah had prophesied that Judah's captivity in Babylon would last seventy years (Jeremiah 25:11-12; 29:10).
- **1:6** — The expression "strengthened their hands" is a Hebrew idiom for lending practical assistance and encouragement, echoing the decree in verse 4 to support the returning exiles.
- **1:9** — The Hebrew nouns translated "basins" and "knives" occur only here in the Hebrew Bible, making their exact identification uncertain.
- **1:11** — The Masoretic total of five thousand four hundred is larger than the sum of the items enumerated in verses 9 and 10, likely because those earlier verses list only the principal vessels.
