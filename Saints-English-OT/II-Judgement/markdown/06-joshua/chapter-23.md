## Text

**23:1** Now it came to pass, many days after the LORD had given rest to Israel from all their enemies on every side, that Joshua was old and advanced in years.

**23:2** Then Joshua called all Israel, their elders, their heads, their judges, and their officers, and said to them, "I am old and advanced in years.

**23:3** And you have seen all that the LORD your God has done to all these nations because of you, for the LORD your God, He is the One who has fought for you.

**23:4** Behold, I have allotted to you these remaining nations as an inheritance for your tribes, from the Jordan, together with all the nations that I have cut off, as far as the Great Sea toward the setting of the sun.

**23:5** And the LORD your God Himself will thrust them back from before you and dispossess them from your presence, and you shall possess their land, just as the LORD your God has spoken to you.

**23:6** Therefore be very strong to keep and to do all that is written in the Book of the Law of Moses, not turning aside from it to the right hand or to the left,

**23:7** that you do not mingle with these nations, these that remain among you; and you shall not make mention of the name of their gods, nor swear by them, nor serve them, nor bow down to them.

**23:8** Rather, you shall cleave to the LORD your God, just as you have done to this day.

**23:9** For the LORD has driven out from before you great and mighty nations; and as for you, not a man has stood before you to this day.

**23:10** One man of you shall chase a thousand, for the LORD your God, He is the One who fights for you, just as He spoke to you.

**23:11** Therefore be very watchful over your souls, to love the LORD your God.

**23:12** For if you indeed turn back, and cleave to the remnant of these nations, these that remain among you, and intermarry with them, and go in among them and they among you,

**23:13** know for a certainty that the LORD your God will no longer drive out these nations from before you; rather, they shall become a snare and a trap to you, and a scourge on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land which the LORD your God has given you.

**23:14** And behold, this day I am going the way of all the earth. And you know in all your hearts and in all your souls that not one word has failed of all the good things which the LORD your God spoke concerning you; all have come to pass for you, not one word of them has failed.

**23:15** And it shall come to pass, that just as every good thing has come upon you which the LORD your God spoke to you, so the LORD will bring upon you every evil thing, until He has destroyed you from this good land which the LORD your God has given you.

**23:16** When you transgress the covenant of the LORD your God, which He commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them, then the anger of the LORD shall burn against you, and you shall perish swiftly from the good land which He has given you."

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — 'I have allotted' translates Hebrew hippalti, literally 'I have caused to fall,' referring to the division of tribal territories by casting lots.
- **23:13** — 'Scourge' translates a rare Hebrew term (shotet) related to 'whip,' depicting the remaining nations as an enduring affliction upon Israel.
- **23:14** — 'The way of all the earth' is a solemn Hebrew idiom for death as the universal lot of humanity.
