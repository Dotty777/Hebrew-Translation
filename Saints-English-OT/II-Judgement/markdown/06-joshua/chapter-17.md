## Text

**17:1** There was also an allotment for the tribe of Manasseh, for he was the firstborn of Joseph: for Machir, the firstborn of Manasseh and father of Gilead, since he was a man of war, Gilead and Bashan belonged to him.

**17:2** And an allotment was made for the remaining sons of Manasseh according to their clans: for the sons of Abiezer, the sons of Helek, the sons of Asriel, the sons of Shechem, the sons of Hepher, and the sons of Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh the son of Joseph according to their clans.

**17:3** Now Zelophehad the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, had no sons, only daughters. And these are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**17:4** They came near before Eleazar the priest, before Joshua the son of Nun, and before the leaders, saying, "The LORD commanded Moses to grant us an inheritance in the midst of our brethren." Therefore, according to the word of the LORD, he granted them an inheritance in the midst of their father's brethren.

**17:5** Thus ten portions fell to Manasseh, apart from the land of Gilead and Bashan, which lies beyond the Jordan,

**17:6** because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance in the midst of his sons; and the land of Gilead belonged to the remaining sons of Manasseh.

**17:7** Now the boundary of Manasseh ran from Asher to Michmethath, which faces Shechem; and the boundary proceeded southward toward the inhabitants of En Tappuah.

**17:8** The region of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, yet Tappuah itself on the border of Manasseh belonged to the sons of Ephraim.

**17:9** Then the boundary descended to the Brook of Kanah, on the south side of the brook—these cities belonged to Ephraim within the cities of Manasseh—while the boundary of Manasseh lay on the north side of the brook, and its farthest limits reached to the sea.

**17:10** The south side belonged to Ephraim and the north side to Manasseh, with the sea as its border; they joined Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.

**17:11** Within Issachar and Asher, Manasseh also possessed Beth Shean and its dependent towns, Ibleam and its dependent towns, the inhabitants of Dor and its dependent towns, the inhabitants of En Dor and its dependent towns, the inhabitants of Taanach and its dependent towns, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its dependent towns—three elevated regions.

**17:12** Yet the sons of Manasseh were unable to take possession of these cities, for the Canaanites resolved to remain dwelling in that land.

**17:13** And it came to pass, when the children of Israel grew strong, that they subjected the Canaanites to forced labor, yet did not completely dispossess them.

**17:14** Then the sons of Joseph spoke with Joshua, saying, "Why have you assigned to me only one lot and one portion as an inheritance, when I am a numerous people, since the LORD has blessed me so abundantly until now?"

**17:15** Joshua answered them, "If you are indeed a numerous people, go up for yourself into the forest and clear ground for yourself there in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, since the hill country of Ephraim is too narrow for you."

**17:16** And the sons of Joseph replied, "The hill country is not sufficient for us; moreover, all the Canaanites who dwell in the valley land possess chariots of iron—both those in Beth Shean and its dependent towns and those in the Valley of Jezreel."

**17:17** Then Joshua spoke to the house of Joseph—to Ephraim and to Manasseh—saying, "You are a numerous people and possess great might; you shall not have merely one allotment.

**17:18** Rather, the hill country shall belong to you; though it is a forest, you shall clear it, and its farthest limits shall be yours. For you shall indeed dispossess the Canaanites, even though they have chariots of iron and even though they are mighty."

## Translator Notes

- **17:7** — Literally 'to the right hand,' which denotes southward in Hebrew geographical orientation facing the rising sun.
- **17:11** — The Hebrew term rendered 'dependent towns' is literally 'her daughters,' signifying satellite villages attached to a major city, while 'three elevated regions' translates a rare phrase referring to three upland districts.
- **17:14** — The sons of Joseph address Joshua in the collective first-person singular, speaking as a single united house to request a larger territorial allotment.
