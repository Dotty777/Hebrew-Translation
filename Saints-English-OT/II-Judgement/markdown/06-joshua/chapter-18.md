## Text

**18:1** Then the entire assembly of the children of Israel gathered together at Shiloh and erected the tent of meeting there; and the land was subdued before them.

**18:2** Yet among the children of Israel there remained seven tribes whose inheritance had not yet been apportioned.

**18:3** So Joshua said to the children of Israel, "How long will you slacken your hands from going in to take possession of the land which the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you?

**18:4** Appoint for yourselves three men from each tribe, and I will send them out. They shall arise, walk throughout the land, write a description of it in accordance with their inheritance, and return to me.

**18:5** They shall divide it into seven portions: Judah shall remain within its territory on the south, and the house of Joseph shall remain within their territory on the north.

**18:6** You shall record the land in seven portions and bring the description here to me, so that I may cast lots for you in this place before the LORD our God.

**18:7** For the Levites have no portion in your midst, because the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance; and Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their inheritance beyond the Jordan toward the east, which Moses the servant of the LORD gave them."

**18:8** Then the men arose and departed; and Joshua charged those who were setting out to record the land, saying, "Go, walk throughout the land, write a description of it, and return to me; and here I will cast lots for you before the LORD at Shiloh."

**18:9** So the men went and traversed the land, and recorded it city by city in seven portions upon a scroll; then they returned to Joshua at the encampment in Shiloh.

**18:10** And Joshua cast lots for them at Shiloh before the LORD; and there Joshua apportioned the land to the children of Israel according to their divisions.

**18:11** Now the lot of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin came up according to their clans, and the territory of their allotment lay between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph.

**18:12** On the northern side their boundary began from the Jordan; the boundary ascended to the slope of Jericho on the north, continued upward through the hill country westward, and its farthest limits reached to the wilderness of Beth Aven.

**18:13** From there the boundary crossed over to Luz, along the southern slope of Luz—that is, Bethel—and the boundary descended to Ataroth Addar, upon the mountain that lies south of Lower Beth Horon.

**18:14** Then the boundary turned and curved around on the western side southward, from the mountain that faces Beth Horon on the south, and its farthest limits reached to Kiriath Baal—that is, Kiriath Jearim—a city belonging to the sons of Judah. This formed the western side.

**18:15** The southern side began at the edge of Kiriath Jearim; the boundary extended westward and came out at the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

**18:16** Then the boundary descended to the foot of the mountain facing the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, which lies at the northern end of the Valley of Rephaim; it descended through the Valley of Hinnom to the southern slope of the Jebusite ridge, and went down to En Rogel.

**18:17** From there it turned northward, came out at En Shemesh, continued to Geliloth opposite the Ascent of Adummim, and descended to the Stone of Bohan the son of Reuben.

**18:18** It passed along to the slope facing the Arabah on the north and went down into the Arabah.

**18:19** Then the boundary crossed over to the northern slope of Beth Hoglah, and the farthest limits of the boundary reached to the northern bay of the Salt Sea, at the southern mouth of the Jordan. This formed the southern boundary.

**18:20** And the Jordan formed its border on the eastern side. This was the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, bounded on every side according to their clans.

**18:21** Now the cities belonging to the tribe of the sons of Benjamin according to their clans were Jericho, Beth Hoglah, and Emek Keziz,

**18:22** Beth Arabah, Zemaraim, and Bethel,

**18:23** Avvim, Parah, and Ophrah,

**18:24** Chephar Ammonah, Ophni, and Geba—twelve cities along with their surrounding villages;

**18:25** Gibeon, Ramah, and Beeroth,

**18:26** Mizpah, Chephirah, and Mozah,

**18:27** Rekem, Irpeel, and Taralah,

**18:28** Zela, Haeleph, the Jebusite city, that is, Jerusalem, Gibeath, and Kiriath—fourteen cities along with their surrounding villages. This was the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to their clans.

## Translator Notes

- **18:12** — The Masoretic ketiv reads the singular verb 'and it was,' while the qere reads the plural 'and they were' in grammatical agreement with 'its farthest limits'; this translation follows the qere.
- **18:14** — As in verse 12, the Masoretic ketiv has a singular verb whereas the qere reads the plural verb matching 'its farthest limits'; this translation follows the qere.
- **18:19** — The Masoretic ketiv reads a singular verb with 'its limits,' while the qere reads a plural verb with 'the limits of the boundary'; this translation follows the qere.
- **18:24** — The Masoretic ketiv reads the place name as 'Chephar Haammoni,' whereas the qere reads 'Chephar Haammonah,' rendered here as Chephar Ammonah.
