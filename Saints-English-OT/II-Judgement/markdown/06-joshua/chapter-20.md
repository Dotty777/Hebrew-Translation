## Text

**20:1** And the LORD spoke to Joshua, saying,

**20:2** "Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'Appoint for yourselves the cities of refuge, of which I spoke to you through Moses,

**20:3** that the slayer who kills a person unintentionally, without knowledge, may flee there; and they shall be a refuge for you from the avenger of blood.'"

**20:7** So they set apart Kedesh in Galilee, in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath Arba—that is, Hebron—in the hill country of Judah.

**20:8** And beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they appointed Bezer in the wilderness on the tableland, from the tribe of Reuben, Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.

**20:9** These were the cities appointed for all the children of Israel and for the sojourner who dwells among them, that whoever kills a person unintentionally might flee there, and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood until he stood before the congregation.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — Unintentionally, without knowledge distinguishes accidental manslaughter from premeditated murder.
- **20:8** — Golan follows the traditional Hebrew reading (Qere); the written text (Ketiv) reads Galon.
