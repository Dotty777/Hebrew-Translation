## Text

**15:1** Now the lot for the tribe of the children of Judah, according to their families, reached to the border of Edom, the Wilderness of Zin southward, at the utmost end of the south.

**15:2** And their southern border began at the end of the Salt Sea, from the bay that faces southward.

**15:3** It went out to the south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, passed over to Zin, and went up on the south side of Kadesh Barnea; then it passed along to Hezron, went up to Addar, and turned around to Karka.

**15:4** And it passed over to Azmon and went out to the Brook of Egypt, and the ends of the border were at the sea. This shall be your southern border.

**15:5** And the eastern border was the Salt Sea as far as the mouth of the Jordan. And the border on the northern side was from the bay of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan.

**15:6** Then the border went up to Beth Hoglah and passed north of Beth Arabah; and the border went up to the stone of Bohan the son of Reuben.

**15:7** And the border went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor, and turned northward toward Gilgal, which is opposite the Ascent of Adummim, on the south side of the brook; then the border passed along to the waters of En Shemesh, and its ends were at En Rogel.

**15:8** Then the border went up by the Valley of the Son of Hinnom to the southern slope of the Jebusite, which is Jerusalem; and the border went up to the top of the mountain that lies before the Valley of Hinnom westward, which is at the end of the Valley of Rephaim northward.

**15:9** And the border curved from the top of the mountain to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah, and went out to the cities of Mount Ephron; then the border curved to Baalah, which is Kiriath Jearim.

**15:10** Then the border turned around from Baalah westward to Mount Seir, passed along to the northern slope of Mount Jearim, which is Chesalon, went down to Beth Shemesh, and passed on to Timnah.

**15:11** And the border went out to the slope of Ekron northward; and the border curved to Shikkeron, passed along to Mount Baalah, and went out to Jabneel; and the ends of the border were at the sea.

**15:12** And the western border was the Great Sea and its coastline. This was the border of the children of Judah all around, according to their families.

**15:13** Now to Caleb the son of Jephunneh he gave a portion among the children of Judah, according to the command of the LORD to Joshua, namely, Kiriath Arba—Arba was the father of Anak—which is Hebron.

**15:14** And Caleb drove out from there the three sons of Anak: Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, the offspring of Anak.

**15:15** Then he went up from there against the inhabitants of Debir. Now the name of Debir formerly was Kiriath Sepher.

**15:16** And Caleb said, "He who strikes Kiriath Sepher and captures it, to him I will give Achsah my daughter as a wife."

**15:17** And Othniel the son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb, captured it; and he gave him Achsah his daughter as a wife.

**15:18** Now it came to pass, when she came, that she urged him to ask her father for a field. And she dismounted from the donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What do you wish?"

**15:19** And she said, "Give me a blessing; since you have given me the land of the Negev, give me also springs of water." So he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

**15:20** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Judah according to their families.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Hebrew word translated "bay" literally means "tongue," referring to the southern inlet of the Salt Sea.
- **15:4** — The Masoretic Ketiv reads the singular verb "was," while the Qere reads the plural "were" to agree with the plural noun "ends"; the translation follows the Qere. The shift to the second-person plural "your" echoes the southern border description in Numbers 34:3-5.
- **15:19** — "Negev" refers to the arid southern region of Judah, which made springs of water vital for sustaining land and flocks.
- **15:32** — The summary total "twenty-nine" faithfully renders the Masoretic Text, though the preceding catalog lists more than twenty-nine individual names, some of which may be compound names or dependent settlements.
- **15:47** — The Masoretic Ketiv reads "the border," while the Qere reads "the Great" for "the Great Sea" (the Mediterranean); the translation follows the Qere.
- **15:52** — The Masoretic Text reads "Rumah," whereas some Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions read "Dumah."
- **15:53** — The Masoretic Ketiv spells the town name "Janim," while the Qere reads "Janum"; the translation follows the Qere.
- **15:63** — The Masoretic Ketiv uses the imperfect form of the verb "be able," while the Qere uses the perfect form; the translation follows the Qere.
