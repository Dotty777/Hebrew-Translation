## Text

**19:1** The second lot came out for Simeon, for the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families. And their inheritance was within the inheritance of the children of Judah.

**19:2** They had in their inheritance Beersheba, Sheba, and Moladah,

**19:3** Hazar Shual, Balah, and Ezem,

**19:4** Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah,

**19:5** Ziklag, Beth Marcaboth, and Hazar Susah,

**19:6** Beth Lebaoth, and Sharuhen: thirteen cities and their villages.

**19:7** Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan: four cities and their villages;

**19:8** and all the villages that were around these cities as far as Baalath Beer, Ramath of the South. This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families.

**19:9** From the portion of the children of Judah came the inheritance of the children of Simeon, for the share of the children of Judah was too great for them; therefore the children of Simeon received their inheritance within their inheritance.

**19:10** The third lot came up for the children of Zebulun according to their families, and the border of their inheritance reached as far as Sarid.

**19:11** Their border went up westward to Maralah, touched Dabbasheth, and reached the brook that faces Jokneam.

**19:12** Then from Sarid it turned eastward toward the rising of the sun to the border of Chisloth Tabor, went out to Daberath, and went up to Japhia.

**19:13** From there it passed along eastward toward the sunrise to Gath Hepher, to Eth Kazin, and went out to Rimmon, curving toward Neah.

**19:14** Then the border went around it on the north to Hannathon, and its endpoints were the Valley of Jiphthah El.

**19:15** Also Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem: twelve cities and their villages.

**19:16** This was the inheritance of the children of Zebulun according to their families, these cities and their villages.

**19:17** The fourth lot came out for Issachar, for the children of Issachar according to their families.

**19:18** And their territory included Jezreel, Chesulloth, Shunem,

**19:19** Hapharaim, Shion, Anaharath,

**19:20** Rabbith, Kishion, Ebez,

**19:21** Remeth, En Gannim, En Haddah, and Beth Pazzez.

**19:22** And the border reached Tabor, Shahazimah, and Beth Shemesh; and the endpoints of their border were at the Jordan: sixteen cities and their villages.

**19:23** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Issachar according to their families, the cities and their villages.

**19:24** The fifth lot came out for the tribe of the children of Asher according to their families.

**19:25** And their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, Achshaph,

**19:26** Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal; it reached Carmel on the west, and Shihor Libnath.

**19:27** Then it turned toward the rising of the sun to Beth Dagon, and reached Zebulun and the Valley of Jiphthah El northward to Beth Emek and Neiel, and went out to Cabul on the left,

**19:28** and to Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as Great Sidon.

**19:29** Then the border turned to Ramah and as far as the fortified city of Tyre; then the border turned to Hosah, and its endpoints were at the sea from the region of Achzib.

**19:30** Also Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob: twenty-two cities and their villages.

**19:31** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Asher according to their families, these cities and their villages.

**19:32** The sixth lot came out for the children of Naphtali, for the children of Naphtali according to their families.

**19:33** And their border ran from Heleph, from the oak in Zaanannim, and Adami Nekeb, and Jabneel, as far as Lakkum; and its endpoints were at the Jordan.

**19:34** Then the border turned westward to Aznoth Tabor, and went out from there to Hukkok; it reached Zebulun on the south, and reached Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan toward the rising of the sun.

**19:35** And the fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Chinnereth,

**19:36** Adamah, Ramah, Hazor,

**19:37** Kedesh, Edrei, En Hazor,

**19:38** Iron, Migdal El, Horem, Beth Anath, and Beth Shemesh: nineteen cities and their villages.

**19:39** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Naphtali according to their families, the cities and their villages.

**19:40** The seventh lot came out for the tribe of the children of Dan according to their families.

**19:41** And the territory of their inheritance included Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir Shemesh,

**19:42** Shaalabbin, Aijalon, Ithlah,

**19:43** Elon, Timnah, Ekron,

**19:44** Eltekeh, Gibbethon, Baalath,

**19:45** Jehud, Bene Berak, Gath Rimmon,

**19:46** Me Jarkon, and Rakkon, with the territory opposite Joppa.

**19:47** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Dan according to their families, these cities and their villages.

**19:48** And the territory of the children of Dan went beyond them; so the children of Dan went up and fought against Leshem, captured it, and struck it with the edge of the sword. They possessed it and dwelt in it, and they called Leshem Dan, after the name of Dan their father.

**19:49** When they had finished dividing the land as an inheritance according to its borders, the children of Israel gave an inheritance to Joshua the son of Nun in their midst.

**19:50** According to the command of the LORD they gave him the city for which he asked, Timnath Serah in the hill country of Ephraim; and he built the city and dwelt in it.

**19:51** These are the inheritances which Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the children of Israel distributed as an inheritance by lot in Shiloh before the LORD, at the doorway of the tent of meeting. So they finished dividing the land.

## Translator Notes

- **19:22** — Shahazimah follows the traditional Hebrew reading (Qere); the written text (Ketiv) has Shahazumah.
- **19:27** — On the left reflects the Hebrew directional idiom for north when facing east.
- **19:29** — And its endpoints were follows the traditional Hebrew reading (Qere) rather than the written variant (Ketiv) of the verb.
- **19:48** — Went beyond them renders a Hebrew idiom indicating that the Danites' allotted territory slipped from them or proved insufficient, prompting their northern campaign.
