## Text

**2:1** Now Joshua the son of Nun sent out two men from Shittim as spies secretly, saying, "Go, view the land and Jericho." So they went and came to the house of a harlot whose name was Rahab, and lodged there.

**2:2** And it was told to the king of Jericho, saying, "Behold, men from the children of Israel have come here tonight to search out the land."

**2:3** So the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who have come to you, who have entered your house, for they have come to search out all the land."

**2:4** Then the woman took the two men and hid them. And she said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.

**2:5** And it happened as the gate was about to be shut, in the darkness, that the men went out. I do not know where the men went; pursue them quickly, for you will overtake them."

**2:6** Now she had brought them up to the roof and concealed them under the stalks of flax which she had laid in order upon the roof.

**2:7** So the men pursued after them along the road to the Jordan as far as the fords; and they shut the gate as soon as those who pursued them had gone out.

**2:8** Now before they lay down, she came up to them on the roof,

**2:9** and said to the men, "I know that the LORD has given you the land, that the terror of you has fallen upon us, and that all the inhabitants of the land have melted away because of you.

**2:10** For we have heard how the LORD dried up the waters of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom you utterly destroyed.

**2:11** And as soon as we heard, our hearts melted, and no spirit remained any longer in any man because of you; for the LORD your God, He is God in heaven above and on earth beneath.

**2:12** Now therefore, please swear to me by the LORD, since I have shown lovingkindness to you, that you also will show lovingkindness to my father's house, and give me a true token,

**2:13** and keep alive my father, my mother, my brothers, my sisters, and all that belongs to them, and deliver our lives from death."

**2:14** So the men answered her, "Our lives for yours, even to death, if you do not report this business of ours! And it shall be, when the LORD gives us the land, that we will deal with you in lovingkindness and truth."

**2:15** Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was on the side of the city wall, and she dwelt on the wall.

**2:16** And she said to them, "Go to the hill country, lest the pursuers encounter you; and hide yourselves there three days until the pursuers have returned, and afterward you may go on your way."

**2:17** Then the men said to her, "We shall be guiltless of this oath of yours which you have made us swear:

**2:18** Behold, when we come into the land, you shall bind this cord of scarlet thread in the window through which you let us down, and you shall gather your father, your mother, your brothers, and all your father's household to yourself into the house.

**2:19** And it shall be that whoever goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood shall be on his own head, and we shall be guiltless; and whoever is with you in the house, his blood shall be on our head if a hand is laid upon him.

**2:20** And if you report this business of ours, then we shall be free from your oath which you have made us swear."

**2:21** And she said, "According to your words, so let it be." Then she sent them away, and they departed; and she bound the scarlet cord in the window.

**2:22** So they departed and came to the hill country, and remained there three days until the pursuers returned. And the pursuers sought them all along the road, but did not find them.

**2:23** Then the two men returned, came down from the hill country, crossed over, and came to Joshua the son of Nun, and recounted to him all that had befallen them.

**2:24** And they said to Joshua, "Truly the LORD has delivered all the land into our hand; and indeed, all the inhabitants of the land have melted away before us."

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — The Hebrew literally reads "and hid him" with a singular suffix, used distributively for hiding each of the two spies.
- **2:13** — The Ketiv (written consonants) reads a form that may be taken as singular or defective plural ("my sister" or "my sisters"), while the Qere (read tradition) explicitly reads plural "my sisters," followed here.
- **2:18** — The Hebrew word for "cord" (tiqvah) is identical in form to the word for "hope" or "expectation," lending quiet resonance to Rahab's sign of deliverance.
