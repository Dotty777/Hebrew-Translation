## Text

**6:1** Now Jericho was tightly shut up because of the children of Israel; none went out, and none came in.

**6:2** And the LORD said to Joshua, "See, I have given Jericho into your hand, along with its king and the mighty men of valor.

**6:3** You shall march around the city, all you men of war, going around the city once. Thus you shall do for six days.

**6:5** And it shall come to pass, when they make a long blast with the ram's horn, and when you hear the sound of the trumpet, that all the people shall shout with a great shout; then the wall of the city shall fall down flat, and the people shall go up, every man straight before him."

**6:6** Then Joshua the son of Nun summoned the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests bear seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark of the LORD."

**6:7** And he said to the people, "Go forward and march around the city, and let the armed men pass on before the ark of the LORD."

**6:8** And it came to pass, when Joshua had spoken to the people, that the seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams' horns before the LORD went forward and blew the trumpets, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed behind them.

**6:9** The armed men marched before the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard came after the ark, while the trumpets were blown continually.

**6:10** Now Joshua had commanded the people, saying, "You shall not shout nor let your voice be heard, nor shall any word go forth from your mouth, until the day I say to you, 'Shout!' Then you shall shout."

**6:11** So he caused the ark of the LORD to go around the city, encircling it once; then they came into the camp and lodged in the camp.

**6:12** And Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the LORD.

**6:13** And the seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark of the LORD marched on continually and blew the trumpets; the armed men marched before them, and the rear guard came after the ark of the LORD, while the trumpets were blown continually.

**6:14** And on the second day they marched around the city once and returned to the camp. Thus they did for six days.

**6:15** And it came to pass on the seventh day that they rose early at the dawning of the day and marched around the city in the same manner seven times; only on that day did they march around the city seven times.

**6:16** And at the seventh time, when the priests blew the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, "Shout, for the LORD has given you the city!

**6:17** And the city shall be devoted to destruction, it and all that is in it, to the LORD; only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers whom we sent.

**6:18** And as for you, keep yourselves from the things devoted to destruction, lest you bring yourselves under the ban when you take of the devoted things, and make the camp of Israel devoted to destruction, and bring trouble upon it.

**6:19** But all the silver and gold, and vessels of bronze and iron, are holy to the LORD; they shall go into the treasury of the LORD."

**6:20** So the people shouted, and the trumpets were blown. And it came to pass, when the people heard the sound of the trumpet, that the people shouted with a great shout, and the wall fell down flat. Then the people went up into the city, every man straight before him, and they captured the city.

**6:21** And they utterly destroyed all that was in the city, both man and woman, young and old, and ox and sheep and donkey, with the edge of the sword.

**6:22** But to the two men who had spied out the land Joshua said, "Go into the house of the harlot, and bring out from there the woman and all that belongs to her, just as you swore to her."

**6:23** So the young men who had spied went in and brought out Rahab, her father, her mother, her brothers, and all that belonged to her; they also brought out all her kindred and placed them outside the camp of Israel.

**6:24** And they burned the city with fire, and all that was in it; only the silver and the gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, they put into the treasury of the house of the LORD.

**6:25** But Joshua spared Rahab the harlot, her father's household, and all that belonged to her; and she dwells in the midst of Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

**6:26** Then Joshua charged them with an oath at that time, saying, "Cursed be the man before the LORD who rises up and builds this city, Jericho; at the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he shall set up its gates."

**6:27** So the LORD was with Joshua, and his fame was throughout all the land.

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — The qere reads kesham'akhem ("when you hear"), whereas the ketiv reads besham'akhem ("at your hearing").
- **6:7** — The qere reads singular wayyomer ("and he said," referring to Joshua), whereas the ketiv reads plural wayyomeru ("and they said").
- **6:9** — The qere reads the construct participle toqe'ei ("who blew"), whereas the ketiv reads the perfect verb taq'u ("they blew").
- **6:13** — The qere reads the infinitive absolute halokh (indicating continuous action), whereas the ketiv reads the participle holekh ("going").
- **6:17** — "Devoted to destruction" renders Hebrew cherem, denoting things or persons irrevocably set apart to the LORD under the ban of total destruction.
