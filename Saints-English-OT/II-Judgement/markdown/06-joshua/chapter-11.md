## Text

**11:1** Now it came to pass, when Jabin king of Hazor heard this, that he sent to Jobab king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, to the king of Achshaph,

**11:2** and to the kings who were in the north, in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinneroth, in the lowland, and in the heights of Dor on the west,

**11:3** to the Canaanites on the east and on the west, the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, and the Jebusites in the hill country, and the Hivites below Hermon in the land of Mizpah.

**11:4** So they went out, they and all their armies with them, a great multitude, as numerous as the sand that is upon the seashore, with very many horses and chariots.

**11:5** Then all these kings assembled together, and they came and encamped together at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.

**11:6** And the LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid before them, for tomorrow at this very time I will give them all up slain before Israel; their horses you shall hamstring, and their chariots you shall burn with fire."

**11:7** So Joshua and all the men of war with him came against them suddenly by the waters of Merom, and they fell upon them.

**11:8** And the LORD delivered them into the hand of Israel; they struck them and pursued them as far as Great Sidon, to Misrephoth Maim, and to the Valley of Mizpeh eastward; and they struck them down until they left them no survivor.

**11:9** So Joshua did to them just as the LORD had spoken to him: their horses he hamstrung, and their chariots he burned with fire.

**11:10** Then Joshua turned back at that time and captured Hazor, and its king he struck down with the sword; for Hazor formerly was the head of all those kingdoms.

**11:11** And they struck every soul who was in it with the edge of the sword, utterly destroying them; nothing that breathed remained, and he burned Hazor with fire.

**11:12** And all the cities of those kings, and all their kings, Joshua captured, and he struck them with the edge of the sword, utterly destroying them, just as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded.

**11:13** Yet as for all the cities that stood on their mounds, Israel did not burn them, except Hazor alone, which Joshua burned.

**11:14** And all the spoil of those cities, along with the livestock, the children of Israel took as plunder for themselves; only every person they struck with the edge of the sword until they had destroyed them, and they left nothing that breathed.

**11:15** Just as the LORD had commanded Moses His servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did; he did not omit a single thing of all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

**11:16** Thus Joshua took all this land: the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowland, the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel and its lowland,

**11:17** from Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir, even as far as Baal Gad in the Valley of Lebanon below Mount Hermon; and all their kings he captured, struck down, and put to death.

**11:18** For many days Joshua made war with all those kings.

**11:19** There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, except the Hivites, the inhabitants of Gibeon; all the others they took in battle.

**11:20** For it was of the LORD to harden their hearts to meet Israel in battle, that they might be utterly destroyed and receive no mercy, but be annihilated, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**11:21** And Joshua came at that time and cut off the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, from all the hill country of Judah, and from all the hill country of Israel; Joshua utterly destroyed them along with their cities.

**11:22** None of the Anakim were left in the land of the children of Israel; only in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod did some remain.

**11:23** So Joshua took the whole land, according to all that the LORD had spoken to Moses; and Joshua gave it as an inheritance to Israel according to their divisions by their tribes. Then the land had rest from war.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — The Hebrew naphoth denotes elevated uplands or heights along the coastal plain near Dor, and Chinneroth is the plural form of the name for the Sea of Galilee and its adjacent region.
- **11:13** — Hebrew tillam refers to the fortified mounds, or tells, formed by successive layers of settlement upon which ancient Canaanite cities stood.
- **11:17** — Hebrew ha-har he-halaq literally means 'the smooth mountain' or 'the bare mountain,' marking the southern boundary of Joshua's campaigns toward Seir.
