## Text

**21:1** Then the heads of the fathers' households of the Levites came near to Eleazar the priest, and to Joshua the son of Nun, and to the heads of the fathers' households of the tribes of the children of Israel.

**21:2** And they spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, "The LORD commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities in which to dwell, together with their pasturelands for our livestock."

**21:3** So the children of Israel gave to the Levites out of their inheritance, according to the command of the LORD, these cities and their pasturelands.

**21:4** Now the lot came out for the families of the Kohathites. And the sons of Aaron the priest, who were of the Levites, received by lot thirteen cities from the tribe of Judah, from the tribe of the Simeonites, and from the tribe of Benjamin.

**21:5** And the remaining sons of Kohath received by lot ten cities from the families of the tribe of Ephraim, from the tribe of Dan, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**21:6** And the sons of Gershon received by lot thirteen cities from the families of the tribe of Issachar, from the tribe of Asher, from the tribe of Naphtali, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.

**21:7** The sons of Merari according to their families received twelve cities from the tribe of Reuben, from the tribe of Gad, and from the tribe of Zebulun.

**21:8** Thus the children of Israel gave these cities with their pasturelands by lot to the Levites, as the LORD had commanded by the hand of Moses.

**21:9** So they gave from the tribe of the children of Judah and from the tribe of the children of Simeon these cities which are designated by name.

**21:10** And they fell to the sons of Aaron, of the families of the Kohathites, of the sons of Levi, for the first lot belonged to them.

**21:11** And they gave them Kiriath Arba, the father of Anak—which is Hebron—in the hill country of Judah, together with its pasturelands around it.

**21:12** But the fields of the city and its villages they gave to Caleb the son of Jephunneh as his possession.

**21:13** Thus to the sons of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron with its pasturelands, the city of refuge for the manslayer, and Libnah with its pasturelands,

**21:14** and Jattir with its pasturelands, and Eshtemoa with its pasturelands,

**21:15** and Holon with its pasturelands, and Debir with its pasturelands,

**21:16** and Ain with its pasturelands, and Juttah with its pasturelands, and Beth Shemesh with its pasturelands—nine cities from these two tribes;

**21:17** and from the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon with its pasturelands, Geba with its pasturelands,

**21:18** Anathoth with its pasturelands, and Almon with its pasturelands—four cities.

**21:19** All the cities of the sons of Aaron, the priests, were thirteen cities with their pasturelands.

**21:20** As for the families of the sons of Kohath, the Levites, the rest of the sons of Kohath, the cities of their lot were from the tribe of Ephraim.

**21:21** And they gave them Shechem with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim, the city of refuge for the manslayer, and Gezer with its pasturelands,

**21:22** and Kibzaim with its pasturelands, and Beth Horon with its pasturelands—four cities;

**21:23** and from the tribe of Dan: Eltekeh with its pasturelands, Gibbethon with its pasturelands,

**21:24** Aijalon with its pasturelands, and Gath Rimmon with its pasturelands—four cities;

**21:25** and from the half-tribe of Manasseh: Taanach with its pasturelands and Gath Rimmon with its pasturelands—two cities.

**21:26** All the cities with their pasturelands for the remaining families of the sons of Kohath were ten.

**21:27** And to the sons of Gershon, of the families of the Levites, they gave from the half-tribe of Manasseh: Golan in Bashan with its pasturelands, the city of refuge for the manslayer, and Beeshterah with its pasturelands—two cities;

**21:28** and from the tribe of Issachar: Kishion with its pasturelands, Daberath with its pasturelands,

**21:29** Jarmuth with its pasturelands, and En Gannim with its pasturelands—four cities;

**21:30** and from the tribe of Asher: Mishal with its pasturelands, Abdon with its pasturelands,

**21:31** Helkath with its pasturelands, and Rehob with its pasturelands—four cities;

**21:32** and from the tribe of Naphtali: Kedesh in Galilee with its pasturelands, the city of refuge for the manslayer, Hammoth Dor with its pasturelands, and Kartan with its pasturelands—three cities.

**21:33** All the cities of the Gershonites according to their families were thirteen cities with their pasturelands.

**21:34** And to the families of the sons of Merari, the rest of the Levites, they gave from the tribe of Zebulun: Jokneam with its pasturelands, Kartah with its pasturelands,

**21:35** Dimnah with its pasturelands, and Nahalal with its pasturelands—four cities;

**21:36** ⟦and from the tribe of Reuben: Bezer with its pasturelands, and Jahaz with its pasturelands,⟧

**21:37** ⟦Kedemoth with its pasturelands, and Mephaath with its pasturelands—four cities;⟧

**21:38** and from the tribe of Gad: Ramoth in Gilead with its pasturelands, the city of refuge for the manslayer, and Mahanaim with its pasturelands,

**21:39** Heshbon with its pasturelands, and Jazer with its pasturelands—four cities in all.

**21:40** So all the cities for the sons of Merari according to their families, who remained of the families of the Levites, were by their lot twelve cities.

**21:42** Each of these cities had its pasturelands all around it; thus it was with every one of these cities.

**21:43** Thus the LORD gave Israel all the land that He had sworn to give to their fathers, and they possessed it and dwelt in it.

**21:44** And the LORD granted them rest on every side, according to all that He had sworn to their fathers. Not a man of all their enemies stood before them; the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand.

**21:45** Not a single word failed of every good promise that the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel; everything came to pass.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Hebrew term rendered pasturelands denotes the open, unbuilt grazing and common lands surrounding each Levitical city for livestock, as prescribed in Numbers 35:2-5.
- **21:27** — The Hebrew Qere reads Golan, which is followed here, whereas the consonantal Ketiv spells the name with transposed letters as Galon or Golon.
- **21:36** — Verses 36 and 37 are enclosed in double brackets because they are absent from several leading Masoretic manuscripts, including the Leningrad Codex, though they are preserved in other Hebrew manuscripts, the Septuagint, and 1 Chronicles 6:78-79, and are required to reach the stated totals of twelve Merarite cities in verse 40 and forty-eight Levitical cities in verse 41.
- **21:45** — The phrase Not a single word failed translates the Hebrew idiom meaning literally that not a word fell to the ground, signifying the complete fulfillment of the LORD's covenant promises.
