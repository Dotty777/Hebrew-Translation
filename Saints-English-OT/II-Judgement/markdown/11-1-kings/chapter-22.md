## Text

**22:1** And they remained three years without war between Syria and Israel.

**22:2** And it came to pass in the third year that Jehoshaphat the king of Judah went down to the king of Israel.

**22:3** And the king of Israel said to his servants, "Do you know that Ramoth Gilead is ours, yet we remain still, not taking it out of the hand of the king of Syria?"

**22:4** Then he said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you go with me to war at Ramoth Gilead?" And Jehoshaphat answered the king of Israel, "I am as you are, my people are as your people, and my horses are as your horses."

**22:5** And Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Inquire, please, for the word of the LORD today."

**22:6** Then the king of Israel assembled the prophets, about four hundred men, and asked them, "Shall I march against Ramoth Gilead for battle, or shall I hold back?" And they replied, "Go up, and the Lord will give it into the king's hand."

**22:7** And Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not still a prophet of the LORD here, that we may inquire of him?"

**22:8** So the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man by whom to inquire of the LORD, yet I hate him, for he does not prophesy good concerning me, but only evil—Micaiah the son of Imlah." And Jehoshaphat said, "Let the king not say so."

**22:9** Then the king of Israel called an officer and said, "Bring Micaiah the son of Imlah quickly."

**22:10** Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah were sitting each upon his throne, arrayed in their robes, at a threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of Samaria; and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

**22:11** And Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah had made for himself horns of iron; and he said, "Thus says the LORD: 'With these you shall gore Syria until they are consumed.'"

**22:12** And all the prophets were prophesying likewise, saying, "Go up to Ramoth Gilead and prosper, for the LORD will deliver it into the hand of the king."

**22:13** Then the messenger who had gone to summon Micaiah spoke to him, saying, "Behold now, the words of the prophets with one mouth declare good to the king. Let your word, please, be like the word of one of them, and speak what is good."

**22:14** And Micaiah said, "As the LORD lives, whatever the LORD says to me, that I will speak."

**22:15** When he came to the king, the king said to him, "Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth Gilead to battle, or shall we refrain?" And he said to him, "Go up and prosper, for the LORD will deliver it into the hand of the king."

**22:16** So the king said to him, "How many times must I make you swear that you speak nothing to me except truth in the name of the LORD?"

**22:17** Then he said, "I saw all Israel scattered upon the mountains, like sheep that have no shepherd. And the LORD said, 'These have no master; let each return to his house in peace.'"

**22:18** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not tell you that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but only evil?"

**22:19** Then he said, "Therefore hear the word of the LORD: I saw the LORD sitting upon His throne, and all the host of heaven standing beside Him, on His right hand and on His left.

**22:20** And the LORD said, 'Who will entice Ahab, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth Gilead?' And one spoke in this manner, and another spoke in that manner.

**22:21** Then a spirit came forth and stood before the LORD, and said, 'I will entice him.' And the LORD said to him, 'By what means?'

**22:22** And he said, 'I will go forth and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' And He said, 'You shall entice him, and also prevail. Go forth and do so.'

**22:23** Now therefore, behold, the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, and the LORD has spoken calamity concerning you."

**22:24** Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah upon the cheek, and said, "Which way did the Spirit of the LORD pass from me to speak with you?"

**22:25** And Micaiah said, "Behold, you shall see on that day when you go into an inner chamber to hide yourself."

**22:26** So the king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah, and return him to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king's son,

**22:27** and say, 'Thus says the king: Put this man in the prison house, and feed him bread of affliction and water of affliction, until I come in peace.'"

**22:28** And Micaiah said, "If you indeed return in peace, the LORD has not spoken by me." And he said, "Hear, all you peoples!"

**22:29** So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah went up to Ramoth Gilead.

**22:30** And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and enter into the battle, but you, put on your own robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself and entered into the battle.

**22:31** Now the king of Syria had commanded his thirty-two captains of the chariots, saying, "Do not fight with small or great, but only with the king of Israel."

**22:32** And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, that they said, "Surely he is the king of Israel!" So they turned aside against him to fight, and Jehoshaphat cried out.

**22:33** And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw that he was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from pursuing him.

**22:34** Now a certain man drew his bow at random and struck the king of Israel between the joints and the armor. So he said to the driver of his chariot, "Turn your hand and carry me out of the army, for I am wounded."

**22:35** And the battle mounted up that day, and the king was propped up in his chariot facing Syria, and he died in the evening; and the blood from the wound ran out into the hollow of the chariot.

**22:36** Then a cry passed through the camp as the sun was going down, saying, "Every man to his city, and every man to his own land!"

**22:37** So the king died and was brought to Samaria, and they buried the king in Samaria.

**22:38** And the chariot was washed by the pool of Samaria, and the dogs licked up his blood while the harlots bathed, according to the word of the LORD which He had spoken.

**22:39** Now the rest of the acts of Ahab, and all that he did, and the ivory house which he built, and all the cities that he built, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**22:40** So Ahab slept with his fathers, and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.

**22:41** Now Jehoshaphat the son of Asa began to reign over Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel.

**22:42** Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah the daughter of Shilhi.

**22:43** And he walked in all the way of Asa his father; he did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the eyes of the LORD.

**22:44** Yet the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense upon the high places.

**22:45** And Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.

**22:46** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, and his might that he showed, and how he made war, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**22:51** And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father. Then Jehoram his son reigned in his place.

**22:52** Ahaziah the son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned over Israel two years.

**22:53** And he did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and walked in the way of his father and in the way of his mother, and in the way of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who had caused Israel to sin.

**22:54** And he served Baal and worshiped him, and provoked the LORD God of Israel to anger, according to all that his father had done.

## Translator Notes

- **22:6** — The prophets here use the Hebrew title Adonai ("the Lord") rather than the covenant name YHWH ("the LORD"), which appears in verses 12 and 15.
- **22:13** — Following the Masoretic qere ("your word," singular), which agrees with the singular verb; the ketiv reads the plural "your words."
- **22:21** — Literally "the spirit"; Hebrew commonly uses the definite article for a specific figure newly introduced into a narrative scene.
- **22:38** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "and the harlots bathed," underscoring the ignominious fulfillment of the word of the LORD against Ahab.
