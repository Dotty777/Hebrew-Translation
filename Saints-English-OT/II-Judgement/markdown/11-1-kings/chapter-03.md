## Text

**3:2** Only the people were offering sacrifices at the high places, for no house had been built for the name of the LORD until those days.

**3:3** Now Solomon loved the LORD, walking in the statutes of his father David; only he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

**3:4** And the king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for that was the great high place; Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings upon that altar.

**3:5** In Gibeon the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream by night; and God said, "Ask what I shall give you."

**3:6** And Solomon said, "You have shown great lovingkindness to Your servant my father David, as he walked before You in truth, in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with You; and You have preserved for him this great lovingkindness, and have given him a son sitting upon his throne, as it is this day.

**3:7** And now, O LORD my God, You have made Your servant king in place of my father David; yet I am but a little youth; I do not know how to go out or come in.

**3:8** And Your servant is in the midst of Your people whom You have chosen, a vast people who cannot be numbered or counted for multitude.

**3:9** Give therefore to Your servant a listening heart to judge Your people, to discern between good and evil. For who is able to judge this great people of Yours?"

**3:10** And the matter was pleasing in the sight of the Lord, that Solomon had asked this thing.

**3:11** Then God said to him, "Because you have asked this thing, and have not asked many days for yourself, nor have asked wealth for yourself, nor have asked the life of your enemies, but have asked for yourself discernment to hear justice,

**3:12** behold, I have acted according to your words; behold, I have given you a wise and discerning heart, so that none like you has been before you, nor after you shall any arise like you.

**3:13** And what you have not asked I have also given you, both riches and honor, so that no man among the kings shall be like you all your days.

**3:14** And if you walk in My ways, keeping My statutes and My commandments, as your father David walked, then I will lengthen your days."

**3:15** Then Solomon awoke, and behold, it was a dream. And he came to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and offered up burnt offerings and presented peace offerings, and made a feast for all his servants.

**3:16** Then two women who were harlots came to the king and stood before him.

**3:17** And the one woman said, "O my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house, and I gave birth while with her in the house.

**3:18** And it came to pass on the third day after I gave birth that this woman also gave birth. And we were together; there was no stranger with us in the house, only the two of us in the house.

**3:19** And this woman's son died in the night, because she lay upon him.

**3:20** So she arose in the middle of the night and took my son from my side while your maidservant slept, and laid him in her bosom, and laid her dead son in my bosom.

**3:21** And when I arose in the morning to nurse my son, behold, he was dead; yet when I looked closely at him in the morning, behold, it was not my son whom I had borne."

**3:22** Then the other woman said, "No! Rather, my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one." And the first said, "No! Rather, your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one." Thus they spoke before the king.

**3:23** And the king said, "This one says, 'This is my son, the living one, and your son is the dead one'; and that one says, 'No! Rather, your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.'"

**3:24** Then the king said, "Bring me a sword." So they brought the sword before the king.

**3:25** And the king said, "Cut the living child in two, and give half to the one, and half to the other."

**3:26** Then the woman whose son was the living one spoke to the king, for her compassion burned within her for her son, and she said, "O my lord, give her the living babe, and by no means slay him!" But the other said, "He shall be neither mine nor yours; divide him!"

**3:27** So the king answered and said, "Give her the living babe, and by no means slay him; she is his mother."

**3:28** And all Israel heard of the judgment which the king had rendered, and they stood in awe before the king; for they saw that the wisdom of God was within him to execute justice.

## Translator Notes

- **3:9** — Hebrew literally reads "a hearing heart," denoting a mind receptive to God's wisdom and attentive in rendering judgment; "great" translates a word literally meaning "heavy" or "weighty."
- **3:10** — Here and in verse 15, the Masoretic Text uses Adonai ("the Lord") rather than the Tetragrammaton ("the LORD").
- **3:26** — Hebrew literally reads "her compassions grew warm," a vivid idiom expressing intense maternal yearning and tenderness.
