## Text

**6:1** And the king commanded, and they quarried great stones, costly stones, hewn stones, to lay the foundation of the house. And it came to pass in the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of Israel came out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv, which is the second month, that he began to build the house for the LORD. In the fourth year the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid, in the month of Ziv. And in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, which is the eighth month, the house was completed in all its parts and according to all its specifications; thus he was seven years in building it.

**6:2** Now the house that King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits in length, twenty in width, and thirty cubits in height.

**6:3** And the vestibule in front of the sanctuary of the house was twenty cubits in length across the width of the house, and ten cubits in width in front of the house.

**6:4** And he made for the house windows with recessed frames.

**6:5** Against the wall of the house he built a side structure all around, against the walls of the house all around, both of the sanctuary and of the inner sanctuary; and he made side chambers all around.

**6:6** The lowest story of the side structure was five cubits wide, the middle was six cubits wide, and the third was seven cubits wide; for on the outside of the house he made recessed ledges all around, so that the beams would not be fastened into the walls of the house.

**6:7** And the house, while it was being built, was built of finished stone dressed at the quarry, so that neither hammer nor axe nor any tool of iron was heard in the house while it was being built.

**6:8** The entrance to the middle side chamber was on the right side of the house; and they went up by winding stairs to the middle story, and from the middle to the third.

**6:9** So he built the house and finished it, and he roofed the house with beams and planks of cedar.

**6:10** And he built the side structure against the entire house, five cubits in height, and it was fastened to the house with timbers of cedar.

**6:15** And he lined the walls of the house on the inside with boards of cedar; from the floor of the house to the beams of the ceiling he paneled them on the inside with wood, and he overlaid the floor of the house with planks of cypress.

**6:16** And he built twenty cubits at the rear of the house with boards of cedar from the floor to the rafters; he built it on the inside as an inner sanctuary, as the Most Holy Place.

**6:17** And the house—that is, the sanctuary in front—was forty cubits long.

**6:19** And he prepared the inner sanctuary in the innermost part of the house, to set there the ark of the covenant of the LORD.

**6:20** And the interior of the inner sanctuary was twenty cubits in length, twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits in height; and he overlaid it with pure gold, and he overlaid the cedar altar.

**6:21** So Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold; and he drew chains of gold across before the inner sanctuary, and overlaid it with gold.

**6:22** The whole house he overlaid with gold, until the entire house was finished; also the whole altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary he overlaid with gold.

**6:23** Within the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, each ten cubits in height.

**6:24** One wing of the cherub was five cubits, and the other wing of the cherub was five cubits; ten cubits from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other.

**6:25** And the second cherub was also ten cubits; both cherubim had the same measure and the same form.

**6:26** The height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and likewise that of the second cherub.

**6:27** Then he set the cherubim inside the inner house; and the wings of the cherubim were spread out so that the wing of the one touched one wall, and the wing of the second cherub touched the other wall, and their wings toward the middle of the house touched wing to wing.

**6:28** And he overlaid the cherubim with gold.

**6:29** And all the walls of the house round about he carved with engraved figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, both in the inner and the outer rooms.

**6:30** And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold, both in the inner and the outer rooms.

**6:31** And for the entrance of the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel and doorposts were five-sided.

**6:32** The two doors were of olive wood, and he carved upon them carvings of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold; and he beat out the gold over the cherubim and over the palm trees.

**6:33** In like manner he made for the entrance of the sanctuary four-sided doorposts of olive wood,

**6:34** and two doors of cypress wood; the two leaves of the one door were folding, and the two leaves of the second door were folding.

**6:35** And he carved upon them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold smoothly fitted upon the carved work.

**6:36** And he built the inner court with three courses of hewn stone and a course of cedar beams.

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Hebrew architectural expression refers to framed windows with narrowed or recessed openings.
- **6:5** — Following the Qere (read form) here and in verses 6 and 10 for the side structure built around the temple walls.
- **6:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "the middle side chamber," though some ancient versions read "the lowest."
- **6:31** — The Hebrew term rendered "five-sided" here, and "four-sided" in verse 33, may alternatively denote a fifth part and a fourth part of the wall's width.
