## Text

**2:1** Now the days of David drew near that he should die, and he charged Solomon his son, saying,

**2:2** "I am going the way of all the earth; therefore be strong, and be a man.

**2:3** Keep the charge of the LORD your God, to walk in His ways and to observe His statutes, His commandments, His judgments, and His testimonies, as it is written in the Law of Moses, so that you may prosper in everything you do and in whatever direction you turn,

**2:4** that the LORD may establish His word which He spoke concerning me, saying, 'If your sons guard their way, to walk before Me in truth with all their heart and with all their soul,' saying, 'a man shall not be cut off from you from the throne of Israel.'

**2:5** Moreover, you yourself know what Joab the son of Zeruiah did to me, and what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, to Abner the son of Ner and to Amasa the son of Jether, whom he slew, shedding the blood of war in peace and putting the blood of war upon the belt around his waist and upon the sandals on his feet.

**2:6** Act therefore according to your wisdom, and do not let his gray hair go down in peace to Sheol.

**2:7** But show lovingkindness to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be among those who eat at your table; for in this way they drew near to me when I fled before Absalom your brother.

**2:8** And behold, you have with you Shimei the son of Gera, the Benjamite from Bahurim, who cursed me with a grievous curse on the day I went to Mahanaim; yet he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the LORD, saying, 'I will not put you to death with the sword.'

**2:9** Now therefore, do not hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man, and you know what you should do to him; bring his gray hair down to Sheol with blood."

**2:10** Then David slept with his fathers, and he was buried in the City of David.

**2:11** And the days that David reigned over Israel were forty years: seven years he reigned in Hebron, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three years.

**2:12** Then Solomon sat upon the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was very firmly established.

**2:13** Now Adonijah the son of Haggith came to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon. And she said, "Do you come in peace?" And he said, "In peace."

**2:14** Moreover he said, "I have a word for you." And she said, "Speak."

**2:15** Then he said, "You know that the kingdom was mine, and that all Israel had set their faces upon me to reign; yet the kingdom has turned about and become my brother's, for it was his from the LORD.

**2:16** And now I ask one petition of you; do not turn me away." And she said to him, "Speak."

**2:17** So he said, "Please speak to King Solomon, for he will not turn you away, that he may give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife."

**2:18** And Bathsheba said, "Very well; I will speak for you to the king."

**2:19** Therefore Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak to him concerning Adonijah. And the king rose to meet her and bowed down before her; then he sat upon his throne and had a throne placed for the king's mother, and she sat at his right hand.

**2:20** Then she said, "I ask one small petition of you; do not turn me away." And the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not turn you away."

**2:21** So she said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as a wife."

**2:22** And King Solomon answered and said to his mother, "Why then do you ask Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask the kingdom for him also, for he is my elder brother—for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab the son of Zeruiah!"

**2:23** Then King Solomon swore by the LORD, saying, "May God do so to me, and more also, if Adonijah has not spoken this word against his own life!

**2:24** Now therefore, as the LORD lives, who has established me and seated me upon the throne of my father David, and who has made a house for me as He spoke, Adonijah shall surely be put to death today!"

**2:25** So King Solomon sent by the hand of Benaiah the son of Jehoiada; and he struck him down, and he died.

**2:26** And to Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go to Anathoth, to your own fields, for you are a man deserving of death; yet on this day I will not put you to death, because you bore the ark of the Lord GOD before my father David, and because you were afflicted in everything in which my father was afflicted."

**2:27** So Solomon expelled Abiathar from being priest to the LORD, in order to fulfill the word of the LORD which He spoke concerning the house of Eli at Shiloh.

**2:28** Then the report came to Joab, for Joab had turned after Adonijah, though he had not turned after Absalom. And Joab fled to the tent of the LORD and took hold of the horns of the altar.

**2:29** And it was told to King Solomon that Joab had fled to the tent of the LORD, and behold, he was beside the altar. Then Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him down."

**2:30** So Benaiah came to the tent of the LORD and said to him, "Thus says the king, 'Come out!'" And he said, "No, for I will die here." Then Benaiah brought back word to the king, saying, "Thus spoke Joab, and thus he answered me."

**2:31** And the king said to him, "Do as he has spoken, and strike him down and bury him, that you may remove from me and from my father's house the innocent blood which Joab shed.

**2:32** Thus the LORD shall bring his blood back upon his own head, because he struck down two men more righteous and better than he, and slew them with the sword while my father David did not know it—Abner the son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa the son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.

**2:33** Their blood shall therefore return upon the head of Joab and upon the head of his descendants forever; but upon David and his descendants, upon his house and his throne, there shall be peace forever from the LORD."

**2:34** So Benaiah the son of Jehoiada went up and struck him down and put him to death, and he was buried in his own house in the wilderness.

**2:35** Then the king appointed Benaiah the son of Jehoiada in his place over the army, and the king appointed Zadok the priest in the place of Abiathar. These were the chief officers who were over Solomon's work: five hundred and fifty who ruled over the people doing the work. And three times a year Solomon offered burnt offerings and peace offerings upon the altar which he had built to the LORD, and he burned incense beside it, which was before the LORD. So he finished the house.

**2:36** Then the king sent and summoned Shimei, and said to him, "Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and dwell there, and do not go out from there anywhere.

**2:37** For it shall be, on the day you go out and cross over the Brook Kidron, know for certain that you shall surely die; your blood shall be upon your own head."

**2:38** And Shimei said to the king, "The word is good. As my lord the king has spoken, so your servant will do." So Shimei dwelt in Jerusalem many days.

**2:39** Now it came to pass at the end of three years that two of Shimei's servants fled to Achish the son of Maacah, king of Gath. And they told Shimei, saying, "Behold, your servants are in Gath."

**2:40** So Shimei arose, saddled his donkey, and went to Gath to Achish to seek his servants; and Shimei went and brought his servants from Gath.

**2:41** And it was reported to Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had returned.

**2:42** Then the king sent and summoned Shimei, and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by the LORD and solemnly warn you, saying, 'On the day you go out and walk anywhere, know for certain that you shall surely die'? And you said to me, 'The word is good; I have heard it.'

**2:43** Why then have you not kept the oath of the LORD and the commandment which I laid upon you?"

**2:44** The king also said to Shimei, "You know all the evil, which your own heart knows, that you did to my father David; therefore the LORD shall return your evil upon your own head.

**2:45** But King Solomon shall be blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before the LORD forever."

**2:46** So the king commanded Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck him down, and he died. Thus the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon. Judah and Israel were many, as the sand by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking and rejoicing. And Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the River to the land of the Philistines, and as far as the border of Egypt; they brought tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life. And Judah and Israel dwelt securely, each man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **2:24** — The Hebrew preserves a Ketiv/Qere orthographic variant on the verb "seated me," with the Masoretic Qere reading a standard Hiphil form with first-person suffix; the translation follows the Qere.
- **2:26** — "A man deserving of death" translates the Hebrew idiom "a man of death," and "the Lord GOD" renders the covenantal divine title Adonai YHWH.
- **2:35** — The Saints Hebrew Critical Text includes here the summary of Solomon's five hundred and fifty chief officers and his thrice-yearly burnt offerings, peace offerings, and incense before the LORD, corresponding to material placed in chapter 9 in the standard Masoretic arrangement and reflected here in the 3 Kingdoms tradition.
- **2:46** — The Saints Hebrew Critical Text includes here the summary of Israel's multitude, Solomon's dominion from the River (Euphrates) to the border of Egypt, and the security of Judah and Israel from Dan to Beersheba, corresponding to material placed in chapters 4 and 5 in the standard Masoretic arrangement.
