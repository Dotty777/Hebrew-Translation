## Text

**5:1** And these officers provided sustenance for King Solomon and for all who came to the table of King Solomon, each man in his month; they let nothing be lacking. Also barley and straw for the horses and the swift steeds they brought to the place where he was, each man according to his appointed charge.

**5:2** Now Solomon's provision for one day was thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of meal,

**5:3** ten fattened oxen, twenty oxen from the pasture, and one hundred sheep, besides deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened fowl.

**5:4** For he held dominion over all the region beyond the River, from Tiphsah even to Gaza, over all the kings beyond the River; and he had peace on every side all around him.

**5:9** And God gave Solomon wisdom and exceedingly great understanding, and breadth of heart like the sand that is upon the seashore.

**5:10** And the wisdom of Solomon surpassed the wisdom of all the sons of the East and all the wisdom of Egypt.

**5:11** For he was wiser than all mankind—than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; and his fame was in all the nations round about.

**5:12** He spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered one thousand and five.

**5:13** He also spoke of trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon even to the hyssop that springs forth out of the wall; and he spoke of beasts, and of birds, and of creeping things, and of fish.

**5:14** Now Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt, and took the daughter of Pharaoh and brought her into the City of David until he had finished building his own house, and the house of the LORD, and the wall of Jerusalem round about. And people came from all the peoples to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom. Pharaoh king of Egypt had gone up and captured Gezer and burned it with fire, and had slain the Canaanites who dwelt in the city, and had given it as a dowry to his daughter, the wife of Solomon.

**5:15** Now Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon, because he had heard that they had anointed him king in place of his father; for Hiram had always been a friend to David.

**5:16** Then Solomon sent word to Hiram, saying,

**5:17** "You know concerning my father David, that he was unable to build a house for the name of the LORD his God because of the warfare that surrounded him, until the LORD placed them beneath the soles of my feet.

**5:18** But now the LORD my God has granted me rest on every side; there is neither adversary nor evil occurrence.

**5:19** And behold, I purpose to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, as the LORD spoke to my father David, saying, 'Your son, whom I will set upon your throne in your place, he shall build the house for My name.'

**5:20** Now therefore, give command that they cut down cedars for me out of Lebanon; and my servants shall be with your servants, and I will give you wages for your servants according to all that you say, for you know that there is no man among us who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**5:21** And it came to pass, when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced exceedingly and said, "Blessed be the LORD this day, who has given to David a wise son over this great people."

**5:22** Then Hiram sent word to Solomon, saying, "I have heard what you sent to me; I will fulfill all your desire concerning timber of cedar and timber of cypress.

**5:23** My servants shall bring them down from Lebanon to the sea, and I will set them in rafts upon the sea to the place of which you send word to me, and I will break them apart there, and you shall take them away; and you shall fulfill my desire by giving food for my household."

**5:24** So Hiram supplied Solomon with cedar timber and cypress timber according to all his desire.

**5:25** And Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat as food for his household, and twenty kors of beaten oil; thus Solomon gave to Hiram year by year.

**5:26** And the LORD gave wisdom to Solomon, just as He had promised him; and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them made a covenant.

**5:27** Then King Solomon raised a levy of forced labor out of all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.

**5:28** And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month in shifts: one month they were in Lebanon and two months at home; and Adoniram was over the levy.

**5:29** Solomon also had seventy thousand burden-bearers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hill country,

**5:30** besides the chief officers of Solomon who were over the work, three thousand three hundred, who had charge over the people carrying out the work.

**5:32** So the builders of Solomon and the builders of Hiram and the Gebalites did the hewing, and prepared the timber and the stones to build the house.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — A kor was a large dry and liquid measure, approximately two hundred and twenty liters or about six bushels.
- **5:4** — The phrase "beyond the River" designates the territory west of the Euphrates River from Tiphsah down to Gaza.
- **5:17** — Following the Qere (read form), "my feet"; the Ketiv (written form) reads "his feet," referring to David.
- **5:24** — The Hebrew text uses the variant spelling "Hirom" here and in 5:32 for Hiram, king of Tyre.
