## Text

**21:1** Now Ben-Hadad king of Syria assembled all his host, and thirty-two kings were with him, with horses and chariots. And he went up, laid siege to Samaria, and fought against it.

**21:2** Then he sent messengers into the city to Ahab king of Israel,

**21:3** and said to him, "Thus says Ben-Hadad: 'Your silver and your gold belong to me; your fairest wives and your children also belong to me.'"

**21:4** And the king of Israel answered and said, "According to your word, my lord the king, I am yours, and all that is mine."

**21:5** Then the messengers came back and said, "Thus speaks Ben-Hadad, saying, 'Indeed I sent to you, saying, "You shall give to me your silver and your gold, your wives and your children,"

**21:6** yet about this time tomorrow I will send my servants to you, and they shall search your house and the houses of your servants; and it shall come to pass that whatever is delightful to your eyes, they shall place in their hands and carry away.'"

**21:7** So the king of Israel summoned all the elders of the land and said, "Consider now, and see how this man seeks evil; for he sent to me for my wives, my children, my silver, and my gold, and I did not withhold them from him."

**21:8** And all the elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen, nor consent."

**21:9** Therefore he said to the messengers of Ben-Hadad, "Say to my lord the king, 'All that you sent for to your servant at the first I will do, but this matter I cannot do.'" And the messengers departed and brought back word to him.

**21:10** Then Ben-Hadad sent to him and said, "May the gods do so to me, and more also, if the dust of Samaria shall suffice for handfuls for all the people who follow at my feet."

**21:11** And the king of Israel answered and said, "Tell him, 'Do not let him who girds on his armor boast like him who takes it off.'"

**21:12** And it came to pass, when he heard this word—while he and the kings were drinking in the pavilions—that he said to his servants, "Set yourselves in array!" So they set themselves in array against the city.

**21:13** And behold, a certain prophet drew near to Ahab king of Israel and said, "Thus says the LORD: 'Have you seen all this great multitude? Behold, I am giving it into your hand today, and you shall know that I am the LORD.'"

**21:14** And Ahab said, "By whom?" And he said, "Thus says the LORD: 'By the young men of the governors of the provinces.'" Then he said, "Who shall begin the battle?" And he answered, "You."

**21:15** Then he mustered the young men of the governors of the provinces, and they were two hundred and thirty-two; and after them he mustered all the people, all the children of Israel—seven thousand.

**21:16** And they marched out at noon, while Ben-Hadad was drinking himself drunk in the pavilions, he and the kings, the thirty-two kings who were aiding him.

**21:17** The young men of the governors of the provinces went out first. And Ben-Hadad sent out to inquire, and they reported to him, saying, "Men have come out from Samaria."

**21:18** So he said, "If they have come out for peace, capture them alive; and if they have come out for war, capture them alive."

**21:19** So these went out from the city—the young men of the governors of the provinces, and the army that followed behind them.

**21:20** And each one struck down his man; so the Syrians fled, and Israel pursued them, and Ben-Hadad king of Syria escaped on a horse with the horsemen.

**21:21** Then the king of Israel went out and struck the horses and the chariots, and he struck the Syrians with a great slaughter.

**21:22** And the prophet drew near to the king of Israel and said to him, "Go, strengthen yourself; take heed and see what you should do, for at the turn of the year the king of Syria will come up against you."

**21:23** Now the servants of the king of Syria said to him, "Their gods are gods of the hills; therefore they were stronger than we. Yet let us fight against them in the plain, and surely we shall be stronger than they.

**21:24** And do this thing: remove the kings, each from his post, and appoint commanders in their place.

**21:25** And number for yourself an army like the army that fell from you, horse for horse and chariot for chariot; then let us fight against them in the plain, and surely we shall be stronger than they." And he heeded their voice and did so.

**21:26** So it came to pass at the turn of the year that Ben-Hadad mustered the Syrians and went up to Aphek to wage war against Israel.

**21:27** And the children of Israel were mustered and provisioned, and went out to meet them. And the children of Israel encamped opposite them like two little flocks of goats, while the Syrians filled the land.

**21:28** Then a man of God drew near and spoke to the king of Israel, saying, "Thus says the LORD: 'Because the Syrians have said, "The LORD is God of the hills, and He is not God of the valleys," therefore I will give all this great multitude into your hand, and you shall know that I am the LORD.'"

**21:29** And they encamped one opposite the other for seven days. And it came to pass on the seventh day that the battle was joined, and the children of Israel struck down one hundred thousand foot soldiers of the Syrians in one day.

**21:30** And the rest fled to Aphek, into the city, and the wall fell upon twenty-seven thousand of the men who remained. And Ben-Hadad fled and came into the city, into an inner chamber.

**21:31** Then his servants said to him, "Behold now, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel are merciful kings. Please let us put sackcloth around our waists and ropes upon our heads, and go out to the king of Israel; perhaps he will spare your life."

**21:32** So they girded sackcloth around their waists and put ropes on their heads, and came to the king of Israel and said, "Your servant Ben-Hadad says, 'Please let me live.'" And he said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother."

**21:33** Now the men were watching eagerly for a favorable sign, and they quickly seized upon his word and said, "Your brother Ben-Hadad!" Then he said, "Go, bring him." So Ben-Hadad came out to him, and he brought him up into the chariot.

**21:34** And Ben-Hadad said to him, "The cities that my father took from your father I will restore, and you may establish market streets for yourself in Damascus, just as my father established in Samaria." Then Ahab said, "On the basis of this covenant I will let you go." So he made a covenant with him and sent him away.

**21:35** Now a certain man of the sons of the prophets said to his companion by the word of the LORD, "Strike me, please." Yet the man refused to strike him.

**21:36** Then he said to him, "Because you have not obeyed the voice of the LORD, behold, as soon as you depart from me, a lion shall strike you down." And when he departed from his side, a lion found him and struck him down.

**21:37** Then he found another man and said, "Strike me, please." And the man struck him hard and wounded him.

**21:38** So the prophet went and waited for the king along the road, having disguised himself with a bandage over his eyes.

**21:39** And as the king was passing by, he cried out to the king and said, "Your servant went out into the midst of the battle; and behold, a man turned aside and brought a man to me, and said, 'Guard this man; if by any means he goes missing, then your life shall be for his life, or else you shall pay a talent of silver.'

**21:40** And while your servant was occupied here and there, he vanished." And the king of Israel said to him, "So shall your judgment be; you yourself have decided it."

**21:41** Then he quickly removed the bandage from over his eyes, and the king of Israel recognized him, that he was one of the prophets.

**21:42** And he said to him, "Thus says the LORD: 'Because you have let go out of your hand the man whom I devoted to destruction, therefore your life shall be for his life, and your people for his people.'"

**21:43** So the king of Israel went to his house, sullen and vexed, and came to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — In the Saints chapter order, which follows the sequence of 3 Kingdoms, this chapter corresponds to 1 Kings 20 in standard Masoretic numbering; the Hebrew name Aram is rendered traditionally as Syria.
- **21:23** — The Hebrew plural form may be rendered either "Their gods are gods of the hills," reflecting Syrian polytheism, or "Their God is a God of the hills," as addressed directly of the LORD in verse 28.
- **21:33** — The rare Hebrew idiom expresses the messengers eagerly seizing upon and confirming Ahab's unguarded word "brother" as a binding pledge of clemency.
- **21:38** — The rare Hebrew term apher denotes a headcloth or bandage placed over the eyes, distinct from epher ("ashes"), used here to conceal the prophet's identity.
- **21:41** — Ketiv reads the standard preposition me'al, while Qere reads the poetic form me'alei ("from over"), both conveying the same meaning.
