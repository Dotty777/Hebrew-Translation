## Text

**20:1** And it came to pass after these things that Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard which was in Jezreel, beside the palace of Ahab king of Samaria.

**20:2** So Ahab spoke to Naboth, saying, "Give me your vineyard, that it may be mine for a vegetable garden, because it is near, beside my house; and I will give you a better vineyard than it in its place. If it is good in your eyes, I will give you its price in silver."

**20:3** But Naboth said to Ahab, "Far be it from me, by the LORD, that I should give the inheritance of my fathers to you!"

**20:4** So Ahab came into his house sullen and vexed because of the word which Naboth the Jezreelite had spoken to him; for he had said, "I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers." And he lay down on his bed, and turned away his face, and did not eat bread.

**20:5** Then Jezebel his wife came to him and spoke to him, "Why is your spirit so sullen, and why are you not eating bread?"

**20:6** And he spoke to her, "Because I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite, and said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for silver, or if you prefer, I will give you a vineyard in its place.' Yet he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

**20:7** Then Jezebel his wife said to him, "Do you now exercise kingship over Israel? Arise, eat bread, and let your heart be glad; I myself will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite."

**20:8** So she wrote letters in Ahab's name, sealed them with his seal, and sent letters to the elders and to the nobles who were in his city, dwelling with Naboth.

**20:9** And she wrote in the letters, saying, "Proclaim a fast, and seat Naboth at the head of the people;

**20:10** and seat two men, sons of Belial, opposite him, and let them bear witness against him, saying, 'You have cursed God and the king.' Then bring him out and stone him, that he may die."

**20:11** So the men of his city, the elders and the nobles who dwelt in his city, did as Jezebel had sent word to them, just as it was written in the letters which she had sent to them.

**20:12** They proclaimed a fast, and seated Naboth at the head of the people.

**20:13** And the two men, sons of Belial, came in and sat opposite him; and the men of Belial bore witness against him, against Naboth, in the presence of the people, saying, "Naboth has cursed God and the king!" Then they brought him outside the city and stoned him with stones, so that he died.

**20:14** Then they sent word to Jezebel, saying, "Naboth has been stoned and is dead."

**20:15** And it came to pass, when Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, that Jezebel said to Ahab, "Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for silver; for Naboth is not alive, but dead."

**20:16** And it came to pass, when Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, that Ahab arose to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, to take possession of it.

**20:17** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

**20:18** "Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria. Behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone down to take possession of it.

**20:19** And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: Have you murdered and also taken possession?' And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: In the place where the dogs licked the blood of Naboth, the dogs shall lick your blood, even yours.'"

**20:20** And Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, O my enemy?" And he answered, "I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do evil in the sight of the LORD.

**20:21** Behold, I will bring calamity upon you; I will sweep away those who come after you, and will cut off from Ahab every male, both bond and free, in Israel;

**20:22** and I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah, because of the provocation with which you have provoked wrath, and have caused Israel to sin.

**20:23** And concerning Jezebel also the LORD has spoken, saying, 'The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the rampart of Jezreel.'

**20:24** Whoever belongs to Ahab and dies in the city, the dogs shall eat; and whoever dies in the field, the birds of the heavens shall eat."

**20:25** Surely there was no one like Ahab, who sold himself to do evil in the sight of the LORD, whom Jezebel his wife incited.

**20:26** And he acted very abominably in going after idols, according to all that the Amorites had done, whom the LORD had cast out before the children of Israel.

**20:27** And it came to pass, when Ahab heard these words, that he tore his garments, and put sackcloth on his flesh, and fasted, and lay in sackcloth, and walked about quietly.

**20:28** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

**20:29** "Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before Me? Because he has humbled himself before Me, I will not bring the calamity in his days; in the days of his son I will bring the calamity upon his house."

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — In the 3 Kingdoms chapter order followed here, the account of Naboth's vineyard appears as chapter 20 (corresponding to 1 Kings 21 in the standard Masoretic chapter division).
- **20:8** — Ketiv/Qere: the written form (ketiv) reads definite "the letters," whereas the read form (qere) followed here reads indefinite "letters."
- **20:10** — "Sons of Belial" is a Hebrew idiom for worthless or wicked men; "cursed" translates a Hebrew verb literally meaning "blessed," used as a reverent euphemism to avoid writing "curse" directly beside the name of God.
- **20:21** — "Every male" renders a blunt Hebrew idiom for every male member of the house, and "both bond and free" is a merism encompassing everyone without exception.
