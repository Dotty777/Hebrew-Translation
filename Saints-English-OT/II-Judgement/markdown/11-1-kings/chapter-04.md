## Text

**4:1** So King Solomon was king over all Israel.

**4:2** And these were the officials whom he had: Azariah the son of Zadok, the priest;

**4:3** Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder;

**4:4** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, over the army; Zadok and Abiathar, priests;

**4:5** Azariah the son of Nathan, over the deputies; Zabud the son of Nathan, a priest, the king's friend;

**4:6** Ahishar, over the household; and Adoniram the son of Abda, over the forced labor.

**4:7** And Solomon had twelve deputies over all Israel, who provided for the king and his household; each one had to provide for one month in the year.

**4:8** And these are their names: Ben-Hur, in the hill country of Ephraim;

**4:9** Ben-Deker, in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth Shemesh, and Elon Beth Hanan;

**4:10** Ben-Hesed, in Arubboth; to him belonged Sochoh and all the land of Hepher;

**4:11** Ben-Abinadab, in all the region of Dor; Taphath the daughter of Solomon was his wife;

**4:12** Baana the son of Ahilud, in Taanach, Megiddo, and all Beth Shean, which is beside Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth Shean to Abel Meholah, as far as beyond Jokmeam;

**4:13** Ben-Geber, in Ramoth Gilead; to him belonged the villages of Jair the son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead; to him also belonged the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, sixty great cities with walls and bronze bars;

**4:14** Ahinadab the son of Iddo, in Mahanaim;

**4:15** Ahimaaz, in Naphtali; he also took Basemath the daughter of Solomon as his wife;

**4:16** Baana the son of Hushai, in Asher and in Aloth;

**4:17** Shimei the son of Ela, in Benjamin;

**4:18** Geber the son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan; and he was the one deputy who was in the land;

**4:19** Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Hebrew kohen ordinarily means "priest," though in royal lists it may also indicate a high court minister or counselor.
- **4:7** — The Masoretic qere reads "the one" (translated "each one") with the definite article, while the ketiv reads "one" without the article.
- **4:8** — In verses 8 through 13, five of Solomon's deputies are listed by patronymic names (literally "the son of Hur," "the son of Deker," "the son of Hesed," "the son of Abinadab," and "the son of Geber").
- **4:16** — Translated according to the Masoretic vocalization as "in Aloth"; alternatively, the Hebrew may be read as the town name Bealoth.
