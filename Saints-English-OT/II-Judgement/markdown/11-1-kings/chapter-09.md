## Text

**9:1** Now it came to pass, when Solomon had finished building the house of the LORD and the house of the king, and all the desire of Solomon that he was pleased to accomplish,

**9:2** that the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, just as He had appeared to him at Gibeon.

**9:3** And the LORD said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your supplication that you have pleaded before Me; I have consecrated this house which you have built, to place My name there forever, and My eyes and My heart shall be there all the days.

**9:4** And as for you, if you walk before Me as David your father walked, in integrity of heart and in uprightness, to do according to all that I have commanded you, and keep My statutes and My judgments,

**9:5** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, just as I spoke concerning David your father, saying, 'A man shall not be cut off for you from the throne of Israel.'

**9:6** But if you and your sons surely turn away from following Me, and do not keep My commandments and My statutes that I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**9:7** then I will cut off Israel from the face of the land that I have given to them, and the house that I have consecrated for My name I will cast away from before My face; and Israel shall become a proverb and a byword among all the peoples.

**9:8** And though this house shall be exalted, everyone who passes by it shall be appalled and shall hiss; and they shall say, 'Why has the LORD done thus to this land and to this house?'

**9:9** Then they shall answer, 'Because they forsook the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and took hold of other gods, and bowed down to them and served them; therefore the LORD has brought upon them all this calamity.'" However, Pharaoh's daughter went up from the City of David to her house that he had built for her; then he built the Millo.

**9:10** Now it came to pass at the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the two houses, the house of the LORD and the house of the king—

**9:11** Hiram king of Tyre had supplied Solomon with cedar timber and cypress timber and gold, according to all his desire—that King Solomon then gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.

**9:12** Then Hiram went out from Tyre to see the cities that Solomon had given him, and they were not pleasing in his eyes.

**9:13** So he said, "What cities are these that you have given me, my brother?" And he called them the land of Cabul, to this day.

**9:14** And Hiram sent to the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold.

**9:26** And King Solomon built a fleet of ships at Ezion-Geber, which is beside Eloth on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom.

**9:27** And Hiram sent his servants with the fleet, seamen who knew the sea, together with the servants of Solomon.

**9:28** And they went to Ophir and took from there four hundred and twenty talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — Hebrew literally reads 'shall be exalted,' understood concessively in contrast to the desolation that follows; compare 2 Chronicles 7:21.
- **9:9** — The Qere reads plural 'and bowed down,' matching the plural verbs around it, whereas the Ketiv omits the final plural letter. The closing sentence on Pharaoh's daughter and the Millo stands here in this Hebrew text, corresponding to 9:24 in the standard Masoretic order.
- **9:13** — The name Cabul plays on a Hebrew expression meaning 'as nothing' or 'good-for-nothing,' reflecting Hiram's displeasure with the cities.
- **9:26** — Red Sea translates Hebrew Yam Suph, literally 'Sea of Reeds,' referring here to the Gulf of Aqaba.
