## Text

**18:1** And it came to pass after many days that the word of the LORD came to Elijah in the third year, saying, "Go, present yourself to Ahab, and I will give rain upon the face of the ground."

**18:2** So Elijah went to present himself to Ahab; and the famine was severe in Samaria.

**18:3** And Ahab summoned Obadiah, who was over the household. Now Obadiah feared the LORD exceedingly.

**18:4** For it was so, when Jezebel cut off the prophets of the LORD, that Obadiah took one hundred prophets and hid them, fifty men to a cave, and sustained them with bread and water.

**18:5** And Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs of water and to all the brooks; perhaps we may find grass and keep the horses and mules alive, so that we do not have to cut off any of the beasts."

**18:6** So they divided the land between them to pass through it: Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself.

**18:7** Now as Obadiah was on the way, behold, Elijah met him. And he recognized him, and fell upon his face, and said, "Is this indeed you, my lord Elijah?"

**18:8** And he said to him, "It is I. Go, tell your lord, 'Behold, Elijah is here.'"

**18:9** Then he said, "How have I sinned, that you are delivering your servant into the hand of Ahab, to put me to death?

**18:10** As the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to seek you; and when they said, 'He is not here,' he made the kingdom and the nation swear an oath that they could not find you.

**18:11** And now you say, 'Go, tell your lord: Behold, Elijah is here.'

**18:12** And it shall come to pass, as soon as I depart from you, that the Spirit of the LORD will carry you to a place I do not know; and when I come to tell Ahab and he cannot find you, he will slay me. Yet your servant has feared the LORD from my youth.

**18:13** Was it not told to my lord what I did when Jezebel slew the prophets of the LORD, how I hid one hundred men of the prophets of the LORD, by fifties in a cave, and sustained them with bread and water?

**18:14** And now you say, 'Go, tell your lord: Behold, Elijah is here,' and he will slay me!"

**18:15** Then Elijah said, "As the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, I will surely present myself to him today."

**18:16** So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him; and Ahab went to meet Elijah.

**18:17** And it came to pass, when Ahab saw Elijah, that Ahab said to him, "Is this indeed you, O troubler of Israel?"

**18:18** And he answered, "I have not troubled Israel, but you and your father's house have, in that you have forsaken the commandments of the LORD and you have gone after the Baals.

**18:19** Now therefore, send and gather all Israel to me on Mount Carmel, and the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal, and the four hundred prophets of Asherah, who eat at Jezebel's table."

**18:20** So Ahab sent throughout all the children of Israel, and gathered the prophets together on Mount Carmel.

**18:21** And Elijah drew near to all the people and said, "How long will you limp between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow Him; but if Baal, follow him." Yet the people did not answer him a word.

**18:22** Then Elijah said to the people, "I alone remain a prophet of the LORD, whereas Baal's prophets are four hundred and fifty men.

**18:23** Therefore let them give us two bulls, and let them choose one bull for themselves, and cut it in pieces, and lay it upon the wood, yet put no fire under it; and I will prepare the other bull, and lay it upon the wood, yet put no fire under it.

**18:24** Then call upon the name of your god, and I will call upon the name of the LORD; and the God who answers by fire, He is God." So all the people answered and said, "The word is good."

**18:25** Now Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "Choose one bull for yourselves and prepare it first, for you are many; and call upon the name of your god, yet put no fire under it."

**18:26** So they took the bull which had been given to them and prepared it, and called upon the name of Baal from morning even until noon, saying, "O Baal, answer us!" Yet there was no voice, and no one answered. And they limped about the altar which had been made.

**18:27** And it came to pass at noon that Elijah mocked them and said, "Cry aloud, for he is a god; either he is meditating, or he has turned aside, or he is on a journey, or perhaps he is asleep and must be awakened."

**18:28** So they cried aloud, and cut themselves according to their custom with swords and lances, until the blood gushed out upon them.

**18:29** And when midday had passed, they prophesied until the time of the evening offering; yet there was no voice, no one answered, and no one paid heed.

**18:30** Then Elijah said to all the people, "Draw near to me." So all the people drew near to him. And he repaired the altar of the LORD that had been torn down.

**18:31** And Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of the LORD had come, saying, "Israel shall be your name."

**18:32** Then with the stones he built an altar in the name of the LORD; and he made a trench around the altar, large enough to hold two seahs of seed.

**18:33** And he arranged the wood, and cut the bull in pieces, and laid it upon the wood.

**18:34** And he said, "Fill four jars with water and pour it upon the burnt offering and upon the wood." Then he said, "Do it a second time," and they did it a second time; and he said, "Do it a third time," and they did it a third time.

**18:35** So the water ran all around the altar, and he also filled the trench with water.

**18:36** And it came to pass, at the time of the evening offering, that Elijah the prophet drew near and said, "O LORD God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known this day that You are God in Israel and that I am Your servant, and that I have done all these things at Your word.

**18:37** Answer me, O LORD, answer me, that this people may know that You, O LORD, are God, and that You have turned their hearts back again."

**18:38** Then the fire of the LORD fell and consumed the burnt offering, and the wood, and the stones, and the dust, and it licked up the water that was in the trench.

**18:39** Now when all the people saw it, they fell upon their faces; and they said, "The LORD, He is God! The LORD, He is God!"

**18:40** And Elijah said to them, "Seize the prophets of Baal! Do not let one of them escape!" So they seized them; and Elijah brought them down to the Brook Kishon and slew them there.

**18:41** Then Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, eat and drink; for there is the sound of the rushing of rain."

**18:42** So Ahab went up to eat and drink. And Elijah went up to the summit of Carmel; then he bowed down to the ground and placed his face between his knees.

**18:43** And he said to his servant, "Go up now, look toward the sea." So he went up and looked, and said, "There is nothing." And he said, "Go back seven times."

**18:44** And it came to pass at the seventh time that he said, "Behold, a small cloud, like a man's hand, is rising out of the sea!" Then he said, "Go up, say to Ahab, 'Harness your chariot and go down, lest the rain detain you.'"

**18:45** And it came to pass in a little while that the heavens grew dark with clouds and wind, and there was a heavy rain. So Ahab rode away and went to Jezreel.

**18:46** Then the hand of the LORD was upon Elijah; and he girded up his loins and ran ahead of Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:21** — The Hebrew participle rendered 'limp' vividly portrays hobbling back and forth between two divided loyalties, creating deliberate wordplay with the same verbal root used of Baal's prophets limping about the altar in verse 26.
- **18:27** — The phrase 'he has turned aside' renders a rare Hebrew noun often understood as a pointed euphemism for stepping aside to relieve oneself or being preoccupied.
- **18:32** — Two seahs of seed represents a traditional dry measure of roughly fifteen liters, indicating a trench large enough to hold a substantial volume of water.
- **18:36** — The Masoretic Qere reads the singular 'at Your word,' whereas the Ketiv reads the plural 'at Your words'; the translation follows the Qere.
