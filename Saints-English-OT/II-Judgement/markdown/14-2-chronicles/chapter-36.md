## Text

**36:1** Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and enthroned him as king in place of his father in Jerusalem.

**36:2** Jehoahaz was twenty-three years of age when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.

**36:3** Now the king of Egypt deposed him at Jerusalem, and exacted a fine upon the land of one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold.

**36:4** Then the king of Egypt appointed Eliakim his brother as king over Judah and Jerusalem, and altered his name to Jehoiakim; and Necho seized Jehoahaz his brother and brought him away to Egypt.

**36:5** Jehoiakim was twenty-five years of age when he came to the throne, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem; and he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD his God.

**36:6** Against him marched up Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and bound him in bronze fetters to lead him away to Babylon.

**36:7** Nebuchadnezzar also carried some of the vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon, and placed them within his own temple in Babylon.

**36:8** Now the remainder of the deeds of Jehoiakim, and the abominations which he committed, and that which was found against him, behold, they are recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. Then Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.

**36:9** Jehoiachin was eight years of age when he came to the throne, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem; and he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD.

**36:10** At the turning of the year, King Nebuchadnezzar sent and had him brought to Babylon, together with the precious vessels of the house of the LORD, and appointed Zedekiah his brother as king over Judah and Jerusalem.

**36:11** Zedekiah was twenty-one years of age when he came to the throne, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.

**36:12** He did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD his God, and did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke from the mouth of the LORD.

**36:13** Furthermore, he rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, who had required him to swear by God; and he stiffened his neck and hardened his heart against returning to the LORD, the God of Israel.

**36:14** Moreover, all the chief officers of the priests and the people multiplied their unfaithful transgressions, following all the abominations of the nations; and they defiled the house of the LORD which He had consecrated in Jerusalem.

**36:15** Yet the LORD, the God of their fathers, sent warnings to them by the hand of His messengers, rising early and sending again and again, for He had compassion upon His people and upon His dwelling place.

**36:16** But they continually mocked the messengers of God, despised His words, and scoffed at His prophets, until the wrath of the LORD mounted against His people, until there remained no remedy.

**36:17** Therefore He brought up against them the king of the Chaldeans, who slew their young men with the sword within the house of their sanctuary, and spared neither young man nor maiden, neither the aged nor the frail; He delivered them all into his hand.

**36:18** And all the vessels of the house of God, both great and small, together with the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king and of his princes—all of these he carried away to Babylon.

**36:19** They set fire to the house of God, tore down the wall of Jerusalem, consumed all its palaces with fire, and reduced all its prized articles to ruin.

**36:20** And those who remained as a remnant from the sword he led into exile to Babylon, where they became servants to him and to his sons until the reign of the kingdom of Persia,

**36:21** in fulfillment of the word of the LORD through the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had enjoyed its sabbaths; throughout all the days of its desolation it kept sabbath, until seventy years were completed.

**36:22** Now in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, that the word of the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah might be brought to completion, the LORD roused the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, so that he sent a proclamation throughout his entire realm and set it down in writing as well, declaring:

**36:23** "Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: The LORD, the God of heaven, has bestowed upon me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has charged me to build a house for Him in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whoever is among you of all His people, may the LORD his God be with him, and let him go up."

## Translator Notes

- **36:9** — The Masoretic Text reads eight years here, whereas the parallel account in 2 Kings 24:8 states that Jehoiachin was eighteen years old.
- **36:10** — The Hebrew term for brother is used here in the wider sense of kinsman, since Zedekiah was Jehoiachin's uncle.
- **36:14** — The Masoretic qere reads the defective infinitive construct paired with its cognate noun to intensify the charge of covenant unfaithfulness, whereas the ketiv records a fuller orthographic spelling.
- **36:17** — The Masoretic qere reads the standard plural gentilic for Chaldeans, while the ketiv preserves an alternative longer spelling.
