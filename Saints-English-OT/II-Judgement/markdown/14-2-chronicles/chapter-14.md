## Text

**14:1** And Asa did what was good and upright in the eyes of the LORD his God.

**14:2** He removed the foreign altars and the high places, shattered the sacred pillars, and cut down the Asherah poles.

**14:3** He also commanded Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to observe the law and the commandment.

**14:4** Moreover he removed from all the cities of Judah the high places and the incense altars, and the kingdom was quiet before him.

**14:5** He built fortified cities in Judah, for the land was at rest; he had no war in those years, because the LORD had granted him rest.

**14:6** Therefore he said to Judah, "Let us build these cities and surround them with walls and towers, gates and bars. The land is still before us, because we have sought the LORD our God; we have sought Him, and He has granted us rest on every side." So they built and prospered.

**14:7** And Asa had an army of three hundred thousand from Judah who bore large shields and spears, and two hundred and eighty thousand from Benjamin who carried shields and drew bows; all of these were mighty men of valor.

**14:8** Then Zerah the Cushite marched out against them with an army of one million men and three hundred chariots, and he came as far as Mareshah.

**14:9** So Asa went out to meet him, and they drew up in battle array in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.

**14:10** And Asa cried out to the LORD his God and said, "O LORD, it is nothing for You to help, whether the mighty or those who have no strength; help us, O LORD our God, for we rely on You, and in Your name we have come against this multitude. O LORD, You are our God; let no mortal man prevail against You."

**14:11** So the LORD struck down the Cushites before Asa and before Judah, and the Cushites fled.

**14:12** Asa and the people who were with him pursued them as far as Gerar, and so many of the Cushites fell that no life remained among them, for they were broken before the LORD and before His host; and they carried away exceedingly much plunder.

**14:13** They also struck all the cities around Gerar, for the dread of the LORD had fallen upon them; and they plundered all the cities, because great spoil was in them.

**14:14** They struck the tents of livestock as well, captured sheep in great abundance and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, 14:1 corresponds to 14:2 in many English Bibles, which number MT 13:23 as 14:1.
- **14:4** — The Hebrew term hammanim denotes small stone incense altars used in illicit cultic worship.
- **14:8** — Hebrew literally reads "a thousand thousands"; Cushite refers to the region of Cush south of Egypt, often called Ethiopia.
- **14:10** — Literally "there is nothing with You to help between many and one without strength," meaning human numbers make no difference to the LORD.
