## Text

**30:1** Then Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and also wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh, to come to the house of the LORD in Jerusalem to observe the Passover to the LORD, the God of Israel.

**30:2** For the king and his officials and all the assembly in Jerusalem had taken counsel to observe the Passover in the second month.

**30:3** For they were not able to observe it at that time, because the priests had not consecrated themselves in sufficient number, nor had the people gathered together to Jerusalem.

**30:4** And the matter was right in the eyes of the king and in the eyes of all the assembly.

**30:5** So they established a decree to send a proclamation throughout all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan, that they should come to observe the Passover to the LORD, the God of Israel, in Jerusalem; for they had not observed it in great numbers as it was written.

**30:6** Then the couriers went with the letters from the hand of the king and his officials throughout all Israel and Judah, and according to the commandment of the king, saying, "O children of Israel, return to the LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, that He may return to the escaped remnant remaining to you from the hand of the kings of Assyria.

**30:7** And do not be like your fathers and like your brethren, who acted unfaithfully against the LORD, the God of their fathers, so that He gave them over to desolation, as you see.

**30:8** Now do not stiffen your neck as your fathers did; yield your hand to the LORD, and enter His sanctuary, which He has consecrated forever, and serve the LORD your God, that the fierceness of His wrath may turn away from you.

**30:9** For if you return to the LORD, your brethren and your children will find compassion before those who led them captive, so that they will return to this land; for the LORD your God is gracious and merciful, and He will not turn His face away from you if you return to Him."

**30:10** So the couriers passed from city to city through the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, even as far as Zebulun, but the people laughed them to scorn and mocked them.

**30:11** Nevertheless, some men from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem.

**30:12** Also the hand of God was upon Judah to give them one heart to carry out the commandment of the king and the officials, by the word of the LORD.

**30:13** Now a great multitude of people gathered at Jerusalem to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread in the second month, an exceedingly great assembly.

**30:14** And they arose and removed the altars that were in Jerusalem, and all the incense altars they removed and cast into the Brook Kidron.

**30:15** Then they slaughtered the Passover lamb on the fourteenth day of the second month. And the priests and the Levites were ashamed, and consecrated themselves, and brought burnt offerings into the house of the LORD.

**30:16** They stood in their appointed place according to their ordinance, according to the Law of Moses the man of God; the priests sprinkled the blood received from the hand of the Levites.

**30:17** For there were many in the assembly who had not consecrated themselves; therefore the Levites were over the slaughtering of the Passover lambs for everyone who was not clean, to consecrate them to the LORD.

**30:18** For a multitude of the people, many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun, had not cleansed themselves, yet they ate the Passover otherwise than as it was written. But Hezekiah prayed for them, saying, "May the good LORD provide atonement for

**30:19** everyone who sets his whole heart to seek God, the LORD, the God of his fathers, though not according to the purification of the sanctuary."

**30:20** And the LORD listened to Hezekiah and healed the people.

**30:21** So the children of Israel who were present in Jerusalem kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with great gladness; and the Levites and the priests praised the LORD day by day with resounding instruments to the LORD.

**30:22** And Hezekiah spoke encouragingly to all the Levites who showed good understanding in the service of the LORD. And they ate throughout the feast for the seven days, sacrificing peace offerings and making confession to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**30:23** Then the whole assembly took counsel to observe another seven days, and they observed those seven days with gladness.

**30:24** For Hezekiah king of Judah contributed to the assembly one thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep, and the officials contributed to the assembly one thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep; and a great number of priests consecrated themselves.

**30:25** And the whole assembly of Judah rejoiced, with the priests and the Levites, and all the assembly that came from Israel, and the sojourners who came from the land of Israel and those who dwelt in Judah.

**30:26** So there was great joy in Jerusalem, for since the days of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, there had been nothing like this in Jerusalem.

**30:27** Then the Levitical priests arose and blessed the people, and their voice was heard; and their prayer came to His holy habitation, to heaven.

## Translator Notes

- **30:5** — The Hebrew phrase literally means "in great numbers" or "in multitude," referring to nationwide observance, though some understand it temporally as "for a long time."
- **30:8** — "Yield your hand" renders a Hebrew idiom meaning literally "give a hand," denoting submission and pledged allegiance.
- **30:18** — The Masoretic verse division separates the preposition "for" at the close of verse eighteen from its object at the opening of verse nineteen, carrying Hezekiah's prayer across both verses.
- **30:27** — The Hebrew places "the priests" and "the Levites" in direct apposition without a conjunction, expressing the traditional designation "the Levitical priests."
