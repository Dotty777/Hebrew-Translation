## Text

**32:1** After these deeds and this faithfulness, Sennacherib king of Assyria came and entered Judah; he encamped against the fortified cities, and purposed to breach them for himself.

**32:2** And Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come, and that his face was set to make war against Jerusalem.

**32:3** Therefore he took counsel with his officers and his mighty men to stop up the waters of the springs that were outside the city, and they helped him.

**32:4** So a great multitude gathered together, and they stopped up all the springs and the brook that flowed through the midst of the land, saying, "Why should the kings of Assyria come and find abundant water?"

**32:5** And he took courage, and rebuilt all the broken wall and raised it up to the towers, and built another wall outside; he also strengthened the Millo in the City of David, and made weapons and shields in abundance.

**32:6** Then he appointed commanders of war over the people, and gathered them to himself in the broad square at the gate of the city, and spoke to their hearts, saying,

**32:7** "Be strong and courageous; do not fear nor be dismayed before the king of Assyria, nor before all the multitude that is with him; for there is more with us than with him.

**32:8** With him is an arm of flesh, but with us is the LORD our God, to help us and to fight our battles." And the people relied upon the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.

**32:9** After this Sennacherib king of Assyria sent his servants to Jerusalem—while he himself was before Lachish, and all his imperial power with him—to Hezekiah king of Judah and to all Judah who were in Jerusalem, saying,

**32:10** "Thus says Sennacherib king of Assyria: Upon what do you place your trust, that you abide under siege in Jerusalem?

**32:11** Is not Hezekiah enticing you to give you over to die by famine and by thirst, saying, 'The LORD our God shall deliver us from the grasp of the king of Assyria'?

**32:12** Has not this same Hezekiah taken away His high places and His altars, and spoken to Judah and to Jerusalem, saying, 'Before one altar you shall worship, and upon it you shall burn incense'?

**32:13** Do you not know what I and my fathers have done to all the peoples of the lands? Were the gods of the nations of those lands at all able to deliver their land from my hand?

**32:14** Who among all the gods of these nations that my fathers utterly destroyed was able to deliver his people from my hand, that your God should be able to deliver you from my hand?

**32:15** Now therefore, do not let Hezekiah deceive you, nor mislead you in this manner, and do not believe him; for no god of any nation or kingdom has been able to deliver his people from my hand or from the hand of my fathers. How much less shall your God deliver you from my hand!"

**32:16** And his servants spoke still more against the LORD God and against His servant Hezekiah.

**32:17** He also wrote letters to revile the LORD, the God of Israel, and to speak against Him, saying, "As the gods of the nations of the lands have not delivered their people from my hand, so the God of Hezekiah shall not deliver His people from my hand."

**32:18** Then they called out with a loud voice in the language of Judah to the people of Jerusalem who were on the wall, to frighten them and terrify them, so that they might capture the city.

**32:19** And they spoke against the God of Jerusalem as against the gods of the peoples of the earth, the work of the hands of man.

**32:20** And King Hezekiah and Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, prayed concerning this, and cried out to heaven.

**32:21** Then the LORD sent an angel, who cut off every mighty man of valor, leader, and commander in the camp of the king of Assyria. So he returned in shame of face to his own land; and when he entered the house of his god, some of his own offspring struck him down there with the sword.

**32:22** Thus the LORD saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib king of Assyria, and from the hand of all others, and guided them on every side.

**32:23** And many brought offerings to the LORD at Jerusalem, and precious gifts to Hezekiah king of Judah, so that he was exalted in the sight of all the nations from that time onward.

**32:24** In those days Hezekiah fell sick to the point of death; and he prayed to the LORD, and He spoke to him and gave him a sign.

**32:25** Yet Hezekiah did not make return according to the favor bestowed upon him, for his heart was lifted up; therefore wrath came upon him, and upon Judah and Jerusalem.

**32:26** Then Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, both he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of the LORD did not come upon them in the days of Hezekiah.

**32:27** Now Hezekiah had exceedingly great riches and honor; and he made for himself treasuries for silver, for gold, for precious stones, for spices, for shields, and for all kinds of precious articles,

**32:28** storehouses also for the yield of grain, new wine, and oil, and stalls for every kind of livestock, and flocks for the folds.

**32:29** Moreover he provided cities for himself, and possessions of flocks and herds in abundance; for God had given him very great wealth.

**32:30** This same Hezekiah also stopped up the upper outlet of the waters of Gihon, and directed them straight down to the west side of the City of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.

**32:31** Yet in the matter of the envoys of the princes of Babylon, who were sent to him to inquire about the sign that had occurred in the land, God left him to himself to test him, that He might know all that was in his heart.

**32:32** Now the rest of the acts of Hezekiah, and his deeds of devotion, behold, they are written in the vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel.

**32:33** So Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the ascent of the tombs of the sons of David; and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem rendered honor to him at his death. Then Manasseh his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **32:21** — The Masoretic Qere reads plural construct 'and some of those who came forth from his own body' (rendered 'some of his own offspring'), whereas the Ketiv has a singular form.
- **32:22** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads 'and guided them on every side,' whereas the Septuagint and Vulgate read 'and gave them rest.'
- **32:28** — The Masoretic Hebrew literally reads 'and flocks for the folds,' reversing the expected order 'folds for the flocks.'
