## Text

**7:1** Now when Solomon had finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the LORD filled the house.

**7:2** And the priests could not enter the house of the LORD, because the glory of the LORD had filled the house of the LORD.

**7:3** When all the children of Israel saw how the fire came down, and the glory of the LORD upon the house, they bowed their faces to the ground upon the pavement, and worshiped, giving thanks to the LORD: "For He is good, for His lovingkindness endures forever."

**7:4** Then the king and all the people offered sacrifices before the LORD.

**7:5** King Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty-two thousand oxen and one hundred and twenty thousand sheep. Thus the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.

**7:6** And the priests stood at their posts of duty, and the Levites with the musical instruments of the LORD, which King David had made to give thanks to the LORD—"For His lovingkindness endures forever"—whenever David offered praise through their hands; and the priests sounded trumpets opposite them, while all Israel stood.

**7:7** Furthermore, Solomon consecrated the middle of the court that was before the house of the LORD; for there he offered the burnt offerings and the fat of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar which Solomon had made was not able to contain the burnt offering, the grain offering, and the fat.

**7:8** At that same time Solomon kept the feast seven days, and all Israel with him, an exceedingly great assembly from the entrance of Hamath to the Brook of Egypt.

**7:9** And on the eighth day they held a solemn assembly, for they observed the dedication of the altar seven days, and the feast seven days.

**7:10** On the twenty-third day of the seventh month he sent the people away to their tents, joyful and glad of heart for the goodness that the LORD had shown to David, to Solomon, and to His people Israel.

**7:11** Thus Solomon finished the house of the LORD and the house of the king; and Solomon prosperously accomplished all that came into his heart to make in the house of the LORD and in his own house.

**7:12** Then the LORD appeared to Solomon by night, and said to him: "I have heard your prayer, and have chosen this place for Myself as a house of sacrifice.

**7:13** When I shut up the heavens and there is no rain, or command the locust to devour the land, or send pestilence among My people,

**7:14** if My people, upon whom My name is called, humble themselves, and pray and seek My countenance, and turn from their evil ways, then I Myself will hear from heaven, and I will forgive their sin and heal their land.

**7:15** Now My eyes shall be open and My ears attentive to the prayer made in this place.

**7:16** For now I have chosen and consecrated this house, that My name may be there forever; and My eyes and My heart shall be there continually.

**7:17** As for you, if you walk before Me as your father David walked, and do according to all that I have commanded you, and keep My statutes and My judgments,

**7:18** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom, just as I covenanted with your father David, saying, 'You shall not fail to have a man ruling in Israel.'

**7:19** But if you turn away and forsake My statutes and My commandments which I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**7:20** then I will uproot them from My land which I have given them; and this house which I have consecrated for My name I will cast out of My sight, and will make it a proverb and a byword among all peoples.

**7:21** And as for this house, which was so exalted, everyone who passes by it will be astonished and say, 'Why has the LORD done thus to this land and to this house?'

**7:22** Then they will answer, 'Because they forsook the LORD God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and laid hold of other gods, and bowed down to them and served them; therefore He has brought all this calamity upon them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:6** — The Masoretic Text has a ketiv/qere orthographic variant on the participle for sounding trumpets, with the ketiv spelling the form with a doubled tsade and the qere reading the standard Hiphil participle; both carry the same meaning and the qere is translated.
- **7:14** — Upon whom My name is called renders the Hebrew idiom of covenant ownership, and I Myself reflects the emphatic Hebrew subject pronoun introducing the divine response.
- **7:20** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts from the second-person plural in verse 19 to the third-person plural (them) here when pronouncing the judgment of exile upon the nation.
