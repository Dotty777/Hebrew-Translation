## Text

**17:1** Then Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place, and he strengthened himself against Israel.

**17:2** And he placed troops in all the fortified cities of Judah, and set garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had captured.

**17:3** Now the LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the earlier ways of his father David and did not seek the Baals.

**17:4** Rather, he sought the God of his father and walked in His commandments, and not according to the deeds of Israel.

**17:5** Therefore the LORD established the kingdom in his hand; and all Judah gave tribute to Jehoshaphat, and he had wealth and honor in abundance.

**17:6** And his heart was emboldened in the ways of the LORD; moreover, he removed the high places and the Asherah poles from Judah.

**17:7** Also in the third year of his reign he sent his officials—Ben-Hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach in the cities of Judah.

**17:8** And with them were the Levites: Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-Adonijah, the Levites; and with them Elishama and Jehoram, the priests.

**17:9** So they taught in Judah, having the Book of the Law of the LORD with them; and they went throughout all the cities of Judah and taught among the people.

**17:10** And the dread of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were around Judah, so that they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.

**17:11** Also some of the Philistines brought gifts and silver as tribute to Jehoshaphat, and the Arabians likewise brought him flocks: seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred male goats.

**17:12** So Jehoshaphat continued to grow exceedingly great, and he built fortresses and store cities in Judah.

**17:13** And he had abundant stores in the cities of Judah, and men of war, mighty men of valor, in Jerusalem.

**17:14** These were their enrollments according to their fathers' houses: Of Judah, the commanders of thousands: Adnah the commander, and with him three hundred thousand mighty men of valor;

**17:15** and next to him was Jehohanan the commander, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand;

**17:16** and next to him was Amasiah the son of Zichri, who willingly offered himself to the LORD, and with him two hundred thousand mighty men of valor.

**17:17** And of Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty man of valor, and with him two hundred thousand men armed with bow and shield;

**17:18** and next to him was Jehozabad, and with him one hundred and eighty thousand equipped for war.

**17:19** These were those who served the king, besides those whom the king had stationed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **17:6** — Literally "his heart was lifted up," an expression that elsewhere denotes pride, here used positively of courageous devotion in the ways of the LORD.
- **17:8** — Follows the Qere reading "Shemiramoth"; the Ketiv spelling is "Shemarimoth."
- **17:13** — Hebrew "melakhah" literally means "work" or "enterprise," here referring to abundant supplies and royal works stored in the cities of Judah.
