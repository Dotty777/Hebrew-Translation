## Text

**2:1** And Solomon numbered seventy thousand men to bear burdens, eighty thousand men to quarry in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred to oversee them.

**2:2** Then Solomon sent to Huram king of Tyre, saying, "As you dealt with David my father and sent him cedars to build himself a house to dwell in,

**2:3** behold, I am building a house for the name of the LORD my God, to dedicate it to Him, to burn fragrant incense before Him, for the continual showbread, and for the burnt offerings morning and evening, on the Sabbaths, on the New Moons, and on the appointed feasts of the LORD our God; this is appointed forever for Israel.

**2:4** And the house which I am building will be great, for our God is greater than all the gods.

**2:5** But who has strength to build Him a house, since heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain Him? And who am I that I should build Him a house, except to burn incense before Him?

**2:6** Therefore now send me a skilled man to work in gold and silver, in bronze and iron, in purple and crimson and blue, and who knows how to make engravings, to work with the skilled men who are with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom David my father provided.

**2:7** Send me also cedar, cypress, and algum timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut the timber of Lebanon; and behold, my servants will be with your servants,

**2:8** to prepare timber for me in abundance, for the house which I am building will be great and wonderful.

**2:9** And behold, I will give to your servants, the woodsmen who cut the timber, twenty thousand kors of crushed wheat, twenty thousand kors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil."

**2:10** Then Huram king of Tyre answered in writing, which he sent to Solomon: "Because the LORD loves His people, He has made you king over them."

**2:11** Huram also said, "Blessed be the LORD God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, for He has given King David a wise son, endowed with prudence and understanding, who will build a house for the LORD and a house for his kingdom!

**2:12** And now I have sent a skilled man, endowed with understanding, Huram-Abi,

**2:13** the son of a woman of the daughters of Dan, and his father was a man of Tyre, skilled to work in gold and silver, bronze and iron, stone and wood, purple and blue, fine linen and crimson, and to make any engraving and devise any design that may be assigned to him, together with your skilled men and the skilled men of my lord David your father.

**2:14** Now therefore, the wheat and the barley, the oil and the wine of which my lord has spoken, let him send to his servants.

**2:15** And we will cut whatever timber you need from Lebanon, and bring it to you in rafts by sea to Joppa; and you shall carry it up to Jerusalem."

**2:16** Then Solomon numbered all the men who were sojourners in the land of Israel, after the census by which David his father had numbered them; and there were found one hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.

**2:17** And he appointed seventy thousand of them to bear burdens, eighty thousand to quarry in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred as overseers to set the people to work.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification followed here, chapter 2 begins at this verse (corresponding to 2:2 in many English translations, while 1:18 corresponds to 2:1).
- **2:9** — A kor was a dry measure of roughly six bushels (about 220 liters), and a bath was a liquid measure of roughly six gallons (about 22 liters).
- **2:12** — Huram-Abi literally means 'Huram my father,' using 'father' as an honorific title for a master craftsman or chief artisan (cf. 2 Chronicles 4:16).
