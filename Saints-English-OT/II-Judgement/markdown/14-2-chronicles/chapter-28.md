## Text

**28:1** Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; and he did not do what was right in the sight of the LORD, as his father David had done.

**28:2** For he walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, and also made molten images for the Baals.

**28:3** He also burned incense in the Valley of Ben-Hinnom, and burned his sons in the fire, according to the abominations of the nations whom the LORD had dispossessed before the children of Israel.

**28:4** And he sacrificed and burned incense on the high places, upon the hills, and under every green tree.

**28:5** Therefore the LORD his God delivered him into the hand of the king of Syria; and they struck him and carried away from him a great multitude of captives, and brought them to Damascus. He was also delivered into the hand of the king of Israel, who struck him with a great slaughter.

**28:6** For Pekah the son of Remaliah slew in Judah one hundred and twenty thousand in one day, all of them valiant men, because they had forsaken the LORD God of their fathers.

**28:7** And Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, slew Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the overseer of the house, and Elkanah who was second to the king.

**28:8** Then the children of Israel carried away captive from their brethren two hundred thousand—women, sons, and daughters—and also plundered much spoil from them, and brought the spoil to Samaria.

**28:9** Now a prophet of the LORD was there, whose name was Oded; and he went out before the army that came to Samaria, and said to them, "Behold, in the wrath of the LORD God of your fathers against Judah, He has delivered them into your hand; yet you have slain them in a fury that has reached up to heaven.

**28:10** And now you intend to subdue the children of Judah and Jerusalem to be male and female servants for yourselves; yet do you not yourselves have guilt against the LORD your God?

**28:11** Now therefore hear me, and return the captives whom you have taken captive from your brethren, for the fierce wrath of the LORD is upon you."

**28:12** Then certain men of the heads of the children of Ephraim—Azariah the son of Johanan, Berechiah the son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of Hadlai—stood up against those who came from the war,

**28:13** and said to them, "You shall not bring the captives in here, for what you intend would bring guilt against the LORD upon us, adding to our sins and to our guilt; for our guilt is great, and fierce wrath is upon Israel."

**28:14** So the armed men left the captives and the spoil before the leaders and all the assembly.

**28:15** Then the men who were designated by name rose up and took charge of the captives, and from the spoil they clothed all who were naked among them; they dressed them and gave them sandals, fed them and gave them drink, and anointed them; and they conveyed all the feeble ones on donkeys, and brought them to their brethren at Jericho, the city of palm trees. Then they returned to Samaria.

**28:16** At that same time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria to help him.

**28:17** For again the Edomites had come, struck Judah, and carried away captives.

**28:18** The Philistines also had raided the cities of the lowland and of the South of Judah, and had captured Beth-Shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Socho with its villages, Timnah with its villages, and Gimzo with its villages; and they dwelt there.

**28:19** For the LORD humbled Judah because of Ahaz king of Israel, for he had cast off restraint in Judah and had dealt most unfaithfully with the LORD.

**28:20** And Tilgath-Pilneser king of Assyria came against him and distressed him, and did not strengthen him.

**28:21** For Ahaz took a portion from the house of the LORD, from the house of the king, and from the leaders, and gave it to the king of Assyria; yet it was of no help to him.

**28:22** Now in the time of his distress he became even more unfaithful to the LORD—this same King Ahaz.

**28:23** For he sacrificed to the gods of Damascus who had struck him, saying, "Because the gods of the kings of Syria help them, I will sacrifice to them that they may help me." Yet they became the ruin of him and of all Israel.

**28:24** So Ahaz gathered the vessels of the house of God, cut in pieces the vessels of the house of God, shut up the doors of the house of the LORD, and made for himself altars in every corner of Jerusalem.

**28:25** And in every single city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, and provoked to anger the LORD God of his fathers.

**28:26** Now the rest of his acts and all his ways, from first to last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**28:27** So Ahaz rested with his fathers, and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem; for they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel. Then Hezekiah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — The Masoretic Hebrew verb here explicitly means burned, whereas the parallel account in Second Kings sixteen verse three uses a verb meaning caused to pass through.
- **28:16** — The Masoretic Text reads the plural, kings of Assyria, likely referring to the Assyrian royal line or imperial administration.
- **28:19** — Chronicles here refers to Ahaz as king of Israel, using Israel as a covenantal title for the kingdom of Judah.
- **28:20** — Chronicles consistently renders the Assyrian monarch's name as Tilgath-Pilneser rather than Tiglath-Pileser.
