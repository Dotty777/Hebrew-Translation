## Text

**6:1** Then Solomon spoke: "The LORD has said He would dwell in the thick darkness.

**6:2** And I have built an exalted house for You, a settled place for Your dwelling forever."

**6:3** Then the king turned his face around and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel was standing.

**6:4** And he said: "Blessed be the LORD God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth to my father David, and with His hands has fulfilled it, saying,

**6:5** 'Since the day that I brought My people out from the land of Egypt, I have not chosen any city from all the tribes of Israel in which to build a house, that My name might be there; nor did I choose any man to be a ruler over My people Israel.

**6:6** Yet I have chosen Jerusalem, that My name might be there; and I have chosen David to be over My people Israel.'

**6:7** Now it was in the heart of my father David to build a house for the name of the LORD God of Israel.

**6:8** But the LORD said to my father David, 'Because it was in your heart to build a house for My name, you did well that it was in your heart.

**6:9** Nevertheless, you shall not build the house; rather your son who shall come forth from your own loins, he shall build the house for My name.'

**6:10** So the LORD has established His word which He spoke, and I have risen in the place of my father David and sit upon the throne of Israel, just as the LORD spoke; and I have built the house for the name of the LORD God of Israel.

**6:11** And there I have set the ark, in which is the covenant of the LORD that He made with the children of Israel."

**6:12** Then he stood before the altar of the LORD in the presence of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread forth his hands.

**6:13** For Solomon had made a bronze platform, five cubits in its length, five cubits in its width, and three cubits in its height, and had placed it in the midst of the court; and he stood upon it, and knelt down upon his knees in the presence of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread forth his hands toward heaven.

**6:14** And he said: "O LORD God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven or on earth, who keeps the covenant and lovingkindness with Your servants who walk before You with all their heart.

**6:15** You have kept with Your servant David my father what You promised him; indeed, You spoke with Your mouth and have fulfilled it with Your hand, as it is this day.

**6:16** Therefore now, O LORD God of Israel, keep with Your servant David my father what You spoke to him, saying, 'You shall not fail to have a man before Me sitting upon the throne of Israel, if only your sons take heed to their way, to walk in My law as you have walked before Me.'

**6:17** Now then, O LORD God of Israel, let Your word be confirmed, which You have spoken to Your servant David.

**6:18** But will God indeed dwell with mankind upon the earth? Behold, heaven and the highest heaven cannot contain You—how much less this house which I have built!

**6:19** Yet turn toward the prayer of Your servant and his supplication, O LORD my God, to listen to the cry and the prayer which Your servant is praying before You:

**6:20** that Your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place where You have said You would put Your name, to hear the prayer which Your servant prays toward this place.

**6:21** And hear the supplications of Your servant and of Your people Israel, when they pray toward this place; hear from the place of Your dwelling, from heaven, and when You hear, forgive.

**6:22** If a man sins against his neighbor, and an oath is laid upon him to make him swear, and he comes and swears an oath before Your altar in this house,

**6:23** then hear from heaven, and act, and judge Your servants, requiting the wicked by bringing his way upon his own head, and justifying the righteous by giving to him according to his righteousness.

**6:24** And if Your people Israel are defeated before an enemy because they have sinned against You, and they return and confess Your name, and pray and make supplication before You in this house,

**6:25** then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of Your people Israel, and bring them back to the land which You gave to them and to their fathers.

**6:26** When the heavens are shut up and there is no rain because they have sinned against You, if they pray toward this place and confess Your name, and turn from their sin when You afflict them,

**6:27** then hear in heaven, and forgive the sin of Your servants, Your people Israel, for You teach them the good way in which they should walk; and send rain upon Your land which You have given to Your people as an inheritance.

**6:28** When there is famine in the land, when there is pestilence, blight or mildew, locust or caterpillar, when their enemies besiege them in the land of their gates, whatever plague or whatever sickness there is,

**6:29** whatever prayer, whatever supplication is made by any man, or by all Your people Israel, when each one knows his own affliction and his own grief, and spreads forth his hands toward this house,

**6:30** then hear from heaven, Your dwelling place, and forgive, and render to every man according to all his ways, whose heart You know—for You, You alone, know the hearts of the sons of men—

**6:31** that they may fear You, to walk in Your ways all the days that they live upon the face of the land which You gave to our fathers.

**6:32** Moreover, concerning the foreigner, who is not of Your people Israel, yet comes from a distant land for the sake of Your great name and Your mighty hand and Your outstretched arm, when they come and pray toward this house,

**6:33** then hear from heaven, from Your dwelling place, and do according to all for which the foreigner calls to You, in order that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name and fear You, as do Your people Israel, and that they may know that this house which I have built is called by Your name.

**6:34** When Your people go out to battle against their enemies, by whatever way You shall send them, and they pray to You toward this city which You have chosen and the house which I have built for Your name,

**6:35** then hear from heaven their prayer and their supplication, and maintain their cause.

**6:36** When they sin against You—for there is no man who does not sin—and You become angry with them and deliver them before an enemy, so that their captors carry them away captive to a land far off or near,

**6:37** yet if they take it to heart in the land where they have been carried captive, and repent and make supplication to You in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have committed iniquity, and we have acted wickedly,'

**6:38** and if they return to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity, where they have carried them captive, and pray toward their land which You gave to their fathers, and the city which You have chosen, and toward the house which I have built for Your name,

**6:39** then hear from heaven, from Your dwelling place, their prayer and their supplications, and maintain their cause, and forgive Your people who have sinned against You.

**6:40** Now, my God, I pray, let Your eyes be open and let Your ears be attentive to the prayer made in this place.

**6:41** Now therefore, arise, O LORD God, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength. Let Your priests, O LORD God, be clothed with salvation, and let Your godly ones rejoice in goodness.

**6:42** O LORD God, do not turn away the face of Your anointed; remember the mercies of Your servant David."

## Translator Notes

- **6:13** — The Hebrew phrase kiyor nechoshet literally refers to a bronze basin-shaped dais or platform; five cubits by five cubits and three cubits high corresponds to roughly seven and a half feet square and four and a half feet high.
- **6:26** — The phrase when You afflict them follows the traditional sense of divine discipline through drought, though the Masoretic vocalization of the Hebrew verb can also be read as when You answer them.
- **6:42** — The mercies of Your servant David translates Hebrew chasdei Dawid, referring to the steadfast covenant promises and lovingkindnesses pledged to David, echoing Psalm 132:8-10.
