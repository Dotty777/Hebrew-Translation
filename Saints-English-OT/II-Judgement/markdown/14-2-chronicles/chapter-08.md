## Text

**8:1** Now it came to pass at the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the house of the LORD and his own house,

**8:2** that Solomon rebuilt the cities which Huram had given to Solomon, and caused the children of Israel to dwell there.

**8:3** And Solomon went to Hamath Zobah and prevailed against it.

**8:4** He also built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the storage cities which he built in Hamath.

**8:5** He built Upper Beth Horon and Lower Beth Horon, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars;

**8:6** also Baalath, and all the storage cities that belonged to Solomon, and all the chariot cities and the cities of the horsemen, and every desire of Solomon which he desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion.

**8:7** As for all the people who remained of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of Israel,

**8:8** of their children who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel had not destroyed, Solomon levied them for forced labor to this day.

**8:9** But Solomon did not appoint any of the children of Israel as servants for his work; for they were men of war, commanders of his officers, and commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.

**8:10** And these were the chief officers of King Solomon, two hundred and fifty, who ruled over the people.

**8:11** Now Solomon brought the daughter of Pharaoh up from the City of David to the house that he had built for her; for he said, "My wife shall not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, because the places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy."

**8:12** Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of the LORD which he had built before the vestibule,

**8:13** even according to the daily rate, offering according to the commandment of Moses for the Sabbaths, for the New Moons, and for the appointed feasts three times a year: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks, and at the Feast of Tabernacles.

**8:14** And he appointed, according to the ordinance of David his father, the divisions of the priests to their service, and the Levites to their charges, to praise and to minister before the priests as the duty of every day required, and the gatekeepers by their divisions at every gate; for so was the commandment of David the man of God.

**8:15** And they did not turn aside from the commandment of the king concerning the priests and the Levites in any matter, or concerning the treasuries.

**8:16** Thus all the work of Solomon was established, up to the day the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid and until it was completed; so the house of the LORD was finished.

**8:17** Then Solomon went to Ezion Geber and to Eloth on the shore of the sea, in the land of Edom.

**8:18** And Huram sent him ships by the hand of his servants, and servants who knew the sea; and they went with the servants of Solomon to Ophir, and took from there four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **8:10** — Follows the Qere reading (hannitsavim, 'those stationed' or 'chief officers'); the Ketiv writes the plural noun form (hanetsivim, 'prefects' or 'deputies') with the same basic sense.
- **8:16** — Literally 'up to the day of the foundation of the house of the LORD and until its completion,' encompassing both the preparatory work before the foundation was laid and the building work until it was finished.
- **8:18** — Follows the Qere orthographic form ('oniyyot, 'ships') rather than the plene Ketiv spelling.
