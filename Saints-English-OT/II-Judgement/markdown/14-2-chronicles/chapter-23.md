## Text

**23:1** And in the seventh year Jehoiada strengthened himself, and took the captains of hundreds—Azariah the son of Jeroham, Ishmael the son of Jehohanan, Azariah the son of Obed, Maaseiah the son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat the son of Zichri—into covenant with him.

**23:2** And they went about through Judah and gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah, and the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel; and they came to Jerusalem.

**23:3** Then all the assembly made a covenant with the king in the house of God. And he said to them, "Behold, the king's son shall reign, just as the LORD spoke concerning the sons of David.

**23:4** This is the thing that you shall do: one-third of you who come in on the Sabbath, of the priests and of the Levites, shall be gatekeepers of the thresholds;

**23:5** and one-third shall be at the king's house, and one-third at the Gate of the Foundation; and all the people shall be in the courts of the house of the LORD.

**23:6** But let no one enter the house of the LORD except the priests and those of the Levites who minister; they may enter, for they are holy, while all the people shall keep the charge of the LORD.

**23:7** And the Levites shall surround the king on every side, every man with his weapons in his hand; and whoever enters the house shall be put to death. And be with the king when he comes in and when he goes out."

**23:8** So the Levites and all Judah did according to all that Jehoiada the priest had commanded. And each took his men, those who were coming in on the Sabbath together with those who were going out on the Sabbath; for Jehoiada the priest had not dismissed the divisions.

**23:9** And Jehoiada the priest gave to the captains of hundreds the spears, the bucklers, and the shields that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of God.

**23:10** And he stationed all the people, every man with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and by the house, all around the king.

**23:11** Then they brought out the king's son, put the crown on him, gave him the testimony, and made him king. And Jehoiada and his sons anointed him and declared, "Long live the king!"

**23:12** Now when Athaliah heard the sound of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people in the house of the LORD.

**23:13** And she looked, and behold, the king was standing by his pillar at the entrance, and the princes and the trumpeters were beside the king; and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets, along with the singers with musical instruments and those who led the praise. Then Athaliah tore her garments and cried out, "Treason! Treason!"

**23:14** Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the captains of hundreds who were appointed over the army, and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks, and whoever follows her shall be slain with the sword." For the priest had said, "Do not put her to death in the house of the LORD."

**23:15** So they laid hands on her; and when she came to the entrance of the Horse Gate at the king's house, they put her to death there.

**23:16** Then Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king, that they should be the people of the LORD.

**23:17** And all the people went to the house of Baal and tore it down; they broke its altars and its images in pieces, and slew Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars.

**23:18** Moreover Jehoiada committed the oversight of the house of the LORD into the hand of the Levitical priests, whom David had assigned over the house of the LORD to offer the burnt offerings of the LORD, as it is written in the Law of Moses, with rejoicing and with singing, according to the direction of David.

**23:19** And he stationed the gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the LORD, so that no one who was unclean in any matter should enter.

**23:20** Then he took the captains of hundreds, the nobles, the rulers of the people, and all the people of the land, and brought the king down from the house of the LORD; and they entered through the Upper Gate into the king's house, and seated the king upon the throne of the kingdom.

**23:21** So all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; for they had put Athaliah to death with the sword.

## Translator Notes

- **23:11** — The Hebrew word for 'testimony' refers to a copy of the covenant law placed in the king's custody at his coronation, echoing Deuteronomy 17:18-19.
- **23:13** — The Hebrew phrase rendered 'those who led the praise' literally means 'those making known to praise,' describing musical directors who guided the liturgical singing.
- **23:18** — The Hebrew literally reads 'the priests, the Levites,' denoting the Levitical priesthood, and 'by the hands of David,' meaning according to David's established order.
