## Text

**11:1** Now Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, and he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, one hundred and eighty thousand chosen men who were warriors, to fight against Israel, that he might restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.

**11:2** Then the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,

**11:3** "Speak to Rehoboam the son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying,

**11:4** 'Thus says the LORD: You shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren. Return, every man to his house, for this thing has come from Me.'" So they listened to the words of the LORD and turned back from going against Jeroboam.

**11:5** And Rehoboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and he built cities for defense in Judah.

**11:6** He built Bethlehem, Etam, and Tekoa,

**11:7** Beth Zur, Soco, and Adullam,

**11:8** Gath, Mareshah, and Ziph,

**11:9** Adoraim, Lachish, and Azekah,

**11:10** Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, which are in Judah and in Benjamin, fortified cities.

**11:11** And he strengthened the fortresses, and placed commanders in them, with stores of food, oil, and wine.

**11:12** In every single city he put large shields and spears, and he made them exceedingly strong. Thus Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.

**11:13** Moreover, the priests and the Levites who were in all Israel presented themselves to him from all their territories.

**11:14** For the Levites left their pasturelands and their possessions and came to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off from serving as priests to the LORD.

**11:15** And he appointed for himself priests for the high places, for the goat idols, and for the calves that he had made.

**11:16** And after them, out of all the tribes of Israel, those who set their heart to seek the LORD God of Israel came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD God of their fathers.

**11:17** So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and made Rehoboam the son of Solomon strong for three years, because they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.

**11:18** Now Rehoboam took for himself as wife Mahalath the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David, and of Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse.

**11:19** And she bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.

**11:20** After her he took Maacah the daughter of Absalom; and she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.

**11:21** Now Rehoboam loved Maacah the daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and his concubines; for he took eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

**11:22** And Rehoboam appointed Abijah the son of Maacah as chief, to be leader among his brethren, for he intended to make him king.

**11:23** And he dealt wisely, and dispersed some of all his sons throughout all the lands of Judah and Benjamin, to every fortified city; he gave them provisions in abundance, and sought many wives for them.

## Translator Notes

- **11:15** — The Hebrew word translated goat idols literally means hairy ones or satyrs, referring to pagan goat-shaped deities.
- **11:18** — The Ketiv reads son, while the Qere followed here reads daughter; Abihail is introduced without a conjunction, indicating Mahalath's maternal lineage.
- **11:23** — Dispersing his sons across the fortified towns secured loyalty in the provinces while preventing rival claims around the throne in Jerusalem.
