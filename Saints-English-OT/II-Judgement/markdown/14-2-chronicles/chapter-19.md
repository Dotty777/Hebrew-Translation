## Text

**19:1** Then Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned in peace to his house in Jerusalem.

**19:2** Then Jehu the son of Hanani the seer went forth to meet him, and said to King Jehoshaphat, "Ought you to aid the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, wrath is upon you from before the LORD.

**19:3** Nevertheless, good things are found in you, in that you have purged the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart to seek God."

**19:4** So Jehoshaphat dwelt in Jerusalem; and he went out again among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim, and brought them back to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**19:5** Then he appointed judges in the land throughout all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city.

**19:6** And he said to the judges, "Consider what you are doing, for you do not judge for man but for the LORD, and He is with you in the matter of judgment.

**19:7** Now therefore, let the fear of the LORD be upon you; take heed and act carefully, for there is no iniquity with the LORD our God, nor partiality, nor taking of bribes."

**19:8** Moreover, in Jerusalem Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites and the priests, and some of the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel, for the judgment of the LORD and for controversies; and they returned to Jerusalem.

**19:9** And he commanded them, saying, "Thus you shall act in the fear of the LORD, in faithfulness and with a whole heart:

**19:10** Whatever dispute comes before you from your brethren who dwell in their cities, between bloodshed and bloodshed, between law and commandment, statutes and ordinances, you shall warn them, lest they incur guilt before the LORD and wrath come upon you and your brethren. Act thus, and you shall not incur guilt.

**19:11** And behold, Amariah the chief priest is over you in every matter of the LORD; and Zebadiah the son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, in every matter of the king; and the Levites shall serve as officers before you. Be strong and act, and may the LORD be with the good."

## Translator Notes

- **19:7** — Hebrew "partiality" is literally "lifting up of faces," an idiom for showing unjust favoritism in judgment.
- **19:8** — Follows the Masoretic text "and they returned to Jerusalem"; some ancient versions reflect a different vocalization meaning "for the inhabitants of Jerusalem."
- **19:10** — "Between bloodshed and bloodshed" refers to judging different kinds of homicide, such as distinguishing accidental manslaughter from deliberate murder.
