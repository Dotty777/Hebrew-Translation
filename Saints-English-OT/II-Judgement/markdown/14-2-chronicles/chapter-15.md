## Text

**15:1** Now the Spirit of God came upon Azariah the son of Oded.

**15:2** And he went out to meet Asa and said to him, "Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: the LORD is with you while you are with Him. If you seek Him, He will let Himself be found by you; yet if you forsake Him, He will forsake you.

**15:3** For many days Israel was without the true God, without a teaching priest, and without the law;

**15:4** but in their distress they turned back to the LORD, the God of Israel, and sought Him, and He was found by them.

**15:5** In those times there was no peace for the one going out or the one coming in, for great tumults afflicted all the inhabitants of the lands.

**15:6** Nation was crushed by nation and city by city, for God threw them into turmoil with every kind of distress.

**15:7** As for you, therefore, be strong, and do not let your hands grow weak, for your work shall have its reward."

**15:8** When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and put away the detestable idols from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities that he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim. He also renewed the altar of the LORD that stood before the vestibule of the LORD.

**15:9** Then he gathered all Judah and Benjamin, along with those who sojourned with them from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon; for great numbers from Israel had defected to him when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.

**15:10** So they assembled at Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.

**15:11** And on that day they sacrificed to the LORD from the spoil they had brought seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.

**15:12** Then they entered into a covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and with all their soul;

**15:13** and whoever would not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, was to be put to death, whether small or great, whether man or woman.

**15:14** They swore an oath to the LORD with a loud voice, with shouting, with trumpets, and with horns.

**15:15** And all Judah rejoiced concerning the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and sought Him with all their desire, and He was found by them; and the LORD granted them rest on every side.

**15:16** King Asa also removed his mother Maachah from the office of queen mother, because she had made a repulsive image for Asherah; and Asa cut down her repulsive image, crushed it, and burned it at the Brook Kidron.

**15:17** Yet the high places were not removed from Israel; nevertheless, Asa's heart remained wholly devoted all his days.

**15:18** He also brought into the house of God the dedicated things of his father and his own dedicated things—silver and gold and vessels.

**15:19** And there was no war until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.

## Translator Notes

- **15:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "the prophecy, Oded the prophet," naming the father of Azariah who spoke in 15:1.
- **15:16** — Hebrew em ("mother") is used broadly here for Asa's grandmother Maachah, who still held the royal office of queen mother (gebirah).
