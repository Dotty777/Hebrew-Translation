## Text

**20:1** Now it came to pass after this that the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, and with them some of the Ammonites, came against Jehoshaphat to battle.

**20:2** Then some came and reported to Jehoshaphat, saying, "A great multitude is coming against you from beyond the sea, from Aram; and behold, they are in Hazazon Tamar, which is En Gedi."

**20:3** And Jehoshaphat was afraid, and he set his face to seek the LORD and proclaimed a fast throughout all Judah.

**20:4** So Judah assembled to seek help from the LORD; indeed, from all the cities of Judah they came to seek the LORD.

**20:5** Then Jehoshaphat stood in the congregation of Judah and Jerusalem, in the house of the LORD, before the new court,

**20:6** and said, "O LORD, God of our fathers, are You not God in heaven, and do You not rule over all the kingdoms of the nations? In Your hand are power and might, so that none can stand against You.

**20:7** Did You not, O our God, dispossess the inhabitants of this land before Your people Israel, and give it to the seed of Abraham Your friend forever?

**20:8** And they dwelt in it, and built You a sanctuary in it for Your name, saying,

**20:9** 'If calamity comes upon us—sword, judgment, pestilence, or famine—we will stand before this house and before You, for Your name is in this house, and we will cry out to You in our distress, and You will hear and save.'

**20:10** And now, behold, the sons of Ammon and Moab and Mount Seir, whom You did not permit Israel to invade when they came from the land of Egypt, for they turned aside from them and did not destroy them—

**20:11** behold, they are repaying us by coming to cast us out of Your possession which You have given us to inherit.

**20:12** O our God, will You not judge them? For we have no strength before this great multitude that is coming against us; neither do we know what to do, but our eyes are upon You."

**20:13** Now all Judah stood before the LORD, with their little ones, their wives, and their children.

**20:14** Then the Spirit of the LORD came upon Jahaziel the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, a Levite of the sons of Asaph, in the midst of the assembly.

**20:15** And he said, "Give ear, all Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat! Thus says the LORD to you: 'Do not fear nor be dismayed before this great multitude, for the battle is not yours, but God's.

**20:16** Tomorrow go down against them. Behold, they are ascending by the Ascent of Ziz, and you shall find them at the end of the valley, before the wilderness of Jeruel.

**20:17** It is not for you to fight in this battle; take your stand, stand firm, and behold the salvation of the LORD on your behalf, O Judah and Jerusalem. Do not fear nor be dismayed; tomorrow go out against them, for the LORD is with you.'"

**20:18** Then Jehoshaphat bowed his head with his face to the ground, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell down before the LORD, worshiping the LORD.

**20:19** And the Levites of the sons of the Kohathites and of the sons of the Korahites stood up to praise the LORD, the God of Israel, with a voice exceedingly loud.

**20:20** So they rose early in the morning and went forth to the wilderness of Tekoa; and as they went out, Jehoshaphat stood and said, "Hear me, O Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem! Put your trust in the LORD your God, and you shall be established; believe His prophets, and you shall prosper."

**20:21** And when he had taken counsel with the people, he appointed singers to the LORD and those who would offer praise in the splendor of holiness, going out before the armed men and saying, "Give thanks to the LORD, for His lovingkindness endures forever."

**20:22** Now at the time they began with joyful song and praise, the LORD set ambushes against the sons of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, who had come against Judah, and they were routed.

**20:23** For the sons of Ammon and Moab rose up against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to utterly annihilate and destroy them; and when they had made an end of the inhabitants of Seir, every man helped to destroy his fellow.

**20:24** And when Judah came to the watchtower overlooking the wilderness, they turned toward the multitude, and behold, there were corpses fallen to the ground, and not one had escaped.

**20:25** When Jehoshaphat and his people came to plunder their spoil, they found among them in abundance—goods, corpses, and precious articles—which they stripped off for themselves until they could carry no more; and they spent three days gathering the spoil, for it was exceedingly great.

**20:26** And on the fourth day they assembled in the Valley of Berachah, for there they blessed the LORD; therefore they called the name of that place the Valley of Berachah to this day.

**20:27** Then all the men of Judah and Jerusalem returned, with Jehoshaphat at their head, to go back to Jerusalem with joy, for the LORD had made them rejoice over their enemies.

**20:28** So they came to Jerusalem with harps, lyres, and trumpets, to the house of the LORD.

**20:29** And the dread of God fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought against the enemies of Israel.

**20:30** Thus the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was quiet, for his God gave him rest all around.

**20:31** So Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah. He was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah the daughter of Shilhi.

**20:32** And he walked in the way of his father Asa and did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the sight of the LORD.

**20:33** Nevertheless the high places were not removed, for as yet the people had not directed their hearts to the God of their fathers.

**20:34** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, from first to last, behold, they are written in the records of Jehu the son of Hanani, which were entered into the Book of the Kings of Israel.

**20:35** After this, Jehoshaphat king of Judah allied himself with Ahaziah king of Israel, who acted wickedly.

**20:36** He joined with him to build ships to go to Tarshish, and they built ships in Ezion Geber.

**20:37** Then Eliezer the son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, "Because you have allied yourself with Ahaziah, the LORD has shattered your works." And the ships were wrecked, so that they were unable to go to Tarshish.

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — The Masoretic Text reads "some of the Ammonites," whereas the Septuagint reads "some of the Meunites" (compare 2 Chronicles 26:7).
- **20:2** — The Masoretic Text reads "from Aram" (Syria); one Hebrew manuscript and many commentators suggest "from Edom," given the southern location of En Gedi.
- **20:25** — The Masoretic Text reads "corpses" (pegarim), while a few Hebrew manuscripts read "garments" (begadim), differing by a single consonant.
- **20:26** — The Hebrew name Berachah means "blessing," echoing the people's blessing of the LORD there.
