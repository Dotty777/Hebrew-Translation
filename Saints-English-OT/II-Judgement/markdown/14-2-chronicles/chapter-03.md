## Text

**3:1** Now Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where He had appeared to David his father, which he prepared at the place of David, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**3:2** And he began to build on the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

**3:3** Now these are the foundation measurements which Solomon laid for building the house of God: the length in cubits according to the former measure was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

**3:4** And the vestibule in front of the house was twenty cubits in length across the width of the house, and its height was one hundred and twenty cubits; and he overlaid it on the inside with pure gold.

**3:5** And the main hall he paneled with cypress wood, overlaid it with fine gold, and set palm trees and chains upon it.

**3:6** He also adorned the house with precious stones for beauty, and the gold was gold of Parvaim.

**3:7** And he overlaid the house—the beams, the thresholds, its walls, and its doors—with gold, and he carved cherubim on the walls.

**3:8** And he made the Most Holy Place: its length across the width of the house was twenty cubits, and its width was twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with six hundred talents of fine gold.

**3:9** And the weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold; he also overlaid the upper chambers with gold.

**3:10** In the Most Holy Place he made two cherubim of sculpted work, and they overlaid them with gold.

**3:11** The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits in total length: one wing of the first was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing was five cubits, touching the wing of the other cherub;

**3:12** and one wing of the other cherub was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing was five cubits, joined to the wing of the first cherub.

**3:13** The wings of these cherubim spanned twenty cubits; they stood on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.

**3:14** And he made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and worked cherubim upon it.

**3:15** Also he made in front of the house two pillars thirty-five cubits high, and the capital on the top of each was five cubits.

**3:16** And he made chains in the inner sanctuary and put them on the tops of the pillars; and he made one hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.

**3:17** And he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and the other on the left; he called the name of the one on the right Jachin, and the name of the one on the left Boaz.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — The Masoretic Text explicitly records the height of the vestibule as one hundred and twenty cubits; some ancient versions read twenty cubits.
- **3:10** — The Hebrew term rendered 'sculpted work' (tsaatsuim) occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes carved or sculpted imagery.
- **3:16** — Hebrew baddebir literally means 'in the inner sanctuary' and is sometimes interpreted as 'as in the inner sanctuary' or 'like a necklace.'
- **3:17** — The ketiv and qere on 'the one on the right' differ only in spelling (with or without an internal yod). Jachin means 'He shall establish,' and Boaz means 'in Him is strength.'
