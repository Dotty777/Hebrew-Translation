## Text

**17:1** In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea the son of Elah began to reign in Samaria over Israel, reigning nine years.

**17:2** And he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, yet not like the kings of Israel who were before him.

**17:3** Against him came up Shalmaneser king of Assyria; and Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.

**17:4** But the king of Assyria uncovered a conspiracy in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So, king of Egypt, and had brought up no tribute to the king of Assyria as year by year; therefore the king of Assyria confined him and bound him in prison.

**17:5** Then the king of Assyria marched throughout all the land, and went up to Samaria and besieged it three years.

**17:6** In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and carried Israel away into exile to Assyria, and settled them in Halah and by the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

**17:7** Now this came to pass because the children of Israel had sinned against the LORD their God, who had brought them up out of the land of Egypt, from beneath the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and they had feared other gods,

**17:8** and walked in the statutes of the nations whom the LORD had dispossessed from before the children of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, which they had instituted.

**17:9** And the children of Israel secretly did things that were not right against the LORD their God; and they built for themselves high places in all their cities, from watchtower to fortified city.

**17:10** They set up for themselves sacred pillars and Asherah poles upon every high hill and beneath every green tree.

**17:11** And there they burned incense on all the high places, like the nations whom the LORD had carried away from before them; and they did evil things to provoke the LORD to anger.

**17:12** And they served idols, concerning which the LORD had said to them, "You shall not do this thing."

**17:13** Yet the LORD testified against Israel and against Judah, by the hand of all the prophets, every seer, saying, "Turn from your evil ways, and keep My commandments and My statutes, according to all the law which I commanded your fathers, and which I sent to you by the hand of My servants the prophets."

**17:14** Yet they would not listen, but stiffened their necks, like the neck of their fathers, who did not believe in the LORD their God.

**17:15** They rejected His statutes and His covenant that He had made with their fathers, and His testimonies which He had testified against them; and they went after vanity and became vain, and went after the nations that were all around them, concerning whom the LORD had commanded them that they should not do like them.

**17:16** So they forsook all the commandments of the LORD their God, and made for themselves molten images—two calves—and made an Asherah pole, and worshiped all the host of heaven, and served Baal.

**17:17** And they made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, and practiced divination and soothsaying, and sold themselves to do what was evil in the sight of the LORD, to provoke Him to anger.

**17:18** Therefore the LORD was exceedingly angry with Israel, and removed them from His presence; none was left except the tribe of Judah alone.

**17:19** Judah also did not keep the commandments of the LORD their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel which they had made.

**17:20** And the LORD rejected all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of plunderers, until He had cast them out from His presence.

**17:21** For He tore Israel away from the house of David, and they made Jeroboam the son of Nebat king. Then Jeroboam drove Israel away from following the LORD, and caused them to commit a great sin.

**17:22** And the children of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam which he committed; they did not depart from them,

**17:23** until the LORD removed Israel from His presence, as He had spoken by the hand of all His servants the prophets. So Israel was carried away into exile from their own land to Assyria, to this day.

**17:24** Then the king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim, and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the children of Israel; and they took possession of Samaria and dwelt in its cities.

**17:25** And it came to pass, at the beginning of their dwelling there, that they did not fear the LORD; therefore the LORD sent lions among them, which slew some of them.

**17:26** So they spoke to the king of Assyria, saying, "The nations whom you have carried into exile and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the custom of the God of the land; therefore He has sent lions among them, and behold, they are slaying them, because they do not know the custom of the God of the land."

**17:27** Then the king of Assyria commanded, saying, "Send back there one of the priests whom you carried away from there, and let them go and dwell there; and let him teach them the custom of the God of the land."

**17:28** Then one of the priests whom they had carried away from Samaria came and dwelt in Bethel, and he taught them how they should fear the LORD.

**17:29** Yet every nation continued making its own gods, and placed them in the houses of the high places which the Samaritans had made, every nation in their cities in which they dwelt.

**17:30** The men of Babylon made Succoth-Benoth, the men of Cuth made Nergal, and the men of Hamath made Ashima,

**17:31** and the Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak; and the Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

**17:32** They also feared the LORD, and appointed for themselves from every class among them priests of the high places, who sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places.

**17:33** They feared the LORD, yet they served their own gods, according to the custom of the nations from among whom they had been carried into exile.

**17:34** To this day they continue doing according to the former customs: they do not fear the LORD, nor do they act according to their statutes or their ordinances, or according to the law and the commandment which the LORD commanded the children of Jacob, whom He named Israel,

**17:35** with whom the LORD had made a covenant and commanded them, saying, "You shall not fear other gods, nor bow down to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them;

**17:36** but the LORD, who brought you up from the land of Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm, Him you shall fear, and to Him you shall bow down, and to Him you shall sacrifice.

**17:37** And the statutes, and the ordinances, and the law, and the commandment which He wrote for you, you shall be careful to observe always; and you shall not fear other gods.

**17:38** And the covenant that I have made with you, you shall not forget, neither shall you fear other gods;

**17:39** but the LORD your God you shall fear; and He shall deliver you from the hand of all your enemies."

**17:40** Yet they did not listen, but they continued doing according to their former custom.

**17:41** So these nations feared the LORD, yet they served their carved images; also their children and their children's children continue doing as their fathers did, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **17:13** — Translates the Masoretic qere "all the prophets, every seer"; the ketiv reads "all His prophets" (or "every prophet of His").
- **17:21** — Translates the Masoretic qere "drove away" (from the root meaning to thrust or entice away); the ketiv preserves an unusual orthographic form with the same sense.
- **17:27** — The Hebrew text uses plural verbs ("let them go and dwell there") before returning to the singular ("let him teach them"), encompassing the priest together with those accompanying him.
- **17:31** — Translates the Masoretic qere "the gods of Sepharvaim," correcting the defective spellings in the ketiv.
