## Text

**14:1** In the second year of Joash son of Joahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, began to reign.

**14:2** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Jehoaddan of Jerusalem.

**14:3** And he did what was right in the sight of the LORD, yet not like David his father; according to all that Joash his father had done, so he did.

**14:4** Only the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**14:5** And it came to pass, as soon as the kingdom was established firmly in his hand, that he struck down his servants who had slain the king his father.

**14:6** Yet he did not put to death the children of the slayers, according to what is written in the Book of the Law of Moses, in which the LORD commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not be put to death for their children, nor shall children be put to death for their fathers; rather, each man shall be put to death for his own sin."

**14:7** He struck down ten thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt, and captured Sela in war, and called its name Joktheel to this very day.

**14:8** Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us look one another in the face."

**14:9** And Jehoash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The thistle that was in Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' Then a wild beast of the field that was in Lebanon passed by and trampled down the thistle.

**14:10** You have indeed struck down Edom, and your heart has lifted you up. Enjoy your honor and remain in your own house; for why should you provoke disaster, so that you fall—you and Judah with you?"

**14:11** Yet Amaziah would not listen. Therefore Jehoash king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah looked one another in the face at Beth Shemesh, which belongs to Judah.

**14:12** And Judah was defeated before Israel, and they fled, every man to his tent.

**14:13** Then Jehoash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Jehoash, son of Ahaziah, at Beth Shemesh; and he came to Jerusalem and broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate, four hundred cubits.

**14:14** And he took all the gold and silver, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the LORD and in the treasuries of the king's house, together with hostages, and returned to Samaria.

**14:15** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoash which he did, and his might, and how he fought with Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

**14:16** So Jehoash slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; and Jeroboam his son reigned in his place.

**14:17** And Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.

**14:18** Now the rest of the acts of Amaziah, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

**14:19** And they formed a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; yet they sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.

**14:20** Then they brought him back upon horses, and he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the City of David.

**14:21** And all the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah.

**14:22** He built Elath and restored it to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.

**14:23** In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel, began to reign in Samaria, and reigned forty-one years.

**14:24** And he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he had caused Israel to sin.

**14:25** He restored the border of Israel from the entrance of Hamath to the Sea of the Arabah, according to the word of the LORD, the God of Israel, which He had spoken by the hand of His servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet, who was from Gath Hepher.

**14:26** For the LORD saw that the affliction of Israel was exceedingly bitter; there was neither bond nor free left, nor was there any helper for Israel.

**14:27** And the LORD had not said that He would blot out the name of Israel from under heaven; therefore He delivered them by the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash.

**14:28** Now the rest of the acts of Jeroboam, and all that he did, and his might, how he waged war, and how he restored to Israel Damascus and Hamath, which had belonged to Judah, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

**14:29** So Jeroboam slept with his fathers, with the kings of Israel; and Zechariah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Qere (read text) gives the name Jehoaddan, whereas the Ketiv (written text) reads Jehoaddin.
- **14:6** — The Qere reads the passive form "shall be put to death," matching the preceding clauses (cf. Deuteronomy 24:16), whereas the Ketiv reads the active "shall die."
- **14:7** — The Ketiv and Qere differ only on the definite article attached to "Salt" in "the Valley of Salt," with no change in meaning.
- **14:13** — The Qere reads the singular verb "he came," referring to Jehoash, whereas the Ketiv reads the plural "they came."
- **14:28** — The Hebrew reads literally "restored Damascus and Hamath for Judah in Israel," pointing to northern territories once subject to the united kingdom of David and Solomon.
