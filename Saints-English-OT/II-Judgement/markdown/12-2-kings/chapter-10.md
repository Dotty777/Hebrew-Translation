## Text

**10:1** Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. And Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to the rulers of Jezreel, the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab's sons, saying,

**10:2** "Now, as soon as this letter reaches you, since your master's sons are with you, and with you are chariots and horses, a fortified city also, and weapons,

**10:3** select the best and most upright of your master's sons, set him on his father's throne, and fight for your master's house."

**10:4** But they feared exceedingly and said, "Behold, the two kings did not stand before him; how then shall we stand?"

**10:5** So he who was over the palace, and he who was over the city, together with the elders and the guardians, sent to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants, and all that you say to us we will do. We will not make any man king; do what is good in your sight."

**10:6** Then he wrote them a letter a second time, saying, "If you are for me, and if you listen to my voice, take the heads of the men who are your master's sons, and come to me at Jezreel by this time tomorrow." Now the king's sons, seventy men, were with the great men of the city, who were rearing them.

**10:7** And it came to pass, when the letter reached them, that they took the king's sons and slaughtered seventy men, put their heads in baskets, and sent them to him at Jezreel.

**10:8** Then a messenger came and told him, saying, "They have brought the heads of the king's sons." And he said, "Lay them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until morning."

**10:9** And it came to pass in the morning that he went out and stood, and said to all the people, "You are righteous. Behold, I conspired against my master and slew him; yet who struck down all these?

**10:10** Know now that nothing of the word of the LORD shall fall to the earth, which the LORD spoke concerning the house of Ahab; for the LORD has performed what He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah."

**10:11** So Jehu struck down all who remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, and all his great men, his close companions, and his priests, until he left him no survivor.

**10:12** Then he arose and departed, and went to Samaria; along the way he was at Beth Eked of the Shepherds.

**10:13** And Jehu met the brethren of Ahaziah king of Judah, and said, "Who are you?" And they answered, "We are the brethren of Ahaziah, and we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother."

**10:14** So he said, "Seize them alive!" And they seized them alive, and slaughtered them at the pit of Beth Eked, forty-two men; and he did not leave a single man of them.

**10:15** When he departed from there, he met Jehonadab the son of Rechab coming to meet him; and he greeted him and said to him, "Is your heart upright, as my heart is with your heart?" And Jehonadab answered, "It is." And Jehu said, "If it is, give me your hand." So he gave him his hand, and he brought him up to himself into the chariot.

**10:16** Then he said, "Come with me, and see my zeal for the LORD." So they had him ride in his chariot.

**10:17** And when he came to Samaria, he struck down all who remained to Ahab in Samaria until he had destroyed him, according to the word of the LORD which He spoke to Elijah.

**10:18** Then Jehu gathered all the people together and said to them, "Ahab served Baal a little, but Jehu will serve him much.

**10:19** Now therefore, summon to me all the prophets of Baal, all his worshipers, and all his priests. Let no man be missing, for I have a great sacrifice for Baal; whoever is missing shall not live." Yet Jehu acted with subtlety, in order to destroy the worshipers of Baal.

**10:20** And Jehu said, "Consecrate a solemn assembly for Baal." So they proclaimed it.

**10:21** Then Jehu sent throughout all Israel; and all the worshipers of Baal came, so that not a man remained who did not come. And they entered the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was filled from one end to the other.

**10:22** And he said to the one in charge of the wardrobe, "Bring out vestments for all the worshipers of Baal." So he brought out the vestments for them.

**10:23** Then Jehu and Jehonadab the son of Rechab entered the house of Baal, and he said to the worshipers of Baal, "Search and see lest there be here with you any of the servants of the LORD, rather than the worshipers of Baal alone."

**10:24** So they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had stationed eighty men outside for himself, and he said, "Whoever lets any of the men escape whom I bring into your hands, his own life shall be in place of his life."

**10:25** And it came to pass, as soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, that Jehu said to the guards and to the officers, "Go in and strike them down; let no man come out!" So they struck them down with the edge of the sword; and the guards and the officers cast them out, and went into the inner sanctuary of the house of Baal.

**10:26** And they brought out the sacred pillars of the house of Baal and burned them.

**10:27** They also broke down the sacred pillar of Baal, and tore down the house of Baal and turned it into a latrine to this day.

**10:28** Thus Jehu eradicated Baal from Israel.

**10:29** However, Jehu did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, with which he had made Israel sin—that is, the golden calves that were at Bethel and at Dan.

**10:30** And the LORD said to Jehu, "Because you have done well in carrying out what is right in My sight, and have done to the house of Ahab according to all that was in My heart, your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel."

**10:31** Yet Jehu did not take heed to walk in the law of the LORD God of Israel with all his heart; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, with which he had made Israel sin.

**10:32** In those days the LORD began to cut off parts of Israel; and Hazael struck them throughout all the territory of Israel:

**10:33** from the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, all the land of Gilead—the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the Manassites—from Aroer, which is by the Brook Arnon, including Gilead and Bashan.

**10:34** Now the rest of the acts of Jehu, all that he did, and all his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**10:35** So Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria. Then Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

**10:36** And the period that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty-eight years.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "rulers of Jezreel" (though the letters are sent to Samaria) and literally "the guardians, Ahab," referring to those who reared Ahab's sons.
- **10:12** — Beth Eked of the Shepherds literally means "the house of binding of the shepherds," likely a shearing station along the road from Jezreel to Samaria.
- **10:25** — "Inner sanctuary" translates a Hebrew word normally meaning "city" or "citadel," used here for the innermost chamber of Baal's temple.
- **10:27** — The Qere (read form) is a euphemism meaning "a latrine" (literally "places of going out"), replacing the coarser Ketiv (written form) meaning "a dung-house."
