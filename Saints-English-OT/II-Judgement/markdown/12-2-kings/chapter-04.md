## Text

**4:1** Now a certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant my husband is dead, and you know that your servant feared the LORD; and the creditor has come to take my two children for himself as slaves."

**4:2** So Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" And she said, "Your maidservant has nothing at all in the house except a flask of oil."

**4:3** Then he said, "Go, borrow vessels for yourself from outside, from all your neighbors—empty vessels; do not gather just a few.

**4:4** And you shall go in and shut the door behind yourself and behind your sons, and pour into all these vessels, and set aside each one that is full."

**4:5** So she departed from him and shut the door behind herself and behind her sons; they kept bringing the vessels to her, and she kept pouring out.

**4:6** And it came to pass, when the vessels were full, that she said to her son, "Bring me another vessel." And he said to her, "There is not another vessel." Then the oil ceased.

**4:7** Then she came and told the man of God. And he said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt; and you and your sons shall live on the rest."

**4:8** Now it came to pass one day that Elisha crossed over to Shunem, where there was a prominent woman, and she pressed him to eat bread. And so it was, as often as he passed by, he would turn aside there to eat bread.

**4:9** And she said to her husband, "Behold, now I know that he is a holy man of God who passes by us continually.

**4:10** Let us make, please, a small walled upper chamber, and let us place there for him a bed, a table, a chair, and a lampstand; and it shall be, whenever he comes to us, that he may turn aside there."

**4:11** And it came to pass one day that he came there, and he turned aside into the upper chamber and lay down there.

**4:12** Then he said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite woman." So he called her, and she stood before him.

**4:13** And he said to him, "Say now to her, 'Behold, you have shown all this earnest care for us. What is to be done for you? Would you have a word spoken for you to the king or to the commander of the army?'" And she answered, "I dwell in the midst of my own people."

**4:14** So he said, "What then is to be done for her?" And Gehazi answered, "Indeed, she has no son, and her husband is old."

**4:15** And he said, "Call her." When he had called her, she stood in the doorway.

**4:16** Then he said, "At this appointed season, about this time next year, you shall embrace a son." And she said, "No, my lord, man of God, do not lie to your maidservant!"

**4:17** And the woman conceived and bore a son at that appointed season, about that time the next year, just as Elisha had spoken to her.

**4:18** Now the child grew. And it came to pass one day that he went out to his father, to the reapers.

**4:19** And he said to his father, "My head, my head!" So he said to the servant, "Carry him to his mother."

**4:20** When he had carried him and brought him to his mother, he sat on her knees until noon, and then died.

**4:21** And she went up and laid him on the bed of the man of God, shut the door behind him, and went out.

**4:22** Then she called to her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, that I may run to the man of God and return."

**4:23** And he said, "Why are you going to him today? It is neither the New Moon nor the Sabbath." And she said, "It is well."

**4:24** Then she saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on and go forward; do not slacken the pace for me unless I tell you."

**4:25** So she departed and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. And it came to pass, when the man of God saw her from afar, that he said to his servant Gehazi, "Behold, there is that Shunammite woman!

**4:26** Please run now to meet her, and say to her, 'Is it well with you? Is it well with your husband? Is it well with the child?'" And she answered, "It is well."

**4:27** Now when she came to the man of God at the mountain, she caught hold of his feet; and Gehazi drew near to push her away. But the man of God said, "Leave her alone, for her soul is in bitter distress, and the LORD has hidden it from me and has not told me."

**4:28** Then she said, "Did I ask a son of my lord? Did I not say, 'Do not deceive me'?"

**4:29** Then he said to Gehazi, "Gird up your loins, and take my staff in your hand, and go. If you meet anyone, do not greet him; and if anyone greets you, do not answer him; and lay my staff upon the face of the boy."

**4:30** And the mother of the boy said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So he arose and followed her.

**4:31** Now Gehazi went on ahead of them and laid the staff upon the face of the boy, but there was neither voice nor response. Therefore he returned to meet him and told him, saying, "The boy has not awakened."

**4:32** When Elisha came into the house, behold, the boy was dead, laid upon his bed.

**4:33** So he went in, shut the door behind the two of them, and prayed to the LORD.

**4:34** And he went up and lay upon the child, and put his mouth upon his mouth, his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands; and he stretched himself out upon him, and the flesh of the child grew warm.

**4:35** Then he returned and walked back and forth in the house, once this way and once that way, and went up and stretched himself out upon him; and the boy sneezed seven times, and the boy opened his eyes.

**4:36** And he called Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite woman." So he called her. And when she came in to him, he said, "Take up your son."

**4:37** So she went in, fell at his feet, and bowed herself to the ground; then she took up her son and went out.

**4:38** And Elisha returned to Gilgal, and there was a famine in the land. Now the sons of the prophets were sitting before him, and he said to his servant, "Set on the large pot, and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

**4:39** So one went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a wild vine, and gathered from it his garment full of wild gourds, and came and sliced them into the pot of stew, for they did not know what they were.

**4:40** Then they poured it out for the men to eat. Now it came to pass, as they were eating of the stew, that they cried out and said, "Man of God, there is death in the pot!" And they could not eat it.

**4:41** So he said, "Then bring some flour." And he cast it into the pot, and said, "Pour it out for the people, that they may eat." And there was nothing harmful in the pot.

**4:42** Now a man came from Baal-shalishah and brought the man of God bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley bread, and fresh ears of grain in his sack. And he said, "Give it to the people, that they may eat."

**4:43** But his attendant said, "What, shall I set this before a hundred men?" He said again, "Give it to the people, that they may eat; for thus says the LORD, 'They shall eat and have some left over.'"

**4:44** So he set it before them; and they ate and had some left over, according to the word of the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Here and in 4:3, 4:7, 4:16, and 4:23, the ketiv preserves archaic second-person feminine singular forms, while the qere provides the standard Masoretic forms.
- **4:5** — Qere reads a feminine participle form meaning pouring out; ketiv preserves a variant spelling of the same verb with identical sense.
- **4:16** — Literally at this appointed season, as the time of life, a Hebrew idiom denoting the corresponding time in the coming year.
- **4:42** — The Hebrew noun rendered sack occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and refers to a pouch or bag for carrying grain.
