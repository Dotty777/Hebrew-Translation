## Text

**3:1** Now Jehoram the son of Ahab became king over Israel at Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

**3:2** And he did evil in the sight of the LORD, though not like his father and his mother; for he removed the sacred pillar of Baal that his father had made.

**3:3** Nevertheless he clung to the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who had made Israel sin; he did not depart from them.

**3:4** Now Mesha king of Moab was a sheepbreeder, and he regularly paid the king of Israel one hundred thousand lambs and the wool of one hundred thousand rams.

**3:5** But it came to pass, when Ahab died, that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

**3:6** So King Jehoram went out of Samaria on that day and mustered all Israel.

**3:7** Then he went and sent word to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me against Moab to battle?" And he said, "I will go up; I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses."

**3:8** And he said, "Which way shall we go up?" And he answered, "By way of the Wilderness of Edom."

**3:9** So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom went, and made a circuit of a seven days' journey; and there was no water for the camp nor for the beasts that followed them.

**3:10** And the king of Israel said, "Alas! For the LORD has called these three kings together to deliver them into the hand of Moab."

**3:11** But Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no prophet of the LORD here, that we may inquire of the LORD through him?" Then one of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, "Elisha the son of Shaphat is here, who poured water upon the hands of Elijah."

**3:12** And Jehoshaphat said, "The word of the LORD is with him." So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat and the king of Edom went down to him.

**3:13** Then Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What have I to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother." But the king of Israel said to him, "No, for the LORD has called these three kings together to deliver them into the hand of Moab."

**3:14** And Elisha said, "As the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, surely were it not that I have regard for the presence of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would not look toward you nor see you.

**3:15** But now bring me a musician." And it came to pass, as the musician played, that the hand of the LORD came upon him.

**3:16** And he said, "Thus says the LORD: Make this valley full of trenches.

**3:17** For thus says the LORD: You shall not see wind, nor shall you see rain; yet that valley shall be filled with water, so that you, your livestock, and your beasts shall drink.

**3:18** And this is a light thing in the sight of the LORD; He will also deliver Moab into your hand.

**3:19** And you shall strike every fortified city and every choice city, and you shall fell every good tree, and stop up every spring of water, and mar every good plot of land with stones."

**3:20** Now it came to pass in the morning, when the grain offering was offered, that behold, water came by way of Edom, and the land was filled with water.

**3:21** Now all Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight against them; and all who were able to gird on armor and older were summoned, and they stood at the border.

**3:22** And they rose early in the morning, and the sun shone upon the water; and the Moabites saw the water opposite them as red as blood.

**3:23** And they said, "This is blood! The kings have surely destroyed one another, and each man has struck down his neighbor. Now therefore, Moab, to the spoil!"

**3:24** So when they came to the camp of Israel, Israel rose up and struck the Moabites, so that they fled before them; and they struck into the land, even striking down Moab.

**3:25** And they tore down the cities, and upon every good plot of land each man cast his stone and filled it; and they stopped up every spring of water and felled every good tree, until only its stones were left in Kir Haraseth. And the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

**3:26** And when the king of Moab saw that the battle was too fierce for him, he took with him seven hundred men who drew the sword, to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

**3:27** Then he took his eldest son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him as a burnt offering upon the wall. And there was great wrath against Israel; so they departed from him and returned to their own land.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — Mesha king of Moab is also attested in the ninth-century BC Mesha Stele, which records his rebellion against the house of Omri from a Moabite perspective.
- **3:16** — "Full of trenches" renders the Hebrew repetition "trenches, trenches," an idiom denoting many water-catching pits dug across the dry valley floor.
- **3:24** — Ketiv/Qere: the translation follows the qere, "and they struck into it," referring to the land of Moab; the ketiv writes "and they entered into it."
- **3:27** — "Great wrath against Israel" translates a terse Hebrew phrase understood either as fierce Moabite fury provoked by the king's sacrifice or as divine displeasure leading to Israel's withdrawal.
