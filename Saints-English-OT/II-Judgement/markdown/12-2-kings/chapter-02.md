## Text

**2:1** And it came to pass, when the LORD was about to take Elijah up into heaven by a whirlwind, that Elijah and Elisha went from Gilgal.

**2:2** Then Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, for the LORD has sent me as far as Bethel." But Elisha said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

**2:3** And the sons of the prophets who were at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the LORD is taking away your master from over your head?" And he said, "I also know it; keep silent."

**2:4** Then Elijah said to him, "Elisha, please stay here, for the LORD has sent me to Jericho." And he said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

**2:5** And the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho drew near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the LORD is taking away your master from over your head?" And he answered, "I also know it; keep silent."

**2:6** Then Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, for the LORD has sent me to the Jordan." And he said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

**2:7** And fifty men of the sons of the prophets went and stood opposite them at a distance, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

**2:8** Now Elijah took his mantle, rolled it up, and struck the waters; and they were divided this way and that, so that the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

**2:9** And it came to pass, when they had crossed over, that Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I may do for you, before I am taken away from you." And Elisha said, "Please let a double portion of your spirit be upon me."

**2:10** So he said, "You have asked a hard thing. If you see me when I am taken from you, it shall be so for you; but if not, it shall not be so."

**2:11** And it came to pass, as they walked on and talked, that behold, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them; and Elijah ascended by a whirlwind into heaven.

**2:12** And Elisha watched and cried out, "My father, my father, the chariot of Israel and its horsemen!" And he saw him no more. Then he took hold of his own garments and tore them into two pieces.

**2:13** He also lifted up the mantle of Elijah that had fallen from him, and returned and stood by the bank of the Jordan.

**2:14** Then he took the mantle of Elijah that had fallen from him and struck the waters, and said, "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" And when he also had struck the waters, they were divided this way and that, and Elisha crossed over.

**2:15** And when the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho saw him opposite them, they said, "The spirit of Elijah rests upon Elisha." And they came to meet him and bowed down to the ground before him.

**2:16** Then they said to him, "Behold now, there are with your servants fifty valiant men; please let them go and search for your master, lest the Spirit of the LORD has taken him up and cast him upon one of the mountains or into one of the valleys." And he said, "You shall not send them."

**2:17** Yet they urged him until he was ashamed, so he said, "Send them." Therefore they sent fifty men, and they searched for three days, but did not find him.

**2:18** And when they returned to him, while he was staying at Jericho, he said to them, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not go'?"

**2:19** Then the men of the city said to Elisha, "Behold now, the site of the city is good, as my lord sees; but the waters are bad, and the land is barren."

**2:20** And he said, "Bring me a new bowl, and put salt in it." So they brought it to him.

**2:21** Then he went out to the spring of the waters and cast salt there, and said, "Thus says the LORD: I have healed these waters; from them there shall be no more death or barrenness."

**2:22** So the waters were healed to this day, according to the word of Elisha which he spoke.

**2:23** Then he went up from there to Bethel; and as he was going up along the way, young lads came out from the city and mocked him, and said to him, "Go up, you baldhead! Go up, you baldhead!"

**2:24** So he turned behind him and saw them, and cursed them in the name of the LORD. And two female bears came out of the woods and mauled forty-two of the youths.

**2:25** Then he went from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **2:9** — "A double portion" reflects the firstborn's legal inheritance share (Deuteronomy 21:17), expressing Elisha's request to succeed Elijah as his chief prophetic heir.
- **2:16** — Ketiv/Qere: the ketiv writes an alternative plural spelling of "the valleys," while the qere reads the standard vocalized plural form translated here.
- **2:19** — "Barren" renders a Hebrew participle meaning literally "bereaving" or "causing miscarriage and unfruitfulness," repeated in verse 21.
