## Text

**12:1** Jehoash was seven years old when he began to reign.

**12:2** In the seventh year of Jehu, Jehoash began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.

**12:3** And Jehoash did what was right in the sight of the LORD all his days in which Jehoiada the priest instructed him.

**12:4** Yet the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**12:5** And Jehoash said to the priests, "All the money of the consecrated offerings that is brought into the house of the LORD—the assessment money for each man, the money for each person's valuation, and all the money that comes into a man's heart to bring into the house of the LORD—

**12:6** let the priests receive it for themselves, each from his acquaintance, and let them repair the breaches of the house, wherever any breach is found."

**12:7** Now it came to pass, in the twenty-third year of King Jehoash, that the priests had not repaired the breaches of the house.

**12:8** So King Jehoash called Jehoiada the priest and the priests, and said to them, "Why are you not repairing the breaches of the house? Now therefore, do not take money from your acquaintances, but give it for the breaches of the house."

**12:9** And the priests consented neither to receive money from the people nor to repair the breaches of the house.

**12:10** Then Jehoiada the priest took a chest, bored a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar, on the right side as one enters the house of the LORD; and the priests who guarded the threshold put into it all the money that was brought into the house of the LORD.

**12:11** And it came to pass, whenever they saw that there was much money in the chest, that the king's scribe and the high priest went up, put it in bags, and counted the money that was found in the house of the LORD.

**12:12** Then they gave the money that had been weighed out into the hands of those who did the work, who had the oversight of the house of the LORD; and they paid it out to the carpenters and builders who worked on the house of the LORD,

**12:13** and to the masons and stonecutters, and for buying timber and hewn stone to repair the breaches of the house of the LORD, and for all that was spent on the house to repair it.

**12:14** However, there were not made for the house of the LORD silver basins, snuffers, bowls, trumpets, or any vessels of gold or silver from the money brought into the house of the LORD.

**12:15** For they gave it to those who did the work, and with it they repaired the house of the LORD.

**12:16** Moreover, they did not ask for an accounting from the men into whose hands they delivered the money to pay the workmen, for they dealt faithfully.

**12:17** The money from trespass offerings and the money from sin offerings was not brought into the house of the LORD; it belonged to the priests.

**12:18** At that time Hazael king of Syria went up, fought against Gath, and captured it; then Hazael set his face to go up against Jerusalem.

**12:19** And Jehoash king of Judah took all the consecrated things that his fathers—Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, kings of Judah—had dedicated, along with his own consecrated things and all the gold found in the treasuries of the house of the LORD and of the king's house, and sent them to Hazael king of Syria. So he withdrew from Jerusalem.

**12:20** Now the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**12:21** And his servants arose and formed a conspiracy, and struck Joash down at Beth Millo, on the way down to Silla.

**12:22** For Jozachar the son of Shimeath and Jehozabad the son of Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died. And they buried him with his fathers in the City of David, and Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification, chapter 12 begins with this verse, which appears as 11:21 in many English Bibles; consequently, the remaining verses of chapter 12 are numbered one higher than in those versions.
- **12:5** — Hebrew kesef over, literally money of one passing over, refers either to the census assessment paid by each man who passed the numbering (Exodus 30:13) or to current silver.
- **12:10** — The Hebrew Qere reads miyyamin (on the right side), whereas the Ketiv reads beyamin (at the right side); the translation follows the Qere.
- **12:12** — Two Ketiv/Qere variants occur here: the Qere reads yedei (hands) against Ketiv yad (hand), and hammufqadim (who had the oversight, Hophal participle) against Ketiv happequdim; the translation follows the Qere in both places.
- **12:18** — The Hebrew name Aram is rendered traditionally as Syria throughout chapters 12 and 13.
