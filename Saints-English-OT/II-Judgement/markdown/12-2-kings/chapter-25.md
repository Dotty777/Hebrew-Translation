## Text

**25:1** Now it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came, he and all his army, against Jerusalem, and encamped against it; and they built a siege wall against it all around.

**25:2** So the city came under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.

**25:3** On the ninth day of the month, the famine grew severe in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.

**25:4** Then the city was breached, and all the men of war fled by night by way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the king's garden, while the Chaldeans were around the city; and he went by the way of the Arabah.

**25:5** Yet the forces of the Chaldeans pursued after the king and overtook him in the plains of Jericho, and all his army dispersed from him.

**25:6** So they seized the king and brought him up to the king of Babylon at Riblah, and they pronounced judgment upon him.

**25:7** And they slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes; then he put out the eyes of Zedekiah, bound him with bronze fetters, and brought him to Babylon.

**25:8** Now in the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month—which was the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon—Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, a servant of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.

**25:9** And he burned the house of the LORD and the house of the king, and all the houses of Jerusalem; even every great house he burned with fire.

**25:11** And the rest of the people who remained in the city, and the deserters who had deserted to the king of Babylon, along with the rest of the multitude, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away into exile.

**25:12** But the captain of the guard left some of the poorest of the land to be vinedressers and plowmen.

**25:13** Moreover, the Chaldeans broke in pieces the pillars of bronze that were in the house of the LORD, and the stands and the bronze sea that were in the house of the LORD, and carried their bronze to Babylon.

**25:14** They also took the pots, the shovels, the trimmers, the ladles, and all the vessels of bronze with which they ministered.

**25:15** And the firepans and the basins—what was of gold as gold, and what was of silver as silver—the captain of the guard took away.

**25:16** As for the two pillars, the one sea, and the stands which Solomon had made for the house of the LORD, the bronze of all these vessels could not be weighed.

**25:17** The height of the one pillar was eighteen cubits, and upon it was a capital of bronze; and the height of the capital was three cubits, with latticework and pomegranates upon the capital all around, all of bronze. And the second pillar had the same, with latticework.

**25:18** Then the captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and the three keepers of the threshold.

**25:19** And from the city he took one officer who was appointed over the men of war, and five men of those who saw the king's face who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the army who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the city.

**25:20** So Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took them and brought them to the king of Babylon at Riblah.

**25:21** And the king of Babylon struck them down and put them to death at Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus Judah was carried away into exile from its own land.

**25:22** Now over the people who remained in the land of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had left, he appointed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan.

**25:23** And when all the commanders of the forces, they and the men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah, they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah—Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, Johanan the son of Kareah, Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah the son of the Maacathite, they and their men.

**25:24** And Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, and said to them, "Do not be afraid of the servants of the Chaldeans. Dwell in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall be well with you."

**25:25** Now it came to pass in the seventh month that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, of the royal seed, came, and ten men with him, and they struck down Gedaliah so that he died, along with the Jews and the Chaldeans who were with him at Mizpah.

**25:26** Then all the people, from the least to the greatest, and the commanders of the forces arose and went to Egypt, for they were afraid of the Chaldeans.

**25:27** Now it came to pass in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, that Evil-Merodach king of Babylon, in the year that he began to reign, lifted up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah out of prison.

**25:28** And he spoke kindly to him, and set his throne above the thrones of the kings who were with him in Babylon.

**25:29** So he changed his prison garments, and ate bread continually before him all the days of his life.

**25:30** And as for his allowance, a continual allowance was given to him from the king, a portion for each day, all the days of his life.

## Translator Notes

- **25:4** — The Hebrew text omits the verb before "by night," which is supplied as "fled" from the context, and shifts to a singular verb ("he went") referring to King Zedekiah.
- **25:6** — Literally "they spoke judgment with him," a Hebrew idiom for holding a judicial hearing and pronouncing formal sentence.
- **25:17** — The Masoretic qere reads the plural "cubits," whereas the written ketiv has the singular "cubit."
- **25:27** — "Lifted up the head" is a Hebrew idiom meaning to restore a prisoner to favor and grant release.
