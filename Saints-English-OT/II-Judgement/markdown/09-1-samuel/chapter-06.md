## Text

**6:1** Now the ark of the LORD was in the territory of the Philistines seven months.

**6:2** And the Philistines called for the priests and the diviners, saying, "What shall we do with the ark of the LORD? Make known to us with what we shall send it to its place."

**6:3** And they said, "If you send away the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it empty; rather, by all means return a guilt offering to Him. Then you shall be healed, and it will be made known to you why His hand has not turned away from you."

**6:4** Then they said, "What is the guilt offering that we shall return to Him?" And they answered, "According to the number of the lords of the Philistines: five golden tumors and five golden mice, for one plague was upon all of them and upon your lords.

**6:5** Therefore you shall make likenesses of your tumors and likenesses of your mice that are ravaging the land, and you shall give glory to the God of Israel; perhaps He will lighten His hand from you, from your gods, and from your land.

**6:6** Why then should you harden your hearts, as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? When He had dealt severely with them, did they not let them go, and they departed?

**6:7** Now therefore, take and make one new cart, and take two milk cows on which no yoke has ever come; and hitch the cows to the cart, and take their calves back home, away from following them.

**6:8** Then take the ark of the LORD and set it on the cart, and put the articles of gold which you are returning to Him as a guilt offering in a chest beside it; then send it away, and let it go.

**6:9** And watch: if it goes up by the way of its own border to Beth Shemesh, then He has done us this great evil. But if not, then we shall know that it was not His hand that struck us; it happened to us by chance."

**6:10** So the men did this: they took two milk cows and hitched them to the cart, and shut up their calves at home.

**6:11** And they set the ark of the LORD on the cart, and the chest with the golden mice and the likenesses of their tumors.

**6:12** Then the cows went straight along the way toward Beth Shemesh; they walked along the one highway, lowing as they went, and did not turn aside to the right or to the left. And the lords of the Philistines walked after them to the border of Beth Shemesh.

**6:13** Now the people of Beth Shemesh were reaping the wheat harvest in the valley; and they lifted up their eyes and saw the ark, and rejoiced to see it.

**6:14** Then the cart came into the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh and stood still there, where a large stone was. And they split the wood of the cart and offered the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**6:15** And the Levites took down the ark of the LORD and the chest that was with it, in which were the articles of gold, and set them upon the large stone. And the men of Beth Shemesh offered burnt offerings and sacrificed sacrifices that day to the LORD.

**6:16** And when the five lords of the Philistines saw it, they returned to Ekron the same day.

**6:17** Now these are the golden tumors which the Philistines returned as a guilt offering to the LORD: one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, and one for Ekron;

**6:18** and the golden mice, according to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords, from fortified city to unwalled village, even as far as Great Abel, on which they set down the ark of the LORD, which remains to this day in the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh.

**6:19** And He struck the men of Beth Shemesh, because they had looked into the ark of the LORD; He struck among the people seventy men, fifty thousand men. And the people mourned because the LORD had struck the people with a great slaughter.

**6:20** And the men of Beth Shemesh said, "Who is able to stand before the LORD, this holy God? And to whom shall He go up from us?"

**6:21** So they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath Jearim, saying, "The Philistines have brought back the ark of the LORD; come down and take it up to you."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — Here and in verse five, the Qere reads techorim, meaning tumors or growths, whereas the Ketiv reads ophelim, meaning swellings or tumors.
- **6:18** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads Avel haggedolah, rendered Great Abel, whereas verses fourteen and fifteen refer to a large stone, even haggedolah.
- **6:19** — The Masoretic Hebrew places seventy men and fifty thousand men side by side without a conjunction, a famously compressed numerical expression.
