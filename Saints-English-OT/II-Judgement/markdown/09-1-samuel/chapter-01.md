## Text

**1:1** Now there was a certain man of Ramathaim Zophim, from the hill country of Ephraim, and his name was Elkanah the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephraimite.

**1:2** And he had two wives: the name of the one was Hannah, and the name of the second was Peninnah. Peninnah had children, yet Hannah had no children.

**1:3** Now this man went up from his city year by year to worship and to sacrifice to the LORD of hosts in Shiloh. And there the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests to the LORD.

**1:4** And whenever the day came that Elkanah offered sacrifice, he would give portions to Peninnah his wife and to all her sons and her daughters.

**1:5** Yet to Hannah he would give a double portion, for he loved Hannah, though the LORD had closed her womb.

**1:6** And her rival also provoked her bitterly to vex her, because the LORD had shut up her womb.

**1:7** So he did year after year: as often as she went up to the house of the LORD, thus she provoked her; therefore she wept and would not eat.

**1:8** Then Elkanah her husband said to her, "Hannah, why do you weep? And why do you not eat? And why is your heart grieved? Am I not better to you than ten sons?"

**1:9** So Hannah rose up after eating in Shiloh, and after drinking. Now Eli the priest was sitting upon the seat by the doorpost of the temple of the LORD.

**1:10** And she was in bitterness of soul, and prayed to the LORD and wept bitterly.

**1:11** Then she vowed a vow and said, "O LORD of hosts, if You will indeed look upon the affliction of Your maidservant and remember me, and not forget Your maidservant, and will give to Your maidservant a male child, then I will give him to the LORD all the days of his life, and no razor shall come upon his head."

**1:12** And it came to pass, as she continued praying before the LORD, that Eli watched her mouth.

**1:13** Now Hannah was speaking in her heart; only her lips moved, yet her voice was not heard. Therefore Eli thought her to be drunk.

**1:14** So Eli said to her, "How long will you be drunk? Put your wine away from you!"

**1:15** And Hannah answered and said, "No, my lord, I am a woman of a sorrowful spirit. I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink, yet I have poured out my soul before the LORD.

**1:16** Do not regard your maidservant as a worthless woman, for out of the abundance of my complaint and my grief I have spoken until now."

**1:17** Then Eli answered and said, "Go in peace, and may the God of Israel grant your petition which you have asked of Him."

**1:18** And she said, "Let your handmaid find favor in your sight." So the woman went her way and ate, and her countenance was no longer downcast.

**1:19** Then they rose early in the morning and worshiped before the LORD, and returned and came to their house in Ramah. And Elkanah knew Hannah his wife, and the LORD remembered her.

**1:20** And it came to pass in the course of time that Hannah conceived and bore a son; and she called his name Samuel, saying, "Because I have asked for him from the LORD."

**1:21** Now the man Elkanah and all his household went up to offer to the LORD the yearly sacrifice and his vow.

**1:22** Yet Hannah did not go up, for she said to her husband, "Not until the child is weaned; then I will bring him, that he may appear before the LORD and abide there forever."

**1:23** So Elkanah her husband said to her, "Do what is good in your eyes; remain until you have weaned him. Only may the LORD establish His word." Thus the woman remained and nursed her son until she weaned him.

**1:24** And when she had weaned him, she brought him up with her, with three bulls, and one ephah of flour, and a skin of wine, and brought him to the house of the LORD in Shiloh; and the child was yet young.

**1:25** Then they slaughtered the bull, and brought the child to Eli.

**1:26** And she said, "Oh, my lord! As your soul lives, my lord, I am the woman who stood by you here, praying to the LORD.

**1:27** For this child I prayed, and the LORD has granted me my petition which I asked of Him.

**1:28** Therefore I also have lent him to the LORD; all the days that he lives he is lent to the LORD." And he worshiped the LORD there.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — The Hebrew phrase rendered "a double portion" is literally "one portion of two faces (appayim)," traditionally understood as a double or especially generous share.
- **1:16** — Hebrew "daughter of Belial" (literally "worthlessness" or "wickedness"), an idiom for a base or wicked person; compare "worthless men" in 2:12.
- **1:20** — Hannah connects the name Samuel with the Hebrew verb shaal ("to ask"), a wordplay continued in 1:27-28 where the same root also conveys "to lend" or "dedicate."
- **1:24** — Translating the Masoretic Hebrew "three bulls" strictly; the Septuagint reads "a three-year-old bull."
