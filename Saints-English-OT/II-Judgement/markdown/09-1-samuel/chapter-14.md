## Text

**14:1** Now it happened one day that Jonathan the son of Saul said to the young man who bore his armor, "Come, and let us cross over to the garrison of the Philistines that is on the other side." Yet he did not tell his father.

**14:2** And Saul was staying at the outskirts of Gibeah under the pomegranate tree that is in Migron, and the people who were with him were about six hundred men.

**14:3** And Ahijah the son of Ahitub, the brother of Ichabod, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli, the priest of the LORD in Shiloh, was bearing an ephod. And the people did not know that Jonathan had departed.

**14:4** Now between the passes by which Jonathan sought to cross over to the garrison of the Philistines, there was a crag of rock on one side and a crag of rock on the other side; and the name of the one was Bozez, and the name of the other was Seneh.

**14:5** The one crag rose steep on the north opposite Michmash, and the other on the south opposite Geba.

**14:6** Then Jonathan said to the young man who bore his armor, "Come, and let us cross over to the garrison of these uncircumcised men; perhaps the LORD will act for us, for there is no restraint upon the LORD to save by many or by few."

**14:7** And his armor-bearer said to him, "Do all that is in your heart; turn as you will. Behold, I am with you according to your heart."

**14:8** Then Jonathan said, "Behold, we are crossing over to the men, and we will show ourselves to them.

**14:9** If they say thus to us, 'Stand still until we come to you,' then we will stand in our place and will not go up to them.

**14:10** But if they say thus, 'Come up to us,' then we will go up, for the LORD has delivered them into our hand; and this shall be the sign for us."

**14:11** So both of them showed themselves to the garrison of the Philistines. And the Philistines said, "Behold, Hebrews are coming out of the holes where they have hidden themselves!"

**14:12** And the men of the garrison answered Jonathan and his armor-bearer and said, "Come up to us, and we will make something known to you." Then Jonathan said to his armor-bearer, "Go up after me, for the LORD has delivered them into the hand of Israel."

**14:13** So Jonathan climbed up upon his hands and upon his feet, with his armor-bearer after him; and they fell before Jonathan, while his armor-bearer put them to death behind him.

**14:14** And that first slaughter which Jonathan and his armor-bearer struck down was about twenty men, within about half a furrow of a yoke of field.

**14:15** And there was trembling in the camp, in the field, and among all the people; the garrison and the raiding band also trembled, and the earth quaked, so that it became a terror from God.

**14:16** Now the watchmen of Saul in Gibeah of Benjamin looked, and behold, the multitude was melting away and scattering to and fro.

**14:17** Then Saul said to the people who were with him, "Take a count now and see who has gone from among us." And when they had taken a count, behold, Jonathan and his armor-bearer were not there.

**14:18** And Saul said to Ahijah, "Bring near the ark of God." For the ark of God was on that day with the children of Israel.

**14:19** And it came to pass while Saul was speaking to the priest that the tumult in the camp of the Philistines grew more and more; therefore Saul said to the priest, "Withdraw your hand."

**14:20** Then Saul and all the people who were with him rallied together and came to the battle; and behold, every man's sword was against his fellow, in a very great confusion.

**14:21** Moreover, the Hebrews who had been with the Philistines before that time, who had gone up with them into the camp from the surrounding country, even they also turned to be with Israel, who were with Saul and Jonathan.

**14:22** Likewise all the men of Israel who had hidden themselves in the hill country of Ephraim heard that the Philistines had fled, and they also pressed hard after them in the battle.

**14:23** So the LORD saved Israel on that day, and the battle passed beyond Beth Aven.

**14:24** Now the men of Israel were hard-pressed on that day, for Saul had bound the people with an oath, saying, "Cursed be the man who eats any food until evening, and until I have avenged myself on my enemies." So none of the people tasted any food.

**14:25** And all the people of the land entered a forest, and there was honey upon the surface of the field.

**14:26** And when the people came into the forest, behold, honey was flowing; yet no one brought his hand to his mouth, for the people feared the oath.

**14:27** But Jonathan had not heard when his father bound the people with the oath; therefore he stretched out the tip of the staff that was in his hand and dipped it in a honeycomb, and brought his hand to his mouth, and his eyes brightened.

**14:28** Then one of the people spoke up and said, "Your father strictly bound the people with an oath, saying, 'Cursed be the man who eats food this day.'" And the people were faint.

**14:29** Then Jonathan said, "My father has troubled the land. Look now, how my eyes have brightened because I tasted a little of this honey.

**14:30** How much more if the people had eaten freely today of the spoil of their enemies which they found! For now would not the slaughter among the Philistines have been much greater?"

**14:31** Now they struck down the Philistines on that day from Michmash to Aijalon, and the people were exceedingly faint.

**14:32** And the people rushed upon the spoil, and took sheep, oxen, and calves, and slaughtered them on the ground; and the people ate them with the blood.

**14:33** Then they told Saul, saying, "Behold, the people are sinning against the LORD by eating with the blood." And he said, "You have dealt treacherously; roll a large stone to me today."

**14:34** Then Saul said, "Disperse among the people and say to them, 'Bring near to me every man his ox and every man his sheep, and slaughter them here and eat; and do not sin against the LORD by eating with the blood.'" So all the people brought every man his ox by his hand that night and slaughtered them there.

**14:35** Then Saul built an altar to the LORD; this was the first altar that he built to the LORD.

**14:36** And Saul said, "Let us go down after the Philistines by night and plunder them until the light of morning, and let us not leave a man among them." And they said, "Do all that is good in your eyes." Then the priest said, "Let us draw near here to God."

**14:37** So Saul inquired of God, "Shall I go down after the Philistines? Will You deliver them into the hand of Israel?" But He did not answer him on that day.

**14:38** Then Saul said, "Come near here, all you leaders of the people, and know and see how this sin has happened today.

**14:39** For as the LORD lives, who saves Israel, though it be in Jonathan my son, he shall surely die." Yet there was not one among all the people who answered him.

**14:40** Then he said to all Israel, "You shall be on one side, and I and Jonathan my son will be on the other side." And the people said to Saul, "Do what is good in your eyes."

**14:41** Therefore Saul said to the LORD God of Israel, "Give a true lot." And Jonathan and Saul were taken, while the people went free.

**14:42** And Saul said, "Cast lots between me and Jonathan my son." And Jonathan was taken.

**14:43** Then Saul said to Jonathan, "Tell me what you have done." And Jonathan told him and said, "I indeed tasted a little honey with the tip of the staff that was in my hand; behold, I must die."

**14:44** And Saul said, "May God do so and more also; for you shall surely die, Jonathan."

**14:45** But the people said to Saul, "Shall Jonathan die, who has accomplished this great salvation in Israel? Far be it! As the LORD lives, not a single hair of his head shall fall to the ground, for he has worked with God this day." So the people ransomed Jonathan, and he did not die.

**14:46** Then Saul went up from pursuing the Philistines, and the Philistines went to their own place.

**14:47** So Saul secured the kingship over Israel, and fought against all his enemies on every side: against Moab, against the children of Ammon, against Edom, against the kings of Zobah, and against the Philistines; and wherever he turned, he inflicted defeat.

**14:48** And he acted valiantly, and struck down Amalek, and delivered Israel from the hand of those who plundered them.

**14:49** Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchishua; and the names of his two daughters were these: the name of the firstborn was Merab, and the name of the younger was Michal.

**14:50** And the name of Saul's wife was Ahinoam the daughter of Ahimaaz; and the name of the commander of his army was Abner the son of Ner, Saul's uncle.

**14:51** And Kish was the father of Saul, and Ner the father of Abner was the son of Abiel.

**14:52** Now there was fierce war against the Philistines all the days of Saul; and whenever Saul saw any mighty man or any valiant man, he gathered him to himself.

## Translator Notes

- **14:14** — The Hebrew expression translated "half a furrow of a yoke of field" refers to a small plot of ground, roughly half an acre, measured by what a yoke of oxen could plow.
- **14:27** — Following the Masoretic Qere "brightened" (restored in strength and vitality); the Ketiv reads "saw."
- **14:32** — Following the Masoretic Qere "rushed" and "the spoil"; the Ketiv reads "acted" (or "made") and "spoil" without the definite article.
- **14:41** — The phrase "Give a true lot" translates the concise Masoretic Hebrew literally reading "Give perfection," a solemn petition for an unerring verdict by lot.
