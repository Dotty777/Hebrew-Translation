## Text

**9:1** Now there was a man of Benjamin whose name was Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Bechorath, the son of Aphiah, the son of a Benjamite, a mighty man of valor.

**9:2** And he had a son whose name was Saul, a choice young man and handsome; there was not a man among the children of Israel more handsome than he. From his shoulders upward he was taller than all the people.

**9:3** Now the donkeys of Kish, Saul's father, were lost. And Kish said to his son Saul, "Please take one of the young servants with you, and arise, go search for the donkeys."

**9:4** So he passed through the hill country of Ephraim and passed through the land of Shalishah, but they did not find them. Then they passed through the land of Shaalim, and they were not there. Then he passed through the land of the Benjamites, yet they did not find them.

**9:5** When they had come to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant who was with him, "Come, let us return, lest my father cease caring about the donkeys and become anxious for us."

**9:6** And he said to him, "Behold, now, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is held in honor; all that he speaks surely comes to pass. Now let us go there; perhaps he will tell us concerning our journey on which we have come."

**9:7** Then Saul said to his servant, "Behold, if we go, what shall we bring the man? For the bread is gone from our vessels, and there is no gift to bring to the man of God. What do we have?"

**9:8** And the servant answered Saul again and said, "Behold, there is found in my hand a fourth of a shekel of silver. I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."

**9:9** Formerly in Israel, thus a man would say when he went to inquire of God, "Come, let us go to the seer," for he who is called a prophet today was formerly called a seer.

**9:10** Then Saul said to his servant, "Your word is good; come, let us go." So they went to the city where the man of God was.

**9:11** As they were going up the ascent to the city, they encountered young women going out to draw water, and they said to them, "Is the seer in this place?"

**9:12** And they answered them and said, "He is; behold, he is right ahead of you. Make haste now, for today he has come to the city, because the people have a sacrifice today on the high place.

**9:13** As soon as you enter the city, you will surely find him before he goes up to the high place to eat; for the people will not eat until he comes, because he must bless the sacrifice, and afterward those who are invited will eat. Now therefore go up, for about this time you will find him."

**9:14** So they went up to the city. As they were coming into the midst of the city, behold, Samuel was coming out toward them to go up to the high place.

**9:15** Now the LORD had revealed in Samuel's ear one day before Saul came, saying,

**9:16** "Tomorrow about this time I will send to you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him as prince over My people Israel; and he shall save My people from the hand of the Philistines. For I have looked upon My people, because their cry has come to Me."

**9:17** And when Samuel saw Saul, the LORD answered him, "Behold the man of whom I spoke to you! This one shall govern My people."

**9:18** Then Saul drew near to Samuel in the midst of the gate and said, "Please tell me, where is the house of the seer?"

**9:19** And Samuel answered Saul and said, "I am the seer. Go up before me to the high place, for you shall eat with me today; and in the morning I will let you go and will declare to you all that is in your heart.

**9:20** And as for your donkeys that were lost three days ago, do not set your heart upon them, for they have been found. And for whom is all that is desirable in Israel? Is it not for you and for all your father's house?"

**9:21** And Saul answered and said, "Am I not a Benjamite, of the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and my family the least of all the families of the tribe of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me after this manner?"

**9:22** Then Samuel took Saul and his servant and brought them into the hall, and gave them a place at the head of those who were invited, who were about thirty men.

**9:23** And Samuel said to the cook, "Bring the portion which I gave you, of which I said to you, 'Set it apart with you.'"

**9:24** So the cook lifted up the thigh with what was upon it and set it before Saul. And he said, "Behold what was reserved; it is set before you. Eat, for it has been kept for you until the appointed time, since I said, 'I have invited the people.'" So Saul ate with Samuel on that day.

**9:25** And when they had come down from the high place into the city, he spoke with Saul upon the roof.

**9:26** And they arose early; and it came to pass about the breaking of the dawn that Samuel called to Saul up to the roof, saying, "Arise, that I may send you on your way." So Saul arose, and both of them went outside, he and Samuel.

**9:27** As they were going down to the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the servant to pass on ahead of us." And he passed on. "But as for you, stand still now, that I may make you hear the word of God."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Following the Qere, "of Benjamin" is read as a single tribal name, whereas the Ketiv divides it into two words ("from Ben-yamin"). The Hebrew phrase rendered "a mighty man of valor" denotes both personal strength and social standing or wealth.
- **9:15** — "Revealed in Samuel's ear" reflects the Hebrew idiom "uncovered the ear of Samuel," denoting a private disclosure from the LORD.
- **9:26** — Following the Qere, "up to the roof" includes the Hebrew directional ending toward the rooftop, whereas the Ketiv reads simply "the roof."
