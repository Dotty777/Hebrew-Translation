## Text

**30:1** Now it came to pass, when David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, that the Amalekites had raided the Negev and Ziklag, and had struck Ziklag and burned it with fire.

**30:2** And they had taken captive the women who were in it, both small and great; they did not put anyone to death, but drove them away and went on their way.

**30:3** So David and his men came to the city, and behold, it was burned with fire, and their wives, their sons, and their daughters had been taken captive.

**30:4** Then David and the people who were with him lifted up their voices and wept, until they had no more strength to weep.

**30:5** And David's two wives had been taken captive: Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**30:6** Now David was greatly distressed, for the people spoke of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was bitter, every man for his sons and for his daughters. But David strengthened himself in the LORD his God.

**30:7** Then David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Please bring the ephod here to me." And Abiathar brought the ephod to David.

**30:8** So David inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I pursue after this raiding band? Shall I overtake them?" And He said to him, "Pursue, for you shall surely overtake them and without fail rescue all."

**30:9** So David set out, he and the six hundred men who were with him, and they came to the Brook Besor, where those who remained behind stayed.

**30:10** But David pursued, he and four hundred men; for two hundred men stayed behind, who were too exhausted to cross the Brook Besor.

**30:11** Then they found an Egyptian man in the open field and brought him to David; and they gave him bread and he ate, and they gave him water to drink.

**30:12** And they gave him a slice of a cake of figs and two clusters of raisins; and when he had eaten, his spirit returned to him, for he had eaten no bread nor drunk water for three days and three nights.

**30:13** Then David said to him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" And he said, "I am a young Egyptian man, the servant of an Amalekite; and my master abandoned me because three days ago I fell sick.

**30:14** We made a raid upon the Negev of the Cherethites, and upon that which belongs to Judah, and upon the Negev of Caleb; and we burned Ziklag with fire."

**30:15** And David said to him, "Will you lead me down to this raiding band?" And he said, "Swear to me by God that you will neither put me to death nor deliver me into the hand of my master, and I will lead you down to this raiding band."

**30:16** So he led him down, and behold, they were spread out over the face of all the land, eating and drinking and celebrating, because of all the great spoil which they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.

**30:17** Then David struck them down from twilight until the evening of the next day; and not a man of them escaped, except four hundred young men who mounted camels and fled.

**30:18** So David rescued all that the Amalekites had taken, and David rescued his two wives.

**30:19** And nothing of theirs was missing, neither small nor great, neither sons nor daughters, neither spoil nor anything that they had taken from them; David brought back everything.

**30:20** Then David took all the flocks and the herds, which they drove ahead of the other livestock, and they said, "This is David's spoil."

**30:21** Now David came to the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow David, whom they had left to stay at the Brook Besor; and they came out to meet David and to meet the people who were with him. And when David drew near to the people, he inquired after their welfare.

**30:22** Then every wicked and worthless man among the men who had gone with David answered and said, "Because they did not go with us, we will not give them any of the spoil which we have recovered, except to every man his wife and his children, that they may lead them away and depart."

**30:23** But David said, "You shall not do so, my brethren, with that which the LORD has given us; for He has preserved us and delivered into our hand the raiding band that came against us.

**30:24** And who will listen to you in this matter? For as is the share of him who goes down into the battle, so shall be the share of him who stays by the baggage; they shall share alike."

**30:25** So it was, from that day forward, that he made it a statute and an ordinance for Israel to this day.

**30:26** Now when David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the spoil to the elders of Judah, to his friends, saying, "Behold, a blessing for you from the spoil of the enemies of the LORD,"

**30:27** to those who were in Bethel, to those who were in Ramoth of the Negev, to those who were in Jattir,

**30:28** to those who were in Aroer, to those who were in Siphmoth, to those who were in Eshtemoa,

**30:29** to those who were in Rachal, to those who were in the cities of the Jerahmeelites, to those who were in the cities of the Kenites,

**30:30** to those who were in Hormah, to those who were in Bor-ashan, to those who were in Athach,

**30:31** to those who were in Hebron, and to all the places where David himself and his men had roamed.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — The Negev refers to the arid southern steppe region extending south of the hill country of Judah.
- **30:20** — The concise Hebrew clause literally reads, they drove before that livestock, indicating that David's men drove the newly captured Amalekite flocks and herds ahead of their own recovered livestock as David's special portion of the spoil.
- **30:22** — The Masoretic Text reads with me in the singular, aligning with the distributive subject every wicked and worthless man, before shifting to the plural we will not give.
- **30:30** — Bor-ashan follows the Masoretic Hebrew literally, meaning Cistern of Smoke, a town in southern Judah associated with the territory of Simeon.
