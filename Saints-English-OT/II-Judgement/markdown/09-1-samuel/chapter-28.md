## Text

**28:1** Now it came to pass in those days that the Philistines gathered their forces together for war, to fight against Israel. And Achish said to David, "Know for certain that you shall go out with me into the camp, you and your men."

**28:2** And David said to Achish, "Therefore you yourself shall know what your servant will do." And Achish said to David, "Therefore I will appoint you guardian of my head forever."

**28:3** Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had lamented for him and buried him in Ramah, in his own city. And Saul had removed the mediums and the spiritists from the land.

**28:4** Then the Philistines gathered together and came and encamped at Shunem. So Saul gathered all Israel together, and they encamped at Gilboa.

**28:5** When Saul saw the camp of the Philistines, he was afraid, and his heart trembled exceedingly.

**28:6** And when Saul inquired of the LORD, the LORD did not answer him, either by dreams, or by Urim, or by the prophets.

**28:7** Then Saul said to his servants, "Seek out for me a woman who is a medium, that I may go to her and inquire of her." And his servants said to him, "Behold, there is a woman who is a medium at En Dor."

**28:8** So Saul disguised himself and put on other garments, and he went, he and two men with him, and they came to the woman by night. And he said, "Please divine for me by a familiar spirit, and bring up for me the one whom I shall name to you."

**28:9** Then the woman said to him, "Behold, you know what Saul has done, how he has cut off the mediums and the spiritists from the land. Why then are you laying a snare for my life, to put me to death?"

**28:10** And Saul swore to her by the LORD, saying, "As the LORD lives, no punishment shall come upon you for this thing."

**28:11** Then the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" And he said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

**28:12** When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice. And the woman spoke to Saul, saying, "Why have you deceived me? For you are Saul!"

**28:13** And the king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?" And the woman said to Saul, "I saw divine beings ascending out of the earth."

**28:14** Then he said to her, "What is his appearance?" And she said, "An old man is ascending, and he is wrapped in a robe." And Saul knew that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and prostrated himself.

**28:15** Now Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?" And Saul answered, "I am in great distress; for the Philistines are warring against me, and God has departed from me and answers me no more, neither by the hand of prophets nor by dreams. Therefore I have called for you, that you may make known to me what I shall do."

**28:16** Then Samuel said, "Why then do you ask me, seeing the LORD has departed from you and has become your adversary?

**28:17** And the LORD has done for Himself just as He spoke by my hand; for the LORD has torn the kingdom out of your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.

**28:18** Because you did not obey the voice of the LORD nor execute His fierce wrath upon Amalek, therefore the LORD has done this thing to you this day.

**28:19** Moreover, the LORD will also deliver Israel with you into the hand of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons shall be with me; the LORD will also deliver the camp of Israel into the hand of the Philistines."

**28:20** Immediately Saul fell his full length upon the ground, and was exceedingly afraid because of the words of Samuel; moreover, there was no strength in him, for he had eaten no bread all that day and all that night.

**28:21** And the woman came to Saul and saw that he was greatly terrified, and she said to him, "Behold, your maidservant has obeyed your voice, and I have put my life in my hand and have heeded the words which you spoke to me.

**28:22** Now therefore, please listen also to the voice of your maidservant, and let me set a morsel of bread before you; and eat, that you may have strength when you go on your way."

**28:23** But he refused and said, "I will not eat." Yet his servants, together with the woman, urged him strongly, and he listened to their voice. So he arose from the ground and sat upon the bed.

**28:24** Now the woman had a fattened calf in the house, and she hastened to slaughter it; and she took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread from it.

**28:25** So she brought it before Saul and before his servants, and they ate. Then they rose up and went away that night.

## Translator Notes

- **28:8** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text spells the feminine singular imperative "divine" with a medial vav, while the traditional reading (Qere) vocalizes the standard imperative form.
- **28:13** — Hebrew elohim is construed here with a plural participle to denote a supernatural or ghostly apparition, though Saul refers to the figure in the singular in verse 14.
- **28:17** — The Masoretic Text reads "for Himself," emphasizing that the LORD acted on His own behalf to fulfill what He had spoken through Samuel.
