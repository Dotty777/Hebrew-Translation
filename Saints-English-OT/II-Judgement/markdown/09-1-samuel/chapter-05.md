## Text

**5:1** Now the Philistines took the ark of God and brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod.

**5:2** And the Philistines took the ark of God and brought it into the house of Dagon, and set it beside Dagon.

**5:3** And when the people of Ashdod arose early the next day, behold, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD. So they took Dagon and restored him to his place.

**5:4** And when they arose early the next morning, behold, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD; and the head of Dagon and both palms of his hands lay severed upon the threshold; only the trunk of Dagon was left to him.

**5:5** Therefore neither the priests of Dagon nor any who enter the house of Dagon tread upon the threshold of Dagon in Ashdod to this day.

**5:6** Now the hand of the LORD was heavy upon the people of Ashdod, and He devastated them and struck them with tumors, both Ashdod and its territories.

**5:7** And when the men of Ashdod saw how it was, they said, "The ark of the God of Israel must not remain with us, for His hand is severe upon us and upon Dagon our god."

**5:8** Therefore they sent and gathered all the lords of the Philistines to themselves, and said, "What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel?" And they answered, "Let the ark of the God of Israel be carried around to Gath." So they carried the ark of the God of Israel there.

**5:9** And it came to pass, after they had carried it there, that the hand of the LORD was against the city with an exceedingly great panic; and He struck the men of the city, from the smallest to the greatest, and tumors broke out upon them.

**5:10** Therefore they sent the ark of God to Ekron. And it came to pass, as the ark of God entered Ekron, that the Ekronites cried out, saying, "They have brought the ark of the God of Israel around to us, to slay us and our people!"

**5:11** So they sent and gathered all the lords of the Philistines, and said, "Send away the ark of the God of Israel, and let it return to its own place, lest it slay us and our people." For there was a deadly panic throughout all the city; the hand of God was exceedingly heavy there.

**5:12** And the men who did not die were struck with tumors, and the cry of the city went up to heaven.

## Translator Notes

- **5:4** — Only the trunk of Dagon was left to him renders the concise Hebrew phrase literally meaning only Dagon remained upon him, referring to the statue's torso.
- **5:6** — Tumors here and in verses nine and twelve follows the Masoretic Qere reading, substituted in public reading for the Ketiv term meaning swellings or boils.
- **5:10** — To us, to slay us and our people, here and similarly in verse eleven, renders a Hebrew corporate singular literally reading to me, to slay me and my people, with Ekron speaking with one voice.
