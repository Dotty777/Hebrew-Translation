## Text

**11:1** Then Nahash the Ammonite came up and encamped against Jabesh Gilead; and all the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, "Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you."

**11:2** And Nahash the Ammonite answered them, "On this condition I will make a covenant with you, that I may gouge out every right eye among you, and thus lay a reproach upon all Israel."

**11:3** Then the elders of Jabesh said to him, "Grant us seven days' respite, that we may send messengers throughout all the territory of Israel; and then, if there is no one to save us, we will come out to you."

**11:4** So the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul and spoke these words in the hearing of the people. And all the people lifted up their voices and wept.

**11:5** Now behold, Saul was coming behind the oxen from the field; and Saul said, "What troubles the people, that they are weeping?" And they recounted to him the words of the men of Jabesh.

**11:6** Then the Spirit of God came mightily upon Saul when he heard these words, and his anger was greatly kindled.

**11:7** So he took a yoke of oxen and cut them in pieces, and sent them throughout all the territory of Israel by the hand of messengers, saying, "Whoever does not come out after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done to his oxen." And the dread of the LORD fell upon the people, and they came out as one man.

**11:8** When he mustered them at Bezek, the children of Israel were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand.

**11:9** And they said to the messengers who had come, "Thus shall you say to the men of Jabesh Gilead: 'Tomorrow, by the time the sun grows hot, you shall have deliverance.'" Then the messengers came and told the men of Jabesh, and they were glad.

**11:10** Therefore the men of Jabesh said, "Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you may do with us according to all that seems good in your eyes."

**11:11** So it was, on the next day, that Saul divided the people into three companies; and they came into the midst of the camp in the morning watch, and struck down the Ammonites until the heat of the day. And it came to pass that those who remained were scattered, so that no two of them were left together.

**11:12** Then the people said to Samuel, "Who is he who said, 'Shall Saul reign over us?' Bring the men, that we may put them to death."

**11:13** But Saul said, "Not a man shall be put to death on this day, for today the LORD has accomplished deliverance in Israel."

**11:14** Then Samuel said to the people, "Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingdom there."

**11:15** So all the people went to Gilgal, and there they made Saul king before the LORD in Gilgal. And there they sacrificed peace offerings before the LORD, and there Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced exceedingly.

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — Following the Qere, "when he heard" reads the temporal preposition "as/when," whereas the Ketiv reads "in/upon" with essentially the same sense.
- **11:9** — Following the Qere, "by the time the sun grows hot" reads the preposition "about/as," whereas the Ketiv reads "in the heat of the sun."
- **11:14** — "Renew the kingdom" signifies a solemn public confirmation of Saul's kingship before the LORD following his victory at Jabesh Gilead.
