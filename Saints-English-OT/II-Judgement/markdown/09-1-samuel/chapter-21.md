## Text

**21:1** Then he arose and departed, and Jonathan went into the city.

**21:2** And David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest; and Ahimelech trembled at meeting David, and said to him, "Why are you alone, and no man is with you?"

**21:3** And David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king has commanded me on a matter, and said to me, 'Let no man know anything of the matter about which I am sending you and which I have commanded you.' And I have appointed the young men to such and such a place.

**21:4** Now therefore, what is under your hand? Give five loaves of bread into my hand, or whatever is found."

**21:5** Then the priest answered David and said, "There is no common bread under my hand, but there is holy bread—if only the young men have kept themselves from women."

**21:6** And David answered the priest and said to him, "Truly, women have been kept from us for about three days since I came out, and the vessels of the young men are holy, even though the journey is common; how much more today shall it be holy in the vessel!"

**21:7** So the priest gave him holy bread; for there was no bread there except the bread of the Presence, which had been removed from before the LORD, in order to set out warm bread on the day it was taken away.

**21:8** Now a certain man of the servants of Saul was there that day, detained before the LORD; and his name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of the herdsmen who belonged to Saul.

**21:9** And David said to Ahimelech, "Is there not here under your hand a spear or a sword? For I have brought neither my sword nor my weapons in my hand, because the king's business was urgent."

**21:10** And the priest said, "The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck down in the Valley of Elah—behold, it is wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you wish to take that for yourself, take it, for there is no other besides it here." And David said, "There is none like it; give it to me."

**21:11** Then David arose and fled that day from before Saul, and came to Achish the king of Gath.

**21:12** And the servants of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, the king of the land? Did they not sing in response concerning this man in the dances, saying, 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

**21:13** And David laid these words to his heart, and feared exceedingly before Achish the king of Gath.

**21:14** So he disguised his sanity before their eyes, and feigned madness in their hands; and he scratched marks on the doors of the gate, and let his spittle run down upon his beard.

**21:15** Then Achish said to his servants, "Behold, you see the man is mad. Why have you brought him to me?

**21:16** Am I lacking in madmen, that you have brought this man to behave like a madman in my presence? Shall this man come into my house?"

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew versification, chapter 21 begins with this verse (numbered 20:42b in many English translations), shifting all subsequent verse numbers in chapter 21 by one.
- **21:6** — The Hebrew phrase rendered 'vessels' (keli) may refer to the young men's gear or figuratively to their bodies; the closing clause is compressed in Hebrew and indicates that their ritual purity is all the more certain on the present occasion.
- **21:7** — Literally 'bread of the face' (showbread), the twelve consecrated loaves regularly replaced before the LORD (Leviticus 24:5–9).
