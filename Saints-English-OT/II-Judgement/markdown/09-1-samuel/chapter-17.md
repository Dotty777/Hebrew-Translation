## Text

**17:1** Now the Philistines gathered their hosts together for battle, and they assembled at Socoh, which belongs to Judah; and they encamped between Socoh and Azekah, in Ephes-dammim.

**17:2** And Saul and the men of Israel assembled together, and encamped in the Valley of Elah, and drew up the battle line to meet the Philistines.

**17:3** And the Philistines stood on the mountain on one side, and Israel stood on the mountain on the other side, and the valley was between them.

**17:4** And a champion came forth from the camps of the Philistines, whose name was Goliath, from Gath; his height was six cubits and a span.

**17:5** He had a helmet of bronze upon his head, and he was clad in a coat of scale armor; and the weight of the armor was five thousand shekels of bronze.

**17:6** And bronze greaves were upon his legs, and a bronze javelin was between his shoulders.

**17:7** And the shaft of his spear was like a weaver's beam, and the blade of his spear weighed six hundred shekels of iron; and the shield-bearer went before him.

**17:8** And he stood and cried out to the ranks of Israel, and said to them, "Why do you come forth to draw up for battle? Am I not the Philistine, and you the servants of Saul? Choose a man for yourselves, and let him come down to me.

**17:9** If he is able to fight with me and slay me, then we will become your servants; but if I prevail against him and slay him, then you shall become our servants and serve us."

**17:10** And the Philistine said, "I defy the ranks of Israel this day; give me a man, that we may fight together."

**17:11** When Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, they were dismayed and exceedingly afraid.

**17:32** Then David said to Saul, "Let no man's heart fail on account of him; your servant will go and do battle with this Philistine."

**17:33** And Saul said to David, "You are not able to go against this Philistine to fight with him; for you are a youth, and he has been a man of war from his youth."

**17:34** But David said to Saul, "Your servant was keeping his father's flock, and whenever a lion or a bear came and carried off a lamb from the herd,

**17:35** I went out after it and struck it, and rescued it from its mouth; and when it rose up against me, I seized it by its beard, and struck it and slew it.

**17:36** Your servant has struck down both the lion and the bear; and this uncircumcised Philistine shall be like one of them, because he has defied the ranks of the living God."

**17:37** Moreover David said, "The LORD, who delivered me from the paw of the lion and from the paw of the bear, He will deliver me from the hand of this Philistine." And Saul said to David, "Go, and the LORD be with you."

**17:38** Then Saul clothed David with his own battle garments, and put a helmet of bronze upon his head; he also clothed him with a coat of mail.

**17:39** And David girded his sword over his battle garments, and tried to walk, for he had not tested them. And David said to Saul, "I am not able to walk in these, for I have not tested them." So David put them off from himself.

**17:40** Then he took his staff in his hand, and chose for himself five smooth stones from the brook, and placed them in the shepherd's bag that he had, even in the pouch; and his sling was in his hand. And he drew near to the Philistine.

**17:42** And when the Philistine looked and saw David, he disdained him; for he was a youth, ruddy and of fair appearance.

**17:43** So the Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come to me with staves?" And the Philistine cursed David by his gods.

**17:44** And the Philistine said to David, "Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the heavens and to the beasts of the field."

**17:45** Then David said to the Philistine, "You come to me with a sword, and with a spear, and with a javelin; but I come to you in the name of the LORD of hosts, the God of the ranks of Israel, whom you have defied.

**17:46** This day the LORD will deliver you into my hand, and I will strike you down and sever your head from you; and this day I will give the carcasses of the host of the Philistines to the birds of the heavens and to the wild beasts of the earth, that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel.

**17:47** And all this assembly shall know that the LORD does not deliver with sword and spear; for the battle belongs to the LORD, and He will give you into our hand."

**17:48** And it came to pass, when the Philistine arose and came forward and drew near to meet David, that David hastened and ran toward the battle line to meet the Philistine.

**17:49** Then David reached his hand into the bag and took from there a stone, and slung it and struck the Philistine on his forehead; and the stone sank into his forehead, and he fell upon his face to the earth.

**17:51** Therefore David ran and stood over the Philistine, and took his sword and drew it out of its sheath, and put him to death, and cut off his head with it. And when the Philistines saw that their mighty champion was dead, they fled.

**17:52** Then the men of Israel and Judah arose and shouted, and pursued the Philistines as far as the entrance to the valley and to the gates of Ekron. And the slain of the Philistines fell along the way to Shaaraim, even as far as Gath and Ekron.

**17:53** And the children of Israel returned from hotly pursuing the Philistines, and they plundered their camps.

**17:54** And David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem, but his armor he placed in his tent.

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — Hebrew ish-habbenayim literally means a man of the space between, denoting a single-combat champion who stepped out between two opposing battle lines.
- **17:7** — The Masoretic Qere reads the wood or shaft of his spear, which is translated here, whereas the Ketiv reads the arrow or point of his spear.
- **17:52** — The Masoretic text reads the entrance to the valley, Hebrew Gai, as the initial boundary of the pursuit.
