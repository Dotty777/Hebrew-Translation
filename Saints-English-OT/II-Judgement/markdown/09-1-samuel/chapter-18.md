## Text

**18:6** Now it came to pass as they were coming home, when David returned from striking down the Philistine, that the women came out from all the cities of Israel, singing and dancing, to meet King Saul, with timbrels, with joy, and with three-stringed instruments.

**18:7** And the women sang to one another as they made merry, and said, "Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands."

**18:8** Then Saul was exceedingly angry, and this saying was displeasing in his eyes; and he said, "They have ascribed to David ten thousands, and to me they have ascribed the thousands; and what more remains for him except the kingdom?"

**18:9** So Saul eyed David with suspicion from that day onward.

**18:12** Now Saul was afraid of David, because the LORD was with him, but had departed from Saul.

**18:13** Therefore Saul removed him from his presence, and appointed him as his commander over a thousand; and he went out and came in before the people.

**18:14** And David acted wisely in all his ways, and the LORD was with him.

**18:15** Therefore, when Saul saw that he acted very wisely, he stood in dread of him.

**18:16** But all Israel and Judah loved David, because he went out and came in before them.

**18:20** Now Michal, the daughter of Saul, loved David. And they told Saul, and the matter was pleasing in his eyes.

**18:21** And Saul said, "I will give her to him, that she may become a snare to him, and that the hand of the Philistines may be upon him." Therefore Saul said to David, "A second time you shall become my son-in-law this day."

**18:22** And Saul commanded his servants, "Speak to David in secret, saying, 'Behold, the king delights in you, and all his servants love you; now therefore, become the king's son-in-law.'"

**18:23** So the servants of Saul spoke these words in the ears of David. And David said, "Is it a light thing in your eyes to become the king's son-in-law, seeing that I am a poor man and lightly esteemed?"

**18:24** And the servants of Saul told him, saying, "According to these words David has spoken."

**18:25** Then Saul said, "Thus you shall say to David, 'The king has no desire for a bride-price, except for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines, to take vengeance upon the king's enemies.'" Now Saul purposed to make David fall by the hand of the Philistines.

**18:26** When his servants told David these words, the matter was pleasing in the eyes of David to become the king's son-in-law. And the appointed days had not yet been fulfilled.

**18:27** Therefore David arose and went, he and his men, and struck down two hundred men among the Philistines; and David brought their foreskins, and they delivered them in full count to the king, that he might become the king's son-in-law. Then Saul gave him Michal his daughter as a wife.

**18:28** And Saul saw and knew that the LORD was with David, and that Michal, the daughter of Saul, loved him.

**18:29** Thus Saul feared David yet the more; and Saul became David's enemy continually.

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — The Masoretic Qere reads to sing, translated here, while the Ketiv preserves an alternate spelling of the verb. The Hebrew term shalishim denotes a musical instrument, likely three-stringed or triangular, whose exact form is uncertain.
- **18:9** — The Masoretic Qere reads oyein, a participle derived from the word for eye meaning to watch with jealousy or suspicion, whereas the Ketiv writes a shorter consonantal form.
- **18:14** — The Hebrew participle maskil conveys both acting with prudent wisdom and enjoying consequent success.
