## Text

**2:1** And Hannah prayed and said, "My heart exults in the LORD; my horn is exalted in the LORD. My mouth is opened wide over my enemies, because I rejoice in Your salvation.

**2:2** There is none holy like the LORD, for there is none besides You, nor is there any rock like our God.

**2:3** Do not speak any longer so very proudly; let no arrogance come forth from your mouth, for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by Him actions are weighed.

**2:4** The bows of the mighty men are broken, and those who stumbled are girded with strength.

**2:5** Those who were full have hired themselves out for bread, and the hungry have ceased to hunger; even the barren woman has borne seven, and she who had many sons has languished.

**2:6** The LORD puts to death and makes alive; He brings down to Sheol and brings up.

**2:7** The LORD makes poor and makes rich; He brings low and also lifts up.

**2:8** He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap, to seat them with nobles and make them inherit a throne of glory. For the pillars of the earth belong to the LORD, and He has set the world upon them.

**2:9** He will guard the feet of His saints, yet the wicked shall be silenced in darkness; for by strength no man shall prevail.

**2:10** The adversaries of the LORD shall be shattered; against them He shall thunder in the heavens. The LORD shall judge the ends of the earth; He shall give strength to His king, and exalt the horn of His anointed."

**2:11** Then Elkanah went to Ramah to his house; and the boy ministered to the LORD before Eli the priest.

**2:12** Now the sons of Eli were worthless men; they did not know the LORD.

**2:13** And the custom of the priests with the people was this: when any man offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come while the meat was boiling, with a three-pronged fleshhook in his hand.

**2:14** And he would thrust it into the pan, or kettle, or cauldron, or pot; all that the fleshhook brought up, the priest took for himself. Thus they did to all Israel who came there to Shiloh.

**2:15** Moreover, before they burned the fat, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who sacrificed, "Give meat to roast for the priest, for he will not accept boiled meat from you, but raw."

**2:16** And if the man said to him, "Let them surely burn the fat first, and then take for yourself as much as your soul desires," then he would answer, "No, for you shall give it now; and if not, I will take it by force."

**2:17** Therefore the sin of the young men was exceedingly great before the LORD, for the men despised the offering of the LORD.

**2:18** Now Samuel ministered before the LORD, a boy girded with a linen ephod.

**2:19** And his mother would make him a little robe and bring it up to him year by year, when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

**2:20** And Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife, and say, "May the LORD give you offspring from this woman in place of the loan that was lent to the LORD." Then they would go back to his home.

**2:21** And indeed the LORD visited Hannah, so that she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. Meanwhile the boy Samuel grew before the LORD.

**2:22** Now Eli was very old; and he heard all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they lay with the women who served at the doorway of the tent of meeting.

**2:23** So he said to them, "Why do you do such things as these? For I hear of your evil doings from all these people.

**2:24** No, my sons! For it is not a good report that I hear; you cause the LORD's people to transgress.

**2:25** If one man sins against another, God will mediate for him; yet if a man sins against the LORD, who shall intercede for him?" Nevertheless they did not heed the voice of their father, because the LORD desired to put them to death.

**2:26** And the boy Samuel continued to grow in stature, and in favor both with the LORD and also with men.

**2:27** Then a man of God came to Eli and said to him, "Thus says the LORD: Did I not plainly reveal Myself to the house of your father when they were in Egypt, belonging to Pharaoh's house?

**2:28** And did I not choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be My priest, to go up to My altar, to burn incense, and to wear an ephod before Me? And I gave to the house of your father all the fire offerings of the children of Israel.

**2:29** Why then do you kick at My sacrifice and at My offering which I have commanded in My habitation, and honor your sons above Me, to make yourselves fat with the choicest of all the offerings of Israel My people?

**2:30** Therefore the LORD, the God of Israel, declares: I indeed said that your house and the house of your father should walk before Me forever; yet now the LORD declares: Far be it from Me! For those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed.

**2:31** Behold, the days are coming when I will cut off your arm and the arm of your father's house, so that there shall not be an old man in your house.

**2:32** And you shall behold distress in the habitation, amid all the good that He will bestow upon Israel; and there shall never be an old man in your house for all time.

**2:33** Yet the man of yours whom I do not cut off from My altar shall remain to consume your eyes and to grieve your soul; and all the increase of your house shall die in the prime of manhood.

**2:34** And this shall be the sign to you that shall come upon your two sons, upon Hophni and Phinehas: in one day both of them shall die.

**2:35** Then I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest who shall do according to what is in My heart and in My soul; and I will build for him a sure house, and he shall walk before My anointed forever.

**2:36** And it shall come to pass that everyone who is left in your house shall come to bow down to him for a piece of silver and a loaf of bread, and shall say, 'Please assign me to one of the priestly offices, that I may eat a morsel of bread.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — Translating the Masoretic qere ("and by Him"); the written ketiv reads "and not."
- **2:16** — Translating the Masoretic qere ("No"); the written ketiv reads "to him," yielding "then he would say to him, For you shall give it now."
- **2:25** — The Hebrew Elohim may denote "God" or "judges," and the verb palal plays on both the legal sense ("mediate/arbitrate") and the prayerful sense ("intercede").
- **2:32** — The Hebrew phrase tsar maon is difficult and may be rendered either "distress in the habitation" or "an adversary in the habitation."
