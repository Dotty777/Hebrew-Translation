## Text

**10:1** Then Samuel took a flask of oil and poured it upon his head, and kissed him and said, "Has not the LORD anointed you as prince over His inheritance?

**10:2** When you have departed from me today, you will find two men by Rachel's tomb in the territory of Benjamin at Zelzah; and they will say to you, 'The donkeys which you went to search for have been found. And behold, your father has left off caring about the donkeys and is anxious for you, saying, "What shall I do for my son?"'

**10:3** Then you shall go on forward from there and come to the terebinth tree of Tabor, and three men going up to God at Bethel will meet you there, one carrying three young goats, another carrying three loaves of bread, and another carrying a skin of wine.

**10:4** And they will greet you and give you two loaves of bread, which you shall receive from their hand.

**10:5** After that you shall come to the hill of God, where the garrison of the Philistines is. And it shall come to pass, when you have come there to the city, that you will meet a band of prophets coming down from the high place, with a harp, a tambourine, a flute, and a lyre before them; and they will be prophesying.

**10:6** Then the Spirit of the LORD will come mightily upon you, and you will prophesy with them and be turned into another man.

**10:7** And let it be, when these signs come to you, that you do for yourself whatever your hand finds to do; for God is with you.

**10:8** And you shall go down before me to Gilgal; and behold, I am coming down to you to offer burnt offerings and to sacrifice sacrifices of peace offerings. Seven days you shall wait until I come to you and make known to you what you shall do."

**10:9** And it came to pass, as he turned his shoulder to go from Samuel, that God gave him another heart; and all those signs came to pass on that day.

**10:10** When they came there to Gibeah, behold, a band of prophets met him; and the Spirit of God came mightily upon him, and he prophesied in their midst.

**10:11** And it came to pass, when all who knew him formerly saw that, behold, he prophesied among the prophets, that the people said one to another, "What is this that has come upon the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:12** Then a man from that place answered and said, "And who is their father?" Therefore it became a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:13** And when he had finished prophesying, he came to the high place.

**10:14** Then Saul's uncle said to him and to his servant, "Where did you go?" And he said, "To search for the donkeys; and when we saw that they were nowhere to be found, we went to Samuel."

**10:15** And Saul's uncle said, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

**10:16** So Saul said to his uncle, "He plainly told us that the donkeys had been found." But concerning the matter of the kingdom, of which Samuel had spoken, he did not tell him.

**10:17** Then Samuel called the people together to the LORD at Mizpah,

**10:18** and said to the children of Israel, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'I brought up Israel out of Egypt, and delivered you from the hand of Egypt and from the hand of all the kingdoms that were oppressing you.'

**10:19** Yet today you have rejected your God, who Himself saves you from all your adversities and your distresses; and you have said to Him, 'Set a king over us!' Now therefore present yourselves before the LORD by your tribes and by your thousands."

**10:20** And when Samuel had brought all the tribes of Israel near, the tribe of Benjamin was taken.

**10:21** Then he brought the tribe of Benjamin near by its families, and the family of the Matrites was taken. And Saul the son of Kish was taken. But when they sought him, he was not found.

**10:22** Therefore they inquired of the LORD further, "Has the man come here yet?" And the LORD answered, "Behold, he has hidden himself among the baggage."

**10:23** So they ran and brought him from there; and when he stood among the people, he was taller than all the people from his shoulders upward.

**10:24** And Samuel said to all the people, "Do you see him whom the LORD has chosen, that there is none like him among all the people?" So all the people shouted and said, "Long live the king!"

**10:25** Then Samuel spoke to the people the ordinance of the kingdom, and wrote it in a book and laid it up before the LORD. And Samuel sent all the people away, every man to his house.

**10:26** Saul also went to his house at Gibeah; and valiant men went with him, whose hearts God had touched.

**10:27** But worthless men said, "How can this man save us?" So they despised him, and brought him no present. But he held his peace.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — "The hill of God" renders Hebrew Gibeat Ha'elohim, a sacred high place associated with the town of Gibeah mentioned in verse 10.
- **10:7** — Following the Qere, the verb "come" uses the standard feminine plural form agreeing with "these signs," while the Ketiv preserves a slight spelling variant.
- **10:19** — The Masoretic Text reads "to Him" (lo) rather than the homophone "no," and "thousands" refers to traditional tribal and military subdivisions of Israel.
- **10:27** — "Worthless men" renders the Hebrew idiom "sons of Belial," meaning base, lawless, or rebellious men.
