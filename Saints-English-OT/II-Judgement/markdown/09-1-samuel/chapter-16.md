## Text

**16:1** Now the LORD said to Samuel, "How long will you mourn over Saul, seeing that I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go; I am sending you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have provided for Myself a king among his sons."

**16:2** And Samuel said, "How can I go? If Saul hears of it, he will slay me." Then the LORD said, "Take a heifer from the herd with you, and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to the LORD.'

**16:3** Then invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will make known to you what you shall do; and you shall anoint for Me the one whom I name to you."

**16:4** So Samuel did what the LORD had spoken, and came to Bethlehem. And the elders of the city came trembling to meet him, and said, "Is your coming in peace?"

**16:5** And he said, "In peace; I have come to sacrifice to the LORD. Consecrate yourselves, and come with me to the sacrifice." Then he consecrated Jesse and his sons, and invited them to the sacrifice.

**16:6** Now it came to pass, when they arrived, that he looked on Eliab and said, "Surely before the LORD stands His anointed!"

**16:7** But the LORD said to Samuel, "Do not gaze upon his appearance or upon the height of his stature, for I have rejected him. For it is not as man sees: man looks to the outward appearance, whereas the LORD looks to the heart."

**16:8** Then Jesse called Abinadab and made him pass before Samuel. And he said, "Neither has the LORD chosen this one."

**16:9** Then Jesse made Shammah pass by. And he said, "Neither has the LORD chosen this one."

**16:10** Thus Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel. And Samuel said to Jesse, "The LORD has not chosen these."

**16:11** And Samuel said to Jesse, "Are all the young men here?" Then he said, "There still remains the youngest, and behold, he is tending the flock." And Samuel said to Jesse, "Send and bring him, for we will not sit down until he comes here."

**16:12** So he sent and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, with handsome eyes and a pleasing appearance. And the LORD said, "Arise, anoint him, for this is the one!"

**16:13** Then Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brethren; and the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon David from that day forward. So Samuel arose and went to Ramah.

**16:14** Now the Spirit of the LORD departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the LORD tormented him.

**16:15** And Saul's servants said to him, "Behold now, an evil spirit from God is tormenting you.

**16:16** Let our lord now command your servants who stand before you to seek out a man who is skilled in playing the harp; and it shall be, when an evil spirit from God is upon you, that he shall play with his hand, and you shall be well."

**16:17** So Saul said to his servants, "Provide for me now a man who plays well, and bring him to me."

**16:18** Then one of the young men answered and said, "Behold, I have seen a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite, who is skilled in playing, a mighty man of valor, a man of war, discerning in speech, and a man of handsome form; and the LORD is with him."

**16:19** Therefore Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, "Send to me David your son, who is with the flock."

**16:20** And Jesse took a donkey laden with bread, a skin of wine, and one young goat, and sent them by the hand of David his son to Saul.

**16:21** So David came to Saul and stood before him; and Saul loved him greatly, and he became his armorbearer.

**16:22** Then Saul sent word to Jesse, saying, "Please let David stand before me, for he has found favor in my sight."

**16:23** And so it was, whenever the spirit from God came upon Saul, that David would take the harp and play with his hand; then relief would come to Saul and he would be well, and the evil spirit would depart from him.

## Translator Notes

- **16:7** — The expression "to the outward appearance" literally reads "to the eyes" in Hebrew, contrasting human judgment based on what is visible with the LORD's discernment of the heart.
- **16:11** — The Hebrew verb nasov ("sit down") literally means "turn around" or "recline in a circle," referring to gathering around the sacrificial feast.
- **16:14** — The Hebrew phrase ruach ra'ah ("an evil spirit") refers to a distressing or injurious spirit operating under the LORD's sovereign permission.
- **16:20** — The Hebrew literally reads "a donkey of bread," an idiomatic expression for a donkey laden with loaves of bread.
