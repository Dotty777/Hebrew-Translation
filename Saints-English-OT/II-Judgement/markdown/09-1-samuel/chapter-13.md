## Text

**13:2** Saul chose for himself three thousand men of Israel. Two thousand were with Saul in Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel, and one thousand were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin. As for the rest of the people, he sent every man away to his tent.

**13:3** And Jonathan struck down the garrison of the Philistines that was in Geba, and the Philistines heard of it. Then Saul blew the trumpet throughout all the land, saying, "Let the Hebrews hear!"

**13:4** And all Israel heard it said, "Saul has struck down the garrison of the Philistines, and Israel has also become odious to the Philistines." So the people were summoned together after Saul to Gilgal.

**13:5** Then the Philistines gathered together to fight with Israel, thirty thousand chariots and six thousand horsemen, and people like the sand that is on the seashore in multitude. And they came up and encamped in Michmash, to the east of Beth Aven.

**13:6** When the men of Israel saw that they were in distress—for the people were hard pressed—the people hid themselves in caves, in thickets, among rocks, in vaults, and in cisterns.

**13:7** And some of the Hebrews crossed over the Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead. As for Saul, he was still in Gilgal, and all the people followed him trembling.

**13:8** Then he waited seven days, according to the time appointed by Samuel. Yet Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the people were scattering from him.

**13:9** So Saul said, "Bring near to me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." And he offered up the burnt offering.

**13:10** And it came to pass, as soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, that behold, Samuel came; and Saul went out to meet him, to greet him.

**13:11** And Samuel said, "What have you done?" And Saul said, "Because I saw that the people were scattering from me, and that you did not come within the appointed days, and that the Philistines had assembled at Michmash,

**13:12** therefore I said, 'Now the Philistines will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not sought the favor of the LORD.' So I compelled myself, and offered up the burnt offering."

**13:13** And Samuel said to Saul, "You have acted foolishly. You have not kept the commandment of the LORD your God, which He commanded you. For now the LORD would have established your kingdom over Israel forever.

**13:14** But now your kingdom shall not stand. The LORD has sought for Himself a man after His own heart, and the LORD has appointed him to be prince over His people, because you have not kept what the LORD commanded you."

**13:15** Then Samuel arose and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. And Saul numbered the people who were present with him, about six hundred men.

**13:16** Now Saul, and Jonathan his son, and the people who were present with them, remained in Geba of Benjamin, while the Philistines encamped in Michmash.

**13:17** And raiders came out from the camp of the Philistines in three companies: one company turned toward the road to Ophrah, to the land of Shual;

**13:18** another company turned toward the road to Beth Horon; and another company turned toward the road of the border that overlooks the Valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness.

**13:19** Now no blacksmith was to be found throughout all the land of Israel, for the Philistines said, "Lest the Hebrews make swords or spears."

**13:20** So all Israel would go down to the Philistines to sharpen each man his plowshare, his mattock, his ax, and his sickle.

**13:21** And the charge was a pim for the plowshares, and for the mattocks, and for the three-pronged forks, and for the axes, and to set the goads.

**13:22** So it came to pass on the day of battle that neither sword nor spear was found in the hand of any of the people who were with Saul and Jonathan; yet they were found with Saul and with Jonathan his son.

**13:23** And the garrison of the Philistines went out to the pass of Michmash.

## Translator Notes

- **13:8** — The written text (ketiv) and read text (qere) differ slightly in the vocalization and spelling of the verb "waited," with the qere followed here.
- **13:19** — The written text (ketiv) has the singular verb "said," while the read text (qere), followed here, has the plural verb agreeing with "the Philistines."
- **13:20** — The first and fourth agricultural tools share closely related Hebrew forms of the same root, traditionally rendered as two distinct iron implements such as a plowshare and a sickle.
- **13:21** — A "pim" was an ancient weight equal to about two-thirds of a shekel of silver, attested by inscribed stone weights recovered in archaeological excavations.
