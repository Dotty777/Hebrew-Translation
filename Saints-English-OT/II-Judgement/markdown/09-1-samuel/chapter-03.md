## Text

**3:1** Now the lad Samuel was ministering to the LORD before Eli. And the word of the LORD was rare in those days; there was no widespread vision.

**3:2** And it came to pass in that day, while Eli was lying down in his place, and his eyes had begun to grow dim so that he could not see,

**3:3** and the lamp of God had not yet gone out, while Samuel was lying down in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was,

**3:4** that the LORD called to Samuel. And he said, "Here I am!"

**3:5** So he ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." And he said, "I did not call; go back and lie down." So he went and lay down.

**3:6** Then the LORD called yet again, "Samuel!" So Samuel arose and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." And he said, "I did not call, my son; go back and lie down."

**3:7** Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, nor had the word of the LORD yet been revealed to him.

**3:8** And the LORD called Samuel again a third time. So he arose and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." Then Eli perceived that the LORD was calling the lad.

**3:9** Therefore Eli said to Samuel, "Go, lie down; and it shall be, if He calls to you, that you shall say, 'Speak, LORD, for Your servant hears.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**3:10** Then the LORD came and stood, and called as at the other times, "Samuel! Samuel!" And Samuel said, "Speak, for Your servant hears."

**3:11** Then the LORD said to Samuel, "Behold, I am about to do a thing in Israel at which both ears of everyone who hears it shall tingle.

**3:12** In that day I will carry out against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house, from beginning to end.

**3:13** For I have declared to him that I will judge his house forever for the iniquity which he knew, because his sons were bringing a curse upon themselves, and he did not restrain them.

**3:14** Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the iniquity of the house of Eli shall not be atoned for by sacrifice or offering forever."

**3:15** So Samuel lay down until morning, and he opened the doors of the house of the LORD. And Samuel was afraid to tell the vision to Eli.

**3:16** Then Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son!" And he answered, "Here I am."

**3:17** And he said, "What is the word that He spoke to you? Please do not conceal it from me. May God do so to you, and more also, if you conceal anything from me of all the words that He spoke to you."

**3:18** Then Samuel told him all the words, and concealed nothing from him. And he said, "It is the LORD; let Him do what is good in His sight."

**3:19** So Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him and let none of his words fall to the ground.

**3:20** And all Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, knew that Samuel was established as a prophet of the LORD.

**3:21** And the LORD appeared again at Shiloh, for the LORD revealed Himself to Samuel at Shiloh by the word of the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Hebrew word translated rare literally means precious or scarce, and widespread translates a participle meaning breaking forth widely.
- **3:13** — Bringing a curse upon themselves follows the Masoretic Hebrew text; ancient scribal tradition identifies this phrasing as a reverential euphemism avoiding explicit mention of cursing God.
- **3:19** — Let none of his words fall to the ground is a Hebrew idiom meaning that every prophetic word Samuel spoke came to pass.
