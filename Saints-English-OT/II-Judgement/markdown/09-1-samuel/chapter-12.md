## Text

**12:1** And Samuel said to all Israel, "Behold, I have heeded your voice in all that you have spoken to me, and have set a king over you.

**12:2** And now, behold, the king walks before you; as for me, I have grown old and gray-headed, and behold, my sons are with you. And I have walked before you from my youth to this day.

**12:3** Here I am. Testify against me before the LORD and before His anointed: Whose ox have I taken, or whose donkey have I taken? Whom have I defrauded? Whom have I oppressed? Or from whose hand have I accepted a bribe to close my eyes with it? And I will restore it to you."

**12:4** And they said, "You have not defrauded us, nor have you oppressed us, nor have you taken anything from the hand of any man."

**12:5** Then he said to them, "The LORD is witness against you, and His anointed is witness this day, that you have found nothing in my hand." And they replied, "He is witness."

**12:6** Then Samuel said to the people, "It is the LORD who appointed Moses and Aaron, and who brought your fathers up from the land of Egypt.

**12:7** Now therefore, take your stand, that I may plead with you before the LORD concerning all the righteous deeds of the LORD which He has done with you and with your fathers.

**12:8** When Jacob had gone into Egypt, and your fathers cried out to the LORD, then the LORD sent Moses and Aaron, who brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them in this place.

**12:9** Yet they forgot the LORD their God; so He sold them into the hand of Sisera, commander of the army of Hazor, and into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the king of Moab, and they fought against them.

**12:10** Then they cried out to the LORD and said, 'We have sinned, because we have forsaken the LORD and served the Baals and the Ashtaroth; but now deliver us from the hand of our enemies, and we will serve You.'

**12:11** And the LORD sent Jerubbaal, and Bedan, and Jephthah, and Samuel, and He delivered you from the hand of your enemies on every side, so that you dwelt in safety.

**12:12** And when you saw that Nahash king of the sons of Ammon came against you, you said to me, 'No, but a king shall reign over us,' though the LORD your God was your king.

**12:13** Now therefore, behold the king whom you have chosen, whom you have asked for! And behold, the LORD has set a king over you.

**12:14** If you fear the LORD and serve Him, and heed His voice, and do not rebel against the command of the LORD, then both you and the king who reigns over you shall continue following the LORD your God.

**12:15** However, if you do not heed the voice of the LORD, but rebel against the command of the LORD, then the hand of the LORD shall be against you and against your fathers.

**12:16** Now therefore, take your stand and behold this great thing which the LORD is about to do before your eyes:

**12:17** Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call upon the LORD, and He will send thunder and rain, that you may know and see that your wickedness is great, which you have done in the sight of the LORD, in asking for a king for yourselves."

**12:18** So Samuel called upon the LORD, and the LORD sent thunder and rain that day; and all the people greatly feared the LORD and Samuel.

**12:19** And all the people said to Samuel, "Pray for your servants to the LORD your God, that we may not die; for we have added to all our sins this evil, to ask for a king for ourselves."

**12:20** Then Samuel said to the people, "Do not fear. You have indeed done all this evil; yet do not turn aside from following the LORD, but serve the LORD with all your heart.

**12:21** And do not turn aside after vain things, which cannot profit or deliver, for they are vanity.

**12:22** For the LORD will not forsake His people, for the sake of His great name, because it has pleased the LORD to make you a people for Himself.

**12:23** Moreover, as for me, far be it from me that I should sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you; but I will instruct you in the good and upright way.

**12:24** Only fear the LORD, and serve Him in truth with all your heart; for consider what great things He has done for you.

**12:25** Yet if you persist in doing wickedly, both you and your king shall be swept away."

## Translator Notes

- **12:10** — The written text (ketiv) has the singular verb "he said," while the read text (qere), translated here, has the plural "they said."
- **12:11** — "Bedan" follows the Masoretic Hebrew text; some ancient versions read "Barak," while Jewish tradition often identifies Bedan with Samson ("of Dan").
- **12:15** — The Hebrew literally reads "against you and against your fathers," often understood comparatively as "against you, just as it was against your fathers."
- **12:21** — The Hebrew term for "vain things" and "vanity" (tohu) denotes emptiness and nothingness, highlighting the complete futility of idols.
