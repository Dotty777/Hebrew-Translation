## Text

**20:1** Then David fled from Naioth in Ramah, and came and said before Jonathan, "What have I done? What is my iniquity, and what is my sin before your father, that he seeks my life?"

**20:2** And he said to him, "Far be it! You shall not die. Behold, my father does nothing, either great or small, without revealing it to me. And why should my father conceal this thing from me? It is not so!"

**20:3** Then David swore again and said, "Your father certainly knows that I have found favor in your sight, and he has said, 'Do not let Jonathan know this, lest he be grieved.' Yet truly, as the LORD lives and as your soul lives, there is but a step between me and death."

**20:4** Then Jonathan said to David, "Whatever your soul desires, I will do for you."

**20:5** And David said to Jonathan, "Behold, tomorrow is the New Moon, and I ought indeed to sit with the king to eat; but let me go, that I may hide myself in the field until the third evening.

**20:6** If your father misses me at all, then you shall say, 'David earnestly asked leave of me that he might hasten to Bethlehem his city, for there is a yearly sacrifice there for all the family.'

**20:7** If he says thus, 'It is well,' your servant will have peace; but if he is greatly angered, know that evil is determined by him.

**20:8** Therefore show lovingkindness to your servant, for you have brought your servant into a covenant of the LORD with you. Yet if there is iniquity in me, put me to death yourself; why then should you bring me to your father?"

**20:9** And Jonathan said, "Far be it from you! For if I knew certainly that evil was determined by my father to come upon you, would I not tell you about it?"

**20:10** Then David said to Jonathan, "Who will tell me, or what if your father answers you harshly?"

**20:11** And Jonathan said to David, "Come, and let us go out into the field." So the two of them went out into the field.

**20:12** Then Jonathan said to David, "By the LORD, the God of Israel, when I have sounded out my father about this time tomorrow, or the third day, and behold, there is goodwill toward David, shall I not then send to you and reveal it to you?

**20:13** May the LORD do so to Jonathan, and much more also! If it pleases my father to do you evil, then I will reveal it to you and send you away, that you may go in peace. And may the LORD be with you as He has been with my father.

**20:14** And not only while I am still alive shall you show me the lovingkindness of the LORD, that I may not die;

**20:15** but you shall not cut off your lovingkindness from my house forever—no, not even when the LORD has cut off every one of the enemies of David from the face of the earth."

**20:16** So Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David: "May the LORD require it at the hand of David's enemies."

**20:17** And Jonathan made David swear again because of his love for him, for he loved him as his own soul.

**20:18** Then Jonathan said to him, "Tomorrow is the New Moon, and you will be missed, because your seat will be empty.

**20:19** And when you have stayed three days, go down quickly and come to the place where you hid yourself on the day of the affair, and abide beside the stone Ezel.

**20:20** And I will shoot three arrows to the side of it, as though I were shooting at a target.

**20:21** And behold, I will send the lad, saying, 'Go, find the arrows.' If I expressly say to the lad, 'Behold, the arrows are on this side of you, take them,' then come; for there is peace for you and no harm, as the LORD lives.

**20:22** But if I say thus to the young man, 'Behold, the arrows are beyond you,' go your way, for the LORD has sent you away.

**20:23** And as for the matter of which we have spoken, I and you, behold, the LORD is between me and you forever."

**20:24** So David hid himself in the field. And when the New Moon came, the king sat down to the meal to eat.

**20:25** Now the king sat upon his seat, as at other times, upon the seat by the wall. And Jonathan arose, and Abner sat by Saul's side, but David's place was empty.

**20:26** Yet Saul did not speak anything that day, for he thought, "Something has befallen him; he is not clean—surely he is not clean."

**20:27** And it came to pass on the day after the New Moon, the second day, that David's place was empty. And Saul said to Jonathan his son, "Why has the son of Jesse not come to the meal, either yesterday or today?"

**20:28** So Jonathan answered Saul, "David earnestly asked leave of me to go to Bethlehem.

**20:29** And he said, 'Please let me go, for our family has a sacrifice in the city, and my brother himself has commanded me to be there. And now, if I have found favor in your sight, please let me slip away and see my brethren.' Therefore he has not come to the king's table."

**20:30** Then Saul's anger burned against Jonathan, and he said to him, "You son of a perverse, rebellious woman! Do I not know that you have chosen the son of Jesse to your own shame, and to the shame of your mother's nakedness?

**20:31** For as long as the son of Jesse lives upon the earth, neither you nor your kingdom shall be established. Now therefore, send and bring him to me, for he shall surely die!"

**20:32** And Jonathan answered Saul his father, and said to him, "Why should he be put to death? What has he done?"

**20:33** Then Saul hurled the spear at him to strike him; so Jonathan knew that it was determined by his father to put David to death.

**20:34** So Jonathan arose from the table in fierce anger, and ate no food on the second day of the month, for he was grieved for David, because his father had dishonored him.

**20:35** And it came to pass in the morning that Jonathan went out into the field at the time appointed with David, and a little lad was with him.

**20:36** Then he said to his lad, "Run, please find the arrows which I shoot." As the lad ran, he shot an arrow beyond him.

**20:37** And when the lad came to the place of the arrow which Jonathan had shot, Jonathan cried out after the lad and said, "Is not the arrow beyond you?"

**20:38** And Jonathan cried out after the lad, "Hurry, make haste, do not stand still!" So Jonathan's lad gathered up the arrows and came to his master.

**20:39** Now the lad knew nothing; only Jonathan and David knew the matter.

**20:40** Then Jonathan gave his weapons to his lad, and said to him, "Go, carry them to the city."

**20:41** As soon as the lad had gone, David arose from the south side, and fell on his face to the ground and bowed down three times. And they kissed one another and wept together, David even more.

**20:42** Then Jonathan said to David, "Go in peace, since we have both sworn in the name of the LORD, saying, 'The LORD shall be between me and you, and between my descendants and your descendants, forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — The qere reads the negative particle with the imperfect verb ("does nothing"), whereas the ketiv displays an older spelling of the negative with a perfect verb.
- **20:14** — The Masoretic Hebrew employs compressed negative syntax connecting Jonathan's request for covenantal lovingkindness during his own lifetime with its continuance to his house in verse fifteen.
- **20:24** — The qere reads the preposition "to" ("to the meal"), while the ketiv reads the closely related preposition "at" or "upon."
- **20:38** — The qere reads the plural "the arrows," matching verses twenty and thirty-six, whereas the ketiv reads the singular "the arrow," matching verses thirty-six and thirty-seven.
- **20:41** — "From the south side" faithfully renders the Masoretic Hebrew expression meaning "from beside the south."
