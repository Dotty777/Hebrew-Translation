## Text

**7:1** Now it came to pass in the fourth year of King Darius that the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, in Chislev.

**7:2** Then Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-Melech and his men to seek the favor of the LORD,

**7:3** to speak to the priests who belong to the house of the LORD of hosts, and to the prophets, saying, "Should I weep in the fifth month and abstain, as I have done these many years?"

**7:4** Then the word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying,

**7:5** "Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests, saying, 'When you fasted and lamented in the fifth and in the seventh months during these seventy years, did you indeed fast for Me—for Me?

**7:6** And when you ate and when you drank, were you not the ones who ate, and you the ones who drank?

**7:7** Should you not have heard the words which the LORD proclaimed through the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and at ease, with her cities around her, and the South and the Lowland were inhabited?'"

**7:8** Then the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, saying,

**7:9** "Thus says the LORD of hosts, saying, 'Administer true justice, and show lovingkindness and compassion, each man to his brother.

**7:10** Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the sojourner or the poor, and let none of you devise evil in your hearts against his brother.'

**7:11** But they refused to pay heed; they turned a stubborn shoulder and made their ears dull so that they would not hear.

**7:12** Indeed, they made their hearts as hard as flint, refusing to hear the law and the words which the LORD of hosts had sent by His Spirit through the former prophets. Therefore great wrath came from the LORD of hosts.

**7:13** Therefore it came to pass that just as He called and they did not hear, 'so they shall call and I will not hear,' says the LORD of hosts.

**7:14** 'And I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations which they did not know. Thus the land became desolate behind them, with no one passing through or returning; for they turned the pleasant land into a desolation.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Hebrew syntax allows reading Bethel either as the subject sending the delegation or as part of a compound personal name, Bethel-Sharezer; the Masoretic accentuation favors taking Bethel as the sender.
- **7:7** — "The South" translates the Hebrew Negev, and "the Lowland" translates the Shephelah, the western foothills of Judah.
- **7:12** — The Hebrew word rendered "flint" (shamir) refers to an exceedingly hard stone such as adamant or emery.
