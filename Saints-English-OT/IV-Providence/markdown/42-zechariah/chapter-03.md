## Text

**3:1** Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the Angel of the LORD, and Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him.

**3:2** And the LORD said to Satan, "The LORD rebuke you, Satan! Even the LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Is this not a firebrand snatched from the fire?"

**3:3** Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and was standing before the Angel.

**3:4** Then He answered and spoke to those who stood before Him, saying, "Take off the filthy garments from him." And to him He said, "See, I have removed your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you with stately robes."

**3:5** And I said, "Let them set a clean turban upon his head." So they set the clean turban upon his head and clothed him with garments, while the Angel of the LORD stood by.

**3:6** Then the Angel of the LORD solemnly charged Joshua, saying,

**3:7** "Thus says the LORD of hosts: If you will walk in My ways, and if you will keep My charge, then you shall also govern My house and also guard My courts, and I will grant you places to walk among these who stand here.

**3:8** Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, you and your companions who sit before you, for they are men of a sign; for behold, I am bringing forth My Servant the Branch.

**3:9** For behold, the stone that I have set before Joshua: upon one stone are seven eyes. Behold, I will engrave its inscription, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day.

**3:10** In that day, declares the LORD of hosts, every one of you shall invite his neighbor under the vine and under the fig tree."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Hebrew pairs the noun 'hassatan' ("Satan" or "the Adversary") with the cognate verb 'lesitno' ("to accuse him" or "to act as adversary against him").
- **3:5** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads first-person 'wa'omar' ("And I said"), portraying Zechariah himself speaking aloud in the heavenly council.
- **3:7** — The rare Hebrew form 'mahlekim' ("places to walk") denotes free right of access among the angelic attendants standing in the divine court.
- **3:8** — The Hebrew 'Tsemach' ("Branch" or "Shoot") is a royal messianic title echoing Jeremiah 23:5 and 33:15.
