## Text

**2:1** Then I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, there were four horns.

**2:2** And I said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem."

**2:3** Then the LORD showed me four craftsmen.

**2:4** And I said, "What are these coming to do?" So he spoke, saying, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, so that no man lifted up his head; yet these have come to terrify them, to cast down the horns of the nations that lifted up their horn against the land of Judah to scatter it."

**2:5** Then I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, a man with a measuring line in his hand.

**2:6** So I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see what is its breadth and what is its length."

**2:7** And behold, the angel who spoke with me went out, and another angel came out to meet him,

**2:8** and said to him, "Run, speak to that young man, saying, 'Jerusalem shall be inhabited as unwalled villages, because of the multitude of men and livestock in its midst.

**2:9** For I, declares the LORD, will be to it a wall of fire all around, and I will be the glory in its midst.'"

**2:10** "Up! Up! Flee from the land of the north," declares the LORD, "for I have spread you abroad like the four winds of the heavens," declares the LORD.

**2:11** "Up, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon."

**2:12** For thus says the LORD of hosts: "After glory He has sent Me to the nations that plunder you; for whoever touches you touches the apple of His eye.

**2:13** For behold, I will shake My hand over them, and they shall become plunder for their servants. Then you shall know that the LORD of hosts has sent Me."

**2:14** "Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion! For behold, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst," declares the LORD.

**2:15** "And many nations shall be joined to the LORD in that day, and they shall become My people. And I will dwell in your midst, and you shall know that the LORD of hosts has sent Me to you."

**2:16** And the LORD shall inherit Judah as His portion in the holy land, and shall again choose Jerusalem.

**2:17** Be silent, all flesh, before the LORD, for He has roused Himself from His holy habitation.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Following the Masoretic Hebrew versification, chapter 2 begins with the vision of the four horns and four craftsmen; verses 2:1-4 correspond to 1:18-21 and verses 2:5-17 correspond to 2:1-13 in many English Bibles.
- **2:12** — The terse Hebrew phrase 'achar kavod' ("After glory") is variously understood as "following His manifestation of glory" or "for the sake of His honor."
- **2:17** — The interjection 'has' ("Be silent") is a solemn liturgical summons to reverent stillness before the LORD's decisive action.
