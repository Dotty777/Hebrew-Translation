## Text

**6:1** Then I turned and lifted up my eyes, and looked, and behold, four chariots were coming forth from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**6:2** With the first chariot were red horses, and with the second chariot black horses,

**6:3** with the third chariot white horses, and with the fourth chariot dappled horses, strong steeds.

**6:4** Then I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**6:5** And the angel answered and said to me, "These are the four spirits of heaven, going forth after presenting themselves before the Lord of all the earth.

**6:6** The one with the black horses is going forth to the north country, and the white ones have gone forth after them, while the dappled ones have gone forth to the south country."

**6:7** Then the strong steeds went forth and sought to go, to walk to and fro through the earth. And He said, "Go, walk to and fro through the earth." So they walked to and fro through the earth.

**6:8** Then He called out to me and spoke to me, saying, "See, those who go forth to the north country have given rest to My Spirit in the north country."

**6:9** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**6:10** "Take from the exiles—from Heldai, from Tobijah, and from Jedaiah—and come on that same day, and enter the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, who have come from Babylon.

**6:11** Take silver and gold, make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

**6:12** And speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: Behold, a man whose name is Branch! From his own place he shall branch out, and he shall build the temple of the LORD.

**6:13** Indeed, he shall build the temple of the LORD, and he shall bear majesty, and shall sit and rule upon his throne. And he shall be a priest upon his throne, and the counsel of peace shall be between the two of them.'

**6:14** Now the crowns shall be for Helem, for Tobijah, for Jedaiah, and for Hen the son of Zephaniah, as a memorial in the temple of the LORD.

**6:15** And those who are far off shall come and build in the temple of the LORD. Then you shall know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you. And this shall come to pass if you diligently obey the voice of the LORD your God."

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The Hebrew adjective rendered "strong steeds" here and in verse 7 denotes strength, though some interpreters take it as a horse color such as bay or reddish.
- **6:5** — The Hebrew noun rendered "spirits" can also mean "winds."
- **6:13** — The Hebrew clause may also be translated "there shall be a priest beside his throne," so that "the two of them" refers either to the royal and priestly offices united in one figure or to the Branch and the priest together.
- **6:14** — The plural noun "crowns" is paired with a singular verb in Hebrew, suggesting an elaborate composite crown. The Masoretic Text preserves the forms Helem and Hen here where verse 10 reads Heldai and Josiah.
