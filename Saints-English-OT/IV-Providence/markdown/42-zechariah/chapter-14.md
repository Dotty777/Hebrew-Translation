## Text

**14:1** Behold, a day is coming for the LORD, and your spoil shall be divided in your midst.

**14:2** For I will gather all the nations against Jerusalem to battle; and the city shall be captured, the houses plundered, and the women violated. Half of the city shall go forth into exile, but the remnant of the people shall not be cut off from the city.

**14:3** Then the LORD shall go forth and fight against those nations, as on the day He fights in the day of battle.

**14:4** And in that day His feet shall stand upon the Mount of Olives, which lies before Jerusalem on the east. And the Mount of Olives shall be split in half from east to west into an exceedingly great valley; and half of the mountain shall move toward the north, and half of it toward the south.

**14:5** And you shall flee through the valley of My mountains, for the valley of the mountains shall reach to Azal; yes, you shall flee just as you fled from before the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. Then the LORD my God shall come, and all the holy ones with You.

**14:6** And it shall come to pass in that day that there shall be no light, neither brightness nor frost.

**14:7** And it shall be one day, known to the LORD—neither day nor night—and it shall come to pass that at evening time there shall be light.

**14:8** And it shall come to pass in that day that living waters shall flow forth from Jerusalem, half of them toward the eastern sea and half of them toward the western sea; in summer and in winter it shall be so.

**14:9** And the LORD shall become King over all the earth; in that day the LORD shall be one, and His name one.

**14:10** All the land shall be turned into a plain from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem; and Jerusalem shall be lifted up and abide in her place, from the Gate of Benjamin to the place of the First Gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's winepresses.

**14:11** And people shall dwell in her, and there shall no longer be utter destruction; and Jerusalem shall dwell in security.

**14:12** And this shall be the plague with which the LORD shall strike all the peoples who have waged war against Jerusalem: their flesh shall rot away while they still stand upon their feet, their eyes shall rot in their sockets, and their tongues shall rot in their mouths.

**14:13** And it shall come to pass in that day that a great tumult from the LORD shall be among them; and each man shall seize the hand of his neighbor, and his hand shall rise up against the hand of his neighbor.

**14:14** And Judah also shall fight at Jerusalem; and the wealth of all the surrounding nations shall be gathered together—gold and silver and garments in exceedingly great abundance.

**14:15** And thus shall be the plague upon the horse, the mule, the camel, and the donkey, and upon all the beasts that shall be in those camps, even as this plague.

**14:16** And it shall come to pass that all who remain of all the nations that came against Jerusalem shall go up year by year to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, and to observe the Feast of Tabernacles.

**14:17** And it shall come to pass that whoever among the families of the earth does not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, upon them there shall be no rain.

**14:18** And if the family of Egypt does not go up and does not enter, then upon them there shall be no rain; there shall be the plague with which the LORD shall strike the nations that do not go up to observe the Feast of Tabernacles.

**14:19** This shall be the punishment of Egypt and the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to observe the Feast of Tabernacles.

**14:20** In that day there shall be upon the bells of the horses, "HOLINESS TO THE LORD." And the pots in the house of the LORD shall be like the basins before the altar.

**14:21** And every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah shall be holy to the LORD of hosts; and all those who sacrifice shall come and take of them and boil in them. And there shall no longer be a Canaanite in the house of the LORD of hosts in that day.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Masoretic qere reads tishakhavnah ("shall be lain with," a euphemism for being violated), substituted in reading for the coarser ketiv verb of the same meaning.
- **14:5** — The Masoretic Text shifts abruptly to direct second-person address ("with You") referring to the LORD; "holy ones" denotes the heavenly host attending His coming.
- **14:6** — The Masoretic qere reads the noun veqippaon ("and frost" or "congealing") alongside yeqarot ("brightness"), whereas the ketiv reads the verb yiqpaun ("the bright luminaries shall congeal/diminish").
- **14:18** — The Hebrew phrase velo alehem ("and not upon them") is elliptical, supplying "rain" from verse seventeen before declaring that the plague will nevertheless strike Egypt.
- **14:21** — Hebrew kenaani literally means "Canaanite," a term also used in biblical Hebrew to mean "trader" or "merchant."
