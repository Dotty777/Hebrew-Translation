## Text

**1:1** In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet, saying,

**1:2** "The LORD was fiercely angry with your fathers.

**1:3** Therefore say to them, 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: Return to Me, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will return to you, says the LORD of hosts.

**1:4** Do not be like your fathers, to whom the former prophets cried out, saying, "Thus says the LORD of hosts: Turn now from your evil ways and your evil deeds." Yet they did not listen nor give heed to Me, declares the LORD.

**1:5** Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, do they live forever?

**1:6** Yet My words and My statutes, which I commanded My servants the prophets, did they not overtake your fathers? So they repented and said, "Just as the LORD of hosts purposed to do to us, according to our ways and according to our deeds, so He has dealt with us."'"

**1:7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet, saying:

**1:8** I saw by night, and behold, a man was riding upon a red horse, standing among the myrtle trees in the deep hollow; and behind him were horses: red, sorrel, and white.

**1:9** Then I said, "My lord, what are these?" And the angel who spoke with me said to me, "I will show you what these are."

**1:10** And the man who stood among the myrtle trees answered and said, "These are the ones whom the LORD has sent to walk to and fro through the earth."

**1:11** Then they answered the Angel of the LORD, who stood among the myrtle trees, and said, "We have walked to and fro through the earth, and behold, all the earth is sitting still and at rest."

**1:12** Then the Angel of the LORD answered and said, "O LORD of hosts, how long will You not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which You have had indignation these seventy years?"

**1:13** And the LORD answered the angel who spoke with me with good words, words of comfort.

**1:14** So the angel who spoke with me said to me, "Proclaim, saying, 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: I am zealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with great zeal.

**1:15** And I am exceedingly angry with the nations that are at ease; for I was only a little angry, yet they furthered the calamity.

**1:16** Therefore thus says the LORD: I have returned to Jerusalem with mercy; My house shall be built in it, declares the LORD of hosts, and a measuring line shall be stretched out over Jerusalem.'

**1:17** Proclaim yet again, saying, 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: My cities shall again overflow with prosperity; and the LORD will again comfort Zion, and will again choose Jerusalem.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Qere reads 'uma'alelekem' ("and your deeds"), whereas the Ketiv has the longer orthographic form 'uma'alilekem' with the same meaning.
- **1:8** — The Hebrew 'metsulah' denotes a low-lying hollow, glen, or ravine, and 'seruqqim' refers to sorrel or reddish-brown horses.
- **1:16** — The Qere reads 'weqaw' ("and a measuring line"), while the Ketiv has the alternate spelling 'weqaweh' with the same sense.
