## Text

**9:1** The burden of the word of the LORD is against the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is its resting place—for the eye of mankind and of all the tribes of Israel is toward the LORD—

**9:2** and against Hamath also, which borders upon it, and against Tyre and Sidon, though they are exceedingly wise.

**9:3** Tyre has built herself a stronghold, and heaped up silver like the dust, and fine gold like the mire of the streets.

**9:4** Behold, the Lord will dispossess her and strike down her power in the sea, and she herself shall be consumed by fire.

**9:5** Ashkelon shall see it and fear; Gaza also shall writhe in great anguish, and Ekron, for her hope has been put to shame. The king shall perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall not be inhabited.

**9:6** A mixed people shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.

**9:7** I will remove his blood from his mouth and his abominations from between his teeth. Then he also shall remain as a remnant for our God; he shall be like a clan in Judah, and Ekron like a Jebusite.

**9:8** Then I will encamp at My house as a guard against any who pass by and return, and no oppressor shall overrun them anymore; for now I have seen with My own eyes.

**9:9** Rejoice exceedingly, O daughter of Zion! Shout aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem! Behold, your King comes to you; righteous and victorious is He, humble and mounted upon a donkey, even upon a colt, the foal of a she-donkey.

**9:10** I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem, and the bow of war shall be cut off. He shall speak peace to the nations, and His dominion shall extend from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth.

**9:11** As for you also, because of the blood of your covenant, I have set your captives free from the pit in which there is no water.

**9:12** Return to the stronghold, O prisoners of hope! Even this very day I declare that I will restore double to you.

**9:13** For I have bent Judah for Myself as a bow and fitted Ephraim to it; I will rouse your sons, O Zion, against your sons, O Greece, and I will make you like the sword of a mighty warrior.

**9:14** Then the LORD will appear over them, and His arrow will flash forth like lightning; the Lord GOD will sound the trumpet and march in the whirlwinds of the south.

**9:15** The LORD of hosts will shield them; they shall devour and subdue the slingstones. They shall drink and roar as with wine, and they shall be filled like the sacrificial bowls, like the corners of the altar.

**9:16** On that day the LORD their God will save them as the flock of His people; for like the jewels of a crown, they shall gleam over His land.

**9:17** For how great is His goodness, and how great is His beauty! Grain shall make the young men flourish, and new wine the maidens.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Literally "to the LORD belongs the eye of mankind," expressing either that mankind's eyes are directed toward the LORD or that the LORD's watchful eye is upon all humanity and Israel.
- **9:6** — The Hebrew term mamzer denotes one of illegitimate or mixed birth, used here for a foreign or mixed population settling in Ashdod.
- **9:9** — The Hebrew participle rendered "victorious" (nosha) literally means "saved" or "endowed with salvation," portraying the King as delivered by God and bringing deliverance.
- **9:13** — The Hebrew name Javan is the traditional biblical designation for Greece and the Ionian peoples.
- **9:17** — The masculine singular pronoun "His" may refer either to the LORD Himself or to His restored people and land described in the preceding verse.
