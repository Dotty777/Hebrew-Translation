## Text

**13:1** In that day there shall be a fountain opened for the house of David and for the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for sin and for impurity.

**13:2** "And it shall come to pass in that day," declares the LORD of hosts, "that I will cut off the names of the idols from the land, and they shall be remembered no more; and I will also cause the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness to depart from the land."

**13:3** And it shall come to pass, if any man still prophesies, then his father and his mother who brought him forth shall say to him, "You shall not live, for you have spoken falsehood in the name of the LORD." And his father and his mother who brought him forth shall pierce him through when he prophesies.

**13:4** And it shall come to pass in that day that the prophets shall be ashamed, each man of his vision when he prophesies; neither shall they wear a mantle of hair in order to deceive.

**13:5** But he shall say, "I am no prophet; I am a man who tills the ground, for a man acquired me from my youth."

**13:6** And one shall say to him, "What are these wounds between your hands?" And he shall say, "Those with which I was struck in the house of those who love me."

**13:7** "Awake, O sword, against My shepherd, and against the man who is My associate," declares the LORD of hosts. "Strike the shepherd, and the flock shall be scattered; and I will turn My hand against the little ones."

**13:8** "And it shall come to pass in all the land," declares the LORD, "that two parts in it shall be cut off and perish, but the third shall remain in it.

**13:9** And I will bring the third part into the fire, and refine them as silver is refined, and test them as gold is tested. They shall call upon My name, and I will answer them. I will say, 'They are My people,' and they shall say, 'The LORD is my God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — The rare Hiphil verb hiqnani literally means "acquired me" or "made me a bondservant," indicating lifelong servitude to a landowner; some traditions understand it as "taught me to tend livestock."
- **13:6** — Literally "between your hands," a Hebrew idiom referring to the chest, back, or upper body.
- **13:8** — Hebrew pi-shenayim literally means "a portion of two," denoting two-thirds in contrast with "the third" that remains.
