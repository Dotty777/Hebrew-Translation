## Text

**12:1** The burden of the word of the LORD concerning Israel. Thus declares the LORD, who stretches out the heavens, lays the foundation of the earth, and forms the spirit of man within him:

**12:2** "Behold, I am making Jerusalem a cup of staggering to all the surrounding peoples, and Judah also shall be in the siege against Jerusalem.

**12:3** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will make Jerusalem a burdensome stone for all the peoples; all who lift it shall grievously lacerate themselves, and all the nations of the earth shall gather against her.

**12:4** In that day," declares the LORD, "I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness; I will open My eyes upon the house of Judah, and every horse of the peoples I will strike with blindness.

**12:5** Then the chiefs of Judah shall say in their heart, 'The inhabitants of Jerusalem are a strength to me through the LORD of hosts, their God.'

**12:6** In that day I will make the chiefs of Judah like a firepot among wood and like a flaming torch among sheaves; they shall devour all the surrounding peoples on the right hand and on the left, while Jerusalem shall again dwell in her own place, in Jerusalem.

**12:7** The LORD will save the tents of Judah first, so that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem may not be exalted above Judah.

**12:8** In that day the LORD will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem; the feeble one among them in that day shall be like David, and the house of David shall be like God, like the Angel of the LORD before them.

**12:9** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will seek to destroy all the nations that come against Jerusalem.

**12:10** And I will pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and supplications; and they shall look upon Me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for Him as one mourns for an only child, and grieve bitterly for Him as one grieves bitterly for a firstborn.

**12:11** In that day the mourning in Jerusalem shall be great, like the mourning of Hadad-Rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.

**12:12** And the land shall mourn, each family by itself: the family of the house of David by itself, and their wives by themselves; the family of the house of Nathan by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**12:13** the family of the house of Levi by itself, and their wives by themselves; the family of the Shimeites by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**12:14** all the remaining families, each family by itself, and their wives by themselves."

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Hebrew noun translated "burden" denotes a weighty prophetic oracle or solemn pronouncement.
- **12:2** — The concise Hebrew phrasing of the second clause indicates that Judah as well will be caught up in the siege directed against Jerusalem.
- **12:10** — The Masoretic Text explicitly reads first-person "look upon Me" before shifting to third-person "mourn for Him," closely identifying the LORD with the one who is pierced.
