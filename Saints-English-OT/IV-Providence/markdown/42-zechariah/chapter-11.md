## Text

**11:1** Open your doors, O Lebanon, that fire may devour your cedars.

**11:2** Wail, O cypress, for the cedar has fallen, because the mighty ones are laid waste! Wail, O oaks of Bashan, for the impenetrable forest has come down!

**11:3** The sound of the wailing of the shepherds, for their glory is laid waste! The sound of the roaring of young lions, for the pride of the Jordan is laid waste!

**11:4** Thus says the LORD my God: "Shepherd the flock for slaughter,

**11:5** whose buyers slay them and are not held guilty, and those who sell them say, 'Blessed be the LORD, for I have become rich!' and their own shepherds have no pity on them.

**11:6** For I will no longer have pity on the inhabitants of the land," declares the LORD. "Behold, I am delivering mankind, every man into the hand of his neighbor and into the hand of his king; they shall crush the land, and I will not rescue them from their hand."

**11:7** So I shepherded the flock for slaughter, indeed the afflicted of the flock. And I took for myself two staffs: the one I called Favor, and the other I called Union; and I shepherded the flock.

**11:8** I cut off the three shepherds in one month; my soul grew impatient with them, and their soul also detested me.

**11:9** Then I said, "I will not shepherd you. Let what is dying die, and let what is to be cut off be cut off; and let those that remain each devour the flesh of her neighbor."

**11:10** And I took my staff Favor and broke it in two, to annul my covenant which I had made with all the peoples.

**11:11** So it was annulled on that day, and thus the afflicted of the flock who were watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD.

**11:12** Then I said to them, "If it is good in your eyes, give me my wages; and if not, refrain." So they weighed out as my wages thirty pieces of silver.

**11:13** And the LORD said to me, "Cast it to the potter—that magnificent price at which I was valued by them!" So I took the thirty pieces of silver and cast them into the house of the LORD, to the potter.

**11:14** Then I broke my second staff, Union, in two, to annul the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

**11:15** And the LORD said to me, "Take for yourself once more the equipment of a foolish shepherd.

**11:16** For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not attend to the perishing, nor seek the young, nor heal the injured, nor nourish the sound; instead, he will devour the flesh of the fat ones and tear off their hooves."

**11:17** Woe to the worthless shepherd who forsakes the flock! A sword be upon his arm and upon his right eye! His arm shall completely wither, and his right eye shall be utterly darkened!

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the Ketiv reads "fortified" or "inaccessible," while the Qere reads a form associated with "vintage" or "impenetrable"; the translation follows the Qere in the sense of a dense, impenetrable forest.
- **11:7** — The Masoretic Text reads "indeed, the afflicted of the flock" (matching verse 11). The symbolic staff names Favor and Union represent the Hebrew terms for "delight" or "beauty" and "bonds."
- **11:13** — The Masoretic Text reads "the potter." The phrase "that magnificent price" is sharp divine irony for thirty pieces of silver, the compensation paid for a gored slave in Exodus 21:32.
- **11:17** — The Hebrew participles for "shepherd" and "who forsakes" carry poetic archaic endings rather than first-person singular possessive suffixes.
