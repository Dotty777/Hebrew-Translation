## Text

**2:1** Behold, upon the mountains are the feet of him who brings good tidings, who proclaims peace! Celebrate your feasts, O Judah, fulfill your vows; for the wicked one shall never again pass through you; he is utterly cut off.

**2:2** The scatterer has come up against your face! Guard the rampart, watch the road, fortify the loins, make your strength exceedingly firm!

**2:3** For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob, like the majesty of Israel; for the ravagers have emptied them out and ruined their vine branches.

**2:4** The shield of his mighty men is dyed red, the men of valor are clad in scarlet; the chariots gleam with fiery steel in the day of his preparation, and the cypress spears are brandished.

**2:5** The chariots race madly through the streets, they rush to and fro in the broad squares; their appearance is like blazing torches, they dart about like lightning.

**2:6** He summons his nobles; they stumble in their march. They hasten to her wall, and the protective shelter is set in place.

**2:7** The gates of the rivers are flung open, and the palace melts away.

**2:8** It is decreed: she is stripped bare, she is carried away; and her maidservants moan like the voice of doves, beating upon their breasts.

**2:9** Nineveh has been like a pool of water from of old, yet they are fleeing away. "Stand! Stand!"—yet no one turns back.

**2:10** Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold! There is no end to the treasure, wealth from every precious vessel.

**2:11** Desolate, empty, and laid waste! Hearts melt and knees tremble, anguish seizes all loins, and the faces of them all turn pale.

**2:12** Where is the den of the lions, and the feeding ground of the young lions, where the lion and the lioness walked, along with the lion's cub, and none made them afraid?

**2:13** The lion tore enough for his cubs and strangled prey for his lionesses; he filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh.

**2:14** "Behold, I am against you," declares the LORD of hosts, "and I will burn her chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions; I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall be heard no more."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — In Masoretic versification, chapter 2 begins here, corresponding to 1:15 in many English versions. The qere shortens the infinitive before the maqqef hyphen without changing the sense to pass through.
- **2:4** — The Hebrew term peladot is a rare word denoting steel or iron chariot fittings, and beroshim, literally cypresses, poetically refers to cypress-shafted spears.
- **2:6** — The qere reads the singular bahalikhatam, in their march, whereas the ketiv reads the plural form, in their marches or courses.
- **2:8** — The Hebrew wehutzzav is rendered here as a passive verb meaning It is decreed; some interpreters take it instead as a proper name or royal title, Huzzab.
- **2:14** — The Masoretic Text reads her chariots with a third-person feminine suffix, a poetic shift of person amidst the direct second-person address to Nineveh.
