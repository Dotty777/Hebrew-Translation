## Text

**3:1** Woe to the city of bloodshed! She is wholly filled with deceit and plunder; prey never departs.

**3:2** The crack of the whip and the rattle of the wheel, the galloping steed and the bounding chariot!

**3:3** Horsemen charging, the flash of the sword and the glitter of the spear, a multitude of the slain and a great heap of carcasses! There is no end to the dead bodies, and they stumble over their corpses.

**3:4** Because of the multitude of the harlotries of the harlot, fair and alluring, the mistress of sorceries, who sells nations through her harlotries and families through her sorceries.

**3:5** "Behold, I am against you," declares the LORD of hosts, "and I will uncover your skirts over your face; I will show the nations your nakedness and the kingdoms your disgrace.

**3:6** I will cast abominable filth upon you, treat you with contempt, and set you as a spectacle.

**3:7** And it shall come to pass that all who look upon you will flee from you and say, 'Nineveh is laid waste! Who will bemoan her?' Where shall I seek comforters for you?"

**3:8** Are you better than No-Amon, that sat among the rivers, with waters all around her, whose rampart was the sea, and whose wall was of the sea?

**3:9** Cush was her strength, and Egypt also, without limit; Put and the Lubim were your helpers.

**3:10** Yet even she went into exile, into captivity; her little ones also were dashed to pieces at the head of every street. They cast lots for her honorable men, and all her great men were bound in chains.

**3:11** You also shall become drunken; you shall be hidden away. You also shall seek a stronghold from the enemy.

**3:12** All your fortresses are fig trees with first-ripe figs: if they are shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater.

**3:13** Behold, your people in your midst are women! The gates of your land are opened wide to your enemies; fire has devoured your bars.

**3:14** Draw water for the siege, strengthen your fortresses! Go into the clay and tread the mortar, take hold of the brick mold!

**3:15** There the fire shall devour you, the sword shall cut you off; it shall consume you like the creeping locust. Multiply yourself like the creeping locust, multiply yourself like the swarming locust!

**3:16** You have multiplied your merchants more than the stars of the heavens; the creeping locust plunders and flies away.

**3:17** Your princes are like the swarming locusts, and your marshals like clouds of grasshoppers that settle on the stone walls on a cold day; when the sun rises, they flee away, and their place is not known, where they are.

**3:18** Your shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria; your nobles lie at rest. Your people are scattered upon the mountains, and there is no one to gather them.

**3:19** There is no alleviation for your injury; your wound is grievous. All who hear the news of you clap their hands over you, for upon whom has your wickedness not passed continually?

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The qere reads wekhashelu, and they stumble, continuing the description with a prefixed conjunction, whereas the ketiv reads yikkashelu, they stumble.
- **3:8** — No-Amon is the Hebrew name for Thebes, the famed capital of Upper Egypt straddling the Nile and its canals, which fell to Assyria in 663 BC.
- **3:9** — The Masoretic Text reads your helpers with a second-person feminine suffix, momentarily addressing No-Amon directly before returning to third-person narration in verse 10.
