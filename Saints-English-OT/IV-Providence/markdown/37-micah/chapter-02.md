## Text

**2:1** Woe to those who devise iniquity and plot evil upon their beds! At the light of morning they carry it out, because it is in the power of their hand.

**2:2** They covet fields and seize them by violence, and houses, and carry them off; thus they oppress a man and his household, a man and his inheritance.

**2:3** Therefore thus says the LORD: "Behold, against this family I am devising calamity, from which you shall not remove your necks; nor shall you walk haughtily, for it shall be a time of calamity."

**2:4** In that day one shall take up a taunt against you and wail with a bitter lamentation, saying, "We are utterly ruined! He changes the portion of my people; how He removes it from me! To the rebellious He divides our fields!"

**2:5** Therefore you shall have no one to cast the measuring line by lot in the assembly of the LORD.

**2:6** "Do not prophesy!" thus they prophesy. They shall not prophesy of these things; disgrace shall not depart.

**2:7** Should it be said, O house of Jacob, "Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient? Are these His doings?" Do not My words do good to him who walks uprightly?

**2:8** Lately My people have risen up like an enemy; you strip the splendid robe from off the garment of those who pass by securely, as men returning from war.

**2:9** The women of My people you drive out from their pleasant houses; from their little children you take away My splendor forever.

**2:10** Arise and depart, for this is not the resting place; because of uncleanness it shall bring destruction, even a grievous destruction.

**2:11** If a man walking in wind and falsehood should tell lies, saying, "I will prophesy to you of wine and strong drink," he would be the very prophet for this people!

**2:12** I will surely assemble all of you, O Jacob; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel. I will set them together like sheep in a fold, like a flock in the midst of its pasture; they shall resound loudly because of men.

**2:13** The one who breaks through goes up before them; they break through, pass through the gate, and go out by it; their king passes on before them, with the LORD at their head.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — "A bitter lamentation" renders the emphatic Hebrew wordplay nehi nihyah (literally "a wailing of what has come to pass"), stressing total devastation.
- **2:6** — The Hebrew verb nataph literally means "to drip" and is used idiomatically for speaking prophetically or preaching; yissag literally means "shall turn back" or "depart," though some take it as "overtake."
- **2:12** — The Hebrew botsrah means "enclosure" or "sheepfold," though it may alternatively be read as the place name Bozrah, noted for its pastures and flocks.
