## Text

**4:1** And it shall come to pass in the latter days that the mountain of the house of the LORD shall be firmly established at the summit of the mountains, and it shall be lifted up above the hills; and peoples shall stream to it.

**4:2** And many nations shall come and say, "Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, and to the house of the God of Jacob, so that He may instruct us in His ways, and that we may walk in His paths." For from Zion the law shall go forth, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

**4:3** He shall judge between many peoples and render decisions for mighty nations afar off; they shall hammer their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation shall not lift up sword against nation, nor shall they learn warfare anymore.

**4:4** And every man shall sit beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, with no one to make them afraid; for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.

**4:5** For all the peoples walk, every one in the name of his god; yet we shall walk in the name of the LORD our God forever and ever.

**4:6** "In that day," declares the LORD, "I will gather the lame, and assemble the outcast, even those whom I have afflicted.

**4:7** And I will make the lame a remnant, and the one cast far off a mighty nation; and the LORD shall reign over them on Mount Zion from this time forth and forevermore."

**4:8** And as for you, O tower of the flock, stronghold of the daughter of Zion, to you it shall come; indeed, the former dominion shall return, the kingdom to the daughter of Jerusalem.

**4:9** Now why do you cry out aloud? Is there no king in your midst? Has your counselor perished, that anguish has seized you like a woman in labor?

**4:10** Writhe and strain in labor, O daughter of Zion, like a woman giving birth; for now you shall go forth from the city and dwell in the open field, and you shall go as far as Babylon. There you shall be delivered; there the LORD shall redeem you from the hand of your enemies.

**4:11** And now many nations have assembled against you, saying, "Let her be defiled, and let our eyes gloat over Zion."

**4:12** Yet they do not know the thoughts of the LORD, nor do they discern His counsel; for He has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.

**4:13** Arise and thresh, O daughter of Zion; for I will make your horn iron, and I will make your hooves bronze. You shall crush many peoples in pieces, and I will devote their gain to the LORD, and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

**4:14** Now muster yourself in troops, O daughter of troops; he has laid siege against us. With a rod they shall strike the judge of Israel upon the cheek.

## Translator Notes

- **4:8** — Tower of the flock translates Hebrew Migdal-Eder, and stronghold translates Ophel, both used here as poetic designations of Zion's royal eminence.
- **4:13** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads first-person singular, I will devote, using the verb for setting spoils apart exclusively to the LORD.
- **4:14** — The Hebrew pairs a verb that can mean either muster in troops or gash oneself in mourning with daughter of troops. In many English versions this verse is numbered as 5:1.
