## Text

**6:1** Hear now what the LORD says: "Arise, plead your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice."

**6:2** Hear, O mountains, the controversy of the LORD, and you enduring foundations of the earth; for the LORD has a controversy with His people, and He will contend with Israel.

**6:3** "O My people, what have I done to you, and how have I wearied you? Testify against Me!

**6:4** For I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and from the house of bondage I redeemed you; and I sent before you Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

**6:5** O My people, remember now what Balak king of Moab devised, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him, from Shittim to Gilgal, that you may know the righteous acts of the LORD."

**6:6** "With what shall I come before the LORD, and bow myself before the God on high? Shall I come before Him with burnt offerings, with calves a year old?

**6:7** Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousands of rivers of oil? Shall I offer my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?"

**6:8** He has declared to you, O man, what is good; and what does the LORD require from you except to practice justice, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?

**6:9** The voice of the LORD cries out to the city—and sound wisdom shall see Your name: Hear the rod, and the One who has appointed it!

**6:10** "Are there still treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and a scant measure that is accursed?

**6:11** Shall I count pure the one with wicked scales, and with a bag of deceitful weights?

**6:12** For her rich men are full of violence, her inhabitants speak falsehood, and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.

**6:13** Therefore I also have made you sick by striking you, making you desolate on account of your sins.

**6:14** You shall eat, yet you shall not be satisfied, and your hunger shall remain within you; you shall carry away, yet you shall not preserve, and whatever you do preserve I will give over to the sword.

**6:15** You shall sow, yet you shall not reap; you shall tread the olives, yet you shall not anoint yourself with oil; and sweet wine, yet you shall not drink wine.

**6:16** For the statutes of Omri are diligently kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and you walk in their counsels; therefore I will give you over to desolation, and her inhabitants to hissing, and you shall bear the reproach of My people."

## Translator Notes

- **6:9** — Shall see Your name follows the Masoretic vocalization from the verb meaning to see, though some ancient versions and commentators connect the consonants with fearing the LORD's name.
- **6:13** — Have made you sick translates the Masoretic verb from the root meaning to afflict with sickness, indicating a grievous blow, rather than the variant reading have begun found in some ancient versions.
- **6:14** — Hunger renders a rare Hebrew noun occurring only here in Scripture, denoting inward emptiness or hollowness.
- **6:16** — My people follows the Masoretic Hebrew text directly.
