## Text

**5:1** Yet you, O Bethlehem Ephrathah, though you are small among the thousands of Judah, out of you shall come forth for Me One who shall be Ruler in Israel, whose goings forth are from ancient times, from the days of eternity.

**5:2** Therefore He shall give them up until the time when she who is in labor has given birth; then the rest of His brethren shall return to the children of Israel.

**5:3** And He shall stand and shepherd in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD His God; and they shall abide, for now He shall be great even to the ends of the earth.

**5:4** And this One shall be peace. When the Assyrian comes into our land, and when he treads in our citadels, then we shall raise up against him seven shepherds and eight princes of men.

**5:5** They shall shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod at its entrances; thus He shall deliver us from the Assyrian when he comes into our land and when he treads within our borders.

**5:6** Then the remnant of Jacob shall be in the midst of many peoples like dew from the LORD, like showers upon the grass, which does not wait for man, nor tarry for the sons of men.

**5:7** And the remnant of Jacob shall be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep, which, when it passes through, tramples down and tears in pieces, and there is no one to deliver.

**5:8** Your hand shall be lifted high over your adversaries, and all your enemies shall be cut off.

**5:9** "And it shall come to pass in that day," declares the LORD, "that I will cut off your horses from your midst and destroy your chariots.

**5:10** I will cut off the cities of your land and tear down all your strongholds.

**5:11** I will cut off sorceries from your hand, and you shall have no soothsayers.

**5:12** I will also cut off your carved images and your sacred pillars from your midst, and you shall no longer bow down to the work of your hands.

**5:13** I will uproot your Asherah poles from your midst, and I will destroy your cities.

**5:14** And I will execute vengeance in anger and wrath upon the nations that have not obeyed."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Thousands reflects the Hebrew term denoting both numerical thousands and tribal clans or divisions of Judah. Verses 5:1 through 5:14 follow Masoretic numbering, corresponding to 5:2 through 5:15 in many English versions.
- **5:5** — At its entrances translates the literal Masoretic Hebrew; some interpreters connect the noun with drawn swords by comparison with Psalm 55:21.
