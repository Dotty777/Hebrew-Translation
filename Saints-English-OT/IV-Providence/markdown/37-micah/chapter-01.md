## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Micah the Moreshite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he beheld concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

**1:2** Hear, all you peoples; give ear, O earth, and all that fills it! Let the Lord GOD be a witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple.

**1:3** For behold, the LORD is coming forth from His place; He will descend and tread upon the high places of the earth.

**1:4** The mountains shall melt beneath Him, and the valleys shall be split apart, like wax before the fire, like waters poured down a steep descent.

**1:5** All this is because of the transgression of Jacob and because of the sins of the house of Israel. What is the transgression of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what are the high places of Judah? Are they not Jerusalem?

**1:6** Therefore I will make Samaria a heap of ruins in the open field, places for planting a vineyard; I will pour down her stones into the valley, and I will lay bare her foundations.

**1:7** All her carved images shall be beaten to pieces, all her hires shall be burned with fire, and all her idols I will lay desolate; for from the hire of a harlot she gathered them, and to the hire of a harlot they shall return.

**1:8** Because of this I will lament and wail; I will go barefoot and naked; I will raise a lamentation like the jackals and a mourning like the ostriches.

**1:9** For her wound is incurable; for it has come even to Judah; it has reached to the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem.

**1:10** Do not tell it in Gath; do not weep at all! In Beth-le-aphrah, roll yourself in the dust.

**1:11** Pass on your way, O inhabitant of Shaphir, in nakedness and shame! The inhabitant of Zaanan does not go forth; the lamentation of Beth-ezel shall take away from you its standing place.

**1:12** For the inhabitant of Maroth waits anxiously for good, because calamity has come down from the LORD to the gate of Jerusalem.

**1:13** Harness the chariot to the swift steeds, O inhabitant of Lachish—she was the beginning of sin to the daughter of Zion, for in you were found the transgressions of Israel.

**1:14** Therefore you shall give parting gifts to Moresheth-gath; the houses of Achzib shall prove a deception to the kings of Israel.

**1:15** Yet again I will bring a conqueror against you, O inhabitant of Mareshah; the glory of Israel shall come even to Adullam.

**1:16** Make yourself bald and shear your head for the children of your delight; widen your baldness like the vulture, for they have gone into exile from you.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Qere and Ketiv differ slightly in the spelling of the construct plural bamotei ("high places of"), without altering the meaning.
- **1:8** — The Qere reads sholal ("barefoot" or "stripped"), whereas the Ketiv reads sheilal; both describe the humbled state of a captive in mourning.
- **1:10** — The Qere reads the imperative hitpallashi ("roll yourself"), whereas the Ketiv reads the first-person form ("I have rolled myself"). This verse launches a sequence of Hebrew wordplays on town names, pairing Beth-le-aphrah ("House of Dust") with "dust."
- **1:14** — The name Achzib plays on the Hebrew word akhzav ("deception," used of a stream that runs dry), continuing Micah's laments over the Judean towns.
