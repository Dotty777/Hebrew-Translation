## Text

**3:1** And I said: "Hear now, O heads of Jacob, and you rulers of the house of Israel: Is it not for you to know justice?

**3:2** You who hate good and love evil, who tear their skin from off them and their flesh from off their bones,

**3:3** who also eat the flesh of my people, flay their skin from off them, shatter their bones, and chop them up as for the pot, like meat within a cauldron.

**3:4** Then they shall cry out to the LORD, but He will not answer them; He will even hide His face from them at that time, because they have made their deeds evil."

**3:5** Thus says the LORD: "Concerning the prophets who lead My people astray, who bite with their teeth and cry, 'Peace!'—yet against him who puts nothing into their mouths they consecrate war:

**3:6** therefore it shall be night to you without vision, and it shall be darkness to you without divination. The sun shall go down upon the prophets, and the day shall grow dark over them.

**3:7** The seers shall be put to shame, and the diviners confounded; indeed, they shall all cover their lips, for there is no answer from God."

**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power by the Spirit of the LORD, and with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his transgression and to Israel his sin.

**3:9** Hear this now, O heads of the house of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel, who abhor justice and pervert all that is upright,

**3:10** who build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with iniquity.

**3:11** Her heads judge for a bribe, her priests give instruction for a price, and her prophets practice divination for silver; yet they lean upon the LORD, saying, "Is not the LORD in our midst? No calamity shall come upon us."

**3:12** Therefore, on account of you, Zion shall be plowed like a field, Jerusalem shall become heaps of ruins, and the mountain of the house like the wooded heights.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Qere reads the masculine noun ra ("evil"), whereas the Ketiv has the feminine form raah; the meaning is the same.
- **3:5** — "Consecrate war" renders the Hebrew idiom qiddeshu milchamah, referring to the formal proclamation of war accompanied by sacred rites.
- **3:12** — "The mountain of the house" refers to the temple mount in Jerusalem; this exact oracle is later quoted by the elders of the land in Jeremiah 26:18.
