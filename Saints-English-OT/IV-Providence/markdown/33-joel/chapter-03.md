## Text

**3:1** And it shall be afterward that I will pour out My Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions.

**3:2** And even upon the manservants and upon the maidservants in those days I will pour out My Spirit.

**3:3** And I will display wonders in the heavens and on the earth: blood and fire and columns of smoke.

**3:4** The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the coming of the great and fearsome day of the LORD.

**3:5** And it shall be that everyone who calls upon the name of the LORD shall be delivered; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be deliverance, as the LORD has spoken, and among the survivors whom the LORD calls.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — In the Masoretic Text, Joel 3 consists of five verses (3:1–5), corresponding to Joel 2:28–32 in most English Bibles.
- **3:2** — The Masoretic Text reads "the manservants and the maidservants" without a possessive pronoun, unlike the Septuagint and Acts 2:18 ("My servants").
