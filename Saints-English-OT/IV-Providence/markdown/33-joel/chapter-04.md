## Text

**4:1** For behold, in those days and at that time, when I turn back the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,

**4:2** I will assemble all the nations and bring them down into the Valley of Jehoshaphat; and there I will enter into judgment with them concerning My people and My inheritance Israel, whom they scattered among the nations, and My land they divided.

**4:3** And for My people they cast lots; they gave a boy for a harlot, and sold a girl for wine, and drank.

**4:4** Moreover, what are you to Me, O Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you rendering a recompense to Me? And if you are retaliating against Me, swiftly and speedily I will return your recompense upon your own head.

**4:5** Because you have taken My silver and My gold, and have brought My finest treasures into your temples;

**4:6** and the children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem you have sold to the Greeks, so as to remove them far from their border.

**4:7** Behold, I am rousing them from the place to which you sold them, and I will return your recompense upon your own head.

**4:8** And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the children of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, to a distant nation; for the LORD has spoken.

**4:9** Proclaim this among the nations: Consecrate war! Rouse the mighty men! Let all the men of war draw near; let them come up.

**4:10** Hammer your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears; let the weak one say, "I am a mighty man."

**4:11** Hasten and come, all you nations on every side, and gather yourselves together. There bring down Your mighty ones, O LORD!

**4:12** Let the nations be roused and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat; for there I will sit to judge all the nations on every side.

**4:13** Thrust in the sickle, for the harvest has ripened. Come, tread, for the winepress is full; the vats overflow, for great is their wickedness.

**4:14** Multitudes, multitudes in the Valley of Decision! For near is the day of the LORD in the Valley of Decision.

**4:15** The sun and the moon grow dark, and the stars withdraw their radiance.

**4:16** And the LORD will roar from Zion, and from Jerusalem He will utter His voice, and the heavens and the earth will quake; yet the LORD is a refuge for His people and a stronghold for the children of Israel.

**4:17** Then you shall know that I am the LORD your God, who dwells in Zion, My holy mountain; and Jerusalem shall be holy, and strangers shall pass through her no more.

**4:18** And it shall come to pass in that day that the mountains shall drip with sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the streambeds of Judah shall run with water; and a fountain shall go forth from the house of the LORD and water the Valley of Shittim.

**4:19** Egypt shall become a desolation, and Edom shall become a wilderness of desolation, on account of the violence done against the children of Judah, because they have shed innocent blood in their land.

**4:20** Yet Judah shall be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation.

**4:21** And I will cleanse their blood which I have not cleansed; and the LORD dwells in Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Hebrew Qere reads the causative form meaning "I turn back" or "I restore," whereas the Ketiv reads the simple stem. The idiom "turn back the captivity" can also signify restoring the fortunes; Joel chapter 4 in the Masoretic Hebrew corresponds to chapter 3 in most English Bibles.
- **4:2** — The name Jehoshaphat means "the LORD judges," underscoring the scene of universal judgment in this valley, which is also called the Valley of Decision in verse 14.
- **4:9** — "Consecrate war" reflects the ancient Near Eastern and Israelite custom of ritual sanctification and sacrificial preparation prior to battle.
- **4:18** — "The Valley of Shittim" literally means "the Valley of Acacias," evoking an arid valley transformed by the waters issuing from the temple of the LORD.
- **4:21** — The Masoretic Hebrew uses the verb meaning "cleanse" or "acquit" (clearing from bloodguilt), whereas some ancient versions such as the Septuagint read a similar Hebrew root meaning "avenge."
