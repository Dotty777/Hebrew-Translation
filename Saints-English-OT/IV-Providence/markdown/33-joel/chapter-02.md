## Text

**2:1** Blow a trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm on My holy mountain! Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble, for the day of the LORD is coming, for it is near.

**2:2** A day of darkness and deep gloom, a day of clouds and thick darkness! Like the dawn spread over the mountains, a great and mighty people; there has never been the like of them from of old, nor shall there be again after them, through the years of generation after generation.

**2:3** A fire devours before them, and behind them a flame blazes; the land is like the garden of Eden before them, and behind them a desolate wilderness, and surely nothing escapes them.

**2:4** Their appearance is like the appearance of horses, and like horsemen, so they run.

**2:5** Like the noise of chariots they leap upon the tops of the mountains, like the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble, like a mighty people arrayed for battle.

**2:6** Before them the peoples are in anguish; all faces turn pale.

**2:7** Like mighty men they run; like men of war they scale the wall. Each one marches in his own course, and they do not swerve from their paths.

**2:8** They do not jostle one another; every man marches along his own highway. Though they plunge through the weapons, they are not cut off.

**2:9** They rush upon the city, they run along the wall, they climb up into the houses; they enter through the windows like a thief.

**2:10** The earth quakes before them, the heavens tremble; the sun and the moon grow dark, and the stars withdraw their brightness.

**2:11** And the LORD utters His voice before His army, for His camp is exceedingly great, and mighty is the one who executes His word. For the day of the LORD is great and exceedingly fearsome, and who can endure it?

**2:12** "Yet even now," declares the LORD, "return to Me with all your heart, with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning."

**2:13** And rend your heart and not your garments, and return to the LORD your God, for He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abundant in lovingkindness, and He relents concerning calamity.

**2:14** Who knows whether He will turn and relent, and leave a blessing behind Him—a grain offering and a drink offering for the LORD your God?

**2:15** Blow a trumpet in Zion, consecrate a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly.

**2:16** Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the elders, gather the little children and those who nurse at the breast; let the bridegroom go forth from his chamber, and the bride from her canopy.

**2:17** Between the vestibule and the altar let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, weep; and let them say, "Spare Your people, O LORD, and do not give Your heritage to reproach, that the nations should rule over them. Why should they say among the peoples, 'Where is their God?'"

**2:18** Then the LORD became zealous for His land and had compassion upon His people.

**2:19** The LORD answered and said to His people, "Behold, I am sending you the grain, the new wine, and the fresh oil, and you shall be satisfied with them; and I will no longer make you a reproach among the nations.

**2:20** I will remove the northerner far from you and drive him into a parched and desolate land, his vanguard into the eastern sea and his rear guard into the western sea; and his stench shall rise, and his foul odor shall ascend, for he has done great things.

**2:21** Do not fear, O land; be glad and rejoice, for the LORD has done great things!

**2:22** Do not fear, O beasts of the field, for the pastures of the wilderness have grown green; for the tree bears its fruit, and the fig tree and the vine yield their full strength.

**2:23** And you children of Zion, be glad and rejoice in the LORD your God; for He has given you the early rain in righteousness, and He has poured down for you the rain—the early rain and the latter rain—in the first month.

**2:24** The threshing floors shall be filled with grain, and the vats shall overflow with new wine and fresh oil.

**2:25** And I will restore to you the years that the swarming locust has eaten—the creeping locust, the stripping locust, and the gnawing locust—My great army which I sent among you.

**2:26** You shall eat in abundance and be satisfied, and praise the name of the LORD your God, who has dealt wondrously with you; and My people shall never be put to shame.

**2:27** Then you shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the LORD your God and there is no other; and My people shall never be put to shame."

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The rare Hebrew idiom qibbetsu fa'rur (cf. Nahum 2:10) literally speaks of faces gathering a flush or pallor, expressing sudden terror.
- **2:17** — The Hebrew verb mashal here may mean either "rule over" or "make a byword of" among the nations.
- **2:20** — The clause "for he has done great things" portrays the northern invader's arrogant presumption and stands in deliberate contrast to the same Hebrew phrase applied to the LORD in v. 21.
- **2:23** — Hebrew ha-moreh ("the early rain") can also mean "the teacher," yielding an alternate reading "the teacher of righteousness," though the parallel clause favors autumn rain; "in the first month" translates Hebrew ba-rishon ("in the first").
