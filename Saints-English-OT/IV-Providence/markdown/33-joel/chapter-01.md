## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Joel, the son of Pethuel.

**1:2** Hear this, O elders, and give ear, all inhabitants of the land: Has this happened in your days, or even in the days of your fathers?

**1:3** Recount it to your children, and let your children tell their children, and their children to another generation.

**1:4** What the shearing locust left, the swarming locust has eaten; and what the swarming locust left, the young locust has eaten; and what the young locust left, the devouring locust has eaten.

**1:5** Awake, O drunkards, and weep; wail, all who drink wine, over the sweet wine, because it has been cut off from your mouth.

**1:6** For a nation has come up against my land, mighty and beyond number; its teeth are the teeth of a lion, and it has the fangs of a fierce lion.

**1:7** It has turned my vine into a desolation and my fig tree into splinters; it has stripped it completely bare and cast it aside; its branches have turned white.

**1:8** Lament like a virgin girded with sackcloth for the husband of her youth.

**1:9** The grain offering and the drink offering have been cut off from the house of the LORD; the priests, the ministers of the LORD, mourn.

**1:10** The field is devastated, the ground mourns; for the grain is laid waste, the new wine has dried up, and the fresh oil languishes.

**1:11** Be put to shame, O plowmen; wail, O vinedressers, over the wheat and over the barley, for the harvest of the field has perished.

**1:12** The vine has dried up, and the fig tree has languished; the pomegranate, the palm also, and the apple tree—all the trees of the field are withered; indeed, gladness has withered away from the sons of men.

**1:13** Gird yourselves and lament, O priests; wail, O ministers of the altar. Come, pass the night in sackcloth, O ministers of my God, for the grain offering and the drink offering are withheld from the house of your God.

**1:14** Consecrate a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly; gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land to the house of the LORD your God, and cry out to the LORD.

**1:15** Alas for the day! For the day of the LORD is near, and like destruction from the Almighty it shall come.

**1:16** Has not the food been cut off before our very eyes, and joy and gladness from the house of our God?

**1:17** The seeds shrivel beneath their clods; the storehouses lie desolate, and the granaries are broken down, for the grain has withered.

**1:18** How the beasts groan! The herds of cattle wander in perplexity because there is no pasture for them; even the flocks of sheep suffer punishment.

**1:19** To You, O LORD, I cry out; for fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and flame has scorched all the trees of the field.

**1:20** Even the beasts of the field pant after You, for the streams of water have dried up, and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The four Hebrew locust terms (gazam, arbeh, yeleq, and chasil) represent either successive stages of locust development or relentless waves of devastation.
- **1:6** — "My land" here (and "my vine" and "my fig tree" in v. 7) may be understood either as the prophet speaking of his own homeland or as the LORD speaking of His covenant land.
- **1:12** — Across verses 10–12, the Hebrew weaves a wordplay between nearly identical forms of the roots meaning "to dry up" (yavesh) and "to be put to shame" (bosh).
- **1:15** — "Like destruction from the Almighty" renders the striking Hebrew assonance ke-shod mi-Shadday.
