## Text

**1:1** The words of Amos, who was among the herdsmen from Tekoa, which he beheld regarding Israel during the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and during the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

**1:2** And he said, "The LORD roars from Zion, and utters His voice from Jerusalem; the pastures of the shepherds mourn, and the summit of Carmel dries up."

**1:3** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not revoke it, because they threshed Gilead with iron threshing sledges.

**1:4** So I will send fire into the house of Hazael, and it shall consume the citadels of Ben-Hadad.

**1:5** And I will break the bar of Damascus, and cut off the inhabitant from the Valley of Aven, and him who wields the scepter from Beth-Eden; and the people of Aram shall go into exile to Kir," says the LORD.

**1:6** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Gaza, and for four, I will not revoke it, because they carried away an entire population into exile, to deliver them up to Edom.

**1:7** So I will send fire upon the wall of Gaza, and it shall consume her citadels.

**1:8** I will cut off the inhabitant from Ashdod, and the one who holds the scepter from Ashkelon; I will turn My hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines shall perish," says the Lord GOD.

**1:9** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, I will not revoke it, because they delivered up an entire exiled community to Edom, and did not remember the covenant of brotherhood.

**1:10** So I will send fire upon the wall of Tyre, and it shall consume her citadels."

**1:11** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Edom, and for four, I will not revoke it, because he pursued his brother with the sword and stifled his compassion; his anger tore perpetually, and he kept his wrath forever.

**1:12** So I will send fire upon Teman, and it shall consume the citadels of Bozrah."

**1:13** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of the sons of Ammon, and for four, I will not revoke it, because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead, in order to enlarge their border.

**1:14** So I will kindle a fire in the wall of Rabbah, and it shall consume her citadels, amid shouting on the day of battle, with a tempest on the day of the whirlwind.

**1:15** And their king shall go into exile, he and his princes together," says the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Hebrew noqedim denotes sheepbreeders or livestock raisers, a specialized pastoral occupation also attested in 2 Kings 3:4.
- **1:3** — The graded numerical formula "for three... and for four" conveys an overflowing measure of guilt, and the pronoun "it" refers to the decreed judgment that the LORD will not turn back.
- **1:15** — "Their king" follows the Masoretic vocalization malkam; the same consonants can also be read as Milcom, the national deity of the Ammonites.
