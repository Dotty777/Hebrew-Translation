## Text

**2:1** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, I will not revoke it, because he burned the bones of the king of Edom to lime.

**2:2** So I will send fire upon Moab, and it shall consume the citadels of Kerioth; and Moab shall die amid tumult, with shouting and with the sound of the trumpet.

**2:3** And I will cut off the judge from her midst, and all her princes I will slay with him," says the LORD.

**2:4** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, I will not revoke it, because they have rejected the law of the LORD and have not kept His statutes, and their lies, after which their fathers walked, have led them astray.

**2:5** So I will send fire upon Judah, and it shall consume the citadels of Jerusalem."

**2:6** Thus says the LORD: "For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, I will not revoke it, because they sell the righteous for silver, and the needy for a pair of sandals—

**2:7** those who trample the head of the poor into the dust of the earth, and turn aside the way of the afflicted; a man and his father go in to the same young woman, so as to profane My holy name.

**2:8** And they stretch themselves out beside every altar upon garments taken in pledge, and in the house of their god they drink the wine of those who have been fined.

**2:9** Yet it was I who destroyed the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of the cedars, and who was strong as the oaks; yet I destroyed his fruit above and his roots beneath.

**2:10** And it was I who brought you up from the land of Egypt, and led you forty years in the wilderness, to possess the land of the Amorite.

**2:11** And I raised up some of your sons as prophets, and some of your young men as Nazirites. Is it not indeed so, O sons of Israel?" declares the LORD.

**2:12** "But you gave the Nazirites wine to drink, and commanded the prophets, saying, 'You shall not prophesy.'

**2:13** Behold, I will press down beneath you, as a cart presses down that is full of sheaves.

**2:14** Flight shall perish from the swift, the strong shall not retain his strength, and the mighty man shall not deliver his life;

**2:15** the one who handles the bow shall not stand, the swift of foot shall not escape, and the one who rides the horse shall not deliver his life;

**2:16** and he who is stout of heart among the mighty shall flee away naked in that day," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — The Hebrew participle rendered "trample" can also mean "pant after," vividly depicting ruthless greed directed against the destitute.
- **2:8** — "The house of their god" translates Hebrew beit eloheihem, distancing the LORD from Israel's compromised sanctuaries where cultic feasting was funded by extortion.
- **2:13** — The Hebrew verb rendered "press down" occurs only in this verse; some interpreters understand it as making the earth groan or creak under a heavy load.
