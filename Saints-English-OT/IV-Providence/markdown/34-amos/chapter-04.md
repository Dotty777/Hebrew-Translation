## Text

**4:1** Hear this word, you cows of Bashan, who dwell on the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the poor, who crush the needy, who say to their masters, "Bring, and let us drink!"

**4:2** The Lord GOD has sworn by His holiness: "Behold, days are coming upon you when they shall take you away with hooks, and the last of you with fishhooks.

**4:3** And you shall go out through the breaches, each woman straight before her, and you shall be cast forth toward Harmon," says the LORD.

**4:4** "Come to Bethel and transgress; at Gilgal multiply transgression! Bring your sacrifices every morning, and your tithes every three days.

**4:5** Offer a thank offering of leavened bread, and proclaim freewill offerings—make them heard! For so you love to do, O children of Israel," says the Lord GOD.

**4:6** "I also gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities, and lack of bread in all your places; yet you have not returned to Me," says the LORD.

**4:7** "I also withheld the rain from you when there were yet three months until the harvest. I made it rain upon one city, and upon another city I did not make it rain; one portion was rained upon, and the portion upon which it did not rain dried up.

**4:8** So two or three cities wandered to one city to drink water, and they were not satisfied; yet you have not returned to Me," says the LORD.

**4:9** "I struck you with blight and mildew; the abundance of your gardens and your vineyards, your fig trees and your olive trees the devouring locust consumed; yet you have not returned to Me," says the LORD.

**4:10** "I sent among you a pestilence after the manner of Egypt; I slew your young men with the sword, along with your captured horses, and I made the stench of your camps ascend even into your nostrils; yet you have not returned to Me," says the LORD.

**4:11** "I overthrew some among you, as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a firebrand snatched from the burning; yet you have not returned to Me," says the LORD.

**4:12** "Therefore thus I will do to you, O Israel; because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel!"

**4:13** For behold, He who forms the mountains and creates the wind, who declares to man what his thought is, who turns the dawn into gloom and treads upon the high places of the earth—the LORD God of hosts is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — Harmon (Hebrew ha-Harmonah) appears only here in the Hebrew Bible and may denote a northern region of exile; the Masoretic verb is vocalized in the active stem though traditionally understood passively.
- **4:9** — Hebrew ha-gazam refers to a devouring species of locust (often called the cutting locust or palmerworm), echoing the covenant curses of Deuteronomy 28.
- **4:13** — The possessive suffix in "his thought" (Hebrew secho) refers to man, declaring that God knows and reveals human thoughts, though some interpret it as God disclosing His own counsel.
