## Text

**6:1** Woe to those who are at ease in Zion, and to those who trust in the mountain of Samaria—the notable men of the foremost of the nations, to whom the house of Israel comes!

**6:2** Pass over to Calneh and see; and from there go to Hamath the great; then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than these kingdoms? Or is their territory greater than your territory?

**6:3** you who put far off the day of calamity, yet bring near the seat of violence;

**6:4** who lie upon beds of ivory and sprawl upon their couches, and eat lambs from the flock and calves from the midst of the stall;

**6:5** who chant idly to the sound of the harp, and like David invent for themselves instruments of song;

**6:6** who drink from bowls of wine and anoint themselves with the choicest oils, yet are not grieved over the ruin of Joseph!

**6:7** Therefore they shall now go into exile at the head of the exiles, and the revelry of those who lounge shall pass away.

**6:8** The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself, says the LORD God of hosts: "I abhor the pride of Jacob, and I hate his strongholds; therefore I will deliver up the city and all that fills it."

**6:9** And it shall come to pass that if ten men remain in one house, they shall die.

**6:10** Then his kinsman, the one who burns him, shall take him up to bring the bones out of the house, and shall say to the one in the innermost recesses of the house, "Is there still anyone with you?" And he shall say, "No." Then he shall say, "Hush! For we must not make mention of the name of the LORD."

**6:11** For behold, the LORD commands, and He shall strike the great house into fragments, and the little house into fissures.

**6:12** Do horses run upon the rock? Does one plow there with oxen? Yet you have turned justice into poison, and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood—

**6:13** you who rejoice in what is nothing, who say, "Have we not by our own strength taken horns for ourselves?"

**6:14** "For behold, I am raising up a nation against you, O house of Israel," says the LORD, the God of hosts, "and they shall oppress you from the entrance of Hamath to the Brook of the Arabah."

## Translator Notes

- **6:7** — Hebrew marzeach designates a lavish aristocratic banquet or feasting association known for indulgent drinking and revelry.
- **6:10** — Burning a corpse (Hebrew mesarefo) was extraordinary in Israelite custom and signals extreme crisis from plague or siege, while the warning to remain silent reflects terror of drawing further judgment from God.
- **6:12** — The Masoretic text reads babaqarim ("with oxen"), with the rocky ground implied as the field being plowed; some commentators redivide the consonants to read "plow the sea with oxen."
- **6:13** — The Hebrew plays on two Transjordanian towns recently retaken by Israel: "what is nothing" (lo dabar) puns on Lo-debar, and "horns" (qarnayim, a figure for military strength) puns on Karnaim.
