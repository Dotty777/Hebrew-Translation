## Text

**8:1** Thus the Lord GOD showed me: behold, a basket of summer fruit.

**8:2** And He said, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will not pass by them anymore.

**8:3** And the songs of the temple shall turn to wailing in that day," declares the Lord GOD. "Many shall be the dead bodies; in every place they shall cast them out—hush!"

**8:4** Hear this, you who trample the needy, and bring the afflicted of the land to an end,

**8:5** saying, "When will the New Moon be past, that we may sell grain, and the Sabbath, that we may open the wheat market—making the ephah small and the shekel great, and falsifying deceitful balances,

**8:6** that we may buy the poor for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals, and sell even the refuse of the wheat?"

**8:7** The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob: "Surely I will never forget any of their works."

**8:8** Will the land not tremble for this, and everyone mourn who dwells in it? All of it shall rise like the Nile, heave and subside like the River of Egypt.

**8:9** "And it shall come to pass in that day," declares the Lord GOD, "that I will make the sun go down at noon, and I will darken the earth in broad daylight.

**8:10** I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; I will bring sackcloth upon every waist, and baldness upon every head; I will make it like mourning for an only son, and the end of it like a bitter day."

**8:11** "Behold, the days are coming," declares the Lord GOD, "that I will send a famine upon the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD."

**8:12** They shall wander from sea to sea, and from north even to east; they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it.

**8:13** In that day the fair virgins and the young men shall faint from thirst.

**8:14** Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria, and say, "As your god lives, O Dan!" and, "As the way of Beersheba lives!"—they shall fall and shall not rise again.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The vision turns on a Hebrew soundplay between qayits ("summer fruit") in verses 1-2 and qets ("end") in verse 2.
- **8:4** — Ketiv reads anwe ("meek" or "humble"), while the Qere followed here reads aniyye ("afflicted" or "poor" of the land).
- **8:8** — Ketiv reads wnshqh, while the Qere followed here reads wenishqe'ah ("and subside"), matching the parallel imagery in Amos 9:5.
- **8:14** — "The guilt of Samaria" (ashmat Shomeron) condemns the northern cult and may involve a polemical wordplay on a pagan deity name such as Ashimah.
