## Text

**5:1** Hear this word that I lift up over you, a lamentation, O house of Israel:

**5:2** "She has fallen; she shall rise no more, the virgin Israel! She lies forsaken upon her land; there is no one to raise her up."

**5:3** For thus says the Lord GOD: "The city that marches out a thousand shall have a hundred left, and the one that marches out a hundred shall have ten left, for the house of Israel."

**5:4** For thus says the LORD to the house of Israel: "Seek Me and live;

**5:5** but do not seek Bethel, and do not go into Gilgal, nor cross over to Beersheba; for Gilgal shall surely go into exile, and Bethel shall become nothing."

**5:6** Seek the LORD and live, lest He break forth like fire upon the house of Joseph, and devour it, with no one to quench it for Bethel—

**5:7** you who turn justice into wormwood, and cast righteousness down to the earth!

**5:8** He who made the Pleiades and Orion, and turns the shadow of death into morning, and darkens day into night; who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out upon the face of the earth—the LORD is His name.

**5:9** He flashes sudden destruction upon the strong, so that ruin comes upon the fortress.

**5:10** They hate the one who reproves in the gate, and they abhor the one who speaks uprightly.

**5:11** Therefore, because you trample upon the poor and exact tributes of grain from him, though you have built houses of hewn stone, you shall not dwell in them; you have planted pleasant vineyards, yet you shall not drink their wine.

**5:12** For I know how many are your transgressions and how mighty are your sins—you who afflict the righteous, who take a bribe, and turn aside the needy in the gate.

**5:13** Therefore the prudent man shall keep silent at that time, for it is an evil time.

**5:14** Seek good and not evil, that you may live; and so the LORD God of hosts shall be with you, just as you have said.

**5:15** Hate evil, love good, and establish justice in the gate; perhaps the LORD God of hosts will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.

**5:16** Therefore thus says the LORD God of hosts, the Lord: "In all the broad squares there shall be wailing, and in all the streets they shall say, 'Alas! Alas!' They shall summon the farmer to mourning, and to wailing those skilled in lamentation;

**5:17** and in all the vineyards there shall be wailing, for I will pass through your midst," says the LORD.

**5:18** Woe to those who long for the day of the LORD! For what purpose is the day of the LORD to you? It is darkness, and not light—

**5:19** as if a man fled from a lion, and a bear met him; or went into the house, and leaned his hand against the wall, and a serpent bit him!

**5:20** Is not the day of the LORD darkness, and not light—gloom, with no brightness in it?

**5:21** "I hate, I despise your feasts, and I will not savor your solemn assemblies.

**5:22** Though you offer up to Me burnt offerings and your grain offerings, I will not accept them, nor will I regard the peace offerings of your fattened beasts.

**5:23** Take away from Me the noise of your songs; to the melody of your harps I will not listen.

**5:24** But let justice roll on like waters, and righteousness like an unfailing stream.

**5:25** Did you bring near to Me sacrifices and grain offerings in the wilderness for forty years, O house of Israel?

**5:26** So you shall carry away Sikkuth your king and Kiyyun your images, the star of your gods, which you made for yourselves.

**5:27** Therefore I will send you into exile beyond Damascus," says the LORD, whose name is the God of hosts.

## Translator Notes

- **5:5** — The Hebrew employs sharp wordplay on the northern sanctuaries: Gilgal shall surely go into exile (galoh yigleh), and Bethel ("house of God") shall become nothingness or iniquity (aven).
- **5:8** — Hebrew Kimah and Kesil are ancient astronomical names traditionally identified with the constellations Pleiades and Orion.
- **5:26** — Sikkuth and Kiyyun are Mesopotamian astral deities linked with Saturn, vocalized in the Masoretic text with the vowels of shiqquz ("abomination") to express disdain for idolatry.
