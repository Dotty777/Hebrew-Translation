## Text

**9:1** I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and He said: "Strike the capitals, that the thresholds may shake, and shatter them upon the heads of them all. And the last of them I will slay with the sword; not one of them who flees shall get away, and not one of them who escapes shall be delivered.

**9:2** Though they dig into Sheol, from there My hand shall take them; and though they ascend to heaven, from there I will bring them down.

**9:3** And though they hide themselves at the summit of Carmel, from there I will search them out and take them; and though they conceal themselves from before My eyes at the bottom of the sea, from there I will command the serpent, and it shall bite them.

**9:4** And though they go into captivity before their enemies, from there I will command the sword, and it shall kill them; and I will set My eye upon them for evil and not for good."

**9:5** The Lord GOD of hosts is He who touches the earth and it melts, and all who dwell in it mourn; all of it rises like the Nile and subsides like the River of Egypt.

**9:6** It is He who builds His upper chambers in the heavens, and has founded His vault upon the earth; who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out upon the face of the earth—the LORD is His name.

**9:7** "Are you not like the sons of the Cushites to Me, O children of Israel?" declares the LORD. "Did I not bring up Israel from the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Caphtor, and Aram from Kir?"

**9:8** "Behold, the eyes of the Lord GOD are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from the face of the earth—yet I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob," declares the LORD.

**9:9** "For behold, I will command, and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations, as one shakes in a sieve, yet not a pebble shall fall to the earth.

**9:10** All the sinners of My people shall die by the sword, who say, 'The calamity shall not overtake nor confront us.'"

**9:11** "In that day I will raise up the tabernacle of David that has fallen, and wall up its breaches; I will raise up its ruins, and rebuild it as in the days of old,

**9:12** that they may possess the remnant of Edom, and all the nations that are called by My name," declares the LORD who does this.

**9:13** "Behold, the days are coming," declares the LORD, "when the plowman shall overtake the reaper, and the treader of grapes him who sows the seed; the mountains shall drip sweet wine, and all the hills shall flow.

**9:14** I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel; they shall rebuild the desolate cities and inhabit them; they shall plant vineyards and drink their wine; they shall also make gardens and eat their fruit.

**9:15** I will plant them upon their land, and they shall never again be uprooted from their land which I have given them," says the LORD your God.

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — "Upper chambers" and "vault" depict the heavens and earth as the structured tiers of God's cosmic dwelling.
- **9:11** — The Masoretic Hebrew alternates among feminine plural, masculine singular, and feminine singular suffixes ("their breaches," "his ruins," "rebuild it"), smoothed to "its" and "it" in English.
- **9:12** — Translated strictly from the Masoretic Hebrew ("possess the remnant of Edom"), rather than the Septuagint reading reflected in Acts 15:17.
- **9:14** — "Restore the fortunes" renders the Hebrew idiom shuv shevut, literally "turn the turning" or "bring back the captivity."
