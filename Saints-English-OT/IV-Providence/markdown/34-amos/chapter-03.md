## Text

**3:1** Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, O sons of Israel, against the whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying:

**3:2** "Only you have I known from among all the families of the earth; therefore I will visit upon you all your iniquities."

**3:3** Shall two walk together unless they have agreed to meet?

**3:4** Does a lion roar in the forest when there is no prey for him? Does a young lion utter his voice from his den unless he has made a capture?

**3:5** Does a bird fall into a snare on the earth when no trap is set for it? Does a snare spring up from the ground when it has caught nothing at all?

**3:6** If a trumpet is sounded in a city, do the people not tremble? If calamity comes upon a city, has the LORD not done it?

**3:7** Indeed, the Lord GOD does nothing unless He has revealed His secret counsel to His servants the prophets.

**3:8** A lion has roared—who will not fear? The Lord GOD has spoken—who will not prophesy?

**3:9** Proclaim upon the citadels in Ashdod, and upon the citadels in the land of Egypt, and say, "Assemble yourselves upon the mountains of Samaria, and behold the great tumults in her midst and the oppressions within her."

**3:10** "They do not know how to do what is right," declares the LORD, "those who store up violence and plunder in their citadels."

**3:11** Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: "An adversary shall be all around the land; he shall bring down your strength from you, and your citadels shall be plundered."

**3:12** Thus says the LORD: "As the shepherd rescues from the mouth of the lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so shall the sons of Israel be rescued who dwell in Samaria, with the corner of a bed and the damask of a couch."

**3:13** "Hear and testify against the house of Jacob," declares the Lord GOD, the God of hosts:

**3:14** "For on the day I punish Israel for his transgressions, I will also bring punishment upon the altars of Bethel; and the horns of the altar shall be cut off and fall to the ground.

**3:15** I will strike down the winter house along with the summer house; the houses of ivory shall perish, and the great houses shall come to an end," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — Hebrew no'adu denotes two parties meeting by prior appointment rather than merely sharing general agreement.
- **3:11** — The opening Hebrew clause is tersely elliptical, literally "An adversary, and all around the land," conveying the suddenness of the impending siege.
- **3:12** — "The damask of a couch" renders the difficult Masoretic phrase uvidmesheq ares, traditionally understood as fine fabric or a small surviving portion of a luxurious couch.
- **3:15** — "The great houses" translates Hebrew battim rabbim, which may also be rendered "many houses."
