## Text

**1:1** In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came by the hand of Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,

**1:2** "Thus says the LORD of hosts, saying: This people have said, 'The time has not come—the time for the house of the LORD to be built.'"

**1:3** Then the word of the LORD came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying,

**1:4** "Is it time for you yourselves to dwell in your paneled houses, while this house lies waste?"

**1:5** Now therefore, thus says the LORD of hosts: "Set your heart upon your ways!

**1:6** You have sown much, yet bring in little; you eat, yet are not filled; you drink, yet do not have your fill; you clothe yourselves, yet no one is warm; and he who earns wages earns them for a pierced bag."

**1:7** Thus says the LORD of hosts: "Set your heart upon your ways!

**1:8** Go up to the hill country, bring timber, and build the house, that I may take pleasure in it and be glorified," says the LORD.

**1:9** "You looked for much, and behold, it came to little; and when you brought it home, I blew upon it. For what reason?" declares the LORD of hosts. "Because of My house that lies in ruins, while every man of you runs to his own house.

**1:10** Therefore, because of you, the heavens have withheld the dew, and the earth has withheld its produce.

**1:11** And I called for a drought upon the land and upon the mountains, upon the grain and upon the new wine and upon the fresh oil, upon whatever the ground brings forth, upon man and upon beast, and upon all the labor of the hands."

**1:12** Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, with all the remnant of the people, heeded the voice of the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, as the LORD their God had sent him; and the people feared before the LORD.

**1:13** Then Haggai, the messenger of the LORD, spoke with the message of the LORD to the people, saying, "I am with you," declares the LORD.

**1:14** So the LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people; and they came and worked on the house of the LORD of hosts, their God,

**1:15** on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Hebrew charev ("in ruins" or "desolate") forms a deliberate wordplay with chorev ("drought") in verse 11, linking the neglected state of the temple with the judgment upon the land.
- **1:5** — Literally "set your heart upon your ways," a recurring expression in Haggai (1:7; 2:15, 18) calling the people to deliberate moral and spiritual reflection.
- **1:8** — The Qere reads the cohortative Niphal form "and I will be glorified" (translated here), whereas the Ketiv omits the final cohortative letter without altering the sense.
- **1:13** — The Hebrew pairs mal'akh ("messenger") with mal'akhut ("message" or "commission"), emphasizing Haggai's direct prophetic authority from the LORD.
