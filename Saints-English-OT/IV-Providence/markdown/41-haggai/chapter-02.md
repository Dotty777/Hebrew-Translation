## Text

**2:1** In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the word of the LORD came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:2** "Say now to Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying:

**2:3** 'Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? Is it not, in comparison with it, as nothing in your eyes?

**2:4** Yet now be strong, O Zerubbabel, declares the LORD; and be strong, O Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest; and be strong, all you people of the land, declares the LORD, and work; for I am with you, declares the LORD of hosts.

**2:5** According to the word that I covenanted with you when you came out of Egypt, so My Spirit abides in your midst; do not fear.

**2:6** For thus says the LORD of hosts: Yet once more—it is a little while—and I will shake the heavens and the earth, and the sea and the dry land;

**2:7** and I will shake all the nations, and the desired treasures of all the nations shall come, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of hosts.

**2:8** Mine is the silver, and Mine is the gold, declares the LORD of hosts.

**2:9** Greater shall be the latter glory of this house than the former, says the LORD of hosts; and in this place I will give peace, declares the LORD of hosts.'"

**2:10** On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:11** "Thus says the LORD of hosts: Ask now the priests for instruction, saying,

**2:12** 'If a man carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and with his fold he touches bread or stew, or wine or oil, or any kind of food, will it become holy?'" And the priests answered and said, "No."

**2:13** Then Haggai said, "If one who is unclean by a dead body touches any of these, will it become unclean?" And the priests answered and said, "It will become unclean."

**2:14** Then Haggai answered and said, "So is this people, and so is this nation before Me," declares the LORD, "and so is every work of their hands; and whatever they offer there is unclean.

**2:15** And now, set your heart carefully from this day onward—before stone was laid upon stone in the temple of the LORD.

**2:16** Throughout those days, when one came to a grain heap of twenty measures, there were ten; when one came to the wine vat to draw fifty measures from the winepress, there were twenty.

**2:17** I struck you with blight and with mildew and with hail, even all the work of your hands; yet you did not turn to Me," declares the LORD.

**2:18** "Set your heart carefully from this day onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day that the foundation of the temple of the LORD was laid—set your heart upon it.

**2:19** Is the seed still in the barn? Indeed, as yet the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not yielded fruit. From this day I will bless you."

**2:20** And the word of the LORD came a second time to Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying,

**2:21** "Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, saying, 'I am about to shake the heavens and the earth.

**2:22** And I will overthrow the throne of kingdoms, and I will destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations; and I will overthrow the chariots and their riders, and the horses and their riders shall go down, every one by the sword of his brother.

**2:23** In that day, declares the LORD of hosts, I will take you, O Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, My servant, declares the LORD, and I will make you like a signet ring; for I have chosen you, declares the LORD of hosts.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — Hebrew chemdat ("desire" or "precious things") is a singular construct noun paired with a plural verb ("shall come"), pointing to the collective wealth of the nations (cf. v. 8), though traditionally also read messianically as "the Desire of all nations."
- **2:9** — The Hebrew adjective "latter" may grammatically modify either "glory" ("the latter glory of this house") or "this house" ("the glory of this latter house").
- **2:11** — Hebrew torah here denotes an authoritative priestly ruling or instruction concerning ritual holiness and defilement.
- **2:15** — Hebrew wama'lah literally means "and upward" or "and onward"; here it directs attention from the present day back to the period before temple construction resumed, whereas in verse 18 it looks forward from the same day.
- **2:23** — A signet ring symbolized royal authority and personal favor; making Zerubbabel a signet ring reverses the earlier judgment pronounced against his grandfather Jehoiachin (Jeremiah 22:24).
