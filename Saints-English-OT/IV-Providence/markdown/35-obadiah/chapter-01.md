## Text

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah. Thus says the Lord GOD concerning Edom. We have heard tidings from the LORD, and an envoy has been sent among the nations, saying, "Arise, and let us rise up against her to war."

**1:2** "Behold, I have made you small among the nations; you are exceedingly despised.

**1:3** The arrogance of your heart has deceived you, you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is on high, who says in his heart, 'Who will bring me down to the earth?'

**1:4** Though you mount up on high like the eagle, and though your nest is set among the stars, from there I will bring you down," says the LORD.

**1:5** If thieves came to you, if destroyers of the night—how you are brought to ruin!—would they not steal only what is enough for them? If grape harvesters came to you, would they not leave gleanings?

**1:6** How Esau has been searched out! How his hidden treasures have been sought out!

**1:7** All the men of your covenant have driven you to the border; the men at peace with you have deceived you and prevailed against you. Those who eat your bread lay a snare beneath you; there is no understanding in him.

**1:8** "Shall I not in that day," says the LORD, "destroy the wise men from Edom, and understanding from the mountain of Esau?

**1:9** And your mighty men shall be dismayed, O Teman, so that every man from the mountain of Esau may be cut off by slaughter.

**1:10** For the violence against your brother Jacob, shame shall cover you, and you shall be cut off forever.

**1:11** In the day that you stood aloof, in the day that strangers carried off his wealth, and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots for Jerusalem, even you were like one of them.

**1:12** And do not gloat over the day of your brother, in the day of his misfortune; do not rejoice over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; and do not boast with your mouth in the day of distress.

**1:13** Do not enter the gate of My people in the day of their calamity; do not gloat, even you, over his affliction in the day of his calamity; and do not lay hands on his wealth in the day of his calamity.

**1:14** And do not stand at the crossroads to cut off his fugitives; and do not deliver up his survivors in the day of distress.

**1:15** For the day of the LORD is near upon all the nations; as you have done, it shall be done to you; your recompense shall return upon your own head.

**1:16** For as you have drunk upon My holy mountain, so shall all the nations drink continually; they shall drink and swallow down, and they shall be as though they had never been.

**1:17** But upon Mount Zion there shall be deliverance, and it shall be holy; and the house of Jacob shall possess their possessions.

**1:18** And the house of Jacob shall be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau as stubble; they shall blaze among them and consume them, and no survivor shall remain to the house of Esau," for the LORD has spoken.

**1:19** Those of the Negev shall possess the mountain of Esau, and those of the lowland shall possess the Philistines; they shall possess the fields of Ephraim and the fields of Samaria, and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.

**1:20** And the exiles of this host of the children of Israel shall possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath, and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad shall possess the cities of the Negev.

**1:21** And deliverers shall go up to Mount Zion to judge the mountain of Esau, and the kingdom shall be the LORD's.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Hebrew word for rock also echoes the name of Sela, a chief mountain stronghold of Edom.
- **1:7** — The rare Hebrew term rendered snare can also signify an ambush, and the phrase your bread stands elliptically for those who shared Edom's table in covenant fellowship.
- **1:12** — The Hebrew negative jussives in verses twelve through fourteen vividly address Edom as though the fall of Jerusalem were unfolding in the present, and may also be understood as reproving what Edom should not have done.
- **1:20** — The Hebrew syntax is compressed; this host translates a rare term meaning army or rampart, and ancient Sepharad is commonly identified with Sardis in Asia Minor.
