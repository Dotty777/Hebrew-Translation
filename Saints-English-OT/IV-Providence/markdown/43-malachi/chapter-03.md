## Text

**3:1** "Behold, I am sending My messenger, and he shall clear the way before Me; and the Lord whom you seek shall suddenly come to His temple, even the Messenger of the covenant in whom you delight; behold, He is coming," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:2** Yet who can endure the day of His coming, and who shall stand when He appears? For He is like a refiner's fire and like fullers' lye.

**3:3** He shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; He shall purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and silver, so that they shall present an offering to the LORD in righteousness.

**3:4** Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem shall be pleasing to the LORD, as in the days of old and as in ancient years.

**3:5** "Then I will draw near to you for judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against those who swear falsely, and against those who oppress the hired worker in his wages, the widow and the fatherless, and those who thrust aside the sojourner and do not fear Me," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:6** "For I, the LORD, have not changed; therefore you, O sons of Jacob, have not been consumed.

**3:7** From the days of your fathers you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the LORD of hosts. Yet you say, "In what way shall we return?"

**3:8** "Shall a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me! But you say, 'How have we robbed You?' In the tithe and the contribution!

**3:9** You are cursed with a curse, for it is Me whom you are robbing—this entire nation!

**3:10** Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in My house; and test Me now in this," says the LORD of hosts, "if I will not open for you the windows of heaven and pour down for you a blessing until there is no more room to hold it.

**3:11** And I will rebuke the devourer for your sake, so that it shall not destroy the fruit of your ground; nor shall your vine in the field fail to bear its fruit," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:12** "And all the nations shall call you blessed, for you shall be a land of delight," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:13** "Your words have been harsh against Me," says the LORD. Yet you say, "What have we spoken against You?"

**3:14** "You have said, 'It is in vain to serve God; and what gain is it that we have kept His charge, and that we have walked as mourners before the LORD of hosts?

**3:15** And now we call the proud blessed; indeed, those who practice wickedness are built up; indeed, they even test God and escape!'"

**3:16** Then those who feared the LORD spoke one to another, and the LORD gave ear and heard; and a book of remembrance was written before Him for those who fear the LORD and who esteem His name.

**3:17** "They shall be Mine," says the LORD of hosts, "as My treasured possession on the day that I act; and I will spare them as a man spares his own son who serves him.

**3:18** Then you shall once more discern between the righteous and the wicked, between the one who serves God and the one who does not serve Him."

**3:19** "For behold, the day is coming, burning like a furnace; and all the proud, yes, every doer of wickedness, shall be stubble; and the day that is coming shall set them ablaze," says the LORD of hosts, "so that it will leave them neither root nor branch.

**3:20** But for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness shall arise with healing in its wings; and you shall go forth and leap like calves from the stall.

**3:21** And you shall tread down the wicked, for they shall be ashes beneath the soles of your feet on the day that I act," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:22** "Behold, I am sending you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and awesome day of the LORD.

**3:23** And he shall turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the hearts of the children to their fathers, lest I come and strike the land with a decree of utter destruction."

**3:24** "Remember the law of Moses My servant, which I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel—the statutes and judgments."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — My messenger echoes the prophet's name in 1:1, while the Messenger of the covenant stands in parallel with the Lord who comes suddenly to His temple.
- **3:17** — Treasured possession translates Hebrew segullah, the covenant term for Israel as the LORD's prized inheritance.
- **3:24** — In this Hebrew versification, chapter 3 extends through verse 24 and closes with the summons to remember the law of Moses.
