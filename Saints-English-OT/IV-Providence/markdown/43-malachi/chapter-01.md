## Text

**1:1** The oracle of the word of the LORD to Israel by the hand of Malachi.

**1:2** "I have loved you," says the LORD. Yet you say, "In what manner have You loved us?" "Was not Esau Jacob's brother?" declares the LORD. "Yet I have loved Jacob,

**1:3** but Esau I have hated; I have laid his mountains desolate and given his heritage to the jackals of the wilderness."

**1:4** Though Edom says, "We have been shattered, yet we will return and rebuild the ruins," thus says the LORD of hosts: "They may build, but I will tear down; and they shall be called 'The Territory of Wickedness,' and 'The People with whom the LORD is indignant forever.'"

**1:5** Your own eyes shall see it, and you yourselves shall say, "Great is the LORD beyond the territory of Israel!"

**1:6** "A son honors a father, and a servant his master. If then I am a father, where is My honor? And if I am a master, where is My reverence?" says the LORD of hosts to you, O priests who despise My name. "Yet you say, 'In what way have we despised Your name?'

**1:7** By presenting defiled food upon My altar! Yet you say, 'In what way have we defiled You?' By saying, 'The table of the LORD is to be despised.'

**1:8** And when you present the blind for sacrifice, is it not evil? And when you present the lame and the sick, is it not evil? Offer it now to your governor! Will he be pleased with you, or will he receive you favorably?" says the LORD of hosts.

**1:9** And now, implore the favor of God, that He may be gracious to us! "With such a thing coming from your hand, will He receive any of you favorably?" says the LORD of hosts.

**1:10** "Oh, that there were even one among you who would shut the doors, so that you would not kindle fire on My altar in vain! I have no delight in you," says the LORD of hosts, "nor will I accept an offering from your hand.

**1:11** For from the rising of the sun even to its setting, My name is great among the nations; and in every place incense is presented to My name, and a pure offering; for great is My name among the nations," says the LORD of hosts.

**1:12** "But you profane it by saying, 'The table of the Lord is defiled, and its produce, even its food, is to be despised.'

**1:13** You also say, 'Behold, what a weariness!' and you snort at it," says the LORD of hosts. "And you bring what was taken by violence, and the lame, and the sick; thus you bring the offering! Shall I accept that from your hand?" says the LORD.

**1:14** "Cursed be the deceiver who has a male in his flock, and makes a vow, yet sacrifices to the Lord what is blemished; for I am a great King," says the LORD of hosts, "and My name is feared among the nations."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Oracle translates Hebrew massa, literally a burden or solemn prophetic pronouncement. The name Malachi means My messenger.
- **1:13** — Snort at it renders a Hebrew verb expressing contemptuous dismissal; ancient scribal tradition noted this clause among passages safeguarded for reverence toward God.
