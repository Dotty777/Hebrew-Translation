## Text

**3:1** Woe to her who is rebellious and defiled, to the oppressing city!

**3:2** She has not listened to the voice; she has not accepted correction; she has not trusted in the LORD; she has not drawn near to her God.

**3:3** Her princes in her midst are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves that leave no bone until the morning.

**3:4** Her prophets are reckless, treacherous men; her priests have profaned what is holy; they have done violence to the law.

**3:5** The LORD is righteous in her midst; He will do no unrighteousness. Morning by morning He brings His justice to light; He does not fail, yet the unjust knows no shame.

**3:6** "I have cut off nations; their corner towers are in ruins. I have laid waste their streets, so that no one passes by. Their cities are laid desolate, without a man, without an inhabitant.

**3:7** I said, 'Surely you will fear Me, you will accept correction'; so that her dwelling would not be cut off, despite all that I had appointed against her. Yet they rose early and corrupted all their deeds.

**3:8** Therefore wait for Me," declares the LORD, "until the day I rise up for plunder; for My determination is to gather the nations, to assemble the kingdoms, to pour out upon them My indignation, all My fierce anger; for by the fire of My jealousy the whole earth shall be consumed.

**3:9** For then I will restore to the peoples a pure language, that all of them may call upon the name of the LORD, to serve Him with one accord.

**3:10** From beyond the rivers of Cush, My worshipers, the daughter of My dispersed ones, shall bring My offering.

**3:11** On that day you shall not be put to shame for all your deeds by which you have transgressed against Me; for then I will remove from your midst your proudly exultant ones, and you shall no longer be haughty on My holy mountain.

**3:12** And I will leave in your midst a humble and lowly people, and they shall take refuge in the name of the LORD."

**3:13** The remnant of Israel shall do no unrighteousness and speak no lies, nor shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth; for they shall pasture and lie down, and no one shall make them afraid.

**3:14** Sing aloud, O daughter of Zion! Shout for joy, O Israel! Rejoice and exult with all your heart, O daughter of Jerusalem!

**3:15** The LORD has taken away your judgments; He has cleared away your enemy. The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst; you shall fear evil no more.

**3:16** On that day it shall be said to Jerusalem: "Do not fear; O Zion, do not let your hands grow weak."

**3:17** The LORD your God is in your midst, a Mighty One who will save; He will rejoice over you with gladness, He will quiet you in His love, He will exult over you with singing.

**3:18** "I will gather those who grieve over the appointed festival; they belong to you, upon whom reproach was a burden.

**3:19** Behold, at that time I will deal with all who afflict you; I will save the lame and gather the outcast, and I will make them a praise and a renown in every land where they were put to shame.

**3:20** At that time I will bring you home, even at the time when I gather you; for I will make you a renown and a praise among all the peoples of the earth, when I restore your fortunes before your eyes," says the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — For plunder follows the Masoretic vocalization le'ad, meaning for prey or plunder, whereas some ancient versions read le'ed, as a witness.
- **3:9** — With one accord translates the literal Hebrew idiom with one shoulder, conveying united service.
- **3:17** — He will quiet you in His love follows the Masoretic verb yacharish, meaning to be quiet or to quiet someone, expressing serene love that stills fear.
- **3:18** — The Hebrew wording is terse, literally referring to those grieving away from the appointed festival whom the LORD gathers because reproach rested upon them as a burden.
