## Text

**2:1** Gather yourselves together, yes, gather together, O shameless nation,

**2:2** before the decree brings forth—the day passes away like chaff—before the fierce anger of the LORD comes upon you, before the day of the anger of the LORD comes upon you.

**2:3** Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land, who have carried out His justice; seek righteousness, seek humility. Perhaps you will be hidden in the day of the anger of the LORD.

**2:4** For Gaza shall be forsaken, and Ashkelon a desolation; they shall drive out Ashdod at noonday, and Ekron shall be uprooted.

**2:5** Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast, the nation of the Cherethites! The word of the LORD is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines: "I will destroy you, so that no inhabitant remains."

**2:6** And the seacoast shall become pastures, with meadows for shepherds and folds for flocks.

**2:7** And the coast shall belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; upon them they shall pasture, and in the houses of Ashkelon they shall lie down at evening; for the LORD their God will visit them and restore their fortunes.

**2:8** "I have heard the reproach of Moab, and the revilings of the children of Ammon, with which they have reproached My people and boasted arrogantly against their territory.

**2:9** Therefore, as I live," declares the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, "surely Moab shall be like Sodom, and the children of Ammon like Gomorrah—a land possessed by nettles, and salt pits, and a desolation forever. The remnant of My people shall plunder them, and the rest of My nation shall possess them."

**2:10** This they shall have in return for their pride, because they have reproached and magnified themselves against the people of the LORD of hosts.

**2:11** The LORD will be awesome to them, for He will famish all the gods of the earth; and every man shall worship Him from his own place, all the coastlands of the nations.

**2:12** "You also, O Cushites, shall be slain by My sword."

**2:13** And He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy Assyria, and He will make Nineveh a desolation, a dry waste like the wilderness.

**2:14** Herds shall lie down in her midst, every beast of the nations; both the desert owl and the bittern shall lodge on her capitals; a voice shall sing in the window; desolation shall be on the threshold, for He has laid bare her cedarwork.

**2:15** This is the exultant city that dwelt securely, that said in her heart, "I am, and there is none besides me." How she has become a desolation, a resting place for beasts! Everyone who passes by her shall hiss and shake his hand.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Shameless nation renders Hebrew lo nikhsaf, meaning either without desire for the LORD or without shame.
- **2:4** — The Hebrew employs wordplay connecting the cities with their doom: Gaza, Azzah, shall be forsaken, azuvah, and Ekron shall be uprooted, te'aqer.
- **2:7** — The Hebrew ketiv reads shevutam and the qere reads shevitam; both express the idiom of turning back captivity or restoring fortunes, rendered here following the qere.
- **2:11** — Famish reflects the vivid Hebrew verb meaning to make lean, depicting the gods of the nations deprived of worship and offerings.
