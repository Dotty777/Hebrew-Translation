## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah the son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah.

**1:2** "I will utterly sweep away all things from the face of the earth," declares the LORD.

**1:3** "I will sweep away man and beast; I will sweep away the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea, and the stumbling blocks along with the wicked; and I will cut off mankind from the face of the earth," declares the LORD.

**1:4** "I will also stretch out My hand against Judah, and against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and I will cut off from this place the remnant of Baal, and the very name of the idolatrous priests along with the priests,

**1:5** and those who bow down on the housetops to the host of heaven, and those who bow down and swear oaths to the LORD, yet also swear by Milcom,

**1:6** and those who have turned back from following the LORD, and who have not sought the LORD nor inquired of Him."

**1:7** Be silent before the Lord GOD! For the day of the LORD is near; for the LORD has prepared a sacrifice, and He has consecrated His invited guests.

**1:8** "And it shall come to pass on the day of the LORD's sacrifice that I will punish the princes and the king's sons, and all who clothe themselves in foreign apparel.

**1:9** On that day I will also punish all who leap over the threshold, who fill their master's house with violence and deceit.

**1:10** And on that day," declares the LORD, "there shall be the sound of an outcry from the Fish Gate, a wailing from the Second Quarter, and a great crashing from the hills."

**1:11** Wail, O inhabitants of Maktesh! For all the merchant people are silenced; all who weigh out silver are cut off.

**1:12** "And it shall come to pass at that time that I will search Jerusalem with lamps, and punish the men who are settled on their dregs, who say in their heart, 'The LORD will not do good, nor will He do evil.'

**1:13** Therefore their wealth shall become plunder, and their houses a desolation; they shall build houses, but shall not inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, but shall not drink their wine."

**1:14** The great day of the LORD is near; it is near and hastens exceedingly. The sound of the day of the LORD is bitter; there the mighty warrior cries aloud.

**1:15** A day of wrath is that day—a day of distress and anguish, a day of ruin and devastation, a day of darkness and deep gloom, a day of cloud and thick darkness,

**1:16** a day of trumpet blast and battle cry against the fortified cities and against the high corner towers.

**1:17** "And I will bring distress upon mankind, and they shall walk like the blind, because they have sinned against the LORD; their blood shall be poured out like dust, and their flesh like dung."

**1:18** Neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the LORD; and by the fire of His jealousy the whole earth shall be consumed, for a full and sudden end He will make of all the inhabitants of the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — Milcom renders Hebrew Malkam, the Ammonite deity, which can also be read as their king, denoting syncretistic oaths alongside oaths to the LORD.
- **1:10** — The Second Quarter translates Hebrew Mishneh, a newer residential district of Jerusalem.
- **1:11** — Maktesh, literally the Mortar, was a hollow commercial district of Jerusalem; the merchant people translates Hebrew people of Canaan, an idiom for traders.
- **1:12** — Settled on their dregs draws on a winemaking image of wine left undisturbed on its sediment, illustrating spiritual complacency.
