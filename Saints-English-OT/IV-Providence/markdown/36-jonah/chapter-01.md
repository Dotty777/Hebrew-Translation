## Text

**1:1** And the word of the LORD came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,

**1:2** "Arise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim against it, for their wickedness has ascended before Me."

**1:3** But Jonah arose to flee to Tarshish from the presence of the LORD. He went down to Joppa and found a ship bound for Tarshish; so he paid its fare and went down into it to go with them to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD.

**1:4** Then the LORD hurled a great wind upon the sea, and a great tempest arose on the sea, so that the ship seemed about to break apart.

**1:5** Then the mariners were afraid, and every man cried out to his god, and they hurled the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten the weight upon them. Meanwhile, Jonah had gone down into the lowest parts of the vessel, had lain down, and had fallen into a deep sleep.

**1:6** So the captain drew near to him and said to him, "What is the matter with you, O sleeper? Arise, call upon your God! Perhaps God will give thought to us, so that we may not perish."

**1:7** And each man said to his companion, "Come, let us cast lots, that we may know on whose account this calamity has come upon us." So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

**1:8** Then they said to him, "Tell us, please, on whose account has this calamity come upon us? What is your occupation, and from where do you come? What is your country, and of what people are you?"

**1:9** And he replied to them, "I am a Hebrew; it is the LORD, the God of heaven, whom I fear, who made the sea and the dry land."

**1:10** Then the men feared with a great fear and said to him, "What is this that you have done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.

**1:11** Then they said to him, "What shall we do to you, that the sea may grow calm for us?" For the sea was growing ever more tempestuous.

**1:12** And he said to them, "Lift me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will grow calm for you; for I know that it is on my account that this great tempest is upon you."

**1:13** Nevertheless, the men rowed hard to turn back to the dry land, but they were not able, for the sea kept growing ever more tempestuous against them.

**1:14** Therefore they cried out to the LORD and said, "We pray, O LORD, do not let us perish for the life of this man, and do not lay innocent blood upon us; for You, O LORD, have done as it pleased You."

**1:15** So they lifted up Jonah and hurled him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its fury.

**1:16** Then the men feared the LORD with a great fear, and they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and vowed vows.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Hebrew verb translated 'hurled' recurs across chapter 1 for the LORD hurling the wind (1:4), the mariners hurling the cargo (1:5), and finally hurling Jonah into the sea (1:12, 1:15). The clause 'seemed about to break apart' literally personifies the ship as reckoning or thinking itself about to break.
- **1:6** — The rare Hebrew verb translated 'give thought' appears only here in the Hebrew Bible and expresses an Aramaic-related idiom for taking considerate care.
- **1:16** — The Hebrew expression 'feared the LORD with a great fear' uses a cognate accusative that echoes the sailors' earlier terror (1:10) while marking its transformation into reverent worship.
