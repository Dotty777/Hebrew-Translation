## Text

**14:1** Samaria shall bear her guilt, for she has rebelled against her God. They shall fall by the sword; their little ones shall be dashed in pieces, and their pregnant women shall be ripped open.

**14:2** Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God, for you have stumbled through your iniquity.

**14:3** Take words with you and return to the LORD. Say to Him, "Take away all iniquity, and accept what is good, and we will offer our lips as bulls.

**14:4** Assyria shall not save us; we will not ride on horses, nor will we say anymore, 'Our gods,' to the work of our hands; for in You the fatherless finds mercy."

**14:5** I will heal their apostasy; I will love them freely, for My anger has turned from him.

**14:6** I will be like the dew to Israel; he shall blossom like the lily and strike his roots like Lebanon.

**14:7** His tender shoots shall spread forth; his splendor shall be like the olive tree, and his fragrance like Lebanon.

**14:8** Those who dwell in his shadow shall return; they shall flourish like grain and blossom like the vine; his renown shall be like the wine of Lebanon.

**14:9** Ephraim shall say, "What more have I to do with idols?" It is I who have answered and will watch over him. I am like a luxuriant cypress tree; from Me your fruit is found.

**14:10** Who is wise, that he may understand these things? Who is discerning, that he may know them? For the ways of the LORD are upright; the righteous shall walk in them, yet transgressors shall stumble in them.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew text, this verse begins chapter 14 (numbered as 13:16 in many English Bibles), so each following verse in chapter 14 is numbered one higher than in those editions.
- **14:3** — The Hebrew reads literally "we will render bulls, our lips," expressing verbal praise and confession offered in place of sacrificial bulls.
- **14:9** — The verb "shall say" is implied by the abrupt shift to Ephraim's renunciation of idols, which is immediately followed by the LORD's reply.
