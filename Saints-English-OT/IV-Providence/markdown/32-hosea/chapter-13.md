## Text

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he exalted himself in Israel. Yet through Baal he incurred guilt, and he died.

**13:2** And now they sin even more, and have made for themselves a molten image from their silver, idols according to their own skill, all of it the work of craftsmen. Concerning them they say, "Let the men who offer sacrifice kiss the calves!"

**13:3** Therefore they shall be like the morning cloud and like the early dew that quickly passes away, like chaff driven by a whirlwind from a threshing floor, and like smoke from a chimney.

**13:4** Yet I am the LORD your God since the land of Egypt; you shall know no God besides Me, and there is no savior apart from Me.

**13:5** I knew you in the wilderness, in the land of scorching drought.

**13:6** According to their pasture they were satisfied; they were filled, and their heart was lifted up; therefore they forgot Me.

**13:7** So I will be to them like a lion; like a leopard beside the road I will lie in wait.

**13:8** I will confront them like a bear bereaved of her cubs, and I will tear open the enclosure of their heart; there I will devour them like a fierce lion, and the wild beast of the field shall rip them apart.

**13:9** O Israel, you are destroyed, for you are against Me, against your help.

**13:10** Where now is your king, that he may save you in all your cities? And where are your judges, of whom you said, "Give me a king and princes"?

**13:11** I gave you a king in My anger, and took him away in My wrath.

**13:12** The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin is stored away.

**13:13** The pangs of a woman in childbirth come upon him; he is an unwise son, for when the time arrives he does not present himself at the opening of the womb.

**13:14** From the hand of Sheol I will ransom them; from death I will redeem them. Where are your plagues, O Death? Where is your destruction, O Sheol? Compassion shall be hidden from My eyes.

**13:15** Though he may flourish among his brethren, an east wind shall come, the wind of the LORD ascending from the wilderness; his spring shall dry up, and his fountain shall become parched. It shall plunder the treasury of every precious vessel.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Hebrew construct phrase rendered "the men who offer sacrifice" can also be understood literally as "those who offer human sacrifices."
- **13:9** — The concise Hebrew wording of this verse is difficult and may also be rendered, "You are destroyed, O Israel, yet in Me is your help."
- **13:10** — The rare Hebrew form ehi (occurring here and in 13:14) functions as an interrogative meaning "Where?", though it is spelled like the verb "I will be."
- **13:14** — Although the opening two clauses lack an explicit interrogative particle in the Hebrew, they may also be read as rhetorical questions ("Shall I ransom them...?") leading into the refusal of compassion at the close of the verse.
