## Text

**2:1** Yet the number of the children of Israel shall be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or numbered; and it shall be in the place where it is said to them, "You are not My people," there it shall be said to them, "Sons of the living God."

**2:2** Then the children of Judah and the children of Israel shall be gathered together, and they shall appoint for themselves one head, and they shall go up out of the land; for great shall be the day of Jezreel.

**2:3** Say to your brothers, "My people," and to your sisters, "Shown mercy."

**2:4** Contend with your mother, contend! For she is not My wife, nor am I her husband. Let her put away her harlotries from her face, and her adulteries from between her breasts;

**2:5** Lest I strip her naked and expose her as on the day she was born, and make her like a wilderness, and render her like a parched land, and slay her with thirst.

**2:6** And upon her children I will have no mercy, for they are children of harlotry.

**2:7** For their mother has played the harlot; she who conceived them has acted shamefully. For she said, "I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my linen, my oil and my drink."

**2:8** Therefore, behold, I will hedge up your way with thorns, and I will raise a wall against her, so that she shall not find her paths.

**2:9** She shall pursue her lovers, yet she shall not overtake them; she shall seek them, yet she shall not find them. Then she shall say, "I will go and return to my first husband, for it was better for me then than now."

**2:10** And she did not know that it was I who gave her the grain, the new wine, and the oil, and multiplied silver and gold for her, which they used for Baal.

**2:11** Therefore I will take back My grain in its time and My new wine in its season, and I will withdraw My wool and My linen, given to cover her nakedness.

**2:12** And now I will uncover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and no one shall deliver her out of My hand.

**2:13** I will also cause all her mirth to cease—her feasts, her New Moons, her Sabbaths, and all her appointed festivals.

**2:14** And I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees, of which she said, "These are my wages that my lovers have given me." And I will make them a forest, and the beasts of the field shall devour them.

**2:15** I will punish her for the days of the Baals, when she burned incense to them, adorned herself with her rings and her jewelry, and went after her lovers; and Me she forgot, declares the LORD.

**2:16** Therefore, behold, I will allure her, lead her into the wilderness, and speak to her heart.

**2:17** And from there I will give her her vineyards, and the Valley of Achor as a door of hope; and there she shall answer as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.

**2:18** And it shall come to pass in that day, declares the LORD, that you will call Me "My Husband," and will no longer call Me "My Master."

**2:19** For I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and they shall no more be remembered by their name.

**2:20** And in that day I will make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, with the birds of the heavens, and with the creeping things of the ground; and I will break the bow, the sword, and war from the land, and I will make them lie down in safety.

**2:21** And I will betroth you to Myself forever; I will betroth you to Myself in righteousness and in justice, in lovingkindness and in mercy.

**2:22** I will betroth you to Myself in faithfulness, and you shall know the LORD.

**2:23** And it shall come to pass in that day that I will answer, declares the LORD; I will answer the heavens, and they shall answer the earth.

**2:24** And the earth shall answer the grain, the new wine, and the oil; and they shall answer Jezreel.

**2:25** And I will sow her for Myself in the land; and I will have mercy on Lo-Ruhamah, and I will say to Lo-Ammi, "You are My people," and he shall say, "My God."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — In the Masoretic Hebrew text, chapter 2 begins here; verses 2:1-25 correspond to 1:10-2:23 in most English versions.
- **2:3** — The Hebrew reads Ammi ("My people") and Ruhamah ("Shown mercy"), directly reversing the judgment names Lo-Ammi and Lo-Ruhamah in chapter 1.
- **2:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts mid-verse from second-person address ("your way") to third-person ("her wall... her paths").
- **2:17** — The Valley of Achor means "Valley of Trouble" (Joshua 7:24-26). The Hebrew verb translated "answer" can also mean "sing," evoking Israel's song at the Exodus while anticipating the responsive chain in verses 23-24.
- **2:18** — The Hebrew plays on Ishi ("My Husband") and Baali ("My Master"), the latter sharing the name of the Canaanite deity Baal mentioned in the next verse.
