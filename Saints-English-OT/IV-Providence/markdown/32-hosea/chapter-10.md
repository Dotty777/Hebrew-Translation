## Text

**10:1** Israel is a luxuriant vine; he yields fruit for himself. According to the abundance of his fruit he multiplied altars; according to the goodness of his land they made fine sacred pillars.

**10:2** Their heart is divided; now they shall bear their guilt. He will break down their altars; He will ruin their sacred pillars.

**10:3** For now they shall say, "We have no king, because we did not fear the LORD; and as for the king, what can he do for us?"

**10:4** They speak mere words, swearing falsely in making a covenant; therefore judgment springs up like poisonous weeds in the furrows of the field.

**10:5** The inhabitants of Samaria shall fear for the calves of Beth Aven; indeed, its people mourn over it, and its idolatrous priests tremble over it—over its glory, because it has departed into exile from it.

**10:6** It also shall be carried to Assyria as tribute to King Jareb. Ephraim shall receive shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his own counsel.

**10:7** As for Samaria, her king is cut off like a twig upon the face of the waters.

**10:8** The high places of Aven, the sin of Israel, shall also be destroyed; the thorn and thistle shall grow up upon their altars. And they shall say to the mountains, "Cover us!" and to the hills, "Fall on us!"

**10:9** From the days of Gibeah you have sinned, O Israel; there they stood. Shall not war overtake them in Gibeah against the sons of iniquity?

**10:10** When I desire, I will chasten them; and peoples shall be gathered against them when they are bound for their two iniquities.

**10:11** Ephraim is a trained heifer that loves to thresh, and I passed over her fair neck; now I will harness Ephraim, Judah shall plow, and Jacob shall break his clods.

**10:12** Sow for yourselves in righteousness; reap according to lovingkindness; break up your fallow ground, for it is time to seek the LORD, until He comes and rains righteousness upon you.

**10:13** You have plowed wickedness; you have reaped iniquity; you have eaten the fruit of falsehood, because you trusted in your own way, in the multitude of your mighty men.

**10:14** Therefore a tumult shall arise among your people, and all your fortresses shall be despoiled, as Shalman despoiled Beth Arbel in the day of battle—the mother was dashed in pieces with her children.

**10:15** Thus shall Bethel do to you because of the evil of your wickedness; at dawn the king of Israel shall surely be cut off.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Hebrew participle boqeq can mean either luxuriant or emptying; luxuriant fits the parallel clauses describing abundant fruit and a prosperous land.
- **10:5** — The Masoretic text uses the feminine plural calves alongside masculine singular pronouns referring back to the chief idol of Beth Aven, and the verb yagilu can mean either trembling in dread or former rejoicing.
- **10:10** — Following the Masoretic Qere onotam, their iniquities; the Ketiv eynotam reads their eyes or their furrows.
