## Text

**5:1** Hear this, O priests! Pay attention, O house of Israel! And give ear, O house of the king! For the judgment is against you, because you have been a snare at Mizpah and a net spread out upon Tabor.

**5:2** The revolters have gone deep in slaughter, yet I am a chastisement to them all.

**5:3** I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hidden from Me; for now, O Ephraim, you have played the harlot, and Israel is defiled.

**5:4** Their deeds do not permit them to return to their God, for a spirit of harlotry is in their midst, and they do not know the LORD.

**5:5** The pride of Israel testifies against him to his face; therefore Israel and Ephraim shall stumble in their iniquity, and Judah also shall stumble with them.

**5:6** With their flocks and with their herds they shall go to seek the LORD, yet they shall not find Him; He has withdrawn Himself from them.

**5:7** They have dealt treacherously against the LORD, for they have borne illegitimate children. Now a month shall devour them together with their portions.

**5:8** Blow the ram's horn in Gibeah, and the trumpet in Ramah! Sound the alarm at Beth-aven: "Behind you, O Benjamin!"

**5:9** Ephraim shall become a desolation in the day of rebuke; among the tribes of Israel I have declared what is sure.

**5:10** The princes of Judah have become like those who move a boundary stone; upon them I will pour out My wrath like water.

**5:11** Ephraim is oppressed, crushed in judgment, because he willingly followed a human precept.

**5:12** Therefore I am like a moth to Ephraim, and like rottenness to the house of Judah.

**5:13** When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound, then Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to King Jareb; yet he is not able to heal you, nor can he cure your wound.

**5:14** For I will be like a lion to Ephraim, and like a young lion to the house of Judah. I, even I, will tear in pieces and depart; I will carry away, and none shall rescue.

**5:15** I will depart and return to My place, until they acknowledge their guilt and seek My face; in their affliction they will earnestly seek Me.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — Follows the Masoretic text literally ("in slaughter the revolters have gone deep"), referring to deep entanglement in bloodshed or idolatrous sacrifices.
- **5:7** — Hebrew chodesh means both "month" (indicating sudden ruin) and "New Moon" (implying that their own festival observances will consume their heritage).
- **5:11** — Hebrew tsav ("precept" or "command") likely refers to the unauthorized cultic ordinances established in the northern kingdom.
- **5:13** — "King Jareb" is a Hebrew epithet meaning "King Contender" (and perhaps a play on the Akkadian royal title for "the great king"), referring to the king of Assyria.
