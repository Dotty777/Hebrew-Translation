## Text

**9:1** Do not rejoice, O Israel, with exultation like the peoples, for you have played the harlot, departing from your God; you have loved a harlot's hire upon all the threshing floors of grain.

**9:2** The threshing floor and the winepress shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail her.

**9:3** They shall not dwell in the land of the LORD; instead, Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and in Assyria they shall eat what is unclean.

**9:4** They shall not pour out wine to the LORD, nor shall they be pleasing to Him. Their sacrifices shall be to them like the bread of mourners; all who eat of it shall be defiled. For their bread shall be for their own appetite; it shall not come into the house of the LORD.

**9:5** What will you do on the day of the appointed assembly, and on the day of the feast of the LORD?

**9:6** For behold, they have departed because of destruction; Egypt shall gather them, and Memphis shall bury them. Nettles shall inherit the precious things of their silver; thorns shall be in their tents.

**9:7** The days of visitation have come; the days of recompense have come; Israel shall know it! The prophet is a fool, the man of the spirit is mad, because of the greatness of your iniquity, and great is the hostility.

**9:8** The watchman of Ephraim is with my God; yet the prophet is a fowler's snare upon all his ways, and hostility is in the house of his God.

**9:9** They have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah; He will remember their iniquity, He will punish their sins.

**9:10** Like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel; like the earliest fruit on the fig tree in its first season I saw your fathers. Yet they came to Baal-Peor and consecrated themselves to shame, and they became detestable things like what they loved.

**9:11** As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away like a bird—no birth, no pregnancy, and no conception.

**9:12** Though they raise their children, yet I will bereave them until not a man is left; yes, woe to them also when I depart from them!

**9:13** Ephraim, as I saw Tyre, is planted in a pleasant meadow; yet Ephraim must bring out his sons to the slayer.

**9:14** Give them, O LORD—what will You give? Give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts!

**9:15** All their wickedness is in Gilgal, for there I hated them. Because of the evil of their deeds I will drive them out of My house. I will love them no more; all their princes are rebels.

**9:16** Ephraim is stricken, their root is dried up; they shall yield no fruit. Even though they give birth, yet I will slay the precious offspring of their womb.

**9:17** My God will cast them away, because they have not listened to Him; and they shall be wanderers among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Hebrew shifts to the feminine singular "her," personifying Israel as the unfaithful wife addressed in verse 1.
- **9:8** — The Hebrew syntax of the opening clause is compressed and may also be understood as "Ephraim is a watchman with my God," contrasting true prophetic watchfulness with the snares laid against the prophet.
- **9:10** — "Shame" (boshet) is a polemical substitute for Baal, recalling Israel's apostasy at Baal-Peor in the wilderness (Numbers 25:1-5).
- **9:16** — Translates the Masoretic qere negative particle bal ("not"); the ketiv has the synonymous poetic negative beli.
