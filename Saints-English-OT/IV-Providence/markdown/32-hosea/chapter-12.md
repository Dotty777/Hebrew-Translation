## Text

**12:1** Ephraim has encompassed Me with falsehood, and the house of Israel with deceit; yet Judah still walks with God, and is faithful with the Holy One.

**12:2** Ephraim feeds on the wind and pursues the east wind; all the day long he multiplies falsehood and violence. They make a covenant with Assyria, and oil is carried to Egypt.

**12:3** The LORD also has a controversy with Judah, and He will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his deeds He will repay him.

**12:4** In the womb he grasped his brother by the heel, and in his vigor he strove with God.

**12:5** Yes, he strove with the Angel and prevailed; he wept and pleaded with Him. At Bethel He found him, and there He spoke with us.

**12:6** Even the LORD, the God of hosts—the LORD is His memorial.

**12:7** Therefore you, return to your God; observe lovingkindness and justice, and wait upon your God continually.

**12:8** A merchant—in his hand are scales of deceit; he loves to oppress.

**12:9** And Ephraim has said, "Surely I have become rich; I have found wealth for myself. In all my labors they shall find in me no iniquity that is sin."

**12:10** Yet I am the LORD your God from the land of Egypt; I will again make you dwell in tents, as in the days of the appointed feast.

**12:11** I have also spoken to the prophets, and I have multiplied visions; and by the hand of the prophets I have given parables.

**12:12** If Gilead is iniquity, surely they have become worthless. In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls; indeed, their altars shall be like heaps of stones upon the furrows of the field.

**12:13** Now Jacob fled to the country of Aram; Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept flocks.

**12:14** By a prophet the LORD brought Israel up out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was kept.

**12:15** Ephraim has provoked bitter anger; therefore He will leave his bloodguilt upon him, and his Lord will repay him for his reproach.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Following the Masoretic Hebrew versification, where 12:1 corresponds to 11:12 in some English Bibles; qedoshim is a plural of majesty referring to the Holy One.
- **12:5** — The Masoretic text reads immanu, with us, uniting the later generation of Israel with the revelation given to Jacob at Bethel.
- **12:8** — The Hebrew word rendered merchant is Kena'an, Canaan, used idiomatically for a trader with ironic reference to Ephraim.
