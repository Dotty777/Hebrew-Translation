## Text

**4:1** Hear the word of the LORD, O children of Israel, for the LORD has a controversy with those who dwell in the land, because there is no truth, and no lovingkindness, and no knowledge of God in the land.

**4:2** Swearing and lying, murdering, stealing, and committing adultery—they break out, and bloodshed touches bloodshed.

**4:3** Therefore the land shall mourn, and everyone who dwells in it shall languish, along with the beasts of the field and the birds of the heavens; even the fish of the sea shall be taken away.

**4:4** Yet let no man contend, and let no man reprove, for your people are like those who strive with a priest.

**4:5** So you shall stumble by day, and the prophet also shall stumble with you by night; and I will destroy your mother.

**4:6** My people are cut off for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I also will reject you from serving as priest to Me; and since you have forgotten the law of your God, I too will forget your children.

**4:7** The more they multiplied, the more they sinned against Me; I will exchange their glory for shame.

**4:8** They feed upon the sin of My people, and direct their desire toward their iniquity.

**4:9** And it shall be: like people, like priest. I will visit his ways upon him, and repay him for his deeds.

**4:10** They shall eat, yet not be satisfied; they shall play the harlot, yet not increase, because they have ceased to heed the LORD.

**4:11** Harlotry, wine, and new wine take away the understanding.

**4:12** My people inquire of their piece of wood, and their staff declares to them; for a spirit of harlotry has led them astray, and they have played the harlot, departing from their God.

**4:13** Upon the tops of the mountains they offer sacrifices, and upon the hills they burn incense, beneath oak, poplar, and terebinth, because their shade is pleasant. Therefore your daughters play the harlot, and your brides commit adultery.

**4:14** I will not punish your daughters when they play the harlot, nor your brides when they commit adultery; for the men themselves turn aside with harlots, and offer sacrifices with shrine prostitutes. Thus a people without understanding shall come to ruin.

**4:15** Though you play the harlot, O Israel, do not let Judah become guilty; do not come to Gilgal, nor go up to Beth-aven, nor swear, "As the LORD lives!"

**4:16** For like a stubborn heifer, Israel has become stubborn; now shall the LORD pasture them like a lamb in an open meadow?

**4:17** Ephraim is bound to idols; leave him alone.

**4:18** When their carousing is over, they continually play the harlot; her rulers dearly love shame.

**4:19** A wind has bound her up in its wings, and they shall be put to shame because of their sacrifices.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Masoretic text reads literally "like those who contend with a priest," evoking the stubborn defiance of priestly judicial authority (cf. Deut. 17:12).
- **4:6** — Follows the Qere reading "and I will reject you"; the Ketiv contains an extra orthographic aleph with the same meaning.
- **4:8** — The Hebrew word for "sin" also denotes a "sin offering," underscoring the irony that the priests feed on the offerings for sin and thus desire the people's iniquity to increase.
- **4:15** — Beth-aven ("house of wickedness") is Hosea's scornful wordplay on Bethel ("house of God"), a prominent center of northern idolatrous worship.
