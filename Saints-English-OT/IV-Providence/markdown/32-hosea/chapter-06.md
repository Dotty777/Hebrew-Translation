## Text

**6:1** Come, let us return to the LORD; for He Himself has torn, and He will heal us; He has struck, and He will bind us up.

**6:2** After two days He will restore us to life; on the third day He will raise us up, that we may live before Him.

**6:3** Let us know, let us press on to know the LORD! His coming forth is as sure as the dawn; He shall come to us like the rain, like the spring rain that waters the earth.

**6:4** What shall I do with you, O Ephraim? What shall I do with you, O Judah? For your lovingkindness is like a morning cloud, and like the dew that vanishes early.

**6:5** Therefore I have hewn them down by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of My mouth, and your judgments go forth as the light.

**6:6** For I delight in lovingkindness and not sacrifice, and in the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.

**6:7** Yet like Adam they have transgressed the covenant; there they have dealt treacherously against Me.

**6:8** Gilead is a city of workers of iniquity, tracked with blood.

**6:9** As bands of robbers lie in wait for a man, so the company of priests murder along the road to Shechem; for they have committed villainy.

**6:10** In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing: there is harlotry in Ephraim; Israel is defiled.

**6:11** Also, O Judah, a harvest is appointed for you, when I restore the fortunes of My people.

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The verb translated "waters" (yoreh) is also the Hebrew noun for the autumn or "early rain," evoking both the spring and autumn rains of the land.
- **6:5** — Follows the Masoretic text ("your judgments," that is, the verdicts pronounced against Israel and Judah) going forth clearly as the light.
- **6:7** — Hebrew ke-adam may mean "like Adam" or "like mortal men," and "there" points to the place of covenant unfaithfulness elaborated in the verses that follow.
- **6:10** — Follows the Qere reading sha'aruriyyah ("a horrible thing"), an orthographic variant of the Ketiv with the same sense.
