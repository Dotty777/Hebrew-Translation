## Text

**7:1** When I would heal Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim is uncovered, and the evil deeds of Samaria; for they deal in falsehood: a thief enters in, and a band of raiders plunders outside.

**7:2** And they do not consider in their heart that I remember all their wickedness; now their own doings encompass them; they are before My face.

**7:3** With their wickedness they gladden a king, and with their lies, princes.

**7:4** All of them are adulterers; they are like an oven blazing from a baker, who ceases from stirring the fire from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.

**7:5** On the day of our king, the princes grew sick with the heat of wine; he stretched out his hand with scoffers.

**7:6** For they bring their heart near like an oven while they lie in wait; all the night their baker sleeps, and in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire.

**7:7** All of them grow hot like an oven, and they devour their judges; all their kings have fallen, yet there is none among them who calls to Me.

**7:8** Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples; Ephraim has become a cake not turned over.

**7:9** Strangers have consumed his strength, yet he does not know it; gray hairs also are sprinkled upon him, yet he does not know it.

**7:10** And the pride of Israel testifies to his face; yet they have not returned to the LORD their God, nor have they sought Him for all this.

**7:11** Ephraim has become like a silly dove, without understanding; they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria.

**7:12** As they go, I will spread My net over them; I will bring them down like the birds of the heavens; I will chastise them according to the report to their congregation.

**7:13** Woe to them, for they have strayed from Me! Destruction to them, for they have transgressed against Me! Though I would redeem them, yet they have spoken lies against Me.

**7:14** They do not cry out to Me from their heart, when they wail upon their beds; for grain and new wine they gather together, yet they rebel against Me.

**7:15** Though I disciplined and strengthened their arms, yet they devise evil against Me.

**7:16** They return, yet not to the Most High; they are like a treacherous bow. Their princes shall fall by the sword for the rage of their tongue; this shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **7:5** — The Hebrew verb translated "grew sick" may also be rendered causatively, "made him sick," depicting court officials intoxicating the king during a conspiracy.
- **7:12** — "According to the report to their congregation" translates a compact Hebrew phrase referring to the covenant warnings publicly proclaimed to Israel's assembly.
- **7:16** — "Not to the Most High" renders the terse Hebrew expression lo al, literally "not upward" or "not to the One on high."
