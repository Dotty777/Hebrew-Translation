## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Hosea the son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel.

**1:2** When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife of harlotry and children of harlotry, for the land utterly plays the harlot, departing from the LORD."

**1:3** Then he went and took Gomer the daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore a son to him.

**1:4** And the LORD said to him, "Call his name Jezreel, for yet a little while, and I will avenge the bloodshed of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and I will bring to an end the kingdom of the house of Israel.

**1:5** And it shall be in that day that I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel."

**1:6** And she conceived again and bore a daughter. Then He said to him, "Call her name Lo-Ruhamah, for I will no longer have mercy on the house of Israel, that I should forgive them at all.

**1:7** Yet I will have mercy on the house of Judah, and I will save them by the LORD their God; and I will not save them by bow, or by sword, or by battle, by horses, or by horsemen."

**1:8** After she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son.

**1:9** Then He said, "Call his name Lo-Ammi, for you are not My people, and I will not be yours."

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The name Jezreel means "God sows" and recalls the bloody purge of Ahab's house carried out by Jehu at Jezreel (2 Kings 9-10).
- **1:6** — Lo-Ruhamah means "Not Shown Mercy." The closing Hebrew clause literally reads "for lifting I will lift for them," understood either as continuing the negation ("to forgive them at all") or adversatively ("but I will surely carry them away").
- **1:9** — Lo-Ammi means "Not My People." The final clause literally reads "and I will not be to you," echoing the divine covenant name "I AM" (Ehyeh) in Exodus 3:14.
