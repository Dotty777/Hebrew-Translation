## Text

**3:1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, set to Shigionoth.

**3:2** O LORD, I have heard the report of You, and I fear; O LORD, bring Your work to life in the midst of the years; in the midst of the years make it known; in wrath, remember mercy.

**3:3** God comes from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. His majesty overspreads the heavens, and His praise fills the earth.

**3:4** His radiance is like the daylight; rays flash from His hand, and there is the hiding place of His might.

**3:5** Pestilence marches before Him, and plague goes forth at His feet.

**3:6** He stood and measured the earth; He looked and startled the nations. The everlasting mountains were shattered; the ancient hills bowed down. His ways are everlasting.

**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan in distress; the curtains of the land of Midian trembled.

**3:8** Was Your indignation against the rivers, O LORD? Was Your anger against the rivers, or Your fury against the sea, that You rode upon Your horses, upon Your chariots of salvation?

**3:9** You laid bare Your bow; oaths were sworn over the shafts of Your word. Selah. You cleaved the earth with rivers.

**3:10** The mountains saw You and writhed; a torrent of waters swept by. The deep uttered its voice and lifted its hands on high.

**3:11** The sun and moon stood still in their lofty abode at the light of Your arrows as they sped, at the gleam of Your glittering spear.

**3:12** In indignation You marched through the earth; in anger You threshed the nations.

**3:13** You went forth for the deliverance of Your people, for the deliverance of Your anointed; You crushed the head from the house of the wicked, laying it bare from foundation to neck. Selah.

**3:14** You pierced with his own shafts the head of his warriors; they stormed out like a whirlwind to scatter me, their exultation like that of those who devour the afflicted in secret.

**3:15** You trampled the sea with Your horses, the surging heap of mighty waters.

**3:16** I heard, and my inward parts trembled; my lips quivered at the sound. Rottenness entered into my bones, and I shook beneath myself, yet I will wait quietly for the day of distress to come upon the people who invade us.

**3:17** Though the fig tree does not bud, and no yield is on the vines; though the produce of the olive fails, and the terraced fields produce no food; though the flock is cut off from the fold, and no cattle are in the stalls—

**3:18** yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice in the God of my salvation.

**3:19** GOD the Lord is my strength; He makes my feet like the feet of deer, and causes me to tread upon my high places. For the choirmaster, with my stringed instruments.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Shigionoth is an uncertain musical or liturgical term, likely denoting a passionate, heightened poetic style (cf. Psalm 7:1).
- **3:9** — The three-word Hebrew clause literally containing "oaths," "rods/shafts," and "word" is a celebrated crux, expressing the divinely sworn decree behind God's weapons of judgment.
- **3:19** — The Masoretic order of the divine names here is rendered "GOD the Lord," and the final musical subscription dedicates the prophet's prayer for liturgical performance.
