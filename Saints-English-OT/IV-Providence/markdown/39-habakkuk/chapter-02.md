## Text

**2:1** I will take my stand at my watchpost and station myself upon the rampart; I will keep watch to see what He will speak to me, and what I shall answer concerning my complaint.

**2:2** Then the LORD answered me and said, "Write down the vision and make it plain upon the tablets, so that one who reads it may run.

**2:3** For the vision is still for the appointed time; it hastens toward the end and will not prove false. Though it lingers, wait for it; for it shall surely come, it shall not delay."

**2:4** Behold, his soul is puffed up and is not upright within him; yet the righteous man shall live by his faith.

**2:5** Moreover, wine is a betrayer; an arrogant man does not abide at rest. He widens his appetite like Sheol, and like death he is never satisfied; he gathers to himself all the nations and assembles for himself all the peoples.

**2:6** Shall not all these take up a taunt against him, with derisive riddles aimed at him, and say, "Woe to him who piles up what is not his own—how long?—and weighs himself down with pledges!

**2:7** Shall not your creditors suddenly rise up, and those who make you tremble awake? Then you shall become plunder for them.

**2:8** Because you have plundered many nations, all the remnant of the peoples shall plunder you, on account of human bloodshed and violence done to the land, to the city, and to all who dwell in it.

**2:9** Woe to him who gains unjust profit for his house, that he may set his nest on high and be delivered from the grasp of calamity!

**2:10** You have plotted shame for your house by cutting off many peoples, and you have sinned against your own soul.

**2:11** For the stone shall cry out from the wall, and the beam from the timber shall answer it.

**2:12** Woe to him who builds a town with bloodshed and establishes a city by iniquity!

**2:13** Behold, is it not from the LORD of hosts that the peoples labor only for the fire, and the nations weary themselves for emptiness?

**2:14** For the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the LORD's glory, even as the waters overspread the sea.

**2:15** Woe to him who gives his neighbor drink, pouring out your venom and making him drunk as well, so as to gaze upon their nakedness!

**2:16** You are sated with disgrace instead of honor; drink, you too, and show your uncircumcision! The cup of the LORD's right hand shall come around to you, and utter disgrace shall come upon your glory.

**2:17** For the violence done to Lebanon shall overwhelm you, and the destruction of the beasts that terrified them, on account of human bloodshed and violence done to the land, to the city, and to all who dwell in it.

**2:18** What profit is a carved image when its maker has shaped it—a cast metal image, a teacher of falsehood? For the craftsman trusts in his own handiwork when he fashions speechless idols.

**2:19** Woe to him who says to wood, 'Awake!' and to a silent stone, 'Arise!' Can it give instruction? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, yet there is no breath at all within it."

**2:20** But the LORD is in His holy temple; be hushed before Him, all the earth!

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — Hebrew emunah encompasses both steadfast faith and loyal faithfulness, standing in sharp contrast to the puffed-up soul of the arrogant oppressor.
- **2:6** — Hebrew avtit denotes pledges or heavy debts accumulated through extortionate lending.
- **2:16** — The Masoretic text reads "show your uncircumcision," an image of utter disgrace; several ancient witnesses reflect a transposed consonant meaning "stagger."
- **2:17** — The Masoretic verbal form yechitan is grammatically unusual and is rendered here as "that terrified them," referring to the havoc inflicted on Lebanon's wildlife.
