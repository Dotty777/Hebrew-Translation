## Text

**1:1** The burden that Habakkuk the prophet beheld.

**1:2** How long, O LORD, have I cried for help, and You do not hear? I cry out to You, "Violence!" and You do not save.

**1:3** Why do You make me see iniquity, and look upon trouble? Destruction and violence are before me; there is strife, and contention rises up.

**1:4** Therefore the law grows numb, and justice never comes forth; for the wicked hems in the righteous, therefore judgment comes forth perverted.

**1:5** Look among the nations and watch; be astounded and amazed! For I am performing a work in your days that you would not believe, even if it were recounted.

**1:6** For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans, that bitter and impetuous nation, which marches through the broad expanse of the earth to possess dwellings that are not its own.

**1:7** They are dreadful and fearsome; their justice and dignity proceed from themselves.

**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards and fiercer than wolves of the evening; their horsemen charge forth, and their horsemen come from afar; they swoop like an eagle hastening to devour.

**1:9** All of them come for violence; the thrust of their faces presses eagerly forward, and they gather captives like the sand.

**1:10** They scoff at kings, and rulers are a laughingstock to them; they laugh at every fortress, for they heap up earthen mounds and capture it.

**1:11** Then they sweep by like the wind and pass through, becoming guilty—they whose own might is their god.

**1:12** Are You not from of old, O LORD my God, my Holy One? We shall not die. O LORD, You have appointed them for judgment; and O Rock, You have established them for correction.

**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to behold evil, and You cannot look upon wrongdoing; why then do You look on the treacherous, and keep silent when the wicked swallows up one more righteous than he?

**1:14** You make mankind like the fish of the sea, like creeping things that have no ruler over them.

**1:15** He brings all of them up with a hook; he drags them away in his net and gathers them into his dragnet; therefore he rejoices and exults.

**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net and burns incense to his dragnet, for through them his portion is rich and his food is sumptuous.

**1:17** Shall he therefore keep emptying his net, and continually slay the nations without sparing?

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Hebrew massa carries the dual sense of "burden" and "prophetic oracle," conveying the weighty gravity of the message.
- **1:9** — The rare Hebrew noun megammat occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes an eager thrust or sweeping horde pressing forward.
- **1:12** — The Masoretic text reads "We shall not die," translated here as written; rabbinic tradition notes this verse among the tiqqun soferim, suggesting an original reading "You shall not die."
