## Overview

This directory contains the **Saints Traditional English (NKJV-Style)** translation of the Hebrew & Biblical Aramaic Old Testament, translated completely fresh from `Saints-Hebrew-Critical-Text` under the locked style policy ([`STYLE.md`](STYLE.md)) and shared term glossary ([`glossary.json`](glossary.json)).

- **Register & Method:** Complete formal equivalence in elevated, worship-ready modern grammar (`you`/`your`, modern verbs, American spelling, modern proper names, zero contractions, zero obsolete 1611 particles) with strict own-source fidelity (`יהוה`/`יָהּ` → `the LORD` (all-caps), ketiv/qere clean; never cross-harmonized).
- **Companion Modern English edition:** [`../Modern-English-OT/`](../Modern-English-OT/README.md).
