## Text

**3:1** Now these are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day the LORD spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.

**3:2** And these are the names of the sons of Aaron: the firstborn, Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**3:3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he ordained to act as priests.

**3:4** But Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD when they presented strange fire before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar acted as priests in the presence of Aaron their father.

**3:5** The LORD then spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:6** "Bring near the tribe of Levi and have them stand before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister to him.

**3:7** And they shall guard his charge and the charge of the entire congregation before the tent of meeting, to carry out the service of the tabernacle.

**3:8** They shall guard all the articles of the tent of meeting and the charge of the sons of Israel, to carry out the service of the tabernacle.

**3:9** You shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons; they are completely given to him from the sons of Israel.

**3:10** But Aaron and his sons you shall appoint, and they shall guard their priesthood; and the outsider who draws near shall be put to death."

**3:11** The LORD then spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:12** "Now look, I Myself have taken the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn, the opener of the womb from the sons of Israel, and the Levites shall be Mine.

**3:13** For every firstborn is Mine; on the day I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated to Myself every firstborn in Israel, from man to animal. Mine they shall be; I am the LORD."

**3:14** The LORD then spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,

**3:15** "Register the sons of Levi according to their fathers' houses, according to their clans; every male from one month old and upward you shall register."

**3:16** So Moses registered them at the word of the LORD, just as he had been commanded.

**3:17** Now these were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**3:18** And these are the names of the sons of Gershon according to their clans: Libni and Shimei.

**3:19** And the sons of Kohath according to their clans: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**3:20** And the sons of Merari according to their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites according to their fathers' houses.

**3:21** Of Gershon: the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites; these are the clans of the Gershonites.

**3:22** Their registered men, by the number of every male from one month old and upward—their registered men were seven thousand five hundred.

**3:23** The clans of the Gershonites were to encamp behind the tabernacle westward.

**3:24** And the leader of the father's house for the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael.

**3:25** Now the charge of the sons of Gershon in the tent of meeting was the tabernacle and the tent, its covering, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**3:26** and the hangings of the courtyard, and the screen for the entrance of the courtyard that is around the tabernacle and around the altar, and its cords for all its service.

**3:27** And of Kohath: the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites, and the clan of the Uzzielites; these are the clans of the Kohathites.

**3:28** By the number of every male from one month old and upward, there were eight thousand six hundred guarding the charge of the sanctuary.

**3:29** The clans of the sons of Kohath were to encamp on the side of the tabernacle southward.

**3:30** And the leader of the father's house for the clans of the Kohathites was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

**3:31** Their charge was the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, and the sanctuary utensils with which they minister, and the screen, and all its service.

**3:32** And the leader of the leaders of the Levites was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, with oversight over those guarding the charge of the sanctuary.

**3:33** Of Merari: the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites; these are the clans of Merari.

**3:34** And their registered men, by the number of every male from one month old and upward, were six thousand two hundred.

**3:35** And the leader of the father's house for the clans of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail; they were to encamp on the side of the tabernacle northward.

**3:36** Now the appointed charge of the sons of Merari was the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its pillars, its bases, all its articles, and all its service,

**3:37** and the pillars of the courtyard all around, with their bases, their pegs, and their cords.

**3:38** And those encamping in front of the tabernacle eastward, before the tent of meeting toward the sunrise, were Moses and Aaron and his sons, guarding the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the sons of Israel; and the outsider who draws near shall be put to death.

**3:39** All the registered men of the Levites whom Moses and Aaron registered at the word of the LORD, according to their clans, every male from one month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.

**3:40** The LORD then said to Moses, "Register every firstborn male of the sons of Israel from one month old and upward, and take the number of their names.

**3:41** And you shall take the Levites for Me—I am the LORD—in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of every firstborn among the livestock of the sons of Israel."

**3:42** So Moses registered, just as the LORD had commanded him, every firstborn among the sons of Israel.

**3:43** And all the firstborn males, by the number of names, from one month old and upward, according to their registered men, came to twenty-two thousand two hundred seventy-three.

**3:44** The LORD then spoke to Moses, saying,

**3:45** "Take the Levites in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock; and the Levites shall be Mine. I am the LORD.

**3:46** And as for the redemption of the two hundred seventy-three who exceed the Levites from among the firstborn of the sons of Israel,

**3:47** you shall take five shekels each, per head; by the sanctuary shekel you shall take them—the shekel is twenty gerahs.

**3:48** And you shall give the silver to Aaron and to his sons, the redemption price of those in excess among them."

**3:49** So Moses took the redemption silver from those in excess of those redeemed by the Levites.

**3:50** From the firstborn of the sons of Israel he took the silver: one thousand three hundred sixty-five, by the sanctuary shekel.

**3:51** And Moses gave the redemption silver to Aaron and to his sons at the word of the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — Hebrew mille yadam is literally 'he filled their hand' (Piel perfect of male, 'fill'), the standard priestly idiom for ordination or installation into office.
- **3:7** — The cognate verb-and-noun idiom shamar mishmeret (literally 'guard a guard-duty' or 'keep a charge') recurs throughout the chapter (vv. 7, 8, 28, 32, 38) for both protective vigilance over the sanctuary and assigned custodial duties.
- **3:9** — Hebrew netunim netunim is literally 'given, given' (a doubled Qal passive participle of natan, 'give'), using repetition to express complete dedication.
- **3:39** — The Masoretic Text places extraordinary points (puncta extraordinaria) above 'and Aaron,' an ancient scribal marker of textual doubt (cf. vv. 15-16). In addition, the MT total here is 22,000, whereas the clan figures in vv. 22, 28, and 34 (7,500 + 8,600 + 6,200) sum to 22,300.
- **3:46** — Hebrew peduyim (plural of the Qal passive participle of padah, 'redeem') functions as an abstract noun for 'redemption' or 'redemption price' in vv. 46, 48, and 51 (alongside pidyom in v. 49), whereas peduye halwiyyim in v. 49 concretely denotes 'those redeemed by the Levites.'
