## Text

**31:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "Exact the vengeance of the sons of Israel from the Midianites; after that you will be gathered to your people."

**31:3** And Moses spoke to the people, saying, "Equip men from among you for the campaign, and let them move against Midian to inflict the vengeance of the LORD on Midian.

**31:4** A thousand per tribe, a thousand per tribe, from all the tribes of Israel you shall send to the campaign."

**31:5** So there were supplied out of the thousands of Israel a thousand per tribe, twelve thousand equipped for the campaign.

**31:6** And Moses sent them, a thousand per tribe, to the campaign—them and Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the campaign—with the vessels of the sanctuary and the alarm trumpets in his hand.

**31:7** And they campaigned against Midian, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they killed every male.

**31:8** And they killed the kings of Midian in addition to their fallen—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian—and Balaam son of Beor they killed with the sword.

**31:9** And the sons of Israel took the women of Midian and their little ones captive, and all their animals and all their herds and all their wealth they plundered.

**31:10** And all their cities in their dwelling places and all their encampments they burned with fire.

**31:11** And they took all the spoil and all the booty, of both people and animals.

**31:12** And they brought the captives, the booty, and the spoil to Moses and to Eleazar the priest and to the congregation of the sons of Israel, to the camp in the plains of Moab, which are by the Jordan at Jericho.

**31:13** And Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the chieftains of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp.

**31:14** And Moses grew furious with the appointed officers of the force, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, who were coming from the battle campaign.

**31:15** And Moses said to them, "Have you kept every female alive?

**31:16** Look, these were the ones who, by the word of Balaam, brought the sons of Israel to commit unfaithfulness against the LORD in the matter of Peor, and the plague was in the congregation of the LORD.

**31:17** And now, kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has known a man by lying with a male.

**31:18** But all the little ones among the women who have not known lying with a male, keep alive for yourselves.

**31:19** And as for you, encamp outside the camp seven days; everyone who has killed a person and everyone who has touched anyone killed, you shall purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.

**31:20** And every garment, every article of leather, everything made of goat hair, and every article of wood you shall purify."

**31:21** And Eleazar the priest said to the men of the army who had gone into battle, "This is the statute of the law which the LORD commanded Moses:

**31:22** However, the gold and the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead—

**31:23** everything that can go into the fire you shall pass through the fire, and it will be clean; still, it must be purified with water for impurity. And anything that cannot go into the fire you shall pass through the water.

**31:24** And you shall wash your garments on the seventh day and you will be clean, and after that you may come into the camp."

**31:25** And the LORD said to Moses, saying,

**31:26** "Take a count of the captured booty, of both people and animals, you and Eleazar the priest and the heads of the fathers of the congregation;

**31:27** and divide the booty in half between those who engaged in the battle, who went out to the campaign, and all the congregation.

**31:28** And set apart a tribute for the LORD from the men of battle who went out to the campaign: one living being out of five hundred, from the people, from the cattle, from the donkeys, and from the sheep.

**31:29** From their half you shall take it, and you shall give it to Eleazar the priest as the LORD's contribution.

**31:30** And from the half belonging to the sons of Israel you shall take one selected out of fifty—from the people, from the cattle, from the donkeys, and from the sheep, from all the animals—and give them to the Levites, the keepers of the charge of the tabernacle of the LORD."

**31:31** So Moses and Eleazar the priest did just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**31:32** Now the booty, the remainder of the plunder that the troops of the army had plundered, was six hundred seventy-five thousand sheep,

**31:33** and seventy-two thousand cattle,

**31:34** and sixty-one thousand donkeys,

**31:35** and human persons, from the women who had not known lying with a male—thirty-two thousand persons in all.

**31:36** And the half, the share of those who went out on the campaign, was three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep in number,

**31:37** and the tribute for the LORD from the sheep was six hundred seventy-five.

**31:38** And the cattle were thirty-six thousand, and their tribute for the LORD was seventy-two.

**31:39** And the donkeys were thirty thousand five hundred, and their tribute for the LORD was sixty-one.

**31:40** And the human persons were sixteen thousand, and their tribute for the LORD was thirty-two persons.

**31:41** And Moses gave the tribute, the LORD's contribution, to Eleazar the priest, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**31:42** And from the half of the sons of Israel, which Moses had divided off from the men who campaigned—

**31:43** now the congregation's half was three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred from the sheep,

**31:44** and thirty-six thousand cattle,

**31:45** and thirty thousand five hundred donkeys,

**31:46** and sixteen thousand human persons—

**31:47** Moses took from the half of the sons of Israel the selected share, one out of fifty, from the people and from the animals, and gave them to the Levites, the keepers of the charge of the tabernacle of the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**31:48** Then the appointed officers who were over the thousands of the army, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, drew near to Moses,

**31:49** and they said to Moses, "Your servants have taken a count of the men of battle who are under our hand, and not one man of us is missing.

**31:50** So we have presented the LORD's offering, what each man found in articles of gold—armlets and bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and beaded ornaments—to make atonement for our lives before the LORD."

**31:51** And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, every article of craftsmanship.

**31:52** And all the gold of the contribution that they set apart for the LORD was sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty shekels, from the commanders of thousands and from the commanders of hundreds.

**31:53** The men of the army had plundered, each man for himself.

**31:54** And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and they brought it into the tent of meeting as a reminder for the sons of Israel before the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **31:5** — Hebrew vayyimmasru (Niphal of masar), a rare verb occurring in Biblical Hebrew only here ("were supplied/delivered") and in v. 16 (Qal infinitive construct limsor-ma'al, "to commit unfaithfulness").
- **31:20** — Hebrew titchatta'u (Hithpael imperfect 2mp of chata', normally reflexive "purify yourselves" as in v. 19) here governs fronted objects, indicating that they are to purify those items for themselves.
- **31:23** — Hebrew mey niddah literally means "water of impurity" (or "separation"), referring to the ritual water prepared with the ashes of the red heifer for removing corpse-impurity (Numbers 19:9).
- **31:30** — Hebrew achuz (Qal passive participle of achaz, "grasp/take"; also with the definite article in v. 47) denotes a share drawn or selected out of a larger group.
- **31:50** — Hebrew kumaz occurs only here and in Exodus 35:22; its exact design is uncertain, likely referring to globular gold beads or a beaded necklace.
