## Text

**35:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**35:2** "Command the sons of Israel that they give to the Levites, from the inheritance of their possession, cities to live in; and you shall give to the Levites pastureland for the cities all around them.

**35:3** The cities shall be theirs to live in, and their pasturelands shall be for their livestock, for their property, and for all their animals.

**35:4** The pasturelands of the cities that you give to the Levites shall reach from the wall of the city outward a thousand cubits all around.

**35:5** And you shall measure outside the city on the east side two thousand cubits, and on the south side two thousand cubits, and on the west side two thousand cubits, and on the north side two thousand cubits, with the city in the middle. This shall be theirs as the pasturelands of the cities.

**35:6** The cities that you give to the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, which you shall give so that the killer may flee there, and in addition to them you shall give forty-two cities.

**35:7** All the cities that you give to the Levites shall be forty-eight cities, both them and their pasturelands.

**35:8** And as for the cities that you give from the possession of the sons of Israel, from the larger you shall take more and from the smaller you shall take fewer; each one, in proportion to his inheritance that they inherit, shall give from his cities to the Levites."

**35:9** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**35:10** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**35:11** then you shall designate for yourselves cities that shall be cities of refuge for you, so that a killer who strikes down a person unintentionally may flee there.

**35:12** The cities shall be a refuge for you from the avenger, so that the killer will not die until he stands before the congregation for judgment.

**35:13** And the cities that you give shall be six cities of refuge for you.

**35:14** Three of the cities you shall give beyond the Jordan, and three of the cities you shall give in the land of Canaan; they shall be cities of refuge.

**35:15** For the sons of Israel, and for the sojourner and for the temporary resident among them, these six cities shall be a refuge, so that anyone who strikes down a person unintentionally may flee there.

**35:16** But if he struck him with an iron object and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

**35:17** And if he struck him with a stone in the hand by which one might die, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

**35:18** Or if he struck him with a wooden object in the hand by which one might die, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

**35:19** The avenger of blood himself shall put the murderer to death; when he meets him, he himself shall put him to death.

**35:20** And if in hatred he pushed him, or threw something at him while lying in wait, and he died,

**35:21** or in enmity struck him with his hand, and he died, the one who struck him shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer. The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death when he meets him.

**35:22** But if suddenly, without enmity, he pushed him, or threw any object at him without lying in wait,

**35:23** or without seeing dropped on him any stone by which one might die, and he died, though he was not his enemy and was not seeking his harm,

**35:24** then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck him and the avenger of blood according to these judgments.

**35:25** And the congregation shall rescue the killer from the hand of the avenger of blood, and the congregation shall return him to his city of refuge to which he had fled; and he shall live in it until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil.

**35:26** But if the killer ever goes outside the boundary of his city of refuge to which he flees,

**35:27** and the avenger of blood finds him outside the boundary of his city of refuge, and the avenger of blood kills the killer, there shall be no bloodguilt on him.

**35:28** For he must live in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest; but after the death of the high priest, the killer may return to the land of his possession.

**35:29** And these things shall be for you a statute of judgment throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

**35:30** If anyone strikes down a person, the murderer shall be put to death on the testimony of witnesses; but one witness shall not testify against a person so that he dies.

**35:31** And you shall not take a ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death, for he shall surely be put to death.

**35:32** And you shall not take a ransom for one who has fled to his city of refuge, so that he might return to live in the land before the death of the priest.

**35:33** You shall not pollute the land where you are, for blood is what pollutes the land; and for the land, atonement cannot be made for the blood shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

**35:34** And you shall not defile the land where you are living, in whose midst I dwell; for I, the LORD, dwell among the sons of Israel.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:6** — Hebrew uses the single root רצח (רֹצֵחַ) throughout this chapter both for an accidental or untried killer (vv. 6, 11-12, 25-28) and for a culpable murderer under capital sentence (vv. 16-19, 21, 30-31), as well as for lawful execution by the avenger of blood (vv. 27, 30).
- **35:11** — וְהִקְרִיתֶם is the Hiphil of קרה ("cause to meet" or "make accessible"), used here for designating conveniently located cities of refuge.
- **35:25** — Hebrew expresses "who was anointed" via an active impersonal construction (אֲשֶׁר־מָשַׁח אֹתוֹ, literally "whom one anointed"); a similar indefinite active construction appears in v. 30 (יִרְצַח אֶת־הָרֹצֵחַ, literally "one shall kill the murderer").
- **35:32** — MT vocalizes לָנוּס as the Qal infinitive construct ("for fleeing"), understood here substantivally as "for one who has fled," and reads simply הַכֹּהֵן ("the priest") without "high" (contrast vv. 25, 28).
