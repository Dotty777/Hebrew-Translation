## Text

**26:1** that the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest:

**26:2** "Take a head count of the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, from twenty years old and above, according to their fathers' houses, everyone who goes out to military service in Israel."

**26:3** So Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**26:4** "From twenty years old and above," just as the LORD had commanded Moses. Now the sons of Israel who came out from the land of Egypt were:

**26:5** Reuben, the firstborn of Israel. The sons of Reuben: from Hanoch, the clan of the Hanochites; from Pallu, the clan of the Palluites;

**26:6** from Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; from Carmi, the clan of the Carmites.

**26:7** These are the clans of the Reubenites, and those registered among them were forty-three thousand seven hundred thirty.

**26:8** Now the sons of Pallu: Eliab.

**26:9** And the sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. These are the same Dathan and Abiram, the summoned men of the congregation, who waged strife against Moses and against Aaron in the congregation of Korah, when they waged strife against the LORD.

**26:10** Then the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and Korah at the death of the congregation, as the fire consumed two hundred fifty men, and they became a warning sign.

**26:11** Yet the sons of Korah did not die.

**26:12** The sons of Simeon according to their clans: from Nemuel, the clan of the Nemuelites; from Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; from Jachin, the clan of the Jachinites;

**26:13** from Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites; from Shaul, the clan of the Shaulites.

**26:14** These are the clans of the Simeonites: twenty-two thousand two hundred.

**26:15** The sons of Judah: Er and Onan; and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.

**26:16** So the sons of Judah according to their clans were: from Shelah, the clan of the Shelanites; from Perez, the clan of the Perezites; from Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites.

**26:17** And the sons of Perez were: from Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; from Hamul, the clan of the Hamulites.

**26:18** These are the clans of Judah according to those registered among them: seventy-six thousand five hundred.

**26:19** The sons of Issachar according to their clans: from Tola, the clan of the Tolaites; from Puvah, the clan of the Punites;

**26:20** from Jashub, the clan of the Jashubites; from Shimron, the clan of the Shimronites.

**26:21** These are the clans of Issachar according to those registered among them: sixty-four thousand three hundred.

**26:22** The sons of Zebulun according to their clans: from Sered, the clan of the Seredites; from Elon, the clan of the Elonites; from Jahleel, the clan of the Jahleelites.

**26:23** These are the clans of the Zebulunites according to those registered among them: sixty thousand five hundred.

**26:24** The sons of Gad according to their clans: from Zephon, the clan of the Zephonites; from Haggi, the clan of the Haggites; from Shuni, the clan of the Shunites;

**26:25** from Ozni, the clan of the Oznites; from Eri, the clan of the Erites;

**26:26** from Arod, the clan of the Arodites; from Areli, the clan of the Arelites.

**26:27** These are the clans of the sons of Gad according to those registered among them: forty thousand five hundred.

**26:28** The sons of Asher according to their clans: from Imnah, the clan of the Imnites; from Ishvi, the clan of the Ishvites; from Beriah, the clan of the Beriites.

**26:29** Of the sons of Beriah: from Heber, the clan of the Heberites; from Malchiel, the clan of the Malchielites.

**26:30** And the name of Asher's daughter was Serah.

**26:31** These are the clans of the sons of Asher according to those registered among them: fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**26:32** The sons of Joseph according to their clans: Manasseh and Ephraim.

**26:33** The sons of Manasseh: from Machir, the clan of the Machirites; and Machir fathered Gilead; from Gilead, the clan of the Gileadites.

**26:34** These are the sons of Gilead: from Iezer, the clan of the Iezerites; from Helek, the clan of the Helekites;

**26:35** and from Asriel, the clan of the Asrielites; and from Shechem, the clan of the Shechemites;

**26:36** and from Shemida, the clan of the Shemidaites; and from Hepher, the clan of the Hepherites.

**26:37** And Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters; and the names of Zelophehad's daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**26:38** These are the clans of Manasseh, and those registered among them were fifty-two thousand seven hundred.

**26:39** These are the sons of Ephraim according to their clans: from Shuthelah, the clan of the Shuthelahites; from Becher, the clan of the Becherites; from Tahan, the clan of the Tahanites.

**26:40** And these are the sons of Shuthelah: from Eran, the clan of the Eranites.

**26:41** These are the clans of the sons of Ephraim according to those registered among them: thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the sons of Joseph according to their clans.

**26:42** The sons of Benjamin according to their clans: from Bela, the clan of the Belaites; from Ashbel, the clan of the Ashbelites; from Ahiram, the clan of the Ahiramites;

**26:43** from Shephupham, the clan of the Shuphamites; from Hupham, the clan of the Huphamites.

**26:44** And the sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: the clan of the Ardites; from Naaman, the clan of the Naamites.

**26:45** These are the sons of Benjamin according to their clans, and those registered among them were forty-five thousand six hundred.

**26:46** These are the sons of Dan according to their clans: from Shuham, the clan of the Shuhamites. These are the clans of Dan according to their clans.

**26:47** All the clans of the Shuhamites according to those registered among them: sixty-four thousand four hundred.

**26:48** The sons of Naphtali according to their clans: from Jahzeel, the clan of the Jahzeelites; from Guni, the clan of the Gunites;

**26:49** from Jezer, the clan of the Jezerites; from Shillem, the clan of the Shillemites.

**26:50** These are the clans of Naphtali according to their clans, and those registered among them were forty-five thousand four hundred.

**26:51** These are those registered of the sons of Israel: six hundred one thousand seven hundred thirty.

**26:52** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**26:53** "Among these the land shall be apportioned as an inheritance by the number of names.

**26:54** For the larger you shall enlarge its inheritance, and for the smaller you shall reduce its inheritance; each shall be given its inheritance in proportion to its registered men.

**26:55** Only by lot shall the land be apportioned; they shall inherit according to the names of their fathers' tribes.

**26:56** According to the decision of the lot, its inheritance shall be apportioned between large and small."

**26:57** And these are those registered of the Levites according to their clans: from Gershon, the clan of the Gershonites; from Kohath, the clan of the Kohathites; from Merari, the clan of the Merarites.

**26:58** These are the clans of Levi: the clan of the Libnites, the clan of the Hebronites, the clan of the Mahlites, the clan of the Mushites, the clan of the Korahites. And Kohath fathered Amram.

**26:59** And the name of Amram's wife was Jochebed, daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi in Egypt; and to Amram she bore Aaron, Moses, and Miriam their sister.

**26:60** And Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar were born to Aaron.

**26:61** Yet Nadab and Abihu died when they presented unauthorized fire before the LORD.

**26:62** And those registered among them were twenty-three thousand, every male from a month old and above; for they were not registered among the sons of Israel, because no inheritance was given to them among the sons of Israel.

**26:63** These are those registered by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who registered the sons of Israel in the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan at Jericho.

**26:64** And among these there was not a man from those registered by Moses and Aaron the priest, who registered the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.

**26:65** For the LORD had said concerning them, "They will certainly die in the wilderness." And not a man of them remained, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **26:2** — "Take a head count" translates a Hebrew idiom literally meaning "lift up the head," used for conducting a census.
- **26:4** — The quotation opens elliptically with "From twenty years old and above," resuming the census command of verse 2 without repeating its verb. Because "the sons of Israel" lacks the direct-object marker used with "Moses," it is taken here as the heading for the tribal register starting in verse 5, though the Masoretic accents link it with the preceding clause.
- **26:9** — "The summoned men" translates the qere (read form), a plural construct adjective meaning "summoned ones"; the ketiv (written form) uses the Qal passive participle of the same root with essentially the same sense.
- **26:10** — The Hebrew noun translated "warning sign" literally denotes a "standard," "banner," or "signal pole," used here figuratively for a public warning.
- **26:24** — In native Masoretic order, the census lists Gad (26:15–18), Judah (26:19–22), Issachar (26:23–25), Zebulun (26:26–27), Manasseh (26:28–34), Ephraim (26:35–37), Benjamin (26:38–41), Dan (26:42–43), and Asher (26:44–47); in the Saints.Bible parallel display, Gad is aligned at 26:24–27 within a reordered tribal sequence across 26:15–47.
- **26:55** — The Hebrew pairs a passive verb (Niphal of the root meaning "divide" or "apportion") with the direct-object marker before "the land," an impersonal passive construction also seen in verse 60.
- **26:59** — "Who was born to Levi" translates an active Hebrew clause with an unexpressed feminine subject, literally "whom one bore to Levi."
- **26:61** — "Unauthorized fire" translates a Hebrew phrase literally meaning "strange" or "foreign fire," denoting fire not prescribed by the ritual command.
- **26:62** — "Were not registered" translates a rare Hothpaal (passive of Hithpael) form of the verb meaning "register" or "muster."
