## Text

**28:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**28:2** "Command the sons of Israel and say to them, 'My offering—My food for My fire offerings, My soothing fragrance—you shall take care to present to Me at its appointed time.'

**28:3** And you shall say to them, 'This is the fire offering that you shall present to the LORD: two unblemished male lambs a year old each day, as a continual burnt offering.

**28:4** The one lamb you shall prepare in the morning, and the second lamb you shall prepare at twilight,

**28:5** and one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a grain offering, mixed with a quarter of a hin of beaten oil.

**28:6** It is a continual burnt offering that was prepared at Mount Sinai for a soothing fragrance, a fire offering to the LORD.

**28:7** And its drink offering shall be a quarter of a hin for the one lamb; in the sanctuary pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the LORD.

**28:8** And the second lamb you shall prepare at twilight; like the grain offering of the morning and like its drink offering you shall prepare it, a fire offering of soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**28:9** And on the Sabbath day: two unblemished male lambs a year old, and two tenth-measures of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with oil, and its drink offering.

**28:10** This is the burnt offering of each Sabbath on its Sabbath, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

**28:11** And at the beginnings of your months you shall present a burnt offering to the LORD: two bulls of the herd, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, unblemished;

**28:12** and three tenth-measures of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with oil, for each bull; and two tenth-measures of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with oil, for the one ram;

**28:13** and one tenth-measure each of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with oil, for each lamb—a burnt offering of soothing fragrance, a fire offering to the LORD.

**28:14** And their drink offerings of wine shall be half a hin for each bull, a third of a hin for the ram, and a quarter of a hin for each lamb. This is the burnt offering of each month in its month, for the months of the year.

**28:15** Also one male goat for a sin offering to the LORD shall be prepared in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

**28:16** And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the Passover to the LORD.

**28:17** And on the fifteenth day of this month is a festival; for seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten.

**28:18** On the first day there shall be a holy assembly; you shall not do any occupational work.

**28:19** And you shall present a fire offering, a burnt offering to the LORD: two bulls of the herd, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old; they shall be unblemished for you.

**28:20** And their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil; you shall prepare three tenth-measures for each bull and two tenth-measures for the ram.

**28:21** One tenth-measure each you shall prepare for each lamb, for the seven lambs;

**28:22** and one male goat for a sin offering, to make atonement for you.

**28:23** Apart from the morning burnt offering, which is for the continual burnt offering, you shall prepare these.

**28:24** Like these you shall prepare each day for seven days—the food of a fire offering of soothing fragrance to the LORD; it shall be prepared in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

**28:25** And on the seventh day there shall be a holy assembly for you; you shall not do any occupational work.

**28:26** Also on the day of the firstfruits, when you present a new grain offering to the LORD at your Weeks, there shall be a holy assembly for you; you shall not do any occupational work.

**28:27** And you shall present a burnt offering for a soothing fragrance to the LORD: two bulls of the herd, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old;

**28:28** and their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenth-measures for each bull, two tenth-measures for the one ram,

**28:29** one tenth-measure each for each lamb, for the seven lambs;

**28:30** one male goat to make atonement for you.

**28:31** Apart from the continual burnt offering and its grain offering you shall prepare them—they shall be unblemished for you—along with their drink offerings.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:4** — "At twilight" translates the Hebrew dual phrase ben ha'arbayim, literally "between the two evenings," denoting late afternoon or dusk.
- **28:7** — "Pour out" renders the Hiphil infinitive absolute hassekh (from nasakh) used with imperative force alongside its cognate noun nesekh ("drink offering"). "Strong drink" (shekhar) denotes fermented beverage, whereas 28:14 specifies wine (yayin).
- **28:9** — "Tenth-measures" translates Hebrew 'esronim (singular 'issaron in 28:13, 21, 29), a dry measure equal to one-tenth of an ephah (explicitly stated in 28:5).
- **28:26** — "At your Weeks" translates beshavu'otekhem directly without supplying "Feast of," referring to the Festival of Weeks (Shavuot), seven weeks after Passover.
- **28:31** — "Its grain offering" carries a masculine singular suffix (minchato) referring back to the continual offering (hattamid), while "along with their drink offerings" (veniskehem) stands in a trailing position at the end of the Hebrew clause coordinated with the festival offerings to be prepared.
