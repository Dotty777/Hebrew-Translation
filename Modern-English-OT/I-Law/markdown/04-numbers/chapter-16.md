## Text

**16:1** Now Korah, son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, took with him Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On, son of Peleth, sons of Reuben,

**16:2** and they rose up before Moses, together with two hundred fifty men from the sons of Israel, leaders of the congregation, those summoned to the assembly, men of renown.

**16:3** They assembled against Moses and against Aaron and said to them, "Enough from you! For the entire congregation, all of them, are holy, and the LORD is in their midst. Why then do you exalt yourselves above the assembly of the LORD?"

**16:4** Moses heard, and he fell on his face.

**16:5** And he spoke to Korah and to all his company, saying, "In the morning the LORD will make known who belongs to Him and who is holy, and He will bring him near to Himself; the one whom He chooses, He will bring near to Himself.

**16:6** Do this: take firepans for yourselves, Korah and all his company,

**16:7** and put fire in them and place incense on them before the LORD tomorrow. Then the man whom the LORD chooses, he is the holy one. Enough from you, sons of Levi!"

**16:8** Then Moses said to Korah, "Listen now, sons of Levi:

**16:9** Is it a small matter to you that the God of Israel has set you apart from the congregation of Israel, to bring you near to Himself, to carry out the service of the tabernacle of the LORD, and to stand before the congregation to minister to them?

**16:10** And He has brought you near, and all your brothers, the sons of Levi, with you; and are you seeking the priesthood as well?

**16:11** Therefore it is against the LORD that you and all your company are banded together. And as for Aaron, what is he that you grumble against him?"

**16:12** Then Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, but they said, "We will not come up!

**16:13** Is it a small matter that you have brought us up from a land flowing with milk and honey to put us to death in the wilderness, that you must also completely lord it over us?

**16:14** Moreover, you have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, nor have you given us an inheritance of field and vineyard. Will you gouge out the eyes of those men? We will not come up!"

**16:15** Then Moses became very angry and said to the LORD, "Pay no regard to their offering. I have not taken a single donkey from them, nor have I harmed a single one of them."

**16:16** Then Moses said to Korah, "You and all your company, be present before the LORD, you and they and Aaron, tomorrow.

**16:17** And each man take his firepan, put incense on them, and each man present his firepan before the LORD, two hundred fifty firepans; and you and Aaron, each man his firepan."

**16:18** So each man took his firepan, and they put fire on them and placed incense on them, and they stood at the entrance of the tent of meeting, along with Moses and Aaron.

**16:19** And Korah assembled the entire congregation against them at the entrance of the tent of meeting. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to the entire congregation.

**16:20** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**16:21** "Separate yourselves from the midst of this congregation, so that I may consume them in an instant."

**16:22** But they fell on their faces and said, "God, God of the spirits of all flesh, will one man sin, yet You be enraged against the entire congregation?"

**16:23** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:24** "Speak to the congregation, saying, 'Withdraw from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.'"

**16:25** Then Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel went after him.

**16:26** And he spoke to the congregation, saying, "Move away, please, from the tents of these wicked men, and do not touch anything that belongs to them, or else you will be swept away in all their sins."

**16:27** So they withdrew from all around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. And Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrance of their tents, along with their wives, their sons, and their little ones.

**16:28** Then Moses said, "By this you will know that the LORD has sent me to perform all these deeds, that it is not from my own mind:

**16:29** If these men die the death of all mankind, and the fate of all mankind is visited on them, then it is not the LORD who has sent me.

**16:30** But if the LORD creates something unprecedented, and the ground opens wide its mouth and swallows them and all that belongs to them, and they go down alive to Sheol, then you will know that these men have scorned the LORD."

**16:31** It happened, just as he finished speaking all these words, that the ground that was beneath them split open.

**16:32** And the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households, along with all the people who belonged to Korah and all the possessions.

**16:33** So they and all that belonged to them went down alive to Sheol, and the earth covered over them, and they perished from the midst of the assembly.

**16:34** And all Israel who were around them fled at their cry, for they said, "The earth might swallow us!"

**16:35** Fire also went out from the LORD and devoured the two hundred fifty men who presented the incense.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Qal verb vayyiqqach ('and he took') lacks an explicit direct object before the conjunction on 'and Dathan'; 'took with him' smooths the elliptical syntax while preserving the singular verb governed by Korah.
- **16:11** — Ketiv/Qere: the Ketiv reads tlwnw (reflecting a Niphal or Qal form), while the Qere reads tallinu (Hiphil imperfect 2mp of lun, 'you grumble', matching the Hiphil participle mallinim in 17:20); the translation follows the Qere.
- **16:22** — Hebrew ha-ish echad uses the interrogative particle with qamets before the initial guttural aleph, framing a rhetorical question ('will one man sin...?').
- **16:24** — Hebrew mishkan ('dwelling') is singular in construct before all three names ('Korah, Dathan, and Abiram'), using the same noun applied to the LORD's tabernacle in 16:9 and 17:28.
- **16:30** — Hebrew beri'ah yivra literally means 'creates a creation'; the noun beri'ah occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and pairs with its cognate verb bara to denote an unprecedented creative act.
