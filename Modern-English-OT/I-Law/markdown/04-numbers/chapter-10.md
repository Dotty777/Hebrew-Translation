## Text

**10:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**10:2** "Make for yourself two trumpets of silver; you shall make them of hammered work, and they shall serve you for summoning the congregation and for moving out the camps.

**10:3** When they blow them, the whole congregation shall gather to you at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**10:4** But if they blow one, then the leaders, the heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather to you.

**10:5** When you blow an alarm, the camps encamped on the east side shall set out.

**10:6** When you blow an alarm a second time, the camps encamped on the south side shall set out; they shall blow an alarm for their departures.

**10:7** When convening the assembly, however, you shall blow, and you shall not sound an alarm.

**10:8** The sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets; and they shall be for you a perpetual statute throughout your generations.

**10:9** And when you go to war in your land against the adversary who oppresses you, then you shall sound an alarm with the trumpets, and you will be remembered before the LORD your God and saved from your enemies.

**10:10** And on your day of rejoicing, at your set festivals, and at the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and over the sacrifices of your peace offerings, and they shall serve you as a memorial before your God. I am the LORD your God."

**10:11** Now in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month, the cloud was lifted from above the tabernacle of the testimony.

**10:12** So the sons of Israel set out by their stages from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.

**10:13** They set out for the first time at the command of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**10:14** The banner of the camp of the sons of Judah set out first by their forces, and over its force was Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**10:15** Over the force of the tribe of the sons of Issachar was Nethanel son of Zuar.

**10:16** And over the force of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon.

**10:17** Then the tabernacle was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari set out, carrying the tabernacle.

**10:18** Next the banner of the camp of Reuben set out by their forces, and over its force was Elizur son of Shedeur.

**10:19** Over the force of the tribe of the sons of Simeon was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

**10:20** And over the force of the tribe of the sons of Gad was Eliasaph son of Deuel.

**10:21** Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the sanctuary, and the tabernacle was set up by the time they arrived.

**10:22** Next the banner of the camp of the sons of Ephraim set out by their forces, and over its force was Elishama son of Ammihud.

**10:23** Over the force of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.

**10:24** And over the force of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin was Abidan son of Gideoni.

**10:25** Then the banner of the camp of the sons of Dan, the rear guard for all the camps, set out by their forces, and over its force was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

**10:26** Over the force of the tribe of the sons of Asher was Pagiel son of Ochran.

**10:27** And over the force of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali was Ahira son of Enan.

**10:28** This was the order of departure of the sons of Israel by their forces, and they set out.

**10:29** Then Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law, "We are setting out for the place of which the LORD said, 'I will give it to you.' Come with us and we will do good to you, for the LORD has spoken good concerning Israel."

**10:30** But he said to him, "I will not go; instead, I will go to my own land and to my relatives."

**10:31** Then he said, "Please do not leave us, because you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you will serve as eyes for us.

**10:32** And if you go with us, whatever good the LORD does for us, we will do that same good to you."

**10:33** So they set out from the mountain of the LORD on a three-day journey, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD traveled ahead of them for a three-day journey to scout out a resting place for them.

**10:34** Now whenever the ark set out, Moses would say, "Rise up, LORD, and may Your enemies scatter, and may those who hate You flee from Your presence."

**10:35** And when it came to rest, he would say, "Return, LORD, to the ten thousands of the thousands of Israel."

**10:36** And the cloud of the LORD was above them by day when they set out from the camp.

## Translator Notes

- **10:21** — "The sanctuary" (ha-miqdāš) refers metonymically to the sacred furnishings of the sanctuary carried by the Kohathites (cf. Num 4:15). The passive rendering "was set up" represents an impersonal third-person plural Hiphil (wəhēqîmû, literally "and they set up"), referring to the Gershonites and Merarites arriving ahead of them.
- **10:34** — In the Hebrew, the cloud verse is 10:34 and the Song of the Ark is 10:35-36; this edition's verse numbering places the Song of the Ark at 10:34-35 and the cloud verse at 10:36, the order also found in the Septuagint.
- **10:35** — The infinitive construct bənuḥōh ("when it came to rest") exhibits an archaic third-person masculine singular suffix (-ōh). The phrase "to the ten thousands of the thousands of Israel" renders an unmarked directive accusative construct chain (riḇəḇōṯ ʾalpê yiśrāʾēl) following the imperative "Return."
