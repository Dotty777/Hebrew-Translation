## Text

**5:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:2** "Command the sons of Israel to send out from the camp every leprous person, and everyone who has a discharge, and everyone unclean through a corpse.

**5:3** Both male and female you shall send out; outside the camp you shall send them, so that they do not defile their camps, in the midst of which I dwell."

**5:4** And the sons of Israel did so, and they sent them outside the camp; just as the LORD had spoken to Moses, so the sons of Israel did.

**5:5** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:6** "Speak to the sons of Israel, 'When a man or a woman commits any of the sins of mankind, committing a breach of faith against the LORD, and that person incurs guilt,

**5:7** then they shall confess their sin that they have committed; and he shall repay his restitution in full, add a fifth of it to it, and give it to the one against whom he incurred guilt.

**5:8** But if the man has no kinsman-redeemer to whom the restitution can be repaid, the restitution that is repaid belongs to the LORD, for the priest, besides the ram of atonement by which he makes atonement for him.

**5:9** Also every contribution from all the holy gifts of the sons of Israel that they present to the priest shall be his.

**5:10** And each man's holy gifts shall be his own; whatever a man gives to the priest shall belong to him.'"

**5:11** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:12** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'If the wife of any man turns aside and commits a breach of faith against him,

**5:13** and a man lies with her in sexual union, and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband, and she remains concealed though she has become defiled, and there is no witness against her, and she was not caught,

**5:14** and a spirit of jealousy passes over him and he becomes jealous of his wife when she has become defiled, or a spirit of jealousy passes over him and he becomes jealous of his wife when she has not become defiled,

**5:15** then the man shall bring his wife to the priest and shall bring her offering on her behalf, a tenth of an ephah of barley flour; he shall not pour oil on it nor put frankincense on it, for it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering of remembrance, calling iniquity to remembrance.

**5:16** Then the priest shall bring her near and station her before the LORD.

**5:17** And the priest shall take holy water in a clay vessel, and the priest shall take some of the dust that is on the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water.

**5:18** Then the priest shall station the woman before the LORD, loosen the hair of the woman's head, and place on her palms the grain offering of remembrance—it is a grain offering of jealousy—while in the priest's hand shall be the water of bitterness that inflicts a curse.

**5:19** And the priest shall put her under oath and say to the woman, "If no man has lain with you, and if you have not turned aside to uncleanness while under your husband's authority, be free from this water of bitterness that inflicts a curse.

**5:20** But if you have turned aside while under your husband's authority, and if you have become defiled, and a man besides your husband has lain with you—"

**5:21** then the priest shall put the woman under the oath of the curse, and the priest shall say to the woman, "May the LORD make you a curse and an oath in the midst of your people, when the LORD causes your thigh to fall away and your belly to swell;

**5:22** and may this water that inflicts a curse enter your inward parts to make the belly swell and to make the thigh fall away." And the woman shall say, "Amen, amen."

**5:23** Then the priest shall write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the water of bitterness.

**5:24** And he shall make the woman drink the water of bitterness that inflicts a curse, and the water that inflicts a curse shall enter her, causing bitterness.

**5:25** Then the priest shall take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman's hand, and he shall wave the grain offering before the LORD and bring it near to the altar.

**5:26** And the priest shall take a handful of the grain offering as its memorial portion and offer it up in smoke on the altar, and afterward he shall make the woman drink the water.

**5:27** When he has made her drink the water, then it shall happen that if she has become defiled and has committed a breach of faith against her husband, the water that inflicts a curse shall enter her, causing bitterness, and her belly shall swell and her thigh shall fall away, and the woman shall become a curse among her people.

**5:28** But if the woman has not become defiled and she is clean, then she shall be acquitted and shall conceive seed.

**5:29** This is the law of jealousy, when a wife turns aside while under her husband's authority and becomes defiled,

**5:30** or a man over whom a spirit of jealousy passes so that he becomes jealous of his wife; then he shall station the woman before the LORD, and the priest shall carry out all of this law upon her.

**5:31** And the man shall be free from iniquity, but that woman shall bear her iniquity.'"

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — Hebrew tsarua (Qal passive participle of tsara) denotes someone afflicted with the ritual skin condition traditionally called leprosy (tsara'at; cf. Lev 13-14), a broader category than modern Hansen's disease.
- **5:7** — Hebrew asham ("guilt") here concretely denotes the restitution owed for a wrong, and be-rosho (literally "in its head/principal") means the full principal amount.
- **5:10** — The Hebrew syntax is terse: et-qodashav ("his holy gifts") is marked by the particle et yet serves as the grammatical subject of the plural verb yihyu ("shall be"), leaving the referent of the first "his" (the donor or the priest) open to interpretation.
- **5:18** — Hebrew literally reads "loosen the head of the woman" (para et-rosh ha-ishshah), an idiom for unbinding her hair so it hangs loose (cf. Lev 10:6; 21:10).
- **5:22** — Both latsbot ("to make swell") and lanpil ("to make fall away") are syncopated Hiphil infinitives construct with elided he (for le-hatzbot and le-hanpil), with lanpil also retaining an unassimilated nun.
