## Text

**20:1** The sons of Israel, the entire congregation, arrived in the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people remained in Kadesh; Miriam died there, and she was buried there.

**20:2** And there was no water for the congregation, so they assembled against Moses and against Aaron.

**20:3** The people disputed with Moses and spoke, saying, "If only we had expired when our brothers expired before the LORD!

**20:4** And why have you brought the assembly of the LORD into this wilderness, that we and our livestock should die there?

**20:5** And why did you bring us up from Egypt to bring us to this bad place? It is not a place of seed, or figs, or grapevines, or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink."

**20:6** Moses and Aaron came from before the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and they fell on their faces, and the glory of the LORD appeared to them.

**20:7** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:8** "Take the staff and assemble the congregation, you and your brother Aaron. Speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will yield its water; so you will bring out water for them from the rock and give the congregation and their livestock to drink."

**20:9** Moses took the staff from the presence of the LORD, just as He had commanded him.

**20:10** Moses and Aaron convened the assembly in front of the rock, and he said to them, "Listen now, you rebels! Are we to bring water out of this rock for you?"

**20:11** Then Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff, and abundant water came out, so the congregation and their livestock drank.

**20:12** And the LORD said to Moses and to Aaron, "Because you did not trust in Me to regard Me as holy before the eyes of the sons of Israel, therefore you will not bring this assembly into the land that I have given to them."

**20:13** These are the waters of Meribah, where the sons of Israel disputed with the LORD, and He showed Himself to be holy among them.

**20:14** Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "This is what your brother Israel says: You yourself know all the hardship that has found us,

**20:15** how our fathers went down to Egypt, and we lived in Egypt for many days, and the Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers.

**20:16** We cried out to the LORD, and He heard our voice; He sent an angel and brought us out from Egypt. Look, we are now at Kadesh, a city at the edge of your border.

**20:17** Let us pass through your land, please. We will not pass through any field or vineyard, nor will we drink well water. We will travel along the King's Road; we will not turn to the right or to the left until we pass through your territory."

**20:18** But Edom said to him, "You shall not pass through me, otherwise I will come out to meet you with the sword."

**20:19** And the sons of Israel said to him, "We will go up by the highway, and if we drink your water, I and my herds, then I will pay its price. Only, nothing more: let me pass through on my feet."

**20:20** He answered, "You shall not pass through." And Edom came out to meet him with a numerous people and with a strong hand.

**20:21** Edom refused to let Israel pass through his territory; therefore Israel turned aside from him.

**20:22** They set out from Kadesh, and the sons of Israel, the entire congregation, arrived at Mount Hor.

**20:23** The LORD said to Moses and to Aaron at Mount Hor, along the border of the land of Edom, saying,

**20:24** "Aaron is to be gathered to his kin, for he will not come into the land that I have given to the sons of Israel, because you rebelled against My word at the waters of Meribah.

**20:25** Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and bring them up onto Mount Hor,

**20:26** and strip his garments off Aaron and clothe Eleazar his son with them. Then Aaron will be gathered and will die there."

**20:27** Moses did as the LORD had commanded, and they went up Mount Hor before the eyes of the entire congregation.

**20:28** Moses stripped his garments off Aaron and clothed Eleazar his son with them. Aaron died there at the summit of the mountain, and Moses and Eleazar went down from the mountain.

**20:29** When the entire congregation saw that Aaron had expired, they wept for Aaron thirty days, the entire house of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **20:13** — The place-name Meribah ("disputing" or "quarreling") plays on the verb rib ("disputed"). The reflexive Niphal of qadash ("showed Himself to be holy") is followed by bam, which can mean either "among them" or "through them."
- **20:19** — The idiom raq ein-davar literally means "only, there is no matter/thing," conveying "nothing more" or "with no trouble," followed by the cohortative e'evorah ("let me pass through").
- **20:24** — Hebrew ammav is plural ("his peoples" or "his kin"), referring to ancestors and relatives in the idiom "gathered to his kin." The phrase "My word" literally translates pi ("My mouth"), denoting divine command.
