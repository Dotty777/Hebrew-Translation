## Text

**4:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**4:2** "Take a count of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, by their clans, by their fathers' house,

**4:3** from thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone who enters active duty to do work in the tent of meeting.

**4:4** This is the service of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting: the most sacred things.

**4:5** Aaron and his sons shall come in when the camp sets out, and they shall take down the screening veil and cover the ark of the testimony with it.

**4:6** Then they shall place on it a covering of fine leather, spread a cloth wholly of blue on top, and set its poles in place.

**4:7** Over the table of the Presence they shall spread a blue cloth and place on it the platters, the ladles, the bowls, and the pitchers for the drink offering; and the continual bread shall be on it.

**4:8** Then they shall spread over them a scarlet cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and set its poles in place.

**4:9** And they shall take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for the light, its lamps, its tongs, its pans, and all its oil vessels with which they minister to it.

**4:10** They shall put it and all its utensils inside a covering of fine leather and set it on the carrying bar.

**4:11** Over the gold altar they shall spread a blue cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and set its poles in place.

**4:12** And they shall take all the utensils of ministry with which they minister in the sanctuary, put them inside a blue cloth, cover them with a covering of fine leather, and set them on the carrying bar.

**4:13** They shall clear the ashes from the altar and spread a purple cloth over it.

**4:14** Then they shall place on it all its utensils with which they minister at it—the firepans, the meat forks, the shovels, and the basins, all the utensils of the altar—and spread over it a covering of fine leather, and set its poles in place.

**4:15** When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the sacred things and all the furnishings of the sanctuary as the camp sets out, after that the sons of Kohath shall come to carry them; yet they shall not touch the sacred things, or they will die. These are the load of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting.

**4:16** And the oversight of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, is the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the continual grain offering, and the anointing oil—the oversight of the whole tabernacle and all that is in it, in the sanctuary and in its furnishings."

**4:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**4:18** "Do not cause the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites to be cut off from among the Levites;

**4:19** rather, do this for them so that they will live and not die when they approach the most sacred things: Aaron and his sons shall come in and assign them, each man to his service and to his load.

**4:20** But they shall not go in to look at the sacred things even for an instant, or they will die."

**4:21** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:22** "Take a count of the sons of Gershon also, by their fathers' house, by their clans.

**4:23** From thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, you shall register them: everyone who enters to serve on active duty, to perform service in the tent of meeting.

**4:24** This is the service of the clans of the Gershonites, in serving and in carrying:

**4:25** they shall carry the curtains of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, its covering, and the covering of fine leather that is on top of it, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**4:26** and the hangings of the courtyard, and the screen for the entrance of the gate of the courtyard that is all around the tabernacle and around the altar, and their cords, and all the equipment for their service; and everything that is to be done with them, they shall perform.

**4:27** All the service of the sons of the Gershonites—in all their carrying and in all their service—shall be at the command of Aaron and his sons; and you shall assign to them as a charge all their load.

**4:28** This is the service of the clans of the sons of the Gershonites in the tent of meeting, and their charge shall be under the hand of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.

**4:29** As for the sons of Merari, by their clans, by their fathers' house, you shall register them.

**4:30** From thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, you shall register them: everyone who enters active duty to perform the service of the tent of meeting.

**4:31** And this is their carrying charge, for all their service in the tent of meeting: the planks of the tabernacle, its crossbars, its pillars, and its bases,

**4:32** and the pillars of the courtyard all around, their bases, their pegs, and their cords, along with all their equipment and all their service; and by name you shall assign the items of their carrying charge.

**4:33** This is the service of the clans of the sons of Merari, for all their service in the tent of meeting, under the hand of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest."

**4:34** So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of the congregation registered the sons of the Kohathites by their clans and by their fathers' house,

**4:35** from thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone who entered active duty for service in the tent of meeting.

**4:36** And their registered men by their clans were two thousand seven hundred and fifty.

**4:37** These were the registered men of the clans of the Kohathites, everyone who served in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**4:38** And the registered men of the sons of Gershon, by their clans and by their fathers' house,

**4:39** from thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone who entered active duty for service in the tent of meeting—

**4:40** their registered men, by their clans, by their fathers' house, were two thousand six hundred and thirty.

**4:41** These were the registered men of the clans of the sons of Gershon, everyone who served in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD.

**4:42** And the registered men of the clans of the sons of Merari, by their clans, by their fathers' house,

**4:43** from thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone who entered active duty for service in the tent of meeting—

**4:44** their registered men by their clans were three thousand two hundred.

**4:45** These were the registered men of the clans of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**4:46** All the registered Levites whom Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of Israel registered, by their clans and by their fathers' house,

**4:47** from thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone who entered to perform the service of labor and the service of carrying in the tent of meeting—

**4:48** their registered men were eight thousand five hundred and eighty.

**4:49** At the command of the LORD they were registered by the hand of Moses, each man to his service and to his load; and those registered by him were just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Literally, "Lift up the head of" (using the Qal infinitive absolute imperatively), a Hebrew idiom for taking a census or headcount (also in 4:22).
- **4:6** — The Hebrew term tachash (also in 4:8, 10-12, 14, 25) is of uncertain meaning, referring either to fine tanned leather or to the hide of a marine animal such as a dugong or dolphin.
- **4:13** — The Hebrew verb dishshenu is a privative Piel of deshen ("fatty ashes"), literally "de-ash," meaning to clear away the ashes from the altar.
- **4:20** — Hebrew keballa (preposition ke- with the Piel infinitive construct of bala, "swallow up") literally means "as a swallowing," idiomatically "for an instant"; some alternatively construe it transitively with the following object as "while the sacred things are being covered" or "dismantled."
- **4:49** — The 3ms active verb paqad ("he registered") is used impersonally here for the passive ("they were registered"), and ufequdav is the Qal passive participle with a 3ms agentive suffix, literally "and those registered by him."
