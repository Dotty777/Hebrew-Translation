## Text

**29:1** And in the seventh month, on the first of the month, there shall be a holy assembly for you; you shall not do any occupational work. It shall be a day of trumpet blasts for you.

**29:2** And you shall prepare a burnt offering for a soothing fragrance to the LORD: one bull from the herd, one ram, seven one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:3** and their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram,

**29:4** and one-tenth for each lamb of the seven lambs;

**29:5** and one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you,

**29:6** besides the burnt offering of the new moon and its grain offering, and the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings according to their ordinance, for a soothing fragrance, a fire offering to the LORD.

**29:7** And on the tenth of this seventh month there shall be a holy assembly for you, and you shall afflict your souls; you shall not do any work.

**29:8** And you shall present a burnt offering to the LORD, a soothing fragrance: one bull from the herd, one ram, seven one-year-old male lambs—they shall be unblemished for you;

**29:9** and their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the one ram,

**29:10** one-tenth for each lamb of the seven lambs;

**29:11** one male goat as a sin offering, besides the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

**29:12** And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month there shall be a holy assembly for you; you shall not do any occupational work, and you shall celebrate a festival to the LORD for seven days.

**29:13** And you shall present a burnt offering, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD: thirteen bulls from the herd, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs—they shall be unblemished;

**29:14** and their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each bull of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths for each ram of the two rams,

**29:15** and one-tenth for each lamb of the fourteen lambs;

**29:16** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

**29:17** And on the second day: twelve bulls from the herd, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:18** and their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to the ordinance;

**29:19** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

**29:20** And on the third day: eleven bulls, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:21** and their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to the ordinance;

**29:22** and one male goat for a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.

**29:23** And on the fourth day: ten bulls, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:24** their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to the ordinance;

**29:25** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

**29:26** And on the fifth day: nine bulls, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:27** and their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to the ordinance;

**29:28** and one male goat for a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.

**29:29** And on the sixth day: eight bulls, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:30** and their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to the ordinance;

**29:31** and one male goat for a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offerings.

**29:32** And on the seventh day: seven bulls, two rams, fourteen one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:33** and their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to their ordinance;

**29:34** and one male goat for a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

**29:35** On the eighth day there shall be a solemn assembly for you; you shall not do any occupational work.

**29:36** And you shall present a burnt offering, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD: one bull, one ram, seven one-year-old male lambs, unblemished;

**29:37** their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bull, for the ram, and for the lambs, according to their number, according to the ordinance;

**29:38** and one male goat for a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Hebrew meleket avodah, literally work of labor or service, denotes regular occupational work, in contrast to the total prohibition of any work on the Day of Atonement in 29:7; teruah literally means a loud blast or shout, referring here to sounding trumpets or horns.
- **29:3** — Hebrew issaron (plural esronim) is a specific dry measure equal to one-tenth of an ephah; the word ephah is implied by the unit name throughout this chapter.
- **29:15** — The Hebrew doubles issaron distributively to express one-tenth per lamb; the Masoretic text also places one of its fifteen extraordinary scribal dots (puncta extraordinaria) over the waw of the first issaron.
- **29:31** — The Masoretic text preserves subtle variations in the drink-offering suffixes across the days of the festival: plural 'their drink offerings' in 29:19, plural 'its drink offerings' in 29:31, and 'according to their ordinance' in 29:33, rather than standardizing to the singular forms used on the other days.
- **29:35** — Hebrew atseret, derived from a root meaning to restrain or hold back, denotes a solemn closing assembly marking the conclusion of the seven-day festival.
