## Text

**32:1** Now the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad had abundant livestock, exceedingly numerous; and they saw the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead, and look, the place was a place for livestock.

**32:2** Then the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben came and spoke to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the leaders of the assembly, saying,

**32:3** "Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon,

**32:4** the land that the LORD struck down before the assembly of Israel, is a land for livestock, and your servants have livestock."

**32:5** And they said, "If we have found favor in your eyes, may this land be given to your servants for a possession; do not make us cross the Jordan."

**32:6** Then Moses said to the sons of Gad and to the sons of Reuben, "Will your brothers go into battle while you stay here?

**32:7** And why do you turn away the heart of the sons of Israel from crossing into the land that the LORD has given them?

**32:8** Your fathers acted in this way when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to see the land.

**32:9** They went up as far as the Valley of Eshcol and saw the land, and they turned away the heart of the sons of Israel so that they would not enter the land that the LORD had given them.

**32:10** Then the anger of the LORD burned on that day, and He swore, saying,

**32:11** 'Surely the men who came up from Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, shall not see the land that I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, because they did not follow Me wholeheartedly,

**32:12** except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, because they followed the LORD wholeheartedly.'

**32:13** So the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He made them wander in the wilderness for forty years, until the whole generation that had done evil in the eyes of the LORD came to an end.

**32:14** And look, you have risen up in place of your fathers, a breed of sinful men, to add yet more to the burning anger of the LORD against Israel.

**32:15** If you turn back from following Him, He will once again leave them in the wilderness, and you will ruin all this people."

**32:16** Then they approached him and said, "We will build sheepfolds for our livestock here and cities for our little ones,

**32:17** but as for us, we will arm ourselves in haste ahead of the sons of Israel until we have brought them to their place, and our little ones will dwell in the fortified cities because of the inhabitants of the land.

**32:18** We will not return to our houses until the sons of Israel have each taken possession of his inheritance.

**32:19** For we will not inherit with them across the Jordan and onward, because our inheritance has come to us on the side of the Jordan toward the east."

**32:20** Then Moses said to them, "If you do this thing, if you arm yourselves before the LORD for battle,

**32:21** and every armed man among you crosses the Jordan before the LORD until He has driven out His enemies from before Him,

**32:22** and the land is subdued before the LORD, then afterward you may return, and you will be free of guilt toward the LORD and toward Israel; and this land shall be yours for a possession before the LORD.

**32:23** But if you do not do so, look, you have sinned against the LORD, and know that your sin will find you.

**32:24** Build for yourselves cities for your little ones and folds for your flocks, and you shall do what has gone out from your mouth."

**32:25** Then the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben spoke to Moses, saying, "Your servants will do as my lord commands.

**32:26** Our little ones, our wives, our livestock, and all our animals will be there in the cities of Gilead,

**32:27** but your servants will cross over, every man armed for warfare, before the LORD for battle, as my lord speaks."

**32:28** So concerning them, Moses commanded Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel.

**32:29** And Moses said to them, "If the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before the LORD, and the land is subdued before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead for a possession.

**32:30** But if they do not cross over with you armed, then they shall take possession among you in the land of Canaan."

**32:31** Then the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben answered, saying, "What the LORD has spoken to your servants, so we will do.

**32:32** We will cross over armed before the LORD into the land of Canaan, and the possession of our inheritance shall be with us across the Jordan."

**32:33** So Moses gave to them—the sons of Gad, the sons of Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph—the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land according to its cities with their borders, the cities of the land all around.

**32:34** And the sons of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,

**32:35** Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,

**32:36** Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran, fortified cities, and sheepfolds.

**32:37** And the sons of Reuben built Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,

**32:38** Nebo, Baal-meon—with their names changed—and Sibmah; and they gave names to the cities that they built.

**32:39** Now the sons of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead and captured it, and drove out the Amorites who were in it.

**32:40** So Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he dwelt in it.

**32:41** And Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their tent-villages, and he called them Havvoth-jair.

**32:42** And Nobah went and captured Kenath and its daughter towns, and he called it Nobah, by his own name.

## Translator Notes

- **32:7** — Ketiv writes a consonantal waw where Qere reads teni'un, the Hiphil imperfect 2nd person masculine plural with paragogic nun of the root nw' ('turn away, discourage'), matching the Hiphil verb in 32:9.
- **32:11** — Hebrew 'im introducing an oath expresses an emphatic negation ('Surely ... shall not'). The idiom 'follow wholeheartedly' renders the Piel of ml' ('fill, fulfill') followed by 'aharay ('after Me'), echoed in 32:12.
- **32:14** — The Hebrew noun tarbut ('breed, brood, offspring,' derived from rbh, 'increase, multiply') is a hapax legomenon, occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **32:19** — Hebrew me'ever ('across, on the side of') is used twice in this verse for opposite sides of the Jordan, disambiguated first for the west ('and onward') and then for the east ('toward the east').
- **32:38** — The aside 'with their names changed' translates musabbot shem, a Hophal feminine plural participle of sbb ('turn, change') modifying Nebo and Baal-meon. The final clause literally reads 'and they called by names the names of the cities that they built.'
