## Text

**6:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When a man or a woman makes an extraordinary vow, the vow of a Nazirite, to dedicate himself to the LORD,

**6:3** he shall abstain from wine and strong drink; he shall drink no vinegar of wine or vinegar of strong drink, nor shall he drink any liquid steeped from grapes, nor eat grapes, fresh or dried.

**6:4** All the days of his dedication he shall not eat anything produced from the grapevine, from the seeds even to the skin.

**6:5** All the days of the vow of his dedication no razor shall pass over his head; until the days are fulfilled for which he dedicates himself to the LORD, he shall be holy, letting the locks of the hair of his head grow long.

**6:6** All the days of his dedication to the LORD he shall not go near a dead body.

**6:7** For his father or for his mother, for his brother or for his sister, he shall not defile himself for them when they die, because his dedication to his God is on his head.

**6:8** All the days of his dedication he is holy to the LORD.

**6:9** And if someone dies beside him very suddenly and defiles his dedicated head, then he shall shave his head on the day of his cleansing; on the seventh day he shall shave it.

**6:10** Then on the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**6:11** And the priest shall offer one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering, and make atonement for him for what he sinned concerning the dead body, and he shall consecrate his head on that day.

**6:12** And he shall dedicate to the LORD the days of his dedication and bring a male lamb a year old as a guilt offering; but the former days shall lapse, because his dedication became defiled.

**6:13** Now this is the law of the Nazirite: on the day the days of his dedication are fulfilled, he shall be brought to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**6:14** And he shall present his offering to the LORD: one unblemished male lamb a year old as a burnt offering, one unblemished ewe lamb a year old as a sin offering, and one unblemished ram as peace offerings,

**6:15** and a basket of unleavened bread, loaves of fine flour mixed with oil and unleavened wafers spread with oil, together with their grain offering and their drink offerings.

**6:16** Then the priest shall present them before the LORD and offer his sin offering and his burnt offering.

**6:17** And he shall offer the ram as a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD, together with the basket of unleavened bread; and the priest shall also offer its grain offering and its drink offering.

**6:18** Then the Nazirite shall shave his dedicated head at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and he shall take the hair of his dedicated head and put it on the fire that is under the sacrifice of the peace offerings.

**6:19** And the priest shall take the boiled shoulder from the ram, and one unleavened loaf from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and place them on the palms of the Nazirite after he has shaved off his dedicated hair.

**6:20** Then the priest shall wave them as a wave offering before the LORD; it is a holy portion for the priest, together with the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution. And afterward the Nazirite may drink wine.

**6:21** This is the law of the Nazirite who makes a vow, and of his offering to the LORD for his dedication, apart from whatever else he can afford; according to the vow that he makes, so he shall do, in addition to the law of his dedication.'"

**6:22** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:23** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, 'In this way you shall bless the sons of Israel, saying to them'—so they shall place My name on the sons of Israel, and I will bless them:

**6:24** 'May the LORD bless you and guard you;

**6:25** may the LORD cause His face to shine toward you and be gracious to you;

**6:26** may the LORD lift up His face toward you and establish peace for you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Hebrew yafli (Hiphil of pala) means "makes an extraordinary/special [vow]" or "specifically vows"; the root nazar (Hiphil hizzir, noun nezer) means "separate/dedicate," underlying the term Nazirite (nazir) and used with min in v. 3 for "abstain from."
- **6:4** — Both chartzannim ("seeds") and zag ("skin") occur only here in the Hebrew Bible; while ancient interpreters debated which term referred to grape seeds and which to skins, together they forbid every part of the grape.
- **6:13** — Hebrew yavi oto (literally "he shall bring him") uses an impersonal third-person active verb with passive force, "he shall be brought."
- **6:23** — In this Hebrew text, the divine promise ("so they shall place My name on the sons of Israel, and I will bless them") appears at the end of v. 23 before the priestly blessing, rather than as a separate v. 27 after the blessing as in standard Masoretic editions.
- **6:26** — Hebrew repeats panav elekha ("His face toward you") in both vv. 25 and 26, and concludes with the jussive ve-yasem (from sim, "set/establish," echoing v. 23 "place My name") rather than natan ("give").
