## Text

**27:1** Then the daughters of Zelophehad—son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph—approached. And these are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**27:2** They stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the leaders and all the congregation at the entrance of the tent of meeting, saying,

**27:3** "Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the midst of the congregation that banded together against the LORD in the congregation of Korah; instead, he died in his own sin, and he had no sons.

**27:4** Why should our father's name be removed from the midst of his clan because he had no son? Give us a holding among our father's brothers."

**27:5** So Moses presented their case before the LORD.

**27:6** And the LORD said to Moses, saying,

**27:7** "The daughters of Zelophehad are speaking rightly. You shall certainly give them a holding of inheritance among their father's brothers, and you shall transfer their father's inheritance to them.

**27:8** And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'When a man dies and has no son, then you shall transfer his inheritance to his daughter.

**27:9** And if he has no daughter, then you shall give his inheritance to his brothers.

**27:10** And if he has no brothers, then you shall give his inheritance to his father's brothers.

**27:11** And if his father has no brothers, then you shall give his inheritance to his closest relative from his clan, and he shall take possession of it.'" And it shall be a statute of judgment for the sons of Israel, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**27:12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go up to this mountain of the Abarim and see the land that I have given to the sons of Israel.

**27:13** Once you have seen it, you as well shall be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron was gathered,

**27:14** because in the wilderness of Zin, during the contention of the congregation, you rebelled against My command to sanctify Me at the waters in their sight." Those are the waters of Meribah of Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin.

**27:15** Then Moses spoke to the LORD, saying,

**27:16** "May the LORD, God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation,

**27:17** who will go out before them and who will come in before them, and who will bring them out and who will bring them in, so that the congregation of the LORD will not be like a flock that has no shepherd."

**27:18** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Take for yourself Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is spirit, and lay your hand upon him;

**27:19** and have him stand before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation, and charge him in their sight.

**27:20** And you shall place some of your dignity upon him, so that all the congregation of the sons of Israel may obey.

**27:21** And before Eleazar the priest he shall stand, who will inquire for him by the judgment of the Urim before the LORD. At his word they shall go out, and at his word they shall come in—he, and all the sons of Israel with him, and all the congregation."

**27:22** So Moses did just as the LORD commanded him. He took Joshua and had him stand before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation.

**27:23** Then he laid his hands upon him and charged him, just as the LORD had spoken by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Hebrew plays on the root ya'ad ("appoint/assemble") by pairing the noun "congregation" ('edah, used twice) with the Niphal participle "banded together" (hanno'adim), which is masculine plural to agree with the collective sense of the feminine singular noun.
- **27:14** — "Command" renders Hebrew pi (literally "My mouth"), with the plural verb "you rebelled" addressing both Moses and Aaron. "To sanctify Me" is a declarative Hiphil infinitive of qadash, meaning to treat or uphold God as holy.
- **27:18** — Hebrew ruach ("spirit") lacks the definite article here and denotes spiritual endowment or the Spirit of God equipping Joshua for leadership. Moses is told to lay his singular "hand" on Joshua here, whereas in 27:23 he lays his dual "hands" on him.
- **27:20** — "Some of your dignity" translates partitive min with hod ("splendor, majesty, dignity"), indicating a shared portion of Moses's leadership authority rather than its complete transfer before his death.
