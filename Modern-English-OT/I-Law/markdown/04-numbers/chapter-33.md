## Text

**33:1** These are the journeys of the sons of Israel, who went out from the land of Egypt according to their divisions, by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

**33:2** And Moses wrote down their points of departure according to their journeys at the command of the LORD; and these are their journeys according to their points of departure.

**33:3** They departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month. On the day after the Passover, the sons of Israel went out with an uplifted hand before the eyes of all the Egyptians.

**33:4** Meanwhile, the Egyptians were burying those whom the LORD had struck down among them, every firstborn; and on their gods the LORD executed judgments.

**33:5** The sons of Israel departed from Rameses and camped at Succoth.

**33:6** They departed from Succoth and camped at Etham, which is at the edge of the wilderness.

**33:7** They departed from Etham and turned back to Pi-hahiroth, which faces Baal-zephon, and they camped before Migdol.

**33:8** They departed from before Hahiroth and passed through the middle of the sea into the wilderness; then they walked a three-day journey in the wilderness of Etham and camped at Marah.

**33:9** They departed from Marah and came to Elim. At Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there.

**33:10** They departed from Elim and camped by the Sea of Reeds.

**33:11** They departed from the Sea of Reeds and camped in the wilderness of Sin.

**33:12** They departed from the wilderness of Sin and camped at Dophkah.

**33:13** They departed from Dophkah and camped at Alush.

**33:14** They departed from Alush and camped at Rephidim, and there was no water there for the people to drink.

**33:15** They departed from Rephidim and camped in the wilderness of Sinai.

**33:16** They departed from the wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah.

**33:17** They departed from Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth.

**33:18** They departed from Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah.

**33:19** They departed from Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez.

**33:20** They departed from Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah.

**33:21** They departed from Libnah and camped at Rissah.

**33:22** They departed from Rissah and camped at Kehelathah.

**33:23** They departed from Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shepher.

**33:24** They departed from Mount Shepher and camped at Haradah.

**33:25** They departed from Haradah and camped at Makheloth.

**33:26** They departed from Makheloth and camped at Tahath.

**33:27** They departed from Tahath and camped at Terah.

**33:28** They departed from Terah and camped at Mithkah.

**33:29** They departed from Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah.

**33:30** They departed from Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth.

**33:31** They departed from Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan.

**33:32** They departed from Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad.

**33:33** They departed from Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah.

**33:34** They departed from Jotbathah and camped at Abronah.

**33:35** They departed from Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber.

**33:36** They departed from Ezion-geber and camped in the wilderness of Zin, that is, Kadesh.

**33:37** They departed from Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, at the edge of the land of Edom.

**33:38** Aaron the priest went up to Mount Hor at the command of the LORD and died there, in the fortieth year after the sons of Israel went out from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month.

**33:39** And Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old at his death on Mount Hor.

**33:40** And the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who was dwelling in the Negev in the land of Canaan, heard about the arrival of the sons of Israel.

**33:41** They departed from Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.

**33:42** They departed from Zalmonah and camped at Punon.

**33:43** They departed from Punon and camped at Oboth.

**33:44** They departed from Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim, at the border of Moab.

**33:45** They departed from Iyim and camped at Dibon-gad.

**33:46** They departed from Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim.

**33:47** They departed from Almon-diblathaim and camped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.

**33:48** They departed from the mountains of Abarim and camped in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

**33:49** They camped along the Jordan, from Beth-jeshimoth as far as Abel-shittim, in the plains of Moab.

**33:50** Then the LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**33:51** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you cross over the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**33:52** then you shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, destroy all their figured images, destroy all their cast images, and demolish all their high places.

**33:53** And you shall take possession of the land and dwell in it, for to you I have given the land to possess it.

**33:54** You shall take the land as an inheritance by lot according to your clans: to a larger one you shall increase its inheritance, and to a smaller one you shall reduce its inheritance. Wherever the lot comes out for each one, it shall belong to him; according to the tribes of your fathers you shall take your inheritance.

**33:55** But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you, then those of them whom you leave remaining will become barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides, and they will harass you in the land in which you dwell.

**33:56** And it will happen that just as I intended to do to them, I will do to you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **33:4** — Hebrew uses the Piel participle of qabar ('bury'), expressing the extensive burial of many dead across Egypt.
- **33:8** — The Hebrew reads 'from before Hahiroth' (mippenei hahirot), without the 'Pi-' of 33:7.
- **33:10** — Hebrew yam-suph literally means 'Sea of Reeds,' the Septuagint calls it the Red Sea.
- **33:53** — The Hiphil of yarash means 'drive out' when taking inhabitants as its object (33:52, 55), but 'take possession of' when taking the land itself as its object here, paralleling the Qal infinitive 'to possess' at the end of the verse.
- **33:55** — Hebrew sikkim ('barbs') is a biblical hapax legomenon denoting sharp thorns or barbs, paired with tseninim ('thorns'); the verb tsarar ('harass') conveys active hostility.
