## Text

**17:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Tell Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, to lift up the firepans from the midst of the burning, and scatter the fire at a distance, for they have become holy.

**17:3** As for the firepans of these men who sinned at the cost of their lives, let them be made into hammered sheets as a plating for the altar, for they presented them before the LORD, and they became holy; and they shall be a sign to the sons of Israel."

**17:4** So Eleazar the priest took the bronze firepans that those who were burned had presented, and they were hammered out as a plating for the altar,

**17:5** as a reminder to the sons of Israel, so that no outsider, who is not from the seed of Aaron, should draw near to burn incense before the LORD, and so that he would not become like Korah and like his company, just as the LORD had spoken to him through Moses.

**17:6** On the next day, the entire congregation of the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and against Aaron, saying, "You have put the people of the LORD to death!"

**17:7** Now as the congregation was assembling against Moses and against Aaron, they turned toward the tent of meeting, and look, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the LORD appeared.

**17:8** Then Moses and Aaron came to the front of the tent of meeting.

**17:9** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:10** "Remove yourselves from the midst of this congregation, so that I may consume them in an instant." And they fell on their faces.

**17:11** Then Moses said to Aaron, "Take the firepan, put fire from the altar on it, and place incense on it; carry it quickly to the congregation and make atonement for them, for wrath has gone out from the presence of the LORD; the plague has begun!"

**17:12** So Aaron took it just as Moses had spoken and ran into the midst of the assembly, and look, the plague had begun among the people. So he put on the incense and made atonement for the people.

**17:13** He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was halted.

**17:14** Now those who died in the plague numbered fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died over the matter of Korah.

**17:15** Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the plague had been halted.

**17:16** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:17** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and take from them a staff for each father's house, from all their leaders according to their fathers' houses, twelve staffs. You shall write each man's name on his staff.

**17:18** And you shall write Aaron's name on the staff of Levi, for there is to be one staff for the head of their fathers' house.

**17:19** Then you shall deposit them in the tent of meeting before the testimony, where I meet with you.

**17:20** And it will be that the man whom I choose, his staff will sprout; so I will quiet the grumblings of the sons of Israel against Me, which they are grumbling against you."

**17:21** So Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and all their leaders gave him a staff for each leader, according to their fathers' houses, twelve staffs, and Aaron's staff was among their staffs.

**17:22** And Moses deposited the staffs before the LORD in the tent of the testimony.

**17:23** Now on the next day Moses went into the tent of the testimony, and look, Aaron's staff for the house of Levi had sprouted; it had brought forth buds, bloomed with blossoms, and yielded ripe almonds.

**17:24** Then Moses brought out all the staffs from before the LORD to all the sons of Israel; and they looked, and each man took his staff.

**17:25** And the LORD said to Moses, "Return Aaron's staff before the testimony for safekeeping as a sign to the sons of rebellion, so that you may bring their grumblings against Me to an end, and they will not die."

**17:26** And Moses did this: just as the LORD commanded him, so he did.

**17:27** Then the sons of Israel said to Moses, "Look, we are perishing! We are lost, all of us are lost!

**17:28** Everyone who draws near, who draws near to the tabernacle of the LORD, dies! Are we to perish altogether?"

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — Hebrew ha-chatta'im ha'elleh be-nafshotam literally reads 'these sinners in/against their lives', using the preposition be- of price or cost ('at the cost of their lives'). The noun riqqu'ei ('hammered sheets') occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **17:10** — Hebrew herommu is a Niphal imperative 2mp from the geminate root rmm (a by-form of rum, 'be lifted up'), commanding Moses and Aaron to remove themselves from the congregation.
- **17:20** — Hebrew va-hashikkoti is the sole Hiphil occurrence of shakhakh ('subside / abate') in the Hebrew Bible, expressing God's act of quieting the Israelites' grumblings.
- **17:28** — The emphatic repetition ha-qarev ha-qarev ('who draws near, who draws near') reflects the people's terror, and Qal perfect tamnu with ligvoa literally asks 'have we come to an end in perishing?' (i.e., 'are we to perish altogether?').
