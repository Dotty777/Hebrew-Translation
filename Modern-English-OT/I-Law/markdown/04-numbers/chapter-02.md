## Text

**2:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**2:2** "The sons of Israel shall camp, each man beside his own banner, with the emblems of their fathers' house; they shall camp at a distance, facing the tent of meeting all around.

**2:3** Those who camp on the east, toward the rising sun, shall be the banner of the camp of Judah by their forces; the leader of the sons of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab,

**2:4** and his force, their mustered men, numbers seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**2:5** And those who camp next to him shall be the tribe of Issachar; the leader of the sons of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar,

**2:6** and his force, his mustered men, numbers fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**2:7** The tribe of Zebulun: the leader of the sons of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon,

**2:8** and his force, his mustered men, numbers fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**2:9** All the mustered men of the camp of Judah number one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred by their forces. They shall set out first.

**2:10** The banner of the camp of Reuben shall be on the south, by their forces; the leader of the sons of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur,

**2:11** and his force, his mustered men, numbers forty-six thousand five hundred.

**2:12** And those who camp next to him shall be the tribe of Simeon; the leader of the sons of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,

**2:13** and his force, their mustered men, numbers fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**2:14** And the tribe of Gad: the leader of the sons of Gad is Eliasaph son of Reuel,

**2:15** and his force, their mustered men, numbers forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**2:16** All the mustered men of the camp of Reuben number one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty by their forces, and they shall set out second.

**2:17** Then the tent of meeting, the camp of the Levites, shall set out in the middle of the camps; just as they camp, so they shall set out, each man at his post, by their banners.

**2:18** The banner of the camp of Ephraim, by their forces, shall be on the west; the leader of the sons of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud,

**2:19** and his force, their mustered men, numbers forty thousand five hundred.

**2:20** And next to him shall be the tribe of Manasseh; the leader of the sons of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,

**2:21** and his force, their mustered men, numbers thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**2:22** And the tribe of Benjamin: the leader of the sons of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni,

**2:23** and his force, their mustered men, numbers thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**2:24** All the mustered men of the camp of Ephraim number one hundred eight thousand one hundred by their forces, and they shall set out third.

**2:25** The banner of the camp of Dan shall be on the north, by their forces; the leader of the sons of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai,

**2:26** and his force, their mustered men, numbers sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**2:27** And those who camp next to him shall be the tribe of Asher; the leader of the sons of Asher is Pagiel son of Ochran,

**2:28** and his force, their mustered men, numbers forty-one thousand five hundred.

**2:29** And the tribe of Naphtali: the leader of the sons of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan,

**2:30** and his force, their mustered men, numbers fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**2:31** All the mustered men of the camp of Dan number one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set out last, by their banners."

**2:32** These are the mustered men of the sons of Israel by their fathers' house; all the mustered men of the camps by their forces numbered six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.

**2:33** Yet the Levites were not mustered among the sons of Israel, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**2:34** And the sons of Israel did according to everything that the LORD had commanded Moses; so they camped by their banners and so they set out, each man by his clans, with his fathers' house.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — Hebrew degel ("banner") denotes a tribal military standard and, by extension throughout this chapter, the three-tribe division grouped under it, paired here with the plural otot ("emblems") for the smaller insignia of each ancestral house. Hebrew minneged denotes a position facing opposite from a distance.
- **2:6** — Here and in verses 8 and 11, the Masoretic Text uses the third-person masculine singular suffix in pequdav ("his mustered men", referring to the leader or tribe), whereas the other nine tribal entries use the third-person masculine plural suffix in pequdehem ("their mustered men", referring to the sons of the tribe).
- **2:14** — The Masoretic Text reads Reuel here, whereas Numbers 1:14, 7:42, 7:47, and 10:20 read Deuel, reflecting the graphical similarity between Hebrew resh and dalet.
- **2:17** — "At his post" translates the Hebrew idiom al-yado (literally "at his hand"), denoting an appointed place or station. "The camp of the Levites" stands in direct apposition to "the tent of meeting" without a conjunction or preposition in the Hebrew.
- **2:33** — "Were ... mustered" renders the rare Hothpaal reflexive-passive form hotpaqdu (from paqad), attested in the Hebrew Bible only in Numbers 1:47, 2:33, 26:62, and 1 Kings 20:27.
