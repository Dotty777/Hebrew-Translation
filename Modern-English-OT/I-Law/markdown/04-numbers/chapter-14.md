## Text

**14:1** So the whole congregation raised their voice and cried out, and the people wept that night.

**14:2** All the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and against Aaron, and the whole congregation said to them, "If only we had died in the land of Egypt! Or if only we had died in this wilderness!

**14:3** And why is the LORD bringing us to this land to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will be plunder. Is it not better for us to return to Egypt?"

**14:4** And they said to each other, "Let us appoint a leader, and let us return to Egypt."

**14:5** And Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before the whole assembly of the congregation of the sons of Israel.

**14:6** And Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, from among those who had explored the land, tore their garments.

**14:7** And they spoke to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, "The land through which we passed to explore it, the land is very, very good.

**14:8** If the LORD takes pleasure in us, then He will bring us to this land and give it to us, a land that is flowing with milk and honey.

**14:9** Only do not rebel against the LORD; and as for you, do not be afraid of the people of the land, for they are our bread. Their shade has departed from over them, and the LORD is with us; do not be afraid of them."

**14:10** And the whole congregation spoke of stoning them with stones, but the glory of the LORD appeared at the tent of meeting to all the sons of Israel.

**14:11** And the LORD said to Moses, "How long will this people scorn Me? And how long will they not trust in Me, in spite of all the signs that I have done in their midst?

**14:12** I will strike them with pestilence and disinherit them, and I will make you into a nation greater and stronger than they."

**14:13** But Moses said to the LORD, "Then the Egyptians will hear, for You brought this people up from their midst by Your power,

**14:14** and they will tell the inhabitants of this land. They have heard that You, LORD, are in the midst of this people—for You, LORD, appear eye to eye, and Your cloud stands over them, and in a pillar of cloud You go before them by day and in a pillar of fire by night.

**14:15** Now if You put this people to death as one man, then the nations that have heard the report of You will say,

**14:16** 'Because the LORD was unable to bring this people into the land that He swore to them, He slaughtered them in the wilderness.'

**14:17** And now, please, let the power of the Lord be great, just as You spoke, saying,

**14:18** 'The LORD is slow to anger and abundant in loyal love, pardoning iniquity and transgression, yet He will certainly not acquit the guilty, bringing the iniquity of fathers to account upon children, upon the third and upon the fourth generations.'

**14:19** Please forgive the iniquity of this people according to the greatness of Your loyal love, and just as You have pardoned this people from Egypt until now."

**14:20** And the LORD said, "I have forgiven, in accordance with your word.

**14:21** Nevertheless, as I live, and as the entire earth will be filled with the glory of the LORD,

**14:22** all the men who saw My glory and My signs that I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, yet tested Me these ten times and did not listen to My voice—

**14:23** not one of them will see the land that I swore to their fathers, and none of those who scorn Me will see it.

**14:24** But as for My servant Caleb, because a different spirit has been with him and he has followed Me wholeheartedly, I will bring him into the land where he went, and his offspring will take possession of it.

**14:25** Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites are living in the valley. Tomorrow turn around and set out into the wilderness by the way to the Sea of Reeds."

**14:26** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**14:27** "How long must I bear with this evil congregation who are grumbling against Me? I have heard the grumblings of the sons of Israel that they are grumbling against Me.

**14:28** Tell them, 'As I live, declares the LORD, surely just as you have spoken in My ears, so will I do to you:

**14:29** In this wilderness your corpses will fall, even all of you who were registered, according to your full count, from twenty years old and upward, who have grumbled against Me.

**14:30** You will certainly not come into the land I lifted up My hand to settle you in, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

**14:31** But as for your little ones, who you said would be plunder, I will bring them in, and they will come to know the land that you rejected.

**14:32** Yet your own corpses will fall in this wilderness.

**14:33** And your children will be shepherds in the wilderness for forty years, and they will bear your acts of prostitution until your corpses come to an end in the wilderness.

**14:34** By the number of the days in which you explored the land, forty days—for each day a year—you will bear your iniquities for forty years, and you will know My hostility.

**14:35** I, the LORD, have spoken. Surely I will do this to this whole evil congregation who have assembled against Me: in this wilderness they will come to an end, and there they will die.'"

**14:36** Now the men whom Moses sent to explore the land, who returned and caused the whole congregation to grumble against him by spreading a bad report against the land—

**14:37** those men who spread an evil report about the land died by a plague before the LORD.

**14:38** But Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh stayed alive from among those men who went to explore the land.

**14:39** And Moses spoke these words to all the sons of Israel, and the people mourned deeply.

**14:40** They rose early in the morning and went up to the crest of the hill country, saying, "Here we are, and we will go up to the place about which the LORD spoke, for we have sinned."

**14:41** But Moses said, "Why then are you violating the command of the LORD? And it will not succeed!

**14:42** Do not go up, for the LORD is not in your midst, so that you will not be defeated before your enemies.

**14:43** For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there in front of you, and you will fall by the sword; since you have turned back from following the LORD, the LORD will not be with you."

**14:44** Yet they presumed to go up to the crest of the hill country, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD and Moses did not move from the midst of the camp.

**14:45** Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down, and they struck them and crushed them as far as Hormah.

## Translator Notes

- **14:9** — Hebrew uses two vivid idioms here: "our bread" means easily consumed or defeated, and "their shade" is a metaphor for their shelter and protection.
- **14:18** — The emphatic Piel infinitive absolute plus imperfect literally reads "acquitting He will not acquit," with "the guilty" implied; the Qal participle rendered "bringing ... to account" denotes judicial reckoning or consequence.
- **14:21** — The Niphal verb takes a definite direct-object marker before "the entire earth" in a passive construction, and the oath formula continues into v. 23, where conditional "if they see" functions idiomatically as an emphatic negative oath.
- **14:34** — "My hostility" translates the rare noun tenuah (occurring elsewhere only in the plural at Job 33:10), derived from a root meaning to frustrate, oppose, or alienate.
- **14:36** — Translated according to the Qere, a Hiphil causative form ("caused ... to grumble") matching the direct object "the whole congregation"; the written Ketiv form is Niphal ("grumbled").
