## Text

**1:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they went out from the land of Egypt, saying,

**1:2** "Take the tally of the entire assembly of the sons of Israel, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, every male by the head.

**1:3** From twenty years old and upward, everyone in Israel who goes out to war, you and Aaron shall register them according to their divisions.

**1:4** Furthermore, one man for each tribe shall be with you, each one the head of his fathers' house.

**1:5** Now these are the names of the men who shall stand with you: for Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;

**1:6** for Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

**1:7** for Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;

**1:8** for Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;

**1:9** for Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;

**1:10** for the sons of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;

**1:11** for Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;

**1:12** for Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;

**1:13** for Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran;

**1:14** for Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;

**1:15** for Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

**1:16** These were those summoned from the assembly, leaders of their fathers' tribes; they were heads of the thousands of Israel.

**1:17** Then Moses and Aaron took these men who had been specified by name.

**1:18** And they convened the entire assembly on the first day of the second month, and they declared their lineage according to their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, by the head.

**1:19** As the LORD had commanded Moses, so he registered them in the wilderness of Sinai.

**1:20** Now the sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, by the head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:21** those registered of them, for the tribe of Reuben, were forty-six thousand five hundred.

**1:22** Of the sons of Simeon, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, those registered of him, by the count of names, by the head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:23** those registered of them, for the tribe of Simeon, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**1:24** Of the sons of Judah, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:25** those registered of them, for the tribe of Judah, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**1:26** Of the sons of Issachar, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:27** those registered of them, for the tribe of Issachar, were fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**1:28** Of the sons of Zebulun, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:29** those registered of them, for the tribe of Zebulun, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**1:30** Of the sons of Joseph, of the sons of Ephraim, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:31** those registered of them, for the tribe of Ephraim, were forty thousand five hundred.

**1:32** Of the sons of Manasseh, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:33** those registered of them, for the tribe of Manasseh, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**1:34** Of the sons of Benjamin, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:35** those registered of them, for the tribe of Benjamin, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**1:36** Of the sons of Gad, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:37** those registered of them, for the tribe of Gad, were forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**1:38** Of the sons of Dan, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:39** those registered of them, for the tribe of Dan, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**1:40** Of the sons of Asher, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:41** those registered of them, for the tribe of Asher, were forty-one thousand five hundred.

**1:42** The sons of Naphtali, their lineages, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the count of names, from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out to war:

**1:43** those registered of them, for the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**1:44** These are those registered, whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel registered, twelve men; each one was for his fathers' house.

**1:45** Thus all those registered of the sons of Israel, by their fathers' houses, from twenty years old and upward, everyone in Israel who goes out to war:

**1:46** all those registered came to six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.

**1:47** But the Levites, by their fathers' tribe, were not registered among them.

**1:48** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**1:49** "Only the tribe of Levi you shall not register, and you shall not take their tally among the sons of Israel.

**1:50** But as for you, appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its articles, and over everything that belongs to it. They themselves shall carry the tabernacle and all its articles, and they shall minister to it, and they shall camp around the tabernacle.

**1:51** When the tabernacle sets out, the Levites shall take it down, and when the tabernacle camps, the Levites shall raise it up; and the outsider who draws near shall be put to death.

**1:52** And the sons of Israel shall camp, each by his own camp and each by his own banner, according to their divisions.

**1:53** The Levites, however, shall camp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that no wrath will be upon the assembly of the sons of Israel; and the Levites shall keep watch over the tabernacle of the testimony."

**1:54** So the sons of Israel did; according to everything the LORD commanded Moses, thus they did.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Hebrew se'u et-ro'sh literally means "lift up the head of," an idiom for taking a tally or census (also in 1:49). Likewise, legulgelotam literally means "according to their skulls," denoting an individual headcount.
- **1:16** — Ketiv qry'y (adjectival qatil plural construct, "chosen/called ones of") vs. Qere qeru'e (Qal passive participle plural construct of qara', "those summoned of"); translated following the Qere. The noun 'alpe ("thousands") can also denote tribal military divisions or clans.
- **1:18** — The verb wayyityalledu ("and they declared their lineage") is a biblical hapax legomenon in the Hithpael stem of yalad ("bear/beget"), expressing reflexive declaration or enrollment of one's ancestry.
- **1:47** — The verb hotpaqedu ("were registered") is in the rare Hothpaal (passive reflexive) stem of paqad, contrasting with the Qal stem used throughout this chapter for official census registration and the Hiphil stem in 1:50 ("appoint").
- **1:51** — The substantivized Qal participle hazzar ("the outsider," from zur, "be a stranger") denotes any unauthorized non-Levite who approaches the tabernacle and its sacred service.
