## Text

**30:1** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

**30:2** Then Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the word that the LORD has commanded:

**30:3** When a man vows a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath to bind an obligation upon himself, he shall not profane his word; he shall do according to everything that goes out from his mouth.

**30:4** And when a woman vows a vow to the LORD and binds an obligation in her father's house, in her youth,

**30:5** and her father hears her vow and her obligation that she has bound upon herself, and her father keeps silent toward her, then all her vows shall stand, and every obligation that she has bound upon herself shall stand.

**30:6** But if her father overrules her on the day he hears of it, not one of her vows or her obligations that she has bound upon herself shall stand; and the LORD will pardon her, because her father overruled her.

**30:7** And if she indeed marries a husband while her vows are upon her, or a hasty utterance of her lips by which she has bound herself,

**30:8** and her husband hears of it, and on the day he hears of it keeps silent toward her, then her vows shall stand, and her obligations that she has bound upon herself shall stand.

**30:9** But if on the day her husband hears of it he overrules her, then he nullifies her vow that is upon her and the hasty utterance of her lips by which she has bound herself; and the LORD will pardon her.

**30:10** And as for the vow of a widow or a divorced woman, everything that she has bound upon herself shall stand upon her.

**30:11** And if she vows in her husband's house, or binds an obligation upon herself with an oath,

**30:12** and her husband hears of it and keeps silent toward her, and does not overrule her, then all her vows shall stand, and every obligation that she has bound upon herself shall stand.

**30:13** But if her husband indeed nullifies them on the day he hears of them, nothing that goes out from her lips concerning her vows or concerning the obligation upon herself shall stand; her husband has nullified them, and the LORD will pardon her.

**30:14** Every vow and every oath of obligation to afflict the soul, her husband may uphold it, or her husband may nullify it.

**30:15** But if her husband keeps completely silent toward her from day to day, then he upholds all her vows or all her obligations that are upon her; he has upheld them, because he kept silent toward her on the day he heard of them.

**30:16** But if he indeed nullifies them after hearing of them, then he shall bear her iniquity."

**30:17** These are the statutes that the LORD commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, between a father and his daughter in her youth, in her father's house.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — In Masoretic versification, 30:1 closes the festival-offering instructions of chapters 28-29; most English Bibles number this verse as 29:40 and consequently number 30:2-17 as 30:1-16.
- **30:3** — Hebrew hishava is a Niphal infinitive absolute continuing the finite verb 'vows' (yiddor); lo yachel uses the Hiphil of halal, literally meaning 'he shall not treat as profane' or violate his spoken word.
- **30:6** — The Hiphil verb heni (from nu, to hold back, restrain, or forbid) takes the woman herself as its direct object, whereas hefer (from parar, to nullify, 30:9, 13-16) takes the vow or obligation as its object.
- **30:7** — Hebrew hayo tihyeh le-ish uses the Qal infinitive absolute plus imperfect of hayah with le-ish, literally 'if she indeed becomes a man's,' an idiom for marriage; mivta (occurring only here and in 30:9) derives from bata, meaning to speak hastily or rashly.
- **30:14** — Hebrew le-annot nefesh (Piel infinitive construct of anah plus nefesh, literally 'to afflict a soul') refers to self-denial such as fasting, matching the same idiom in 29:7; the husband's authority to 'uphold' (Hiphil of qum) corresponds to the Qal 'stand' used earlier for valid vows.
