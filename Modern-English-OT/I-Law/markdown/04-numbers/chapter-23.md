## Text

**23:1** Then Balaam said to Balak, "Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me here."

**23:2** Balak did just as Balaam had spoken, and Balak and Balaam offered up a bull and a ram on each altar.

**23:3** Then Balaam said to Balak, "Station yourself beside your burnt offering, and I will go; perhaps the LORD will come to meet me, and whatever He shows me I will tell you." So he went to a bare height.

**23:4** Then God met Balaam, and he said to Him, "I have set in order the seven altars, and I have offered up a bull and a ram on each altar."

**23:5** And the LORD put a word in Balaam's mouth and said, "Return to Balak, and in this way you shall speak."

**23:6** So he returned to him, and there he was, standing beside his burnt offering, he and all the officials of Moab.

**23:7** Then he took up his oracle and said, "From Aram Balak has led me, the king of Moab from the mountains of the east: 'Come, curse Jacob for me, and come, denounce Israel!'

**23:8** How shall I curse the one God has not cursed? And how shall I denounce the one the LORD has not denounced?

**23:9** For from the summit of the crags I see him, and from the hills I gaze upon him; look, a people that dwells apart, and among the nations it does not reckon itself.

**23:10** Who has counted the dust of Jacob, or the number of a fourth part of Israel? May my soul die the death of the upright, and may my latter end be like his!"

**23:11** Then Balak said to Balaam, "What have you done to me? I took you to curse my enemies, and look, you have thoroughly blessed them!"

**23:12** He replied and said, "What the LORD puts in my mouth—must I not take care to speak it?"

**23:13** Then Balak said to him, "Please come with me to another place from which you can see them; you will see only their outer edge and will not see all of them. And curse them for me from there."

**23:14** So he took him to the field of Zophim, to the summit of Pisgah, and he built seven altars and offered up a bull and a ram on each altar.

**23:15** He said to Balak, "Station yourself here beside your burnt offering, while I myself am met over there."

**23:16** Then the LORD met Balaam, put a word in his mouth, and said, "Return to Balak, and in this way you shall speak."

**23:17** So he came to him, and there he was, standing beside his burnt offering, and the officials of Moab were with him. And Balak said to him, "What has the LORD spoken?"

**23:18** Then he took up his oracle and said, "Rise, Balak, and hear; give ear to me, son of Zippor!

**23:19** God is no man, that He should lie, nor a son of man, that He should relent. Has He Himself said, and will He not act? Or has He spoken, and will He not establish it?

**23:20** Look, I have received a charge to bless; He has blessed, and I cannot turn it back.

**23:21** He has not observed iniquity in Jacob, nor has He seen trouble in Israel; the LORD his God is with him, and the shout of a king is in his midst.

**23:22** God brings them out from Egypt; He is to him like the horns of a wild ox.

**23:23** For no enchantment is against Jacob, and no divination is against Israel; at this time it will be said of Jacob and of Israel, 'What God has done!'

**23:24** Look, a people—like a lioness it rises, and like a lion it lifts itself up; it will not lie down until it devours prey and drinks the blood of those killed."

**23:25** Then Balak said to Balaam, "Neither curse them at all, nor bless them at all!"

**23:26** Balaam replied and said to Balak, "Did I not speak to you, saying, 'Everything that the LORD speaks, that I will do'?"

**23:27** Then Balak said to Balaam, "Please come, I will take you to another place; perhaps it will be right in the eyes of God, and you will curse them for me from there."

**23:28** So Balak took Balaam to the summit of Peor, which overlooks the wasteland.

**23:29** Balaam said to Balak, "Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me here."

**23:30** And Balak did just as Balaam had said, and he offered up a bull and a ram on each altar.

## Translator Notes

- **23:3** — The noun shephi is understood here adverbially as "to a bare height" (cf. Isaiah 41:18; Jeremiah 3:2), though some older traditions interpret it as "alone" or "quietly."
- **23:10** — The Masoretic Text reads the noun "number" (mispar) with "a fourth part" (rova', possibly alluding to one of Israel's four camp divisions); some ancient versions read the verb "who has numbered."
- **23:15** — The Niphal imperfect iqqareh ("am met") has no explicit object in Hebrew, and the repeated adverb koh ("here ... over there") contrasts Balak's position with Balaam's.
- **23:22** — The rare plural noun to'aphoth with re'em ("wild ox") refers to the animal's towering horns or loftiness. The second clause literally reads "like the horns of a wild ox to him" and may also be understood as "Israel has, as it were, the horns of a wild ox."
- **23:23** — The preposition beth ("against") may alternatively be read locatively ("in Jacob ... in Israel"), and the preposition lamed ("of") may also be rendered "to Jacob and to Israel."
