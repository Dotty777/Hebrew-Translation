## Text

**12:1** And Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses on account of the Cushite woman he had taken, because he had taken a Cushite woman.

**12:2** They said, "Has the LORD spoken solely through Moses alone? Has He not spoken through us as well?" And the LORD heard.

**12:3** Now the man Moses was exceedingly humble, more than all humanity on the face of the ground.

**12:4** All at once the LORD said to Moses, to Aaron, and to Miriam, "Go out, the three of you, to the tent of meeting." And the three of them went out.

**12:5** Then the LORD descended in a column of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent; He summoned Aaron and Miriam, and both of them stepped out.

**12:6** He said, "Listen now to My words: If there is a prophet of yours, I, the LORD, make Myself known to him in a vision; in a dream I speak with him.

**12:7** My servant Moses is not like that; in all My house he is trustworthy.

**12:8** Mouth to mouth I speak with him, in plain sight and not in riddles, and he gazes at the form of the LORD. Why then did you not fear to speak against My servant, against Moses?"

**12:9** The LORD's anger flared against them, and He went away.

**12:10** And the cloud moved away from above the tent, and look, Miriam was leprous, like snow. Aaron turned to Miriam, and look, she was leprous.

**12:11** Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, do not lay sin on us, because we have acted foolishly and because we have sinned.

**12:12** Please do not let her be like someone dead, who, when coming out from his mother's womb, has half his flesh consumed."

**12:13** Then Moses cried out to the LORD, saying, "God, please, heal her, please!"

**12:14** And the LORD said to Moses, "If her father had truly spat in her face, would she not be disgraced for seven days? Let her be confined outside the camp for seven days, and afterward she may be gathered back in."

**12:15** So Miriam was confined outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not journey on until Miriam was gathered back in.

**12:16** Afterward the people journeyed from Hazeroth and encamped in the wilderness of Paran.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The verb "spoke" (Hebrew Piel 3fs) is feminine singular, agreeing first with Miriam before Aaron is named. The idiom "spoke against" uses the same verb-preposition construction (d-b-r + b) that means "speak through" in v. 2 and "speak with" in vv. 6 and 8.
- **12:6** — Following the Masoretic accents, "the LORD" opens the main clause ("I, the LORD, make Myself known"), though some construe it with the preceding clause as "a prophet of the LORD among you." The verb "make Myself known" is Hithpael of y-d-a.
- **12:8** — "In plain sight" renders the adverbial masculine noun mar'eh (appearance/sight), contrasting with the feminine noun mar'ah (vision) in v. 6.
- **12:10** — The Pual participle rendered "leprous" refers to tzaraat, a biblical scaly skin affliction broader than modern Hansen's disease; the Hebrew text has no separate word for "white" before "like snow."
