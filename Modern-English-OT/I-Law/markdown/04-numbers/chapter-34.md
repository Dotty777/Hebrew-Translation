## Text

**34:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:2** "Command the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you come into the land of Canaan, this is the land that will fall to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan by its boundaries.

**34:3** Your southern side shall be from the wilderness of Zin beside Edom, and your southern boundary shall be from the edge of the Salt Sea eastward.

**34:4** Then the boundary shall turn for you south of the Ascent of Akrabbim and pass on to Zin, and its outer limits shall be south of Kadesh-barnea; then it shall go out to Hazar-addar and pass on to Azmon.

**34:5** And the boundary shall turn from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt, and its outer limits shall be at the sea.

**34:6** For the western boundary, the Great Sea and its border shall be yours; this shall be your western boundary.

**34:7** And this shall be your northern boundary: from the Great Sea you shall mark out your line to Mount Hor.

**34:8** From Mount Hor you shall mark out a line to Lebo-hamath, and the outer limits of the boundary shall be at Zedad.

**34:9** Then the boundary shall go out to Ziphron, and its outer limits shall be at Hazar-enan. This shall be your northern boundary.

**34:10** And you shall mark out for yourselves the eastern boundary from Hazar-enan to Shepham.

**34:11** The boundary shall go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain; then the boundary shall go down and reach the slope of the Sea of Chinnereth on the east.

**34:12** And the boundary shall go down to the Jordan, and its outer limits shall be at the Salt Sea. This shall be the land for you by its boundaries all around.'"

**34:13** Then Moses commanded the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the land that you shall inherit for yourselves by lot, which the LORD has commanded to give to the nine tribes and the half-tribe.

**34:14** For the tribe of the sons of the Reubenites according to their fathers' households and the tribe of the sons of the Gadites according to their fathers' households have taken their inheritance, and the half-tribe of Manasseh has taken its inheritance.

**34:15** The two tribes and the half-tribe have taken their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho, eastward toward the sunrise."

**34:16** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:17** "These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land for you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.

**34:18** And you shall take one leader from each tribe to apportion the land as an inheritance.

**34:19** And these are the names of the men: for the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh.

**34:20** And for the tribe of the sons of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud.

**34:21** For the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon.

**34:22** And for the tribe of the sons of Dan, a leader, Bukki son of Jogli.

**34:23** For the sons of Joseph: for the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, a leader, Hanniel son of Ephod.

**34:24** And for the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, a leader, Kemuel son of Shiphtan.

**34:25** And for the tribe of the sons of Zebulun, a leader, Elizaphan son of Parnach.

**34:26** And for the tribe of the sons of Issachar, a leader, Paltiel son of Azzan.

**34:27** And for the tribe of the sons of Asher, a leader, Ahihud son of Shelomi.

**34:28** And for the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, a leader, Pedahel son of Ammihud."

**34:29** These are the ones whom the LORD commanded to apportion the inheritance to the sons of Israel in the land of Canaan.

## Translator Notes

- **34:4** — Ketiv reads singular והיה ("shall be"), while Qere reads plural וְהָיוּ agreeing with the plural subject תּוֹצְאֹתָיו ("its outer limits," literally "its outgoings").
- **34:6** — MT reads literally הַיָּם הַגָּדוֹל וּגְבוּל ("the Great Sea and a boundary"), referring either to the Mediterranean Sea and its coastal border or using the conjunction epexegetically ("the Great Sea as a boundary").
- **34:8** — לְבֹא חֲמָת is translated as the northern place name Lebo-hamath; it can also be rendered literally as "the entrance of Hamath."
- **34:17** — Here and in v. 18 the Qal of נחל (יִנְחֲלוּ, לִנְחֹל) carries the transitive sense "apportion as an inheritance" (parallel to the Piel לְנַחֵל in v. 29), whereas v. 13 uses the Hithpael (תִּתְנַחֲלוּ) for "inherit for yourselves."
