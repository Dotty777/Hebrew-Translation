## Text

**18:1** Then the LORD said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your father's house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood.

**18:2** Also bring near with you your brothers, the tribe of Levi, your father's tribe, so that they may be joined to you and minister to you, while you and your sons with you are before the tent of the testimony.

**18:3** They shall keep your charge and the charge of the entire tent; only they shall not draw near to the articles of the sanctuary or to the altar, so that neither they nor you will die.

**18:4** They shall be joined to you and keep the charge of the tent of meeting for all the service of the tent, and no outsider shall draw near to you.

**18:5** And you shall keep the charge of the sanctuary and the charge of the altar, so that there will no longer be wrath upon the sons of Israel.

**18:6** And look, I Myself have taken your brothers the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel; they are a gift to you, given to the LORD to perform the service of the tent of meeting.

**18:7** But you and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for every matter of the altar and for what is inside the curtain, and you shall serve. I give your priesthood as a service of gift, and the outsider who draws near shall be put to death."

**18:8** Then the LORD spoke to Aaron, "And look, I Myself have given you the charge of My contributions; all the holy things of the sons of Israel I have given to you as a share, and to your sons as a lasting statute.

**18:9** This shall be yours from the most holy things, from the fire: every offering of theirs—every grain offering of theirs, every sin offering of theirs, and every guilt offering of theirs that they return to Me—is most holy, for you and for your sons.

**18:10** As a most holy thing you shall eat it; every male may eat it; it shall be holy to you.

**18:11** And this is yours: the contribution of their gift, with all the wave offerings of the sons of Israel; I have given them to you and to your sons and to your daughters with you as a lasting statute. Everyone who is clean in your house may eat it.

**18:12** All the best of the fresh oil and all the best of the new wine and the grain—the first of them that they give to the LORD—I have given to you.

**18:13** The firstfruits of everything in their land that they bring to the LORD shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house may eat it.

**18:14** Every devoted thing in Israel shall be yours.

**18:15** Everything that opens the womb of all flesh, which they present to the LORD, both of man and of animal, shall be yours; yet you shall certainly redeem the firstborn of man, and the firstborn of unclean animals you shall redeem.

**18:16** And as for its redemption, from a month old you shall redeem it at your valuation: five shekels of silver by the shekel of the sanctuary—it is twenty gerahs.

**18:17** However, you shall not redeem the firstborn of an ox, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat; they are holy. You shall splash their blood on the altar and turn their fat into smoke as a fire offering, for a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**18:18** And their flesh shall be yours; like the breast of the wave offering and like the right thigh, it shall be yours.

**18:19** All the contributions of the holy things that the sons of Israel lift up to the LORD I have given to you and to your sons and to your daughters with you as a lasting statute. It is an everlasting covenant of salt before the LORD for you and for your offspring with you."

**18:20** Then the LORD said to Aaron, "In their land you shall not inherit, and there shall be no portion for you in their midst; I am your portion and your inheritance in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**18:21** And to the sons of Levi, look, I have given every tithe in Israel as an inheritance, in exchange for their service that they perform—the service of the tent of meeting.

**18:22** And the sons of Israel shall no longer draw near to the tent of meeting, to bear sin and die.

**18:23** Rather, the Levite himself shall perform the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall bear their iniquity; it shall be a lasting statute throughout your generations, and in the midst of the sons of Israel they shall inherit no inheritance.

**18:24** For the tithe of the sons of Israel, which they lift up to the LORD as a contribution, I have given to the Levites as an inheritance; therefore I have said concerning them, 'In the midst of the sons of Israel they shall inherit no inheritance.'"

**18:25** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:26** "And to the Levites you shall speak and say to them, 'When you take from the sons of Israel the tithe that I have given you from them as your inheritance, then you shall lift up from it a contribution to the LORD, a tithe from the tithe.

**18:27** And your contribution shall be reckoned to you like the grain from the threshing floor and like the fullness from the winepress.

**18:28** Thus you also shall lift up a contribution to the LORD from all your tithes that you take from the sons of Israel, and from it you shall give the LORD's contribution to Aaron the priest.

**18:29** From all your gifts you shall lift up every contribution to the LORD, from all its best, its consecrated part from it.'

**18:30** And you shall say to them, 'When you lift up its best from it, then it shall be reckoned to the Levites like the produce of the threshing floor and like the produce of the winepress.

**18:31** And you may eat it in any place, you and your household, for it is wages for you in exchange for your service in the tent of meeting.

**18:32** And you will bear no sin on account of it when you lift up its best from it; and you shall not profane the holy things of the sons of Israel, so that you will not die.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:2** — The Niphal verb yillawu ("be joined") plays on the tribal name Levi (lewi), a wordplay also echoed in verse 4 (nilwu).
- **18:8** — Hebrew moshchah ("share") is understood either as a measured allotment from a root meaning "measure" or as an entitlement granted by reason of priestly anointing from mashach ("anoint").
- **18:10** — Hebrew beqodesh haqqodashim is translated here with the preposition be- of essence ("as a most holy thing"); some take it locatively as "in a most holy place," referring to the sacred courtyard precinct.
- **18:12** — Hebrew chelev literally means "fat," used idiomatically here and in verses 29, 30, and 32 for the richest or best portion of produce.
- **18:29** — Hebrew miqdesho ("its consecrated part") is a suffixed form of miqdash (elsewhere "sanctuary"), denoting here the sacred portion set apart from the tithe.
