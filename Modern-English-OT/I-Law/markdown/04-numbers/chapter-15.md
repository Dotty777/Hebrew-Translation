## Text

**15:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you come into the land of your dwelling places, which I am giving to you,

**15:3** and you prepare a fire offering to the LORD, a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a solemn vow or as a voluntary offering or at your appointed festivals, to produce a soothing fragrance to the LORD from the herd or from the flock,

**15:4** then the one presenting his offering to the LORD shall present a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of oil.

**15:5** And you shall prepare a quarter of a hin of wine for the drink offering, with the burnt offering or for the sacrifice, for each lamb.

**15:6** Or for a ram you shall prepare a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil,

**15:7** and for the drink offering you shall present a third of a hin of wine as a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**15:8** And when you prepare a bull from the herd as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a solemn vow or as peace offerings to the LORD,

**15:9** then one shall present with the bull from the herd a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil;

**15:10** and you shall present half a hin of wine for the drink offering, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**15:11** In this way it shall be done for each ox, or for each ram, or for an animal among the sheep or among the goats.

**15:12** In accordance with the number you prepare, in this way you shall do for each one in accordance with their number.

**15:13** Every native shall do these things in this way in presenting a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**15:14** And if a sojourner resides with you, or anyone who is in your midst throughout your generations, and prepares a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD, just as you do, so he shall do.

**15:15** As for the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the residing sojourner, a perpetual statute throughout your generations; as you are, so shall the sojourner be before the LORD.

**15:16** There shall be one law and one ordinance for you and for the sojourner who resides with you.'"

**15:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:18** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you enter the land into which I am bringing you,

**15:19** then it shall be that when you eat from the bread of the land, you shall lift up a contribution to the LORD.

**15:20** As the first of your dough you shall lift up a loaf as a contribution; like the contribution of a threshing floor, so you shall lift it up.

**15:21** From the first of your dough you shall give a contribution to the LORD throughout your generations.

**15:22** And when you err and do not carry out all these commandments that the LORD has spoken to Moses,

**15:23** all that the LORD has commanded you by the hand of Moses, from the day the LORD gave command and onward throughout your generations,

**15:24** then it shall be that if it is done inadvertently, out of the sight of the congregation, the whole congregation shall prepare one bull from the herd for a burnt offering, for a soothing fragrance to the LORD, with its grain offering and its drink offering according to the ordinance, and one male goat for a sin offering.

**15:25** And the priest shall make atonement for all the congregation of the sons of Israel, and they shall be forgiven, because it was an inadvertent error, and they have brought their offering, a fire offering to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD for their inadvertent error.

**15:26** So all the congregation of the sons of Israel and the sojourner who resides in their midst shall be forgiven, because for all the people it happened inadvertently.

**15:27** And if one person sins inadvertently, then that person shall present a year-old female goat for a sin offering.

**15:28** And the priest shall make atonement for the person who errs, when he sins inadvertently, before the LORD, to make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven.

**15:29** For the native among the sons of Israel and for the sojourner who resides in their midst, there shall be one law for you, for the one who acts inadvertently.

**15:30** But the person who acts with a high hand, whether native or sojourner, that one blasphemes the LORD, and that person shall be cut off from the midst of his people.

**15:31** Because he has despised the word of the LORD and violated His commandment, that person shall certainly be cut off; his guilt is upon him.'"

**15:32** Now the sons of Israel were in the wilderness, and they found a man gathering sticks on the Sabbath day.

**15:33** And those who found him gathering sticks brought him forward to Moses and to Aaron and to the whole congregation.

**15:34** And they placed him in custody, because it had not been specified what should be done to him.

**15:35** Then the LORD said to Moses, "The man shall certainly be put to death; the whole congregation shall stone him with stones outside the camp."

**15:36** So the whole congregation brought him outside the camp and stoned him with stones, and he died, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**15:37** Then the LORD said to Moses,

**15:38** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them that they shall make tassels for themselves on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and they shall put a cord of blue on the tassel of each corner.

**15:39** It shall be a tassel for you, and you shall look at it and remember all the commandments of the LORD and carry them out, and you shall not go roving after your heart and after your eyes, after which you prostitute yourselves,

**15:40** so that you may remember and carry out all My commandments and be holy to your God.

**15:41** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be God to you; I am the LORD your God."

## Translator Notes

- **15:4** — The Hebrew noun issaron literally means a tenth-part, referring to the standard dry measure equal to one-tenth of an ephah (similarly in 15:6 and 15:9).
- **15:20** — The phrase lift up a contribution translates the Hiphil of rum with its cognate noun terumah (also in 15:19), denoting a portion lifted off and set apart from a larger bulk; dough renders the plural noun arisot (also in 15:21), referring to batches of dough or coarse-ground meal.
- **15:30** — The expression with a high hand translates the Hebrew idiom beyad ramah, denoting deliberate, brazen defiance in contrast to the inadvertent errors of 15:24-29.
- **15:35** — The command shall stone translates the Qal infinitive absolute ragom used with imperative force to issue a solemn order, with the whole congregation as its explicit subject.
- **15:39** — The verb go roving translates the Qal imperfect of tur (to explore, scout, roam), echoing the same verb used repeatedly for scouting the land in Numbers 13-14.
