## Text

**13:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Send men for yourself to scout out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the sons of Israel; you shall send one man each for the tribe of his fathers, every one a chieftain among them."

**13:3** Moses sent them out from the wilderness of Paran by the word of the LORD; they were all men, heads of the sons of Israel.

**13:4** These are their names. For the tribe of Reuben: Shammua, son of Zaccur.

**13:5** For the tribe of Simeon: Shaphat, son of Hori.

**13:6** For the tribe of Judah: Caleb, son of Jephunneh.

**13:7** For the tribe of Issachar: Igal, son of Joseph.

**13:8** For the tribe of Ephraim: Hoshea, son of Nun.

**13:9** For the tribe of Benjamin: Palti, son of Raphu.

**13:10** For the tribe of Zebulun: Gaddiel, son of Sodi.

**13:11** For the tribe of Joseph, for the tribe of Manasseh: Gaddi, son of Susi.

**13:12** For the tribe of Dan: Ammiel, son of Gemalli.

**13:13** For the tribe of Asher: Sethur, son of Michael.

**13:14** For the tribe of Naphtali: Nahbi, son of Vophsi.

**13:15** For the tribe of Gad: Geuel, son of Machi.

**13:16** These are the names of the men Moses sent to scout out the land, and Moses named Hoshea, son of Nun, Joshua.

**13:17** Moses sent them to scout out the land of Canaan, and he said to them, "Go up this way into the Negev, and go up into the hill country.

**13:18** See the land, what it is like, and the people dwelling on it: are they strong or weak? Are they few or numerous?

**13:19** And what is the land where they dwell like—is it good or bad? And what are the cities where they dwell like—are they in camps or in fortresses?

**13:20** And what is the land like—is it rich or lean? Are there trees in it or none? Strengthen yourselves and take some of the land's fruit." Now those days were the days of the first-ripening grapes.

**13:21** So they went up and scouted out the land, from the wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, at the entrance to Hamath.

**13:22** They went up into the Negev, and he came as far as Hebron; Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the offspring of Anak, were there. Now Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan of Egypt.

**13:23** Then they came as far as the Valley of Eshcol, and from there they cut off a vine branch and one cluster of grapes; two of them carried it on a pole, along with some of the pomegranates and some of the figs.

**13:24** That place was named the Valley of Eshcol on account of the cluster the sons of Israel had cut off from there.

**13:25** They returned from scouting out the land at the close of forty days.

**13:26** They went and came to Moses, to Aaron, and to the entire assembly of the sons of Israel, to the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh. They brought word back to them and to the entire assembly, and showed them the fruit of the land.

**13:27** They reported to him and said, "We came into the land where you sent us; it truly flows with milk and honey, and here is its fruit.

**13:28** Yet the people dwelling in the land are mighty, and the cities are fortified and exceedingly large; furthermore, we saw the offspring of Anak there.

**13:29** Amalek dwells in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, the Jebusites, and the Amorites dwell in the hill country; and the Canaanites dwell beside the sea and alongside the Jordan."

**13:30** Then Caleb hushed the people before Moses and said, "Let us surely go up and take possession of it, for we can indeed prevail over it."

**13:31** Yet the men who had gone up alongside him said, "We cannot go up against the people, because they are stronger than we are."

**13:32** They put out a slanderous report to the sons of Israel concerning the land they had scouted out, saying, "The land through which we passed to scout it out is a land that consumes its inhabitants, and all the people we saw in its midst are men of great stature.

**13:33** And there we saw the Nephilim—the sons of Anak are from the Nephilim—and in our own eyes we were like grasshoppers, and so we were in their eyes."

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Hebrew verb tur ("scout out") denotes reconnaissance or surveying rather than covert spying. The Hebrew shifts from a singular command addressed to Moses ("Send for yourself") to a plural verb ("you shall send").
- **13:21** — The Hebrew expression lebo chamath can be rendered either directionally as "at the entrance to Hamath" or as the northern border place name Lebo-hamath.
- **13:22** — The Masoretic text shifts from plural "they went up" to singular "he came," often taken distributively or with particular reference to Caleb (cf. Josh 14:12-14), whereas the Septuagint and Samaritan Pentateuch harmonize to the plural.
- **13:30** — "Before Moses" renders el-Mosheh, which can mean "in Moses' presence" or "against/concerning Moses." Both "surely go up" and "can indeed prevail" reflect emphatic Hebrew infinitive absolute constructions.
