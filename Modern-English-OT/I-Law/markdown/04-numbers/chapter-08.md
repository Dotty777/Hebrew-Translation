## Text

**8:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Speak to Aaron and tell him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall shed light toward the front of the lampstand.'"

**8:3** And Aaron did thus: he set up its lamps toward the front of the lampstand, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:4** Now this was the construction of the lampstand: hammered gold; from its base to its blossom, it was hammered work. According to the form that the LORD had shown Moses, so he made the lampstand.

**8:5** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:6** "Take the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel and purify them.

**8:7** And thus you shall do to them to purify them: sprinkle water for purification upon them, and they shall pass a razor over all their flesh, wash their garments, and purify themselves.

**8:8** Then they shall take a young bull of the herd and its grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil; and you shall take a second young bull of the herd for a sin offering.

**8:9** You shall bring the Levites near before the tent of meeting, and you shall assemble the entire congregation of the sons of Israel.

**8:10** Then you shall bring the Levites near before the LORD, and the sons of Israel shall lay their hands upon the Levites.

**8:11** And Aaron shall wave the Levites as a wave offering before the LORD from the sons of Israel, so that they may perform the service of the LORD.

**8:12** And the Levites shall lay their hands upon the heads of the bulls, then you shall offer the one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD, to atone for the Levites.

**8:13** You shall station the Levites before Aaron and before his sons, and wave them as a wave offering to the LORD.

**8:14** Thus you shall set the Levites apart from the midst of the sons of Israel, and the Levites shall belong to Me.

**8:15** Afterward the Levites shall enter to serve the tent of meeting; you shall purify them and wave them as a wave offering.

**8:16** For they are completely given to Me from the midst of the sons of Israel. In place of what opens every womb, the firstborn of all among the sons of Israel, I have taken them for Myself.

**8:17** For every firstborn among the sons of Israel belongs to Me, both human and animal; on the day I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated them to Myself.

**8:18** So I have taken the Levites in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel.

**8:19** And I have given the Levites as gifts to Aaron and to his sons from the midst of the sons of Israel, to perform the service of the sons of Israel in the tent of meeting and to atone for the sons of Israel, so that there will be no plague among the sons of Israel when the sons of Israel approach the sanctuary."

**8:20** And Moses, Aaron, and the entire congregation of the sons of Israel did to the Levites in accordance with everything that the LORD had commanded Moses regarding the Levites; so the sons of Israel did to them.

**8:21** And the Levites cleansed themselves from sin and washed their garments, and Aaron waved them as a wave offering before the LORD, and Aaron atoned for them to purify them.

**8:22** Afterward the Levites entered to perform their service in the tent of meeting before Aaron and before his sons; as the LORD had commanded Moses regarding the Levites, thus they did for them.

**8:23** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:24** "This is what applies to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward, each shall enter to serve a term of duty in the service of the tent of meeting,

**8:25** but from fifty years old he shall withdraw from the duty of the service and shall serve no longer.

**8:26** He may minister to his brothers in the tent of meeting to keep the charge, yet he shall perform no service. In this way you shall do for the Levites regarding their charges."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Hiphil infinitive construct of alah (literally "when you cause the lamps to go up") is the technical expression for setting up and lighting the lamps on the lampstand.
- **8:11** — The cognate phrase "wave as a wave offering" (Hiphil of nuph with tenuphah) denotes a ritual ceremony of presentation or elevation transferring the Levites into the service of the LORD.
- **8:16** — Hebrew emphatically repeats the Qal passive participle netunim ("given, given") to stress complete dedication, and uses the unique noun pitrat ("what opens," found only here) instead of the usual peter.
- **8:21** — "Cleansed themselves from sin" renders the privative Hithpael of chata (ritual decontamination from sin or impurity), distinct from the Piel of taher ("to purify them") at the end of the verse.
- **8:24** — "Serve a term of duty" renders the cognate idiom litzbo tzaba (Qal infinitive construct of tzaba with its noun), a military term extended here to the Levites' scheduled sanctuary duty.
