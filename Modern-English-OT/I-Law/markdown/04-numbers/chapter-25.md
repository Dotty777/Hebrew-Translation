## Text

**25:1** Israel stayed in Shittim, and the people began to engage in sexual immorality with the daughters of Moab.

**25:2** They invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods; then the people ate and prostrated themselves to their gods.

**25:3** Thus Israel bound itself to Baal of Peor, and the LORD's anger burned against Israel.

**25:4** And the LORD said to Moses, "Take all the heads of the people and impale them for the LORD facing the sun, so that the burning anger of the LORD may turn back from Israel."

**25:5** Then Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Each of you, kill his men who have bound themselves to Baal of Peor."

**25:6** And look, a man from the sons of Israel came and brought a Midianite woman near to his brothers before the eyes of Moses and before the eyes of the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, while they were weeping at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**25:7** Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw this, got up from the midst of the congregation, and took a spear in his hand.

**25:8** He went after the man of Israel into the tent chamber and pierced the two of them, the man of Israel and the woman, into her belly. Then the plague was halted from the sons of Israel.

**25:9** And those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.

**25:10** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:11** "Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned back My wrath from the sons of Israel by being zealous with My zeal in their midst, so that I did not put an end to the sons of Israel in My zeal.

**25:12** Therefore say, 'Look, I am giving him My covenant of peace,

**25:13** and it will be for him and for his offspring after him a covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the sons of Israel.'"

**25:14** Now the name of the man of Israel who was struck down, the one who was struck down with the Midianite woman, was Zimri, son of Salu, a leader of a father's house among the Simeonites.

**25:15** And the name of the Midianite woman who was struck down was Cozbi, daughter of Zur; he was the head of the clans of a father's house in Midian.

**25:16** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:17** "Treat the Midianites as enemies and strike them down,

**25:18** for they are treating you as enemies with their schemes by which they schemed against you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, daughter of a leader of Midian, their sister, who was struck down on the day of the plague over the matter of Peor."

## Translator Notes

- **25:4** — The rare Hiphil imperative hoqa' (from yaqa', "be dislocated/wrenched") denotes a public ritual execution, understood as impaling, hanging, or publicly exposing the offenders before the LORD.
- **25:8** — The verse plays on two rare, similar-sounding Hebrew nouns: haqqubbah ("the tent chamber" or vaulted alcove, a hapax legomenon) and qobatah ("her belly/abdomen").
- **25:11** — Hebrew qinna' (Piel) and its cognate noun qin'ah convey ardent covenantal zeal as well as fierce jealousy, repeated across vv. 11 and 13.
- **25:12** — Hebrew beriti shalom places the noun "peace" in apposition to "My covenant" ("My covenant, peace"), the standard Hebrew idiom for expressing "My covenant of peace" when the governing noun already carries a pronominal suffix.
