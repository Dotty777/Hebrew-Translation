## Text

**36:1** Then the heads of the fathers' houses belonging to the clan of the sons of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from among the clans of the sons of Joseph, approached and spoke before Moses and before the chieftains, the heads of the fathers' houses of the sons of Israel.

**36:2** They said, "The LORD commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the sons of Israel, and my lord was commanded by the LORD to give our brother Zelophehad's inheritance to his daughters.

**36:3** Now if they become wives to any of the sons of the tribes of the sons of Israel, then their inheritance will be deducted from the inheritance of our fathers and will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they will belong; and it will be deducted from the lot of our inheritance.

**36:4** And when the jubilee comes for the sons of Israel, their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they will belong, and their inheritance will be deducted from the inheritance of our fathers' tribe."

**36:5** Then Moses commanded the sons of Israel at the word of the LORD, saying, "The tribe of the sons of Joseph is speaking rightly.

**36:6** This is the matter that the LORD has commanded concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, 'Let them become wives to whoever is good in their eyes; only they must become wives within the clan of their father's tribe.

**36:7** And no inheritance belonging to the sons of Israel shall pass from tribe to tribe, for each of the sons of Israel shall cling to the inheritance of his fathers' tribe.

**36:8** And every daughter who possesses an inheritance from the tribes of the sons of Israel shall become a wife to one from the clan of her father's tribe, so that each of the sons of Israel may possess the inheritance of his fathers.

**36:9** And no inheritance shall pass from one tribe to another tribe, for each of the tribes of the sons of Israel shall cling to its inheritance.'"

**36:10** As the LORD commanded Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did.

**36:11** Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, became wives to the sons of their uncles.

**36:12** They became wives within the clans of the sons of Manasseh, son of Joseph, and their inheritance stayed with the tribe of their father's clan.

**36:13** These are the commandments and the judgments that the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses to the sons of Israel on the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan at Jericho.

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — Hebrew pairs the active Piel in the first clause ("the LORD commanded my lord") with the passive Pual in the second ("my lord was commanded by the LORD"), using the preposition be- ("by") to mark the divine agent of the passive verb.
- **36:3** — The opening conditional ("Now if they become...") renders a paratactic Hebrew weqatal sequence. After the feminine singular Niphal verb agreeing with "their inheritance," the verse shifts to masculine singular Niphal forms ("will be added" and "will be deducted") used impersonally of the transferred portion.
- **36:6** — The pronouns in "their eyes" and "their father" use masculine plural suffixes in Hebrew even though they refer to Zelophehad's daughters, in contrast to the feminine plural suffixes used in verses 3, 4, 11, and 12.
- **36:12** — Hebrew reverses the construct order found in verses 6 and 8 ("the clan of their/her father's tribe"), reading literally "the tribe of their father's clan."
- **36:13** — Hebrew literally reads "beside the Jordan of Jericho," a construct phrase used repeatedly in Numbers to designate the stretch of the Jordan River adjacent to Jericho.
