## Text

**31:1** Then he heard the words of the sons of Laban, saying, "Jacob has taken everything that belonged to our father, and out of what belonged to our father he has made all this wealth."

**31:2** Jacob saw Laban's face, and look, it was not toward him as yesterday and the day before.

**31:3** And the LORD said to Jacob, "Return to the land of your fathers and to your birthplace, and I will be with you."

**31:4** So Jacob sent and summoned Rachel and Leah to the field, to his flock,

**31:5** and said to them, "I see your father's face, that it is not toward me as yesterday and the day before, yet the God of my father has been with me.

**31:6** And you yourselves know that with all my strength I have served your father.

**31:7** Yet your father has cheated me and changed my wages ten times, but God did not allow him to do harm to me.

**31:8** If he spoke like this, 'The speckled will be your wages,' then all the flock bore speckled ones; and if he spoke like this, 'The striped will be your wages,' then all the flock bore striped ones.

**31:9** So God has taken away the livestock of your father and given it to me.

**31:10** And it happened at the time the flock was mating that I lifted up my eyes and saw in a dream, and look, the male goats mounting the flock were striped, speckled, and dappled.

**31:11** Then the angel of God said to me in the dream, 'Jacob,' and I said, 'Here I am.'

**31:12** And He said, 'Lift up your eyes now and see: all the male goats mounting the flock are striped, speckled, and dappled, for I have seen everything that Laban is doing to you.

**31:13** I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed a pillar, where you vowed a vow to Me. Now rise, go out from this land, and return to the land of your birth.'"

**31:14** Then Rachel and Leah replied and said to him, "Do we still have a portion and inheritance in the house of our father?

**31:15** Are we not counted as foreigners by him? For he has sold us, and has also utterly consumed our money.

**31:16** Indeed, all the riches that God has taken away from our father belong to us and to our children. Now then, do everything that God has said to you."

**31:17** Then Jacob rose up and lifted his children and his wives onto the camels.

**31:18** And he drove away all his livestock and all his property that he had amassed, the livestock of his own acquiring that he had amassed in Paddan-aram, to go to Isaac his father, to the land of Canaan.

**31:19** Now Laban had gone to shear his flock, and Rachel stole the household idols that belonged to her father.

**31:20** So Jacob deceived Laban the Aramean, in that he did not tell him that he was fleeing.

**31:21** So he fled, he and all that belonged to him; he arose and crossed over the River, and set his face toward the hill country of Gilead.

**31:22** And it was reported to Laban on the third day that Jacob had fled.

**31:23** So he took his kinsmen with him and pursued after him a journey of seven days, and he caught up with him in the hill country of Gilead.

**31:24** But God came to Laban the Aramean in a dream by night and said to him, "Guard yourself so that you do not speak with Jacob either good or bad."

**31:25** So Laban overtook Jacob. Now Jacob had pitched his tent in the hill country, and Laban with his kinsmen pitched camp in the hill country of Gilead.

**31:26** Then Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done, that you deceived me and drove off my daughters like captives of the sword?

**31:27** Why did you flee in secret and deceive me, and did not tell me, so that I could have sent you off with gladness and with songs, with tambourine and with lyre?

**31:28** And you did not permit me to kiss my sons and my daughters. Now you have acted foolishly in doing so.

**31:29** It is within the power of my hand to do harm to you, but the God of your father said to me last night, 'Guard yourself from speaking with Jacob either good or bad.'

**31:30** And now, you have indeed departed because you yearned deeply for the house of your father—why did you steal my gods?"

**31:31** Then Jacob answered and said to Laban, "Because I was afraid, for I thought you might forcibly take your daughters from me.

**31:32** The person with whom you find your gods shall not live. Before our kinsmen, identify for yourself whatever is with me and take it for yourself." Now Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen them.

**31:33** So Laban went into Jacob's tent and into Leah's tent and into the tent of the two female servants, but he did not find them. Then he went out from Leah's tent and went into Rachel's tent.

**31:34** Now Rachel had taken the household idols, placed them in the camel's saddle, and sat on them. And Laban felt through the whole tent, but did not find them.

**31:35** And she said to her father, "May my lord not be angry that I am unable to stand up before you, for the way of women is upon me." So he searched, yet he did not find the household idols.

**31:36** Then Jacob grew angry and contended with Laban; and Jacob answered and said to Laban, "What is my transgression? What is my sin, that you have hotly pursued after me?

**31:37** Now that you have felt through all my belongings, what have you found from all the belongings of your house? Place it here before my kinsmen and your kinsmen, so that they may judge between the two of us.

**31:38** These twenty years I have been with you: your ewes and your female goats have not miscarried, and the rams of your flock I have not eaten.

**31:39** What was torn by wild animals I did not bring to you; I myself bore the loss of it. From my hand you demanded it, whether stolen by day or stolen by night.

**31:40** So I was: by day scorching heat consumed me, and frost by night, and my sleep fled from my eyes.

**31:41** These twenty years I have spent in your house—I served you fourteen years for your two daughters, and six years for your flock, yet you changed my wages ten times.

**31:42** Had the God of my father, the God of Abraham and the Fear of Isaac, not been for me, indeed by now you would have sent me off empty-handed. My affliction and the toil of my hands God saw, and He passed judgment last night."

**31:43** Then Laban answered and said to Jacob, "The daughters are my daughters, and the children are my children, and the flock is my flock, and everything that you see belongs to me. Yet what can I do today to these daughters of mine, or to their children whom they have borne?

**31:44** Now then, come, let us make a covenant, I and you, and let it serve as a witness between me and you."

**31:45** Then Jacob took a stone and raised it up as a pillar.

**31:46** And Jacob said to his kinsmen, "Gather stones." So they took stones and made a heap, and they ate there beside the heap.

**31:47** And Laban named it Jegar-sahadutha, while Jacob named it Galeed.

**31:48** Then Laban said, "This heap is a witness between me and you today." Therefore its name was called Galeed. And Laban said to Jacob, "Look at this heap, and look at the pillar that I have set up between me and you."

**31:49** And Mizpah, for he said, "May the LORD keep watch between me and you when we are hidden from one another.

**31:50** If you afflict my daughters, or if you take wives besides my daughters—no man is with us—see, God is a witness between me and you.

**31:52** This heap is a witness, and the pillar is a witness, that I will not pass beyond this heap to you, and that you will not pass beyond this heap and this pillar to me, for harm.

**31:53** May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor judge between us, the God of their father." And Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.

**31:54** Then Jacob offered a sacrifice in the hill country and invited his kinsmen to eat bread; so they ate bread and lodged in the hill country.

## Translator Notes

- **31:13** — Hebrew ha-el Bet-el is an unusual construct construction in which the first noun retains the definite article before a proper name, conveying "the God of Bethel."
- **31:20** — Literally "stole the heart (mind) of Laban," a Hebrew idiom for deceiving someone without their knowledge, playing on the verb "steal" (ganav) used in v. 19 and repeated in vv. 26, 27, 30, 32, and 39.
- **31:39** — The Piel verb achattennah (from chata, normally "sin") carries the restitutive sense "bear the loss of it." The two forms genuvti ("stolen") preserve the archaic construct ending -i (hireq compaginis) before "day" and "night."
- **31:47** — Laban gives the stone heap an Aramaic name (Jegar-sahadutha) and Jacob gives it the equivalent Hebrew name (Galeed); both mean "heap of witness."
- **31:48** — In the Saints Hebrew Critical Text, the clause numbered 31:51 in standard Masoretic editions ("And Laban said to Jacob...") is placed at the end of 31:48 (with no separate verse 51); the first-person verb yariti ("I have set up") is translated verbatim as Laban claims credit for establishing the boundary marker.
