## Text

**11:1** Now all the earth had one language and one set of words.

**11:2** And it happened, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a valley plain in the land of Shinar and settled there.

**11:3** They said, each to his neighbor, "Come, let us make bricks and fire them thoroughly." So brick served them for stone, and bitumen served them for mortar.

**11:4** And they said, "Come, let us build a city for ourselves and a tower whose top is in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, or else we will be scattered across the face of all the earth."

**11:5** Then the LORD went down to see the city and the tower that the sons of mankind had built.

**11:6** And the LORD said, "Look, they are one people, and all of them have one language; this is what they have started to do, and now nothing that they devise to do will be withheld from them.

**11:7** Come, let Us go down and confuse their language there, so that each will not understand his neighbor's language."

**11:8** So the LORD scattered them from there across the face of all the earth, and they ceased building the city.

**11:9** For this reason its name was called Babel, for there the LORD confused the language of all the earth, and from there the LORD scattered them across the face of all the earth.

**11:10** These are the generations of Shem: Shem was one hundred years old, and he fathered Arpachshad two years after the flood.

**11:11** And Shem lived five hundred years after fathering Arpachshad, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:12** Now Arpachshad lived thirty-five years, and he fathered Shelah.

**11:13** And Arpachshad lived four hundred three years after fathering Shelah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:14** Now Shelah lived thirty years, and he fathered Eber.

**11:15** And Shelah lived four hundred three years after fathering Eber, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:16** And Eber lived thirty-four years, and he fathered Peleg.

**11:17** And Eber lived four hundred thirty years after fathering Peleg, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:18** And Peleg lived thirty years, and he fathered Reu.

**11:19** And Peleg lived two hundred nine years after fathering Reu, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:20** And Reu lived thirty-two years, and he fathered Serug.

**11:21** And Reu lived two hundred seven years after fathering Serug, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:22** And Serug lived thirty years, and he fathered Nahor.

**11:23** And Serug lived two hundred years after fathering Nahor, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:24** And Nahor lived twenty-nine years, and he fathered Terah.

**11:25** And Nahor lived one hundred nineteen years after fathering Terah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**11:26** And Terah lived seventy years, and he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

**11:27** And these are the generations of Terah: Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran, while Haran fathered Lot.

**11:28** And Haran died during the lifetime of Terah his father, in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans.

**11:29** And Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves; Abram's wife was named Sarai, and Nahor's wife was named Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah and the father of Iscah.

**11:30** And Sarai was barren, having no child.

**11:31** And Terah took his son Abram, and Lot son of Haran, his grandson, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, the wife of his son Abram, and they went out together from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan; but they came as far as Haran and settled there.

**11:32** And the days of Terah amounted to two hundred five years, and Terah died in Haran.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Hebrew literally reads "one lip" (saphah, idiomatically "language") and "single/unified words" (devarim achadim, plural of echad indicating a uniform set of words).
- **11:7** — The verb "confuse" is a rare Qal cohortative form (navelah, from balal, "to mix/confuse") that prepares the wordplay on "Babel" in v. 9; "understand" translates shama', literally "hear."
- **11:28** — "During the lifetime of Terah his father" renders the Hebrew idiom 'al-peney ("upon the face of"), meaning while his father was still alive (cf. Num 3:4).
- **11:31** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads literally "and they went out with them" (wayyetse'u ittam), rendered idiomatically as "they went out together."
