## Text

**38:1** Now it happened at that time that Judah went down away from his brothers and turned aside to an Adullamite man whose name was Hirah.

**38:2** There Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite man whose name was Shua; he took her and went in to her.

**38:3** She conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Er.

**38:4** Then she conceived again and bore a son, and she called his name Onan.

**38:5** Once more she bore a son, and she called his name Shelah; and he was in Chezib when she bore him.

**38:6** Now Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.

**38:7** But Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the eyes of the LORD, so the LORD put him to death.

**38:8** Then Judah said to Onan, "Go in to your brother's wife, fulfill the duty of a brother-in-law to her, and raise up offspring for your brother."

**38:9** But Onan knew that the offspring would not belong to him; so whenever he went in to his brother's wife, he would waste it on the ground, so as not to give offspring to his brother.

**38:10** What he did was evil in the eyes of the LORD, so He put him to death as well.

**38:11** Then Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Live as a widow in your father's house until Shelah my son grows up"—for he thought, "He might die too, like his brothers." So Tamar went and lived in her father's house.

**38:12** After many days had passed, Shua's daughter, Judah's wife, died. When Judah was comforted, he went up to the shearers of his flock at Timnah, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite.

**38:13** It was reported to Tamar, saying, "Look, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his flock."

**38:14** So she removed her widow's garments, covered herself with a veil, wrapped herself up, and sat at the entrance of Enaim, which is on the road to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah had grown up, yet she had not been given to him as a wife.

**38:15** When Judah saw her, he thought her to be a prostitute, because she had covered her face.

**38:16** He turned aside to her by the road and said, "Come, please, let me come in to you"—for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, "What will you give me so that you may come in to me?"

**38:17** He said, "I myself will send a young goat from the flock." And she said, "If you will give a pledge until you send it."

**38:18** He said, "What is the pledge that I should give you?" And she said, "Your signet, your cord, and your staff that is in your hand." So he gave them to her and went in to her, and she conceived by him.

**38:19** Then she rose and went away, removed her veil, and put on her widow's garments.

**38:20** Judah sent the young goat by the hand of his friend the Adullamite to retrieve the pledge from the woman's hand, yet he did not find her.

**38:21** He asked the men of her place, saying, "Where is the shrine prostitute who was at Enaim by the road?" And they said, "There has been no shrine prostitute here."

**38:22** So he returned to Judah and said, "I did not find her; and what is more, the men of the place said, 'There has been no shrine prostitute here.'"

**38:23** Judah said, "Let her keep them for herself, or we will become an object of contempt. Look, I sent this young goat, and you did not find her."

**38:24** Now about three months later, it was reported to Judah, saying, "Tamar your daughter-in-law has acted as a prostitute, and look, she is also pregnant by prostitution!" And Judah said, "Bring her out and let her be burned!"

**38:25** As she was being brought out, she sent word to her father-in-law, saying, "By the man to whom these belong, I am pregnant." And she said, "Please identify whose signet, cords, and staff these are."

**38:26** Judah recognized them and said, "She is more righteous than I am, because I did not give her to Shelah my son." And he never again knew her.

**38:27** Now when the time came for her to give birth, look, there were twins in her womb.

**38:28** And while she was giving birth, one put out a hand; and the midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it on his hand, saying, "This one came out first."

**38:29** Yet just as he drew back his hand, look, his brother came out! And she said, "What a breach you have broken through for yourself!" So his name was called Perez.

**38:30** Afterward his brother came out, on whose hand was the scarlet thread, and his name was called Zerah.

## Translator Notes

- **38:8** — "Fulfill the duty of a brother-in-law" translates the Piel denominative imperative yabbem (from yabam, brother-in-law), referring to the levirate marriage custom of raising an heir for a deceased childless brother (cf. Deut 25:5-10).
- **38:9** — Literally "he would ruin/waste toward the ground" (Piel weqatal shichet with directional artsah), leaving the object ("his seed") elliptical.
- **38:21** — Hirah uses qedeshah ("shrine prostitute," literally a consecrated woman) rather than zonah ("prostitute," v. 15); the place name Enaim (also v. 14) literally means "two springs."
- **38:25** — Tamar's imperative "Please identify" (hakker-na, Hiphil of nkr) and Judah's response "recognized" (wayyakker, v. 26) deliberately echo the exact Hebrew wording used in 37:32-33 regarding Joseph's tunic.
- **38:29** — The midwife's exclamation plays on the root prts ("break through" / "breach") to explain the name Perez, and alekha ("for yourself") can also mean "upon you." The verb wayyiqra ("his name was called," also v. 30) is an impersonal third-person masculine singular.
