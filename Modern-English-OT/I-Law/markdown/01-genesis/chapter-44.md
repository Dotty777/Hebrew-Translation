## Text

**44:1** Then he commanded the one who was over his house, saying, "Fill the men's sacks with food, as much as they are able to carry, and place each man's silver in the mouth of his sack.

**44:2** And my cup, the silver cup, you shall place in the mouth of the youngest one's sack, together with the silver for his grain." And he did according to the word of Joseph that he had spoken.

**44:3** The morning grew light, and the men were sent off, they and their donkeys.

**44:4** They had gone out of the city and had not gone far when Joseph said to the one who was over his house, "Get up, pursue after the men, and when you overtake them, say to them, 'Why have you repaid evil in return for good?

**44:5** Is this not the one from which my lord drinks, and with which he indeed practices divination? You have done evil in what you have done.'"

**44:6** So he overtook them and spoke these words to them.

**44:7** And they said to him, "Why does my lord speak words like these? Far be it from your servants to do a thing like this!

**44:8** Look, the silver that we found in the mouths of our sacks we brought back to you from the land of Canaan. How then could we steal silver or gold from your lord's house?

**44:9** Whichever of your servants it is found with shall die, and we too will become slaves to my lord."

**44:10** And he said, "Even now, according to your words, so be it: the one with whom it is found shall become my slave, and you shall be innocent."

**44:11** Then they hurried, and each man lowered his sack to the ground, and each man opened his sack.

**44:12** And he searched; he began with the oldest and finished with the youngest, and the cup was found in Benjamin's sack.

**44:13** Then they tore their garments, and each man loaded his donkey, and they returned to the city.

**44:14** So Judah and his brothers came to Joseph's house while he was still there, and they fell before him to the ground.

**44:15** And Joseph said to them, "What is this deed that you have done? Did you not know that a man like me can indeed practice divination?"

**44:16** Then Judah said, "What shall we say to my lord? What shall we speak, and how can we justify ourselves? God has found the iniquity of your servants. Here we are, slaves to my lord, both we and the one in whose hand the cup was found."

**44:17** But he said, "Far be it from me to do this! The man in whose hand the cup was found, he shall become my slave; as for you, go up in peace to your father."

**44:18** Then Judah drew near to him and said, "Please, my lord, let your servant speak a word in the ears of my lord, and do not let your anger burn against your servant, for you are just like Pharaoh.

**44:19** My lord asked his servants, saying, 'Do you have a father or a brother?'

**44:20** And we said to my lord, 'We have an aged father and a young child of his old age; his brother is dead, so he alone is left of his mother, and his father loves him.'

**44:21** Then you said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me so that I may set my eye on him.'

**44:22** And we said to my lord, 'The boy cannot leave his father, for if he leaves his father, he will die.'

**44:23** But you said to your servants, 'If your youngest brother does not come down with you, you will not see my face again.'

**44:24** So it happened, when we went up to your servant my father, that we told him the words of my lord.

**44:25** And our father said, 'Go back, buy a little food for us.'

**44:26** But we said, 'We cannot go down. If our youngest brother is with us, then we will go down; for we cannot see the man's face while our youngest brother is not with us.'

**44:27** Then your servant my father said to us, 'You yourselves know that my wife bore two sons to me;

**44:28** and the one went out from me, and I said, "Surely he has been torn to pieces!" and I have not seen him until now.

**44:29** If you take this one also from my presence and harm befalls him, then you will bring my gray hair down in misery to Sheol.'

**44:30** So now, when I come to your servant my father and the boy is not with us, since his soul is bound to the boy's soul,

**44:31** it will happen, when he sees that the boy is not there, that he will die; and your servants will bring the gray hair of your servant our father down in grief to Sheol.

**44:32** For your servant pledged himself for the boy to my father, saying, 'If I do not bring him to you, then I shall be guilty before my father for all time.'

**44:33** Now therefore, please let your servant remain in place of the boy as a slave to my lord, and let the boy go up with his brothers.

**44:34** For how can I go up to my father while the boy is not with me? Let me not look upon the calamity that would find my father!"

## Translator Notes

- **44:5** — The Masoretic Hebrew begins directly with "Is this not the one...", whereas the Septuagint adds "Why have you stolen my silver cup?" before it. The phrase "indeed practices divination" renders the Piel infinitive absolute plus Piel imperfect of the verb for practicing divination, repeated in 44:15.
- **44:16** — The verb "justify ourselves" is the Hithpael imperfect of the root for righteousness, showing consonant metathesis and emphatic assimilation with the prefix. Judah also plays on the Hebrew noun for servant and slave, using the same word both for courtly self-reference ("your servants") and for actual bondage ("slaves to my lord"), as again in 44:33.
- **44:28** — The emphatic phrase "has been torn to pieces" pairs a Qal infinitive absolute with a Pual perfect passive of the verb for tearing prey, intensifying the certainty of the passive action.
- **44:34** — The closing clause uses the conjunction of apprehension independently to express urgent dread ("Let me not look upon..."), and "find" echoes the key verb for finding repeated throughout the chapter.
