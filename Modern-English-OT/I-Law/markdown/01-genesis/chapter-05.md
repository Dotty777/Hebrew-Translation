## Text

**5:1** This is the book of Adam's generations. On the day God created man, in the likeness of God He made him.

**5:2** Male and female He created them, and He blessed them and called their name Man on the day they were created.

**5:3** And Adam lived one hundred thirty years, and he fathered a son in his likeness, according to his image, and he called his name Seth.

**5:4** And the days of Adam after fathering Seth were eight hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:5** And all of Adam's days that he lived were nine hundred thirty years, and he died.

**5:6** And Seth lived one hundred five years, and he fathered Enosh.

**5:7** And Seth lived eight hundred seven years after fathering Enosh, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:8** And all of Seth's days were nine hundred twelve years, and he died.

**5:9** And Enosh lived ninety years, and he fathered Kenan.

**5:10** And Enosh lived eight hundred fifteen years after fathering Kenan, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:11** And all of Enosh's days were nine hundred five years, and he died.

**5:12** And Kenan lived seventy years, and he fathered Mahalalel.

**5:13** And Kenan lived eight hundred forty years after fathering Mahalalel, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:14** And all of Kenan's days were nine hundred ten years, and he died.

**5:15** And Mahalalel lived sixty-five years, and he fathered Jared.

**5:16** And Mahalalel lived eight hundred thirty years after fathering Jared, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:17** And all of Mahalalel's days were eight hundred ninety-five years, and he died.

**5:18** And Jared lived one hundred sixty-two years, and he fathered Enoch.

**5:19** And Jared lived eight hundred years after fathering Enoch, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:20** And all of Jared's days were nine hundred sixty-two years, and he died.

**5:21** And Enoch lived sixty-five years, and he fathered Methuselah.

**5:22** And Enoch walked with God three hundred years after fathering Methuselah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:23** And all of Enoch's days were three hundred sixty-five years.

**5:24** And Enoch walked with God, and he was gone, for God had taken him.

**5:25** And Methuselah lived one hundred eighty-seven years, and he fathered Lamech.

**5:26** And Methuselah lived seven hundred eighty-two years after fathering Lamech, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:27** And all of Methuselah's days were nine hundred sixty-nine years, and he died.

**5:28** And Lamech lived one hundred eighty-two years, and he fathered a son.

**5:29** And he called his name Noah, saying, "This one will comfort us from our work and from the painful labor of our hands, from the ground that the LORD has cursed."

**5:30** And Lamech lived five hundred ninety-five years after fathering Noah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:31** And all of Lamech's days were seven hundred seventy-seven years, and he died.

**5:32** And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Hebrew adam serves both as the proper name "Adam" and as the generic noun "man/humankind," creating a wordplay across 5:1-3 where God names male and female together "Man" (adam).
- **5:3** — Literally "and he fathered in his likeness, according to his image"; "a son" is implied by the naming clause that follows. The prepositions be- ("in") and ke- ("according to") appear here in the reverse order of Genesis 1:26.
- **5:22** — In place of the chapter's repeated formula wayehi ("and he lived"), the narrator uses the Hithpael verb wayyithallek ("walked" continually or intimately) for Enoch's life with God here and in 5:24.
- **5:29** — Hebrew yenachamenu is the Piel imperfect of nacham ("comfort"), forming a sound play with the name Noah (noach, linked to nuach, "rest"); the Septuagint instead renders "will give us rest."
