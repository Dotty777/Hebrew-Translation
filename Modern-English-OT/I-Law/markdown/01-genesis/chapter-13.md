## Text

**13:1** Then Abram went up out of Egypt, he and his wife and all that belonged to him, and Lot with him, into the Negev.

**13:2** Now Abram was very wealthy in livestock, in silver, and in gold.

**13:3** And he went by stages from the Negev as far as Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the start, between Bethel and Ai,

**13:4** to the place of the altar that he had made there previously, and Abram called on the name of the LORD there.

**13:5** Lot as well, who was going along with Abram, had flocks and herds and tents.

**13:6** And the land could not support them living together, because their possessions were great, and they were unable to live together.

**13:7** Then a dispute arose between the herders of Abram's livestock and the herders of Lot's livestock; now the Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land at that time.

**13:8** So Abram said to Lot, "Please let there be no dispute between me and you, and between my herders and your herders, because we are brothers.

**13:9** Is not all the land before you? Separate yourself from me, please. If you take the left, I will go to the right; and if you take the right, I will go to the left."

**13:10** So Lot lifted up his eyes and saw the entire plain of the Jordan, that all of it was well watered—before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah—like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, as one goes toward Zoar.

**13:11** Then Lot chose for himself the entire plain of the Jordan, and Lot journeyed eastward; and they separated, each from his brother.

**13:12** Abram lived in the land of Canaan, while Lot lived in the cities of the plain and moved his tent as far as Sodom.

**13:13** Now the men of Sodom were very wicked and sinful against the LORD.

**13:14** And after Lot had separated from him, the LORD said to Abram, "Lift up your eyes, please, and look from the place where you are—to the north and to the south, to the east and to the west;

**13:15** for all the land that you see, to you I will give it, and to your seed forever.

**13:16** And I will make your seed like the dust of the earth, so that if a man is able to count the dust of the earth, your seed too could be counted.

**13:17** Rise, walk about in the land, along its length and its breadth, for to you I will give it."

**13:18** Then Abram moved his tent, and he came and settled among the oaks of Mamre, which are in Hebron; and he built an altar to the LORD there.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The adjective "wealthy" (kaved) literally means "heavy," creating a verbal echo with the "severe" (kaved) famine in 12:10.
- **13:10** — "As one goes toward Zoar" translates the Hebrew geographic idiom bo'akah ("your coming [to]"), a Qal infinitive construct of bo' with a second-person masculine singular suffix used impersonally.
- **13:11** — Hebrew miqqedem literally means "from the east" or "on the east" (cf. 11:2), and here idiomatically denotes traveling eastward from the hills of Bethel toward the Jordan plain.
- **13:17** — "Walk about" translates the Hithpael imperative of halak (hithhallek), conveying iterative roaming throughout the land as a formal gesture of taking possession.
