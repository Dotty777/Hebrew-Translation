## Text

**17:1** Abram was ninety-nine years old when the LORD appeared to Abram and said to him, "I am God Almighty. Walk before Me and be blameless,

**17:2** and I will set My covenant between Me and you, and I will multiply you very greatly."

**17:3** Then Abram fell upon his face, and God spoke with him, saying,

**17:4** "As for Me, look, My covenant is with you, and you shall become the father of a multitude of nations.

**17:5** Your name shall no longer be called Abram; rather, your name shall be Abraham, for I have appointed you father of a multitude of nations.

**17:6** And I will make you very fruitful indeed; I will make you into nations, and kings will come out from you.

**17:7** And I will establish My covenant between Me and you, and your seed after you through their generations, as a perpetual covenant, to be God for you and for your seed after you.

**17:8** And I will give to you and to your seed after you the land of your sojournings, all the land of Canaan, as a perpetual possession, and I will be God for them."

**17:9** Then God said to Abraham, "And as for you, you must keep My covenant—you and your seed after you through their generations.

**17:10** This is My covenant that you must keep between Me and you and your seed after you: every male among you is to be circumcised.

**17:11** And you shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, and it shall serve as a sign of the covenant between Me and you.

**17:12** At eight days old, every male among you shall be circumcised through your generations—whether born in the house or purchased with silver from any foreigner who is not from your seed.

**17:13** Both the one born in your house and the one purchased with your silver must certainly be circumcised, and My covenant in your flesh shall be a perpetual covenant.

**17:14** And as for any uncircumcised male who has not been circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin, that person shall be cut off from his people; he has violated My covenant."

**17:15** God also said to Abraham, "Regarding Sarai your wife, you are not to call her name Sarai; instead, her name shall be Sarah.

**17:16** I will bless her, and I will also give you a son by her; yes, I will bless her so that she becomes nations, and kings of peoples will come from her."

**17:17** Then Abraham fell upon his face and laughed, and he said in his heart, "To a man a hundred years old, will a child be born? And Sarah, at ninety years old—will she give birth?"

**17:18** And Abraham said to God, "If only Ishmael might live in Your presence!"

**17:19** But God said, "No; Sarah your wife is going to bear you a son, and you shall call his name Isaac. And I will establish My covenant with him as a perpetual covenant for his seed after him.

**17:20** As for Ishmael, I have heard you: see, I have blessed him, and I will make him fruitful and multiply him very greatly. Twelve princes he will father, and I will make him into a great nation.

**17:21** Yet My covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this set time in the next year."

**17:22** And when He had finished speaking with him, God ascended from Abraham.

**17:23** Then Abraham took his son Ishmael, along with all those born in his house and all those purchased with his silver—every male among the men of Abraham's household—and he circumcised the flesh of their foreskin on that very day, just as God had spoken with him.

**17:24** Abraham was ninety-nine years old at the time he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

**17:25** And his son Ishmael was thirteen years old at the time he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

**17:26** On that very day Abraham was circumcised, together with his son Ishmael.

**17:27** And all the men of his household, both those born in the house and those purchased with silver from a foreigner, were circumcised with him.

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — "God Almighty" translates Hebrew El Shaddai, whose etymology is uncertain (possibly "God of the mountain" or "God of sufficiency"). The verb "walk" renders the Hithpael imperative of halakh, which can express habitual conduct.
- **17:11** — The form unemaltem ("you shall circumcise") is analyzed either as a Qal perfect from a by-form namal taking a direct object ("the flesh of your foreskin") or as an unusual Niphal of mul (compare the Niphal with the direct-object marker in 17:14 and 17:25).
- **17:14** — The Masoretic Text has no "on the eighth day" here (found in the Samaritan Pentateuch and Septuagint). The feminine demonstrative pronoun is written h-w-a by Pentateuchal convention and read hi ("that").
- **17:16** — The Masoretic Text consistently uses feminine singular forms ("I will bless her... she will become... from her"), whereas the Septuagint, Syriac, and Vulgate shift to masculine pronouns referring to the promised son.
