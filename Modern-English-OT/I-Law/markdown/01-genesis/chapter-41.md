## Text

**41:1** It came about at the close of two full years that Pharaoh was dreaming, and look, he was standing beside the Nile.

**41:2** And look, coming up from the Nile were seven cows, fine in appearance and plump in flesh, and they grazed in the marsh reeds.

**41:3** And look, seven other cows were coming up after them from the Nile, ugly in appearance and thin in flesh, and they stood beside the cows along the bank of the Nile.

**41:4** Then the cows that were ugly in appearance and thin in flesh ate the seven cows that were fine in appearance and plump. And Pharaoh woke up.

**41:5** He fell asleep and dreamed a second time: look, seven heads of grain were coming up on a single stalk, plump and good.

**41:6** And look, seven heads of grain, thin and seared by the east wind, were sprouting after them.

**41:7** And the thin heads of grain swallowed up the seven plump and full heads of grain. Then Pharaoh woke up, and look, it was a dream.

**41:8** In the morning his spirit was disturbed, so he sent and summoned all the magicians of Egypt and all its wise men. Pharaoh recounted his dream to them, yet there was no one to interpret them for Pharaoh.

**41:9** Then the chief cupbearer spoke to Pharaoh, saying, "I mention my offenses today.

**41:10** Pharaoh grew angry with his servants and placed me in custody in the house of the chief of the guard, me and the chief baker.

**41:11** We dreamed a dream on one night, both he and I; each man dreamed according to the interpretation of his dream.

**41:12** Now a Hebrew youth was there with us, a servant of the chief of the guard. We told him, and he interpreted our dreams for us; to each man he interpreted according to his dream.

**41:13** And just as he interpreted for us, so it came about: me he restored to my post, and him he hanged."

**41:14** Then Pharaoh sent and summoned Joseph, and they rushed him out of the dungeon. He shaved and changed his garments, and came to Pharaoh.

**41:15** Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have dreamed a dream, and there is no one to interpret it. Yet I have heard it said concerning you, 'When you hear a dream, you are able to interpret it.'"

**41:16** Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, "It is not from me; God will answer for the welfare of Pharaoh."

**41:17** Then Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, "In my dream, there I was, standing along the bank of the Nile.

**41:18** And look, coming up from the Nile were seven cows, plump in flesh and fine in form, and they grazed in the marsh reeds.

**41:19** And look, seven other cows were coming up after them, scrawny and very ugly in form and gaunt in flesh; I have never seen any like them in all the land of Egypt for ugliness.

**41:20** And the gaunt and ugly cows ate the first seven plump cows.

**41:21** Yet when they had gone inside them, it could not be known that they had gone inside them, and their appearance was ugly as at the beginning. Then I woke up.

**41:22** Then I saw in my dream, and look, seven heads of grain were coming up on a single stalk, full and good.

**41:23** And look, seven heads of grain, shriveled, thin, seared by the east wind, were sprouting after them.

**41:24** And the thin heads of grain swallowed up the seven good heads of grain. Then I told the magicians, yet there was no one to declare it to me."

**41:25** Joseph said to Pharaoh, "The dream of Pharaoh is one; what God is about to do, He has declared to Pharaoh.

**41:26** The seven good cows are seven years, and the seven good heads of grain are seven years; it is one dream.

**41:27** And the seven gaunt and ugly cows coming up after them are seven years, and the seven empty heads of grain, seared by the east wind, will be seven years of famine.

**41:28** This is the matter that I spoke to Pharaoh: what God is about to do, He has shown to Pharaoh.

**41:29** Look, seven years are coming—great abundance in all the land of Egypt.

**41:30** Then seven years of famine will arise after them, and all the abundance will be forgotten in the land of Egypt, and the famine will consume the land.

**41:31** And the abundance will not be known in the land because of that famine afterward, for it will be exceedingly severe.

**41:32** And as for the dream being repeated to Pharaoh twice, it is because the matter is established by God, and God is hastening to do it.

**41:33** Now then, let Pharaoh look out for a man who is discerning and wise, and place him over the land of Egypt.

**41:34** Let Pharaoh act and appoint overseers over the land, and let him levy a fifth on the land of Egypt during the seven years of abundance.

**41:35** Let them gather all the food of these coming good years, and heap up grain under the hand of Pharaoh as food in the cities, and keep it.

**41:36** The food will be a reserve for the land for the seven years of famine that will take place in the land of Egypt, so that the land will not be cut off by the famine."

**41:37** And the matter was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of all his servants.

**41:38** Then Pharaoh said to his servants, "Can we find anyone like this, a man who has the Spirit of God in him?"

**41:39** Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has made all this known to you, there is no one discerning and wise like you.

**41:40** You shall be over my house, and at your word all my people shall submit; only with respect to the throne will I be greater than you."

**41:41** Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Look, I have placed you over all the land of Egypt."

**41:42** Pharaoh removed his signet ring from his hand and set it on Joseph's hand; he dressed him in fine linen garments and placed the gold chain around his neck.

**41:43** And he had him ride in his second chariot, and they called out before him, "Bow the knee!" Thus he placed him over all the land of Egypt.

**41:44** And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh, yet without you no one shall lift his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt."

**41:45** Pharaoh called Joseph's name Zaphenath-paneah, and he gave him Asenath, daughter of Potiphera priest of On, as his wife. So Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.

**41:46** Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh, king of Egypt. Then Joseph went out from before Pharaoh and passed through all the land of Egypt.

**41:47** During the seven years of abundance, the land produced bountifully.

**41:48** He gathered all the food of the seven years that occurred in the land of Egypt and placed food in the cities; the food from the surrounding field of each city he placed inside it.

**41:49** So Joseph heaped up grain like the sand of the sea, in very great quantity, until he stopped counting, for it was beyond number.

**41:50** Now two sons were born to Joseph before the year of famine arrived, whom Asenath, daughter of Potiphera priest of On, bore for him.

**41:51** Joseph named the firstborn Manasseh, "For God has caused me to forget all my toil and all my father's house."

**41:52** And he named the second Ephraim, "For God has caused me to be fruitful in the land of my suffering."

**41:53** Then the seven years of abundance that had been in the land of Egypt came to a close.

**41:54** And the seven years of famine began to arrive, just as Joseph had said. There was famine in all the lands, yet throughout all the land of Egypt there was bread.

**41:55** When the entire land of Egypt grew famished, the people cried out to Pharaoh for bread; and Pharaoh said to all Egypt, "Go to Joseph; do whatever he tells you."

**41:56** Now the famine was over all the face of the earth; so Joseph opened all that held reserves and sold grain to Egypt, and the famine grew severe in the land of Egypt.

**41:57** And all the earth came into Egypt to Joseph to buy grain, for the famine had grown severe throughout all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **41:16** — Hebrew bil'aday ("apart from me") disclaims human credit, followed by a wordplay on the Qal verb 'anah ("answered" ... "will answer") with shalom ("welfare/peace"), expressing that God Himself will provide an answer bringing peace to Pharaoh.
- **41:34** — The Piel denominative verb chimmesh (from chamesh, "five") means to take or levy a fifth part of the land's yield during the seven years of abundance.
- **41:40** — Hebrew 'al-pikha yishshaq is literally "by your mouth [all my people] shall kiss" (Qal imperfect of nashaq), an idiom for paying homage, ordering oneself, or submitting to authority.
- **41:43** — Hebrew 'abrek is likely an Egyptian loanword used as a public acclamation (perhaps "Pay attention!" or "Bow the knee!"), traditionally linked in Hebrew to the root barak ("kneel"). The clause "Thus he placed him" translates a Qal infinitive absolute (wenaton) continuing the preceding narrative verbs.
- **41:56** — The Masoretic Text reads literally "opened all that was in them," an elliptical expression for the grain depots holding Egypt's reserves. The Qal verb shabar here means "sold grain," whereas the same Qal root in 41:57 means "to buy grain."
