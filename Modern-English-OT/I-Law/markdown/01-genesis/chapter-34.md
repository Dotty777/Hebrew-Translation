## Text

**34:1** Dinah, Leah's daughter whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land.

**34:2** Shechem, the son of Hamor the Hivite, ruler of the land, saw her, and he took her, lay with her, and violated her.

**34:3** His soul clung to Dinah, daughter of Jacob; he loved the young woman and spoke to the heart of the young woman.

**34:4** Shechem said to Hamor his father, "Get this girl for me as a wife."

**34:5** Jacob heard that he had defiled his daughter Dinah; his sons were with his livestock in the field, so Jacob remained silent until they came.

**34:6** Hamor, Shechem's father, went out to Jacob to speak with him.

**34:7** Jacob's sons came from the field as soon as they heard, and the men were grieved and exceedingly angry, because he had committed a disgraceful deed in Israel by lying with Jacob's daughter—and such a thing must not be done.

**34:8** Hamor spoke with them, saying, "My son Shechem—his soul longs for your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife.

**34:9** And form marriage ties with us: you will give us your daughters, and you will take our daughters for yourselves.

**34:10** With us you will dwell, and the land will be before you; dwell, trade through it, and take possession in it."

**34:11** Then Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, "May I find favor in your eyes; whatever you tell me, I will give.

**34:12** Make the bride-price and gift very great for me, and I will give just as you say to me; and give me the young woman as a wife."

**34:13** Jacob's sons answered Shechem and Hamor his father with deceit and spoke, because he had defiled Dinah their sister.

**34:14** And they said to them, "We are not able to do this thing—to give our sister to a man who has a foreskin—for that is a disgrace to us.

**34:15** Only on this basis will we consent to you: if you become like us, by having every male among you circumcised.

**34:16** Then we will give our daughters to you, and your daughters we will take for ourselves; we will dwell with you, and we will become one people.

**34:17** But if you do not listen to us so as to be circumcised, we will take our daughter and depart."

**34:18** Their words were good in the eyes of Hamor and in the eyes of Shechem, the son of Hamor.

**34:19** The young man did not delay in doing the thing, for he took delight in the daughter of Jacob; and he was more honored than all of his father's house.

**34:20** Hamor and Shechem his son came to the gate of their city, and they spoke to the men of their city, saying,

**34:21** "These men are at peace with us; let them dwell in the land and trade through it, for look, the land is broad on every side before them. Their daughters we will take for ourselves as wives, and our daughters we will give to them.

**34:22** Only on this basis will the men consent to us to dwell with us, to become one people: by having every male among us circumcised, just as they are circumcised.

**34:23** Their livestock and their property and all their animals—will they not be ours? Let us only consent to them, and they will dwell with us."

**34:24** So all those who went out through the gate of his city listened to Hamor and to Shechem his son, and every male was circumcised—all those who went out through the gate of his city.

**34:25** Now on the third day, while they were in pain, two of Jacob's sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers, each took his sword, came against the unsuspecting city, and killed every male.

**34:26** Hamor and Shechem his son they killed by the edge of the sword, and they took Dinah from Shechem's house and went out.

**34:27** The sons of Jacob came upon those who had been killed and plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister.

**34:28** Their flocks, their herds, and their donkeys, and what was in the city and what was in the field, they took.

**34:29** And all their wealth, all their little ones, and their wives they took captive and plundered, and all that was in the house.

**34:30** And Jacob said to Simeon and to Levi, "You have brought trouble upon me by making me a stench among the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. And I am few in number; they will gather against me and strike me down, and I will be destroyed, I and my household."

**34:31** They replied, "Is he to treat our sister like a prostitute?"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — The verb rendered 'violated her' (wayye'anneha, Piel of anah II) literally means 'humbled/humiliated her' and is used in Hebrew for sexual violation. The preceding form otah with shakhav ('lay') functions here like 'with her' (cf. 34:7).
- **34:3** — The phrase 'spoke to the heart of' (dibber al-lev) is a Hebrew idiom for speaking tenderly, persuasively, or reassuringly. Here and in 34:12 the word for 'young woman' exhibits the Pentateuchal spelling ha-na'ar pointed to be read ha-na'arah.
- **34:21** — The expression 'broad on every side' translates the Hebrew idiom rachavat-yadayim, literally 'wide of two hands,' signifying ample room in every direction.
- **34:25** — The adverbial noun betach ('in security') is syntactically ambiguous: it can describe the city as resting unsuspecting and off guard (as translated here), or it can describe Simeon and Levi as attacking boldly and without opposition.
