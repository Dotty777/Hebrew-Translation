## Text

**6:1** Now it happened, when man began to grow numerous on the surface of the ground and daughters were born to them,

**6:2** that the sons of God saw the daughters of man, that they were lovely, and they took for themselves wives from all whom they chose.

**6:3** Then the LORD said, "My Spirit will not remain in man forever, since he too is flesh, and his days will be one hundred and twenty years."

**6:4** The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and after that as well, when the sons of God would go in to the daughters of man and they bore offspring to them. They were the mighty warriors who were from ancient times, the men of renown.

**6:5** And the LORD saw that the evil of man was great on the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil all day long.

**6:6** So the LORD regretted that He had made man on the earth, and He was pained to His heart.

**6:7** And the LORD said, "I will wipe out man whom I created from the surface of the ground, from man to animals, to creeping things, and to the birds of the sky, for I regret that I made them."

**6:8** Noah, however, found favor in the eyes of the LORD.

**6:9** These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a righteous man, blameless among his generations; with God Noah walked.

**6:10** And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**6:11** And the earth became corrupt before God, and the earth became filled with violence.

**6:12** And God looked at the earth, and look, it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth.

**6:13** Then God said to Noah, "The end of all flesh has come before Me, for the earth is full of violence on account of them; and look, I am about to destroy them along with the earth.

**6:14** Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood; make the ark with compartments, and coat it inside and outside with pitch.

**6:15** And this is how you are to make it: the length of the ark is to be three hundred cubits, its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits.

**6:16** You shall make an opening for light for the ark, and finish it to a cubit from the top, and place the doorway of the ark in its side; make it with lower, second, and third stories.

**6:17** And as for Me, look, I am bringing the flood of waters upon the earth to destroy all flesh in which is the spirit of life from beneath the heavens; everything that is on the earth will perish.

**6:18** Yet I will establish My covenant with you, and you shall go into the ark, you and your sons and your wife and the wives of your sons with you.

**6:19** And of every living thing, of all flesh, you shall bring two of each into the ark to keep them alive with you; they shall be male and female.

**6:20** Of the birds according to their kind, and of the animals according to their kind, of every creeping thing of the ground according to its kind, two of each will come to you to keep them alive.

**6:21** And as for you, take for yourself of every kind of food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself, and it shall be food for you and for them."

**6:22** So Noah did this; according to all that God commanded him, just so he did.

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The rare verb yadon, occurring only here in the Masoretic Text, has an uncertain meaning, rendered either 'remain/abide' or, if linked to din, 'contend/strive.' The Masoretic pointing beshaggam parses as 'in that also' ('since he too'), rather than 'in their straying.'
- **6:13** — Hebrew repeats the same root shachat across verses 11-13 and 17—Niphal 'became corrupt' (vv. 11-12), Hiphil 'corrupted' (v. 12) and 'destroy' (v. 13), and Piel 'destroy' (v. 17)—highlighting measure-for-measure judgment.
- **6:14** — Gopher wood is a hapax legomenon naming an unidentified timber species, often conjectured to be cypress or another resinous wood; qinnim literally means 'nests,' here denoting compartments.
- **6:16** — Tsohar occurs in the singular only here and is connected with the root for midday brightness ('opening for light' or skylight), though some interpret it via an Arabic cognate as a 'roof.'
