## Text

**1:1** In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

**1:2** Now the earth was formless and empty, and darkness was over the face of the deep, and the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters.

**1:3** Then God said, "Let there be light," and light came to be.

**1:4** God saw the light, that it was good, and God made a division between the light and the darkness.

**1:5** God named the light "day," and the darkness He named "night." And there was evening, and there was morning: one day.

**1:6** Then God said, "Let there be an expanse in the middle of the waters, and let it make a division between waters and waters."

**1:7** So God made the expanse, and He made a division between the waters that were below the expanse and the waters that were above the expanse; and it was so.

**1:8** God named the expanse "heavens." And there was evening, and there was morning: a second day.

**1:9** Then God said, "Let the waters below the heavens be gathered into one place, and let the dry land appear"; and it was so.

**1:10** God named the dry land "earth," and the collection of the waters He named "seas"; and God saw that it was good.

**1:11** Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation: plants yielding seed, and fruit trees producing fruit according to their kind, whose seed is in them, on the earth"; and it was so.

**1:12** The earth brought forth vegetation: plants yielding seed according to their kind, and trees producing fruit whose seed is in them, according to their kind. And God saw that it was good.

**1:13** And there was evening, and there was morning: a third day.

**1:14** Then God said, "Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens to make a division between the day and the night, and let them serve as signs and for appointed times, and for days and years;

**1:15** and let them serve as lights in the expanse of the heavens to shine light on the earth"; and it was so.

**1:16** God made the two great lights, the greater light to govern the day and the smaller light to govern the night, and also the stars.

**1:17** And God set them in the expanse of the heavens to shine light on the earth,

**1:18** and to govern the day and the night, and to make a division between the light and the darkness. And God saw that it was good.

**1:19** And there was evening, and there was morning: a fourth day.

**1:20** Then God said, "Let the waters teem with teeming living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth across the face of the expanse of the heavens."

**1:21** God created the great sea creatures and every moving living creature with which the waters teemed, according to their kinds, and every bird of wing according to its kind; and God saw that it was good.

**1:22** And God blessed them, saying, "Bear fruit and increase, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds increase on the earth."

**1:23** And there was evening, and there was morning: a fifth day.

**1:24** Then God said, "Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kind: livestock, crawling creatures, and wild animals of the earth according to their kind"; and it was so.

**1:25** God made the wild animals of the earth according to their kind, and the livestock according to their kind, and every crawling creature of the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.

**1:26** Then God said, "Let Us make humanity in Our image, after Our likeness, and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every crawling creature that crawls on the earth."

**1:27** So God created humanity in His image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.

**1:28** And God blessed them, and God said to them, "Bear fruit and increase, and fill the earth and subdue it; and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living creature that crawls on the earth."

**1:29** Then God said, "Look, I have given you every plant yielding seed that is on the face of all the earth, and every tree that has in it the fruit of a tree yielding seed; they will be food for you.

**1:30** And to every wild animal of the earth and to every bird of the heavens and to everything that crawls on the earth, everything that has a living soul in it, I have given every green plant for food"; and it was so.

**1:31** God saw all that He had made, and look, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning: the sixth day.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Hebrew has the plural hashamayim, so "the heavens" (contrast the Greek and Latin singular); the wording follows the locked style policy example.
- **1:2** — Tohu wavohu is a rhyming pair meaning unformed and unfilled, rendered "formless and empty." Ruach can mean wind, breath or spirit; with "of God" and the participle "hovering" (as of a bird over its young, Deut 32:11) it is rendered "the Spirit of God."
- **1:5** — The Hebrew says yom echad, literally "day one" or "one day," with a cardinal number; the following days use ordinals ("a second day"), and only the sixth has the article.
- **1:6** — Raqia (from a verb meaning to beat out or spread) is rendered "expanse" throughout.
- **1:8** — Shamayim is rendered "heavens" here to stay consistent with 1:1.
- **1:16** — Hebrew lememsheleth, literally "for the dominion of," is rendered "to govern" here (matching the related verbal infinitive limshol in 1:18).
- **1:21** — Tanninim can mean large sea creatures or sea monsters; "great sea creatures" keeps the plain sense.
- **1:26** — Adam without the article is the collective "humanity"; the plural "Us/Our" is kept as in the Hebrew.
- **1:27** — Ha-adam is followed by the singular pronoun "him" and then the plural "them"; both are kept as in the Hebrew.
- **1:30** — Nefesh chayyah, rendered "living creature" elsewhere, appears here in a relative clause, so it is rendered "a living soul." The verb "I have given" is carried over from 1:29, as the Hebrew implies.
