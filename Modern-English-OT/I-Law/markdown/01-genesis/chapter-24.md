## Text

**24:1** Now Abraham was old, far along in years, and the LORD had blessed Abraham in everything.

**24:2** Abraham said to his servant, the eldest of his household, who ruled over everything that belonged to him, "Please lay your hand under my thigh,

**24:3** so that I may make you swear by the LORD, the God of the heavens and the God of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose midst I am living;

**24:4** instead, you will go to my land and to my relatives, and take a wife for my son, for Isaac."

**24:5** The servant said to him, "What if the woman is unwilling to come after me to this land? Should I really bring your son back to the land from which you came out?"

**24:6** Abraham said to him, "Be on your guard that you do not bring my son back there.

**24:7** The LORD, the God of the heavens, who took me from my father's house and from the land of my birth, and who spoke to me, and who swore to me, saying, 'I will give this land to your seed,' He will send His angel ahead of you, and you will take a wife for my son from there.

**24:8** And if the woman is unwilling to come after you, then you will be clear of this oath of mine; only do not bring my son back there."

**24:9** So the servant laid his hand under the thigh of his master Abraham and swore to him regarding this matter.

**24:10** Then the servant took ten camels from his master's camels and set out, with all the choicest goods of his master in his hand; so he rose and went to Aram-naharaim, to the city of Nahor.

**24:11** He had the camels kneel down outside the city beside the well of water at the time of evening, at the time the women who draw water go out.

**24:12** He said, "LORD, God of my master Abraham, please bring about success before me today, and show loyal love to my master Abraham.

**24:13** Look, I am stationed beside the spring of water, while the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water.

**24:14** Let it be that the young woman to whom I say, 'Please tilt your jar so that I may drink,' and who says, 'Drink, and I will also give your camels a drink,' may she be the one You have designated for Your servant, for Isaac; and through this I will know that You have shown loyal love to my master."

**24:15** Now before he had finished speaking, there was Rebekah coming out, who had been born to Bethuel, son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother, with her jar on her shoulder.

**24:16** Now the young woman was very lovely in appearance, a virgin, and no man had known her. She went down to the spring, filled her jar, and came up.

**24:17** Then the servant ran to meet her and said, "Please let me sip a little water from your jar."

**24:18** She said, "Drink, my lord." She hurried and lowered her jar onto her hand and gave him a drink.

**24:19** Once she had finished giving him a drink, she said, "I will draw water for your camels too, until they have finished drinking."

**24:20** She hurried and poured out her jar into the watering trough, then ran again to the well to draw water, and drew for all his camels.

**24:21** Meanwhile, the man was watching her in wonder, keeping silent to learn whether the LORD had made his way successful or not.

**24:22** When the camels had finished drinking, the man took a gold ring, half a shekel in weight, and two bracelets for her wrists, ten shekels of gold in weight.

**24:23** He said, "Whose daughter are you? Please tell me: is there a place in your father's house for us to spend the night?"

**24:24** She answered him, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, son of Milcah, whom she bore for Nahor."

**24:25** And she said to him, "We have an abundance of both straw and fodder, and a place to spend the night as well."

**24:26** Then the man bowed his head and prostrated himself before the LORD.

**24:27** He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who has not abandoned His loyal love and His faithfulness toward my master. As for me, the LORD has guided me along the way to the house of my master's brothers."

**24:28** Then the young woman ran and reported to her mother's household about these things.

**24:29** Now Rebekah had a brother, and his name was Laban; and Laban ran outside to the man, to the spring.

**24:30** As soon as he saw the ring and the bracelets on his sister's wrists, and heard the words of his sister Rebekah, saying, "This is what the man spoke to me," he came to the man, and there he was, standing beside the camels by the spring.

**24:31** He said, "Come in, you who are blessed by the LORD! Why are you standing outside, when I have made the house ready, along with a place for the camels?"

**24:32** So the man went into the house. He unharnessed the camels and gave straw and fodder to the camels, along with water to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him.

**24:33** Then something to eat was set before him, but he said, "I will not eat until I have spoken my words." He said, "Speak."

**24:34** He said, "I am Abraham's servant.

**24:35** The LORD has blessed my master exceedingly, and he has become great; He has given him flocks and herds, silver and gold, male and female servants, and camels and donkeys.

**24:36** And Sarah, my master's wife, bore a son for my master after she had grown old, and he has given him everything that belongs to him.

**24:37** My master made me swear, saying, 'You must not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I am living;

**24:38** rather, you must go to my father's house and to my clan, and take a wife for my son.'

**24:39** I said to my master, 'What if the woman will not come after me?'

**24:40** And he said to me, 'The LORD, in whose presence I have walked, will send His angel with you and make your way successful, and you will take a wife for my son from my clan and from my father's house.

**24:41** Then you will be clear of my oath once you come to my clan; and if they do not give her to you, you will be clear of my oath.'

**24:42** So I came today to the spring and said, 'LORD, God of my master Abraham, if You are indeed making my way successful, on which I am going;

**24:43** look, I am stationed beside the spring of water. Let it be that the maiden who comes out to draw water, and to whom I say, "Please give me a little water to drink from your jar,"

**24:44** and who says to me, "Drink yourself, and I will also draw water for your camels," may she be the woman whom the LORD has designated for my master's son.'

**24:45** Before I had finished speaking in my heart, there was Rebekah coming out with her jar on her shoulder, and she went down to the spring and drew water. I said to her, 'Please give me a drink.'

**24:46** She hurried and lowered her jar off her shoulder and said, 'Drink, and I will also give your camels a drink.' So I drank, and she gave the camels a drink as well.

**24:47** Then I asked her and said, 'Whose daughter are you?' And she said, 'The daughter of Bethuel, son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore for him.' So I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her wrists.

**24:48** Then I bowed my head and prostrated myself before the LORD, and blessed the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who had guided me along the true way to take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.

**24:49** And now, if you intend to show loyal love and faithfulness toward my master, tell me; and if not, tell me, so that I may turn to the right or to the left."

**24:50** Then Laban and Bethuel answered and said, "From the LORD this matter has come out; we cannot speak either bad or good to you.

**24:51** Look, Rebekah is before you; take her and go, and may she become a wife for your master's son, just as the LORD has spoken."

**24:52** When Abraham's servant heard their words, he prostrated himself to the ground before the LORD.

**24:53** Then the servant brought out silver articles and gold articles, along with garments, and gave them to Rebekah; and choice gifts he gave to her brother and to her mother.

**24:54** Then they ate and drank, he and the men who were with him, and spent the night. When they rose in the morning, he said, "Send me off to my master."

**24:55** Her brother and her mother said, "Let the young woman stay with us a few days, or ten; afterward she may go."

**24:56** He said to them, "Do not detain me, now that the LORD has made my way successful. Send me off so that I may go to my master."

**24:57** They said, "Let us call the young woman and ask her directly."

**24:58** So they called for Rebekah and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" She replied, "I will go."

**24:59** So they sent off their sister Rebekah and her nurse, along with Abraham's servant and his men.

**24:60** They blessed Rebekah and said to her, "Our sister, become thousands of ten thousands, and may your seed take possession of the gate of those who hate him!"

**24:61** Then Rebekah rose, along with her female attendants, and they rode on the camels and went after the man. So the servant took Rebekah and set out.

**24:62** Now Isaac had come from the approach to Beer-lahai-roi, for he was living in the land of the Negev.

**24:63** Isaac went out to meditate in the field as evening approached; he raised his eyes and looked, and there were camels coming.

**24:64** Rebekah raised her eyes and saw Isaac, and she slipped down from the camel.

**24:65** She said to the servant, "Who is that man over there walking in the field to meet us?" The servant replied, "That is my master." Then she took the veil and covered herself.

**24:66** Then the servant recounted to Isaac all the things he had done.

**24:67** Isaac brought her into the tent of his mother Sarah; he took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her. So Isaac found comfort after the death of his mother.

## Translator Notes

- **24:12** — The Hiphil imperative of qarah ("bring about success") literally means "cause to happen" or "arrange a favorable encounter."
- **24:21** — The participle translated "watching ... in wonder" is a rare Hithpael form of sha'ah (with metathesis of the prefix t and initial shin), conveying intent, silent amazement.
- **24:22** — Hebrew beka denotes a half-shekel weight; in the second clause, "ten of gold" idiomatically leaves the unit "shekels" unstated.
- **24:32** — The unexpressed third-person singular subject of "unharnessed" and "gave" shifts implicitly from the servant back to his host, Laban.
- **24:33** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text preserves an unusual passive spelling of sim ("set"), while the read text (qere, translated here) gives the regular Hophal passive wayyiqtol "was set."
- **24:43** — Hebrew almah denotes a marriageable unmarried young woman, distinct from na'arah ("young woman") used in verses 14, 16, 28, 55, and 57.
- **24:48** — Hebrew "daughter of my master's brother" uses kinship terms broadly, since Rebekah is the granddaughter of Abraham's brother Nahor.
- **24:62** — Hebrew mibbo' ("from the approach to" or "from coming to") follows the Masoretic Text; Beer-lahai-roi is the well named in Genesis 16:14.
- **24:63** — The verb translated "meditate" (Hebrew lasuach) occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; its exact sense is uncertain, with proposals including "meditate" (linked to siach) and "stroll" or "walk about."
- **24:67** — Hebrew literally reads "into the tent, Sarah his mother" (a rare appositional construct) and concludes with "after his mother," with her death implied from context.
