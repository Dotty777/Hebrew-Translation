## Text

**47:1** Then Joseph came and reported to Pharaoh, saying, "My father and my brothers, with their flocks, their herds, and everything that belongs to them, have come from the land of Canaan, and here they are in the land of Goshen."

**47:2** And he took five men from among his brothers and set them before Pharaoh.

**47:3** Pharaoh said to his brothers, "What is your work?" They replied to Pharaoh, "Your servants are shepherds, both we and our fathers."

**47:4** And they said to Pharaoh, "We have come to sojourn in the land, because there is no pasture for the flocks belonging to your servants, since the famine is severe in the land of Canaan. Now please let your servants settle in the land of Goshen."

**47:5** Then Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, saying, "Your father and your brothers have come to you.

**47:6** The land of Egypt lies before you. Settle your father and your brothers in the best part of the land; let them settle in the land of Goshen. And if you know that there are capable men among them, appoint them as overseers of livestock over what belongs to me."

**47:7** Joseph then brought in his father Jacob and had him stand before Pharaoh, and Jacob blessed Pharaoh.

**47:8** Pharaoh asked Jacob, "How many are the days of the years of your life?"

**47:9** Jacob answered Pharaoh, "The days of the years of my sojourning are one hundred thirty years. Few and difficult have been the days of the years of my life, and they have not reached the days of the years of my fathers' lives during the days of their sojourning."

**47:10** Then Jacob blessed Pharaoh and went out from before Pharaoh.

**47:11** Joseph settled his father and his brothers and gave them property in the land of Egypt, in the best part of the land, in the land of Rameses, just as Pharaoh had commanded.

**47:12** Joseph also sustained his father, his brothers, and his father's entire household with bread in proportion to their little ones.

**47:13** Now there was no bread in all the land, because the famine was very severe, and the land of Egypt and the land of Canaan grew faint on account of the famine.

**47:14** Joseph collected all the silver found in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan for the grain they were buying, and Joseph brought the silver into Pharaoh's house.

**47:15** When the silver was exhausted from the land of Egypt and from the land of Canaan, all the Egyptians came to Joseph and said, "Give us bread! Why should we die in front of you? For silver has run out."

**47:16** Then Joseph said, "Give over your livestock, and I will give to you in exchange for your livestock, if silver has run out."

**47:17** So they brought their livestock to Joseph, and Joseph gave them bread in exchange for the horses, the flocks, the cattle, and the donkeys; he provided them with bread in exchange for all their livestock during that year.

**47:18** When that year came to an end, they came to him in the second year and said to him, "We will not conceal from my lord that the silver is spent and the herds of livestock belong to my lord; nothing remains before my lord except our bodies and our land.

**47:19** Why should we die before your eyes, both we and our land? Buy us and our land in exchange for bread, and we along with our land will become servants to Pharaoh. And give seed so that we may live and not die, and the land will not become desolate."

**47:20** Then Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh, because the Egyptians sold each one his field, since the famine prevailed over them; and the land became Pharaoh's.

**47:21** As for the people, he relocated them to the cities, from one end of the border of Egypt to the other.

**47:22** Only the priests' land he did not buy, because the priests had a set allowance from Pharaoh, and they ate their allowance that Pharaoh gave them; for that reason they did not sell their land.

**47:23** Joseph then said to the people, "Look, I have bought you and your land for Pharaoh today. Here is seed for you, and you shall sow the land.

**47:24** And it shall be at the harvests that you must give a fifth to Pharaoh, while the four parts will be yours—for seed of the field, for your food and for those in your households, and for your little ones to eat."

**47:25** They replied, "You have kept us alive! May we find favor in the eyes of my lord, and we will be servants to Pharaoh."

**47:26** So Joseph established it as a statute over the land of Egypt to this day, that the fifth belongs to Pharaoh; only the land of the priests alone did not become Pharaoh's.

**47:27** Now Israel settled in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen; they acquired property in it, and they became fruitful and increased greatly.

**47:28** And Jacob lived seventeen years in the land of Egypt, and the days of Jacob, the years of his life, were one hundred forty-seven years.

**47:29** When the days drew near for Israel to die, he summoned his son Joseph and said to him, "If now I have found favor in your eyes, please place your hand under my thigh and deal with me in loyal love and faithfulness: please do not bury me in Egypt.

**47:30** But when I lie down with my fathers, carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their burial site." He answered, "I myself will do according to your word."

**47:31** And he said, "Swear to me," so he swore to him. Then Israel bowed down at the head of the bed.

## Translator Notes

- **47:13** — Hebrew wattelah is the Qal wayyiqtol of the rare verb lahah ("grow faint, languish"), occurring as a finite verb only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **47:17** — Hebrew wayenahalem is the Piel wayyiqtol of nahal, which normally means "lead" or "guide gently," used here in the extended sense of providing for or carrying people through hardship.
- **47:21** — Masoretic Hebrew reads the Hiphil of abar ("relocated/moved") with "to the cities," whereas the Samaritan Pentateuch and Septuagint reflect a single-consonant difference yielding the Hiphil of abad ("reduced to servitude as slaves").
- **47:23** — Hebrew he ("here is") is a rare presentative interjection occurring only here and in Ezekiel 16:43 in the Hebrew Bible, cognate with Biblical Aramaic ha.
- **47:31** — Masoretic vocalization reads ham-mittah ("the bed"), whereas the Septuagint (followed in Hebrews 11:21) vocalizes the same unpointed consonants as ham-matteh ("the staff").
