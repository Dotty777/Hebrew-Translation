## Text

**21:1** Now the LORD attended to Sarah just as He had said, and the LORD did for Sarah just as He had spoken.

**21:2** And Sarah conceived and bore a son for Abraham in his old age, at the appointed time that God had spoken of.

**21:3** Abraham gave the name Isaac to his son who was born to him, whom Sarah had borne to him.

**21:4** And Abraham circumcised Isaac his son at eight days old, just as God had commanded him.

**21:5** And Abraham was a hundred years old when Isaac his son was born to him.

**21:6** And Sarah said, "God has brought about laughter for me; whoever hears will laugh with me."

**21:7** And she said, "Who would have told Abraham, 'Sarah would nurse children'? Yet I have borne a son in his old age."

**21:8** The child grew and was weaned, and Abraham prepared a great banquet on the day Isaac was weaned.

**21:9** But Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian woman, whom she had borne for Abraham, mocking.

**21:10** And she said to Abraham, "Drive out this slave woman and her son, for the son of this slave woman will not inherit with my son, with Isaac."

**21:11** And the matter was deeply troubling in the eyes of Abraham on account of his son.

**21:12** But God said to Abraham, "Do not let it be troubling in your eyes because of the boy and because of your slave woman. In all that Sarah says to you, listen to her voice, for in Isaac seed will be named for you.

**21:13** And I will also make the son of the slave woman into a nation, for he is your seed."

**21:14** Abraham got up early in the morning, took bread and a skin of water, and gave them to Hagar—placing them on her shoulder—together with the child, and sent her away. And she went and wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba.

**21:15** And the water from the skin ran out, and she cast the child beneath one of the shrubs.

**21:16** Then she went and sat down by herself across from him at a distance, about a bowshot away, for she said, "Let me not watch the death of the child." And she sat across from him, raised her voice, and wept.

**21:17** And God heard the boy's voice, and the angel of God called out to Hagar from heaven and said to her, "What is wrong with you, Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has listened to the voice of the boy where he is.

**21:18** Rise up, lift the boy, and keep a firm hand on him, for I will make a great nation out of him."

**21:19** And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water; she went and filled the skin with water and gave a drink to the boy.

**21:20** And God was with the boy, and he grew up; he settled in the wilderness and became a bowman.

**21:21** He settled in the wilderness of Paran, and his mother took for him a wife from the land of Egypt.

**21:22** Now at that time, Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, spoke to Abraham and said, "God is with you in everything that you do.

**21:23** So now, swear to me by God here that you will not break faith with me, nor with my offspring, nor with my posterity; according to the loyal love that I have shown to you, you will show to me and to the land in which you have lived as a foreigner."

**21:24** Abraham said, "I myself will swear."

**21:25** Now Abraham reproved Abimelech on account of the well of water that Abimelech's servants had seized by force.

**21:26** And Abimelech said, "I do not know who did this thing; neither did you inform me, nor have I heard of it until today."

**21:27** Then Abraham took flocks and herds and gave them to Abimelech, and both of them made a covenant.

**21:28** And Abraham placed seven ewe lambs of the flock by themselves.

**21:29** Then Abimelech said to Abraham, "What are these seven ewe lambs that you have placed by themselves?"

**21:30** And he said, "You are to take the seven ewe lambs from my hand, in order that it may be a witness for me that I dug this well."

**21:31** For this reason he called that place Beersheba, because both of them swore an oath there.

**21:32** And they made a covenant at Beersheba; then Abimelech and Phicol, commander of his army, got up and returned to the land of the Philistines.

**21:33** Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba, and there he called on the name of the LORD, the Eternal God.

**21:34** And Abraham lived as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines for many days.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Hebrew 'oto' is pointed as the direct-object pronoun resuming 'the appointed time' ("which God had spoken of"), though some take it as an alternative form of 'itto' ("with him").
- **21:9** — Hebrew 'metsacheq' is the Piel participle of 'tsachaq' (the same root as Isaac's name and the Qal verb "laugh" in v. 6), with a semantic range covering "laughing," "playing," or "mocking." The Masoretic Text ends with this participle and does not include the Septuagint addition "with Isaac her son."
- **21:14** — Hebrew 'sam al-shikhmah' ("placing on her shoulder") is an asyndetic circumstantial clause interrupting the objects of "gave," so "together with the child" resumes what Abraham handed over to Hagar rather than placing the child on her shoulder.
- **21:31** — The place name Beersheba ('be'er sheva') carries a double wordplay, meaning both "well of seven" (linking to the seven ewe lambs in vv. 28-30) and "well of the oath" (linking to the Niphal verb 'nishbe'u', "swore an oath").
