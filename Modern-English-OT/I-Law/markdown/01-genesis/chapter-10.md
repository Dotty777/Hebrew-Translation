## Text

**10:1** Now these are the generations of Noah's sons—Shem, Ham, and Japheth—and sons were born to them after the flood.

**10:2** Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**10:3** And Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**10:4** And Javan's sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

**10:5** From these the coastlands of the nations separated in their lands, each according to his language, by their clans, in their nations.

**10:6** And Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

**10:7** And Cush's sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca; and Raamah's sons were Sheba and Dedan.

**10:8** Now Cush fathered Nimrod; he began to be a mighty one on the earth.

**10:9** He was a mighty hunter in the sight of the LORD; for this reason it is said, "Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter in the sight of the LORD."

**10:10** And the beginning of his realm was Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh in the land of Shinar.

**10:11** Out of that land he went into Assyria, and he built Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, Calah,

**10:12** and Resen, between Nineveh and Calah—that is the great city.

**10:13** And Mizraim fathered Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim,

**10:14** Pathrusim, Casluhim—from whom the Philistines came out—and Caphtorim.

**10:15** And Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn, Heth,

**10:16** the Jebusite, the Amorite, the Girgashite,

**10:17** the Hivite, the Arkite, the Sinite,

**10:18** the Arvadite, the Zemarite, and the Hamathite; and afterward the clans of the Canaanite were dispersed.

**10:19** And the border of the Canaanite extended from Sidon, going toward Gerar as far as Gaza; going toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim as far as Lasha.

**10:20** These are Ham's sons, by their clans, by their languages, in their lands, in their nations.

**10:21** And to Shem—the father of all the sons of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder—children were born as well.

**10:22** Shem's sons were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram.

**10:23** And Aram's sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash.

**10:24** Now Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.

**10:25** And two sons were born to Eber: the name of the first was Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided, and the name of his brother was Joktan.

**10:26** And Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**10:27** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**10:28** Obal, Abimael, Sheba,

**10:29** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab; all of these were Joktan's sons.

**10:30** And their dwelling place extended from Mesha, going toward Sephar, the mountain of the east.

**10:31** These are Shem's sons, by their clans, by their languages, in their lands, by their nations.

**10:32** These are the clans of Noah's sons, according to their generations, in their nations; and from these the nations separated on the earth after the flood.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "Dodanim," whereas the Samaritan Pentateuch, the Septuagint, and most manuscripts of 1 Chronicles 1:7 read "Rodanim" due to the visual similarity of dalet and resh.
- **10:11** — The Hebrew clause "min-ha'arets hahi yatsa Ashur" is grammatically ambiguous: taking "Ashur" as an unmarked accusative of destination yields "he [Nimrod] went into Assyria," whereas taking "Ashur" as the subject yields "Asshur went out from that land."
- **10:19** — Hebrew "bo'akhah" (repeated here and in v. 30) is a Qal infinitive construct of "bo" ("go" or "enter") with an archaic second-person suffix ("your going"), used idiomatically as a directional marker meaning "going toward."
- **10:21** — The construct phrase "achi Yefet haggadol" is syntactically ambiguous in Hebrew and can be read either as "the brother of Japheth the elder" or as "the elder brother of Japheth."
- **10:25** — The name "Peleg" (meaning "division" or "water channel") is linked by wordplay to the Niphal verb "niflegah" ("was divided").
