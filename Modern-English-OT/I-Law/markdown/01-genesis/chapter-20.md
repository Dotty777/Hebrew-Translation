## Text

**20:1** Then Abraham traveled on from there to the land of the Negev and settled between Kadesh and Shur, and he resided as a foreigner in Gerar.

**20:2** Abraham said concerning Sarah his wife, "She is my sister." So Abimelech, the king of Gerar, sent and took Sarah.

**20:3** Yet God came to Abimelech in a dream at night and said to him, "Look, you are a dead man because of the woman whom you have taken, since she is married to a husband."

**20:4** Now Abimelech had not come near her, so he said, "Lord, will You kill even a righteous nation?

**20:5** Did he not say to me himself, 'She is my sister'? And she, she herself also said, 'He is my brother.' In the integrity of my heart and in the cleanness of my hands I did this."

**20:6** Then God said to him in the dream, "I too know that you did this in the integrity of your heart, and I Myself also kept you from sinning against Me; therefore I did not allow you to touch her.

**20:7** So now, return the man's wife, because he is a prophet, and he will pray for you, and you will live. Yet if you do not return her, know that you will certainly die, you and all who belong to you."

**20:8** Abimelech rose early in the morning, summoned all his servants, and spoke all these things in their hearing; and the men were greatly afraid.

**20:9** Then Abimelech summoned Abraham and said to him, "What have you done to us? And in what way have I sinned against you, that you have brought a great sin upon me and upon my kingdom? You have done to me deeds that should not be done."

**20:10** Abimelech also said to Abraham, "What did you see, that you have done this thing?"

**20:11** Abraham answered, "Because I thought, 'Surely there is no fear of God in this place, and they will kill me on account of my wife.'

**20:12** Furthermore, in truth she is my sister, my father's daughter, yet not my mother's daughter; and she became my wife.

**20:13** And when God caused me to wander from my father's house, I said to her, 'This is your kindness that you shall show to me: in every place we come to, say concerning me, "He is my brother."'"

**20:14** Then Abimelech took flocks and herds, and male servants and female servants, and gave them to Abraham, and he returned his wife Sarah to him.

**20:15** Abimelech said, "Look, my land is before you; settle wherever it is good in your eyes."

**20:16** And to Sarah he said, "Look, I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver; see, it is a covering of the eyes for you before all who are with you, and with everyone you are vindicated."

**20:17** Then Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his maidservants, so that they gave birth.

**20:18** For the LORD had completely closed every womb of the house of Abimelech on account of Sarah, Abraham's wife.

## Translator Notes

- **20:6** — The form 'from sinning' (mechato) is an unusual Qal infinitive construct of chata ('to sin') spelled without its final silent aleph.
- **20:13** — The Hiphil verb 'caused to wander' (hitu) is grammatically plural in agreement with the plural form of Elohim ('God'), an occasional Hebrew construction (cf. Genesis 35:7) that here also fits Abraham addressing a foreign king.
- **20:16** — The idiom 'a covering of the eyes' (kesut einayim) denotes compensation that removes reproach and prevents suspicion. The final clause (we-et kol wenokachat) pairs the preposition et ('with/before') with a Niphal feminine singular participle of ykach ('vindicated' or 'cleared').
