## Text

**46:1** Then Israel set out with all that belonged to him, and he came to Beersheba and offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.

**46:2** God spoke to Israel in night visions, saying, "Jacob, Jacob." He replied, "Here I am."

**46:3** He said, "I am God, the God of your father. Do not fear going down to Egypt, because there I will make you into a great nation.

**46:4** I Myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I Myself will also surely bring you up, and Joseph will place his hand on your eyes."

**46:5** Jacob then rose from Beersheba, and the sons of Israel carried their father Jacob, their little ones, and their wives in the wagons Pharaoh had sent to carry him.

**46:6** They took their livestock and the possessions they had acquired in the land of Canaan, and they came to Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him.

**46:7** His sons and his sons' sons with him, his daughters and his sons' daughters, and all his offspring he brought with him into Egypt.

**46:8** These are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt, Jacob and his sons: Jacob's firstborn, Reuben.

**46:9** The sons of Reuben were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

**46:10** The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of the Canaanite woman.

**46:11** The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**46:12** The sons of Judah were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah—though Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan—and the sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.

**46:13** The sons of Issachar were Tola, Puvah, Job, and Shimron.

**46:14** The sons of Zebulun were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.

**46:15** These are the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Paddan-aram, together with his daughter Dinah; all the persons of his sons and his daughters were thirty-three.

**46:16** The sons of Gad were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.

**46:17** The sons of Asher were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah; and the sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel.

**46:18** These are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban had given to his daughter Leah; she bore these to Jacob—sixteen persons.

**46:19** The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife, were Joseph and Benjamin.

**46:20** Manasseh and Ephraim were born to Joseph in the land of Egypt, whom Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore to him.

**46:21** The sons of Benjamin were Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.

**46:22** These are the sons of Rachel who were born to Jacob—fourteen persons in all.

**46:23** And the sons of Dan: Hushim.

**46:24** The sons of Naphtali were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.

**46:25** These are the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban had given to his daughter Rachel; she bore these to Jacob—seven persons in all.

**46:26** All the persons belonging to Jacob who came to Egypt—those coming from his loins, apart from the wives of Jacob's sons—were sixty-six persons in all.

**46:27** And Joseph's sons who were born to him in Egypt were two persons; all the persons of Jacob's household who came to Egypt were seventy.

**46:28** Now he sent Judah ahead of him to Joseph to direct the way before him to Goshen, and they came to the land of Goshen.

**46:29** So Joseph harnessed his chariot and went up to Goshen to meet his father Israel; he appeared to him, fell on his neck, and wept on his neck for a long while.

**46:30** Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die, after seeing your face, for you are still alive."

**46:31** Joseph said to his brothers and to his father's household, "I will go up and report to Pharaoh, and say to him, 'My brothers and my father's household, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me.

**46:32** And the men are shepherds, for they have been livestock keepers, and they have brought their flocks, their herds, and everything that belongs to them.'

**46:33** So it will be that when Pharaoh summons you and says, 'What is your work?'

**46:34** you must say, 'Your servants have been livestock keepers from our youth until now, both we and our fathers,' so that you may settle in the land of Goshen, because every shepherd is an abomination to the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **46:3** — Hebrew meredah is an unusual Qal infinitive construct of yarad ("go down") with a feminine ending, following the preposition min ("from"), literally "from going down."
- **46:4** — Hebrew combines a Hiphil imperfect of alah ("bring up") with the emphatic particle gam and a Qal infinitive absolute of the same root (aloh) to express emphatic certainty ("also surely bring you up"). "Place his hand on your eyes" is a literal idiom for closing the eyes of a dying parent.
- **46:20** — The singular Niphal passive verb ("was born") takes the object marker et before the compound subject ("Manasseh and Ephraim"), a standard Classical Hebrew impersonal passive construction.
- **46:28** — Masoretic Hebrew reads lehorot, the Hiphil infinitive construct of yarah ("to direct" or "show the way"), whereas the Septuagint reflects a different verbal root meaning "to meet."
