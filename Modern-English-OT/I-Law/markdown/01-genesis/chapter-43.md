## Text

**43:1** And the famine was heavy in the land.

**43:2** And it happened, when they had finished eating the grain they had brought from Egypt, that their father said to them, "Return, buy a little food for us."

**43:3** Then Judah said to him, "The man strictly warned us, saying, 'You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.'

**43:4** If you are going to send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you.

**43:5** But if you are not going to send him, we will not go down; because the man said to us, 'You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"

**43:6** Then Israel said, "Why did you deal badly with me by telling the man whether you still had a brother?"

**43:7** And they said, "The man inquired closely about us and our relatives, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Do you have a brother?' So we told him in accordance with these words. Could we possibly have known that he would say, 'Bring down your brother'?"

**43:8** Then Judah said to his father Israel, "Send the boy with me, and we will rise and go, so that we may live and not die, both we and you and our little ones too.

**43:9** I myself will be surety for him; from my hand you may require him. If I do not bring him to you and place him before you, then let me bear the guilt before you for all time.

**43:10** For had we not lingered, surely by now we would have returned two times."

**43:11** Then Israel their father said to them, "If so, then do this: take some of the prized produce of the land in your containers, and bring down a gift to the man, a little balm and a little honey, gum and myrrh, pistachio nuts and almonds.

**43:12** Take twice the silver in your hand, and return in your hand the silver that was returned in the mouth of your bags; perhaps it was an oversight.

**43:13** Take your brother too; rise and return to the man.

**43:14** And may God Almighty give you compassion before the man, so that he will release to you your other brother and Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved."

**43:15** So the men took this gift, and they took twice the silver in their hand, along with Benjamin; and they rose up, went down to Egypt, and stood before Joseph.

**43:16** When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the one who was over his house, "Bring the men into the house; slaughter an animal and make preparations, because the men will eat with me at noon."

**43:17** So the man did as Joseph had said, and the man brought the men into Joseph's house.

**43:18** And the men were afraid because they had been brought into Joseph's house, and they said, "Because of the silver that returned in our bags the first time, we are being brought in, so that he may rush upon us and fall upon us, and take us as slaves, along with our donkeys."

**43:19** So they approached the man who was over Joseph's house and spoke to him at the entrance of the house.

**43:20** They said, "Please, my lord, we indeed came down the first time to buy food.

**43:21** And it happened, when we came to the lodging place and opened our bags, look, each man's silver was in the mouth of his bag, our silver in its full weight; so we have returned it in our hand.

**43:22** And we have brought down other silver in our hand to buy food; we do not know who placed our silver in our bags."

**43:23** And he said, "Peace be to you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has given you hidden treasure in your bags; your silver came to me." Then he brought Simeon out to them.

**43:24** So the man brought the men into Joseph's house; he gave water and they washed their feet, and he gave fodder to their donkeys.

**43:25** And they prepared the gift for the coming of Joseph at noon, because they had heard that they would eat bread there.

**43:26** When Joseph came into the house, they brought the gift that was in their hand into the house to him, and they bowed down to him to the ground.

**43:27** Then he inquired about their well-being and said, "Is it well with your aged father of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?"

**43:28** And they said, "It is well with your servant, our father; he is still alive." Then they bowed low and prostrated themselves.

**43:29** He lifted his eyes and saw Benjamin his brother, the son of his mother, and said, "Is this your youngest brother, the one you spoke to me about?" And he said, "God be gracious to you, my son."

**43:30** And Joseph hurried off, because his compassion was deeply stirred toward his brother, and he sought a place to weep; so he went into the inner chamber and wept there.

**43:31** Then he washed his face and came out; restraining himself, he said, "Set out the food."

**43:32** And they set food for him by himself, for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians who were eating with him by themselves, because the Egyptians could not eat bread with the Hebrews, since that is an abomination to the Egyptians.

**43:33** So they sat down before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth; and the men marveled, looking at one another.

**43:34** He had portions carried to them from before him, and Benjamin's portion was five times greater than the portions of all of them. And they drank and drank freely with him.

## Translator Notes

- **43:11** — 'Prized produce' translates a rare Hebrew noun (zimrah, found only here in this sense) denoting the choice or celebrated yield of the land; 'gum and myrrh' refer to aromatic botanical resins (likely tragacanth gum and labdanum).
- **43:18** — 'Rush upon us' translates the rare Hithpoel infinitive of the root meaning 'roll' (literally 'roll oneself upon'), idiomatically expressing an overwhelming assault or pretext for accusation.
- **43:28** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text (ketiv) lacks the second final waw and appears as a singular verb, while the read tradition (qere) reads the plural 'prostrated themselves,' agreeing with the preceding plural verb.
- **43:34** — 'Five times greater' is literally 'five hands'; 'drank freely' translates the Qal of skr, which ranges from drinking to full satisfaction to becoming intoxicated.
