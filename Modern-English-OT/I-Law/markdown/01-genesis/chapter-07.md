## Text

**7:1** And the LORD said to Noah, "Enter the ark, you and your entire household, for I have seen you to be righteous before Me in this generation.

**7:2** From every clean animal you shall take for yourself by sevens, a male and its mate, and from the animals that are not clean, two, a male and its mate;

**7:3** also from the birds of the heavens by sevens, male and female, to keep offspring alive on the surface of the entire earth.

**7:4** For in seven more days I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe out every existing thing that I have made from the surface of the ground."

**7:5** So Noah did according to everything that the LORD had commanded him.

**7:6** Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters arrived on the earth.

**7:7** So Noah went into the ark, and his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons with him, because of the waters of the flood.

**7:8** Of the clean animals and of the animals that are not clean, and of the birds and everything that creeps on the ground,

**7:9** two by two they came to Noah into the ark, male and female, just as God had commanded Noah.

**7:10** And it happened after the seven days that the waters of the flood came upon the earth.

**7:11** In the six hundredth year of the life of Noah, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that day all the springs of the great deep split open, and the windows of the heavens were opened.

**7:12** And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.

**7:13** On that very day Noah, and Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and the wife of Noah and the three wives of his sons with them, went into the ark,

**7:14** they and every wild animal according to its kind, and all the livestock according to its kind, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth according to its kind, and all the birds according to their kind, every bird, everything with wings.

**7:15** And they came to Noah into the ark, two by two from all flesh in which was the spirit of life.

**7:16** And those that came in, male and female from all flesh, came in just as God had commanded him; and the LORD shut him inside.

**7:17** Now the flood lasted forty days on the earth, and the waters increased and lifted the ark, and it rose up above the earth.

**7:18** And the waters prevailed and increased greatly upon the earth, and the ark moved over the surface of the waters.

**7:19** And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth, and all the high mountains that were beneath the entire heavens were covered.

**7:20** Fifteen cubits upward the waters prevailed, and the mountains were covered.

**7:21** And all flesh that moved on the earth perished: birds, livestock, wild animals, and every swarming thing that swarms on the earth, and all mankind.

**7:22** Everything in whose nostrils was the breath of the spirit of life, of all that was on the dry land, died.

**7:23** So He wiped out every existing thing that was on the surface of the ground, from man to animals, to creeping things, and to the birds of the heavens, and they were wiped out from the earth; and only Noah remained, and those who were with him in the ark.

**7:24** And the waters prevailed over the earth for one hundred and fifty days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The distributive expression shivah shivah (lit. 'seven, seven') means 'by sevens,' often understood as seven pairs in light of the following idiom ish we'ishto (lit. 'a man and his wife,' used here for animal mates).
- **7:4** — Hayequm (from qum, 'to stand/exist') denotes 'every existing thing' or living substance; the same term recurs in 7:23.
- **7:23** — The opening verb wayyimach is vocalized as active Qal ('He wiped out,' governing the direct object marker et) with God as implicit subject, followed by the passive Niphal wayyimmachu ('and they were wiped out').
