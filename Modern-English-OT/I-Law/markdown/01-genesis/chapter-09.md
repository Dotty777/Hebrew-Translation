## Text

**9:1** Then God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, "Be fruitful and grow numerous, and fill the earth.

**9:2** And the dread of you and the terror of you will be upon every beast of the earth and upon every bird of the sky; along with everything that moves on the ground and all the fish of the sea, they have been given into your hand.

**9:3** Every moving creature that is alive will be food for you; just as with the green vegetation, I have given everything to you.

**9:4** However, you must not eat flesh with its life—its blood.

**9:5** And indeed, I will demand your own lifeblood; from the hand of every beast I will demand it, and from the hand of man—from the hand of each person's brother—I will demand the life of man.

**9:6** One who sheds the blood of man, by man his blood will be shed, because in God's image He made man.

**9:7** As for you, be fruitful and grow numerous; teem on the earth and grow numerous in it."

**9:8** Then God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him, saying,

**9:9** "As for Me, look, I am establishing My covenant with you and with your offspring after you,

**9:10** and with every living creature that is with you—among the birds, the livestock, and every beast of the earth with you—from all that came out of the ark to every beast of the earth.

**9:11** And I will establish My covenant with you: all flesh will never again be cut off by the waters of the flood, and there will never again be a flood to destroy the earth."

**9:12** Then God said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I am giving between Me and you and every living creature that is with you, for generations forever:

**9:13** I have placed My bow in the cloud, and it will be a sign of a covenant between Me and the earth.

**9:14** And it will happen, when I gather a cloud over the earth, that the bow will appear in the cloud,

**9:15** and I will remember My covenant that is between Me and you and every living creature among all flesh; and the waters will never again become a flood to destroy all flesh.

**9:16** The bow will be in the cloud, and I will see it to remember an everlasting covenant between God and every living creature among all flesh that is on the earth."

**9:17** Then God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant I have established between Me and all flesh that is on the earth."

**9:18** The sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham was the father of Canaan.

**9:19** These three were Noah's sons, and from these the whole earth was dispersed.

**9:20** And Noah began as a man of the soil, and he planted a vineyard.

**9:21** He drank from the wine and grew drunk, and he uncovered himself in the middle of his tent.

**9:22** Ham, Canaan's father, saw his father's nakedness, and he told his two brothers outside.

**9:23** Then Shem and Japheth took the cloak, placed it across the shoulders of both of them, walked backward, and covered their father's nakedness. With their faces turned backward, they did not see their father's nakedness.

**9:24** Then Noah woke from his wine and learned what his youngest son had done to him.

**9:25** He said, "Cursed be Canaan; a slave of slaves he will be to his brothers."

**9:26** And he said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Shem, and let Canaan be a slave to him.

**9:27** May God grant wide space to Japheth, and may he dwell in the tents of Shem, and let Canaan be a slave to him."

**9:28** And Noah lived after the flood three hundred fifty years.

**9:29** Thus all the days of Noah came to nine hundred fifty years, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — Hebrew "et-dimkhem lenafshoteikhem" literally means "your blood belonging to your lives" (that is, lifeblood). The idiom "miyyad ish achiv" (literally "from the hand of a man, his brother") denotes accountability required from any fellow human being.
- **9:14** — Hebrew "be'aneni anan" uses a Piel infinitive construct of the denominative verb "anan" with its cognate noun ("when I cloud a cloud"), meaning "when I gather a cloud."
- **9:20** — The Hebrew wording "vayyachel Noach ish ha'adamah vayyitta karem" (using the Hiphil of "chalal," "begin") is literally "Noah began, a man of the soil, and planted a vineyard." It can be understood either as "began as a man of the soil" or, taking the two verbs together, as "Noah, a man of the soil, was the first to plant a vineyard."
- **9:21** — The verb "vayyitgal" is a Hithpael reflexive form of "galah" ("uncover"), hence "uncovered himself." The final word "oholo" ("his tent") displays the archaic Masoretic spelling with final he ("-oh") rather than vav ("-o").
- **9:27** — The Hiphil jussive "yaft" (from "patah," "be wide" or "spacious") creates a wordplay on the name "Yefet" (Japheth). The subject of "veyishkon" ("may he dwell") is grammatically ambiguous and could refer either to Japheth or to God; likewise the poetic suffix in "lamo" (here and in v. 26) can mean either "to him" or "to them."
