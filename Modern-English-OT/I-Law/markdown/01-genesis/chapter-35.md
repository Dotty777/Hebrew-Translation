## Text

**35:1** Then God said to Jacob, "Rise, go up to Bethel and settle there, and make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you were fleeing from Esau your brother."

**35:2** Then Jacob said to his household and to all those with him, "Remove the foreign gods that are in your midst, and purify yourselves and change your clothes.

**35:3** Let us rise and go up to Bethel, that I may make an altar there to the God who answered me in the day of my distress and has been with me on the way that I have gone."

**35:4** So they gave Jacob all the foreign gods that were in their hands, together with the rings that were in their ears; and Jacob hid them beneath the terebinth that was by Shechem.

**35:5** Then they set out, and a terror from God was upon the cities around them, so they did not pursue Jacob's sons.

**35:6** Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan—that is, Bethel—he and all the people who were with him.

**35:7** There he built an altar and called the place El-bethel, because God had revealed Himself to him there when he was fleeing from his brother.

**35:8** And Deborah, the nurse of Rebekah, died and was buried below Bethel, beneath the oak; and he called its name Allon-bacuth.

**35:9** God appeared once more to Jacob when he came from Paddan-aram, and blessed him.

**35:10** And God said to him, "Your name is Jacob; your name will not be called Jacob any longer; rather, Israel will be your name." So He called his name Israel.

**35:11** Then God said to him, "I am God Almighty; be fruitful and multiply. A nation and an assembly of nations will arise from you, and kings will come out from your loins.

**35:12** The land that I gave to Abraham and to Isaac, to you I will give it; and to your seed after you I will give the land."

**35:13** And God ascended from him at the place where He had spoken with him.

**35:14** Then Jacob set up a pillar at the place where He had spoken with him, a pillar of stone; he poured a drink offering upon it and poured oil upon it.

**35:15** And Jacob called the place where God had spoken with him by the name Bethel.

**35:16** Then they set out from Bethel; while there was still some distance to reach Ephrath, Rachel went into labor, and she had severe difficulty in giving birth.

**35:17** As she was having severe difficulty in giving birth, the midwife said to her, "Do not be afraid, for this one also is a son for you."

**35:18** And as her soul was leaving—for she was dying—she called his name Ben-oni, but his father called him Benjamin.

**35:19** Rachel died and was buried along the way to Ephrath—that is, Bethlehem.

**35:20** And Jacob erected a pillar upon her burial place; it is the pillar of Rachel's burial place to this day.

**35:22** Now while Israel was living in that land, Reuben went and lay with his father's concubine Bilhah, and Israel heard of it. And the sons of Jacob were twelve.

**35:23** The sons of Leah: Jacob's firstborn Reuben, and Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.

**35:24** The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.

**35:25** And the sons of Bilhah, Rachel's maidservant: Dan and Naphtali.

**35:26** And the sons of Zilpah, Leah's maidservant: Gad and Asher. These are Jacob's sons who were born to him in Paddan-aram.

**35:27** Then Jacob came to Isaac his father at Mamre, Kiriath-arba—that is, Hebron—where Abraham and Isaac had sojourned.

**35:28** And Isaac's days were one hundred and eighty years.

**35:29** Then Isaac breathed his last, died, and was gathered to his people, old and full of days; and Esau and Jacob, his sons, buried him.

## Translator Notes

- **35:7** — The verb 'revealed Himself' (niglu, Niphal perfect of galah) is grammatically plural in agreement with the plural form ha-Elohim ('God'), a rare Hebrew construction when the reference is to God (cf. Gen 20:13).
- **35:8** — 'Allon-bacuth' means 'Oak of Weeping.' Here the tree is an oak (allon), distinct from the terebinth (elah) mentioned in 35:4.
- **35:16** — The phrase rendered 'some distance' (kivrat ha-aretz, literally 'a stretch of land') refers to an uncertain ancient measure of distance.
- **35:18** — 'Ben-oni' means 'Son of my sorrow' (or 'Son of my misfortune'), whereas 'Benjamin' (Binyamin) means 'Son of the right hand.'
- **35:22** — The Masoretic text carries double cantillation and a traditional mid-verse pause immediately after 'and Israel heard of it,' breaking off abruptly before introducing the list of Jacob's twelve sons.
- **35:21 *(additional)*** — Migdal-eder means 'Tower of the Flock.'
