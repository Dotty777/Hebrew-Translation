## Text

**45:1** Then Joseph could not restrain himself before all those standing beside him, and he called out, "Send every man out from beside me!" So no man stood with him when Joseph made himself known to his brothers.

**45:2** And he let out his voice in weeping, and the Egyptians heard it, and the house of Pharaoh heard it.

**45:3** Then Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?" But his brothers were unable to answer him, for they were terrified in his presence.

**45:4** Then Joseph said to his brothers, "Please draw near to me." And they drew near. And he said, "I am Joseph your brother, whom you sold into Egypt.

**45:5** And now, do not be grieved and do not be angry with yourselves that you sold me to this place, for God sent me ahead of you for the preservation of life.

**45:6** For these two years the famine has been in the midst of the land, and there are still five years in which there will be neither plowing nor harvest.

**45:7** So God sent me ahead of you to establish for you a remnant on the earth and to keep you alive for a great deliverance.

**45:8** So now, it was not you who sent me here, but God; and He has appointed me as a father to Pharaoh, and as lord over all his house, and as ruler over all the land of Egypt.

**45:9** Hurry and go up to my father, and say to him, 'This is what your son Joseph says: God has appointed me lord over all Egypt; come down to me, do not delay.

**45:10** And you shall live in the land of Goshen, and you shall be near to me, you and your sons and your sons' sons, and your flocks and your herds and all that belongs to you.

**45:11** And I will sustain you there, for there are still five years of famine, so that you do not come to poverty, you and your household and all that belongs to you.'

**45:12** And look, your eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my own mouth that is speaking to you.

**45:13** And you shall tell my father of all my glory in Egypt and of all that you have seen, and you must hurry and bring my father down here."

**45:14** Then he fell upon the neck of Benjamin his brother and wept, and Benjamin wept on his neck.

**45:15** And he kissed all his brothers and wept over them, and after that his brothers spoke with him.

**45:16** Now the report was heard in the house of Pharaoh, saying, "Joseph's brothers have come." And it was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of his servants.

**45:17** Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Say to your brothers, 'Do this: load your beasts and go, enter the land of Canaan,

**45:18** and take your father and your households and come to me, and I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and you will eat the fat of the land.'

**45:19** Now as for you, you are commanded: 'Do this: take wagons for yourselves from the land of Egypt for your little ones and for your wives, and carry your father and come.

**45:20** And let your eye have no regret for your belongings, for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.'"

**45:21** And the sons of Israel did so; and Joseph gave them wagons according to Pharaoh's command, and he gave them provisions for the journey.

**45:22** To all of them, to each man, he gave changes of garments, but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of garments.

**45:23** And to his father he sent as follows: ten male donkeys carrying some of the best things of Egypt, and ten female donkeys carrying grain and bread and food for his father on the journey.

**45:24** So he sent his brothers off, and they departed; and he said to them, "Do not become agitated on the way."

**45:25** Then they went up from Egypt and came to the land of Canaan, to Jacob their father.

**45:26** And they told him, saying, "Joseph is still alive, and indeed he is ruler over all the land of Egypt!" And his heart grew faint, for he did not believe them.

**45:27** But when they spoke to him all the words of Joseph that he had spoken to them, and he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him, the spirit of Jacob their father revived.

**45:28** Then Israel said, "It is enough! Joseph my son is still alive. Let me go and see him before I die."

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — The infinitive construct "when Joseph made himself known" is a Hithpael form of the verb meaning "to know" that preserves its historical initial waw as a consonant.
- **45:5** — The phrase "do not be angry with yourselves" renders an idiom literally meaning "do not let it burn in your eyes", and "for the preservation of life" translates a noun cognate with the verb "to keep alive" in 45:7.
- **45:7** — The closing phrase "to keep you alive for a great deliverance" uses a Hiphil infinitive construct of the verb "to live" followed by a prepositional phrase that can denote either the result ("for a great deliverance") or the resulting group ("as a great company of survivors").
- **45:19** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads a second-person singular Pual passive ("Now as for you, you are commanded"), whereby Pharaoh authorizes Joseph to issue the directive, whereas the Septuagint renders an active imperative ("Now command them").
- **45:24** — The command "Do not become agitated" uses a Qal verb denoting trembling, agitation, or angry excitement, which in this context encompasses both anxious distress and quarreling among the brothers.
