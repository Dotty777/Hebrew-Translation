## Text

**36:1** And these are the generations of Esau; he is Edom.

**36:2** Esau took his wives from among the daughters of Canaan: Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite; and Oholibamah, daughter of Anah, daughter of Zibeon the Hivite;

**36:3** and Basemath, daughter of Ishmael, sister of Nebaioth.

**36:4** Adah gave birth to Eliphaz for Esau, and Basemath gave birth to Reuel,

**36:5** and Oholibamah gave birth to Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These are Esau's sons who were born to him in the land of Canaan.

**36:6** Then Esau took his wives, his sons, his daughters, and all the persons of his household, and his livestock, all his animals, and all his property that he had acquired in the land of Canaan, and he went to a land away from Jacob his brother.

**36:7** For their possessions were too abundant for them to live together, and the land of their sojournings was unable to sustain them because of their livestock.

**36:8** And Esau settled in the highlands of Seir; Esau is Edom.

**36:9** And these are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom, in the highlands of Seir.

**36:10** These are the names of the sons of Esau: Eliphaz, son of Adah, wife of Esau; Reuel, son of Basemath, wife of Esau.

**36:11** Now the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.

**36:12** And Timna was a concubine to Eliphaz, son of Esau, and she gave birth to Amalek for Eliphaz. These are the sons of Adah, wife of Esau.

**36:13** And these are the sons of Reuel: Nahath and Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah. These were the sons of Basemath, wife of Esau.

**36:14** And these were the sons of Oholibamah, daughter of Anah, daughter of Zibeon, wife of Esau: she gave birth to Jeush, Jalam, and Korah for Esau.

**36:15** These are the chiefs of the sons of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz, Esau's firstborn: Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zepho, Chief Kenaz,

**36:16** Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, Chief Amalek. These are the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Adah.

**36:17** And these are the sons of Reuel, son of Esau: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Shammah, Chief Mizzah. These are the chiefs of Reuel in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Basemath, wife of Esau.

**36:18** And these are the sons of Oholibamah, wife of Esau: Chief Jeush, Chief Jalam, Chief Korah. These are the chiefs of Oholibamah, daughter of Anah, wife of Esau.

**36:19** These are the sons of Esau, and these are their chiefs; he is Edom.

**36:20** These are the sons of Seir the Horite, residents of the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

**36:21** Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These are the Horite chiefs, the sons of Seir, in the land of Edom.

**36:22** Now the sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam, and the sister of Lotan was Timna.

**36:23** And these are the sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, and Ebal; Shepho and Onam.

**36:24** And these are the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. This is the Anah who found the hot springs in the wilderness while he was tending the donkeys for his father Zibeon.

**36:25** And these are the children of Anah: Dishon, and Oholibamah, daughter of Anah.

**36:26** And these are the sons of Dishan: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**36:27** These are Ezer's sons: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.

**36:28** These are Dishan's sons: Uz and Aran.

**36:29** These are the Horite chiefs: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah,

**36:30** Chief Dishon, Chief Ezer, Chief Dishan. These are the Horite chiefs, according to their chiefdoms, in the land of Seir.

**36:31** And these are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before a king reigned over the sons of Israel:

**36:32** Now Bela, son of Beor, reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**36:33** And Bela died, and Jobab, son of Zerah, from Bozrah, reigned in his place.

**36:34** And Jobab died, and Husham, from the land of the Temanites, reigned in his place.

**36:35** And Husham died, and Hadad, son of Bedad, who struck down Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith.

**36:36** And Hadad died, and Samlah, from Masrekah, reigned in his place.

**36:37** And Samlah died, and Shaul, from Rehoboth by the river, reigned in his place.

**36:38** And Shaul died, and Baal-hanan, son of Achbor, reigned in his place.

**36:39** And Baal-hanan, son of Achbor, died, and Hadar reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Pau, and the name of his wife was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezahab.

**36:40** And these are the names of the chiefs of Esau, according to their families, according to their places, by their names: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,

**36:41** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,

**36:42** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,

**36:43** Chief Magdiel, Chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom, according to their settlements in the land of their possession. He is Esau, the father of Edom.

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — Hebrew bat ('daughter') here designates Oholibamah as the granddaughter of Zibeon (cf. vv. 24-25; similarly in v. 14), and the Masoretic text calls Zibeon 'the Hivite' rather than 'the Horite' (cf. vv. 20-21).
- **36:5** — The Qere (read form) is Jeush, matching v. 18, while the Ketiv (written consonants) reads Jeish; the same Ketiv/Qere variant occurs in v. 14.
- **36:15** — Hebrew alluf, related to elef ('thousand' or 'clan'), denotes the leader of a tribal clan; in v. 30 the plural phrase le-allufeihem literally means 'according to their chiefs' (that is, chief by chief or according to their chiefdoms).
- **36:24** — Hebrew ha-yemim occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; its meaning is uncertain, though 'hot springs' is the prevailing scholarly understanding (ancient versions also suggest 'water' or 'mules').
- **36:26** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads Dishan here (identical to v. 28), whereas 1 Chronicles 1:41 and the Samaritan Pentateuch read Dishon, matching the fifth son of Seir in v. 21.
