## Text

**29:1** Then Jacob lifted his feet and went to the land of the sons of the east.

**29:2** He looked, and there was a well in the field, and there three flocks of sheep were lying beside it, because from that well they would water the flocks; and the stone over the mouth of the well was large.

**29:3** All the flocks would be gathered there, and they would roll the stone away from the mouth of the well, water the sheep, and return the stone over the mouth of the well to its place.

**29:4** Then Jacob said to them, "My brothers, where are you from?" They answered, "We are from Haran."

**29:5** He asked them, "Do you know Laban, son of Nahor?" They replied, "We know him."

**29:6** And he said to them, "Is it well with him?" They answered, "It is well, and look, his daughter Rachel is coming with the sheep."

**29:7** Then he said, "Look, the day is still high; it is not the time for the livestock to be gathered. Water the sheep, and go graze them."

**29:8** They replied, "We cannot until all the flocks have been gathered and they roll the stone away from the mouth of the well; then we will water the sheep."

**29:9** While he was still talking with them, Rachel arrived with the sheep that belonged to her father, because she was a shepherdess.

**29:10** Now when Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and the sheep of Laban his mother's brother, Jacob stepped forward and rolled the stone away from the mouth of the well, and he watered the sheep of Laban his mother's brother.

**29:11** Jacob kissed Rachel, and he lifted his voice and wept.

**29:12** Then Jacob told Rachel that he was her father's relative and that he was the son of Rebekah, so she ran and told her father.

**29:13** When Laban heard the report about Jacob, his sister's son, he ran to meet him, embraced him, kissed him, and brought him into his house. Then he recounted all these things to Laban.

**29:14** And Laban said to him, "Truly, you are my bone and my flesh." So he stayed with him a full month.

**29:15** Then Laban said to Jacob, "Just because you are my relative, should you serve me for nothing? Tell me, what should your wages be?"

**29:16** Now Laban had two daughters: the older one's name was Leah, and the younger one's name was Rachel.

**29:17** Leah's eyes were delicate, while Rachel was beautiful in figure and beautiful in appearance.

**29:18** Jacob loved Rachel, and he said, "I will serve you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter."

**29:19** Laban replied, "It is better for me to give her to you than for me to give her to another man; stay with me."

**29:20** Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and in his eyes they were like a few days because he loved her.

**29:21** Then Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, because my days are fulfilled, so that I may go in to her."

**29:22** So Laban gathered all the men of the place and made a banquet.

**29:23** When evening came, he took Leah, his daughter, and brought her to him, and he went in to her.

**29:24** Laban gave Zilpah, his female servant, to his daughter Leah as a female servant.

**29:25** When morning came, look, it was Leah! So he said to Laban, "What is this that you have done to me? Is it not for Rachel that I served with you? And why have you deceived me?"

**29:26** Laban answered, "It is not done this way in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn.

**29:27** Fulfill the week of this one, and we will give you this one as well, for the service you will serve with me—another seven years."

**29:28** Jacob did so and fulfilled the week of this one; then he gave him Rachel, his daughter, to be his wife.

**29:29** And Laban gave Bilhah, his female servant, to his daughter Rachel as her female servant.

**29:30** Then he went in to Rachel as well, and he also loved Rachel more than Leah; and he served with him another seven years.

**29:31** Now the LORD saw that Leah was hated, so He opened her womb, while Rachel was barren.

**29:32** Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she called his name Reuben, for she said, "Because the LORD has seen my affliction, now my husband will surely love me."

**29:33** She conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she said, "Because the LORD has heard that I am hated, He has given me this one as well." So she called his name Simeon.

**29:34** She conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she said, "Now this time my husband will be joined to me, because I have given birth to three sons for him." For this reason his name was called Levi.

**29:35** She conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she said, "This time I will give thanks to the LORD." For this reason she called his name Judah. Then she stopped giving birth.

## Translator Notes

- **29:12** — Hebrew literally reads "her father's brother," using the word for brother in the wider sense of a close male relative (as also in 29:15), since Jacob was Rebekah's son and thus Laban's nephew.
- **29:17** — The Hebrew adjective rendered "delicate" literally means soft or tender; applied to eyes only here in the Hebrew Bible, it is variously understood as describing weak, dull eyes or soft, gentle eyes.
- **29:27** — The Hebrew verb form can be parsed either as Qal first-person plural cohortative ("and we will give"), matching the direct object marker and Laban's "our place" in 29:26, or as Niphal third-person feminine singular ("and this one also will be given").
- **29:31** — The Qal passive participle "hated" (also in 29:33) is used in Hebrew idiomatic contrast with "loved" (29:30) to denote being unloved or less favored.
- **29:34** — Unlike 29:35 (third-person feminine singular, "she called"), the Masoretic text here has third-person masculine singular, functioning either as an indefinite subject ("his name was called") or referring to Jacob ("he called").
