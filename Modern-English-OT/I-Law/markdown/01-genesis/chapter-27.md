## Text

**27:1** And it happened when Isaac had grown old and his eyes became too dim to see, that he called Esau, his older son, and said to him, "My son." And he said to him, "Here I am."

**27:2** And he said, "Look now, I have grown old; I do not know the day of my death.

**27:3** Now then, please take your equipment, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field and hunt game for me,

**27:4** and make savory dishes for me, just as I love, and bring them to me so that I may eat, in order that my soul may bless you before I die."

**27:5** Now Rebekah was listening as Isaac spoke to Esau his son. So Esau went to the field to hunt game to bring back.

**27:6** Then Rebekah said to Jacob her son, "Look, I heard your father speaking to Esau your brother, saying,

**27:7** 'Bring me game and make savory dishes for me so that I may eat and bless you before the LORD before my death.'

**27:8** So now, my son, listen to my voice in what I am commanding you.

**27:9** Please go to the flock and take for me from there two good young goats, and I will make them into savory dishes for your father, just as he loves.

**27:10** Then you will bring them to your father and he will eat, in order that he may bless you before his death."

**27:11** And Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Look, Esau my brother is a hairy man, while I am a smooth man.

**27:12** Perhaps my father will feel me, and I will be in his eyes like a mocker, and I will bring a curse upon myself and not a blessing."

**27:13** And his mother said to him, "Let your curse be upon me, my son; only listen to my voice, and go, take them for me."

**27:14** So he went and took them and brought them to his mother, and his mother made savory dishes, just as his father loved.

**27:15** Then Rebekah took the finest garments of Esau, her older son, which were with her in the house, and dressed Jacob, her younger son, in them.

**27:16** And she put the skins of the young goats over his hands and over the smoothness of his neck.

**27:17** Then she placed the savory dishes and the bread that she had made into the hand of Jacob, her son.

**27:18** He came to his father and said, "My father." And he said, "Here I am; who are you, my son?"

**27:19** Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau, your firstborn. I have done just as you spoke to me. Please rise, sit up, and eat from my game, in order that your soul may bless me."

**27:20** Then Isaac said to his son, "How have you been so quick to find it, my son?" And he said, "Because the LORD your God caused it to happen before me."

**27:21** And Isaac said to Jacob, "Come near, please, and let me feel you, my son, whether you are indeed my son Esau or not."

**27:22** So Jacob came near to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said, "The voice is Jacob's voice, yet the hands are Esau's hands."

**27:23** And he did not recognize him, for his hands were hairy like the hands of Esau his brother; so he blessed him.

**27:24** And he said, "Are you indeed my son Esau?" And he said, "I am."

**27:25** Then he said, "Bring it near to me that I may eat from my son's game, so that my soul may bless you." So he brought it near to him, and he ate; and he brought him wine, and he drank.

**27:26** Then Isaac his father said to him, "Come near, please, and kiss me, my son."

**27:27** So he came near and kissed him. And he smelled the scent of his garments and blessed him, and said, "See, the scent of my son is like the scent of a field that the LORD has blessed.

**27:28** May God give you from the dew of heaven and from the richness of the earth, and plenty of grain and new wine.

**27:29** May peoples serve you, and may nations bow down to you. Be master over your brothers, and may your mother's sons bow down to you. Let those who curse you be cursed, and let those who bless you be blessed."

**27:30** And it happened, right when Isaac finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had barely gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, that Esau his brother came in from his hunt.

**27:31** He also made savory dishes and brought them to his father, and he said to his father, "Let my father rise and eat from his son's game, in order that your soul may bless me."

**27:32** Then Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" And he said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau."

**27:33** Then Isaac trembled with an exceedingly great trembling and said, "Who then is the one who hunted game and brought it to me, so that I ate of all of it before you came, and I blessed him? Yes, and blessed he will be!"

**27:34** When Esau heard the words of his father, he cried out with an exceedingly great and bitter cry, and said to his father, "Bless me, even me, my father!"

**27:35** And he said, "Your brother came with deceit, and he took your blessing."

**27:36** And he said, "Is he not rightly named Jacob, for he has tripped me up these two times? My birthright he took, and look, now he has taken my blessing!" Then he said, "Have you not set aside a blessing for me?"

**27:37** Isaac answered and said to Esau, "Look, I have set him as master over you, and all his brothers I have given to him as servants, and with grain and new wine I have sustained him. So for you, then, what can I do, my son?"

**27:38** Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me, even me, my father!" And Esau lifted up his voice and wept.

**27:39** Then Isaac his father answered and said to him, "Look, apart from the richness of the earth your dwelling will be, and apart from the dew of heaven from above.

**27:40** By your sword you will live, and your brother you will serve; yet it will happen when you grow restless that you will tear his yoke off your neck."

**27:41** So Esau harbored animosity against Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him. And Esau said in his heart, "The days of mourning for my father are drawing near; then I will kill Jacob my brother."

**27:42** Now the words of Esau, her older son, were reported to Rebekah. So she sent and called for Jacob, her younger son, and said to him, "Look, Esau your brother is comforting himself regarding you, intending to kill you.

**27:43** So now, my son, listen to my voice: rise, flee to Laban my brother, to Haran,

**27:44** and stay with him a few days, until your brother's fury turns away,

**27:45** until your brother's anger turns away from you and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send and fetch you from there. Why should I be bereaved of both of you in a single day?"

**27:46** Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I loathe my life because of the daughters of Heth. If Jacob takes a wife from the daughters of Heth, like these, from the daughters of the land, what is life to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — Ketiv reads a feminine form or "provisions," while qere reads the standard masculine noun "game," matching the rest of the chapter. The noun rendered "quiver" occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and derives from a root meaning "to hang."
- **27:29** — Ketiv writes a singular (or defective plural) verb for "bow down," while qere reads the explicit plural matching the plural subject "nations."
- **27:36** — Esau plays on the name Jacob (Ya'aqov) and the Qal verb 'aqav ("to grasp the heel, trip up, supplant"), echoing Jacob's naming at birth in Genesis 25:26.
- **27:39** — The Hebrew preposition min ("from") is deliberately ambiguous here, allowing either the privative sense "apart from" or "away from" (contrasting with Jacob's blessing in v. 28) or the partitive sense "of" or "from" (echoing v. 28).
- **27:40** — The rare Hiphil verb tarid (from rud) means "grow restless" or "roam restlessly," and the Qal verb paraqta literally means "tear off" or "wrench off."
