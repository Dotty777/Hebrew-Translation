## Text

**23:1** Now Sarah's life was one hundred twenty-seven years, the years of Sarah's life.

**23:2** And Sarah died in Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan; and Abraham came to mourn for Sarah and to weep over her.

**23:3** Then Abraham stood up from beside his dead and spoke to the sons of Heth, saying,

**23:4** "I am a foreigner and a settler among you. Give me a holding for a burial place among you, so that I may bury my dead out of my presence."

**23:5** And the sons of Heth replied to Abraham, saying to him,

**23:6** "Listen to us, my lord: you are a prince of God in our midst. In the finest of our burial places, bury your dead. No man among us will withhold his burial place from you to keep you from burying your dead."

**23:7** Then Abraham stood up and bowed low to the people of the land, to the sons of Heth.

**23:8** And he spoke with them, saying, "If it is your desire that I bury my dead out of my presence, listen to me and appeal to Ephron the son of Zohar for me,

**23:9** so that he may give me the cave of Machpelah that belongs to him, which is at the edge of his field. For its full price let him give it to me in your midst as a holding for a burial place."

**23:10** Now Ephron was sitting in the midst of the sons of Heth; and Ephron the Hittite replied to Abraham in the hearing of the sons of Heth, before all who entered the gate of his city, saying,

**23:11** "No, my lord, listen to me: the field I give to you, and the cave that is in it, to you I give it. In the sight of the sons of my people I give it to you; bury your dead."

**23:12** Then Abraham bowed low before the people of the land.

**23:13** And he spoke to Ephron in the hearing of the people of the land, saying, "Only, if you will, please listen to me: I give the silver for the field; take it from me, so that I may bury my dead there."

**23:14** And Ephron replied to Abraham, saying to him,

**23:15** "My lord, listen to me: land valued at four hundred shekels of silver, between me and you, what is it? Now bury your dead."

**23:16** So Abraham listened to Ephron, and Abraham weighed out to Ephron the silver that he had spoken of in the hearing of the sons of Heth, four hundred shekels of silver, current with the merchant.

**23:17** So the field of Ephron that was in Machpelah, which was facing Mamre, the field and the cave that was in it, and all the trees that were in the field, which were within its entire boundary all around, stood confirmed

**23:18** to Abraham as a purchased property in the sight of the sons of Heth, among all who entered the gate of his city.

**23:19** And after this, Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave of the field of Machpelah facing Mamre, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan.

**23:20** Thus the field and the cave that was in it stood confirmed to Abraham as a holding for a burial place from the sons of Heth.

## Translator Notes

- **23:6** — The phrase "prince of God" (nesi' 'elohim) is translated literally, though in Hebrew it can also serve as a superlative idiom meaning "a mighty prince." The verb rendered "withhold" (yikhleh) follows III-He spelling and inflection while carrying the sense of the III-Aleph root kala' ("withhold, restrain").
- **23:11** — The three occurrences of the Qal perfect of natan ("I give") function as performative perfects, effecting a formal offer or legal declaration in the act of speaking (likewise in 23:13).
- **23:13** — The Hebrew opening (literally "only if you, would that, listen to me") combines an elliptical conditional ("if you [will]") with the optative particle lu uniquely placed before the imperative "listen to me" to express urgent politeness.
- **23:16** — The commercial idiom "current with the merchant" (literally "passing to the merchant," with singular collective soher) refers to weighed silver of standard purity and value accepted in trade.
- **23:17** — The Qal verb wayyaqom ("stood confirmed," literally "arose/stood," repeated in 23:20) is a legal term for property becoming validly transferred and permanently established in a buyer's ownership.
