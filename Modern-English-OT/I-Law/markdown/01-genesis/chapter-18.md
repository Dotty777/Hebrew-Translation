## Text

**18:1** Then the LORD appeared to him at the oaks of Mamre while he was sitting at the entrance of the tent during the heat of the day.

**18:2** He raised his eyes and looked, and see, three men were standing near him. When he saw them, he ran to meet them from the entrance of the tent and bowed down to the ground.

**18:3** And he said, "Lord, if now I have found favor in Your eyes, please do not go on past Your servant.

**18:4** Let a little water please be fetched, and wash your feet, and recline beneath the tree.

**18:5** And let me fetch a morsel of bread, and sustain your hearts; afterward you may pass on, since you have passed by your servant." And they said, "Do so, just as you have spoken."

**18:6** Abraham then hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Hurry! Three seahs of choice flour—knead it and make bread cakes."

**18:7** And to the herd Abraham ran; he took a calf that was tender and fine, and gave it to the young attendant, and he hurried to prepare it.

**18:8** Then he brought curds and milk, along with the calf that he had prepared, and set them before them; and he was standing beside them beneath the tree while they ate.

**18:9** Then they said to him, "Where is your wife Sarah?" And he said, "Right there in the tent."

**18:10** And He said, "I will certainly return to you when the season returns, and look, your wife Sarah will have a son." Now Sarah was listening at the entrance of the tent, which was behind Him.

**18:11** Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in days, and the way of women had ceased for Sarah.

**18:12** So Sarah laughed within herself, saying, "After I have grown worn out, am I to have pleasure, when my lord is old?"

**18:13** Then the LORD said to Abraham, "Why is it that Sarah laughed, saying, 'Will I truly give birth, when I have grown old?'

**18:14** Is any matter too extraordinary for the LORD? At the set time I will return to you, when the season returns, and Sarah will have a son."

**18:15** Then Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," because she was afraid. Yet He said, "No, indeed, you did laugh."

**18:16** Then the men got up from there and looked down over Sodom, while Abraham was walking with them to see them off.

**18:17** And the LORD said, "Am I to conceal from Abraham what I am about to do,

**18:18** since Abraham is certain to become a great and powerful nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him?

**18:19** For I have known him so that he may charge his children and his household after him, and they will keep the way of the LORD by practicing righteousness and justice, in order that the LORD may bring upon Abraham what He has spoken concerning him."

**18:20** Then the LORD said, "The outcry concerning Sodom and Gomorrah is truly great, and their sin is truly very grave.

**18:21** Let Me go down now and see whether they have acted altogether according to its outcry that has come to Me; and if not, I will know."

**18:22** And the men turned from there and walked toward Sodom, while Abraham remained standing before the LORD.

**18:23** Then Abraham approached and said, "Will You truly sweep away the righteous along with the wicked?

**18:24** Perhaps there are fifty righteous people within the city; will You truly sweep it away and not spare the place on account of the fifty righteous who are in its midst?

**18:25** Far be it from You to do a thing like this—to put the righteous to death along with the wicked, so that the righteous becomes like the wicked! Far be it from You! Will the Judge of the whole earth not do justice?"

**18:26** And the LORD said, "If I find fifty righteous people in Sodom, within the city, then I will spare the entire place for their sake."

**18:27** Abraham answered and said, "Look now, I have taken it upon myself to speak to the Lord, even though I am dust and ashes.

**18:28** Perhaps the fifty righteous fall short by five; will You destroy the entire city on account of the five?" And He said, "If I find forty-five there, I will not destroy it."

**18:29** He spoke to Him yet once more and said, "Perhaps forty will be found there." And He said, "I will not do it for the sake of the forty."

**18:30** Then he said, "Please may the Lord not grow angry, and let me speak: perhaps thirty will be found there." And He said, "If I find thirty there, I will not do it."

**18:31** And he said, "Look now, I have taken it upon myself to speak to the Lord: perhaps twenty will be found there." And He said, "I will not destroy it for the sake of the twenty."

**18:32** Then he said, "Please may the Lord not grow angry, and let me speak just this once: perhaps ten will be found there." And He said, "I will not destroy it for the sake of the ten."

**18:33** Then the LORD departed after He had finished speaking to Abraham, while Abraham returned to his place.

## Translator Notes

- **18:3** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes the address as Adonai ("Lord," with qamets, the divine title), whereas verses 4-5 switch to plural verbs and pronouns addressing all three visitors collectively.
- **18:10** — "When the season returns" translates the Hebrew idiom ka'et hayyah (literally "at the living/reviving time," indicating about this time next year; compare 17:21 and 18:14).
- **18:19** — "I have known him" renders the Qal of yada, which in covenantal contexts conveys intimate recognition and election (compare Amos 3:2).
- **18:21** — The Hebrew phrase asu khalah is syntactically difficult; taking kalah adverbially yields "acted altogether" according to the outcry, though elsewhere the phrase means "make a complete end" and a Masoretic paseq divides the two words.
- **18:22** — Traditional rabbinic lists include this clause ("Abraham remained standing before the LORD") among the scribal emendations (tiqqune soferim) for an original wording in which the LORD stood before Abraham; this translation follows the Masoretic Text verbatim.
