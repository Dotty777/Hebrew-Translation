## Text

**8:1** Then God remembered Noah and every wild animal and all the livestock that were with him in the ark, and God made a wind pass over the earth, and the waters subsided.

**8:2** And the springs of the deep and the windows of the heavens were shut off, and the rain from the heavens was held back.

**8:3** And the waters steadily receded from the earth, and the waters diminished at the end of one hundred and fifty days.

**8:4** And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.

**8:5** And the waters kept diminishing until the tenth month; in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.

**8:6** And it happened at the end of forty days that Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made.

**8:7** And he sent out the raven, and it went out, going back and forth until the waters dried up from the earth.

**8:8** Then he sent out the dove from him to see whether the waters had abated from the surface of the ground.

**8:9** But the dove found no resting place for the sole of its foot, and it returned to him into the ark, for water was on the surface of the entire earth; so he reached out his hand and took it, and brought it to himself into the ark.

**8:10** And he waited yet another seven days, and again he sent out the dove from the ark.

**8:11** And the dove came to him at evening time, and look, a freshly plucked olive leaf was in its beak; so Noah knew that the waters had abated from the earth.

**8:12** Then he waited yet another seven days and sent out the dove, and it did not return to him again.

**8:13** And it happened in the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the waters had dried off the earth; and Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and see, the surface of the ground had dried.

**8:14** And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth had become dry.

**8:15** And God spoke to Noah, saying,

**8:16** "Go out from the ark, you, and your wife, and your sons, and the wives of your sons with you.

**8:17** Bring out with you every living creature that is with you from all flesh, birds and animals and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, so that they may swarm on the earth and be fruitful and multiply upon the earth."

**8:18** Then Noah came out, and his sons and his wife and the wives of his sons with him.

**8:19** Every living creature, every creeping thing, and every bird, everything that moves upon the earth, came out from the ark according to their families.

**8:20** And Noah built an altar to the LORD, and he took from every clean animal and from every clean bird and offered up burnt offerings on the altar.

**8:21** And the LORD smelled the soothing fragrance, and the LORD said to His heart, "I will not again curse the ground on account of man, for the inclination of man's heart is evil from his youth, nor will I again strike down every living thing as I have done.

**8:22** Throughout all the remaining days of the earth, sowing and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night will not cease."

## Translator Notes

- **8:13** — Verse 13 uses charav ('dried off,' free of standing water), whereas verse 14 uses yavesh ('become dry,' thoroughly desiccated), distinguishing two stages of drying.
- **8:17** — Ketiv reads hotse' (standard contracted Hiphil imperative of yatsa'); Qere reads haytse' (retaining the initial radical yod in the Hiphil imperative). Both forms mean 'bring out.'
- **8:21** — 'Soothing fragrance' (reach hannichoach) derives from the root nuach ('to rest'), echoing Noah's name (Noach), the ark coming to rest (wattanach, 8:4), and the dove's 'resting place' (manoach, 8:9).
