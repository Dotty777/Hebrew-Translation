## Text

**19:1** The two angels reached Sodom in the evening, while Lot was sitting in the gate of Sodom. Lot saw them, got up to meet them, and bowed down, his face to the ground.

**19:2** He said, "Now look, my lords, please turn aside to your servant's house, spend the night, and wash your feet; then you may rise early and go on your way." They replied, "No, instead we will spend the night in the open square."

**19:3** Yet he pressed them very strongly, so they turned aside to him and entered his house. He prepared a feast for them and baked unleavened bread, and they ate.

**19:4** Before they lay down, the men of the city, the men of Sodom, encircled the house, from youth to old man, all the people from every quarter.

**19:5** They called out to Lot and said to him, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so that we may know them."

**19:6** Lot went out to them into the doorway, and he closed the door behind him.

**19:7** He said, "Please, my brothers, do not act wickedly.

**19:8** Look now, I have two daughters who have not known a man. Please let me bring them out to you, and do to them according to what is good in your eyes. Only do not do anything to these men, since they have come under the shadow of my roof."

**19:9** But they said, "Step back!" And they said, "This one came to reside as a foreigner, yet he keeps acting as judge! Now we will do worse to you than to them." So they pressed very hard against the man, against Lot, and stepped forward to break down the door.

**19:10** Then the men stretched out their hands, brought Lot to themselves inside the house, and closed the door.

**19:11** And they struck the men who were at the doorway of the house with blindness, from small to great, so that they grew weary trying to find the doorway.

**19:12** Then the men said to Lot, "Who else do you have here? A son-in-law, and your sons, and your daughters, and all that you have in the city, bring them out of the place,

**19:13** for we are going to destroy this place, because the outcry against them has grown great before the LORD, and the LORD has sent us to destroy it."

**19:14** So Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, who were marrying his daughters, and said, "Get up, go out from this place, for the LORD is going to destroy the city." Yet he seemed like someone joking in the eyes of his sons-in-law.

**19:15** As dawn rose, the angels hurried Lot, saying, "Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here, or else you will be swept away in the city's punishment."

**19:16** Yet he lingered; so the men took hold of his hand, his wife's hand, and the hands of his two daughters, because of the LORD's compassion on him, and they brought him out and placed him outside the city.

**19:17** When they had brought them outside, he said, "Escape for your life! Do not look behind you, and do not stop anywhere in the plain. Escape to the hill country, or else you will be swept away."

**19:18** But Lot said to them, "No, please, my lord!

**19:19** Look now, your servant has found favor in your eyes, and you have magnified your loyal love that you have shown to me in preserving my life; yet I cannot escape to the hill country, or else the disaster will overtake me and I will die.

**19:20** See now, this city is near enough to flee there, and it is a small one. Please let me escape there! Is it not a small one? Then my soul will live."

**19:21** He said to him, "Look, I grant you favor in this matter as well, so that I will not overthrow the city of which you have spoken.

**19:22** Hurry, escape there, because I cannot do a thing until you get there." For that reason, the name of the city was called Zoar.

**19:23** The sun had come up over the land as Lot entered Zoar.

**19:24** Then the LORD rained down sulfur and fire on Sodom and on Gomorrah, from the LORD out of the heavens.

**19:25** He overthrew those cities, the entire plain, all the inhabitants of the cities, and the vegetation of the ground.

**19:26** His wife, however, looked back from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt.

**19:27** Abraham rose early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before the LORD.

**19:28** He gazed down toward Sodom and Gomorrah and over the entire face of the land of the plain, and he looked, and there the smoke of the land was going up like the smoke of a furnace.

**19:29** So when God destroyed the cities of the plain, God remembered Abraham and sent Lot out from the middle of the overthrow when He overthrew the cities where Lot had lived.

**19:30** Then Lot went up from Zoar and lived in the hill country, and his two daughters were with him, because he was afraid to live in Zoar. So he lived in a cave, he and his two daughters.

**19:31** The firstborn said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man on the earth to come in to us according to the way of all the earth.

**19:32** Come, let us give our father wine to drink and let us lie with him, so that we may preserve seed from our father."

**19:33** So they gave their father wine to drink that night, and the firstborn came in and lay with her father, and he did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

**19:34** The next day the firstborn said to the younger, "Look, I lay with my father last night; let us give him wine to drink tonight as well, and you go in and lie with him, so that we may preserve seed from our father."

**19:35** So they gave their father wine to drink that night as well, and the younger rose and lay with him; and he did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

**19:36** So the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.

**19:37** The firstborn gave birth to a son and called his name Moab; he is the father of Moab to this day.

**19:38** And the younger, she also gave birth to a son and called his name Ben-ammi; he is the father of the sons of Ammon to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **19:9** — The post-positive Qal infinitive absolute in 'keeps acting as judge' (wayyishpot shafot) emphasizes continual or persistent action. The verse also plays on the root ngsh ('step back' ... 'stepped forward').
- **19:11** — The rare plural noun sanwerim ('blindness', occurring only here and 2 Kings 6:18) appears to denote a sudden, disorienting blindness rather than permanent physical loss of sight.
- **19:18** — Although Lot addresses both angels ('to them'), the pausal form Adonai is followed by singular verbs and pronouns in v. 19; it may be understood as honorific singular 'my lord' (addressing the spokesman of v. 17), pausal plural 'my lords', or the divine title 'Lord'.
- **19:21** — Literally 'I have lifted your face', a Hebrew idiom for showing favor or granting a request. In v. 22, the name Zoar (Tsoar) echoes Lot's repeated word 'a small one' (mitsar) in v. 20.
- **19:33** — The Masoretic Text places a scribal dot (punctum extraordinarium) above the letter vav in 'when she got up' (uvequmah), traditionally marking a textual or interpretive question on the form (contrast the shorter spelling without vav in v. 35).
