## Text

**49:1** Then Jacob called for his sons and said, "Assemble yourselves, so that I may declare to you what will happen to you in the latter days.

**49:2** Gather together and listen, sons of Jacob, and listen to Israel your father.

**49:3** Reuben, you are my firstborn, my strength and the beginning of my vigor, excelling in dignity and excelling in might.

**49:4** Turbulent as water, you shall not excel, because you went up onto your father's bed; then you defiled it—he went up onto my couch!

**49:5** Simeon and Levi are brothers; instruments of violence are their blades.

**49:6** Into their council let my soul not enter; with their assembly let my honor not be joined, for in their anger they killed men, and at their whim they hamstrung oxen.

**49:7** Cursed is their anger, for it is ferocious, and their fury, for it is harsh; I will divide them among Jacob and scatter them throughout Israel.

**49:8** Judah, you are the one your brothers will praise; your hand will be on the nape of your enemies' necks; the sons of your father will bow down to you.

**49:9** A lion's cub is Judah; from the prey, my son, you have come up. He crouched, he lay down like a lion, and like a lioness—who will rouse him?

**49:10** A scepter will not depart from Judah, nor a commander's staff from between his feet, until Shiloh comes, and to him will belong the obedience of peoples.

**49:11** He tethers his young donkey to the vine, and the colt of his donkey to the choice vine; he washes his garment in wine, and his cloak in the blood of grapes.

**49:12** Darker are his eyes than wine, and whiter are his teeth than milk.

**49:13** Zebulun will settle by the shore of the seas; he himself will serve as a haven for ships, and his border will be toward Sidon.

**49:14** Issachar is a strong-boned donkey, lying between the sheepfolds.

**49:15** He saw that rest was good and that the land was delightful; so he bent his shoulder to bear burdens and became subject to forced labor.

**49:16** Dan will judge his people, as one of Israel's tribes.

**49:17** May Dan be a serpent along the road, a horned viper beside the path, biting the heels of a horse so that its rider falls backward.

**49:18** For Your deliverance I wait, LORD.

**49:19** Gad—a raiding band will raid him, yet he himself will raid at their heel.

**49:20** From Asher, his food will be rich, and he himself will furnish the delicacies of a king.

**49:21** Naphtali is a doe set free, one who gives words of beauty.

**49:22** A fruitful branch is Joseph, a fruitful branch beside a spring; its offshoots climb over a wall.

**49:23** Archers dealt bitterly with him, shot at him, and harried him;

**49:24** yet his bow remained steadfast, and the arms of his hands stayed nimble by the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob—from there comes the Shepherd, the Stone of Israel—

**49:25** by the God of your father, may He help you, and by the Almighty, may He bless you with blessings of the heavens above, blessings of the deep lying below, blessings of breasts and womb.

**49:26** The blessings of your father have surpassed the blessings of my progenitors, as far as the farthest limit of the eternal hills; may they be on Joseph's head, and on the crown of the head of the one set apart among his brothers.

**49:27** Benjamin is a wolf that tears; in the morning he devours prey, and at evening he apportions plunder."

**49:28** All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father spoke to them when he blessed them; he blessed them, each one according to his own blessing.

**49:29** Then he commanded them and said to them, "I am about to be gathered to my people; bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,

**49:30** in the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, facing Mamre in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought together with the field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial possession.

**49:31** There they buried Abraham and Sarah, his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah, his wife; and there I buried Leah.

**49:32** The purchase of the field and the cave that is in it was from the sons of Heth."

**49:33** After Jacob finished commanding his sons, he gathered his feet onto the bed, breathed his last, and was gathered to his people.

## Translator Notes

- **49:4** — The hapax noun pachaz denotes reckless turbulence or boiling over; the verse abruptly shifts from second person ("you defiled") to third person (alah, "he went up") as Jacob turns in indignation.
- **49:5** — The rare noun mekherotehem occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; it is most often understood as "blades" or "swords," though some scholars interpret it as "schemes" or "agreements."
- **49:10** — The Masoretic text reads the single word Shiloh, traditionally taken as a messianic title or place name; several ancient versions understand it as shelloh, "the one to whom it belongs" (cf. Ezek 21:27).
- **49:21** — Following the Masoretic vocalization ("doe" and "words of beauty"); with altered vocalization of the same consonants, some interpreters read either "that bears lovely fawns" or "a spreading terebinth that yields lovely branches."
- **49:26** — The Masoretic text vocalizes horai ("my progenitors") followed by ad-ta'avat ("as far as the limit/bounty of"); by redividing and repointing horai ad, the Septuagint and several modern versions read "the ancient mountains."
