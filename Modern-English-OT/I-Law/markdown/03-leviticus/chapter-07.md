## Text

**7:1** And this is the law of the guilt offering: it is most holy.

**7:2** At the place where they slaughter the burnt offering, they shall slaughter the guilt offering, and he shall dash its blood against the altar all around.

**7:3** And he shall present all its fat from it: the fat tail and the fat that covers the inner parts,

**7:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is at the loins; and he shall remove the lobe on the liver along with the kidneys.

**7:5** And the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar as a fire offering to the LORD; it is a guilt offering.

**7:6** Every male among the priests may eat it. It shall be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.

**7:7** As the sin offering is, so is the guilt offering; there is one law for them: it shall belong to the priest who makes atonement with it.

**7:8** And as for the priest who presents any man's burnt offering, the hide of the burnt offering which he presented shall belong to that priest himself.

**7:9** And every grain offering that is baked in an oven, and all that is made in a deep pan or on a griddle, shall belong to the priest who presents it.

**7:10** And every grain offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, shall belong to all the sons of Aaron, each one like his brother.

**7:11** And this is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings which one presents to the LORD:

**7:12** If he presents it for thanksgiving, then along with the sacrifice of thanksgiving he shall present unleavened cakes mixed with oil, unleavened wafers brushed with oil, and cakes of well-stirred fine flour mixed with oil.

**7:13** Along with cakes of leavened bread he shall present his offering, together with the thanksgiving sacrifice of his peace offerings.

**7:14** And from it he shall present one out of each offering as a contribution to the LORD; it shall belong to the priest who dashes the blood of the peace offerings.

**7:15** And the flesh of the thanksgiving sacrifice of his peace offerings shall be eaten on the day of his offering; he shall not leave any of it until morning.

**7:16** But if the sacrifice of his offering is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he presents his sacrifice, and on the next day what remains of it may also be eaten.

**7:17** Yet what remains of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burned in the fire.

**7:18** And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings is eaten at all on the third day, it shall not be accepted; it shall not be credited to the one who presents it. It shall be a foul thing, and the person who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.

**7:19** And the flesh that touches anything unclean shall not be eaten; it shall be burned in the fire. And as for the flesh, everyone who is clean may eat flesh.

**7:20** But the person who eats flesh from the sacrifice of peace offerings that belongs to the LORD while his uncleanness is upon him, that person shall be cut off from his people.

**7:21** And when a person touches anything unclean, whether human uncleanness, or an unclean animal, or any unclean detestable thing, and eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings that belongs to the LORD, that person shall be cut off from his people.

**7:22** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**7:23** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'You shall not eat any fat of ox, sheep, or goat.

**7:24** And the fat of an animal that dies naturally and the fat of one torn to pieces may be used for any work, but you shall not eat it at all.

**7:25** For whoever eats fat from an animal from which one presents a fire offering to the LORD, the person who eats it shall be cut off from his people.

**7:26** And you shall not eat any blood in any of your dwelling places, whether of bird or of animal.

**7:27** Every person who eats any blood, that person shall be cut off from his people.'"

**7:28** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**7:29** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'The one who presents the sacrifice of his peace offerings to the LORD shall bring his offering to the LORD from the sacrifice of his peace offerings.

**7:30** His own hands shall bring the fire offerings of the LORD; he shall bring the fat with the breast, so as to wave the breast as a wave offering before the LORD.

**7:31** And the priest shall turn the fat into smoke on the altar, but the breast shall belong to Aaron and to his sons.

**7:32** And you shall give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from the sacrifices of your peace offerings.

**7:33** For the one among the sons of Aaron who presents the blood of the peace offerings and the fat, the right thigh shall be his as a portion.

**7:34** For I have taken the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution from the sons of Israel, from the sacrifices of their peace offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as a permanent statute from the sons of Israel.'"

**7:35** This is the anointing portion of Aaron and the anointing portion of his sons from the fire offerings of the LORD, on the day they were presented to serve as priests to the LORD,

**7:36** which the LORD commanded to be given to them from the sons of Israel on the day He anointed them, a permanent statute throughout their generations.

**7:37** This is the law for the burnt offering, for the grain offering, for the sin offering, for the guilt offering, for the ordination, and for the sacrifice of peace offerings,

**7:38** which the LORD commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day He commanded the sons of Israel to present their offerings to the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai.

## Translator Notes

- **7:18** — Hebrew piggul is a rare cultic term for sacrificial flesh rendered ritually foul and unacceptable, distinct from sheqets, detestable thing, in 7:21.
- **7:35** — Hebrew mishhat, construct of mishhah, denotes an allotted or anointing portion and plays on the verb His anointing in 7:36; the subjectless third-person masculine singular Hiphil hiqriv is translated impersonally as they were presented.
- **7:37** — Hebrew milluim literally means fillings, referring to the priestly ordination rite of filling the hands and its accompanying sacrifices.
