## Text

**19:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**19:2** "Speak to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel and say to them, 'You are to be holy, because I, the LORD your God, am holy.

**19:3** Each of you must revere his mother and his father, and you must observe My Sabbaths; I am the LORD your God.

**19:4** Do not turn toward idols, and do not make cast-metal gods for yourselves; I am the LORD your God.

**19:5** When you offer a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD, you must sacrifice it so that it will be accepted on your behalf.

**19:6** It is to be eaten on the day of your sacrifice and on the following day, and whatever remains until the third day shall be burned in fire.

**19:7** And if it is indeed eaten on the third day, it is a foul thing; it will not be accepted.

**19:8** Whoever eats it will bear his iniquity, because he has profaned the LORD's holy thing, and that person shall be cut off from his people.

**19:9** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish reaping to the edge of your field, nor gather the gleanings of your harvest.

**19:10** And you shall not glean your vineyard, nor gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard; you shall leave them for the poor and for the resident alien. I am the LORD your God.

**19:11** You shall not steal; you shall not deceive, and you shall not lie, anyone to his fellow countryman.

**19:12** And you shall not swear in My name to a falsehood, and thus profane the name of your God; I am the LORD.

**19:13** You must not oppress your neighbor or rob him; the wages of a hired laborer must not stay overnight with you until morning.

**19:14** You shall not curse a deaf person, nor set a stumbling block before a blind person; rather, you shall fear your God. I am the LORD.

**19:15** You shall not commit injustice in judgment: you shall not favor a poor man, nor show deference to a great man; in righteousness you shall judge your fellow countryman.

**19:16** You shall not walk about as a slanderer among your people; you shall not stand against the blood of your neighbor. I am the LORD.

**19:17** You shall not hate your brother in your heart; you shall surely rebuke your fellow countryman, and not bear sin on account of him.

**19:18** You shall not take vengeance, nor hold a grudge against the sons of your people; instead, you shall love your neighbor as yourself. I am the LORD.

**19:19** You shall observe My statutes: you shall not breed two kinds of your livestock together, you shall not sow your field with two kinds of seed, and a garment of two kinds of mixed weave shall not come upon you.

**19:20** Now if a man lies with a woman in sexual relations, and she is a slave woman designated for another man, yet she has not at all been redeemed nor has freedom been granted to her, there shall be an inquiry; they shall not be put to death, because she had not been freed.

**19:21** He shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, a ram as a guilt offering.

**19:22** Then the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering before the LORD on account of his sin that he has committed, and he will be forgiven for his sin that he has committed.

**19:23** And when you enter the land and plant any kind of food tree, you shall count its fruit as uncircumcised; for three years it shall be uncircumcised for you; it shall not be eaten.

**19:24** And in the fourth year, all its fruit shall be holy, for praise to the LORD.

**19:25** Then in the fifth year you may eat its fruit, so as to increase its yield for you. I am the LORD your God.

**19:26** You must not eat anything with the blood; you must not practice divination, nor engage in fortune-telling.

**19:27** You shall not round off the edges of your head, nor disfigure the edge of your beard.

**19:28** You shall not make any gash in your flesh for the dead, nor place any tattoo mark on yourselves; I am the LORD.

**19:29** Do not profane your daughter by causing her to engage in prostitution, so that the land will not turn to prostitution and the land become filled with depravity.

**19:30** You must observe My Sabbaths and revere My sanctuary; I am the LORD.

**19:31** Do not turn toward mediums or toward spirit-diviners; do not seek to become defiled by them. I am the LORD your God.

**19:32** Before one with gray hair you shall rise, and you shall honor the presence of an old man, and you shall fear your God; I am the LORD.

**19:33** And when a resident alien lives with you in your land, you shall not mistreat him.

**19:34** The resident alien who lives with you shall be to you like a native from among you, and you shall love him as yourself, because you were resident aliens in the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.

**19:35** You shall not commit injustice in judgment: in measurement of length, in weight, or in capacity.

**19:36** You shall have honest scales, honest weights, an honest ephah, and an honest hin. I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt.

**19:37** Therefore you shall observe all My statutes and all My judgments, and carry them out; I am the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — Hebrew lirsonkem literally means "for your acceptance," referring to cultic acceptability before the LORD (matching the Niphal verb yeraseh, "it will be accepted," in v. 7) rather than mere personal inclination.
- **19:16** — Hebrew al-dam re'eka literally means "upon/against the blood of your neighbor," an idiom understood either as taking a stand against a neighbor's life (for instance, through false accusation) or as standing idly by while a neighbor's life is in mortal danger.
- **19:19** — The rare Hebrew noun sha'atnez (occurring only here and in Deut 22:11) denotes cloth of mixed weave, explained in Deut 22:11 specifically as wool and linen woven together.
- **19:20** — Hebrew biqqoret is a hapax legomenon from the root bqr ("inquire/examine"), denoting a judicial inquiry or assessment, though interpreters have also understood it as a penalty, scourging, or indemnity. Note also the emphatic construction hofdeh lo nifdatah, pairing a Hophal infinitive absolute with a Niphal perfect ("not at all been redeemed").
- **19:23** — Hebrew va'araltem orlato et-piryo literally says "you shall treat as uncircumcised its foreskin, namely its fruit," applying the metaphor of uncircumcision to forbid eating a fruit tree's produce during its first three years.
