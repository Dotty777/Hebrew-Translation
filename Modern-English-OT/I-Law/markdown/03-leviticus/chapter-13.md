## Text

**13:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**13:2** "When a person has a swelling, a rash, or a bright spot on the skin of his flesh, and it becomes an affliction of leprosy on the skin of his flesh, he shall be brought to Aaron the priest or to one of his sons, the priests.

**13:3** The priest shall examine the affliction on the skin of the flesh; if hair in the affliction has turned white and the appearance of the affliction is deeper than the skin of his flesh, it is an affliction of leprosy. When the priest examines him, he shall declare him unclean.

**13:4** But if it is a white bright spot on the skin of his flesh, and its appearance is not deeper than the skin, and its hair has not turned white, then the priest shall confine the afflicted person for seven days.

**13:5** The priest shall examine him on the seventh day, and look, the affliction has stayed unchanged in his sight, the affliction has not spread on the skin; then the priest shall confine him for seven days a second time.

**13:6** Then the priest shall examine him on the seventh day a second time, and look, the affliction has faded and the affliction has not spread on the skin; then the priest shall declare him clean. It is a rash; he shall wash his garments and be clean.

**13:7** But if the rash indeed spreads on the skin after he has shown himself to the priest for his cleansing, he shall show himself to the priest a second time.

**13:8** The priest shall examine him, and look, the rash has spread on the skin; then the priest shall declare him unclean. It is leprosy.

**13:9** When an affliction of leprosy is on a person, he shall be brought to the priest.

**13:10** The priest shall examine him, and look, there is a white swelling on the skin, and it has turned hair white, and there is a patch of raw flesh in the swelling.

**13:11** It is chronic leprosy on the skin of his flesh, and the priest shall declare him unclean. He shall not confine him, for he is unclean.

**13:12** And if the leprosy indeed breaks out on the skin, and the leprosy covers all the skin of the afflicted person, from his head to his feet, as far as the priest's eyes can see,

**13:13** then the priest shall examine him, and look, the leprosy has covered all his flesh; then he shall declare the afflicted person clean. All of him has turned white; he is clean.

**13:14** But on the day raw flesh appears on him, he shall be unclean.

**13:15** The priest shall examine the raw flesh and declare him unclean. The raw flesh is unclean; it is leprosy.

**13:16** Or if the raw flesh changes back and turns to white, then he shall come to the priest.

**13:17** The priest shall examine him, and look, the affliction has turned to white; then the priest shall declare the afflicted person clean. He is clean.

**13:18** Now when there is a boil in the flesh, on its skin, and it is healed,

**13:19** and in the place of the boil there is a white swelling or a reddish-white bright spot, he shall show himself to the priest.

**13:20** The priest shall examine it, and look, its appearance is lower than the skin and its hair has turned white; then the priest shall declare him unclean. It is an affliction of leprosy; it has broken out in the boil.

**13:21** But if the priest examines it, and look, there is no white hair in it, and it is not lower than the skin, and it is faded, then the priest shall confine him for seven days.

**13:22** And if it indeed spreads on the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is an affliction.

**13:23** But if the bright spot stays in its place and has not spread, it is the scar of the boil, and the priest shall declare him clean.

**13:24** Or when there is a burn from fire on the skin of the flesh, and the raw patch of the burn becomes a bright spot, reddish-white or white,

**13:25** then the priest shall examine it, and look, hair has turned white in the bright spot and its appearance is deeper than the skin. It is leprosy; it has broken out in the burn. So the priest shall declare him unclean; it is an affliction of leprosy.

**13:26** But if the priest examines it, and look, there is no white hair in the bright spot, and it is not lower than the skin, and it is faded, then the priest shall confine him for seven days.

**13:27** The priest shall examine him on the seventh day. If it indeed spreads on the skin, then the priest shall declare him unclean; it is an affliction of leprosy.

**13:28** But if the bright spot stays in its place, has not spread on the skin, and is faded, it is the swelling of the burn, and the priest shall declare him clean, for it is the scar of the burn.

**13:29** Now when a man or a woman has an affliction on the head or on the beard,

**13:30** then the priest shall examine the affliction, and look, its appearance is deeper than the skin, and there is fine yellowish hair in it; then the priest shall declare him unclean. It is a scall; it is leprosy of the head or of the beard.

**13:31** But if the priest examines the affliction of the scall, and look, its appearance is not deeper than the skin, and there is no black hair in it, then the priest shall confine the one afflicted with the scall for seven days.

**13:32** The priest shall examine the affliction on the seventh day, and look, the scall has not spread, and there is no yellowish hair in it, and the appearance of the scall is not deeper than the skin;

**13:33** then he shall shave himself, but he shall not shave the scall, and the priest shall confine the person with the scall for seven days a second time.

**13:34** Then the priest shall examine the scall on the seventh day, and look, the scall has not spread on the skin and its appearance is not deeper than the skin; then the priest shall declare him clean. He shall wash his garments and be clean.

**13:35** But if the scall indeed spreads on the skin after his cleansing,

**13:36** then the priest shall examine him, and look, the scall has spread on the skin; the priest need not search for the yellowish hair. He is unclean.

**13:37** But if the scall has stayed unchanged in his sight and black hair has grown in it, the scall is healed. He is clean, and the priest shall declare him clean.

**13:38** Now when a man or a woman has bright spots on the skin of their flesh, white bright spots,

**13:39** then the priest shall examine them, and look, there are faded white bright spots on the skin of their flesh; it is a pale eruption that has broken out on the skin. He is clean.

**13:40** Now when a man's head becomes bare of hair, he is bald; he is clean.

**13:41** And if his head becomes bare of hair from the border of his face, he is bald at the forehead; he is clean.

**13:42** But if there is a reddish-white affliction on the bald head or on the bald forehead, it is leprosy breaking out on his bald head or on his bald forehead.

**13:43** Then the priest shall examine him, and look, the swelling of the affliction is reddish-white on his bald head or on his bald forehead, like the appearance of leprosy on the skin of the flesh.

**13:44** He is a leprous man; he is unclean. The priest shall surely declare him unclean; his affliction is on his head.

**13:45** As for the leprous person who has the affliction, his garments shall be torn, his head shall be left unkempt, he shall cover his upper lip, and he shall call out, 'Unclean, unclean!'

**13:46** All the days that the affliction is on him, he shall be unclean. He is unclean; he shall dwell alone; outside the camp shall be his dwelling place.

**13:47** Now when a garment has an affliction of leprosy in it, whether in a wool garment or in a linen garment,

**13:48** or in warp or in weft of linen and wool, or in leather or in anything made of leather,

**13:49** if the affliction is greenish or reddish in the garment or in the leather, or in the warp or in the weft, or in any leather article, it is an affliction of leprosy, and it shall be shown to the priest.

**13:50** The priest shall examine the affliction and shut away the afflicted article for seven days.

**13:51** He shall examine the affliction on the seventh day. If the affliction has spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the weft, or in the leather, for whatever use the leather is made, the affliction is a destructive leprosy; it is unclean.

**13:52** So he shall burn the garment, or the warp or the weft, in wool or in linen, or any leather article in which the affliction is; for it is a destructive leprosy; it shall be burned in the fire.

**13:53** But if the priest examines it, and look, the affliction has not spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the weft, or in any leather article,

**13:54** then the priest shall command that they wash the item in which the affliction is, and he shall shut it away for seven days a second time.

**13:55** Then the priest shall examine it after the affliction has been washed, and look, the affliction has not changed its appearance, though the affliction has not spread; it is unclean. You shall burn it in the fire; it is a corrosion, whether on its bare back or on its bare front.

**13:56** But if the priest examines it, and look, the affliction has faded after it has been washed, then he shall tear it out of the garment or out of the leather, or out of the warp or out of the weft.

**13:57** And if it appears again in the garment, or in the warp, or in the weft, or in any leather article, it is breaking out; you shall burn in the fire the item in which the affliction is.

**13:58** But as for the garment, or the warp, or the weft, or any leather article that you wash, if the affliction departs from them, then it shall be washed a second time and be clean.

**13:59** This is the law for an affliction of leprosy in a garment of wool or of linen, or in the warp or the weft, or in any leather article, to declare it clean or to declare it unclean."

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Hebrew tsara'at (here "leprosy") encompasses a range of ritual skin, garment, and house afflictions broader than modern Hansen's disease.
- **13:4** — Hebrew literally reads "the affliction" (hannega), using abstract-for-concrete metonymy for the afflicted person; the same usage recurs in verses 12, 13, and 17.
- **13:5** — Hebrew amad be'einav literally means "stood in his eyes," indicating that the affliction appeared unchanged under the priest's observation.
- **13:10** — Hebrew michyat basar chai literally means "a reviving patch of living flesh," referring to exposed raw flesh; michyah (from chayah, "to live/revive") likewise denotes the raw regenerating area of a burn in verse 24.
- **13:30** — Hebrew neteq (from the root nataq, "to pull away/pluck") denotes a scaly scalp or beard disorder associated with hair loss, rendered "scall."
- **13:33** — Hebrew contrasts the reflexive Hithpael verb hitgallach ("shave himself") with the transitive Piel yegalleach ("shave [the scall]").
- **13:39** — Hebrew bohaq occurs only here in the Old Testament and denotes a harmless, pale skin eruption distinct from ritual leprosy.
- **13:51** — Hebrew mam'eret (Hiphil participle of ma'ar) literally means "causing pain" or "eating away," denoting an active, destructive affliction in fabric, leather, or walls (cf. 14:44).
- **13:55** — Hebrew hukkabbes is a rare Hothpaal infinitive construct ("after being washed"); the verse also reuses qarachatto and gabbachto ("its bald back or bald front," cf. vv. 42-43) metaphorically for the worn underside or topside of the fabric.
