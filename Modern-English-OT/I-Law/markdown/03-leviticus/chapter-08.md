## Text

**8:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Take Aaron and his sons with him, along with the garments, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread,

**8:3** and assemble the entire congregation at the entrance of the tent of meeting."

**8:4** Moses did just as the LORD commanded him, and the congregation assembled at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**8:5** Then Moses said to the congregation, "This is the matter the LORD has commanded to be done."

**8:6** Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.

**8:7** He put the tunic on him, wrapped the sash around him, clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him; then he wrapped the woven band of the ephod around him and secured the ephod onto him with it.

**8:8** Then he set the breastpiece on him and put the Urim and the Thummim into the breastpiece.

**8:9** He set the turban on his head, and on the turban, at the front, he set the gold plate, the sacred diadem, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:10** Moses took the anointing oil, anointed the tabernacle and everything that was in it, and consecrated them.

**8:11** He sprinkled some of it onto the altar seven times, and he anointed the altar and all its implements, along with the basin and its base, to consecrate them.

**8:12** Next he poured some of the anointing oil onto Aaron's head and anointed him to consecrate him.

**8:13** Then Moses brought Aaron's sons forward, clothed them with tunics, wrapped sashes around them, and bound caps on them, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:14** Then he led forward the bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons pressed their hands on the head of the bull for the sin offering.

**8:15** He slaughtered it, and Moses took the blood and put it with his finger on the horns of the altar all around, and purified the altar; then he poured out the blood at the base of the altar and consecrated it to make atonement for it.

**8:16** He took all the fat that was on the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses turned them into smoke on the altar.

**8:17** But the bull, along with its hide, its flesh, and its dung, he burned with fire outside the camp, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:18** Then he presented the ram for the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons pressed their hands on the head of the ram.

**8:19** He slaughtered it, and Moses dashed the blood against the altar all around.

**8:20** He cut the ram into its pieces, and Moses turned the head, the pieces, and the suet into smoke.

**8:21** He washed the inner parts and the legs with water, and Moses turned the entire ram into smoke on the altar. It was a burnt offering for a soothing fragrance; it was a fire offering to the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:22** Then he presented the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons pressed their hands on the head of the ram.

**8:23** He slaughtered it, and Moses took some of its blood and placed it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

**8:24** Next he brought Aaron's sons forward, and Moses placed some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet; then Moses dashed the blood against the altar all around.

**8:25** He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat that was on the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys along with their fat, and the right thigh.

**8:26** And from the basket of unleavened bread that was before the LORD, he took one unleavened cake, one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer, and set them upon the fat portions and upon the right thigh.

**8:27** He placed all of them upon the palms of Aaron and upon the palms of his sons, and he waved them as a wave offering before the LORD.

**8:28** Then Moses took them off their palms and turned them into smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering. They were an ordination offering for a soothing fragrance; it was a fire offering to the LORD.

**8:29** Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD; from the ram of ordination it became Moses' portion, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**8:30** Then Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar, and sprinkled it upon Aaron, upon his garments, upon his sons, and upon the garments of his sons with him; thus he consecrated Aaron, his garments, his sons, and the garments of his sons with him.

**8:31** Then Moses said to Aaron and to his sons, "Boil the flesh at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and there you shall eat it, together with the bread that is in the basket of ordination, just as I commanded, saying, 'Aaron and his sons are to eat it.'

**8:32** What is left over from the flesh and from the bread you shall burn with fire.

**8:33** You shall not go out from the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until the day the days of your ordination are fulfilled, because for seven days you will be ordained.

**8:34** Just as has been done on this day, so the LORD has commanded to be done to make atonement for you.

**8:35** You shall stay at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days and keep the LORD's charge, so that you will not die; for so I have been commanded."

**8:36** So Aaron and his sons did all the things that the LORD had commanded by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — The rare Qal verb vayye'pod (cognate with 'ephod') occurs only here and in Exodus 29:5, meaning to bind or secure the ephod in place with its woven band (cheshev).
- **8:15** — 'Purified' renders the privative Piel of chata' (vaychatte'), cognate with 'sin offering' (chatta't), denoting the ritual cleansing of the altar from sin or impurity; compare its denominative sense in 9:15.
- **8:16** — 'Turned into smoke' translates the Hiphil of qatar (hiqtir), used throughout Leviticus for aromatic altar offerings, in deliberate contrast to saraf ('burned with fire' in 8:17, 32; 9:11) for non-altar disposal.
- **8:31** — The Masoretic text vocalizes tsivveiti as active Piel ('I commanded'), in contrast to the passive Pual tsuvveiti ('I have been commanded') in 8:35.
- **8:33** — This verse plays three times on the root male' ('be full'): 'fulfilled' (Qal infinitive melo't), 'ordination' (millu'im, literally 'fillings'), and 'you will be ordained' (impersonal third-person Piel yemalle' et-yedkhem, literally 'one will fill your hand').
