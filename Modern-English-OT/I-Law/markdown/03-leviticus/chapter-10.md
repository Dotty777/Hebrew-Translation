## Text

**10:1** Aaron's sons, Nadab and Abihu, each took his firepan, placed fire inside them, laid incense over it, and presented strange fire before the LORD, which He had not commanded them.

**10:2** Fire then went out from before the LORD and devoured them, and they died before the LORD.

**10:3** Moses told Aaron, "This is what the LORD declared, saying, 'Through those who are near Me I will be treated as holy, and before all the people I will be honored.'" And Aaron remained silent.

**10:4** Moses called to Mishael and Elzaphan, sons of Uzziel, Aaron's uncle, and said to them, "Come near, carry your kinsmen away from the front of the sanctuary to outside the camp."

**10:5** So they came near and carried them in their tunics to outside the camp, just as Moses had spoken.

**10:6** Moses then said to Aaron and to his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, "Do not let the hair of your heads hang loose, and do not tear your garments, so that you will not die and He will not grow wrathful against the entire congregation; rather, your kinsmen, the entire house of Israel, will weep over the burning that the LORD has kindled.

**10:7** And do not go out from the entrance of the tent of meeting, or else you will die, because the anointing oil of the LORD is upon you." So they acted according to the word of Moses.

**10:8** Then the LORD spoke to Aaron, saying,

**10:9** "Drink neither wine nor fermented drink, you or your sons with you, when you enter the tent of meeting, so that you do not die; this is a lasting statute across your generations,

**10:10** so that you may distinguish between what is holy and what is common, and between what is unclean and what is clean,

**10:11** and instruct the sons of Israel in all the statutes that the LORD spoke to them by the hand of Moses."

**10:12** Then Moses spoke to Aaron and to his remaining sons, Eleazar and Ithamar: "Take the grain offering remaining from the fire offerings of the LORD and eat it unleavened beside the altar, because it is most holy.

**10:13** You must eat it in a holy place, because it is your allotted portion and your sons' allotted portion from the fire offerings of the LORD, for that is how I was commanded.

**10:14** As for the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution, you may eat them in a clean place—you, your sons, and your daughters with you—because they have been given as your allotted portion and your sons' allotted portion from the peace-offering sacrifices of the sons of Israel.

**10:15** The thigh of the contribution and the breast of the wave offering they must bring along with the fire offerings of the fat portions, to wave as a wave offering before the LORD; and it will belong to you and to your sons with you as a lasting allotment, just as the LORD has commanded."

**10:16** Then Moses diligently inquired about the male goat of the sin offering, and look, it had been burned up. So he grew wrathful against Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's remaining sons, saying,

**10:17** "Why did you not eat the sin offering in the place of the sanctuary, since it is most holy and He gave it to you to bear the iniquity of the congregation, to make atonement for them before the LORD?

**10:18** Look, its blood was not brought inside the sanctuary; you certainly should have eaten it in the sanctuary, just as I commanded."

**10:19** Then Aaron spoke to Moses, "Look, today they presented their sin offering and their burnt offering before the LORD, and yet things like these have happened to me. If I had eaten a sin offering today, would that have been good in the eyes of the LORD?"

**10:20** Moses heard this, and it was good in his eyes.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — The Niphal imperfect verbs eqqadesh and ekkabed carry reflexive-passive force, conveying that God will be treated as holy and honored or display His holiness and glory.
- **10:6** — The Hebrew literally says do not loosen your heads, an idiom for letting the hair hang unkempt or uncovered as a mourning custom.
- **10:18** — The Hophal passive verb huva takes the direct-object marker et before its grammatical subject, its blood, and the infinitive absolute with imperfect akhol tokhelu expresses strong unfulfilled obligation.
