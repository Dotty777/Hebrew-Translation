## Text

**2:1** "'When a person presents an offering of grain to the LORD, fine flour shall be his offering; he shall pour oil over it and place frankincense upon it.

**2:2** Then he shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests, and he shall scoop out from it his full handful of its fine flour and of its oil, along with all its frankincense; and the priest shall turn its memorial portion into smoke on the altar, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**2:3** And what remains from the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons; it is most holy from the fire offerings of the LORD.

**2:4** Now when you present an offering of grain baked in an oven, it shall be unleavened loaves of fine flour mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers brushed with oil.

**2:5** And if your offering is a grain offering on a griddle, it shall be fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened.

**2:6** Break it into morsels and pour oil over it; it is a grain offering.

**2:7** And if your offering is a grain offering from a deep pan, it shall be made of fine flour in oil.

**2:8** Then you shall bring to the LORD the grain offering that is made from these; it shall be presented to the priest, and he shall bring it near to the altar.

**2:9** And the priest shall lift out from the grain offering its memorial portion and turn it into smoke on the altar, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**2:10** And what remains from the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons; it is most holy from the fire offerings of the LORD.

**2:11** No grain offering that you present to the LORD shall be made leavened, for you shall not turn any leaven or any honey into smoke as a fire offering to the LORD.

**2:12** As an offering of firstfruits you may present them to the LORD, yet they shall not go up onto the altar for a soothing fragrance.

**2:13** And you shall season every grain offering of yours with salt, and you shall not allow the salt of the covenant of your God to be lacking from your grain offering; with every offering of yours you shall present salt.

**2:14** And if you present a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you shall present young ears of grain parched in the fire, crushed fresh grain, as the grain offering of your firstfruits.

**2:15** Then you shall put oil on it and place frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.

**2:16** And the priest shall turn its memorial portion into smoke, part of its crushed grain and part of its oil, along with all its frankincense, a fire offering to the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Hebrew qorban minchah is a construct phrase literally meaning "an offering of a tribute/gift"; in Leviticus, minchah specifically designates the grain offering, and nephesh ("soul/person") introduces any individual worshiper.
- **2:2** — Hebrew azkarah ("memorial portion," derived from zakar, "to remember") refers to the representative portion of the grain offering turned into smoke on the altar before the LORD. The preposition al ("along with") here carries an inclusive sense.
- **2:6** — Hebrew patot is a Qal infinitive absolute of patat ("break into bits") used with imperatival force, paired with its cognate plural noun pittim ("morsels").
- **2:14** — Hebrew aviv denotes fresh, young ears of ripening grain, and geres karmel denotes crushed kernels of newly ripened produce (karmel here means fresh garden or field grain, as in 23:14, not the mountain).
