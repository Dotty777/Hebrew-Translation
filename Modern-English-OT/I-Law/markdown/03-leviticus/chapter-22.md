## Text

**22:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:2** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons that they keep themselves separate from the holy things of the sons of Israel, which they dedicate to Me, so that they do not profane My holy name. I am the LORD.

**22:3** Say to them, 'Throughout your generations, whichever man among all your seed approaches the holy things that the sons of Israel dedicate to the LORD while his uncleanness is on him, that person shall be cut off from before Me. I am the LORD.

**22:4** No man from the seed of Aaron who is leprous or has a discharge shall eat of the holy things until he is clean. And whoever touches anything unclean from a dead person, or a man from whom an emission of semen goes out,

**22:5** or a man who touches any swarming creature by which he becomes unclean, or a human being by whom he becomes unclean through any uncleanness of his—

**22:6** the person who touches such a thing shall be unclean until the evening, and he shall not eat from the holy things unless he has washed his flesh in water.

**22:7** Once the sun has gone down, he shall be clean, and afterward he may eat from the holy things, for it is his food.

**22:8** He shall not eat a carcass or anything torn, so as to become unclean by it. I am the LORD.

**22:9** They shall keep My charge, so that they do not bear sin on account of it and die by it when they profane it. I am the LORD who sanctifies them.

**22:10** No outsider shall eat anything holy; a temporary resident of a priest or a hired worker shall not eat anything holy.

**22:11** Yet if a priest buys a person as a purchase with his money, that one may eat of it; and those born in his house, they may eat of his food.

**22:12** And if the daughter of a priest marries a man who is an outsider, she herself shall not eat of the contribution of the holy things.

**22:13** But if the daughter of a priest becomes a widow or divorced, and has no seed, and returns to the house of her father as in her youth, she may eat from the food of her father; yet no outsider shall eat of it.

**22:14** And if a man eats anything holy unintentionally, he shall add a fifth part to it and give the holy thing to the priest.

**22:15** They shall not profane the holy things of the sons of Israel which they contribute to the LORD,

**22:16** and so cause them to bear the iniquity of guilt when they eat their holy things, for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.'"

**22:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:18** "Speak to Aaron, to his sons, and to all the sons of Israel, and say to them, 'Any man from the house of Israel or from the resident alien in Israel who presents his offering, for any of their vows or for any of their freewill offerings that they present to the LORD as a burnt offering,

**22:19** for your acceptance it must be an unblemished male among the cattle, among the sheep, or among the goats.

**22:20** You shall not present anything that has a defect in it, for it will not be for acceptance on your behalf.

**22:21** And when a man presents a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD to fulfill a vow or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock, it must be unblemished for acceptance; no defect shall be in it.

**22:22** Anything blind, or fractured, or maimed, or having a weeping sore, or scabies, or scabs—these you shall not present to the LORD, nor shall you place a fire offering from them on the altar to the LORD.

**22:23** As for an ox or a sheep that has an elongated or stunted limb, you may prepare it as a freewill offering, but for a vow it will not be accepted.

**22:24** Anything bruised, or crushed, or torn, or cut you shall not present to the LORD, nor shall you do this in your land.

**22:25** And from the hand of a foreigner you shall not present any of these as the food of your God, for their disfigurement is in them, a defect is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.'"

**22:26** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22:27** "When an ox or a sheep or a goat is born, it shall stay seven days with its mother, and from the eighth day onward it shall be accepted as a fire-offering gift to the LORD.

**22:28** Yet as for an ox or a sheep, you shall not slaughter it and its young on one day.

**22:29** And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to the LORD, for your acceptance you shall sacrifice it.

**22:30** On that day it must be eaten; you shall not leave any of it over until morning. I am the LORD.

**22:31** You shall observe My commandments and do them. I am the LORD.

**22:32** And you shall not profane My holy name, and I shall be treated as holy in the midst of the sons of Israel; I am the LORD who sanctifies you,

**22:33** who brought you out from the land of Egypt to be God to you. I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Niphal yinnazeru (from nazar) means 'keep themselves separate' or 'abstain.' In the Masoretic clause order, the relative clause 'which they dedicate to Me' follows 'My holy name,' resuming 'the holy things of the sons of Israel.'
- **22:13** — Hebrew zera ("seed") denotes living children/offspring.
- **22:16** — The Hiphil weqatal vehissi'u (from nasa', 'cause to bear') has an ambiguous object pronoun 'otam ('them'), referring either to the Israelites (whom negligent priests cause to incur guilt) or reflexively to the offenders themselves; 'avon 'ashmah is a construct chain, literally 'iniquity of guilt.'
- **22:19** — Hebrew lirtsonekhem ('for your acceptance,' also in v. 29) indicates that the offering must meet the standard to be accepted on the worshiper's behalf, matching leratson ('for acceptance') in vv. 20-21 and the Niphal of ratsah ('be accepted') in vv. 23, 25, and 27.
- **22:24** — The four Qal passive participles (ma'uk, katut, natuq, karut) refer to methods of animal castration or testicular injury; the closing clause ('nor shall you do this in your land') is commonly understood to forbid castrating animals in Israel.
