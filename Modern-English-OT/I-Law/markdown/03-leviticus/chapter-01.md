## Text

**1:1** Now the LORD called to Moses and spoke to him out of the tent of meeting, saying,

**1:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When any person from among you presents an offering to the LORD, from the livestock, from the herd and from the flock, you shall present your offering.

**1:3** If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall present an unblemished male; he shall present it at the entrance of the tent of meeting, for his acceptance before the LORD.

**1:4** He shall press his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted on his behalf to make atonement for him.

**1:5** He shall slaughter the young bull before the LORD, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall present the blood and dash the blood all around against the altar that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**1:6** Then he shall skin the burnt offering and cut it up into its pieces.

**1:7** Then the sons of Aaron the priest shall place fire on the altar and set wood in order on the fire.

**1:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall set the pieces, the head, and the suet in order on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar.

**1:9** Its inner parts and its legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall turn all of it into smoke on the altar, a burnt offering, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**1:10** Now if his offering is from the flock, from the sheep or from the goats, for a burnt offering, he shall present an unblemished male.

**1:11** He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall dash its blood against the altar all around.

**1:12** Then he shall cut it up into its pieces, along with its head and its suet, and the priest shall set them in order on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar.

**1:13** But the inner parts and the legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall present all of it and turn it into smoke on the altar; it is a burnt offering, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**1:14** Now if his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering from the birds, then he shall present his offering from the turtledoves or from the young pigeons.

**1:15** And the priest shall present it at the altar, pinch off its head, and turn it into smoke on the altar, and its blood shall be drained out against the wall of the altar.

**1:16** Then he shall remove its crop with its feathers and throw it beside the altar on the east side, to the place of the ashes.

**1:17** He shall tear it open by its wings, but he shall not separate it; and the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire; it is a burnt offering, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Hebrew li-retsono (from ratson, "favor/acceptance") means "for his acceptance," echoing the Niphal verb ve-nirtsah ("it will be accepted") in 1:4; some traditional interpreters render it "of his own free will."
- **1:5** — Hebrew zaraq ("dash/throw") denotes tossing blood in volume from a basin against the altar sides, distinct from the Hiphil hizzah ("sprinkle" drops with a finger, as in Lev 4:6).
- **1:9** — The Hiphil verb hiqtir ("turn into smoke") denotes burning a sacrifice on the altar to produce aromatic smoke, distinct from ordinary burning (saraph); ishsheh ("fire offering") stands here in the construct state before reach nichoach ("soothing fragrance," from the root nuach, "to rest/soothe").
- **1:16** — Hebrew be-notsatah carries a feminine suffix referring back to the feminine noun mur'ah ("crop"). Elsewhere notsah means "feathers/plumage" (likely the feathers on the skin over the crop), though many ancient and modern interpreters take it here as a homonym meaning "its contents."
