## Text

**24:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:2** "Command the sons of Israel that they bring to you pure beaten olive oil for the light, to keep a lamp lit continually.

**24:3** Outside the curtain of the testimony in the tent of meeting, Aaron shall arrange it from evening until morning before the LORD continually; it is a permanent statute throughout your generations.

**24:4** Upon the pure lampstand he shall arrange the lamps before the LORD continually.

**24:5** And you shall take fine flour and bake it into twelve loaves; each loaf shall be of two tenth-measures.

**24:6** And you shall set them in two rows, six to a row, on the pure table before the LORD.

**24:7** And you shall put pure frankincense on each row, that it may be a memorial portion for the bread, a fire offering to the LORD.

**24:8** Every Sabbath day he shall arrange it before the LORD continually; it is from the sons of Israel, an everlasting covenant.

**24:9** And it shall belong to Aaron and to his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place; for it is most holy to him from the fire offerings of the LORD, a permanent portion."

**24:10** Now the son of an Israelite woman, and he was the son of an Egyptian man, went out among the sons of Israel; and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp.

**24:11** And the son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name and cursed. So they brought him to Moses. Now his mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**24:12** And they placed him in custody, so that it might be made clear to them by the mouth of the LORD.

**24:13** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**24:14** "Bring the one who cursed outside the camp, and let all who heard lay their hands on his head, and let the whole congregation stone him.

**24:15** And to the sons of Israel you shall speak, saying, 'Any man who curses his God shall bear his sin.

**24:16** And the one who blasphemes the name of the LORD shall surely be put to death; the whole congregation shall surely stone him. Both the resident alien and the native, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death.

**24:17** And if a man strikes down any human life, he shall surely be put to death.

**24:18** And the one who strikes down the life of an animal shall repay it, life for life.

**24:19** And if a man inflicts an injury on his fellow countryman, as he did, so shall it be done to him:

**24:20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he inflicts an injury on a person, so shall it be inflicted on him.

**24:21** And the one who strikes down an animal shall repay it, but the one who strikes down a person shall be put to death.

**24:22** You shall have one legal standard; it shall be for the resident alien as for the native, for I am the LORD your God.'"

**24:23** Then Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and they brought the one who cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones. Thus the sons of Israel did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — Hebrew ma-arakot (derived from arak, "arrange") denotes ordered rows or stacks of loaves arranged upon the pure table.
- **24:11** — Hebrew vayyiqqov (Qal wayyiqtol of naqav) with et-ha-shem ("the Name," referring to the LORD's name, cf. v. 16) means "blasphemed/reviled" here, though naqav can also mean "designate" or "pronounce distinctly."
- **24:12** — Hebrew lifrosh (Qal infinitive construct of parash, "make clear/distinct") is used impersonally with al-pi YHWH (lit. "by the mouth of the LORD") to indicate waiting for a clear divine verdict.
- **24:19** — Hebrew mum ("injury") is the same term used for a physical defect or blemish in Leviticus 21-22; the active Qal yitten ("inflicts") in vv. 19-20 is mirrored by the passive Niphal yinnaten ("shall be inflicted") of the same verb natan at the end of v. 20.
