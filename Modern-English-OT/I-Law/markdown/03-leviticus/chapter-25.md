## Text

**25:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you enter the land that I am giving to you, then the land shall observe a Sabbath to the LORD.

**25:3** For six years you shall sow your field, and for six years you shall prune your vineyard and gather its yield.

**25:4** But in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath of complete rest for the land, a Sabbath to the LORD; you shall not sow your field, and you shall not prune your vineyard.

**25:5** You shall not reap the aftergrowth of your harvest, and you shall not gather the grapes of your unpruned vine; it shall be a year of complete rest for the land.

**25:6** And the Sabbath of the land shall be yours for food: for you, and for your male servant and for your female servant, and for your hired worker and for your temporary resident who sojourn with you.

**25:7** And for your livestock and for the wild animals that are in your land, all its yield shall be for eating.

**25:8** And you shall count for yourself seven sabbaths of years, seven years seven times, so that the days of the seven sabbaths of years shall amount to forty-nine years for you.

**25:9** Then you shall sound a loud blast of the ram's horn in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month; on the Day of Atonement you shall sound the ram's horn throughout all your land.

**25:10** And you shall consecrate the fiftieth year and proclaim release across the land for all its inhabitants. It shall be a Jubilee for you; each of you shall return to his possession, and to his clan each of you shall return.

**25:11** That fiftieth year shall be a Jubilee for you; you shall not sow, and you shall not reap its aftergrowth, and you shall not gather from its unpruned vines.

**25:12** For it is a Jubilee; it shall be holy to you. From the field you shall eat its yield.

**25:13** In this year of Jubilee, each of you shall return to his possession.

**25:14** And if you make a sale to your neighbor or buy from the hand of your neighbor, none of you shall wrong his brother.

**25:15** According to the number of years after the Jubilee you shall buy from your neighbor; according to the number of years of yields he shall sell to you.

**25:16** In proportion to the greater number of years you shall increase its purchase price, and in proportion to the smaller number of years you shall reduce its purchase price, for it is a number of yields that he is selling to you.

**25:17** So none of you shall wrong his neighbor, but you shall fear your God, for I am the LORD your God.

**25:18** Therefore you shall carry out My statutes, and keep My judgments and carry them out, so that you will dwell on the land in security.

**25:19** And the land will give its fruit, and you will eat to satisfaction and dwell in security on it.

**25:20** And if you say, "What will we eat in the seventh year? Look, we will not sow, and we will not gather our yield,"

**25:21** then I will command My blessing for you in the sixth year, so that it will produce the yield for the three years.

**25:22** And you will sow in the eighth year, and eat from the old yield; until the ninth year, until its yield comes in, you will eat the old.

**25:23** And the land shall not be sold irrevocably, for the land belongs to Me; for you are sojourners and temporary residents with Me.

**25:24** And in all the land of your possession, you shall grant redemption for the land.

**25:25** If your brother becomes poor and sells part of his possession, then his nearest redeemer shall come and redeem what his brother has sold.

**25:26** And if a man has no redeemer, yet he prospers and finds enough for its redemption,

**25:27** then he shall calculate the years since its sale and pay back the balance to the man to whom he sold it, and he shall return to his possession.

**25:28** But if he has not found enough to pay him back, then what he sold shall remain in the hand of the one who bought it until the year of Jubilee; and in the Jubilee it shall be released, and he shall return to his possession.

**25:29** And if a man sells a dwelling house in a walled city, then its right of redemption shall remain until the end of the year of its sale; for a full year its right of redemption shall remain.

**25:30** But if it is not redeemed before a full year is completed for it, then the house in the city that has a wall shall stand irrevocably confirmed to the one who bought it, throughout his generations; it shall not be released in the Jubilee.

**25:31** The houses of the villages, however, that have no wall around them shall be reckoned with the field of the land; they shall have a right of redemption, and in the Jubilee they shall be released.

**25:32** As for the cities of the Levites, the houses of the cities of their possession, a perpetual right of redemption shall belong to the Levites.

**25:33** And if one of the Levites redeems a house, then the house that was sold in the city of his possession shall be released in the Jubilee; for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the sons of Israel.

**25:34** But the field of the pastureland of their cities shall not be sold, for it is a perpetual possession for them.

**25:35** And if your brother becomes poor and his hand falters beside you, then you shall sustain him as a sojourner and a temporary resident, so that he may live with you.

**25:36** Do not take interest or profit from him, but fear your God, so that your brother may live with you.

**25:37** You shall not give him your money at interest, and for profit you shall not give your food.

**25:38** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan, to be God to you.

**25:39** And if your brother becomes poor beside you and sells himself to you, you shall not impose on him the labor of a slave.

**25:40** Like a hired worker, like a temporary resident he shall be with you; until the year of Jubilee he shall serve with you.

**25:41** Then he shall go out from you, he and his children with him, and he shall return to his clan, and to the possession of his fathers he shall return.

**25:42** For they are My servants, whom I brought out from the land of Egypt; they shall not be sold as a slave is sold.

**25:43** You shall not rule over him with harshness, but you shall fear your God.

**25:44** As for your male slave and your female slave whom you may have: from the nations that are around you, from them you may buy a male slave and a female slave.

**25:45** Moreover, from the children of the temporary residents who sojourn with you, from them you may buy, and from their clan who are with you, whom they have fathered in your land; and they shall become a possession for you.

**25:46** And you may leave them as an inheritance to your children after you, to inherit as a possession; forever you may make them labor. But over your brothers, the sons of Israel, none of you shall rule over his brother with harshness.

**25:47** And if a sojourner and temporary resident with you prospers, and your brother beside him becomes poor and sells himself to a sojourner, a temporary resident with you, or to an offshoot of a sojourner's clan,

**25:48** after he has sold himself, he shall have a right of redemption; one of his brothers may redeem him,

**25:49** or his uncle or his uncle's son may redeem him, or a close blood relative from his clan may redeem him; or if he prospers, he may redeem himself.

**25:50** Then he shall calculate with his buyer from the year he sold himself to him until the year of Jubilee, and the money of his sale shall correspond to the number of years; his time with him shall be like the days of a hired worker.

**25:51** If there are still many years, in proportion to them he shall pay back his redemption price from the money of his purchase.

**25:52** And if few years remain until the year of Jubilee, then he shall calculate with him; in proportion to his years he shall pay back his redemption price.

**25:53** Like a hired worker year by year he shall be with him; he shall not rule over him with harshness in your sight.

**25:54** And if he is not redeemed by these means, then he shall go out in the year of Jubilee, he and his children with him.

**25:55** For to Me the sons of Israel are servants; they are My servants, whom I brought out from the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — Hebrew nezirekha (from nazir, "consecrated/untrimmed one") metaphorically designates an unpruned vine whose tendrils grow freely during the Sabbatical year, like a Nazirite's uncut hair (also in v. 11).
- **25:26** — The verb "prospers" renders the Hebrew idiom hissigah yado (Hiphil of nasag plus yad, literally "his hand reaches/attains"), denoting the acquisition of sufficient financial means (also in vv. 47 and 49).
- **25:30** — Ketiv/Qere: The ketiv (written form) reads lo ("not," i.e., "that does not have a wall"), whereas the qere (read form, translated here) reads lo ("to it," i.e., "that has a wall"), matching the contrast with unwalled villages in vv. 29 and 31.
- **25:33** — The Masoretic text reads an active Qal verb ("if one of the Levites redeems") without a negative particle (unlike the Vulgate, which supplies "not"), indicating that even a house redeemed by a fellow Levite reverts at the Jubilee. Literally "the sale of a house and the city of his possession," with the conjunction functioning epexegetically ("in the city of his possession").
- **25:42** — Hebrew uses the single noun eved ("servant/slave") both for Israel as the LORD's servants and for chattel slaves (also in v. 55), underscoring that Israel already belongs to the LORD and cannot be permanently enslaved to human masters.
