## Text

**9:1** Now on the eighth day, Moses summoned Aaron and his sons and the elders of Israel.

**9:2** He said to Aaron, "Take for yourself a calf from the herd for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering, unblemished, and present them before the LORD.

**9:3** And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering, and a calf and a lamb, a year old and unblemished, for a burnt offering,

**9:4** as well as an ox and a ram for peace offerings to sacrifice before the LORD, and a grain offering blended with oil, because today the LORD will appear to you.'"

**9:5** They took what Moses had commanded to the front of the tent of meeting, and the entire congregation approached and stood before the LORD.

**9:6** Then Moses said, "This is the matter the LORD has commanded you to do, so that the glory of the LORD may appear to you."

**9:7** Then Moses said to Aaron, "Approach the altar, offer your sin offering and your burnt offering, and make atonement for yourself and for the people; then offer the people's offering and make atonement for them, just as the LORD has commanded."

**9:8** So Aaron approached the altar and slaughtered the calf for the sin offering that was for himself.

**9:9** The sons of Aaron presented the blood to him; he dipped his finger into the blood and placed it on the horns of the altar, and he poured the blood out at the base of the altar.

**9:10** But the fat, the kidneys, and the lobe from the liver, from the sin offering, he turned into smoke on the altar, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**9:11** And the flesh and the hide he burned with fire outside the camp.

**9:12** Then he slaughtered the burnt offering; Aaron's sons delivered the blood to him, and he dashed it against the altar all around.

**9:13** They also delivered the burnt offering to him in its pieces, along with the head, and he turned them into smoke on the altar.

**9:14** He washed the entrails and the lower legs and turned them into smoke on top of the burnt offering on the altar.

**9:15** Next he presented the people's offering; he took the male goat for the sin offering that was for the people, slaughtered it, and offered it as a sin offering, just like the first.

**9:16** Then he presented the burnt offering and offered it according to the regulation.

**9:17** He presented the grain offering, filled his palm with some of it, and turned it into smoke on the altar, in addition to the morning burnt offering.

**9:18** Then he slaughtered the ox and the ram, the sacrifice of peace offerings that was for the people. Aaron's sons delivered the blood to him, and he dashed it against the altar all around.

**9:19** And the fat portions from the ox and from the ram—the fat tail, the covering fat, the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver—

**9:20** they set the fat portions on the breasts, and he turned the fat portions into smoke on the altar.

**9:21** As for the breasts and the right thigh, Aaron waved them as a wave offering before the LORD, just as Moses had commanded.

**9:22** Then Aaron raised his hands toward the people and blessed them, and he came down from offering the sin offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings.

**9:23** Moses and Aaron entered the tent of meeting, then came out and blessed the people; and the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people.

**9:24** Fire came out from before the LORD and consumed the burnt offering and the fat portions on the altar. All the people saw it, shouted for joy, and fell on their faces.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The verb nir'ah is a Niphal perfect (qatal) of certainty used with 'today' to announce the imminent appearance of the LORD's glory fulfilled in 9:23.
- **9:15** — Here the Piel of chata' (vaychatte'ehu) takes the sacrificial goat as its direct object in a denominative sense, 'offered it as a sin offering,' contrasting with its privative sense ('purified' the altar) in 8:15.
- **9:19** — Verse 19 has no finite verb in Hebrew; it introduces the fat portions as a fronted topic (casus pendens) resumed as the direct object in 9:20. 'Covering fat' renders the Piel participle hamekhasseh (literally 'that which covers,' referring to the fat covering the entrails).
- **9:22** — The Masoretic text writes yadav defectively without the yod of the dual, but vocalizes it as the dual 'his hands' (contrasting with singular yado, 'his hand,' in 8:23).
