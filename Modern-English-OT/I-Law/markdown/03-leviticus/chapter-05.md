## Text

**5:1** "'Now if a person sins and hears the voice of an adjuration, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or has known, if he does not report it, then he shall bear his iniquity.

**5:2** Or if a person touches any unclean thing, whether the carcass of an unclean wild animal, or the carcass of an unclean domestic animal, or the carcass of an unclean swarming creature, and it is hidden from him, yet he is unclean and becomes guilty;

**5:3** or if he touches human uncleanness, whatever his uncleanness may be by which he becomes unclean, and it is hidden from him, yet he comes to know it and becomes guilty;

**5:4** or if a person swears, uttering rashly with the lips to do harm or to do good, in whatever a person may utter rashly in an oath, and it is hidden from him, yet he comes to know it and becomes guilty in one of these.

**5:5** So it shall be, when he becomes guilty in one of these, that he shall confess that concerning which he has sinned.

**5:6** And he shall bring to the LORD his guilt offering for his sin that he has sinned, a female from the flock, a female lamb or a female goat, as a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him on account of his sin.

**5:7** But if his hand does not reach enough for a lamb, then he shall bring to the LORD, as his guilt offering for that which he has sinned, two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering.

**5:8** He shall bring them to the priest, who shall present the one for the sin offering first, and wring its head at the back of its neck, yet he shall not sever it.

**5:9** He shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar, while the remainder of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar; it is a sin offering.

**5:10** The second he shall prepare as a burnt offering according to the ordinance. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him on account of his sin that he has sinned, and he will be forgiven.

**5:11** But if his hand cannot reach two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring as his offering for that which he has sinned a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering; he shall not put oil on it, nor place frankincense on it, for it is a sin offering.

**5:12** He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall scoop from it a full handful as its memorial portion and turn it into smoke on the altar, upon the fire offerings of the LORD; it is a sin offering.

**5:13** Thus the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has sinned in any one of these, and he will be forgiven. And it shall belong to the priest, like the grain offering.'"

**5:14** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:15** "If a person commits an act of unfaithfulness and sins inadvertently concerning the holy things of the LORD, then he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, according to your valuation in silver shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary, as a guilt offering.

**5:16** He shall make restitution for what he has sinned against the holy thing, and shall add a fifth to it and give it to the priest. Then the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.

**5:17** Now if a person sins and does any one of all the commandments of the LORD regarding things that must not be done, though he does not know it, yet he becomes guilty and shall bear his iniquity.

**5:18** He shall bring an unblemished ram from the flock, according to your valuation, as a guilt offering to the priest. Then the priest shall make atonement for him for his inadvertent error in which he erred though he did not know it, and he will be forgiven.

**5:19** It is a guilt offering; he has surely become guilty before the LORD."

**5:20** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**5:21** "If a person sins and commits an act of unfaithfulness against the LORD, and lies to his neighbor regarding a deposit, or a security, or robbery, or extorts from his neighbor,

**5:22** or finds lost property and lies about it, and swears falsely concerning any one of all the things that a person may do, sinning in them,

**5:23** then it shall be, when he has sinned and become guilty, that he shall restore what he took by robbery, or what he obtained by extortion, or the deposit that was deposited with him, or the lost property that he found,

**5:24** or anything about which he swore falsely; he shall make restitution for it in full and add a fifth to it, and give it to the one to whom it belongs on the day of his guilt.

**5:25** Then he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, according to your valuation, as a guilt offering to the priest.

**5:26** And the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD, and he will be forgiven for any one of all the things that he may do so as to become guilty in it."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Literally 'voice of an adjuration' (qol alah), referring to a solemn curse or oath pronounced publicly to compel anyone with knowledge of a matter to come forward and testify.
- **5:6** — In 5:6-7 the noun asham ('guilt offering' or 'guilt penalty') designates the offering even though its sacrificial type is specified as chattat ('sin offering'), before asham denotes a distinct ram offering in 5:15-26.
- **5:19** — Hebrew pairs the Qal infinitive absolute with the Qal perfect of asham (ashom asham) for emphatic certainty, 'he has surely become guilty.'
- **5:21** — Verses 5:20-26 follow the Masoretic numbering. The rare phrase tesumet yad (literally 'a placing of the hand', occurring only here) denotes a security, pledge, or entrusted partnership.
- **5:24** — Hebrew berosho literally means 'in its head', an idiom for the full principal amount; in the next clause the Masoretic text uses the plural chamishitav ('its fifths') rather than the singular form seen in 5:16.
