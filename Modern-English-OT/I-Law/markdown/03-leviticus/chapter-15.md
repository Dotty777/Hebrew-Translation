## Text

**15:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'Whenever any man has a discharge from his flesh, his discharge is unclean.

**15:3** And this shall be his uncleanness in his discharge: whether his flesh flows with his discharge or his flesh is sealed off from his discharge, that is his uncleanness.

**15:4** Every bed on which the man with the discharge lies shall become unclean, and every article on which he sits shall become unclean.

**15:5** And anyone who touches his bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:6** And the one who sits on the article on which the man with the discharge sits shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:7** And the one who touches the flesh of the man with the discharge shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:8** And if the man with the discharge spits on one who is clean, then that person shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:9** And every saddle on which the man with the discharge rides shall become unclean.

**15:10** And whoever touches anything that is beneath him shall be unclean until evening; and the one who carries those things shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:11** And anyone whom the man with the discharge touches without having rinsed his hands in water shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:12** And an earthenware vessel that the man with the discharge touches shall be broken, while every wooden article shall be rinsed in water.

**15:13** When the man with the discharge becomes clean from his discharge, he shall count for himself seven days for his cleansing, and wash his garments and bathe his flesh in flowing water; then he shall be clean.

**15:14** On the eighth day he shall take for himself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and he shall come before the LORD to the entrance of the tent of meeting and give them to the priest.

**15:15** And the priest shall offer them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering; thus the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD on account of his discharge.

**15:16** Now if an emission of semen goes out from a man, he shall bathe all his flesh in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:17** And every garment and every piece of leather on which there is an emission of semen shall be washed in water, and it shall be unclean until evening.

**15:18** As for a woman with whom a man lies with an emission of semen, they shall bathe in water, and they shall be unclean until evening.

**15:19** When a woman has a discharge, and the discharge in her flesh is blood, she shall remain in her menstrual separation for seven days; and whoever touches her shall be unclean until evening.

**15:20** Everything on which she lies during her menstrual separation shall become unclean, and everything on which she sits shall become unclean.

**15:21** And whoever touches her bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:22** And whoever touches any article on which she sits shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:23** And if it is on the bed or on the article on which she is sitting, when someone touches it, he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:24** And if a man actually lies with her so that her menstrual separation comes upon him, he shall be unclean for seven days, and every bed on which he lies shall become unclean.

**15:25** Now if a woman has a discharge of her blood flowing for many days outside the time of her menstrual separation, or if she has a discharge beyond her menstrual separation, for all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness she shall be as in the days of her menstrual separation; she is unclean.

**15:26** Every bed on which she lies throughout all the days of her discharge shall be to her like the bed of her menstrual separation, and every article on which she sits shall be unclean, like the uncleanness of her menstrual separation.

**15:27** And whoever touches them shall become unclean; he shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.

**15:28** And if she becomes clean from her discharge, she shall count for herself seven days, and afterward she shall be clean.

**15:29** On the eighth day she shall take for herself two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and bring them to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**15:30** And the priest shall offer the one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering; thus the priest shall make atonement for her before the LORD on account of the discharge of her uncleanness.'

**15:31** So you shall set the sons of Israel apart from their uncleanness, in order that they do not die in their uncleanness by defiling My tabernacle that is in their midst.

**15:32** This is the law for the one who has a discharge and for the one from whom an emission of semen goes out, becoming unclean by it,

**15:33** and for the woman who is unwell in her menstrual separation, and for the one who has a discharge, for both the male and the female, and for a man who lies with an unclean woman."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Hebrew basar ("flesh") is used here and in vv. 3 and 19 as a euphemism for the genital organ.
- **15:3** — Hebrew rar (Qal perfect of the hapax legomenon verb rir, "ooze" or "run") and hechtim (Hiphil perfect of chatam, "seal up" or "obstruct") cover both a continuous discharge and a blocked one.
- **15:13** — Literally "living water" (mayim chayyim), the standard Hebrew idiom for fresh, flowing water from a spring or stream.
- **15:23** — The masculine pronoun hu ("it") has no explicit antecedent and is widely understood to refer to any object lying on the bed or article on which the menstruating woman sits.
- **15:31** — The verb vehizzartem is the Hiphil of nazar ("set apart" or "keep separate"); some ancient versions reflect a form of zahar ("warn").
