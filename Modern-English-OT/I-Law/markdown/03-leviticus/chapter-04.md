## Text

**4:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**4:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'When a person sins inadvertently against any of the commandments of the LORD regarding things that must not be done, and does any one of them:

**4:3** if the anointed priest sins, bringing guilt on the people, then for his sin that he has sinned he shall present an unblemished bull from the herd to the LORD as a sin offering.

**4:4** He shall bring the bull to the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, press his hand on the head of the bull, and slaughter the bull before the LORD.

**4:5** Then the anointed priest shall take some of the blood of the bull and bring it into the tent of meeting.

**4:6** The priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the curtain of the sanctuary.

**4:7** The priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense before the LORD, which is in the tent of meeting, and he shall pour out all the blood of the bull at the base of the altar of burnt offering that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**4:8** All the fat of the bull of the sin offering he shall lift off from it: the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat that is on the inner parts,

**4:9** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is at the loins, and the lobe on the liver, which he shall remove along with the kidneys,

**4:10** just as it is lifted off from the ox of the sacrifice of peace offerings. Then the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar of burnt offering.

**4:11** But the hide of the bull and all its flesh, along with its head and its legs, its inner parts and its dung,

**4:12** the entire bull he shall carry outside the camp to a clean place, to where the fatty ashes are poured out, and burn it on wood in the fire; on the place where the fatty ashes are poured out it shall be burned.

**4:13** Now if the whole congregation of Israel errs inadvertently, and a matter is hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they do any one of all the commandments of the LORD regarding things that must not be done, and become guilty,

**4:14** when the sin regarding which they have sinned becomes known, then the assembly shall present a bull from the herd as a sin offering and bring it before the tent of meeting.

**4:15** The elders of the congregation shall press their hands on the head of the bull before the LORD, and one shall slaughter the bull before the LORD.

**4:16** Then the anointed priest shall bring some of the blood of the bull into the tent of meeting.

**4:17** The priest shall dip his finger in some of the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD, in front of the curtain.

**4:18** He shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is before the LORD, which is in the tent of meeting, and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**4:19** All its fat he shall lift off from it and turn it into smoke on the altar.

**4:20** He shall do with the bull just as he did with the bull of the sin offering; so shall he do with it. Thus the priest shall make atonement for them, and they will be forgiven.

**4:21** Then he shall carry the bull outside the camp and burn it just as he burned the first bull; it is the sin offering of the assembly.

**4:22** When a leader sins and inadvertently does any one of all the commandments of the LORD his God regarding things that must not be done, and becomes guilty,

**4:23** or if his sin in which he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring his offering, a goat, an unblemished male.

**4:24** He shall press his hand on the head of the male goat and slaughter it in the place where one slaughters the burnt offering before the LORD; it is a sin offering.

**4:25** Then the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and he shall pour out its blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering.

**4:26** All its fat he shall turn into smoke on the altar, like the fat of the sacrifice of peace offerings. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him on account of his sin, and he will be forgiven.

**4:27** Now if one person from the people of the land sins inadvertently, by doing one of the commandments of the LORD regarding things that must not be done, and becomes guilty,

**4:28** or if his sin that he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring his offering, a goat, an unblemished female, for his sin that he has sinned.

**4:29** He shall press his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter the sin offering in the place of the burnt offering.

**4:30** Then the priest shall take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and he shall pour out all its blood at the base of the altar.

**4:31** All its fat he shall remove, just as the fat is removed from the sacrifice of peace offerings, and the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar for a soothing fragrance to the LORD. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.

**4:32** And if he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he shall bring an unblemished female.

**4:33** He shall press his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it as a sin offering in the place where one slaughters the burnt offering.

**4:34** Then the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and he shall pour out all its blood at the base of the altar.

**4:35** All its fat he shall remove, just as the fat of the lamb is removed from the sacrifice of peace offerings, and the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar, upon the fire offerings of the LORD. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has sinned, and he will be forgiven.'"

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Hebrew clause literally reads 'commandments of the LORD which are not to be done' (Niphal imperfect of asah), an idiom referring specifically to negative prohibitions.
- **4:10** — Hebrew distinguishes ritual burning on the altar, using Hiphil hiqtir ('turn into smoke', as here), from burning outside the camp for disposal, using Qal and Niphal saraph ('burn', as in 4:12).
- **4:15** — The verb for slaughtering is masculine singular (Qal weqatal of shachat) with an indefinite subject ('one shall slaughter'), matching the impersonal singular usage in 4:24 and 4:33.
- **4:22** — This verse unusually opens the legal case with the relative particle asher ('when') rather than im or ki ('if'); in 4:23 and 4:28, the conjunction o ('or') introduces the sub-condition ('or if') with a Hophal perfect verb ('is made known').
