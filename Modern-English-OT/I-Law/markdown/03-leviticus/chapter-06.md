## Text

**6:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:2** "Command Aaron and his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the burnt offering: the burnt offering itself shall remain on the hearth upon the altar all night until the morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it.

**6:3** And the priest shall put on his linen garment, and he shall put linen undergarments over his flesh; then he shall take up the ashes into which the fire consumes the burnt offering on the altar, and set them beside the altar.

**6:4** And he shall remove his garments, put on other garments, and bring the ashes outside the camp to a clean place.

**6:5** And the fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not go out. The priest shall burn wood on it morning by morning, arrange the burnt offering on it, and turn the fat portions of the peace offerings into smoke on it.

**6:6** A continual fire shall be kept burning on the altar; it shall not go out.

**6:7** And this is the law of the grain offering: the sons of Aaron shall present it before the LORD, at the front of the altar.

**6:8** And he shall take up from it a handful of the fine flour of the grain offering and of its oil, together with all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and turn its memorial portion into smoke on the altar, a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**6:9** And what remains of it Aaron and his sons shall eat; it shall be eaten unleavened in a holy place; in the courtyard of the tent of meeting they shall eat it.

**6:10** It shall not be baked with leaven. As their portion I have given it from My fire offerings; it is most holy, like the sin offering and like the guilt offering.

**6:11** Every male among the sons of Aaron may eat it; it is a permanent statute throughout your generations from the fire offerings of the LORD. Whatever touches them shall become holy.'"

**6:12** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:13** "This is the offering of Aaron and his sons which they shall present to the LORD on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a continual grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

**6:14** It shall be prepared with oil on a griddle; you shall bring it well stirred. You shall present the baked portions of a grain offering of pieces as a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**6:15** And the anointed priest in his place from among his sons shall prepare it; by a permanent statute, it shall be wholly turned into smoke to the LORD.

**6:16** And every grain offering of a priest shall be a whole offering; it shall not be eaten."

**6:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:18** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the sin offering: at the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the sin offering shall be slaughtered before the LORD; it is most holy.

**6:19** The priest who offers it as a sin offering shall eat it; it shall be eaten in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.

**6:20** Whatever touches its flesh shall become holy; and when any of its blood spatters onto a garment, you shall wash that on which it spattered in a holy place.

**6:21** And an earthenware vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; but if it was boiled in a bronze vessel, it shall be scoured and rinsed with water.

**6:22** Every male among the priests may eat it; it is most holy.

**6:23** And no sin offering any of whose blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the sanctuary shall be eaten; it shall be burned in the fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — Hebrew moqedah is a rare feminine noun from the root meaning to burn, occurring only here for the hearth or burning surface of the altar; the verb tuqad is a Hophal imperfect of the same root denoting fire kept continually burning.
- **6:7** — Hebrew haqrev is a Hiphil infinitive absolute functioning as a command with an explicit subject, the sons of Aaron.
- **6:14** — Hebrew murbekhet is a Hophal participle indicating flour thoroughly stirred or scalded with liquid, and tufine is a hapax legomenon of uncertain derivation traditionally linked to the verb meaning to bake, hence baked portions.
- **6:20** — Hebrew yizzeh is the intransitive Qal imperfect of the verb meaning to spatter, distinct from its transitive Hiphil meaning to sprinkle; the feminine suffix on aleha refers back to the spattered garment or stained part even though beged is grammatically masculine.
