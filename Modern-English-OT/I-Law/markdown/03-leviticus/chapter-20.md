## Text

**20:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:2** "And to the sons of Israel you shall say, 'Anyone from the sons of Israel, or from the resident aliens who live in Israel, who gives any of his seed to Molech shall surely be put to death; the people of the land shall stone him with stones.

**20:3** And I Myself will set My face against that man and cut him off from the midst of his people, because he gave some of his seed to Molech, so as to defile My sanctuary and to profane My holy name.

**20:4** And if the people of the land ever hide their eyes from that man when he gives some of his seed to Molech, so as not to put him to death,

**20:5** then I Myself will set My face against that man and against his family, and I will cut off him and all who prostitute themselves after him, prostituting themselves after Molech, from the midst of their people.

**20:6** As for the person who turns toward mediums and toward spirit-diviners to prostitute himself after them, I will set My face against that person and cut him off from the midst of his people.

**20:7** Therefore sanctify yourselves and be holy, because I am the LORD your God.

**20:8** And you shall observe My statutes and carry them out; I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

**20:9** Indeed, anyone who curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death; he has cursed his father or his mother; his bloodguilt is on him.

**20:10** Now if a man commits adultery with another man's wife, one who commits adultery with the wife of his neighbor, the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

**20:11** And if a man lies with his father's wife, he has uncovered the nakedness of his father; the two of them shall surely be put to death; their bloodguilt is on them.

**20:12** And if a man lies with his daughter-in-law, the two of them shall surely be put to death; they have committed a perversion; their bloodguilt is on them.

**20:13** And if a man lies with a male as one lies with a woman, the two of them have committed an abomination; they shall surely be put to death; their bloodguilt is on them.

**20:14** And if a man takes a woman and her mother, it is depravity; they shall burn him and them in fire, so that there will be no depravity in your midst.

**20:15** And if a man has sexual relations with an animal, he shall surely be put to death, and you shall kill the animal.

**20:16** And if a woman approaches any animal to mate with it, you must kill the woman and the animal; they shall surely be put to death; their bloodguilt is on them.

**20:17** And if a man takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness while she sees his nakedness, it is a disgrace, and they shall be cut off before the eyes of the sons of their people; he has uncovered the nakedness of his sister, and he shall bear his iniquity.

**20:18** And if a man lies with a menstruating woman and uncovers her nakedness, he has laid bare her flow, and she has uncovered the flow of her blood; the two of them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.

**20:19** And the nakedness of your mother's sister or of your father's sister you shall not uncover, for such a person has laid bare his own close relative; they shall bear their iniquity.

**20:20** And a man who lies with his uncle's wife has uncovered the nakedness of his uncle; they shall bear their sin, and they shall die childless.

**20:21** And if a man takes the wife of his brother, it is an impurity; he has uncovered the nakedness of his brother; they shall be childless.

**20:22** Therefore you shall observe all My statutes and all My judgments and carry them out, so that the land into which I am bringing you to live will not vomit you out.

**20:23** And you shall not walk in the statutes of the nation that I am casting out from before you, because they did all these things, and I loathed them.

**20:24** So I said to you, "You yourselves shall inherit their land, and I Myself will give it to you to possess it, a land flowing with milk and honey." I am the LORD your God, who separated you from the peoples.

**20:25** Therefore you must distinguish between the clean animal and the unclean, and between the unclean bird and the clean; and you must not make yourselves detestable through animal, through bird, or through anything that creeps along the ground, which I have separated for you to treat as unclean.

**20:26** So you shall be holy to Me, because I, the LORD, am holy, and I have separated you from the peoples to be Mine.

**20:27** Now a man or a woman in whom there is a spirit of the dead or a divining spirit shall surely be put to death; they shall stone them with stones; their bloodguilt is on them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — Hebrew zera ("seed") here denotes a person's children/offspring (retaining the verbal link with Lev 18:20–21; cf. 20:2, 4).
- **20:9** — Hebrew damav (literally "his bloods," plural) is a standard legal idiom for bloodguilt, here meaning the offender bears sole responsibility for his own execution. The conjunction ve- ("and") in "his father and his mother" carries the legal disjunctive sense "or" (as also in v. 19).
- **20:17** — Hebrew hesed here is a rare homonym (hesed II, cf. Prov 14:34) meaning "disgrace" or "reproach," distinct from the common word hesed ("loyal love, kindness").
- **20:25** — The Piel infinitive letamme carries a declarative or estimative sense ("to treat/declare as unclean"), while the verse arranges the pairs chiastically: clean-to-unclean for animals, then unclean-to-clean for birds.
- **20:27** — Hebrew ki-yihyeh bahem ov o yidde'oni literally means "when there is in them a spirit of the dead or a divining ('knowing') spirit." By metonymy, the same two nouns denote the mediums and spirit-diviners who consult such spirits in 19:31 and 20:6.
