## Text

**23:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'The appointed times of the LORD, which you shall proclaim as holy assemblies, these are My appointed times.

**23:3** For six days work may be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, a holy assembly; you shall not do any work. It is a Sabbath to the LORD in all your dwelling places.

**23:4** These are the appointed times of the LORD, holy assemblies which you shall proclaim at their appointed time.

**23:5** In the first month, on the fourteenth of the month, between the two evenings, is Passover to the LORD.

**23:6** And on the fifteenth day of this month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the LORD; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

**23:7** On the first day you shall have a holy assembly; you shall not do any occupational work.

**23:8** And you shall present a fire offering to the LORD for seven days. On the seventh day is a holy assembly; you shall not do any occupational work.'"

**23:9** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:10** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you enter the land that I am giving to you and reap its harvest, then you shall bring the sheaf of the first of your harvest to the priest.

**23:11** And he shall wave the sheaf before the LORD for your acceptance; on the day after the Sabbath the priest shall wave it.

**23:12** And on the day you wave the sheaf, you shall prepare an unblemished male lamb a year old for a burnt offering to the LORD.

**23:13** And its grain offering shall be two tenth-measures of fine flour mixed with oil, a fire offering to the LORD, a soothing fragrance; and its drink offering shall be wine, a quarter of a hin.

**23:14** And you shall not eat bread, roasted grain, or fresh grain until this very day, until you have brought the offering of your God. It is a permanent statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.

**23:15** And you shall count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day you bring the sheaf of the wave offering; seven complete Sabbaths they shall be.

**23:16** Until the day after the seventh Sabbath you shall count fifty days, and you shall present a new grain offering to the LORD.

**23:17** From your dwelling places you shall bring bread for a wave offering, two loaves of two tenth-measures; they shall be of fine flour; they shall be baked with leaven, as firstfruits to the LORD.

**23:18** And with the bread you shall present seven unblemished male lambs a year old, and one bull of the herd, and two rams. They shall be a burnt offering to the LORD, with their grain offering and their drink offerings, a fire offering, a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**23:19** And you shall prepare one male goat for a sin offering, and two male lambs a year old for a sacrifice of peace offerings.

**23:20** And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits as a wave offering before the LORD, with two male lambs; they shall be holy to the LORD for the priest.

**23:21** And you shall make a proclamation on this very day; you shall have a holy assembly. You shall not do any occupational work. It is a permanent statute in all your dwelling places throughout your generations.

**23:22** And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not completely reap the edge of your field when you reap, nor shall you glean the gleanings of your harvest; you shall leave them for the poor and for the resident alien. I am the LORD your God.'"

**23:23** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:24** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'In the seventh month, on the first of the month, you shall have a complete rest, a memorial with loud blasts, a holy assembly.

**23:25** You shall not do any occupational work, and you shall present a fire offering to the LORD.'"

**23:26** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:27** "Indeed, on the tenth of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement; you shall have a holy assembly, and you shall humble your souls and present a fire offering to the LORD.

**23:28** And you shall not do any work on this very day, for it is a day of atonement, to make atonement for you before the LORD your God.

**23:29** For every person who is not humbled on this very day shall be cut off from his kin.

**23:30** And as for any person who does any work on this very day, I will destroy that person from the midst of his people.

**23:31** You shall not do any work. It is a permanent statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.

**23:32** It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you shall humble your souls. On the ninth of the month at evening, from evening to evening, you shall observe your Sabbath."

**23:33** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23:34** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Booths for seven days to the LORD.

**23:35** On the first day is a holy assembly; you shall not do any occupational work.

**23:36** For seven days you shall present a fire offering to the LORD. On the eighth day you shall have a holy assembly, and you shall present a fire offering to the LORD. It is a solemn gathering; you shall not do any occupational work.

**23:37** These are the appointed times of the LORD, which you shall proclaim as holy assemblies, for presenting a fire offering to the LORD, a burnt offering and a grain offering, a sacrifice and drink offerings, the requirement of each day on its own day,

**23:38** besides the Sabbaths of the LORD, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your freewill offerings, which you give to the LORD.

**23:39** Indeed, on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you gather in the produce of the land, you shall celebrate the festival of the LORD for seven days; on the first day shall be a complete rest, and on the eighth day shall be a complete rest.

**23:40** And you shall take for yourselves on the first day the fruit of splendid trees, fronds of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the stream, and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God for seven days.

**23:41** And you shall celebrate it as a festival to the LORD for seven days in the year. It is a permanent statute throughout your generations; in the seventh month you shall celebrate it.

**23:42** In booths you shall dwell for seven days; every native in Israel shall dwell in booths,

**23:43** so that your generations may know that I made the sons of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out from the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'"

**23:44** So Moses declared the appointed times of the LORD to the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — Hebrew beyn ha-arbayim (dual of erev) literally means "between the two evenings," an idiom referring either to twilight between sunset and dusk or to the late afternoon hours before sunset.
- **23:7** — Hebrew meleket avodah literally means "work of labor/service," denoting regular occupational work, in contrast to the total prohibition of all melakah ("work") on the Sabbath (v. 3) and the Day of Atonement (vv. 28-31).
- **23:11** — Hebrew mimmohorat ha-shabbat ("on the day after the Sabbath") has been interpreted either as the day after the first festival day of Unleavened Bread or as the day after the weekly Sabbath falling during the festival; similarly, "seven complete Sabbaths" in v. 15 denotes seven full weeks culminating in the seventh Sabbath (v. 16).
- **23:13** — Hebrew esronim (plural of issaron, "tenth-measure") is a dry measure equal to one-tenth of an ephah; the spelling veniskoh ("and its drink offering") preserves an archaic third-person masculine singular suffix written with he.
- **23:27** — Hebrew ve-innitem et-nafshoteikem (Piel of anah II) literally means "humble/afflict your souls," an idiom for fasting and self-denial; the corresponding Pual passive te-unneh ("is humbled") appears in v. 29.
