## Text

**3:1** "'Now if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, if he is presenting from the herd, whether male or female, he shall present it unblemished before the LORD.

**3:2** He shall press his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall dash the blood against the altar all around.

**3:3** And he shall present from the sacrifice of peace offerings a fire offering to the LORD: the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat that is on the inner parts,

**3:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; he shall remove it along with the kidneys.

**3:5** Then the sons of Aaron shall turn it into smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering that is on the wood that is on the fire, a fire offering of a soothing fragrance to the LORD.

**3:6** Now if his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD is from the flock, male or female, he shall present it unblemished.

**3:7** If he is presenting a lamb as his offering, then he shall present it before the LORD.

**3:8** He shall press his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it before the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall dash its blood against the altar all around.

**3:9** Then from the sacrifice of peace offerings he shall present a fire offering to the LORD: its fat, the whole fat tail, which he shall remove close beside the backbone, and the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat that is on the inner parts,

**3:10** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; he shall remove it along with the kidneys.

**3:11** And the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar, food of a fire offering to the LORD.

**3:12** And if his offering is a goat, then he shall present it before the LORD.

**3:13** He shall press his hand on its head and slaughter it before the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall dash its blood against the altar all around.

**3:14** Then from it he shall present his offering, a fire offering to the LORD: the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat that is on the inner parts,

**3:15** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; he shall remove it along with the kidneys.

**3:16** And the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar, food of a fire offering for a soothing fragrance; all fat belongs to the LORD.

**3:17** It is a permanent statute for your generations in all your dwelling places: you shall eat no fat and no blood.'"

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Hebrew zevach shelamim is literally "a sacrifice of peace offerings" (plural shelamim, related to shalom, "peace/wholeness/well-being"), a shared sacrificial meal of fellowship and thanksgiving.
- **3:4** — Hebrew ha-yoteret al ha-kaved ("the lobe on the liver," from yatar, "to project/be left over") refers to the protruding caudate lobe of the liver; al ha-kelayot uses al in the sense "along with the kidneys."
- **3:9** — Hebrew ha-alyah temimah denotes the entire broad fat tail of Near Eastern sheep (temimah here meaning "whole/intact"), removed near ha-atseh ("the backbone" or lower spine, a term occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible).
- **3:11** — Hebrew lechem ishsheh (literally "food/bread of a fire offering") uses lechem in its broad sense of "food" to designate the altar portion allotted to the LORD (compare 3:16; 21:6; Num 28:2).
