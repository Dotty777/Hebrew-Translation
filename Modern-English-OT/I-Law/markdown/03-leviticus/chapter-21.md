## Text

**21:1** And the LORD said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and tell them, 'None of them shall make himself unclean for a dead person among his people,

**21:2** except for his blood relative who is near to him: for his mother, for his father, for his son, for his daughter, and for his brother,

**21:3** and for his virgin sister who is near to him, who has not belonged to a man; for her he may make himself unclean.

**21:4** He shall not make himself unclean as a leader among his people, so as to profane himself.

**21:5** They shall not make a bald spot on their heads, nor shave the edge of their beards, nor cut a gash in their flesh.

**21:6** They shall be holy to their God and shall not profane the name of their God, for it is the fire offerings of the LORD, the food of their God, that they present; so they must be holy.

**21:7** They shall not take a woman who is a prostitute or profaned, and they shall not take a woman divorced from her husband, for he is holy to his God.

**21:8** You shall treat him as holy, because he is the one who presents the food of your God; he shall be holy to you, for I, the LORD who sanctifies you, am holy.

**21:9** And if the daughter of a priest profanes herself through prostitution, it is her father whom she profanes; she shall be burned with fire.

**21:10** Now the priest who is the highest among his brothers, on whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been ordained to wear the garments, shall not leave his head unkempt nor tear his garments.

**21:11** He shall not go near any dead bodies; not for his father or for his mother shall he make himself unclean.

**21:12** He shall not depart from the sanctuary, nor shall he profane the sanctuary of his God, for the dedication of the anointing oil of his God is upon him. I am the LORD.

**21:13** And as for him, he shall take a woman in her virginity.

**21:14** A widow, a divorced woman, a profaned woman, a prostitute—these he shall not take; rather, he shall take a virgin from his own people as a wife,

**21:15** and he shall not profane his offspring among his people, for I am the LORD who sanctifies him.'"

**21:16** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**21:17** "Speak to Aaron, saying, 'Any man from your offspring throughout their generations who has a defect in him shall not approach to present the food of his God.

**21:18** For no man who has a defect in him shall approach: a man who is blind, or lame, or disfigured, or with an elongated limb,

**21:19** or a man who has a fractured foot or a fractured hand,

**21:20** or one who is hunchbacked, or stunted, or has a clouding in his eye, or scabies, or scabs, or a crushed testicle.

**21:21** No man from the offspring of Aaron the priest who has a defect in him shall come near to present the fire offerings of the LORD; a defect is in him; he shall not come near to present the food of his God.

**21:22** He may eat the food of his God, from the most holy things and from the holy things;

**21:23** yet he shall not come to the curtain nor come near the altar, because a defect is in him, and he shall not profane My sanctuaries, for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.'"

**21:24** Thus Moses spoke to Aaron, to his sons, and to all the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **21:4** — Hebrew ba'al be'ammav is syntactically difficult and may mean 'as a leader among his people' or 'as a husband among his people' (referring to a wife or relatives by marriage). The form lehehallo is a Niphal infinitive construct of halal ('profane') with a 3ms suffix, 'so as to profane himself.'
- **21:5** — Ketiv writes yqrhh with an initial yod; Qere reads the cognate noun qorhah ('a bald spot' or 'baldness').
- **21:9** — The verb tehel is Niphal imperfect 3fs of halal I ('profane herself'), echoing the Piel participle mehallelet ('she profanes') in the following clause.
- **21:10** — Literally 'and one has filled his hand' (Piel perfect of male' with an impersonal subject), the standard priestly idiom for ordination.
- **21:20** — Several rare defect terms in vv. 18-20 have uncertain exact meanings: harum (v. 18) likely denotes a cleft or flattened nose or face, saru'a (v. 18) an abnormally long limb, daq (v. 20) someone stunted or a dwarf, and teballul (v. 20) a blurring or spot in the eye.
