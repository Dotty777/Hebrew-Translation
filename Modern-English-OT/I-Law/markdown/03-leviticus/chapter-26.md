## Text

**26:1** "You shall not make idols for yourselves, nor shall you erect a carved image or a standing pillar for yourselves, nor shall you set an engraved stone in your land to bow down upon it, for I am the LORD your God.

**26:2** My Sabbaths you shall observe, and My sanctuary you shall revere; I am the LORD.

**26:3** If you walk in My statutes and observe My commandments and carry them out,

**26:4** then I will give your rains in their season, and the land will yield its produce, and the trees of the field will yield their fruit.

**26:5** Threshing will overtake the grape harvest for you, and the grape harvest will overtake the sowing, and you will eat your bread to satisfaction and dwell securely in your land.

**26:6** I will give peace in the land, and you will lie down with no one to terrify you; I will eliminate vicious beasts from the land, and a sword will not pass through your land.

**26:7** And you will pursue your enemies, and they will fall before you by the sword.

**26:8** Five of you will pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you will pursue ten thousand, and your enemies will fall before you by the sword.

**26:9** I will turn toward you and make you fruitful and numerous, and I will uphold My covenant with you.

**26:10** You will eat old provisions long stored, and you will carry out the old because of the new.

**26:11** And I will set My dwelling place in your midst, and My soul will not loathe you.

**26:12** And I will walk about in your midst, and I will be God to you, and you will be a people to Me.

**26:13** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt so that you would not be slaves to them; and I broke the bars of your yoke and caused you to walk upright.

**26:14** But if you do not listen to Me and do not carry out all these commandments,

**26:15** and if you reject My statutes, and if your soul loathes My judgments, so that you do not carry out all My commandments and break My covenant,

**26:16** then I also will do this to you: I will appoint terror over you, wasting disease and burning fever that make the eyes fail and cause the soul to languish; and you will sow your seed in vain, and your enemies will eat it.

**26:17** And I will set My face against you, and you will be defeated before your enemies; those who hate you will dominate you, and you will flee though no one pursues you.

**26:18** And if even after these things you do not listen to Me, then I will discipline you further, sevenfold for your sins.

**26:19** I will break the pride of your might, and I will make your sky like iron and your land like bronze.

**26:20** Your strength will be exhausted in vain, and your land will not yield its produce, nor will the trees of the land yield their fruit.

**26:21** And if you walk in opposition to Me and are unwilling to listen to Me, then I will add further plagues upon you, sevenfold according to your sins.

**26:22** And I will unleash the wild beasts of the field against you, and they will bereave you of children, cut off your livestock, and make you few, and your roads will become desolate.

**26:23** And if through these things you are not disciplined by Me, but walk in opposition to Me,

**26:24** then I too will walk in opposition to you, and I Myself will strike you sevenfold for your sins.

**26:25** And I will bring upon you a sword wreaking the vengeance of the covenant; and when you gather into your cities, I will send pestilence into your midst, and you will be given into the hand of an enemy.

**26:26** When I break the staff of bread for you, ten women will bake your bread in one oven and bring back your bread by weight, and you will eat, yet you will not be satisfied.

**26:27** And if despite this you do not listen to Me, but walk in opposition to Me,

**26:28** then I will walk in furious opposition to you, and I too will discipline you sevenfold for your sins.

**26:29** And you will eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you will eat.

**26:30** And I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars, and I will lay your corpses upon the corpses of your idols; and My soul will loathe you.

**26:31** And I will turn your cities into ruins and make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will not smell your soothing fragrance.

**26:32** And I Myself will make the land desolate, so that your enemies who dwell in it will be appalled at it.

**26:33** And you I will scatter among the nations, and I will unsheathe a sword after you; and your land will become a desolation, and your cities will become ruins.

**26:34** Then the land will make up for its Sabbaths all the days it lies desolate, while you are in the land of your enemies; then the land will rest and repay its Sabbaths.

**26:35** All the days it lies desolate it will keep the rest it did not keep on your Sabbaths, while you dwelt upon it.

**26:36** And as for those among you who remain, I will bring faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies; the sound of a driven leaf will pursue them, and they will flee as though fleeing a sword, and they will fall though no one pursues.

**26:37** And they will stumble over one another as if before a sword, though there is no pursuer; and you will have no ability to stand before your enemies.

**26:38** And you will perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies will devour you.

**26:39** And those among you who remain will waste away in their iniquity in the lands of your enemies, and also in the iniquities of their fathers they will waste away with them.

**26:40** Yet if they confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, in their unfaithfulness by which they broke faith with Me, and also that they walked in opposition to Me,

**26:41** so that I too walked in opposition to them and brought them into the land of their enemies; if then their uncircumcised heart is humbled, and then they pay for their iniquity,

**26:42** then I will remember My covenant with Jacob, and also My covenant with Isaac, and also My covenant with Abraham I will remember, and the land I will remember.

**26:43** Yet the land will be abandoned by them and will make up for its Sabbaths while it lies desolate without them, and they themselves will pay for their iniquity, precisely because they rejected My judgments and their soul loathed My statutes.

**26:44** Yet even for all this, while they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them, nor will I loathe them so as to destroy them completely, breaking My covenant with them; for I am the LORD their God.

**26:45** But for their sake I will remember the covenant of the ancestors, whom I brought out from the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, to be God to them; I am the LORD."

**26:46** These are the statutes and the judgments and the laws that the LORD set between Himself and the sons of Israel at Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **26:21** — The rare noun rendered 'opposition' occurs in the Hebrew Bible only in Leviticus 26 (vv. 21, 23, 24, 27, 28, 40, 41). Derived from a root meaning 'encounter' or 'run counter to,' it denotes defiant contrariety or hostility, though some ancient interpreters took it as treating God's deeds as mere chance.
- **26:30** — The Hebrew noun rendered 'incense altars' was historically associated with the sun, but West Semitic archaeological inscriptions identify the term as an incense altar or brazier.
- **26:34** — The verb rendered 'make up for' (Qal) and 'repay' (a rare Hiphil perfect form) reflects the restitution sense of paying off an unpaid debt, linking the land's Sabbaths with Israel paying for its iniquity in vv. 41 and 43; some take the Qal here in the homonymous sense 'enjoy.' The form rendered 'it lies desolate' (also in vv. 35, 43) is a rare Hophal infinitive construct of the root meaning 'be desolate.'
- **26:41** — The Hebrew imperfect verb 'walked' expresses the past consequence of Israel's opposition in v. 40, and the compound conjunction 'if then' resumes the conditional clause before the covenant promise in v. 42.
- **26:42** — Literally 'My covenant, Jacob' (and similarly with Isaac and Abraham), an unusual Hebrew construction in which a suffixed noun stands directly before a proper name to mean 'My covenant with Jacob.'
