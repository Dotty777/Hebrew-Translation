## Text

**11:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them,

**11:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'These are the living creatures that you may eat from among all the beasts that are upon the earth:

**11:3** Any among the beasts that has a divided hoof, cleaving a split completely through the hooves, and brings up the cud—that you may eat.

**11:4** However, these you must not eat from among those that bring up the cud or those that divide the hoof: the camel, because it brings up the cud yet does not divide the hoof—it is unclean for you;

**11:5** and the rock hyrax, because it brings up the cud yet does not divide the hoof—it is unclean for you;

**11:6** and the hare, because it brings up the cud yet does not divide the hoof—it is unclean for you;

**11:7** and the pig, because it divides the hoof and cleaves a split completely through the hoof, yet it does not chew the cud—it is unclean for you.

**11:8** You must not eat any of their flesh, nor touch their carcass; they are unclean for you.

**11:9** These you may eat from everything that is in the water: anything in the water, in the seas or in the streams, that has fins and scales—those you may eat.

**11:10** But everything in the seas and in the streams that lacks fins and scales, out of all the swarming creatures of the water and every living creature in the water—they are abhorrent to you.

**11:11** Abhorrent they must remain to you; you must not eat any of their flesh, and you must regard their carcass as abhorrent.

**11:12** Everything in the water that lacks fins and scales is abhorrent to you.

**11:13** And among the birds, these you must regard as abhorrent; they are not to be eaten, they are abhorrent: the eagle, the bearded vulture, and the black vulture,

**11:14** the kite, and the falcon according to its kind,

**11:15** every raven according to its kind,

**11:16** the ostrich, the nighthawk, the seagull, and the hawk according to its kind,

**11:17** the little owl, the cormorant, and the great owl,

**11:18** the barn owl, the desert owl, and the carrion vulture,

**11:19** the stork, the heron according to its kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

**11:20** Every winged swarming creature that walks on all fours is abhorrent to you.

**11:21** However, these you may eat from among all winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours: those that have jointed legs above their feet for leaping upon the earth.

**11:22** Of them, you may eat these: the locust according to its kind, the bald locust according to its kind, the cricket according to its kind, and the grasshopper according to its kind.

**11:23** But every winged swarming creature that has four feet is abhorrent to you.

**11:24** And through these you will become unclean: anyone touching their carcass will be unclean until evening,

**11:25** and anyone carrying any part of their carcass must wash his garments and remain unclean until evening.

**11:26** Regarding every beast that divides the hoof yet does not cleave a split through it, and does not bring up the cud—they are unclean for you; anyone touching them will become unclean.

**11:27** And everything walking on its paws among all the living creatures that walk on all fours—they are unclean for you; anyone touching their carcass will be unclean until evening,

**11:28** and the one carrying their carcass must wash his garments and remain unclean until evening; they are unclean for you.

**11:29** Now these are what are unclean for you among the swarming creatures that swarm upon the earth: the mole rat, the mouse, and the great lizard according to its kind,

**11:30** the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink, and the chameleon.

**11:31** These are the unclean ones for you among all the swarming creatures; anyone touching them once they are dead will be unclean until evening.

**11:32** Furthermore, anything on which one of them falls when they are dead will become unclean—whether any wooden item, garment, leather, or sackcloth, any article with which work is performed; it must be put into water and remain unclean until evening, and then it will be clean.

**11:33** And if one of them falls inside any clay vessel, everything inside it will become unclean, and the vessel itself you must break.

**11:34** Any food that may be eaten on which water comes will become unclean, and any drink that may be drunk in any vessel will become unclean.

**11:35** Everything on which any part of their carcass falls will become unclean; an oven or a cooking stove must be broken down—they are unclean, and unclean they will remain for you.

**11:36** Yet a spring or a cistern, a gathering of water, will remain clean, though anyone touching their carcass will become unclean.

**11:37** And if any part of their carcass falls on any sowing seed that is to be sown, it remains clean.

**11:38** But if water has been put on seed and any part of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean for you.

**11:39** Now if one of the beasts that is for you to eat dies, the one touching its carcass will be unclean until evening.

**11:40** The one eating any of its carcass must wash his garments and remain unclean until evening, and the one carrying its carcass must wash his garments and remain unclean until evening.

**11:41** Now every swarming creature that swarms upon the earth is abhorrent; it must not be eaten.

**11:42** Everything that goes on its belly, and everything that walks on all fours, even up to everything with many feet among all the swarming creatures that swarm upon the earth—you must not eat them, because they are abhorrent.

**11:43** Do not make yourselves abhorrent through any swarming creature that swarms, and do not make yourselves unclean by them so that you become defiled by them.

**11:44** Because I am the LORD your God, sanctify yourselves and become holy, since I am holy; and do not make yourselves unclean through any swarming creature that creeps upon the earth.

**11:45** Because I am the LORD, the One bringing you up out of the land of Egypt to be God to you; so you must be holy, because I am holy.

**11:46** This is the law concerning the beasts, the birds, every living creature that moves in the water, and every creature that swarms upon the earth,

**11:47** to distinguish between what is unclean and what is clean, and between the living creature that may be eaten and the living creature that must not be eaten.'"

## Translator Notes

- **11:13** — In verses 13-19, the Hebrew noun oph covers all winged creatures, including the bat, and exact species identifications for several of the rarer birds remain uncertain.
- **11:21** — The written ketiv reads lo meaning not, whereas the read qere translated here reads lo meaning to it, yielding that have jointed legs.
- **11:43** — The verb wenitmetem is a Niphal weqatal of the root for uncleanness written defectively without the final aleph, highlighting the resulting state of defilement.
