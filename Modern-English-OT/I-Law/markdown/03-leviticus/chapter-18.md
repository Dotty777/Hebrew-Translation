## Text

**18:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and tell them, 'I am the LORD your God.

**18:3** You must not do according to the practice of the land of Egypt, where you lived, and you must not do according to the practice of the land of Canaan, into which I am bringing you; and you must not walk in their statutes.

**18:4** My ordinances you shall do, and My statutes you shall keep, to walk in them; I am the LORD your God.

**18:5** And you shall keep My statutes and My ordinances; a person who does them will live by them. I am the LORD.

**18:6** No man among you shall approach any close kin of his flesh to uncover nakedness; I am the LORD.

**18:7** You must not uncover the nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother; she is your mother; you must not uncover her nakedness.

**18:8** You must not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife; it is the nakedness of your father.

**18:9** As for the nakedness of your sister, the daughter of your father or the daughter of your mother, whether born at home or born outside, you must not uncover their nakedness.

**18:10** As for the nakedness of your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter, you must not uncover their nakedness, for they are your nakedness.

**18:11** As for the nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife, born to your father—she is your sister—you must not uncover her nakedness.

**18:12** You must not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister; she is your father's close kin.

**18:13** You must not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister, for she is your mother's close kin.

**18:14** You must not uncover the nakedness of your father's brother; you must not approach his wife; she is your aunt.

**18:15** You must not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law; she is your son's wife; you must not uncover her nakedness.

**18:16** You must not uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife; it is the nakedness of your brother.

**18:17** You must not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter; you must not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to uncover her nakedness; they are close kin; it is depravity.

**18:18** And you must not take a woman in addition to her sister as a rival, to uncover her nakedness alongside her during her lifetime.

**18:19** And you must not approach a woman during her menstrual uncleanness to uncover her nakedness.

**18:20** And with your neighbor's wife you must not lie sexually, to become defiled by her.

**18:21** And you must not give any of your offspring to make them pass over to Molech, and you must not profane the name of your God; I am the LORD.

**18:22** And with a male you must not lie as one lies with a woman; it is an abomination.

**18:23** And with any animal you must not lie sexually, to become defiled by it; and a woman must not stand before an animal for it to mate with her; it is perversion.

**18:24** Do not defile yourselves by any of these things, for by all these things the nations whom I am expelling from before you have become defiled.

**18:25** And the land became defiled, so I visited its iniquity upon it, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.

**18:26** But you yourselves shall keep My statutes and My ordinances, and you must not commit any of these abominations, neither the native-born nor the resident alien who resides in your midst—

**18:27** for the men of the land who were before you committed all these abominations, and the land became defiled—

**18:28** so that the land will not vomit you out when you defile it, just as it vomited out the nation that was before you.

**18:29** For anyone who commits any of these abominations, the persons who commit them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.

**18:30** Therefore you shall keep My charge, so as not to practice any of the abominable statutes that were practiced before you, and you must not defile yourselves by them; I am the LORD your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — The Hebrew pairs the distributive singular phrase "any man ... his flesh" with the second-person plural verb lo' tiqrevu ("you shall not approach"); she'er besaro is literally "flesh of his flesh," an idiom for close blood kin.
- **18:18** — The Qal infinitive construct litsror derives from tsarar ("to be a rival wife" or "to create rivalry," cognate with tsarah, "rival wife," in 1 Sam 1:6).
- **18:20** — Literally, "you must not give your lying for seed" (lo'-titten shekhavtekha lezara'), an idiom for sexual intercourse; the noun zera' ("seed") also creates a verbal link to "your offspring" (mizzar'akha) in v. 21.
- **18:21** — The Hiphil infinitive construct leha'avir (literally "to cause to pass over") appears here without the phrase "through the fire" found in parallel texts (e.g., Deut 18:10; 2 Kgs 23:10), denoting dedication or child sacrifice to Molech.
- **18:30** — Hebrew uses the same noun huqqot ("statutes") for the nations' "abominable statutes" (cf. v. 3) as for the LORD's "statutes" in vv. 4, 5, and 26, underscoring the deliberate contrast between them.
