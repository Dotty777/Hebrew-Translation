## Text

**16:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses following the death of the two sons of Aaron, when they came near before the LORD and died.

**16:2** And the LORD said to Moses, "Speak to your brother Aaron that he not come at just any time into the holy place inside the curtain, before the atonement cover that is upon the ark, so that he does not die; for I will appear in the cloud above the atonement cover.

**16:3** With this Aaron shall come into the holy place: with a young bull from the herd for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering.

**16:4** He shall put on a holy linen tunic, and linen undergarments shall be upon his flesh; he shall fasten a linen sash around himself and wrap on a linen turban. These are holy garments, so he shall bathe his flesh in water and put them on.

**16:5** And from the congregation of the sons of Israel he shall take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.

**16:6** Then Aaron shall present the bull of the sin offering that is for himself, and make atonement for himself and for his household.

**16:7** He shall take the two male goats and station them before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**16:8** And Aaron shall cast lots over the two male goats, one lot for the LORD and one lot for Azazel.

**16:9** Then Aaron shall present the goat on which the lot came up for the LORD and offer it as a sin offering.

**16:10** But the goat on which the lot came up for Azazel shall be stationed alive before the LORD to make atonement over it, in order to send it away to Azazel into the wilderness.

**16:11** Then Aaron shall present the bull of the sin offering that is for himself and make atonement for himself and for his household, and he shall slaughter the bull of the sin offering that is for himself.

**16:12** He shall take a censer full of coals of fire from the altar before the LORD, and his two handfuls of finely ground fragrant incense, and bring them inside the curtain.

**16:13** He shall put the incense on the fire before the LORD, and the cloud of incense shall cover the atonement cover that is over the testimony, so that he does not die.

**16:14** He shall take some of the blood of the bull and sprinkle it with his finger on the front of the atonement cover on the east side; and in front of the atonement cover he shall sprinkle some of the blood with his finger seven times.

**16:15** Then he shall slaughter the goat of the sin offering that is for the people and bring its blood inside the curtain, and do with its blood just as he did with the blood of the bull, and sprinkle it upon the atonement cover and in front of the atonement cover.

**16:16** So he shall make atonement for the holy place on account of the uncleannesses of the sons of Israel and on account of their transgressions, regarding all their sins; and likewise he shall do for the tent of meeting, which resides with them in the midst of their uncleannesses.

**16:17** No person shall be in the tent of meeting when he enters to make atonement in the holy place until he comes out; and he shall make atonement for himself, for his household, and for the entire assembly of Israel.

**16:18** Then he shall go out to the altar that is before the LORD and make atonement for it; he shall take some of the blood of the bull and some of the blood of the goat and put it on the horns of the altar all around.

**16:19** With his finger he shall sprinkle some of the blood upon it seven times, and he shall cleanse it and sanctify it from the uncleannesses of the sons of Israel.

**16:20** When he has finished making atonement for the holy place, the tent of meeting, and the altar, he shall present the live goat.

**16:21** Then Aaron shall lay both of his hands on the head of the live goat and confess over it all the iniquities of the sons of Israel and all their transgressions, in all their sins; and he shall put them on the head of the goat and send it away into the wilderness by the hand of a designated man.

**16:22** The goat shall carry upon itself all their iniquities to an isolated land, and he shall release the goat in the wilderness.

**16:23** Then Aaron shall come into the tent of meeting and take off the linen garments that he had put on when he went into the holy place, and leave them there.

**16:24** He shall bathe his flesh in water in a holy place and put on his garments; then he shall come out and offer his burnt offering and the burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for the people.

**16:25** And he shall turn the fat of the sin offering into smoke on the altar.

**16:26** And the one who releases the goat for Azazel shall wash his garments and bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he may enter the camp.

**16:27** And the bull of the sin offering and the goat of the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, shall be carried outside the camp, and they shall burn with fire their hides, their flesh, and their dung.

**16:28** Then the one who burns them shall wash his garments and bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he may enter the camp.

**16:29** And this shall be a perpetual statute for you: in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month, you shall afflict your souls and shall not do any work, neither the native nor the resident alien who resides among you.

**16:30** For on this day atonement shall be made for you to cleanse you; from all your sins you shall be clean before the LORD.

**16:31** It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you shall afflict your souls; it is a perpetual statute.

**16:32** And the priest who is anointed and ordained to serve as priest in place of his father shall make atonement, and he shall put on the linen garments, the holy garments.

**16:33** He shall make atonement for the holy sanctuary, and he shall make atonement for the tent of meeting and for the altar; he shall also make atonement for the priests and for all the people of the assembly.

**16:34** And this shall be a perpetual statute for you, to make atonement for the sons of Israel for all their sins once a year." And he did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **16:2** — Hebrew hakkapporet ("atonement cover") is derived from the root kafar ("cover" or "atone").
- **16:8** — The meaning of Hebrew Azazel is uncertain; it has been understood variously as a wilderness figure or demon, a remote rugged place, or a term signifying complete removal ("the goat that departs").
- **16:21** — Hebrew ish itti uses the hapax legomenon adjective itti (from et, "time"), meaning a man ready or designated for the occasion. In addition, yadav ("his hands") is written defectively without yod in the Masoretic Text.
- **16:22** — Hebrew erets gezerah (from gazar, "cut off") literally means "a cut-off land," denoting an isolated, uninhabited region.
- **16:32** — Literally "whose hand one fills," using the Hebrew idiom mille yad ("fill the hand") for priestly ordination; both yimshach ("anoint") and yemalle ("fill") are indefinite third-person singular active verbs rendered passively.
