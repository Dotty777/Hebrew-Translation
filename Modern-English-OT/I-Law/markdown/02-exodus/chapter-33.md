## Text

**33:1** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, "Go, ascend from here, you and the people whom you brought up from the land of Egypt, to the land concerning which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, 'To your seed I will give it'—

**33:2** and I will send an angel ahead of you, and I will expel the Canaanites, the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—

**33:3** to a land flowing with milk and honey. For I will not ascend in your midst, because you are a stiff-necked people, or else I might consume you along the way."

**33:4** When the people heard this grim word, they mourned, and not a man put his ornaments upon himself.

**33:5** The LORD said to Moses, "Say to the sons of Israel, 'You are a stiff-necked people. Were I to ascend in your midst for a single moment, I would consume you. So now, remove your ornaments from upon you, so that I may know what I will do to you.'"

**33:6** So the sons of Israel stripped off their own ornaments, from Mount Horeb on.

**33:7** Now Moses would take the tent and pitch it for himself outside the camp, far away from the camp, and he called it the tent of meeting. Whenever anyone was seeking the LORD, he would go out to the tent of meeting that was outside the camp.

**33:8** Whenever Moses went out to the tent, all the people would get up and station themselves, each man at the entrance of his tent, and they would look after Moses until he went into the tent.

**33:9** And as Moses went into the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the entrance of the tent, and He would speak with Moses.

**33:10** All the people would see the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, and all the people would rise and bow down, each man at the entrance of his tent.

**33:11** And the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, the way a man speaks to his friend. Afterward he would return to the camp, while his attendant Joshua the son of Nun, a young man, would not depart from within the tent.

**33:12** Then Moses said to the LORD, "See, You are saying to me, 'Bring this people up,' yet You have not made known to me whom You will send with me. Still You have said, 'I know you by name, and you have also found favor in My eyes.'

**33:13** Now then, if I have found favor in Your eyes, please make Your ways known to me so that I may know You, in order that I may find favor in Your eyes; and see that this nation is Your people."

**33:14** He said, "My presence will go, and I will grant you rest."

**33:15** Then he said to Him, "If Your presence does not go, do not bring us up from here.

**33:16** And by what, then, will it be known that I have found favor in Your eyes, I and Your people? Is it not through Your going with us, so that we are set apart, I and Your people, from every people upon the face of the ground?"

**33:17** The LORD said to Moses, "This thing also, which you have spoken, I will do, because you have found favor in My eyes and I know you by name."

**33:18** Then he said, "Please let me see Your glory."

**33:19** He said, "I Myself will make all My goodness pass before your face, and I will proclaim the name of the LORD before you; and I will show favor to whomever I show favor, and I will have compassion on whomever I have compassion."

**33:20** Yet He said, "You are not able to see My face, for no human can see Me and live."

**33:21** Then the LORD said, "Here is a place beside Me; station yourself upon the rock.

**33:22** When My glory is passing by, I will place you in the crevice of the rock, and I will shield you with My palm until I have passed by.

**33:23** Then I will remove My palm, and you will see My back; yet My face will not be seen."

## Translator Notes

- **33:6** — The preposition in mehar Chorev ("from Mount Horeb") has pregnant force, marking the starting point in place and time ("from Mount Horeb on").
- **33:7** — Verses 7-11 use Hebrew imperfect (yiqtol) and waw-consecutive perfect (weqatal) verbs throughout to depict repeated, customary actions in the past ("would take," "would go out," "would speak").
- **33:14** — "My presence" renders Hebrew panay, literally "My face," linking God's promise here and in v. 15 directly to the face imagery of vv. 11, 20, and 23. Hebrew has no words corresponding to "with you" here or in v. 15.
- **33:19** — The double idem per idem construction repeats Qal forms of chanan ("show favor, be gracious") and Piel forms of racham ("have compassion"), expressing God's sovereign freedom in bestowing mercy.
