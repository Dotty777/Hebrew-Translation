## Text

**38:1** Then Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood: two and a half cubits was its length, one and a half cubits its width, and one and a half cubits its height.

**38:2** He overlaid it with pure gold on the inside and on the outside, and he made for it a molding of gold all around.

**38:3** He cast for it four gold rings on its four feet, two rings on its one side and two rings on its other side.

**38:4** And he inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark to carry the ark.

**38:5** He made an atonement cover of pure gold: two and a half cubits was its length, and one and a half cubits its width.

**38:6** He made two cherubim of gold; of hammered work he made them, at the two ends of the atonement cover.

**38:7** One cherub was at the end on one side and one cherub at the end on the other side; out of the atonement cover he made the cherubim at its two ends.

**38:8** The cherubim had their wings spread upward, shielding the atonement cover with their wings, and their faces were toward one another; toward the atonement cover were the faces of the cherubim.

**38:9** Then he made the table of acacia wood: two cubits was its length, one cubit its width, and one and a half cubits its height.

**38:10** He cast for it four gold rings and placed the rings on the four corners that belonged to its four legs. Alongside the rim were the rings, as holders for the poles to carry the table.

**38:11** And he made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold to carry the table.

**38:12** He made the utensils that were on the table of pure gold: its platters, its ladles, its bowls, and the pitchers with which drink offerings were to be poured.

**38:13** He made the lampstand of pure gold. Of hammered work he made the lampstand, its base and its shaft; its cups, its bulbs, and its flowers were one piece with it.

**38:15** And six branches extended from its sides: three lampstand branches from its one side and three lampstand branches from its other side.

**38:16** There were three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the one branch, with a bulb and a flower, and three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the other branch, with a bulb and a flower; so it was for the six branches extending from the lampstand.

**38:17** He made its seven lamps, and its tongs and its firepans, of pure gold.

**38:18** He overlaid the planks with gold and made their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and he overlaid the bars with gold.

**38:22** Then he made the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood: five cubits was its length and five cubits its width—it was square—and three cubits its height.

**38:23** He made all the utensils of the altar: the pots, the shovels, the sprinkling bowls, the forks, and the firepans; all its utensils he made of bronze.

**38:24** He made for the altar a grating of bronze meshwork, beneath its ledge from below to its midpoint. And he cast four rings at the four corners of the bronze grating as holders for the poles.

**38:25** He made the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of spices, the work of a perfumer.

**38:26** He made the bronze basin and its bronze stand from the mirrors of the serving women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**38:27** And from it Moses and Aaron and his sons would wash their hands and their feet. Whenever they entered the tent of meeting and when they approached the altar, they would wash, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **38:5** — Hebrew kapporet ('atonement cover') derives from the root k-p-r ('to atone/cover') and denotes the golden lid over the ark associated with atonement.
- **38:7** — Ketiv writes qtswwtw, lacking the yod of the plural possessive suffix, while qere reads the standard plural form qetsotav ('its ends'); both carry the same sense, using a masculine suffix for the grammatically feminine kapporet ('atonement cover').
- **38:26** — The Qal participle and perfect of tsava ('to serve/perform assigned duty') describe women performing organized service at the entrance of the tent of meeting; mar'ot denotes polished bronze mirrors.
- **38:27** — The Qal weqatal ('would wash') followed by the Qal yiqtol ('would wash') expresses repeated, customary action whenever Moses, Aaron, and his sons entered the tent or approached the altar.
- **37:12 *(additional)*** — A handbreadth was roughly three inches, or about seven and a half centimeters.
- **37:24 *(additional)*** — A talent weighed roughly seventy-five pounds, or about thirty-four kilograms.
