## Text

**14:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**14:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel that they turn back and encamp before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea; before Baal-zephon, directly opposite it, you shall encamp beside the sea.

**14:3** And Pharaoh will say about the sons of Israel, 'They are wandering bewildered in the land; the wilderness has closed in on them.'

**14:4** And I will harden Pharaoh's heart, and he will pursue after them; then I will win glory over Pharaoh and over all his army, and the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD." And they did so.

**14:5** And it was reported to the king of Egypt that the people had fled; then the heart of Pharaoh and his servants turned against the people, and they said, "What is this that we have done, that we have sent Israel away from serving us?"

**14:6** So he harnessed his chariot and took his people with him.

**14:7** He took six hundred select chariots and all the chariots of Egypt, with officers over them all.

**14:8** And the LORD hardened the heart of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, so that he pursued after the sons of Israel; meanwhile, the sons of Israel were going out with a raised hand.

**14:9** The Egyptians pursued after them and overtook them encamped beside the sea—all the chariot horses of Pharaoh, his horsemen, and his army—by Pi-hahiroth, before Baal-zephon.

**14:10** And as Pharaoh approached, the sons of Israel lifted up their eyes, and look, the Egyptians were marching after them; and they were greatly afraid, and the sons of Israel cried out to the LORD.

**14:11** And they said to Moses, "Was it for lack of any graves in Egypt that you took us to die in the wilderness? What is this that you have done to us, bringing us out of Egypt?

**14:12** Is not this the word we spoke to you in Egypt, saying, 'Let us alone so that we may serve the Egyptians'? For it is better for us to serve the Egyptians than for us to die in the wilderness."

**14:13** Then Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid; take your stand and see the salvation of the LORD that He will perform for you today, for the way you have seen the Egyptians today, you will not see them again forever.

**14:14** The LORD will fight on your behalf, and you shall remain silent."

**14:15** And the LORD said to Moses, "Why do you cry out to Me? Speak to the sons of Israel that they set out.

**14:16** And you, raise your staff and stretch out your hand over the sea and split it, so that the sons of Israel may go into the midst of the sea on dry ground.

**14:17** And as for Me, look, I am about to harden the heart of the Egyptians so that they will go in after them; and I will win glory over Pharaoh and over all his army, over his chariots and over his horsemen.

**14:18** Then the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD, when I win glory over Pharaoh, over his chariots and over his horsemen."

**14:19** Now the angel of God, who was going ahead of the camp of Israel, moved and went behind them; the pillar of cloud also moved from ahead of them and stood behind them.

**14:20** So it came between the camp of Egypt and the camp of Israel; and there was the cloud and the darkness, yet it lit up the night, so that the one did not come near the other all night.

**14:21** Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the LORD pushed the sea back with a fierce east wind all night and made the sea into dry land; and the waters were split.

**14:22** So the sons of Israel went into the midst of the sea on dry ground, with the waters as a wall for them on their right and on their left.

**14:23** The Egyptians pursued and went in after them—all of Pharaoh's horses, his chariots, and his horsemen—into the midst of the sea.

**14:24** And it happened in the morning watch that the LORD looked down toward the camp of Egypt in the pillar of fire and cloud, and threw the camp of Egypt into confusion.

**14:25** He removed the wheels of their chariots so that they drove them with difficulty; and the Egyptians said, "Let us flee from before Israel, for the LORD is fighting for them against Egypt!"

**14:26** And the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, that the waters may return over the Egyptians, over their chariots, and over their horsemen."

**14:27** So Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and at the approach of morning the sea returned to its steady flow while the Egyptians were fleeing to meet it; and the LORD shook the Egyptians into the midst of the sea.

**14:28** And the waters returned and covered the chariots and the horsemen—all of Pharaoh's army that had gone after them into the sea; not a single one among them was left.

**14:29** Yet the sons of Israel walked on dry ground in the midst of the sea, with the waters as a wall for them on their right and on their left.

**14:30** And on that day the LORD saved Israel out of the hand of Egypt, and Israel saw the Egyptians dead along the shore of the sea.

**14:31** And Israel saw the great hand that the LORD exercised against Egypt, and the people feared the LORD, and they trusted in the LORD and in Moses His servant.

## Translator Notes

- **14:11** — Hebrew stacks two negatives, hamibbeli ein (lit. "from lack of there-not-being"), for emphatic sarcasm.
- **14:13** — Hebrew asher re'item et-mitsrayim can be read adverbially ("the way you have seen the Egyptians," contrasting with seeing them dead in v. 30) or relatively ("the Egyptians whom you have seen").
- **14:20** — Translates the Masoretic text directly (wayhi he'anan wehahoshekh wayya'er et-hallaylah), which concisely captures the cloud's dual effect of darkness and illumination without harmonizing to ancient versions.
- **14:25** — The Masoretic text reads wayyasar (Hiphil of sur, "removed" or "turned aside"), rather than the Samaritan/Septuagint variant "bound/clogged." The noun kevedut ("difficulty/heaviness") shares the root k-v-d with the LORD's "winning glory" in vv. 4, 17, and 18.
- **14:27** — Hebrew etan denotes a perennial, steady flow or normal state of water, and the Piel wayna'er (from na'ar) vividly pictures the LORD shaking the Egyptians off into the sea (cf. Ps 136:15).
