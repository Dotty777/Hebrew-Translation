## Text

**16:1** And they set out from Elim, and the entire congregation of the sons of Israel arrived at the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after they went out from the land of Egypt.

**16:2** And the entire congregation of the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and against Aaron in the wilderness.

**16:3** And the sons of Israel said to them, "If only we had died by the hand of the LORD in the land of Egypt, when we sat beside the pot of meat, when we ate bread to satisfaction; for you have brought us out into this wilderness to put this entire assembly to death by hunger."

**16:4** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Look, I am going to rain bread from heaven for you; and the people shall go out and gather each day's portion on its day, in order that I may test them, whether they will walk in My instruction or not.

**16:5** And it shall be on the sixth day that they will prepare what they bring in, and it will be double what they gather day by day."

**16:6** Then Moses and Aaron said to all the sons of Israel, "In the evening you will know that the LORD brought you out from the land of Egypt,

**16:7** and in the morning you will see the glory of the LORD, because He hears your grumblings against the LORD. As for us, what are we that you grumble against us?"

**16:8** And Moses said, "When the LORD gives you meat to eat in the evening and bread in the morning to be satisfied, because the LORD hears your grumblings that you are grumbling against Him—as for us, what are we? Your grumblings are not against us, but against the LORD."

**16:9** And Moses said to Aaron, "Say to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, 'Draw near before the LORD, for He has heard your grumblings.'"

**16:10** And as Aaron was speaking to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, they turned toward the wilderness, and look, the glory of the LORD appeared in the cloud.

**16:11** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:12** "I have heard the grumblings of the sons of Israel. Speak to them, saying, 'At twilight you will eat meat, and in the morning you will be satisfied with bread; then you will know that I am the LORD your God.'"

**16:13** So it was in the evening that quail came up and covered the camp, and in the morning a layer of dew lay around the camp.

**16:14** And when the layer of dew lifted, look, on the surface of the wilderness there was something fine and flaky, as fine as frost on the ground.

**16:15** When the sons of Israel saw it, they said one to another, "What is it?" because they did not know what it was. And Moses said to them, "It is the bread that the LORD has given you for food.

**16:16** This is the word that the LORD has commanded: 'Gather from it, each one according to what he eats, an omer per head, according to the number of your persons; each of you shall take for those who are in his tent.'"

**16:17** And the sons of Israel did so; they gathered, some gathering much and some little.

**16:18** And when they measured it by the omer, the one who gathered much had no surplus, and the one who gathered little had no shortage; each one had gathered according to what he ate.

**16:19** And Moses said to them, "No one is to leave any of it until morning."

**16:20** Yet they did not listen to Moses; some men left part of it until morning, and it teemed with worms and turned foul, and Moses grew angry with them.

**16:21** So they gathered it every morning, each one according to what he ate; and whenever the sun grew hot, it melted away.

**16:22** And on the sixth day they gathered double the bread, two omers for each person; and all the leaders of the congregation came and reported it to Moses.

**16:23** And he said to them, "It is what the LORD has spoken: Tomorrow is a solemn rest, a holy Sabbath to the LORD. What you will bake, bake, and what you will boil, boil; and set aside all the surplus for yourselves for safekeeping until the morning."

**16:24** So they set it aside until the morning, as Moses had commanded, and it did not turn foul, and there were no maggots in it.

**16:25** And Moses said, "Eat it today, because today is a Sabbath to the LORD; you will not find it in the field today.

**16:26** For six days you are to gather it, but on the seventh day, a Sabbath, there will be none."

**16:27** And on the seventh day some from among the people went out to gather, yet they did not find any.

**16:28** And the LORD said to Moses, "How long will you refuse to observe My commandments and My instructions?

**16:29** See that the LORD has given you the Sabbath; for that reason He is giving you two days' bread on the sixth day. Stay each of you right where he is; no one is to go out from his place on the seventh day."

**16:30** And the people rested on the seventh day.

**16:31** And the house of Israel called its name manna; it was white like coriander seed, and its taste was like a wafer with honey.

**16:32** And Moses said, "This is the word that the LORD has commanded: 'A full omer of it is for safekeeping throughout your generations, in order that they may see the bread that I fed you in the wilderness when I brought you out from the land of Egypt.'"

**16:33** And Moses said to Aaron, "Take one jar and put a full omer of manna into it, and set it before the LORD for safekeeping throughout your generations."

**16:34** Just as the LORD had commanded Moses, so Aaron set it before the testimony for safekeeping.

**16:35** And the sons of Israel ate the manna for forty years, until they reached an inhabited land; they ate the manna until they reached the edge of the land of Canaan.

**16:36** Now an omer is one-tenth of an ephah.

## Translator Notes

- **16:2** — Ketiv reads the Hiphil wayyallinu while the Masoretic Qere reads the Niphal wayyillonu, both meaning 'they grumbled'; the translation follows the Qere.
- **16:7** — Ketiv reads the Niphal tillonu while the Masoretic Qere reads the Hiphil tallinu, both meaning 'you grumble'; the translation follows the Qere.
- **16:14** — Hebrew mechuspas is a hapax legomenon quadrilateral passive participle, understood from Semitic cognates to mean 'flaky' or 'scale-like.'
- **16:15** — Hebrew man hu uses a rare dialectal or Aramaizing interrogative ('What is it?'), creating a wordplay with the name man ('manna') in 16:31.
- **16:20** — Hebrew wayyarum is a hapax verb meaning 'teem with worms' or 'breed worms,' related to the noun rimmah ('maggots') in 16:24.
