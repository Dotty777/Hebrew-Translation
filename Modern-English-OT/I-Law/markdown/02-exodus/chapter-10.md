## Text

**10:1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, for I Myself have made his heart heavy and the heart of his servants, so that I may set these signs of Mine in his midst,

**10:2** and so that you may recount in the ears of your son and your son's son how I dealt ruthlessly with Egypt, and My signs that I placed among them; and you will know that I am the LORD."

**10:3** So Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and said to him, "This is what the LORD, the God of the Hebrews, says: 'How long do you refuse to humble yourself before Me? Let My people go so that they may serve Me.

**10:4** For if you refuse to let My people go, look, tomorrow I am bringing locusts into your territory.

**10:5** And they will cover the surface of the land, so that no one will be able to see the land; and they will eat the remainder of what escaped, which was left for you from the hail, and they will eat every tree sprouting for you from the field.

**10:6** And your houses will be filled, and the houses of all your servants, and the houses of all Egypt, something that neither your fathers nor your fathers' fathers have seen from the day they came to be on the earth until this day.'" Then he turned and went out from Pharaoh.

**10:7** Then Pharaoh's servants said to him, "How long will this man be a snare to us? Let the men go so that they may serve the LORD their God. Do you not yet know that Egypt is ruined?"

**10:8** So Moses and Aaron were brought back to Pharaoh, and he said to them, "Go, serve the LORD your God. Who exactly are the ones going?"

**10:9** And Moses said, "With our young and with our old we will go; with our sons and with our daughters, with our flocks and with our herds we will go, for we have a feast to the LORD."

**10:10** Then he said to them, "So may the LORD be with you, just as I will let you and your little ones go! Look, evil is right before your face.

**10:11** Not so! Go now, you men, and serve the LORD, for that is what you are seeking." Then they were driven out from Pharaoh's presence.

**10:12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt for the locusts, so that they may come up over the land of Egypt and eat every plant of the land, everything that the hail left behind."

**10:13** So Moses stretched out his staff over the land of Egypt, and the LORD drove an east wind across the land all that day and all that night. When morning came, the east wind had carried in the locusts.

**10:14** And the locusts came up over all the land of Egypt and settled in all the territory of Egypt, extremely dense; before them there had never been locusts like them, and after them there will never be such again.

**10:15** They covered the surface of all the land, so that the land grew dark; and they ate every plant of the land and all the fruit of the trees that the hail had left over, so that nothing green was left over on the trees or among the plants of the field in all the land of Egypt.

**10:16** Then Pharaoh hurried to summon Moses and Aaron and said, "I have sinned against the LORD your God and against you.

**10:17** And now, please forgive my sin just this once, and plead with the LORD your God so that He may remove from me only this death."

**10:18** So he went out from Pharaoh and pleaded with the LORD.

**10:19** And the LORD turned the wind into a very strong west wind, and it lifted up the locusts and drove them into the Sea of Reeds; not a single locust remained in all the territory of Egypt.

**10:20** But the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not let the sons of Israel go.

**10:21** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, so that there may be darkness over the land of Egypt, darkness one can feel."

**10:22** So Moses stretched out his hand toward the sky, and there was dense darkness in all the land of Egypt for three days.

**10:23** They did not see one another, nor did anyone rise from his place for three days; but all the sons of Israel had light in their dwellings.

**10:24** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and said, "Go, serve the LORD; only let your flocks and your herds be left behind. Even your little ones may go with you."

**10:25** But Moses said, "You yourself must also put sacrifices and burnt offerings into our hands, so that we may offer them to the LORD our God.

**10:26** And our livestock too must go with us; not a hoof will be left behind, for we will take from them to serve the LORD our God, and we ourselves do not know with what we are to serve the LORD until we arrive there."

**10:27** But the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he was unwilling to let them go.

**10:28** Then Pharaoh said to him, "Get away from me! Watch yourself: do not see my face again, for on the day you see my face, you will die!"

**10:29** And Moses said, "You have spoken rightly; I will never see your face again."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Hiphil verb הִכְבַּדְתִּי ("made heavy") depicts Pharaoh's heart as dull and unresponsive, distinct from the Piel verb חִזֵּק ("strengthened/hardened") in 10:20, 27 and 11:10. The Masoretic singular suffix in בְּקִרְבּוֹ ("in his midst") refers to Pharaoh or his realm.
- **10:5** — "The surface of the land" translates the Hebrew idiom עֵין הָאָרֶץ, literally "the eye of the land" (also in 10:15).
- **10:10** — Pharaoh's opening clause is a sarcastic imprecation ("So may the LORD be with you, just as..."). The idiom רָעָה נֶגֶד פְּנֵיכֶם ("evil is right before your face") can mean either that they harbor evil intentions or that disaster awaits them directly ahead.
- **10:19** — Hebrew רוּחַ־יָם (literally "sea wind") denotes a west wind blowing from the Mediterranean. יָמָּה סּוּף is literally "to the Sea of Reeds," traditionally rendered "the Red Sea" following the Septuagint.
- **10:21** — וְיָמֵשׁ חֹשֶׁךְ ("darkness one can feel") uses an impersonal Hiphil jussive of the root מושׁ (a by-form of משׁשׁ, "to feel/grope"), literally "so that one may feel darkness."
