## Text

**40:1** Now the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**40:2** "On the day of the first month, on the first of the month, you shall set up the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.

**40:3** You shall place the ark of the testimony there, and screen the ark with the curtain.

**40:4** You shall bring in the table and lay out its arrangement, and bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.

**40:5** You shall put the gold altar for incense in front of the ark of the testimony, and place the screen for the entrance of the tabernacle.

**40:6** You shall put the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.

**40:8** You shall place the courtyard all around, and put up the screen of the courtyard gate.

**40:9** You shall take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and everything that is in it, and consecrate it and all its articles, and it will be holy.

**40:10** And you shall anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its articles, and consecrate the altar, and the altar will be most holy.

**40:12** You shall bring Aaron and his sons near to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and wash them with water.

**40:13** You shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and anoint him and consecrate him, so that he may serve as a priest to Me.

**40:14** And you shall bring his sons near and clothe them with tunics.

**40:15** You shall anoint them just as you anointed their father, so that they may serve as priests to Me; and their anointing is to be for them an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations."

**40:16** So Moses did; in accordance with everything that the LORD commanded him, so he did.

**40:17** It happened in the first month, in the second year, on the first of the month, that the tabernacle was set up.

**40:18** Moses set up the tabernacle: he put down its bases, placed its frames, put in its crossbars, and set up its pillars.

**40:19** He spread out the tent over the tabernacle and placed the covering of the tent over it on top, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**40:20** He took the testimony and put it into the ark, placed the poles on the ark, and put the atonement cover on the ark above.

**40:21** He brought the ark into the tabernacle, placed the screening curtain, and screened the ark of the testimony, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**40:22** He put the table in the tent of meeting, on the side of the tabernacle toward the north, outside the curtain.

**40:23** He laid out an arrangement of bread on it before the LORD, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**40:24** He placed the lampstand in the tent of meeting, opposite the table, on the side of the tabernacle toward the south.

**40:25** And he lit the lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**40:26** He placed the gold altar in the tent of meeting, in front of the curtain.

**40:27** And he burned aromatic incense on it, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**40:29** And he placed the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting, and offered up the burnt offering and the grain offering on it, just as the LORD commanded Moses.

**40:33** He set up the courtyard all around the tabernacle and the altar, and put up the screen of the courtyard gate. And Moses completed the work.

**40:34** Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, while the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

**40:35** And Moses was unable to go into the tent of meeting, because the cloud rested on it and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

**40:36** And whenever the cloud was lifted up from above the tabernacle, the sons of Israel would break camp throughout all their journeys.

**40:37** But if the cloud was not lifted up, then they would not break camp until the day it was lifted up.

**40:38** For the cloud of the LORD was over the tabernacle by day, and fire would be in it by night, before the eyes of the whole house of Israel throughout all their journeys.

## Translator Notes

- **40:2** — The Hebrew phrase literally reads "on the day of the first month," likely referring to the opening new-moon day of the first month, and is immediately specified by "on the first of the month."
- **40:4** — The Hiphil verb from the root alah (literally "cause to go up," also in 40:25) with lamps denotes either lighting them so the flame rises or mounting the detachable oil lamps atop the lampstand.
- **40:15** — The unusual Hebrew idiom combining a finite form of the verb hayah ("to be") with its own infinitive construct (literally "and it shall be to be") expresses enduring appointment: their anointing is to stand for them as an everlasting priesthood.
- **40:20** — The Hebrew term kapporet ("atonement cover") derives from the root kafar ("to atone, cover"), naming the gold lid of the ark as the place of atonement; the traditional rendering "mercy seat" entered English via the Septuagint and Vulgate.
- **40:35** — The Qal verb shakan ("rested, dwelt") plays on the cognate noun mishkan ("tabernacle," literally "dwelling place") at the end of the verse.
