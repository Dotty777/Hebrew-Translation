## Text

**27:1** And you shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide; the altar shall be square, and its height shall be three cubits.

**27:2** And you shall make its horns on its four corners; its horns shall be integral with it, and you shall overlay it with bronze.

**27:3** And you shall make its pots to clear away its ashes, along with its shovels, its basins, its forks, and its firepans; you shall make all its utensils of bronze.

**27:4** And you shall make for it a grating, a meshwork of bronze, and on the mesh you shall make four rings of bronze at its four corners.

**27:5** And you shall place it under the ledge of the altar from below, so that the mesh extends to half the height of the altar.

**27:6** You shall also make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with bronze.

**27:7** And its poles shall be inserted into the rings, so that the poles are on the two sides of the altar when carrying it.

**27:8** You shall make it hollow, of boards; just as was shown to you on the mountain, so they shall make it.

**27:9** And you shall make the courtyard of the tabernacle. On the south side, southward, there shall be hangings for the courtyard of twisted fine linen, one hundred cubits in length for the one side,

**27:10** and its pillars shall be twenty, and their twenty bases of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.

**27:11** And likewise for the north side along the length, there shall be hangings one hundred cubits in length, and its pillars shall be twenty, and their twenty bases of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.

**27:12** And for the width of the courtyard on the west side, there shall be hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars shall be ten, and their bases ten.

**27:13** And the width of the courtyard on the east side, toward the sunrise, shall be fifty cubits.

**27:14** There shall be fifteen cubits of hangings for the one flank; their pillars shall be three, and their bases three.

**27:15** And for the second flank, fifteen cubits of hangings; their pillars shall be three, and their bases three.

**27:16** And for the gate of the courtyard there shall be a screen of twenty cubits, of blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen, the work of an embroiderer; their pillars shall be four, and their bases four.

**27:17** All the pillars of the courtyard all around shall be banded with silver; their hooks shall be of silver, and their bases of bronze.

**27:18** The length of the courtyard shall be one hundred cubits, and the width fifty cubits at each end, and the height five cubits, of twisted fine linen, with their bases of bronze.

**27:19** All the utensils of the tabernacle in all its service, and all its pegs, and all the pegs of the courtyard, shall be of bronze.

**27:20** Now you shall command the sons of Israel so that they bring to you pure, pounded olive oil for the lighting, to cause a lamp to burn continually.

**27:21** In the tent of meeting, outside the veil that is before the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall set it in order from evening until morning before the LORD; it is an everlasting statute for their generations from the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — 'To clear away its ashes' translates a privative Piel infinitive construct (ledashsheno) derived from deshen ('fat-ashes'), meaning to remove the ashes from the altar.
- **27:7** — 'Its poles shall be inserted' renders a 3ms Hophal verb (wehuva') followed by the direct object marker et before the plural noun ('its poles'), an impersonal passive construction common in Biblical Hebrew.
- **27:8** — 'As was shown to you' renders an active 3ms Hiphil verb (her'ah, literally 'one showed you' or 'He showed you') used impersonally, parallel to the Hophal passive in 26:30.
- **27:18** — 'Fifty cubits at each end' translates the Hebrew idiom 'fifty by fifty' (chamishshim bachamishshim), indicating a uniform width of fifty cubits across both the western and eastern ends (see 27:12-13).
