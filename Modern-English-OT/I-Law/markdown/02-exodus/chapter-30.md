## Text

**30:1** And you shall make an altar for burning incense; out of acacia wood you shall make it.

**30:2** One cubit shall be its length and one cubit its width; it shall be square, and its height shall be two cubits; its horns shall be part of it.

**30:3** And you shall overlay it with pure gold—its top, its walls all around, and its horns—and you shall make a gold molding for it all around.

**30:4** And you shall make two gold rings for it beneath its molding; you shall make them on two of its sides, on its two opposite sides, and they shall serve as holders for poles to carry it by them.

**30:5** And you shall make the poles out of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

**30:6** And you shall set it in front of the veil that is by the ark of the testimony, in front of the atonement cover that is over the testimony, where I will meet with you.

**30:7** And Aaron shall burn incense of aromatic spices on it; morning by morning, when he tends the lamps, he shall burn it.

**30:8** And when Aaron lights the lamps at twilight, he shall burn it, a continual incense before the LORD throughout your generations.

**30:9** You shall not offer up unauthorized incense on it, nor a burnt offering, nor a grain offering; and you shall not pour out a drink offering on it.

**30:10** And Aaron shall make atonement on its horns once a year; with some of the blood of the sin offering of atonement he shall make atonement on it once a year throughout your generations. It is most holy to the LORD."

**30:11** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:12** "When you take a census of the sons of Israel for their numbering, each man shall give a ransom for his life to the LORD when they are numbered, so that there will not be a plague among them when they are numbered.

**30:13** This is what everyone who passes over to those who are numbered shall give: half a shekel by the sanctuary shekel—the shekel is twenty gerahs—half a shekel as a contribution to the LORD.

**30:14** Everyone who passes over to those who are numbered, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the LORD's contribution.

**30:15** The rich shall not exceed and the poor shall not fall short of the half shekel in giving the LORD's contribution to make atonement for your lives.

**30:16** And you shall take the atonement silver from the sons of Israel and assign it for the service of the tent of meeting, and it shall serve for the sons of Israel as a memorial before the LORD to make atonement for your lives."

**30:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:18** "You shall also make a bronze basin, and its stand of bronze, for washing; and you shall place it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water into it.

**30:19** And from it Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet.

**30:20** When they go into the tent of meeting, they shall wash with water so that they will not die; or when they approach the altar to minister, to burn an offering by fire to the LORD,

**30:21** they shall wash their hands and their feet so that they will not die; and it shall be an everlasting statute for them, for him and for his seed throughout their generations."

**30:22** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:23** "And you, take for yourself the finest spices: five hundred shekels of flowing myrrh, half as much of fragrant cinnamon—two hundred and fifty—and two hundred and fifty of fragrant cane,

**30:24** and five hundred of cassia by the sanctuary shekel, and a hin of olive oil.

**30:25** And you shall make it into a holy anointing oil, a compounded perfume, the work of a perfumer; it shall be a holy anointing oil.

**30:26** And you shall anoint with it the tent of meeting, the ark of the testimony,

**30:27** the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, the incense altar,

**30:28** the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand.

**30:29** And you shall consecrate them, and they shall be most holy; anything that touches them shall become holy.

**30:30** You shall also anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them to serve as priests to Me.

**30:31** And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'This shall be to Me a holy anointing oil throughout your generations.

**30:32** It shall not be poured on human flesh, and according to its composition you shall not make any like it; it is holy, and holy it shall be to you.

**30:33** Anyone who compounds any like it, or who puts some of it on an unauthorized person, shall be cut off from his people.'"

**30:34** And the LORD said to Moses, "Take for yourself aromatic spices—stacte, onycha, and galbanum—aromatic spices with pure frankincense; there shall be an equal portion of each.

**30:35** And you shall make it into incense, a perfume, the work of a perfumer, seasoned with salt, pure and holy.

**30:36** And you shall grind some of it very fine and place some of it in front of the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you; it shall be most holy to you.

**30:37** And as for the incense that you shall make, according to its composition you shall not make any for yourselves; to you it shall be holy to the LORD.

**30:38** Anyone who makes any like it to smell it shall be cut off from his people."

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — The construct noun miqtar ("burning" or "place of burning," from the root qtr) is a hapax legomenon in the Hebrew Bible, literally rendering the phrase "an altar of the burning of incense."
- **30:12** — "Take a census" translates the Hebrew idiom nasa et-rosh (literally "lift up the head"), used for reckoning a sum or tallying individuals in a muster.
- **30:25** — "A compounded perfume, the work of a perfumer" reflects a triple cognate wordplay on the Hebrew root rqh (roqah mirqahat maaseh roqeah, literally "a perfume of compounding, the work of a perfumer").
- **30:32** — The rare verb form yisakh ("be poured") is a hapax form associated with the roots swk ("anoint, pour oil") or nsk ("pour") and functions with passive sense here.
- **30:35** — "Seasoned with salt" translates the Pual participle memullah (from the root mlh, "to salt"), indicating incense mixed with salt rather than merely blended.
