## Text

**3:1** Now Moses was tending the flock of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and he led the flock beyond the wilderness and came to the mountain of God, to Horeb.

**3:2** And the angel of the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush; he looked, and see—the bush was burning with fire, yet the bush was not being consumed.

**3:3** Then Moses said, "Let me turn aside now and see this great sight, why the bush does not burn up."

**3:4** And the LORD saw that he had turned aside to see, so God called out to him from within the bush and said, "Moses, Moses!" And he answered, "Here I am."

**3:5** And He said, "Do not approach here; slip off your sandals from your feet, because the place where you are standing is holy ground."

**3:6** And He said, "I am the God of your father—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." So Moses concealed his face, because he was afraid to gaze at God.

**3:7** And the LORD said, "I have indeed seen the affliction of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their outcry because of their taskmasters; for I know their pains.

**3:8** And I have come down to rescue them from the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up out of that land to a good and wide land, to a land flowing with milk and honey, to the place of the Canaanites and the Hittites and the Amorites and the Perizzites and the Hivites and the Jebusites.

**3:9** And now, look, the outcry of the sons of Israel has come to Me, and I have also seen the oppression by which the Egyptians are oppressing them.

**3:10** And now, come, that I may send you to Pharaoh; and bring out My people, the sons of Israel, from Egypt."

**3:11** And Moses said to God, "Who am I, that I would go to Pharaoh and that I would bring out the sons of Israel from Egypt?"

**3:12** And He said, "Indeed, I will be with you, and this is the sign for you that I Myself have sent you: when you bring the people out from Egypt, you will serve God on this mountain."

**3:13** And Moses said to God, "Look, I am going to the sons of Israel and will say to them, 'The God of your fathers has sent me to you,' and they will say to me, 'What is His name?' What am I to say to them?"

**3:14** And God said to Moses, "I WILL BE WHO I WILL BE." And He said, "This is what you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'I WILL BE has sent me to you.'"

**3:15** And God said further to Moses, "This is what you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'The LORD, the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob—has sent me to you.' This is My name forever, and this is My memorial from generation to generation.

**3:16** Go and gather the elders of Israel and say to them, 'The LORD, the God of your fathers, has appeared to me—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—saying, "I have indeed attended to you and to what has been done to you in Egypt,

**3:17** and I have said that I will bring you up from the affliction of Egypt into the land of the Canaanites and the Hittites and the Amorites and the Perizzites and the Hivites and the Jebusites, into a land flowing with milk and honey."'

**3:18** And they will heed your voice; then you and the elders of Israel shall come to the king of Egypt and say to him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met us; and now, please let us go a journey of three days into the wilderness, so that we may offer sacrifice to the LORD our God.'

**3:19** Yet I Myself know that the king of Egypt will not permit you to go, not even by a strong hand.

**3:20** So I will extend My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders that I will perform in its midst; and after that he will send you out.

**3:21** And I will give favor to this people in the eyes of the Egyptians; and it will happen that when you go, you will not go empty-handed.

**3:22** Instead, each woman shall ask her neighbor and the woman lodging in her house for objects of silver and objects of gold, and garments; and you shall put them on your sons and on your daughters, and so you will plunder the Egyptians."

## Translator Notes

- **3:14** — Hebrew Ehyeh asher ehyeh ("I WILL BE WHO I WILL BE") uses the Qal imperfect of hayah ("be"), echoing "I will be with you" in v. 12; it is also widely rendered timelessly as "I AM WHO I AM" and connects by wordplay to the divine name YHWH ("the LORD") in v. 15.
- **3:16** — Hebrew paqod paqadti pairs the Qal infinitive absolute and perfect of paqad ("attend to," "visit," or "take careful notice of") for emphasis, echoing Joseph's final words in Genesis 50:24-25.
- **3:19** — The Masoretic Text reads literally velo beyad chazaqah ("and not by a strong hand," i.e., not even when faced with a strong hand), whereas the Septuagint reads "unless by a mighty hand."
- **3:22** — Hebrew venitsaltem is the Piel of natsal ("strip" or "plunder"), forming an ironic wordplay with the Hiphil of the same root in v. 8 (lehattsilo, "to rescue them").
