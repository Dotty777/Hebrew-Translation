## Text

**25:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel so that they take up a contribution for Me; from every man whose heart prompts him, you shall take My contribution.

**25:3** And this is the contribution that you shall take from them: gold, silver, and bronze;

**25:4** blue, purple, and crimson yarn, fine linen, and goat hair;

**25:5** ram skins dyed red, dugong skins, and acacia wood;

**25:7** onyx stones and stones for mounting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

**25:8** And they shall make a sanctuary for Me, and I will dwell in their midst.

**25:9** According to everything that I am showing you, the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its furnishings, so you shall make it.

**25:10** And they shall make an ark of acacia wood; its length shall be two and a half cubits, its width one and a half cubits, and its height one and a half cubits.

**25:11** And you shall overlay it with pure gold; inside and outside you shall overlay it, and you shall make a gold molding on it all around.

**25:12** And you shall cast four gold rings for it and place them on its four feet, two rings on its one side and two rings on its other side.

**25:13** And you shall make poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

**25:14** And you shall insert the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, so as to carry the ark with them.

**25:15** In the rings of the ark the poles shall remain; they shall not come out from it.

**25:16** And you shall place into the ark the testimony that I will give to you.

**25:17** And you shall make an atonement cover of pure gold; its length shall be two and a half cubits, and its width one and a half cubits.

**25:18** You shall also make two cherubim of gold; you shall make them of hammered work at the two ends of the atonement cover.

**25:19** Make one cherub at this end and one cherub at the other end; out of the atonement cover itself you shall make the cherubim on its two ends.

**25:20** The cherubim shall spread their wings upward, sheltering the atonement cover with their wings, with their faces toward one another; the faces of the cherubim shall be toward the atonement cover.

**25:21** And you shall set the atonement cover on top of the ark, and into the ark you shall place the testimony that I will give to you.

**25:22** And I will meet with you there, and I will speak with you from above the atonement cover, from between the two cherubim which are on the ark of the testimony, concerning everything that I will command you for the sons of Israel.

**25:23** And you shall make a table of acacia wood; its length shall be two cubits, its width one cubit, and its height one and a half cubits.

**25:24** And you shall overlay it with pure gold, and you shall make a gold molding for it all around.

**25:25** And you shall make a handbreadth frame for it all around, and you shall make a gold molding for its frame all around.

**25:26** And you shall make four gold rings for it, and place the rings on the four corners that belong to its four legs.

**25:27** Right beside the frame the rings shall be, as housings for poles to carry the table.

**25:28** And you shall make the poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold, and the table shall be carried by them.

**25:29** And you shall make its platters, its ladles, its pitchers, and its bowls with which drink offerings are poured; of pure gold you shall make them.

**25:30** And you shall place bread of the Presence on the table before Me continually.

**25:31** And you shall make a lampstand of pure gold. Of hammered work the lampstand shall be made; its base and its shaft, its cups, its knobs, and its blossoms shall be of one piece with it.

**25:32** And six branches shall extend from its sides: three lampstand branches from one side of it, and three lampstand branches from its other side.

**25:33** Three almond-shaped cups shall be on one branch, a knob and a blossom, and three almond-shaped cups on the next branch, a knob and a blossom; so for the six branches extending from the lampstand.

**25:34** And on the lampstand there shall be four almond-shaped cups, with its knobs and its blossoms.

**25:35** And a knob shall be beneath two branches, of one piece with it, and a knob beneath two branches, of one piece with it, and a knob beneath two branches, of one piece with it, for the six branches extending from the lampstand.

**25:36** Their knobs and their branches shall be of one piece with it; all of it shall be a single hammered work of pure gold.

**25:37** And you shall make its lamps, seven of them, and its lamps shall be mounted so that they cast light over the area in front of it.

**25:38** And its tongs and its firepans shall be of pure gold.

**25:39** Out of a talent of pure gold it shall be made, along with all these utensils.

**25:40** And see to it that you make them according to their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain."

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — Hebrew techashim (plural of tachash) is of uncertain meaning; it is commonly linked either to Arabic cognates for marine mammals such as the dugong or dolphin, or to Egyptian and Akkadian terms for fine tanned or beaded leather.
- **25:9** — Hebrew hammishkan ("the tabernacle") literally means "the dwelling place," directly echoing the verb veshakhanti ("and I will dwell," Qal weqatal of shakhan) in verse 8.
- **25:17** — Hebrew kapporet ("atonement cover") is derived from the root kaphar ("atone" or "cover") and denotes the solid gold lid of the ark that served as the focal place of atonement.
- **25:28** — Hebrew venissa-vam et-hashshulchan illustrates the classical Hebrew construction in which a passive Niphal verb (here Niphal weqatal of nasa, "be carried") retains the direct object marker et before its grammatical subject.
- **25:33** — Hebrew meshuqqadim is a denominative Pual participle derived from shaqed ("almond"), describing cups fashioned in the shape of almonds or almond blossoms.
