## Text

**37:1** All the wise-hearted among those doing the work made the tabernacle of ten curtains of twisted fine linen, and blue, purple, and crimson yarn; with cherubim, the work of a skilled designer, he made them.

**37:2** The length of each curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain was four cubits; all the curtains were of one measurement.

**37:3** He made the veil of blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and twisted fine linen; as the work of a skilled designer he made it with cherubim.

**37:4** He made for it four pillars of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold; their hooks were of gold, and he cast for them four bases of silver.

**37:5** He made a screen for the entrance of the tent of blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and twisted fine linen, the work of an embroiderer,

**37:6** and its five pillars with their hooks; he overlaid their tops and their bands with gold, and their five bases were of bronze.

**37:7** He made the courtyard. For the south side, facing southward, the hangings of the courtyard were of twisted fine linen, one hundred cubits.

**37:8** Their pillars were twenty and their bases were twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

**37:9** And for the north side, one hundred cubits; their pillars were twenty and their bases were twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

**37:10** And for the west side, hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars were ten and their bases were ten; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

**37:11** And for the east side, facing eastward, fifty cubits.

**37:12** The hangings for the one flank were fifteen cubits; their pillars were three and their bases were three.

**37:13** And for the second flank, on this side and on that side of the gate of the courtyard, the hangings were fifteen cubits; their pillars were three and their bases were three.

**37:14** All the hangings of the courtyard all around were of twisted fine linen.

**37:15** The bases for the pillars were of bronze, the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver, and the overlaying of their tops was of silver; and all the pillars of the courtyard were banded with silver.

**37:16** The screen for the gate of the courtyard was the work of an embroiderer, of blue, purple, and crimson yarn, and twisted fine linen; it was twenty cubits in length, and the height in width was five cubits, matching the hangings of the courtyard.

**37:17** Their pillars were four and their bases were four, of bronze; their hooks were of silver, and the overlaying of their tops and their bands were of silver.

**37:18** All the pegs for the tabernacle and for the courtyard all around were of bronze.

**37:19** These are the records of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the testimony, as it was recorded at the command of Moses—the service of the Levites under the hand of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.

**37:20** Now Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made everything that the LORD had commanded Moses.

**37:21** And with him was Oholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an artisan, a skilled designer, and an embroiderer in blue, in purple, in crimson, and in fine linen.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The Hebrew shifts within the verse from a plural Qal wayyiqtol (wayya'asu, "they made") with the collective subject to a singular Qal perfect ('asah, "he made"), referring to the lead artisan Bezalel.
- **37:16** — Literally "height in width" (qomah verohav), reflecting the fact that the vertical height of the hanging corresponded to the woven width of the cloth.
- **37:17** — The Hebrew uses third-person masculine plural possessive suffixes ("their pillars," "their bases") by attraction to the formulaic wording of verses 8-13, although they refer to the singular gate screen of verse 16.
