## Text

**34:1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Carve for yourself two tablets of stone like the first ones, and I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you smashed.

**34:2** Be prepared for the morning, and go up in the morning to Mount Sinai, and take your stand before Me there on the top of the mountain.

**34:3** No one shall go up with you, nor let anyone even be seen anywhere on the mountain; even the flocks and the herds are not to graze opposite that mountain."

**34:4** So he carved two tablets of stone like the first ones, and Moses rose early in the morning and went up to Mount Sinai, just as the LORD had commanded him, and took in his hand two tablets of stone.

**34:5** Then the LORD came down in the cloud and took His stand with him there, and proclaimed the name of the LORD.

**34:6** And the LORD passed in front of him and called out, "The LORD, the LORD, a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, and great in loyal love and faithfulness,

**34:7** preserving loyal love for thousands, forgiving iniquity and rebellion and sin; yet He will certainly not acquit the guilty, calling to account the iniquity of fathers upon children and upon children's children, upon the third and upon the fourth generations."

**34:8** And Moses hurried and bowed low to the ground and worshiped.

**34:9** He said, "If now I have found favor in Your eyes, Lord, let the Lord please walk in our midst, for they are a stiff-necked people, and pardon our iniquity and our sin, and claim us as Your inheritance."

**34:10** And He said, "Look, I am making a covenant. In front of all your people I will perform wonders that have not been created in all the earth or among all the nations; and all the people in whose midst you are will see the work of the LORD, for what I am doing with you is awe-inspiring.

**34:11** Keep carefully what I am commanding you today. Look, I am driving out from before you the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

**34:12** Be on your guard so that you do not make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land into which you are going, or they will become a snare in your midst.

**34:13** Instead, you shall tear down their altars, smash their standing stones, and cut down their Asherah poles,

**34:14** for you shall not bow down to another god, because the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God—

**34:15** so that you do not make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, and when they prostitute themselves after their gods and sacrifice to their gods, they invite you and you eat from their sacrifice,

**34:16** and you take some of their daughters for your sons, and their daughters prostitute themselves after their gods and cause your sons to prostitute themselves after their gods.

**34:17** You shall not make cast-metal gods for yourself.

**34:18** You shall keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of Abib, because in the month of Abib you went out from Egypt.

**34:19** Every first offspring of the womb is Mine, and all your livestock that is male, the first offspring of ox and sheep.

**34:20** You shall redeem the first offspring of a donkey with a sheep, and if you do not redeem it, you shall break its neck. Every firstborn of your sons you shall redeem, and no one shall appear before Me empty-handed.

**34:21** For six days you shall labor, but on the seventh day you shall rest; during plowing and during harvest you shall rest.

**34:22** And you shall observe for yourself the Feast of Weeks, the firstfruits of the wheat harvest, and the Feast of Ingathering at the turn of the year.

**34:23** Three times each year all your males shall appear before the Sovereign, the LORD, the God of Israel.

**34:24** For I will dispossess nations from before you and widen your territory, and no one will covet your land when you go up to appear before the LORD your God three times each year.

**34:25** You shall not slaughter the blood of My sacrifice with anything leavened, nor shall the sacrifice of the Feast of the Passover remain overnight until the morning.

**34:26** The choicest of the firstfruits of your soil you shall bring into the house of the LORD your God. You shall not boil a young goat in the milk of its mother."

**34:27** And the LORD said to Moses, "Write for yourself these words, for according to these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel."

**34:28** And he was there with the LORD for forty days and forty nights; he neither ate bread nor drank water. And he wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant, the Ten Words.

**34:29** Now it happened as Moses came down from Mount Sinai, with the two tablets of the testimony in the hand of Moses as he came down from the mountain, that Moses did not know that the skin of his face was radiant from his speaking with Him.

**34:30** Aaron and all the sons of Israel saw Moses, and look, the skin of his face was radiant, and they were afraid to draw near to him.

**34:31** But Moses called out to them, so Aaron and all the leaders in the congregation turned back to him, and Moses spoke to them.

**34:32** Afterward all the sons of Israel drew near, and he commanded them everything that the LORD had spoken with him on Mount Sinai.

**34:33** And Moses finished speaking with them and placed a veil over his face.

**34:34** But whenever Moses went in before the LORD to speak with Him, he would remove the veil until he came out; and he would come out and speak to the sons of Israel whatever he was commanded.

**34:35** And the sons of Israel would see the face of Moses, that the skin of Moses's face was radiant; then Moses would place the veil back over his face until he went in to speak with Him.

## Translator Notes

- **34:5** — The verb "proclaimed" (Qal wayyiqtol of qara) has no explicit subject; while the idiom "call on the name of the LORD" often has a human subject, the grammatical continuity of the three verbs in this verse and the promise in 33:19 indicate that the LORD Himself is proclaiming His name.
- **34:7** — Literally "and acquitting He will not acquit" (Piel infinitive absolute with Piel imperfect of naqah), with the object ("the guilty") implied by the context. "Third" and "fourth" translate masculine plural substantives (shilleshim, ribbeim) denoting descendants of the third and fourth generations.
- **34:19** — The rare Masoretic form tizzakar is parsed as a denominative Niphal imperfect from zakar ("male"), meaning "is male" or "is born male"; some scholars revocalize or emend the consonants to hazzakar ("the male").
- **34:28** — The verb "wrote" (Qal wayyiqtol of katav) lacks an explicit subject; grammatically it continues Moses from the preceding clauses and 34:27, though 34:1 and Deuteronomy 10:4 identify the LORD as the One who wrote on the tablets. "The Ten Words" translates aseret haddevarim literally (traditionally known as the Ten Commandments).
- **34:29** — The verb qaran ("was radiant," Qal perfect) is a denominative from qeren ("horn"), used here for emitting rays of light (compare Habakkuk 3:4); the Latin Vulgate's literal rendering "horned" gave rise to traditional depictions of Moses with horns.
