## Text

**39:1** All the gold used for the work, in all the work of the sanctuary—the gold of the wave offering—came to twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and thirty shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary.

**39:2** And the silver from those registered of the congregation was one hundred talents and one thousand, seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary.

**39:3** A beka per head—half a shekel by the shekel of the sanctuary—for everyone who passed over to those registered, from twenty years old and upward, for six hundred and three thousand, five hundred and fifty.

**39:4** And the one hundred talents of silver were used for casting the bases of the sanctuary and the bases of the veil—one hundred bases for the one hundred talents, one talent per base.

**39:5** And from the one thousand, seven hundred and seventy-five shekels he made hooks for the pillars, overlaid their tops, and fitted them with bands.

**39:6** And the bronze of the wave offering was seventy talents and two thousand, four hundred shekels.

**39:8** And the bases of the courtyard all around, the bases of the gate of the courtyard, all the pegs of the tabernacle, and all the pegs of the courtyard all around.

**39:9** With it he made the bases of the entrance of the tent of meeting, the bronze altar, the bronze grating that belonged to it, and all the utensils of the altar.

**39:10** So all the work of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was finished; the sons of Israel did according to everything that the LORD commanded Moses, just so they did.

**39:13** Then they brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its furnishings, its clasps, its planks, its crossbars, its pillars, and its bases;

**39:14** the ark of the testimony, its poles, and the atonement cover;

**39:15** the gold altar, the anointing oil, the incense of spices, and the screen for the entrance of the tent;

**39:16** the pure lampstand, its lamps—the lamps arranged in order—all its utensils, and the oil for the light;

**39:17** the table, all of its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;

**39:18** the finely woven garments for ministering in the sanctuary, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests;

**39:19** the courtyard hangings, its pillars and its bases, the screen for the courtyard gate, its ropes and its pegs, and all the furnishings for the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;

**39:20** and the covering of ram skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather skins, and the screening veil.

**39:22** According to everything that the LORD commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did all the work.

**39:23** And Moses saw all the work, and look, they had done it; just as the LORD commanded, so they had done. And Moses blessed them.

## Translator Notes

- **39:3** — A beka is an ancient weight unit equivalent to half a shekel, as the verse immediately explains.
- **39:8** — In native Masoretic order (Exod 38:30–31), 38:30 supplies the governing verb ("With it he made...") and 38:31 continues the list of direct objects introduced by the object marker et. In the Saints.Bible parallel display, these two verses are inverted as 39:9 and 39:8.
- **39:18** — The exact meaning of Hebrew serad is debated; it is widely connected with plaited, braided, or finely woven fabric, though ancient versions often understood it as 'service garments.'
- **39:20** — The precise meaning of Hebrew techashim is uncertain; scholars variously understand it as fine tanned leather, cognate with Egyptian ths, or dugong/sea-cow hides, cognate with Arabic tukhas.
