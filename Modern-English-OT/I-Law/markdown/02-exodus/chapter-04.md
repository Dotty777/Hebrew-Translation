## Text

**4:1** Moses answered and said, "But look, they will not believe me and they will not listen to my voice, for they will say, 'The LORD did not appear to you.'"

**4:2** Then the LORD said to him, "What is this in your hand?" He said, "A staff."

**4:3** He said, "Throw it to the ground." So he threw it to the ground, and it became a snake, and Moses fled from before it.

**4:4** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Reach out your hand and seize it by its tail"—so he reached out his hand and took firm hold of it, and it became a staff in his palm—

**4:5** "in order that they may believe that the LORD, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, appeared to you."

**4:6** Then the LORD said to him again, "Please put your hand into your bosom." He put his hand into his bosom, and he drew it out, and look, his hand was leprous, like snow.

**4:7** Then He said, "Put your hand back into your bosom." So he put his hand back into his bosom, and he drew it out from his bosom, and look, it was restored like his flesh.

**4:8** "And it will happen, if they do not believe you and do not listen to the voice of the first sign, that they will believe the voice of the latter sign.

**4:9** And it will happen, if they do not believe even these two signs and do not listen to your voice, that you shall take some of the water of the Nile and pour it out onto the dry ground; and the water that you take from the Nile will become blood on the dry ground."

**4:10** Then Moses said to the LORD, "Please, Lord, I am not a man of words, neither yesterday nor the day before, nor since You have spoken to Your servant, for I am heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue."

**4:11** The LORD said to him, "Who made a mouth for mankind, or who makes one mute or deaf, or seeing or blind? Is it not I, the LORD?

**4:12** So now, go, and I Myself will be with your mouth and will instruct you in what you are to speak."

**4:13** But he said, "Please, Lord, send now by the hand of whomever You will send."

**4:14** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Moses, and He said, "Is there not Aaron your brother, the Levite? I know that he indeed speaks well. Moreover, look, he is coming out to meet you, and he will see you and rejoice in his heart.

**4:15** You shall speak to him and place the words in his mouth; and I Myself will be with your mouth and with his mouth, and I will instruct you in what you shall do.

**4:16** He himself will speak for you to the people; and it will be that he will serve as a mouth for you, and you will serve as God to him.

**4:17** And this staff you shall take in your hand, with which you will perform the signs."

**4:18** So Moses went and returned to Jether his father-in-law, and he said to him, "Please let me go and return to my brothers who are in Egypt and see whether they are still alive." And Jethro said to Moses, "Go in peace."

**4:19** And the LORD said to Moses in Midian, "Go, return to Egypt, for all the men who were seeking your life have died."

**4:20** Moses took his wife and his sons, mounted them on a donkey, and returned to the land of Egypt; and Moses took the staff of God in his hand.

**4:21** The LORD said to Moses, "When you go to return to Egypt, see that you perform before Pharaoh all the wonders that I have placed in your hand; yet I Myself will harden his heart, and he will not let the people go.

**4:22** Then you shall say to Pharaoh, 'This is what the LORD says: My son, My firstborn, is Israel.

**4:23** And I said to you, "Let My son go so that he may serve Me," yet you refused to let him go. Look, I am about to kill your son, your firstborn.'"

**4:24** Now it happened on the way, at the lodging place, that the LORD met him and sought to put him to death.

**4:25** Then Zipporah took a flint and cut off the foreskin of her son, and she touched it to his feet and said, "Indeed, a bridegroom of blood you are to me!"

**4:26** So He let go of him. Then she said, "A bridegroom of blood," because of the circumcision.

**4:27** And the LORD said to Aaron, "Go into the wilderness to meet Moses." He went, met him at the mountain of God, and kissed him.

**4:28** And Moses reported to Aaron all the words of the LORD with which He had sent him, and all the signs that He had commanded him to perform.

**4:29** So Moses and Aaron went and gathered all the elders of the sons of Israel.

**4:30** And Aaron spoke all the words that the LORD had spoken to Moses, and he performed the signs before the eyes of the people.

**4:31** Then the people believed; and they heard that the LORD had visited the sons of Israel and that He had seen their affliction, so they bowed down and worshiped.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The written form (ketiv) coalesces the two words as one (mzh), while the read form (qere), translated here, divides them as mah-zeh ("what is this").
- **4:13** — Literally "send now by the hand of [whom] You will send," an elliptical construct phrase before a finite verb (tishlach) expressing Moses's reluctance and asking God to send someone else.
- **4:18** — The Masoretic Text gives the shorter form of the name, Jether (yeter), in the first clause and the fuller form, Jethro (yitro), in the second.
- **4:21** — The Hebrew verb (achazzeq, Piel of chazaq) literally means "strengthen" or "make firm," used with "heart" to signify making Pharaoh resolute and obstinate.
- **4:25** — The masculine pronouns in vv. 24-26 leave unspecified whether the LORD confronted Moses or his son, and "feet" (raglayw) may be a Hebrew euphemism for the genitals. The difficult expression "bridegroom of blood" (chatan-damim) links the blood of circumcision to a covenantal or protective rite.
