## Text

**23:1** You shall not carry a false report; do not put your hand with a wicked person to be a malicious witness.

**23:2** You shall not follow after the many for evil, nor shall you testify in a dispute by turning aside after the many to pervert justice.

**23:3** And you shall not favor a poor person in his dispute.

**23:4** If you come upon your enemy's ox or his donkey straying, you must certainly bring it back to him.

**23:5** If you see the donkey of someone who hates you lying under its burden, you must hold back from abandoning it to him; you must certainly free it with him.

**23:6** You shall not pervert the justice of your needy one in his dispute.

**23:7** Stay far from a false matter, and do not kill an innocent and righteous person, for I will not declare a wicked person righteous.

**23:8** And you shall not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds the open-eyed and twists the words of the righteous.

**23:9** And you shall not oppress a sojourner; you yourselves know the soul of a sojourner, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

**23:10** And for six years you shall sow your land and gather its produce,

**23:11** but in the seventh year you shall release it and leave it fallow, so that the needy of your people may eat, and what they leave the wild animals of the field may eat. Likewise you shall do with your vineyard and your olive grove.

**23:12** For six days you shall do your work, and on the seventh day you shall stop working, so that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your female servant and the sojourner may be refreshed.

**23:13** And be careful regarding everything that I have said to you; you shall not mention the name of other gods; it shall not be heard from your mouth.

**23:14** Three times in the year you shall celebrate a festival to Me.

**23:15** You shall keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread: for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of Abib, for in it you went out from Egypt; and no one shall appear before Me empty-handed.

**23:16** And you shall keep the Feast of Harvest, of the firstfruits of your labors that you sow in the field, and the Feast of Ingathering at the close of the year, when you gather in your labors from the field.

**23:17** Three times in the year all your males shall appear before the Sovereign, the LORD.

**23:18** You shall not offer the blood of My sacrifice together with what is leavened, nor shall the fat of My festival remain overnight until morning.

**23:19** You shall bring the choicest of the firstfruits of your ground to the house of the LORD your God. You shall not cook a young goat in its mother's milk.

**23:20** Look, I am sending an angel ahead of you to guard you on the way and to bring you to the place I have prepared.

**23:21** Watch yourself in his presence and listen to his voice; do not defy him, for he will not forgive your transgression, because My name is within him.

**23:22** But if you indeed listen to his voice and do everything that I speak, then I will be an enemy to your enemies, and I will oppose those who oppose you.

**23:23** For My angel will go ahead of you and bring you to the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, and I will wipe them out.

**23:24** You shall not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor act according to their deeds; rather, you must completely tear them down and thoroughly smash their sacred pillars.

**23:25** You shall serve the LORD your God, and He will bless your bread and your water, and I will remove illness from your midst.

**23:26** There shall not be one who miscarries or is barren in your land; the number of your days I will fulfill.

**23:27** I will send My dread ahead of you, and I will throw into panic all the people among whom you come, and I will cause all your enemies to turn their backs to you.

**23:28** And I will send the hornets ahead of you, and they will drive out the Hivites, the Canaanites, and the Hittites from before you.

**23:29** I will not drive them out from before you in one year, or else the land will become desolate and the wild animals of the field will multiply against you.

**23:30** Little by little I will drive them out from before you, until you become fruitful and inherit the land.

**23:31** And I will fix your border from the Sea of Reeds to the sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the River, for I will give into your hand the inhabitants of the land, and you shall drive them out from before you.

**23:32** You shall not make a covenant with them or with their gods.

**23:33** They shall not dwell in your land, or else they will cause you to sin against Me; for if you serve their gods, it will certainly become a snare to you."

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — Hebrew plays on two stems of the root natah ('turn/incline'): the Qal infinitive construct lintot ('to turn aside' after the many) and the Hiphil infinitive construct lehattot ('to bend/pervert' justice, with the object 'justice' implied from the parallel Hiphil verb in 23:6).
- **23:5** — Hebrew pairs two verbs from homonymous roots 'azav: 'azav I ('leave, abandon') with 'to him,' followed by an emphatic Qal infinitive absolute and imperfect of 'azav II ('free, release, help') with 'with him.' Alternatively, 'you must hold back' can be read as continuing the conditional clause ('and you would refrain from helping him, you must certainly help him').
- **23:17** — Hebrew reads ha'adon followed by the divine name ('the Sovereign, the LORD'), using the definite singular noun ha'adon rather than the usual 'adonai before the divine name.
- **23:25** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts in mid-verse from third-person singular ('and He will bless,' referring to the LORD your God) back to first-person singular ('and I will remove'), a change preserved here without harmonizing to the ancient versions.
- **23:28** — The collective feminine singular noun hatsir'ah is understood as 'the hornets' (also in Deut 7:20 and Josh 24:12), though some lexicographers relate it to a Semitic root meaning 'panic' or 'discouragement.'
