## Text

**32:1** The people saw that Moses was slow to come down from the mountain, and the people assembled against Aaron and said to him, "Get up, make for us gods who will go ahead of us; for this Moses, the man who brought us up from the land of Egypt—we do not know what has happened to him."

**32:2** Aaron said to them, "Pull off the gold rings that are in the ears of your wives, your sons, and your daughters, and bring them to me."

**32:3** All the people pulled off from themselves the gold rings that were in their ears and brought them to Aaron.

**32:4** He took it from their hand, shaped it with an engraving tool, and made it into a calf of cast metal. Then they said, "These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up from the land of Egypt."

**32:5** Aaron saw this and built an altar in front of it; then Aaron proclaimed and said, "Tomorrow is a festival to the LORD."

**32:6** So they rose early the next day, offered up burnt offerings, and brought near peace offerings. Then the people sat down to eat and drink, and stood up to revel.

**32:7** The LORD spoke to Moses, "Go, descend, for your people, whom you brought up from the land of Egypt, have acted corruptly.

**32:8** They have quickly turned aside from the way that I commanded them. They have made for themselves a calf of cast metal, bowed down to it, sacrificed to it, and said, 'These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up from the land of Egypt.'

**32:10** So now leave Me alone, so that My anger may burn against them and I may consume them; and I will make you into a great nation."

**32:11** Then Moses pleaded for the favor of the LORD his God and said, "Why, LORD, should Your anger burn against Your people, whom You brought out from the land of Egypt with great power and with a strong hand?

**32:12** Why should the Egyptians say, 'For evil He brought them out, to kill them in the mountains and to consume them off the face of the ground'? Turn back from the burning of Your anger, and relent over the harm against Your people.

**32:13** Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, Your servants, to whom You swore by Yourself and spoke to them, 'I will multiply your seed like the stars of the sky, and all this land that I have spoken of I will give to your seed, and they will inherit it forever.'"

**32:14** So the LORD relented over the harm that He had spoken of doing to His people.

**32:15** Moses turned and descended from the mountain, the two tablets of the testimony in his hand—tablets written on both their sides; on one side and on the other they were written.

**32:16** Now the tablets were the handiwork of God, and the writing was the writing of God, incised upon the tablets.

**32:17** Joshua heard the sound of the people in their shouting, and he said to Moses, "A sound of battle is in the camp."

**32:18** He said, "It is not the sound of crying out in victory, nor the sound of crying out in defeat; a sound of singing is what I hear."

**32:19** When he drew near to the camp and saw the calf and the dances, the anger of Moses burned; and he hurled the tablets from his hands and shattered them at the base of the mountain.

**32:20** He took the calf that they had made, burned it in the fire, and ground it until it was fine; he scattered it across the face of the water and made the sons of Israel drink it.

**32:21** Moses said to Aaron, "What has this people done to you, that you brought a great sin upon them?"

**32:22** Aaron said, "Do not let my lord's anger burn; you yourself know the people, that they are given to evil.

**32:23** They said to me, 'Make for us gods who will go ahead of us; for this Moses, the man who brought us up from the land of Egypt—we do not know what has happened to him.'

**32:24** So I said to them, 'Who has gold?' They pulled it off and gave it to me; and I threw it into the fire, and this calf came out."

**32:25** Moses saw the people, that they were unrestrained, for Aaron had let them go unrestrained, to be a mockery among those who rise against them.

**32:26** So Moses stood at the gate of the camp and said, "Who is for the LORD? To me!" And all the sons of Levi gathered to him.

**32:27** He said to them, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Each of you, put his sword on his thigh, cross through and return from gate to gate in the camp, and kill—each man his brother, each man his friend, and each man his relative.'"

**32:28** The sons of Levi did according to the word of Moses, and there fell from the people on that day about three thousand men.

**32:29** Moses said, "Consecrate yourselves today for the LORD—for each man was against his son and against his brother—so that He may grant a blessing upon you today."

**32:30** On the following day, Moses said to the people, "You yourselves have sinned a great sin. Now I will ascend to the LORD; perhaps I may make atonement on behalf of your sin."

**32:31** So Moses returned to the LORD and said, "Please, this people has sinned a great sin, and they have made for themselves gods of gold.

**32:32** Now then, if You will forgive their sin—and if not, please erase me from Your book that You have written."

**32:33** The LORD said to Moses, "Whoever has sinned against Me, I will erase him from My book.

**32:34** Now go, lead the people to the place I spoke of to you. Look, My angel will go ahead of you; yet on the day when I call to account, I will call them to account for their sin."

**32:35** And the LORD struck the people because they had made the calf that Aaron made.

## Translator Notes

- **32:4** — "Shaped it with an engraving tool" translates Hebrew wayyatsar oto bacharet (from tsur II, "fashion, shape," and cheret, "stylus, engraving tool"). Some scholars alternatively read tsur I ("bind up") and cheret ("bag, pouch," cf. 2 Kgs 5:23), yielding "tied it up in a bag."
- **32:18** — The verse plays on two stems of the root anah: the first two occurrences are Qal infinitives construct (anot, "crying out, shouting"), while the third is pointed as a Piel infinitive construct (annot, "singing").
- **32:24** — Hebrew hitparaqu can be parsed either as a Hithpael third-person plural perfect in pause ("they pulled it off"), matching the Masoretic accents, or as a Hithpael masculine plural imperative ("pull it off!") within Aaron's quoted speech.
- **32:29** — "Consecrate yourselves" renders the Hebrew idiom mil'u yedkhem, literally "fill your hand" (here Qal imperative of male'), the standard expression for priestly ordination. The following clause is elliptical, literally "for each man against his son and against his brother."
- **32:32** — Moses' conditional sentence breaks off without a stated conclusion after "if You will forgive their sin" (aposiopesis), leaving the implied relief unspoken in emotional urgency.
