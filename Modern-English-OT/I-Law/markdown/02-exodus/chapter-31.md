## Text

**31:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "See, I have called Bezalel by name, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

**31:3** and I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, with understanding, with knowledge, and with all craftsmanship,

**31:4** to devise designs, to work in gold, in silver, and in bronze,

**31:5** and in cutting stone for setting, and in carving wood, to work in all craftsmanship.

**31:6** And look, I Myself have appointed with him Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and into the heart of everyone who is wise of heart I have put wisdom, so that they shall make everything that I have commanded you:

**31:7** the tent of meeting, the ark for the testimony, the atonement cover that is over it, and all the furnishings of the tent,

**31:8** the table and its utensils, the pure lampstand and all its utensils, the incense altar,

**31:9** the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, the basin and its stand,

**31:10** the finely woven garments, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, the garments of his sons to serve as priests,

**31:11** and the anointing oil and the incense of aromatic spices for the holy place; they shall do according to everything that I have commanded you."

**31:12** Then the LORD said to Moses,

**31:13** "As for you, speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Above all, My Sabbaths you shall keep, for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, so that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

**31:14** And you shall keep the Sabbath, for it is holy to you; whoever profanes it shall certainly be put to death, for anyone who does work on it, that person shall be cut off from the midst of his people.

**31:15** Six days work may be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of full rest, holy to the LORD; anyone who does work on the Sabbath day shall certainly be put to death.

**31:16** So the sons of Israel shall keep the Sabbath, to observe the Sabbath throughout their generations as an everlasting covenant.

**31:17** Between Me and the sons of Israel it is a sign forever, for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.'"

**31:18** Then He gave to Moses, as He finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, the two tablets of the testimony, stone tablets written with the finger of God.

## Translator Notes

- **31:10** — "The finely woven garments" translates bigde has-serad, containing the rare noun serad (only in Exodus 31, 35, and 39), understood either as plaited or woven work or, following ancient versions, as garments of service.
- **31:14** — "Whoever profanes it" renders a plural Piel participle (mehalleyha, literally "its profaners") paired with a singular Hophal verb (yumat, intensified by the Qal infinitive absolute mot), a distributive Hebrew construction meaning "each one who profanes it shall certainly be put to death."
- **31:17** — "Was refreshed" translates wayyinnapash, the Niphal of npsh (cognate with nephesh, "breath, life, soul"), literally "caught His breath," paired with shavat ("ceased, rested").
