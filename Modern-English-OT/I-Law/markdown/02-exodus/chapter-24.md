## Text

**24:1** Now to Moses He said, "Come up to the LORD, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy from the elders of Israel, and bow down from a distance.

**24:2** Moses alone shall approach the LORD, while they shall not approach, and the people shall not go up with him."

**24:3** Then Moses came and recounted to the people all the words of the LORD and all the rulings. And all the people responded with one voice and said, "We will do all the words that the LORD has spoken."

**24:4** So Moses wrote all the words of the LORD. Then he rose early in the morning and built an altar at the base of the mountain, with twelve standing stones for the twelve tribes of Israel.

**24:5** Then he sent young men of the sons of Israel, and they offered up burnt offerings and sacrificed peace offerings of bulls to the LORD.

**24:6** Moses took half of the blood and placed it in the basins, and half of the blood he splashed on the altar.

**24:7** Then he took the scroll of the covenant and read it in the ears of the people. And they said, "Everything that the LORD has spoken we will do and we will obey."

**24:8** So Moses took the blood and splashed it on the people, and he said, "Here is the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you on the basis of all these words."

**24:9** Then Moses went up, along with Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy from the elders of Israel.

**24:10** And they saw the God of Israel, and beneath His feet was something like a work of lapis lazuli pavement, and like the very heavens in purity.

**24:11** Yet against the nobles of the sons of Israel He did not extend His hand; and they gazed on God, and they ate and drank.

**24:12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Come up to Me onto the mountain and stay there, and I will give you the tablets of stone, and the law and the commandment that I have written to instruct them."

**24:13** Then Moses rose, together with Joshua his attendant, and Moses went up to the mountain of God.

**24:14** Now to the elders he said, "Stay here for us until we return to you. Look, Aaron and Hur are with you; whoever has a dispute, let him approach them."

**24:15** So Moses went up onto the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

**24:16** And the glory of the LORD dwelt on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days. Then He called to Moses on the seventh day from within the cloud.

**24:17** And the appearance of the glory of the LORD was like a consuming fire on the summit of the mountain, before the eyes of the sons of Israel.

**24:18** Then Moses went into the midst of the cloud and went up onto the mountain, and Moses remained on the mountain forty days and forty nights.

## Translator Notes

- **24:7** — The verb venishma (Qal imperfect of shama) literally means "and we will hear" or "listen," carrying the covenantal sense of attentive obedience, placed after "we will do" (naaseh).
- **24:10** — Hebrew livnat hassappir literally refers to brickwork or tiling of sappir, a deep blue gemstone widely identified in antiquity as lapis lazuli rather than modern sapphire.
- **24:11** — Hebrew atsilei ("nobles") is a rare term denoting chief or prominent men; the verb vayyechezu (Qal wayyiqtol of chazah, "gaze upon" or "see in vision") shifts from the ordinary verb raah ("saw") used in verse 10.
- **24:14** — Hebrew baal devarim literally means "owner of words" or "master of matters," an idiom denoting someone who has a legal case or dispute to resolve.
