## Text

**29:1** "Now this is the thing that you shall do for them to consecrate them to serve as priests to Me: take one bull from the herd and two rams without blemish,

**29:2** and unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil. You shall make them of fine wheat flour.

**29:3** You shall place them in one basket and present them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

**29:4** Then you shall bring Aaron and his sons near to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.

**29:5** You shall take the garments and clothe Aaron with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece, and fasten it on him with the woven band of the ephod.

**29:6** You shall set the turban on his head and place the holy crown on the turban.

**29:7** And you shall take the anointing oil, pour it onto his head, and anoint him.

**29:8** You shall bring his sons near and clothe them with tunics.

**29:9** You shall tie sashes around them, Aaron and his sons, and bind caps on them; and the priesthood shall be theirs by a perpetual statute. So you shall ordain Aaron and his sons.

**29:10** Then you shall bring the bull near in front of the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the bull's head.

**29:11** You shall slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**29:12** You shall take some of the bull's blood and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and all the blood you shall pour out at the base of the altar.

**29:13** You shall take all the fat that covers the entrails, the lobe on the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and offer them in smoke on the altar.

**29:14** But the flesh of the bull, its hide, and its dung you shall burn with fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.

**29:15** You shall then take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the ram's head.

**29:16** You shall slaughter the ram, take its blood, and dash it against the altar all around.

**29:17** Then you shall cut the ram into its pieces, wash its entrails and its legs, and place them with its pieces and with its head.

**29:18** You shall offer the entire ram in smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a soothing fragrance; it is a fire offering to the LORD.

**29:19** Then you shall take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the ram's head.

**29:20** You shall slaughter the ram, take some of its blood, and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear and on the lobes of his sons' right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet, and dash the blood against the altar all around.

**29:21** Then you shall take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle them on Aaron and on his garments, and on his sons and on his sons' garments with him. And he shall become holy, along with his garments, his sons, and his sons' garments with him.

**29:22** You shall also take from the ram the fat and the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and the right thigh, for it is a ram of ordination;

**29:23** and one round loaf of bread, one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD.

**29:24** You shall place all of them on the palms of Aaron and on the palms of his sons, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

**29:25** Then you shall take them from their hands and offer them in smoke on the altar on top of the burnt offering, for a soothing fragrance before the LORD; it is a fire offering to the LORD.

**29:26** You shall take the breast from the ram of ordination that is for Aaron and wave it as a wave offering before the LORD, and it shall be your portion.

**29:27** You shall consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution, which was waved and which was lifted up from the ram of ordination, from what is for Aaron and from what is for his sons.

**29:28** It shall belong to Aaron and to his sons as a perpetual due from the sons of Israel, for it is a contribution; and it shall be a contribution from the sons of Israel from the sacrifices of their peace offerings, their contribution to the LORD.

**29:29** The holy garments of Aaron shall belong to his sons after him, for them to be anointed in them and to be ordained in them.

**29:30** For seven days the priest in his place from among his sons, who enters the tent of meeting to minister in the holy place, shall wear them.

**29:31** And the ram of ordination you shall take, and you shall boil its flesh in a holy place.

**29:32** Aaron and his sons shall eat the ram's flesh and the bread that is in the basket at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**29:33** They shall eat those things by which atonement was made, to ordain them, to consecrate them; but an outsider shall not eat of them, for they are holy.

**29:34** And if any of the flesh of ordination or of the bread remains until the morning, then you shall burn what remains with fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is holy.

**29:35** So you shall do for Aaron and for his sons, in accordance with all that I have commanded you; for seven days you shall ordain them.

**29:36** You shall offer a bull as a sin offering each day for atonement, and you shall purify the altar when you make atonement for it, and anoint it to consecrate it.

**29:37** For seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and consecrate it; and the altar shall be most holy, and anything that touches the altar shall become holy.

**29:38** And this is what you shall offer upon the altar: two year-old lambs each day, continually.

**29:39** The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer at twilight.

**29:40** With the one lamb there shall be a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a drink offering of a fourth of a hin of wine.

**29:41** And the second lamb you shall offer at twilight; you shall do with it as with the morning grain offering and its drink offering, for a soothing fragrance, a fire offering to the LORD.

**29:42** It is a continual burnt offering for your generations at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak to you there.

**29:43** And there I will meet with the sons of Israel, and it will be consecrated by My glory.

**29:44** I will consecrate the tent of meeting and the altar, and Aaron and his sons I will consecrate to serve as priests to Me.

**29:45** And I will dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel, and I will be God to them.

**29:46** And they will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out from the land of Egypt so that I might dwell in their midst; I am the LORD their God."

## Translator Notes

- **29:9** — Ordain translates the Hebrew idiom meaning literally fill the hand of (Piel of ml' with yad), denoting formal installation into priestly office; the cognate plural noun millu'im (ordination) appears in vv. 22, 26, 27, 31, and 34.
- **29:27** — Hebrew pairs two ritual nouns with their cognate Hophal passive verbs: wave offering (tenufah) with was waved (hunaf, from nuf), and contribution (terumah) with was lifted up (huram, from rum).
- **29:36** — Purify the altar translates the privative Piel of ht' (literally de-sin or purge upon the altar), referring to ritual cleansing from impurity or sin.
- **29:42** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts from plural you in I will meet with you (lakhem) to singular you in to speak to you (elekha), distinguishing the LORD's meeting with Israel from His direct address to Moses.
- **29:43** — The masculine singular Niphal verb rendered it will be consecrated (niqdash) refers back to the tent of meeting (or the place of meeting) mentioned in v. 42.
