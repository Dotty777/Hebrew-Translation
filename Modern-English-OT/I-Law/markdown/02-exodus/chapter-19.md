## Text

**19:1** In the third month after the sons of Israel had gone out from the land of Egypt, on that day they came into the wilderness of Sinai.

**19:2** They set out from Rephidim, came into the wilderness of Sinai, and camped in the wilderness; and Israel camped there in front of the mountain.

**19:3** Now Moses went up to God, and the LORD called to him from the mountain, saying, "This is what you shall say to the house of Jacob and declare to the sons of Israel:

**19:4** 'You yourselves have seen what I did to Egypt, and how I carried you on wings of eagles and brought you to Myself.

**19:5** So now, if you will truly heed My voice and keep My covenant, then you shall be to Me a special treasure from among all the peoples, for the whole earth belongs to Me;

**19:6** and you yourselves shall be for Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.' These are the words that you shall speak to the sons of Israel."

**19:7** Then Moses came and summoned the elders of the people, and placed before them all these words that the LORD had commanded him.

**19:8** And all the people answered together and said, "Everything the LORD has spoken we will do." Then Moses brought back the words of the people to the LORD.

**19:9** The LORD said to Moses, "Look, I am coming to you in the dense cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you and may also trust in you forever." Then Moses told the words of the people to the LORD.

**19:10** And the LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their clothes,

**19:11** and let them be ready for the third day, for on the third day the LORD will come down in the sight of all the people upon Mount Sinai.

**19:12** You shall set boundaries for the people all around, saying, 'Guard yourselves against going up on the mountain or touching its edge; anyone who touches the mountain shall certainly be put to death.

**19:13** No hand shall touch him; rather, he shall surely be stoned or surely be shot through. Whether beast or man, he shall not live. When the ram's horn sounds a long blast, they may go up on the mountain.'"

**19:14** Then Moses descended from the mountain to the people and consecrated the people, and they washed their clothes.

**19:15** And he said to the people, "Be ready by the third day; do not go near a woman."

**19:16** Now on the third day, when morning came, there were thunder and lightning, and a heavy cloud upon the mountain, and a very loud sound of a ram's horn, and all the people who were in the camp trembled.

**19:17** Then Moses brought the people out from the camp to meet God, and they stationed themselves at the base of the mountain.

**19:18** Now Mount Sinai smoked completely, because the LORD had come down upon it in fire; and its smoke went up like the smoke of a furnace, and the entire mountain trembled violently.

**19:19** And the sound of the ram's horn kept growing louder and louder; Moses would speak, and God would answer him with a voice.

**19:20** Then the LORD descended upon Mount Sinai, to the summit of the mountain; and the LORD summoned Moses to the summit of the mountain, and Moses went up.

**19:21** And the LORD said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people, or else they will break through to the LORD to look, and many of them will fall.

**19:22** Moreover, let the priests who draw near to the LORD consecrate themselves, or else the LORD will break out against them."

**19:23** Moses said to the LORD, "The people cannot go up to Mount Sinai, for You Yourself warned us, saying, 'Set boundaries around the mountain and consecrate it.'"

**19:24** Then the LORD said to him, "Go, descend, and then come up, you and Aaron with you; but the priests and the people must not break through to come up to the LORD, or else He will break out against them."

**19:25** Then Moses descended to the people and told them.

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — The noun segullah ('special treasure') designates a king's private, prized possession, denoting Israel's unique status as the LORD's own treasured people.
- **19:13** — The phrase 'when the ram's horn sounds a long blast' translates bimshokh hayyovel (literally 'at the drawing out of the ram's horn'), referring to a prolonged signal blast; 'stoned' and 'shot through' both employ emphatic infinitive-absolute plus Niphal imperfect constructions.
- **19:19** — Hebrew bekol literally means 'with a voice' or 'with a sound,' understood either as an audible voice so the people could hear (as promised in v. 9) or as thunder echoing v. 16.
- **19:25** — Hebrew vayyomer alehem ('and he said to them') unusually concludes without quoting Moses' words, indicating succinctly that he relayed the LORD's warning to the people.
