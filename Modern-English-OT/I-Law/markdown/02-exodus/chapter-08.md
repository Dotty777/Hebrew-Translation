## Text

**8:1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your hand with your staff over the rivers, over the canals, and over the pools, and bring up the frogs over the land of Egypt.'"

**8:2** So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

**8:3** Yet the magicians did the same with their secret arts, and they brought up frogs over the land of Egypt.

**8:4** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Plead with the LORD so that He may remove the frogs from me and from my people, and I will send the people away so that they may sacrifice to the LORD."

**8:5** And Moses said to Pharaoh, "Take the honor over me: for what time shall I plead for you, for your servants, and for your people, to cut off the frogs from you and from your houses? Only in the Nile will they remain."

**8:6** And he said, "For tomorrow." And he said, "According to your word, so that you may know that there is no one like the LORD our God.

**8:7** The frogs will depart from you, from your houses, from your servants, and from your people; only in the Nile will they remain."

**8:8** Then Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to the LORD concerning the frogs that He had imposed upon Pharaoh.

**8:9** And the LORD did according to the word of Moses, and the frogs died out of the houses, out of the courtyards, and out of the fields.

**8:10** They piled them up in heaps upon heaps, and the land stank.

**8:11** When Pharaoh saw that there was relief, he made his heart heavy and did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**8:12** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the ground, and it will become gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.'"

**8:13** And they did so: Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the ground, and the gnats came upon people and upon animals; all the dust of the ground became gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.

**8:14** The magicians did likewise with their secret arts to bring out the gnats, yet they were not able; and the gnats were on people and on animals.

**8:15** Then the magicians said to Pharaoh, "It is the finger of God." Yet Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**8:16** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and station yourself before Pharaoh—look, he is going out to the water—and say to him, 'This is what the LORD says: Send My people away so that they may serve Me.

**8:17** For if you do not send My people away, look, I am sending a swarm of flies against you, against your servants, against your people, and into your houses; and the houses of Egypt will be full of the swarm of flies, and also the ground on which they are.

**8:18** Yet on that day I will set apart the land of Goshen, on which My people stand, so that no swarm of flies will be there, in order that you may know that I am the LORD in the midst of the land.

**8:19** And I will put redemption between My people and your people; tomorrow this sign will take place.'"

**8:20** And the LORD did so: a heavy swarm of flies came into Pharaoh's house and the houses of his servants, and throughout all the land of Egypt the land was being ruined because of the swarm of flies.

**8:21** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Go, sacrifice to your God in the land."

**8:22** And Moses said, "It is not right to do so, for we would be sacrificing what is an abomination to the Egyptians to the LORD our God. If we sacrifice what is an abomination to the Egyptians before their eyes, will they not stone us?

**8:23** We will go a journey of three days into the wilderness and sacrifice to the LORD our God, just as He tells us."

**8:24** And Pharaoh said, "I myself will send you away, and you may sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness; only you must not go very far away. Plead on my behalf."

**8:25** And Moses said, "Look, I am going out from you, and I will plead with the LORD so that the swarm of flies will depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people tomorrow; only let Pharaoh not deal deceitfully again by not sending the people away to sacrifice to the LORD."

**8:26** So Moses went out from Pharaoh and pleaded with the LORD.

**8:27** And the LORD did according to the word of Moses, and the swarm of flies departed from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; not one remained.

**8:28** Yet Pharaoh made his heart heavy this time also, and he did not send the people away.

## Translator Notes

- **8:5** — The Hithpael imperative of פאר ("Take the honor over me") is a polite idiom by which Moses grants Pharaoh the privilege of specifying the time for the frogs to be removed.
- **8:11** — The Hiphil infinitive absolute הַכְבֵּד ("made heavy") continues the narrative sequence of the preceding finite verb. Throughout Exodus 8-9, כבד ("make/become heavy," 8:11, 28; 9:7, 34) is kept distinct from חזק ("grow/make hard," 8:15; 9:12, 35) and echoes the "heavy" (כָּבֵד) plagues.
- **8:17** — The Hebrew pairs two stems of the root שׁלח: Piel מְשַׁלֵּחַ ("send away") and Hiphil מַשְׁלִיחַ ("send [against]"). The collective noun עָרֹב denotes a dense swarm of biting insects or flies.
- **8:19** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads פְדֻת ("redemption, deliverance"), portraying a redeeming separation between Israel and Egypt, whereas ancient versions such as the Septuagint render it more generally as "division" or "distinction."
- **8:27** — The verb form וַיָּסַר (from סור) can be parsed either as Qal ("departed," matching Moses's Qal verb in 8:25 and lacking the object marker אֶת) or as Hiphil ("He removed").
