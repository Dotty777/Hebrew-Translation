## Text

**22:1** If the thief is found in the act of breaking in, and he is struck and dies, there is no bloodguilt for him.

**22:2** If the sun has risen upon him, there is bloodguilt for him. He must certainly pay restitution; if he possesses nothing, then he shall be sold for his theft.

**22:3** If what was stolen is actually found alive in his hand, whether ox, donkey, or sheep, he shall pay double.

**22:4** When a man causes a field or a vineyard to be grazed, and releases his livestock so that it grazes in another's field, he shall pay restitution from the best of his field and the best of his vineyard.

**22:5** When fire breaks out and catches thorns, so that stacked grain or the standing grain or the field is consumed, the one who kindled the blaze must certainly pay restitution.

**22:6** When a man gives money or articles to his neighbor to keep, and it is stolen from the man's house, if the thief is found, he shall pay double.

**22:7** If the thief is not found, then the owner of the house shall draw near to God to establish whether he has stretched out his hand to his neighbor's goods.

**22:8** In every case of transgression, whether for an ox, for a donkey, for a sheep, for a garment, or for any lost item of which someone says, "This is it," the case of the two of them shall come to God; the one whom God declares guilty shall pay double to his neighbor.

**22:9** When a man gives a donkey, or an ox, or a sheep, or any animal to his neighbor to keep, and it dies or is injured or is carried off with no one seeing,

**22:10** an oath by the LORD shall be between the two of them that he has not stretched out his hand to his neighbor's goods. Its owner shall accept it, and he shall not pay restitution.

**22:11** But if it is indeed stolen from him, he shall pay restitution to its owner.

**22:12** If it is indeed torn to pieces, he shall bring it as evidence; for what was torn he shall not pay restitution.

**22:13** And when a man borrows something from his neighbor, and it is injured or dies without its owner being with it, he must certainly pay restitution.

**22:14** If its owner is with it, he shall not pay restitution; if it was hired, it came for its rental fee.

**22:15** And if a man seduces a virgin who is not betrothed and lies with her, he must certainly pay the bride-price for her to be his wife.

**22:16** If her father firmly refuses to give her to him, he shall weigh out silver according to the bride-price for virgins.

**22:17** You shall not let a sorceress live.

**22:18** Anyone who lies with an animal must certainly be put to death.

**22:19** Anyone who sacrifices to any god, except to the LORD alone, shall be devoted to destruction.

**22:20** You shall not mistreat a sojourner, nor shall you oppress him, for you were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

**22:21** You shall not afflict any widow or fatherless child.

**22:22** If you afflict him in any way, and he indeed cries out to Me, I will certainly hear his cry.

**22:23** And My anger will burn, and I will kill you by the sword; your wives will become widows, and your children will be fatherless.

**22:24** If you lend money to My people, to the poor person with you, you shall not be like a creditor to him; you shall not impose interest upon him.

**22:25** If you indeed take your neighbor's garment as a pledge, you shall return it to him by sunset.

**22:26** For that is his only covering; it is his garment for his skin. In what will he sleep? And when he cries out to Me, I will hear, for I am gracious.

**22:27** You shall not revile God, nor shall you curse a leader among your people.

**22:28** You shall not delay your full yield or your flowing juice; you shall give the firstborn of your sons to Me.

**22:29** You shall do likewise with your ox and your flock: for seven days it shall be with its mother; on the eighth day you shall give it to Me.

**22:30** And you shall be holy people to Me, and you shall not eat meat torn in the field; you shall throw it to the dogs.

## Translator Notes

- **22:4** — Hebrew uses two stems of the root ba'ar (Hiphil 'cause to be grazed' and Piel 'graze'), playing on the cognate noun be'ir ('livestock,' spelled with the archaic 3ms suffix -oh) and anticipating the homonymous root ba'ar ('burn') in 22:5.
- **22:8** — Both occurrences of 'God' translate elohim (with the definite article in the first clause), which in the second clause takes a 3mp Hiphil imperfect verb (yarshi'un, 'declare guilty'). While some ancient and traditional interpretations understand elohim here and in 22:7 as human judges, the literal rendering 'God' aligns with the oath before the LORD in 22:10.
- **22:14** — The terse Hebrew idiom ba bisekharo (literally 'it came in its hire') means that any risk of injury or death was already covered by the rental fee paid for the hired animal.
- **22:28** — Literally 'your fullness and your tear/dripping'; mele'ah denotes the full yield of harvested grain, while the rare noun dema' (occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible) refers metaphorically to the pressed liquid juice of wine and olive oil.
