## Text

**17:1** Then the entire congregation of the sons of Israel set out from the wilderness of Sin by their stages at the command of the LORD, and they camped at Rephidim; yet there was no water for the people to drink.

**17:2** Then the people contended with Moses and said, "Give us water so that we may drink." Moses said to them, "Why are you contending with me? Why are you testing the LORD?"

**17:3** But the people thirsted there for water, and the people grumbled against Moses and said, "Why then did you bring us up from Egypt, to put me and my children and my livestock to death with thirst?"

**17:4** So Moses cried out to the LORD, saying, "What am I to do with this people? A little longer and they will stone me."

**17:5** The LORD said to Moses, "Pass on ahead of the people, and take some of the elders of Israel with you; also take in your hand your staff with which you struck the Nile, and go.

**17:6** See, I will stand before you there on the rock at Horeb; you shall strike the rock, and water will come out from it, and the people will drink." And Moses did so in the sight of the elders of Israel.

**17:7** And he called the name of the place Massah and Meribah, because of the contention of the sons of Israel and because they tested the LORD, saying, "Is the LORD in our midst, or not?"

**17:8** Now Amalek came and fought with Israel at Rephidim.

**17:9** And Moses said to Joshua, "Choose men for us, and go out and fight against Amalek. Tomorrow I will take my stand on the top of the hill with the staff of God in my hand."

**17:10** So Joshua did just as Moses had said to him, to fight against Amalek; and Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

**17:11** And it happened that whenever Moses lifted his hand, Israel prevailed; yet whenever he let his hand down, Amalek prevailed.

**17:12** Now Moses' hands were heavy, so they took a stone and placed it under him, and he sat on it; and Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on this side and one on that side. Thus his hands remained steady until the setting of the sun.

**17:13** And Joshua overpowered Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.

**17:14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Write this as a memorial in the book and put it in the ears of Joshua, for I will completely wipe out the memory of Amalek from under the heavens."

**17:15** And Moses built an altar and called its name "The LORD is my banner."

**17:16** And he said, "For a hand is upon the throne of the LORD; the LORD will be at war against Amalek from generation to generation."

## Translator Notes

- **17:7** — The place names echo the verbs in v. 2 and v. 7: Massah derives from nasah (Piel, 'to test'), and Meribah derives from rib (Qal, 'to contend').
- **17:12** — Hebrew literally reads 'and his hands were steadiness' (using the feminine singular noun emunah with a singular verb), expressing that Moses' hands remained steadily upheld.
- **17:16** — Hebrew reads 'upon the kes of Yah,' using a rare shortened form of kisse ('throne') paired with the shortened divine name Yah. The idiom 'a hand upon the throne of the LORD' is understood either as a solemn oath gesture or as Amalek's hostile hand raised against God's throne.
