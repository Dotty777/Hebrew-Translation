## Text

**15:1** Then Moses and the sons of Israel sang this song to the LORD and spoke, saying, "I will sing to the LORD, for He is surpassingly exalted; horse and its rider He has cast into the sea.

**15:2** My strength and song is the LORD, and He has become salvation for me; this is my God, and I will glorify Him, the God of my father, and I will lift Him high.

**15:3** The LORD is a man of battle; the LORD is His name.

**15:4** The chariots of Pharaoh and his army He has hurled into the sea, and the finest of his officers were sunk in the Sea of Reeds.

**15:5** Deeps covered them; they descended into the depths like a stone.

**15:6** Your right hand, LORD, is majestic in strength; Your right hand, LORD, crushes the enemy.

**15:7** And in the abundance of Your majesty You tear down those who rise against You; You send out Your burning wrath; it devours them like stubble.

**15:8** And by the blast of Your nostrils waters were heaped up; flowing streams stood upright like a mound; deeps congealed in the heart of the sea.

**15:9** The enemy said, 'I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide the plunder; my soul will have its fill of them; I will unsheathe my sword; my hand will dispossess them.'

**15:10** You blew with Your breath; the sea covered them; they plunged like lead in mighty waters.

**15:11** Who is like You among the gods, LORD? Who is like You, majestic in holiness, awe-inspiring in praises, performing wonders?

**15:12** You extended Your right hand; the earth swallowed them.

**15:13** In Your loyal love You led the people whom You redeemed; by Your strength You guided them to Your holy dwelling.

**15:14** Peoples have heard; they quake; anguish has seized the inhabitants of Philistia.

**15:15** Then the chieftains of Edom were terrified; trembling seizes the mighty ones of Moab; all the inhabitants of Canaan melted away.

**15:16** Terror and dread will fall upon them; by the greatness of Your arm they will grow still like a stone, until Your people pass over, LORD, until the people whom You have acquired pass over.

**15:17** You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain of Your inheritance, the place for Your dwelling that You have made, LORD, the sanctuary, Lord, that Your hands established.

**15:18** The LORD will reign forever and ever."

**15:19** For Pharaoh's horses entered the sea with his chariots and his horsemen, and the LORD turned the waters of the sea back over them, while the sons of Israel walked on dry ground in the middle of the sea.

**15:20** Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron's sister, took the tambourine in her hand, and all the women went out behind her with tambourines and dances.

**15:21** And Miriam responded to them, "Sing to the LORD, for He is surpassingly exalted; horse and its rider He has cast into the sea."

**15:22** Then Moses led Israel onward from the Sea of Reeds, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; they walked three days in the wilderness and found no water.

**15:23** And they arrived at Marah, yet they could not drink water from Marah, for it was bitter; therefore its name was called Marah.

**15:24** And the people grumbled against Moses, saying, "What are we to drink?"

**15:25** And he cried out to the LORD, and the LORD showed him a tree; he threw it into the water, and the water turned sweet. There He set for them a statute and an ordinance, and there He tested them.

**15:26** And He said, "If you carefully listen to the voice of the LORD your God, and do what is upright in His eyes, and pay attention to His commandments, and observe all His statutes, none of the diseases that I placed on Egypt will I place on you, for I am the LORD who heals you."

**15:27** And they arrived at Elim, and there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees; so they camped there beside the water.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Hebrew uses the shortened poetic divine name Yah and the form zimrat, 'song' (or possibly 'might/defense'), sharing the first-person possessive force of 'my strength.' The verb rendered 'I will glorify Him' is a hapax legomenon Hiphil of navah, meaning 'glorify/beautify' or possibly 'prepare a dwelling for.'
- **15:4** — Hebrew yam-suph literally means 'Sea of Reeds' (so also in 15:22), traditionally known as the Red Sea following the Septuagint.
- **15:6** — The Niphal participle ne'dari ('majestic') carries the archaic poetic linking vowel known as hireq compaginis.
- **15:25** — Hebrew uses masculine singular pronominal suffixes ('for him,' 'tested him') referring collectively to the people of Israel, which is grammatically singular.
