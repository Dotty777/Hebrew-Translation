## Text

**7:1** Then the LORD said to Moses, "See, I have appointed you as God to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother will be your prophet.

**7:2** You will speak all that I command you, and Aaron your brother will speak to Pharaoh so that he should release the sons of Israel from his land.

**7:3** But I Myself will harden Pharaoh's heart, and I will multiply My signs and My wonders in the land of Egypt.

**7:4** And Pharaoh will not listen to you, so I will set My hand against Egypt and bring My divisions, My people the sons of Israel, out of the land of Egypt by great judgments.

**7:5** And the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD when I stretch out My hand over Egypt and bring the sons of Israel out from among them."

**7:6** Then Moses and Aaron acted; exactly as the LORD had commanded them, so they did.

**7:7** Now Moses was eighty years old and Aaron was eighty-three years old at the time they spoke to Pharaoh.

**7:8** Then the LORD said to Moses and to Aaron,

**7:9** "When Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, 'Produce a wonder for yourselves,' then you will say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down before Pharaoh,' and it will become a serpent."

**7:10** So Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh, and they did so, just as the LORD had commanded; and Aaron threw his staff down before Pharaoh and before his servants, and it became a serpent.

**7:11** Then Pharaoh too summoned the wise men and the sorcerers; and they too, the magicians of Egypt, did the same through their secret arts.

**7:12** Each man threw his staff down, and they became serpents; yet Aaron's staff swallowed up their staffs.

**7:13** Yet Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**7:14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Pharaoh's heart is heavy; he has refused to release the people.

**7:15** Go to Pharaoh in the morning; look, he is going out to the water. Station yourself to meet him on the bank of the Nile, and take in your hand the staff that was turned into a snake,

**7:16** and say to him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you, saying: Release My people so that they may serve Me in the wilderness. Yet look, up to now you have not listened.

**7:17** This is what the LORD says: By this you will know that I am the LORD. Look, I am about to strike the water that is in the Nile with the staff that is in My hand, and it will be turned into blood.

**7:18** The fish that are in the Nile will die, and the Nile will stink, and the Egyptians will weary themselves trying to drink water from the Nile.'"

**7:19** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, over their rivers, over their canals, over their ponds, and over every collection of their waters, so that they become blood,' and there will be blood throughout all the land of Egypt, both in wood and in stone."

**7:20** So Moses and Aaron did so, just as the LORD had commanded; and he lifted up the staff and struck the water that was in the Nile before the eyes of Pharaoh and before the eyes of his servants, and all the water that was in the Nile was turned into blood.

**7:21** And the fish that were in the Nile died, and the Nile stank, so that the Egyptians could not drink water from the Nile; and the blood was throughout all the land of Egypt.

**7:22** Yet the magicians of Egypt did the same through their secret arts; so Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had spoken.

**7:23** Then Pharaoh turned and went into his house, and he did not set his heart even on this.

**7:24** Then all the Egyptians dug around the Nile for water to drink, because they were unable to drink from the waters of the Nile.

**7:25** And seven days were completed after the LORD struck the Nile.

**7:26** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh and say to him, 'This is what the LORD says: Release My people so that they may serve Me.

**7:27** But if you are unwilling to release them, look, I am about to plague all your territory with frogs.

**7:28** And the Nile will teem with frogs; they will come up and go into your house, into your bedroom, and onto your bed, into the houses of your servants and among your people, and into your ovens and into your kneading troughs.

**7:29** And upon you, and upon your people, and upon all your servants the frogs will come up.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Hebrew netattika elohim literally means "I have given/made you God," using natan ("give/appoint") with a double accusative to designate Moses as God's authoritative representative before Pharaoh, with Aaron acting as his prophetic spokesman.
- **7:13** — Exodus 7 uses three distinct Hebrew roots for Pharaoh's obduracy: qashah in the Hiphil ("make hard/stiff," 7:3), chazaq in the intransitive Qal ("grow hard/strong," 7:13, 22), and the stative kaved (literally "heavy," i.e., "unyielding," 7:14).
- **7:17** — The phrase "in My hand" (beyadi) continues the LORD's first-person oracle ("I am about to strike," anoki makkeh), attributing the action of His human agent holding the staff (7:15, 19-20) directly to Himself (cf. 7:25).
- **7:19** — Hebrew uba'etsim uba'avanim (literally "in the woods and in the stones," i.e., "in wood and in stone") refers to water held in wooden and stone containers.
- **7:26** — Verses 7:26-29 follow the Masoretic versification; the Vulgate numbers them 8:1-4.
