## Text

**26:1** Now you shall make the tabernacle out of ten curtains of twisted fine linen, and blue, purple, and crimson; you shall make them with cherubim, the work of a skilled designer.

**26:2** The length of each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; there shall be one measurement for all the curtains.

**26:3** Five of the curtains shall be joined one to another, and five curtains shall be joined one to another.

**26:4** And you shall make loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain at the end of the joined set, and you shall do likewise on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second joined set.

**26:5** You shall make fifty loops on the one curtain, and you shall make fifty loops at the end of the curtain that is in the second joined set; the loops shall correspond one to another.

**26:6** And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and join the curtains one to another with the clasps, so that the tabernacle shall be one.

**26:7** And you shall make curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle; you shall make them as eleven curtains.

**26:8** The length of each curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; there shall be one measurement for the eleven curtains.

**26:9** And you shall join five of the curtains by themselves and six of the curtains by themselves, and you shall double over the sixth curtain at the front of the tent.

**26:10** And you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain that is outermost in the joined set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain of the second joined set.

**26:11** And you shall make fifty clasps of bronze, and put the clasps into the loops and join the tent together, so that it shall be one.

**26:12** And as for the overhanging excess of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that remains over shall hang down over the back of the tabernacle.

**26:13** And the cubit on this side and the cubit on that side of what remains over in the length of the curtains of the tent shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle, on this side and on that side, to cover it.

**26:14** And you shall make a covering for the tent of ram skins dyed red, and a covering of dugong skins on top.

**26:15** And you shall make the planks for the tabernacle of acacia wood, standing upright.

**26:16** Ten cubits shall be the length of a plank, and a cubit and a half the width of each plank.

**26:17** Each plank shall have two tenons, set parallel one to another; so you shall do for all the planks of the tabernacle.

**26:18** And you shall make the planks for the tabernacle: twenty planks for the south side, southward.

**26:19** And you shall make forty bases of silver under the twenty planks: two bases under one plank for its two tenons, and two bases under the next plank for its two tenons.

**26:20** And for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, there shall be twenty planks,

**26:21** and their forty bases of silver: two bases under one plank, and two bases under the next plank.

**26:22** And for the rear of the tabernacle, westward, you shall make six planks.

**26:23** And you shall make two planks for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear.

**26:24** And they shall be doubled at the bottom, and together they shall be complete at the top, at the single ring; so it shall be for both of them; they shall be for the two corners.

**26:25** So there shall be eight planks with their bases of silver, sixteen bases: two bases under one plank, and two bases under the next plank.

**26:26** And you shall make crossbars of acacia wood: five for the planks of the one side of the tabernacle,

**26:27** and five crossbars for the planks of the second side of the tabernacle, and five crossbars for the planks of the side of the tabernacle at the rear, westward.

**26:28** And the middle crossbar, in the center of the planks, shall run through from end to end.

**26:29** And you shall overlay the planks with gold, and make their rings of gold as housings for the crossbars; and you shall overlay the crossbars with gold.

**26:30** And you shall set up the tabernacle according to its specification that you were shown on the mountain.

**26:31** And you shall make a veil of blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen; it shall be made with cherubim, the work of a skilled designer.

**26:32** And you shall hang it on four pillars of acacia wood overlaid with gold, their hooks of gold, on four bases of silver.

**26:33** And you shall hang the veil beneath the clasps, and bring the ark of the testimony in there, inside the veil; and the veil shall separate the Holy Place from the Holy of Holies for you.

**26:34** And you shall put the atonement cover on the ark of the testimony in the Holy of Holies.

**26:35** And you shall place the table outside the veil, with the lampstand across from the table on the south side of the tabernacle, while the table you shall put on the north side.

**26:36** And you shall make a screen for the entrance of the tent of blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen, the work of an embroiderer.

**26:37** And you shall make five pillars of acacia wood for the screen and overlay them with gold, their hooks of gold; and you shall cast five bases of bronze for them.

## Translator Notes

- **26:3** — The expression rendered 'one to another' is literally the feminine Hebrew idiom 'a woman to her sister' (recurring in 26:5, 6, 17).
- **26:14** — The meaning of the Hebrew plural noun translated 'dugong skins' (techashim) is uncertain; it is variously understood as hides of Red Sea marine mammals such as dugongs or dolphins, or as fine tanned or beaded leather.
- **26:17** — 'Tenons' translates a Hebrew noun literally meaning 'hands' (yadot). The rare Pual participle rendered 'set parallel' (meshullabot) comes from a root associated with rungs or crosspieces, indicating two matched projections aligned with each other.
- **26:24** — The Masoretic text pairs two different terms here: the Qal participle 'doubled' or 'twinned' (to'amim, from ta'am) at the bottom, and the adjective 'complete' or 'whole' (tammim, from tamam) at the top, though some ancient versions read 'twinned' in both places. 'At the top' is literally the distributive singular 'at its top.'
- **26:34** — 'Atonement cover' translates Hebrew kapporet (traditionally 'mercy seat'), derived from the verb root meaning 'to atone' or 'cover' and referring to the golden lid of the ark.
