## Text

**6:1** And the LORD said to Moses, "Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh, because by a strong hand he will release them, and by a strong hand he will drive them from his land."

**6:2** And God spoke to Moses and said to him, "I am the LORD.

**6:3** And I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob as God Almighty, yet as for My name, the LORD, I was not known to them.

**6:4** Moreover, I established My covenant with them to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their sojournings, in which they sojourned.

**6:5** And I Myself have also heard the groaning of the sons of Israel, whom the Egyptians are forcing into servitude, and I have remembered My covenant.

**6:6** Therefore say to the sons of Israel, 'I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from beneath the burdens of Egypt, and I will rescue you from their servitude, and I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with great judgments.

**6:7** And I will take you for Myself as a people, and I will be God to you; then you will know that I am the LORD your God, the One who brings you out from beneath the burdens of Egypt.

**6:8** And I will bring you into the land that I lifted My hand to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, and I will give it to you as a possession. I am the LORD.'"

**6:9** So Moses spoke in this way to the sons of Israel, but they did not listen to Moses because of shortness of spirit and because of harsh servitude.

**6:10** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:11** "Go in, speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, so that he will release the sons of Israel from his land."

**6:12** But Moses spoke before the LORD, saying, "Look, the sons of Israel have not listened to me; how then will Pharaoh listen to me, when I am uncircumcised of lips?"

**6:13** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, and He commanded them concerning the sons of Israel and concerning Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

**6:14** These are the heads of their fathers' houses: the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi; these are the clans of Reuben.

**6:15** And the sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of the Canaanite woman; these are the clans of Simeon.

**6:16** And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari; and the years of Levi's life were one hundred thirty-seven years.

**6:17** The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei, according to their clans.

**6:18** And the sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel; and the years of Kohath's life were one hundred thirty-three years.

**6:19** And the sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites according to their generations.

**6:20** And Amram took Jochebed, his aunt, for himself as a wife, and she bore Aaron and Moses to him; and the years of Amram's life were one hundred thirty-seven years.

**6:21** And the sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

**6:22** And the sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

**6:23** And Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, for himself as a wife, and she bore to him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**6:24** And the sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the clans of the Korahites.

**6:25** And Eleazar, the son of Aaron, took one of the daughters of Putiel for himself as a wife, and she bore Phinehas to him. These are the heads of the fathers of the Levites according to their clans.

**6:26** This is the Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, "Bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt according to their divisions."

**6:27** They were the ones who spoke to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to bring the sons of Israel out of Egypt; this was Moses and Aaron.

**6:28** Now it came about on the day the LORD spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,

**6:29** that the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, "I am the LORD; speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, all that I am speaking to you."

**6:30** But Moses said before the LORD, "Look, I am uncircumcised of lips, so how will Pharaoh listen to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — The preposition be- before El Shaddai ("God Almighty") is a beth essentiae ("as / in the capacity of"). The phrase "My name, the LORD" stands in casus pendens (or adverbial specification) before the Niphal perfect noda'ti (root yd'), which may be understood passively ("I was not known") or reflexively ("I did not make Myself known").
- **6:8** — "Lifted My hand" renders the literal Hebrew idiom nasati et-yadi, a gesture accompanying a solemn oath.
- **6:9** — "Shortness of spirit" translates the Hebrew idiom qotser ruach (literally "shortness of spirit/breath"), denoting exhaustion, anguish, or despondency.
- **6:12** — "Uncircumcised of lips" translates the Hebrew idiom aral sefatayim (repeated in 6:30), denoting speech that is impeded, awkward, or unpersuasive.
