## Text

**28:1** Now you, bring near to yourself Aaron your brother, and his sons with him, from the midst of the sons of Israel, so that he may serve as priest to Me—Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron.

**28:2** And you shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for splendor.

**28:3** And you shall speak to all who are wise of heart, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, and they shall make the garments of Aaron to consecrate him, so that he may serve as priest to Me.

**28:4** And these are the garments that they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a tunic of checkered weave, a turban, and a sash. They shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons, so that he may serve as priest to Me.

**28:5** And they shall take the gold, the blue, the purple, the crimson, and the fine linen.

**28:6** And they shall make the ephod of gold, blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen, the work of a skilled designer.

**28:7** It shall have two joining shoulder pieces at its two edges, and it shall be joined together.

**28:8** And the woven band for fastening it, which is on it, shall be like its workmanship, of one piece with it: of gold, blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen.

**28:9** And you shall take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel:

**28:10** six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the remaining six on the second stone, according to their births.

**28:11** Like the work of a stone craftsman, as the engravings of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel; you shall make them enclosed in gold filigree settings.

**28:12** And you shall place the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of reminder for the sons of Israel; and Aaron shall bear their names before the LORD on his two shoulders as a reminder.

**28:13** And you shall make gold filigree settings.

**28:14** And two chains of pure gold you shall make twisted, of braided work; and you shall attach the braided chains onto the filigree settings.

**28:15** And you shall make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a skilled designer; like the work of the ephod you shall make it: of gold, blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen you shall make it.

**28:16** It shall be square, folded double: a span its length and a span its width.

**28:17** And you shall fill it with a mounting of stones, four rows of stones: a row of carnelian, topaz, and emerald shall be the first row.

**28:18** And the second row shall be turquoise, lapis lazuli, and chalcedony.

**28:19** And the third row shall be jacinth, agate, and amethyst.

**28:20** And the fourth row shall be beryl, onyx, and jasper; they shall be framed in gold filigree in their mountings.

**28:21** And the stones shall be according to the names of the sons of Israel, twelve according to their names; like the engravings of a signet, each according to his name, they shall be for the twelve tribes.

**28:22** And you shall make on the breastpiece twisted chains of braided work, of pure gold.

**28:29** And Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel in the breastpiece of judgment on his heart when he enters the holy place, as a reminder before the LORD continually.

**28:30** And you shall place into the breastpiece of judgment the Urim and the Thummim, and they shall be on the heart of Aaron when he comes before the LORD; thus Aaron shall bear the judgment of the sons of Israel on his heart before the LORD continually.

**28:31** And you shall make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue.

**28:32** And its head opening shall be in its center; it shall have a border all around its opening, the work of a weaver; it shall be like the opening of a coat of mail, so that it will not be torn.

**28:33** And you shall make on its hem pomegranates of blue, purple, and crimson, on its hem all around, and bells of gold between them all around:

**28:34** a bell of gold and a pomegranate, a bell of gold and a pomegranate, on the hem of the robe all around.

**28:35** And it shall be on Aaron for ministering, and its sound shall be heard when he enters the holy place before the LORD and when he goes out, so that he will not die.

**28:36** And you shall make a plate of pure gold, and you shall engrave on it, as the engravings of a signet, 'Holiness to the LORD.'

**28:37** And you shall place it on a blue cord, and it shall be on the turban; it shall be at the front of the turban.

**28:38** And it shall be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron shall bear the guilt of the holy things that the sons of Israel consecrate, regarding all their holy gifts; and it shall be on his forehead continually, for their acceptance before the LORD.

**28:39** And you shall weave the tunic of fine linen in a checkered pattern, and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and you shall make a sash, the work of an embroiderer.

**28:40** And for the sons of Aaron you shall make tunics, and you shall make sashes for them, and you shall make caps for them, for glory and for splendor.

**28:41** And you shall clothe Aaron your brother and his sons with him in them, and you shall anoint them, ordain them, and consecrate them, so that they may serve as priests to Me.

**28:42** And make for them linen undergarments to cover naked flesh; they shall reach from the waist to the thighs.

**28:43** And they shall be on Aaron and on his sons when they enter the tent of meeting, or when they approach the altar to minister in the holy place, so that they will not bear guilt and die. It shall be a perpetual statute for him and for his seed after him.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**28:24** *(after `28:22`)* And you shall attach the two braided cords of gold to the two rings at the edges of the breastpiece.

**28:25** *(after `28:22`)* And the two ends of the two braided cords you shall attach to the two filigree settings, and place them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod at its front.

**28:26** *(after `28:22`)* And you shall make two gold rings and set them on the two ends of the breastpiece, along its border facing inward toward the ephod.

**28:27** *(after `28:22`)* And you shall make two gold rings and place them low on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, toward its front, right beside its seam above the woven band of the ephod.

**28:28** *(after `28:22`)* And they shall tie the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so that it rests upon the woven band of the ephod and the breastpiece will not slip loose from the ephod.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Piel infinitive construct with a masculine singular suffix (לְכַהֲנוֹ־לִי, literally "for his serving as priest to Me") focuses on Aaron as head of the priesthood even when his sons are named alongside him (compare 28:4, contrasting with the plural Piel verb וְכִהֲנוּ in 28:41).
- **28:14** — The rare term מִגְבָּלֹת (cognate with גַּבְלֻת in 28:22) is paired with מַעֲשֵׂה עֲבֹת ("braided work") and denotes twisted, cord-like craftsmanship, though some interpreters connect it with גְּבוּל ("border/end").
- **28:17** — The precise mineralogical identification of several of the twelve Hebrew gemstone names in 28:17-20 (including פִּטְדָה, יָהֲלֹם, לֶשֶׁם, and תַּרְשִׁישׁ) remains uncertain in modern scholarship; established ancient Near Eastern and standard equivalents are used here.
- **28:41** — "Ordain them" renders the Piel idiom וּמִלֵּאתָ אֶת־יָדָם (literally "and you shall fill their hand"), the standard priestly expression for installing and authorizing priests in office.
