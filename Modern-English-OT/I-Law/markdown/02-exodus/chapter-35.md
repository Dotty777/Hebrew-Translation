## Text

**35:1** Then Moses assembled the whole congregation of the sons of Israel and said to them, "These are the things that the LORD has commanded to be done:

**35:2** For six days work shall be done, but on the seventh day it shall be holy for you, a Sabbath of complete rest to the LORD; everyone who does work on it shall be put to death.

**35:3** You shall not light a fire in any of your dwelling places on the Sabbath day."

**35:4** And Moses spoke to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the thing that the LORD has commanded, saying:

**35:5** 'Take from among yourselves a contribution for the LORD. Everyone whose heart is willing shall bring it, the contribution for the LORD: gold, silver, and bronze,

**35:6** and blue, purple, and crimson yarn, fine linen, and goat hair,

**35:7** and ram skins dyed red, skins of fine leather, and acacia wood,

**35:9** and onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

**35:10** And let everyone among you who is skilled of heart come and make everything that the LORD has commanded:

**35:11** the tabernacle, its tent and its covering, its clasps and its planks, its crossbars, its pillars, and its bases;

**35:12** the ark and its poles, the atonement cover, and the veil of the screen;

**35:13** the table and its poles and all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;

**35:14** and the lampstand for the light, its utensils, its lamps, and the oil for the light;

**35:16** the altar of burnt offering and the bronze grating that belongs to it, its poles and all its utensils, the basin and its stand;

**35:19** the finely woven garments for ministering in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests.'"

**35:20** Then the whole congregation of the sons of Israel went out from before Moses.

**35:21** So they came, every person whose heart stirred him and everyone whose spirit made him willing; they brought the contribution for the LORD for the work of the tent of meeting, for all its service, and for the holy garments.

**35:22** The men came along with the women—all who were willing of heart brought brooches, earrings, signet rings, and pendants, every article of gold—as well as every man who lifted up a wave offering of gold to the LORD.

**35:23** And everyone with whom were found blue, purple, and crimson yarn, fine linen, goat hair, ram skins dyed red, and skins of fine leather brought them.

**35:24** Everyone offering a contribution of silver and bronze brought the contribution for the LORD, and everyone with whom acacia wood was found for any work of the service brought it.

**35:25** And every woman skilled of heart spun with her hands, and they brought spun thread: the blue, the purple, the crimson yarn, and the fine linen.

**35:26** And all the women whose heart stirred them with skill spun the goat hair.

**35:27** And the leaders brought the onyx stones and the stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece,

**35:28** and the spices and the oil for light, for the anointing oil, and for the fragrant incense.

**35:29** Every man and woman whose heart made them willing to bring anything for all the work that the LORD had commanded by the hand of Moses to be done—the sons of Israel brought a freewill offering to the LORD.

**35:30** And Moses said to the sons of Israel, "Look, the LORD has called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.

**35:31** He has filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, with understanding, with knowledge, and with every kind of craftsmanship,

**35:32** and to devise designs, to work in gold, in silver, and in bronze,

**35:33** and in the cutting of stone for setting, and in the carving of wood, to execute every work of design.

**35:34** And He has put it in his heart to teach, both him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.

**35:35** He has filled them with skill of heart to do every work of a craftsman, of a designer, of an embroiderer in blue, in purple, in crimson yarn, and in fine linen, and of a weaver—makers of every kind of work and devisers of designs."

## Translator Notes

- **35:7** — The exact meaning of tehashim (also in 35:23) is uncertain; it is variously understood as finely tanned or beaded leather (comparing Egyptian and Akkadian cognates) or as the hides of a Red Sea marine mammal such as the dugong or dolphin.
- **35:19** — The rare term serad ("finely woven") occurs only in Exodus 31:10; 35:19; 39:1, 41; it likely refers to braided or plaited textile work, though ancient versions often understood it as garments for sanctuary service.
- **35:22** — The precise jewelry item denoted by kumaz ("pendants," also in Numbers 31:50) is uncertain, likely gold beads, necklaces, or armbands. "Lifted up a wave offering" renders the Hiphil perfect of nuf with its cognate noun tenufah, denoting a ritual presentation or dedication offering.
- **35:17 *(additional)*** — The noun hatser ("courtyard") takes both a masculine suffix ("its pillars") and a feminine suffix ("its bases") within the same verse.
