## Text

**19:1** When the LORD your God cuts off the nations whose land the LORD your God is giving to you, and you dispossess them and live in their cities and in their houses,

**19:2** you are to set apart three cities for yourself within your land, which the LORD your God is giving to you to possess.

**19:3** You are to prepare the road for yourself and divide into three parts the territory of your land that the LORD your God is granting you as an inheritance, so that every killer may flee there.

**19:4** And this is the case of the killer who may flee there and live: one who strikes his neighbor unintentionally, without having hated him beforehand.

**19:5** When someone goes with his neighbor into the forest to chop wood, and his hand swings with the axe to cut down the tree, and the iron slips off the wood and hits his neighbor so that he dies, that person may flee to one of these cities and live.

**19:6** Otherwise, the avenger of blood might chase after the killer while his heart is hot, and overtake him because the road is long, and strike him fatally, even though no sentence of death applied to him, since he had not hated him beforehand.

**19:7** Therefore I am commanding you, saying, "You are to set apart three cities for yourself."

**19:8** And if the LORD your God expands your territory, just as He swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land that He spoke of giving to your fathers,

**19:9** provided you keep this entire commandment to carry it out, which I am commanding you today, to love the LORD your God and to walk in His ways at all times, then you are to add three more cities for yourself in addition to these three,

**19:10** so that innocent blood will not be shed in the midst of your land, which the LORD your God is giving to you as an inheritance, and bloodguilt will not rest upon you.

**19:11** But if a man hates his neighbor and lies in wait for him, and rises up against him and strikes him fatally so that he dies, and he flees to one of these cities,

**19:12** then the elders of his city are to send and take him from there, and deliver him into the hand of the avenger of blood so that he dies.

**19:13** Your eye must not show pity on him, and you must purge the blood of the innocent from Israel, so that it may go well for you.

**19:14** You must not move back your neighbor's boundary, which earlier generations marked out, in your inheritance that you will inherit in the land which the LORD your God is giving to you to possess.

**19:15** A single witness must not stand against a man for any iniquity or for any sin, regarding any offense he may commit; on the word of two witnesses or on the word of three witnesses a matter shall stand.

**19:16** If a malicious witness rises against a man to testify against him of a transgression,

**19:17** then the two men who have the dispute are to stand before the LORD, before the priests and the judges who will be in those days.

**19:18** And the judges must investigate thoroughly; and if indeed the witness is a false witness, having testified falsely against his brother,

**19:19** then you are to do to him just as he schemed to do to his brother, and you must purge the evil from your midst.

**19:20** And the rest will hear and be afraid, and will not again do anything like this evil thing in your midst.

**19:21** And your eye must not show pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — Literally "without knowledge" and "from yesterday and the third day," standard Hebrew idioms for acting accidentally and having no prior hostility.
- **19:10** — Hebrew damim (plural of dam, "blood") denotes culpability for shed blood, appearing here with a singular verb.
- **19:16** — "Malicious witness" is literally "a witness of violence," referring to one who gives false testimony to inflict unjust harm.
