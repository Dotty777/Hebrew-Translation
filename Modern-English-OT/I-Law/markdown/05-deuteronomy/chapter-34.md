## Text

**34:1** And Moses ascended from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the summit of Pisgah that faces Jericho. And the LORD showed him the whole land: Gilead as far as Dan,

**34:2** and all of Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and the whole land of Judah as far as the western sea,

**34:3** and the Negev, and the Plain—the valley of Jericho, the city of palms—as far as Zoar.

**34:4** And the LORD said to him, "This is the land about which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, 'To your offspring I will give it.' I have caused you to see it with your own eyes, yet you shall not cross over there."

**34:5** And there Moses, the servant of the LORD, died in the land of Moab, at the LORD's command.

**34:6** And He buried him in the valley in the land of Moab, across from Beth-peor; yet no one knows his burial site to this day.

**34:7** And Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died; his eye had not grown dim, and his vitality had not fled.

**34:8** And the sons of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days; then the days of weeping in mourning for Moses were completed.

**34:9** And Joshua, the son of Nun, was full of a spirit of wisdom, because Moses had laid his hands upon him; so the sons of Israel heeded him and did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**34:10** And no prophet like Moses has arisen again in Israel, whom the LORD knew face to face,

**34:11** in all the signs and the wonders that the LORD sent him to perform in the land of Egypt, against Pharaoh, and against all his servants, and against his whole land,

**34:12** and in all the mighty hand and in all the great awe-inspiring work that Moses performed before the eyes of all Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **34:5** — "At the LORD's command" translates the Hebrew idiom עַל־פִּי יְהֹוָה, literally "by the mouth of the LORD."
- **34:6** — The verb וַיִּקְבֹּר is a third-person masculine singular active Qal wayyiqtol ("and He buried him"), with the LORD as the understood subject, though it is sometimes interpreted impersonally ("he was buried").
- **34:7** — The noun לֵחֹה ("his vitality," from לֵחַ, literally "moisture" or "freshness") is a hapax legomenon with the archaic third-person masculine singular suffix -ֹה, and the verb נָס literally means "fled."
