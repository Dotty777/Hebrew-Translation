## Text

**2:1** Then we turned and traveled toward the wilderness along the road to the Red Sea, just as the LORD had spoken to me, and we went around Mount Seir for many days.

**2:2** Then the LORD said to me:

**2:3** "You have gone around this mountain long enough; turn yourselves northward.

**2:4** And command the people, saying, 'You are about to pass through the territory of your brothers, the sons of Esau, who live in Seir. They will fear you, so guard yourselves very closely.

**2:5** Do not engage them in conflict, for I will not give you any of their land—not even a stepping place for the sole of a foot—because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

**2:6** You shall buy food from them with silver and eat, and you shall also purchase water from them with silver and drink.

**2:7** For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hand; He has known your walking through this great wilderness. Throughout these forty years the LORD your God has been with you, and you have not lacked anything.'"

**2:8** So we passed on from our brothers, the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, away from the Arabah road, from Elath and from Ezion-geber; then we turned and passed along the road to the wilderness of Moab.

**2:9** Then the LORD said to me, "Do not show hostility toward Moab, and do not engage them in battle, for I will not give you any of its land as a possession, because I have given Ar to the sons of Lot as a possession."

**2:10** The Emim lived in it formerly, a people great and numerous and tall like the Anakim.

**2:11** They too are reckoned as Rephaim, like the Anakim, though the Moabites call them Emim.

**2:12** And in Seir the Horites lived formerly, but the sons of Esau dispossessed them, and destroyed them from before themselves, and lived in their place, just as Israel did to the land of its possession that the LORD gave to them.

**2:13** "Now rise up and cross the Wadi Zered for yourselves." So we crossed the Wadi Zered.

**2:14** And the days that we traveled from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Wadi Zered totaled thirty-eight years, until the entire generation—the men of war—had perished from the midst of the camp, just as the LORD had sworn to them.

**2:15** Moreover, the hand of the LORD was against them to rout them from the midst of the camp until they perished.

**2:16** So it happened, when all the men of war had finished dying from the midst of the people,

**2:17** that the LORD spoke to me, saying:

**2:18** "This day you are crossing the border of Moab, at Ar.

**2:19** When you draw near opposite the sons of Ammon, do not show hostility toward them and do not engage them in conflict, for I will not give you any of the land of the sons of Ammon as a possession, because I have given it to the sons of Lot as a possession."

**2:20** It too is reckoned as a land of Rephaim; Rephaim lived in it formerly, though the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,

**2:21** a people great and numerous and tall like the Anakim. Yet the LORD destroyed them from before them, and they dispossessed them and lived in their place,

**2:22** just as He did for the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, when He destroyed the Horites from before them, and they dispossessed them and have lived in their place until this day.

**2:23** And as for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came out from Caphtor, destroyed them and lived in their place.

**2:24** "Rise up, set out, and cross the Wadi Arnon! Look, I have given into your hand Sihon king of Heshbon, the Amorite, and his land. Begin to take possession, and engage him in battle.

**2:25** This day I will begin to place the dread of you and the fear of you upon the peoples beneath all the heavens, who will hear the report of you and will quake and writhe before you."

**2:26** Then I dispatched messengers from the Wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon, king of Heshbon, with words of peace, saying:

**2:27** "Let me pass through your land. I will walk strictly along the road; I will not turn aside right or left.

**2:28** Sell me food for silver and I will eat, and give me water for silver and I will drink—just let me pass through on foot,

**2:29** just as the sons of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD our God is giving to us."

**2:30** Yet Sihon, king of Heshbon, was not willing to let us pass by him, for the LORD your God made his spirit stiff and made his heart stubborn, so as to give him into your hand, as it is this day.

**2:31** Then the LORD said to me, "Look, I have begun to give Sihon and his land over before you. Begin—take possession, to possess his land."

**2:32** Then Sihon came out to meet us—he and all his people—for battle at Jahaz.

**2:33** And the LORD our God gave him over before us, and we struck down him and his sons and all his people.

**2:34** So we captured all his cities at that time and devoted to complete destruction every city—men, women, and little ones; we left no survivor.

**2:35** Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, together with the spoil of the cities that we captured.

**2:36** From Aroer, which is on the rim of the Wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the wadi, all the way to Gilead, there was not a town that stood too high for us; the LORD our God gave all of it over before us.

**2:37** Only to the land of the sons of Ammon you did not draw near—all along the side of the Wadi Jabbok, and the cities of the hill country, and all that the LORD our God had commanded.

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The Hebrew uses two distinct Qal verbs here: shavar ("buy grain/food," cf. its Hiphil "sell food" in 2:28) and the rare verb karah ("purchase/trade for," distinct from the homonymous karah "dig").
- **2:13** — Hebrew nachal ("wadi") denotes a seasonal stream-bed and its valley or gorge, distinguishing it from gay ("valley") in 3:29.
- **2:15** — "Rout them" translates le-hummam, the Qal infinitive construct of hamam ("throw into panic, rout, crush"), portraying divine judgment driving the rebellious generation out of the camp until they perished.
- **2:33** — The Masoretic text spells "his sons" defectively as banaw (without the yod of the plural suffix -ayw), though its vocalization reads the plural "his sons" rather than singular "his son."
- **2:34** — "Devoted to complete destruction" translates the Hiphil of charam (the cherem ban), denoting the irreversible setting apart of a conquered population or city to the LORD for total destruction.
