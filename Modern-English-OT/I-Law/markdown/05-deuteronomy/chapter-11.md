## Text

**11:1** So you shall love the LORD your God, and you shall keep His charge, His statutes, His judgments, and His commandments at all times.

**11:2** And know today—for I am not addressing your children, who have not known and who have not seen—the discipline of the LORD your God, His greatness, His strong hand, and His outstretched arm,

**11:3** and His signs and His deeds that He did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and to all his land,

**11:4** and what He did to the army of Egypt, to its horses and to its chariots, how He made the waters of the Sea of Reeds flow over them as they pursued after you, and the LORD destroyed them to this day,

**11:5** and what He did for you in the wilderness until you arrived at this place,

**11:6** and what He did to Dathan and to Abiram, the sons of Eliab, son of Reuben, how the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, along with their households, their tents, and every living thing that was at their feet in the midst of all Israel;

**11:7** for it is your own eyes that saw all the great work of the LORD that He did.

**11:8** So you shall keep the entire commandment that I am commanding you today, in order that you may be strong, and enter and possess the land that you are crossing over to possess,

**11:9** and in order that you may lengthen your days on the land that the LORD swore to your fathers to give to them and to their seed, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**11:10** For the land that you are entering to possess is not like the land of Egypt from which you came out, where you would sow your seed and water it with your foot like a vegetable garden.

**11:11** Rather, the land that you are crossing over to possess is a land of mountains and valleys; it drinks water from the rain of the heavens,

**11:12** a land that the LORD your God looks after; continually the eyes of the LORD your God are upon it, from the beginning of the year to the end of the year.

**11:13** And it will come about, if you carefully listen to My commandments that I am commanding you today, to love the LORD your God and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul,

**11:14** that I will give the rain of your land in its season, the early rain and the late rain, and you will gather your grain, your new wine, and your fresh oil.

**11:15** And I will give grass in your field for your livestock, and you will eat and be satisfied.

**11:16** Guard yourselves, or else your heart will be enticed, and you will turn aside and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**11:17** and the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and He will shut up the heavens so that there is no rain, and the ground will not give its yield, and you will perish quickly off the good land that the LORD is giving you.

**11:18** So you shall set these words of mine upon your heart and upon your soul, and you shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

**11:19** And you shall teach them to your children, speaking about them when you sit in your house, when you walk on the road, when you lie down, and when you rise up.

**11:20** And you shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,

**11:21** in order that your days and the days of your children may multiply on the land that the LORD swore to your fathers to give them, like the days of the heavens above the earth.

**11:22** For if you carefully keep this entire commandment that I am commanding you to do, to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, and to cling to Him,

**11:23** then the LORD will dispossess all these nations from before you, and you will dispossess nations larger and stronger than you.

**11:24** Every place where the sole of your foot treads will belong to you; from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the River Euphrates, as far as the western sea will be your territory.

**11:25** No one will stand firm before you; the LORD your God will put the dread of you and the fear of you over the face of all the land where you tread, just as He spoke to you.

**11:26** See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:

**11:27** the blessing, if you listen to the commandments of the LORD your God that I am commanding you today,

**11:28** and the curse, if you do not listen to the commandments of the LORD your God, but turn aside from the way that I am commanding you today to go after other gods that you have not known.

**11:29** And it will come about, when the LORD your God brings you into the land that you are entering to possess, that you shall set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

**11:30** Are they not across the Jordan, beyond the road toward the setting sun, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh?

**11:31** For you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you, and you will possess it and live in it.

**11:32** And you shall be careful to do all the statutes and the judgments that I am setting before you today.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — The parenthetical Hebrew clause "for not with your children" (ki lo et-beneikhem) is elliptical, lacking an explicit verb; "I am not addressing" is supplied for clarity, while the accusative series in verses 2-6 is governed by "know" and resumed in verse 7.
- **11:6** — "At their feet" (be-ragleihem) is a Hebrew idiom meaning "in their train" or "following them," referring to the people and animals belonging to the company of Dathan and Abiram.
- **11:13** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts into first-person divine speech in verses 13-15 ("My commandments ... I will give"), with Moses speaking directly in the voice of the LORD before returning to third-person reference to the LORD in verse 17.
- **11:27** — The relative particle asher here carries conditional force ("if"), standing directly parallel to im ("if") in verse 28.
