## Text

**28:1** And it will come about that if you truly listen to the voice of the LORD your God, taking care to carry out all His commands that I am commanding you today, the LORD your God will place you highest above all the nations of the earth.

**28:2** And all these blessings will come on you and catch up with you, if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God.

**28:3** You will be blessed in the city, and you will be blessed in the field.

**28:4** The fruit of your womb will be blessed, and the fruit of your soil, and the fruit of your livestock—the offspring of your cattle and the increase of your flock.

**28:5** Your basket and your kneading bowl will be blessed.

**28:6** You will be blessed in your coming in, and you will be blessed in your going out.

**28:7** The LORD will cause your enemies who rise up against you to be struck down before you; by one way they will go out against you, and by seven ways they will flee before you.

**28:8** The LORD will command the blessing to be with you in your granaries and in every undertaking of your hand, and He will bless you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

**28:9** The LORD will establish you for Himself as a holy people, just as He swore to you, if you keep the commands of the LORD your God and walk in His ways.

**28:10** And all the peoples of the earth will see that the name of the LORD is called over you, and they will fear you.

**28:11** And the LORD will cause you to overflow with prosperity, in the fruit of your womb, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your soil, upon the land that the LORD swore to your fathers to give you.

**28:12** The LORD will open for you His good treasury, the heavens, to give the rain of your land at its time and to bless every work of your hand; and you will lend to many nations, while you yourself will not borrow.

**28:13** And the LORD will set you as the head and not the tail, and you will be only above and will not be below, if you listen to the commands of the LORD your God that I am commanding you today, to keep and to carry out,

**28:14** and you do not turn aside from any of the words that I am commanding you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

**28:15** But it will come about that if you do not listen to the voice of the LORD your God, taking care to carry out all His commands and His statutes that I am commanding you today, then all these curses will come on you and catch up with you.

**28:16** You will be cursed in the city, and you will be cursed in the field.

**28:17** Your basket and your kneading bowl will be cursed.

**28:18** The fruit of your womb will be cursed, and the fruit of your soil, the offspring of your cattle, and the increase of your flock.

**28:19** You will be cursed in your coming in, and you will be cursed in your going out.

**28:20** The LORD will send against you curse, panic, and rebuke in every undertaking of your hand that you do, until you are destroyed and until you perish swiftly, because of the wickedness of your deeds, in that you have forsaken Me.

**28:21** The LORD will cause pestilence to cling to you until He wipes you out from the land that you are entering to possess.

**28:22** The LORD will strike you with wasting disease, with fever, with inflammation, with scorching heat, with the sword, with blight, and with mildew, and they will pursue you until you perish.

**28:23** And your heavens that are above your head will be bronze, and the earth that is beneath you will be iron.

**28:24** The LORD will turn the rain of your land into fine dust and earth; from the heavens it will come down on you until you are destroyed.

**28:25** The LORD will cause you to be struck down before your enemies; by one way you will go out against them, and by seven ways you will flee before them; and you will become an object of horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**28:26** And your corpse will become food for all the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, with no one to scare them away.

**28:27** The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with hemorrhoids, with scabs, and with the itch, from which you will not be able to be healed.

**28:28** The LORD will strike you with madness, with blindness, and with confusion of heart.

**28:29** And you will be groping at midday, just as a blind man gropes in thick darkness, and you will not make your ways succeed; and you will be only oppressed and robbed all the time, with no rescuer.

**28:30** You will betroth a woman, yet another man will lie with her; you will build a house, yet you will not live in it; you will plant a vineyard, yet you will not enjoy its produce.

**28:31** Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, yet you will not eat from it; your donkey will be seized from before you and will not return to you; your flock will be given to your enemies, and you will have no rescuer.

**28:32** Your sons and your daughters will be given to another people, while your eyes watch and pine away for them all day long, and there will be no power in your hand.

**28:33** A people whom you have not known will eat the fruit of your soil and all your toil, and you will be only oppressed and crushed all the time.

**28:34** And you will go mad from the sight of your eyes that you will see.

**28:35** The LORD will strike you with malignant boils on the knees and on the legs, from which you will not be able to be healed, from the sole of your foot even to the top of your head.

**28:36** The LORD will lead you and your king whom you appoint over yourself to a nation that you and your fathers have not known, and there you will serve other gods—wood and stone.

**28:37** And you will become an object of shock, a proverb, and a taunt among all the peoples where the LORD drives you.

**28:38** You will take out much seed to the field, yet you will gather little, because the locust will devour it.

**28:39** You will plant vineyards and tend them, yet you will not drink wine nor gather the harvest, because the worm will eat it.

**28:40** You will have olive trees in all your territory, yet you will not rub yourself with oil, because your olives will fall off.

**28:41** You will father sons and daughters, yet they will not belong to you, because they will go into captivity.

**28:42** The cricket will take possession of all your trees and the fruit of your soil.

**28:43** The resident foreigner who is in your midst will ascend above you higher and higher, while you will descend lower and lower.

**28:44** He will lend to you, yet you will not lend to him; he will become the head, and you will become the tail.

**28:45** And all these curses will come on you and pursue you and catch up with you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commands and His statutes that He commanded you.

**28:46** And they will be upon you as a sign and a wonder, and upon your offspring forever.

**28:47** Because you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and gladness of heart for the abundance of everything,

**28:48** you will serve your enemies whom the LORD will send against you, in hunger, in thirst, in nakedness, and in the lack of everything; and He will place an iron yoke upon your neck until He destroys you.

**28:49** The LORD will raise up a nation against you from far away, from the end of the earth, just as the eagle swoops, a nation whose language you will not understand,

**28:50** a fierce-faced nation that will show no regard for the elderly and will show no pity to the young.

**28:51** And it will eat the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your soil until you are destroyed; it will not leave you grain, new wine, or fresh oil, nor the offspring of your cattle or the increase of your flock, until it causes you to perish.

**28:52** And it will besiege you in all your gates until your high and fortified walls in which you trust come down across all your land; and it will besiege you in all your gates across all your land that the LORD your God has given you.

**28:53** Then you will eat the fruit of your womb, the flesh of your sons and your daughters whom the LORD your God has given you, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will press hard on you.

**28:54** The man among you who is tender and very delicate—his eye will turn begrudging toward his brother, toward the wife of his bosom, and toward the rest of his children whom he leaves remaining,

**28:55** so that he will not give to any of them from the flesh of his children that he is eating, because nothing has been left to him, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will press hard on you in all your gates.

**28:56** The woman among you who is tender and delicate, who would not attempt to place the sole of her foot on the ground out of delicacy and tenderness—her eye will turn begrudging toward the husband of her bosom, toward her son, and toward her daughter,

**28:57** and toward her afterbirth that comes out from between her feet and toward her children whom she bears, because she will eat them in secret in the lack of everything, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will press hard on you in your gates.

**28:58** If you do not take care to carry out all the words of this law written in this book, to fear this glorious and fearsome name, the LORD your God,

**28:59** then the LORD will make your afflictions and the afflictions of your offspring extraordinary—great and enduring afflictions, and severe and enduring sicknesses.

**28:60** And He will bring back upon you all the diseases of Egypt that you dreaded, and they will cling to you.

**28:61** Even every sickness and every affliction that is not written in the book of this law, the LORD will bring them on you until you are destroyed.

**28:62** And you will be left few in number, whereas you had been like the stars of the heavens in multitude, because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD your God.

**28:63** And it will come about that just as the LORD rejoiced over you to do good to you and to multiply you, so the LORD will rejoice over you to cause you to perish and to destroy you; and you will be uprooted from the land that you are entering to possess.

**28:64** And the LORD will scatter you among all the peoples, from one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth, and there you will serve other gods that you and your fathers have not known—wood and stone.

**28:65** And among those nations you will find no repose, and there will be no place of rest for the sole of your foot; and there the LORD will give you a quaking heart, pining of eyes, and despair of soul.

**28:66** And your life will hang in suspense before you; you will feel dread night and day, and you will have no certainty of your life.

**28:67** In the morning you will say, "I wish it were evening!" and in the evening you will say, "I wish it were morning!" because of the dread of your heart that you will feel and the sight of your eyes that you will see.

**28:68** And the LORD will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I told you, "You will not see it again." And there you will put yourselves up for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, with no buyer.

**28:69** These are the words of the covenant that the LORD commanded Moses to make with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant that He made with them at Horeb.

## Translator Notes

- **28:22** — The Masoretic vocalization reads cherev ('sword'); an alternate vocalization of the same consonants, chorev, means 'drought,' which aligns with the surrounding climatic and crop afflictions.
- **28:25** — After plural 'your enemies,' the Hebrew shifts to collective masculine singular suffixes (literally 'against him ... before him'), rendered 'them' for English agreement.
- **28:27** — The ketiv reads ba'ophalim ('with swellings' or 'with tumors'); the Masoretic qere substitutes the euphemistic battechorim ('with hemorrhoids'), followed in the translation.
- **28:30** — The ketiv reads yishgalennah ('will violate/ravish her'); the Masoretic qere substitutes the euphemistic yishkavennah ('will lie with her'), followed here. In the final clause, the Piel verb chalal literally means 'make common' or 'deconsecrate,' referring to harvesting a new vineyard's fruit for ordinary use (cf. Lev 19:23-25; Deut 20:6).
- **28:32** — The clause 'there will be no power in your hand' renders the Hebrew idiom 'ein le'el yadekha (literally 'there is not in the strength of your hand'), expressing complete helplessness.
- **28:42** — 'The cricket' translates the rare noun tselatsal, derived from a root meaning 'to buzz' or 'whirr,' referring to a buzzing locust or cricket; yeyaresh is the sole Piel occurrence of yarash ('possess, take over') in the Hebrew Bible.
- **28:48** — After plural 'your enemies,' Hebrew uses a collective masculine singular suffix on yeshallechennu (literally 'whom the LORD will send him'). The subject of 'He will place ... until He destroys' is the LORD.
- **28:54** — 'His eye will turn begrudging' renders the Hebrew idiom tera' 'eino (literally 'his eye will be evil toward,' repeated in v. 56), denoting extreme stinginess and hostility.
- **28:66** — 'Will hang in suspense' translates telu'im (Qal passive participle of tala', a by-form of talah, 'hang'), portraying life as hanging precariously in constant uncertainty.
- **28:68** — 'You will put yourselves up for sale' translates the Hithpael hitmakkartem (conative reflexive of makar, 'sell'), vividly depicting desperate self-enslavement that fails because no one will buy.
