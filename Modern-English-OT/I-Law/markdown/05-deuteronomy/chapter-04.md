## Text

**4:1** And now, Israel, listen to the statutes and to the judgments that I am teaching you to carry out, so that you may live and enter and possess the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving to you.

**4:2** You must not add to the word that I am commanding you, and you must not subtract from it, in order to keep the commandments of the LORD your God that I am commanding you.

**4:3** Your very eyes saw what the LORD did at Baal-peor, for every man who walked after Baal-peor, the LORD your God wiped him out from your midst.

**4:4** Yet you who cling to the LORD your God, all of you are alive today.

**4:5** Look, I have taught you statutes and judgments, just as the LORD my God commanded me, so that you do so in the midst of the land that you are entering to possess.

**4:6** Keep them and carry them out, for this is your wisdom and your discernment in the eyes of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, "Truly, a wise and discerning people is this great nation."

**4:7** For what great nation is there that has a god so close to it as the LORD our God is whenever we call to Him?

**4:8** And what great nation is there that has statutes and judgments as righteous as all this law that I am placing before you today?

**4:9** Only watch yourself and guard your soul very carefully, so that you do not forget the things your eyes saw and so that they do not depart from your heart all the days of your life; and make them known to your children and to your children's children—

**4:10** the day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb, when the LORD said to me, "Assemble the people to Me, and I will let them hear My words, so that they may learn to fear Me all the days that they live on the earth, and that they may teach their children."

**4:11** Then you drew near and stood at the foot of the mountain, while the mountain was burning with fire up to the heart of the heavens—darkness, cloud, and thick gloom.

**4:12** And the LORD spoke to you from within the fire. You were hearing the sound of words, yet you were seeing no form—only a voice.

**4:13** He declared to you His covenant that He commanded you to carry out—the ten words—and He wrote them on two stone tablets.

**4:14** And at that time the LORD commanded me to teach you statutes and judgments, for you to carry them out in the land that you are crossing over to possess.

**4:15** So watch very carefully over your souls, since you did not see any form on the day the LORD spoke to you at Horeb from within the fire,

**4:16** so that you do not act corruptly and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of any statue, the figure of male or female,

**4:17** the figure of any animal that is on the earth, the figure of any winged bird that flies in the sky,

**4:18** the figure of anything creeping on the ground, the figure of any fish that is in the waters below the earth;

**4:19** and so that you do not lift your eyes toward the heavens and see the sun and the moon and the stars, all the host of the heavens, and be led astray and bow down to them and serve them—those that the LORD your God has apportioned to all the peoples beneath all the heavens.

**4:20** But the LORD took you and brought you out from the iron furnace, from Egypt, to become a people of inheritance for Him, as on this day.

**4:21** Now the LORD grew angry with me on account of you, and He swore that I would not cross over the Jordan and would not go into the good land that the LORD your God is giving to you as an inheritance.

**4:22** For I am going to die in this land; I am not crossing over the Jordan. Yet you are crossing over and will possess this good land.

**4:23** Be on guard for yourselves, so that you do not forget the covenant of the LORD your God that He made with you and make for yourselves a carved image—a form of anything the LORD your God has forbidden you.

**4:24** For the LORD your God—He is a devouring fire, a jealous God.

**4:25** When you have children and children's children and grow old in the land, and act corruptly and make a carved image—a form of anything—and do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD your God to provoke Him to anger,

**4:26** I summon the heavens and the earth to witness against you today that you will surely perish swiftly from the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess. You will not prolong your days on it, for you will certainly be wiped out.

**4:27** And the LORD will disperse you among the peoples, and you will remain few in number among the nations where the LORD will drive you.

**4:28** And there you will serve gods, the work of human hands—wood and stone—that do not see and do not hear and do not eat and do not smell.

**4:29** Yet from there you will seek the LORD your God and find Him, when you search for Him with your whole heart and with your whole soul.

**4:30** In your distress, when all these things find you in the latter days, you will return to the LORD your God and listen to His voice.

**4:31** For the LORD your God is a compassionate God; He will not abandon you or destroy you or forget the covenant of your fathers that He swore to them.

**4:32** For inquire now about earlier days that were before you, since the day God created mankind on the earth, and from one end of the heavens to the other: has anything like this great thing ever occurred, or has anything like it been heard?

**4:33** Has any people heard the voice of God speaking from within the fire, just as you yourself heard, and lived?

**4:34** Or has a god ever attempted to come and take a nation for himself from the midst of another nation, with testings, with signs and wonders, with war, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with great terrors, according to all that the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

**4:35** You yourself were made to see, so that you would know that the LORD—He is God; there is no other apart from Him.

**4:36** From the heavens He caused you to hear His voice to discipline you, and on the earth He made you see His great fire, and you heard His words from within the fire.

**4:37** And because He loved your fathers and chose their seed after them, He brought you out by His presence, with His great strength, from Egypt,

**4:38** to dispossess nations greater and mightier than you from before you, to bring you in, to give to you their land as an inheritance, as on this day.

**4:39** So know today, and bring it back to your heart, that the LORD—He is God in the heavens above and on the earth below; there is no other.

**4:40** And keep His statutes and His commandments that I am commanding you today, so that it may be well with you and with your children after you, and so that you may prolong your days on the land that the LORD your God is giving to you for all time.

**4:41** Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan, toward the sunrise,

**4:42** for a killer to flee there who kills his neighbor unintentionally and had not hated him previously, so that he might flee to one of these cities and live:

**4:43** Bezer in the wilderness, in the land of the plateau, for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.

**4:44** And this is the law that Moses placed before the sons of Israel.

**4:45** These are the testimonies, the statutes, and the judgments that Moses spoke to the sons of Israel when they came out of Egypt,

**4:46** beyond the Jordan, in the valley facing Beth-peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who resided in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel struck down when they came out of Egypt.

**4:47** And they took possession of his land and the land of Og king of Bashan—the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, toward the sunrise—

**4:48** from Aroer, which is on the rim of the Wadi Arnon, as far as Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,

**4:49** and all the Arabah beyond the Jordan eastward, as far as the Sea of the Arabah, beneath the slopes of Pisgah.

## Translator Notes

- **4:7** — The Hebrew phrase for "a god so close" pairs a grammatically plural adjective with the morphologically plural noun for deity, and can be rendered either "a god so close" or "gods so close."
- **4:13** — Literally "the ten words," the Hebrew phrase traditionally referred to as the Ten Commandments.
- **4:23** — The Piel verb rendered "has forbidden you" literally means "commanded you," used elliptically in a prohibition for "commanded you not to make."
- **4:37** — The Masoretic Hebrew literally has singular suffixes, "his seed after him," functioning as a collective or distributive singular after plural "your fathers."
- **4:48** — Mount Sion reflects the Masoretic spelling Si'on, which is distinct from Jerusalem's Zion and is likely a variant of Sirion in Deuteronomy 3:9, another name for Mount Hermon.
