## Text

**30:1** "And it will be, when all these things come upon you, the blessing and the curse that I have placed before you, and you call them back to your heart among all the nations to which the LORD your God has banished you,

**30:2** and you return to the LORD your God and listen to His voice according to everything that I am commanding you today, you and your children, with all your heart and with all your soul,

**30:3** then the LORD your God will restore your fortunes and show compassion to you, and He will again gather you from all the peoples to which the LORD your God has scattered you.

**30:4** If your banished ones are at the end of the heavens, from there the LORD your God will gather you, and from there He will take you.

**30:5** And the LORD your God will bring you to the land that your fathers possessed, and you will possess it; and He will make you prosper and make you more numerous than your fathers.

**30:6** Then the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your offspring, to love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, for the sake of your life.

**30:7** And the LORD your God will place all these sworn curses upon your enemies and upon those who hate you, who pursued you.

**30:8** And you yourself will again listen to the voice of the LORD, and do all His commandments that I am commanding you today.

**30:9** Then the LORD your God will make you abound in every work of your hand, in the fruit of your body, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground, for good; for the LORD will again delight over you for good, just as He delighted over your fathers,

**30:10** if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commandments and His statutes that are written in this book of the law, if you return to the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**30:11** For this commandment that I am commanding you today is not beyond you, nor is it far away.

**30:12** It is not in the heavens, for anyone to say, 'Who will go up for us to the heavens and get it for us, and cause us to hear it, so that we may do it?'

**30:13** Nor is it beyond the sea, for anyone to say, 'Who will cross over for us to the other side of the sea and get it for us, and cause us to hear it, so that we may do it?'

**30:14** Rather, the word is very near to you, in your mouth and in your heart, for you to do it.

**30:15** Look, I have placed before you today life and good, and death and evil,

**30:16** in that I am commanding you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in His ways, and to keep His commandments and His statutes and His ordinances; then you will live and become numerous, and the LORD your God will bless you in the land that you are entering to possess.

**30:17** But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are drawn away and bow down to other gods and serve them,

**30:18** I announce to you today that you will certainly perish; you will not prolong your days on the land that you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess.

**30:19** I summon the heavens and the earth as witnesses against you today: life and death I have placed before you, the blessing and the curse. Choose life, therefore, so that you may live, you and your offspring,

**30:20** loving the LORD your God, listening to His voice, and clinging to Him; for He is your life and the length of your days, so that you may live on the land that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them."

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — "restore your fortunes" renders the Hebrew idiom shuv shevut (Qal of shuv with the cognate noun shevut), meaning to reverse a condition and restore former well-being, while playing acoustically on captivity (root shavah) in exilic contexts.
- **30:11** — "not beyond you" translates the Niphal participle of pala with the preposition min ("too extraordinary/difficult for you"), denoting what exceeds human grasp or capability.
- **30:16** — The Masoretic text begins directly with the conjunction asher ("in that") linking to v. 15, without the longer conditional clause ("If you listen to the commandments of the LORD your God") found in the Septuagint.
- **30:18** — "certainly perish" renders the emphatic Qal infinitive absolute paired with the Qal imperfect of avad (avod tovedun), underscoring the certainty of destruction.
