## Text

**1:1** These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel on the other side of the Jordan, in the wilderness, in the Arabah facing Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab.

**1:2** It is eleven days from Horeb, by the way of Mount Seir, as far as Kadesh-barnea.

**1:3** Now it happened in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first of the month, that Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that the LORD had commanded him regarding them,

**1:4** after he had struck down Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.

**1:5** On the other side of the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses resolved to explain this law, saying:

**1:6** The LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb, saying, "You have stayed at this mountain long enough.

**1:7** Turn and set out, and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all their neighbors in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the lowland, in the Negev, and by the seacoast, the land of the Canaanites and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the Euphrates River.

**1:8** Look, I have put the land before you. Go in and possess the land that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them and to their seed after them."

**1:9** Then I spoke to you at that time, saying, "I am unable to bear you alone.

**1:10** The LORD your God has multiplied you, and here you are today, like the stars of the heavens in multitude.

**1:11** May the LORD, the God of your fathers, add to you a thousand times as many as you are and bless you, just as He has spoken to you!

**1:12** How can I bear by myself your trouble, your burden, and your disputes?

**1:13** Bring forward for yourselves wise, discerning, and well-known men for your tribes, and I will set them as your heads."

**1:14** Then you answered me and said, "The thing you have spoken is good to do."

**1:15** So I took the heads of your tribes, men who were wise and well-known, and made them heads over you: leaders of thousands, leaders of hundreds, leaders of fifties, leaders of tens, and officers for your tribes.

**1:16** And I commanded your judges at that time, saying, "Hear disputes between your brothers and judge with righteousness between a man and his brother or the resident alien with him.

**1:17** You shall not show favoritism in judgment; you shall hear the small and the great equally. You shall not dread any man, for judgment belongs to God. And the matter that is too difficult for you, you shall bring before me, and I will hear it."

**1:18** And at that time I commanded you all the things that you were to do.

**1:19** Then we set out from Horeb and walked through all that great and fearsome wilderness you saw, by the way of the hill country of the Amorites, just as the LORD our God commanded us, and we came as far as Kadesh-barnea.

**1:20** Then I said to you, "You have come as far as the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God is giving to us.

**1:21** Look, the LORD your God has put the land before you. Go up, possess it, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you. Do not be afraid, and do not be dismayed."

**1:22** Then all of you drew near to me and said, "Let us send men ahead of us so that they may explore the land for us and bring word back to us about the way by which we should go up and the cities to which we will come."

**1:23** The matter seemed good in my eyes, so I took twelve men from you, one man for each tribe.

**1:24** They turned and went up into the hill country, came as far as the Valley of Eshcol, and spied it out.

**1:25** They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us, and they brought word back to us and said, "The land that the LORD our God is giving to us is good."

**1:26** Yet you were unwilling to go up, and you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God.

**1:27** You grumbled in your tents and said, "Because the LORD hates us, He brought us out from the land of Egypt to give us into the hand of the Amorites to destroy us.

**1:28** Where are we going up? Our brothers have caused our hearts to melt, saying, 'The people are greater and taller than we are; the cities are great and fortified up to the heavens; and moreover we saw sons of the Anakim there.'"

**1:29** And I said to you, "Do not be terrified and do not be afraid of them.

**1:30** The LORD your God, who goes before you, He Himself will fight for you, according to all that He did for you in Egypt before your eyes,

**1:31** and in the wilderness, where you saw how the LORD your God carried you, just as a man carries his son, along the entire way you walked until you reached this place."

**1:32** Yet despite this word, you did not trust in the LORD your God,

**1:33** who went before you along the way to seek out a place for you to camp, in fire by night to show you the way in which you should walk, and in the cloud by day.

**1:34** And the LORD heard the sound of your words; He grew angry and swore, saying,

**1:35** "Not a man among these men, this evil generation, shall see the good land that I swore to give to your fathers,

**1:36** except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and to him I will give the land on which he trod, and to his sons, because he followed the LORD completely."

**1:37** Even with me the LORD grew angry because of you, saying, "You too shall not enter there.

**1:38** Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall enter there. Strengthen him, for he will give it to Israel as an inheritance.

**1:39** And your little ones, who you said would become plunder, and your children, who today do not know good and evil, they shall enter there, and to them I will give it, and they shall possess it.

**1:40** But you, turn back and set out into the wilderness along the way of the Sea of Reeds."

**1:41** Then you answered and said to me, "We have sinned against the LORD. We ourselves will go up and fight, according to all that the LORD our God commanded us." Then each of you strapped on his weapons of war, and you considered it a light thing to go up into the hill country.

**1:42** And the LORD said to me, "Say to them, 'Do not go up and do not fight, for I am not in your midst, so that you will not be struck down before your enemies.'"

**1:43** So I spoke to you, yet you did not listen; you rebelled against the command of the LORD, and you acted arrogantly and went up into the hill country.

**1:44** Then the Amorites who lived in that hill country came out against you and pursued you just as bees do, and they crushed you in Seir as far as Hormah.

**1:45** Then you came back and wept before the LORD, yet the LORD did not listen to your voice, nor did He pay attention to you.

**1:46** So you stayed at Kadesh many days, for as many days as you stayed.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "in Ashtaroth, at Edrei" without a conjunction before "at Edrei," indicating where Og resided and where he was defeated, whereas the Septuagint inserts "and."
- **1:17** — "Show favoritism" renders the Hebrew idiom "recognize faces" (Hiphil of nakar with panim), denoting partiality in legal judgment.
- **1:35** — Following the oath verb in 1:34, the Hebrew particle im ("if") introduces a standard negative oath formula with an omitted self-imprecation, giving the emphatic sense "Not a man... shall see."
- **1:41** — "Considered it a light thing" translates a unique Hiphil form of the root hun (occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible), meaning to regard a task as easy or treat it lightly.
- **1:46** — "For as many days as you stayed" preserves the Hebrew idem per idem idiom (literally "like the days that you stayed"), used to summarize an extended period already known to the listeners.
