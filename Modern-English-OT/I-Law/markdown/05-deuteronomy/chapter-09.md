## Text

**9:1** Listen, Israel: today you are crossing the Jordan to go in and take possession of nations greater and mightier than you, large cities fortified up to the heavens,

**9:2** a great and tall people, the sons of the Anakim, whom you know and about whom you have heard, "Who can stand firm before the sons of Anak?"

**9:3** So know today that the LORD your God is the One crossing over ahead of you as a devouring fire; He will wipe them out, and He will subdue them before you. Then you will dispossess them and cause them to perish quickly, just as the LORD spoke to you.

**9:4** Do not say in your heart, when the LORD your God thrusts them out from before you, "Because of my righteousness the LORD has brought me in to take possession of this land." Rather, it is because of the wickedness of these nations that the LORD is dispossessing them from before you.

**9:5** It is not because of your righteousness or the uprightness of your heart that you are going in to take possession of their land; instead, it is because of the wickedness of these nations that the LORD your God is dispossessing them from before you, and in order to uphold the word that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

**9:6** So know that it is not on account of your righteousness that the LORD your God is giving this good land to you to take possession of it, because you are a stiff-necked people.

**9:7** Remember, do not forget how you provoked the LORD your God to wrath in the wilderness; from the day you went out from the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have been rebelling against the LORD.

**9:8** At Horeb as well, you provoked the LORD to wrath, and the LORD grew angry enough with you to wipe you out.

**9:9** When I went up the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant that the LORD had made with you, I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights; I ate no bread and drank no water.

**9:10** Then the LORD gave to me the two stone tablets, written with the finger of God; on them were all the very words that the LORD spoke with you on the mountain from within the fire on the day of the assembly.

**9:11** At the end of forty days and forty nights, the LORD gave to me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant.

**9:12** And the LORD said to me, "Get up, go down quickly from here, because your people whom you brought out of Egypt have corrupted themselves. They have turned aside quickly from the way that I commanded them; they have made for themselves a cast image."

**9:13** Then the LORD said to me, "I have seen this people, and look, they are a stiff-necked people.

**9:14** Leave Me alone, so that I may wipe them out and erase their name from beneath the heavens; and I will make you into a nation mightier and more numerous than they."

**9:15** Then I turned and went down from the mountain, as the mountain was blazing with fire, with the two tablets of the covenant in my two hands.

**9:16** I saw, and look, you had sinned against the LORD your God: you had made for yourselves a cast calf; you had turned aside quickly from the way that the LORD had commanded you.

**9:17** So I grasped the two tablets, threw them from my two hands, and shattered them before your eyes.

**9:18** Then I fell prostrate before the LORD as at the first, forty days and forty nights; I ate no bread and drank no water, on account of all your sin that you had sinned, in doing what was evil in the eyes of the LORD to provoke Him to anger.

**9:19** For I dreaded the anger and fury with which the LORD was wrathful against you, so as to wipe you out. Yet the LORD listened to me that time as well.

**9:20** And the LORD grew very angry with Aaron, so as to wipe him out; so I prayed on behalf of Aaron as well at that time.

**9:21** Then I took your sin that you had made, the calf, and burned it in the fire; I pounded it, grinding it thoroughly until it became fine dust, and I threw its dust into the stream coming down from the mountain.

**9:22** And at Taberah, and at Massah, and at Kibroth-hattaavah, you kept provoking the LORD to wrath.

**9:23** And when the LORD sent you from Kadesh-barnea, saying, "Go up and take possession of the land that I have given to you," you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God; you did not trust Him, and you did not listen to His voice.

**9:24** You have been rebelling against the LORD since the day I knew you.

**9:25** So I fell prostrate before the LORD for the forty days and the forty nights that I prostrated myself, because the LORD had said He would wipe you out.

**9:26** And I prayed to the LORD and said, "Lord GOD, do not destroy Your people and Your inheritance, whom You redeemed through Your greatness, whom You brought out of Egypt with a strong hand.

**9:27** Remember Your servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; do not pay attention to the stubbornness of this people, or to their wickedness, or to their sin.

**9:28** Otherwise, the land from which You brought us out will say, 'Because the LORD was unable to bring them into the land that He spoke of to them, and because He hated them, He brought them out to put them to death in the wilderness.'

**9:29** Yet they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out with Your great strength and with Your outstretched arm."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — In the Masoretic Hebrew, Israel's quoted inner speech ends at "this land" (1st person "my righteousness ... brought me"), after which the adversative waw introduces Moses's correction in the 2nd person ("from before you").
- **9:21** — "Pounded it, grinding it thoroughly" reflects the Qal wayyiqtol of katat followed by two adverbial infinitives absolute: the Qal infinitive absolute of tahan ("grinding") and the Hiphil infinitive absolute of yatav ("thoroughly").
- **9:23** — "Command" translates the Hebrew idiom "mouth" (pi), referring to the LORD's spoken directive.
- **9:28** — The singular noun "the land" (ha'arets) takes a plural verb ("will say", yo'meru) by constructio ad sensum, referring by metonymy to the inhabitants of Egypt.
