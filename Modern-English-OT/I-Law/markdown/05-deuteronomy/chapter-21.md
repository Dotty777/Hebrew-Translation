## Text

**21:1** If someone who has been killed is found in the land that the LORD your God is giving to you to possess, fallen in the field, and it is not known who struck him,

**21:2** then your elders and your judges shall go out and measure to the cities that are around the one who was killed.

**21:3** And for the city that is nearest to the one who was killed, the elders of that city shall take a heifer from the herd that has not been worked, that has not pulled in a yoke.

**21:4** And the elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a valley with a perennial stream, which is neither worked nor sown, and break the heifer's neck there in the valley.

**21:5** Then the priests, the sons of Levi, shall step forward, for the LORD your God has chosen them to minister to Him and to bless in the name of the LORD, and by their word every dispute and every assault is to be decided.

**21:6** And all the elders of that city who are nearest to the one who was killed shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley.

**21:7** And they shall answer and say, "Our hands have not shed this blood, and our eyes have not seen it.

**21:8** Atone for Your people Israel, whom You have redeemed, LORD, and do not place innocent blood in the midst of Your people Israel." Then the blood shall be atoned for on their behalf.

**21:9** And you will purge the innocent blood from your midst when you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD.

**21:10** When you go out to war against your enemies, and the LORD your God gives them into your hand, and you take captives from them,

**21:11** and you see among the captives a woman of beautiful form, and you desire her and would take her for yourself as a wife,

**21:12** then you shall bring her inside your house, and she shall shave her head and trim her nails.

**21:13** She shall also remove the garment of her captivity from herself, and remain in your house and weep for her father and her mother a full month. After that, you may go in to her and be her husband, and she shall become your wife.

**21:14** And if you take no delight in her, then you are to send her away as she chooses; you must certainly not sell her for money. You must not treat her as a slave, because you have humbled her.

**21:15** If a man has two wives, one loved and the other hated, and they bear him sons, both the loved one and the hated one, and the firstborn son belongs to the hated one,

**21:16** then on the day he bequeaths to his sons what belongs to him, he may not treat the son of the loved one as firstborn ahead of the son of the hated one, who is the firstborn.

**21:17** Rather, he must recognize the firstborn, the son of the hated one, by giving him a double portion of all that is found belonging to him, for he is the firstfruits of his vigor; the right of the birthright belongs to him.

**21:18** If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who does not listen to the voice of his father or to the voice of his mother, and though they discipline him he will not listen to them,

**21:19** then his father and his mother shall take hold of him and bring him out to the elders of his city and to the gate of his place.

**21:20** And they shall say to the elders of his city, "This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious; he does not listen to our voice; he is a glutton and a drunkard."

**21:21** Then all the men of his city shall stone him with stones so that he dies, and you must purge the evil from your midst; and all Israel will hear and be afraid.

**21:22** And if a man has committed a sin carrying a sentence of death and is put to death, and you hang him on a tree,

**21:23** his corpse must not remain overnight on the tree; rather, you must certainly bury him on that day, for one who is hanged is a curse of God, so that you do not defile your land that the LORD your God is giving to you as an inheritance.

## Translator Notes

- **21:7** — Translating the Masoretic qere shafekhu (3rd-person common plural, matching dual "our hands"); the ketiv shafekhah has a feminine singular or archaic feminine plural ending.
- **21:8** — "Shall be atoned for" renders a rare Nithpael (hybrid Niphal-Hithpael passive-reflexive) form of kafar ("atone").
- **21:12** — "Trim her nails" is literally "do" or "tend her nails"; the idiom usually means paring the nails (cf. 2 Sam 19:24), though some ancient interpreters understood it as letting them grow as a sign of mourning.
- **21:14** — The rare Hithpael verb hit-ammer (occurring only here and Deut 24:7) denotes treating someone harshly, exploiting them, or dealing with them as a slave.
- **21:23** — The Hebrew construct "a curse of God" (qillelat elohim) may be understood either as a subjective genitive ("cursed by God") or as an objective genitive ("an affront to God").
