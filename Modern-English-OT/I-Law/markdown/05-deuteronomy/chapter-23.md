## Text

**23:1** A man shall not take his father's wife, and he shall not uncover the edge of his father's garment.

**23:2** No one wounded by crushing or having his male organ cut off shall enter the assembly of the LORD.

**23:3** A person of illicit birth shall not enter the assembly of the LORD; even a tenth generation of his shall not enter the assembly of the LORD.

**23:4** An Ammonite or a Moabite shall not enter the assembly of the LORD; even a tenth generation of theirs shall not enter the assembly of the LORD forever,

**23:5** because they did not come to meet you with bread and water along the road as you came out of Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam the son of Beor from Pethor of Aram-naharaim to curse you.

**23:6** Yet the LORD your God was unwilling to listen to Balaam; rather, the LORD your God turned the curse for you into a blessing, because the LORD your God loved you.

**23:7** You shall not seek their peace or their welfare all your days, forever.

**23:8** You shall not loathe an Edomite, for he is your brother; you shall not loathe an Egyptian, for you were a resident alien in his land.

**23:9** Children who are born to them—a third generation of theirs—may enter the assembly of the LORD.

**23:10** When you go out as a camp against your enemies, you shall guard yourself from every evil thing.

**23:11** If there is a man among you who is not clean from an occurrence at night, then he shall go outside the camp; he shall not enter into the midst of the camp.

**23:12** And as evening draws near, he shall bathe in water, and when the sun goes down, he may enter into the midst of the camp.

**23:13** And you shall have a designated place outside the camp, and there you shall go out.

**23:14** And you shall have a spade along with your equipment; and when you sit down outside, you shall dig with it, and turn back and cover your excrement.

**23:15** For the LORD your God walks about in the midst of your camp to rescue you and to hand your enemies over before you; so your camp shall be holy, that He may not see any indecent thing among you and turn back from following you.

**23:16** You shall not surrender to his master a slave who has escaped to you from his master.

**23:17** With you he shall live in your midst, in the place that he chooses in one of your gates, wherever it is good for him; you shall not oppress him.

**23:18** There shall not be a female shrine prostitute from among the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be a male shrine prostitute from among the sons of Israel.

**23:19** You shall not bring the earnings of a prostitute or the price of a dog into the house of the LORD your God for any vow, for both of them are an abomination to the LORD your God.

**23:20** You shall not lend at interest to your brother: interest on money, interest on food, interest on anything that may be lent at interest.

**23:21** To a foreigner you may lend at interest, but to your brother you shall not lend at interest, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all that you put your hand to in the land that you are entering to possess.

**23:22** When you make a vow to the LORD your God, you shall not delay in paying it, for the LORD your God will surely demand it from you, and there will be sin in you.

**23:23** And if you refrain from vowing, there will be no sin in you.

**23:24** You shall keep and do what goes out from your lips, just as you vowed to the LORD your God as a freewill offering, what you spoke with your mouth.

**23:25** When you come into your neighbor's standing grain, you may pluck heads of grain with your hand, but you shall not swing a sickle over your neighbor's standing grain.

**23:26** When you come into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes as you desire, to your fill, but you shall not put any into your container.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — Hebrew kenaph aviv literally means 'the wing/corner of his father,' referring to the corner of a garment or covering as an idiom for marital rights and intimacy (cf. Ruth 3:9).
- **23:3** — Hebrew mamzer (occurring only here and in Zech 9:6) denotes someone born of an illicit or forbidden union; its exact legal scope is debated.
- **23:5** — Hebrew sakhar ('he hired') is singular, referring specifically to Moab, though rendered 'they hired' to agree with the plural verb earlier in the verse. Aram-naharaim literally means 'Aram of the Two Rivers,' i.e., Upper Mesopotamia.
- **23:14** — Hebrew azenekha ('your equipment/gear') is a hapax legomenon whose exact sense is uncertain, commonly understood as personal tools, weapons, or field gear.
- **23:18** — Hebrew qedeshah (fem.) and qadesh (masc.) literally mean 'consecrated woman' and 'consecrated man,' referring to cultic personnel associated with illicit sexual rites, paired with 'prostitute' and 'dog' (likely a contemptuous metaphor for a male prostitute) in v. 19.
