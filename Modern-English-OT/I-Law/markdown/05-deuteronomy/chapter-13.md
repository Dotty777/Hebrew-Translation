## Text

**13:1** Every word that I am commanding you, you shall take care to do it; you shall not add to it, and you shall not subtract from it.

**13:2** If a prophet or a dreamer of a dream rises up in your midst and gives you a sign or a wonder,

**13:3** and the sign or the wonder comes to pass about which he spoke to you, saying, "Let us go after other gods"—whom you have not known—"and let us serve them,"

**13:4** you must not listen to the words of that prophet or to the dreamer of that dream, because the LORD your God is testing you to know whether you love the LORD your God with your whole heart and with your whole soul.

**13:5** You shall walk after the LORD your God, and you shall fear Him; you shall keep His commandments and listen to His voice; you shall serve Him and cling to Him.

**13:6** Now that prophet or the dreamer of that dream shall be put to death, because he spoke rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt and ransomed you from the house of slaves, to lead you astray from the path on which the LORD your God commanded you to walk. So you shall sweep away the evil from your midst.

**13:7** If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or your daughter, or the wife of your bosom, or your friend who is like your own soul, incites you in secret, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods"—whom you have not known, you and your fathers,

**13:8** of the gods of the peoples around you, those near to you or those far from you, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth—

**13:9** you must not consent to him, nor listen to him; your eye must not spare him, nor must you show compassion or cover for him.

**13:10** Instead, you shall certainly kill him; your hand shall be against him first to put him to death, and the hand of all the people afterward.

**13:11** And you shall stone him with stones so that he dies, because he sought to lead you astray from the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves.

**13:12** And all Israel will hear and be afraid, and they will not again do anything like this evil thing in your midst.

**13:13** If you hear in one of your cities that the LORD your God is giving you to dwell there, saying,

**13:14** "Worthless men have gone out from your midst and have led the residents of their city astray, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods'"—whom you have not known—

**13:15** then you shall inquire, examine, and question thoroughly; and look, if the matter is true and confirmed, that this abomination has been done in your midst,

**13:16** you shall certainly strike down the residents of that city with the edge of the sword, devoting it to destruction—along with everything that is in it and its livestock—with the edge of the sword.

**13:17** You shall gather all its plunder into the middle of its public square and burn the city and all its plunder with fire as a whole offering to the LORD your God. It shall become a ruin mound forever; it shall not be built again.

**13:18** Nothing from what is devoted to destruction shall cling to your hand, so that the LORD may turn from His burning anger and grant you compassion, show you mercy, and multiply you, just as He swore to your fathers,

**13:19** if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God by keeping all His commandments that I am commanding you today, doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — The Masoretic Text begins chapter 13 with this verse; in the verse division of the Latin Vulgate tradition it is 12:32, and the remaining verses of chapter 13 are numbered one lower there.
- **13:6** — The Hiphil of nadach (literally "thrust/drive away") is used consistently in vv. 6, 11, and 14 for driving or leading someone astray from faithfulness to the LORD. Here sarah ("rebellion") literally denotes turning aside or defection.
- **13:14** — Hebrew anashim bene-beliyya'al is literally "men, sons of worthlessness" (or "wickedness"). The same noun beliyya'al recurs in 15:9 for a "worthless" thought.
- **13:16** — The Hiphil infinitive absolute hacharem ("devoting to destruction," from charam) denotes placing persons or property under the irreversible ban (cherem, v. 18) dedicated to the LORD. In v. 17, kalil ("whole offering") is a sacrificial term for an offering consumed entirely by fire.
