## Text

**6:1** Now this is the commandment, the statutes and the judgments, that the LORD your God commanded to be taught to you, to carry out in the land to which you are crossing over to possess it,

**6:2** so that you may fear the LORD your God—to keep all His statutes and His commandments that I am commanding you, you and your son and your son's son, all the days of your life—and so that your days may be prolonged.

**6:3** So listen, Israel, and take care to do so, that things may go well for you and that you may multiply greatly, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, promised you—a land flowing with milk and honey.

**6:4** Listen, Israel: the LORD is our God, the LORD is one.

**6:5** And you must love the LORD your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul, and with all your might.

**6:6** And these words that I am commanding you today must be upon your heart.

**6:7** You must teach them repeatedly to your children and speak of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk along the way, and when you lie down, and when you get up.

**6:8** You must tie them as a sign upon your hand, and they are to be frontlets between your eyes.

**6:9** And you must write them upon the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

**6:10** And it will happen when the LORD your God brings you into the land that He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to you—great and good cities that you did not build,

**6:11** and houses full of every good thing that you did not fill, and hewn cisterns that you did not hew, vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant—and you eat and are satisfied,

**6:12** then guard yourself so that you do not forget the LORD, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves.

**6:13** The LORD your God you must fear, and Him you must serve, and by His name you must swear.

**6:14** You must not go after other gods, from among the gods of the peoples who are all around you,

**6:15** for the LORD your God among you is a jealous God—or else the anger of the LORD your God will burn against you, and He will destroy you from the face of the earth.

**6:16** You must not test the LORD your God, just as you tested Him at Massah.

**6:17** You must carefully keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and His testimonies and His statutes that He commanded you.

**6:18** And you must do what is upright and good in the eyes of the LORD, so that things may go well for you, and so that you may enter and possess the good land that the LORD swore to your fathers,

**6:19** by thrusting out all your enemies from your presence, just as the LORD spoke.

**6:20** When your son asks you in the future, saying, "What are the testimonies and the statutes and the judgments that the LORD our God commanded you?"

**6:21** then you must say to your son, "We were slaves to Pharaoh in Egypt, and the LORD brought us out from Egypt with a strong hand.

**6:22** And the LORD displayed signs and wonders, great and devastating, against Egypt, against Pharaoh, and against his entire household, before our eyes.

**6:23** And He brought us out from there so that He might bring us in, to give us the land that He swore to our fathers.

**6:24** And the LORD commanded us to carry out all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God for our good always, to keep us alive just as on this day.

**6:25** And righteousness will be ours if we take care to carry out this entire commandment before the LORD our God, just as He commanded us."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The verbless Hebrew confession literally reads 'the LORD our God, the LORD one,' and can also be rendered 'the LORD our God, the LORD is one' or 'the LORD is our God, the LORD alone.'
- **6:5** — 'Might' renders Hebrew me'od, ordinarily an adverb ('very' or 'exceedingly'), here used as a noun denoting one's entire strength or capacity.
- **6:7** — 'Teach them repeatedly' renders the Piel of shanan (literally 'to sharpen'), denoting diligent, repeated instruction.
- **6:16** — Hebrew contains a wordplay between 'you tested' (nissitem, Piel of nasah) and 'Massah' (hammassah, literally 'the Testing').
