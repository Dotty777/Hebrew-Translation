## Text

**5:1** Moses called to all Israel and said to them, "Listen, Israel, to the statutes and the judgments that I am speaking in your ears today; learn them and take care to carry them out.

**5:2** The LORD our God cut a covenant with us at Horeb.

**5:3** It was not with our fathers that the LORD cut this covenant, but with us—we who are here today, all of us alive.

**5:4** Face to face the LORD spoke with you on the mountain from within the fire—

**5:5** I was standing between the LORD and you at that time to declare to you the word of the LORD, because you were afraid of the fire and did not go up on the mountain—saying,

**5:6** 'I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves.

**5:7** You must not have other gods in My presence.

**5:8** You must not make for yourself a carved image, any form of what is in the heavens above, or on the earth below, or in the waters beneath the earth.

**5:9** You must not bow down to them or serve them, because I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the guilt of fathers upon children, and upon the third and upon the fourth generations of those who hate Me,

**5:10** yet showing loyal love to thousands, to those who love Me and to those who keep My commandments.

**5:11** You must not take up the name of the LORD your God in vain, because the LORD will not acquit the one who takes up His name in vain.

**5:12** Keep the Sabbath day to consecrate it, just as the LORD your God commanded you.

**5:13** Six days you are to labor and carry out all your work,

**5:14** but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God; you must not do any work—you, or your son, or your daughter, or your male slave, or your female slave, or your ox, or your donkey, or any of your livestock, or your foreign resident who is within your gates—so that your male slave and your female slave may rest just like you.

**5:15** Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and that the LORD your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm. For this reason the LORD your God has commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.

**5:16** Honor your father and your mother, just as the LORD your God has commanded you, so that your days may be prolonged and so that things may go well for you upon the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

**5:17** And you must not commit adultery.

**5:18** You must not murder.

**5:19** And you must not steal.

**5:20** And you must not testify against your neighbor as a false witness.

**5:21** And you must not covet your neighbor's wife, and you must not crave your neighbor's house, his field, his male slave or his female slave, his ox or his donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor.'

**5:22** These words the LORD spoke to your entire assembly on the mountain from within the fire, the cloud, and the thick darkness, with a loud voice, and He added nothing more. Then He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me.

**5:23** And when you heard the voice from within the darkness, while the mountain was blazing with fire, you drew near to me, all the heads of your tribes and your elders.

**5:24** And you said, 'Look, the LORD our God has shown us His glory and His greatness, and His voice we have heard from within the fire. This day we have seen that God speaks with man and he remains alive.

**5:25** And now, why should we die? For this great fire will devour us. If we continue hearing the voice of the LORD our God any further, we will die.

**5:26** For who among all flesh has heard the voice of the living God speaking from within the fire, as we have, and stayed alive?

**5:27** You draw near and listen to everything that the LORD our God says, and you speak to us everything that the LORD our God speaks to you, and we will listen and do it.'

**5:28** Then the LORD heard the sound of your words as you spoke to me, and the LORD said to me, 'I have heard the sound of the words of this people that they spoke to you; they have done well in all they have spoken.

**5:29** If only they had this heart in them to fear Me and to keep all My commandments always, so that things might go well for them and for their children forever!

**5:30** Go, tell them, "Go back to your tents."

**5:31** But as for you, stand here with Me, so that I may speak to you the entire commandment and the statutes and the judgments that you are to teach them, so that they may carry them out in the land that I am giving them to possess.'

**5:32** Therefore take care to do just as the LORD your God commanded you; you must not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**5:33** You must walk in the entire way that the LORD your God commanded you, so that you may live, and so that things may go well for you, and you may lengthen your days in the land that you will possess."

## Translator Notes

- **5:5** — This verse forms a parenthetical aside in Hebrew interrupting 'spoke' in 5:4 and 'saying' at the end of 5:5, which introduces the LORD's words in 5:6-21.
- **5:10** — Qere reads 'My commandments,' matching first-person 'those who love Me'; ketiv writes 'His commandments' (or 'His commandment').
- **5:17** — In this Hebrew text, the prohibition against adultery stands at 5:17 and murder at 5:18, an ancient sequence also attested in Papyrus Nash and early Greek witnesses.
- **5:27** — The second Hebrew pronoun 'you' (ve'att) is spelled and pointed in the shortened form normally used for the feminine singular, a rare Masoretic masculine usage also found in Numbers 11:15 and Ezekiel 28:14.
- **5:29** — 'If only' renders the Hebrew optative idiom mi-yitten (literally 'Who will give'), expressing an earnest wish.
