## Text

**26:1** Now when you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and settle in it,

**26:2** you must take a portion of the first of all the fruit of the soil that you bring in from your land, which the LORD your God is giving you, place it in a basket, and go to the place that the LORD your God will choose to have His name dwell there.

**26:3** You shall go to the priest who serves in those days and say to him, "Today I declare to the LORD your God that I have entered the land that the LORD swore to our fathers to give us."

**26:4** The priest shall then take the basket from your hand and set it down in front of the altar of the LORD your God.

**26:5** Then you shall answer and say before the LORD your God, "A perishing Aramean was my father; he went down to Egypt and resided there as an alien with few people, and there he became a great, mighty, and numerous nation.

**26:6** And the Egyptians mistreated us, afflicted us, and placed harsh labor upon us.

**26:7** We cried out to the LORD, the God of our fathers, and the LORD listened to our voice and saw our affliction, our hardship, and our oppression.

**26:8** Then the LORD brought us out from Egypt with a strong hand and an extended arm, with great dread, and with signs and wonders.

**26:9** He brought us to this place and gave this land to us, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**26:10** And now, look, I have brought the first of the fruit of the soil that You have given to me, LORD." Then you must set it down before the LORD your God and bow down before the LORD your God.

**26:11** You shall rejoice in all the good that the LORD your God has given you and your household, you together with the Levite and the resident alien who is in your midst.

**26:12** When you have finished setting aside the entire tithe of your produce in the third year, the year of the tithe, and have given it to the Levite, the resident alien, the fatherless, and the widow, so that they may eat within your gates and be satisfied,

**26:13** then you shall say before the LORD your God, "I have cleared out the sacred portion from the house, and I have also given it to the Levite, the resident alien, the fatherless, and the widow, according to Your whole command that You commanded me. I have not turned aside from Your commands, nor have I forgotten.

**26:14** I have not eaten any of it during my mourning, nor have I cleared out any of it while unclean, nor have I given any of it for the dead. I have listened to the voice of the LORD my God; I have acted according to everything that You commanded me.

**26:15** Look down from Your holy dwelling place, from the heavens, and bless Your people Israel and the soil that You have given to us, just as You swore to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey."

**26:16** This very day the LORD your God is commanding you to carry out these statutes and judgments; therefore you must keep and do them with all your heart and with all your soul.

**26:17** Today you have affirmed the LORD to be God for you, and that you will walk in His ways, keep His statutes, His commands, and His judgments, and listen to His voice.

**26:18** And today the LORD has affirmed you to be a people of treasured possession for Himself, just as He spoke to you, and that you are to keep all His commands,

**26:19** and that He will place you high above all the nations that He has made, for praise, for renown, and for splendor, and that you will be a holy people to the LORD your God, just as He has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **26:5** — Hebrew arammi obed uses the Qal active participle of the verb meaning to perish or be lost, literally "a perishing Aramean," also widely understood as "a wandering Aramean" referring to Jacob's vulnerable nomadic state or famine-driven descent into Egypt.
- **26:12** — The form la'ser is a syncopated Hiphil infinitive construct of the verb meaning to tithe, denoting the formal setting aside or delivery of the third-year tithe.
- **26:14** — Hebrew be'oni, "during my mourning," derives from a noun meaning sorrow or mourning as in Hosea 9:4, a state that rendered consecrated food ritually unfit to eat.
- **26:17** — The Hiphil of the verb meaning "to say," he'emarta here and he'emireka in verse 18, occurs only in these two verses in the Hebrew Bible, expressing a solemn mutual covenantal affirmation or eliciting of a pledge between Israel and the LORD.
