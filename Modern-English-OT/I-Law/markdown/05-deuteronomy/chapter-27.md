## Text

**27:1** And Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Observe the entire commandment that I am commanding you today.

**27:2** And it will come about, on the day you cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD your God is giving to you, that you shall erect large stones for yourself and plaster them with lime.

**27:3** And you shall write on them all the words of this law when you cross over, so that you may come into the land that the LORD your God is giving to you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, promised you.

**27:4** And it will come about, when you cross the Jordan, that you shall erect on Mount Ebal these stones concerning which I am commanding you today, and you shall plaster them with lime.

**27:5** And you shall build an altar there to the LORD your God, an altar of stones; you shall not wield iron over them.

**27:6** With whole stones you shall build the altar of the LORD your God, and you shall offer up burnt offerings on it to the LORD your God.

**27:7** And you shall sacrifice peace offerings, eat there, and rejoice in the presence of the LORD your God.

**27:8** And you shall write on the stones all the words of this law clearly and thoroughly."

**27:9** Then Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Keep silent and listen, Israel! On this day you have become a people belonging to the LORD your God.

**27:10** So you shall listen to the voice of the LORD your God and carry out His commandments and His statutes that I am commanding you today."

**27:11** And Moses commanded the people on that day, saying:

**27:12** "These shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people when you cross the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.

**27:13** And these shall stand for the curse on Mount Ebal: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.

**27:14** Then the Levites shall speak out and say to every man of Israel with a loud voice:

**27:15** 'Cursed is the man who makes a carved image or a cast image, an abomination to the LORD, the work of the hands of a craftsman, and places it in secret.' And all the people shall respond and say, 'Amen.'

**27:16** 'Cursed is the one who treats his father or his mother with contempt.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:17** 'Cursed is the one who shifts his neighbor's boundary.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:18** 'Cursed is the one who leads a blind person astray on the road.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:19** 'Cursed is the one who subverts the justice due a resident alien, a fatherless child, or a widow.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:20** 'Cursed is the one who lies with his father's wife, because he has uncovered the edge of his father's garment.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:21** 'Cursed is the one who lies with any animal.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:22** 'Cursed is the one who lies with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:23** 'Cursed is the one who lies with his mother-in-law.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:24** 'Cursed is the one who strikes down his neighbor in secret.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:25** 'Cursed is the one who takes a bribe to strike down a life of innocent blood.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**27:26** 'Cursed is the one who does not uphold the words of this law by doing them.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'"

## Translator Notes

- **27:4** — The Masoretic Text reads "on Mount Ebal," whereas the Samaritan Pentateuch reads "on Mount Gerizim."
- **27:8** — "clearly and thoroughly" renders two adverbial infinitives absolute: the Piel infinitive absolute of ba'ar ("make plain/clear") and the Hiphil infinitive absolute of yatav ("do well/thoroughly").
- **27:9** — "Keep silent" renders the Hiphil imperative of sakat, a word occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible, calling for hushed attention.
- **27:20** — "uncovered the edge of his father's garment" renders a Hebrew idiom (literally "uncovered his father's wing/hem," cf. Deut 23:1) denoting the violation of his father's marital bed.
- **27:26** — The Masoretic Text reads "the words of this law" without "all" (contrast vv. 3 and 8), whereas the Samaritan Pentateuch and Septuagint include "all."
