## Text

**3:1** Then we turned and went up along the road to Bashan, and Og, king of Bashan, came out to meet us—he and all his people—for battle at Edrei.

**3:2** Then the LORD said to me, "Do not be afraid of him, for into your hand I have given him, along with all his people and his land; and you shall do to him just as you did to Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."

**3:3** So the LORD our God also gave into our hand Og, king of Bashan, and all his people, and we struck him down until no survivor was left to him.

**3:4** And we captured all his cities at that time; there was not a town that we did not take from them—sixty cities, the entire district of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**3:5** All these were fortified cities with high walls, double gates, and bars, apart from very many unwalled cities.

**3:6** And we devoted them to complete destruction, just as we had done to Sihon, king of Heshbon, devoting to complete destruction every city—men, women, and little ones.

**3:7** But all the livestock and the spoil of the cities we took as plunder for ourselves.

**3:8** So at that time we took the land from the hand of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the Wadi Arnon as far as Mount Hermon.

**3:9** The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, while the Amorites call it Senir.

**3:10** All the cities of the tableland, along with all Gilead and all Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei—the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**3:11** For only Og, king of Bashan, was left from the remnant of the Rephaim. Look, his bed was a bed of iron—is it not in Rabbah of the sons of Ammon? Nine cubits is its length and four cubits its width, by a man's cubit.

**3:12** So we took possession of this land at that time: from Aroer, which is beside the Wadi Arnon, and half the hill country of Gilead with its cities, I gave to the Reubenites and to the Gadites.

**3:13** And the rest of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh—the entire district of Argob, with all Bashan; that is called the land of Rephaim.

**3:14** Jair the son of Manasseh took the entire district of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and named them—that is, Bashan—after his own name, Havvoth-jair, until this day.

**3:15** And I gave Gilead to Machir.

**3:16** And to the Reubenites and to the Gadites I gave from Gilead even to the Wadi Arnon—the middle of the wadi as a border—and as far as the Wadi Jabbok, the border of the sons of Ammon,

**3:17** as well as the Arabah, with the Jordan as a border, from Chinnereth even to the Sea of the Arabah—the Salt Sea—below the slopes of Pisgah on the east.

**3:18** Then I commanded you at that time, saying, "The LORD your God has given you this land to possess it. You shall cross over armed for battle ahead of your brothers, the sons of Israel—all the men of valor.

**3:19** Only your wives, your little ones, and your livestock—I know that you have much livestock—shall remain in your cities that I have given to you,

**3:20** until the LORD gives rest to your brothers, just as to you, and they too take possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving to them beyond the Jordan. Then you shall return, each man to his possession that I have given to you."

**3:21** And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying, "Your own eyes have seen everything that the LORD your God did to these two kings; thus the LORD will do to all the kingdoms where you are crossing over.

**3:22** You must not fear them, for the LORD your God—He is the one who fights for you."

**3:23** Then I implored the LORD at that time, saying:

**3:24** "Lord GOD, You have begun to show Your servant Your greatness and Your strong hand; for what god is there in the heavens or on the earth who can do anything like Your works and Your mighty deeds?

**3:25** Please let me cross over and see the good land that is beyond the Jordan, this good hill country and Lebanon."

**3:26** Yet the LORD was furious with me on your account and did not listen to me. And the LORD said to me, "Enough for you! Do not speak to Me any further about this matter.

**3:27** Go up to the summit of Pisgah and lift your eyes westward, northward, southward, and eastward, and see with your own eyes, for you shall not cross this Jordan.

**3:28** And command Joshua, strengthen him, and encourage him, for he will cross over ahead of this people, and he will cause them to inherit the land that you will see."

**3:29** And we stayed in the valley facing Beth-peor.

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — Hebrew be-ever ha-Yarden ("beyond the Jordan") literally means "across/on the side of the Jordan" and shifts perspective within this chapter—referring to the eastern side here, and to the western side (Canaan) in 3:20 and 3:25.
- **3:14** — Havvoth-jair (Hebrew Chavvot Ya'ir) literally means "tent-villages of Jair."
- **3:16** — Hebrew tokh ha-nachal u-gevul (literally "the middle of the wadi and a border") uses an explicative waw idiomatically to define the boundary line itself, as also in "with the Jordan as a border" in 3:17.
- **3:26** — "Was furious with me" translates va-yit'abber, the Hithpael of avar II ("be furious, overflow with wrath"), forming a sharp wordplay with the homonymous Qal verb avar I ("cross over") in Moses's plea in 3:25 and God's refusal in 3:27.
