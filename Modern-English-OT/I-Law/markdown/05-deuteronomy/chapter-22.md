## Text

**22:1** You shall not see your brother's ox or his sheep wandering astray and hide yourself from them; you shall surely return them to your brother.

**22:2** If your brother is not near you, or you do not know him, you shall gather it inside your house, and it shall be with you until your brother searches for it, and you shall return it to him.

**22:3** And so you shall do with his donkey, and so you shall do with his garment, and so you shall do with every lost thing of your brother that is lost from him and that you find; you may not hide yourself.

**22:4** You shall not see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen on the road and hide yourself from them; you shall surely lift them up with him.

**22:5** A man's attire shall not be on a woman, and a man shall not wear a woman's garment, for everyone who does these things is an abomination to the LORD your God.

**22:6** If a bird's nest happens to be before you along the road, in any tree or on the ground, with chicks or eggs, and the mother is sitting on the chicks or on the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the young.

**22:7** You shall surely send the mother away, and the young you may take for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may lengthen your days.

**22:8** When you build a new house, then you shall make a parapet for your roof, and you shall not place bloodguilt on your house if someone falls from it.

**22:9** You shall not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, otherwise the full yield—the seed that you sow and the produce of the vineyard—will become consecrated.

**22:10** You shall not plow with an ox and with a donkey together.

**22:11** You shall not wear mixed fabric, wool and linen together.

**22:12** You shall make twisted tassels for yourself on the four corners of your cloak, with which you cover yourself.

**22:13** If a man takes a wife, and goes in to her, and hates her,

**22:14** and lays baseless charges against her, and brings out a bad name against her, and says, "I took this woman, and when I came near to her, I did not find signs of virginity in her,"

**22:15** then the father of the young woman and her mother shall take the signs of the young woman's virginity and bring them out to the elders of the city at the gate.

**22:16** And the father of the young woman shall say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as a wife, and he hated her,

**22:17** and look, he has laid baseless charges, saying, 'I did not find signs of virginity in your daughter.' Yet these are the signs of my daughter's virginity." And they shall spread out the garment before the elders of the city.

**22:18** Then the elders of that city shall take the man and discipline him.

**22:19** And they shall fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give it to the father of the young woman, because he has brought out a bad name against a virgin of Israel. And she shall be his wife; he may not send her away all his days.

**22:20** But if this matter is true, that signs of virginity were not found for the young woman,

**22:21** then they shall bring the young woman out to the entrance of her father's house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones so that she dies, because she has done a disgraceful thing in Israel by committing sexual immorality in her father's house. And you shall clear away the evil from your midst.

**22:22** If a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, then both of them shall die—the man who lay with the woman, and the woman. And you shall clear away the evil from Israel.

**22:23** If there is a young woman, a virgin betrothed to a man, and a man finds her in the city and lies with her,

**22:24** then you shall bring both of them out to the gate of that city, and stone them with stones so that they die—the young woman because she did not cry out in the city, and the man because he violated the wife of his neighbor. And you shall clear away the evil from your midst.

**22:25** But if in the field the man finds the betrothed young woman, and the man overpowers her and lies with her, then the man who lay with her, he alone, shall die.

**22:26** And to the young woman you shall do nothing; the young woman has no sin deserving death, for just as when a man rises up against his neighbor and murders him, so is this matter.

**22:27** For he found her in the field; the betrothed young woman cried out, and there was no one to rescue her.

**22:28** If a man finds a young woman, a virgin who has not been betrothed, and takes hold of her and lies with her, and they are found,

**22:29** then the man who lay with her shall give fifty shekels of silver to the father of the young woman, and she shall be his wife because he violated her; he may not send her away all his days.

## Translator Notes

- **22:5** — Hebrew keli-gever ('an article/equipment of a man') is broader than simlah ('garment') in the second clause and can encompass male attire, accessories, or gear.
- **22:9** — Hebrew tiqdash (Qal imperfect of qadash) literally means 'will become holy/consecrated,' referring to produce forfeited to the sanctuary and thus forbidden for ordinary use.
- **22:11** — Hebrew shaatnez is a technical term for mixed-fiber fabric, immediately defined here as wool and linen combined together.
- **22:14** — Hebrew alilot devarim (lit. 'pretexts/deeds of words,' recurring in v. 17) denotes trumped-up or baseless accusations.
- **22:15** — Here and in vv. 16, 20-21, and 23-29 (unlike v. 19), the consonantal text writes the archaic Pentateuchal form naar without final he, vocalized as a qere perpetuum for feminine naarah ('young woman').
