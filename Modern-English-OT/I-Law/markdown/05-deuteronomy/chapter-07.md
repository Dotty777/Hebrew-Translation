## Text

**7:1** When the LORD your God brings you into the land that you are entering to take possession of, and He dislodges many nations from before you—the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—seven nations more numerous and more powerful than you,

**7:2** and when the LORD your God gives them over before you and you strike them down, you must wholly devote them to destruction. You must not make a covenant with them, and you must not show them favor.

**7:3** You must not intermarry with them: you must not give your daughter to his son, nor take his daughter for your son.

**7:4** For he will turn your son away from following Me, and they will serve other gods; then the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and He will swiftly wipe you out.

**7:5** Instead, this is what you must do to them: you must pull down their altars, shatter their standing stones, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their carved images in the fire.

**7:6** For you are a holy people for the LORD your God; it is you whom the LORD your God has chosen to be a treasured people for Himself from among all the peoples who are on the face of the earth.

**7:7** It was not because you were more numerous than all the peoples that the LORD set His affection on you and chose you, since you were the fewest among all the peoples.

**7:8** Rather, because of the LORD's love for you and because He kept the oath He swore to your fathers, the LORD brought you out by a strong hand and redeemed you from the house of slaves, from the hand of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

**7:9** So you must know that the LORD your God—He is God, the faithful God who keeps the covenant and loyal love for those who love Him and for those who keep His commandments, to a thousand generations,

**7:10** and who repays those who hate Him to their face in order to destroy them; He will not delay with one who hates Him—to his face He will repay him.

**7:11** So you must keep the commandment, the statutes, and the judgments that I command you today, by doing them.

**7:12** Then it will happen that because you listen to these judgments, and keep them and do them, the LORD your God will keep for you the covenant and the loyal love that He swore to your fathers.

**7:13** He will love you, bless you, and make you numerous; He will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your soil—your grain, your new wine, and your fresh oil, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock—on the land that He swore to your fathers to give you.

**7:14** You will be blessed more than all the peoples; there will be neither barren male nor barren female among you, nor among your livestock.

**7:15** The LORD will remove every illness from you, and He will not place on you any of the dreadful diseases of Egypt that you knew, but will put them on all those who hate you.

**7:16** You must devour all the peoples whom the LORD your God is giving to you; your eye must show no pity on them, and you must not serve their gods, for that will be a snare to you.

**7:17** If you say in your heart, "These nations are more numerous than I am; how will I be able to dispossess them?"

**7:18** you must not be afraid of them; be sure to remember what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt:

**7:19** the great tests that your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, the strong hand and the outstretched arm, with which the LORD your God brought you out; in the same way the LORD your God will do to all the peoples before whom you are afraid.

**7:20** Furthermore, the LORD your God will send the hornet among them until those who remain and those who hide themselves from before you perish.

**7:21** You must not be terrified before them, because the LORD your God is among you—a great and fearsome God.

**7:22** The LORD your God will dislodge these nations from before you little by little; you will not be able to finish them off swiftly, or else the wild animals of the field will multiply against you.

**7:23** But the LORD your God will give them over before you and throw them into a massive panic until they are wiped out.

**7:24** He will put their kings into your hand, and you will cause their name to perish from beneath the heavens; no man will stand firm before you until you have wiped them out.

**7:25** You must burn the carved images of their gods in the fire. Do not covet the silver and gold on them and take it for yourself, so that you are not ensnared by it; for it is a detestable thing to the LORD your God.

**7:26** You must not bring a detestable thing into your house, or you will become a thing devoted to destruction just like it; you must completely loathe it and completely detest it, for it is a thing devoted to destruction.

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — The Hiphil verb yasir, he will turn away, has a singular subject referring back to the foreign father in 7:3, and the phrase me'acharay, from following Me, shifts abruptly into first-person divine speech before returning to third-person reference to the LORD.
- **7:9** — The form mitsvotav, His commandments, is spelled defectively without yod in the Masoretic text here and in 8:2, a traditional ketiv/qere orthographic variant whose vocalized qere reads the plural suffix.
- **7:10** — The Hebrew shifts mid-clause from plural lesone'av, those who hate Him, to distributive singular el-panav leha'avido, literally to his face to destroy him, using the idiom to his face to signify direct, personal repayment.
- **7:13** — The rare Deuteronomic phrases shegar-alafekha, the offspring of your cattle, and ashterot tso'nekha, the young of your flock, denote livestock fertility; ashterot shares an etymology with the fertility goddess Astarte but functions here purely as a term for the young of sheep and goats.
- **7:20** — The singular collective noun hatsir'ah traditionally means the hornet, as in Exodus 23:28 and Joshua 24:12, though some lexicographers understand it figuratively as panic or discouragement.
