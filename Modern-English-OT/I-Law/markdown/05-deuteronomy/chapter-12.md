## Text

**12:1** These are the statutes and the judgments that you shall be careful to do in the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you to possess, all the days that you live on the earth.

**12:2** You shall completely destroy all the places where the nations whom you are dispossessing served their gods, on the high mountains, on the hills, and under every lush tree.

**12:3** And you shall tear down their altars, shatter their sacred pillars, and burn their Asherah poles with fire; and you shall cut down the carved images of their gods and destroy their name from that place.

**12:4** You shall not do like this to the LORD your God.

**12:5** Instead, you shall seek out the place that the LORD your God will choose from all your tribes to put His name there, His dwelling place, and there you shall come.

**12:6** And there you shall bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution of your hand, your vows and your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock.

**12:7** And there you shall eat before the LORD your God, and you shall rejoice in every undertaking of your hand, you and your households, in which the LORD your God has blessed you.

**12:8** You shall not do according to all that we are doing here today, each man whatever is right in his own eyes,

**12:9** for up to now you have not come to the resting place and to the inheritance that the LORD your God is giving you.

**12:10** But when you cross the Jordan and live in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to inherit, and He gives you rest from all your enemies on every side so that you live in security,

**12:11** then to the place that the LORD your God will choose to make His name dwell, there you shall bring all that I am commanding you: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution of your hand, and all the choicest of your vows that you vow to the LORD.

**12:12** And you shall rejoice before the LORD your God, you, your sons and your daughters, your male slaves and your female slaves, and the Levite who is within your gates, since he has no portion or inheritance with you.

**12:13** Guard yourself that you do not offer up your burnt offerings in every place that you see;

**12:14** rather, in the place that the LORD will choose in one of your tribes, there you shall offer up your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I am commanding you.

**12:15** However, to the full desire of your soul you may slaughter and eat meat, according to the blessing of the LORD your God that He has given you within all your gates; the unclean and the clean may eat it, like the gazelle and like the deer.

**12:16** Only the blood you shall not eat; you shall pour it out on the ground like water.

**12:17** You are not permitted to eat within your gates the tithe of your grain, your new wine, or your fresh oil, or the firstborn of your herd or your flock, or any of your vows that you vow, or your freewill offerings, or the contribution of your hand;

**12:18** rather, you shall eat them before the LORD your God in the place that the LORD your God will choose—you, your son and your daughter, your male slave and your female slave, and the Levite who is within your gates—and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God in every undertaking of your hand.

**12:19** Guard yourself that you do not forsake the Levite all your days on your land.

**12:20** When the LORD your God enlarges your territory, just as He spoke to you, and you say, "I will eat meat," because your soul desires to eat meat, then you may eat meat to the full desire of your soul.

**12:21** If the place that the LORD your God will choose to put His name there is too far from you, then you may slaughter from your herd and from your flock that the LORD has given you, just as I have commanded you, and you may eat within your gates to the full desire of your soul.

**12:22** Indeed, just as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you may eat it; the unclean and the clean together may eat it.

**12:23** Only be firm not to eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you shall not eat the life with the meat.

**12:24** You shall not eat it; onto the ground you shall pour it out like water.

**12:25** You shall not eat it, in order that it may go well for you and for your children after you, because you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD.

**12:26** Only the holy things that are yours, and your vows, you shall carry and come to the place that the LORD will choose.

**12:27** And you shall offer your burnt offerings, the meat and the blood, on the altar of the LORD your God; and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out on the altar of the LORD your God, and the meat you shall eat.

**12:28** Be careful to listen to all these words that I am commanding you, in order that it may go well for you and for your children after you forever, because you do what is good and right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

**12:29** When the LORD your God cuts off from before you the nations whom you are entering to dispossess, and you dispossess them and live in their land,

**12:30** guard yourself that you are not ensnared into following them after they are destroyed from before you, and that you do not inquire about their gods, saying, "How did these nations serve their gods, so that I too may do the same?"

**12:31** You shall not do like this to the LORD your God, for every abomination to the LORD that He hates they have done for their gods; indeed, even their sons and their daughters they burn in the fire for their gods.

## Translator Notes

- **12:5** — The noun rendered "His dwelling place" (shikhno, from shakhan, "to dwell") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; the Masoretic accentuation pairs it directly with "you shall seek."
- **12:12** — "Within your gates" is a recurring Deuteronomic idiom (also vv. 15, 17, 18, 21) denoting "in your towns" or "within your local settlements."
- **12:22** — The Niphal passive verb ye'akhel ("is eaten") governs direct-object markers before its logical subjects ("the gazelle and the deer"), a classic impersonal passive construction in Biblical Hebrew (literally "as one eats the gazelle and the deer").
- **12:30** — "Ensnared into following them" renders a pregnant Hebrew construction (tinnaqesh achareihem, Niphal of naqash, literally "be ensnared after them").
