## Text

**14:1** You are sons of the LORD your God. You shall not gash yourselves, nor make a bald spot between your eyes for the dead.

**14:2** Because you are a holy people to the LORD your God, and it is you whom the LORD has chosen to be for Him a treasured people out of all the peoples who are on the face of the earth.

**14:3** You must not eat any abomination.

**14:4** These are the animals that you may eat: ox, sheep, and goat;

**14:5** deer, gazelle, fallow deer, wild goat, addax, oryx, and mountain sheep.

**14:6** And every animal that parts the hoof, having a cleft splitting it into two hooves, and chews the cud among the animals—that one you may eat.

**14:7** However, these you must not eat from among those that chew the cud or those that part the cleft hoof: the camel, the hare, and the rock hyrax, for although they chew the cud, they do not part the hoof; they are unclean to you.

**14:8** And the pig, because it parts the hoof yet does not chew the cud, is unclean to you. You shall not eat from their flesh, and you shall not touch their carcasses.

**14:9** These you may eat from all that are in the waters: everything that has fins and scales you may eat.

**14:10** Yet everything that does not have fins and scales you must not eat; it is unclean to you.

**14:11** Every clean bird you may eat.

**14:12** Yet these are the ones among them that you must not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, and the osprey;

**14:13** the buzzard, the falcon, and the kite according to its kind;

**14:14** every raven according to its kind;

**14:15** the ostrich, the nighthawk, the seagull, and the hawk according to its kind;

**14:16** the little owl, the great owl, and the barn owl;

**14:17** the desert owl, the Egyptian vulture, and the cormorant;

**14:18** the stork, the heron according to its kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

**14:19** And every winged swarming creature is unclean to you; they must not be eaten.

**14:20** Every clean winged creature you may eat.

**14:21** You must not eat any animal that died naturally; you may give it to the resident alien who is within your gates so that he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner, because you are a holy people to the LORD your God. You must not boil a young goat in the milk of its mother.

**14:22** You must certainly tithe all the produce of your seed that comes forth from the field year by year.

**14:23** And you shall eat before the LORD your God, in the place that He chooses to cause His name to dwell there, the tithe of your grain, your new wine, and your fresh oil, and the firstborn of your herd and your flock, in order that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always.

**14:24** And if the journey is too long for you, so that you cannot carry it, because the place that the LORD your God chooses to place His name there is too far from you when the LORD your God blesses you,

**14:25** then you shall trade it for silver, tie up the silver in your hand, and go to the place that the LORD your God chooses.

**14:26** You may spend the silver on whatever your soul longs for—on cattle or sheep, on wine or strong drink, or on anything your soul asks of you—and you shall eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household.

**14:27** And the Levite who is within your gates you must not forsake, because he has no share or inheritance with you.

**14:28** At the end of three years, you shall bring out the entire tithe of your produce in that year and store it within your gates.

**14:29** Then the Levite—because he has no share or inheritance with you—and the resident alien, the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates shall come, eat, and be satisfied, so that the LORD your God may bless you in every work of your hand that you do.

## Translator Notes

- **14:5** — The exact zoological identifications of several wild ruminants in this list—especially yachmur ("fallow deer"), aqqo ("wild goat"), dishon ("addax"), teo ("oryx"), and zamer ("mountain sheep")—remain uncertain.
- **14:8** — Hebrew velo gerah is elliptical (literally "and not cud"), omitting the participle for chewing/bringing up the cud from v. 7.
- **14:13** — The Masoretic Text lists three birds here (ra'ah, ayyah, and dayyah), whereas the parallel list in Leviticus 11:14 has two (da'ah and ayyah); exact species identifications for many of the unclean birds in vv. 12-18 are uncertain.
- **14:21** — Hebrew nevelah refers specifically to the carcass of an animal that died naturally rather than by slaughter (compare "their carcasses" in v. 8). The Qal infinitive absolute makhor ("sell") functions permissively alongside the finite verb tittenennah ("you may give it").
