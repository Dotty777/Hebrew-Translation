## Text

**29:1** Then Moses called to all Israel and said to them, "You yourselves saw everything that the LORD did before your eyes in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land,

**29:2** the great testings that your eyes saw, those great signs and wonders.

**29:3** Yet the LORD has not given you a heart to know, or eyes to see, or ears to hear, until this day.

**29:4** And I led you for forty years in the wilderness; your garments did not wear out on you, and your sandal did not wear out on your foot.

**29:5** You did not eat bread, nor did you drink wine or intoxicating drink, so that you would know that I am the LORD your God.

**29:6** Then you came to this place, and Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us for battle, and we struck them down.

**29:7** And we took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, to the Gadites, and to half the tribe of the Manassites.

**29:8** Therefore you shall keep the words of this covenant and do them, so that you may succeed in everything that you do.

**29:9** You are standing today, all of you, before the LORD your God: your heads, your tribes, your elders, and your officers, every man of Israel,

**29:10** your little ones, your wives, and your resident alien who is in the midst of your camps, from the cutter of your wood to the drawer of your water,

**29:11** so that you may enter into the covenant of the LORD your God and into His oath, which the LORD your God is making with you today,

**29:12** in order to establish you today for Himself as a people, and that He may be God to you, just as He spoke to you and just as He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

**29:13** And it is not with you alone that I am making this covenant and this oath,

**29:14** but with the one who is here with us standing today before the LORD our God, and with the one who is not here with us today—

**29:15** for you yourselves know how we lived in the land of Egypt, and how we passed through the midst of the nations through which you passed,

**29:16** and you saw their detestable things and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which were with them—

**29:17** so that there may not be among you a man or woman, or a clan or tribe, whose heart is turning away today from the LORD our God to go to serve the gods of those nations, so that there may not be among you a root bearing poison and wormwood.

**29:18** And it will be, when he hears the words of this sworn curse, that he will bless himself in his heart, saying, 'Peace will be mine, though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart'—so as to sweep away the watered with the dry.

**29:19** The LORD will not be willing to pardon him; rather, then the anger of the LORD and His jealousy will smoke against that man, and every sworn curse written in this book will lie upon him, and the LORD will wipe out his name from under the heavens.

**29:20** And the LORD will set him apart for calamity from all the tribes of Israel, according to all the sworn curses of the covenant written in this book of the law.

**29:21** And the coming generation, your children who will rise up after you, and the foreigner who comes from a distant land will say, when they see the plagues of that land and its sicknesses with which the LORD has made it sick—

**29:22** all its land is sulfur and salt, a burned waste; it is not sown, nor does it bring forth growth, nor does any plant come up in it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in His anger and in His wrath—

**29:23** all the nations will say, 'Why has the LORD done so to this land? What is this great burning of anger?'

**29:24** And they will say, 'Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD, the God of their fathers, which He made with them when He brought them out from the land of Egypt,

**29:25** and they went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods that they did not know and that He had not apportioned to them.

**29:26** So the anger of the LORD burned against that land, to bring upon it the entire curse written in this book;

**29:27** and the LORD uprooted them from their soil in anger, and in wrath, and in great fury, and hurled them into another land, as it is this day.'

**29:28** The hidden things belong to the LORD our God, while the revealed things belong to us and to our children forever, to do all the words of this law."

## Translator Notes

- **29:4** — Here and in v. 5 ("I led you... I am the LORD your God"), Moses shifts abruptly from third-person reference to the LORD (vv. 1-3) into first-person divine speech before returning to first-person plural with Israel ("us... we") in v. 6.
- **29:8** — "succeed" translates the Hiphil of sakal, which encompasses both acting wisely and achieving success as the fruit of wisdom.
- **29:9** — The Masoretic text attaches the second-person plural suffix to both nouns (rashekem shivtekem, "your heads, your tribes"), either listing them in series or placing "your tribes" in apposition to "your heads" (unlike the construct reading "heads of your tribes" in the Septuagint and Samaritan Pentateuch).
- **29:18** — "so as to sweep away the watered with the dry" renders the Qal infinitive construct of saphah with two feminine adjectives (haravah and hatsemeah), probably a merism for total devastation; Hebrew alah ("sworn curse" here and in vv. 19-20, 30:7) denotes a covenant oath backed by imprecations (rendered "oath" in vv. 11, 13).
- **29:28** — In the Masoretic text, extraordinary dots (puncta extraordinaria) mark the ten letters of lanu ulevanenu ("to us and to our children") and the first letter (ayin) of ad ("forever"), an ancient scribal marking whose original significance is debated.
