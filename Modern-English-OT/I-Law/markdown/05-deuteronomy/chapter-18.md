## Text

**18:1** The Levitical priests, the entire tribe of Levi, shall have no portion or inheritance with Israel; they shall eat the fire offerings of the LORD and His inheritance.

**18:2** And he shall have no inheritance in the midst of his brothers; the LORD Himself is his inheritance, as He spoke to him.

**18:3** And this shall be the rightful due of the priests from the people, from those who offer a sacrifice, whether ox or sheep: one shall give to the priest the foreleg, the two cheeks, and the stomach.

**18:4** The firstfruits of your grain, your new wine, and your fresh oil, and the first of the shearing of your flock, you shall give to him.

**18:5** For the LORD your God has chosen him from all your tribes to stand to minister in the name of the LORD, he and his sons always.

**18:6** And if a Levite comes from any one of your gates out of all Israel where he sojourns, and comes with all the desire of his soul to the place that the LORD will choose,

**18:7** then he may minister in the name of the LORD his God, just like all his brothers the Levites who stand there before the LORD.

**18:8** They shall eat portion for portion, apart from his sales of ancestral property.

**18:9** When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving to you, you shall not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations.

**18:10** No one shall be found among you who causes his son or his daughter to pass through the fire, a practitioner of divination, a soothsayer, an omen reader, or a sorcerer,

**18:11** or a caster of spells, or one who consults a medium or a spiritist, or one who inquires of the dead.

**18:12** Because everyone who does these things is an abomination to the LORD, and on account of these abominations the LORD your God is dispossessing them from before you.

**18:13** You shall be blameless with the LORD your God.

**18:14** For these nations that you are dispossessing listen to soothsayers and to diviners; but as for you, the LORD your God has not permitted this to you.

**18:15** The LORD your God will raise up a prophet for you from your midst, from your brothers, like me; to him you shall listen,

**18:16** according to all that you requested from the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, "May I not hear the voice of the LORD my God again, and may I not see this great fire anymore, so that I will not die."

**18:17** And the LORD said to me, "They have done well in what they have spoken.

**18:18** I will raise up a prophet for them from the midst of their brothers, like you, and I will place My words in his mouth, and he will speak to them everything that I command him.

**18:19** And it shall be that the man who does not listen to My words that he speaks in My name—I Myself will demand an accounting from him.

**18:20** Yet the prophet who acts presumptuously in speaking a word in My name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet shall die."

**18:21** And if you say in your heart, "How can we know the word that the LORD has not spoken?"

**18:22** When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD and the word does not happen or come about, that is the word that the LORD has not spoken. In presumption the prophet has spoken it; you shall not dread him.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — "His inheritance" (ve-nachalato) refers to the sacred dues, offerings, and portions belonging to the LORD that are allotted to the tribe of Levi for sustenance.
- **18:8** — Hebrew levad mimkarav al-ha'avot literally reads "apart from his sales upon/concerning the fathers," a compressed legal phrase referring to personal proceeds from the sale of patrimonial holdings.
- **18:10** — Verses 10-11 use technical participial forms for forbidden occult practices, including me'onen (Polel participle, "soothsayer/augur"), menachesh (Piel participle, "omen reader"), chover chaver ("binder/caster of spells"), and sho'el ov ve-yid'oni ("inquirer of a ghost/medium or familiar spirit").
- **18:22** — "You shall not dread him" translates the Qal imperfect lo tagur mimmennu (from gur II, "stand in dread/awe of"), a stronger term for fearful deference than the more general yare' ("fear").
