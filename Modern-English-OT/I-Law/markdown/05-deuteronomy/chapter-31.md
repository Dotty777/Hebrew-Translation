## Text

**31:1** And Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel.

**31:2** He said to them, "I am one hundred and twenty years old today; I can no longer go out and come in, and the LORD has said to me, 'You shall not cross over this Jordan.'

**31:3** The LORD your God Himself is crossing over before you; He will destroy these nations from before you, and you will dispossess them. Joshua himself is crossing over before you, just as the LORD has spoken.

**31:4** And the LORD will do to them just as He did to Sihon and to Og, kings of the Amorites, and to their land, whom He destroyed.

**31:5** And the LORD will give them over before you, and you are to do to them according to the entire commandment that I have commanded you.

**31:6** Be strong and take courage; do not fear and do not tremble before them, for the LORD your God Himself is the One going with you; He will not let go of you, and He will not abandon you."

**31:7** Then Moses called to Joshua and said to him before the eyes of all Israel, "Be strong and take courage, for you will go with this people into the land that the LORD swore to their fathers to give to them, and you will cause them to inherit it.

**31:8** And the LORD Himself is the One going before you; He will be with you. He will not let go of you, and He will not abandon you. Do not fear, and do not be dismayed."

**31:9** And Moses wrote this law and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, the bearers of the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and to all the elders of Israel.

**31:10** Then Moses commanded them, saying, "At the end of every seven years, at the appointed time of the year of release, during the Feast of Booths,

**31:11** when all Israel comes to appear in the presence of the LORD your God in the place that He will choose, you are to read this law before all Israel in their hearing.

**31:12** Assemble the people—the men, the women, the little ones, and your resident alien who is within your gates—so that they may hear and so that they may learn, and fear the LORD your God, and take care to do all the words of this law.

**31:13** And their children, who have not known, will hear and learn to fear the LORD your God all the days that you live on the land that you are crossing over the Jordan to possess."

**31:14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Look, the days have drawn near for you to die. Call Joshua, and station yourselves in the tent of meeting, so that I may commission him." So Moses and Joshua went and stationed themselves in the tent of meeting.

**31:15** Then the LORD appeared in the tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood over the doorway of the tent.

**31:16** And the LORD said to Moses, "Look, you are about to lie down with your fathers; and this people will rise up and prostitute themselves after the foreign gods of the land into whose midst they are going, and they will abandon Me and break My covenant that I made with them.

**31:17** Then My anger will burn against them on that day, and I will abandon them and hide My face from them, and they will be devoured. And many calamities and distresses will overtake them, and they will say on that day, 'Is it not because our God is not in our midst that these calamities have overtaken us?'

**31:18** And I will certainly hide My face on that day on account of all the evil that they have done, for they have turned to other gods.

**31:19** And now, write down this song for yourselves, and teach it to the sons of Israel; place it in their mouths, in order that this song may be a witness for Me against the sons of Israel.

**31:20** For when I bring them into the land flowing with milk and honey that I swore to their fathers, and they eat and are filled and grow fat, they will turn to other gods and serve them, and they will treat Me with contempt and break My covenant.

**31:21** And it will come about, when many calamities and distresses overtake them, that this song will testify before them as a witness—for it will not be forgotten from the mouth of their seed—for I know their inclination, which they are forming today, before I bring them into the land of which I swore."

**31:22** Then Moses wrote down this song on that day and taught it to the sons of Israel.

**31:23** And He commissioned Joshua the son of Nun and said, "Be strong and take courage, for you will bring the sons of Israel into the land that I swore to them, and I Myself will be with you."

**31:24** And it happened, when Moses had finished writing the words of this law in a book to their completion,

**31:25** that Moses commanded the Levites, the bearers of the ark of the covenant of the LORD, saying,

**31:26** "Take this book of the law and place it at the side of the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and it will be there as a witness against you.

**31:27** For I myself know your rebellion and your stiff neck; look, while I am still alive with you today, you have been rebellious against the LORD—how much more after my death!

**31:28** Assemble to me all the elders of your tribes and your officials, so that I may speak these words in their hearing and summon the heavens and the earth to witness against them.

**31:29** For I know that after my death you will certainly act corruptly and turn aside from the way that I have commanded you, and calamity will come upon you in the latter days, because you will do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD, so as to provoke Him to anger by the work of your hands."

**31:30** Then Moses spoke in the hearing of the entire assembly of Israel the words of this song to their completion.

## Translator Notes

- **31:6** — The Hiphil verb from rph literally means 'let go of' or 'drop', paired with Qal azav ('abandon'), the same verb that recurs in verses 8, 16, and 17.
- **31:7** — MT reads Qal imperfect tavo ('you will go') with the preposition et ('with'), contrasting with verse 23, which uses Hiphil tavi ('you will bring') with the direct object marker et.
- **31:11** — MT points the infinitive construct as Niphal leraot ('to appear'), with et-pene functioning adverbially as 'in the presence of' or 'before'.
- **31:19** — The Hebrew imperatives shift from second-person masculine plural ('write ... for yourselves', addressing both Moses and Joshua) to second-person masculine singular (Piel 'teach it' and Qal 'place it', addressing Moses).
- **31:23** — MT supplies no explicit subject noun for the verb 'commissioned'; the first-person divine promises ('I swore ... I Myself will be with you') and the fulfillment of verse 14 identify the speaker as the LORD.
