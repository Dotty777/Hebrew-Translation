## Text

**33:1** And this is the blessing by which Moses, the man of God, blessed the sons of Israel before his death.

**33:2** And he said, "The LORD came from Sinai and rose from Seir upon them; He blazed forth from Mount Paran and arrived from myriads of holy ones; from His right hand was a fiery law for them.

**33:3** Truly, He loves the peoples; all His holy ones are in Your hand; they settled down at Your feet, and each one receives from Your words.

**33:4** Moses commanded a law for us, a possession for the congregation of Jacob.

**33:5** And He became king in Jeshurun as the heads of the people gathered, the tribes of Israel as one.

**33:6** May Reuben live and not die, and may his men not be few."

**33:7** And this is for Judah; he said, "Hear Judah's voice, LORD, and bring him to his people. With his own hands he strives for himself, and may You be a help against his foes."

**33:8** And concerning Levi he said, "Your Thummim and Your Urim belong to Your faithful man, whom You tested at Massah, with whom You strove at the waters of Meribah;

**33:9** who said regarding his father and his mother, 'I have not seen him,' and did not recognize his brothers, and did not know his sons; for they kept Your word and guard Your covenant.

**33:10** They shall teach Your judgments to Jacob and Your law to Israel; they shall set incense before Your nostrils and a whole burnt offering on Your altar.

**33:11** Bless his strength, LORD, and be pleased with the work of his hands; crush the loins of those who rise against him and of those who hate him, so that they may not rise."

**33:12** Concerning Benjamin he said, "The beloved of the LORD shall dwell in safety beside Him; He shields him all day long, and between his shoulders He dwells."

**33:13** And concerning Joseph he said, "Blessed by the LORD is his land, with the choice bounty of the heavens, with dew, and with the deep that lies beneath,

**33:14** and with the choice bounty of the yields of the sun, and with the choice bounty thrust forth by the months,

**33:15** and with the finest of the ancient mountains, and with the choice bounty of the everlasting hills,

**33:16** and with the choice bounty of the earth and its fullness, and the favor of the One who dwelt in the bush. May it come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the head of the one set apart among his brothers.

**33:17** The firstborn of his bull—splendor belongs to him, and the horns of a wild ox are his horns; with them he will gore peoples together, to the ends of the earth. And these are the myriads of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of Manasseh."

**33:18** And concerning Zebulun he said, "Rejoice, Zebulun, when you go out, and Issachar, in your tents.

**33:19** They will summon peoples to the mountain; there they will offer sacrifices of righteousness, for they will draw in the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures buried in the sand."

**33:20** And concerning Gad he said, "Blessed is the One who enlarges Gad; he dwells like a lion and tears the arm, indeed the crown of the head.

**33:21** And he selected the first portion for himself, for there a commander's allotment was reserved; and he arrived with the heads of the people; he carried out the righteousness of the LORD, and His judgments with Israel."

**33:22** And concerning Dan he said, "Dan is a lion cub; he springs forth from Bashan."

**33:23** And concerning Naphtali he said, "Naphtali, satisfied with favor and filled with the LORD's blessing, possess the sea and the south."

**33:24** And concerning Asher he said, "Blessed above sons is Asher; let him be the favored one of his brothers, and let him dip his foot in oil.

**33:25** Iron and bronze shall be your bolts, and according to your days shall your strength be.

**33:26** There is no one like God, Jeshurun, who rides the heavens for your help, and in His majesty, the skies.

**33:27** A dwelling place is the ancient God, and beneath are everlasting arms; He expelled the enemy from before you and said, 'Destroy!'

**33:28** Thus Israel dwells in safety—the spring of Jacob alone—in a land of grain and new wine; indeed, his heavens drip dew.

**33:29** Happy are you, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD, the shield of your help and the One who is the sword of your majesty! Your enemies will cringe before you, and upon their high places you will tread."

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — Ketiv/Qere: the ketiv is written as one word (אשדת, often taken as "fiery slopes" or "flashing fire"), while the qere divides it into two words (אֵשׁ דָּת, "fire of law," i.e., "a fiery law"), which is translated here. Also, "myriads of holy ones" renders מֵרִבְבֹת קֹדֶשׁ (literally "from myriads of holiness").
- **33:3** — The verb תֻּכּוּ is a hapax legomenon (usually parsed as a Pual perfect of an uncertain root תכה), understood as "settled down" or "followed closely" at Your feet. The following verb יִשָּׂא is third-person masculine singular, used distributively ("each one receives").
- **33:6** — Literally the second clause reads "and let his men be a number" (מִסְפָּר, idiomatically "few in number"); the negative particle אַל ("not") from the preceding jussive is understood to carry over by poetic ellipsis, though some read it concessively ("though his men be few").
- **33:12** — The antecedents of the third-person masculine singular pronouns in the final clause (וּבֵין כְּתֵפָיו שָׁכֵן) are ambiguous: either the LORD dwells between Benjamin's shoulders (evoking the slopes/ridges of Benjamin's territory), as rendered here, or Benjamin dwells securely between the LORD's shoulders.
- **33:25** — Both מִנְעָלֶךָ ("your bolts," from נעל "to lock/bolt," though anciently also understood as "your sandals") and דׇּבְאֶךָ (understood as "your strength," or by some comparative Semitic scholars "your security/rest") are hapax legomena in Biblical Hebrew.
