## Text

**32:1** Listen attentively, heavens, and I will speak, and let the earth hear the utterances of my mouth.

**32:2** May my instruction drip like rain, may my speech flow like dew, like light rain upon fresh grass, and like abundant showers upon green plants.

**32:3** For I will call out the name of the LORD; give greatness to our God!

**32:4** The Rock—flawless is His work, for all His ways are justice; a God of faithfulness, and there is no wrongdoing; He is righteous and upright.

**32:5** They have behaved corruptly toward Him; they are not His children—the blemish is theirs, a crooked and warped generation.

**32:6** Is it to the LORD that you repay this, foolish people without wisdom? Is He not your Father who acquired you? He made you and established you.

**32:7** Remember the ancient days; discern the years of generation after generation. Ask your father, and he will report it to you; your elders, and they will speak to you.

**32:8** When the Most High gave inheritances to the nations, when He separated the sons of Adam, He fixed the borders of the peoples according to the number of the sons of Israel.

**32:9** For the share of the LORD is His people; Jacob is the measured lot of His inheritance.

**32:10** He found him in a wilderness land, and in a desolate, howling wasteland; He surrounded him, He attended to him, He protected him like the pupil of His eye.

**32:11** Like an eagle that rouses its nest and hovers over its fledglings, He spread out His wings, took him, and bore him upon His pinions.

**32:12** The LORD by Himself led him, and with Him there was no foreign god.

**32:13** He caused him to ride upon the heights of the earth, and he ate the yield of the field; He nursed him with honey from the crag, and oil from the flint rock,

**32:14** curds of cattle and milk of the flock, along with the fat of lambs, rams that are offspring of Bashan, and male goats, along with the fat of the kidneys of wheat; and you drank the blood of the grape, foaming wine.

**32:15** Then Jeshurun grew fat and kicked—you grew fat, you grew thick, you were gorged—and he abandoned God who made him, and treated with contempt the Rock of his salvation.

**32:16** They roused Him to jealousy with alien gods; with abominations they angered Him.

**32:17** They sacrificed to demons, which are not God—to gods they did not know, new ones that arrived recently, before whom your fathers did not shudder.

**32:18** The Rock who fathered you, you neglected, and you forgot the God who brought you forth.

**32:19** The LORD saw it and scorned them, because of the vexation of His sons and His daughters.

**32:20** And He said, "I will conceal My face from them; I will see what their outcome will be, for they are a generation of perversity, children with no faithfulness in them.

**32:21** They have roused Me to jealousy with what is not a god; they have angered Me with their worthless things. So I Myself will rouse them to jealousy with those who are not a people; with a foolish nation I will anger them.

**32:22** For a fire has flared up in My anger, and it blazes all the way to the lowest Sheol; it devours the earth and its produce, and sets ablaze the foundations of the mountains.

**32:23** I will pile calamities upon them; My arrows I will exhaust against them.

**32:24** They will be drained by famine, devoured by burning fever and bitter pestilence; the teeth of beasts I will let loose against them, along with the venom of creatures crawling in the dust.

**32:25** From outside, the sword will bereave, and from within the chambers, terror—both young man and virgin, the nursing infant along with the gray-haired man.

**32:26** I said, 'I would cleave them in pieces; I would cause their remembrance to cease from humanity,'

**32:27** had I not dreaded the vexation of the enemy, that their foes might misinterpret it, that they might say, 'Our hand has risen high, and it was not the LORD who performed all this.'"

**32:28** For they are a nation bereft of counsel, and in them there is no discernment.

**32:29** If only they were wise, they would comprehend this; they would discern their outcome!

**32:30** How could one pursue a thousand, and two drive ten thousand into flight, unless their Rock had sold them, and the LORD had handed them over?

**32:31** For their rock is not like our Rock, and our enemies themselves are judges.

**32:32** For from the vine of Sodom is their vine, and from the terraced fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are grapes of poison, and clusters of bitterness are theirs.

**32:33** The venom of serpents is their wine, and the cruel poison of cobras.

**32:34** "Is it not stored away with Me, sealed in My storehouses?

**32:35** Mine are vengeance and repayment, for the time when their foot totters; for the day of their ruin is near, and the things prepared for them are hastening on."

**32:36** For the LORD will render justice for His people, and He will feel compassion for His servants, when He sees that strength has vanished and no one remains, neither restrained nor free.

**32:37** Then He will say, "Where are their gods—the rock where they sought refuge,

**32:38** who used to eat the fat of their sacrifices and drink the wine of their drink offering? Let them arise and aid you; let them be a shelter over you!

**32:39** See now that I, I am He, and there is no god with Me; I Myself put to death and bring to life; I have wounded, and I Myself will heal, and there is no one who rescues from My hand.

**32:40** For I raise My hand to heaven and say, 'As I live forever,

**32:41** when I sharpen the flash of My sword and My hand grasps justice, I will return vengeance to My foes, and those who hate Me I will repay.

**32:42** I will make My arrows drunk on blood, and My sword will devour flesh—from the blood of the killed and the captives, from the unshorn heads of the enemy.'"

**32:43** Acclaim His people, nations, for He will avenge the blood of His servants; He will return vengeance to His foes, and will make atonement for His land, His people.

**32:44** Then Moses came and spoke all the words of this song within the hearing of the people, he and Hoshea the son of Nun.

**32:45** And Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel.

**32:46** Then he said to them, "Set your heart on all the words that I am testifying to you today, so that you may command them to your children, to observe and carry out all the words of this law.

**32:47** For it is not an empty word for you; rather, it is your life, and through this word you will lengthen your days upon the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

**32:48** Then the LORD spoke to Moses on that very day, saying,

**32:49** "Ascend to this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab facing Jericho, and view the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the sons of Israel as a possession.

**32:50** And die upon the mountain you are ascending, and be gathered to your kin, just as your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his kin,

**32:51** because you acted unfaithfully against Me among the sons of Israel at the waters of Meribah of Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin—because you did not regard Me as holy among the sons of Israel.

**32:52** For from a distance you will see the land, yet you will not enter there—into the land that I am giving to the sons of Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **32:5** — The Masoretic syntax is condensed and difficult, literally reading "acted corruptly toward Him, not His children, their blemish," with the singular Piel verb שִׁחֵת referring to Israel collectively.
- **32:8** — The Masoretic Text reads "the sons of Israel" (בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל), whereas a Dead Sea Scroll manuscript (4QDeutj) reads "sons of God" and the Septuagint reads "angels of God."
- **32:13** — Ketiv במותי and qere בָּמֳתֵי represent an orthographic variant in the construct plural of בָּמָה ("heights" or "high places"), with the qere omitting the vav; both carry the same meaning.
- **32:26** — The hapax verb אַפְאֵיהֶם (Hiphil imperfect 1cs of פאה with 3mp suffix) is understood either as "cleave them in pieces" or, denominatively from פֵּאָה ("corner, edge"), as "scatter them to the corners."
- **32:43** — In the Masoretic Text, the Hiphil imperative הַרְנִינוּ takes עַמּוֹ ("His people") directly without a preposition ("Acclaim His people, nations"), and אַדְמָתוֹ עַמּוֹ ("His land, His people") is asyndetic; 4QDeutq and the Septuagint preserve longer texts with additional lines.
