## Text

**2:1** How the Lord in His anger has shrouded the daughter of Zion in a cloud! He has hurled from heaven to earth the glory of Israel, and He did not remember His footstool on the day of His anger.

**2:2** The Lord has swallowed up all the dwellings of Jacob and did not spare them; in His fury He has torn down the strongholds of the daughter of Judah. He has brought them down to the ground; He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.

**2:3** In burning anger He has cut off every horn of Israel; He has drawn back His right hand from before the enemy. He has burned in Jacob like a flaming fire that devours on every side.

**2:4** He has bent His bow like an enemy, with His right hand poised like an adversary, and He has killed all who were precious to the eye. In the tent of the daughter of Zion He has poured out His wrath like fire.

**2:5** The Lord has become like an enemy; He has swallowed up Israel. He has swallowed up all its citadels and destroyed its strongholds, and He has multiplied mourning and lamentation in the daughter of Judah.

**2:6** He has ravaged His booth like a garden; He has destroyed His appointed meeting place. The LORD has caused appointed festival and Sabbath to be forgotten in Zion, and in the indignation of His anger He has spurned king and priest.

**2:7** The Lord has rejected His altar and repudiated His sanctuary; He has delivered into the hand of the enemy the walls of her citadels. They raised a clamor in the house of the LORD as on the day of an appointed festival.

**2:8** The LORD planned to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion; He stretched out a measuring line and did not draw back His hand from swallowing up. He caused rampart and wall to mourn; together they languish.

**2:9** Her gates have sunk into the ground; He has ruined and shattered her bars. Her king and her princes are among the nations; there is no law, and her prophets also find no vision from the LORD.

**2:10** The elders of the daughter of Zion sit on the ground and keep silent; they have thrown dust on their heads and wrapped themselves in sackcloth. The virgins of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground.

**2:11** My eyes are spent with tears; my inward parts are in turmoil. My liver is poured out to the ground over the ruin of the daughter of my people, as little child and nursing infant grow faint in the squares of the city.

**2:12** To their mothers they say, "Where are grain and wine?" as they grow faint like the wounded in the squares of the city, as their life is poured out into their mothers' bosom.

**2:13** What can I testify for you? What can I liken to you, daughter of Jerusalem? What can I compare to you, so that I may comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion? For your ruin is as vast as the sea; who can heal you?

**2:14** Your prophets have seen for you falsehood and folly; they have not exposed your iniquity so as to restore your fortunes, but have seen for you false and misleading oracles.

**2:15** All who pass along the road clap their hands at you; they hiss and shake their heads at the daughter of Jerusalem: "Is this the city of which they say, 'The perfection of beauty, joy of all the earth'?"

**2:16** All your enemies open their mouths wide against you; they hiss and gnash their teeth. They say, "We have swallowed her up! Surely this is the day we waited for; we have found it, we have seen it!"

**2:17** The LORD has done what He purposed; He has carried out His word which He commanded from days of old. He has torn down and did not spare; He has made the enemy rejoice over you and exalted the horn of your adversaries.

**2:18** Their heart cried out to the Lord. Wall of the daughter of Zion, let tears stream down like a torrent day and night; give yourself no respite, and do not let the pupil of your eye rest.

**2:19** Arise, cry aloud in the night, at the beginning of the watches! Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord; lift up your hands to Him for the lives of your little children, who faint from hunger at the head of every street.

**2:20** "See, LORD, and look! With whom have You dealt like this? Should women eat their own fruit, the little children of their tender care? Should priest and prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

**2:21** Young and old lie on the ground in the streets; my virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword. You have killed them on the day of Your anger; You have slaughtered and did not spare.

**2:22** You summoned, as on the day of an appointed festival, my terrors on every side; and on the day of the LORD's anger there was no fugitive or survivor. Those whom I tenderly cared for and raised, my enemy has annihilated."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The hapax legomenon yaib is a Hiphil imperfect 3ms of a denominative verb from ab ("cloud"), meaning to cover or shroud with a cloud, used poetically for past action after the lament exclamation "How."
- **2:13** — Following MT aidk (Hiphil imperfect 1cs with 2fs suffix of ud, "testify, cite as witness"), asking what witness or precedent can be adduced to console Jerusalem.
- **2:14** — Translates the Qere shebutek in the idiom heshib shebut ("restore fortunes"); the Ketiv reads shebitek ("your captivity," i.e., "turn back your captivity").
- **2:18** — Following MT strictly (Qal perfect 3ms with 3mp suffix on "heart," referring to the people of Zion), before the poem abruptly shifts to direct address of the wall of the daughter of Zion.
