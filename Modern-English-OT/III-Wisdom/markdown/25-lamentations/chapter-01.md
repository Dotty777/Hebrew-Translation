## Text

**1:1** How she sits alone, the city great in people! She has become like a widow; great among the nations, a princess among the provinces, she has become subject to forced labor.

**1:2** Bitterly she weeps in the night, and her tears are upon her cheek. She has no comforter among all her lovers; all her companions have acted treacherously against her; they have become her enemies.

**1:3** Judah has gone into exile because of affliction and heavy servitude; she dwells among the nations and finds no resting place. All her pursuers have overtaken her in the narrow straits.

**1:4** Zion's roads mourn, with no one coming to the appointed festival. All her gates lie desolate; her priests groan, her virgins are grieved, and she herself is in bitterness.

**1:5** Her adversaries have become the head; her enemies are at ease, for the LORD has afflicted her because of the multitude of her transgressions. Her little children have gone into captivity before the adversary.

**1:6** From the daughter of Zion all her splendor has departed. Her princes have become like stags that find no pasture, and they have gone without strength before the pursuer.

**1:7** Jerusalem remembers, in the days of her affliction and wandering, all her precious things that were from days of old. When her people fell into the hand of an adversary and there was no helper for her, the adversaries saw her and mocked at her collapse.

**1:8** Jerusalem has sinned severely; therefore she has become an object of scorn. All who honored her hold her in contempt, because they have seen her nakedness; she herself also groans and turns away.

**1:9** Her uncleanness is in her skirts; she did not consider her end. So she came down astonishingly; there is no comforter for her. "See, LORD, my affliction, for the enemy has magnified himself!"

**1:10** Over all her precious things the adversary has spread out his hand; indeed, she has seen nations enter her sanctuary, those whom You commanded that they should not enter Your assembly.

**1:11** All her people groan as they seek bread; they have given their precious things for food to restore life. "See, LORD, and look, for I have become contemptible!

**1:12** Is it nothing to you, all who pass along the road? Look and see whether there is any anguish like my anguish, which has been dealt to me, with which the LORD has afflicted me on the day of His burning anger.

**1:13** From on high He sent fire into my bones, and it mastered them; He spread a net for my feet and turned me back. He has made me desolate, faint all the day.

**1:14** The yoke of my transgressions was fastened by His hand; they were intertwined and came up upon my neck. He has caused my strength to fail; the Lord has given me into the hands of those against whom I cannot rise.

**1:15** The Lord has spurned all my mighty men in my midst; He has summoned an appointed assembly against me to shatter my young men. As in a winepress, the Lord has trodden the virgin daughter of Judah.

**1:16** Because of these things I weep; my eye, my eye streams with water, for far from me is a comforter, one who restores my life. My children have become desolate, for the enemy has prevailed."

**1:17** Zion spreads out her hands, yet there is no comforter for her. The LORD has commanded concerning Jacob that those around him should be his adversaries; Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them.

**1:18** "The LORD is righteous, for I have rebelled against His command. Listen, please, all the peoples, and see my anguish: my virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.

**1:19** I called to my lovers, but they deceived me. My priests and my elders perished in the city while they sought food for themselves to restore their life.

**1:20** See, LORD, for I am in distress; my inward parts are in turmoil. My heart is overturned within me, for I have utterly rebelled. Outside, the sword bereaves; inside the house, it is like death.

**1:21** They have heard that I groan; there is no comforter for me. All my enemies have heard of my calamity; they rejoice that You have done it. You have brought the day You proclaimed; let them become like me!

**1:22** Let all their wickedness come before You, and deal with them just as You have dealt with me because of all my transgressions; for my groanings are many, and my heart is faint."

## Translator Notes

- **1:8** — MT vocalizes the hapax legomenon lenidah from the root nud, meaning an object of head-shaking or scorn, though many scholars take it as an orthographic variant of niddah, an unclean or impure thing as in 1:17, matching the purity imagery of 1:8-9.
- **1:12** — Literally "not to you," an elliptical Hebrew expression commonly understood as a rhetorical question, "Is it nothing to you?" or as a wish, "May this not happen to you."
- **1:13** — The difficult Masoretic form wayyirdennah is parsed here as Qal wayyiqtol 3ms of radah or radad ("subdue, master, prevail over") with a 3fs suffix referring collectively to the bones, rather than emending to Hiphil of yarad ("made it descend").
- **1:14** — The hapax legomenon nisqad (Niphal perfect 3ms) is understood from context and Aramaic/rabbinic cognates as "was bound on" or "fastened," matching the imagery of a yoke and intertwined cords.
- **1:21** — The verb hebeta is literally a Hiphil perfect 2ms ("You have brought"), referring to the day of judgment already brought upon Zion; many grammarians also note it may function as a precative perfect ("Bring the day You announced").
