## Text

**144:1** A praise of David. I will exalt You, my God, the King, and I will bless Your name forever and ever.

**144:2** Each day I will bless You, and I will praise Your name forever and ever.

**144:3** Great is the LORD and exceedingly worthy of praise, and there is no searching out His greatness.

**144:4** Generation to generation will extol Your works, and they will proclaim Your mighty deeds.

**144:5** I will meditate on the splendor of the glory of Your majesty and on the accounts of Your wonders.

**144:6** And they will tell of the power of Your awe-inspiring acts, and I will recount Your greatness.

**144:7** They will pour out the remembrance of Your abundant goodness, and they will sing joyfully of Your righteousness.

**144:8** Gracious and compassionate is the LORD, slow to anger and great in loyal love.

**144:9** Good is the LORD to all, and His compassion is over all His works.

**144:10** All Your works will thank You, LORD, and Your faithful ones will bless You.

**144:11** They will tell of Your kingdom's glory and speak of Your mighty power,

**144:12** to make known to the children of mankind His mighty deeds and the glory of the splendor of His kingdom.

**144:13** Your kingdom is a kingdom of all ages, and Your dominion extends through every generation.

**144:14** The LORD supports all who are falling and lifts upright all who are bent down.

**144:15** The eyes of all look expectantly to You, and You give them their food in its time,

**144:16** opening Your hand and satisfying every living creature's desire.

**144:17** Righteous is the LORD in all His ways, and faithful in all His works.

**144:18** Near is the LORD to all who call to Him, to all who call to Him in truth.

**144:19** He will fulfill the desire of those who fear Him, and will hear their cry for help and save them.

**144:20** The LORD guards all who love Him, but all the wicked He will wipe out.

**144:21** The praise of the LORD my mouth will speak, and may all flesh bless His holy name forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **144:6** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text (ketiv) has plural וגדלותיך ("Your great deeds"), while the read text (qere) translated here has singular וּגְדֻלָּתְךָ ("Your greatness"), agreeing with the 3fs resumptive suffix on the Piel verb אֲסַפְּרֶנָּה ("I will recount it").
- **144:12** — The MT shifts from 2nd-person suffixes in v. 11 to 3rd-person suffixes here (גְּבוּרֹתָיו, "His mighty deeds"; מַלְכוּתוֹ, "His kingdom"); some ancient versions harmonize both to 2nd person ("Your").
- **144:13** — This psalm is an alphabetical acrostic in Hebrew; the MT moves directly from the Mem verse (v. 13) to the Samekh verse (v. 14) with no Nun verse (a Nun verse appears in 11QPsa, LXX, and Syriac).
- **144:16** — The noun רָצוֹן ("desire," cf. v. 19) can also denote God's "favor" or "goodwill," allowing the alternate sense "satisfying every living creature with favor."
