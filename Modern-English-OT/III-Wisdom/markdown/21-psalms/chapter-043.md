## Text

**43:1** For the music leader: of the sons of Korah, a Maskil.

**43:2** God, with our ears we have heard; our fathers have recounted to us the deed You performed in their days, in days of old.

**43:3** You, with Your hand, dispossessed nations, yet You planted them; You afflicted peoples, yet You made them spread out.

**43:4** For they did not possess the land by their sword, nor did their arm save them; rather, it was Your right hand and Your arm and the light of Your face, because You favored them.

**43:5** You Yourself are my King, God; command deliverances for Jacob.

**43:6** By You we will thrust down our foes; in Your name we will trample those who rise against us.

**43:7** For I do not trust in my bow, and my sword will not save me.

**43:8** Instead, You have saved us from our foes, and those who hate us You have put to shame.

**43:9** In God we have gloried all day long, and to Your name we will give thanks forever. Selah.

**43:10** Yet You have rejected and humiliated us, and You do not march out with our armies.

**43:11** You turn us back from a foe, and those who hate us have taken plunder for themselves.

**43:12** You hand us over like sheep for food, and among the nations You have scattered us.

**43:13** You sell Your people for no wealth, and You have not raised their price.

**43:14** You set us as a reproach to our neighbors, a mockery and ridicule to those around us.

**43:15** You set us as a byword among the nations, a shaking of the head among the peoples.

**43:16** All day long my humiliation is before me, and the shame of my face has covered me,

**43:17** at the voice of one who reproaches and reviles, because of an enemy and one seeking vengeance.

**43:18** All of this has come upon us, yet we have not forgotten You, and we have not acted falsely toward Your covenant.

**43:19** Our heart has not drawn back, nor have our steps turned aside from Your path.

**43:20** Yet You have crushed us in the place of jackals, and You have covered us over with deathly shadow.

**43:21** If we had forgotten the name of our God and spread out our palms to a foreign god,

**43:22** would God not search this out? For He knows the hidden things of the heart.

**43:23** Yet on Your account we are put to death all day long; we are reckoned like sheep for slaughter.

**43:24** Wake up! Why do You sleep, Lord? Awaken! Do not reject us forever.

**43:25** Why do You hide Your face, forgetting our affliction and our oppression?

**43:26** For our soul has bowed low to the dust; our belly has clung to the earth.

**43:27** Rise up as a help for us, and redeem us for the sake of Your faithful love.

## Translator Notes

- **43:3** — Hiphil horashta (dispossessed) forms a deliberate wordplay with Qal yaresho (possess) in 43:4; Piel watteshallechem (made them spread out) continues the vine-planting image of wattitta'em (planted them) with Israel as object, though some take it as sent them away referring to the peoples.
- **43:13** — Hebrew belo-hon literally means for no wealth, and Piel ribbitah (increased/raised high) with bimchireihem (in their prices) expresses that God set no high price and sought no gain in selling His people.
- **43:19** — The negative lo in the first clause carries double-duty force into the second clause (nor have our steps turned aside), where the third-person feminine singular wayyiqtol verb wattett (Qal of natah) agrees with feminine ashureinu (our steps).
- **43:20** — Hebrew ki functions adversatively or concessively here (Yet) following the protestation of loyalty in 43:18-19; the Masoretic pointing tsalmawet treats the noun as shadow of death, while the consonantal form likely means deep darkness, captured here by deathly shadow.
- **43:27** — Hebrew ezratah is a poetic lengthened form of ezrah (help) with the emphatic ending -atah, standing directly after the imperative qumah (Rise up).
