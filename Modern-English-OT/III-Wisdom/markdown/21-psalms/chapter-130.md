## Text

**130:1** A song of the ascents. Of David. LORD, my heart is not lofty, and my eyes are not lifted high; nor do I walk in great matters or in things too extraordinary for me.

**130:2** Indeed, I have calmed and stilled my soul, like a weaned child upon his mother; my soul within me is like that weaned child.

**130:3** Hope in the LORD, Israel, from now on and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **130:1** — The verb hillakhti is the Piel stem of halakh ("walk"), expressing habitual conduct or going about, and niphlaot is the Niphal participle of pala ("be extraordinary" or "beyond comprehension").
- **130:2** — The opening im-lo (literally "if not") is an emphatic oath formula asserting what follows; shivviti (Piel of shavah, "level, calm") and domamti (Polel of damam, "still, silence") depict quieting inner restlessness, while alay (literally "upon me") is a Hebrew idiom for "within me" echoing aley immo ("upon his mother").
