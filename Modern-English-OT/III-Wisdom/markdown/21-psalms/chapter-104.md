## Text

**104:1** Give thanks to the LORD, call on His name; make His acts known among the peoples.

**104:2** Sing to Him, make melody to Him; speak about all His marvelous deeds.

**104:3** Boast in His holy name; let the heart of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

**104:4** Seek out the LORD and His strength; seek His face constantly.

**104:5** Remember His marvelous deeds that He performed, His wonders and the judgments of His mouth,

**104:6** offspring of Abraham His servant, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**104:7** He is the LORD our God; throughout all the earth are His judgments.

**104:8** He has remembered His covenant forever, the word He commanded for a thousand generations,

**104:9** which He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac.

**104:10** And He established it for Jacob as a statute, for Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**104:11** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan, the allotment of your inheritance."

**104:12** When they were men few in number, very few, and sojourners in it,

**104:13** they went about from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people.

**104:14** He did not permit anyone to oppress them, and because of them He reproved kings:

**104:15** "Do not touch My anointed ones, and to My prophets do no harm."

**104:16** Then He summoned a famine on the land; He broke every staff of bread.

**104:17** He sent a man ahead of them; Joseph was sold as a slave.

**104:18** They afflicted his foot with a fetter; his soul came into iron.

**104:19** Until the time his word came about, the utterance of the LORD refined him.

**104:20** The king sent and freed him, the ruler of peoples, and released him.

**104:21** He appointed him master of his house and ruler over all his property,

**104:22** to bind his officials at his will, and to make his elders wise.

**104:23** So Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

**104:24** And He made His people exceedingly fruitful, and made them mightier than their adversaries.

**104:25** He turned their heart to hate His people, to plot craftily against His servants.

**104:26** He sent His servant Moses, and Aaron, whom He chose.

**104:27** They performed among them the words of His signs, and wonders in the land of Ham.

**104:28** He sent darkness, and it grew dark; and they did not rebel against His word.

**104:29** He turned their waters into blood and killed their fish.

**104:30** Their land teemed with frogs, in the chambers of their kings.

**104:31** He spoke, and a swarm of flies came, gnats throughout all their territory.

**104:32** He made their rains hail, flaming fire in their land.

**104:33** And He struck their vines and their fig trees, and broke to pieces the trees of their territory.

**104:34** He spoke, and locusts came, and creeping locusts beyond number,

**104:35** and they devoured every plant in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.

**104:36** And He struck every firstborn in their land, the first of all their vigor.

**104:37** And He brought them out with silver and gold, and not one among His tribes stumbled.

**104:38** Egypt rejoiced when they went out, for dread of them had fallen on them.

**104:39** He spread out a cloud for a covering, and fire to illuminate the night.

**104:40** They asked, and He brought quail, and with bread of heaven He satisfied them.

**104:41** He opened the rock, and waters streamed out; they flowed through the dry places as a river.

**104:42** For He remembered His holy word, and Abraham His servant.

**104:43** And He brought out His people with gladness, His chosen ones with joyful singing.

**104:44** And He gave to them the lands of the nations, and they possessed the toil of the peoples,

**104:45** in order that they might keep His statutes and guard His laws. Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **104:18** — The Masoretic read form (qere) is singular ("his foot"), whereas the written form (ketiv) is plural ("his feet"). In the second clause, the feminine Qal perfect verb ("came") takes the feminine noun "his soul" (meaning his person or neck) as its subject, with "iron" as the adverbial accusative of goal.
- **104:22** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads the Qal infinitive "to bind" (rather than "to instruct"), reflecting Joseph's authority over Pharaoh's officials; "at his will" translates the idiom "by his soul/desire."
- **104:28** — The qere (read form) is singular ("His word"), while the ketiv (written form) is plural ("His words"). The Masoretic text explicitly includes the negative particle ("and they did not rebel"), referring either to Moses and Aaron carrying out God's commands or to the plagues obeying His word.
- **104:40** — The Hebrew Qal perfect verb "asked" is grammatically singular, functioning collectively for the people of Israel (rendered "They asked" to agree with the plural pronoun "them" in the second clause).
