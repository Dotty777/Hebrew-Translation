## Text

**73:1** A maskil of Asaph. Why, God, have You cast us off forever? Why does Your anger smoke against the flock of Your pasture?

**73:2** Remember Your congregation, which You acquired of old, which You redeemed as the tribe of Your inheritance, and Mount Zion, in which You dwelt.

**73:3** Lift Your steps toward the perpetual ruins; the enemy has damaged everything in the holy place.

**73:4** Your adversaries roared within Your assembly place; they set their own signs as signs.

**73:5** It appeared like one lifting axes upward in a thicket of trees.

**73:6** And now they smash its carved work altogether with hatchet and hammers.

**73:7** They consigned Your sanctuary to the fire; they profaned the dwelling place of Your name to the ground.

**73:8** They said in their heart, "Let us crush them altogether." They burned down all the assembly places of God in the land.

**73:9** Our own signs we have not seen; there is no longer a prophet, and no one with us knows how long.

**73:10** How long, God, will the adversary taunt? Will the enemy scorn Your name forever?

**73:11** Why do You draw back Your hand and Your right hand? From the midst of Your bosom, consume them!

**73:12** Yet God is my King from of old, performing acts of salvation in the midst of the earth.

**73:13** You parted the sea by Your strength; You shattered the heads of the sea monsters upon the waters.

**73:14** You smashed the heads of Leviathan; You gave him as food to the people of the desert.

**73:15** You broke open spring and torrent; You dried up perennial rivers.

**73:16** The day is Yours, and the night is Yours also; You established the light and the sun.

**73:17** You fixed all the boundaries of the earth; summer and winter, You formed them.

**73:18** Remember this: the enemy has taunted the LORD, and a foolish people have scorned Your name.

**73:19** Do not hand over the soul of Your turtledove to the wild beast; do not forever forget the life of Your afflicted ones.

**73:20** Look to the covenant, for the dark places of the land are filled with dwellings of violence.

**73:21** Do not let the crushed one turn back humiliated; may the afflicted and the needy praise Your name.

**73:22** Arise, God, plead Your cause; remember the taunt against You from a fool all day long.

**73:23** Do not forget the voice of Your adversaries, the tumult of those rising against You, ascending continually.

## Translator Notes

- **73:5** — The Hebrew syntax is terse: yivvada' (Niphal imperfect of yada', "it appeared/was recognized") is followed by kemevi' lema'lah ("like one lifting upward") with plural qardummot ("axes") in a thicket of trees.
- **73:6** — Qere we'attah ("and now") is translated here; Ketiv w't either reflects defective orthography for we'attah or reads we'et ("and a time"). Both kashshil ("hatchet") and kelappot ("hammers") are biblical hapax legomena.
- **73:11** — Qere heqeka ("Your bosom," i.e., the fold of a garment) is translated here; Ketiv hwqk reflects a rare variant spelling hoqeka. The closing imperative kalleh (Piel of kalah, "consume/destroy") forms a pregnant construction implying "draw Your right hand out from Your bosom and consume them."
- **73:14** — Hebrew le'am letsiyyim literally reads "to the people, the desert dwellers"; here 'am ("people") may denote either human desert inhabitants or, poetically, a host of wilderness creatures (compare Proverbs 30:25-26).
- **73:19** — Hebrew hayyat appears twice with wordplay: first with an archaic feminine absolute ending meaning "to the wild beast" (following the Masoretic accents), and second in construct with "Your afflicted ones" meaning "the life of" (or "the community of") Your afflicted ones.
