## Text

**47:1** A song, a psalm of the sons of Korah.

**47:2** The LORD is great and highly to be praised in the city of our God, His holy mountain.

**47:3** Lovely in height, the joy of the entire earth, is Mount Zion, the farthest reaches of the north, the city of the great King.

**47:4** In her citadels, God is known as a stronghold.

**47:5** For look, the kings assembled; they advanced together.

**47:6** They saw, and so they were stunned; they were dismayed, they fled in haste.

**47:7** Trembling gripped them there, agony like that of a woman giving birth.

**47:8** With an east wind You shatter the ships of Tarshish.

**47:9** Just as we heard, so we have seen in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God; God establishes her forever. Selah.

**47:10** We have pondered Your loyal love, God, in the midst of Your temple.

**47:11** Like Your name, God, so is Your praise to the bounds of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness.

**47:12** Let Mount Zion rejoice; let the daughters of Judah exult on account of Your judgments.

**47:13** Go around Zion and encircle her; count her towers.

**47:14** Set your heart on her rampart; pass through her citadels, so that you may recount it to a coming generation.

**47:15** For this God is our God forever and ever; He will lead us even to death.

## Translator Notes

- **47:3** — The noun נוֹף ('height, elevation') is a Hebrew hapax legomenon, and יַרְכְּתֵי צָפוֹן literally means 'the flanks/recesses of the north,' also evoking the northern mountain Zaphon.
- **47:14** — פַּסְּגוּ is a Piel imperative hapax legomenon meaning 'pass through' or 'inspect closely.'
- **47:15** — The Masoretic text divides עַל־מוּת into two words ('to/beyond death,' with מוּת pointed as the infinitive construct); some read it as a single word meaning 'forever' or as a musical direction.
