## Text

**101:1** A prayer for an afflicted one when he grows faint and pours out his lament before the LORD.

**101:2** LORD, hear my prayer, and may my cry for help come to You.

**101:3** Do not hide Your face from me on the day I am in distress; bend Your ear to me; on the day I call, quickly answer me.

**101:4** For my days have vanished in smoke, and my bones are scorched like a hearth.

**101:5** Struck down like grass, my heart has withered, for I have forgotten to eat my bread.

**101:6** From the sound of my groaning, my bone clings to my flesh.

**101:7** I resemble an owl of the wilderness; I have become like a little owl of the ruins.

**101:8** I stay awake, and I have become like a solitary bird on a roof.

**101:9** All day long my enemies reproach me; those who rage against me swear oaths by me.

**101:10** For I eat ashes as bread, and I mix my drink with weeping,

**101:11** because of Your indignation and Your fury, for You lifted me up and flung me away.

**101:12** My days are like a shadow stretched out, and as for me, I wither like grass.

**101:13** Yet You, LORD, sit enthroned forever, and Your remembrance is from generation to generation.

**101:14** You will rise up; You will show compassion to Zion, for it is time to show grace to her, because the set time has arrived.

**101:15** For Your servants delight in her stones, and they pity her dust.

**101:16** Then nations will fear the LORD's name, and all the kings of the earth, Your glory.

**101:17** For the LORD has built Zion; He has been seen in His glory.

**101:18** He has turned toward the prayer of the destitute and has not disdained their prayer.

**101:19** Let this be written for a later generation, and a people to be created will praise the LORD.

**101:20** For He looked down from the height of His holiness; the LORD gazed from heaven toward the earth,

**101:21** to hear the groaning of a prisoner, to unbind those doomed to death,

**101:22** so that they may recount in Zion the LORD's name, and His praise in Jerusalem,

**101:23** when peoples are assembled together, and kingdoms, to serve the LORD.

**101:24** Along the way He has weakened my strength; He has cut short my days.

**101:25** I say, "My God, do not carry me off in the middle of my days; Your years are through generation after generation."

**101:26** Long ago You founded the earth, and heaven is the work of Your hands.

**101:27** They will perish, yet You will stand firm; and all of them will wear away like a garment; like a robe You will replace them, and they will pass away.

**101:28** Yet You are the same, and Your years will not end.

**101:29** The children of Your servants will abide, and before You their seed will be established.

## Translator Notes

- **101:7** — The Hebrew bird names קָאַת (possibly a pelican or a desert owl) and כּוֹס (little owl) designate unclean birds associated with desolate places, and their exact ornithological identification remains uncertain.
- **101:9** — The participle מְהוֹלָלַי (Poal of הלל) literally refers to those maddened or raging against the speaker, while בִּי נִשְׁבָּעוּ (literally, swear by me) is an idiom for using someone's misery as a curse formula.
- **101:21** — Literally, sons of death (בְּנֵי תְמוּתָה), a Hebrew idiom denoting people sentenced or doomed to die.
- **101:24** — Ketiv reads כחו (his strength), whereas Qere reads כֹּחִי (my strength); the translation follows Qere, matching the first-person my days in the parallel clause.
- **101:27** — The verse uses a wordplay on the root חלף, shifting from the Hiphil תַּחֲלִיפֵם (You will replace or change them) to the Qal וְיַחֲלֹפוּ (and they will pass away).
