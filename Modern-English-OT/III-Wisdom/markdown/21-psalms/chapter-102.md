## Text

**102:1** Of David. Bless the LORD, my soul, and all my inward parts, His holy name.

**102:2** Bless the LORD, my soul, and do not forget any of His benefits.

**102:3** He is the One who pardons all your iniquity, who heals all your illnesses,

**102:4** who redeems your life out of the pit, who crowns you with faithful love and compassion,

**102:5** who satisfies your lifetime with good, so that your youth renews itself like the eagle's.

**102:6** The LORD carries out acts of righteousness and judgments for all who are oppressed.

**102:7** He made His ways known to Moses; to the sons of Israel, His deeds.

**102:8** Compassionate and gracious is the LORD, slow to anger and great in faithful love.

**102:9** He will not contend perpetually, nor will He retain anger forever.

**102:10** Not according to our sins has He dealt with us, and not according to our iniquities has He repaid us.

**102:11** For as high as heaven is above the earth, so mighty is His faithful love over those who fear Him.

**102:12** As far as east is from west, He has put our transgressions far from us.

**102:13** Just as a father feels compassion for children, so the LORD feels compassion for those who fear Him.

**102:14** For He Himself knows our form, mindful that we are dust.

**102:15** As for a mortal, his days are like grass; like a blossom of the field, so he blossoms.

**102:16** For a wind passes over it, and it is no more, and its place no longer recognizes it.

**102:17** Yet the LORD's faithful love is from eternity to eternity upon those who fear Him, and His righteousness is for children's children,

**102:18** for those who keep His covenant and for those who remember His precepts to carry them out.

**102:19** In the heavens the LORD has established His throne, and His kingdom rules over everything.

**102:20** Bless the LORD, His angels, mighty in power, who carry out His word, heeding the voice of His word.

**102:21** Bless the LORD, all His hosts, His attendants who carry out His will.

**102:22** Bless the LORD, all His works, in all the places of His rule. Bless the LORD, my soul.

## Translator Notes

- **102:3** — Verses 3 through 5 employ the poetic second-person feminine singular pronominal suffix -î (as in עֲוֺנֵכִי and תַּחֲלוּאָיְכִי), addressing the psalmist's feminine soul (נַפְשִׁי).
- **102:5** — The noun עֶדְיֵךְ is lexically difficult; it normally means ornament or finery, but in parallel with your youth it is widely understood as lifetime or existence.
- **102:9** — The Qal verb יִטּוֹר (from נטר, keep or guard) is used absolutely in Hebrew as an idiom for retaining anger or holding a grudge.
- **102:14** — The noun יִצְרֵנוּ (our form, from יצר, to form) recalls humanity's shaping from dust, and זָכוּר is a Qal passive participle of זכר used statively with an active sense, mindful.
