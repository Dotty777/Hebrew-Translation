## Text

**116:1** All nations, praise the LORD; all the peoples, acclaim Him!

**116:2** For His loyal love has prevailed over us, and the faithfulness of the LORD is forever. Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **116:1** — 'Acclaim Him' translates the Piel imperative of shabach, a poetic synonym for praise prominent in Aramaic, and Hebrew uses the definite article with 'the peoples' in contrast to anarthrous 'nations.'
- **116:2** — 'Has prevailed' translates the Qal perfect of gabar ('be mighty' or 'prevail'), and the second clause is verbless in Hebrew, literally 'and the faithfulness of the LORD forever.'
