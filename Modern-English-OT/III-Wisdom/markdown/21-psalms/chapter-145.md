## Text

**145:1** Praise the LORD! My soul, praise the LORD!

**145:2** I will praise the LORD throughout my life; I will make music to my God as long as I exist.

**145:3** Do not trust in nobles, in a human being, with whom there is no salvation.

**145:4** His breath goes out, he returns to his ground; on that day his plans perish.

**145:5** Happy is the one who has the God of Jacob as his help, whose hope is upon the LORD his God,

**145:6** the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea and everything that is in them, who keeps truth forever,

**145:7** who carries out justice for the oppressed, who gives bread to the hungry. The LORD releases those who are bound.

**145:8** The LORD opens the eyes of the blind; the LORD lifts upright those who are bent down; the LORD loves the righteous.

**145:9** The LORD guards the resident foreigners; He sustains the orphan and the widow, but the way of the wicked He subverts.

**145:10** The LORD will reign forever, your God, Zion, from generation to generation. Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **145:4** — The wordplay between בֶּן־אָדָם ("a human being," v. 3) and אֲדָמָה ("ground," v. 4) recalls humanity's return to the soil. The noun עֶשְׁתֹּנֹתָיו ("his plans") is a biblical hapax legomenon of Aramaic origin.
- **145:8** — Literally "opens the blind"; the Qal verb פקח is used specifically of opening eyes (and rarely ears), so English supplies "the eyes of."
- **145:9** — The rare Polel imperfect יְעוֹדֵד ("He sustains," from עוד) contrasts with the Piel imperfect יְעַוֵּת ("He subverts," from עות, literally "make crooked").
