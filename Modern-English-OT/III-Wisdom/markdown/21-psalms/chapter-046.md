## Text

**46:1** For the music director, of the sons of Korah; a psalm.

**46:2** All peoples, clap your hands; shout aloud to God with a voice of jubilation.

**46:3** For the LORD, the Most High, is awesome—a great King over the whole earth.

**46:4** He subdues peoples beneath us, and nations beneath our feet.

**46:5** He chooses for us our inheritance, the pride of Jacob, whom He has loved. Selah.

**46:6** God has ascended amid a shout, the LORD with the sound of a ram's horn.

**46:7** Make music to God, make music; make music to our King, make music.

**46:8** For God is King of all the earth; make music with a Maskil.

**46:9** God reigns as King over the nations; God sits upon the throne of His holiness.

**46:10** The nobles of the peoples have gathered together as the people of the God of Abraham; for to God belong the shields of the earth; He is exceedingly exalted.

## Translator Notes

- **46:4** — The rare Hiphil verb yadber, from dbr II meaning to subdue or bring under as in Psalm 18:48 MT, is distinct from the common Piel verb meaning to speak.
- **46:8** — Make music renders the Piel imperative of zmr, repeated four times in verse 7 and cognate to psalm in verse 1. Maskil, as in 44:1 (MT 45:1), may denote the psalm genre or serve adverbially as skillfully.
- **46:10** — The Masoretic Text reads am, as the people of the God of Abraham, whereas the Septuagint and Syriac vocalize the consonants as im, with the God of Abraham. Shields of the earth is a metaphor for earthly rulers and defenders.
