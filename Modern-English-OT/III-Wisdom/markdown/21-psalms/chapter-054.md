## Text

**54:1** For the music director, with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.

**54:2** Listen closely, God, to my prayer, and do not conceal Yourself from my plea for favor.

**54:3** Pay attention to me and answer me; I roam restlessly in my lament and groan aloud,

**54:4** because of an enemy's voice, from the oppression of a wicked man; for they make trouble fall upon me, and in anger they harbor animosity against me.

**54:5** My heart writhes inside me, and terrors of death have fallen upon me.

**54:6** Fear and trembling come into me, and shuddering covers me.

**54:7** And I said, "Who will give me wings like a dove? I would fly away and settle down."

**54:8** Look, I would wander far off; I would spend the night in the wilderness. Selah.

**54:9** I would hurry to a place of escape for myself from a rushing wind, from a storm.

**54:10** Confound, Lord, divide their language, for I have seen violence and conflict in the city.

**54:11** Day and night they circle around it upon its walls, and iniquity and misery are inside it.

**54:12** Ruin is inside it, and oppression and deceit do not depart from its public square.

**54:13** For it is not an enemy who insults me—then I could endure it; it is not someone who hates me who has magnified himself against me—then I could hide from him.

**54:14** Yet it is you, a man of my own rank, my close companion and my intimate friend,

**54:15** we who together used to hold sweet counsel; in the house of God we used to walk among the throng.

**54:16** May death come unawares upon them; may they descend to Sheol alive, for evils are in their lodging, inside them.

**54:17** As for me, I will call out to God, and the LORD will save me.

**54:18** Evening and morning and noon, I will lament and moan, and He will hear my voice.

**54:19** He has redeemed my life in peace from the battle against me, for in great numbers they were against me.

**54:20** God will hear and answer them—He who sits enthroned from ancient times, Selah—those for whom there are no changes and who do not fear God.

**54:21** He has stretched out his hands against those at peace with him; he has profaned his covenant.

**54:22** Smooth were the buttery utterances of his mouth, yet battle was in his heart; softer than oil were his words, yet they were unsheathed swords.

**54:23** Cast your load upon the LORD, and He Himself will sustain you; never will He permit the righteous to totter.

**54:24** But You, God, will bring them down into the well of destruction; men of bloodshed and deceit will not reach half their days. Yet as for me, I will trust in You.

## Translator Notes

- **54:3** — The rare Hiphil verb 'arid (from rud) denotes restless roaming or agitation, paired with the Hiphil cohortative 'ahimah (from hum, 'be in turmoil, groan aloud').
- **54:16** — The Qere reads two words, yashi mavet (defective Hiphil jussive of nasha' II, 'deceive/come unawares,' plus 'death'), whereas the Ketiv writes a single plural noun yeshimot ('desolations').
- **54:19** — Literally 'for in/as many they were with me' (beth essentiae with rabbim and 'immadi), where 'with me' in a battle context carries the adversarial sense 'engaged against me,' though some interpret it as 'on my side.'
- **54:20** — MT vocalizes veya'anem as Qal of 'anah I ('and answer them,' i.e., in judgment), whereas some ancient versions read Piel ('humble/afflict them'). Literally 'there are no changes for them' (chalifot) indicates that they do not reform or experience humbling reversals.
- **54:22** — MT vocalizes a plural verb chalqu with the hapax plural noun machama'ot ('buttery utterances/things of his mouth'), whereas revocalizing the noun as a comparative would give 'smoother than butter,' parallel to 'softer than oil.'
