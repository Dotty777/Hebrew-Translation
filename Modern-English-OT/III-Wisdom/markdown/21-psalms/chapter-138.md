## Text

**138:1** For the music director. Of David. A psalm. LORD, You have examined me, and You know me.

**138:2** You know my sitting down and my rising up; You perceive my thought from a distance.

**138:3** You sift my path and my lying down, and You are familiar with all my ways.

**138:4** For there is not a word on my tongue; see, LORD, You know all of it.

**138:5** Behind and in front You have hemmed me in, and You have set Your hand upon me.

**138:6** Such knowledge is too marvelous for me; it is set high; I cannot reach it.

**138:7** Where could I go from Your Spirit? And where from Your presence could I flee?

**138:8** If I ascend to the heavens, there You are; and if I spread my bed in Sheol, look, You are there.

**138:9** If I lift up the wings of the dawn, if I settle at the farthest end of the sea,

**138:10** even there Your hand will guide me, and Your right hand will grasp me.

**138:11** And if I say, "Surely darkness will engulf me, and the light around me will be night,"

**138:12** even darkness is not dark before You, and night shines like the day; as darkness is, so is light.

**138:13** For You created my innermost parts; You wove me together in the womb of my mother.

**138:14** I will give thanks to You because I am awesomely and marvelously formed; Your works are marvelous, and my soul knows it thoroughly.

**138:15** My bones were not hidden from You when I was fashioned in secret, embroidered in the lowest parts of the earth.

**138:16** Your eyes saw my unformed substance, and in Your book all of them were written; days were formed, and to Him belongs one among them.

**138:17** And to me, how precious are Your thoughts, God! How immense are their totals!

**138:18** Were I to count them, they would be more numerous than the sand; I awaken, and still I am with You.

**138:19** If only You would kill the wicked, God! And men of bloodshed, turn away from me!

**138:20** For they speak of You with malicious intent; Your adversaries take up Your name in vain.

**138:21** LORD, do I not hate those who hate You, and detest those who rise against You?

**138:22** With utter hatred I hate them; they have become enemies to me.

**138:23** Examine me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my troubled thoughts;

**138:24** and see if there is any way of pain in me, and guide me in the eternal way.

## Translator Notes

- **138:3** — The Piel verb 'zerita' (from z-r-h, 'winnow' or 'sift,' or a homonym meaning 'measure out') is used metaphorically for close scrutiny of the psalmist's movement and rest.
- **138:6** — Translates the Qere 'peli'ah' ('too marvelous/wonderful'); the Ketiv 'pl'yh' represents an alternate feminine adjective formation with the same meaning.
- **138:11** — The rare Qal verb 'yeshufeni' (from sh-w-f) means 'bruise' or 'crush' in Genesis 3:15 and Job 9:17, and here conveys darkness overwhelming or engulfing the speaker.
- **138:16** — Translates the Qere 'we-lo' ('and to Him [belongs] one among them,' or 'for it' referring to 'my unformed substance'); the Ketiv reads 'we-lo'' ('and not'), yielding 'when there was not yet one of them.' The noun 'golem' ('unformed substance') occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **138:20** — The Masoretic wording is terse: 'yomrukha' is a shortened Qal form of 'yo'mrukha' ('speak of You'), 'nasu'' reflects Qal perfect 'nas'u' ('take up,' with 'Your name' implied from the idiom of Exodus 20:7), and 'arekha' means 'Your adversaries' (cf. 1 Samuel 28:16).
