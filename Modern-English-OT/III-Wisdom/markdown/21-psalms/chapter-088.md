## Text

**88:1** A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.

**88:2** Of the LORD's acts of loyal love I will sing forever; to every generation I will make Your faithfulness known with my mouth.

**88:3** For I have said, "Forever loyal love will be built up; as for the heavens, You establish Your faithfulness in them."

**88:4** "I have cut a covenant with My chosen one; I have sworn an oath to My servant David,

**88:5** 'Forever I will establish your seed, and I will build up your throne to every generation.'" Selah.

**88:6** And the heavens will praise Your wonder, LORD, indeed Your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones.

**88:7** For who in the skies compares with the LORD? Who resembles the LORD among the divine beings?

**88:8** God is greatly revered in the council of the holy ones, and awe-inspiring above all who surround Him.

**88:9** LORD, God of hosts, who is mighty like You, LORD? And Your faithfulness is all around You.

**88:10** You rule over the surging of the sea; when its waves lift up, You calm them.

**88:11** You crushed Rahab like one killed; with the arm of Your strength You scattered Your enemies.

**88:12** Yours are the heavens, and Yours too is the earth; the world and its fullness, You founded them.

**88:13** North and south, You created them; Tabor and Hermon ring out with joy at Your name.

**88:14** Yours is an arm with might; Your hand is strong, and Your right hand is lifted high.

**88:15** Righteousness and justice are the base of Your throne; loyal love and truth go before Your face.

**88:16** Blessed are the people who know the shout of joy; LORD, in the light of Your face they walk.

**88:17** In Your name they exult all day long, and by Your righteousness they are lifted high.

**88:18** For You are the splendor of their strength, and by Your favor our horn is lifted high.

**88:19** For to the LORD belongs our shield, and to the Holy One of Israel belongs our king.

**88:20** Then You spoke in a vision to Your loyal ones and said, "I have placed help upon a mighty warrior; I have lifted high a chosen one from the people.

**88:21** I have found My servant David; I have anointed him with My holy oil,

**88:22** with whom My hand will remain established; indeed, My arm will make him strong.

**88:23** No enemy will exact tribute from him, nor will a son of injustice afflict him.

**88:24** And I will smash his adversaries before his face, and those who hate him I will strike down.

**88:25** And My faithfulness and My loyal love will be with him, and in My name his horn will be lifted high.

**88:26** And I will place his hand upon the sea, and his right hand upon the rivers.

**88:27** He will call out to Me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.'

**88:28** And I too will appoint him as firstborn, the highest above the kings of the earth.

**88:29** Forever I will keep My loyal love for him, and My covenant will remain faithful to him.

**88:30** And I will set his seed for all time, and his throne like the days of the heavens.

**88:31** If his sons abandon My law and do not walk in My ordinances,

**88:32** if they profane My statutes and do not keep My commands,

**88:33** then I will punish their rebellion with a rod and their iniquity with blows.

**88:34** Yet I will not revoke My loyal love from him, nor will I prove false to My faithfulness.

**88:35** I will not profane My covenant, nor will I change what has come from My lips.

**88:36** Once I swore by My holiness: I will surely not lie to David.

**88:37** His seed will continue forever, and his throne like the sun before Me.

**88:38** Like the moon it will stand established forever, and the witness in the skies is faithful." Selah.

**88:39** Yet You have spurned and rejected; You have been furious with Your anointed.

**88:40** You have repudiated the covenant of Your servant; You have profaned his crown to the ground.

**88:41** You have broken through all his walls; You have turned his fortresses into ruin.

**88:42** All who pass by on the road have plundered him; he has become a taunt to his neighbors.

**88:43** You have lifted high the right hand of his adversaries; You have caused all his enemies to rejoice.

**88:44** Furthermore, You turn back the blade of his sword, and You have not kept him standing in the battle.

**88:45** You have caused his luster to cease, and his throne You have hurled to the ground.

**88:46** You have shortened his days of youth; You have wrapped shame over him. Selah.

**88:47** How long, LORD? Will You remain hidden forever? Will Your wrath blaze like fire?

**88:48** Remember, as for me, how brief life is; for what futility You have created all the sons of man!

**88:49** Who is the man who will live and not see death, who will rescue his soul from the hand of Sheol? Selah.

**88:50** Where are Your former acts of loyal love, Lord, which You swore to David in Your faithfulness?

**88:51** Remember, Lord, the taunt against Your servants, how I carry in my bosom that of all the many peoples,

**88:52** with which Your enemies have taunted, LORD, with which they have taunted the footsteps of Your anointed.

**88:53** May the LORD be blessed forever! Amen and Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **88:7** — Hebrew bene elim ("divine beings") literally means "sons of gods" or "sons of mighty ones," a poetic idiom for members of God's heavenly council (parallel to "the holy ones" in 88:6, 8 [MT 89:6, 8]).
- **88:18** — The qere tarum (Qal imperfect 3fs, "our horn is lifted high") is translated here; the ketiv reads a Hiphil imperfect 2ms ("You lift high our horn").
- **88:23** — Hebrew yashi with the preposition be (nasha I, Hiphil) denotes a creditor making exactions ("exact tribute from him"), though some derive it from nasha II ("deceive" or "outwit").
- **88:29** — The ketiv and qere differ only orthographically (plene versus defective spelling before maqqef); both are the Qal imperfect 1cs of shamar ("I will keep").
- **88:45** — Hebrew mitteharo ("his luster") is a difficult form, parsed either as the preposition min plus tohar ("luster, brightness, purity") or as a rare prefixed noun from the same root.
- **88:51** — The Hebrew clause is compressed and unusual in word order, literally reading "my bearing in my bosom all many peoples," carrying over "taunt" from the first half of the verse by ellipsis.
