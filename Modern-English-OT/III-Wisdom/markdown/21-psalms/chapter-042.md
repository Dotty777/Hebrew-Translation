## Text

**42:1** Vindicate me, God, and argue my case against an unfaithful nation; rescue me from a man of deceit and injustice.

**42:2** For You are the God of my stronghold; why have You rejected me? Why must I walk about in gloom because of the oppression of an enemy?

**42:3** Send forth Your light and Your truth; may they guide me; may they bring me to Your holy mountain and to Your dwelling places.

**42:4** Then I will come to the altar of God, to God, the gladness of my rejoicing; and I will give thanks to You with the lyre, God, my God.

**42:5** Why are you bowed down, my soul, and why are you groaning within me? Wait in hope for God, for I will yet give thanks to Him, the deliverance of my face and my God.

## Translator Notes

- **42:2** — Hebrew ethallekh uses the iterative/reflexive Hithpael stem of halakh (walk about), distinguishing this lament from the Qal verb elekh (go) in the parallel verse at 41:10 (MT 42:10).
- **42:4** — Hebrew simchat gili (literally the gladness of my rejoicing) is a construct phrase combining two near-synonymous nouns for superlative emphasis (my highest joy).
