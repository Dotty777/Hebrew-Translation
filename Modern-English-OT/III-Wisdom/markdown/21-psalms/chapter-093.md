## Text

**93:1** God of vengeance, LORD, God of vengeance, shine out!

**93:2** Lift Yourself up, Judge of the earth; bring back recompense upon the arrogant.

**93:3** How long will the wicked, LORD, how long will the wicked triumph?

**93:4** They pour out, they speak insolence; all doers of iniquity boast.

**93:5** Your people, LORD, they crush, and Your inheritance they oppress.

**93:6** Widow and sojourner they kill, and orphans they murder.

**93:7** And they say, "The LORD does not see, and the God of Jacob does not pay attention."

**93:8** Pay attention, you brutish ones among the people; and you fools, when will you gain insight?

**93:9** The One who planted the ear, will He not hear? Or the One who shaped the eye, will He not look?

**93:10** The One who disciplines nations, will He not reprove—the One who teaches mankind knowledge?

**93:11** The LORD knows the thoughts of mankind, that they are vapor.

**93:12** How blessed is the man whom You discipline, LORD, and whom You teach from Your instruction,

**93:13** to grant him quiet from days of adversity, until a pit is dug for the wicked one.

**93:14** For the LORD will not desert His people, and His inheritance He will not forsake.

**93:15** For justice will return to righteousness, and after it will follow all who are upright of heart.

**93:16** Who will rise up for me against those who do evil? Who will stand firm for me against doers of iniquity?

**93:17** Had the LORD not been a help to me, my soul would soon have dwelt in silence.

**93:18** When I said, "My foot has slipped," Your loyal love, LORD, sustained me.

**93:19** In the abundance of my anxious thoughts within me, Your comforts gladden my soul.

**93:20** Can a throne of ruin be joined with You, one that shapes trouble by statute?

**93:21** They band together against the life of a righteous person, and innocent blood they pronounce guilty.

**93:22** Yet the LORD has become a fortress to me, and my God the rock of my shelter.

**93:23** And He has brought back upon them their iniquity, and through their wickedness He will cut them off; the LORD our God will cut them off.

## Translator Notes

- **93:4** — "Boast" renders the rare Hithpael of amar ("speak"), used reflexively here in the sense of vaunting or talking arrogantly of oneself.
- **93:7** — Here (and in v. 12) the Hebrew uses Yah, the poetic shortened form of the divine name, rendered "the LORD."
- **93:10** — The final participial clause ("the One who teaches mankind knowledge") has no explicit interrogative predicate in the Hebrew, functioning either in apposition to the opening subject or as an elliptical question answered immediately in v. 11.
- **93:17** — "Silence" (dumah) is a poetic Hebrew designation for the grave or the realm of the dead.
- **93:20** — "Be joined with You" renders an unusually vocalized form of chabar ("be allied, joined"), and the phrase aley-choq ("by statute") can mean either "under the cover of a legal decree" or "contrary to statute."
