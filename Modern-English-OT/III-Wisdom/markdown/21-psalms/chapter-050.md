## Text

**50:1** For the music director. A psalm of David.

**50:2** When the prophet Nathan came to him, after he went in to Bathsheba.

**50:3** Show me grace, God, according to Your loyal love; according to the abundance of Your compassion, wipe away my transgressions.

**50:4** Wash me fully from my iniquity, and purify me from my sin.

**50:5** For I myself know my transgressions, and my sin is continually before me.

**50:6** Against You, You alone, I have sinned, and I have done what is evil in Your eyes, so that You are in the right when You speak, and without fault when You judge.

**50:7** Look, in iniquity I was brought forth, and my mother conceived me in sin.

**50:8** Look, You delight in truth in the inward parts, and in what is hidden You make me know wisdom.

**50:9** Cleanse me from sin with hyssop, and I will be pure; wash me, and I will become whiter than snow.

**50:10** Make me hear gladness and joy; may the bones You have crushed exult.

**50:11** Hide Your face from my sins, and wipe away all my iniquities.

**50:12** Create a pure heart for me, God, and renew a firm spirit within me.

**50:13** Do not throw me out from Your presence, and do not take away Your Holy Spirit from me.

**50:14** Bring back to me the gladness of Your salvation, and may a willing spirit uphold me.

**50:15** I will instruct transgressors in Your ways, and sinners will turn back to You.

**50:16** Rescue me from bloodguilt, God, God of my salvation; my tongue will shout joyfully of Your righteousness.

**50:17** Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will proclaim Your praise.

**50:18** For You do not delight in a sacrifice, or else I would give one; You find no pleasure in a burnt offering.

**50:19** God's sacrifices are a broken spirit; a broken and crushed heart, God, You will not scorn.

**50:20** In Your good pleasure, do good to Zion; build Jerusalem's walls.

**50:21** Then You will delight in sacrifices of righteousness, burnt offering and whole offering; then they will offer up bulls on Your altar.

## Translator Notes

- **50:4** — Ketiv writes the Hiphil infinitive absolute harbeh, while Qere reads the shortened Hiphil imperative herev from rabah, meaning "multiply"; here it functions adverbially with the following imperative to express thorough or repeated washing.
- **50:8** — The rare plural noun tuhot, translated "inward parts," occurs only here and in Job 38:36; it likely derives from the root tuah, meaning "cover over," and denotes the hidden inner self.
- **50:9** — The verb translated "Cleanse me from sin" is a privative Piel of hata, "to sin," meaning "to de-sin" or purify ritually from sin, as in purification rites with hyssop in Numbers 19:19.
- **50:14** — The verb tismekeni can be parsed either as third feminine singular with "a willing spirit" as subject, translated here, or as second masculine singular addressed to God with "a willing spirit" as an instrumental accusative.
