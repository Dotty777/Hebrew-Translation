## Text

**128:1** A song of the ascents. "Often they have harassed me since my youth," let Israel now say:

**128:2** "Often they have harassed me since my youth, yet they have not overpowered me.

**128:3** On my back plowers plowed; they lengthened their furrow.

**128:4** The LORD is righteous; He has severed the rope of the wicked."

**128:5** Let all who hate Zion be disgraced and turned back.

**128:6** Let them be like grass on rooftops, which dries up before it shoots up,

**128:7** with which no reaper fills his hand, nor a sheaf-gatherer his arms;

**128:8** and passersby do not say, "The LORD's blessing be upon you; we bless you in the name of the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **128:3** — The qere reads the singular lemaanitam ("their furrow"), whereas the ketiv reads the plural ("their furrows"); the preposition le- marks the direct object of the Hiphil verb heerikhu ("they lengthened").
- **128:6** — The Qal verb shalaph normally means "draw out" or "pull up"; here with the rare conjunction qadmat ("before") it is taken either intransitively ("before it shoots up") or with an indefinite subject ("before one pulls it up").
- **128:7** — The noun chitsno ("his arms") literally denotes the bosom or fold of a garment where cut stalks are cradled, and meammer ("sheaf-gatherer") is a Piel participle occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible.
