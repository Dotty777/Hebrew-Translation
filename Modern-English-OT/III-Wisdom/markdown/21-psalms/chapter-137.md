## Text

**137:1** Of David. I will give thanks to You with my whole heart; in the presence of the gods I will sing praise to You.

**137:2** I will bow down toward Your holy temple and give thanks to Your name because of Your loyal love and Your truth, for You have made Your word great above all Your name.

**137:3** In the day I called out, You answered me; You emboldened me in my soul with strength.

**137:4** LORD, all the kings of the earth will give thanks to You, because they have heard the words of Your mouth.

**137:5** And they will sing of the LORD's ways, because the glory of the LORD is great.

**137:6** For the LORD is high, yet He looks on the lowly, and He knows the haughty from a distance.

**137:7** Though I walk in the midst of distress, You will keep me alive; against the anger of my enemies You will stretch out Your hand, and Your right hand will save me.

**137:8** The LORD will bring it to completion on my behalf; LORD, Your loyal love endures forever; do not abandon the works of Your hands.

## Translator Notes

- **137:1** — The Hebrew 'elohim' here denotes 'the gods' (or heavenly beings; some take it as human judges or rulers). Unlike the Septuagint, the Masoretic text does not include the divine name 'LORD' in this verse.
- **137:2** — Masoretic accentuation treats 'above all Your name' as a single prepositional phrase and 'Your word' as the direct object of the Hiphil verb 'higdalta' ('You have made great'), with no conjunction linking 'name' and 'word.'
- **137:6** — The form 'yeyeda' is an unusual Masoretic spelling and pointing of the Qal imperfect of 'yada' ('know') with a double yod.
- **137:8** — The idiom 'yigmor ba'adi' literally means 'will complete on my behalf,' with no explicit object expressed; 'ma'asei yadekha' ('the works of Your hands') is plural in the Masoretic text.
