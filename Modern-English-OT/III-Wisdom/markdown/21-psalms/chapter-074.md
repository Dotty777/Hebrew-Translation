## Text

**74:1** For the music director: "Do Not Destroy." A psalm of Asaph, a song.

**74:2** We give thanks to You, God; we give thanks, and Your name is near; people recount Your marvelous works.

**74:3** "When I take an appointed time, I Myself will judge with uprightness.

**74:4** The earth and all its inhabitants are melting; I Myself have steadied its pillars." Selah.

**74:5** I said to those who boast, "Do not boast," and to the wicked, "Do not raise a horn.

**74:6** Do not raise your horn on high; do not speak with a defiant neck."

**74:7** For raising up does not come from the east or from the west, nor from the wilderness;

**74:8** rather, God is judge: one He brings low, and another He raises up.

**74:9** For a cup is in the LORD's hand, and the wine foams, full of a spiced mixture, and He pours from it; indeed, all the wicked of the earth will drain out and drink its dregs.

**74:10** But I will proclaim it forever; I will sing praise to the God of Jacob.

**74:11** And I will cut off all the horns of the wicked, while the horns of the righteous will be raised high.

## Translator Notes

- **74:2** — The Masoretic verb rendered "people recount" is an impersonal third-person plural Piel perfect of the verb meaning to tell or recount, literally "they recount Your marvelous works."
- **74:6** — The negative particle "do not" from the first clause extends poetically to the second clause. The final phrase is literally "with a defiant neck," though the word rendered "defiant" elsewhere serves as a noun meaning "insolence," allowing "speak insolence with outstretched neck."
- **74:7** — The final Hebrew word can be parsed either as a Hiphil infinitive of the verb meaning to raise up used substantively ("raising up"), echoing verses 5, 6, and 8, or as the plural noun "mountains" forming a construct phrase "wilderness of mountains" with the preceding word.
- **74:9** — The noun rendered "spiced mixture" denotes a blend of wine and aromatic spices; the two Qal imperfect verbs "drain out and drink" stand side by side without a conjunction for emphasis.
