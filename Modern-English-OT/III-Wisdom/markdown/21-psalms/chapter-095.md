## Text

**95:1** Sing a new song to the LORD; sing to the LORD, all the earth.

**95:2** Sing to the LORD, bless His name; proclaim the good news of His salvation from day to day.

**95:3** Recount His glory among the nations, His wondrous deeds among all the peoples.

**95:4** For the LORD is great and highly to be praised; He is to be feared beyond all gods.

**95:5** For all the gods of the peoples are empty idols, yet the LORD made the heavens.

**95:6** Majesty and splendor are before Him; might and beauty are in His sanctuary.

**95:7** Ascribe to the LORD, clans of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and might.

**95:8** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name; carry an offering and come into His courtyards.

**95:9** Bow down to the LORD in the splendor of holiness; quake at His presence, all the earth.

**95:10** Say among the nations, "The LORD reigns! Indeed, the world is established; it will not be shaken. He will judge the peoples with uprightness."

**95:11** Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth exult; let the sea and its fullness roar.

**95:12** Let the field rejoice, and all that is in it; then all the trees of the forest will ring out in joy

**95:13** in the presence of the LORD, for He is coming, for He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with His faithfulness.

## Translator Notes

- **95:5** — The Hebrew pairs elohim ("gods") with elilim ("empty idols"), a wordplay contrasting the living God with false deities characterized as worthless or nothing.
- **95:9** — The construct phrase hadrat-qodesh ("the splendor of holiness") uses a noun from the same root as "splendor" in 95:6 (MT 96:6) and can refer either to the LORD's own holy majesty or to the sacred attire worn by worshipers.
- **95:13** — The form ba ("is coming") is identical in vocalization for both the Qal perfect and the Qal participle of bo, expressing the LORD's imminent arrival; the doubled ki ba emphasizes certainty.
