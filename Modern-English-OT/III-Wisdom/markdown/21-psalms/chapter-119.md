## Text

**119:1** A song of the ascents. To the LORD I called in my distress, and He answered me.

**119:2** LORD, rescue my soul from lips of falsehood, from a tongue of deceit.

**119:3** What will He give to you, and what will He add to you, tongue of deceit?

**119:4** Sharpened arrows of a warrior, with live coals of broom trees.

**119:5** Woe to me, because I have sojourned in Meshech; I have dwelt beside the tents of Kedar.

**119:6** Long enough has my soul dwelt with one who hates peace.

**119:7** I am peace, yet when I speak, they are for war.

## Translator Notes

- **119:3** — The third-person masculine singular verbs (Qal yitten, "give," and Hiphil yosif, "add") have no explicit subject, referring either to the LORD from verse 2 (echoing the Hebrew oath formula) or to an indefinite subject, "What will be given to you, and what will be added to you."
- **119:5** — Meshech (in northern Asia Minor) and Kedar (an Arabian nomadic tribe) represent distant, hostile regions; Hebrew uses the unique form oyah (for oy, "woe") and the Qal verb gur ("sojourn as a resident alien").
- **119:7** — Hebrew reads literally "I am peace" (a verbless nominal clause without a preposition), contrasting sharply with the final clause, literally "they are for the war."
