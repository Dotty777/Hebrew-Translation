## Text

**56:1** For the music director: "Do Not Destroy." A Miktam of David, when he fled from before Saul in the cave.

**56:2** Show me grace, God, show me grace, for in You my soul has taken refuge; and in the shadow of Your wings I will take refuge until calamities pass by.

**56:3** I call out to God Most High, to God who brings it to completion for me.

**56:4** He will send from heaven and save me; He puts to shame the one who tramples on me. Selah. God will send His loyal love and His truth.

**56:5** My soul is in the midst of lions; I lie down among fiery ones, sons of men whose teeth are spears and arrows, and whose tongue is a sharp sword.

**56:6** Be exalted above the heavens, God; may Your glory be above all the earth.

**56:7** They made a net ready for my steps; my soul bowed low. They dug a pit in front of me; they fell into its midst. Selah.

**56:8** My heart is firm, God, my heart is firm; I will sing and make music.

**56:9** Wake up, my glory! Wake up, harp and lyre! I will wake the dawn.

**56:10** I will give thanks to You among the peoples, Lord; I will make music to You among the nations.

**56:11** For Your loyal love is great up to the heavens, and Your truth up to the skies.

**56:12** Be exalted above the heavens, God; may Your glory be above all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **56:4** — The Piel perfect cheref ("puts to shame" or "reproaches") is taken with God as subject and the Qal participle sho'afi ("the one who tramples on me" or "pants after me") as object; alternatively, the clause may be read circumstantially as "though the one who tramples on me taunts."
- **56:5** — The Qal active participle lohatim literally means "blazing ones" or "fiery ones," used metaphorically for fierce, devouring enemies in apposition to "sons of men."
- **56:7** — The Masoretic Text reads the singular Qal perfect kafaf ("bowed low"), taken intransitively with "my soul" as subject (or impersonally, "my soul was bowed down"), rather than the plural verb found in some ancient versions.
