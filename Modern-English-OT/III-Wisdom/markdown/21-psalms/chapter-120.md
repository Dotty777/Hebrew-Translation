## Text

**120:1** A song for the ascents. I raise my eyes to the mountains. Where will my help come from?

**120:2** My help is from the LORD, the maker of heaven and earth.

**120:3** May He not let your foot slip; may the One who guards you not slumber.

**120:4** Look, the One who guards Israel neither slumbers nor sleeps.

**120:5** The LORD is your guardian; the LORD is your shade at your right hand.

**120:6** During the day the sun will not strike you, nor the moon at night.

**120:7** The LORD will guard you from every evil; He will guard your soul.

**120:8** The LORD will guard your going out and your coming back, from now and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **120:1** — Unlike the other Songs of the Ascents (which use the construct phrase "song of the ascents"), this superscription uses the preposition le- ("for the ascents").
- **120:3** — Hebrew uses the jussive negative particle al ("may ... not") in both clauses of verse 3 before shifting to the indicative negative lo ("not") in verse 4, though poetic al can also express emphatic negation ("He will not").
- **120:5** — The Hebrew root shamar ("guard, keep") recurs six times in verses 3-8 (as Qal participles in verses 3-5 and Qal imperfects in verses 7-8), unifying the psalm around the LORD's constant protection.
