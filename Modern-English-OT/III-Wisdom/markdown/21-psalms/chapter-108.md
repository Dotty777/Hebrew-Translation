## Text

**108:1** For the music director; a psalm of David. God of my praise, do not remain silent.

**108:2** For they have opened a wicked mouth and a deceitful mouth against me; they have spoken to me with a tongue of falsehood.

**108:3** And words of hatred have encircled me, and they have fought against me without reason.

**108:4** In return for my love they accuse me, while I am at prayer.

**108:5** And they have laid evil upon me in return for good, and hatred in return for my love.

**108:6** Put a wicked man over him, and may an accuser stand at his right hand.

**108:7** When he is judged, may he go out guilty, and may his prayer turn into sin.

**108:8** May his days be few; may someone else take his office.

**108:9** May his children become fatherless, and his wife a widow.

**108:10** May his children constantly wander about and beg, and may they search far from their ruins.

**108:11** May a creditor ensnare all that is his, and may strangers loot his labor.

**108:12** May there be no one to continue faithful love to him, and no one to show favor to his fatherless children.

**108:13** May his descendants be cut off; in the next generation may their name be wiped out.

**108:14** May the iniquity of his forefathers be remembered before the LORD, and may his mother's sin not be wiped out.

**108:15** May they remain before the LORD continually, and may He cut off their memory from the earth,

**108:16** because he did not remember to practice faithful love, but pursued an afflicted and needy man, one broken in heart, to put him to death.

**108:17** He loved cursing, and it came upon him; he had no delight in blessing, and it stayed far from him.

**108:18** He clothed himself with cursing like his robe, and it went like water into his inward parts and like oil into his bones.

**108:19** May it be for him like clothing he wraps himself in, and like a belt he continually fastens on.

**108:20** May this be the wages for my accusers from the LORD, and for those who speak evil against my soul.

**108:21** But You, GOD the Lord, act for me for the sake of Your name; because Your faithful love is good, rescue me.

**108:22** For I am afflicted and needy, and my heart is pierced within me.

**108:23** Like a shadow as it stretches out, I pass away; I am shaken off like a locust.

**108:24** My knees falter from fasting, and my flesh has wasted away for lack of fat.

**108:25** And I have become an object of reproach to them; when they see me, they shake their heads.

**108:26** Help me, LORD my God; save me according to Your faithful love.

**108:27** And may they know that this is Your hand; You, LORD, have done it.

**108:28** Let them curse, but You bless; when they rise up, they will be put to shame, while Your servant will rejoice.

**108:29** May my accusers be clothed with disgrace, and may they wrap themselves in their shame as in a robe.

**108:30** I will thank the LORD abundantly with my mouth, and in the midst of the many I will praise Him.

**108:31** For He stands at the right hand of a needy person, to save him from those who pass judgment on his soul.

## Translator Notes

- **108:4** — The verbless clause translated "while I am at prayer" is literally "and I am prayer," expressing complete absorption in prayer. The verb "accuse" shares the root satan with "accuser" in v. 6 and "accusers" in vv. 20 and 29.
- **108:10** — The verb pair noa yanuu combines a Qal infinitive absolute with the imperfect for emphasis ("constantly wander about"), followed by the Piel of shaal ("beg"). The final clause follows the Masoretic reading darshu ("search") rather than the Septuagint's "be driven out."
- **108:21** — The reversed divine title, the divine name followed by Adonai ("GOD the Lord"), points the divine name with the vowels of Elohim to avoid reading Adonai twice. The imperative phrase aseh itti (literally "do with me") is an elliptical idiom meaning "act favorably on my behalf."
- **108:23** — The verb nehelakhti is the sole Niphal occurrence of halakh ("go") in the Hebrew Bible, conveying being swept or carried away like a lengthening evening shadow.
