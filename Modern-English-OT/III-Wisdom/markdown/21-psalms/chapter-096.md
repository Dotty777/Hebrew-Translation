## Text

**96:1** The LORD reigns! Let the earth exult; let many coastlands be glad.

**96:2** Cloud and dense darkness are around Him; the foundation of His throne is righteousness and justice.

**96:3** Fire goes ahead of Him and burns up His foes on every side.

**96:4** His lightning bolts lit up the world; the earth saw and quaked.

**96:5** Mountains melted like wax before the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth.

**96:6** The heavens have declared His righteousness, and all the peoples have seen His glory.

**96:7** May all who serve carved images be put to shame, those who boast in empty idols; bow down to Him, all you gods.

**96:8** Zion heard and was glad, and the daughters of Judah exulted because of Your judgments, LORD.

**96:9** For You, LORD, are Most High above all the earth; You are exceedingly exalted above all gods.

**96:10** You who love the LORD, hate evil! He guards the lives of His devoted ones; from the hand of the wicked He delivers them.

**96:11** Light has been sown for the righteous, and for the upright in heart, gladness.

**96:12** Be glad in the LORD, you righteous, and give thanks to the remembrance of His holiness.

## Translator Notes

- **96:1** — The Hebrew noun iyyim ("coastlands") denotes not only islands but also distant coastal lands and maritime regions.
- **96:7** — The Hishtaphel form hishtachavu ("bow down") is identical for both the second-person plural imperative ("bow down to Him, all you gods") and the third-person plural perfect ("all gods have bowed down to Him"); context and early witnesses favor the imperative.
- **96:8** — "The daughters of Judah" is a Hebrew idiom referring to the surrounding towns and villages dependent on Judah's central cities.
- **96:11** — The Masoretic Text reads the Qal passive participle zarua ("has been sown"), portraying light as seed planted for the righteous, whereas some ancient versions read the similar verb zarach ("dawns" or "shines").
- **96:12** — Literally "to the remembrance of His holiness," where zeker ("remembrance" or "memorial") denotes the name and renown by which the LORD is remembered and invoked.
