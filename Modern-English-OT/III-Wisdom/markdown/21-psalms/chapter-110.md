## Text

**110:1** Praise the LORD! I will thank the LORD with a whole heart, in the council of the upright and in the congregation.

**110:2** The works of the LORD are great, sought out by all who take delight in them.

**110:3** His work is majesty and splendor, and His righteousness stands forever.

**110:4** He has made a memorial for His wondrous deeds; gracious and compassionate is the LORD.

**110:5** He has given provision to those who fear Him; He will forever remember His covenant.

**110:6** He has declared to His people the power of His works, by giving them the inheritance of nations.

**110:7** The works of His hands are truth and justice; all His precepts are reliable.

**110:8** They are sustained forever and ever, carried out in truth and uprightness.

**110:9** He sent redemption to His people; He has decreed His covenant forever; His name is holy and awe-inspiring.

**110:10** The beginning of wisdom is the fear of the LORD; good insight belongs to all who practice them; His praise stands forever.

## Translator Notes

- **110:1** — Following the opening "Praise the LORD," this psalm is a 22-line alphabetic acrostic in Hebrew, with each successive poetic line beginning with the next letter of the Hebrew alphabet from alef to tav.
- **110:2** — The Qal passive participle derushim ("sought out") is followed by lekhol-cheftseihem ("by all who take delight in them"), with the plural suffix referring back to the LORD's works.
- **110:10** — The plural pronoun in "all who practice them" (oseihem) translates the Masoretic suffix literally, referring back to the LORD's precepts in vv. 7-8.
