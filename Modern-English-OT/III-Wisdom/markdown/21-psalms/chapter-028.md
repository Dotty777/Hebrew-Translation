## Text

**28:1** A psalm of David. Give to the LORD, sons of the mighty; give to the LORD glory and strength.

**28:2** Give to the LORD the glory of His name; bow down to the LORD in holy splendor.

**28:3** The voice of the LORD is above the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is above many waters.

**28:4** The voice of the LORD is with power; the voice of the LORD is with splendor.

**28:5** The voice of the LORD breaks cedars, and the LORD smashes the cedars of Lebanon.

**28:6** And He makes them leap like a calf—Lebanon and Sirion like a young wild ox.

**28:7** The voice of the LORD carves out flames of fire.

**28:8** The voice of the LORD makes the wilderness tremble; the LORD makes the wilderness of Kadesh tremble.

**28:9** The voice of the LORD causes does to calve and lays forests bare; and in His temple everyone says, "Glory!"

**28:10** The LORD sat enthroned over the flood, and the LORD sits enthroned as King forever.

**28:11** The LORD will grant His people strength; the LORD will bless His people with peace.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — Literally "sons of gods" or "sons of mighty ones," a poetic idiom for heavenly beings of the divine court.
- **28:9** — The Polel verb literally means "causes does to writhe in birth" (from the same root as the Hiphil verb in v. 8), following the Masoretic vocalization for "does" rather than an emendation to "oaks."
- **28:10** — The Hebrew noun for "flood" occurs elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible only for the Genesis flood; the sequence from perfect ("sat enthroned") to imperfect-consecutive ("sits enthroned") contrasts the LORD's past triumph with His eternal reign.
