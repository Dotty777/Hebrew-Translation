## Text

**141:1** A Maskil of David, while he was in the cave; a prayer.

**141:2** I cry out with my voice to the LORD; with my voice I plead to the LORD for favor.

**141:3** Before Him I pour out my complaint; before Him I declare my distress.

**141:4** When my spirit grows faint within me, You Yourself know my path. Along the road where I walk, they have concealed a trap for me.

**141:5** Look toward the right and see: there is no one who acknowledges me; escape has vanished from me; there is no one caring for my soul.

**141:6** I have cried out to You, LORD; I said, "You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living."

**141:7** Pay attention to my outcry, for I have been reduced very low; rescue me from my pursuers, for they are stronger than I.

**141:8** Bring my soul out of confinement to give thanks to Your name; the righteous will encircle me, because You will deal generously with me.

## Translator Notes

- **141:1** — "Maskil" transliterates a technical psalm superscription of uncertain exact sense, connected to the root sakal ("have insight" or "act skillfully").
- **141:5** — Habbit is a Hiphil infinitive absolute of nabat ("look") functioning imperatively alongside the Qal imperative ure'eh ("and see").
- **141:8** — Masger ("confinement, prison") derives from sagar ("shut in"). The Hiphil imperfect yakhtiru ("will encircle") from katar means "surround me," or possibly "crown themselves on my account."
