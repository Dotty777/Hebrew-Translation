## Text

**82:1** A song, a psalm of Asaph.

**82:2** God, let there be no stillness for You; do not stay silent, and do not remain quiet, God.

**82:3** For look, Your enemies are in tumult, and those who hate You have lifted up their heads.

**82:4** Against Your people they shrewdly devise secret counsel, and they take counsel together against Your sheltered ones.

**82:5** They have said, "Come, and let us blot them out from being a nation, so that the name of Israel will no longer be remembered."

**82:6** For with one heart they have taken counsel together; against You they strike a covenant:

**82:7** the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites,

**82:8** Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia with the residents of Tyre.

**82:9** Assyria also has allied itself with them; they have become an arm for the sons of Lot. Selah.

**82:10** Do to them as to Midian, as to Sisera, as to Jabin at the Kishon stream.

**82:11** They were wiped out at En-dor; they became dung for the soil.

**82:12** Make their nobles like Oreb and like Zeeb, and all their rulers like Zebah and like Zalmunna,

**82:13** who said, "Let us take possession of the pastures of God for ourselves."

**82:14** My God, make them like tumbleweed, like stubble before the wind.

**82:15** As fire burns a forest, and as a flame scorches mountains,

**82:16** so pursue them with Your storm, and with Your whirlwind terrify them.

**82:17** Fill their faces with disgrace, so that they may seek Your name, LORD.

**82:18** May they be ashamed and terrified forever; may they be humiliated and perish;

**82:19** and let them know that You—Your name is the LORD—alone are Most High over all the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **82:4** — Hebrew tsefunekha ("Your sheltered ones") is the Qal passive participle of tsafan ("hide, treasure, shelter"), referring to God's protected people.
- **82:9** — Hebrew "an arm" (zeroa') is a metaphor for military strength and active support.
- **82:14** — Hebrew galgal literally means "wheel" or "whirling thing," here referring to windblown tumbleweed or swirling dry plant debris.
- **82:19** — Hebrew places shimkha YHWH ("Your name is the LORD") in emphatic apposition between 'attah ("You") and levaddekha ("alone").
