## Text

**92:1** The LORD reigns; He is arrayed in majesty. The LORD is arrayed; He has belted Himself with strength. Surely the world is set firm; it will not be shaken.

**92:2** Your throne is set firm from long ago; You are from eternity.

**92:3** The rivers have lifted up, LORD, the rivers have lifted up their voice; the rivers lift up their crashing waves.

**92:4** Above the voices of many waters—mighty are the breakers of the sea—mighty on high is the LORD.

**92:5** Your testimonies are exceedingly reliable; holiness is fitting for Your house, LORD, for length of days.

## Translator Notes

- **92:3** — The Hebrew noun translated "crashing waves" (dokyam) is a hapax legomenon from the root dkh ("pound, crush"), literally "their pounding." The shift from two Qal perfect verbs ("have lifted up") to an imperfect verb ("lift up") underscores the ongoing roar of the rivers.
- **92:4** — The Masoretic Hebrew places the plural adjective addirim ("mighty") directly before "breakers of the sea" without repeating the comparative preposition min ("above/more than") from the opening phrase. Translating the middle clause parenthetically preserves the unemended Masoretic syntax and the repeated root addir ("mighty").
- **92:5** — "For length of days" renders the Hebrew idiom le-orek yamim literally, expressing unending duration.
