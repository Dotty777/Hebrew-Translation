## Text

**135:1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good, for His faithful love lasts forever.

**135:2** Give thanks to the God of gods, for His faithful love lasts forever.

**135:3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:4** to the One who alone performs great wonders, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:5** to the One who made the heavens with understanding, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:6** to the One who spread out the earth over the waters, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:7** to the One who made great lights, for His faithful love lasts forever:

**135:8** the sun for dominion over the day, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:9** the moon and stars for dominion over the night, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:10** to the One who struck down Egypt in their firstborn, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:11** and brought out Israel from their midst, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:12** with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:13** to the One who split the Red Sea into parts, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:14** and caused Israel to pass through its midst, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:15** yet overturned Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:16** to the One who led His people in the wilderness, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:17** to the One who struck down great kings, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:18** and killed mighty kings, for His faithful love lasts forever:

**135:19** Sihon king of the Amorites, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:20** and Og king of Bashan, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:21** and gave their land as an inheritance, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:22** an inheritance for Israel, His servant, for His faithful love lasts forever.

**135:23** He is the One who remembered us in our low condition, for His faithful love lasts forever,

**135:24** and delivered us from our adversaries, for His faithful love lasts forever;

**135:25** He gives food to all flesh, for His faithful love lasts forever.

**135:26** Give thanks to the God of the heavens, for His faithful love lasts forever.

## Translator Notes

- **135:1** — The refrain ki le'olam chasdo is a verbless Hebrew clause literally reading "for forever is His chesed," where chesed denotes loyal, covenantal love.
- **135:13** — Hebrew legozer ... ligzarim uses a cognate verb-and-noun phrase ("to the One who cuts into cut parts"), and yam-suph literally means "Sea of Reeds," traditionally known as the Red Sea.
- **135:15** — Hebrew ni'er (Piel perfect of na'ar) literally means "shook off" or "shook out," echoing Exodus 14:27.
- **135:24** — Hebrew wayyifreqenu (Qal wayyiqtol of paraq) literally means "tore us away" or "pulled us free," a verb used in late Biblical Hebrew and Aramaic for rescuing or delivering.
