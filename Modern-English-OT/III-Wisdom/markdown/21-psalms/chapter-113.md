## Text

**113:1** When Israel came out from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people speaking a foreign language,

**113:2** Judah became His holy place, Israel His realm.

**113:3** The sea saw and fled; the Jordan turned backward.

**113:4** The mountains leaped like rams, the hills like young sheep.

**113:5** What is the matter with you, sea, that you flee; Jordan, that you turn backward;

**113:6** mountains, that you leap like rams; hills, like young sheep?

**113:7** Earth, quake in the presence of the Lord, in the presence of the God of Jacob,

**113:8** who transforms the rock into a pool of water, flint into a wellspring of water.

**113:9** Not to us, LORD, not to us, but give glory to Your name on account of Your loyal love, on account of Your faithfulness.

**113:10** Why should the nations say, "Where, then, is their God?"

**113:11** Yet our God is in the heavens; everything He desires, He does.

**113:12** Their idols are silver and gold, a work of human hands.

**113:13** They have mouths, yet they do not speak; they have eyes, yet they do not see.

**113:14** They have ears, yet they do not hear; they have noses, yet they do not smell.

**113:15** Hands are theirs, yet they do not touch; feet are theirs, yet they do not walk; they utter no sound with their throats.

**113:16** Like them their makers will become, everyone who trusts in them.

**113:17** Israel, trust in the LORD; He is their helper and their shield.

**113:18** House of Aaron, trust in the LORD; He is their helper and their shield.

**113:19** You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD; He is their helper and their shield.

**113:20** The LORD has remembered us; He will bless: He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron.

**113:21** He will bless those who fear the LORD, the small along with the great.

**113:22** May the LORD increase you, you and your children.

**113:23** Blessed are you by the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

**113:24** The heavens are heavens belonging to the LORD, but the earth He has given to the children of humanity.

**113:25** The dead will not praise the LORD, nor will any who descend into silence.

**113:26** Yet we will bless the LORD from now on and forever. Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **113:1** — The Qal active participle lo'ez ("speaking a foreign language") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes speech unintelligible to Israel.
- **113:8** — Both halves of this verse employ archaic poetic linking endings: hireq compaginis (-i) on the Qal participle "who transforms" and holem compaginis (-o) on the construct noun "wellspring of."
- **113:15** — Whereas vv. 13-14 state literally "a mouth/eyes/ears/nose they have," v. 15 shifts to possessive suffixes ("their hands," "their feet") standing in casus pendens before masculine plural verbs whose subject remains the idols themselves. Both "touch" here and "smell" in v. 14 carry the archaic paragogic nun ending (-un).
- **113:17** — Here and in vv. 18-19, the Hebrew shifts from a second-person imperative ("trust") to third-person suffixes ("their helper and their shield"), reflecting an antiphonal liturgical call and response. The noun ezer literally means "help" and is used concretely of God as helper.
