## Text

**106:1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good, for His loyal love is forever.

**106:2** Let those redeemed by the LORD say this, whom He redeemed from the hand of the foe.

**106:3** And He gathered them from the lands, from the east and from the west, from the north and from the sea.

**106:4** They wandered in the wilderness, on a wasteland path; they did not find a city to live in.

**106:5** Hungry and thirsty as well, their soul grew faint within them.

**106:6** Then they cried out to the LORD in their distress; He rescued them from their anguish.

**106:7** He guided them along a straight path, to go to a city to live in.

**106:8** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love and for His marvelous deeds toward the children of mankind.

**106:9** For He has satisfied the yearning soul, and He has filled the hungry soul with good things.

**106:10** There were those sitting in darkness and deep shadow, prisoners of affliction and iron.

**106:11** For they rebelled against God's words and scorned the counsel of the Most High.

**106:12** So He subdued their heart with hard labor; they stumbled, and there was no helper.

**106:13** Then they cried out to the LORD in their distress; He saved them from their anguish.

**106:14** He brought them out of darkness and deep shadow, and He tore their bonds apart.

**106:15** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love and for His marvelous deeds toward the children of mankind.

**106:16** For He has shattered bronze doors and cut through iron bars.

**106:17** Fools, on account of the path of their transgression and on account of their iniquities, were afflicted.

**106:18** Their soul detested all food, and they reached as far as the gates of death.

**106:19** Then they cried out to the LORD in their distress; He saved them from their anguish.

**106:20** He sent His word and healed them, and He delivered them from their pits.

**106:21** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love and for His marvelous deeds toward the children of mankind.

**106:22** And let them sacrifice thanksgiving offerings and recount His works with joyful shouting.

**106:23** Those going down to the sea in ships, conducting business on mighty waters,

**106:24** they saw the works of the LORD and His marvelous deeds in the deep.

**106:25** And He spoke and raised up a storm wind, which lifted its waves high.

**106:26** They ascended to the heavens, they descended to the depths; their soul melted away in calamity.

**106:27** They reeled and swayed like a drunkard, and all their wisdom was swallowed up.

**106:28** Then they cried out to the LORD in their distress, and He brought them out of their anguish.

**106:29** He brought the storm to stillness, and their waves grew quiet.

**106:30** Then they rejoiced because they became calm, and He led them to the harbor of their desire.

**106:31** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love and for His marvelous deeds toward the children of mankind.

**106:32** Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people and praise Him in the session of the elders.

**106:33** He turns rivers into a wilderness and springs of water into parched ground,

**106:34** a fruitful land into a salt flat, on account of the wickedness of those living in it.

**106:35** He turns a wilderness into a pool of water, and an arid land into springs of water.

**106:36** And there He settles the hungry, and they establish a city to live in.

**106:37** They sow fields and plant vineyards, and they produce a fruitful crop.

**106:38** He blesses them, and they increase greatly, and He does not diminish their livestock.

**106:39** Yet they grow few and sink low because of oppression, calamity, and grief.

**106:40** He pours contempt upon nobles and makes them wander in a wasteland with no path.

**106:41** Yet He lifts the needy safely out of affliction and makes families like a flock.

**106:42** The upright see it and rejoice, and all injustice shuts its mouth.

**106:43** Who is wise? Let him observe these things, and let them closely consider the LORD's acts of loyal love.

## Translator Notes

- **106:3** — Hebrew miyyam literally means "from the sea," which normally denotes the west; because "from the west" has already been named, it stands here in the fourth compass position, often understood as the southern sea or the south.
- **106:4** — Following the Masoretic accentuation with pausal derek ("path"), "on a wasteland path" closes the first half-verse and contrasts with "along a straight path" in v. 7; some instead construe derek with the second clause as "no way to a city to live in."
- **106:10** — Hebrew tsalmavet is pointed by the Masoretes as a compound meaning "shadow of death," though many lexicographers derive it from a root meaning "be dark" and take it as "deep shadow" or "utter gloom."
- **106:25** — The masculine singular suffix on gallaw ("its waves") refers back to the masculine noun "the sea" in v. 23, while the plural suffix on gallehem ("their waves") in v. 29 refers to the waves of the "mighty waters" of v. 23 or the waves threatening the sailors.
- **106:27** — Hebrew titballa is the Hithpael of bala ("swallow"), literally "was swallowed up," an idiom conveying that all their seamanship and skill vanished completely.
