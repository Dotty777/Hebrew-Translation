## Text

**99:1** A psalm for thanksgiving. Shout in triumph to the LORD, all the earth.

**99:2** Serve the LORD with joy; come before Him with jubilant song.

**99:3** Know that the LORD is God; He Himself made us, and we belong to Him; we are His people and the flock of His pasture.

**99:4** Come into His gates with thanksgiving, into His courts with praise; give thanks to Him; bless His name.

**99:5** For the LORD is good; His loyal love lasts forever, and His faithfulness to generation after generation.

## Translator Notes

- **99:1** — Hebrew letodah can mean either 'for thanksgiving' or 'for a thank offering' (compare Lev 7:12). The imperative hari'u (Hiphil of rua) denotes raising a loud shout of acclamation or joy.
- **99:3** — The translation follows the qere (read text) velo ('and to Him,' i.e., 'and we belong to Him'). The ketiv (written text) reads velo spelled with aleph ('and not'), yielding 'and not we ourselves.'
