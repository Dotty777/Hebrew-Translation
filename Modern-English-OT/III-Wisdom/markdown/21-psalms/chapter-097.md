## Text

**97:1** A psalm. Sing a new song to the LORD, for He has done wondrous deeds; His right hand and His holy arm have brought salvation for Him.

**97:2** The LORD has made His salvation known; before the eyes of the nations He has revealed His righteousness.

**97:3** He has remembered His loyal love and His faithfulness toward the house of Israel; all the farthest ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

**97:4** Shout in triumph to the LORD, all the earth; burst forth, ring out in joy, and make music.

**97:5** Make music to the LORD with the lyre, with the lyre and the voice of song.

**97:6** With trumpets and the sound of the ram's horn, shout in triumph before the King, the LORD.

**97:7** Let the sea and its fullness roar, the world and those dwelling in it.

**97:8** May rivers clap their hands; may mountains ring out in joy together

**97:9** in the presence of the LORD, for He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with uprightness.

## Translator Notes

- **97:1** — The Hiphil perfect verb hoshiah ("has brought salvation") is feminine singular, agreeing grammatically with the nearest subject, "His right hand" (feminine in Hebrew), and shares the same root as "salvation" in 97:2-3.
- **97:4** — The Hiphil imperative hariu ("shout in triumph," repeated in 97:6) denotes a loud cry of victory or royal acclamation, often accompanied by horn blasts.
- **97:6** — Hebrew distinguishes two instruments here: the chatsotserot ("trumpets," straight metal instruments used by priests) and the shofar ("ram's horn").
