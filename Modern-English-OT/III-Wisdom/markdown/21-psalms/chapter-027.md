## Text

**27:1** Of David. To You, LORD, I call; my Rock, do not remain deaf to me; otherwise, if You keep silent toward me, I will be likened to those going down into the pit.

**27:2** Hear the sound of my pleas for mercy as I cry out to You for help, as I raise my hands toward Your holy inner sanctuary.

**27:3** Do not pull me away with the wicked and with doers of iniquity, those who speak peace with their companions, though evil is in their heart.

**27:4** Give to them according to their deeds and according to the evil of their practices; according to the work of their hands give to them; return their recompense to them.

**27:5** Because they pay no attention to the deeds of the LORD or to the work of His hands, He will demolish them and will not build them up.

**27:6** Blessed be the LORD, because He has heard the sound of my pleas for mercy.

**27:7** The LORD is my strength and my shield; in Him my heart trusted, and I was helped; so my heart exulted, and with my song I will give Him thanks.

**27:8** The LORD is strength for them, and He is a fortress of deliverances for His anointed one.

**27:9** Deliver Your people and bless Your inheritance; shepherd them and carry them forever.

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — Hebrew debir denotes the inner sanctuary (the Holy of Holies) of the temple, toward which the psalmist raises his hands in prayer.
- **27:7** — Hebrew 'ahodennu is an uncommon poetic Hiphil imperfect of ydh ("give thanks") that retains the stem he rather than eliding it.
- **27:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads lamo ("for them"), referring to God's people anticipated in v. 9; some ancient versions (including the Septuagint and Syriac) read le'ammo ("for His people").
