## Text

**147:1** Laud the LORD, Jerusalem! Praise your God, Zion!

**147:2** For He has reinforced the bars of your gates; He has blessed your children in your midst.

**147:3** He sets your territory at peace; He satisfies you with the choicest wheat.

**147:4** He sends His utterance to the earth; swiftly His word runs.

**147:5** He bestows snow like wool; He strews frost like ash.

**147:6** He hurls His ice like morsels; before His cold, who can stand?

**147:7** He sends His word and makes them melt. He makes His wind blow; waters flow.

**147:8** He proclaims His words to Jacob, His decrees and His rulings to Israel.

**147:9** He has not done so for any nation; and as for rulings, they do not know them. Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **147:3** — "The choicest wheat" translates a Hebrew idiom literally meaning "fat of wheat," where "fat" denotes the richest or finest portion.
- **147:8** — The consonants are written defectively without a yod (ketiv singular, "His word"), whereas the Masoretic vocalization reads the qere plural ("His words"), matching the plural "decrees" and "rulings" that follow.
- **147:9** — The Masoretic Hebrew text reads literally "and as for rulings, they have not known them" without a possessive suffix ("His"), unlike the Septuagint.
