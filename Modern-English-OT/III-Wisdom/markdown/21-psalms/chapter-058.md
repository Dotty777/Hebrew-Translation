## Text

**58:1** For the music director: "Do Not Destroy." A miktam of David, when Saul sent men, and they kept watch over the house to put him to death.

**58:2** Rescue me from my enemies, my God; set me high above those who rise up against me.

**58:3** Rescue me from workers of iniquity, and from men of bloodshed save me.

**58:4** For look, they have lain in wait for my life; fierce men band together against me, for no transgression of mine and no sin of mine, LORD.

**58:5** For no guilt on my part, they run and take their stand; wake up to meet me and see!

**58:6** And You, LORD God of hosts, God of Israel, rouse Yourself to call all the nations to account; show no grace to any who deal treacherously in iniquity. Selah.

**58:7** They come back at evening; they growl like a dog and prowl around the city.

**58:8** Look, they spew forth with their mouths; swords are on their lips, for they say, "Who is listening?"

**58:9** But You, LORD, laugh at them; You deride all the nations.

**58:10** Because of his strength I will keep watch for You, for God is my stronghold.

**58:11** The God of my loyal love will meet me; God will let me look upon my foes.

**58:12** Do not kill them, so that my people do not forget; make them wander by Your power and bring them down, our shield, Lord.

**58:13** For the sin of their mouths, the word of their lips, may they be caught in their pride, and because of the cursing and deception they utter.

**58:14** Bring them to an end in wrath, bring them to an end until they are no more, so that they may know that God rules in Jacob, to the ends of the earth. Selah.

**58:15** And they come back at evening; they growl like a dog and prowl around the city.

**58:16** They wander about for food; if they are not satisfied, they spend the night.

**58:17** But as for me, I will sing of Your strength, and in the morning I will sing joyfully of Your loyal love; for You have been a stronghold for me and a place of refuge on the day of my distress.

**58:18** My strength, to You I will make music, for God is my stronghold, the God of my loyal love.

## Translator Notes

- **58:10** — The Masoretic Text reads uzzo ("his strength"), taken adverbially of the enemy's power ("Because of his strength I will keep watch for You"), whereas verse 18 and some manuscripts and ancient versions read uzzi ("my strength").
- **58:11** — Ketiv reads chasdo ("His loyal love"), while the Qere followed here reads chasdi ("my loyal love," matching verse 18). The Hiphil yar'eni with the preposition be ("let me look upon") idiomatically denotes looking upon foes in vindication or triumph.
- **58:13** — The opening phrases lack an explicit preposition and are taken causally ("For the sin of their mouths, the word of their lips") in parallel with the prepositional phrases at the end of the verse; alternatively, they may form a nominal clause ("The sin of their mouths is the word of their lips").
- **58:16** — Ketiv reads Qal yanu'un ("they wander"), while the Qere followed here reads Hiphil yeni'un (used intransitively, "they wander about," echoing the Hiphil of the same root in verse 12). The final verb vayyalinu matches the Qal form of lin ("spend the night"), though many versions connect it to lun ("grumble" or "growl").
