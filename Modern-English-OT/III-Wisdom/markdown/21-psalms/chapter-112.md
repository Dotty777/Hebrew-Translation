## Text

**112:1** Praise the LORD! Praise, servants of the LORD; praise the name of the LORD.

**112:2** May the name of the LORD be blessed from now on and forever.

**112:3** From the sun's rising to its setting, the name of the LORD is worthy of praise.

**112:4** High above all nations is the LORD; above the heavens is His glory.

**112:5** Who is like the LORD our God, who sits on high,

**112:6** who lowers Himself to look upon the heavens and upon the earth?

**112:7** He raises up the poor from the dust; from the ash heap He lifts the needy high,

**112:8** to seat them with nobles, with the nobles of His people.

**112:9** He settles the barren woman of the house as a glad mother of children. Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **112:5** — Verses 5-9 contain five verb forms with the archaic poetic ending -i (hireq compaginis) attached to four participles and one infinitive construct. The paired Hiphil participles in vv. 5-6 literally contrast making high to sit with making low to look.
- **112:9** — Literally "the barren woman of the house," a construct phrase referring to a childless woman within a household; the Hiphil participle of yashav ("settle / cause to dwell") echoes the same root in v. 5 ("sits") and v. 8 ("seat").
