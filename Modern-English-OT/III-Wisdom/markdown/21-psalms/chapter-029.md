## Text

**29:1** A psalm, a song for the dedication of the house, of David.

**29:2** I will exalt You, LORD, because You have drawn me up and have not allowed my enemies to rejoice over me.

**29:3** LORD my God, I cried out to You for help, and You healed me.

**29:4** LORD, You brought my soul up from Sheol; You restored me to life so that I would not go down to the pit.

**29:5** Sing praises to the LORD, you His loyal ones, and give thanks to His holy memorial.

**29:6** For His anger is for a moment, and in His favor is life; at evening weeping may lodge for the night, but in the morning comes a shout of joy.

**29:7** As for me, I said in my security, "I will never be shaken."

**29:8** LORD, in Your favor You established strength for my mountain; You hid Your face; I became terrified.

**29:9** To You, LORD, I called out, and to the Lord I pleaded for grace:

**29:10** "What gain is there in my blood when I go down to the pit? Will dust give thanks to You? Will it proclaim Your faithfulness?

**29:11** Hear, LORD, and be gracious to me; LORD, be a helper to me!"

**29:12** You turned my mourning into dancing for me; You untied my sackcloth and wrapped me with joy,

**29:13** so that my glory may sing praises to You and not keep silent. LORD my God, forever I will give thanks to You.

## Translator Notes

- **29:4** — Following the qere (read form), a Qal infinitive construct with first-person suffix ("from my going down to the pit"); the ketiv (written form) reads a plural participle, "from among those who go down to the pit."
- **29:5** — Literally "the memorial of His holiness," referring to the holy name by which the LORD is invoked and remembered.
- **29:8** — Literally "You caused strength to stand for my mountain," an image of God establishing the psalmist in unshakable security.
- **29:13** — The Masoretic Text reads simply "glory" without a possessive suffix; here it is a poetic term for one's inner self or being, understood from the context as "my glory."
