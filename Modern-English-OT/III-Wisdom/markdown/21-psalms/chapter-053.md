## Text

**53:1** For the music director, with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.

**53:2** When the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is David not hiding himself with us?"

**53:3** God, by Your name save me, and by Your might vindicate me.

**53:4** God, hear my prayer; listen closely to the words of my mouth.

**53:5** For strangers have risen up against me, and ruthless men have sought my life; they have not placed God before themselves. Selah.

**53:6** Look, God is a helper to me; the Lord is among those who sustain my life.

**53:7** He will turn the evil back upon my lurking foes; in Your faithfulness, wipe them out.

**53:8** I will sacrifice to You with a freewill offering; I will offer thanks to Your name, LORD, because it is good.

**53:9** For He has rescued me out of every distress, and my eye has looked upon my enemies.

## Translator Notes

- **53:6** — The phrase literally reads 'among those who sustain my life' (beth essentiae with a plural Qal participle of samakh), an idiom expressing that the Lord is the preeminent sustainer of the psalmist's life.
- **53:7** — The Qere reads Hiphil yashiv ('He will turn back' the evil), whereas the Ketiv reads Qal yashuv ('the evil will return' upon my foes).
- **53:9** — The Hebrew idiom 'my eye has looked upon' (Qal of ra'ah with the preposition be-) denotes gazing upon defeated enemies in vindication or triumph.
