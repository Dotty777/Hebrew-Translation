## Text

**98:1** The LORD reigns; the peoples tremble. He is seated upon the cherubim; the earth reels.

**98:2** In Zion the LORD is great, and He is high above all the peoples.

**98:3** Let them give thanks to Your name, great and awe-inspiring; He is holy.

**98:4** And the might of the King loves justice. You Yourself have established uprightness; You have carried out justice and righteousness in Jacob.

**98:5** Exalt the LORD our God, and bow low at His footstool; He is holy.

**98:6** Moses and Aaron were among His priests, and Samuel among those calling on His name. They would call to the LORD, and He would answer them.

**98:7** In a pillar of cloud He would speak to them; they kept His testimonies and the statute He gave them.

**98:8** LORD our God, You Yourself answered them; You were to them a God who forgave, yet took vengeance for their deeds.

**98:9** Exalt the LORD our God, and bow low at His holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.

## Translator Notes

- **98:1** — The verb translated 'reels' (Hebrew tanut, Qal imperfect of nut) is a hapax legomenon in the Hebrew Bible meaning to totter, sway, or reel. Both imperfect verbs in this verse ('tremble' and 'reels') may also be read as jussives ('let the peoples tremble ... let the earth reel').
- **98:4** — Hebrew oz melekh is literally 'the might of a king' (a construct phrase expressing 'the mighty King') serving as the grammatical subject of the verb 'loves.'
- **98:8** — Hebrew el nose uses the Qal active participle of nasa ('lift, bear'), an idiom for bearing away or forgiving guilt. Hebrew alilotam literally means 'their deeds' or 'their practices,' referring in context to their sinful actions.
