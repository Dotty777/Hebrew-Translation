## Text

**134:1** Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD's name; praise Him, you servants of the LORD,

**134:2** who stand in the LORD's house, in the courtyards of our God's house.

**134:3** Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good; make music to His name, for it is delightful.

**134:4** For the LORD chose Jacob for Himself, Israel as His personal treasure.

**134:5** For I myself know that the LORD is great, and our Lord is greater than all gods.

**134:6** All that the LORD desires, He does, in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and all depths.

**134:7** He causes clouds to rise from the end of the earth; He makes lightning flashes for the rain; He brings the wind out from His storehouses.

**134:8** He is the One who struck down Egypt's firstborn, from human to animal.

**134:9** He sent signs and portents into your midst, Egypt, against Pharaoh and against all his servants.

**134:10** He is the One who struck down many nations and killed powerful kings:

**134:11** Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan;

**134:12** and He gave their land as an inheritance, an inheritance for Israel, His people.

**134:13** LORD, Your name is forever; LORD, Your remembrance is for generation after generation.

**134:14** For the LORD will judge His people, and He will feel compassion for His servants.

**134:15** The nations' idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands.

**134:16** They have mouths, yet they do not speak; they have eyes, yet they do not see;

**134:17** they have ears, yet they do not listen; indeed, there is no breath at all in their mouths.

**134:18** Their makers will become like them, everyone who trusts in them.

**134:19** House of Israel, bless the LORD; house of Aaron, bless the LORD;

**134:20** house of Levi, bless the LORD; you who fear the LORD, bless the LORD.

**134:21** Blessed be the LORD from Zion, the One dwelling in Jerusalem! Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **134:1** — Hebrew uses the shortened divine name Yah in the opening "Praise the LORD" (halelu yah, literally "Hallelujah") as well as in verses 3, 4, and 21.
- **134:3** — The verb zammeru is a Piel imperative of zamar ("make music" or "sing praise"), and the adjective na'im ("delightful" or "pleasant") has no expressed subject, allowing either "it" (His name, or praising Him) or "He."
- **134:14** — In this covenantal context, yadin (Qal imperfect of din, "judge") carries the sense of deciding in favor of or vindicating His people, and yitnecham (Hithpael imperfect of nacham) expresses being moved to compassion or relenting.
- **134:17** — The verb ya'azinu (Hiphil imperfect of 'azan, derived from 'ozen, "ear") creates a wordplay with "ears" ("listen"), and the unusual double particle 'en-yesh emphasizes the complete absence of breath ("no breath at all").
