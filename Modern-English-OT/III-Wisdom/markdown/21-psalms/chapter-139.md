## Text

**139:1** For the music director. A psalm of David.

**139:2** Rescue me, LORD, from the evil man; guard me from the man of violence,

**139:3** those who plot evils in their heart; every day they stir up wars.

**139:4** They have sharpened their tongue like a snake; a viper's venom is beneath their lips. Selah.

**139:5** Keep me, LORD, from the hands of the wicked; guard me from the man of violence, those who plot to overthrow my steps.

**139:6** The arrogant have concealed a trap and cords for me; they have spread out a net alongside the path; they have set snares for me. Selah.

**139:7** I said to the LORD, "You are my God." Listen attentively, LORD, to the voice of my pleas for grace.

**139:8** GOD, the Lord, strength of my salvation, You have shielded my head on the day of warfare.

**139:9** LORD, do not grant the desires of the wicked; do not advance his scheme, or else they will become exalted. Selah.

**139:10** The head of those who encircle me—may the trouble of their lips cover them.

**139:11** May glowing coals fall upon them; may He cast them into the fire, into deep pits, so that they do not rise.

**139:12** A slanderous man will not be established in the land; as for the man of violence, evil will hunt him down to headlong ruin.

**139:13** I know that the LORD will uphold the cause of the afflicted, the justice of the needy.

**139:14** Indeed, the righteous will give thanks to Your name; the upright will live in Your presence.

## Translator Notes

- **139:8** — The Masoretic text reverses the usual 'Lord GOD' sequence to 'YHWH Adonai,' pointing the Tetragrammaton with the vowels of 'Elohim' ('GOD, the Lord').
- **139:10** — Translates the Qere 'yekhasemo' (3ms Piel imperfect of k-s-h, agreeing with singular 'amal,' 'trouble/mischief'); the Ketiv reads a 3mp verb 'ykswmw' ('may they cover them').
- **139:11** — Translates the Qere 'yimmotu' (Niphal imperfect of m-w-t, 'fall'); the Ketiv reads Hiphil 'ymytw' ('let them cause to fall'). The following Hiphil 'yappilem' ('may He cast them') is active 3ms, and 'mahamorot' ('deep pits') is a hapax legomenon.
- **139:12** — The major poetic accent (oleh we-yored) divides the verse at 'in the land,' placing 'man of violence' and 'evil' in the second half; 'le-madhefot' (from d-h-f, 'thrust/drive headlong') is a hapax legomenon denoting repeated blows or headlong ruin.
- **139:13** — Translates the Qere 'yada'ti' (Qal perfect 1cs, 'I know'); the Ketiv 'yd't' is a defective spelling of the same 1cs form (or 2ms, 'You know').
