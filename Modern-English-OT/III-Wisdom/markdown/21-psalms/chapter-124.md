## Text

**124:1** A song of the ascents. Those trusting in the LORD are like Mount Zion: it will not be shaken; it remains forever.

**124:2** Jerusalem—mountains are all around her, and the LORD is all around His people, from now on and forever.

**124:3** For the scepter of wickedness will not rest on the allotted portion of the righteous, so that the righteous may not extend their hands to injustice.

**124:4** Do good, LORD, to the good and to those upright in their hearts.

**124:5** But those who turn aside onto their crooked paths—the LORD will lead them away along with those who work iniquity. Peace upon Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **124:2** — "Jerusalem" stands as a fronted topic (casus pendens), and the Hebrew pairs the mountains around Jerusalem with the LORD around His people using simple "and" rather than an explicit comparative "as ... so."
- **124:3** — "Allotted portion" translates goral (literally "lot"), referring by metonymy to the inheritance allotted to the righteous. "Injustice" translates avlatah, appending an archaic poetic -ah ending to avlah.
- **124:5** — "Those who turn aside" is the Hiphil participle of natah used intransitively with "their crooked paths" as an adverbial accusative of direction. The particle et before "those who work iniquity" is the preposition "with" rather than a direct object marker, since the Hiphil verb yolikhem ("will lead them away") already carries the object suffix "them."
