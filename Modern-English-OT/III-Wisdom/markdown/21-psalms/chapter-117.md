## Text

**117:1** Offer thanks to the LORD, because He is good; for His faithful love lasts forever.

**117:2** Now let Israel say, "His faithful love lasts forever."

**117:3** Now let the house of Aaron say, "His faithful love lasts forever."

**117:4** Now let those who fear the LORD say, "His faithful love lasts forever."

**117:5** From tight straits I called to the LORD; the LORD answered me, bringing me into an open expanse.

**117:6** The LORD is for me; I will not be afraid. What can a human do to me?

**117:7** The LORD is for me among my helpers, and I will gaze triumphantly at those who hate me.

**117:8** It is better to seek refuge in the LORD than to rely on human beings.

**117:9** It is better to seek refuge in the LORD than to rely on nobles.

**117:10** All nations encircled me; in the name of the LORD I will certainly cut them off.

**117:11** They encircled me, indeed they encircled me; in the name of the LORD I will certainly cut them off.

**117:12** They encircled me like bees; they were snuffed out like a fire of thorns; in the name of the LORD I will certainly cut them off.

**117:13** You pushed me forcefully so that I would fall, yet the LORD helped me.

**117:14** My strength and song is the LORD, and He has become salvation for me.

**117:15** A sound of ringing joy and salvation is in the tents of the righteous: the right hand of the LORD acts with might.

**117:16** The right hand of the LORD is raised high; the right hand of the LORD acts with might.

**117:17** I will not die; rather, I will live and recount the works of the LORD.

**117:18** The LORD severely disciplined me, yet He did not give me up to death.

**117:19** Open the gates of righteousness for me; I will go in through them and thank the LORD.

**117:20** This is the LORD's gate; the righteous will go in through it.

**117:21** I thank You because You answered me, and You have become salvation for me.

**117:22** A stone the builders spurned has become the foremost cornerstone.

**117:23** From the LORD this has come about; it is wonderful in our eyes.

**117:24** This is the day the LORD made; let us exult and be glad in it.

**117:25** Please, LORD, save now! Please, LORD, grant success now!

**117:26** Blessed is the one coming in the name of the LORD; we bless you from the LORD's house.

**117:27** The LORD is God, and He has shone light on us; bind the festival offering with ropes up to the horns of the altar.

**117:28** You are my God, and I will thank You; my God, I will exalt You.

**117:29** Offer thanks to the LORD, because He is good; for His faithful love lasts forever.

## Translator Notes

- **117:5** — Hebrew uses the shortened poetic divine name Yah here and in vv. 14 and 17-19. The clause anani bammerchav is a pregnant construction, literally "answered me in the open expanse," contrasting directly with hammetsar, "the narrow straits/distress."
- **117:12** — The Masoretic Text reads the Pual verb do'akhu ("they were extinguished/snuffed out"), rather than the Septuagint's "they blazed up." In vv. 10-12, amilam is a Hiphil imperfect of mul ("cut off") reinforced by emphatic ki ("certainly").
- **117:13** — The Masoretic Text has the Qal infinitive absolute with a 2ms Qal perfect of dachah (dachoh dechitani, literally "pushing you pushed me"), addressing the adversary directly rather than the passive "I was pushed" of several ancient versions.
- **117:16** — Romemah is a rare feminine singular form from rum ("be high"), matching the feminine subject yemin ("right hand"); it is taken here intransitively ("is raised high"), though some parse it as an active Polel participle lacking the m- prefix ("exalts").
- **117:27** — The command isru-chag ba'avotim ad-qarnot hammizbeach is famously difficult; it is rendered literally here taking chag by metonymy for the festival sacrifice (cf. Exod 23:18; Mal 2:3) and avotim as "ropes/cords," though others understand it as "join the festival procession with leafy branches up to the horns of the altar."
