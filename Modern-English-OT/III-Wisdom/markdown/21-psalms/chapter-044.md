## Text

**44:1** For the music director, according to Shoshannim, of the sons of Korah; a Maskil, a song of love.

**44:2** My heart stirs with a good word; I speak my works to a king; my tongue is the pen of a skilled scribe.

**44:3** You are far more handsome than the sons of mankind; grace has been poured onto your lips; for this reason God has blessed you forever.

**44:4** Strap your sword on your thigh, mighty warrior, in your splendor and your majesty!

**44:5** And in your majesty succeed, ride forth on behalf of truth, humility, and righteousness; and may your right hand teach you fearsome deeds.

**44:6** Your arrows are sharp—peoples fall beneath you—in the heart of the enemies of the king.

**44:7** Your throne, God, is forever and ever; a scepter of equity is the scepter of Your kingdom.

**44:8** You loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, Your God, has anointed You with oil of gladness above Your companions.

**44:9** All your garments are myrrh, aloes, and cassia; from palaces of ivory, stringed instruments gladden you.

**44:10** Daughters of kings are among your esteemed women; the royal consort stands at your right hand in fine gold of Ophir.

**44:11** Listen, daughter, and see, and incline your ear, and forget your people and the house of your father;

**44:12** and the king will long for your beauty; for he is your lord, so bow down to him.

**44:13** And the daughter of Tyre—the wealthy of the people—will seek your favor with a gift.

**44:14** All glorious is the king's daughter within; her garment is of interwoven gold.

**44:15** In embroidered attire she is led to the king; virgins following her, her companions, are brought to you.

**44:16** They are led with gladness and exultation; they come into the king's palace.

**44:17** Instead of your fathers will be your sons; you will appoint them as princes throughout all the earth.

**44:18** I will make your name remembered in every generation; therefore peoples will praise you forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **44:1** — Shoshannim literally means lilies and likely denotes a melody or musical setting. Maskil, a Hiphil participle of the root skl, designates a contemplative or skillfully crafted psalm.
- **44:5** — The Hebrew pairs two Qal imperatives without a conjunction, succeed and ride forth, and joins humility and righteousness with a maqqef in an unusual absolute-noun compound, understood either as an asyndetic pair or as righteous humility.
- **44:6** — The middle clause, peoples fall beneath you, functions as a poetic parenthetical exclamation separating the subject and predicate of the arrow clause.
- **44:7** — Your throne, God renders elohim as a direct vocative, as in the ancient versions, though some interpreters construe the phrase as Your divine throne.
- **44:9** — The form minni is taken as a poetic plural of men, stringed instruments, as in Psalm 150:4; alternatively it may be a poetic form of the preposition min with an indefinite subject.
