## Text

**70:1** In You, LORD, I have taken refuge; may I never be put to shame.

**70:2** In Your righteousness rescue me and deliver me; turn Your ear toward me and save me.

**70:3** Be for me a rock of dwelling to which I can continually come; You have issued the command to save me, for You are my crag and my fortress.

**70:4** My God, deliver me from the hand of the wicked, from the grip of the unjust and the ruthless.

**70:5** For You are my hope, Lord GOD, my trust from my youth.

**70:6** On You I have leaned from the womb; from my mother's body You are the One who brought me out; in You is my praise continually.

**70:7** I have become like a wonder to many, yet You are my refuge of strength.

**70:8** Let my mouth be filled with Your praise, with Your splendor all day long.

**70:9** Do not cast me away in the time of old age; when my strength fails, do not abandon me.

**70:10** For my enemies have spoken about me, and those who watch for my life have taken counsel together,

**70:11** saying, "God has abandoned him; pursue and seize him, for there is no rescuer."

**70:12** God, do not be far from me; my God, hurry to my help!

**70:13** May the adversaries of my life be put to shame and come to an end; may those who seek my harm be wrapped in reproach and disgrace.

**70:14** But as for me, I will continually hope, and I will add to all Your praise.

**70:15** My mouth will recount Your righteousness, and Your salvation all day long, for I do not know their numbers.

**70:16** I will come with the mighty acts of the Lord GOD; I will call to remembrance Your righteousness, Yours alone.

**70:17** God, You have taught me from my youth, and until now I declare Your marvelous deeds.

**70:18** And even to old age and gray hair, God, do not abandon me, until I declare Your arm to the next generation, and Your might to everyone who will come.

**70:19** And Your righteousness, God, extends to the heights; You who have done great things, God, who is like You?

**70:20** You who have shown me many and bitter distresses will again restore me to life, and from the depths of the earth You will again bring me up.

**70:21** You will multiply my greatness, and turn and comfort me.

**70:22** I too will give thanks to You with the harp, for Your faithfulness, my God; I will sing praise to You with the lyre, Holy One of Israel.

**70:23** My lips will ring out with joy when I sing praise to You, and my soul, which You have ransomed.

**70:24** My tongue too will speak of Your righteousness all day long, for they have been put to shame, for they have been humiliated, those who seek my harm.

## Translator Notes

- **70:3** — The Masoretic text reads maon, dwelling or habitation, and tsivvita, You have commanded, whereas the Septuagint harmonizes with a similar psalm to read stronghold.
- **70:6** — Hebrew gozi is a rare Qal participle from guz or gazah, probably meaning the One who severed or brought me out at birth.
- **70:12** — Ketiv writes chishah, a rare spelling with yod, while qere reads chushah, the standard Qal imperative with paragogic he from chush meaning hurry, translated here.
- **70:15** — Hebrew plays on the root sfr between yesapper, Piel meaning will recount, and the rare plural noun seforot meaning numbers or counts.
- **70:20** — Twice in this verse ketiv writes first-person plural suffixes, shown us and restore us to life, while qere reads first-person singular, shown me and restore me to life, matching the final singular verb bring me up and translated here.
