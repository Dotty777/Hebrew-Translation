## Text

**26:1** Of David. The LORD is my light and my deliverance; whom will I fear? The LORD is the fortress of my life; whom will I dread?

**26:2** When those who do evil closed in against me to eat my flesh, those adversaries and enemies of mine, they themselves stumbled and fell.

**26:3** Even if an army camps against me, my heart will not be afraid; even if war rises up against me, in this I trust.

**26:4** One thing I have requested from the LORD; that I will seek: to dwell in the LORD's house all the days of my life, to gaze at the loveliness of the LORD and to inquire in His temple.

**26:5** For He will conceal me in His shelter on a day of calamity; He will hide me in the hiding place of His tent; on a rock He will raise me high.

**26:6** And now my head will rise high above my enemies surrounding me, and I will sacrifice in His tent sacrifices of joyful shouting; I will sing and play music to the LORD.

**26:7** Hear, LORD, my voice as I call, and show me favor and answer me.

**26:8** Concerning You my heart has said, "Seek My face." Your face, LORD, I will seek.

**26:9** Do not hide Your face from me; do not turn aside Your servant in anger. You have been my help; do not desert me and do not abandon me, God of my deliverance.

**26:10** For my father and my mother have abandoned me, yet the LORD will gather me in.

**26:11** Instruct me in Your way, LORD, and guide me on a level path because of those who lie in wait for me.

**26:12** Do not hand me over to the desire of my adversaries, because lying witnesses have risen up against me, and one who breathes out violence.

**26:13** If I had not believed I would see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living—

**26:14** Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart be resolute; and wait for the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **26:4** — Hebrew lebaqqer (Piel infinitive construct of bqr) can mean to inquire, inspect carefully, or contemplate in the sanctuary.
- **26:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew is famously elliptical, combining "concerning/for You" (leka) with the plural Piel imperative "Seek My face" (baqqeshu panay). The psalmist's heart recalls God's own command to His people and responds directly in the second clause.
- **26:13** — Hebrew lule ("if not") is marked in the Masoretic text with extraordinary dots above and below the letters indicating textual uncertainty, and the verse breaks off as an unfinished conditional sentence (aposiopesis) leaving the consequence unspoken.
- **26:14** — Hebrew ya'amets is a Hiphil jussive of 'mts used intransitively ("show strength, be resolute") with "your heart" as subject, paralleling the preceding Qal imperative "be strong."
