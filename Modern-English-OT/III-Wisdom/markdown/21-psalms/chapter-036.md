## Text

**36:1** Of David. Do not become agitated over those who do evil; do not be jealous of those who commit injustice.

**36:2** For they will swiftly wither like grass, and like green vegetation they will fade.

**36:3** Trust in the LORD and do what is good; dwell in the land and feed on faithfulness.

**36:4** Find your delight in the LORD, and He will give you the requests of your heart.

**36:5** Roll your way onto the LORD, trust in Him, and He Himself will act.

**36:6** And He will bring out your righteousness like the light, and your justice like midday.

**36:7** Be silent before the LORD and wait expectantly for Him; do not become agitated over one who makes his way succeed, over a man who carries out schemes.

**36:8** Let go of anger and abandon fury; do not become agitated—it only results in doing evil.

**36:9** For those who do evil will be cut off, but those who wait for the LORD—they will possess the land.

**36:10** A little while longer, and the wicked person will be no more; you will look intently at his place, and he will not be there.

**36:11** But the humble will possess the land and find delight in abundant peace.

**36:12** The wicked person schemes against the righteous one and grinds his teeth at him.

**36:13** The Lord laughs at him, because He sees that his day will come.

**36:14** The wicked have unsheathed a sword and bent their bow, to make the afflicted and needy fall, to slaughter those of upright way.

**36:15** Their sword will go into their own heart, and their bows will be broken.

**36:16** A little belonging to the righteous person is better than the abundance of many wicked people.

**36:17** For the arms of the wicked will be broken, yet the LORD supports the righteous.

**36:18** The LORD knows the days of those who are blameless, and their inheritance will endure forever.

**36:19** They will not be put to shame in a time of calamity, and in days of famine they will have their fill.

**36:20** For the wicked will perish, and the enemies of the LORD are like the splendor of meadows; they are consumed—in smoke they are consumed.

**36:21** A wicked person borrows and does not repay, whereas a righteous person shows grace and gives.

**36:22** For the ones blessed by Him will possess the land, and the ones cursed by Him will be cut off.

**36:23** By the LORD a man's steps are made firm, and He takes pleasure in his way.

**36:24** Though he falls, he will not be hurled down, for the LORD supports his hand.

**36:25** I was young, and I have also grown old, yet I have not seen a righteous person abandoned or his seed seeking bread.

**36:26** Throughout the day he shows grace and lends, and his seed is a blessing.

**36:27** Turn aside from evil and do what is good, and dwell forever.

**36:28** For the LORD loves justice and will not abandon His faithful ones; they are guarded forever, whereas the seed of the wicked is cut off.

**36:29** The righteous will possess the land and dwell on it forever.

**36:30** A righteous person's mouth voices wisdom, and his tongue speaks justice.

**36:31** The instruction of his God is in his heart; his footsteps will not slip.

**36:32** A wicked person lies in wait for the righteous one and seeks to put him to death.

**36:33** The LORD will not abandon him in his hand, nor will He declare him guilty when he is judged.

**36:34** Wait for the LORD and observe His way, and He will raise you up to possess the land; when the wicked are cut off, you will look on.

**36:35** I have seen a ruthless wicked person spreading himself out like a lush native tree.

**36:36** Then he passed away, and look, he was gone; I sought him, yet he was not found.

**36:37** Observe the blameless and look at the upright, for a future belongs to a man of peace.

**36:38** But rebels will be destroyed together; the future of the wicked will be cut off.

**36:39** Now the salvation of the righteous comes from the LORD; He is their stronghold in a time of distress.

**36:40** And the LORD helps them and rescues them; He rescues them from the wicked and saves them, because they have taken refuge in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **36:1** — The verb rendered 'become agitated' is the Hithpael jussive of harah ('burn, become hot'), literally 'do not heat yourself up,' recurring in verses 7 and 8.
- **36:3** — The clause 'feed on faithfulness' translates the Qal imperative of ra'ah ('graze, feed'), though it may also denote tending or cultivating faithfulness.
- **36:20** — The phrase 'splendor of meadows' takes karim as pastures or meadows (echoing the withering grass in verse 2); alternatively, karim can mean 'lambs,' depicting burnt portions vanishing in smoke.
- **36:35** — The participle 'spreading himself out' is Hithpael of arah, and the noun rendered 'native tree' (ezrah) literally means 'a native,' here a tree rooted in its own soil.
- **36:36** — The Masoretic Text has the third-person verb 'he passed away,' shifting to the first-person 'I sought him' in the second half of the verse.
