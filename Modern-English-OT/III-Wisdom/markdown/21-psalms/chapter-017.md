## Text

**17:1** For the music director. Of the servant of the LORD, David, who spoke to the LORD the words of this song on the day the LORD rescued him from the grasp of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

**17:2** And he said, "I deeply love You, LORD, my strength.

**17:3** The LORD is my crag and my fortress and my deliverer, my God, my rock in whom I take refuge, my shield and the horn of my salvation, my high stronghold.

**17:4** I call out to the LORD, who is to be praised, and from my enemies I am saved.

**17:5** The ropes of death encircled me, and torrents of ruin terrified me.

**17:6** The ropes of Sheol surrounded me; the traps of death confronted me.

**17:7** In my distress I called to the LORD, and to my God I cried out for help; from His temple He heard my voice, and my cry for help before Him entered His ears.

**17:8** Then the earth heaved and quaked, and the foundations of the mountains shuddered and heaved to and fro, because He burned with anger.

**17:9** Smoke rose from His nostrils, and fire from His mouth consumed; coals blazed forth from Him.

**17:10** He bent the heavens and descended, and heavy darkness was beneath His feet.

**17:11** He rode a cherub and flew, and He soared on the wings of the wind.

**17:12** He set darkness as His secret place, His canopy around Him—darkness of waters, dense clouds of the skies.

**17:13** Out of the radiance before Him His thick clouds swept past—hail and coals of fire.

**17:14** And the LORD thundered in the heavens, and the Most High sounded His voice—hail and coals of fire.

**17:15** He sent out His arrows and scattered them, and He shot forth lightning bolts and threw them into panic.

**17:16** Then the channels of water came into view, and the foundations of the world were uncovered at Your rebuke, LORD, at the blast of the wind of Your nostrils.

**17:17** He reached out from on high, He took hold of me; He drew me out from great waters.

**17:18** He rescued me from my powerful enemy and from those who hated me, for they were stronger than I.

**17:19** They confronted me on the day of my disaster, yet the LORD became a support to me.

**17:20** He brought me out into an open space; He set me free, because He delighted in me.

**17:21** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the purity of my hands He repaid me.

**17:22** For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not turned wickedly away from my God.

**17:23** For all His judgments were before me, and His statutes I did not remove from me.

**17:24** And I was blameless with Him, and I guarded myself against my iniquity.

**17:25** So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness, according to the purity of my hands before His eyes.

**17:26** With the loyal You prove Yourself loyal; with a blameless man You prove Yourself blameless;

**17:27** with the pure You prove Yourself pure, and with the crooked You prove Yourself shrewd.

**17:28** For You save an afflicted people, but lofty eyes You bring low.

**17:29** For You give light to my lamp; the LORD my God brightens my darkness.

**17:30** For by You I rush through a raiding band, and by my God I leap over a wall.

**17:31** This God—His way is blameless; the word of the LORD is refined; He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

**17:32** For who is God apart from the LORD, and who is a rock besides our God?

**17:33** God is the one who girds me with might and makes my way blameless.

**17:34** He makes my feet like those of deer, and on my high places He makes me stand.

**17:35** He trains my hands for battle, and my arms bend a bow of bronze.

**17:36** You gave me the shield of Your salvation, and Your right hand sustains me, and Your humility makes me great.

**17:37** You widen my stride beneath me, and my ankles have not slipped.

**17:38** I pursued my enemies and caught up with them, and I did not turn back until I had finished them off.

**17:39** I crushed them, and they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet.

**17:40** You girded me with might for battle; You made those who rose against me bow down beneath me.

**17:41** And You turned the backs of my enemies toward me, and those who hated me I annihilated.

**17:42** They cried out for help, yet there was no one to save—even to the LORD, yet He did not answer them.

**17:43** Then I ground them fine like dust before the wind; like mud of the streets I poured them out.

**17:44** You delivered me from the disputes of the people; You appointed me head of the nations; a people I did not know serve me.

**17:45** The moment their ears hear, they obey me; foreigners cower before me.

**17:46** Foreigners wither away and come quaking out of their strongholds.

**17:47** The LORD lives, and blessed is my rock; let the God of my salvation be exalted—

**17:48** the God who grants vengeance to me and subdues peoples beneath me,

**17:49** who delivers me from my enemies. Indeed, above those who rise against me You raise me high; from the man of violence You rescue me.

**17:50** Therefore I will thank You among the nations, LORD, and to Your name I will sing praise.

**17:51** He magnifies the victories of His king and shows loyal love to His anointed, to David and to his offspring forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:2** — Hebrew erchamkha is the sole Qal occurrence of the root r-ch-m (elsewhere Piel or Pual, "have compassion"), expressing deep, heartfelt love.
- **17:15** — Hebrew rab may be parsed as a Qal perfect of r-b-b II ("shot/hurled," parallel to "sent out His arrows") or as an adjective or verb from r-b-b I ("in abundance/multiplied").
- **17:27** — Four Hithpael verbs in 17:26-27 express God's reciprocal dealing; titpattal literally means "show Yourself twisting/tortuous," that is, outmaneuvering the crooked shrewdly.
- **17:36** — Hebrew anvatekha literally means "Your humility" or "Your gentleness," denoting God's gracious condescension toward the psalmist.
- **17:46** — Hebrew yachregu is a hapax legomenon, understood from Aramaic cognates and context as "come quaking" or "emerge trembling."
