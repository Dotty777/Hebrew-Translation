## Text

**133:1** A song of the ascents. Now, bless the LORD, all servants of the LORD, who stand in the house of the LORD during the nights.

**133:2** Raise your hands toward the holy place, and bless the LORD.

**133:3** May the LORD bless you from Zion, the Maker of heaven and earth.

## Translator Notes

- **133:2** — The noun "holy place" (qodesh) stands without a preposition as an adverbial accusative, meaning either directionally "toward the sanctuary" (cf. Ps 28:2) or adverbially "in holiness."
- **133:3** — Hebrew shifts from the plural commands of verses 1-2 to a singular object pronoun ("bless you"), reflecting a blessing pronounced upon the individual worshiper or Israel collectively.
