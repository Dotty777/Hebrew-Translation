## Text

**32:1** Shout joyfully in the LORD, you righteous ones; praise is fitting for the upright.

**32:2** Give thanks to the LORD with a lyre; with a ten-stringed harp make melody to Him.

**32:3** Sing a new song to Him; play the strings skillfully with a shout of acclaim.

**32:4** For upright is the word of the LORD, and all His work is in faithfulness.

**32:5** He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the LORD's loyal love.

**32:6** Through the LORD's word the heavens were made, and through the breath of His mouth their entire host.

**32:7** He gathers the sea's waters like a mound; He places the deeps in storehouses.

**32:8** May all the earth fear the LORD; may all who inhabit the world be in awe of Him.

**32:9** For He Himself spoke, and it came into being; He Himself commanded, and it stood firm.

**32:10** The LORD annuls the counsel of nations; He thwarts the designs of peoples.

**32:11** The LORD's counsel stands firm forever, the designs of His heart through generation after generation.

**32:12** Blessed is the nation that has the LORD as its God, the people He chose as an inheritance for Himself.

**32:13** From the heavens the LORD gazes down; He sees all the sons of mankind.

**32:14** From the established place of His dwelling He looks intently upon all who inhabit the earth,

**32:15** He who forms their hearts altogether, He who discerns all their works.

**32:16** A king is not saved by a great force; a warrior is not rescued by great strength.

**32:17** A horse is a deception for salvation, and by its great might it cannot bring escape.

**32:18** Look, the LORD's eye is upon those who fear Him, upon those who wait for His loyal love,

**32:19** to rescue their soul from death and to preserve them alive during famine.

**32:20** Our soul has waited for the LORD; our help and our shield is He.

**32:21** For in Him our heart is glad, because in His holy name we have trusted.

**32:22** May Your loyal love, LORD, be upon us, just as we have waited for You.

## Translator Notes

- **32:7** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads kanned ('like a mound/heap,' as in Exodus 15:8), whereas the Septuagint and Syriac reflect a different vocalization meaning 'in a wineskin/jar.'
- **32:14** — Hebrew shivto ('His dwelling/sitting') shares the same verbal root yashav with yoshvei ('those who inhabit') at the end of the verse, contrasting the LORD's heavenly throne with all earth's dwellers.
- **32:16** — Hebrew chayil denotes military force or might here and again in verse 17 for the horse's strength, linking human armies and warhorses as inadequate sources of rescue.
