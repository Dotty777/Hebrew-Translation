## Text

**49:1** A psalm of Asaph. The Mighty One, God, the LORD has spoken and called the earth, from the sun's rising to its setting.

**49:2** From Zion, the fullness of beauty, God has shone forth.

**49:3** Our God comes; let Him not keep silent. Before Him fire consumes, and around Him it storms fiercely.

**49:4** He calls to the heavens from above and to the earth to judge His people:

**49:5** "Assemble to Me My loyal ones, those who made a covenant with Me over sacrifice."

**49:6** And the heavens proclaim His righteousness, for God Himself is the judge. Selah.

**49:7** "Listen, My people, and I will speak; Israel, and I will bear witness against you: I am God, your God.

**49:8** I do not rebuke you because of your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are before Me always.

**49:9** I will not take a bull from your house, nor male goats from your pens.

**49:10** For Mine is every wild animal of the forest, the cattle on a thousand mountains.

**49:11** I know all the birds of the mountains, and whatever moves in the field is with Me.

**49:12** If I grew hungry, I would not tell you, for Mine is the world and its fullness.

**49:13** Do I eat the flesh of mighty bulls, or drink the blood of male goats?

**49:14** Sacrifice thanksgiving to God, and fulfill your vows to the Most High,

**49:15** and call to Me in the day of distress; I will rescue you, and you will honor Me."

**49:16** But to the wicked God says, "What right do you have to recount My statutes and take My covenant upon your mouth?

**49:17** Yet you yourself hate discipline, and you throw My words behind you.

**49:18** When you see a thief, you gladly join with him, and your share is with adulterers.

**49:19** You unleash your mouth in evil, and your tongue fabricates deceit.

**49:20** You sit speaking against your brother; you cast slander against the son of your mother.

**49:21** These things you did, and I kept silent; you imagined that I was altogether like you. I will rebuke you and set it in order before your eyes.

**49:22** Understand this now, you who forget God, otherwise I will tear you apart, and there will be no rescuer.

**49:23** Whoever sacrifices thanksgiving honors Me, and to the one who directs his way I will show the salvation of God."

## Translator Notes

- **49:11** — זִיז שָׂדַי pairs the rare noun זִיז ('moving creatures') with the poetic form שָׂדַי ('field'), and עִמָּדִי literally means 'with Me.'
- **49:18** — וַתִּרֶץ is the apocopated Qal wayyiqtol of רָצָה ('be pleased with, agree/associate with'), rather than רוּץ ('run,' reflected in some ancient versions).
- **49:20** — תִּתֶּן־דֹּפִי literally means 'you put a blemish/fault,' using the hapax legomenon דֹּפִי ('blemish, slander').
- **49:23** — וְשָׂם דֶּרֶךְ is a compressed Masoretic expression literally meaning 'and sets a way,' understood as directing or ordering one's conduct.
