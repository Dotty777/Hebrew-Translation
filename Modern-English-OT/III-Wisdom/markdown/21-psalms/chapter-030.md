## Text

**30:1** For the music director. A psalm of David.

**30:2** In You, LORD, I have taken refuge; may I never be put to shame; in Your righteousness rescue me.

**30:3** Turn Your ear to me; deliver me quickly! Be for me a rock stronghold, a house of fortresses to save me.

**30:4** For You are my crag and my fortress, and for the sake of Your name You will direct me and guide me.

**30:5** You will bring me out of the net that they have hidden for me, for You are my stronghold.

**30:6** Into Your hand I entrust my spirit; You have redeemed me, LORD, God of truth.

**30:7** I hate those who pay heed to worthless vanities, but as for me, I trust in the LORD.

**30:8** I will exult and be glad in Your loyal love, because You have seen my affliction; You have known the distresses of my soul,

**30:9** and You have not surrendered me into the hand of an enemy; You have made my feet stand in a spacious place.

**30:10** Be gracious to me, LORD, because I am in distress; my eye has wasted away from grief—my soul and my body.

**30:11** For my life is consumed in sorrow and my years in groaning; my strength has faltered because of my iniquity, and my bones have wasted away.

**30:12** Because of all my foes I have become a disgrace—exceedingly so to my neighbors—and a dread to my acquaintances; those who see me outside flee from me.

**30:13** I have been forgotten from the heart like a dead man; I have become like a ruined vessel.

**30:14** For I have heard the slander of many—terror from all around; as they conspire together against me, they plot to take my life.

**30:15** Yet as for me, in You I trust, LORD; I have said, "You are my God."

**30:16** In Your hand are my times; deliver me from the hand of my enemies and from my pursuers.

**30:17** Make Your face shine upon Your servant; save me by Your loyal love.

**30:18** LORD, may I not be put to shame, for I have called on You; let the wicked be put to shame; let them fall silent in Sheol.

**30:19** May lips of falsehood become mute, those that speak insolence against the righteous with pride and contempt.

**30:20** How great is Your goodness, which You have stored up for those who fear You and performed for those who take refuge in You in the presence of the sons of mankind!

**30:21** You hide them in the hiding place of Your presence from the plots of men; You conceal them in a shelter from the contention of tongues.

**30:22** Blessed be the LORD, because He has wonderfully shown His loyal love to me in a besieged city.

**30:23** As for me, I said in my panic, "I have been cut off from before Your eyes." Yet indeed You heard the voice of my pleas for grace when I cried out to You for help.

**30:24** Love the LORD, all you His loyal ones! The LORD guards the faithful, yet repays in abundance the one who acts with pride.

**30:25** Be strong, and may your heart be courageous, all you who hope in the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **30:7** — Literally "vanities of emptiness," an idiom for worthless idols. The Masoretic Text reads first-person "I hate," whereas some ancient versions read "You hate."
- **30:11** — Following the Masoretic Text, "because of my iniquity"; some ancient versions reflect a different vocalization, "because of my affliction."
- **30:21** — The Hebrew noun rendered "plots" occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and derives from a root meaning "to bind together," hence leagues or conspiracies.
- **30:22** — The Hebrew phrase can mean either "in a besieged city," pointing to the crisis from which the LORD delivered the psalmist, or "in a fortified city," as a metaphor for divine protection.
