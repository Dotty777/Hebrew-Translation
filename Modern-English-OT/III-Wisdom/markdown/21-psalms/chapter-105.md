## Text

**105:1** Praise the LORD! Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good, for His loyal love lasts forever.

**105:2** Who can recount the mighty deeds of the LORD, or make all His praise heard?

**105:3** Blessed are those who keep justice, the one who practices righteousness at all times.

**105:4** Remember me, LORD, with Your favor for Your people; attend to me with Your salvation,

**105:5** so that I may see the welfare of Your chosen ones, be glad in the gladness of Your nation, and exult alongside Your heritage.

**105:6** We have sinned along with our fathers; we have acted perversely, we have acted wickedly.

**105:7** Our fathers in Egypt did not comprehend Your wondrous deeds; they did not remember the abundance of Your acts of loyal love, and they rebelled beside the sea, at the Sea of Reeds.

**105:8** Yet He saved them for the sake of His name, to make His might known.

**105:9** He rebuked the Sea of Reeds, and it dried up; then He led them through the depths as across a wilderness.

**105:10** And He saved them from the hand of one who hated them, and He redeemed them from the enemy's hand.

**105:11** The waters covered their foes; not a single one of them remained.

**105:12** So they believed in His words; they sang out His praise.

**105:13** They were quick to forget His deeds; they did not await His counsel.

**105:14** And they longed with fierce craving in the wilderness, and tested God in the wasteland.

**105:15** So He granted them their request, yet He sent a wasting illness into their souls.

**105:16** And they grew jealous of Moses in the camp, and of Aaron, the LORD's holy one.

**105:17** The ground opened up and swallowed Dathan, and covered over the assembly of Abiram.

**105:18** And fire blazed in their assembly; a flame devoured the wicked.

**105:19** They made a calf at Horeb, and bowed down to a molten image.

**105:20** And they traded their glory for the likeness of an ox that eats grass.

**105:21** They forgot God, their Savior, the One who performed great deeds in Egypt,

**105:22** wondrous deeds in the land of Ham, fearsome deeds beside the Sea of Reeds.

**105:23** Then He declared He would destroy them—had Moses, His chosen one, not stood in the breach before Him to turn back His fury from bringing ruin.

**105:24** And they rejected the desirable land; they did not believe His word.

**105:25** And they grumbled in their tents; they did not heed the voice of the LORD.

**105:26** So He lifted His hand to them to cause them to fall in the wilderness,

**105:27** and to cause their seed to fall among the nations, and to scatter them across the lands.

**105:28** Then they bound themselves to Baal of Peor, and they ate sacrifices to the dead.

**105:29** So they provoked Him to anger by their practices, and a plague burst out among them.

**105:30** But Phinehas stood up and executed judgment, and the plague was halted.

**105:31** And it was credited to him as righteousness, for generation after generation, forever.

**105:32** And they provoked Him to wrath at the waters of Meribah, and it went badly for Moses on account of them,

**105:33** for they rebelled against His Spirit, and he spoke thoughtlessly with his lips.

**105:34** They did not destroy the peoples about whom the LORD had spoken to them.

**105:35** Instead, they mingled among the nations and learned their deeds.

**105:36** And they served their idols, and these became a trap for them.

**105:37** And they sacrificed their sons and their daughters to the demons,

**105:38** and poured out innocent blood—the blood of their sons and their daughters whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan—and the land was profaned by bloodshed.

**105:39** So they became unclean through their deeds, and they prostituted themselves in their practices.

**105:40** Therefore the LORD's anger burned against His people, and He detested His heritage.

**105:41** And He turned them over into the hand of nations, and those who hated them ruled over them.

**105:42** And their enemies oppressed them, and they were subdued beneath their hand.

**105:43** Many times He would rescue them, yet they kept rebelling in their counsel and sank low because of their iniquity.

**105:44** Yet He looked on their distress when He heard their outcry.

**105:45** And He remembered His covenant on their behalf, and He relented in accordance with the abundance of His acts of loyal love.

**105:46** And He caused them to find compassion before all their captors.

**105:47** Save us, LORD our God, and gather us out of the nations, to give thanks to Your holy name, and to boast in Your praise.

**105:48** Blessed be the LORD, God of Israel, from eternity to eternity! And let all the people say, "Amen!" Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **105:20** — The Masoretic Text reads "their glory," referring to God as Israel's glory. Ancient Jewish scribal tradition lists this among the Tiqqune Soferim, where an earlier reading "My glory" or "His glory" was discussed.
- **105:26** — "Lifted His hand" is a Biblical Hebrew idiom for swearing a solemn oath (cf. Num 14:30; Ezek 20:23). In v. 27, the Masoretic Text repeats the Hiphil infinitive of npl ("to cause to fall") rather than "to scatter" as in the parallel Ezekiel 20:23.
- **105:30** — The verb translated "executed judgment" is the Piel of pll, meaning to intervene, arbitrate, or execute judgment (distinct from the Hithpael, "to pray"), fitting Phinehas's decisive action in Numbers 25:7-8.
- **105:33** — The verb translated "rebelled against" is the Hiphil of mrh (as in vv. 7 and 43). Because Moses is named in v. 32 and is the subject of the next clause, "His Spirit" may alternatively be understood as "his spirit" (Moses's spirit), taking the verb in the sense of provoking or embittering him.
- **105:45** — The Hebrew form for "His acts of loyal love" is written defectively without a yod (resembling the singular Ketiv) but vocalized as plural, matching the plural Qere tradition and v. 7. The verb "relented" is the Niphal of nhm, expressing God's turning from judgment in compassion.
