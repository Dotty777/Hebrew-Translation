## Text

**84:1** For the music director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.

**84:2** LORD, You favored Your land; You turned back the captivity of Jacob.

**84:3** You pardoned the iniquity of Your people; You covered over all their sin. Selah.

**84:4** You drew back all Your wrath; You turned back from the burning of Your anger.

**84:5** Restore us, God of our salvation, and put an end to Your indignation with us.

**84:6** Will You stay angry with us forever? Will You draw out Your anger to generation after generation?

**84:7** Will You not turn and give us life again, so that Your people may be glad in You?

**84:8** Show us, LORD, Your loyal love, and give us Your salvation.

**84:9** I will listen to what God the LORD will speak, for He will speak peace to His people and to His loyal ones; and may they not turn back to folly.

**84:10** Truly, His salvation is close to those who fear Him, for glory to dwell in our land.

**84:11** Loyal love and truth have met one another; righteousness and peace have kissed.

**84:12** Truth sprouts from the earth, and righteousness gazes down from heaven.

**84:13** The LORD will also give what is good, and our land will give its produce.

**84:14** Righteousness will walk ahead of Him and make a path of His steps.

## Translator Notes

- **84:2** — Ketiv reads shvut ("fortunes" or "captivity"), while the Qere followed here reads shevit ("captivity," derived from shabah, "take captive").
- **84:5** — The verb shubenu is a Qal imperative of shuv ("turn, return") with an object suffix used transitively to mean "restore us," continuing the psalm's recurring shuv motif (vv. 2, 4, 5, 7, 9).
- **84:14** — The terse second clause literally reads "and will make His steps for a path," personifying righteousness as a herald going ahead of the LORD and turning His footsteps into a way to follow.
