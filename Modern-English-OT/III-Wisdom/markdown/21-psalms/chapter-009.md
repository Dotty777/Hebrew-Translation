## Text

**9:1** For the music director, according to Muth-labben. A psalm of David.

**9:2** I will thank the LORD with all my heart; I will recount all Your wonders.

**9:3** I will rejoice and exult in You; I will sing praises to Your name, Most High.

**9:4** When my enemies turn backward, they stumble and perish from before You.

**9:5** For You have upheld my right and my cause; You sat on the throne, judging righteously.

**9:6** You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked one; their name You have wiped out forever and ever.

**9:7** As for the enemy, they are finished in ruins forever, and cities You have uprooted; the very memory of them has perished.

**9:8** Yet the LORD sits enthroned forever; for judgment He has established His throne.

**9:9** And He Himself will judge the world in righteousness; He will pass judgment on the peoples in equity.

**9:10** So the LORD will be a high refuge for the oppressed, a high refuge in times of distress.

**9:11** And those who know Your name will trust in You, for You have not abandoned those who seek You, LORD.

**9:12** Sing praises to the LORD, who is enthroned in Zion; proclaim His deeds among the peoples.

**9:13** For He who demands an accounting for bloodshed remembers them; He does not forget the outcry of the humble.

**9:14** Show me grace, LORD; look at my affliction from those who hate me, You who raise me up from the gates of death,

**9:15** so that I may recount all Your praises in the gates of Daughter Zion; I will rejoice in Your salvation.

**9:16** Nations have sunk into the pit they made; in the net they hid, their foot has been trapped.

**9:17** The LORD has made Himself known; He has carried out judgment; the wicked one is snared by the work of his hands. Higgaion. Selah.

**9:18** The wicked will return to Sheol—all nations forgetful of God.

**9:19** For the needy will not be forgotten perpetually, nor will the hope of the afflicted perish forever.

**9:20** Arise, LORD; do not let mortal man prevail; let the nations be judged in Your presence.

**9:21** Place dread upon them, LORD; let the nations know they are mortal. Selah.

**9:22** Why, LORD, do You stand at a distance and hide Yourself in times of distress?

**9:23** In arrogance the wicked one hotly pursues the afflicted; let them be seized in the schemes they have devised.

**9:24** For the wicked one boasts over the desire of his soul; he blesses the greedy and spurns the LORD.

**9:25** The wicked one, in the haughtiness of his face—"He will not call to account; there is no God"—such are all his thoughts.

**9:26** His ways endure at all times; Your judgments are high above, beyond his sight; as for all his adversaries, he scoffs at them.

**9:27** He says in his heart, "I will not be shaken; from generation to generation I will not be in misfortune."

**9:28** His mouth is full of cursing, deceits, and oppression; beneath his tongue are trouble and iniquity.

**9:29** He sits in ambush among the villages; in concealed places he kills the innocent; his eyes watch in secret for the helpless.

**9:30** He lies in wait in a concealed place like a lion in its thicket; he lies in wait to snatch the afflicted; he snatches the afflicted as he drags him into his net.

**9:31** He crouches, he stoops low, and the company of the downcast falls by his strong ones.

**9:32** He says in his heart, "God has forgotten; He has concealed His face; He will never see."

**9:33** Arise, LORD! God, raise Your hand; do not forget the humble.

**9:34** Why has the wicked one spurned God? He has said in his heart, "You will not call to account."

**9:35** You have seen—for You Yourself look upon trouble and grief to take it into Your hand; to You the helpless entrusts himself; for the orphan You have been a helper.

**9:36** Break the arm of the wicked one; as for the evildoer, call his wickedness to account until You find none.

**9:37** The LORD is King forever and ever; the nations have perished out of His land.

**9:38** The desire of the humble You have heard, LORD; You will steady their heart; You will make Your ear attentive,

**9:39** to render judgment for the orphan and the oppressed, so that mortal man from the earth will terrify no more.

## Translator Notes

- **9:13** — Following the Masoretic Qere anawim ("the humble"); the Ketiv reads aniyyim ("the afflicted").
- **9:19** — Following the Masoretic Qere aniyyim ("the afflicted"); the Ketiv reads anawim ("the humble").
- **9:21** — Hebrew morah is an orthographic variant of mora ("dread, terror"), with final he written in place of aleph.
- **9:24** — Following the Masoretic accents, berekh (Piel of barakh, "bless") takes the greedy person as its object; alternatively, the greedy person is the subject and berekh is used euphemistically for "curse," yielding "and the greedy one curses and spurns the LORD."
- **9:31** — Following two Masoretic Qere readings: yidkeh (Qal imperfect of dakah, "crouch, be crushed"; Ketiv we-dakhah) and two words chel ka'im ("company of the downcast," agreeing with the singular verb nafal; Ketiv chelka'im, "the helpless").
- **9:33** — Following the Masoretic Qere anawim ("the humble"); the Ketiv reads aniyyim ("the afflicted").
