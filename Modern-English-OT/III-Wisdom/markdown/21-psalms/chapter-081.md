## Text

**81:1** A psalm of Asaph. God has stationed Himself in the assembly of God; in the midst of the gods He judges:

**81:2** "How long will you judge with injustice and show favor to the wicked? Selah.

**81:3** Judge for the weak and the orphan; vindicate the afflicted and the poor.

**81:4** Deliver the weak and the needy; rescue them from the hand of the wicked.

**81:5** They do not know, nor do they understand; they go about in darkness; all the foundations of the earth totter.

**81:6** I Myself said, 'You are gods, and you are all sons of the Most High.'

**81:7** Yet like mortals you will die, and like one of the princes you will fall."

**81:8** Rise up, God, judge the earth, for You Yourself inherit all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **81:1** — Hebrew ba'adat-'el ("in the assembly of God") can also be rendered "in the divine assembly"; the second 'elohim ("gods") refers to heavenly beings or human judges summoned before God.
- **81:2** — Hebrew "lift up the faces of the wicked" (tise'u ... pene resha'im) is an idiom for showing judicial partiality; "Selah" is a liturgical or musical direction of uncertain meaning.
- **81:7** — Hebrew ke'adam can be understood collectively as "like mortals" or as a proper name, "like Adam."
