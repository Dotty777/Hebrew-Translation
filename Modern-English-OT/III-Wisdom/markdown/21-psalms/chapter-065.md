## Text

**65:1** For the music director. A song. A psalm. Raise a joyful shout to God, all the earth.

**65:2** Sing praise to the glory of His name; render His praise glorious.

**65:3** Say to God, "How awe-inspiring are Your deeds! Because of the abundance of Your strength, Your enemies will cringe before You.

**65:4** All the earth will bow down to You and will sing praise to You; they will sing praise to Your name." Selah.

**65:5** Come and see the works of God; He is awe-inspiring in deed toward the sons of mankind.

**65:6** He turned the sea into dry ground; they crossed through the river on foot; there let us rejoice in Him.

**65:7** He rules in His might forever; His eyes keep watch over the nations; let the rebellious not lift themselves up. Selah.

**65:8** Bless our God, you peoples, and make the sound of His praise heard,

**65:9** the One who sets our soul in life and has not allowed our foot to slip.

**65:10** For You have tested us, God; You refined us like the refining of silver.

**65:11** You brought us into the net; You placed a crushing weight upon our loins.

**65:12** You caused men to ride over our heads; we went into fire and into water, yet You brought us out to overflowing abundance.

**65:13** I will enter Your house with burnt offerings; I will fulfill my vows to You,

**65:14** which my lips voiced and my mouth spoke when I was in distress.

**65:15** I will offer up to You burnt offerings of fattened animals, along with the smoke of rams; I will prepare bulls with male goats. Selah.

**65:16** Come, listen—all you who fear God—and let me recount what He has done for my soul.

**65:17** To Him I called out with my mouth, and high praise was under my tongue.

**65:18** If I had regarded iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have heard.

**65:19** Yet surely God has heard; He has paid attention to the voice of my prayer.

**65:20** Blessed be God, who has not turned aside my prayer or His faithful love from me.

## Translator Notes

- **65:3** — The Piel verb yekachashu (from the root kachash, "to act falsely" or "deny") paired with the preposition le denotes cringing or feigning submission before a superior power.
- **65:7** — The Qere (read form) yarumu is a Qal imperfect meaning "lift themselves up" or "exalt themselves" (with the ethical dative lamo, "for themselves"), whereas the Ketiv (written form) yrymw represents a Hiphil form ("raise up").
- **65:11** — The noun muaqah ("crushing weight") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and derives from a root meaning "to press" or "squeeze."
- **65:17** — The form romam may be parsed either as a noun meaning "high praise" or as a Polal perfect ("He was extolled"); "under my tongue" is a Hebrew idiom for words kept ready on the tongue.
