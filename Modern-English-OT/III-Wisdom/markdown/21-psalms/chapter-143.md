## Text

**143:1** Of David. Blessed be the LORD, my rock, who trains my hands for combat, my fingers for war.

**143:2** He is my faithful love and my fortress, my high refuge and my own rescuer, my shield, and in Him I take refuge, the One who subdues my people beneath me.

**143:3** LORD, what is a human being, that You know him, a son of mortal man, that You consider him?

**143:4** A human being resembles a breath; his days are like a shadow passing by.

**143:5** LORD, bend down Your heavens and descend; touch the mountains, and let them smoke.

**143:6** Flash lightning and scatter them; send Your arrows and throw them into confusion.

**143:7** Reach out Your hands from on high; free me and deliver me from many waters, from the hand of foreigners,

**143:8** whose mouths speak untruth, and whose right hand is a right hand of deception.

**143:9** God, I will sing a new song to You; with a harp of ten strings I will make music to You,

**143:10** the One who gives salvation to kings, who frees David His servant from an evil sword.

**143:11** Free me and deliver me from the hand of foreigners, whose mouths speak untruth, and whose right hand is a right hand of deception,

**143:12** so that our sons may be like saplings grown large in their youth, our daughters like corner pillars carved in the design of a palace;

**143:13** our storehouses full, bringing forth one kind after another; our flocks producing thousands, even tens of thousands in our open fields;

**143:14** our cattle heavily laden; no breach and no going out, and no outcry in our open squares.

**143:15** Happy is the people for whom it is like this; happy is the people whose God is the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **143:2** — The MT reads עַמִּי ("my people"); several Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions (LXX, Syriac, Targum) read עַמִּים ("peoples"). The noun חַסְדִּי ("my faithful love") is applied directly to God as a personal epithet.
- **143:6** — The Qal imperative בְּרוֹק ("flash") is the sole verbal occurrence of the root ברק in the Hebrew Bible, paired here with its cognate noun בָּרָק ("lightning").
- **143:12** — The particle אֲשֶׁר functions here as a purpose or result conjunction ("so that"), linking the petition for deliverance in v. 11 to the blessings of vv. 12-14. Both מְגֻדָּלִים ("grown large") and מְחֻטָּבוֹת ("carved") are Pual participles.
- **143:14** — The Pual participle מְסֻבָּלִים ("heavily laden," from סבל) with אַלּוּפֵינוּ ("our cattle") and the clauses "no breach and no going out" can refer either to city security (no breached wall or exile) or to livestock fertility (cattle heavy with young, suffering no miscarriage or loss).
