## Text

**51:1** For the music director. A Maskil of David.

**51:2** When Doeg the Edomite came and reported to Saul and said to him, "David has come to Ahimelech's house."

**51:3** Why do you boast in evil, mighty warrior? God's loyal love continues all day long.

**51:4** Your tongue plans ruin, like a sharpened razor, you doer of deceit.

**51:5** You love evil over good, falsehood over speaking righteousness. Selah.

**51:6** You love all devouring words, you tongue of deceit.

**51:7** God too will demolish you permanently; He will snatch you up and rip you from the tent, and pull you up by the roots from the land of the living. Selah.

**51:8** And the righteous will look on and fear, and they will laugh at him:

**51:9** "Here is the man who would not make God his stronghold, but trusted in the abundance of his wealth; he grew strong in his ruin."

**51:10** But I am like a lush olive tree in God's house; I trust in God's loyal love forever and ever.

**51:11** I will give thanks to You forever because You have acted, and I will wait for Your name, since it is good, before Your loyal ones.

## Translator Notes

- **51:3** — The second clause is a terse verbless statement in the Masoretic Text, literally "God's loyal love all day long," contrasting God's ongoing faithfulness with the warrior's boasting.
- **51:7** — The verb shereshka is a privative Piel of shoresh, "root," meaning "to pull up by the roots," forming a sharp contrast with the lush olive tree in verse 10.
- **51:9** — The noun hawwato, translated "his ruin," repeats the root hawwot from verse 4; it can denote either destructive calamity or, from a homonymous root, greedy desire.
- **51:11** — Translates the Masoretic verb aqawweh, Piel of qawah meaning "to wait or hope for," rather than emending the text to "proclaim."
