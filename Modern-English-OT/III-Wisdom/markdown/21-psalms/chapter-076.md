## Text

**76:1** For the music director, according to Jeduthun. A psalm of Asaph.

**76:2** My voice goes to God, and I cry out; my voice goes to God, and He will give ear to me.

**76:3** In the day of my distress I sought the Lord; at night my hand was stretched out and did not grow numb; my soul refused to be comforted.

**76:4** I remember God and I groan; I meditate, and my spirit grows faint. Selah.

**76:5** You held my eyelids open; I was deeply troubled and could not speak.

**76:6** I pondered the days of old, the years of ancient times.

**76:7** I remember my song in the night; with my heart I meditate, and my spirit searches diligently:

**76:8** Will the Lord cast off forever, and never again show favor?

**76:9** Has His faithful love come to an end forever? Has His promise ceased for all generations?

**76:10** Has God forgotten to show grace? Or has He in anger closed off His compassion? Selah.

**76:11** And I said, "This is my affliction: that the right hand of the Most High has changed."

**76:12** I will remember the acts of the LORD; indeed, I will remember Your wonders from of old.

**76:13** And I will reflect on all Your work, and on Your deeds I will meditate.

**76:14** God, Your way is in holiness. Who is a god as great as God?

**76:15** You are the God who does wonders; You have made Your strength known among the peoples.

**76:16** You redeemed Your people with Your arm, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

**76:17** The waters saw You, God; the waters saw You and convulsed; indeed, the depths trembled.

**76:18** The clouds poured down water; the skies gave voice; even Your arrows darted to and fro.

**76:19** The voice of Your thunder was in the whirlwind; lightning flashes illuminated the world; the earth trembled and quaked.

**76:20** Your way was in the sea, and Your path in great waters, and Your footprints were not known.

**76:21** You guided Your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

## Translator Notes

- **76:1** — Ketiv reads "Jedithun"; Qere reads "Jeduthun," the name of a Levitical chief musician.
- **76:3** — The Niphal perfect verb rendered "was stretched out" normally means "was poured out" or "flowed," here used of a hand extended without ceasing in nightlong prayer.
- **76:11** — This verse is a noted Hebrew crux: the form rendered "my affliction" can alternatively be parsed as a Piel infinitive meaning "my appeal," and "the changing of" (Qal infinitive construct of the verb to change) has the same form as the construct plural noun "the years of."
- **76:12** — Ketiv reads a Hiphil imperfect ("I will recall" or "cause to be remembered"); Qere reads a Qal imperfect ("I will remember"). The divine name here is the shortened poetic form of the LORD's name.
- **76:20** — Ketiv reads the plural "Your paths"; Qere reads the singular "Your path."
