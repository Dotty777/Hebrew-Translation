## Text

**1:1** Blessed is the man who has not walked by the counsel of the wicked, has not stood on the path of sinners, and has not sat in the seat of mockers.

**1:2** Instead, his delight is in the instruction of the LORD, and he meditates on His instruction day and night.

**1:3** And he will be like a tree planted beside channels of water, which gives its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not fade; and everything he does will succeed.

**1:4** The wicked are not like this; instead, they are like chaff that the wind drives off.

**1:5** For this reason, the wicked will not stand up in the judgment, nor sinners in the community of the righteous.

**1:6** For the LORD knows the path of the righteous, yet the path of the wicked will perish.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Hebrew torah literally means "instruction" or "teaching." The Qal verb rendered "meditates" denotes murmuring or reciting softly in reflection, and the same root recurs in 2:1 for the peoples plotting emptiness.
- **1:3** — The noun rendered "channels" (palge) refers specifically to divided watercourses or irrigation canals. The final verb is Hiphil imperfect, functioning either internally ("everything he does will succeed") or causatively ("he brings everything he does to success").
- **1:5** — In this legal setting ("in the judgment"), the Qal verb "stand up" means to withstand scrutiny or remain acquitted rather than mere physical posture.
