## Text

**79:1** For the music director, according to Lilies. A testimony; a psalm of Asaph.

**79:2** Shepherd of Israel, give ear, You who guide Joseph like a flock; You who sit enthroned on the cherubim, shine forth.

**79:3** Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, awaken Your might, and come for our salvation.

**79:4** God, restore us, and make Your face shine, that we may be saved.

**79:5** LORD God of hosts, how long will You fume against the prayer of Your people?

**79:6** You have fed them bread of tears and made them drink tears by the large measure.

**79:7** You make us a cause of strife to our neighbors, and our enemies mock among themselves.

**79:8** God of hosts, restore us, and make Your face shine, that we may be saved.

**79:9** You brought a vine from Egypt; You expelled nations and planted it.

**79:10** You cleared space before it, and it put down its roots and filled the land.

**79:11** Mountains were covered by its shade, and the cedars of God by its branches.

**79:12** It sent out its boughs as far as the sea, and its young shoots to the River.

**79:13** Why have You breached its walls, so that all who pass along the road pluck from it?

**79:14** A boar from the forest gnaws it down, and creatures of the field graze on it.

**79:15** God of hosts, please turn back; look down from heaven and see, and watch over this vine,

**79:16** and the rootstock that Your right hand planted, and over the son whom You strengthened for Yourself.

**79:17** It is burned by fire, it is cut off; at the rebuke of Your face they perish.

**79:18** May Your hand be on the man of Your right hand, on the son of man whom You strengthened for Yourself.

**79:19** So we will not turn away from You; give us life, and we will call on Your name.

**79:20** LORD God of hosts, restore us; make Your face shine, that we may be saved.

## Translator Notes

- **79:5** — As elsewhere in the Elohistic Psalter (see also 79:8, 15, 20 [MT 80:8, 15, 20]), Elohim stands in the absolute state rather than construct Elohei before Tsevaot ("hosts"). The Qal perfect verb ashanta literally means "you have smoked" or "fumed," a metaphor for smoldering anger.
- **79:6** — Hebrew shalish literally denotes "a third" (a substantial dry or liquid measure, cf. Isa 40:12), used metaphorically here for tears poured out in large measure.
- **79:11** — The phrase arzei-el (literally "cedars of God") is a Hebrew idiom for towering, mighty cedars; the Pual perfect verb kossu ("were covered") from the first clause carries over chiastically into the second.
- **79:14** — The quadriliteral verb yekharsemennah (from krsm) is a biblical hapax legomenon meaning to gnaw off or strip bare, and ziz sadai is a poetic phrase for wild moving creatures of the open field.
- **79:16** — Hebrew kannah is a hapax legomenon usually taken as a noun meaning "stock" or "rootstock" (related to ken, "base"), though some parse it as a Qal imperative meaning "protect"; the Masoretic Text reads simply ben ("son") rather than "son of man" as in 79:18 (MT 80:18).
