## Text

**37:1** A psalm of David, to bring to remembrance.

**37:2** LORD, do not reprove me in Your wrath, nor discipline me in Your fury.

**37:3** For Your arrows have sunk down into me, and Your hand has descended upon me.

**37:4** Nothing is sound in my flesh on account of Your indignation; there is no peace in my bones on account of my sin.

**37:5** For my iniquities have passed over my head; like a weighty load, they are too weighty for me.

**37:6** My wounds reek; they fester on account of my foolishness.

**37:7** I am bent and bowed low to the utmost; throughout the day I walk about mourning.

**37:8** For my flanks are full of burning, and nothing is sound in my flesh.

**37:9** I am numbed and crushed to the utmost; I roar from the groaning of my heart.

**37:10** Lord, before You is all my longing, and my sighing is not concealed from You.

**37:11** My heart pounds, my strength has abandoned me, and the light of my eyes—even that is not with me.

**37:12** Those who love me and my companions stand aloof from my affliction, and my relatives stand at a distance.

**37:13** Those seeking my life set snares, and those pursuing my harm speak of ruin; throughout the day they mutter deceits.

**37:14** Yet I, like a deaf person, do not hear; I am like a mute person who does not open his mouth.

**37:15** And I became like a man who does not hear, and in whose mouth there are no rebuttals.

**37:16** For it is You, LORD, for whom I wait; You Yourself will answer, Lord my God.

**37:17** For I said, "Do not let them gloat over me; when my foot slips, they exalt themselves over me."

**37:18** For I am about to stumble, and my pain is before me continually.

**37:19** For I declare my iniquity; I am anxious over my sin.

**37:20** Yet my enemies are vigorous and have grown mighty, and those who hate me falsely have grown numerous.

**37:21** And those who repay evil in return for good act as adversaries against me because I pursue what is good.

**37:22** Do not abandon me, LORD; my God, do not stay far from me.

**37:23** Hurry to my help, Lord, my salvation.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — The heading 'to bring to remembrance' translates the Hiphil infinitive construct of zakar, likely a liturgical designation for invoking God's remembrance or accompanying a memorial offering.
- **37:8** — The word 'burning' translates the Niphal participle of qalah ('roast, parch'), describing feverish inflammation in the flanks.
- **37:11** — The verb 'pounds' is a rare reduplicated Pealal form of sahar ('go around'), depicting a palpitating heart; the plural pronoun translated 'even that' refers loosely back to the light of the eyes.
- **37:20** — The Masoretic Text reads hayyim ('living, vigorous'), contrasting the vitality of the psalmist's enemies with his own weakened condition.
- **37:21** — Ketiv reads a fuller orthographic spelling (rdwpy) and Qere reads rodfi (Qal infinitive construct of radaph with first-person suffix, 'my pursuing'); the translation follows the Qere with no difference in sense.
