## Text

**107:1** A song, a psalm of David.

**107:2** My heart is firm, God; I will sing and make music, even with my glory.

**107:3** Wake up, harp and lyre; I will awaken the dawn.

**107:4** I will give thanks to You among the peoples, LORD, and I will make music to You among the nations.

**107:5** For great above the heavens is Your loyal love, and as far as the clouds is Your faithfulness.

**107:6** Be exalted above the heavens, God, and may Your glory be over all the earth.

**107:7** So that Your beloved ones may be rescued, save by Your right hand and answer me.

**107:8** God spoke in His holiness: "I will exult; I will divide Shechem, and I will measure off the valley of Succoth.

**107:9** Mine is Gilead, Mine is Manasseh; and Ephraim is the stronghold of My head; Judah is My ruler's staff.

**107:10** Moab is My washing basin; onto Edom I will throw My sandal; over Philistia I will shout triumphantly."

**107:11** Who will lead me into the fortified city? Who will guide me as far as Edom?

**107:12** Have You not cast us off, God? And You do not go out, God, with our armies.

**107:13** Grant us help against the foe, for salvation by man is worthless.

**107:14** Through God we will perform valiant deeds, and He Himself will trample our foes.

## Translator Notes

- **107:2** — Hebrew af-kevodi ("even [with] my glory") uses "my glory" as a poetic idiom for one's inner self or soul, functioning adverbially or appositionally with the preceding cohortatives.
- **107:8** — Hebrew be-qodsho can mean either "in His holiness" or "in His sanctuary."
- **107:9** — Hebrew mehoqeqi is the Poel active participle of haqaq ("inscribe, decree") with a first-person suffix, denoting either a commander's scepter ("My ruler's staff") or "My lawgiver."
- **107:10** — Hebrew etroa is the first-person Hithpolel imperfect of rua ("shout in triumph"), continuing God's direct speech over Philistia.
