## Text

**71:1** For Solomon. God, give Your judgments to the king, and Your righteousness to the son of the king.

**71:2** Let him judge Your people in righteousness, and Your afflicted ones with justice.

**71:3** May the mountains bear peace for the people, and the hills through righteousness.

**71:4** Let him defend the afflicted of the people, save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.

**71:5** May they fear You while the sun shines, and while the moon endures, generation after generation.

**71:6** May he descend like rain on mown grass, like showers drenching the earth.

**71:7** May the righteous flourish in his days, and an abundance of peace until there is no moon.

**71:8** And let him exercise dominion from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth.

**71:9** Before him may the dwellers of the desert kneel, and may his enemies lick the dust.

**71:10** May the kings of Tarshish and the coastlands pay tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba present gifts.

**71:11** And may all kings bow low to him; may all nations serve him.

**71:12** For he will rescue the needy who cries for help, and the afflicted who has no helper.

**71:13** He will take pity on the weak and the needy, and he will save the lives of the needy.

**71:14** From oppression and from violence he will redeem their life, and their blood will be precious in his eyes.

**71:15** And may he live, and may some of the gold of Sheba be given to him; may prayer be offered for him continually, and may he be blessed all day long.

**71:16** May there be an abundance of grain in the land on the top of the mountains; may its fruit rustle like Lebanon, and may those from the city blossom like the grass of the earth.

**71:17** May his name remain forever; as long as the sun endures, may his name continue; may people bless themselves by him; may all nations pronounce him blessed.

**71:18** Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone performs wondrous deeds.

**71:19** And blessed be the name of His glory forever, and may the entire earth be filled with His glory. Amen and Amen.

**71:20** The prayers of David son of Jesse are finished.

## Translator Notes

- **71:5** — Masoretic Hebrew reads yir'uka (Qal imperfect of yare', "may they fear You"), addressing God directly, with the literal temporal idioms "with the sun" and "before the moon" expressing perpetual duration. Some ancient versions such as the Septuagint instead reflect a different verb meaning "may he endure."
- **71:15** — The verbs "give," "pray," and "bless" are third-person masculine singular with an indefinite subject, idiomatically rendered as passive ("be given," "prayer be offered," "be blessed"); alternatively, the rescued needy person of verses 12-14 may be the subject.
- **71:16** — The noun pissah ("abundance") is a biblical hapax legomenon, usually linked to an Aramaic root meaning "spread out" or "expand," though some understand it as "a handful."
- **71:17** — Qere yinnon (Niphal imperfect of the rare root nun, cognate with nin "offspring") means "may his name continue" or "be perpetuated"; Ketiv ynyn (vocalized as Hiphil yanin) means "may his name increase" or "put forth shoots."
