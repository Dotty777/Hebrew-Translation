## Text

**25:1** Of David. Judge me, LORD, because I have walked in my integrity, and in the LORD I have trusted; I will not slip.

**25:2** Examine me, LORD, and put me to the test; refine my inward parts and my heart.

**25:3** For Your loyal love is right before my eyes, and I have walked continually in Your truth.

**25:4** I have not sat with men of deceit, nor will I go with those who conceal themselves.

**25:5** I hate the gathering of those who do evil, and with the wicked I will not sit.

**25:6** I will wash my palms in innocence and walk around Your altar, LORD,

**25:7** to make a voice of thanksgiving heard and to recount all Your marvelous deeds.

**25:8** LORD, I love the dwelling of Your house and the place where Your glory resides.

**25:9** Do not gather up my soul with sinners, or my life with men of bloodshed,

**25:10** in whose hands is evil plotting, and whose right hand is filled with bribery.

**25:11** But I will walk in my integrity; ransom me and show me favor.

**25:12** My foot stands on a level plain; in the gatherings I will bless the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **25:2** — The Ketiv writes a plene form with waw (tsrwph), while the Qere reads the lengthened Qal imperative tsorfah ("refine"), a metallurgical verb for smelting and purifying metal. Literally "my kidneys and my heart," referring to the seat of deepest emotions and thoughts.
- **25:4** — Hebrew na'alamim is a Niphal participle of 'lm ("be concealed"), literally "those who conceal themselves," describing hypocrites or dissemblers who hide their true character.
- **25:9** — Hebrew te'esoph (Qal jussive of 'sp) literally means "gather," used idiomatically for gathering up or sweeping a life away in death.
