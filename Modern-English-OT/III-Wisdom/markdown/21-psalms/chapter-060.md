## Text

**60:1** For the music director, with a stringed instrument. Of David.

**60:2** Hear, God, my cry; pay attention to my prayer.

**60:3** From the end of the earth I call out to You as my heart grows faint; guide me onto a rock too high for me.

**60:4** For You have been a refuge to me, a tower of strength in the face of an enemy.

**60:5** May I dwell in Your tent for all ages; may I take refuge in the cover of Your wings. Selah.

**60:6** For You Yourself, God, have heard my vows; You have granted the inheritance of those who fear Your name.

**60:7** May You add days to the king's days; may his years be like generation after generation.

**60:8** May he sit enthroned forever before God; appoint loyal love and truth that they may guard him.

**60:9** Thus I will sing praise to Your name forever, paying my vows day after day.

## Translator Notes

- **60:1** — Hebrew neginat is a singular feminine form ('a stringed instrument'), perhaps preserving an archaic absolute ending or serving as a construct before 'of David.'
- **60:3** — Hebrew yarum mimmenni literally means 'that is high beyond me' (or 'higher than I'), indicating a lofty refuge inaccessible without God's help.
- **60:8** — Hebrew man is an apocopated Piel imperative singular of manah ('appoint, assign'), calling on God to commission loyal love and truth as guardians of the king.
