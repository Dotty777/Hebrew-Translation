## Text

**66:1** For the music director; with stringed instruments. A psalm. A song.

**66:2** May God show favor to us and bless us; may He cause His face to shine with us. Selah.

**66:3** So that Your way may be known in the earth, Your salvation among all nations.

**66:4** Let the peoples give thanks to You, God; let all the peoples give thanks to You.

**66:5** May the nations rejoice and sing out with joy, for You judge the peoples with uprightness and lead the nations on the earth. Selah.

**66:6** Let the peoples give thanks to You, God; let all the peoples give thanks to You.

**66:7** The earth has given its yield; God, our God, will bless us.

**66:8** God will bless us, and all the ends of the earth will fear Him.

## Translator Notes

- **66:2** — Unlike Numbers 6:25, which uses the preposition el ("upon"), the Hebrew here uses ittanu ("with us").
- **66:5** — The verb tanchem is a Hiphil imperfect of nachah ("to lead" or "guide") with a resumptive plural object suffix referring back to the fronted "nations."
