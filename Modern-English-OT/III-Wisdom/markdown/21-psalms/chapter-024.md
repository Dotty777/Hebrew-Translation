## Text

**24:1** Of David. LORD, I lift up my soul to You.

**24:2** My God, I have trusted in You; may I not be put to shame; may my enemies not triumph over me.

**24:3** Truly, no one who waits for You will be put to shame; those who act treacherously without cause will be put to shame.

**24:4** Make Your ways known to me, LORD; teach Your paths to me.

**24:5** Make me walk in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my deliverance; I have waited for You all day long.

**24:6** Remember Your compassions, LORD, and Your acts of loyal love, for they are from ages past.

**24:7** Do not remember the sins of my youth and my rebellions; in accordance with Your loyal love, You Yourself remember me, on account of Your goodness, LORD.

**24:8** The LORD is good and upright; for this reason He instructs sinners in the way.

**24:9** He makes the humble walk in justice and teaches His way to the humble.

**24:10** All the LORD's paths are loyal love and truth for those who guard His covenant and His testimonies.

**24:11** For the sake of Your name, LORD, forgive my iniquity, because it is great.

**24:12** Who is this man who fears the LORD? He will instruct him in the way he ought to choose.

**24:13** His soul will dwell in well-being, and his seed will possess the land.

**24:14** The secret counsel of the LORD belongs to those who fear Him, and He will make His covenant known to them.

**24:15** My eyes are always toward the LORD, for He Himself will bring my feet out of the net.

**24:16** Turn toward me and show me grace, since I am alone and afflicted.

**24:17** The distresses of my heart have expanded; bring me out from my anguish.

**24:18** See my affliction and my hardship, and pardon all my sins.

**24:19** See my enemies, for they have multiplied, and with violent hatred they have hated me.

**24:20** Watch over my soul and rescue me; may I not be put to shame, because I have taken refuge in You.

**24:21** May blamelessness and uprightness guard me, because I have waited for You.

**24:22** Redeem Israel, God, from all its distresses.

## Translator Notes

- **24:11** — The weqatal form vesalachta ("forgive") functions with imperative force following the prepositional phrase, and causal ki ("because it is great") grounds the plea in the sheer magnitude of the guilt.
- **24:12** — The third-person imperfect yivchar ("he ought to choose") is syntactically ambiguous in Hebrew and can refer either to the way the God-fearer should choose or to the way the LORD chooses.
- **24:17** — The Hiphil perfect hirchivu ("have expanded," from rachav, "be wide") is retained from the Masoretic text rather than emended to an imperative ("relieve"), creating a stark wordplay with "distresses" (literally "narrow places") and "anguish" (literally "constrictions").
- **24:18** — This psalm is an alphabetic acrostic in Hebrew; verses 18 and 19 both open with re'eh ("See," letter resh) in place of qof, and verse 22 adds a closing petition beginning with pe.
