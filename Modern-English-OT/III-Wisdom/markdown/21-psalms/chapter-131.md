## Text

**131:1** A Song of the Ascents. Remember, LORD, for David all his affliction,

**131:2** how he swore to the LORD, made a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob:

**131:3** "I will certainly not enter the tent of my house; I will not go up onto the couch of my bedding;

**131:4** I will not grant sleep to my eyes, nor to my eyelids slumber,

**131:5** until I find a place for the LORD, dwelling places for the Mighty One of Jacob."

**131:6** Look, we heard of it in Ephrathah; we found it in the fields of the forest.

**131:7** Let us enter His dwelling places; let us bow down at His footstool.

**131:8** Rise up, LORD, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength.

**131:9** May Your priests clothe themselves with righteousness, and may Your loyal ones shout for joy.

**131:10** On account of David Your servant, do not turn back the face of Your anointed one.

**131:11** The LORD swore to David in truth; He will not turn back from it: "One from the fruit of your body I will place on your throne.

**131:12** If your sons keep My covenant and My testimony that I teach them, their sons as well will sit on your throne for eternity."

**131:13** For the LORD has chosen Zion; He has desired her as a dwelling for Himself:

**131:14** "This is My resting place for eternity; here I will dwell, because I have desired her.

**131:15** Her provisions I will surely bless; her needy I will satisfy with bread.

**131:16** And her priests I will clothe with salvation, and her loyal ones will indeed shout for joy.

**131:17** There I will cause a horn to sprout for David; I have readied a lamp for My anointed one.

**131:18** I will clothe his enemies with shame, yet upon him his crown will gleam."

## Translator Notes

- **131:1** — The Hebrew form translated "his affliction" is a Pual infinitive construct from the root meaning "to be humbled" or "to be afflicted," literally "his being afflicted" or "all the hardship he endured."
- **131:3** — Hebrew introduces the clauses of verses 3-4 with the conditional particle "if," an elliptical oath idiom functioning as an emphatic negative ("I will certainly not...").
- **131:6** — The feminine singular suffix "it" refers to the ark (or the report of it), and "the fields of the forest" is a literal rendering of a phrase that likely alludes to Kiriath-jearim ("City of Forests"), where the ark rested (1 Sam 7:1-2), and can also be read as "the fields of Jaar."
- **131:12** — Hebrew uses the poetic relative pronoun "that" (zo) following the singular noun "My testimony" (written defectively without vav).
- **131:18** — The Qal verb translated "will gleam" (from the root tsits) can mean either "shine/gleam" (cf. the golden plate of Exod 28:36) or "blossom/flourish," echoing the image of "sprout" in verse 17.
