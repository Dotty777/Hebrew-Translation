## Text

**8:1** For the music director, according to the Gittith. A psalm of David.

**8:2** LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name throughout all the earth—You who have set Your splendor above the heavens!

**8:3** From the mouth of young children and nursing infants You have founded strength on account of Your adversaries, to halt the enemy and the avenger.

**8:4** When I see Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and stars You have fixed in place,

**8:5** what is mortal man that You remember him, and a son of man that You attend to him?

**8:6** Yet You have made him a little less than God, and with glory and honor You crown him.

**8:7** You make him rule over the works of Your hands; You have placed everything beneath his feet:

**8:8** flocks and cattle, all of them, and also the animals of the field,

**8:9** birds of the heavens and fish of the sea, whatever travels the pathways of the seas.

**8:10** LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name throughout all the earth!

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — The Gittith (Hebrew ha-gittit) is a musical term of uncertain meaning, likely referring to a tune or instrument associated with Gath or winepresses.
- **8:2** — The Masoretic form tenah is anomalously pointed like the Qal imperative of natan ("give, set") after the relative pronoun asher; traditional Hebrew grammar construes it as a rare infinitive construct form with the sense "You who have set."
- **8:6** — Hebrew elohim ("God") can also denote "divine beings" or "heavenly beings," and ancient versions such as the Septuagint rendered it "angels."
