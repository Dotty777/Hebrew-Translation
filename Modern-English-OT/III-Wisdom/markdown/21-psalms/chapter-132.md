## Text

**132:1** A song of the ascents, of David. Look, how good and how pleasant it is for brothers to dwell together as one!

**132:2** It is like the good oil on the head, descending onto the beard, the beard of Aaron, that descends onto the collar of his garments;

**132:3** like the dew of Hermon that descends upon the mountains of Zion; for there the LORD has commanded the blessing: life for all eternity.

## Translator Notes

- **132:1** — The Hebrew phrase translated "together as one" literally reads "even together" (gam-yachad), an emphatic adverbial expression for living in complete unity.
- **132:2** — Hebrew literally reads "the good oil" (echoing "good" in verse 1), denoting choice fragrant anointing oil, and "the collar of his garments" literally means "the mouth (neck-opening) of his garments" (cf. Exod 28:32). The participle "that descends" repeats the same verb used earlier in the verse and again in verse 3, and may modify either the oil or Aaron's beard.
