## Text

**33:1** Of David, when he altered his demeanor before Abimelech, who drove him out, and he left.

**33:2** At all times I will bless the LORD; continually His praise will be in my mouth.

**33:3** In the LORD my soul will boast; may the humble hear and be glad.

**33:4** Magnify the LORD with me, and together let us lift His name high.

**33:5** I sought the LORD, and He responded to me, and from all my terrors He rescued me.

**33:6** They looked toward Him and became radiant, and may their faces not be ashamed.

**33:7** This afflicted man called out, and the LORD heard, and from all his distresses He saved him.

**33:8** The angel of the LORD pitches camp all around those who fear Him and delivers them.

**33:9** Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the man who takes shelter in Him.

**33:10** Fear the LORD, you His holy ones, for there is no lack for those who fear Him.

**33:11** Young lions become destitute and go hungry, but those who seek the LORD will not lack any good thing.

**33:12** Come, children, listen to me; the fear of the LORD I will teach you.

**33:13** Who is the man who delights in life, who loves days to see good?

**33:14** Guard your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deception.

**33:15** Turn aside from evil and do good; search for peace and pursue it.

**33:16** The LORD's eyes are toward the righteous, and His ears are toward their cry for help.

**33:17** The LORD's face is against those who do evil, to cut off from the earth the memory of them.

**33:18** They cried out, and the LORD heard, and from all their distresses He rescued them.

**33:19** Near is the LORD to those broken in heart, and those crushed in spirit He saves.

**33:20** Many are the calamities of the righteous one, yet from all of them the LORD rescues him.

**33:21** He watches over all his bones; not a single one of them is broken.

**33:22** Evil will put the wicked to death, and those who hate the righteous one will be held guilty.

**33:23** The LORD redeems His servants' lives, and none of all who take shelter in Him will be held guilty.

## Translator Notes

- **33:1** — Hebrew beshannoto et-ta'mo literally means 'when he changed his taste/judgment,' an idiom for feigning madness (compare 1 Samuel 21:13-14, where the king of Gath is named Achish while Abimelech appears here as a Philistine royal name or title). The root ta'am ('taste/judgment') is echoed in 'Taste' in verse 9.
- **33:6** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads third-person plural perfect verbs ('They looked ... and became radiant') followed by a negated jussive ('may their faces not be ashamed'), whereas some ancient versions read second-person imperatives.
- **33:11** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads kefirim ('young lions') with rashu ('become poor/destitute'), portraying even powerful predators going hungry; the Septuagint and Syriac instead render kefirim as 'the rich.'
- **33:18** — The Masoretic Hebrew begins simply with the third-person plural verb tsa'aqu ('They cried out'), referring back to the righteous of verse 16; some ancient versions insert 'the righteous' as an explicit subject.
- **33:23** — Verses 2 through 22 form a Hebrew alphabetic acrostic (omitting a separate line for the letter vav), and verse 23 adds a closing redemption line beginning with the letter pe.
