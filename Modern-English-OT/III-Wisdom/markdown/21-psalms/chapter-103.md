## Text

**103:1** Bless the LORD, my soul! LORD my God, You are exceedingly great; You are robed in majesty and splendor,

**103:2** wrapping Yourself in light like a cloak, stretching out the heavens like a tent curtain.

**103:3** who lays the beams of His upper chambers in the waters, who sets the clouds as His chariot, who walks on the wings of the wind,

**103:4** making winds His messengers, blazing fire His attendants.

**103:5** He founded the earth on its bases; it will not be shaken forever and ever.

**103:6** With the deep like a garment You covered it; above the mountains the waters stood.

**103:7** At Your rebuke they fled; at the sound of Your thunder they rushed away in alarm.

**103:8** They went up over the mountains, they descended into the valleys, to the place that You founded for them.

**103:9** You set a boundary that they must not cross; they will not return to cover the earth.

**103:10** He sends out springs into the ravines; between the mountains they flow along.

**103:11** They provide drink for every wild animal of the field; wild donkeys quench their thirst.

**103:12** By them the birds of the heavens dwell; from among the leafy branches they give voice.

**103:13** From His upper chambers He waters the mountains; with the fruit of Your works the earth is satisfied.

**103:14** He makes grass sprout for the livestock, and plants for the labor of mankind, to bring forth bread from the earth;

**103:15** and wine gladdens the heart of mortal man, to make the face gleam with oil, and bread sustains the heart of mortal man.

**103:16** The trees of the LORD are satisfied, the cedars of Lebanon that He planted,

**103:17** where birds nest; as for the stork, cypress trees are her home.

**103:18** The lofty mountains are for the wild goats; crags are a refuge for the rock hyraxes.

**103:19** He made the moon for appointed seasons; the sun knows its setting.

**103:20** You bring darkness, and it is night; in it every wild animal of the forest moves about.

**103:21** The young lions roar for prey, and they seek their food from God.

**103:22** The sun rises; they draw back, and in their dens they lie down.

**103:23** Man sets out to his work and to his labor until evening.

**103:24** How numerous are Your works, LORD! All of them You made in wisdom; the earth is full of Your creation.

**103:25** Here is the sea, great and broad on both sides; there are teeming things without number, living creatures, small along with great.

**103:26** There ships travel along, and Leviathan, whom You fashioned to play in it.

**103:27** All of them look expectantly to You to give them their food in its time.

**103:28** When You give to them, they gather it; when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with what is good.

**103:29** When You conceal Your face, they are alarmed; when You withdraw their breath, they expire, and to their dust they return.

**103:30** When You send out Your Spirit, they are created, and You restore the face of the ground.

**103:31** May the glory of the LORD be forever; may the LORD be glad in His works!

**103:32** He is the One who gazes at the earth, and it quakes; He touches the mountains, and they smoke.

**103:33** I will sing to the LORD throughout my life; I will make music to my God as long as I exist.

**103:34** May my meditation be sweet to Him; I myself will be glad in the LORD.

**103:35** May sinners be consumed from the earth, and may the wicked exist no longer! Bless the LORD, my soul! Praise the LORD!

## Translator Notes

- **103:4** — The double accusative after the Qal participle of asah ('make') can be construed either as 'making winds His messengers, blazing fire His attendants' (fitting the meteorological imagery of v. 3) or following the Hebrew word order as 'making His messengers winds [or spirits], His attendants a blazing fire' (the construal reflected in the Septuagint).
- **103:8** — The masculine plural Qal imperfects 'went up' and 'descended' continue the grammatical subject 'waters' from vv. 6-7 and match the masculine plural references in vv. 8b-9; alternatively, 'mountains' and 'valleys' may be construed as their subjects ('the mountains rose, the valleys sank down').
- **103:13** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts abruptly within this verse from third person ('His upper chambers') to second person ('Your works'), a frequent feature of hymnic praise in this psalm.
- **103:26** — The Hebrew prepositional phrase 'bo' after the Piel infinitive of sachaq ('play, sport') can refer either to the sea ('to play in it') or to Leviathan with God as subject ('for You to play with it'; cf. Job 41:5).
- **103:29** — The verb tosef ('withdraw') is the Qal imperfect of asaf ('gather in, withdraw') with elided initial aleph (cf. Job 34:14), not Hiphil of yasaf ('add'); the noun ruach ('breath') here echoes 'Your Spirit' (also ruach, or 'Your breath') in v. 30.
