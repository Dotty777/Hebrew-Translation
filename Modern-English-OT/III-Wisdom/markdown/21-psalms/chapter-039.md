## Text

**39:1** For the music director. A psalm of David.

**39:2** I waited earnestly for the LORD, and He inclined toward me and heard my cry for help.

**39:3** He brought me up from a pit of tumult, from the mud of the bog; He caused my feet to stand on a rock; He established my steps.

**39:4** And He placed a new song in my mouth, praise to our God. Many will see and be in awe, and they will trust in the LORD.

**39:5** Blessed is the man who has set the LORD as his trust, and has not turned to the arrogant or to those who swerve toward falsehood.

**39:6** Many are the wondrous deeds You Yourself have done, LORD my God, and Your plans toward us; there is no comparing to You. Should I declare and speak of them, they are too numerous to recount.

**39:7** Sacrifice and grain offering You did not delight in; ears You have dug for me; burnt offering and sin offering You did not ask for.

**39:8** Then I said, "Look, I have come; in the scroll of the book it is written concerning me.

**39:9** I delight in doing Your will, my God, and Your instruction is within my inward parts."

**39:10** I have announced the good news of righteousness in a great assembly; look, I do not hold back my lips; LORD, You Yourself know.

**39:11** Your righteousness I have not concealed within my heart; I have told of Your faithfulness and Your salvation; I have not kept Your loyal love and Your truth hidden from a great assembly.

**39:12** You, LORD, will not hold back Your compassion from me; Your loyal love and Your truth will guard me continually.

**39:13** For calamities without number have closed in around me; my iniquities have caught up with me, and I am unable to see; they are more numerous than the hairs of my head, and my heart has forsaken me.

**39:14** Be pleased, LORD, to rescue me; LORD, hurry to my help.

**39:15** May they be ashamed and disgraced together, those who seek my life to sweep it away; may they turn back and be humiliated, those who delight in my harm.

**39:16** May they be stunned on account of their shame, those who say to me, "Aha, aha!"

**39:17** May all who seek You exult and rejoice in You; may those who love Your salvation say continually, "May the LORD be magnified!"

**39:18** Yet I am afflicted and needy; the Lord gives thought to me. You are my help and my rescuer; my God, do not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **39:4** — The Hebrew pairs two nearly homophonous Qal imperfect verbs, "see" and "fear/be in awe", creating a deliberate sound play.
- **39:6** — The clause "there is no comparing to You" renders a concise idiom combining the negative particle with the Qal infinitive construct of a verb meaning "to set in order" or "to compare."
- **39:7** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads literally "ears You have dug for me", a metaphor for opening attentive, obedient ears; the Septuagint instead reads "a body You prepared for me."
- **39:12** — The Hebrew uses the indicative negation with the Qal imperfect ("will not hold back"), expressing confidence in God's ongoing mercy, though some take it as a plea ("do not withhold").
