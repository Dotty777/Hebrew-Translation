## Text

**86:1** Of the sons of Korah. A psalm, a song. His foundation is on the holy mountains.

**86:2** The LORD loves the gates of Zion above all the dwellings of Jacob.

**86:3** Glorious things are spoken concerning you, city of God. Selah.

**86:4** "I will name Rahab and Babylon among those who know Me; look, Philistia and Tyre, together with Cush—this one was born there."

**86:5** And concerning Zion it will be said, "Each and every one was born in her," and He, the Most High, will establish her.

**86:6** The LORD will count as He writes down the peoples, "This one was born there." Selah.

**86:7** And singers as well as dancers will say, "All my wellsprings are in you."

## Translator Notes

- **86:1** — The noun yesudato ("His foundation") occurs only here and bears a masculine singular suffix referring to the LORD (since Zion and "city" are feminine in vv. 3, 5, 7), pointing to the city He founded on the plural "holy mountains."
- **86:3** — Literally "glorious things, it is spoken concerning you," combining a fronted feminine plural Niphal participle (nichbadot) with an impersonal masculine singular Pual participle (medubbar).
- **86:4** — "Rahab" is a poetic name for Egypt (compare Isa 30:7), listed with Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, and Cush as former rival nations enrolled among those who know God.
- **86:5** — "Each and every one" translates the distributive idiom ish ve-ish (literally "man and man"), paired with the singular passive verb yullad ("was born").
- **86:7** — The Hebrew clause is verbless ("will say" is implied), and the participle cholelim may mean either "dancers" (from chul, "whirl, dance") or "flute players" (denominative from chalil, "flute").
