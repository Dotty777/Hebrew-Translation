## Text

**129:1** A song of the ascents. From the depths I have called to You, LORD.

**129:2** Lord, listen to my voice; may Your ears be attentive to the voice of my pleas for grace.

**129:3** If You, LORD, keep account of iniquities, Lord, who can stand?

**129:4** But with You is forgiveness, so that You may be feared.

**129:5** I wait eagerly for the LORD; my soul waits eagerly, and I hope in His word.

**129:6** My soul waits eagerly for the Lord more than those keeping watch for the morning, those keeping watch for the morning.

**129:7** Hope in the LORD, Israel, for with the LORD is loyal love, and abundant redemption is with Him.

**129:8** And He Himself will redeem Israel from all his iniquities.

## Translator Notes

- **129:3** — The divine name here is the shortened poetic form Yah (rendered "LORD"), paired with Adonai ("Lord"); the Qal verb shamar ("keep, watch") here carries the sense "keep account of."
- **129:6** — The opening clause is verbless in Hebrew, literally "My soul for the Lord," carrying forward the verb "waits" from verse 5; shomerim ("those keeping watch") is the Qal active participle of the same root shamar used in verse 3.
- **129:7** — The noun chesed denotes covenantal loyal love or steadfast faithfulness, and harbeh (the Hiphil infinitive absolute of rabah) functions adjectivally as "abundant."
