## Text

**111:1** Praise the LORD! Happy is the man who fears the LORD, who deeply delights in His commandments.

**111:2** His seed will be powerful in the land; a generation of upright ones will be blessed.

**111:3** Abundance and riches are in his house, and his righteousness stands forever.

**111:4** In the darkness light rises for the upright; he is gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

**111:5** It goes well for the man who is gracious and lends; he manages his affairs with justice.

**111:6** For he will never totter; the righteous one will be in eternal remembrance.

**111:7** He will not fear a bad report; his heart is steady, trusting in the LORD.

**111:8** His heart is sustained; he will not fear, until he looks on his foes.

**111:9** He has scattered widely, he has given to the needy; his righteousness stands forever; his horn will rise in honor.

**111:10** The wicked man will see and grow angry; he will grind his teeth and melt away; the longing of the wicked will perish.

## Translator Notes

- **111:1** — After the opening call "Praise the LORD" (Hebrew halelu-Yah, using the poetic shortened divine name), this psalm is an alphabetic acrostic whose twenty-two poetic lines begin with successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.
- **111:4** — The three masculine singular adjectives "gracious, compassionate, and righteous" have no explicit subject in Hebrew; in context they describe the God-fearing man of vv. 1-9 (echoing "gracious" in v. 5 and "righteous" in v. 6), though some interpreters take the LORD as the implied subject.
- **111:8** — The Hebrew idiom "look on" (ra'ah with the preposition be-) used with foes conveys looking in triumph or vindication upon their defeat.
