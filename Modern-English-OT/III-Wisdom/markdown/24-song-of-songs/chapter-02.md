## Text

**2:1** I am a crocus of Sharon, a lily of the valleys.

**2:2** Like a lily among the thorns, so is my companion among the daughters.

**2:3** Like an apple tree among the trees of the forest, so is my beloved among the sons. In his shade I delighted to sit, and his fruit was sweet to my palate.

**2:4** He brought me to the house of wine, and his banner over me is love.

**2:5** Strengthen me with raisin cakes, revive me with apples, for I am lovesick.

**2:6** His left arm is beneath my head, and his right arm embraces me.

**2:7** I put you under oath, daughters of Jerusalem, by the gazelles or by the does of the field: do not rouse or stir up love until it desires.

**2:8** The voice of my beloved! Look, here he comes, leaping across the mountains, springing across the hills.

**2:9** My beloved resembles a gazelle or a young stag. Look, here he stands behind our wall, gazing through the windows, peering through the lattices.

**2:10** My beloved answered and said to me, "Rise up, my companion, my beautiful one, and come away.

**2:11** For look, the winter has passed; the rain is over, gone its way.

**2:12** The blossoms have appeared in the land; the time of singing has arrived, and the turtledove's voice is heard in our land.

**2:13** The fig tree has ripened its early figs, and the vines in blossom give fragrance. Rise up, my companion, my beautiful one, and come away.

**2:14** My dove, in the clefts of the rock, in the hiding place of the cliff, show me your appearance, let me hear your voice; for your voice is sweet, and your appearance is lovely."

**2:15** Catch for us foxes, little foxes that ruin vineyards, while our vineyards are in blossom.

**2:16** My beloved belongs to me, and I belong to him; he is the one who pastures among the lilies.

**2:17** Until the day breathes and the shadows take flight, turn around, my beloved, be like a gazelle or a young stag on the mountains of Bether.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Chavatstselet (traditionally rendered "rose") refers to a coastal wildflower of the Sharon plain, likely a crocus, narcissus, or asphodel.
- **2:7** — In Hebrew oath syntax, the conditional particle im ("if") expresses a strong prohibition ("do not..."). The 3fs verb techpats ("it desires") agrees with the feminine noun ha'ahavah ("love").
- **2:12** — The noun hazamir can mean either "singing" (from zamar II, matching the turtledove's voice) or "pruning" (from zamar I, referring to spring vine pruning).
- **2:13** — The Ketiv reads lky (an archaic or dialectal 2fs suffix form), whereas the translated Qere reads the standard form lakh ("for yourself"), idiomatic with the imperative "Rise up."
- **2:17** — Harey vater may denote a proper place name ("mountains of Bether") or be descriptive ("cleft/divided mountains," from the root batar, "to cut in two").
