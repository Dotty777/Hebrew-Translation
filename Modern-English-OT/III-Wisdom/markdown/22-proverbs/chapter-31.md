## Text

**31:1** The words of King Lemuel, an oracle with which his mother instructed him.

**31:2** What, my son? And what, son of my womb? And what, son of my vows?

**31:3** Do not give women your vigor, nor your ways to what ruins kings.

**31:4** Not for kings, Lemuel; not for kings is drinking wine, nor for rulers to ask, "Where is strong drink?"

**31:5** Otherwise they may drink and forget what has been decreed, and subvert the cause of all who are afflicted.

**31:6** Give strong drink to someone who is perishing, and wine to those bitter of soul.

**31:7** May he drink and forget his poverty, and no longer remember his hardship.

**31:8** Open your mouth on behalf of the mute, for the cause of all who face ruin.

**31:9** Open your mouth, judge with righteousness, and defend the cause of the afflicted and the needy.

**31:10** Who can find a woman of valor? For far beyond jewels is her worth.

**31:11** Her husband's heart trusts in her, and he will not lack gain.

**31:12** She bestows good on him, and not harm, all the days of her life.

**31:13** She seeks out wool and flax, and works gladly with her hands.

**31:14** She is like a merchant's ships; from a distance she brings her food.

**31:15** She rises while night still remains, and gives provisions to her household and an allotted portion to her servant women.

**31:16** She evaluates a field and acquires it; from the fruit of her hands she plants a vineyard.

**31:17** She binds her waist with strength and fortifies her arms.

**31:18** She discerns that her merchandise is good; at night her lamp does not go out.

**31:19** She extends her hands to the distaff, and her palms grip the spindle.

**31:20** She spreads open her palm to the afflicted, and extends both her hands to the needy.

**31:21** She does not fear snow for her household, because her entire household is clothed in scarlet.

**31:22** She makes tapestries for herself; fine linen and purple are her clothing.

**31:23** Her husband is known at the gates as he sits with the elders of the land.

**31:24** She makes linen wraps and sells them, and supplies belts to the trader.

**31:26** Strength and splendor are her clothing, and she laughs at the day to come.

**31:27** She keeps watch over the ways of her household, and the bread of idleness she does not eat.

**31:28** With wisdom she opens her mouth, and instruction of loyal love is on her tongue. Her children rise and pronounce her blessed; her husband as well, and he praises her:

**31:29** "Many daughters have acted valiantly, but you have risen above them all."

**31:30** Charm is deception and beauty is a vapor; a woman who fears the LORD, she is the one to be praised.

**31:31** Give her from the fruit of her hands, and let her deeds praise her at the gates.

## Translator Notes

- **31:3** — The form lamchot is commonly analyzed as a Hiphil infinitive construct of mchh with elided he ("to destroying/ruining"), governing the Aramaic-style plural melakin ("kings").
- **31:4** — Ketiv/Qere: Qere reads ei ("where?"), rendered here as rulers asking "Where is strong drink?"; Ketiv has the consonants aleph-waw, often vocalized as a noun meaning "desire" (from the root avah) or taken as "or".
- **31:16** — Ketiv/Qere: Qere reads the third-person feminine singular Qal perfect nate'ah ("she plants"), agreeing with the feminine subject; Ketiv omits the final he.
- **31:26** — Native Masoretic order preserves the Hebrew alphabetic acrostic: Ayin (31:25), Pe (31:26), Tsade (31:27), Qoph (31:28). In the Saints.Bible parallel display, MT 31:25 maps to slot 31:26, MT 31:27 maps to slot 31:27, and MT 31:26 + 31:28 are combined at slot 31:28 (leaving slot 31:25 unmapped).
- **31:27** — Ketiv/Qere: Qere reads halikhot ("ways" or "activities"), plural of halikhah; Ketiv transposes the yod and lamed and omits the plural waw.
- **31:28** — The Hebrew numbers the Ayin line ('Strength and splendor are her clothing') as 31:25 and the Pe line ('With wisdom she opens her mouth') as 31:26; this edition shows the Ayin line at 31:26 and joins the Pe line to the Qoph line here at 31:28, with no separate 31:25.
