# Proverbs 19 — Saints Modern English (Hebrew / Masoretic Text)

## Text

**19:3** A person's folly overturns his way, yet his heart grows furious against the LORD.

**19:4** Wealth adds many companions, but a poor person is separated from his companion.

**19:5** A false witness will not be acquitted, and one who breathes out lies will not slip away.

**19:6** Many court the favor of a generous person, and everyone is a companion to one who gives gifts.

**19:7** All the brothers of a poor person hate him; how much more do his companions stay far from him! He pursues words; they belong to him.

**19:8** One who acquires sense loves his soul; one who guards understanding will find good.

**19:9** A false witness will not be acquitted, and one who breathes out lies will perish.

**19:10** Luxury is not suitable for a fool, much less for a slave to rule over officials.

**19:11** A person's insight slows his anger, and his glory is to pass over an offense.

**19:12** Like the growling of a young lion is a king's fury, and like dew on grass is his favor.

**19:13** A foolish son is a calamity to his father, and a wife's quarrels are a persistent dripping.

**19:14** A house and wealth are an inheritance of fathers, yet an insightful wife is from the LORD.

**19:15** Laziness causes a deep sleep to fall, and a negligent soul will go hungry.

**19:16** One who guards a commandment guards his soul; one who despises his ways will die.

**19:17** One who shows favor to a poor person lends to the LORD, and He will pay him back for his deed.

**19:18** Discipline your son, since there is hope, and do not set your desire on causing his death.

**19:19** A person of great wrath bears the penalty, for if you rescue him, you will have to do so again.

**19:20** Listen to counsel and receive discipline, so that you may become wise in your future.

**19:21** There are many plans in a person's heart, yet it is the counsel of the LORD that will stand.

**19:22** What is desired of a person is his loyal love, and better is a poor person than a liar.

**19:23** The fear of the LORD brings life; one spends the night satisfied and will not be visited with harm.

**19:24** A sluggard buries his hand in the bowl, and even to his mouth he will not return it.

**19:25** Strike a scoffer, and a simple person will grow shrewd; rebuke a discerning person, and he will understand knowledge.

**19:26** One who assaults his father and drives off his mother is a son who causes shame and disgrace.

**19:27** Stop listening to discipline, my son, and you will wander from the sayings of knowledge.

**19:28** A worthless witness scoffs at justice, and the mouth of the wicked swallows iniquity.

**19:29** Judgments stand ready for scoffers, and beatings for the back of fools.

## Translator Notes

- **19:7** — Ketiv reads "lo'" ("not"), yielding "he pursues words, but they are gone"; the translated qere reads "lo" ("to him" / "his"), yielding "they belong to him" (meaning his words remain only with himself).
- **19:16** — Ketiv reads the Hophal "yumat" ("will be put to death"); the translated qere reads the Qal "yamut" ("will die").
- **19:19** — Ketiv reads "grl" (possibly "harsh of" or a graphic resh/dalet variant); the translated qere reads "gedol-chemah" ("great of wrath," i.e., a person of great wrath).
- **19:27** — The Qal imperative "stop/cease" followed by the infinitive construct "to wander/stray" functions conditionally or ironically to state the consequence of ignoring discipline; alternatively, the clause warns against instruction that causes straying.
