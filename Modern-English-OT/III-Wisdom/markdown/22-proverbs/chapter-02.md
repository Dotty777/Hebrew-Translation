## Text

**2:1** My son, if you receive my sayings and store up my commandments with you,

**2:2** making your ear listen closely to wisdom, inclining your heart toward understanding,

**2:3** indeed, if you call for insight and lift up your voice for understanding,

**2:4** if you seek her like silver, and like hidden treasures search for her,

**2:5** then you will comprehend the fear of the LORD, and you will find knowledge of God.

**2:6** For the LORD grants wisdom; out of His mouth come knowledge and understanding.

**2:7** For the upright He stores up sound wisdom; He is a shield for those walking in integrity,

**2:8** to guard the paths of justice, and He keeps watch over the way of His faithful ones.

**2:9** Then you will comprehend righteousness, justice, and equity—every good course.

**2:10** When wisdom enters your heart and knowledge is delightful to your soul,

**2:11** discretion will keep watch over you; understanding will protect you,

**2:12** to rescue you from the way of evil, from a man speaking perverse things,

**2:13** those who desert the paths of uprightness to walk in ways of darkness,

**2:14** those who are glad to do evil, and exult in the perversities of evil,

**2:15** whose paths are twisted, and who are devious in their courses;

**2:16** to rescue you from a strange woman, from a foreign woman who makes her sayings smooth,

**2:17** who deserts the companion of her youth and has forgotten the covenant of her God;

**2:18** for her house sinks down toward death, and her courses toward the departed spirits.

**2:19** None who go in to her will return, and they will not reach the paths of life.

**2:20** So you may walk in the way of good people, and keep the paths of the righteous.

**2:21** For the upright will dwell in the land, and the blameless will stay in it,

**2:22** yet the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the treacherous will be torn out of it.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — Qere reads yitspon (Qal imperfect 3ms, "He stores up"); ketiv reads wtspn ("and He stored up" or "and a store").
- **2:16** — Literally "a strange woman" (ishshah zarah) and "a foreign woman" (nokhriyyah), idioms denoting a woman outside the marriage bond, i.e., an adulterous woman (cf. 2:17).
- **2:18** — Hebrew shachah (Qal perfect 3fs of shuach, "sinks down") is feminine despite bayit ("house") normally being masculine; repha'im ("departed spirits") denotes the shades of the dead in the underworld.
- **2:22** — Hebrew yissechu is Qal imperfect 3mp of nasach ("they will tear out"), used impersonally with passive force ("will be torn out").
