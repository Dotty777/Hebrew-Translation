## Text

**4:1** Listen, sons, to a father's discipline, and pay attention to know understanding.

**4:2** For I have given you good learning; do not forsake my instruction.

**4:3** For I was a son to my father, tender and the only one before my mother.

**4:4** And he instructed me and said to me, "May your heart hold fast my words; observe my commandments and live.

**4:5** Acquire wisdom, acquire understanding; do not forget, and do not turn aside from the sayings of my mouth.

**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will watch over you; love her, and she will guard you.

**4:8** Esteem her highly, and she will exalt you; she will honor you when you embrace her.

**4:9** She will set on your head a wreath of grace; a crown of splendor she will bestow on you."

**4:10** Listen, my son, and receive my sayings, and years of life will multiply for you.

**4:11** In the way of wisdom I have instructed you; I have guided you along courses of uprightness.

**4:12** As you walk, your stride will not be cramped, and if you run, you will not stumble.

**4:13** Grasp discipline; do not let go; guard her, for she is your life.

**4:14** Do not go onto the path of the wicked, and do not stride along the way of evil people.

**4:15** Shun it, do not pass along it; swerve away from it and pass on.

**4:16** For they do not sleep unless they do evil, and their sleep is snatched away unless they cause someone to stumble.

**4:17** For they feed on the bread of wickedness, and they drink the wine of violent deeds.

**4:18** Yet the path of the righteous is like radiant light, growing ever brighter until full daylight.

**4:19** The way of the wicked is like thick darkness; they do not know what they stumble over.

**4:20** My son, pay attention to my words; turn your ear to my sayings.

**4:21** Do not let them depart from your eyes; keep them inside your heart.

**4:22** For they are life for those who find them, and healing for their entire body.

**4:23** Above all that is kept, guard your heart, for from it come the wellsprings of life.

**4:24** Remove from yourself crookedness of mouth, and keep deviousness of lips far from yourself.

**4:25** Let your eyes look straight ahead, and let your eyelids face directly in front of you.

**4:26** Weigh the course of your foot, and all your ways will be firm.

**4:27** Do not turn aside to the right or to the left; remove your foot from evil.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**4:7** *(after `4:6`)* The beginning of wisdom: acquire wisdom, and with all your possessions, acquire understanding.

## Translator Notes

- **4:13** — The feminine object suffix on nitsreha ("guard her") and feminine pronoun hi' ("she") refer either back to feminine wisdom (4:5-11) or to discipline (musar, normally masculine) personified alongside wisdom.
- **4:16** — Follows the Qere yakhshilu (Hiphil imperfect of kashal, "they cause someone to stumble"), whereas the Ketiv reads yikhsholu (Qal imperfect, "they stumble").
- **4:18** — Hebrew holekh va'or (literally "going and brightening") is an idiom for continuous increase, and nekhon hayyom (Niphal participle of kun in construct with "the day") literally denotes "the established point of the day," meaning full daylight or midday.
- **4:23** — Hebrew mikkol-mishmar uses the comparative preposition min ("above / more than all that is kept"), and totse'ot chayyim literally means "the outgoings" or "sources of life."
- **4:26** — Hebrew palles (Piel imperative of palas) can mean either "weigh / examine carefully" or "make level" (compare Isaiah 26:7); both lead to the result that all one's ways "will be firm" (yikkonu, Niphal imperfect of kun).
- **4:7 *(additional)*** — Or 'at the cost of all you possess.'
