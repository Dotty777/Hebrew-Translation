# Proverbs 18 — Saints Modern English (Hebrew / Masoretic Text)

## Text

**18:1** One who keeps himself apart seeks his own craving; he erupts against all sound judgment.

**18:2** A fool has no delight in understanding, only in disclosing his own heart.

**18:3** When a wicked person arrives, contempt arrives too, and with dishonor comes reproach.

**18:4** Words from a person's mouth are deep waters; the wellspring of wisdom is a gushing stream.

**18:5** Showing favoritism to a wicked person is not good, nor is turning aside a righteous person in judgment.

**18:6** A fool's lips enter into a dispute, and his mouth calls out for blows.

**18:7** The mouth of a fool is destruction to him, and his lips are a trap for his life.

**18:8** A whisperer's words are like tasty morsels, and they descend into the inner chambers of the belly.

**18:9** Even one who is lax in his work is a brother to a master of destruction.

**18:10** A tower of strength is the name of the LORD; into it a righteous person runs and is set safely on high.

**18:11** The wealth of a rich person is his fortified city, and like a towering wall in his imagination.

**18:12** Before ruin a person's heart grows haughty, and before honor comes humility.

**18:13** One who replies before he listens—it is folly and disgrace to him.

**18:14** A person's spirit endures his illness, but a stricken spirit—who can bear it?

**18:15** The heart of a discerning person acquires knowledge, and the ear of wise people seeks knowledge.

**18:16** A person's gift makes space for him and leads him before great people.

**18:17** The first one in his lawsuit appears in the right, but his neighbor comes and cross-examines him.

**18:18** The lot brings contentions to an end and separates the mighty.

**18:19** A wronged brother is more resistant than a fortified city, and contentions are like the bar of a fortress.

**18:20** From the fruit of a person's mouth his belly will be filled; with the yield of his lips he will be filled.

**18:21** Death and life are in the control of the tongue, and each of those who love it will eat its fruit.

**18:22** Whoever finds a wife has found something good and gains favor from the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — The verb 'yitgalla' ('erupts/breaks out') is the rare Hithpael of 'gala', appearing elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible only in Prov 17:14 and 20:3.
- **18:8** — Hebrew 'mitlahamim' is a rare Hithpael participle occurring only here and in Prov 26:22; based on Semitic cognates meaning to swallow greedily, it denotes choice or tasty morsels, though older versions sometimes rendered it 'wounds'.
- **18:17** — Ketiv/qere variant: the qere reads 'uva' (conjunction 'vav' plus Qal perfect, 'but ... comes'), whereas the ketiv writes the imperfect 'yavo' without the conjunction.
- **18:19** — Ketiv/qere variant: the qere reads 'umidyanim' ('and contentions', matching v. 18), while the ketiv writes the synonymous plural 'umedonim'. The first clause uses an elliptical comparative 'min' ('than') with 'nifsha', the sole Niphal occurrence of 'pasha' ('transgress/wrong').
