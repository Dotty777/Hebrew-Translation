## Text

**7:1** Now it happened, once the king had settled in his house and the LORD had given him rest all around from all his enemies,

**7:2** that the king said to Nathan the prophet, "Look now, I am dwelling in a house of cedars, while the ark of God is dwelling inside the tent curtain."

**7:3** And Nathan said to the king, "All that is in your heart, go and do, because the LORD is with you."

**7:4** Yet it happened during that same night that the word of the LORD came to Nathan, saying,

**7:5** "Go and say to My servant David, 'This is what the LORD says: Will you yourself build a house for Me to dwell in?

**7:6** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought the sons of Israel up out of Egypt down to this day; instead, I have been walking about in a tent and in a tabernacle.

**7:7** In every place where I walked about among all the sons of Israel, did I speak a word with any of the tribes of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built Me a house of cedars?"'

**7:8** Now then, say this to My servant David: 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: It was I who took you from the pasture, from behind the flock, to become leader over My people Israel.

**7:9** And I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a great name, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

**7:10** I will set a place for My people Israel and plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be unsettled no longer; and sons of injustice will not afflict them again as at the first,

**7:11** even from the day when I appointed judges over My people Israel; and I will give you rest from all your enemies. Moreover, the LORD announces to you that the LORD will make a house for you.

**7:12** When your days are full and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who will come out from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom.

**7:13** He is the one who will build a house for My name, and I will firmly establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

**7:14** I Myself will be a father to him, and he will be a son to Me. When he commits wrongdoing, I will reprove him with the rod of men and with the blows of the sons of mankind,

**7:15** yet My loyal kindness will not depart from him, as I removed it from Saul, whom I removed from before you.

**7:16** Your house and your kingdom will stand sure forever before you; your throne will remain established forever.'"

**7:17** In accordance with all these words and with this entire vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**7:18** Then King David went in and sat before the LORD, and he said, "Who am I, Lord GOD, and what is my house, that You have brought me as far as this?

**7:19** Yet even this was small in Your eyes, Lord GOD, and You have spoken also about the house of Your servant for a distant future. And this is the instruction of mankind, Lord GOD!

**7:20** And what more can David still speak to You? For You know Your servant, Lord GOD.

**7:21** On account of Your word and in keeping with Your own heart, You have done all this greatness, to make it known to Your servant.

**7:22** Therefore You are great, LORD God, because there is no one like You, and there is no God apart from You, according to all that we have heard with our own ears.

**7:23** And who is like Your people, like Israel—one nation on the earth whom God went to redeem for Himself as a people, and to set a name for Himself, and to perform for you greatness and awesome deeds for Your land before Your people, whom You redeemed for Yourself from Egypt, from nations and their gods?

**7:24** And You firmly established for Yourself Your people Israel to be a people for Yourself forever, and You, LORD, became their God.

**7:25** And now, LORD God, uphold forever the word that You spoke about Your servant and about his house, and do just as You have spoken;

**7:26** and may Your name grow great forever, as it is said, 'The LORD of hosts is God over Israel,' and the house of Your servant David will remain established before You.

**7:27** For You, LORD of hosts, God of Israel, have uncovered the ear of Your servant, saying, 'I will build a house for you'; therefore Your servant has found his heart to pray this prayer to You.

**7:28** And now, Lord GOD, You Yourself are God, and Your words are truth, and You have spoken this good thing to Your servant.

**7:29** Now then, be pleased and bless the house of Your servant, so that it may endure forever before You; for You, Lord GOD, have spoken, and from Your blessing the house of Your servant will be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **7:7** — MT reads "tribes of Israel" (shivtei Yisrael), reflecting the Masoretic consonantal text rather than "judges of Israel" (shofetei) in the parallel at 1 Chr 17:6.
- **7:16** — MT reads "before you" (lefanekha, 2ms), referring to David, whereas the Septuagint and Syriac read "before Me."
- **7:19** — The clause "And this is the instruction of mankind" (torat ha-adam) is syntactically difficult; it is variously understood as a charter for humanity or as an unmarked rhetorical question, "And is this the custom of mankind?"
- **7:23** — MT exhibits abrupt shifts in person and dense syntax: a plural verb with Elohim ("God went"), a 2mp pronoun ("for you"), "for Your land," and "nations and their gods" (lit., "its gods," with singular suffix), without the verb "driving out" found in 1 Chr 17:21.
- **7:27** — Two Hebrew idioms are rendered literally: "uncovered the ear" (galah ozen, meaning to reveal) and "found his heart" (matsa et-libbo, meaning to summon courage).
