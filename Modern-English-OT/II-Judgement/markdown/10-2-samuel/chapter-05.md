## Text

**5:1** And all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and spoke, saying, "Here we are; we are your bone and your flesh.

**5:2** Even in times gone by, while Saul was king over us, you were the one bringing out and bringing in Israel. And the LORD said to you, 'You shall shepherd My people Israel, and you shall be leader over Israel.'"

**5:3** Then all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David cut a covenant with them in Hebron before the LORD, and they anointed David as king over Israel.

**5:4** David was thirty years of age when he began to reign; he reigned for forty years.

**5:5** In Hebron he reigned over Judah for seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he reigned for thirty-three years over all Israel and Judah.

**5:6** Then the king and his men marched to Jerusalem against the Jebusites, who lived in the land, and they told David, "You will not come in here; rather, the blind and the lame will turn you back," thinking, "David will not come in here."

**5:7** Yet David captured the fortress of Zion, which is the City of David.

**5:8** On that day David said, "Whoever strikes the Jebusites, let him reach the water shaft—and the lame and the blind, who are hated by David's soul!" For this reason people say, "Neither the blind nor the lame shall enter the house."

**5:9** David took up residence in the fortress and named it the City of David, and David built all around, from the Millo inward.

**5:10** And David went on growing greater, for the LORD, God of hosts, was with him.

**5:11** Then Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David, together with cedar timber, woodworkers, and stonemasons; and they built a house for David.

**5:12** Then David knew that the LORD had established him as king over Israel and that He had lifted up his kingdom on account of His people Israel.

**5:13** David also took additional concubines and wives from Jerusalem after he had come from Hebron, and additional sons and daughters were born to David.

**5:14** These are the names of the ones born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,

**5:15** Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, Japhia,

**5:16** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.

**5:17** Now the Philistines heard that they had anointed David as king over Israel, and all the Philistines went up to search for David. David heard and went down to the fortress.

**5:18** Now the Philistines had arrived and fanned out in the Valley of Rephaim.

**5:19** So David inquired of the LORD, asking, "Should I go up against the Philistines? Will You deliver them into my hand?" And the LORD told David, "Go up, for I will indeed deliver the Philistines into your hand."

**5:20** Then David came to Baal-perazim, and David struck them down there; and he said, "The LORD has burst through my enemies before me like a burst of water." For this reason he called the name of that place Baal-perazim.

**5:21** And they left their idols behind there, and David and his men carried them off.

**5:22** Then the Philistines went up once more and fanned out in the Valley of Rephaim.

**5:23** And David inquired of the LORD, and He said, "Do not go up; circle around to their rear and come upon them from opposite the balsam trees.

**5:24** And as soon as you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then move decisively, for then the LORD has gone out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines."

**5:25** David did so, just as the LORD commanded him, and he struck down the Philistines from Geba as far as the approach to Gezer.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — Ketiv divides the letters as "hyyth mwsy'" ("you were one bringing out"), placing the final he on the verb; Qere reads "hayita hammotsi'" ("you were the one bringing out"), placing the definite article on the Hiphil participle to match the following articular participle "wehammevi'" ("and the one bringing in").
- **5:6** — The form "hesirekha" is a Hiphil perfect 3ms with a 2ms object suffix ("will turn you back"), taking "the blind and the lame" as its subject; some alternatively construe it as an infinitive ("unless you remove the blind and the lame").
- **5:8** — The Hebrew clause is elliptical and contains the rare noun "tsinnor" ("water shaft," also in Ps 42:8 MT). For "who are hated," Ketiv reads Qal perfect 3cp "san'u" ("who hate David's soul"), whereas Qere reads Qal passive participle construct "senu'e" ("hated by David's soul").
- **5:24** — Ketiv reads "beshom'akha" ("when you hear"), while Qere reads "keshom'akha" ("as soon as you hear"). The precise tree denoted by "bekha'im" ("balsam trees") is uncertain, and the Qal imperfect "teherats" conveys acting sharply or decisively.
