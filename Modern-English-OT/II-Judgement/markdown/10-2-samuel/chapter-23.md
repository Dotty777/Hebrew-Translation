## Text

**23:1** These are the final words of David: The declaration of David son of Jesse, and the declaration of the man who was raised up on high, the anointed one of the God of Jacob, and the sweet singer of the songs of Israel.

**23:2** The Spirit of the LORD has spoken through me, and His word is on my tongue.

**23:3** The God of Israel has said; to me the Rock of Israel spoke, "One who rules over humanity righteously, one who rules in the fear of God,

**23:4** is like the light of morning as the sun rises, a morning with no clouds, through radiance after rain bringing tender grass out of the earth."

**23:5** For is not my house like this with God? For He has established an everlasting covenant for me, arranged in everything and kept secure; for all my deliverance and every desire, will He not cause them to sprout?

**23:6** But the worthless, all of them, are like thorns cast aside, for no one takes them by hand;

**23:7** and the man who touches them must equip himself with iron and a spear shaft, and with fire they will be utterly burned on the spot.

**23:8** These are the names of the mighty men who belonged to David: Josheb-basshebeth a Tahchemonite, chief of the captains; he was Adino the Eznite, over eight hundred killed on a single occasion.

**23:9** And after him was Eleazar son of Dodo, son of an Ahohite, one of the three mighty men with David when they defied the Philistines who had gathered there for battle, and the men of Israel withdrew.

**23:10** He rose up and struck down the Philistines until his hand grew tired and his hand clung to the sword, and the LORD accomplished a great deliverance on that day; and the people returned after him only to plunder.

**23:11** And after him was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines gathered into a troop where a plot of the field was full of lentils, and the people fled from the Philistines.

**23:12** But he stood his ground in the middle of the plot, defended it, and struck down the Philistines; and the LORD accomplished a great deliverance.

**23:13** Then three of the thirty chief men went down and came at harvest time to David at the cave of Adullam, while a troop of Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.

**23:14** Now David was in the stronghold at that time, and a garrison of the Philistines was then in Bethlehem.

**23:15** David longed deeply and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem that is at the gate?"

**23:16** Then the three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well of Bethlehem that was at the gate, and carried and brought it to David. Yet he was unwilling to drink it, and poured it out as a drink offering to the LORD.

**23:17** And he said, "Far be it from me, LORD, to do this! Is this the blood of the men who went at the cost of their lives?" So he was unwilling to drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

**23:18** Now Abishai, the brother of Joab, son of Zeruiah, was chief of the three. He brandished his spear against three hundred and killed them, and he had a name among the three.

**23:19** Was he not the most honored of the three? So he became their commander, yet he did not attain to the three.

**23:20** And Benaiah son of Jehoiada, son of a man of valor, great in deeds, from Kabzeel, struck down the two Ariels of Moab; he also went down and struck down a lion in the middle of a pit on a snowy day.

**23:21** He also struck down an Egyptian, a man of imposing appearance. In the Egyptian's hand was a spear, yet he went down to him with a club, snatched the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear.

**23:22** These things Benaiah son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

**23:23** He was more honored than the thirty, yet he did not attain to the three; and David appointed him over his bodyguard.

**23:24** Asahel the brother of Joab was among the thirty; Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem,

**23:25** Shammah the Harodite, Elika the Harodite,

**23:26** Helez the Paltite, Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite,

**23:27** Abiezer the Anathothite, Mebunnai the Hushathite,

**23:28** Zalmon the Ahohite, Maharai the Netophathite,

**23:29** Heleb son of Baanah the Netophathite, Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of the sons of Benjamin,

**23:30** Benaiah a Pirathonite, Hiddai from the brooks of Gaash,

**23:31** Abi-albon the Arbathite, Azmaveth the Barhumite,

**23:32** Eliahba the Shaalbonite, the sons of Jashen, Jonathan,

**23:33** Shammah the Hararite, Ahiam son of Sharar the Ararite,

**23:34** Eliphelet son of Ahasbai, son of the Maacathite, Eliam son of Ahithophel the Gilonite,

**23:35** Hezrai the Carmelite, Paarai the Arbite,

**23:36** Igal son of Nathan from Zobah, Bani the Gadite,

**23:37** Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Beerothite, the armor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah,

**23:38** Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite,

**23:39** Uriah the Hittite; thirty-seven in all.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — Both clauses introduced by ki lo function as rhetorical negative questions without an explicit interrogative particle. The final verb is Hiphil (cause to sprout), echoing the sprouting grass of verse 4.
- **23:8** — The Masoretic Text reads 'he was Adino the Eznite' with Qere ha-etsni (Ketiv ha-etsno) and Qere feminine echat (Ketiv masculine echad) to agree with feminine pa'am; the parallel in 1 Chronicles 11:11 instead reads 'he wielded his spear.'
- **23:13** — Qere reads sheloshah ('three'), while Ketiv reads sheloshim ('thirty'). The Masoretic Text also reads 'at harvest time' (el-qatsir), where 1 Chronicles 11:15 has 'to the rock' (al-hatsur).
- **23:18** — Qere reads ha-sheloshah ('the three'), while Ketiv reads ha-sheloshi ('the captains' or a defective spelling of 'the thirty'). The Masoretic Text likewise reads 'the three' twice in verse 19 (compare 'the thirty' in verse 23).
- **23:20** — Qere reads chayil ('valor', Ketiv chay) and ha-ari ('lion', Ketiv ha-aryeh). The term ariel ('Ariels of Moab') is uncertain and is often understood as a title for lion-like champions.
