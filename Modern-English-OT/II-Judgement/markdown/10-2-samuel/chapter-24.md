## Text

**24:1** Once again the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He incited David against them, saying, "Go, count Israel and Judah."

**24:2** Then the king said to Joab, the army commander who was with him, "Please travel throughout all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, and enroll the people, so that I may know the count of the people."

**24:3** Joab said to the king, "May the LORD your God add to the people a hundred times over as many as they are, while the eyes of my lord the king look on! Yet why does my lord the king take pleasure in this matter?"

**24:4** Yet the word of the king prevailed against Joab and against the army commanders. So Joab and the army commanders went out from before the king to enroll the people, Israel.

**24:5** They crossed the Jordan and made camp at Aroer, on the south side of the city that is in the middle of the valley of Gad, and toward Jazer.

**24:6** Next they went into Gilead and to the land of Tahtim-hodshi, and they went on to Dan-jaan and around to Sidon.

**24:7** They came to the fortress of Tyre and all the cities of the Hivites and the Canaanites, and they went out to the Negev of Judah, at Beersheba.

**24:8** So they traveled throughout the entire land, and at the end of nine months and twenty days they came to Jerusalem.

**24:9** Joab gave the king the count of the enrollment of the people: Israel was eight hundred thousand men of valor who drew the sword, and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand men.

**24:10** Then David's heart struck him after he had counted the people. David said to the LORD, "I have sinned greatly in what I have done. And now, LORD, please put away the iniquity of Your servant, for I have behaved very foolishly."

**24:11** When David got up in the morning, the word of the LORD had come to Gad the prophet, David's seer, saying,

**24:12** "Go and speak to David, 'This is what the LORD says: Three things I hold out before you; choose one of them for yourself, and I will do it to you.'"

**24:13** Then Gad came to David and informed him, and he said to him, "Will seven years of famine come upon you in your land? Or will you flee for three months before your adversaries as they pursue you? Or will there be three days of pestilence in your land? Now know and see what word I should take back to the One who sent me."

**24:14** And David said to Gad, "I am in severe distress. Please let us fall into the hand of the LORD, for His mercies are abundant; yet into the hand of man may I not fall."

**24:15** So the LORD brought pestilence on Israel from the morning until the appointed time, and among the people, from Dan to Beersheba, seventy thousand men died.

**24:16** When the angel reached out his hand toward Jerusalem to destroy it, the LORD relented concerning the disaster and said to the angel who was bringing destruction among the people, "Enough! Now let your hand drop." Now the angel of the LORD was beside the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

**24:17** Then David spoke to the LORD as he saw the angel who was striking among the people, and he said, "Look, I am the one who has sinned, and I am the one who has done wickedly; yet these sheep, what have they done? Let Your hand please be against me and against the house of my father."

**24:18** On that day Gad came to David and said to him, "Go up, set up an altar to the LORD at the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."

**24:19** So David went up in accordance with Gad's word, as the LORD had commanded.

**24:20** Araunah looked out and saw the king and his servants coming over toward him, so Araunah went out and bowed low before the king with his face to the ground.

**24:21** Araunah asked, "Why has my lord the king come to his servant?" David replied, "To purchase the threshing floor from you to build an altar to the LORD, so that the plague may be restrained from the people."

**24:22** Then Araunah said to David, "Let my lord the king take and offer up whatever is good in his eyes. See, the oxen are for the burnt offering, and the threshing sledges and the equipment of the oxen are for the wood."

**24:23** All of this Araunah the king gave to the king. And Araunah said to the king, "May the LORD your God accept you."

**24:24** Yet the king replied to Araunah, "No, for I will certainly purchase it from you for a price, and I will not offer up to the LORD my God burnt offerings at no cost." So David purchased the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

**24:25** There David built an altar to the LORD and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings. Then the LORD answered the plea for the land, and the plague was restrained from Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — Hebrew yemin literally means 'right side,' which denotes south when facing east. The phrase 'the valley of Gad' renders the unusual double-definite construction ha-nachal ha-Gad in the Masoretic Text.
- **24:13** — The Masoretic Text reads 'seven years of famine,' whereas the parallel account in 1 Chronicles 21:12 reads 'three years.'
- **24:16** — Qere reads ha-Arawnah ('Araunah,' with the definite article), while Ketiv reads ha-Awarnah; similarly in verse 18 Qere reads Arawnah for Ketiv Aranyah.
- **24:23** — Following the Masoretic accentuation, ha-melekh stands in apposition to Araunah ('Araunah the king gave to the king'); many interpreters instead read ha-melekh as a vocative continuing Araunah's speech from verse 22 ('All of this, King, Araunah gives to the king').
