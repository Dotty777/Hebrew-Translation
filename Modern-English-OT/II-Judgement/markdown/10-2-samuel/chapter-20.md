## Text

**20:1** Now a worthless man happened to be there whose name was Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjaminite. He blew the ram's horn and said, "We have no share in David, and we have no inheritance in the son of Jesse! Each man to his tents, Israel!"

**20:2** So all the men of Israel withdrew from following David to go after Sheba son of Bichri, but the men of Judah clung to their king from the Jordan all the way to Jerusalem.

**20:3** When David came to his house in Jerusalem, the king took the ten women, the concubines whom he had left to keep the house, and placed them in a guarded house and provided for them, but he did not go in to them. So they remained shut up until the day of their death, in living widowhood.

**20:4** Then the king said to Amasa, "Summon the men of Judah to me within three days, and you yourself be present here."

**20:5** So Amasa went to summon Judah, but he delayed beyond the appointed time that he had set for him.

**20:6** Then David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba son of Bichri will do us more harm than Absalom. Take your lord's servants and pursue after him, so that he does not find fortified cities for himself and escape from our sight."

**20:7** So Joab's men went out after him, along with the Cherethites, the Pelethites, and all the mighty men; they went out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri.

**20:8** When they were by the great stone that is in Gibeon, Amasa came to meet them. Now Joab was dressed in his tunic that he wore, and over it was a sword belt with a sword fastened at his waist in its sheath; and as he went forward, it fell out.

**20:9** And Joab said to Amasa, "Is it well with you, my brother?" And Joab's right hand took hold of Amasa's beard to kiss him.

**20:10** Now Amasa was not on guard against the sword that was in Joab's hand; so he struck him with it in the belly and spilled out his entrails onto the ground, and he did not strike him a second time, and he died. Then Joab and his brother Abishai pursued after Sheba son of Bichri.

**20:11** And one of Joab's young men stood beside him and said, "Whoever favors Joab and whoever is for David, after Joab!"

**20:12** Now Amasa was wallowing in blood in the middle of the highway. And when the man saw that all the people stood still, he moved Amasa off the highway into the field and threw a garment over him, when he saw that everyone who came up to him stood still.

**20:13** Once he had been removed from the highway, every man passed on after Joab to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri.

**20:14** Now he passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel and Beth-maacah, and all the Berites; and they assembled and came after him as well.

**20:15** Then they came and besieged him in Abel Beth-maacah, and they heaped up a siege mound against the city, and it stood against the rampart. And all the people who were with Joab were battering to bring the wall down.

**20:16** Then a wise woman called out from the city, "Listen, listen! Please say to Joab, 'Come near here, so that I may speak to you.'"

**20:17** So he came near to her, and the woman said, "Are you Joab?" And he answered, "I am." Then she said to him, "Listen to the words of your maidservant." And he said, "I am listening."

**20:18** Then she spoke, saying, "In former times they used to say, 'Let them surely inquire at Abel,' and thus they settled matters.

**20:19** I am one of the peaceable and faithful of Israel. You are seeking to put to death a city and a mother in Israel. Why would you swallow up the inheritance of the LORD?"

**20:20** Joab answered and said, "Far be it, far be it from me that I should swallow up or destroy!

**20:21** That is not the case. Rather, a man from the hill country of Ephraim, whose name is Sheba son of Bichri, has lifted up his hand against the king, against David. Hand him over alone, and I will withdraw from the city." And the woman said to Joab, "Look, his head will be thrown to you over the wall."

**20:22** Then the woman went to all the people in her wisdom, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri and threw it to Joab. So he blew the ram's horn, and they dispersed from the city, each man to his tents. And Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.

**20:23** Now Joab was over all the army of Israel, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and over the Pelethites.

**20:24** Adoram was over the forced labor, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder.

**20:25** Sheva was scribe, and Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

**20:26** And Ira the Jairite was also a priest to David.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — "In living widowhood" translates almenut chayyut (literally "widowhood of life"), describing their status as living widows while David remained alive.
- **20:5** — For "he delayed," the qere reads wayyocher (from the root achar/yachar), while the ketiv is spelled wyyhr.
- **20:6** — "Escape from our sight" translates wehitzil enenu (Hiphil of natzal plus "our eye/eyes"), literally "deliver [himself from] our eye(s)" or "snatch away our eye(s)," understood idiomatically as slipping out of reach.
- **20:14** — For "they assembled," the qere reads wayyiqqahalu (Niphal of qahal), correcting the transposed letters of the ketiv (wyqlhw); "the Berites" (habberim) renders the Masoretic gentilic directly.
- **20:23** — For "the Cherethites," the qere reads hakkereti (matching 20:7), while the ketiv reads hakkari ("the Carites").
- **20:25** — For the name "Sheva," the qere reads Sheva, while the ketiv is spelled Sheya.
