## Text

**22:1** David spoke the words of this song to the LORD on the day the LORD rescued him from the grasp of all his enemies and from the grasp of Saul.

**22:2** He said, "The LORD is my crag and my stronghold and my own deliverer;

**22:3** The God of my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield and the horn of my salvation, my secure height and my place of escape, my savior—You save me from violence.

**22:4** I call to the LORD, the One to be praised, and from my enemies I am saved.

**22:5** For the breakers of death enclosed me; the torrents of ruin terrified me.

**22:6** The ropes of Sheol encircled me; the traps of death confronted me.

**22:7** In my distress I called to the LORD, and to my God I called; He heard my voice from His temple, and my cry for help was in His ears.

**22:8** Then the earth heaved and quaked; the foundations of the heavens trembled and heaved, because He burned with anger.

**22:9** Smoke rose from His nostrils, and fire from His mouth devoured; coals blazed from Him.

**22:10** He bent the heavens and descended, and dense darkness was beneath His feet.

**22:11** He rode a cherub and flew, and He was seen upon the wings of the wind.

**22:12** He set darkness around Himself as pavilions—a gathering of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

**22:13** From the radiance before Him, coals of fire blazed.

**22:14** The LORD thundered from the heavens, and the Most High gave forth His voice.

**22:15** He sent forth arrows and scattered them, lightning, and threw them into panic.

**22:16** And the channels of the sea were seen; the foundations of the world were uncovered at the LORD's rebuke, from the blast of the wind of His nostrils.

**22:17** He reached out from the heights and took hold of me; He pulled me out of great waters.

**22:18** He rescued me from my mighty enemy, from those who hated me, for they were stronger than I.

**22:19** They confronted me on the day of my disaster, but the LORD became a support to me.

**22:20** He brought me out into a spacious place; He freed me, because He took pleasure in me.

**22:21** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the purity of my hands He repaid me.

**22:22** For I kept the ways of the LORD and did not turn wickedly from my God.

**22:23** For all His judgments were before me; as for His statutes, I did not turn aside from them.

**22:24** And I was blameless toward Him, and I guarded myself from my iniquity.

**22:25** So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my purity before His eyes.

**22:26** With the loyal You prove Yourself loyal; with the blameless mighty man You prove Yourself blameless.

**22:27** With the pure You prove Yourself pure, and with the crooked You prove Yourself shrewd.

**22:28** And You save an afflicted people, while Your eyes are upon the lofty—You bring them low.

**22:29** For You are my lamp, LORD, and the LORD illuminates my darkness.

**22:30** For with You I run against a raiding band; with my God I leap over a wall.

**22:31** God—His way is blameless; the word of the LORD is refined; He is a shield for all who take refuge in Him.

**22:32** For who is God apart from the LORD? And who is a rock apart from our God?

**22:33** God is my fortress of strength, and He clears my way to be blameless.

**22:34** He makes my feet like those of deer, and upon my high places He makes me stand.

**22:35** He teaches my hands for battle, so that my arms bend a bronze bow.

**22:36** You gave me the shield of Your salvation, and Your answering made me great.

**22:37** You widened my stride beneath me, and my ankles did not slip.

**22:38** I pursued my enemies and annihilated them, and I did not turn back until they were finished off.

**22:39** I finished them off and smashed them, and they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet.

**22:40** You girded me with strength for battle; You made those who rose against me kneel beneath me.

**22:41** And You caused my enemies to show me their backs; as for those who hated me, I wiped them out.

**22:42** They looked around, yet there was no one to save—to the LORD, yet He did not answer them.

**22:43** I ground them fine like the dust of the earth; like the mud of the streets I pounded them, I trampled them.

**22:44** You delivered me from the disputes of my people; You preserved me to be head of nations; a people I had not known serves me.

**22:45** Foreigners come cringing to me; as soon as their ears hear, they obey me.

**22:46** Foreigners wither away and come limping out of their fortresses.

**22:47** The LORD lives, and may my rock be blessed; may the God of the rock of my salvation be exalted—

**22:48** the God who grants vengeance to me and brings peoples down beneath me,

**22:49** and who brings me out from my enemies. You lift me high above those who rise against me; from the man of violence You rescue me.

**22:50** Therefore I will thank You, LORD, among the nations, and to Your name I will sing praise.

**22:51** He is the tower of His king's salvation, and He shows loyal love to His anointed, to David and to his seed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **22:33** — The form wayyatter is pointed as a Hiphil wayyiqtol of ntr ('loosen, set free, clear'), rendered here as 'clears' (some lexicographers instead connect it to twr, 'guide, direct'). The qere reads darki ('my way'), followed here, whereas the ketiv reads darko ('his way').
- **22:34** — The qere reads raglay ('my feet'), followed here in harmony with the first-person suffixes in verses 33-34, whereas the ketiv reads raglaw ('his feet').
- **22:36** — The form anotekha is morphologically the Qal infinitive construct of anah ('answer, respond') with a second-person masculine singular suffix ('Your answering'); compare Psalm 18:36, which reads the noun anwatekha ('Your humility' or 'Your gentleness').
- **22:46** — The verb weyahgeru (Qal of hgr, 'limp, hobble' in comparative Semitic usage, with the pregnant preposition 'out of') is rendered 'come limping out of'; compare Psalm 18:46, which reads the metathesized weyahregu ('come trembling out of').
- **22:51** — The qere reads the noun migdol ('tower of'), followed here in a construct chain ('the tower of His king's salvation'), whereas the ketiv reads the Hiphil participle magdil ('making great' or 'magnifying').
