## Text

**3:1** And the war was long between the house of Saul and the house of David; David kept growing stronger, and the house of Saul kept growing weaker.

**3:2** Sons were born to David in Hebron. His firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelite.

**3:3** And his second was Chileab, by Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite; and the third was Absalom, the son of Maacah, the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur.

**3:4** And the fourth was Adonijah, the son of Haggith; and the fifth was Shephatiah, the son of Abital.

**3:5** And the sixth was Ithream, by Eglah, the wife of David. These were born to David in Hebron.

**3:6** Now while the war went on between the house of Saul and the house of David, Abner was strengthening himself in the house of Saul.

**3:7** Now Saul had a concubine, and her name was Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah. And he said to Abner, "Why did you go in to my father's concubine?"

**3:8** And Abner became exceedingly angry at the words of Ish-bosheth, and he said, "Am I a dog's head belonging to Judah? Today I keep showing loyal love to the house of Saul your father, to his brothers and to his companions, and I have not handed you over into the hand of David; yet you call me to account for guilt regarding the woman today!

**3:9** May God do so to Abner, and add even more to him: just as the LORD swore to David, surely so I will do for him,

**3:10** to pass the kingdom over from the house of Saul and to raise up the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, from Dan as far as Beersheba."

**3:11** And he was no longer able to return a word to Abner, because of his fear of him.

**3:12** Then Abner sent messengers to David on his behalf, saying, "Whose is the land?" and saying, "Make your covenant with me, and look, my hand is with you to turn all Israel around to you."

**3:13** And he said, "Good, I myself will make a covenant with you; only one thing I am asking from you, namely: you will not see my face unless you bring Michal the daughter of Saul first, when you come to see my face."

**3:14** Then David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth the son of Saul, saying, "Give me my wife Michal, whom I betrothed to myself for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines."

**3:15** And Ish-bosheth sent and took her away from her husband, from Paltiel the son of Laish.

**3:16** And her husband went with her, walking and weeping behind her as far as Bahurim. Then Abner said to him, "Go, turn back." And he turned back.

**3:17** Now Abner had a word with the elders of Israel, saying, "Already in days past you were seeking David to be king over you.

**3:18** Now then, do it! For the LORD has spoken concerning David, saying, 'By the hand of My servant David I will deliver My people Israel from the hand of the Philistines and from the hand of all their enemies.'"

**3:19** Abner also spoke in the ears of Benjamin; and Abner went as well to speak in the ears of David in Hebron all that was good in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of all the house of Benjamin.

**3:20** So Abner came to David at Hebron, and twenty men were with him. And David made a banquet for Abner and for the men who were with him.

**3:21** And Abner said to David, "Let me rise and go and gather all Israel to my lord the king, so that they may make a covenant with you, and you will reign over everything that your soul desires." Then David sent Abner off, and he went in peace.

**3:22** Now look, David's servants and Joab arrived from the raid, and they brought abundant plunder with them; and Abner was not with David in Hebron, because he had sent him off, and he had gone in peace.

**3:23** Now Joab and all the army that was with him came, and they reported to Joab, saying, "Abner the son of Ner came to the king, and he sent him off, and he went in peace."

**3:24** Then Joab came to the king and said, "What have you done? Look, Abner came to you! Why then did you send him off, so that he is completely gone?

**3:25** You know Abner the son of Ner, that he came to deceive you, and to know your going out and your coming in, and to know everything that you are doing."

**3:26** Then Joab went out from David, and sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from the cistern of Sirah; yet David did not know.

**3:27** When Abner returned to Hebron, Joab turned him aside into the middle of the gate to speak with him privately; and he struck him there in the belly, and he died, for the blood of his brother Asahel.

**3:28** And David heard afterward and said, "I and my kingdom are guiltless before the LORD forever concerning the bloodshed of Abner the son of Ner.

**3:29** May it fall on Joab's head and on all his father's house; and may there never be cut off from the house of Joab anyone who has a discharge, or is afflicted with leprosy, or grasps a spindle, or falls by the sword, or lacks bread."

**3:30** Thus Joab and his brother Abishai killed Abner, because he had put Asahel their brother to death at Gibeon in the battle.

**3:31** And David said to Joab and to all the people who were with him, "Tear your garments, wrap yourselves in sackcloth, and mourn in front of Abner." And King David was walking behind the bier.

**3:32** And they buried Abner in Hebron; the king raised his voice and wept by Abner's grave, and all the people wept.

**3:33** Then the king lamented over Abner and said, "Should Abner die the death of a fool?

**3:34** Your hands were not bound, and your feet were not placed in bronze shackles; as one falls before sons of injustice, you fell." And all the people wept over him once more.

**3:35** Then all the people came to give David bread to eat while it was still day; but David swore, saying, "May God do so to me, and add even more, if I taste bread or anything at all before the sun sets."

**3:36** And all the people observed this, and it was good in their eyes, just as everything the king did was good in the eyes of all the people.

**3:37** So all the people and all Israel knew on that day that it had not come from the king to put Abner the son of Ner to death.

**3:38** Then the king said to his servants, "Do you not know that a commander and a great man has fallen this day in Israel?

**3:39** And today I am weak, even though anointed king, while these men, the sons of Zeruiah, are too harsh for me. May the LORD repay the one who does evil according to his evil."

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Ketiv reads wyldw, lacking the second waw of the Niphal (either a defective spelling or a Qal/passive form); the translation follows the Qere, Niphal wayyiqtol "were born."
- **3:7** — MT omits an explicit subject for "he said"; the speaker is identified as Ish-bosheth in verse 8.
- **3:18** — MT reads hoshia (a Hiphil infinitive construct or 3ms perfect form of yasha) alongside the first-person suffixes "My servant" and "My people"; rendered as a first-person finite promise ("I will deliver") following the surrounding first-person divine speech.
- **3:25** — Ketiv reads the standard noun form mevo'ekha; Qere reads mova'ekha, assimilating its pattern to the paired noun motsa'akha ("your going out"). Both mean "your coming in."
- **3:29** — Hebrew pelekh means "spindle" (cf. Proverbs 31:19), implying a man unfit for warfare; some interpreters understand it here as a "crutch" or "staff."
