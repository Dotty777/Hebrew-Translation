## Text

**6:1** Then David once more gathered all the select men in Israel, thirty thousand.

**6:2** And David rose and went, together with all the people who were with him, from Baale-judah, to bring up from there the ark of God, over which is called the name, the name of the LORD of hosts who sits enthroned above the cherubim.

**6:3** They loaded the ark of God onto a new cart and carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill; and Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, were driving the new cart.

**6:4** And they carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill, with the ark of God, while Ahio was walking in front of the ark.

**6:5** Now David and the entire house of Israel were celebrating before the LORD with all sorts of cypress-wood instruments, and with lyres, harps, tambourines, sistrums, and cymbals.

**6:6** When they arrived at the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah reached out to the ark of God and grasped it, for the oxen had stumbled.

**6:7** Then the anger of the LORD flared against Uzzah, and God struck him down there on account of the irreverence, and he died there beside the ark of God.

**6:8** And David became angry because the LORD had burst out with an outburst against Uzzah, and that place has been called Perez-uzzah to this day.

**6:9** And David feared the LORD on that day, and he said, "How will the ark of the LORD come to me?"

**6:10** So David was not willing to transfer the ark of the LORD to himself into the City of David; instead, David turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

**6:11** Now the ark of the LORD stayed in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite for three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and his entire household.

**6:12** Then it was reported to King David, saying, "The LORD has blessed the household of Obed-edom and all that is his on account of the ark of God." So David went and brought up the ark of God from the house of Obed-edom into the City of David with joy.

**6:13** Now when those carrying the ark of the LORD had stepped six paces, he sacrificed an ox and a fattened calf.

**6:14** And David was whirling with full strength before the LORD, and David was girded with a linen ephod.

**6:15** And David and the entire house of Israel were bringing up the ark of the LORD with shouting and with the sound of the ram's horn.

**6:16** Now as the ark of the LORD was entering the City of David, Michal, daughter of Saul, looked down through the window and saw King David leaping and whirling before the LORD, and she scorned him in her heart.

**6:17** Then they brought the ark of the LORD in and set it in its place in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it; and David offered up burnt offerings before the LORD, as well as peace offerings.

**6:18** And David finished offering up the burnt offering and the peace offerings, and he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of hosts.

**6:19** Then he distributed to all the people, to the entire multitude of Israel, men and women alike, to each person one loaf of bread, one date cake, and one raisin cake. Then all the people departed, each to his house.

**6:20** When David came back to bless his household, Michal, daughter of Saul, went out to meet David and said, "How honored the king of Israel has made himself today, who uncovered himself today in the sight of the maidservants of his servants, just as one of the worthless fellows brazenly uncovers himself!"

**6:21** David replied to Michal, "It was before the LORD, who chose me over your father and over his entire house, to commission me as leader over the people of the LORD, over Israel; so I will celebrate before the LORD.

**6:22** And I will become even more lightly regarded than this, and I will be lowly in my own eyes; yet with the maidservants you spoke of, with them I will be honored."

**6:23** And as for Michal, daughter of Saul, she had no child until the day of her death.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Masoretic Text reads "mibba'ale yehudah" ("from Baale-judah") despite the following "to bring up from there," and repeats the noun "shem" ("the name, the name of the LORD of hosts").
- **6:5** — The Masoretic Text literally reads "bekhol 'atse veroshim" ("with all cypress woods"), understood here as instruments made of cypress wood; the parallel in 1 Chr 13:8 reads "with all might and with songs."
- **6:7** — The noun "hashshal" ("the irreverence") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and is generally connected to a root attested in Aramaic meaning error, carelessness, or irreverent fault.
- **6:19** — The rare noun "'eshpar" (occurring only here and 1 Chr 16:3) is of uncertain meaning; modern scholarship divides between "date cake" (parallel to "'ashishah," "raisin cake") and "portion of meat."
- **6:22** — The Masoretic Text reads "be'enay" ("in my own eyes"), whereas the Septuagint reads "in your eyes."
