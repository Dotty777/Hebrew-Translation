## Text

**9:1** Then David said, "Is there still anyone left from the house of Saul, so that I may show loyal kindness to him for the sake of Jonathan?"

**9:2** Now the house of Saul had a servant whose name was Ziba, and they summoned him to David. The king said to him, "Are you Ziba?" And he said, "Your servant."

**9:3** The king said, "Is there not still someone of the house of Saul, so that I may show the loyal kindness of God to him?" And Ziba said to the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan, who is crippled in his feet."

**9:4** So the king said to him, "Where is he?" And Ziba said to the king, "Look, he is in the house of Machir son of Ammiel, in Lo-debar."

**9:5** Then King David sent and brought him from the house of Machir son of Ammiel, from Lo-debar.

**9:6** When Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, came to David, he fell on his face and bowed down. And David said, "Mephibosheth!" And he said, "Here is your servant."

**9:7** David said to him, "Do not be afraid, for I will surely show loyal kindness to you for the sake of Jonathan your father; I will restore to you all the land of Saul your father, and you yourself will eat bread at my table continually."

**9:8** And he bowed down and said, "What is your servant, that you have turned toward a dead dog like me?"

**9:9** Then the king called Ziba, Saul's attendant, and said to him, "Everything that belonged to Saul and to all his house I have given to the son of your master.

**9:10** You are to work the ground for him—you, your sons, and your servants—and bring in the yield, so that the son of your master may have bread to eat; yet Mephibosheth, the son of your master, will continually eat bread at my table." Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

**9:11** Then Ziba said to the king, "According to all that my lord the king commands his servant, so your servant will do; and Mephibosheth eats at my table like one of the king's sons."

**9:12** Now Mephibosheth had a young son whose name was Mica, and all the residents of Ziba's house were servants to Mephibosheth.

**9:13** So Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, because he continually ate at the king's table; and he was lame in both of his feet.

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — Hebrew "father" (av) here denotes a grandfather or forefather (Saul, father of Jonathan), just as "son" (ben) in vv. 9-10 refers to Saul's grandson Mephibosheth.
- **9:11** — MT reads "at my table" (shulchani), leaving the second clause either within Ziba's reply or as an unintroduced remark of David; the Septuagint and Syriac read "at David's table" (cf. v. 13).
