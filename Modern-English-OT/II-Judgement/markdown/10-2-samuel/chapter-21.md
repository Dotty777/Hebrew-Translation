## Text

**21:1** Now a famine came in the days of David for three years, year after year, and David sought the face of the LORD. And the LORD said, "It is on account of Saul and on account of the house of bloodguilt, because he put the Gibeonites to death."

**21:2** Then the king summoned the Gibeonites and spoke to them. Now the Gibeonites were not from the sons of Israel, but rather from the remnant of the Amorites; and the sons of Israel had sworn to them, yet Saul had sought to strike them down in his zeal for the sons of Israel and Judah.

**21:3** David said to the Gibeonites, "What shall I do for you, and with what shall I make atonement, so that you may bless the inheritance of the LORD?"

**21:4** The Gibeonites said to him, "We have no claim of silver or gold with Saul or with his house, nor is it ours to put any man to death in Israel." And he said, "Whatever you say, I will do for you."

**21:5** And they said to the king, "As for the man who consumed us and who schemed against us that we would be destroyed from remaining within all the territory of Israel,

**21:6** let seven men from his sons be given to us, and we will impale them for the LORD at Gibeah of Saul, the chosen one of the LORD." And the king said, "I myself will give them."

**21:7** However, the king spared Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, on account of the oath of the LORD that was between them, between David and Jonathan son of Saul.

**21:8** So the king took the two sons of Rizpah daughter of Aiah, whom she had borne to Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth, and the five sons of Michal daughter of Saul, whom she had borne to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.

**21:9** He gave them into the hand of the Gibeonites, and they impaled them on the mountain before the LORD, and all seven of them fell together. They were put to death during the first days of harvest, at the beginning of the barley harvest.

**21:10** Then Rizpah daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and spread it out for herself on the rock, from the beginning of harvest until water poured down on them from the sky; and she did not allow the birds of the sky to rest on them by day, nor the wild animals of the field by night.

**21:11** It was told to David what Rizpah daughter of Aiah, Saul's concubine, had done.

**21:12** So David went and took the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son from the citizens of Jabesh-gilead, who had stolen them from the square of Beth-shan, where the Philistines had hung them on the day the Philistines struck Saul down on Gilboa.

**21:13** He brought up from there the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son, and they gathered the bones of those who had been impaled.

**21:14** They buried the bones of Saul and of Jonathan his son in the land of Benjamin at Zela, in the tomb of Kish his father, and they did everything that the king commanded. And God heeded the plea for the land after that.

**21:15** Now the Philistines had war again with Israel, and David went down, and his servants with him, and they fought with the Philistines; and David became exhausted.

**21:16** And Ishbi-benob, who was among the descendants of Rapha, whose spear weighed three hundred shekels of bronze and who was strapped with a new sword, intended to strike down David.

**21:17** But Abishai son of Zeruiah came to his aid, and he struck down the Philistine and put him to death. Then the men of David swore to him, saying, "You must not go out with us to war anymore, so that you will not quench the lamp of Israel."

**21:18** Now after this, there was war again at Gob with the Philistines; then Sibbecai the Hushathite struck down Saph, who was among the descendants of Rapha.

**21:19** And there was war again at Gob with the Philistines, and Elhanan son of Jaare-oregim the Bethlehemite struck down Goliath the Gittite; and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam.

**21:20** There was war again at Gath, and there was a man of tall stature; the fingers on each of his hands and the toes on each of his feet were six, twenty-four in number, and he too was born to Rapha.

**21:21** He taunted Israel, and Jonathan son of Shimeah, the brother of David, struck him down.

**21:22** These four were born to Rapha in Gath, and they fell at the hand of David and at the hand of his servants.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — "Impale" translates the Hiphil of yaqa' (also in v. 9; Hophal participle in v. 13), a rare verb whose exact mode of public execution or exposure is uncertain. The Qere reads the Hophal yuttan ("let ... be given"), whereas the Ketiv has the Niphal form yntn.
- **21:8** — The Masoretic Text reads "Michal" and "she had borne," whereas 1 Sam 18:19 identifies Merab as the wife of Adriel the Meholathite and 2 Sam 6:23 states that Michal had no child.
- **21:16** — Ishbi-benob follows the Qere (weyishbi; Ketiv wyshbw), and "Rapha" (also in vv. 18, 20, 22) renders Hebrew ha-raphah, with the definite article, designating an eponymous giant figure linked with the Rephaim. Hebrew literally says "girded with a new [feminine]," with "sword" implied by the feminine adjective hadashah.
- **21:19** — The Masoretic Text attributes the killing of Goliath the Gittite here to Elhanan son of Jaare-oregim (lit. "Jaare of weavers," echoing "weavers' beam" at the end of the verse); the parallel in 1 Chr 20:5 has Elhanan son of Jair striking down Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite.
- **21:20** — The Qere reads madon ("stature," from the root mdd; cf. 1 Chr 20:6), whereas the Ketiv reads mdyn (a plural form of mad, "measure").
