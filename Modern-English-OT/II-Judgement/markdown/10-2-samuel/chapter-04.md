## Text

**4:1** When the son of Saul heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his hands went limp, and all Israel was alarmed.

**4:2** Now two men, commanders of raiding bands, belonged to the son of Saul; the name of the one was Baanah, and the name of the second was Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, from the sons of Benjamin, for Beeroth also is reckoned to Benjamin.

**4:3** And the Beerothites fled to Gittaim, and they have been sojourning there until this day.

**4:4** Now Jonathan the son of Saul had a son whose feet were crippled. He was five years old when the report concerning Saul and Jonathan arrived from Jezreel; his nurse lifted him up and fled, and while she was hurrying to flee, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

**4:5** Then the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, went and arrived about the heat of the day at the house of Ish-bosheth, while he was lying down for his midday rest.

**4:6** And in there they came as far as the middle of the house, fetching wheat, and they struck him in the belly; and Rechab and his brother Baanah escaped.

**4:7** Now they came into the house while he was lying on his bed in his bedroom, and they struck him and put him to death and removed his head; and they took his head and went along the way of the Arabah all night.

**4:8** And they brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David in Hebron and said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth, the son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life! And the LORD has granted my lord the king vengeance this day on Saul and on his seed."

**4:9** Then David answered Rechab and his brother Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and said to them, "As the LORD lives, who has redeemed my life from every distress,

**4:10** when someone reported to me, saying, 'Look, Saul is dead,' and in his own eyes he was like a bearer of good news, I seized him and killed him in Ziklag, which was the reward for good news that I gave him.

**4:11** How much more, when wicked men have killed a righteous man in his house, on his bed! Now then, shall I not demand his blood from your hand and purge you from the earth?"

**4:12** So David commanded the young men, and they killed them, severed their hands and their feet, and hung them up by the pool in Hebron. As for the head of Ish-bosheth, they took it and buried it in Abner's grave in Hebron.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — MT reads "the son of Saul" without the expected possessive preposition lamed ("to"), rendered here as "belonged to the son of Saul."
- **4:6** — MT reads hennah ("in there") with the participial phrase loqeche chittim ("fetching wheat"), summarizing the assassination before expanding it in verse 7, without the doorkeeper episode found in the Septuagint.
- **4:10** — Hebrew besorah ("good news") is used here in the rare sense of "reward for good news," with irony: execution at Ziklag was the only reward David gave the self-styled bearer of good news (mevasser).
