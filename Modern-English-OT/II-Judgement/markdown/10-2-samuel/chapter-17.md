## Text

**17:1** Then Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Please let me choose twelve thousand men, and I will rise and pursue David tonight.

**17:2** I will come upon him while he is weary and his hands are weak, and I will throw him into panic; all the people who are with him will flee, and I will strike down the king alone.

**17:3** And I will bring all the people back to you. The man whom you seek means the return of everyone; all the people will be at peace."

**17:4** The proposal seemed right in the eyes of Absalom and in the eyes of all the elders of Israel.

**17:5** Then Absalom said, "Please summon Hushai the Archite as well, and let us hear what is in his mouth too."

**17:6** When Hushai came to Absalom, Absalom spoke to him, saying, "Ahithophel has spoken words like this. Should we carry out his word? If not, you speak."

**17:7** Hushai said to Absalom, "This time, the counsel that Ahithophel has given is not good."

**17:8** Hushai continued, "You yourself know your father and his men, that they are mighty men, and they are bitter of soul, like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field. Furthermore, your father is a man of war and will not spend the night with the people.

**17:9** Look, even now he is hidden in one of the pits or in some other place. And when some fall among them at the very start, whoever hears of it will say, 'There has been a slaughter among the people who follow Absalom.'

**17:10** Then even the valiant man whose heart is like the heart of a lion will utterly melt away, for all Israel knows that your father is a mighty man and that those who are with him are valiant men.

**17:11** Therefore I counsel that all Israel be completely gathered to you, from Dan even to Beersheba, like the sand by the sea in multitude, and that you go into battle in person.

**17:12** Then we will come upon him in one of the places where he is found, and we will settle upon him just as the dew falls on the ground; and of him and of all the men who are with him, not even one will be left.

**17:13** And if he withdraws into a city, then all Israel will bring ropes to that city, and we will drag it down to the valley until not even a pebble is found there."

**17:14** Then Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel." For the LORD had ordained to thwart the good counsel of Ahithophel, so that the LORD might bring disaster upon Absalom.

**17:15** Then Hushai said to Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "In such and such a way Ahithophel counseled Absalom and the elders of Israel, and in such and such a way I myself counseled.

**17:16** Now then, send quickly and tell David, 'Do not spend this night on the plains of the wilderness, but by all means cross over, or else the king and all the people who are with him will be swallowed up.'"

**17:17** Now Jonathan and Ahimaaz were staying at En-rogel; a servant girl would go and tell them, and they would go and tell King David, because they could not be seen entering the city.

**17:18** However, a young man saw them and told Absalom. So both of them went quickly and came to the house of a man in Bahurim who had a well in his courtyard, and they went down into it.

**17:19** The woman took a covering and spread it over the mouth of the well, and scattered crushed grain on top of it, and nothing was known.

**17:20** When Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house, they asked, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?" The woman said to them, "They have crossed the stream of water." They searched but did not find them, so they returned to Jerusalem.

**17:21** After they had gone, the two came up out of the well, went on, and reported to King David. They said to David, "Rise and cross the water quickly, for Ahithophel has counseled like this against you."

**17:22** Then David and all the people who were with him rose and crossed the Jordan. By the light of morning, not even one was missing who had not crossed the Jordan.

**17:23** Now when Ahithophel saw that his counsel had not been followed, he saddled his donkey, rose, and went to his house, to his own city. He put his household in order and hanged himself; so he died and was buried in the tomb of his father.

**17:24** Then David came to Mahanaim, while Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.

**17:25** Absalom set Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Now Amasa was the son of a man whose name was Ithra the Israelite, who had gone in to Abigail daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, the mother of Joab.

**17:26** And Israel and Absalom camped in the land of Gilead.

**17:27** When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, Machir son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim—

**17:28** bedding, basins, pottery vessels, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils, roasted seeds,

**17:29** honey, curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd—brought them to David and to the people who were with him to eat, for they said, "The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness."

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — The Masoretic Hebrew is terse: literally 'like the return of all [is] the man whom you seek,' meaning that removing David alone ensures the peaceful return of the whole nation.
- **17:12** — The Qere corrects the feminine Ketiv numeral ('one,' b-a-ch-t) to the masculine (be-achad) to agree grammatically with the masculine noun 'places' (meqomot).
- **17:20** — 'Stream' translates the Hebrew hapax legomenon mikhal (literally 'mikhal of water'), traditionally understood as a small stream or water channel.
- **17:25** — The Masoretic Text reads 'Ithra the Israelite' (compare 1 Chronicles 2:17, 'Jether the Ishmaelite').
- **17:28** — The Masoretic Text repeats qali ('roasted/parched produce') twice, first after the cereal grains and again after the beans and lentils, rendered here as 'roasted grain' and 'roasted seeds.'
