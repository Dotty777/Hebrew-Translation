## Text

**8:1** Now it happened after this that David struck down the Philistines and subdued them, and David took Metheg-ammah out of the hand of the Philistines.

**8:2** He also struck down Moab and measured them with a cord, making them lie down on the ground; he measured off two cords to put to death and one full cord to keep alive. So Moab became servants to David, bearing tribute.

**8:3** David also struck down Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as he went to restore his control at the Euphrates River.

**8:4** And David captured from him one thousand seven hundred horsemen and twenty thousand foot soldiers; David hamstrung all the chariot horses, yet spared from them enough for one hundred chariots.

**8:5** When Aram of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men of Aram.

**8:6** Then David placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and Aram became servants to David, bearing tribute. And the LORD granted victory to David wherever he went.

**8:7** David also took the gold shields that were on the servants of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.

**8:8** And from Betah and from Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took a very large amount of bronze.

**8:9** Now Toi king of Hamath heard that David had struck down the entire army of Hadadezer,

**8:10** so Toi sent his son Joram to King David to inquire of his welfare and to bless him because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him down, for Hadadezer had been at war with Toi. And in his hand were vessels of silver, vessels of gold, and vessels of bronze.

**8:11** King David consecrated these also to the LORD, along with the silver and the gold that he had consecrated from all the nations he had subjugated—

**8:12** from Aram, from Moab, from the sons of Ammon, from the Philistines, from Amalek, and from the plunder of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

**8:13** David also made a name for himself when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand of Aram in the Valley of Salt.

**8:14** He placed garrisons in Edom; throughout all Edom he placed garrisons, and all Edom became servants to David. And the LORD granted victory to David wherever he went.

**8:15** So David reigned over all Israel, and David continually practiced justice and righteousness for all his people.

**8:16** Now Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;

**8:17** Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Seraiah was scribe;

**8:18** and Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over both the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and the sons of David were priests.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — "Metheg-ammah" literally means "the bridle of the cubit" or "bridle of the mother-city," either a place name or an idiom for control of the Philistine chief city (cf. 1 Chr 18:1).
- **8:3** — "Euphrates" (Perat) is a Masoretic qere wela ketiv, read though unwritten in the consonantal text ("at the river"); "restore his control" is literally "bring back his hand."
- **8:13** — MT reads "Aram" here (as also in v. 12), whereas the Valley of Salt and v. 14 point geographically toward Edom (as read by the Septuagint, Syriac, and 1 Chr 18:12); the translation follows MT.
- **8:18** — MT reads "and Benaiah son of Jehoiada and the Cherethites and the Pelethites" without the preposition "over" (cf. 20:23), here rendered with "over" understood from v. 16 and the paired conjunctions as "both ... and." MT also calls David's sons "priests" (kohanim), likely denoting priestly or high royal officials (cf. 1 Chr 18:17).
