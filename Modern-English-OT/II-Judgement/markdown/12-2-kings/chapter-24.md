## Text

**24:1** In his days, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant for three years; then he turned and rebelled against him.

**24:2** The LORD sent against him raiding bands of Chaldeans, raiding bands of Aram, raiding bands of Moab, and raiding bands of the sons of Ammon; He sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of the LORD that He had spoken by the hand of His servants the prophets.

**24:3** Indeed, this came upon Judah at the command of the LORD, to remove them from His presence because of the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he had done,

**24:4** and also because of the blood of the innocent that he had shed, for he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood; and the LORD was not willing to forgive.

**24:5** As for the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

**24:6** So Jehoiakim lay down with his fathers, and Jehoiachin his son became king in his place.

**24:7** And the king of Egypt did not go out of his land again, for the king of Babylon had taken everything that had belonged to the king of Egypt, from the Brook of Egypt to the Euphrates River.

**24:8** Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan from Jerusalem.

**24:9** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that his father had done.

**24:10** At that time, the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up to Jerusalem, and the city came under siege.

**24:11** Then Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to the city while his servants were besieging it.

**24:12** Then Jehoiachin king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon—he, his mother, his servants, his officials, and his court officers. And the king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.

**24:13** He brought out from there all the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the house of the king, and he cut in pieces all the vessels of gold that Solomon king of Israel had made in the temple of the LORD, just as the LORD had spoken.

**24:14** He carried into exile all Jerusalem, all the officials, and all the mighty men of valor—ten thousand exiles—along with all the artisans and the smiths. No one was left except the poorest of the people of the land.

**24:15** He carried Jehoiachin into exile to Babylon; and the king's mother, the king's wives, his court officers, and the leading men of the land he led into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon.

**24:16** All the men of valor, seven thousand, and the artisans and the smiths, one thousand—all of them mighty men who waged war—the king of Babylon brought them into exile to Babylon.

**24:17** Then the king of Babylon made Mattaniah, his uncle, king in his place, and changed his name to Zedekiah.

**24:18** Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

**24:19** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that Jehoiakim had done.

**24:20** For on account of the anger of the LORD, this happened in Jerusalem and in Judah until He cast them away from His presence. And Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.

## Translator Notes

- **24:10** — Following the Qere plural alu ('came up'), agreeing with the plural subject 'servants'; the Ketiv reads the 3ms singular alah.
- **24:14** — Following the Qere construct aseret before 'thousands' ('ten thousand'); the Ketiv reads the absolute form asarah. Hebrew goleh is a collective Qal active participle ('exiles').
- **24:15** — Following the Qere ele ('leading men', literally 'rams', used metaphorically for prominent leaders); the Ketiv reads the variant form ule.
