## Text

**12:1** Jehoash was seven years of age when he became king.

**12:2** In the seventh year of Jehu, Jehoash began to reign, and for forty years he reigned in Jerusalem; the name of his mother was Zibiah from Beersheba.

**12:3** And Jehoash did what was right in the eyes of the LORD through all his days in which Jehoiada the priest taught him.

**12:4** Only the high places were not removed; still the people were sacrificing and burning incense at the high places.

**12:5** Then Jehoash said to the priests, "All the silver of the consecrated things that is brought into the house of the LORD—current silver, each man's silver of personal valuation, all the silver that arises in a man's heart to bring into the house of the LORD—

**12:6** let the priests take it for themselves, each man from his acquaintance; and they themselves shall repair the damage of the house, wherever any damage is found there."

**12:7** Yet it happened in the twenty-third year of King Jehoash that the priests had not repaired the damage of the house.

**12:8** So King Jehoash summoned Jehoiada the priest and the priests and said to them, "Why are you not repairing the damage of the house? Now then, do not take silver from your acquaintances, but give it for the damage of the house."

**12:9** So the priests consented not to take silver from the people and not to repair the damage of the house.

**12:10** Then Jehoiada the priest took a chest, bored a hole in its lid, and placed it beside the altar, on the right side as one enters the house of the LORD; and the priests, the keepers of the threshold, would put into it all the silver brought into the house of the LORD.

**12:11** Whenever they saw that there was abundant silver in the chest, the king's scribe and the high priest would go up, tie it up in bags, and count the silver found in the house of the LORD.

**12:12** Then they would hand over the weighed silver into the hands of those doing the work who were placed in charge of the house of the LORD, and they would pay it out to the woodworkers and to the builders working on the house of the LORD,

**12:13** and to the masons and the stonecutters, and to buy timber and quarried stone to repair the damage of the house of the LORD, and for everything expended on the house for repair.

**12:14** However, no silver basins, wick trimmers, sprinkling bowls, trumpets, or any vessel of gold or vessel of silver were made for the house of the LORD from the silver brought into the house of the LORD.

**12:15** Rather, they would give it to those doing the work, and with it they would repair the house of the LORD.

**12:16** And they did not demand an accounting from the men into whose hand they gave the silver to hand over to those doing the work, for they acted with faithfulness.

**12:17** Silver from a guilt offering and silver from sin offerings was not brought into the house of the LORD; they belonged to the priests.

**12:18** At that time Hazael king of Aram went up, fought against Gath, and captured it; then Hazael set his face to go up against Jerusalem.

**12:19** So Jehoash king of Judah took all the consecrated things that Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, his fathers, the kings of Judah, had consecrated, and his own consecrated things, and all the gold found in the treasuries of the house of the LORD and the house of the king, and sent them to Hazael king of Aram; and he withdrew from Jerusalem.

**12:20** Now the rest of the deeds of Joash and everything he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**12:21** His servants rose up, formed a conspiracy, and struck down Joash at Beth-millo, which goes down to Silla.

**12:22** Jozacar son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer, his servants, struck him down, and he died. They buried him with his fathers in the city of David, and his son Amaziah reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **12:5** — Hebrew kesef 'over ('current silver,' cf. Gen 23:16) can also be read as 'silver of one passing over' in the census (cf. Exod 30:13). Hebrew kesef nafshot 'erko literally means 'silver of persons of his valuation' (cf. Lev 27:2-8).
- **12:10** — Following the Qere ('on the right side,' lit. 'from the right'); the Ketiv uses the preposition 'in/at' rather than 'from' before 'right.'
- **12:12** — Following the pointed Qere reading 'into the hands of' (plural construct, against a singular variant 'hand of') and the Qere Hophal participle 'those placed in charge' (against the Ketiv 'the overseers/appointed ones').
- **12:13** — Hebrew lechozqah is a feminine noun with preposition meaning 'for repair/strengthening' (not a suffix 'her').
- **12:21** — Hebrew hayyored Silla literally reads 'the one going down to Silla' (Qal active participle), likely referring to the road or descent leading down toward Silla.
