## Text

**1:1** And Moab rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab.

**1:2** Ahaziah fell through the latticework in his upper room in Samaria and grew ill. He sent messengers and said to them, "Go, inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, whether I will recover from this illness."

**1:3** But the angel of the LORD spoke to Elijah the Tishbite, "Get up, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and speak to them: 'Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are going to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?

**1:4** Therefore this is what the LORD says: The bed onto which you have gone up, you will not come down from it, for you will certainly die.'" So Elijah went.

**1:5** The messengers returned to him, and he said to them, "Why is it that you have returned?"

**1:6** They said to him, "A man came up to meet us and said to us, 'Go, return to the king who sent you, and speak to him: This is what the LORD says, Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are sending to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore, the bed onto which you have gone up, you will not come down from it, for you will certainly die.'"

**1:7** He spoke to them, "What was the appearance of the man who came up to meet you and spoke these words to you?"

**1:8** They said to him, "A hairy man, with a leather belt fastened around his waist." And he said, "That is Elijah the Tishbite."

**1:9** Then he sent to him a commander of fifty with his fifty. He went up to him, and there he was, sitting on top of the hill. He spoke to him, "Man of God, the king has spoken: Come down."

**1:10** Elijah answered and spoke to the commander of fifty, "Now if I am a man of God, may fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty." So fire came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.

**1:11** Again he sent to him another commander of fifty with his fifty. And he spoke up and said to him, "Man of God, this is what the king says: Come down quickly."

**1:12** Elijah answered and spoke to them, "If I am a man of God, may fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty." So the fire of God came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.

**1:13** Again he sent a third commander of fifty with his fifty. The third commander of fifty went up and came, knelt on his knees before Elijah, pleaded with him, and spoke to him, "Man of God, please let my life and the lives of these fifty servants of yours be precious in your sight.

**1:14** Look, fire came down from heaven and consumed the first two commanders of fifty and their fifties; so now let my life be precious in your sight."

**1:15** Then the angel of the LORD spoke to Elijah, "Go down with him; do not fear him." So he got up and went down with him to the king.

**1:16** And he spoke to him, "This is what the LORD says: Because you have sent messengers to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron—is it because there is no God in Israel to inquire of His word?—therefore, the bed onto which you have gone up, you will not come down from it, for you will certainly die."

**1:17** So he died according to the word of the LORD that Elijah had spoken, and Jehoram began to reign in his place in the second year of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, because he had no son.

**1:18** As for the rest of the deeds of Ahaziah that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The rhetorical question uses an emphatic double negative, literally 'from without there is not God in Israel,' underscoring the offense of consulting Ekron's god; the same construction recurs in 1:6 and 1:16.
- **1:7** — The Hebrew noun mishpat ('judgment, custom, manner') here refers to the man's characteristic appearance and bearing.
- **1:8** — Literally 'a man, possessor of hair,' which may indicate either a physically hairy man or one wearing a coarse garment of hair.
- **1:15** — The form oto here represents the preposition 'with' plus suffix (normally itto), a spelling common in Kings that recurs in 3:12 and 3:26.
