## Text

**23:1** Then the king sent word, and they gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem to him.

**23:2** The king went up to the house of the LORD, and every man of Judah and all the residents of Jerusalem with him, along with the priests, the prophets, and all the people, from the smallest to the greatest. He read in their ears all the words of the book of the covenant that had been found in the house of the LORD.

**23:3** Then the king stood beside the pillar and made the covenant before the LORD, to walk after the LORD and to keep His commandments, His testimonies, and His statutes with all the heart and all the soul, to uphold the words of this covenant that were written in this book. And all the people stood in the covenant.

**23:4** The king commanded Hilkiah the high priest, the priests of the second rank, and the keepers of the threshold to bring out from the temple of the LORD all the vessels made for Baal, for Asherah, and for all the host of heaven. He burned them outside Jerusalem in the fields of Kidron and carried their dust to Bethel.

**23:5** He did away with the idolatrous priests whom the kings of Judah had appointed, who burned incense at the high places in the cities of Judah and the surroundings of Jerusalem, along with those who burned incense to Baal, to the sun, to the moon, to the constellations, and to all the host of heaven.

**23:6** He brought the Asherah out from the house of the LORD, outside Jerusalem to the Brook Kidron, burned it at the Brook Kidron, crushed it to dust, and cast its dust over the grave of the common people.

**23:7** He tore down the houses of the male cult prostitutes that were in the house of the LORD, where the women were weaving tents for Asherah.

**23:8** He brought all the priests from the cities of Judah and defiled the high places where the priests had burned incense, from Geba to Beersheba. He also tore down the high places of the gates that were at the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the governor of the city, which were on a man's left at the gate of the city.

**23:9** Yet the priests of the high places did not ascend to the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem; instead, they ate unleavened bread in the midst of their brothers.

**23:10** He also defiled Topheth, which is in the Valley of Ben-hinnom, so that no one could cause his son or his daughter to pass through the fire to Molech.

**23:11** He did away with the horses that the kings of Judah had given to the sun, at the entrance of the house of the LORD, beside the chamber of Nathan-melech the court officer, which was in the precincts; and he burned the chariots of the sun with fire.

**23:12** The king tore down the altars that were on the roof of the upper chamber of Ahaz, which the kings of Judah had made, along with the altars that Manasseh had made in the two courts of the house of the LORD; he crushed them away from there and cast their dust into the Brook Kidron.

**23:13** The king also defiled the high places that were east of Jerusalem, south of the Mount of Destruction, which Solomon king of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the detestable thing of the Sidonians, for Chemosh the detestable thing of Moab, and for Milcom the abomination of the sons of Ammon.

**23:14** He shattered the standing stones, cut down the Asherim, and filled their place with human bones.

**23:15** Furthermore, the altar that was at Bethel, the high place that Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, had made—he tore down both that altar and the high place; he burned the high place, crushed it to dust, and burned an Asherah.

**23:16** Then Josiah turned and saw the graves that were there on the mountain, and he sent and took the bones from the graves and burned them on the altar and defiled it, according to the word of the LORD that the man of God had proclaimed, who proclaimed these things.

**23:17** Then he said, "What is that monument that I see?" And the men of the city said to him, "It is the grave of the man of God who came from Judah and proclaimed these things that you have done against the altar of Bethel."

**23:18** He said, "Leave him alone; let no man move his bones." So they spared his bones, together with the bones of the prophet who came from Samaria.

**23:19** Josiah also removed all the houses of the high places that were in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel had made to provoke anger, and he did to them according to all the deeds that he had done at Bethel.

**23:20** He slaughtered all the priests of the high places who were there on the altars and burned human bones upon them. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

**23:21** Then the king commanded all the people, saying, "Observe Passover to the LORD your God, as is written in this book of the covenant."

**23:22** For no Passover like this had been observed since the days of the judges who judged Israel, nor in all the days of the kings of Israel and the kings of Judah.

**23:23** Instead, in the eighteenth year of King Josiah, this Passover was observed to the LORD in Jerusalem.

**23:24** Moreover, Josiah purged the mediums and the spiritists, the household gods, the idols, and all the detestable things that were seen in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, in order to uphold the words of the law that were written in the book that Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the LORD.

**23:25** No king before him was like him, who turned back to the LORD with all his heart, all his soul, and all his strength, according to the entire law of Moses; and after him no one like him arose.

**23:26** Still, the LORD did not turn back from the heat of His great anger, with which His anger burned against Judah, because of all the provocations with which Manasseh had provoked Him.

**23:27** And the LORD said, "I will remove Judah also from My presence, just as I removed Israel, and I will reject this city that I have chosen, Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, 'My name will be there.'"

**23:28** As for the rest of the acts of Josiah and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

**23:29** In his days, Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt went up to the king of Assyria by the Euphrates River. King Josiah went to meet him, and he put him to death at Megiddo when he saw him.

**23:30** His servants carried him dead by chariot from Megiddo, brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his tomb. Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah, anointed him, and made him king in place of his father.

**23:31** Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.

**23:32** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that his fathers had done.

**23:33** Pharaoh Neco put him in bonds at Riblah in the land of Hamath so that he would not reign in Jerusalem, and he imposed on the land a fine of one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold.

**23:34** Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king in place of Josiah his father, and changed his name to Jehoiakim. But he took Jehoahaz, and he went to Egypt and died there.

**23:35** Jehoiakim gave the silver and the gold to Pharaoh, yet he assessed the land to give the silver at Pharaoh's command. He exacted the silver and the gold from the people of the land, each man according to his assessment, to give to Pharaoh Neco.

**23:36** Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zebudah daughter of Pedaiah from Rumah.

**23:37** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that his fathers had done.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — MT has the 3ms Piel wayyiqtol wayqatter ('and he burned incense'), understood distributively of the appointed priests. Hebrew mazzalot ('constellations') is a hapax legomenon denoting celestial bodies or zodiac constellations.
- **23:7** — Hebrew battim literally means 'houses'; as the object of the Qal participle oregot ('weaving'), it refers to woven tent-shrines, canopies, or hangings made for Asherah.
- **23:10** — Following the Qere ben-hinnom ('son of Hinnom'); the Ketiv reads the plural bene-hinnom ('sons of Hinnom').
- **23:12** — Hebrew wayyarots mishsham is formally ambiguous: it may be a Qal wayyiqtol of ratsats ('crush/smash') in a pregnant construction ('crushed and removed them from there'), or a Qal wayyiqtol of ruts ('he ran/hurried from there').
- **23:29** — The preposition al before 'the king of Assyria' is rendered 'to' (as often in later Kings, cf. 24:12), reflecting Pharaoh Neco's march to aid Assyria rather than to oppose it.
- **23:33** — Following the Qere mimmelok ('from reigning', i.e., so that he would not reign); the Ketiv reads bimlok ('while reigning').
