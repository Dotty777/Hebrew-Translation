## Text

**21:1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began reigning, and he reigned for fifty-five years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Hephzibah.

**21:2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, following the abominations of the nations that the LORD had dispossessed from before the sons of Israel.

**21:3** He rebuilt the high places that his father Hezekiah had destroyed; he set up altars to Baal and made an Asherah, just as Ahab king of Israel had made, and he bowed down to all the host of the heavens and served them.

**21:4** He also built altars in the house of the LORD, concerning which the LORD had said, "In Jerusalem I will place My name."

**21:5** And he built altars for all the host of the heavens in the two courtyards of the house of the LORD.

**21:6** He made his son pass through the fire, practiced soothsaying and interpreted omens, and appointed a medium and spiritists. He did evil extensively in the eyes of the LORD, to provoke Him to anger.

**21:7** He placed the carved image of the Asherah that he had made inside the house about which the LORD had said to David and to his son Solomon, "In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will place My name forever.

**21:8** And I will not again cause the foot of Israel to wander from the land that I gave to their fathers, provided only that they are careful to act in keeping with all that I have commanded them, and with the whole law that My servant Moses commanded them."

**21:9** Yet they did not listen, and Manasseh led them astray to do more evil than the nations that the LORD had destroyed from before the sons of Israel.

**21:10** Then the LORD spoke by the hand of His servants the prophets, saying,

**21:11** "Because Manasseh, king of Judah, has done these abominations, acting more wickedly than all that the Amorites before him had done, and has also caused Judah to sin through his idols,

**21:12** therefore thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Look, I am bringing calamity upon Jerusalem and Judah, such that both ears of everyone who hears it will ring.

**21:13** I will stretch out over Jerusalem the measuring line of Samaria and the plumb line of the house of Ahab, and I will wipe Jerusalem just as someone wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it face down.

**21:14** I will forsake the remnant of My inheritance, giving them into the hand of their enemies, and they will become plunder and prey for all their enemies,

**21:15** because they have done what is evil in My eyes and have kept provoking Me to anger, from the day that their fathers came out of Egypt until this day."

**21:16** In addition, Manasseh shed innocent blood in great abundance, until he had filled Jerusalem from end to end, apart from his sin by which he caused Judah to sin, doing what was evil in the eyes of the LORD.

**21:17** As for the rest of the deeds of Manasseh, and everything he did, and his sin that he sinned, are they not written in the scroll of the annals of the kings of Judah?

**21:18** Then Manasseh lay down with his fathers and was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza; and his son Amon reigned in his place.

**21:19** Amon was twenty-two years old when he began reigning, and he reigned for two years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Meshullemeth, the daughter of Haruz, from Jotbah.

**21:20** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Manasseh had done.

**21:21** He walked in the entire way that his father had walked; he served the idols that his father had served and bowed down to them.

**21:22** He abandoned the LORD, the God of his fathers, and he did not walk in the LORD's way.

**21:23** Amon's servants conspired against him and put the king to death inside his house.

**21:24** Then the people of the land struck down all the conspirators against King Amon, and the people of the land made his son Josiah king in his place.

**21:25** As for the rest of the deeds of Amon that he did, are they not written in the scroll of the annals of the kings of Judah?

**21:26** They buried him in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and his son Josiah reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **21:6** — The Qal verb 'asah ('made' or 'appointed') with singular 'ov ('medium' or 'necromantic spirit') and plural yidde'onim ('spiritists') refers to instituting or consulting practitioners of necromancy and spiritism.
- **21:12** — The qere reads shom'ah ('everyone who hears it,' Qal participle with a feminine singular suffix referring back to 'calamity'); the ketiv writes sh-m-'-y-w, likely anticipating the ending of 'his ears.'
- **21:16** — The phrase 'from end to end' renders the literal Hebrew idiom 'mouth to mouth,' meaning filled to the brim or throughout the entire city (cf. 2 Kings 10:21; Ezra 9:11).
- **21:26** — The Masoretic verb is third-person masculine singular Qal active ('one buried him,' functioning impersonally as 'they buried him' or 'he was buried'), contrasting with the Niphal passive used for Manasseh in 21:18.
