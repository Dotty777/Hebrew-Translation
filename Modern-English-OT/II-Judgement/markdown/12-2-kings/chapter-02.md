## Text

**2:1** Now it happened, when the LORD was about to bring Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind, that Elijah and Elisha went from Gilgal.

**2:2** Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, because the LORD has sent me as far as Bethel." But Elisha said, "As the LORD lives and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

**2:3** The sons of the prophets who were at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the LORD is taking your master from over your head?" He said, "I also know it; be silent."

**2:4** Elijah said to him, "Elisha, please stay here, because the LORD has sent me to Jericho." But he said, "As the LORD lives and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

**2:5** The sons of the prophets who were at Jericho drew near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the LORD is taking your master from over your head?" He said, "I also know it; be silent."

**2:6** Then Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, because the LORD has sent me to the Jordan." But he said, "As the LORD lives and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

**2:7** Fifty men from the sons of the prophets went and stood across from them at a distance, while the two of them stood beside the Jordan.

**2:8** Elijah took his cloak, folded it up, and struck the water, and it divided to this side and that, so the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

**2:9** When they had crossed over, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I may do for you before I am taken from you." Elisha said, "Please let a double share of your spirit come to me."

**2:10** He said, "You have asked a difficult thing. If you see me being taken from you, it will be so for you; but if not, it will not happen."

**2:11** As they kept walking along and talking, suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them, and Elijah went up in a whirlwind to heaven.

**2:12** Elisha was watching and crying out, "My father, my father, the chariot of Israel and its horsemen!" And he did not see him again. Then he seized his garments and tore them into two pieces.

**2:13** He picked up Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him, returned, and stood at the bank of the Jordan.

**2:14** Then he took Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him, struck the water, and said, "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" When he too struck the water, it divided to this side and that, and Elisha crossed over.

**2:15** When the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho saw him from across the way, they said, "Elijah's spirit has come to rest on Elisha." And they came to meet him and bowed down to him to the ground.

**2:16** They said to him, "Look now, there are fifty strong men with your servants. Please let them go and search for your master; perhaps the Spirit of the LORD has lifted him up and cast him onto one of the mountains or into one of the valleys." But he said, "You must not send them."

**2:17** Yet they pressed him to the point of embarrassment, so he said, "Send them." So they sent fifty men, who searched for three days but did not find him.

**2:18** When they came back to him—he was staying in Jericho—he said to them, "Did I not tell you, 'Do not go'?"

**2:19** The men of the city said to Elisha, "Look now, the site of the city is good, just as my lord sees, but the water is bad and the land causes miscarriage."

**2:20** He said, "Get me a new bowl and put salt in it." And they brought it to him.

**2:21** Then he went out to the spring of the water, threw salt into it, and said, "This is what the LORD says: I have healed this water; no longer will there be death or miscarriage from it."

**2:22** So the water remained healed to this day, according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.

**2:23** From there he went up to Bethel. While he was going up along the road, young boys came out from the city, taunted him, and said to him, "Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!"

**2:24** He turned around, saw them, and cursed them in the name of the LORD. Then two female bears came out of the forest and tore apart forty-two boys from among them.

**2:25** Then he traveled from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he went back to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **2:8** — The verb wayyiglom ('folded it up,' Qal of glm) is a biblical hapax legomenon.
- **2:9** — Literally 'a portion of two' (pi-shenayim), the legal idiom for the firstborn's double share of an inheritance (cf. Deut 21:17), marking Elisha's request to succeed Elijah as primary heir.
- **2:10** — The passive participle luqqach ('being taken') lacks the usual mem- prefix of a Pual participle, or represents a Qal passive participle.
- **2:14** — Following the Masoretic punctuation (atnah on 'Elijah'), aph-hu ('he too') introduces the resumed clause describing Elisha's striking of the water; some read it with the preceding question as 'even He?'
- **2:16** — Ketiv/Qere orthographic variant: the Qere reads haggeyayot (standard plural of gay, 'valleys'), whereas the Ketiv omits the consonantal yod (hgywt), with the same meaning.
