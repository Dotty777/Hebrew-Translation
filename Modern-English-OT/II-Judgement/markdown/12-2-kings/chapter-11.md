## Text

**11:1** Now Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, and she arose and destroyed all the royal seed.

**11:2** But Jehosheba, daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being put to death—him and his nurse, into the bedroom—and they hid him from Athaliah, so that he was not put to death.

**11:3** And he stayed with her in the house of the LORD in hiding for six years, while Athaliah was reigning over the land.

**11:4** Then in the seventh year Jehoiada sent and took the commanders of hundreds of the Carites and of the guards, and brought them to himself into the house of the LORD. He made a covenant with them and put them under oath in the house of the LORD, and he showed them the king's son.

**11:5** And he commanded them, saying, "This is the thing that you are to do: one third of you who come in on the Sabbath shall keep the watch of the king's house;

**11:6** and one third shall be at the gate of Sur, and one third at the gate behind the guards; so you shall keep the watch of the house as a barrier.

**11:7** And the two divisions among you, all who go out on the Sabbath, shall keep the watch of the house of the LORD for the king.

**11:8** And you shall surround the king on every side, each man with his weapons in his hand; and whoever enters the ranks shall be put to death. And be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in."

**11:9** So the commanders of hundreds did according to everything that Jehoiada the priest had commanded. Each man took his men—those coming in on the Sabbath together with those going out on the Sabbath—and they came to Jehoiada the priest.

**11:10** And the priest gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears and the shields that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of the LORD.

**11:11** Then the guards stood, every man with his weapons in his hand, from the right side of the house as far as the left side of the house, alongside the altar and the house, on every side around the king.

**11:12** Then he brought out the king's son and placed upon him the crown and the testimony; and they made him king and anointed him, and they clapped their hands and said, "May the king live!"

**11:13** When Athaliah heard the sound of the guards and the people, she came to the people at the house of the LORD.

**11:14** She looked, and there was the king standing by the pillar according to the custom, with the commanders and the trumpeters beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Then Athaliah tore her garments and cried out, "Treason! Treason!"

**11:15** Then Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of hundreds who were appointed over the army and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks, and whoever follows her, put to death with the sword." For the priest had said, "Let her not be put to death in the house of the LORD."

**11:16** So they laid hands on her, and she went by way of the horses' entrance to the king's house, and there she was put to death.

**11:17** Then Jehoiada made the covenant between the LORD and the king and the people, that they would be a people for the LORD, and also between the king and the people.

**11:18** And all the people of the land went to the house of Baal and tore it down; its altars and its images they thoroughly shattered, and they killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, before the altars. And the priest set overseers over the house of the LORD.

**11:19** Then he took the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, the guards, and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the LORD and came by way of the gate of the guards to the king's house. And he sat on the throne of the kings.

**11:20** So all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; for they had put Athaliah to death with the sword at the king's house.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — The Qere reads the Hophal participle ("who were being put to death") for the irregular Ketiv form; the Hebrew also places "him and his nurse, into the bedroom" directly after "stole him away" without an additional verb and shifts to the plural Hiphil verb "they hid him."
- **11:4** — The Qere reads "hundreds" ("me'ot") for the unusual Ketiv spelling ("me'iyot"), an orthographic Ketiv/Qere variant that recurs in 11:9, 10, and 15.
- **11:6** — "As a barrier" renders the Hebrew hapax legomenon "massah" (from a root meaning "to pull or ward off"), denoting defense against intrusion; "coming in on the Sabbath" (11:5) and "going out on the Sabbath" (11:7) are idioms for guard shifts rotating on and off duty.
- **11:15** — The Hebrew phrase "el-mibbayit la-sederot" literally means "inside/between the ranks," depicting Athaliah being escorted out between the lines of armed guards.
- **11:16** — The Hebrew idiom literally reads "they placed hands for her," understood either as seizing her ("they laid hands on her") or as parting to make way for her to exit the temple precinct.
