## Text

**18:1** Now it happened in the third year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, that Hezekiah son of Ahaz, king of Judah, became king.

**18:2** He was twenty-five years of age when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years; and the name of his mother was Abi, daughter of Zechariah.

**18:3** And he did what was upright in the eyes of the LORD, according to everything that his father David had done.

**18:4** It was he who removed the high places, shattered the sacred pillars, cut down the Asherah, and crushed to pieces the bronze serpent that Moses had made; for until those days the sons of Israel had been burning incense to it, and he called it Nehushtan.

**18:5** In the LORD, the God of Israel, he trusted; and after him there was no one like him among all the kings of Judah, nor among those who were before him.

**18:6** And he clung to the LORD; he did not turn aside from following Him, and he kept His commandments that the LORD commanded Moses.

**18:7** The LORD was with him; wherever he went out, he met with success. And he rebelled against the king of Assyria and did not serve him.

**18:8** He himself struck the Philistines as far as Gaza and its borders, from watchmen's tower to fortified city.

**18:9** And it happened in the fourth year of King Hezekiah—that is, the seventh year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel—that Shalmaneser, king of Assyria, went up against Samaria and laid siege to it.

**18:10** And they captured it at the end of three years; in the sixth year of Hezekiah—that is, the ninth year of Hoshea, king of Israel—Samaria was captured.

**18:11** Then the king of Assyria exiled Israel to Assyria and placed them in Halah, and at the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes,

**18:12** because they did not listen to the voice of the LORD their God, and they transgressed His covenant—all that Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded—they did not listen, and they did not do it.

**18:13** And in the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib, king of Assyria, went up against all the fortified cities of Judah and seized them.

**18:14** And Hezekiah, king of Judah, sent to the king of Assyria at Lachish, saying, "I have sinned; turn back from me. Whatever you place upon me, I will bear." So the king of Assyria laid upon Hezekiah, king of Judah, three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.

**18:15** So Hezekiah gave all the silver that was found in the house of the LORD and in the treasuries of the house of the king.

**18:16** At that time Hezekiah cut off the doors of the temple of the LORD and the doorposts that Hezekiah, king of Judah, had overlaid, and gave them to the king of Assyria.

**18:17** Then the king of Assyria sent Tartan, Rab-saris, and Rabshakeh from Lachish to King Hezekiah with a large army to Jerusalem. And they went up and came to Jerusalem; and when they had gone up and arrived, they stood by the conduit of the upper pool, which is on the highway of the launderer's field.

**18:18** And they called for the king, and there went out to them Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was over the household, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder.

**18:19** And Rabshakeh said to them, "Please say to Hezekiah, 'This is what the great king, the king of Assyria, says: What is this confidence in which you have trusted?

**18:20** You say—only talk of the lips—that there is counsel and might for the war. Now on whom have you trusted, that you rebelled against me?

**18:21** Now look, you have trusted in the staff of this crushed reed, in Egypt; if a man leans on it, it will go into his palm and pierce it. So is Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to all who trust in him.'

**18:22** And if you say to me, 'In the LORD our God we have trusted,' is He not the one whose high places and whose altars Hezekiah removed, and said to Judah and to Jerusalem, 'Before this altar you must bow down in Jerusalem'?

**18:23** And now, please make a wager with my master, the king of Assyria, and I will give you two thousand horses, if you are able to put riders on them yourself.

**18:24** And how will you turn back the face of a single governor among the least of my master's servants, while you trust in Egypt for chariots and for horsemen?

**18:25** Now have I come up apart from the LORD against this place to destroy it? It was the LORD who said to me, 'Go up against this land and destroy it.'"

**18:26** Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebna, and Joah said to Rabshakeh, "Please speak to your servants in Aramaic, for we understand it; and do not speak with us in Judean in the ears of the people who are on the wall."

**18:27** But Rabshakeh said to them, "Is it to your master and to you that my master has sent me to speak these words? Is it not to the men sitting on the wall, who are to eat their excrement and to drink the water of their feet with you?"

**18:28** Then Rabshakeh stood and called out in a loud voice in Judean, and spoke and said, "Hear the word of the great king, the king of Assyria.

**18:29** This is what the king says: Do not allow Hezekiah to deceive you, for he is not able to deliver you from his hand.

**18:30** And do not allow Hezekiah to cause you to trust in the LORD, saying, 'The LORD will certainly deliver us, and this city will not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria.'

**18:31** Do not listen to Hezekiah; for this is what the king of Assyria says: Make peace with me and come out to me, and eat, each one from his vine and each one from his fig tree, and drink, each one the water of his cistern,

**18:32** until I come and take you to a land like your land—a land of grain and new wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of fresh olive oil and honey—so that you may live and not die. And do not listen to Hezekiah when he entices you, saying, 'The LORD will deliver us.'

**18:33** Have the gods of the nations actually delivered, each one his land, from the hand of the king of Assyria?

**18:34** Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah? Did they indeed deliver Samaria from my hand?

**18:35** Who among all the gods of the lands are those who delivered their land from my hand, so that the LORD would deliver Jerusalem from my hand?"

**18:36** But the people kept silent and did not answer him a word, for the command of the king was, "You must not answer him."

**18:37** Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was over the household, Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, came to Hezekiah with torn garments and reported to him the words of Rabshakeh.

## Translator Notes

- **18:16** — The Hebrew verb qissas (Piel, cut off or stripped) takes the doors and the rare term omenot (doorposts or supports, appearing only here in the Hebrew Bible) as direct objects without explicitly naming gold, though stripping the metal plating is implied by had overlaid (sippah, Piel).
- **18:20** — The opening clause is elliptical in Hebrew, literally "You say—only a word of lips—counsel and might for the war," with the second-person Qal perfect amarta (whereas the parallel in Isaiah 36:5 reads first-person "I say").
- **18:27** — The translation follows the Masoretic Qere readings "their excrement" (soatam) and "the water of their feet" (meme raglehem), polite euphemisms read in place of the plainer written Ketiv forms "their dung" (harehem) and "their urine" (shenehem).
- **18:29** — The Masoretic Text literally reads "from his hand" (third-person reference to the king of Assyria, matching "This is what the king says"), whereas some ancient versions read "from my hand" and the parallel in Isaiah 36:14 omits the prepositional phrase.
- **18:31** — Literally "Make a blessing with me" (asu-itti berakah), a Hebrew diplomatic idiom for coming to terms of peace or presenting a tribute of submission.
