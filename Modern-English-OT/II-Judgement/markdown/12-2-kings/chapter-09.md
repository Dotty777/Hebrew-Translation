## Text

**9:1** Now Elisha the prophet summoned one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Gird your waist, take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-Gilead.

**9:2** When you arrive there, look there for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi; go in, have him rise from among his brothers, and bring him into an inner room.

**9:3** Then take the flask of oil and pour it on his head, and say, 'This is what the LORD says: I have anointed you as king over Israel.' Then open the door and flee, and do not wait."

**9:4** So the young man, the young prophet, went to Ramoth-Gilead.

**9:5** When he arrived, look, the commanders of the army were sitting there. He said, "I have a word for you, commander." Jehu asked, "For which one of all of us?" He replied, "For you, commander."

**9:6** So he rose and went into the house; then he poured the oil on his head and said to him, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'I have anointed you as king over the people of the LORD, over Israel.

**9:7** You shall strike down the house of Ahab your master, that I may avenge the blood of My servants the prophets and the blood of all the servants of the LORD from the hand of Jezebel.

**9:8** The whole house of Ahab shall perish, and I will cut off from Ahab every male, both bound and free, in Israel.

**9:9** And I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.

**9:10** As for Jezebel, the dogs will eat her in the plot of Jezreel, and there will be no one to bury her.'" Then he opened the door and fled.

**9:11** When Jehu came out to his master's servants, one said to him, "Is it peace? Why did this madman come to you?" He said to them, "You yourselves know the man and his talk."

**9:12** They said, "That is a lie! Please tell us." So he said, "Such and such he said to me, saying, 'This is what the LORD says: I have anointed you as king over Israel.'"

**9:13** Then they hurried, and each man took his garment and laid it beneath him on the bare steps; and they blew the ram's horn and said, "Jehu is king!"

**9:14** So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Now Joram had been keeping guard at Ramoth-Gilead, he and all Israel, against Hazael king of Aram.

**9:15** But King Jehoram had returned to be healed in Jezreel from the wounds that the Arameans had inflicted on him when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. So Jehu said, "If this is your wish, let no escapee leave the city to go and report it in Jezreel."

**9:16** Then Jehu mounted up and went to Jezreel, for Joram was lying there. And Ahaziah king of Judah had gone down to see Joram.

**9:17** Now the watchman was standing on the tower in Jezreel, and he saw the company of Jehu as he approached, and he said, "I see a company." Jehoram said, "Take a horseman and send him to meet them, and let him say, 'Is it peace?'"

**9:18** So the horseman went to meet him and said, "This is what the king says: 'Is it peace?'" Jehu answered, "What do you have to do with peace? Turn behind me!" Then the watchman reported, saying, "The messenger reached them, but he has not returned."

**9:19** So he sent a second horseman, who came to them and said, "This is what the king says: 'Peace?'" Jehu answered, "What do you have to do with peace? Turn behind me!"

**9:20** The watchman reported, saying, "He reached them, and he has not returned. And the driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he drives madly."

**9:21** Jehoram said, "Hitch up!" So they hitched his chariot. Then Jehoram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his chariot; they went out to meet Jehu and found him at the plot of Naboth the Jezreelite.

**9:22** When Jehoram saw Jehu, he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" He replied, "What peace can there be so long as there are the prostitutions of your mother Jezebel and her many sorceries?"

**9:23** Then Jehoram turned his hands and fled, and he said to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

**9:24** But Jehu filled his hand with the bow and struck Jehoram between his shoulders; the arrow came out through his heart, and he slumped down in his chariot.

**9:25** Then he said to Bidkar his officer, "Lift him up and throw him into the plot of the field of Naboth the Jezreelite; for remember how, when you and I were riding in pairs behind his father Ahab, the LORD laid this oracle upon him:

**9:26** 'Surely I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons yesterday, declares the LORD, and I will repay you on this plot, declares the LORD.' Now then, lift him up and throw him into the plot, according to the word of the LORD."

**9:27** When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled by way of Beth-Haggan. Jehu pursued after him and said, "Strike him too in the chariot!" at the ascent of Gur, which is near Ibleam. And he fled to Megiddo and died there.

**9:28** His servants carried him by chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb with his fathers in the city of David.

**9:29** Now in the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah became king over Judah.

**9:30** When Jehu came to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; she painted her eyes with kohl, adorned her head, and looked out through the window.

**9:31** As Jehu entered the gate, she said, "Is it peace, Zimri, killer of his master?"

**9:32** He lifted his face toward the window and said, "Who is with me? Who?" And two or three eunuchs looked out at him.

**9:33** He said, "Throw her down!" So they threw her down, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and he trampled her.

**9:34** Then he went inside, ate, and drank, and he said, "Attend now to this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

**9:35** So they went to bury her, but they found nothing of her except the skull, the feet, and the palms of the hands.

**9:36** They returned and reported it to him. And he said, "This is the word of the LORD that He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 'In the plot of Jezreel the dogs will eat the flesh of Jezebel,

**9:37** and the corpse of Jezebel will be like dung on the surface of the field in the plot of Jezreel, so that they will not say, "This is Jezebel."'"

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Masoretic text repeats the noun hanna'ar ('the young man'), accented to read 'the young man, the young prophet' or 'the young man, the prophet's attendant.'
- **9:8** — 'Every male' renders the vivid Hebrew idiom mashtin beqir (literally 'one who urinates against a wall'); 'both bound and free' (atsur we'azuv, literally 'restrained and let go') is a merism encompassing every category of person.
- **9:13** — 'The bare steps' translates gerem hamma'alot, literally 'the bone/structure of the steps,' meaning either the bare steps themselves or the top landing of the stairway.
- **9:23** — Literally 'turned his hands,' a chariot-driving idiom for pulling the reins to wheel around.
- **9:27** — The Masoretic text moves straight from Jehu's command at the ascent of Gur to Ahaziah's flight to Megiddo without an explicit clause stating the blow's execution, whereas the Septuagint adds 'and they struck him.'
