## Text

**10:1** Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. So Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to the officials of Jezreel, the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab, saying,

**10:2** "And now, as soon as this letter reaches you, since the sons of your master are with you, and with you are the chariotry and the horses, and a fortified city and the weapons,

**10:3** choose the best and most upright among the sons of your master, and set him on the throne of his father, and fight for the house of your master."

**10:4** But they feared exceedingly, and they said, "Look, the two kings did not stand before him, so how can we ourselves stand?"

**10:5** So the one over the household, and the one over the city, along with the elders and the guardians, sent word to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants, and all that you say to us we will do. We will not make any man king; do what is good in your eyes."

**10:6** Then he wrote a letter to them a second time, saying, "If you are for me and listen to my voice, take the heads of the men, the sons of your master, and come to me at Jezreel about this time tomorrow." Now the king's sons, seventy men, were with the great men of the city, who were bringing them up.

**10:7** And when the letter reached them, they took the sons of the king and slaughtered seventy men; then they put their heads in baskets and sent them to him at Jezreel.

**10:8** And the messenger arrived and reported to him, saying, "They have brought the heads of the king's sons." And he said, "Place them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until the morning."

**10:9** And it happened in the morning that he went out and stood and said to all the people, "You are innocent. Look, I conspired against my master and killed him, yet who struck down all these?

**10:10** Know then that nothing from the word of the LORD will fall to the ground, which the LORD spoke concerning the house of Ahab; and the LORD has done what He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah."

**10:11** Then Jehu struck down all who remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, along with all his great men, his close friends, and his priests, until he left him no survivor.

**10:12** Then he arose and came and went toward Samaria; he was at Beth-eked of the Shepherds along the road.

**10:13** And Jehu met the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah, and he said, "Who are you?" And they said, "We are the brothers of Ahaziah, and we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother."

**10:14** And he said, "Seize them alive!" So they seized them alive and slaughtered them at the cistern of Beth-eked, forty-two men; and he did not leave a single man of them.

**10:15** And he went from there and met Jehonadab son of Rechab coming toward him, and he greeted him and said to him, "Is your heart upright, just as my heart is with your heart?" And Jehonadab said, "It is, indeed it is!" "Give me your hand." So he gave his hand, and he brought him up to himself into the chariot.

**10:16** And he said, "Come with me and look upon my zeal for the LORD." So they had him ride in his chariot.

**10:17** When he came to Samaria, he struck down all who remained of Ahab in Samaria until he destroyed them, according to the word of the LORD which He spoke to Elijah.

**10:18** Then Jehu assembled all the people and said to them, "Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him greatly.

**10:19** So now summon to me all the prophets of Baal, all his worshipers, and all his priests; let not a man be missing, for I have a great sacrifice for Baal. Everyone who is missing will not live." But Jehu acted with cunning in order to destroy the worshipers of Baal.

**10:20** And Jehu said, "Consecrate a solemn assembly for Baal." So they proclaimed it.

**10:21** Then Jehu sent word through all Israel, and all the worshipers of Baal arrived, so that not a single man was left who did not come. They entered the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was filled from end to end.

**10:22** And he said to the one over the wardrobe, "Bring out robes for all the worshipers of Baal." So he brought out the robes for them.

**10:23** Then Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab entered the house of Baal, and he said to the worshipers of Baal, "Search and see that there are none of the servants of the LORD here with you, but only the worshipers of Baal alone."

**10:24** Then they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had stationed eighty men for himself outside and had said, "Whoever lets anyone escape from the men I am bringing into your hands, his life will be in place of that man's life."

**10:25** And it happened, as soon as he finished offering the burnt offering, that Jehu said to the guards and to the officers, "Go in and strike them down; let not a man come out!" So they struck them down with the edge of the sword; and the guards and the officers threw them out and went as far as the citadel of the house of Baal.

**10:26** Then they brought out the standing pillars of the house of Baal and burned them.

**10:27** And they tore down the standing pillar of Baal, and they tore down the house of Baal and turned it into a latrine to this day.

**10:28** So Jehu destroyed Baal from Israel.

**10:29** Yet as for the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he made Israel sin, Jehu did not turn aside from following them—the golden calves that were at Bethel and at Dan.

**10:30** And the LORD said to Jehu, "Because you have done well in doing what is right in My eyes, and have done to the house of Ahab according to everything that was in My heart, sons to the fourth generation will sit for you on the throne of Israel."

**10:31** Yet Jehu did not take care to walk in the law of the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart; he did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam, by which he made Israel sin.

**10:32** In those days the LORD began to cut away at Israel, and Hazael struck them down throughout all the territory of Israel:

**10:33** from the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, all the land of Gilead—the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the Manassites—from Aroer, which is by the valley of the Arnon, both Gilead and Bashan.

**10:34** Now the rest of the deeds of Jehu, and everything that he did, and all his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**10:35** And Jehu lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

**10:36** Now the days that Jehu reigned over Israel were twenty-eight years in Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "the officials of Jezreel" (rather than Samaria, as in some ancient versions) and literally "the guardians of Ahab," referring to the guardians entrusted with raising Ahab's sons.
- **10:12** — The Masoretic Hebrew uses three consecutive wayyiqtol verbs ("arose and came and went"), and "Beth-eked of the Shepherds" literally means "house of binding of the shepherds," likely a sheep-shearing station along the road.
- **10:15** — Masoretic vocalization and cantillation pair the repeated affirmative particle ("yesh wa-yesh," rendered "It is, indeed it is!") as Jehonadab's emphatic reply, followed directly by Jehu's command without an explicit speaker-change clause.
- **10:25** — "Citadel" translates Hebrew "ir" (literally "city"), here denoting the walled inner precinct or stronghold within the temple complex of Baal.
- **10:27** — The Ketiv reads a noun from the root for dung/excrement ("dunghills/outhouses"), while the Masoretic Qere euphemistically substitutes "motsa'ot" (literally "places of going out," i.e., a latrine), which is followed here.
