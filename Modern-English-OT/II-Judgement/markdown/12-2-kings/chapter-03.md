## Text

**3:1** Now Jehoram son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, and he reigned for twelve years.

**3:2** He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, yet not like his father and his mother, because he removed the pillar of Baal that his father had made.

**3:3** Still, he held fast to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin; he did not turn away from them.

**3:4** Now Mesha, king of Moab, was a sheep breeder, and he regularly rendered to the king of Israel one hundred thousand lambs along with the wool of one hundred thousand rams.

**3:5** And it happened, at the death of Ahab, that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

**3:6** So King Jehoram went out from Samaria on that day and mustered all Israel.

**3:7** He went and sent word to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me against Moab for battle?" He replied, "I will go up. As I am, so are you; my people are as your people, and my horses are as your horses."

**3:8** Then he asked, "By which way should we go up?" And he answered, "By the way of the wilderness of Edom."

**3:9** So the king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom set out, and they circled around on a journey of seven days, and there was no water for the camp or for the animals that followed them.

**3:10** Then the king of Israel said, "Alas! The LORD has summoned these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab!"

**3:11** But Jehoshaphat asked, "Is there no prophet of the LORD here, so that we may inquire of the LORD through him?" One of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, "Here is Elisha son of Shaphat, who poured water over Elijah's hands."

**3:12** Jehoshaphat said, "With him is the word of the LORD." Then the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to him.

**3:13** Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What is there between me and you? Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother." But the king of Israel said to him, "No, for the LORD has summoned these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab."

**3:14** Elisha said, "As the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, if it were not that I honor the presence of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, I would neither look toward you nor see you.

**3:15** But now, get me a musician." And it happened, as the musician was playing, that the hand of the LORD came upon him.

**3:16** And he said, "This is what the LORD says: Make this streambed full of trenches.

**3:17** For this is what the LORD says: You will not see wind and you will not see rain, yet that streambed will be filled with water, and you will drink—you, your livestock, and your animals.

**3:18** And this is an easy thing in the eyes of the LORD; He will also give Moab into your hand.

**3:19** You will strike every fortified city and every select city, bring down every good tree, block up every spring of water, and ruin every good plot of land with stones."

**3:20** In the morning, at the time the offering was brought up, suddenly water came from the direction of Edom, and the land was filled with the water.

**3:21** Now all Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, so all who could strap on armor and older were called to arms, and they stood at the border.

**3:22** When they got up early in the morning, the sun was shining upon the water, and from across the way Moab saw the water red like blood.

**3:23** They said, "This is blood! The kings have surely destroyed one another, and each has struck down his companion! Now then, to the plunder, Moab!"

**3:24** They came to the camp of Israel, and Israel rose up and struck Moab, so Moab fled before them; and Israel struck into it, continuing to strike Moab.

**3:25** They tore down the cities, and onto every good plot of land each man threw his stone and filled it; they blocked up every spring of water and brought down every good tree, until they left only its stones in Kir-hareseth. Then the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

**3:26** Seeing that the battle was too strong for him, the king of Moab took with him seven hundred men who drew the sword in order to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

**3:27** Then he took his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him up as a burnt offering on the wall. And great wrath came upon Israel, so they withdrew from him and returned to the land.

## Translator Notes

- **3:16** — The Qal infinitive absolute asoh functions as an imperative ('Make'), and the doubled noun gevim gevim ('trenches, trenches') expresses distribution ('full of trenches').
- **3:23** — The phrase pairs a Hophal infinitive absolute (hochorev) with a Niphal perfect (nechervu) of chrb ('fight, lay waste') for strong emphasis ('have surely destroyed one another').
- **3:24** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the Qere reads wayyakku-vah ('and they struck into it,' referring to Moab's territory), followed by the continuing Hiphil infinitive wehakkot ('continuing to strike'); the Ketiv wybw reads 'and they entered into it.'
- **3:25** — The clause ad-hish'ir avaneyha baqqir chareset is compressed, literally 'until leaving its stones in Kir-hareseth,' indicating that the devastation left only the stonework of Kir-hareseth standing.
- **3:27** — Hebrew qetseph-gadol ('great wrath') does not specify the source of the wrath in the text, leaving open whether it denotes divine displeasure or fierce Moabite fury.
