## Text

**13:1** In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria, reigning seventeen years.

**13:2** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and walked after the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin; he did not turn aside from them.

**13:3** So the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram and into the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael all those days.

**13:4** Then Jehoahaz implored the favor of the LORD, and the LORD listened to him, because He saw the oppression of Israel, that the king of Aram had oppressed them.

**13:5** So the LORD gave Israel a deliverer, and they came out from under the hand of Aram; and the sons of Israel lived in their tents as before.

**13:6** Yet they did not turn aside from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, by which he caused Israel to sin; he walked in them, and the Asherah also remained standing in Samaria.

**13:7** For no troops had been left to Jehoahaz except fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers, because the king of Aram had destroyed them and made them like dust in threshing.

**13:8** Now the rest of the deeds of Jehoahaz and everything he did, and his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**13:9** So Jehoahaz lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and his son Joash became king in his place.

**13:10** In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria, reigning sixteen years.

**13:11** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD; he did not turn aside from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he caused Israel to sin, but walked in them.

**13:12** Now the rest of the deeds of Joash and everything he did, and his might with which he fought against Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**13:13** And Joash lay down with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat upon his throne; and Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

**13:14** Now Elisha had fallen ill with his illness from which he would die; and Joash king of Israel went down to him, wept over his face, and said, "My father, my father! The chariotry of Israel and its horsemen!"

**13:15** And Elisha said to him, "Take a bow and arrows." So he took for himself a bow and arrows.

**13:16** Then he said to the king of Israel, "Set your hand on the bow." And he set his hand on it, and Elisha placed his hands upon the hands of the king.

**13:17** He said, "Open the window eastward," and he opened it. Then Elisha said, "Shoot!" And he shot. And he said, "The LORD's arrow of deliverance, and an arrow of deliverance over Aram! You will strike Aram at Aphek until you bring them to an end."

**13:18** Then he said, "Take the arrows," and he took them. He said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground!" So he struck three times and halted.

**13:19** Then the man of God grew angry with him and said, "You ought to have struck five or six times; then you would have struck Aram until you had brought them to an end, but now you will strike Aram three times."

**13:20** Then Elisha died, and they buried him. Now raiding bands of Moab would come into the land at the coming of the year.

**13:21** It happened, while they were burying a man, that suddenly they saw the raiding band, so they threw the man into Elisha's tomb; and the man went in and touched the bones of Elisha, and he came to life and rose up on his feet.

**13:22** Now Hazael king of Aram had oppressed Israel through all the days of Jehoahaz.

**13:23** Yet the LORD showed them grace, had compassion on them, and turned toward them for the sake of His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; He was not willing to destroy them, nor did He cast them away from His presence until now.

**13:24** Then Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-hadad reigned in his place.

**13:25** Then Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back the cities from the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael, which he had taken from the hand of his father Jehoahaz in war. Three times Joash defeated him and restored the cities of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Hebrew mimmennah ('from it,' feminine singular) refers collectively to the plural noun 'sins' (likewise bah, 'in it,' in 13:6 and 13:11).
- **13:6** — The Masoretic Text shifts from plural 'they did not turn aside' (saru) to masculine singular 'he walked' (halakh), referring either to King Jehoahaz or to Israel collectively.
- **13:7** — Hebrew lo hish'ir is an active Hiphil 3ms verb ('did not leave') without an explicit subject, translated impersonally here ('no troops had been left'), though the unexpressed subject may be the LORD or the king of Aram.
- **13:19** — Hebrew lehakkot ('to strike,' Hiphil infinitive construct with the preposition l-) expresses past obligation here: 'You ought to have struck.'
- **13:20** — Hebrew ba shanah (lit. 'coming of a year' or 'a year came') is a concise idiom meaning at the turn or beginning of the year, when military campaigning resumed in spring.
