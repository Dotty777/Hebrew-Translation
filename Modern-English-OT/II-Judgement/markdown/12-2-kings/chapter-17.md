## Text

**17:1** In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king in Samaria over Israel for nine years.

**17:2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, yet not like the kings of Israel who were before him.

**17:3** Against him Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up, and Hoshea became a servant to him and rendered tribute to him.

**17:4** But the king of Assyria discovered a conspiracy in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt and had not brought up tribute to the king of Assyria as he had year by year; so the king of Assyria confined him and bound him in prison.

**17:5** Then the king of Assyria went up through all the land; he went up to Samaria and laid siege to it for three years.

**17:6** In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and carried Israel into exile to Assyria; he settled them in Halah, and along the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

**17:7** Now this happened because the sons of Israel had sinned against the LORD their God, who had brought them up out of the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and they feared other gods.

**17:8** They walked in the statutes of the nations whom the LORD had dispossessed from before the sons of Israel, and in those that the kings of Israel had made.

**17:9** The sons of Israel did in secret things that were not right against the LORD their God; they built for themselves high places in all their cities, from watchmen's tower to fortified city.

**17:10** They erected for themselves standing pillars and Asherim on every high hill and under every lush tree.

**17:11** There they burned incense at all the high places, like the nations whom the LORD had exiled from before them, and they did evil things to provoke the LORD to anger.

**17:12** They served idols, about which the LORD had said to them, "You must not do this thing."

**17:13** Yet the LORD warned Israel and Judah by the hand of all the prophets, every seer, saying, "Turn back from your evil ways and keep My commandments, My statutes, in accordance with all the law that I commanded your fathers and that I sent to you by the hand of My servants the prophets."

**17:14** Yet they did not listen; instead, they stiffened their neck like the neck of their fathers, who did not trust in the LORD their God.

**17:15** They rejected His statutes and His covenant that He had made with their fathers, as well as His warnings with which He had warned them. They went after futility and became futile, and after the nations that were around them, concerning whom the LORD had commanded them not to act like them.

**17:16** They abandoned all the commandments of the LORD their God and made for themselves cast images, two calves; they made an Asherah, bowed down to all the host of heaven, and served Baal.

**17:17** They caused their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire, practiced divinations and read omens, and sold themselves to do what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, to provoke Him to anger.

**17:18** So the LORD became very angry with Israel and removed them from His presence; none remained except the tribe of Judah alone.

**17:19** Judah also did not keep the commandments of the LORD their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel that they had made.

**17:20** So the LORD rejected all the seed of Israel, afflicted them, and gave them into the hand of plunderers, until He had cast them from His presence.

**17:21** When He had torn Israel from the house of David, and they had made Jeroboam son of Nebat king, Jeroboam drove Israel away from following the LORD and made them sin a great sin.

**17:22** The sons of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam that he committed; they did not turn aside from them,

**17:23** until the LORD removed Israel from His presence, just as He had spoken by the hand of all His servants the prophets. So Israel went into exile from its own land to Assyria to this day.

**17:24** Then the king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim, and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the sons of Israel. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its cities.

**17:25** And it happened at the beginning of their living there that they did not fear the LORD; so the LORD sent lions among them, and they kept killing some of them.

**17:26** So they spoke to the king of Assyria, saying, "The nations that you exiled and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the ordinance of the god of the land; so he has sent lions among them, and look, they are putting them to death, because they do not know the ordinance of the god of the land."

**17:27** Then the king of Assyria gave a command, saying, "Take there one of the priests whom you exiled from there, and let them go and live there, and let him teach them the ordinance of the god of the land."

**17:28** So one of the priests whom they had exiled from Samaria came and lived in Bethel, and he kept teaching them how they should fear the LORD.

**17:29** Yet each nation kept making its own gods and placed them in the houses of the high places that the Samaritans had made, each nation in their cities where they were living.

**17:30** The men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, the men of Cuth made Nergal, and the men of Hamath made Ashima.

**17:31** The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites were burning their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

**17:32** They also feared the LORD, and they appointed for themselves from all their ranks priests of the high places, who officiated for them in the houses of the high places.

**17:33** They feared the LORD, yet they served their own gods according to the custom of the nations from which they had been carried into exile.

**17:34** To this day they act according to the former customs: they do not fear the LORD, nor do they act according to their statutes or their ordinance, or according to the law and the commandment that the LORD commanded the sons of Jacob, whom He named Israel.

**17:35** The LORD made a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, "You must not fear other gods, nor bow down to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them.

**17:36** Rather, the LORD, who brought you up from the land of Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm, Him you must fear, and to Him you must bow down, and to Him you must sacrifice.

**17:37** And the statutes and the ordinances and the law and the commandment that He wrote for you, you must be careful to do all the days, and you must not fear other gods.

**17:38** And the covenant that I made with you, you must not forget, and you must not fear other gods.

**17:39** Rather, the LORD your God you must fear, and He will deliver you from the hand of all your enemies."

**17:40** Yet they did not listen, but they kept acting according to their former custom.

**17:41** So these nations feared the LORD, yet they also served their carved images; their children and their children's children also do just as their fathers did, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **17:9** — The verb rendered "did in secret" (waychappe'u) is a hapax legomenon, the Piel of ch-p-' (a byform of ch-p-h, "cover, conceal"), expressing covert or disguised conduct.
- **17:13** — Qere reads the plural construct form nevi'e ("all the prophets, every seer"), whereas Ketiv reads nvy'w ("His prophet" or "His prophets").
- **17:21** — Because Qal qara ("tore") is transitive in Biblical Hebrew, the LORD (from v. 20) is its implied subject and "Israel" its object. For the following verb, Qere reads wayyaddach (Hiphil of n-d-ch, "drove away"), whereas Ketiv reads wyd' (from the rare byform n-d-').
- **17:27** — The Masoretic Text shifts from singular "one of the priests" to plural jussives ("let them go and live there," likely encompassing the priest together with his household or escorts) before returning to singular ("let him teach them").
- **17:31** — Qere reads elohe ("gods of") and Sepharvayim ("Sepharvaim"), whereas Ketiv writes 'lh and sprym respectively.
