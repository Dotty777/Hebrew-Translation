## Text

**19:1** Then Job replied and said:

**19:2** "How long will you grieve my soul and crush me with words?

**19:3** These ten times you have humiliated me; you are not ashamed to mistreat me.

**19:4** And even if indeed I have erred, with me my error lodges.

**19:5** If truly you exalt yourselves over me and argue my disgrace against me,

**19:6** then know that God has wronged me and drawn His hunting net around me.

**19:7** Look, I shout, 'Violence!' yet I go unanswered; I cry out for help, yet there is no justice.

**19:8** He has walled off my way so that I cannot pass through, and He places darkness over my paths.

**19:9** He has stripped my glory off me and taken away the crown of my head.

**19:10** He demolishes me all around, and I am gone; like a tree He uproots my hope.

**19:11** He has kindled His wrath against me and regards me as one of His adversaries.

**19:12** Together His troops advance; they build up their road against me and encamp all around my tent.

**19:13** He has placed my brothers far away from me, and those who know me are utterly alienated from me.

**19:14** My relatives have held back, and my close companions have forgotten me.

**19:15** Those staying in my house and my female servants regard me as a stranger; in their eyes I have become a foreigner.

**19:16** I call out to my servant, yet he does not reply; with my own mouth I implore him.

**19:17** My breath is repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to the sons of my womb.

**19:18** Even young boys scorn me; when I stand up, they speak against me.

**19:19** All the men of my inner circle detest me, and the ones I loved have turned against me.

**19:20** To my skin and to my flesh my bone clings, and I have escaped by the skin of my teeth.

**19:21** Show me pity, show me pity, you friends of mine, for the hand of God has struck me!

**19:22** Why do you pursue me like God, and are not sated with my flesh?

**19:23** If only my words were written down now! If only they were inscribed in a scroll,

**19:24** with an iron stylus and lead, carved into the rock forever!

**19:25** Yet I myself know that my Redeemer is alive, and at the last He will rise upon the dust.

**19:26** And after this skin of mine has been stripped away, still from my flesh I will gaze upon God,

**19:27** whom I myself will gaze upon for myself, and my eyes will see, and not a stranger. My inmost being fails within my chest!

**19:28** If you say, 'How shall we pursue him?' and that the root of the matter is found in me,

**19:29** fear the sword for yourselves, for fury brings the penalties of the sword, so that you may know there is a judgment."

## Translator Notes

- **19:17** — The verb hannoti is Qal perfect 1cs of hnn II ("be loathsome, foul-smelling"), though traditional exegesis sometimes links it to hnn I ("my pleading"). Literally "sons of my womb" (benei vitni), referring either to full brothers born of the same womb (cf. 3:10) or to Job's own kin.
- **19:26** — Hebrew weahar ori niqqephu-zot (lit. "and after my skin they have stripped off this") uses an impersonal 3cp Piel of nqp II ("cut off, strip away, destroy") with deictic zot ("this"). The preposition in mibbesari ("from my flesh") can denote vantage point ("from/in my flesh") or separation ("apart from my flesh").
- **19:27** — Literally "my kidneys (kilyotai) fail in my bosom (beheqi)," the Hebrew anatomical idiom for one's deepest emotions and longing.
- **19:28** — The Masoretic text reads nimtsa-vi ("is found in me"), shifting from direct quotation into Job's own perspective; some Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions read bo ("in him").
- **19:29** — Ketiv shdyn (shedin) vs. Qere shaddun (relative particle sha, "that," prefixed to dun, a by-form of din, "judgment"); both the written and read forms yield "that there is a judgment."
