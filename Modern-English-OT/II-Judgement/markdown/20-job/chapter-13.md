## Text

**13:1** "Look, all of it my eye has seen; my ear has heard and understood it.

**13:2** Just as you know, I also know; I do not fall short of you.

**13:3** However, I myself would speak to the Almighty, and I wish to argue with God.

**13:4** Yet you are plasterers of falsehood; all of you are worthless healers.

**13:5** If only you would remain utterly silent, and that would be wisdom for you!

**13:6** Listen, please, to my argument, and pay attention to the pleadings of my lips.

**13:7** Will you speak injustice for God, and for Him will you speak deceit?

**13:8** Will you show favoritism toward Him, or will you contend for God?

**13:9** Would it be good when He investigates you? Or as one deceives a mortal, will you deceive Him?

**13:10** He will certainly rebuke you if in secret you show favoritism.

**13:11** Will His majesty not terrify you, and His dread fall upon you?

**13:12** Your memorable sayings are proverbs of ashes; your bulwarks are bulwarks of clay.

**13:13** Stay silent before me so that I myself may speak, and let come upon me whatever will.

**13:14** Why do I carry my flesh in my teeth and place my life in my hand?

**13:15** Even if He kills me, in Him I will hope; nevertheless, I will defend my ways to His face.

**13:16** This too will be my deliverance, for no godless person will come before Him.

**13:17** Listen intently to my speech, and let my declaration be in your ears.

**13:18** Look now, I have laid out a case; I know that I myself will be in the right.

**13:19** Who is the one who will contend with me? For then I would keep silent and expire.

**13:20** Only do not do two things to me; then I will not hide myself from Your face:

**13:21** Move Your hand far away from me, and do not let Your dread terrify me.

**13:22** Then call, and I myself will answer; or I will speak, and answer me back.

**13:23** How many iniquities and sins do I have? Make my transgression and my sin known to me.

**13:24** Why do You hide Your face and regard me as an enemy of Yours?

**13:25** Will You terrorize a blown leaf, and chase dry stubble?

**13:26** For You write down bitter things against me and cause me to inherit the iniquities of my youth.

**13:27** You place my feet in the stocks and keep watch over all my paths; You trace a boundary around the soles of my feet.

**13:28** And he wears out like a decaying thing, like a garment that a moth has eaten.

## Translator Notes

- **13:8** — Show favoritism renders the Hebrew idiom literally meaning 'lift up His face' (similarly in v. 10), denoting judicial partiality.
- **13:15** — The Ketiv reads lo ('not', i.e., 'I have no hope' or 'I will not wait'), whereas the Qere translated here reads lo ('in Him', i.e., 'in Him I will hope').
- **13:16** — Hebrew hu can refer either to the preceding circumstance ('This too', translated here) or directly to God ('He also will be my deliverance').
- **13:27** — Hebrew literally reads 'around the roots of my feet' (an idiom for the soles or footprints), with the Hithpael verb indicating carving or tracing a boundary line.
- **13:28** — The abrupt third-person pronoun 'he' refers to Job himself or to frail humanity in general, bridging into the lament of chapter 14.
