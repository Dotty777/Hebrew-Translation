## Text

**16:1** Then Job replied and said:

**16:2** "I have heard many things like these; all of you are troublesome comforters.

**16:3** Is there an end to words of wind, or what goads you that you reply?

**16:4** I also could speak as you do, if your soul were in place of my soul; I could string words together against you and shake my head over you.

**16:5** I would encourage you with my mouth, and the sympathy of my lips would bring relief.

**16:6** If I speak, my pain is not relieved; and if I refrain, what departs from me?

**16:7** Surely now He has worn me down; You have devastated all my company.

**16:8** And You have shriveled me, and it has become a witness; my gauntness has risen up against me; to my face it testifies.

**16:9** His wrath has torn and hated me; He has gnashed against me with His teeth; my foe sharpens His eyes at me.

**16:10** They have opened wide their mouths against me; in reproach they have struck my cheeks; together they throng against me.

**16:11** God delivers me over to the unjust, and into the hands of the wicked He hurls me.

**16:12** I was undisturbed, and He shattered me; He gripped me by my neck and smashed me to pieces, and He stood me up for Himself as a target.

**16:13** His archers encircle me; He cleaves my kidneys and shows no pity; He pours my gall out onto the ground.

**16:14** He breaches me with breach upon breach; He charges at me like a mighty warrior.

**16:15** I have stitched sackcloth onto my skin and plunged my horn into the dust.

**16:16** My face is inflamed from weeping, and deep gloom is on my eyelids,

**16:17** even though no violence is in my palms, and my prayer is pure.

**16:18** Earth, do not cover my blood, and let there be no halting place for my outcry.

**16:19** Even now, look, in the heavens is my witness, and the one who bears witness for me is in the heights.

**16:20** My friends are my mockers; to God my eye sheds tears,

**16:21** that one might plead for a man with God, as a son of man for his friend.

**16:22** For a few years will come, and I will walk a path from which I will not return."

## Translator Notes

- **16:5** — The noun nid ("sympathy") is a hapax legomenon from the root nwd ("move, shake, show condolence"), permitting either an ironic sense ("mere movement of my lips") or "sympathy/condolence"; the verb yahsok ("bring relief") is used elliptically without an explicit object, anticipating the same root in 16:6.
- **16:8** — The verb qmt ("shriveled"), occurring only here and in 22:16, can mean either "shrivel/wrinkle" or "seize/bind"; paired with kahashi ("my gauntness/leanness"), it pictures Job's wasted body testifying against him.
- **16:16** — The Ketiv reads the feminine singular verb hmrmrh, whereas the Qere reads the plural Pealal verb homarmeru ("are inflamed") to agree grammatically with the plural noun panay ("my face").
- **16:20** — The Hiphil participle melisay ("my mockers") derives from lys, which can mean either "scorn/mock" (cf. Ps 119:51) or "interpret/mediate" (cf. 33:23); in apposition to reay ("my friends"), "my mockers" fits the contrast with Job's tearful appeal to God.
- **16:21** — The Hiphil verb weyokah ("that one might plead") has an unspecified third-person singular subject—either the heavenly witness of 16:19 or an indefinite "one"—followed by a comparative waw in the second clause ("as a son of man [pleads] for his friend").
