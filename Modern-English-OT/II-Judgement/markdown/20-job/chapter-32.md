## Text

**32:1** Then these three men stopped answering Job, for he was righteous in his own eyes.

**32:2** And the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the clan of Ram, burned. Against Job his anger burned because he declared himself righteous rather than God.

**32:3** And against his three friends his anger burned because they had found no answer, yet they had pronounced Job guilty.

**32:4** Now Elihu had waited for Job with words, for they were older than he in days.

**32:5** And Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, and his anger burned.

**32:6** Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, "I am young in days, and you are aged; therefore I shrank back and was afraid to declare my knowledge to you.

**32:7** I said, 'Days should speak, and an abundance of years should make wisdom known.'

**32:8** Yet truly, it is a spirit in a mortal, and the breath of the Almighty gives them understanding.

**32:9** It is not the great who become wise, nor do elders understand justice.

**32:10** Therefore I say, 'Listen to me; I also will declare my knowledge.'

**32:11** Look, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasonings while you searched out words.

**32:12** And I attended closely to you, and look, there was no one who refuted Job, none among you answering his sayings.

**32:13** Beware of saying, 'We have found wisdom; God will defeat him, not a man.'

**32:14** He has not arrayed words against me, and I will not reply to him with your sayings.

**32:15** They are dismayed; they do not answer any longer; words have departed from them.

**32:16** And should I wait because they do not speak, because they stand still and answer no more?

**32:17** I also will answer my part; I also will declare my knowledge.

**32:18** For I am full of words; the spirit in my belly presses me.

**32:19** Look, my belly is like wine that has not been opened; like new wineskins it is about to burst open.

**32:20** Let me speak so that relief may come to me; I will open my lips and answer.

**32:21** Let me show no partiality to any man, nor will I flatter any human.

**32:22** For I do not know how to flatter; my Maker would very soon carry me away."

## Translator Notes

- **32:4** — The Hebrew clause literally reads "waited for Job with words," an unusual construction with the Piel of chakah followed by the direct object marker, indicating that Elihu held back from speaking while Job and the older men spoke.
- **32:9** — Hebrew rabbim literally means "the great" or "the many," here denoting those senior in years or standing, parallel to "elders" in the second clause.
- **32:21** — The Piel verb akhanneh, repeated in 32:22, literally means to bestow an honorific surname or flattering title, hence to flatter.
