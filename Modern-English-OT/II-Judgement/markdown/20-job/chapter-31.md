## Text

**31:1** "I made a covenant with my eyes; how then should I look intently upon a virgin?

**31:2** And what is the portion from God above, and the inheritance from the Almighty on high?

**31:3** Is not calamity for the unjust, and disaster for those who do iniquity?

**31:4** Does not He Himself see my ways, and count all my steps?

**31:5** If I have walked with falsehood, and my foot has hurried toward deceit,

**31:6** let Him weigh me in scales of righteousness, and let God know my integrity.

**31:7** If my step has turned aside from the way, and my heart has walked after my eyes, and any stain has clung to my palms,

**31:8** then let me sow and another eat, and let my produce be uprooted.

**31:9** If my heart has been enticed toward a woman, and I have lain in wait at my neighbor's door,

**31:10** then let my wife grind for another, and let others kneel over her.

**31:11** For that would be depravity, and it would be an iniquity for judges.

**31:12** For it is a fire that would consume as far as Abaddon, and uproot all my harvest.

**31:13** If I have rejected the cause of my male servant or my female servant when they contended with me,

**31:14** what then would I do when God rises up? And when He calls to account, what would I answer Him?

**31:15** Did not the One who made me in the womb make him, and did not One fashion us in the womb?

**31:16** If I have withheld from the poor their desire, or caused the eyes of a widow to fail,

**31:17** or have eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless did not eat from it—

**31:18** for from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and from my mother's womb I have guided her—

**31:19** if I have seen anyone perishing for lack of clothing, or a needy person with no covering,

**31:20** if his loins have not blessed me, and if he did not warm himself with the fleece of my sheep,

**31:21** if I have lifted my hand against the fatherless because I saw my help in the gate,

**31:22** then let my shoulder fall away from its socket, and let my arm be broken from its bone.

**31:23** For calamity from God was a dread to me, and because of His majesty I could not stand.

**31:24** If I have made gold my confidence, or said to fine gold, 'You are my trust,'

**31:25** if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because my hand had gained much,

**31:26** if I have looked at the sun as it shone, or at the moon moving in splendor,

**31:27** and my heart was enticed in secret, and my hand kissed my mouth,

**31:28** that too would be an iniquity for judgment, for I would have denied God above.

**31:29** If I have rejoiced at the calamity of the one who hates me, or exulted when evil found him—

**31:30** no, I have not allowed my mouth to sin by asking for his life with a curse.

**31:31** If the men of my tent have not said, 'Who can find anyone who has not been filled with his meat?'—

**31:32** the sojourner did not lodge in the street; I opened my doors to the traveler.

**31:33** If I have concealed my transgressions like Adam, by hiding my iniquity in my bosom,

**31:34** because I dreaded the great multitude, and the contempt of the families terrified me, so that I kept silent and did not go out the door—

**31:35** If only I had someone to hear me! Here is my signature! Let the Almighty answer me! And if only I had the document my adversary has written!

**31:36** Surely I would carry it upon my shoulder; I would tie it around myself as a crown.

**31:37** I would recount to Him the number of my steps; like a prince I would draw near to Him.

**31:38** If my ground has cried out against me, and its furrows have wept together,

**31:39** if I have eaten its yield without payment, or caused the life of its owners to expire,

**31:40** then let thorns spring up instead of wheat, and stinkweed instead of barley." The words of Job are finished.

## Translator Notes

- **31:11** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere (read form) reads feminine hi' agreeing with zimmah ("depravity") and masculine wehu' agreeing with 'awon ("iniquity"), whereas the Ketiv (written form) reverses the two pronouns.
- **31:15** — The indefinite numeral 'echad ("One") serves as the subject of the Polel verb wayekhunennu ("fashioned us"), emphasizing that the same Creator formed both master and servant in the womb.
- **31:18** — The Qal verb gedelani ("he grew up with me") carries an indirect-object suffix referring to Job caring for the fatherless child from youth, parallel to Hiphil 'anchennah ("I guided her") referring to the widow.
- **31:26** — Hebrew 'or literally means "light" and here denotes the sun in parallel with yareach ("moon").
- **31:33** — Hebrew ke'adam lacks the definite article and may mean either "like Adam" (alluding to Genesis 3) or generically "as humans do."
