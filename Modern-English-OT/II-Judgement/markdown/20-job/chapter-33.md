## Text

**33:1** "However, please hear my speech, Job, and give ear to all my words.

**33:2** Look now, I have opened my mouth; my tongue speaks in my palate.

**33:3** My sayings are the uprightness of my heart, and my lips speak knowledge purely.

**33:4** The Spirit of God made me, and the breath of the Almighty keeps me alive.

**33:5** If you are able, reply to me; array yourself before me; take your stand.

**33:6** Look, toward God I am just like you; from clay I too was pinched off.

**33:7** See, dread of me will not terrify you, and my pressure will not weigh heavy upon you.

**33:8** Indeed, you spoke in my ears, and I heard the sound of words:

**33:9** 'I am pure, with no transgression; I am clean, and I have no iniquity.

**33:10** Look, He finds pretexts against me; He regards me as an enemy to Himself.

**33:11** He places my feet in the stocks; He keeps watch over all my paths.'

**33:12** Look, in this you are not in the right; I will answer you, because God is greater than a mortal.

**33:13** Why have you contended against Him, that He does not answer for any of His matters?

**33:14** For in one way God speaks, and in two, yet no one perceives it.

**33:15** In a dream, a night vision, when deep sleep falls upon men, in slumbers upon a bed,

**33:16** then He uncovers the ear of men and seals their discipline,

**33:17** to turn a person aside from a deed, and to hide pride from a man.

**33:18** He holds back his soul from the pit, and his life from passing away by the weapon.

**33:19** Moreover, he is reproved with pain upon his bed, and in the multitude of his bones continually,

**33:20** so that his life causes him to abhor bread, and his soul desirable food.

**33:21** His flesh wastes away out of sight, and his bones that were not seen are laid bare.

**33:22** And his soul comes near to the pit, and his life to those who put to death.

**33:23** If there is an angel for him, a mediator, one out of a thousand, to declare to a man his uprightness,

**33:24** then he is gracious to him and says, 'Rescue him from going down to the pit; I have found a ransom.'

**33:25** His flesh grows fresher than in youth; he returns to the days of his youthful strength.

**33:26** He prays to God, and He is pleased with him; he sees His face with joyful shouting, and He restores to a mortal his righteousness.

**33:27** He sings before people and says, 'I sinned and perverted what was upright, yet it was not paid back to me.'

**33:28** He has redeemed his soul from passing into the pit, and his life will see the light.

**33:29** Look, God performs all these things, twice, three times, with a man,

**33:30** to restore his soul from the pit, to be illuminated with the light of life.

**33:31** Give heed, Job, listen to me; keep silent, and I myself will speak.

**33:32** If you have words, reply to me; speak, for I desire to declare you righteous.

**33:33** If not, you yourself listen to me; keep silent, and I will teach you wisdom."

## Translator Notes

- **33:7** — The noun akhpi, "my pressure," is a hapax legomenon related to the verb akhaph, "press" or "urge," in Proverbs 16:26; the Masoretic Text is followed here rather than the Septuagint reading "my hand."
- **33:13** — Literally "He does not answer any of His/his matters (or words)." The third-person suffix may refer either to God's own doings and words, or back to "a mortal" in 33:12, meaning He answers none of a mortal's words.
- **33:19** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere reads rov, "the multitude of" his bones, translated here with "pain" carried over from the first clause, whereas the Ketiv reads riv, "strife" in his bones is continual.
- **33:21** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere reads the Pual verb shuppu, "are laid bare," translated here, whereas the Ketiv reads shephi, a noun form meaning "bareness" or a singular form.
- **33:28** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere reads third-person naphsho and chayyato, "his soul" and "his life," translated here as a third-person summary, whereas the Ketiv reads first-person naphshi and chayyati, "my soul" and "my life," continuing the quotation from 33:27.
