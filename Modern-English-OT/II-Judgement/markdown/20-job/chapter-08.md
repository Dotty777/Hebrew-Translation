## Text

**8:1** And Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**8:2** "How long will you utter these things, and will the words of your mouth be a powerful wind?

**8:3** Does God twist justice, or does the Almighty twist righteousness?

**8:4** If your children sinned against Him, then He gave them over into the hand of their transgression.

**8:5** If you yourself diligently seek God and plead with the Almighty for favor,

**8:6** if you are pure and upright, surely now He will awaken on your behalf and restore your righteous dwelling.

**8:7** And though your beginning was small, your end will flourish greatly.

**8:8** For ask now of the former generation, and set your mind on what their fathers searched out.

**8:9** For we are from yesterday and know nothing, because our days upon the earth are a shadow.

**8:10** Will they not teach you, speak to you, and bring forth words from their hearts?

**8:11** Can papyrus grow tall without a marsh? Can reeds flourish without water?

**8:12** While it is still in its fresh greenness and not plucked, it withers before all grass.

**8:13** Like this are the paths of all who forget God, and the hope of a godless man will perish,

**8:14** whose confidence snaps, and his trust is a spider's house.

**8:15** He leans on his house, yet it does not stand; he takes hold of it, yet it does not hold firm.

**8:16** He is lush before the sun, and his shoot spreads forth over his garden.

**8:17** Over a stone heap his roots are intertwined; he gazes upon a house of stones.

**8:18** If he is swallowed up from his place, then it will disown him: 'I have not seen you.'

**8:19** Look, this is the joy of his way, and from the dust others will sprout.

**8:20** Look, God will not reject one who is blameless, nor will He take hold of the hand of evildoers.

**8:21** He will still fill your mouth with laughter and your lips with joyful shouting.

**8:22** Those who hate you will put on shame, and the tent of the wicked will cease to be."

## Translator Notes

- **8:14** — The rare Qal verb yaqot (from qut II) means "snaps" or "is cut off." "A spider's house" translates literal bet akkabish (a spider's web), setting up the wordplay with "his house" in v. 15.
- **8:17** — Hebrew yehezeh (Qal of hazah) literally means "gazes upon" or "sees," paired with literal "house of stones" (echoing "house" in vv. 14-15); some scholars instead connect the verb with a cognate root meaning "grasps" or "clings to."
- **8:19** — The Masoretic accents divide the second clause as "and from the dust | others will sprout," taking singular aher ("another") collectively with the plural verb yismahu ("will sprout"); without that division, the phrase could be read "from another dust they will sprout."
- **8:21** — Hebrew ad here functions adverbially like od ("still" or "yet"), and the Piel verb yemalleh is spelled with a final he instead of alef (for standard yemalle, "fill").
