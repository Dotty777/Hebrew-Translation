## Text

**24:1** "Why are times not kept in reserve by the Almighty, and why do those who know Him not witness His days?

**24:2** People shift boundary markers; they seize a flock and pasture it.

**24:3** The donkey of the fatherless they drive away; they take a widow's ox in pledge.

**24:4** They push the needy off the road; all together, the poor of the land are forced to hide.

**24:5** Look, like wild donkeys in the wilderness, they go out to their labor, searching diligently for food; the barren plain is bread to him for the children.

**24:6** In the field they reap his fodder, and they glean the vineyard of the wicked.

**24:7** Naked they pass the night without clothing, and there is no covering in the cold.

**24:8** By the downpour of the mountains they are drenched, and without shelter they hug a rock.

**24:9** They snatch away the fatherless from the breast, and they take a pledge against the poor.

**24:10** Naked they walk about without clothing, and hungry they carry sheaves.

**24:11** Between their walls they press out oil; they tread winepresses, yet they are thirsty.

**24:12** Out of the city men groan, and the soul of the wounded cries for help; yet God charges no wrong.

**24:13** They are among those who rebel against the light; they do not recognize its ways, and they do not remain in its paths.

**24:14** At daybreak the murderer rises; he kills the poor and the needy, and during the night he is like a thief.

**24:15** And the eye of the adulterer watches for dusk, saying, 'No eye will see me,' and he puts a covering over his face.

**24:16** In the darkness one digs through houses; by day they seal themselves in; they do not know the light.

**24:17** For to all of them alike, deep darkness is morning, because each recognizes the terrors of deep darkness.

**24:18** He is swift on the surface of the waters; their portion in the land is cursed; he does not turn toward the way of the vineyards.

**24:19** Drought and heat alike carry off the snow waters, just as Sheol does those who have sinned.

**24:20** The womb forgets him, the worm feeds sweetly on him; he is remembered no more, and injustice is broken like a tree.

**24:21** He preys on the barren woman who bears no child, and to the widow he does no good.

**24:22** Yet He prolongs the mighty by His power; he rises up when he has no assurance of life.

**24:23** He grants him security, and he is supported; and His eyes are upon their ways.

**24:24** They rise high for a little while, and then he is no more; they are laid low, gathered in like all others, and like the head of an ear of grain they are cut off.

**24:25** And if it is not so, then who can prove me a liar and reduce my speech to nothing?"

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — Qere reads יִקְצוֹרוּ (Qal, "they reap"), whereas ketiv reads יקצירו (apparently Hiphil). MT בְּלִילוֹ ("his fodder") refers to the wicked landowner's crop, parallel to "the wicked" in the second clause.
- **24:11** — שׁוּרֹתָם can mean "their walls" or "their rows" (of olive trees); יַצְהִירוּ is a denominative Hiphil of צהר (from יִצְהָר, "fresh oil") meaning "press out oil."
- **24:12** — MT vocalizes מְתִים ("men") and תִּפְלָה ("wrong/folly"; cf. Job 1:22); some versions revocalize these to מֵתִים ("the dying") and תְּפִלָּה ("prayer").
- **24:18** — MT has no quotation marker ("You say") introducing vv. 18-24 and shifts between singular ("he") and plural ("their") pronouns referring collectively to the wicked.
- **24:22** — מָשַׁךְ (Qal of משׁך) here means "prolongs/sustains" (with God as implied subject, continuing into v. 23), though some render "drags off." בַּחַיִּין exhibits the Aramaizing plural ending -ִין.
