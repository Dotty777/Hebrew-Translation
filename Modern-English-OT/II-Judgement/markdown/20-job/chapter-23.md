## Text

**23:1** Then Job replied and said,

**23:2** "Even today my complaint is rebellious; my hand is heavy despite my groaning.

**23:3** If only I knew where I could find Him, that I might come all the way to His seat!

**23:4** I would set a case in order before Him, and I would fill my mouth with arguments.

**23:5** I would know the words He would answer me, and discern what He would say to me.

**23:6** Would He contend with me in greatness of strength? No! Surely He would give attention to me.

**23:7** There an upright person could reason with Him, and I would be set free forever from my Judge.

**23:8** See, I go forward, yet He is not there, and backward, yet I do not discern Him;

**23:9** on the left, when He is working, I cannot glimpse Him; He turns to the right, yet I do not see Him.

**23:10** Yet He knows the way that is with me; when He has tested me, I will emerge like gold.

**23:11** My foot has held firmly to His step; His way I have kept and have not turned aside.

**23:12** From the command of His lips I have not departed; more than my allotted portion, I have stored up the words of His mouth.

**23:13** Yet He is One, and who can turn Him back? What His soul desires, He does.

**23:15** Therefore I am terrified before His face; when I reflect, I dread Him.

**23:16** And God has caused my heart to grow faint, and the Almighty has terrified me,

**23:17** for I was not cut off before the darkness, nor did He conceal the thick gloom from my face."

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — MT reads יָדִי ("my hand") rather than "His hand" (found in the Septuagint and Syriac), and מְרִי literally means "rebellion/rebellious" (from מרה), though some take it as "bitter."
- **23:9** — The four directional terms in vv. 8-9 (forward, backward, left, right) also serve as the four compass points (east, west, north, south). אָחַז is an apocopated Qal imperfect 1cs of חזה ("see/glimpse"), and יַעְטֹף can mean either "He turns" or "He conceals Himself."
- **23:12** — MT reads מֵחֻקִּי ("more than my allotted portion," i.e., daily food or prescribed measure; cf. Prov 30:8), whereas the Septuagint and Vulgate read "in my bosom" (בְּחֵיקִי).
- **23:17** — The Hebrew is difficult; this translation takes נִצְמַתִּי (Niphal of צמת, "be cut off") with מִפְּנֵי ("before") and extends the negative לֹא across the second clause, where כִּסָּה מִפָּנַי means "He concealed from my face." Alternatively, the verse can be read "Yet I am not silenced because of darkness, though thick gloom covers my face."
