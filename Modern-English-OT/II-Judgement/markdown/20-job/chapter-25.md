## Text

**25:1** And Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**25:2** "Dominion and dread are with Him; He makes peace in His heights.

**25:3** Is there a count for His troops? And over whom does His light not arise?

**25:4** And how can a mortal be righteous with God, and how can someone born of a woman be pure?

**25:5** Look, even the moon does not shine, nor are the stars pure in His eyes;

**25:6** how much less a mortal, a maggot, and a son of man, a worm!"

## Translator Notes

- **25:2** — Hebrew hamshel ("dominion") is a rare substantive use of the Hiphil infinitive absolute of mashal ("to rule"), occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **25:5** — The verb ya'ahil ("shine") is the Hiphil imperfect of a rare root 'ahal ("be bright, shine"), distinct from the homonym "pitch a tent." The Qal perfect zakku ("are pure," from zakak) echoes the related verb yizkeh ("be pure," from zakah) in v. 4.
