## Text

**29:1** Then Job lifted up his discourse once more and said,

**29:2** "If only I were as in former months, as in the days when God guarded me,

**29:3** when He made His lamp shine above my head, and I walked through darkness by His light,

**29:4** just as I was in the days of my prime, when the intimate fellowship of God was over my tent,

**29:5** while the Almighty was still with me, with my children all around me,

**29:6** when my footsteps were bathed in cream, and the rock poured out streams of oil beside me!

**29:7** When I went out to the gate by the city, when I set up my seat in the open square,

**29:8** young men saw me and drew back, and the elderly rose up and stood.

**29:9** Princes held back their words and placed a hand over their mouth.

**29:10** The voice of nobles was stilled, and their tongue clung to their palate.

**29:11** For the ear heard and pronounced me blessed, and the eye saw and bore witness for me,

**29:12** because I rescued the poor who cried out, and the fatherless who had no helper.

**29:13** The blessing of one who was perishing came upon me, and I made the heart of the widow sing with joy.

**29:14** I wore righteousness, and it clothed me; like a robe and turban was my justice.

**29:15** Eyes I was for the blind, and feet I was for the lame.

**29:16** A father I was for the needy, and I searched out the dispute of one I did not know.

**29:17** And I shattered the fangs of the wrongdoer, and from his teeth I flung the prey.

**29:18** And I said, 'I will breathe my last in my nest, and like the sand I will multiply days;

**29:19** my root is open to the waters, and dew will spend the night on my branch;

**29:20** my glory is fresh with me, and my bow in my hand will renew itself.'

**29:21** They listened to me and waited expectantly, and they were silent for my counsel.

**29:22** After my word they would not speak again, and upon them my speech fell in drops.

**29:23** They waited expectantly for me as for the rain, and opened their mouth wide for spring rain.

**29:24** When I smiled at them, they could not believe it, and they did not cause the light of my face to fall.

**29:25** I chose their way and sat at the head, and I dwelt like a king amid the troops, like one who consoles mourners."

## Translator Notes

- **29:4** — Hebrew bimei chorpi means literally "in the days of my autumn," using the fruitful harvest season as a metaphor for the prime of life; sod denotes God's intimate counsel or close fellowship.
- **29:10** — The Niphal plural verb nechbau (literally "hid themselves," here "was stilled") agrees grammatically by attraction with the plural noun "nobles" rather than the singular subject "voice."
- **29:16** — Hebrew rib lo-yadati places a construct noun directly before a verbal clause, meaning either "the dispute of one I did not know" (a stranger's case) or "a dispute I did not know."
- **29:18** — Hebrew chol ordinarily means "sand," though an ancient Jewish interpretive tradition renders it "phoenix," connecting it with the mention of a "nest" in the first line.
- **29:24** — The phrase lo yaaminu (literally "they did not believe") can mean either that those smiled upon could not believe such favor or that Job smiled at them when they lacked confidence.
