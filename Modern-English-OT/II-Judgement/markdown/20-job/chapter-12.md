## Text

**12:1** Then Job answered and said,

**12:2** "Surely you are the people, and with you wisdom will die!

**12:3** I too have a mind just as you do; I do not fall short of you. And who does not have things like these?

**12:4** I have become one who is a laughingstock to his friend, one who called upon God and He answered him—the righteous and blameless man is a laughingstock!

**12:5** For misfortune there is contempt in the thought of one at ease; it stands ready for those whose feet slip.

**12:6** The tents of plunderers are at peace, and security belongs to those who provoke God—to the one who brings a god in his hand.

**12:7** However, ask the beasts now, and they will instruct you; and the birds of the sky, and they will tell you;

**12:8** or speak to the earth, and it will instruct you, and the fish of the sea will report to you.

**12:9** Who does not know among all these that the hand of the LORD has done this,

**12:10** in whose hand is the life of every living creature and the breath of all human flesh?

**12:11** Does not the ear examine words, and the palate taste food for itself?

**12:12** With the aged is wisdom, and in length of days is understanding.

**12:13** With Him are wisdom and power; His are counsel and understanding.

**12:14** See, He tears down, and it will not be rebuilt; He shuts a man in, and it will not be opened.

**12:15** See, He holds back the waters, and they dry up; and He sends them out, and they overturn the earth.

**12:16** With Him are might and sound wisdom; His are the one who errs and the one who leads astray.

**12:17** He makes counselors walk stripped, and judges He makes into fools.

**12:18** He unties the bond of kings and binds a waistcloth around their waists.

**12:19** He makes priests walk stripped, and He overthrows the firmly established.

**12:20** He removes speech from the trustworthy, and the discernment of the elders He takes away.

**12:21** He pours out contempt upon nobles and loosens the sash of the mighty.

**12:22** He uncovers deep things from darkness and brings the shadow of death into light.

**12:23** He causes the nations to grow great, and He destroys them; He spreads out the nations, and He leads them away.

**12:24** He removes understanding from the leaders of the people of the earth and causes them to wander in a wasteland without a way.

**12:25** They grope in darkness without light, and He causes them to stagger like a drunkard."

## Translator Notes

- **12:4** — The Hebrew shifts from first-person singular ehyeh (I have become) to third-person appositional forms (lere'ehu, to his friend; wayya'anehu, and He answered him), casting Job's plight in proverbial third-person terms.
- **12:5** — Reading the Masoretic consonants lpyd as le-fid (for misfortune or calamity, cf. Job 30:24; 31:29), with contempt as the masculine subject of stands ready; the Masoretic vocalization lappid reads a despised torch in the thought of one at ease.
- **12:6** — The final clause la'asher hevi eloah beyado is syntactically difficult; parallel to the preceding le- phrases, it literally reads to the one who brings a god in his hand (making his own might his god), though some take God as subject (to whom God brings provision by His hand).
