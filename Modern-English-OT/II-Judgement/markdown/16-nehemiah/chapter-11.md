## Text

**11:1** Now the officials of the people lived in Jerusalem, and the rest of the people cast lots to bring one out of ten to live in Jerusalem, the holy city, while the nine parts lived in the cities.

**11:2** And the people blessed all the men who offered themselves freely to live in Jerusalem.

**11:3** Now these are the heads of the province who lived in Jerusalem; but in the cities of Judah they lived, each man in his possession in their cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, the temple servants, and the sons of Solomon's servants.

**11:4** And in Jerusalem lived some of the sons of Judah and some of the sons of Benjamin. Of the sons of Judah: Athaiah the son of Uzziah, the son of Zechariah, the son of Amariah, the son of Shephatiah, the son of Mahalalel, from the sons of Perez;

**11:5** and Maaseiah the son of Baruch, the son of Col-hozeh, the son of Hazaiah, the son of Adaiah, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zechariah, the son of the Shilonite.

**11:6** All the sons of Perez who lived in Jerusalem were four hundred sixty-eight men of valor.

**11:7** And these are the sons of Benjamin: Sallu the son of Meshullam, the son of Joed, the son of Pedaiah, the son of Kolaiah, the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ithiel, the son of Jeshaiah.

**11:8** And after him were Gabbai and Sallai, nine hundred twenty-eight.

**11:9** And Joel the son of Zichri was overseer over them, and Judah the son of Hassenuah was second in rank over the city.

**11:10** Of the priests: Jedaiah the son of Joiarib, Jachin,

**11:11** Seraiah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, ruler of the house of God;

**11:12** and their brothers who carried out the work for the house, eight hundred twenty-two; and Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi, the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah,

**11:13** and his brothers, heads of fathers' houses, two hundred forty-two; and Amashsai the son of Azarel, the son of Ahzai, the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer,

**11:14** and their brothers, mighty men of valor, one hundred twenty-eight. And Zabdiel the son of Haggedolim was overseer over them.

**11:15** And of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni;

**11:17** and Mattaniah the son of Mica, the son of Zabdi, the son of Asaph, who was the leader to start giving thanks in prayer, and Bakbukiah, the second in rank among his brothers, and Abda the son of Shammua, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun.

**11:18** All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred eighty-four.

**11:19** And the gatekeepers—Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers who kept guard at the gates—were one hundred seventy-two.

**11:22** And the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi the son of Bani, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Mica, from the sons of Asaph, the singers, in charge of the work of the house of God.

**11:23** For the command of the king was concerning them, and there was a binding provision regarding the singers, as each day required.

**11:24** And Pethahiah the son of Meshezabel, from the sons of Zerah the son of Judah, was at the king's hand in every matter for the people.

**11:25** And regarding the villages in their fields, some of the sons of Judah lived in Kiriath-arba and its dependent towns, and in Dibon and its dependent towns, and in Jekabzeel and its villages,

**11:26** and in Jeshua, and in Moladah, and in Beth-pelet,

**11:27** and in Hazar-shual, and in Beersheba and its dependent towns,

**11:30** Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its dependent towns. And they encamped from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom.

**11:31** And the sons of Benjamin lived starting from Geba, in Michmash and Aija, and in Bethel and its dependent towns.

**11:36** And from the Levites, divisions of Judah were attached to Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **11:14** — Haggedolim is a Hebrew plural form meaning the great ones, here treated as a family or ancestral name; it may alternatively be rendered the son of the great men.
- **11:17** — Translates the qere Jeduthun; the ketiv reads Jedithun. The phrase leader to start giving thanks in prayer renders the Masoretic Hebrew rosh hattechillah yehodeh lattephillah, literally head of the beginning, he gives thanks for the prayer, with the Hiphil imperfect yehodeh retaining its he.
- **11:24** — At the king's hand translates the Hebrew idiom leyad hammelek, denoting service as the king's advisor or liaison.
- **11:25** — Dependent towns here and in 11:27, 30, and 31 translates Hebrew banot, literally daughters, denoting satellite towns attached to a mother city, in distinction from chatserim, unwalled villages.
- **11:36** — The Hebrew is a terse verbless clause literally reading And from the Levites, divisions of Judah for Benjamin, indicating that certain Levitical divisions from Judah were assigned to Benjamin's territory.
- **11:35 *(additional)*** — The Hebrew phrase "the Valley of the Craftsmen" may either name a third settlement or describe the valley of Lod and Ono.
