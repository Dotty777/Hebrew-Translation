## Text

**3:1** Then Eliashib the high priest arose with his brothers the priests, and they built the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set up its doors; they consecrated it as far as the Tower of the Hundred, as far as the Tower of Hananel.

**3:2** And next to him the men of Jericho built. And next to him Zaccur son of Imri built.

**3:3** Now the sons of Hassenaah built the Fish Gate; they laid its beams and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars.

**3:4** Next to them Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, made repairs. Next to them Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, made repairs. Next to them Zadok son of Baana made repairs.

**3:5** Next to them the Tekoites made repairs, but their nobles did not put their necks to the work of their lords.

**3:6** Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate; they laid its beams and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars.

**3:8** Next to him Uzziel son of Harhaiah, of the goldsmiths, made repairs. And next to him Hananiah, one of the perfumers, made repairs; and they restored Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

**3:9** Next to them Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs.

**3:10** Next to them Jedaiah son of Harumaph made repairs, even opposite his house. And next to him Hattush son of Hashabneiah made repairs.

**3:11** Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section and the Tower of the Ovens.

**3:12** Next to him Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs—he and his daughters.

**3:13** Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate; they built it and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars, along with a thousand cubits of the wall as far as the Dung Gate.

**3:14** Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccherem, repaired the Dung Gate; he built it and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars.

**3:15** Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, repaired the Fountain Gate; he built it, roofed it, and set up its doors, its bolts, and its bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah by the king's garden, as far as the stairs that go down from the City of David.

**3:16** After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth-zur, made repairs to a point opposite the tombs of David, and as far as the constructed pool, and as far as the House of the Mighty Men.

**3:17** After him the Levites made repairs: Rehum son of Bani. Next to him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, made repairs for his district.

**3:18** After him their brothers made repairs: Bavvai son of Henadad, ruler of half the district of Keilah.

**3:19** Next to him Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section opposite the ascent to the armory at the Angle.

**3:20** After him Baruch son of Zaccai zealously repaired another section, from the Angle to the doorway of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

**3:21** After him Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, repaired another section, from the doorway of the house of Eliashib to the end of the house of Eliashib.

**3:22** After him the priests, the men of the plain, made repairs.

**3:23** After him Benjamin and Hasshub made repairs opposite their house. After him Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, made repairs beside his house.

**3:24** After him Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section, from the house of Azariah to the Angle and to the Corner.

**3:25** Palal son of Uzai made repairs opposite the Angle and the tower projecting from the upper house of the king, which is by the court of the guard. After him Pedaiah son of Parosh made repairs.

**3:26** Now the temple servants were dwelling on Ophel, as far as a point opposite the Water Gate on the east and the projecting tower.

**3:27** After him the Tekoites repaired another section, from opposite the great projecting tower to the wall of Ophel.

**3:28** Above the Horse Gate the priests made repairs, each one opposite his house.

**3:29** After him Zadok son of Immer made repairs opposite his house. And after him Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the keeper of the East Gate, made repairs.

**3:30** After him Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section. After him Meshullam son of Berechiah made repairs opposite his chamber.

**3:31** After him Malchijah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs as far as the house of the temple servants and of the merchants, opposite the Inspection Gate, and as far as the upper chamber of the Corner.

**3:32** And between the upper chamber of the Corner and the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and the merchants made repairs.

**3:33** Now when Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, he burned with anger and was greatly indignant, and he mocked the Jews.

**3:34** And he spoke before his brothers and the army of Samaria, and said, "What are the feeble Jews doing? Will they restore it for themselves? Will they offer sacrifices? Will they finish in a day? Will they revive the stones from the heaps of dust, burned as they are?"

**3:35** Now Tobiah the Ammonite was beside him, and he said, "Even what they are building—if a fox goes up on it, it will break down their stone wall!"

**3:36** Hear, our God, for we have become an object of contempt; turn back their reproach upon their own head, and give them over as plunder in a land of captivity.

**3:37** Do not cover their iniquity, and do not let their sin be blotted out from before You, for they have provoked anger in front of the builders.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — 'their lords' translates plural Hebrew adonehem, which may refer to the supervisors directing the work or, as a plural of majesty, to 'their Lord' (God) or the governor.
- **3:8** — 'restored' renders Hebrew azab II ('repair, fortify'), distinct from azab I ('leave, forsake'); the same verb recurs in 3:34, where some instead read azab I ('Will they be left to themselves?').
- **3:15** — Ketiv reads plural wy'mydw ('and they set up'), whereas Qere reads singular weya'amid ('and set up'), matching the singular subject Shallun.
- **3:20** — Ketiv reads zby (Zabbai), whereas Qere reads zakkay (Zaccai). 'zealously repaired' translates the rare asyndetic pairing of Hiphil hehera ('burned with zeal') and Hiphil heheziq ('repaired').
- **3:30** — In both 3:30 and 3:31, Ketiv reads 'hry ('after me'), whereas Qere reads aharaw ('after him'), matching the third-person sequence of the list.
- **3:37** — 'provoked anger' renders Hiphil hik'isu used without an explicit object, an idiom denoting that they provoked God to anger in the sight of the builders.
- **3:7 *(additional)*** — The Hebrew phrase translated "belonging to the seat" may indicate that Gibeon and Mizpah were under the administrative jurisdiction of the Persian governor of Trans-Euphrates, or that they repaired up to his official residence.
