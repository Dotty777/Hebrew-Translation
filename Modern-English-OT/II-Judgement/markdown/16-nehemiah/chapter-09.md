## Text

**9:1** Now on the twenty-fourth day of this month, the sons of Israel assembled with fasting, in sackcloth, and with earth on them.

**9:2** The descendants of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners, and they stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.

**9:3** They stood up in their place and read from the book of the law of the LORD their God for a fourth of the day, and for another fourth they were confessing and worshiping the LORD their God.

**9:4** Now on the steps of the Levites stood Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani, and they cried out with a loud voice to the LORD their God.

**9:5** Then the Levites Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah said, "Rise and bless the LORD your God from everlasting to everlasting. Let them bless Your glorious name, which is exalted above all blessing and praise.

**9:6** You alone are the LORD; You made the heavens, the heaven of heavens and all their host, the earth and everything on it, the seas and everything in them. You keep all of them alive, and the host of heaven worships You.

**9:7** You are the LORD God, who chose Abram, brought him out of Ur of the Chaldeans, and gave him the name Abraham.

**9:8** You found his heart faithful before You, and You made the covenant with him to give the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, and the Girgashites—to give it to his descendants. And You have fulfilled Your words, for You are righteous.

**9:9** You saw the affliction of our fathers in Egypt, and You heard their outcry at the Red Sea.

**9:10** You performed signs and wonders against Pharaoh, against all his servants, and against all the people of his land, because You knew that they had acted arrogantly against them; and You made a name for Yourself, as it is this day.

**9:11** You split the sea before them, and they crossed through the middle of the sea on dry ground; their pursuers You threw into the depths, like a stone into mighty waters.

**9:12** With a pillar of cloud You led them by day, and with a pillar of fire by night, to light up for them the way in which they were to go.

**9:13** You came down upon Mount Sinai and spoke with them from heaven, and You gave them upright ordinances and laws of truth, good statutes and commandments.

**9:14** You made known to them Your holy Sabbath, and commandments, statutes, and law You commanded for them by the hand of Moses Your servant.

**9:15** You gave them bread from heaven for their hunger, and You brought out water for them from a rock for their thirst; and You told them to go in to possess the land that You had lifted up Your hand to give to them.

**9:16** Yet they, our fathers, acted arrogantly; they stiffened their neck and did not listen to Your commandments.

**9:17** They refused to listen and did not remember Your wondrous deeds that You had done with them; instead, they stiffened their neck and appointed a leader to return to their slavery in their rebellion. Yet You are a God of forgiveness, gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in loyal love, and You did not abandon them.

**9:18** Even when they made for themselves a calf of cast metal and said, 'This is your god who brought you up out of Egypt,' and committed great blasphemies,

**9:19** You, in Your great compassion, did not abandon them in the wilderness. The pillar of cloud did not depart from over them by day to lead them along the way, nor did the pillar of fire by night to give light to them and to the way in which they were to go.

**9:20** You gave Your good Spirit to impart insight to them; You did not withhold Your manna from their mouths, and You gave them water for their thirst.

**9:21** For forty years You sustained them in the wilderness, so that they were not in want; their garments did not wear out, and their feet did not swell.

**9:22** You gave them kingdoms and peoples and apportioned them to every boundary. So they took possession of the land of Sihon, even the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.

**9:23** You multiplied their sons like the stars of heaven, and You brought them into the land that You had told their fathers to go in to possess.

**9:24** So the sons went in and took possession of the land, and You subdued before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and gave them into their hand—both their kings and the peoples of the land—to do with them according to their will.

**9:25** They captured fortified cities and rich soil, and took possession of houses filled with all good things, hewn cisterns, vineyards, olive groves, and fruit trees in abundance. So they ate, were satisfied, grew fat, and delighted themselves in Your great goodness.

**9:26** But they grew defiant and rebelled against You; they cast Your law behind their backs and killed Your prophets, who had warned them to turn them back to You, and they committed great blasphemies.

**9:27** Therefore You gave them into the hand of their oppressors, who oppressed them. But in the time of their distress they cried out to You, and You heard from heaven; and according to Your great compassion You gave them deliverers who delivered them from the hand of their oppressors.

**9:28** Yet as soon as they had rest, they again did evil before You, so You abandoned them into the hand of their enemies, who ruled over them. Then they cried out to You again, and You heard from heaven and rescued them according to Your compassion many times.

**9:29** You warned them in order to turn them back to Your law, yet they acted arrogantly and did not listen to Your commandments, but sinned against Your ordinances—which, if anyone does them, he will live by them—and they turned a stubborn shoulder, stiffened their neck, and would not listen.

**9:30** For many years You bore with them and warned them by Your Spirit through the hand of Your prophets, yet they would not give ear. Therefore You gave them into the hand of the peoples of the lands.

**9:31** Yet in Your great compassion You did not make a complete end of them or abandon them, for You are a gracious and compassionate God.

**9:32** Now therefore, our God—the great, mighty, and awesome God, who keeps the covenant and loyal love—do not let all the hardship seem small before You that has come upon us, upon our kings, upon our officials, upon our priests, upon our prophets, upon our fathers, and upon all Your people, from the days of the kings of Assyria until this day.

**9:33** Yet You are righteous concerning all that has come upon us, for You have acted in truth, while we have acted wickedly.

**9:34** Our kings, our officials, our priests, and our fathers did not practice Your law, nor did they pay attention to Your commandments or to Your testimonies with which You warned them.

**9:35** Even in their kingdom, amid Your abundant goodness that You gave them, and in the broad and rich land that You set before them, they did not serve You, nor did they turn from their evil deeds.

**9:36** Look, we are slaves today; and as for the land that You gave to our fathers to eat its fruit and its bounty, look, we are slaves on it.

**9:37** And its produce abounds for the kings whom You have set over us because of our sins; they rule over our bodies and over our livestock according to their will, and we are in great distress."

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — In the Masoretic Text, the Levites' call to worship shifts directly to second-person prayer to God in the second half of this verse and continues through v. 37 without naming a new speaker (unlike the Septuagint, which inserts Ezra before v. 6).
- **9:6** — Ketiv reads 't (a rare defective spelling of the second-person masculine singular pronoun); Qere reads 'attah ("You"), which is followed here.
- **9:17** — Ketiv reads wchsd (with an extra conjunction waw); Qere reads chesed, forming werav-chesed ("and abounding in loyal love"), which is followed here. In addition, the Masoretic Text reads bemiryam ("in their rebellion"), whereas some ancient versions harmonize to "in Egypt."
- **9:22** — The clause "apportioned them to every boundary" (literally "apportioned them to a corner/border") idiomatically refers to dividing and allotting territories to every district. The Masoretic Text then repeats "the land" epexegetically ("the land of Sihon, even the land of the king of Heshbon").
- **9:32** — The particle 'et here introduces the grammatical subject ("all the hardship"), a syntactic usage attested in Late Biblical Hebrew (similarly in vv. 19 and 34).
