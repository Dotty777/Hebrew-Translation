## Text

**10:1** And because of all this, we are making a binding agreement and writing it down, and on the sealed document are our officials, our Levites, and our priests.

**10:2** And on the sealed documents were Nehemiah the governor, the son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah.

**10:3** Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah,

**10:4** Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah,

**10:5** Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch,

**10:6** Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

**10:7** Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch,

**10:8** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**10:9** Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah. These were the priests.

**10:10** And the Levites: Jeshua the son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, Kadmiel,

**10:11** and their brothers: Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan,

**10:12** Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah,

**10:13** Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

**10:14** Hodiah, Bani, and Beninu.

**10:15** The heads of the people: Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, Bani,

**10:16** Bunni, Azgad, Bebai,

**10:17** Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin,

**10:18** Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur,

**10:19** Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai,

**10:20** Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,

**10:21** Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir,

**10:22** Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua,

**10:23** Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah,

**10:24** Hoshea, Hananiah, Hasshub,

**10:25** Hallohesh, Pilha, Shobek,

**10:26** Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah,

**10:27** Ahiah, Hanan, Anan,

**10:28** Malluch, Harim, and Baanah.

**10:29** And the rest of the people—the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and all who separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the law of God, their wives, their sons, and their daughters, everyone with knowledge and discernment—

**10:30** join firmly with their brothers, their nobles, and enter into a curse and an oath to walk in the law of God, which was given by the hand of Moses the servant of God, and to keep and perform all the commandments of the LORD our Lord, and His judgments and His statutes;

**10:31** and that we will not give our daughters to the peoples of the land, nor will we take their daughters for our sons.

**10:32** And as for the peoples of the land who bring merchandise or any grain to sell on the Sabbath day, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day; and we will forgo the seventh year and the debt-claim of every hand.

**10:33** We have also established obligations on ourselves to levy a third of a shekel on ourselves each year for the service of the house of our God:

**10:34** for the bread set in rows, the continual grain offering, and the continual burnt offering, for the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the appointed festivals, for the sacred things, and for the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of the house of our God.

**10:35** We also cast lots—the priests, the Levites, and the people—regarding the wood offering, to bring it to the house of our God by our fathers' houses at designated times, year by year, to burn upon the altar of the LORD our God, as is written in the law;

**10:36** and to bring the firstfruits of our soil and the firstfruits of all fruit of every tree, year by year, to the house of the LORD;

**10:37** and to bring the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as is written in the law, and the firstborn of our herds and our flocks, to the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God.

**10:38** And we will bring the first of our dough, our contributions, the fruit of every tree, new wine, and fresh oil to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God, and the tithe of our soil to the Levites; for the Levites themselves are the ones who collect the tithes in all the cities of our labor.

**10:39** And the priest, the son of Aaron, will be with the Levites when the Levites collect the tithes, and the Levites will bring up the tithe of the tithe to the house of our God, to the chambers, to the storehouse.

**10:40** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Levi will bring the contribution of the grain, the new wine, and the fresh oil to the chambers; and there are the vessels of the sanctuary, and the priests who minister, and the gatekeepers, and the singers. And we will not forsake the house of our God.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The phrase on the sealed documents translates the masculine plural Qal passive participle hachatumim, paralleling the singular hechatum in 10:1 and indicating either the sealed documents or those who set their seals. Governor translates hattirshata, a Persian administrative title.
- **10:20** — Translates the qere Nebai; the ketiv reads Nobai.
- **10:30** — Join firmly translates the Hiphil participle machaziqim with the preposition al, literally holding fast upon, expressing firm solidarity in taking the covenant oath.
- **10:32** — The Hebrew literally reads we will leave off the seventh year and the exaction of every hand, referring to letting the land lie fallow in the sabbatical year and canceling every creditor's claim.
- **10:39** — When the Levites collect the tithes translates the syncopated Hiphil infinitive construct baaser, matching the Piel participle measserim in 10:38; both stems here denote collecting the tithes.
