## Text

**1:1** The words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah. It came about in the month of Kislev, in the twentieth year, when I was in Susa the citadel,

**1:2** that Hanani, one of my brothers, arrived—he and men from Judah—and I asked them about the Jews, the escaped remnant who were left from the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

**1:3** They said to me, "The remnant who are left from the captivity there in the province are in great distress and in reproach, and the wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire."

**1:4** When I heard these words, I sat down and wept, and I mourned for days; and I continued fasting and praying before the God of heaven.

**1:5** And I said, "Please, LORD, God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps the covenant and faithful love for those who love Him and keep His commandments:

**1:6** let Your ear please be attentive and Your eyes open to listen to the prayer of Your servant, which I am praying before You today, day and night, on behalf of the sons of Israel, Your servants, while confessing the sins of the sons of Israel that we have sinned against You. Both I and my father's house have sinned.

**1:7** We have indeed acted corruptly against You, and we have not kept the commandments, the statutes, and the ordinances that You commanded Your servant Moses.

**1:8** Please remember the word that You commanded Your servant Moses, saying, 'If you act unfaithfully, I will scatter you among the peoples;

**1:9** but if you return to Me and keep My commandments and do them, even if your outcasts are at the end of the heavens, from there I will gather them and bring them to the place that I have chosen to make My name dwell there.'

**1:10** Now they are Your servants and Your people, whom You redeemed by Your great power and by Your strong hand.

**1:11** Please, Lord, let Your ear be attentive to the prayer of Your servant and to the prayer of Your servants who take delight in fearing Your name; and please grant success to Your servant today, and grant him compassion before this man." Now I was cupbearer to the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:8** — Hebrew omits an explicit conjunction for 'if' here and at the beginning of 1:9, expressing the conditional clauses through juxtaposed statements.
- **1:9** — Ketiv writes an irregular form (whbw'tym), whereas Qere reads the regular Hiphil waw-consecutive perfect of bw' (wahabi'otim, 'and I will bring them').
