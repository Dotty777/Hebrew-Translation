## Text

**12:1** And these are the priests and the Levites who went up with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra,

**12:2** Amariah, Malluch, Hattush,

**12:3** Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth,

**12:7** Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, Jedaiah: these were the heads of the priests and of their brothers in the days of Jeshua.

**12:8** And the Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah; he and his brothers were over the songs of thanksgiving.

**12:9** And Bakbukiah and Unni, their brothers, were opposite them for the watches.

**12:10** And Jeshua fathered Joiakim, and Joiakim fathered Eliashib, and Eliashib fathered Joiada.

**12:11** And Joiada fathered Jonathan, and Jonathan fathered Jaddua.

**12:12** And in the days of Joiakim, there were priests, heads of the fathers' houses: for Seraiah, Meraiah; for Jeremiah, Hananiah;

**12:13** for Ezra, Meshullam; for Amariah, Jehohanan;

**12:14** for Melichu, Jonathan; for Shebaniah, Joseph;

**12:15** for Harim, Adna; for Meraioth, Helkai;

**12:16** for Iddo, Zechariah; for Ginnethon, Meshullam;

**12:17** for Abijah, Zichri; for Miniamin; for Moadiah, Piltai;

**12:18** for Bilgah, Shammua; for Shemaiah, Jehonathan;

**12:19** and for Joiarib, Mattenai; for Jedaiah, Uzzi;

**12:20** for Sallai, Kallai; for Amok, Eber;

**12:21** for Hilkiah, Hashabiah; for Jedaiah, Nethanel.

**12:22** The Levites in the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua were recorded as heads of fathers' houses, and the priests as well, during the reign of Darius the Persian.

**12:23** The sons of Levi, the heads of the fathers' houses, were recorded in the book of the chronicles, even until the days of Johanan son of Eliashib.

**12:24** And the heads of the Levites were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua son of Kadmiel, with their brothers opposite them, to praise and to give thanks, by the command of David the man of God, watch corresponding to watch.

**12:25** Mattaniah and Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers who kept watch at the storehouses of the gates.

**12:26** These were in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua, son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest, the scribe.

**12:27** And at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem, they searched for the Levites from all their places to bring them to Jerusalem, to observe the dedication with joy, with thanksgivings and with song, with cymbals, harps, and lyres.

**12:28** And the sons of the singers assembled, both from the region around Jerusalem and from the villages of the Netophathites,

**12:29** and from Beth-gilgal and from the fields of Geba and Azmaveth, because the singers had built villages for themselves around Jerusalem.

**12:30** Then the priests and the Levites purified themselves, and they purified the people, the gates, and the wall.

**12:31** Then I brought the leaders of Judah up onto the wall, and I stationed two large thanksgiving choirs and processions. One went to the right atop the wall toward the Dung Gate.

**12:32** And Hoshaiah and half of the leaders of Judah walked behind them,

**12:33** and Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam,

**12:34** Judah and Benjamin, and Shemaiah and Jeremiah,

**12:35** and some from the sons of the priests with trumpets: Zechariah son of Jonathan, son of Shemaiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Micaiah, son of Zaccur, son of Asaph,

**12:36** and his brothers, Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani, with the musical instruments of David the man of God; and Ezra the scribe was in front of them.

**12:37** And at the Fountain Gate, straight ahead of them, they went up the steps of the city of David by the ascent to the wall, above the house of David, and as far as the Water Gate on the east.

**12:38** And the second thanksgiving choir went in the opposite direction, and I was behind it with half of the people atop the wall, above the Tower of the Ovens and as far as the Broad Wall,

**12:39** and above the Gate of Ephraim, and by the Old Gate, and by the Fish Gate, the Tower of Hananel, and the Tower of the Hundred, as far as the Sheep Gate; and they stood still at the Gate of the Guard.

**12:40** Then the two thanksgiving choirs stood in the house of God, and I also, with half of the officials with me,

**12:41** and the priests—Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah—with trumpets,

**12:42** and Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer. And the singers made their voices heard, with Jezrahiah as the overseer.

**12:43** And they offered great sacrifices on that day and rejoiced, because God had caused them to rejoice with great joy; and the women and the children rejoiced as well, so that the joy of Jerusalem was heard from far away.

**12:44** And on that day men were appointed over the chambers for the stores, the contributions, the firstfruits, and the tithes, to collect into them, from the fields of the cities, the portions prescribed by the law for the priests and for the Levites; for Judah took joy in the priests and in the Levites who stood in service.

**12:45** And they kept the charge of their God and the charge of purification, along with the singers and the gatekeepers, according to the command of David and his son Solomon.

**12:46** For in the days of David and Asaph long ago, there were heads of the singers, and songs of praise and thanksgivings to God.

**12:47** And all Israel in the days of Zerubbabel and in the days of Nehemiah gave the portions of the singers and the gatekeepers, each day's requirement in its day; and they consecrated the portions for the Levites, and the Levites consecrated the portions for the sons of Aaron.

## Translator Notes

- **12:14** — The translation follows the Qere (read form) Melichu; the Ketiv (written form) reads Malluchi (compare Malluch in 12:2).
- **12:16** — The translation follows the Qere (read form) Iddo; the Ketiv (written form) reads Adaiah (or Iddia).
- **12:17** — In the Masoretic Text, no personal name follows for Miniamin; the phrase carries a disjunctive accent directly before for Moadiah, Piltai, indicating an apparent omission in the Hebrew text.
- **12:31** — Hebrew todot literally means thanksgivings, here denoting choirs giving thanks, and tahalukot is a rare noun meaning processions found only here. The words One went are implied by the contrast with the second thanksgiving choir in 12:38.
- **12:46** — The translation follows the Qere (read form), plural construct heads of the singers; the Ketiv (written form) reads singular head of the singers.
