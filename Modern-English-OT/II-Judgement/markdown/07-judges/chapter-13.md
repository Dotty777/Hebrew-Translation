## Text

**13:1** Now the sons of Israel again did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, and the LORD gave them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.

**13:2** There was a certain man from Zorah, of the clan of the Danites, whose name was Manoah; and his wife was barren and had not given birth.

**13:3** And the angel of the LORD appeared to the woman and said to her, "Look now, you are barren and have not given birth, yet you will conceive and give birth to a son.

**13:4** So now, please be careful, and do not drink wine or strong drink, nor eat anything unclean.

**13:5** For look, you will conceive and give birth to a son, and no razor shall come up on his head, for the boy shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb. And he himself will begin to deliver Israel from the hand of the Philistines."

**13:6** Then the woman came and spoke to her husband, saying, "A man of God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of the angel of God, exceedingly awesome. I did not ask him where he was from, nor did he tell me his name.

**13:7** But he said to me, 'Look, you will conceive and give birth to a son. So now, do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat any unclean thing, for the boy shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb until the day of his death.'"

**13:8** Then Manoah pleaded with the LORD and said, "Please, Lord, let the man of God whom You sent come to us again and teach us what we should do for the boy who is to be born."

**13:9** And God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the angel of God came again to the woman while she was sitting in the field, and Manoah her husband was not with her.

**13:10** So the woman hurried and ran and told her husband, and she said to him, "Look, the man who came to me on that day has appeared to me."

**13:11** Then Manoah arose and went after his wife, and he came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" And he said, "I am."

**13:12** Manoah said, "Now may your words come to pass! What shall be the rule for the boy, and his work?"

**13:13** And the angel of the LORD said to Manoah, "Let her be on guard concerning all that I said to the woman.

**13:14** She shall not eat of anything that comes from the grapevine, nor shall she drink wine or strong drink, nor eat any unclean thing; everything that I commanded her, she shall keep."

**13:15** Then Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, "Please let us detain you, and let us prepare a young goat before you."

**13:16** And the angel of the LORD said to Manoah, "Even if you detain me, I will not eat of your food; and if you prepare a burnt offering, you must offer it up to the LORD." For Manoah did not know that he was the angel of the LORD.

**13:17** Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, "What is your name, so that when your word comes to pass we may honor you?"

**13:18** And the angel of the LORD said to him, "Why is it that you ask for my name, since it is wonderful?"

**13:19** So Manoah took the young goat and the grain offering and offered them up on the rock to the LORD, and he did a wondrous thing while Manoah and his wife were watching.

**13:20** And it happened that when the flame ascended from the altar toward heaven, the angel of the LORD ascended in the flame of the altar. While Manoah and his wife were watching, they fell on their faces to the ground.

**13:21** And the angel of the LORD never again appeared to Manoah or to his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was the angel of the LORD.

**13:22** So Manoah said to his wife, "We will surely die, because we have seen God!"

**13:23** But his wife said to him, "If the LORD had desired to put us to death, He would not have accepted a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hand, nor would He have shown us all these things, nor would He at this time have let us hear such a thing."

**13:24** Then the woman gave birth to a son and called his name Samson; and the boy grew up, and the LORD blessed him.

**13:25** And the Spirit of the LORD began to stir him in Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Translator Notes

- **13:8** — The form hayyullad ('who is to be born') is a Pual participle of yalad without the usual mem prefix, expressing imminent or destined birth.
- **13:12** — Hebrew pairs a singular verb (yavo) with a plural subject (devarekha, 'your words'); mishpat ('rule') and ma'asehu ('his work') refer to the boy's prescribed regimen and calling.
- **13:17** — Ketiv reads plural 'your words' (matching v. 12), while Qere (translated here) reads singular devarkha ('your word') in strict agreement with the singular verb yavo.
- **13:19** — The Hiphil participle umaphli ('doing a wondrous thing,' echoing the adjective peli'i, 'wonderful,' in v. 18) has no explicit subject in Hebrew; following the Masoretic punctuation, it describes the angel of the LORD acting wondrously (detailed in v. 20), though some take the LORD as its subject.
- **13:25** — The infinitive lepha'amo ('to stir him') is the sole Qal occurrence of pa'am in Biblical Hebrew, denoting a strong impelling impulse; Mahaneh-dan means 'Camp of Dan.'
