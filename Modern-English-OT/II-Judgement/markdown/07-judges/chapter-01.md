## Text

**1:1** Now after the death of Joshua, the sons of Israel inquired of the LORD, saying, "Who will go up for us against the Canaanites first to fight them?"

**1:2** And the LORD said, "Judah will go up. Look, I have given the land into his hand."

**1:3** Then Judah said to his brother Simeon, "Come up with me into my allotment so that we may fight against the Canaanites, and I too will go with you into your allotment." So Simeon went with him.

**1:4** Judah went up, and the LORD gave the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hand, and they struck them down at Bezek, ten thousand men.

**1:5** They found Adoni-bezek at Bezek and fought against him, and they struck down the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

**1:6** Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued after him, seized him, and cut off his thumbs and his big toes.

**1:7** Adoni-bezek said, "Seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off used to glean under my table. Just as I have done, so God has repaid me." Then they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

**1:8** Then the sons of Judah fought against Jerusalem and captured it; they struck it with the edge of the sword and set the city on fire.

**1:9** Afterward the sons of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanites who lived in the hill country, the Negev, and the lowland.

**1:10** So Judah went against the Canaanites who lived in Hebron—now the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath-arba—and they struck down Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

**1:11** From there he went against the inhabitants of Debir; now the name of Debir formerly was Kiriath-sepher.

**1:12** Caleb said, "Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and captures it, to him I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife."

**1:13** Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, captured it, so he gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.

**1:14** Now when she arrived, she urged him to ask her father for the field. Then she dismounted from the donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What is it you want?"

**1:15** She said to him, "Grant me a blessing, for you have given me the land of the Negev; now give me springs of water too." Then Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

**1:16** Now the sons of the Kenite, Moses' father-in-law, went up from the City of Palms with the sons of Judah to the wilderness of Judah, which is in the Negev of Arad, and they went and lived with the people.

**1:17** Then Judah went with his brother Simeon, and they struck down the Canaanites who lived in Zephath and devoted it to destruction; so he called the name of the city Hormah.

**1:18** And Judah captured Gaza and its territory, Ashkelon and its territory, and Ekron and its territory.

**1:19** The LORD was with Judah, and he took possession of the hill country, yet he could not dispossess the inhabitants of the valley, because they had chariots of iron.

**1:20** They gave Hebron to Caleb, just as Moses had spoken, and from there he drove out the three sons of Anak.

**1:21** Yet the sons of Benjamin did not dispossess the Jebusites who lived in Jerusalem, so the Jebusites have lived with the sons of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.

**1:22** Now the house of Joseph also went up to Bethel, and the LORD was with them.

**1:23** The house of Joseph had Bethel scouted out; now the name of the city formerly was Luz.

**1:24** The lookouts saw a man coming out of the city and said to him, "Please show us the entrance of the city, and we will show kindness to you."

**1:25** So he showed them the entrance of the city, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword, but they let the man and all his family go.

**1:26** Then the man went into the land of the Hittites, built a city, and called its name Luz; that is its name to this day.

**1:27** Manasseh did not dispossess Beth-shean and its daughter towns, nor Taanach and its daughter towns, nor the inhabitants of Dor and its daughter towns, nor the inhabitants of Ibleam and its daughter towns, nor the inhabitants of Megiddo and its daughter towns; so the Canaanites were determined to live in that land.

**1:28** When Israel grew strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, yet they did not completely dispossess them.

**1:29** And Ephraim did not dispossess the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, so the Canaanites lived in their midst in Gezer.

**1:30** Zebulun did not dispossess the inhabitants of Kitron or the inhabitants of Nahalol, so the Canaanites lived in their midst and became subject to forced labor.

**1:31** Asher did not dispossess the inhabitants of Acco or the inhabitants of Sidon, nor Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob.

**1:32** So the Asherites lived in the midst of the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, for they did not dispossess them.

**1:33** Naphtali did not dispossess the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh or the inhabitants of Beth-anath, but lived in the midst of the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land; yet the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath became subject to forced labor for them.

**1:34** Then the Amorites pressed the sons of Dan back into the hill country, for they did not allow them to go down into the valley.

**1:35** The Amorites were determined to live in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim, yet the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy, and they became subject to forced labor.

**1:36** Now the border of the Amorites was from the ascent of Akrabbim, from Sela and upward.

## Translator Notes

- **1:14** — The rare Qal verb tsanach ("dismounted") occurs only here, Joshua 15:18, and Judges 4:21; paired with "from upon the donkey," it denotes getting down or alighting from a mount.
- **1:19** — Both "took possession of" (vayyoresh) and "to dispossess" (lehorish) are Hiphil forms of yarash, denoting taking possession of territory and dispossessing inhabitants respectively. The construction lo followed by le- plus an infinitive construct expresses inability ("could not dispossess").
- **1:23** — "Had Bethel scouted out" reflects the rare Hiphil stem of tur (usually Qal, "spy out"), expressing causative reconnaissance. In verse 24, "lookouts" translates the Qal participle shomerim ("watchers, guards").
- **1:27** — Translates the Qere yoshevei ("the inhabitants of," plural construct, matching Ibleam and Megiddo) rather than the Ketiv yoshev (singular construct, "the inhabitant of"). "Daughter towns" renders the Hebrew idiom benoteiha (literally "its daughters"), denoting dependent satellite villages.
