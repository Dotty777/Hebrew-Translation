## Text

**10:1** Following Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, son of Dodo, a man of Issachar, rose up to deliver Israel. He resided at Shamir in the highlands of Ephraim.

**10:2** He judged Israel for twenty-three years; then he died and was buried at Shamir.

**10:3** After him, Jair the Gileadite rose up, and he judged Israel for twenty-two years.

**10:4** He had thirty sons riding on thirty donkey colts, and thirty towns belonged to them; to this very day those towns, which are in the land of Gilead, are called Havvoth-jair.

**10:5** Then Jair died and was buried at Kamon.

**10:6** Once more the Israelites did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. They served the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and the gods of Aram, the gods of Sidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the Ammonites, and the gods of the Philistines; they abandoned the LORD and did not serve Him.

**10:7** Then the LORD's anger flared against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of the Philistines and into the hand of the Ammonites.

**10:8** In that year they shattered and crushed the Israelites—for eighteen years, all the Israelites who were across the Jordan, in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead.

**10:9** Moreover, the Ammonites crossed the Jordan to fight against Judah as well, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim, so that Israel was severely distressed.

**10:10** Then the Israelites cried out to the LORD, saying, "We have sinned against You, in that we abandoned our God and served the Baals."

**10:11** The LORD said to the Israelites, "Did I not deliver you from Egypt, and from the Amorites, and from the Ammonites, and from the Philistines?

**10:12** The Sidonians, Amalek, and Maon also oppressed you; you cried out to Me, and I delivered you from their hand.

**10:13** Yet you abandoned Me and served other gods; therefore I will not deliver you anymore.

**10:14** Go and cry out to the gods you have chosen; let them deliver you in your time of distress!"

**10:15** Then the Israelites said to the LORD, "We have sinned. You do to us according to everything that is good in Your eyes; only please rescue us this very day."

**10:16** So they removed the foreign gods from among them and served the LORD, and His soul could no longer endure Israel's misery.

**10:17** Then the Ammonites were called to arms and encamped in Gilead, while the Israelites assembled and encamped at Mizpah.

**10:18** The people, the leaders of Gilead, said to one another, "Who is the man who will begin to fight against the Ammonites? He will become head over all the residents of Gilead."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — The Masoretic Text points both occurrences of the plural noun identically as ayarim, creating a pun between 'donkey colts' (plural of ayir) and 'towns' (normally arim).
- **10:8** — The Hebrew pairs two alliterative verbs from closely related roots, Qal ra'ats ('shatter') and Polel ratsats ('crush/oppress'), intensifying the harshness of the oppression.
- **10:11** — The Masoretic Hebrew lacks an explicit verb in this verse (an anacoluthon), with the implied verb 'deliver' supplied from the climax in verse 12 ('and I delivered you').
- **10:16** — Literally 'His soul grew short over the misery of Israel,' a Hebrew idiom expressing the limit of endurance or patience.
