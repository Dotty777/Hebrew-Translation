## Text

**20:1** All the sons of Israel went out, and the congregation assembled as one man, from Dan even to Beersheba and the land of Gilead, to the LORD at Mizpah.

**20:2** And the chiefs of all the people, all the tribes of Israel, took their stand in the assembly of the people of God: four hundred thousand men on foot who drew a sword.

**20:3** Now the sons of Benjamin heard that the sons of Israel had gone up to Mizpah. And the sons of Israel said, "Speak, how did this evil happen?"

**20:4** Then the Levite man, the husband of the murdered woman, answered and said, "I and my concubine came to Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin, to spend the night.

**20:5** The citizens of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded the house against me by night. They intended to kill me, and they violated my concubine, and she died.

**20:6** So I took hold of my concubine, cut her into pieces, and sent her throughout the whole territory of Israel's inheritance, because they committed depravity and outrage in Israel.

**20:7** Look, all of you are sons of Israel; give your word and counsel here."

**20:8** Then all the people rose up as one man, saying, "Not one of us will go to his tent, and not one of us will turn back to his house.

**20:9** And now this is the thing we will do to Gibeah: against it by lot.

**20:10** We will take ten men out of a hundred throughout all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, to fetch provisions for the people, so that when they come to Geba of Benjamin, they may act according to all the outrage it has committed in Israel."

**20:11** So all the men of Israel gathered against the city as one man, united together.

**20:12** Then the tribes of Israel sent men throughout all the tribes of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has happened among you?

**20:13** Now then, hand over the men, the worthless fellows who are in Gibeah, so that we may put them to death and purge evil from Israel." But the sons of Benjamin were not willing to listen to the voice of their brothers, the sons of Israel.

**20:14** And the sons of Benjamin gathered from the cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the sons of Israel.

**20:15** And on that day the sons of Benjamin were mustered from the cities: twenty-six thousand men who drew a sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who were mustered as seven hundred picked men.

**20:16** Out of all these people, seven hundred picked men were left-handed; every one of them could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

**20:17** And the men of Israel, apart from Benjamin, were mustered: four hundred thousand men who drew a sword; all of these were men of war.

**20:18** Then they arose and went up to Bethel and inquired of God, and the sons of Israel said, "Who shall go up for us first to battle against the sons of Benjamin?" And the LORD said, "Judah first."

**20:19** So the sons of Israel arose in the morning and encamped against Gibeah.

**20:20** Then the men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and the men of Israel drew up battle lines against them facing Gibeah.

**20:21** And the sons of Benjamin came out from Gibeah and on that day destroyed twenty-two thousand men of Israel down to the ground.

**20:22** But the people, the men of Israel, strengthened themselves and once more drew up battle lines in the place where they had drawn up on the first day.

**20:23** And the sons of Israel went up and wept before the LORD until evening, and they inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I draw near again to battle against the sons of my brother Benjamin?" And the LORD said, "Go up against him."

**20:24** So the sons of Israel came near to the sons of Benjamin on the second day.

**20:25** And Benjamin came out from Gibeah to meet them on the second day, and they destroyed another eighteen thousand men of the sons of Israel down to the ground; all of these drew a sword.

**20:26** Then all the sons of Israel and all the people went up and came to Bethel, and they wept and sat there before the LORD, and fasted that day until evening, and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

**20:27** And the sons of Israel inquired of the LORD—for the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days,

**20:28** and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, was standing before it in those days—saying, "Shall I still go out again to battle against the sons of my brother Benjamin, or shall I stop?" And the LORD said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give him into your hand."

**20:29** So Israel set men in ambush all around Gibeah.

**20:30** And the sons of Israel went up against the sons of Benjamin on the third day and drew up in formation against Gibeah, as at the previous times.

**20:31** Then the sons of Benjamin came out to meet the people and were drawn away from the city; and they began to strike down and kill some of the people, as at the previous times, on the highways—one of which goes up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah—in the open field, about thirty men of Israel.

**20:32** And the sons of Benjamin said, "They are being defeated before us as at the first." But the sons of Israel had said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways."

**20:33** Then all the men of Israel rose from their place and drew up in formation at Baal-tamar; and Israel's ambush rushed out from its place, from the open ground of Geba.

**20:34** And ten thousand picked men out of all Israel came from opposite Gibeah, and the battle was heavy; yet they did not know that disaster was closing in on them.

**20:35** And the LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel, and on that day the sons of Israel destroyed twenty-five thousand one hundred men of Benjamin; all of these drew a sword.

**20:36** So the sons of Benjamin saw that they were defeated. Now the men of Israel gave ground to Benjamin because they relied on the ambush that they had set against Gibeah.

**20:37** And the ambush hurried and rushed upon Gibeah; and the ambush spread out and struck the entire city with the edge of the sword.

**20:38** Now the men of Israel had an appointed signal with the ambush: that they would send up a great signal plume of smoke from the city.

**20:39** Then the men of Israel turned about in the battle. Now Benjamin had begun to strike down and kill about thirty men among the men of Israel, for they said, "Surely they are completely defeated before us, as in the first battle."

**20:40** But the signal plume began to rise from the city, a pillar of smoke. And Benjamin looked behind him, and look, the entire city was rising into the sky.

**20:41** Then the men of Israel turned about, and the men of Benjamin were terrified, for they saw that disaster had closed in on them.

**20:42** So they turned away before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness, but the battle overtook them, and those from the cities were destroying them in their midst.

**20:43** They encircled Benjamin, pursued them, and trod them down at their resting place, as far as opposite Gibeah on the east.

**20:44** So eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell; all of these were men of valor.

**20:45** And they turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and they gleaned five thousand men of them on the highways; and they pressed closely after them as far as Gidom and struck down two thousand men of them.

**20:46** So all who fell from Benjamin on that day were twenty-five thousand men who drew a sword; all of these were men of valor.

**20:47** But six hundred men turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and they remained at the rock of Rimmon for four months.

**20:48** And the men of Israel turned back against the sons of Benjamin and struck them with the edge of the sword, from the entire city to the livestock, to everything that was found; moreover, all the cities that were found they set on fire.

## Translator Notes

- **20:13** — The words "sons of" (Hebrew bene) reflect a Masoretic qere reading that is read in tradition though absent from the consonantal text, which writes only "Benjamin" with a plural verb.
- **20:33** — Hebrew maareh is a hapax legomenon from the root meaning "be bare" or "exposed," denoting open or bare ground (or possibly a place name, Maareh-geba). Here and in 20:10 the Masoretic Text reads "Geba" rather than "Gibeah."
- **20:38** — The form herev is a shortened Hiphil imperative or infinitive of ravah ("make great"), used adverbially before the Hiphil infinitive construct "their causing to rise" to describe sending up a great signal plume (masat) of smoke.
- **20:43** — The Hebrew phrase menuhah hidrikuhu is difficult: menuhah literally means "resting place" (perhaps a place name, Menuhah, or adverbially "with ease"), and hidrikuhu is a Hiphil of darakh meaning "trod down" or "overtook."
- **20:48** — Literally "from a city of completeness" (Hebrew meir metom, from the root tamam, "be complete"), denoting the entire city and its population; compare the similar phrase "city of inhabitants" (ir metim) in Deuteronomy 2:34.
