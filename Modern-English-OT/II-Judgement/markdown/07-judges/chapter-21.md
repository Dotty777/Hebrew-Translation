## Text

**21:1** Now the men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, saying, "No man among us will give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife."

**21:2** And the people came to Bethel and sat there until evening before God; they raised their voices and wept with great weeping.

**21:3** And they said, "Why, LORD, God of Israel, has this happened in Israel, so that one tribe is missing from Israel today?"

**21:4** Then on the next day the people rose early, built an altar there, and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings.

**21:5** And the sons of Israel said, "Who is it among all the tribes of Israel who did not go up in the assembly to the LORD?" For the great oath had been made concerning anyone who did not go up to the LORD at Mizpah, saying, "He will certainly be put to death."

**21:6** And the sons of Israel felt sorrow for Benjamin their brother and said, "Today one tribe has been cut off from Israel.

**21:7** What should we do for wives for them, for those who remain, since we have sworn by the LORD not to give any of our daughters to them as wives?"

**21:8** Then they said, "Which one from the tribes of Israel did not go up to the LORD at Mizpah?" And look, not a man from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp, to the assembly.

**21:9** The people were mustered, and look, not a man from the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead was there.

**21:10** So the congregation sent there twelve thousand men from the brave warriors and commanded them, saying, "Go and strike down the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead by the edge of the sword, along with the women and the little ones.

**21:11** And this is the thing that you are to do: every male and every woman who has known lying with a male you must devote to destruction."

**21:12** And they found from among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young virgin women who had not known a man by lying with a male, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

**21:13** So the entire congregation sent word and spoke to the sons of Benjamin who were at the rock of Rimmon, and declared peace to them.

**21:14** At that time Benjamin returned, and they gave them the women whom they had kept alive from among the women of Jabesh-gilead; yet even so they did not suffice for them.

**21:15** Now the people felt sorrow for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a breach among the tribes of Israel.

**21:16** So the elders of the congregation said, "What should we do for wives for those who remain, since the women have been wiped out from Benjamin?"

**21:17** And they said, "Benjamin's surviving remnant must have an inheritance, so that no tribe is blotted out from Israel.

**21:18** Yet we are not able to give them wives from among our daughters." For the sons of Israel had sworn, saying, "Cursed is whoever gives a wife to Benjamin."

**21:19** Then they said, "Look, from year to year there is a festival of the LORD in Shiloh, which lies to the north of Bethel, east of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and to the south of Lebonah."

**21:20** Then they commanded the sons of Benjamin, saying, "Go and wait in ambush in the vineyards,

**21:21** and watch. Look, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then come out from the vineyards and seize for yourselves, each man his wife, from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

**21:22** And it will happen, when their fathers or their brothers come to contend with us, that we will say to them, 'Graciously give them to us, because we did not take for each man his wife in the war, and because it was not you who gave them to them; otherwise you would now be guilty.'"

**21:23** And the sons of Benjamin did just so: they took wives according to their number from the dancing women whom they had carried off, and they went and returned to their inheritance, rebuilt the cities, and settled in them.

**21:24** And at that time the sons of Israel went their way from there, each man to his tribe and to his clan; and they went out from there, each man to his inheritance.

**21:25** In those days there was no king in Israel; each man would do what was right in his eyes.

## Translator Notes

- **21:14** — Hebrew literally reads "and they did not find for them thus"; the Qal of matsa with the preposition le- idiomatically expresses sufficiency, indicating that the four hundred women were still not enough for the surviving Benjaminites.
- **21:17** — Hebrew uses a terse verbless clause, literally "an inheritance of an escaped remnant for Benjamin," conveying that the surviving Benjaminites must have descendants to preserve their tribal inheritance.
- **21:20** — Qere reads the Piel plural wayyetsawwu ("and they commanded"), matching the plural elders of vv. 16-19; ketiv writes the Piel singular wayyetsaw ("and he commanded"), likely agreeing with the congregation as a collective.
- **21:22** — Qere reads the Qal infinitive construct lariv ("to contend") where ketiv writes the medial-waw by-form larov. The closing clause is elliptical in Hebrew (literally "for you did not give to them, at this time you would be guilty"), arguing that because the fathers did not give their daughters voluntarily, they avoid incurring the guilt of breaking the oath.
