## Text

**4:1** Then the sons of Israel again did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, after Ehud had died.

**4:2** So the LORD sold them into the hand of Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; the commander of his army was Sisera, and he lived in Harosheth-hagoyim.

**4:3** Then the sons of Israel cried out to the LORD, for he had nine hundred chariots of iron, and he oppressed the sons of Israel severely for twenty years.

**4:4** And Deborah, a prophetess, wife of Lappidoth, was the one judging Israel at that time.

**4:5** She would sit beneath the palm tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the sons of Israel went up to her for judgment.

**4:6** She sent and summoned Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh-naphtali, and said to him, "Has not the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded, 'Go and deploy on Mount Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men from the sons of Naphtali and from the sons of Zebulun,

**4:7** and I will draw out to you, to the Wadi Kishon, Sisera, the commander of Jabin's army, and his chariots and his multitude, and I will give him into your hand'?"

**4:8** Barak said to her, "If you go with me, then I will go; and if you do not go with me, I will not go."

**4:9** She said, "I will certainly go with you; however, the glory will not be yours on the way that you are going, for the LORD will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman." So Deborah rose and went with Barak to Kedesh.

**4:10** And Barak mustered Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh; ten thousand men went up following at his feet, and Deborah went up with him.

**4:11** Meanwhile, Heber the Kenite had separated from Kain, from the sons of Hobab, Moses' father-in-law, and pitched his tent as far as the oak in Zaanannim, which is beside Kedesh.

**4:12** They reported to Sisera that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor.

**4:13** So Sisera mustered all his chariots—nine hundred chariots of iron—and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth-hagoyim to the Wadi Kishon.

**4:14** Then Deborah said to Barak, "Rise up, for this is the day that the LORD has given Sisera into your hand! Has not the LORD gone out before you?" So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, with ten thousand men behind him.

**4:15** Then the LORD threw Sisera, all the chariots, and the whole army into panic before Barak at the edge of the sword; and Sisera climbed down from the chariot and fled on foot.

**4:16** Meanwhile, Barak pursued the chariots and the army as far as Harosheth-hagoyim, and Sisera's entire army fell by the edge of the sword; not even one remained.

**4:17** Sisera, however, had fled on foot to the tent of Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite, because there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and the household of Heber the Kenite.

**4:18** Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; do not be afraid." So he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

**4:19** He said to her, "Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty." So she opened a skin of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him.

**4:20** He said to her, "Stand at the entrance of the tent, and if anyone comes and questions you, saying, 'Is there any man here?' then say, 'No.'"

**4:21** Then Jael, the wife of Heber, took a tent peg and placed a hammer in her hand; she came to him stealthily and drove the peg into his temple, and it went down into the ground, for he was in a deep sleep and exhausted; and he died.

**4:22** And look, Barak was pursuing Sisera, and Jael went out to meet him and said to him, "Come, let me show you the man you are seeking." So he came in to her, and there was Sisera, fallen dead, with the peg in his temple.

**4:23** Thus God subdued Jabin king of Canaan on that day before the sons of Israel.

**4:24** And the hand of the sons of Israel bore down harder and harder upon Jabin king of Canaan, until they had cut off Jabin king of Canaan.

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — The Qal verb mashak ('draw, pull') is used intransitively here in a military sense ('deploy, march out,' cf. Judges 20:37), setting up a wordplay with the transitive use of the same verb in verse 7 ('I will draw out').
- **4:11** — Ketiv reads bts'nym (likely 'in Zaanim'); Qere reads beza'anannim ('in Zaanannim,' cf. Joshua 19:33), which is followed here. 'Kain' (Qayin) is the eponymous clan name of the Kenites.
- **4:20** — Hebrew 'amod ('Stand') is vocalized as a masculine singular imperative addressed to Jael, whereas the following verbs addressing her are feminine singular.
- **4:21** — The feminine singular verb wattisnah ('and it went down') takes the feminine noun hayyated ('the peg') as its subject. Hebrew wayya'ap ('and exhausted') is a Qal wayyiqtol of 'up ('be faint/exhausted,' cf. 1 Samuel 14:28, 31), continuing the circumstantial explanation of Sisera's state.
