## Text

**9:1** Now Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem to his mother's brothers, and he spoke to them and to the entire clan of the house of his mother's father, saying,

**9:2** "Please speak in the ears of all the lords of Shechem: 'Which is better for you, that seventy men, all the sons of Jerubbaal, rule over you, or that one man rule over you?' And remember that I am your bone and your flesh."

**9:3** So his mother's brothers spoke all these words on his behalf in the ears of all the lords of Shechem, and their heart turned after Abimelech, for they said, "He is our brother."

**9:4** And they gave him seventy silver pieces from the house of Baal-berith, and with them Abimelech hired worthless and reckless men, and they went after him.

**9:5** He came to his father's house at Ophrah and killed his brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy men, on one stone. Yet Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, remained, because he had hidden himself.

**9:6** Then all the lords of Shechem and all of Beth-millo gathered together, and they went and made Abimelech king beside the oak of the pillar that was in Shechem.

**9:7** When they told Jotham, he went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, lifted up his voice, and called out. And he said to them, "Listen to me, lords of Shechem, so that God may listen to you!

**9:8** Indeed, the trees went to anoint a king over themselves, and they said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us.'

**9:9** But the olive tree said to them, 'Should I give up my rich oil, since through me they honor God and men, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:10** Then the trees said to the fig tree, 'Come, you reign over us.'

**9:11** But the fig tree said to them, 'Should I give up my sweetness and my good produce, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:12** Then the trees said to the vine, 'Come, you reign over us.'

**9:13** But the vine said to them, 'Should I give up my new wine, which gladdens God and men, and go to sway over the trees?'

**9:14** Then all the trees said to the thornbush, 'Come, you reign over us.'

**9:15** And the thornbush said to the trees, 'If in truth you are anointing me to be king over you, come, take shelter in my shade; but if not, let fire come out from the thornbush and devour the cedars of Lebanon.'

**9:16** Now then, if you have acted in truth and integrity in making Abimelech king, and if you have done good to Jerubbaal and to his house, and if you have dealt with him according to what his hands deserved—

**9:17** for my father fought for you, risked his life, and delivered you from the hand of Midian,

**9:18** yet you have risen up against my father's house today, killed his sons, seventy men, on one stone, and made Abimelech, the son of his female servant, king over the lords of Shechem because he is your brother—

**9:19** if indeed you have acted in truth and integrity with Jerubbaal and with his house this day, then rejoice in Abimelech, and let him also rejoice in you.

**9:20** But if not, let fire come out from Abimelech and devour the lords of Shechem and Beth-millo, and let fire come out from the lords of Shechem and from Beth-millo and devour Abimelech."

**9:21** Then Jotham fled and escaped, and went to Beer, and lived there because of Abimelech his brother.

**9:22** And Abimelech ruled over Israel for three years.

**9:23** Then God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the lords of Shechem, and the lords of Shechem acted treacherously against Abimelech,

**9:24** so that the violence done to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid upon Abimelech their brother, who killed them, and upon the lords of Shechem, who strengthened his hands to kill his brothers.

**9:25** And the lords of Shechem set ambushers against him on the tops of the mountains, and they robbed everyone who passed by them along the road; and it was reported to Abimelech.

**9:26** Now Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers came and crossed over into Shechem, and the lords of Shechem put their trust in him.

**9:27** They went out into the field, harvested their vineyards, trod the grapes, and held a festival of praise. Then they entered the house of their god, ate and drank, and cursed Abimelech.

**9:28** Then Gaal son of Ebed said, "Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerubbaal, and is not Zebul his officer? Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem! Yet why should we serve him?

**9:29** If only this people were given into my hand! Then I would remove Abimelech." And he said to Abimelech, "Enlarge your army and come out!"

**9:30** When Zebul, the ruler of the city, heard the words of Gaal son of Ebed, his anger burned.

**9:31** So he sent messengers to Abimelech secretly, saying, "Look, Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers have come to Shechem, and look, they are fortifying the city against you.

**9:32** Now therefore, arise by night, you and the people who are with you, and lie in ambush in the field.

**9:33** And in the morning, as the sun rises, get up early and rush against the city; and look, when he and the people who are with him come out toward you, you shall do to him whatever your hand finds to do."

**9:34** So Abimelech and all the people who were with him arose by night, and they lay in ambush against Shechem in four divisions.

**9:35** Then Gaal son of Ebed went out and stood at the entrance of the city gate, and Abimelech and the people who were with him arose from the ambush.

**9:36** When Gaal saw the people, he said to Zebul, "Look, people are coming down from the tops of the mountains." And Zebul said to him, "It is the shadow of the mountains that you are seeing as men."

**9:37** And Gaal spoke yet again and said, "Look, people are coming down from the navel of the land, and one division is coming by way of the oak of the diviners."

**9:38** Then Zebul said to him, "Where is your mouth now, with which you said, 'Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?' Is not this the people whom you despised? Go out now, please, and fight them!"

**9:39** So Gaal went out before the lords of Shechem and fought against Abimelech.

**9:40** And Abimelech pursued him, and he fled before him, and many fell dead right up to the entrance of the gate.

**9:41** Then Abimelech lived at Arumah, and Zebul drove Gaal and his brothers out from living in Shechem.

**9:42** And on the next day, the people went out into the field, and they told Abimelech.

**9:43** So he took the people, divided them into three divisions, and lay in ambush in the field. When he looked, there the people were, coming out of the city; so he rose up against them and struck them down.

**9:44** Then Abimelech and the divisions that were with him rushed forward and stood at the entrance of the city gate, while the two divisions rushed against all who were in the field and struck them down.

**9:45** And Abimelech fought against the city all that day; he captured the city and killed the people who were in it, and he tore down the city and sowed it with salt.

**9:46** When all the lords of the Tower of Shechem heard of it, they entered the vault of the house of El-berith.

**9:47** And it was reported to Abimelech that all the lords of the Tower of Shechem had gathered together.

**9:48** So Abimelech went up to Mount Zalmon, he and all the people who were with him. And Abimelech took the axes in his hand, cut down a branch from the trees, lifted it up, and placed it on his shoulder; and he said to the people who were with him, "What you have seen me do, hurry and do like me!"

**9:49** So all the people also cut down each man his own branch and went after Abimelech, and they placed them against the vault and set the vault on fire over them. Thus all the people of the Tower of Shechem died as well, about a thousand men and women.

**9:50** Then Abimelech went to Thebez, encamped against Thebez, and captured it.

**9:51** Now there was a strong tower in the middle of the city, and all the men and women and all the lords of the city fled there, shut themselves in, and went up onto the roof of the tower.

**9:52** And Abimelech came up to the tower and fought against it, and he approached the entrance of the tower to burn it with fire.

**9:53** Then one woman threw an upper millstone onto Abimelech's head and shattered his skull.

**9:54** Quickly he called to the young man who carried his armor and said to him, "Draw your sword and put me to death, so that they do not say about me, 'A woman killed him.'" So his young man pierced him through, and he died.

**9:55** When the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, each man went to his own place.

**9:56** So God returned Abimelech's evil, which he had done to his father by killing his seventy brothers.

**9:57** And all the evil of the men of Shechem God returned upon their own heads, and the curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came upon them.

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — Ketiv reads mlwkh and Qere reads molkhah, a Qal masculine singular imperative of malakh with paragogic -ah, meaning Reign. The same orthographic variant preserving the internal o-vowel occurs in 9:12 with Ketiv mlwky and Qere molkhi, the feminine singular imperative.
- **9:17** — Risked his life renders the Hebrew idiom literally meaning cast his life away, using the Hiphil of shalakh with minneged.
- **9:29** — The Masoretic Text reads the third-person wayyiqtol And he said to Abimelech, framing Gaal's words as a rhetorical taunt or challenge, rather than a first-person conditional statement.
- **9:31** — Secretly translates the rare noun in betormah, literally in craftiness or stealth from the root ramah, though some read it as a place name, Tormah. Fortifying renders the Qal plural participle tsarim from tsur, meaning enclose, fortify, or besiege, which is often taken contextually as stirring up.
- **9:44** — The Masoretic Text has the plural the divisions with Abimelech, even though 9:43 mentions three divisions in total and the second clause names the two divisions.
