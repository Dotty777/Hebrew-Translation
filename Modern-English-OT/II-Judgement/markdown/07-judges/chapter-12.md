## Text

**12:1** Then the men of Ephraim were summoned, and they crossed over to Zaphon and said to Jephthah, "Why did you cross over to fight against the sons of Ammon and did not call us to go with you? We will burn your house down over you with fire!"

**12:2** Jephthah said to them, "I and my people were in a severe dispute with the sons of Ammon, and I called out to you, yet you did not deliver me from their hand.

**12:3** When I saw that you were no deliverer, I placed my life in my hand and crossed over against the sons of Ammon, and the LORD gave them into my hand. Why then have you come up to me this day to fight against me?"

**12:4** Then Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought with Ephraim; and the men of Gilead struck down Ephraim, because they had said, "You are fugitives of Ephraim, Gilead, in the midst of Ephraim, in the midst of Manasseh."

**12:5** And Gilead captured the fords of the Jordan against Ephraim. And it happened that whenever any of the fugitives of Ephraim said, "Let me cross over," the men of Gilead would say to him, "Are you an Ephraimite?" And he would say, "No."

**12:6** Then they would say to him, "Please say 'Shibboleth.'" And he would say, "Sibboleth," for he could not manage to pronounce it right. Then they would seize him and slaughter him at the fords of the Jordan. So forty-two thousand from Ephraim fell at that time.

**12:7** Jephthah judged Israel for six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died, and he was buried among the cities of Gilead.

**12:8** And after him, Ibzan from Bethlehem judged Israel.

**12:9** He had thirty sons; he sent thirty daughters outside, and he brought thirty daughters in from outside for his sons. And he judged Israel for seven years.

**12:10** And Ibzan died, and he was buried in Bethlehem.

**12:11** And after him, Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel; and he judged Israel for ten years.

**12:12** And Elon the Zebulunite died, and he was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.

**12:13** And after him, Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel.

**12:14** He had forty sons and thirty grandsons who rode on seventy young donkeys; and he judged Israel for eight years.

**12:15** And Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite died, and he was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of the Amalekites.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Hebrew tsaphonah may be understood either as the town 'to Zaphon' in the Jordan valley (cf. Josh 13:27) or directionally as 'northward.'
- **12:2** — The clause rendered 'were in a severe dispute' is literally 'a man of strife I was, I and my people and the sons of Ammon, greatly' (ish riv).
- **12:4** — The Ephraimites' taunt is terse in Hebrew and sets up the verbal irony of 'the fugitives of Ephraim' (pelite Ephrayim) in v. 5.
- **12:6** — The verb yakhin is a Hiphil imperfect of kun ('establish, direct'), used idiomatically for managing to pronounce the word properly; Hebrew contrasts the sibilants sh in shibbolet and s in sibbolet.
