## Text

**2:1** The angel of the LORD went up from Gilgal to Bochim and said, "I brought you up from Egypt and brought you to the land that I swore to your fathers, and I said, 'I will never break My covenant with you,

**2:2** and as for you, you must not make a covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you must tear down their altars.' Yet you have not obeyed My voice. What is this that you have done?

**2:3** Therefore I also say, 'I will not drive them out from before you; they will become thorns in your sides, and their gods will become a snare to you.'"

**2:4** Now when the angel of the LORD spoke these words to all the sons of Israel, the people raised their voices and wept.

**2:5** So they called the name of that place Bochim, and they sacrificed there to the LORD.

**2:6** Now Joshua sent the people away, and the sons of Israel went, each to his inheritance, to possess the land.

**2:7** The people served the LORD all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who lived on after Joshua, who had seen all the great work of the LORD that He had done for Israel.

**2:8** Then Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at one hundred and ten years of age.

**2:9** They buried him within the territory of his inheritance, in Timnath-heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

**2:10** All of that generation were likewise gathered to their fathers, and another generation arose after them who did not know the LORD, nor even the work that He had done for Israel.

**2:11** Then the sons of Israel did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and served the Baals.

**2:12** They abandoned the LORD, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt, and they went after other gods from among the gods of the peoples around them and bowed down to them, and they provoked the LORD to anger.

**2:13** So they abandoned the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.

**2:14** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He gave them into the hand of plunderers, and they plundered them; He also sold them into the hand of their enemies on every side, so that they were no longer able to stand before their enemies.

**2:15** Wherever they went out, the hand of the LORD was against them for harm, just as the LORD had spoken and just as the LORD had sworn to them; and they were in extreme distress.

**2:16** Then the LORD raised up judges, and they delivered them from the hand of their plunderers.

**2:17** Yet they did not listen even to their judges, for they prostituted themselves after other gods and bowed down to them. They turned aside quickly from the way in which their fathers had walked in obeying the commandments of the LORD; they did not do so.

**2:18** Whenever the LORD raised up judges for them, the LORD was with the judge and delivered them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge; for the LORD was moved to pity by their groaning because of those who oppressed and afflicted them.

**2:19** Yet whenever the judge died, they would turn back and act more corruptly than their fathers, going after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them. They did not give up any of their practices or their stubborn way.

**2:20** So the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He said, "Because this nation has transgressed My covenant that I commanded their fathers and has not obeyed My voice,

**2:21** I too will no longer drive out anyone from before them of the nations that Joshua left when he died,

**2:22** in order to test Israel by them, whether they will keep the way of the LORD to walk in it just as their fathers kept it, or not."

**2:23** So the LORD left those nations, without driving them out quickly, and He did not give them into the hand of Joshua.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Hiphil imperfect (yiqtol) a'aleh ("I brought you up") followed by the wayyiqtol va'avi ("and brought you") uses a vivid or archaic past yiqtol to recount the historical deliverance from Egypt. "Bochim" (ha-Bokhim) is the Qal masculine plural participle of bakhah, literally "the Weepers," linking to verses 4-5.
- **2:3** — "Thorns in your sides" renders the concise Masoretic reading letsiddim (literally "for sides"), traditionally understood as an ellipsis of the idiom "thorns in your sides" or "a scourge in your sides" (cf. Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13).
- **2:18** — "Was moved to pity" renders the Niphal imperfect yinnachem (from nacham), which paired with min ("by") denotes being moved to compassion or relenting in response to distress.
- **2:19** — "Did not give up" translates the Hiphil perfect lo hippilu (from naphal, literally "did not let fall" or "drop"), used idiomatically for refusing to abandon entrenched practices.
- **2:22** — Hebrew literally reads "to walk in them" (bam, masculine plural suffix), referring ad sensum to the singular collective "the way of the LORD"; rendered "in it" for English clarity.
