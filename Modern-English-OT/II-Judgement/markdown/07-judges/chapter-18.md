## Text

**18:1** In those days there was no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of the Danites was seeking for itself an inheritance in which to dwell, because up to that day nothing had fallen to it as an inheritance among the tribes of Israel.

**18:2** So the sons of Dan sent from their clan five men out of their whole number, men of valor from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land and to explore it; and they said to them, "Go, explore the land." And they came to the hill country of Ephraim, up to the house of Micah, and spent the night there.

**18:3** While they were near the house of Micah, they recognized the voice of the young Levite; so they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place, and what do you have here?"

**18:4** He said to them, "Micah has done such and such for me; he hired me, and I have become his priest."

**18:5** They said to him, "Please inquire of God so that we may know whether our way, on which we are going, will succeed."

**18:6** The priest said to them, "Go in peace. Your way on which you are going is directly before the LORD."

**18:7** Then the five men went and came to Laish, and saw the people who were in its midst dwelling in security, according to the custom of the Sidonians, quiet and unsuspecting; and there was no possessor of authority in the land humiliating anyone in any matter, and they were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.

**18:8** They came to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their brothers said to them, "What do you have to say?"

**18:9** They said, "Arise, and let us go up against them; for we have seen the land, and look, it is very good! And are you sitting still? Do not be sluggish to go, to enter to take possession of the land.

**18:10** When you enter, you will come to an unsuspecting people, and the land is spacious; for God has given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth."

**18:11** Then they set out from there, from the clan of the Danites, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, six hundred men armed with weapons of war.

**18:12** They went up and encamped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. Therefore they have called that place Mahaneh-dan to this day; look, it is behind Kiriath-jearim.

**18:13** And they passed on from there to the hill country of Ephraim, and came as far as the house of Micah.

**18:14** Then the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish spoke up and said to their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod and teraphim, and a carved image and a cast image? Now then, consider what you should do."

**18:15** So they turned aside there and came to the house of the young Levite, the house of Micah, and inquired about his welfare.

**18:16** Now the six hundred men who were from the sons of Dan, armed with their weapons of war, were standing at the entrance of the gate.

**18:17** And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went up, entered there, and took the carved image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the cast image, while the priest was standing at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men who were armed with the weapons of war.

**18:18** When these entered the house of Micah and took the carved image of the ephod, the teraphim, and the cast image, the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

**18:19** They said to him, "Keep quiet, put your hand over your mouth, and go with us, and be a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be a priest for the house of one man, or for you to be a priest for a tribe and a clan in Israel?"

**18:20** The priest's heart was glad, so he took the ephod, the teraphim, and the carved image, and went into the midst of the people.

**18:21** Then they turned and went, and they placed the little ones, the livestock, and the baggage ahead of them.

**18:22** When they had gone far from the house of Micah, the men who were in the houses beside the house of Micah rallied together and overtook the sons of Dan.

**18:23** They called out to the sons of Dan, who turned their faces around and said to Micah, "What is the matter with you, that you have rallied?"

**18:24** He said, "You have taken my gods that I made, and the priest, and have gone away; and what do I have left? And how is it that you say to me, 'What is the matter with you?'"

**18:25** The sons of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, or else men bitter of soul may fall upon you, and you will lose your life and the lives of your household."

**18:26** And the sons of Dan went on their way; and Micah saw that they were stronger than he, so he turned and went back to his house.

**18:27** So they took what Micah had made, and the priest who had belonged to him, and came upon Laish, upon a quiet and unsuspecting people, and struck them with the edge of the sword; and they burned the city with fire.

**18:28** And there was no deliverer, because it was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone, and it was in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. Then they rebuilt the city and lived in it.

**18:29** And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; however, Laish was the name of the city at first.

**18:30** And the sons of Dan set up the carved image for themselves; and Jonathan the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites until the day of the exile of the land.

**18:31** So they established for themselves Micah's carved image that he had made, all the days that the house of God was in Shiloh.

## Translator Notes

- **18:7** — The difficult Masoretic phrase rendered "no possessor of authority in the land humiliating anyone in any matter" literally reads "none putting to shame (Hiphil participle of kalam) in any matter in the land, a possessor of restraint (yoresh etser)," referring to the absence of any governing ruler to check or harass them.
- **18:18** — The Masoretic Text reads the construct phrase "the carved image of the ephod" (lacking both the definite article on "carved image" and the conjunction found in 18:17); some treat it asyndetically as "the carved image, the ephod."
- **18:25** — Literally "and you will gather in your life and the life of your household" (Qal of asaph), an idiom meaning to bring about one's own death and that of one's household.
- **18:30** — The Masoretic Text reads "Manasseh," traditionally written with a suspended letter nun; omitting that letter yields "Moses," reflecting an ancient scribal tradition linking Jonathan son of Gershom to Moses (cf. Exod 2:22).
