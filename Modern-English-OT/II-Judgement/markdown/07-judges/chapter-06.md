## Text

**6:1** Then the sons of Israel did evil in the eyes of the LORD, so the LORD gave them into the hand of Midian for seven years.

**6:2** The hand of Midian grew strong over Israel; because of Midian, the sons of Israel made for themselves the clefts that are in the mountains, along with the caves and the strongholds.

**6:3** Whenever Israel sowed, Midian and Amalek and the sons of the east would come up; yes, they would come up against them.

**6:4** They would encamp against them and devastate the produce of the land as far as the approach to Gaza, leaving no sustenance in Israel, nor sheep, nor ox, nor donkey.

**6:5** For they and their livestock would come up, along with their tents, and they would come in like locusts in multitude; both they and their camels were without number, and they came into the land to devastate it.

**6:6** Thus Israel was greatly impoverished because of Midian, and the sons of Israel cried out to the LORD.

**6:7** Now when the sons of Israel cried out to the LORD on account of Midian,

**6:8** the LORD sent a prophet to the sons of Israel, and he said to them, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I Myself brought you up from Egypt and led you out of the house of slaves.

**6:9** I rescued you from the hand of Egypt and from the hand of all your oppressors, and I drove them out from before you and gave their land to you.

**6:10** And I told you, 'I am the LORD your God; do not fear the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you live.' Yet you have not listened to My voice."

**6:11** Then the angel of the LORD came and sat beneath the terebinth in Ophrah that belonged to Joash the Abiezrite, while Gideon his son was beating out wheat in the winepress to hide it from Midian.

**6:12** The angel of the LORD appeared to him and said to him, "The LORD is with you, mighty warrior."

**6:13** Gideon said to him, "Pardon me, my lord, if the LORD is with us, why has all this overtaken us? And where are all His wonderful deeds that our fathers recounted to us, saying, 'Did not the LORD bring us up out of Egypt?' Yet now the LORD has deserted us and given us into the grasp of Midian."

**6:14** The LORD turned toward him and said, "Go in this strength of yours and save Israel from the grasp of Midian. Have I not sent you?"

**6:15** And he said to Him, "Pardon me, Lord, by what means can I save Israel? See, my clan is the poorest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest in my father's house."

**6:16** Then the LORD said to him, "Surely I will be with you, and you will strike down Midian as a single man."

**6:17** He said to Him, "Please, if I have found favor in Your eyes, perform for me a sign that it is You speaking with me.

**6:18** Please do not leave here until I come to You and bring out my offering and place it before You." And He said, "I will stay until you come back."

**6:19** Gideon went inside and made ready a young goat and unleavened cakes from an ephah of flour. He put the meat in a basket and put the broth in a pot, and he brought them out to Him beneath the terebinth and presented them.

**6:20** The angel of God said to him, "Take the meat and the unleavened cakes, place them on this rock, and pour out the broth." And he did so.

**6:21** Then the angel of the LORD stretched out the end of the staff that was in his hand and touched the meat and the unleavened cakes; and fire rose up from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened cakes. And the angel of the LORD was gone from his sight.

**6:22** Then Gideon saw that he was the angel of the LORD, and Gideon said, "Alas, Lord GOD! For indeed I have seen the angel of the LORD face to face!"

**6:23** And the LORD said to him, "Peace to you! Do not be afraid; you will not die."

**6:24** So Gideon built an altar there to the LORD and called it The LORD Is Peace. To this day it remains in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

**6:25** That night the LORD said to him, "Take the young bull belonging to your father, and the second bull, seven years old, and tear down the altar of Baal that belongs to your father, and cut down the Asherah that is beside it.

**6:26** Then build an altar to the LORD your God on top of this stronghold in proper order, and take the second bull and offer a burnt offering with the wood of the Asherah that you cut down."

**6:27** So Gideon took ten men from among his servants and did just as the LORD had spoken to him. Because he feared his father's house and the men of the city too much to do it in the daytime, he did it at night.

**6:28** When the men of the city got up early in the morning, look—the altar of Baal had been demolished, the Asherah beside it had been cut down, and the second bull had been offered up on the altar that had been built.

**6:29** They said to one another, "Who has done this thing?" After inquiring and searching, they said, "Gideon son of Joash has done this thing."

**6:30** Then the men of the city said to Joash, "Bring out your son so that he may die, because he has demolished the altar of Baal and because he has cut down the Asherah that was beside it."

**6:31** Then Joash said to all who were standing against him, "Are you the ones to contend for Baal, or are you the ones to save him? Whoever contends for him must be put to death by morning. If he is a god, let him contend for himself, because his altar has been demolished."

**6:32** So on that day he named him Jerubbaal, saying, "Let Baal contend against him, because he demolished his altar."

**6:33** Now all Midian and Amalek and the sons of the east gathered together; they crossed over and encamped in the Valley of Jezreel.

**6:34** Then the Spirit of the LORD clothed Gideon; he blew the ram's horn, and Abiezer was summoned to follow him.

**6:35** He sent messengers throughout all Manasseh, and they too were summoned to follow him; and he sent messengers into Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, and they came up to meet them.

**6:36** Then Gideon said to God, "If You are indeed going to save Israel by my hand, as You have spoken,

**6:37** see, I am setting a wool fleece on the threshing floor. If there is dew on the fleece alone, while all the ground is dry, then I will know that You will save Israel by my hand, as You have spoken."

**6:38** And so it was. He got up early the next morning, squeezed the fleece, and wrung dew from the fleece—a bowl full of water.

**6:39** Then Gideon said to God, "May Your anger not burn against me while I speak just this once. Please let me test just this one time with the fleece: may it be dry on the fleece alone, while on all the ground there is dew."

**6:40** God did so on that night: it was dry on the fleece alone, while on all the ground there was dew.

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — Ketiv reads the Qal imperfect yavo'u ("they would come"), while the Qere followed here reads the frequentative weqatal uva'u ("and they would come in").
- **6:15** — Whereas Gideon addressed his visitor as 'adoni ("my lord") in 6:13, here the MT vocalizes 'adonai ("Lord"), reflecting his direct address to the LORD who spoke in 6:14.
- **6:25** — The MT literally reads "the young bull of the cattle that belongs to your father, and the second bull, seven years old." The conjunction "and" may refer to a second bull (the one sacrificed in vv. 26 and 28) or function epexegetically ("even the second bull").
- **6:34** — Hebrew laveshah 'et-Gid'on literally means "put on Gideon" or "clothed itself with Gideon" (Qal perfect of lavash), a vivid idiom depicting the Spirit enveloping and empowering him as a person puts on a garment.
