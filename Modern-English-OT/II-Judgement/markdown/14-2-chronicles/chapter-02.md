## Text

**2:1** Solomon numbered seventy thousand men as burden-bearers, eighty thousand men as stonecutters in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred overseers over them.

**2:2** Then Solomon sent word to Huram king of Tyre, saying, "As you dealt with my father David and sent him cedars to build himself a house to live in—

**2:3** look, I am about to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, to dedicate it to Him, to burn fragrant incense before Him, and for the continual arrangement of bread, and for burnt offerings morning and evening, on the Sabbaths, on the new moons, and at the appointed festivals of the LORD our God; this rests upon Israel forever.

**2:4** Now the house that I am about to build will be great, for our God is greater than all the gods.

**2:5** Yet who has the strength to build Him a house, since the heavens and the heaven of heavens cannot contain Him? And who am I that I should build Him a house, except to burn incense before Him?

**2:6** So now send me a skilled man to work in gold, silver, bronze, and iron, and in purple, crimson, and blue, and who knows how to make engravings, together with the skilled men who are with me in Judah and in Jerusalem, whom my father David provided.

**2:7** Send me also cedar, cypress, and algum timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut the timber of Lebanon; and see, my servants will be with your servants

**2:8** and to prepare timber for me in abundance, because the house that I am about to build will be great and wondrous.

**2:9** Now look, for the woodcutters who fell the timber, I will give your servants twenty thousand kors of crushed wheat, twenty thousand kors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil."

**2:10** Then Huram king of Tyre answered in a letter that he sent to Solomon: "Because the LORD loves His people, He has made you king over them."

**2:11** And Huram said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made the heavens and the earth, who has given King David a wise son, versed in insight and understanding, who will build a house for the LORD and a house for his kingdom.

**2:12** Now I am sending a skilled man, versed in understanding, Huram-abi,

**2:13** the son of a woman from the daughters of Dan, while his father was a man of Tyre. He knows how to work in gold and silver, in bronze and iron, in stone and wood, and in purple, blue, fine linen, and crimson, and how to make every kind of engraving and devise every design that is given to him, together with your skilled men and the skilled men of my lord David your father.

**2:14** Now then, the wheat and barley, the oil and wine, of which my lord spoke, let him send to his servants.

**2:15** And we will cut timber from Lebanon according to all your need, and bring it to you in rafts by sea to Joppa, and you will bring it up to Jerusalem."

**2:16** Then Solomon numbered all the men who were resident aliens in the land of Israel, after the numbering with which his father David had numbered them, and they were found to be one hundred fifty-three thousand six hundred.

**2:17** He appointed from them seventy thousand as burden-bearers, eighty thousand as stonecutters in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred as overseers to keep the people working.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The כַּאֲשֶׁר ("As") clause is elliptical, leaving the expected main clause ("so deal with me") unexpressed until Solomon's requests in vv. 6-7.
- **2:8** — וְהַפְלֵא is a Hiphil infinitive absolute of פלא used adjectivally or adverbially alongside גָּדוֹל ("great and wondrous").
- **2:9** — MT reads חִטִּים מַכּוֹת ("crushed" or "beaten wheat," associated with the root נכה), whereas the parallel in 1 Kgs 5:11 (MT 5:25) reads מַכֹּלֶת ("food").
- **2:12** — In לְחוּרָם אָבִי, the preposition לְ functions as a Late Biblical Hebrew direct-object marker, and אָבִי (lit. "my father") serves as an honorific title for a master craftsman (cf. 2 Chr 4:16).
