## Text

**22:1** Then the inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah, his youngest son, king in his place, for the raiding band that had come with the Arabs to the camp had killed all the older ones. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah became king.

**22:2** Ahaziah was forty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah daughter of Omri.

**22:3** He also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother was his counselor in acting wickedly.

**22:4** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD like the house of Ahab, for they became his counselors after his father's death, to his destruction.

**22:5** He also followed their counsel and went with Jehoram son of Ahab king of Israel to battle against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead; and the Arameans wounded Joram.

**22:6** So he returned to be healed in Jezreel because of the wounds that they had inflicted on him at Ramah when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. And Azariah son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to see Jehoram son of Ahab in Jezreel, because he was ill.

**22:7** Now Ahaziah's downfall was from God in his coming to Joram; and when he came, he went out with Jehoram toward Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.

**22:8** When Jehu was executing judgment upon the house of Ahab, he found the officials of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers who were attending Ahaziah, and he killed them.

**22:9** He searched for Ahaziah, and they captured him while he was hiding in Samaria; they brought him to Jehu and put him to death. Then they buried him, for they said, "He is a son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the LORD with all his heart." And the house of Ahaziah had no one able to retain strength for the kingdom.

**22:10** Now when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal seed of the house of Judah.

**22:11** But Jehoshabeath the king's daughter took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being put to death, and she placed him and his nurse in the bedroom. So Jehoshabeath, daughter of King Jehoram and wife of Jehoiada the priest—for she was the sister of Ahaziah—hid him from Athaliah, so that she did not put him to death.

**22:12** He stayed with them in the house of God, hiding for six years, while Athaliah was reigning over the land.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "forty-two years old"; the parallel in 2 Kings 8:26 and several ancient versions read "twenty-two."
- **22:6** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "Azariah" (Azaryahu), a variant or scribal interchange for Ahaziah (Achazyahu; cf. 22:1, 7; 2 Kings 8:29).
- **22:7** — "Downfall" translates a rare Hebrew noun (tevusah, occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible) derived from a root meaning "to trample down."
- **22:10** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads vattedabber (Piel of d-b-r), understood here either from a rare root meaning "to destroy" or as an early scribal variant of vatte'abbed ("she destroyed," Piel of a-b-d) in 2 Kings 11:1.
