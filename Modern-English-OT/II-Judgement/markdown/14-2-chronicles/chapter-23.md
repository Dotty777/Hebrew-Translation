## Text

**23:1** In the seventh year, Jehoiada strengthened himself and took the commanders of hundreds—Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri—into a covenant with him.

**23:2** They went around through Judah and gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah and the heads of the fathers of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

**23:3** Then the whole assembly made a covenant in the house of God with the king. And he said to them, "Look, the king's son will reign, just as the LORD spoke concerning the sons of David.

**23:4** This is the thing that you shall do: a third of you who come in on the Sabbath, from the priests and from the Levites, shall be gatekeepers of the thresholds,

**23:5** and a third shall be at the king's house, and a third at the Foundation Gate, while all the people shall be in the courts of the house of the LORD.

**23:6** Let no one enter the house of the LORD except the priests and those of the Levites who minister; they may enter, for they are holy, but all the people shall keep the watch of the LORD.

**23:7** The Levites shall surround the king all around, each man with his weapons in his hand, and whoever enters the house shall be put to death; and be with the king when he comes in and when he goes out."

**23:8** So the Levites and all Judah did according to everything that Jehoiada the priest had commanded. Each man took his men, those coming in on the Sabbath together with those going out on the Sabbath, for Jehoiada the priest had not dismissed the divisions.

**23:9** Then Jehoiada the priest gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears, the shields, and the bucklers that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of God.

**23:10** He stationed all the people, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and by the house, all around the king.

**23:11** Then they brought out the king's son, placed the crown and the testimony on him, and made him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him and said, "Long live the king!"

**23:12** When Athaliah heard the sound of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people at the house of the LORD.

**23:13** She looked, and there was the king standing by his pillar at the entrance, with the commanders and the trumpets beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets, and the singers with musical instruments directing the praise. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and said, "Treason! Treason!"

**23:14** Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders of hundreds who were appointed over the army, and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks, and whoever follows her shall be put to death by the sword." For the priest had said, "Do not put her to death in the house of the LORD."

**23:15** So they laid hands on her, and she came to the entrance of the Horse Gate at the king's house, and they put her to death there.

**23:16** Then Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king, to be a people for the LORD.

**23:17** All the people went to the house of Baal and tore it down; they shattered its altars and its images, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal in front of the altars.

**23:18** Jehoiada placed the duties of the house of the LORD in the hand of the Levitical priests, whom David had apportioned over the house of the LORD to offer up the burnt offerings of the LORD, as it is written in the law of Moses, with joy and with singing, according to the direction of David.

**23:19** He stationed the gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the LORD so that no one unclean in any matter would enter.

**23:20** He took the commanders of hundreds, the nobles, the rulers over the people, and all the people of the land, and brought the king down from the house of the LORD. They came through the Upper Gate into the king's house, and they seated the king on the throne of the kingdom.

**23:21** So all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; and they had put Athaliah to death by the sword.

## Translator Notes

- **23:9** — Hebrew pairs maginnot ('shields') with shelatim ('bucklers'), a rarer term whose exact sense is debated between a type of small or ceremonial shield and a quiver.
- **23:13** — The phrase 'directing the praise' translates modi'im lehallel, a Hiphil participle of yada with a Piel infinitive construct of halal, literally 'making known to praise,' meaning leading or cueing the musical praise.
- **23:15** — The idiom 'they laid hands on her' translates wayyasimu lah yadayim (literally 'they placed hands for her'), which is understood either as seizing her or as making room for her to pass.
- **23:18** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads hakkohanim halwiyyim ('the Levitical priests') without a conjunction between the two nouns.
