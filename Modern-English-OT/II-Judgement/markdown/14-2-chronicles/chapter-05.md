## Text

**5:1** So all the work that Solomon did for the house of the LORD was completed. And Solomon brought in the consecrated things of his father David; and the silver, the gold, and all the utensils he placed in the treasuries of the house of God.

**5:2** Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes, the chiefs of the ancestral houses of the sons of Israel, to Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the city of David, which is Zion.

**5:3** And all the men of Israel assembled to the king at the feast, which was in the seventh month.

**5:4** So all the elders of Israel came, and the Levites lifted up the ark.

**5:5** And they brought up the ark, the tent of meeting, and all the holy utensils that were in the tent; the Levitical priests brought them up.

**5:6** And King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel who had gathered around him were before the ark, sacrificing sheep and oxen that could not be counted or numbered because of their multitude.

**5:7** Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD into its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Most Holy Place, beneath the wings of the cherubim.

**5:8** And the cherubim were spreading out their wings over the place of the ark, so that the cherubim covered over the ark and its poles from above.

**5:9** The poles were long, so that the ends of the poles were visible from the ark in front of the inner sanctuary, yet they were not visible outside; and it is there to this day.

**5:10** In the ark there was nothing except the two tablets that Moses put inside at Horeb, where the LORD made a covenant with the sons of Israel as they came out of Egypt.

**5:11** Now when the priests came out of the holy place—for all the priests who were present had consecrated themselves without keeping to divisions,

**5:12** and all the Levites who were singers, Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and their brothers, clothed in fine linen, with cymbals, harps, and lyres, were standing east of the altar, and with them a hundred and twenty priests sounding trumpets—

**5:13** and the trumpeters and the singers joined as one to make a single sound heard in praising and thanking the LORD, and as they lifted up the sound with trumpets, cymbals, and instruments of song, and praised the LORD, "For He is good, for His faithful love is forever," then the house was filled with a cloud, the house of the LORD.

**5:14** And the priests were not able to stand to serve because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD filled the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **5:5** — The Masoretic Text reads 'the Levitical priests' (literally 'the priests, the Levites') without a conjunction, differing from 'the priests and the Levites' in 1 Kings 8:4.
- **5:9** — Following the Masoretic Text strictly, the ends of the poles were visible 'from the ark' (differing from 'from the holy place' in 1 Kings 8:8), and the final verb is singular ('it is there'), referring to the ark rather than the poles.
- **5:12** — Ketiv reads a reduplicated participle form (mchtsrrym), while the Qere followed here reads machtserim, the Hiphil participle of the denominative verb chatsar ('to sound trumpets').
- **5:13** — Ketiv reads a quadriliteral participle form (lmchtstsrym), while the Qere followed here reads lamchatserim, the Piel participle of chatsar ('the trumpeters'). Verses 5:11-13 form a single suspended Hebrew period culminating in the cloud filling the house of the LORD.
