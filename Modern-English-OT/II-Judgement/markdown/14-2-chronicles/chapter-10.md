## Text

**10:1** And Rehoboam went to Shechem, since all Israel had come to Shechem to establish him as king.

**10:2** And it happened when Jeroboam son of Nebat heard of it, while he was in Egypt, where he had fled from before King Solomon, that Jeroboam returned from Egypt.

**10:3** Then they sent and called for him; and Jeroboam and all Israel came and spoke to Rehoboam, saying,

**10:4** "Your father made our yoke harsh; so now lighten your father's harsh service and his heavy yoke that he placed on us, and we will serve you."

**10:5** And he said to them, "In three more days, return to me." So the people left.

**10:6** Then King Rehoboam consulted with the elders who had stood before his father Solomon while he was alive, saying, "How do you counsel returning an answer to this people?"

**10:7** And they spoke to him, saying, "If you will be good to this people, and show them favor, and speak good words to them, then they will be servants to you always."

**10:8** Yet he abandoned the counsel of the elders that they had counseled him, and he consulted with the young men who had grown up with him, who stood before him.

**10:9** And he said to them, "What do you counsel that we return as an answer to this people who have spoken to me, saying, 'Lighten the yoke that your father laid upon us'?"

**10:10** And the young men who had grown up with him spoke with him, saying, "This is what you are to say to the people who spoke to you, saying, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, so you lighten it from us'; this is what you are to say to them: 'My little finger is thicker than my father's waist.

**10:11** Now then, my father loaded a heavy yoke upon you, but I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, but I, with scorpions.'"

**10:12** On the third day Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, just as the king had spoken, saying, "Return to me on the third day."

**10:13** The king gave them a harsh answer, and King Rehoboam abandoned the counsel of the elders.

**10:14** And he spoke to them following the counsel of the young men, saying, "I will make your yoke heavy, and I will add to it; my father disciplined you with whips, but I, with scorpions."

**10:15** Thus the king did not listen to the people, because a turn of events had come from God, so that the LORD might confirm His word that He had spoken by the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

**10:16** And as for all Israel, because the king did not listen to them, the people replied to the king, saying, "What share do we have in David? We have no inheritance in the son of Jesse! Each man to your tents, Israel! Now see to your own house, David!" Then all Israel went to their tents.

**10:17** Yet as for the sons of Israel dwelling in the cities of Judah, Rehoboam ruled over them as king.

**10:18** Then King Rehoboam sent Hadoram, who was in charge of the forced labor, and the sons of Israel stoned him with stones, and he died. Meanwhile, King Rehoboam hurried to climb into the chariot to flee to Jerusalem.

**10:19** Thus Israel has rebelled against the house of David up to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **10:10** — Qotanni (literally "my small one") is traditionally understood as "my little finger," contrasted idiomatically with motnei ("waist" or "loins"); in 10:11 and 10:14 the final clause ("but I, with scorpions," referring to barbed whips) is elliptical, omitting a repeated verb.
- **10:14** — The Masoretic text of Chronicles reads the 1cs Hiphil imperfect akhbid ("I will make heavy"), whereas 1 Kings 12:14 and some ancient versions read avi hikhbid ("my father made heavy").
- **10:16** — The Masoretic text of Chronicles opens with the fronted topic ve-khol-Yisrael ("and as for all Israel") followed directly by the causal clause ("because the king did not listen to them"), lacking the verb "saw" (vayyar) present in 1 Kings 12:16.
