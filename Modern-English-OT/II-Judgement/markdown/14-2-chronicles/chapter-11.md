## Text

**11:1** Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, and he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, one hundred and eighty thousand chosen men who waged war, to fight against Israel to restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.

**11:2** Then the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,

**11:3** "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying,

**11:4** 'This is what the LORD says: You must not go up and must not fight against your brothers. Return, each man to his own house, for this thing has come about from Me.'" So they listened to the words of the LORD and turned back from going against Jeroboam.

**11:5** Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and he built cities for fortification in Judah.

**11:6** He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,

**11:7** Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,

**11:8** Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,

**11:9** Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,

**11:10** Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, which are in Judah and in Benjamin, fortified cities.

**11:11** He strengthened the fortresses and placed commanders in them, and stores of food, oil, and wine.

**11:12** In every single city he placed large shields and spears, and he strengthened them very greatly. So Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.

**11:13** Moreover, the priests and the Levites who were in all Israel took their stand with him from all their territory.

**11:14** For the Levites left their pasturelands and their possession and went to Judah and to Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off from serving as priests to the LORD,

**11:15** and he appointed priests for himself for the high places, for the goat idols, and for the calves that he had made.

**11:16** After them, those from all the tribes of Israel who set their hearts to seek the LORD, the God of Israel, came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**11:17** They strengthened the kingdom of Judah and fortified Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years, because they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.

**11:18** Rehoboam took for himself as a wife Mahalath daughter of Jerimoth son of David and of Abihail daughter of Eliab son of Jesse.

**11:19** She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.

**11:20** After her he took Maacah daughter of Absalom, and she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.

**11:21** Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines, for he took eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

**11:22** Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah as head, as leader among his brothers, because he intended to make him king.

**11:23** He acted wisely and dispersed some of all his sons throughout all the lands of Judah and Benjamin, to all the fortified cities. He gave them provisions in abundance, and he sought a multitude of wives.

## Translator Notes

- **11:15** — Hebrew se'irim literally means "hairy ones" or "male goats," referring here to goat-shaped idols or demonic deities (cf. Lev 17:7).
- **11:18** — Qere reads bat ("daughter of") against ketiv ben ("son of"). The Hebrew also lacks a conjunction before Abihail, leaving it open whether she was Mahalath's mother ("and of Abihail," consistent with singular verbs and pronouns in vv. 19-20) or a second wife.
- **11:22** — Hebrew ki lehamliko (literally "for to make him king," Hiphil infinitive construct of malak) is an elliptical Late Biblical Hebrew construction expressing Rehoboam's intent.
- **11:23** — Hebrew wayyish'al hamon nashim literally says "and he sought a multitude of wives" without naming an indirect object, though the context of providing for his dispersed sons suggests he sought them for his sons.
