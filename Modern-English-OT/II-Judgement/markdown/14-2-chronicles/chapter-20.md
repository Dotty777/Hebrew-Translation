## Text

**20:1** It happened after this that the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, and some of the Ammonites with them, came against Jehoshaphat for battle.

**20:2** Then some came and reported to Jehoshaphat, saying, "A vast multitude has come against you from across the sea, from Aram, and look, they are in Hazazon-tamar, which is En-gedi."

**20:3** Jehoshaphat grew afraid; he set his face to seek the LORD and called a fast over all Judah.

**20:4** Then Judah gathered together to seek aid from the LORD; from all the cities of Judah as well they came to seek the LORD.

**20:5** Jehoshaphat stood in the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem, in the house of the LORD, before the new court.

**20:6** He said, "LORD, God of our fathers, are You not God in the heavens? You rule over all the kingdoms of the nations; in Your hand are strength and might, and there is no one who can withstand You.

**20:7** Did You not, our God, drive out the inhabitants of this land from before Your people Israel, and give it to the offspring of Abraham, who loved You, forever?

**20:8** They lived in it and built for You in it a sanctuary for Your name, saying,

**20:9** 'If calamity comes upon us, a sword of judgment, or pestilence, or famine, we will stand before this house and before You, for Your name is in this house, and cry out to You out of our distress, and You will hear and save.'

**20:10** And now, look at the sons of Ammon and Moab and Mount Seir, whom You did not allow Israel to invade when they came out of the land of Egypt, for they turned aside from them and did not destroy them.

**20:11** And look, they are repaying us by coming to expel us from Your possession that You gave us to possess.

**20:12** Our God, will You not judge them? For there is no strength in us before this vast multitude that is coming against us, and we do not know what we should do, but our eyes are upon You."

**20:13** Meanwhile, all Judah was standing before the LORD, even their little ones, their wives, and their sons.

**20:14** Then the Spirit of the LORD came upon Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah, the Levite of the sons of Asaph, in the midst of the assembly.

**20:15** He said, "Pay attention, all Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem and King Jehoshaphat. This is what the LORD says to you: 'As for you, do not fear and do not be terrified before this vast multitude, for the battle does not belong to you, but to God.

**20:16** Tomorrow go down against them. Look, they are coming up by the ascent of Ziz, and you will find them at the end of the valley, in front of the wilderness of Jeruel.

**20:17** It is not for you to fight in this. Take your stand, stand firm, and see the salvation of the LORD with you, Judah and Jerusalem. Do not fear and do not be terrified. Tomorrow go out to face them, and the LORD is with you.'"

**20:18** Then Jehoshaphat bowed low with his face to the ground, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell down before the LORD to worship the LORD.

**20:19** And the Levites from the sons of the Kohathites and from the sons of the Korahites stood up to praise the LORD, the God of Israel, with an exceedingly loud voice.

**20:20** They rose early in the morning and went out to the wilderness of Tekoa. As they were going out, Jehoshaphat stood and said, "Hear me, Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem! Trust in the LORD your God, and you will be made secure; trust in His prophets, and you will succeed."

**20:21** He took counsel with the people and appointed singers to the LORD and those offering praise in holy splendor, going out before the armed force and saying, "Give thanks to the LORD, for His loyal love is forever."

**20:22** At the time they began with singing and praise, the LORD set ambushers against the sons of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, who had come against Judah, and they were routed.

**20:23** The sons of Ammon and Moab stood up against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to devote them to destruction and annihilate them; and when they had finished with the inhabitants of Seir, they each helped to bring destruction upon his companion.

**20:24** When Judah arrived at the lookout of the wilderness, they turned toward the multitude, and look, they were corpses fallen to the ground, and no one had escaped.

**20:25** Then Jehoshaphat and his people came to plunder their spoil, and they found among them in abundance goods, corpses, and precious articles, and they stripped for themselves more than they could carry. They spent three days plundering the spoil, because it was so abundant.

**20:26** On the fourth day they assembled in the Valley of Beracah, for there they blessed the LORD; therefore they called the name of that place the Valley of Beracah to this day.

**20:27** Then every man of Judah and Jerusalem turned back, with Jehoshaphat at their head, to return to Jerusalem with joy, for the LORD had made them rejoice over their enemies.

**20:28** They came to Jerusalem with harps, lyres, and trumpets, to the house of the LORD.

**20:29** And the dread of God came upon all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought against the enemies of Israel.

**20:30** So the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was quiet, and his God gave him rest on every side.

**20:31** Thus Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah. He was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.

**20:32** He walked in the way of his father Asa and did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the eyes of the LORD.

**20:33** Yet the high places were not removed, and the people still had not directed their heart to the God of their fathers.

**20:34** Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, the first and the last, see, they are written in the record of Jehu son of Hanani, which was entered in the book of the kings of Israel.

**20:35** After this, Jehoshaphat king of Judah allied himself with Ahaziah king of Israel; he acted wickedly in doing so.

**20:36** He joined in partnership with him to build ships to go to Tarshish, and they built ships in Ezion-geber.

**20:37** Then Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, "Because you have allied yourself with Ahaziah, the LORD has broken down your works." So the ships were wrecked and were not able to go to Tarshish.

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "some of the Ammonites" (meha-ammonim); the Septuagint reads "Meunites" (cf. 2 Chronicles 26:7), which many textual critics regard as a letter transposition.
- **20:2** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "from Aram"; many commentators conjecture an original reading "from Edom" given the similarity of the Hebrew letters dalet and resh and the southern location of En-gedi.
- **20:20** — The Hebrew plays on two stems of the same root (aman): the Hiphil imperative "trust" (ha'aminu) and the Niphal imperfect "you will be made secure" (te'amenu).
- **20:25** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "corpses" (u-fegarim); some Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions reflect "garments" (u-vegadim).
- **20:35** — The verb "allied himself" (etchabbar) is an Aramaized Hithpael perfect using an initial aleph prefix instead of the standard Hebrew he.
