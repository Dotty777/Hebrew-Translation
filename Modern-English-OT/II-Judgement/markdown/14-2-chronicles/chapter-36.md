## Text

**36:1** And the people of the land took Jehoahaz, Josiah's son, and made him king in place of his father in Jerusalem.

**36:2** Joahaz was twenty-three years old when he began his reign, and he reigned for three months in Jerusalem.

**36:3** Then the king of Egypt deposed him in Jerusalem and fined the land a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.

**36:4** And the king of Egypt made his brother Eliakim king over Judah and Jerusalem, and he changed his name to Jehoiakim. As for his brother Joahaz, Neco took him and brought him into Egypt.

**36:5** Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began his reign, and he reigned for eleven years in Jerusalem; and he did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD his God.

**36:6** Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, marched up against him and bound him in bronze shackles to lead him to Babylon.

**36:7** And Nebuchadnezzar brought some of the vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon, and he placed them in his palace in Babylon.

**36:8** As for the rest of the deeds of Jehoiakim, and his abominations that he committed, and what was found against him, see, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. And his son Jehoiachin reigned in his place.

**36:9** Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began his reign, and he reigned for three months and ten days in Jerusalem; and he did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD.

**36:10** And at the return of the year, King Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him to Babylon along with the treasured vessels of the house of the LORD, and he made Zedekiah, his brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem.

**36:11** Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began his reign, and he reigned for eleven years in Jerusalem.

**36:12** And he did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD his God; he did not humble himself in the presence of Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke from the mouth of the LORD.

**36:13** Moreover, he rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, who had put him under oath by God, and he stiffened his neck and hardened his heart so as not to return to the LORD, the God of Israel.

**36:14** Also, all the leaders of the priests and the people multiplied their unfaithfulness according to all the abominations of the nations, and they defiled the house of the LORD that He had consecrated in Jerusalem.

**36:15** And the LORD, the God of their fathers, sent to them by the hand of His messengers, rising early and sending, for He felt compassion for His people and for His dwelling.

**36:16** But they kept ridiculing the messengers of God, and scorning His words, and deriding His prophets, until the fury of the LORD rose up against His people, until there was no healing.

**36:17** So He brought the king of the Chaldeans up against them, who killed their young men by the sword inside the house of their sanctuary and felt no compassion for young man or virgin, elderly or very old; all of them He gave into his hand.

**36:18** And all the vessels of the house of God, both large and small, together with the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king and his officials, all of it he brought to Babylon.

**36:19** Then they burned the house of God and tore down the wall of Jerusalem; all its citadels they burned with fire, and all its treasured vessels they destroyed.

**36:20** And he exiled the remnant from the sword to Babylon, and they became servants for him and for his sons until the kingdom of Persia began to reign,

**36:21** in order to fulfill the word of the LORD through the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had made up for its sabbaths. Throughout all the days it lay desolate, it kept sabbath, so as to fulfill seventy years.

**36:22** And in the first year of Cyrus, king of Persia, so that the word of the LORD through the mouth of Jeremiah would come to completion, the LORD roused the spirit of Cyrus, king of Persia, and he sent out a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and also in writing, saying,

**36:23** "This is what Cyrus, king of Persia, says: 'The LORD, the God of heaven, has given all the kingdoms of the earth to me, and He Himself has commissioned me to build a house for Him in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whoever among you is from all His people, may the LORD his God be with him, and let him go up.'"

## Translator Notes

- **36:7** — Or 'his temple'; the Hebrew noun hekal can denote either a royal palace or a temple.
- **36:9** — The Masoretic Text reads 'eight years old'; the parallel account in 2 Kings 24:8 and some ancient versions read 'eighteen years old.'
- **36:10** — Hebrew literally reads 'his brother,' using the noun broadly for a close male kinsman; according to 2 Kings 24:17, Zedekiah was Jehoiachin's uncle.
- **36:15** — 'Rising early and sending' translates paired Hebrew infinitives absolute (Hiphil of shakam and Qal of shalach), an idiom expressing earnest, persistent action.
- **36:21** — Or 'enjoyed'; the Hebrew verb ratsah here can mean either 'to pay off, make good' or 'to take pleasure in, enjoy,' echoing Leviticus 26:34, 43.
