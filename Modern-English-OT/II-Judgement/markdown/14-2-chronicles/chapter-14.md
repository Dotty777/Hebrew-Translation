## Text

**14:1** Asa did what was good and upright in the eyes of the LORD his God.

**14:2** He removed the foreign altars and the high places, shattered the standing stones, and chopped down the Asherim.

**14:3** He told Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to carry out the law and the commandment.

**14:4** He removed from all the cities of Judah the high places and the incense altars, and the kingdom was quiet before him.

**14:5** He built fortified cities in Judah, because the land was quiet and there was no war against him in those years, for the LORD had given him rest.

**14:6** He said to Judah, "Let us build these cities and enclose them with a wall and towers, gates and bars. The land is still before us, for we sought the LORD our God; we sought Him, and He gave us rest on every side." So they built and succeeded.

**14:7** Asa had an army of three hundred thousand from Judah carrying large shields and spears, and two hundred eighty thousand from Benjamin carrying shields and drawing bows; all of these were mighty men of valor.

**14:8** Then Zerah the Cushite marched out against them with an army of one million and three hundred chariots, and he came as far as Mareshah.

**14:9** Asa went out to face him, and they drew up for battle in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.

**14:10** Then Asa called out to the LORD his God and said, "LORD, there is no one beside You to help, whether the mighty or the one without strength. Help us, LORD our God, for we lean on You, and in Your name we have come against this multitude. LORD, You are our God; let no mortal prevail against You."

**14:11** So the LORD struck the Cushites before Asa and before Judah, and the Cushites fled.

**14:12** Asa and the people who were with him pursued them as far as Gerar, and so many of the Cushites fell that there was no recovery for them, for they were shattered before the LORD and before His army. They carried off a very great amount of plunder.

**14:13** They struck all the cities around Gerar, for the terror of the LORD was upon them; and they looted all the cities, because there was much loot in them.

**14:14** They also struck the tents of livestock, captured sheep in abundance and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **14:6** — The Hebrew form odennu attaches a masculine singular suffix to the particle od before the feminine noun ha'aretz ("the land"), functioning neutrally as "it is still."
- **14:8** — The Hebrew expresses "one million" literally as "a thousand thousands" (elef alafim).
- **14:10** — The compact Hebrew clause ein-immekha la'zor bein rav le'ein koach uses bein...le to express "whether...or" (or "between...and"), emphasizing that only the LORD gives victory regardless of human strength. The verb atzar ("prevail") reflects Chronicles' usage meaning "retain strength" or "prevail."
