## Text

**17:1** Then his son Jehoshaphat became king in his place, and he strengthened himself against Israel.

**17:2** He stationed military forces in all the fortified cities of Judah, and he stationed garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.

**17:3** The LORD was with Jehoshaphat, for he walked in the earlier ways of David his father and did not seek the Baals.

**17:4** Rather, he sought the God of his father and walked in His commandments, and not according to the practice of Israel.

**17:5** So the LORD established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah gave tribute to Jehoshaphat; and he had wealth and honor in abundance.

**17:6** His heart grew bold in the ways of the LORD, and furthermore he removed the high places and the Asherim from Judah.

**17:7** In the third year of his reign he dispatched his officials—Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach in the cities of Judah.

**17:8** With them were the Levites—Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, the Levites—and with them were Elishama and Jehoram, the priests.

**17:9** They taught in Judah, and the book of the law of the LORD was with them; they went around through all the cities of Judah and taught among the people.

**17:10** The dread of the LORD came upon all the kingdoms of the lands surrounding Judah, and they did not wage war with Jehoshaphat.

**17:11** Some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat tribute and silver as a levy, and the Arabians also brought him flocks—seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred male goats.

**17:12** Jehoshaphat continued to grow exceedingly great, and he built citadels and storage cities in Judah.

**17:13** He had abundant supplies in the cities of Judah, and soldiers—men of mighty valor—in Jerusalem.

**17:14** This was their enrollment according to their fathers' houses: for Judah, the commanders of thousands—Adnah the commander, and with him were three hundred thousand men of mighty valor.

**17:15** At his side was Jehohanan the commander, and with him were two hundred eighty thousand.

**17:16** At his side was Amasiah the son of Zichri, who willingly offered himself to the LORD, and with him were two hundred thousand men of mighty valor.

**17:17** From Benjamin was Eliada, a man of mighty valor, and with him were two hundred thousand equipped with bow and shield.

**17:18** At his side was Jehozabad, and with him were one hundred eighty thousand equipped for military service.

**17:19** These were those who served the king, in addition to those whom the king had stationed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **17:6** — Literally "his heart grew high" (Qal of gbh). Elsewhere in Chronicles this idiom denotes pride (e.g., 26:16; 32:25), but with "in the ways of the LORD" it uniquely conveys bold devotion and courage.
- **17:8** — The qere (read form) is Shemiramoth; the ketiv (written consonants) reads wshmrymwt (Shemerimoth).
- **17:13** — The noun translated "supplies" (melakah) ordinarily means "work" or "business," but can also denote property or stores (cf. 1 Sam 15:9). Here the feminine noun takes the masculine verb form hayah.
