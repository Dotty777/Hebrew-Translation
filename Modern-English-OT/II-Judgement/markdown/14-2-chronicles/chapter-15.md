## Text

**15:1** Now as for Azariah the son of Oded, the Spirit of God came upon him.

**15:2** He went out to meet Asa and said to him, "Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: the LORD is with you as long as you are with Him. If you seek Him, He will let Himself be found by you, yet if you abandon Him, He will abandon you.

**15:3** For many days Israel was without the God of truth, without a teaching priest, and without law.

**15:4** Yet in their distress they turned back to the LORD, the God of Israel; they searched for Him, and He let Himself be found by them.

**15:5** In those times there was no peace for the one going out or the one coming in, for great tumults were upon all the inhabitants of the lands.

**15:6** They were crushed, nation by nation and city by city, because God threw them into tumult with all kinds of distress.

**15:7** But as for you, be strong and do not let your hands grow weak, because there is a reward for your work."

**15:8** When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he strengthened himself and cleared away the detestable things from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities that he had captured from the hill country of Ephraim. He also renewed the altar of the LORD that was in front of the portico of the LORD.

**15:9** He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and those sojourning with them from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon, because people from Israel had defected to him in great numbers when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.

**15:10** They gathered in Jerusalem during the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.

**15:11** On that day they sacrificed to the LORD from the plunder they had brought, seven hundred cattle and seven thousand sheep.

**15:12** They entered the covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and with all their soul.

**15:13** And anyone who would not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, was to be put to death, from small to great, from man to woman.

**15:14** They swore an oath to the LORD in a loud voice, with shouting, with trumpets, and with horns.

**15:15** All Judah rejoiced over the oath, because with all their heart they had sworn, and with all their desire they had searched for Him, and He let Himself be found by them. And the LORD gave them rest on every side.

**15:16** Even Maacah, the mother of King Asa, he removed from being queen mother, because she had made a repulsive image for Asherah. Asa cut down her repulsive image, pulverized it, and burned it at the Kidron Brook.

**15:17** The high places, however, did not disappear from Israel; nevertheless, Asa's heart was undivided all his days.

**15:18** He brought the consecrated things of his father and his own consecrated things into the house of God: silver, gold, and vessels.

**15:19** There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Niphal verb yimmatze ("will let Himself be found") carries tolerative force, indicating God's willing response to those who seek Him; the same form recurs in 15:4 and 15:15.
- **15:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads literally "the prophecy, Oded the prophet" (vehannevu'ah Oded hannavi), naming Oded rather than "Azariah son of Oded" as in 15:1; the translation follows the Hebrew text without harmonizing to ancient versions.
- **15:16** — Hebrew mifletzet ("repulsive image"), from a root meaning "to shudder," refers to a loathsome cult object made for Asherah; the Hiphil verb vayyadeq ("pulverized it") specifically denotes crushing fine.
