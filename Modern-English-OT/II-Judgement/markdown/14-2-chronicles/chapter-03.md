## Text

**3:1** Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where He had appeared to his father David, which he prepared at the place of David, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**3:2** He began to build in the second month, on the second day, in the fourth year of his reign.

**3:3** Now these are the foundations Solomon laid for building the house of God: the length, in cubits by the former measure, was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

**3:4** And the vestibule that was in front, its length across the width of the house was twenty cubits, and the height was one hundred twenty; and he overlaid it on the inside with pure gold.

**3:5** The great hall he paneled with cypress wood, and covered it with fine gold, and worked palm trees and chains onto it.

**3:6** He overlaid the house with precious stones for splendor, and the gold was gold of Parvaim.

**3:7** He covered the house—the beams, the thresholds, its walls, and its doors—with gold, and he engraved cherubim on the walls.

**3:8** Then he made the chamber of the Most Holy Place: its length across the width of the house was twenty cubits, and its width was twenty cubits; and he covered it with fine gold amounting to six hundred talents.

**3:9** The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold, and the upper chambers he covered with gold.

**3:10** In the chamber of the Most Holy Place he made two cherubim of sculpted work, and they overlaid them with gold.

**3:11** The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits in length: one wing of the first was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing was five cubits, touching the wing of the other cherub.

**3:12** And one wing of the second cherub was five cubits, touching the wall of the house, and the other wing was five cubits, joined to the wing of the other cherub.

**3:13** The wings of these cherubim extended twenty cubits; they stood on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.

**3:14** He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and he worked cherubim onto it.

**3:15** In front of the house he made two pillars thirty-five cubits in length, and the capital on the top of each was five cubits.

**3:16** He made chains in the inner sanctuary and put them on the top of the pillars; and he made one hundred pomegranates and set them on the chains.

**3:17** He set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and one on the left; he called the name of the one on the right Jachin, and the name of the one on the left Boaz.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — MT reads אֲשֶׁר הֵכִין בִּמְקוֹם דָּוִיד ("which he prepared at the place of David," with מְקוֹם in the construct state), whereas LXX and Vulgate transpose the wording to "at the place that David had prepared." The unexpressed subject of the Niphal נִרְאָה ("He had appeared") is the LORD.
- **3:10** — צַעֲצֻעִים ("sculpted work") is a biblical hapax legomenon whose exact sense is uncertain, commonly understood as carved, sculpted, or molded metalwork.
- **3:16** — MT reads בַּדְּבִיר ("in the inner sanctuary"); a commonly proposed emendation is כָּרָבִיד ("like a necklace").
- **3:17** — Ketiv הימיני vs. Qere הַיְמָנִי is an orthographic variant; both mean "the one on the right."
