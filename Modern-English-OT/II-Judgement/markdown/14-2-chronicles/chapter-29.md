## Text

**29:1** Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he began reigning, and for twenty-nine years he reigned in Jerusalem; the name of his mother was Abijah daughter of Zechariah.

**29:2** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, in accordance with all that David his father had done.

**29:3** He himself, in the first year of his reign, in the first month, opened the doors of the house of the LORD and repaired them.

**29:4** He brought in the priests and the Levites and gathered them into the eastern square.

**29:5** He said to them, "Listen to me, Levites! Now consecrate yourselves and consecrate the house of the LORD, the God of your fathers, and bring the defilement out of the holy place.

**29:6** For our fathers acted unfaithfully and did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD our God. They abandoned Him, turned their faces away from the dwelling place of the LORD, and turned their backs.

**29:7** Moreover, they shut the doors of the vestibule, extinguished the lamps, burned no incense, and offered up no burnt offering in the holy place to the God of Israel.

**29:8** So the wrath of the LORD came upon Judah and Jerusalem, and He made them an object of terror, of desolation, and of hissing, just as you yourselves see with your eyes.

**29:9** Look, our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons, our daughters, and our wives are in captivity because of this.

**29:10** Now I have it in my heart to make a covenant with the LORD, the God of Israel, so that His fierce anger may turn back from us.

**29:11** My sons, do not be remiss now, for it is you whom the LORD has chosen to stand before Him, to minister to Him, and to be His ministers and those who burn incense."

**29:12** Then the Levites stood up: Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah, from the sons of the Kohathites; from the sons of Merari, Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel; from the Gershonites, Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah;

**29:13** from the sons of Elizaphan, Shimri and Jeiel; from the sons of Asaph, Zechariah and Mattaniah;

**29:14** from the sons of Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and from the sons of Jeduthun, Shemaiah and Uzziel.

**29:15** They gathered their brothers, consecrated themselves, and entered in accordance with the king's command, by the words of the LORD, to cleanse the house of the LORD.

**29:16** The priests entered the interior of the house of the LORD to cleanse it, and they brought out all the uncleanness they found in the temple of the LORD to the courtyard of the house of the LORD. Then the Levites received it to carry outside to the Kidron Valley.

**29:17** On the first day of the first month they began to consecrate, and on the eighth day of the month they reached the vestibule of the LORD; they consecrated the house of the LORD for eight days, and on the sixteenth day of the first month they finished.

**29:18** Then they went inside to King Hezekiah and said, "We have cleansed the entire house of the LORD, the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the table of the rows of bread and all its utensils.

**29:19** Also, all the utensils that King Ahaz cast aside during his reign in his unfaithfulness we have prepared and consecrated, and here they are before the altar of the LORD."

**29:20** King Hezekiah rose early, gathered the city officials, and went up to the house of the LORD.

**29:21** They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats as a sin offering on behalf of the kingdom, the sanctuary, and Judah. And he instructed the sons of Aaron, the priests, to offer them up on the altar of the LORD.

**29:22** So they slaughtered the bulls, and the priests received the blood and dashed it against the altar. Then they slaughtered the rams and dashed the blood against the altar, and they slaughtered the lambs and dashed the blood against the altar.

**29:23** Next they brought the male goats for the sin offering forward before the king and the assembly, and they laid their hands upon them.

**29:24** The priests slaughtered them and offered their blood as a sin offering on the altar to make atonement for all Israel, because the king had ordered the burnt offering and the sin offering for all Israel.

**29:25** He stationed the Levites in the house of the LORD with cymbals, harps, and lyres, in keeping with the command of David, Gad the king's seer, and Nathan the prophet; indeed, the command was from the LORD by the hand of His prophets.

**29:26** So the Levites took their stand with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.

**29:27** Hezekiah then gave orders to offer up the burnt offering on the altar. At the moment the burnt offering began, the song of the LORD also began, joined by the trumpets and the accompaniment of the instruments of David king of Israel.

**29:28** The entire assembly was worshiping, the singers were singing, and the trumpets were sounding—all of this continued until the burnt offering was complete.

**29:29** When they had finished offering it up, the king and all those present with him knelt down and worshiped.

**29:30** King Hezekiah and the officials also told the Levites to praise the LORD using the words of David and of Asaph the seer. So they gave praise with gladness, and they bowed low and worshiped.

**29:31** Then Hezekiah spoke up and said, "Now you have dedicated yourselves to the LORD; come forward and bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the house of the LORD." So the assembly brought sacrifices and thank offerings, and everyone with a willing heart brought burnt offerings.

**29:32** The count of the burnt offerings that the assembly brought came to seventy bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs—all of these as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**29:33** As for the consecrated offerings, there were six hundred cattle and three thousand sheep.

**29:34** However, the priests were too few and could not skin all the burnt offerings, so their brothers the Levites supported them until the work was finished and until the priests had consecrated themselves, for the Levites were more upright of heart to consecrate themselves than the priests.

**29:35** There were also burnt offerings in abundance, together with the fat portions of the peace offerings and the drink offerings for the burnt offerings; so the service of the house of the LORD was established.

**29:36** Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced at what God had prepared for the people, for the matter had happened suddenly.

## Translator Notes

- **29:8** — Ketiv reads lezeva'ah while Qere reads leza'avah, metathesized forms of the same noun meaning 'an object of trembling or terror'; the translation follows the Qere.
- **29:13** — Ketiv reads Jeuel (Ye'u'el) while Qere reads Jeiel (Ye'i'el); the translation follows the Qere. The same Ketiv/Qere vowel shift appears in 29:14 (Ketiv Jehuel, Qere Jehiel), where the Qere is likewise followed.
- **29:24** — The verb waychatte'u is the Piel denominative of chata' ('offer as a sin offering / purify from sin'), taking 'their blood' as direct object with directional 'against/on the altar.'
- **29:28** — Ketiv reads mechatzetzerim (reduplicated participle) while Qere reads machtzerim (Hiphil participle of chatzar, 'sounding trumpets'); the translation follows the Qere. Both 'the song singing' and 'the trumpets sounding' use collective metonymy for the choir and trumpeters.
- **29:36** — Hebrew prefixes the definite article directly to a finite Hiphil perfect verb in hahekhin ('that which [God] had prepared'), a distinctive Late Biblical Hebrew relative construction common in Chronicles.
