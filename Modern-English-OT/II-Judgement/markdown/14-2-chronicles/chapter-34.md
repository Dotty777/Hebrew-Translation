## Text

**34:1** Josiah was eight years old when he began reigning, and for thirty-one years he reigned in Jerusalem.

**34:2** He did what was upright in the eyes of the LORD and walked in the ways of his father David, and he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**34:3** In the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a youth, he began to seek the God of his father David; and in the twelfth year he began to purify Judah and Jerusalem from the high places, the Asherah poles, the carved images, and the cast images.

**34:4** They tore down the altars of the Baals in his presence, and he chopped down the incense altars that stood high above them. He shattered the Asherah poles, the carved images, and the cast images, crushed them to powder, and scattered it over the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.

**34:5** He also burned the bones of priests upon their altars, and he purified Judah and Jerusalem.

**34:6** And in the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim, and Simeon, even as far as Naphtali, in their ruins on every side,

**34:7** he tore down the altars, pounded the Asherah poles and the carved images to powder, and chopped down all the incense altars throughout all the land of Israel. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

**34:8** In the eighteenth year of his reign, in purifying the land and the house, he sent Shaphan the son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the governor of the city, and Joah the son of Joahaz the recorder to repair the house of the LORD his God.

**34:9** They came to Hilkiah the high priest and handed over the money that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites, the keepers of the threshold, had collected from the hand of Manasseh and Ephraim, from all the remnant of Israel, and from all Judah and Benjamin; and they returned to Jerusalem.

**34:10** They put it into the hand of the workmen who had oversight in the house of the LORD, and the workmen who were working in the house of the LORD gave it to mend and repair the house.

**34:11** They gave it to the craftsmen and the builders to buy quarried stone and timber for binders, and to lay beams for the buildings that the kings of Judah had ruined.

**34:12** The men carried out the work faithfully. Over them were appointed Jahath and Obadiah, the Levites from the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam from the sons of the Kohathites, to supervise. And the Levites, all who were skilled with musical instruments,

**34:13** were over the burden-bearers and supervised everyone doing work for every kind of service; and some of the Levites were scribes, officers, and gatekeepers.

**34:14** While they were taking out the money that had been brought into the house of the LORD, Hilkiah the priest found the book of the law of the LORD given by the hand of Moses.

**34:15** Hilkiah spoke up and said to Shaphan the scribe, "I have found the book of the law in the house of the LORD." And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan.

**34:16** Shaphan brought the book to the king, and he also brought back word to the king, saying, "Everything that was placed into the hand of your servants, they are doing.

**34:17** They have poured out the money that was found in the house of the LORD and have put it into the hand of the overseers and into the hand of the workmen."

**34:18** Then Shaphan the scribe informed the king, saying, "Hilkiah the priest gave me a book." And Shaphan read from it in the presence of the king.

**34:19** Now when the king heard the words of the law, he tore his garments.

**34:20** Then the king commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam the son of Shaphan, Abdon the son of Micah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the servant of the king, saying,

**34:21** "Go, inquire of the LORD on my behalf and on behalf of those who remain in Israel and in Judah concerning the words of the book that was found; for great is the wrath of the LORD that has been poured out on us, because our fathers did not keep the word of the LORD, to act in accordance with all that is written in this book."

**34:22** So Hilkiah and those whom the king had sent went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of Tokhath, the son of Hasrah, keeper of the garments—now she lived in Jerusalem in the Second Quarter—and they spoke to her to this effect.

**34:23** She said to them, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Tell the man who sent you to me,

**34:24** 'This is what the LORD says: Look, I am bringing disaster upon this place and upon its inhabitants, all the curses written in the book that they read before the king of Judah.

**34:25** Because they have forsaken Me and have burned incense to other gods, in order to provoke Me to anger with all the works of their hands, My wrath will be poured out on this place and will not be quenched.'

**34:26** But to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the LORD, thus you are to say to him: 'This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: As for the words that you have heard,

**34:27** because your heart was tender and you humbled yourself before God when you heard His words against this place and against its inhabitants, and you humbled yourself before Me, tore your garments, and wept before Me, I also have heard, declares the LORD.

**34:28** Look, I will gather you to your fathers, and you will be gathered to your grave in peace; your eyes will not see all the disaster that I am bringing upon this place and upon its inhabitants.'" So they brought back word to the king.

**34:29** The king then sent out and gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**34:30** The king went up to the house of the LORD, together with all the men of Judah, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests, the Levites, and all the people, from the greatest to the least. And he read in their hearing all the words of the book of the covenant that had been found in the house of the LORD.

**34:31** Then the king stood in his place and made the covenant before the LORD, to walk after the LORD and to keep His commandments, His testimonies, and His statutes with all his heart and with all his soul, to carry out the words of the covenant that were written in this book.

**34:32** Moreover, he made all who were present in Jerusalem and Benjamin stand by it; and the inhabitants of Jerusalem acted according to the covenant of God, the God of their fathers.

**34:33** Josiah removed all the abominations from all the lands belonging to the sons of Israel, and he made all who were present in Israel serve the LORD their God. Throughout all his days they did not turn aside from following the LORD, the God of their fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **34:4** — The Hebrew term chammanim refers to incense altars or stands, here positioned atop the altars of the Baals. The closing phrase renders an elliptical Hebrew construction, literally 'the graves, those sacrificing to them.'
- **34:6** — The Qere reads a single word whose consonants are widely understood as 'in their ruins' (from chorbah), although the Masoretic patach vocalization formally matches 'with their axes' or 'swords' (from cherev, cf. Ezek 26:9). The Ketiv splits the letters into two words of uncertain sense.
- **34:9** — The Qere reads 'and they returned to Jerusalem' (Qal of shuv), referring to returning after gathering the funds, whereas the Ketiv reads 'and the inhabitants of Jerusalem' as an additional source of the collection.
- **34:22** — The phrase 'and those whom the king had sent' renders an elliptical Hebrew expression, literally 'and whom the king.' Also, the Qere spells Tokhath defectively while the Ketiv writes it plene with a waw.
- **34:25** — The Qere vocalizes 'burned incense' in the Piel stem, the standard stem in Chronicles for illicit offerings, while the Ketiv consonants indicate the Hiphil stem.
