## Text

**31:1** When all this was finished, all Israel who were present went out to the cities of Judah and shattered the standing pillars, chopped down the Asherah poles, and pulled down the high places and the altars out of all Judah and Benjamin, and in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they made a complete end of them. Then all the sons of Israel returned, each man to his holding, to their cities.

**31:2** Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and the Levites according to their divisions, each man according to his service, for both the priests and the Levites, for burnt offerings and for peace offerings, to minister, to give thanks, and to praise within the gates of the camps of the LORD.

**31:3** The king's portion from his own possessions was for the burnt offerings: the morning and evening burnt offerings, and the burnt offerings for the Sabbaths, for the new moons, and for the appointed feasts, as it is written in the law of the LORD.

**31:4** He also instructed the people, the residents of Jerusalem, to give the portion of the priests and the Levites, so that they might hold firmly to the law of the LORD.

**31:5** As soon as the word spread abroad, the sons of Israel abundantly supplied the firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and all the produce of the field, and they brought in the tithe of everything in abundance.

**31:6** The sons of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also brought in the tithe of cattle and sheep, and the tithe of the sacred things that had been consecrated to the LORD their God, and laid them down in heaps upon heaps.

**31:7** In the third month they began laying the foundation of the heaps, and in the seventh month they finished.

**31:8** Then Hezekiah and the officials arrived and saw the heaps, and they blessed the LORD and His people Israel.

**31:9** Hezekiah then inquired of the priests and the Levites regarding the heaps.

**31:10** Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, spoke to him and said, "Since they began bringing the contribution into the house of the LORD, we have eaten and been satisfied and left over in abundance; for the LORD has blessed His people, and this great store is what remains."

**31:11** Then Hezekiah gave orders to prepare chambers in the house of the LORD, and they prepared them.

**31:12** They faithfully brought in the contribution, the tithe, and the sacred things. Over them as chief overseer was Conaniah the Levite, and Shimei his brother was second in rank.

**31:13** Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were supervisors under the hand of Conaniah and Shimei his brother, by appointment of King Hezekiah and Azariah, the chief overseer of the house of God.

**31:14** Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the gatekeeper on the east side, was in charge of the freewill offerings to God, to distribute the contribution to the LORD and the most holy offerings.

**31:15** At his side were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah in the cities of the priests, faithfully distributing to their brothers by divisions, to great and small alike,

**31:16** apart from those of them enrolled by genealogy, males from three years old and upward, for everyone who entered the house of the LORD for each day's requirement, for their service in their duties according to their divisions.

**31:17** The genealogical enrollment of the priests was according to their fathers' houses, and that of the Levites from twenty years old and upward was in their duties by their divisions,

**31:18** including enrollment with all their little ones, their wives, their sons, and their daughters, for the entire assembly; for in their faithfulness they consecrated themselves in holiness.

**31:19** And for the sons of Aaron, the priests, in the pastureland fields of their cities, in city after city, there were men designated by name to distribute portions to every male among the priests and to everyone enrolled by genealogy among the Levites.

**31:20** This is what Hezekiah carried out across all Judah; he did what was good, upright, and true in the presence of the LORD his God.

**31:21** And in every endeavor that he began in the service of the house of God, and in the law and in the commandment, to seek his God, he acted with his whole heart and succeeded.

## Translator Notes

- **31:7** — The clause literally reads 'they began the heaps to lay a foundation' (leyissod, an unusual infinitive construct form of the verb yasad, 'to found/lay a base'), meaning they began piling up the base of the heaps of tithes.
- **31:10** — Three consecutive infinitives absolute ('akhol wesavoa' wehoter, 'eating and being satisfied and leaving over') function as finite verbs, a characteristic feature of Late Biblical Hebrew. The particle 'et before 'this great store' marks an emphasized nominative rather than a direct object.
- **31:12** — Here and in 31:13 the Ketiv writes the name with a waw (kwnnyhw), while the Qere omits the waw (konanyahu, vocalized with qamets qatan), rendered 'Conaniah.'
- **31:17** — Verses 16-18 use the Hithpael infinitive construct hityaches ('genealogical enrollment') nominally, introduced in verse 17 by the topicalizing particle 'et and continued in verse 18 by ulehityaches ('including enrollment').
