## Text

**25:1** Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years. His mother's name was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.

**25:2** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, only not with an undivided heart.

**25:3** Now when the kingdom had grown strong under him, he killed his servants who had struck down the king his father.

**25:4** Yet he did not put their children to death, but acted according to what is written in the law, in the book of Moses, where the LORD commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not die for children, and children shall not die for fathers; instead, each one shall die for his own sin."

**25:5** Amaziah gathered Judah together and stationed them according to fathers' houses, under commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, for all Judah and Benjamin. He mustered them from twenty years old and upward and found them to be three hundred thousand picked men able to go out to war, handling spear and large shield.

**25:6** He also hired from Israel one hundred thousand mighty warriors for one hundred talents of silver.

**25:7** But a man of God came to him, saying, "King, do not let the army of Israel go with you, for the LORD is not with Israel, with any of the sons of Ephraim.

**25:8** Even if you go, act, be strong for the battle; God will cause you to stumble before an enemy, for there is power in God to help and to cause stumbling."

**25:9** Amaziah said to the man of God, "And what is to be done about the hundred talents that I have given to the troop of Israel?" The man of God said, "The LORD can give you far more than this."

**25:10** So Amaziah separated them, the troop that had come to him from Ephraim, to go to their own place. Their anger burned intensely against Judah, and they returned to their own place in burning anger.

**25:11** Then Amaziah strengthened himself, led his people, went to the Valley of Salt, and struck down ten thousand of the sons of Seir.

**25:12** The sons of Judah also took ten thousand captive alive, brought them to the top of the cliff, and hurled them down from the top of the cliff, and all of them were smashed to pieces.

**25:13** Meanwhile, the men of the troop whom Amaziah had sent back from going with him to battle raided the cities of Judah from Samaria as far as Beth-horon; they struck down three thousand of them and took great plunder.

**25:14** Now after Amaziah returned from striking down the Edomites, he brought the gods of the sons of Seir and set them up for himself as gods; before them he would bow down, and to them he would burn incense.

**25:15** Then the anger of the LORD burned against Amaziah, and He sent a prophet to him, who said to him, "Why have you sought the gods of the people, which did not rescue their own people from your hand?"

**25:16** As he was speaking to him, the king said to him, "Have we appointed you a counselor to the king? Stop! Why should they strike you down?" So the prophet stopped and said, "I know that God has determined to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel."

**25:17** Then Amaziah king of Judah took counsel and sent to Joash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us look at each other face to face."

**25:18** Joash king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The thornbush that was in Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' Then a wild beast that was in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thornbush.

**25:19** You say, 'Look, you have struck down Edom,' and your heart has lifted you up to boast. Now stay in your house. Why should you provoke disaster and fall, you and Judah with you?"

**25:20** But Amaziah did not listen, for it was from God, in order to hand them over, because they had sought the gods of Edom.

**25:21** So Joash king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah looked at each other face to face at Beth-shemesh, which belongs to Judah.

**25:22** Judah was routed before Israel, and they fled, each man to his tents.

**25:23** Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh and brought him to Jerusalem; and he broke down four hundred cubits of the wall of Jerusalem, from the Ephraim Gate to the Turning Gate.

**25:24** He took all the gold and silver, all the vessels found in the house of God with Obed-edom, the treasures of the king's house, and the hostages, and returned to Samaria.

**25:25** Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.

**25:26** Now the rest of the deeds of Amaziah, the first and the last, see, are they not written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel?

**25:27** From the time that Amaziah turned away from following the LORD, they formed a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; but they sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.

**25:28** They carried him on horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **25:8** — The Masoretic Text lacks a negative particle before the Hiphil imperfect yakhshilkha ("will cause you to stumble"); the Qal imperatives ("go, act, be strong") are thus read concessively or ironically as a warning that Amaziah will still face defeat if Israel marches with him.
- **25:16** — The verse plays threefold on the root ya'ats ("counsel"): the Qal participle yo'ets ("counselor"), the Qal perfect ya'ats ("has determined"), and the noun atsati ("my counsel"), continuing in v. 17 with the Niphal vayyivva'ats ("took counsel").
- **25:23** — The Masoretic Text calls Amaziah's grandfather Jehoahaz (an alternate name for Ahaziah, as in 21:17 and 22:1) and names the second gate sha'ar ha-poneh (Qal participle of panah, literally "the Turning Gate"), corresponding to "the Corner Gate" (sha'ar ha-pinnah) in 26:9 and 2 Kings 14:13.
- **25:24** — The Masoretic Text omits an explicit finite verb ("he took") before the direct-object phrases marked by et, leaving it to be supplied from context (compare 2 Kings 14:14).
- **25:28** — The Masoretic Text reads "in the city of Judah" (be'ir Yehudah), whereas the parallel in 2 Kings 14:20 reads "in the city of David."
