## Text

**6:1** Then Solomon said, "The LORD said He would dwell in the dense darkness.

**6:2** But I have built an exalted house for You, and an established place for Your dwelling forever."

**6:3** Then the king turned his face around and blessed the entire assembly of Israel, while the entire assembly of Israel was standing.

**6:4** And he said, "Blessed be the LORD, God of Israel, who with His mouth spoke to David my father and with His hands has fulfilled it, saying,

**6:5** 'From the day I brought My people out from the land of Egypt, I did not choose a city from all the tribes of Israel to build a house for My name to be there, and I did not choose a man to be ruler over My people Israel;

**6:6** but I chose Jerusalem for My name to be there, and I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**6:7** And it was on the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the LORD, God of Israel.

**6:8** And the LORD said to David my father, 'Since it was on your heart to build a house for My name, you have done well that it was on your heart.

**6:9** However, you shall not build the house; instead, your son who comes from your loins, he will build the house for My name.'

**6:10** And the LORD has established His word that He spoke, and I have risen in place of David my father and sit upon the throne of Israel, just as the LORD spoke, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, God of Israel.

**6:11** And I have placed the ark there, in which is the covenant of the LORD that He made with the sons of Israel."

**6:12** Then he stood before the altar of the LORD, facing the entire assembly of Israel, and spread out his palms.

**6:13** For Solomon had made a bronze platform and placed it in the middle of the court, five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high; and he stood on it, knelt on his knees facing the entire assembly of Israel, and spread out his palms toward heaven.

**6:14** And he said, "LORD, God of Israel, there is no god like You in heaven and on earth, keeping the covenant and faithful love for Your servants who walk before You with all their heart,

**6:15** who have kept for Your servant David my father what You spoke to him; and You spoke with Your mouth and with Your hand have fulfilled it, as on this day.

**6:16** And now, LORD, God of Israel, keep for Your servant David my father what You spoke to him, saying, 'You shall not have a man cut off from before Me to sit on the throne of Israel, only if your sons keep their way to walk in My law just as you have walked before Me.'

**6:17** And now, LORD, God of Israel, may Your word that You spoke to Your servant David be proved true.

**6:18** Yet will God truly dwell with mankind upon the earth? Look, heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain You, how much less this house that I have built!

**6:19** Still, turn toward the prayer of Your servant and to his supplication, LORD my God, to listen to the cry and to the prayer that Your servant is praying before You,

**6:20** so that Your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place where You said You would place Your name, to listen to the prayer that Your servant prays toward this place.

**6:21** And listen to the supplications of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward this place; may You hear from the place of Your dwelling, from heaven, and as You hear, forgive.

**6:22** If a man sins against his neighbor and an oath is imposed on him to make him swear, and he comes under oath before Your altar in this house,

**6:23** then may You hear from heaven, and act and judge Your servants, repaying the wicked by putting his way upon his head and vindicating the righteous by giving to him according to his righteousness.

**6:24** And if Your people Israel are struck down before an enemy because they sin against You, and they turn back and confess Your name, and pray and plead for mercy before You in this house,

**6:25** then may You hear from heaven, forgive the sin of Your people Israel, and return them to the land that You gave to them and to their fathers.

**6:26** When the heavens are shut and there is no rain because they sin against You, and they pray toward this place and confess Your name, and turn back from their sin because You afflict them,

**6:27** then may You hear in heaven and forgive the sin of Your servants and of Your people Israel, as You instruct them in the good way in which they should walk, and give rain upon Your land that You gave to Your people as an inheritance.

**6:28** If famine occurs in the land, if pestilence occurs, if blight and mildew, locust and caterpillar occur, if their enemies besiege them in the land of their gates, whatever plague and whatever sickness there is,

**6:29** whatever prayer, whatever supplication comes from any person or from all Your people Israel, as each knows his own affliction and his own pain and spreads out his palms toward this house,

**6:30** then may You hear from heaven, Your established dwelling place, and forgive, and give to each person according to all his ways, whose heart You know—for You alone know the heart of the sons of mankind—

**6:31** so that they may fear You, to walk in Your ways all the days that they live upon the face of the land that You gave to our fathers.

**6:32** And concerning the foreigner as well, who is not from Your people Israel, when he comes from a distant land because of Your great name and Your strong hand and Your outstretched arm—when they come and pray toward this house,

**6:33** then may You hear from heaven, from Your established dwelling place, and act according to everything for which the foreigner calls to You, so that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name, to fear You as Your people Israel do, and to know that Your name is called upon this house that I have built.

**6:34** If Your people go out to war against their enemies by the way on which You send them, and they pray to You toward this city that You have chosen and the house that I have built for Your name,

**6:35** then hear their prayer and their supplication from heaven, and uphold their cause.

**6:36** If they sin against You—for there is no person who does not sin—and You become angry with them and give them over before an enemy, so that their captors take them captive to a land far away or near,

**6:37** and if they take it to heart in the land where they were taken captive, and turn back and plead for mercy to You in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have acted perversely, and we have done wickedly,'

**6:38** if they turn back to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity where they were led captive, and pray in the direction of their land that You gave to their fathers, and the city that You chose, and toward the house that I built for Your name,

**6:39** then hear from heaven, from Your established dwelling place, their prayer and their supplications, and uphold their cause, and forgive Your people who have sinned against You.

**6:40** Now, my God, please let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to the prayer of this place.

**6:41** And now arise, LORD God, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength; may Your priests, LORD God, be clothed with salvation, and may Your faithful ones rejoice in what is good.

**6:42** LORD God, do not turn away the face of Your anointed one; remember the acts of faithful love of David Your servant."

## Translator Notes

- **6:13** — The Hebrew noun kiyyor normally denotes a bronze basin or laver, but here uniquely refers to a raised bronze platform or dais measuring five cubits square and three cubits high.
- **6:22** — Hebrew nasa' is an alternate spelling of nasa ('impose, lift'), used here impersonally ('an oath is imposed'). The following clause uva' 'alah is grammatically difficult and can be understood either as 'he comes under oath' (or 'comes and swears') or as 'the oath comes' before the altar.
- **6:26** — The pointed form ta'anem can be parsed either as Hiphil of the root meaning 'be afflicted' ('because You afflict them') or as Qal of 'answer' ('because You answer them'); the context of disciplinary drought favors 'afflict.'
- **6:42** — The plural construct hasde Dawid can be read objectively as 'the acts of faithful love promised to David' (cf. Isa 55:3) or subjectively as 'the faithful deeds of David' (cf. 2 Chr 32:32; 35:26).
