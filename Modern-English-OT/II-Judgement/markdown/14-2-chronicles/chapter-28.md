## Text

**28:1** Ahaz was twenty years old when he began reigning, and for sixteen years he reigned in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, like David his father.

**28:2** Instead, he walked in the ways of the kings of Israel; he also made cast images for the Baals.

**28:3** Furthermore, he burned incense in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom and burned his sons in the fire, in accordance with the abominations of the nations whom the LORD had dispossessed from before the sons of Israel.

**28:4** He sacrificed and burned incense at the high places, on the hills, and beneath every lush tree.

**28:5** So the LORD his God gave him into the hand of the king of Aram; they struck him, took a large body of captives from him, and brought them to Damascus. He was also given into the hand of the king of Israel, who struck him with a great blow.

**28:6** Pekah son of Remaliah killed one hundred twenty thousand in Judah in a single day, all of them valiant men, because they had abandoned the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**28:7** And Zichri, a mighty warrior of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the governor of the palace, and Elkanah, second in rank to the king.

**28:8** The sons of Israel took captive from their brothers two hundred thousand women, sons, and daughters; they also plundered abundant spoil from them and brought the spoil to Samaria.

**28:9** Now a prophet of the LORD was there whose name was Oded, and he went out before the army coming to Samaria and said to them, "Look, in the wrath of the LORD, the God of your fathers, against Judah, He gave them into your hand, yet you killed them in a fury that has reached up to the heavens.

**28:10** And now you intend to subjugate the children of Judah and Jerusalem as male and female slaves for yourselves. Yet do not even you yourselves have acts of guilt against the LORD your God?

**28:11** Now then, listen to me and send back the captives whom you captured from your brothers, for the fierce anger of the LORD is upon you."

**28:12** Then certain men among the heads of the sons of Ephraim—Azariah son of Jehohanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai—rose up against those coming from the campaign.

**28:13** They said to them, "You must not bring the captives here, because you are intending to bring guilt against the LORD upon us, to add to our sins and to our guilt; for our guilt is great, and fierce anger is against Israel."

**28:14** So the armed troops left the captives and the plunder before the officials and the entire assembly.

**28:15** Then the men who were designated by name stood up and took charge of the captives. From the spoil they clothed all the naked among them—they clothed them, put sandals on them, fed them, gave them drink, anointed them, led every feeble one on donkeys, and brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their brothers. Then they returned to Samaria.

**28:16** At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria to help him.

**28:17** For again the Edomites had come, struck Judah, and carried off captives.

**28:18** And the Philistines raided the cities of the Shephelah and the Negev of Judah; they captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco and its villages, Timnah and its villages, and Gimzo and its villages, and they settled there.

**28:19** For the LORD humbled Judah on account of Ahaz king of Israel, because he had cast off restraint in Judah and had acted with grave unfaithfulness against the LORD.

**28:20** So Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria came against him; he brought distress upon him and did not strengthen him.

**28:21** For Ahaz took a share from the house of the LORD, and from the house of the king and of the officials, and gave it to the king of Assyria, yet it was of no help to him.

**28:22** And in the time of his distress he acted even more unfaithfully against the LORD—he, King Ahaz.

**28:23** He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus that had struck him, and he said, "Because the gods of the kings of Aram are the ones who help them, to them I will sacrifice so that they will help me." Yet they became the cause of downfall for him and for all Israel.

**28:24** Ahaz gathered the utensils of the house of God, cut the utensils of the house of God into pieces, shut the doors of the house of the LORD, and made altars for himself at every corner in Jerusalem.

**28:25** And in every single city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, and he provoked the LORD, the God of his fathers, to anger.

**28:26** As for the rest of his acts and all his ways, the first and the last, see, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel.

**28:27** Then Ahaz lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem, because they did not bring him to the tombs of the kings of Israel; and his son Hezekiah became king in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — The Hebrew text reads wayyab'er (Hiphil of ba'ar, 'burned'), stating directly that Ahaz burned his sons in the fire, rather than the idiom 'made pass through the fire' found in 2 Kings 16:3.
- **28:16** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads the plural 'kings of Assyria' (malke Ashshur), even though verse 20 names the single king Tilgath-pilneser; the plural is retained here without harmonizing to the ancient versions.
- **28:19** — The Hebrew calls Ahaz 'king of Israel' (and refers to 'the kings of Israel' in 28:27), reflecting Chronicles' regular practice of applying the covenant name 'Israel' to the Davidic kingdom of Judah.
- **28:20** — Tilgath-pilneser preserves the Chronicles spelling (Tillegath-pilne'eser) of Tiglath-pileser. The verb chazaqo (Qal of chazaq with an object suffix) functions transitively here in the sense 'strengthened him,' contrasting with 'brought distress upon him.'
