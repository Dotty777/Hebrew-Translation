## Text

**8:1** And it happened at the end of twenty years, during which Solomon had built the house of the LORD and his own house,

**8:2** that Solomon built up the cities that Huram had given to Solomon, and he settled the sons of Israel there.

**8:3** Then Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and prevailed against it.

**8:4** And he built Tadmor in the wilderness, along with all the storehouse cities that he built in Hamath.

**8:5** He built Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon as fortified cities with walls, double doors, and bars,

**8:6** as well as Baalath, all the storehouse cities that belonged to Solomon, all the cities for the chariots, the cities for the horsemen, and all that Solomon longed to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and throughout the whole land of his rule.

**8:7** As for all the people who remained from the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not from Israel,

**8:8** from their descendants who remained after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel had not wiped out, Solomon conscripted them for forced labor up to this day.

**8:9** Yet from the sons of Israel, Solomon did not appoint any as slaves for his work; instead, they were men of war, commanders of his captains, and commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.

**8:10** And these were the chiefs of the appointed officers belonging to King Solomon, two hundred fifty, who held authority over the people.

**8:11** Then Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David to the house he had built for her, for he said, "A wife of mine will not dwell in the house of David, king of Israel, because those places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy."

**8:12** Then Solomon offered up burnt offerings to the LORD upon the altar of the LORD, which he had built in front of the portico,

**8:13** according to each day's requirement, offering up in keeping with the command of Moses for the Sabbaths, for the new moons, and for the appointed festivals three times a year: at the Festival of Unleavened Bread, at the Festival of Weeks, and at the Festival of Booths.

**8:14** Following the regulation of his father David, he stationed the divisions of the priests at their service, and the Levites at their posts to praise and to minister before the priests according to each day's requirement, and the gatekeepers in their divisions at every gate; for such was the command of David, the man of God.

**8:15** And they did not turn aside from the command of the king concerning the priests and the Levites in any matter or regarding the treasuries.

**8:16** Thus all the work of Solomon was set in order, up to the day of the founding of the house of the LORD and until its completion; the house of the LORD was finished.

**8:17** Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth along the shore of the sea in the land of Edom.

**8:18** And Huram sent him ships by the hand of his servants, together with servants who knew the sea; they went with the servants of Solomon to Ophir, took four hundred fifty talents of gold from there, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **8:10** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the Qere reads han-nitsavim (Niphal masculine plural participle of natsav, "the appointed/stationed officers"), while the Ketiv reads han-netsivim (plural noun, "the deputies/prefects"). Both denote administrative overseers.
- **8:11** — The masculine plural pronoun hemmah ("they") refers contextually to the places or precincts of David's house, and aron ("ark") is here construed as feminine with the 3fs Qal perfect ba'ah ("has come").
- **8:16** — Literally "up to the day of the founding of the house of the LORD and until its completion," using ad ... ve-ad to span both the preparatory work leading up to foundation day and construction through completion; some versions render the first ad as "from."
- **8:18** — Orthographic Ketiv/Qere variant: the Qere reads the standard spelling oniyyot ("ships"), while the Ketiv has a plene spelling with an extra vav; the meaning is identical.
