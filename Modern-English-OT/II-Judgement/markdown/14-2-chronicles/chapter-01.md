## Text

**1:1** Solomon son of David established himself strongly over his kingdom, and the LORD his God was with him and magnified him exceedingly.

**1:2** Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, to the judges, and to every leader of all Israel, the heads of the fathers' families.

**1:3** So Solomon and the whole assembly with him went to the high place in Gibeon, because there was the tent of meeting of God, which Moses, the LORD's servant, had made in the wilderness.

**1:4** However, David had brought the ark of God up from Kiriath-jearim to the place David had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.

**1:5** And the bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, had made was there in front of the tabernacle of the LORD, and Solomon and the assembly sought it.

**1:6** Solomon went up there to the bronze altar before the LORD, which belonged to the tent of meeting, and he offered up a thousand burnt offerings on it.

**1:7** In that night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, "Ask what I should give you."

**1:8** Solomon said to God, "You have shown great loyal love to my father David and have made me king in his place.

**1:9** Now, LORD God, let Your word to my father David be confirmed, for You have made me king over a people as numerous as the dust of the earth.

**1:10** Now give me wisdom and knowledge, so that I may go out before this people and come in; for who can judge this great people of Yours?"

**1:11** God said to Solomon, "Because this was in your heart, and you did not ask for riches, possessions, or honor, nor the life of those who hate you, and you did not even ask for many days, but asked for wisdom and knowledge for yourself so that you may judge My people over whom I have made you king,

**1:12** wisdom and knowledge are granted to you. And I will give you riches, possessions, and honor such as none of the kings who were before you had, and after you there will be none like it."

**1:13** Then Solomon came to Jerusalem from before the tent of meeting at the high place that was in Gibeon, and he reigned over Israel.

**1:14** Solomon assembled chariots and horsemen; he had one thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.

**1:15** The king made silver and gold like stones in Jerusalem, and he made cedars like the sycamore trees in the lowland in abundance.

**1:16** The import of Solomon's horses was from Egypt and from Kue; the king's merchants would acquire them from Kue for a price.

**1:17** They brought up and exported from Egypt a chariot for six hundred shekels of silver and a horse for one hundred fifty, and in the same way they exported them by their hand for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.

**1:18** Now Solomon resolved to build a house for the name of the LORD and a house for his kingdom.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The form בַּהֵכִין combines the preposition בְּ with the article הַ functioning as a relative marker before the Hiphil perfect הֵכִין (a characteristic Late Biblical Hebrew construction), with an unexpressed antecedent ("the place").
- **1:5** — MT vocalizes שָׁם ("there") rather than שָׂם ("he placed," as in some ancient versions). The masculine singular suffix on וַיִּדְרְשֵׁהוּ ("sought it") refers to the bronze altar or tabernacle as a place of worship (cf. Deut 12:5; 1 Chr 13:3), though it can also refer to the LORD ("sought Him").
- **1:13** — MT reads לַבָּמָה ("to/at the high place," echoing v. 3) before "Jerusalem, from before the tent of meeting," whereas LXX and Vulgate read "from the high place." Here לְ is rendered locatively ("at") in relation to the tent of meeting.
- **1:16** — מִקְוֵא is understood as מִן־קְוֵא ("from Kue," ancient Cilicia in southeastern Anatolia), paralleling Egypt; the traditional Masoretic accentuation and older interpretations took מִקְוֵא as a "drove" or "company."
