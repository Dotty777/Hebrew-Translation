## Text

**7:1** When Solomon had finished praying, fire descended from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the LORD filled the house.

**7:2** And the priests were unable to enter the house of the LORD, because the glory of the LORD had filled the house of the LORD.

**7:3** And all the sons of Israel were watching as the fire descended and the glory of the LORD was upon the house; and they knelt with their faces to the ground on the pavement, worshiped, and gave thanks to the LORD, "For He is good, for His faithful love endures forever."

**7:4** And the king and all the people were offering sacrifice before the LORD.

**7:5** King Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty-two thousand cattle and one hundred and twenty thousand sheep and goats; and the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.

**7:6** The priests stood at their posts, and the Levites as well, with the instruments of song of the LORD that King David had made to give thanks to the LORD—"For His faithful love endures forever"—whenever David offered praise through their hands; and the priests blew trumpets opposite them, while all Israel was standing.

**7:7** And Solomon consecrated the center of the courtyard in front of the house of the LORD, for he presented the burnt offerings and the fat portions of the peace offerings there, because the bronze altar that Solomon had made could not hold the burnt offering, the grain offering, and the fat portions.

**7:8** And Solomon celebrated the festival at that time for seven days, and all Israel with him, a very large assembly, from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt.

**7:9** And on the eighth day they observed a solemn assembly, because they had kept the dedication of the altar for seven days and the festival for seven days.

**7:10** And on the twenty-third day of the seventh month he dismissed the people to their tents, joyful and cheerful of heart over the goodness that the LORD had done for David and for Solomon and for Israel His people.

**7:11** So Solomon finished the house of the LORD and the house of the king; and all that came upon Solomon's heart to do in the house of the LORD and in his own house, he carried out with success.

**7:12** And the LORD appeared to Solomon by night and said to him, "I have heard your prayer, and I have chosen this place for Myself to be a house of sacrifice.

**7:13** If I shut the heavens and there is no rain, and if I command the locust to consume the land, or if I send pestilence among My people,

**7:14** and My people, upon whom My name is called, humble themselves and pray, and seek My face and turn back from their evil ways, then I Myself will hear from heaven, and I will forgive their sin and heal their land.

**7:15** Now My eyes will be open and My ears attentive to the prayer of this place.

**7:16** And now I have chosen and consecrated this house for My name to be there forever, and My eyes and My heart will be there always.

**7:17** And as for you, if you walk before Me just as David your father walked, and act according to all that I have commanded you, and keep My statutes and My judgments,

**7:18** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom, just as I covenanted with David your father, saying, 'You shall not have a man cut off to rule in Israel.'

**7:19** But if you yourselves turn away and forsake My statutes and My commandments that I have placed before you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**7:20** then I will uproot them from My land that I gave to them; and this house that I have consecrated for My name I will cast out from My presence, and I will make it a proverb and a taunt among all the peoples.

**7:21** And as for this house, which was exalted, everyone who passes by it will be appalled and will say, 'Why has the LORD done like this to this land and to this house?'

**7:22** And they will say, 'Because they abandoned the LORD, the God of their fathers, who brought them out from the land of Egypt, and took hold of other gods and bowed down to them and served them; therefore He has brought all this calamity upon them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — Hebrew wehodot ('and gave thanks') is a Hiphil infinitive of the root ydh continuing the preceding narrative verb chain, a syntactic feature common in Late Biblical Hebrew.
- **7:6** — Ketiv reads a reduplicated participial form while Qere reads the Hiphil participle mahtserim from the denominative verb meaning 'sound trumpets'; both convey the same sense ('blew trumpets'), and the Qere is followed.
- **7:18** — Hebrew karatti ('I covenanted,' literally 'I cut') omits its usual object 'covenant' and forms a wordplay with the following Niphal lo'-yikkaret ('shall not be cut off').
- **7:20** — The Masoretic Text shifts from second-person plural address in verse 19 ('you yourselves') to third-person plural here ('uproot them ... gave to them'), a reading preserved directly from the Hebrew source.
