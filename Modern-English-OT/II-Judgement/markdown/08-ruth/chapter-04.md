## Text

**4:1** Meanwhile, Boaz went up to the gate and sat there, and look, the redeemer about whom Boaz had spoken was passing by. He said, "Turn aside, sit down here, so-and-so." So he turned aside and sat down.

**4:2** Then he took ten men from among the elders of the city and said, "Sit here." And they sat down.

**4:3** Then he said to the redeemer, "Naomi, who returned from the territory of Moab, is selling the parcel of the field that belonged to our brother Elimelech.

**4:4** And I thought I would disclose this to you, saying, 'Acquire it before those sitting here and before the elders of my people.' If you will redeem it, redeem it; and if it will not be redeemed, tell me so that I may know, for there is no one apart from you to redeem it, and I am after you." He said, "I myself will redeem it."

**4:5** Then Boaz said, "On the day you acquire the field from the hand of Naomi, you also acquire it from Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the dead man, to raise up the name of the dead man upon his inheritance."

**4:6** Then the redeemer said, "I cannot redeem it for myself, or I might ruin my inheritance. You redeem my right of redemption for yourself, for I cannot redeem it."

**4:7** Now this was done formerly in Israel concerning redemption and concerning exchange, to ratify any matter: a man would pull off his sandal and give it to his neighbor; and this was the attestation in Israel.

**4:8** So the redeemer said to Boaz, "Acquire it for yourself." And he pulled off his sandal.

**4:9** Then Boaz said to the elders and to all the people, "You are witnesses today that I have acquired everything that belonged to Elimelech, and everything that belonged to Chilion and Mahlon, from the hand of Naomi.

**4:10** And I have also acquired Ruth the Moabite, the wife of Mahlon, for myself as a wife, to raise up the name of the dead man upon his inheritance, so that the name of the dead man will not be cut off from among his brothers and from the gate of his place. You are witnesses today."

**4:11** And all the people in the gate and the elders said, "We are witnesses. May the LORD make the woman entering your house like Rachel and like Leah, the two of whom built the house of Israel. Prosper in Ephrathah and make a name in Bethlehem,

**4:12** and may your house become like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, from the offspring the LORD will give you from this young woman."

**4:13** Then Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He went in to her, and the LORD granted her conception, and she gave birth to a son.

**4:14** And the women said to Naomi, "Blessed be the LORD, who has not deprived you of a redeemer today; and may his name be proclaimed in Israel.

**4:15** He will be a restorer of life to you and will sustain your old age, for your daughter-in-law, who loves you, has borne him—she who is better to you than seven sons."

**4:16** And Naomi took the child, placed him in her lap, and became his caregiver.

**4:17** And the neighboring women gave him a name, saying, "A son has been born to Naomi!" And they called his name Obed. He is the father of Jesse, the father of David.

**4:18** Now these are the generations of Perez: Perez became the father of Hezron.

**4:19** And Hezron became the father of Ram, and Ram became the father of Amminadab.

**4:20** And Amminadab became the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon became the father of Salmah.

**4:21** And Salmon became the father of Boaz, and Boaz became the father of Obed.

**4:22** And Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — Hebrew peloni almoni is an idiom for an unnamed person ("such-and-such a one"), deliberately withholding the redeemer's actual name.
- **4:3** — Hebrew makrah (Qal perfect of mkr, literally "has sold") denotes a resolved or imminent transaction, hence "is selling" (compare v. 5, where the field is still acquired from Naomi's hand).
- **4:4** — "Disclose this to you" renders the Hebrew idiom "uncover your ear." The MT reads 3ms Qal yig'al (rendered impersonally, "if it will not be redeemed") after 2ms tig'al, and the Qere reads cohortative we'ed'ah ("so that I may know") against Ketiv w'd'.
- **4:5** — Follows the Masoretic Qere (qanita, "you acquire") and cantillation (atnah on Naomi, keeping "Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the dead man" as a single appositional phrase governed by u-me'et, "and from"); the Ketiv reads qaniti ("I acquire"), and acquiring from Ruth entails taking her in levirate marriage (cf. 4:10).
- **4:20** — The MT spells this name Salmah here (compare "Salma" in 1 Chr 2:11) and Salmon in 4:21.
