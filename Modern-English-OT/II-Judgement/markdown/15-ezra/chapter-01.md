## Text

**1:1** In the first year of Cyrus, king of Persia, so that the word of the LORD from the mouth of Jeremiah would be completed, the LORD roused the spirit of Cyrus, king of Persia, and he had a proclamation go out throughout all his kingdom, and in writing as well, saying,

**1:2** "This is what Cyrus, king of Persia, says: All the kingdoms of the earth the LORD, the God of heaven, has given to me, and He has commissioned me to build a house for Him in Jerusalem, which is in Judah.

**1:3** Anyone among you from all His people—may his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and let him build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel; He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

**1:4** And every survivor from all the places where he sojourns—let the men of his place support him with silver and with gold, with goods and with livestock, along with the freewill offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem."

**1:5** And the heads of the ancestral houses of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and the Levites, arose—everyone whose spirit God had roused—to go up to build the house of the LORD that is in Jerusalem.

**1:6** And all those around them strengthened their hands with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, with livestock, and with costly gifts, in addition to everything that was offered freely.

**1:7** And King Cyrus brought out the vessels of the house of the LORD, which Nebuchadnezzar had brought out from Jerusalem and placed in the house of his gods.

**1:8** Cyrus, king of Persia, brought them out through the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and he counted them over to Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah.

**1:9** And this was their count: thirty gold basins, one thousand silver basins, twenty-nine knives;

**1:10** thirty gold bowls, four hundred ten secondary silver bowls, and one thousand other vessels.

**1:11** All the vessels of gold and of silver totaled five thousand four hundred. Sheshbazzar brought up all of them when the exiled community was brought up from Babylon to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — "support him" translates the Piel of nasa (to lift up), which in Late Biblical Hebrew means to uphold, assist, or supply with material aid. "every survivor" renders the Niphal participle ha-nishar ("the one remaining"), used in post-exilic Hebrew for the surviving remnant of the exile.
- **1:6** — "in addition to everything that was offered freely" renders levad al-kol-hitnaddev, where the Hithpael perfect of nadav ("to volunteer, give freely") functions as an asyndetic relative clause after kol with impersonal passive force.
- **1:9** — Both agartal ("basin") and mahalafim ("knives") occur only here in the Hebrew Bible. Mahalafim is commonly connected with the root halaf ("to pierce, pass through") and Mishnaic Hebrew mahalaf ("slaughtering knife"), though some relate it to the sense "exchange" as "duplicates" or "replacement vessels."
- **1:10** — "secondary silver bowls" translates kefore kesef mishnim, where mishnim (plural of mishneh, "second" or "double") refers to bowls of a second sort or grade, or possibly double or matching bowls.
- **1:11** — The itemized counts in verses 9-10 total 2,499, whereas the Masoretic Text states 5,400 here, perhaps indicating that only the principal items were listed above or reflecting an early numerical divergence in transmission. The Hiphil he'elah ("brought up") pairs with the Niphal infinitive construct he'alot ("was brought up") from the same root alah.
