## Text

**3:1** When the seventh month arrived and the sons of Israel were in the cities, the people assembled as one man to Jerusalem.

**3:2** Then Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers the priests, together with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his brothers, rose up and built the altar of the God of Israel to offer up burnt offerings on it, as is written in the law of Moses, the man of God.

**3:3** They established the altar upon its bases, for dread was upon them from the peoples of the lands, and they offered up burnt offerings on it to the LORD, burnt offerings for the morning and for the evening.

**3:4** They observed the Feast of Booths as is written, and offered the burnt offering of each day by number, according to the ordinance, each day's portion on its day,

**3:5** and after that the continual burnt offering, and those for the new moons and for all the consecrated appointed times of the LORD, and for everyone who freely offered a freewill offering to the LORD.

**3:6** From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer up burnt offerings to the LORD, yet the temple of the LORD had not been founded.

**3:7** And they gave silver to the stonecutters and the craftsmen, and food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and the Tyrians, to bring cedar timbers from Lebanon to the sea at Joppa, according to the authorization of Cyrus king of Persia for them.

**3:8** In the second year of their coming to the house of God in Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak, with the rest of their brothers the priests and the Levites and all who had come from the captivity to Jerusalem, made a start; and they appointed the Levites from twenty years old and upward to direct the work of the house of the LORD.

**3:9** Then Jeshua, with his sons and his brothers, and Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, stood as one to direct those doing the work in the house of God, together with the sons of Henadad, their sons and their brothers, the Levites.

**3:10** When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, they stationed the priests, robed, with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise the LORD according to the order of David king of Israel.

**3:11** And they sang responsively in praising and giving thanks to the LORD, "For He is good, for His loyal love is forever upon Israel." And all the people gave a great shout in praising the LORD because the house of the LORD had been founded.

**3:12** Yet many of the priests and the Levites and the heads of the fathers' houses, the old men who had seen the first house, were weeping with a loud voice when this house was founded before their eyes, while many lifted up their voice with shouting in joy,

**3:13** so that the people could not discern the sound of the shout of joy from the sound of the weeping of the people, because the people were raising a great shout, and the sound was heard far off.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The clause rendered 'for dread was upon them' literally reads 'for in dread upon them' (Hebrew be'emah, a predicative use of the preposition beth). The Qere reads plural wayya'alu ('and they offered up', Hiphil of 'alah), whereas the Ketiv has singular wy'l ('and he offered up').
- **3:10** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes wayya'amidu as a transitive Hiphil ('they stationed', from 'amad) rather than an intransitive Qal ('the priests stood'). The phrase 'according to the order of' is literally 'by the hands of' ('al-yede).
- **3:12** — The clause 'when this house was founded before their eyes' translates a proleptic pronominal suffix on the Qal infinitive construct beyosdo (literally 'at its founding, this house, in their eyes'), a construction characteristic of Late Biblical Hebrew.
