## Text

**7:1** Now after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah,

**7:2** son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub,

**7:3** son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth,

**7:4** son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki,

**7:5** son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest,

**7:6** this Ezra went up from Babylon; he was a skilled scribe in the law of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given. And the king gave him his entire request, since the hand of the LORD his God was upon him.

**7:7** And some of the sons of Israel and some of the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers, and the temple servants went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

**7:8** And he came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, which was the seventh year of the king.

**7:9** For on the first day of the first month was the beginning of the journey up from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month he came to Jerusalem, since the good hand of his God was upon him.

**7:10** For Ezra had directed his heart to search the law of the LORD and to do it, and to teach statute and ordinance in Israel.

**7:11** Now this is the copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe—a scribe of the words of the commandments of the LORD and His statutes for Israel:

**7:12** "Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven: full greetings. And now,

**7:13** a decree is issued by me that anyone in my kingdom from the people of Israel, and its priests and the Levites, who volunteers to go to Jerusalem may go with you.

**7:14** For you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to inquire concerning Judah and Jerusalem according to the law of your God which is in your hand,

**7:15** and to bring silver and gold which the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem,

**7:16** and all the silver and gold that you find in all the province of Babylon, along with the freewill offering of the people and the priests, freely offered for the house of their God which is in Jerusalem.

**7:17** Therefore, with full diligence you shall buy with this money bulls, rams, and lambs, together with their grain offerings and their drink offerings, and you shall offer them upon the altar of the house of your God which is in Jerusalem.

**7:18** And whatever seems good to you and to your brothers to do with the rest of the silver and gold, you may do according to the will of your God.

**7:19** And the vessels that are given to you for the service of the house of your God, deliver in full before the God of Jerusalem.

**7:20** And the rest of what is needed for the house of your God, which it falls to you to provide, you may provide from the king's treasure house.

**7:21** And by me, even I, King Artaxerxes, a decree is issued to all the treasurers who are beyond the River, that whatever Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven, asks of you shall be done with full diligence,

**7:22** up to one hundred talents of silver, up to one hundred kors of wheat, up to one hundred baths of wine, up to one hundred baths of oil, and salt without a prescribed limit.

**7:23** Whatever is by the decree of the God of heaven, let it be done zealously for the house of the God of heaven, for why should there be wrath against the kingdom of the king and his sons?

**7:24** We also make known to you that it is not permitted to impose tribute, tax, or toll upon any of the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers, the temple servants, or the attendants of this house of God.

**7:25** And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God which is in your hand, appoint magistrates and judges who shall judge all the people who are beyond the River—all who know the laws of your God—and you shall instruct anyone who does not know them.

**7:26** And anyone who will not observe the law of your God and the law of the king, let judgment be strictly executed upon him, whether for death, or for banishment, or for confiscation of property, or for imprisonment."

**7:27** Blessed be the LORD, the God of our fathers, who placed something like this in the king's heart, to beautify the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem,

**7:28** and who extended steadfast love to me before the king and his counselors and before all the king's mighty officials. And I gained strength, since the hand of the LORD my God was upon me, and I gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.

## Translator Notes

- **7:9** — Hebrew yesud is a rare construct noun from the root ysd ("found, establish"), literally "the beginning of the ascent/going up."
- **7:12** — Aramaic gemir (Peil passive participle of gmr, "complete") is widely understood as an abbreviated epistolary salutation ("full greetings" or "complete peace"), though it may alternatively mean "accomplished" modifying "scribe."
- **7:18** — Following the Qere forms 'alak ("to you") and 'ehak ("your brothers"); the Ketiv writes both with an older medial yod ('lyk, 'hyk) without change in sense.
- **7:25** — Following the Qere dayenin ("judging", Peal active participle of dyn); the Ketiv writes d'nyn with an aleph glide, a standard Aramaic hollow-verb spelling.
- **7:26** — Following the Qere lishroshi (Ketiv lshrshw), an abstract noun from the root shrsh ("uproot") denoting "uprooting" or "banishment" from the community.
