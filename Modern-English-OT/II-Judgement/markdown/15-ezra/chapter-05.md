## Text

**5:1** Now Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo, the prophets, prophesied to the Jews who were in Judah and in Jerusalem in the name of the God of Israel, who was over them.

**5:2** At that time Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak rose up and began to build the house of God that is in Jerusalem; and with them were the prophets of God, assisting them.

**5:3** At that very time Tattenai, governor of Beyond the River, and Shethar-bozenai and their associates came to them and spoke to them in this way: "Who issued a decree for you to build this house and to complete this structure?"

**5:4** Then we spoke to them as follows: "What are the names of the men who are building this building?"

**5:5** But the eye of their God was upon the elders of the Jews, and they did not halt them until the report should go to Darius, and then a letter should be sent back concerning this.

**5:6** The copy of the letter that Tattenai, governor of Beyond the River, and Shethar-bozenai and his associates, the Apharsachites who were in Beyond the River, sent to King Darius.

**5:7** They sent a report to him, and within it was written as follows: "To King Darius, all peace.

**5:8** Let it be known to the king that we went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built with massive stones, and timber is being set in the walls; and this work is being performed diligently and is prospering in their hands.

**5:9** Then we questioned those elders; we spoke to them as follows: 'Who issued a decree for you to build this house and to complete this structure?'

**5:10** Moreover, we asked them for their names to make them known to you, so that we might record the names of the men who were at their head.

**5:11** And they returned this answer to us, saying, 'We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are building the house that had been built many years before this, and a great king of Israel built it and completed it.

**5:12** Yet because our fathers provoked the God of heaven to anger, He gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean; and he tore down this house and deported the people to Babylon.

**5:13** Nevertheless, in the first year of Cyrus the king of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree for this house of God to be built.

**5:14** Moreover, King Cyrus brought out from the temple of Babylon the vessels of the house of God, of gold and silver, which Nebuchadnezzar had brought out from the temple that was in Jerusalem and carried to the temple of Babylon, and they were given to one named Sheshbazzar, whom he had made governor.

**5:15** And he said to him, "Take these vessels; go, deposit them in the temple that is in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be built on its site."

**5:16** Then that Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundations of the house of God that is in Jerusalem, and from that time to the present it has been under construction, and it is not completed.'

**5:17** And now, if it is pleasing to the king, let a search be made in the treasure house of the king there in Babylon, whether it is so that a decree was issued by King Cyrus to build that house of God in Jerusalem; and let the king send us his will concerning this."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Qere reads nebiyya' ('the prophet', emphatic state with final aleph) for the Ketiv orthographic spelling nby'h; similar orthographic Ketiv/Qere variants occur in 5:12 and 5:15.
- **5:3** — The Aramaic noun 'ushsharna' ('structure', recurring in 5:9) is a loanword of uncertain origin, variously understood as 'structure', 'wall', or 'furnishings/woodwork'.
- **5:4** — The Masoretic Aramaic text reads first-person plural 'amarna' ('we spoke'), preserved literally here rather than harmonized to third-person plural ('they said') as in the Septuagint and Syriac.
- **5:8** — 'Massive stones' translates Aramaic 'eben gelal (literally 'stone of rolling', referring to heavy ashlar blocks moved on rollers), and 'diligently' renders the Persian loanword 'osparna'.
