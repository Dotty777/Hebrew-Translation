## Text

**8:1** And these are the heads of their fathers' houses and their genealogical registration, those who went up with me from Babylon during the reign of King Artaxerxes:

**8:2** From the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; from the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; from the sons of David, Hattush.

**8:3** From the sons of Shecaniah; from the sons of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him one hundred fifty males were registered by genealogy.

**8:4** From the sons of Pahath-moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred males.

**8:5** From the sons of Shecaniah, son of Jahaziel, and with him three hundred males.

**8:6** And from the sons of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him fifty males.

**8:7** And from the sons of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, and with him seventy males.

**8:8** And from the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him eighty males.

**8:9** From the sons of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him two hundred eighteen males.

**8:10** And from the sons of Shelomith, son of Josiphiah, and with him one hundred sixty males.

**8:11** And from the sons of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him twenty-eight males.

**8:12** And from the sons of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him one hundred ten males.

**8:13** And from the sons of Adonikam, the last ones, and these are their names: Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah; and with them sixty males.

**8:14** And from the sons of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur, and with him seventy males.

**8:15** Then I gathered them to the river that flows to Ahava, and we camped there for three days. When I examined the people and the priests, I did not find any of the sons of Levi there.

**8:16** And I sent for Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, leaders, and for Joiarib and Elnathan, men of understanding.

**8:17** And I commanded them to go to Iddo the leader at the place Casiphia, and I put words in their mouths to speak to Iddo, his brother, and the temple servants at the place Casiphia, to bring us ministers for the house of our God.

**8:18** And they brought us, according to the good hand of our God upon us, a man of insight from the sons of Mahli, son of Levi, son of Israel, namely Sherebiah, with his sons and his brothers, eighteen;

**8:19** and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah from the sons of Merari, his brothers and their sons, twenty;

**8:20** and from the temple servants whom David and the officials had given for the service of the Levites, two hundred twenty temple servants; all of them were listed by name.

**8:21** So I proclaimed a fast there by the river Ahava, to humble ourselves before our God and seek from Him a straight way for us, for our little ones, and for all our possessions.

**8:22** For I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to help us against an enemy on the way, because we had spoken to the king, saying, "The hand of our God is upon all who seek Him for good, and His might and His wrath are against all who forsake Him."

**8:23** So we fasted and sought our God about this, and He responded to our plea.

**8:24** Then I set apart twelve from the leaders of the priests, Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and with them ten of their brothers.

**8:25** I weighed out to them the silver, the gold, and the vessels, the contribution for the house of our God that the king, his counselors, his officials, and all Israel who were present had contributed.

**8:26** Thus I weighed into their hands six hundred fifty talents of silver, silver vessels weighing one hundred talents, one hundred talents of gold;

**8:27** and twenty gold bowls valued at one thousand darics, and two vessels of fine burnished bronze, as precious as gold.

**8:28** And I told them, "You are holy to the LORD, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and the gold are a voluntary offering to the LORD, the God of your fathers.

**8:29** Keep watch and guard them until you weigh them out before the leaders of the priests and the Levites, and the leaders of the fathers' houses of Israel in Jerusalem, in the chambers of the house of the LORD."

**8:30** So the priests and the Levites received the weighed silver, gold, and vessels to bring them to Jerusalem, to the house of our God.

**8:31** Then we set out from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem; and the hand of our God was upon us, and He rescued us from the grip of the enemy and those lying in ambush along the way.

**8:32** So we arrived in Jerusalem and stayed there for three days.

**8:33** On the fourth day, the silver, the gold, and the vessels were weighed out in the house of our God into the hand of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest, and with him was Eleazar son of Phinehas; and with them were Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui, the Levites.

**8:34** All of it was by number and by weight, and the entire weight was written down at that time.

**8:35** Those who came from the captivity, the sons of the exile, presented burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve male goats as a sin offering, all of it a burnt offering to the LORD.

**8:36** And they delivered the king's decrees to the king's satraps and the governors of Beyond the River, and they gave support to the people and the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — In the Masoretic Text, 'From the sons of Shecaniah' opens verse 3 without a named leader; it appears to specify the Davidic lineage of Hattush at the end of 8:2 (cf. 1 Chr 3:22). Similarly, MT omits a separate ancestral clan name before Shecaniah in 8:5 and before Shelomith in 8:10.
- **8:14** — Following the qere 'Zaccur' (ketiv reads 'Zabbud'). The Masoretic Text also reads the singular suffix 'with him' despite the two names preceding it.
- **8:17** — Following the qere forms 'I commanded' (Piel of tsavah; ketiv reads 'I sent out', Hiphil of yatsa) and 'the temple servants' (Hebrew ha-netinim, literally 'the given ones'; ketiv reads the Qal passive participle ha-netunim). MT also points the singular 'his brother' without a conjunction before 'the temple servants.'
- **8:24** — MT prefixes the preposition le- to Sherebiah without a conjunction, which may introduce Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten kinsmen as a second group of twelve Levites alongside the twelve priests (cf. 8:18-19, 30) or list them in apposition.
- **8:25** — Following the qere spelling of 'I weighed out' (Qal of shaqal; the ketiv is an orthographic variant with vav). The verb 'had contributed' (Hiphil of rum) uses the prefixed definite article as a relative marker, a feature of Late Biblical Hebrew.
