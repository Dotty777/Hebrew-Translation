## Text

**10:1** Now as Ezra was praying and confessing, weeping and throwing himself down in front of the house of God, a very large assembly gathered to him from Israel, men, women, and children, for the people wept with great weeping.

**10:2** Then Shecaniah the son of Jehiel, of the sons of Elam, answered and said to Ezra, "We have acted unfaithfully against our God and have married foreign women from the peoples of the land; still, now there is hope for Israel concerning this.

**10:3** So now let us make a covenant with our God to send away all the wives and those born from them, by the counsel of the Lord and of those who tremble at the command of our God, and let it be done according to the law.

**10:4** Rise, for the matter rests on you, and we are with you. Be strong and act."

**10:5** Then Ezra rose and made the officials of the priests, the Levites, and all Israel swear to act according to this word, and they swore.

**10:6** Then Ezra rose from in front of the house of God and went to the chamber of Jehohanan the son of Eliashib; and when he went there, he ate no bread and drank no water, for he was mourning over the unfaithfulness of the exiles.

**10:7** And they sent out a proclamation throughout Judah and Jerusalem to all the people of the exile to gather at Jerusalem,

**10:8** and that, as for anyone who did not come within three days, according to the counsel of the officials and the elders, all his possessions would be confiscated, and he himself would be separated from the assembly of the exiles.

**10:9** So all the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered at Jerusalem within the three days. It was the ninth month, on the twentieth of the month; and all the people sat in the open square of the house of God, trembling over the matter and from the rains.

**10:10** Then Ezra the priest rose and said to them, "You have acted unfaithfully and have married foreign women, adding to the guilt of Israel.

**10:11** So now offer confession to the LORD, the God of your fathers, and do His will; and separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from the foreign women."

**10:12** Then all the assembly answered and said with a loud voice, "Indeed, it rests on us to act according to your word.

**10:13** However, the people are numerous, and this is a time of rains, and there is no strength to stand outside; and the work is not for one day or for two, for we have transgressed so greatly in this matter.

**10:14** Please let our officials stand for all the assembly, and let everyone in our cities who has married foreign women come at appointed times, and with them the elders of each city and its judges, until the burning anger of our God is turned away from us concerning this matter."

**10:15** Only Jonathan the son of Asahel and Jahzeiah the son of Tikvah stood against this, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them.

**10:16** And the people of the exile did so. Then Ezra the priest and men who were ancestral heads, according to their fathers' houses, all of them by name, were set apart; and they sat down on the first day of the tenth month to investigate the matter.

**10:17** And they finished with all the men who had married foreign women by the first day of the first month.

**10:18** Now among the sons of the priests who had married foreign women there were found, from the sons of Jeshua the son of Jozadak and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah.

**10:19** And they gave their hand to send away their wives, and being guilty, they brought a ram of the flock for their guilt.

**10:23** And from the Levites: Jozabad; Shimei; Kelaiah, that is, Kelita; Pethahiah; Judah; and Eliezer.

**10:24** And from the singers: Eliashib; and from the gatekeepers: Shallum, Telem, and Uri.

**10:25** And from Israel, from the sons of Parosh: Ramiah, Izziah, Malchijah, Mijamin, Eleazar, Malchijah, and Benaiah.

**10:26** And from the sons of Elam: Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdi, Jeremoth, and Elijah.

**10:27** And from the sons of Zattu: Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza.

**10:28** And from the sons of Bebai: Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, and Athlai.

**10:34** From the sons of Bani: Maadai, Amram, Uel,

**10:35** Benaiah, Bedeiah, Cheluhu,

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The verb translated "married" here and in 10:10, 14, 17, and 18 is the Hiphil of yashav (literally "cause to dwell" or "bring home"), a Late Biblical Hebrew idiom for taking wives. For "Elam," the qere reads Elam while the ketiv spells it Olam.
- **10:3** — The Masoretic vocalization reads Adonai ("the Lord"), whereas some Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions point the word Adoni ("my lord"), referring to Ezra.
- **10:16** — The Masoretic Text reads a Niphal plural verb ("were set apart") with Ezra and the ancestral heads as compound subject, whereas a proposed emendation reads a Hiphil singular ("he selected"). The form ledaryosh ("to investigate") is an unusual spelling of the Qal infinitive construct of darash.
- **10:19** — Literally "and being guilty, a ram of the flock for their guilt," with the verb ("brought" or "offered") implied after the plural adjective ashemim ("guilty").
- **10:44** — For "had taken," the qere reads the Qal perfect plural nas'u in place of the ketiv form. The second clause (literally "and there were among them wives, and they produced children") is difficult in the Masoretic Text and is translated as written rather than emended from 1 Esdras 9:36.
