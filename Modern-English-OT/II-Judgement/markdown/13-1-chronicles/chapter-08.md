**8:20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel,

**8:23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan,

**8:24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah,

**8:25** Iphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak.

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — The Hiphil verb in "they exiled them" is third-person masculine plural and may function as an impersonal passive ("they were exiled"). Verse 7 then uses the same Hiphil root in the singular ("it was he who exiled them"), referring to Gera, with the initial conjunction on Naaman resuming the list of Ehud's sons.
- **8:8** — The Hiphil verb "fathered" is used absolutely here without an expressed direct object ("children" is supplied for English). "After he had sent them away" renders a temporal preposition with the Piel infinitive construct of shalach ("send away," here divorce) and a masculine plural suffix referring to his two wives.
- **8:25** — Ketiv/Qere: the written consonantal text (ketiv) reads "Peniel," while the traditional reading (qere) translated here is "Penuel."
- **8:29** — The verb "lived" is plural before the singular subject "the father of Gibeon," likely referring collectively to him and his household. Unlike the parallel genealogy in 1 Chronicles 9:35, the Masoretic Text here does not include the personal name Jeiel.
- **8:38** — The Masoretic vocalization points "Bocheru" as a proper name rather than "his firstborn," which matches the stated total of six sons.
