## Text

**22:1** Then David said, "This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar for burnt offering for Israel."

**22:2** David gave orders to gather the resident foreigners who were in the land of Israel, and he appointed stonecutters to quarry dressed stones for building the house of God.

**22:3** David also prepared iron in abundance for the nails of the doors of the gates and for the clamps, and bronze in abundance beyond weighing,

**22:4** and cedar timbers beyond number, for the Sidonians and the Tyrians brought cedar timbers in abundance to David.

**22:5** David said, "Solomon my son is young and tender, and the house to be built for the LORD must be exceedingly grand, for renown and splendor throughout all the lands; I will now prepare for it." So David prepared in abundance before his death.

**22:6** Then he summoned his son Solomon and charged him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.

**22:7** David said to Solomon, "My son, as for me, it was in my heart to build a house for the name of the LORD my God.

**22:8** But the word of the LORD came to me, saying, 'You have shed blood in abundance and waged great wars. You shall not build a house for My name, because you have shed much blood on the ground before Me.

**22:9** Look, a son will be born to you; he shall be a man of rest, and I will give him rest from all his enemies on every side. For Solomon shall be his name, and I will grant peace and quiet to Israel in his days.

**22:10** He is the one who will build a house for My name, and he will be a son to Me, and I will be a father to him; and I will establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel forever.'

**22:11** Now, my son, may the LORD be with you, so that you may succeed and build the house of the LORD your God, just as He has spoken concerning you.

**22:12** Only may the LORD grant you insight and understanding, and appoint you over Israel, so that you may keep the law of the LORD your God.

**22:13** Then you will succeed if you take care to carry out the statutes and the ordinances that the LORD commanded Moses concerning Israel. Be strong and resolute; do not be afraid, and do not be dismayed.

**22:14** Now look, in my hardship I have prepared for the house of the LORD one hundred thousand talents of gold and one million talents of silver, and bronze and iron beyond weighing, because it is in such abundance. I have also prepared wood and stones, and you may add to them.

**22:15** Furthermore, you have workers in abundance: stonecutters, craftsmen of stone and wood, and every person skilled in every kind of work.

**22:16** Of the gold, the silver, the bronze, and the iron, there is no number. Arise and act, and may the LORD be with you."

**22:17** David also commanded all the leaders of Israel to help Solomon his son:

**22:18** "Is not the LORD your God with you, and has He not given you rest on every side? For He has given the inhabitants of the land into my hand, and the land has been subdued before the LORD and before His people.

**22:19** Now give your heart and your soul to seeking the LORD your God. Arise and build the sanctuary of the LORD God, to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD and the holy vessels of God into the house that is to be built for the name of the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **22:7** — Ketiv reads "his son" (beno), closing the narrative introduction; Qere reads "My son" (beni), opening David's address to Solomon, and is translated here.
- **22:9** — The name Solomon (Shelomoh) plays on shalom ("peace") in the following clause, reinforcing the theme of "rest" (menuchah) and "give rest" (hanichoti, Hiphil of nuach).
- **22:14** — Hebrew be'onyi literally means "in my affliction" or "in my hardship," expressing the great pains and difficult circumstances under which David gathered these provisions.
