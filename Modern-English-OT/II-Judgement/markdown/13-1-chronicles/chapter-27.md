## Text

**27:1** Now the sons of Israel according to their number—the heads of the fathers' houses, and the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and their officers who served the king in every matter of the divisions coming in and going out month by month for all the months of the year—each division was twenty-four thousand.

**27:2** Over the first division for the first month was Jashobeam the son of Zabdiel; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:3** He was from the sons of Perez, the chief of all the commanders of the armies for the first month.

**27:4** Over the division of the second month was Dodai the Ahohite, and of his division Mikloth was the leader; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:5** The third commander of the army for the third month was Benaiah the son of Jehoiada the priest, as chief; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:6** This is the Benaiah who was a mighty man of the Thirty and over the Thirty, and over his division was Ammizabad his son.

**27:7** The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zebadiah his son after him; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:8** The fifth, for the fifth month, was the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:9** The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:10** The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, from the sons of Ephraim; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:11** The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:12** The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, of the Benjaminites; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:13** The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahites; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:14** The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, from the sons of Ephraim; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:15** The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:16** Now over the tribes of Israel: for the Reubenites, the leader was Eliezer the son of Zichri; for the Simeonites, Shephatiah the son of Maacah;

**27:17** for Levi, Hashabiah the son of Kemuel; for Aaron, Zadok;

**27:18** for Judah, Elihu, one of the brothers of David; for Issachar, Omri the son of Michael;

**27:19** for Zebulun, Ishmaiah the son of Obadiah; for Naphtali, Jerimoth the son of Azriel;

**27:20** for the sons of Ephraim, Hoshea the son of Azaziah; for the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel the son of Pedaiah;

**27:21** for the half of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo the son of Zechariah; for Benjamin, Jaasiel the son of Abner;

**27:22** for Dan, Azarel the son of Jeroham. These were the chiefs of the tribes of Israel.

**27:23** Now David did not take a count of those twenty years old and under, because the LORD had promised to multiply Israel like the stars of the heavens.

**27:24** Joab the son of Zeruiah began to count, but did not finish; and wrath came upon Israel because of this, and the number was not entered in the account of the chronicles of King David.

**27:25** Over the treasuries of the king was Azmaveth the son of Adiel; and over the storehouses in the fields, in the cities, in the villages, and in the towers was Jonathan the son of Uzziah.

**27:26** Over those who did the work of the field for tilling the ground was Ezri the son of Chelub.

**27:27** Over the vineyards was Shimei the Ramathite; and over what was in the vineyards for the storehouses of wine was Zabdi the Shiphmite.

**27:28** Over the olive trees and the sycamore trees that were in the Shephelah was Baal-hanan the Gederite; and over the storehouses of oil was Joash.

**27:29** Over the cattle grazing in Sharon was Shirtai the Sharonite; and over the cattle in the valleys was Shaphat the son of Adlai.

**27:30** Over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite; and over the female donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronothite.

**27:31** Over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagrite. All these were the officials of the property belonging to King David.

**27:32** Now Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding and a scribe; and Jehiel the son of Hachmoni was with the sons of the king.

**27:33** Ahithophel was counselor to the king, and Hushai the Archite was the friend of the king.

**27:34** After Ahithophel came Jehoiada the son of Benaiah, and Abiathar; and the commander of the king's army was Joab.

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — Hebrew 'al mahalugto (lit. "upon his division") is a Late Biblical Hebrew idiom meaning "in" or "assigned to his division," recurring across the monthly roster in vv. 2-15.
- **27:5** — In Hebrew hakkohen rosh, rosh ("chief/head") lacks the definite article and is separated from hakkohen ("the priest") by a disjunctive accent, allowing either "the son of Jehoiada the priest, as chief" (describing Benaiah) or "the son of Jehoiada the chief priest."
- **27:12** — The Ketiv writes one word ("of the Benjaminite"), whereas the Qere divides it into two words (labben yemini, the older two-word gentilic form); both denote the tribe of Benjamin.
- **27:24** — The Qal verb 'alah ("went up") is used in the archival sense "was entered/recorded," and the Masoretic Text reads bemispar ("in the account/enumeration") rather than besefer ("in the book").
- **27:29** — Translates the Qere Shirtai; the Ketiv reads Shitrai.
