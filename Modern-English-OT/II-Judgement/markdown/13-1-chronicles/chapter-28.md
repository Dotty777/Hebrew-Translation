## Text

**28:1** Then David assembled to Jerusalem all the officials of Israel: the chiefs of the tribes, the commanders of the divisions who served the king, the commanders of thousands, the commanders of hundreds, and the officials over all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, along with the court officers, the mighty men, and every mighty man of valor.

**28:2** Then King David rose to his feet and said, "Listen to me, my brothers and my people. As for me, it was in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the LORD and for the footstool of our God, and I made preparations to build.

**28:3** Yet God said to me, 'You are not to build a house for My name, for you are a man of wars and have shed blood.'

**28:4** Still, the LORD, the God of Israel, chose me out of all my father's house to be king over Israel forever; for He chose Judah as leader, and in the house of Judah, my father's house, and among my father's sons He was pleased with me, to make me king over all Israel.

**28:5** And out of all my sons—for the LORD has given me many sons—He chose my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the kingdom of the LORD over Israel.

**28:6** And He said to me, 'Your son Solomon is the one who will build My house and My courts, for I have chosen him for Myself as a son, and I Myself will be a father to him.

**28:7** And I will establish his kingdom forever, if he remains strong to carry out My commandments and My judgments, as on this day.'

**28:8** So now, before the eyes of all Israel, the assembly of the LORD, and in the hearing of our God, keep and seek all the commandments of the LORD your God, in order that you may possess the good land and pass it on as an inheritance to your sons after you forever.

**28:9** And you, my son Solomon, know the God of your father and serve Him with an undivided heart and a willing soul, because the LORD searches all hearts and discerns every inclination of the thoughts. If you seek Him, He will let Himself be found by you; yet if you abandon Him, He will cast you off forever.

**28:10** See now that the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be strong and act."

**28:11** Then David gave his son Solomon the pattern of the vestibule and its buildings, its storerooms, its upper rooms, its inner chambers, and the room of the mercy seat,

**28:12** and the pattern of all that he had by the Spirit: for the courts of the house of the LORD, and for all the surrounding chambers, for the treasuries of the house of God and for the treasuries of the consecrated things;

**28:13** also for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, and for all the work of the service of the house of the LORD, and for all the articles of the service of the house of the LORD;

**28:14** for the golden things, by weight of gold, for all the articles of every kind of service; and for all the silver articles by weight, for all the articles of every kind of service;

**28:15** and the weight for the lampstands of gold and their lamps of gold, by the weight of each lampstand and its lamps, and for the lampstands of silver by weight, for a lampstand and its lamps, according to the service of each lampstand;

**28:16** and the gold by weight for the tables of the arranged bread, for each table, and silver for the tables of silver;

**28:17** and the forks, the basins, and the pitchers of pure gold; and for the golden bowls by weight for each bowl, and for the silver bowls by weight for each bowl;

**28:18** and for the altar of incense, refined gold by weight; and for the pattern of the chariot—the golden cherubim spreading out their wings and covering over the ark of the covenant of the LORD.

**28:19** "All this, in writing from the hand of the LORD upon me, He made clear—all the works of the pattern."

**28:20** And David said to his son Solomon, "Be strong and courageous, and act. Do not be afraid and do not be terrified, because the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not let go of you or abandon you until all the work of the service of the house of the LORD is completed.

**28:21** And here are the divisions of the priests and the Levites for all the service of the house of God, and with you in all the work is every willing person with skill for every kind of service, as well as the officials and all the people for all your commands."

## Translator Notes

- **28:9** — Hebrew yimmatse lakh is the Niphal tolerative of matsa ("He will let Himself be found by you"), and the verb root darash ("searches" / "seek") directly echoes "seek" in v. 8.
- **28:12** — Hebrew baruah carries the definite article and can be understood either as "by the Spirit" (compare v. 19) or "in mind/spirit."
- **28:18** — Hebrew hammerkabah ("the chariot") stands in apposition to "the golden cherubim," and "their wings" is implied with the Qal participle leforesim ("spreading out," cf. Exod 25:20).
- **28:19** — The Masoretic Text shifts abruptly to first-person speech ('alay, "upon me") without an introductory speech formula; hiskil is the Hiphil of sakal ("made clear" or "gave understanding of").
