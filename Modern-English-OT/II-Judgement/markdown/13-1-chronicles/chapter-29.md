## Text

**29:1** Then King David said to the whole assembly, "Solomon my son, the only one whom God has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great, because the palace is not for a human being, but for the LORD God.

**29:2** Now with all my strength I have prepared for the house of my God: the gold for the gold articles, the silver for the silver, the bronze for the bronze, the iron for the iron, and the wood for the wood; onyx stones and stones for setting, antimony stones and variegated stones, every precious stone, and marble stones in abundance.

**29:3** Furthermore, because of my delight in the house of my God, the private treasure of gold and silver that belongs to me I have given to the house of my God, over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house:

**29:4** three thousand talents of gold, from the gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined silver, for overlaying the walls of the buildings,

**29:5** gold for the gold articles and silver for the silver, and for all work by the hand of craftsmen. Now who offers willingly to fill his hand today for the LORD?"

**29:6** Then the leaders of the fathers' houses, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials of the king's work offered willingly.

**29:7** And they gave for the service of the house of God: gold, five thousand talents and ten thousand darics; silver, ten thousand talents; bronze, eighteen thousand talents; and iron, one hundred thousand talents.

**29:8** And any with whom stones were found gave them to the treasury of the house of the LORD, under the hand of Jehiel the Gershonite.

**29:9** Then the people rejoiced over their willing offering, because with a whole heart they had offered willingly to the LORD; and King David also rejoiced with great joy.

**29:10** Then David blessed the LORD before the eyes of the whole assembly, and David said, "Blessed are You, LORD, God of Israel our father, from eternity and to eternity.

**29:11** Yours, LORD, are the greatness, the might, the glory, the eminence, and the majesty, because everything in the heavens and on the earth belongs to You; Yours, LORD, is the kingdom, and You are the One exalted as head over all.

**29:12** Wealth and honor come from Your presence, and You rule over all. In Your hand are strength and might, and it is in Your hand to make great and to strengthen all.

**29:13** And now, our God, we are giving thanks to You and praising Your glorious name.

**29:14** Yet who am I, and who are my people, that we should possess strength to offer willingly like this? For everything is from You, and out of Your hand we have given to You.

**29:15** For we are sojourners before You and temporary residents, like all our fathers; like a shadow are our days on the earth, and there is no hope.

**29:16** LORD our God, all this abundance that we have prepared to build for You a house for Your holy name is from Your hand, and everything belongs to You.

**29:17** And I know, my God, that You examine the heart and delight in uprightness; as for me, in the uprightness of my heart I have willingly offered all these things, and now I have seen with joy Your people who are found here offering willingly to You.

**29:18** LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel our fathers, keep this forever in the inclination of the thoughts of Your people's heart, and establish their heart toward You.

**29:19** And to my son Solomon give a whole heart to keep Your commandments, Your testimonies, and Your statutes, and to do everything, and to build the palace for which I have prepared."

**29:20** Then David said to the whole assembly, "Now bless the LORD your God." So the whole assembly blessed the LORD, the God of their fathers, and bowed low and prostrated themselves before the LORD and before the king.

**29:21** And they offered sacrifices to the LORD and brought up burnt offerings to the LORD on the day after that day: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, a thousand lambs, along with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**29:22** They ate and drank before the LORD on that day with great joy. And a second time they made Solomon son of David king, and they anointed him for the LORD as ruler, and Zadok as priest.

**29:23** So Solomon sat on the throne of the LORD as king in place of his father David; he prospered, and all Israel heeded him.

**29:24** And all the officials and the mighty men, as well as all the sons of King David, gave their hand under King Solomon.

**29:25** And the LORD made Solomon exceedingly great before the eyes of all Israel, and bestowed on him royal majesty such as had not been on any king before him over Israel.

**29:26** And David son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.

**29:27** And the days that he reigned over Israel were forty years: in Hebron he reigned seven years, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three.

**29:28** Then he died at a good old age, full of days, wealth, and honor; and his son Solomon reigned in his place.

**29:29** Now the deeds of King David, first and last—see, they are written in the records of Samuel the seer, and in the records of Nathan the prophet, and in the records of Gad the seer,

**29:30** together with all his reign and his might, and the times that passed over him, and over Israel, and over all the kingdoms of the lands.

## Translator Notes

- **29:5** — Hebrew "fill his hand" (Piel infinitive construct of male with "hand") is an idiom for dedicating oneself or bringing a consecrated offering to the LORD (compare Exodus 32:29).
- **29:11** — Hebrew netsach, translated "eminence," denotes surpassing splendor, enduring preeminence, or triumph. The Hithpael participle hammithnasse ("the One exalted") attributes supreme exaltation to the LORD as head over all.
- **29:16** — Ketiv/Qere: the written consonantal text (Ketiv) has the feminine singular pronoun hi, while the traditional reading (Qere) has the masculine singular pronoun hu, agreeing with the masculine singular antecedent "abundance" (hamon).
- **29:24** — Hebrew "gave their hand under King Solomon" (literally "gave a hand under") is a formal idiom for pledging allegiance and submitting to royal authority.
- **29:29** — Hebrew uses two distinct prophetic titles here translated "seer": Samuel is called haro'eh (Qal participle of ra'ah, "to see"), while Gad is called hachozeh (Qal participle of chazah, "to behold in vision").
