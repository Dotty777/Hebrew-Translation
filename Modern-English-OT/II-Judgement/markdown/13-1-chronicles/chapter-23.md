## Text

**23:1** Now David was old and full of days, and he made his son Solomon king over Israel.

**23:2** And he gathered all the officials of Israel, and the priests and the Levites.

**23:3** The Levites were counted from thirty years old and upward, and their number, head by head among the men, was thirty-eight thousand.

**23:4** "Of these, twenty-four thousand are to supervise the work of the house of the LORD, and six thousand are officers and judges,

**23:5** and four thousand are gatekeepers, and four thousand are praising the LORD with the instruments that I made for giving praise."

**23:6** And David divided them into divisions by the sons of Levi, for Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**23:7** Of the Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.

**23:8** The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the head, and Zetham, and Joel, three.

**23:9** The sons of Shimei: Shelomith, Haziel, and Haran, three. These were the heads of the fathers' houses belonging to Ladan.

**23:10** And the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei, four.

**23:11** Jahath was the head, and Zizah was the second; but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they became a father's house for one enrollment.

**23:12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.

**23:13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. And Aaron was set apart so that he would consecrate the most holy things, he and his sons forever, to burn incense before the LORD, to minister to Him, and to bless in His name forever.

**23:14** Now as for Moses, the man of God, his sons were named under the tribe of Levi.

**23:15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

**23:16** The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the head.

**23:17** And the sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah the head; Eliezer had no other sons, yet the sons of Rehabiah grew exceedingly numerous.

**23:18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the head.

**23:19** The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the head, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.

**23:20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the head and Isshiah the second.

**23:21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

**23:22** Eleazar died and had no sons, only daughters; so the sons of Kish, their relatives, took them as wives.

**23:23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth, three.

**23:24** These were the sons of Levi by their fathers' houses, the heads of the fathers' houses according to their enrollment, by the count of names, head by head, who did the work for the service of the house of the LORD, from twenty years old and upward.

**23:25** For David said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, has granted rest to His people, and He dwells in Jerusalem forever.

**23:26** And furthermore, the Levites need not carry the tabernacle or any of its vessels for its service."

**23:27** For according to the final words of David, these were the numbered sons of Levi from twenty years old and upward.

**23:28** For their station was at the side of the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the LORD, over the courts and over the chambers, and over the purification of every holy thing, and the work of the service of the house of God,

**23:29** and for the bread of the rows, and for the fine flour for a grain offering, and for the unleavened wafers, and for what is cooked on a griddle, and for what is well-mixed, and for every measure of volume and size;

**23:30** and to stand morning by morning to give thanks and praise to the LORD, and likewise at evening;

**23:31** and at every offering of burnt offerings to the LORD, on the Sabbaths, on the new moons, and at the appointed feasts, by the number according to the ordinance required of them, continually before the LORD.

**23:32** So they were to keep the charge of the tent of meeting, and the charge of the sanctuary, and the charge of the sons of Aaron, their brothers, for the service of the house of the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — The Hebrew shifts abruptly into first-person speech with the Qal perfect verb עָשִׂיתִי ("I made") without an explicit introductory speech verb, indicating that the allocation in verses 4-5 is presented as David's own decree.
- **23:9** — Following the qere שְׁלֹמִית ("Shelomith"); the ketiv reads שלמות ("Shelomoth", as in 1 Chronicles 24:22). Note also that verse 10 spells the son of Shimei as Zina (זִינָא), whereas verse 11 spells the same name Zizah (זִיזָה).
- **23:13** — The third-person suffix on the Hiphil infinitive לְהַקְדִּישׁוֹ is grammatically ambiguous: it is translated here as subjective ("so that he would consecrate the most holy things"), though it can also be construed as objective ("to consecrate him as most holy").
- **23:27** — Literally "they were the number of the sons of Levi"; the pronoun הֵמָּה ("they") functions copulatively to identify the Levites enrolled from twenty years old and upward under David's final directive.
