## Text

**18:1** After this it happened that David defeated the Philistines and subdued them, and he took Gath and its villages from the hand of the Philistines.

**18:2** And he defeated Moab, and Moab became servants to David, bearing tribute.

**18:3** And David defeated Hadadezer king of Zobah toward Hamath, as he went to establish his dominion by the Euphrates River.

**18:4** David captured from him one thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers; and David hamstrung all the chariot horses, though he spared enough of them for one hundred chariots.

**18:5** When the Arameans of Damascus came to assist Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men of Aram.

**18:6** Then David stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and Aram became servants to David, bearing tribute. And the LORD gave victory to David wherever he went.

**18:7** David took the shields of gold that were carried by the servants of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.

**18:8** And from Tibhath and from Cun, cities of Hadadezer, David took a very large quantity of bronze; with it Solomon made the bronze sea, the pillars, and the bronze articles.

**18:9** Now Tou king of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer king of Zobah.

**18:10** So he sent his son Hadoram to King David to inquire about his welfare and to bless him because he had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him—for Hadadezer had been at war with Tou—along with all kinds of articles of gold, silver, and bronze.

**18:11** These also King David consecrated to the LORD, together with the silver and the gold that he had carried off from all the nations: from Edom, from Moab, from the sons of Ammon, from the Philistines, and from Amalek.

**18:12** Moreover, Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt.

**18:13** He stationed garrisons in Edom, and all Edom became servants to David. And the LORD gave victory to David wherever he went.

**18:14** So David reigned over all Israel, and he continually practiced justice and righteousness for all his people.

**18:15** Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder.

**18:16** Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Shavsha was the scribe.

**18:17** Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and the sons of David were first in rank at the side of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **18:3** — Hebrew le-hatstiv yado (Hiphil of natsav + yad) reads literally "to set up his hand," an idiom meaning either "to establish his dominion" or "to erect his victory monument."
- **18:6** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads literally "and David stationed in Aram of Damascus," omitting an explicit direct object; "garrisons" (netsivim) is supplied from the parallel wording in 18:13.
- **18:10** — Ketiv/Qere orthographic variant: the ketiv writes lsh'wl (plene) and the qere reads lish'ol-lo (defective spelling with maqqef); both represent the Qal infinitive construct of sha'al ("to inquire").
- **18:16** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "Abimelech" here (compare "Ahimelech" in 2 Samuel 8:17 and 1 Chronicles 24:3, 6, 31).
