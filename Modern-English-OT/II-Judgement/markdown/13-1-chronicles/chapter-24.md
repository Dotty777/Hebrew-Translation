## Text

**24:1** Now concerning the sons of Aaron, their divisions: the sons of Aaron were Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**24:2** Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and they had no sons; so Eleazar and Ithamar ministered as priests.

**24:3** And David, along with Zadok from the sons of Eleazar and Ahimelech from the sons of Ithamar, divided them according to their assigned order in their service.

**24:4** Now the sons of Eleazar were found to be more numerous in male heads than the sons of Ithamar, so they divided them: for the sons of Eleazar, sixteen heads of fathers' houses, and for the sons of Ithamar, eight by their fathers' houses.

**24:5** They divided them by lots, the one with the other, for there were officials of the sanctuary and officials of God from the sons of Eleazar and among the sons of Ithamar.

**24:6** Shemaiah the son of Nethanel the scribe, from the Levites, recorded them in the presence of the king and the officials, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the priests and of the Levites: one father's house taken for Eleazar, and taken in turn for Ithamar.

**24:7** The first lot came out for Jehoiarib; for Jedaiah, the second;

**24:8** for Harim, the third; for Seorim, the fourth;

**24:9** for Malchijah, the fifth; for Mijamin, the sixth;

**24:10** for Hakkoz, the seventh; for Abijah, the eighth;

**24:11** for Jeshua, the ninth; for Shecaniah, the tenth;

**24:12** for Eliashib, the eleventh; for Jakim, the twelfth;

**24:13** for Huppah, the thirteenth; for Jeshebeab, the fourteenth;

**24:14** for Bilgah, the fifteenth; for Immer, the sixteenth;

**24:15** for Hezir, the seventeenth; for Happizzez, the eighteenth;

**24:16** for Pethahiah, the nineteenth; for Jehezkel, the twentieth;

**24:17** for Jachin, the twenty-first; for Gamul, the twenty-second;

**24:18** for Delaiah, the twenty-third; for Maaziah, the twenty-fourth.

**24:19** This was their assigned order for their service, to come into the house of the LORD according to their ordinance by the hand of Aaron their father, just as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded him.

**24:20** Now concerning the remaining sons of Levi: of the sons of Amram, Shubael; of the sons of Shubael, Jehdeiah.

**24:21** Of Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, Isshiah the head.

**24:22** Of the Izharites, Shelomoth; of the sons of Shelomoth, Jahath.

**24:23** And the sons: Jeriah, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

**24:24** The sons of Uzziel: Micah; of the sons of Micah, Shamir.

**24:25** The brother of Micah: Isshiah; of the sons of Isshiah, Zechariah.

**24:26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi; the sons of Jaaziah his son.

**24:27** The sons of Merari, of Jaaziah his son: Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.

**24:28** Of Mahli: Eleazar, and he had no sons.

**24:29** Of Kish: the sons of Kish, Jerahmeel.

**24:30** And the sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites by their fathers' houses.

**24:31** They also cast lots just like their brothers, the sons of Aaron, in the presence of David the king, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the priests and of the Levites, the head of the fathers' house just like his younger brother.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — The Masoretic Text literally reads וְאָחֻז ׀ אָחֻז לְאִיתָמָר ("and taken, taken for Ithamar"), repeating the Qal passive participle of אחז with a Masoretic paseq; this is rendered distributively ("taken in turn"), whereas ancient versions reflect "and one taken for Ithamar."
- **24:23** — The Masoretic Text reads literally וּבְנָי יְרִיָּהוּ ("And the sons: Jeriah"), with a disjunctive accent separating וּבְנָי from יְרִיָּהוּ and omitting both "Hebron" and "the head" that appear in the parallel list in 1 Chronicles 23:19.
- **24:24** — Following the qere שָׁמִיר ("Shamir"); the ketiv reads שמור ("Shamur").
- **24:26** — In both verses 26 and 27, Masoretic conjunctive accents and the spirantized form בְנוֹ bind the word directly to יַעֲזִיָּהוּ as "Jaaziah his son," though some interpreters treat בְנוֹ as a proper name ("Beno") because of the conjunction on וְשֹׁהַם ("and Shoham") in verse 27.
- **24:31** — The phrase אָבוֹת הָרֹאשׁ (literally "the fathers of the head") is an inverted idiomatic expression for the senior head of a fathers' house, placed on equal footing in the casting of lots with his younger brother.
