## Text

**5:1** Now the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel—for he was the firstborn, yet when he profaned his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel, though registration by genealogy was not according to the birthright.

**5:2** For Judah prevailed among his brothers, and a ruler came from him, while the birthright was Joseph's.

**5:3** The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

**5:4** The sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

**5:5** Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,

**5:6** Beerah his son, whom Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria carried into exile; he was chief of the Reubenites.

**5:7** And his kinsmen by his clans, when registered by genealogy according to their generations: the head, Jeiel, and Zechariah,

**5:8** and Bela, son of Azaz, son of Shema, son of Joel; he lived in Aroer and as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

**5:9** And toward the east he lived as far as the entrance to the wilderness from the Euphrates River, for their livestock had grown numerous in the land of Gilead.

**5:10** And in the days of Saul they waged war against the Hagrites, who fell by their hand; and they lived in their tents across the entire territory east of Gilead.

**5:11** And opposite them, the sons of Gad lived in the land of Bashan as far as Salecah.

**5:12** Joel was the head, and Shapham the second, and Janai and Shaphat in Bashan.

**5:13** And their kinsmen according to their fathers' houses were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber—seven.

**5:14** These were the sons of Abihail, son of Huri, son of Jaroah, son of Gilead, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jahdo, son of Buz.

**5:15** Ahi, son of Abdiel, son of Guni, was head of their fathers' house.

**5:16** And they lived in Gilead, in Bashan and in its towns, and in all the pasturelands of Sharon as far as their outer limits.

**5:17** All of them were registered by genealogy in the days of Jotham king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

**5:18** The sons of Reuben, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had men of valor—men bearing shield and sword, bending the bow, and trained for war—forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty going out to military service.

**5:19** And they waged war against the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab.

**5:20** And they were helped against them, and the Hagrites and all who were with them were given into their hand; for they cried out to God in the battle, and He responded favorably to them because they trusted in Him.

**5:21** And they captured their livestock: fifty thousand of their camels, two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand people.

**5:22** For many fell struck down, because the war was from God, and they lived in place of them until the exile.

**5:23** And the sons of the half-tribe of Manasseh lived in the land from Bashan as far as Baal-hermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon; they were numerous.

**5:24** And these were the heads of their fathers' houses: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel—mighty men of valor, men of renown, heads of their fathers' houses.

**5:25** But they acted unfaithfully against the God of their fathers, and prostituted themselves after the gods of the peoples of the land whom God had destroyed from before them.

**5:26** So the God of Israel roused the spirit of Pul king of Assyria and the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria, and he carried the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh into exile, and brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river of Gozan, to this day.

**5:27** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**5:28** And the sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

**5:29** And the children of Amram: Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. And the sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**5:30** Eleazar fathered Phinehas; Phinehas fathered Abishua.

**5:31** And Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi.

**5:32** And Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, and Zerahiah fathered Meraioth.

**5:33** Meraioth fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.

**5:34** And Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz.

**5:35** And Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan.

**5:36** And Johanan fathered Azariah—he was the one who ministered as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

**5:37** And Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.

**5:38** And Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum.

**5:39** And Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah.

**5:40** And Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak.

**5:41** And Jehozadak went away when the LORD carried Judah and Jerusalem into exile by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Hebrew clause וְלֹא לְהִתְיַחֵשׂ לַבְּכֹרָה uses the Hithpael infinitive construct of יחשׂ ("to be registered by genealogy") with negative לֹא to indicate that the genealogical order did not follow the birthright.
- **5:6** — The Masoretic Text of Chronicles spells this Assyrian king's name Tilgath-pilneser (also in 5:26 and 2 Chronicles 28:20), corresponding to Tiglath-pileser in 2 Kings 15:29.
- **5:20** — The form וְנַעְתּוֹר is a Niphal infinitive absolute of עתר functioning as a finite verb continuing the preceding clause, conveying that God let Himself be moved and responded favorably to their plea.
- **5:26** — Pul was the Babylonian throne name of Tilgath-pilneser (Tiglath-pileser III), and the following singular Hiphil verb וַיַּגְלֵם ("and he carried them into exile") treats them as one ruler, using a proleptic object suffix specified by the Late Biblical Hebrew direct-object preposition לְ.
- **5:27** — Verses 5:27–41 in the Masoretic Hebrew versification correspond to 1 Chronicles 6:1–15 in most English translations.
