## Text

**17:1** It happened, once David was settled in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, "See, I am dwelling in a house of cedars, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD is under curtains."

**17:2** Nathan said to David, "Do everything that is in your heart, for God is with you."

**17:3** And it happened on that night that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

**17:4** "Go and say to David My servant, 'This is what the LORD says: You are not the one who will build the house for Me to dwell in.

**17:5** For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought Israel up until this day, but I have been from tent to tent and from one tabernacle to another.

**17:6** In all the places where I walked about among all Israel, did I speak a word with any one of the judges of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd My people, saying, "Why have you not built a house of cedars for Me?"'

**17:7** Now then, thus you shall say to My servant David, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: It was I who took you from the pasture, from behind the flock, to become ruler over My people Israel.

**17:8** And I have been with you everywhere you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a name like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

**17:9** I will appoint a place for My people Israel and plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no longer; nor will the sons of wickedness wear them out anymore, as at the first,

**17:10** even from the days when I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will subdue all your enemies. Moreover, I declare to you that the LORD will build a house for you.

**17:11** And it will happen, when your days are fulfilled to go to be with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who will be one of your sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

**17:12** He is the one who will build a house for Me, and I will establish his throne forever.

**17:13** I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to Me; and I will not remove My loyal love from him, as I removed it from the one who was before you.

**17:14** And I will confirm him in My house and in My kingdom forever, and his throne will be established forever.'"

**17:15** In accordance with all these words and in accordance with all this vision, thus Nathan spoke to David.

**17:16** Then King David came in and sat before the LORD, and he said, "Who am I, LORD God, and what is my house, that You have brought me to this point?

**17:17** Yet even this was small in Your eyes, God, and You have spoken about the house of Your servant for the distant future; and You have looked upon me according to the rank of a man of high standing, LORD God.

**17:18** What more can David add to You for honoring Your servant? For You Yourself know Your servant.

**17:19** LORD, for the sake of Your servant and according to Your own heart, You have performed all this greatness, to make known all the great deeds.

**17:20** LORD, there is no one like You, and there is no God apart from You, in everything that we have heard with our ears.

**17:21** And who is like Your people Israel, the one nation on the earth whom God went to redeem for Himself as a people, to make for Yourself a name of great and awesome deeds, by driving out nations from before Your people whom You redeemed from Egypt?

**17:22** And You appointed Your people Israel to be a people for Yourself forever, and You, LORD, have become their God.

**17:23** And now, LORD, let the word that You have spoken concerning Your servant and concerning his house be confirmed forever, and do just as You have spoken.

**17:24** Let Your name be confirmed and become great forever, saying, 'The LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, is God to Israel,' and the house of David Your servant will be established before You.

**17:25** For You, my God, have uncovered the ear of Your servant that You will build a house for him; therefore Your servant has found boldness to pray before You.

**17:26** And now, LORD, You Yourself are God, and You have spoken this good thing concerning Your servant.

**17:27** And now You have been willing to bless the house of Your servant, so that it may remain before You forever; for You, LORD, have blessed it, and it is blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:5** — Hebrew reads literally "from tent to tent and from a tabernacle" (u-mi-mishkan), an elliptical expression parallel to "from tent to tent" implying "from one tabernacle to another."
- **17:9** — The verb le-valloto is the Piel infinitive construct of balah ("to wear out, waste away"), distinctive to Chronicles (compare 2 Samuel 7:10, "to afflict").
- **17:17** — The Hebrew clause u-re'itani ke-tor ha-'adam ha-ma'alah is syntactically obscure, literally "and You have looked upon me according to the order/rank of the man, the ascent/high degree," rendered here following the Qal vocalization of ra'ah ("looked upon").
- **17:18** — Hebrew le-khavod 'et-'avdekha uses the noun kavod ("honor") with a direct object marker ('et), functioning verbally like an infinitive ("for honoring Your servant").
- **17:25** — Hebrew matsa' 'avdekha uses matsa' ("found") absolutely without an explicit direct object, idiomatically meaning "found boldness/courage" to pray.
