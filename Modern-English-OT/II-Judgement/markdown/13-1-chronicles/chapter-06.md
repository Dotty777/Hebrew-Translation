## Translator Notes

- **6:11** — The Hebrew Qere reads 'sons of' (bene), translated here, whereas the Ketiv has 'his son' (beno).
- **6:13** — The Masoretic Text reads 'the firstborn Vashni, and Abijah,' omitting Joel (cf. 6:18; 1 Samuel 8:2); 'Vashni' is widely thought to reflect 'and the second' after the name Joel dropped out.
- **6:16** — 'Stationed' renders the Hiphil of amad ('cause to stand,' hence appoint or station), echoing the Qal forms of the same root in 6:17, 18, and 24. 'Over the direction of song' translates the Hebrew idiom al-yede-shir, literally 'over the hands of song.'
- **6:20** — The Hebrew Qere reads 'Zuph' (tsuf, matching 1 Samuel 1:1), translated here, whereas the Ketiv has 'Ziph' (tsif).
- **6:33** — 'Were given' renders the Qal passive participle of natan ('give,' netunim), reflecting the Levites' dedication to sanctuary service as in Numbers 3:9 and 8:16.
- **6:34** — 'Burned offerings' translates the Hiphil participle of qatar ('turn into smoke'), a term covering both animal sacrifices on the altar of burnt offering and incense on the altar of incense.
- **6:42** — The Hebrew text uses the plural 'cities of refuge' (are hammiqlat) here and in 6:52, even though only Hebron (here) and Shechem (in 6:52) were designated cities of refuge in these lists (cf. Joshua 21:13, 21).
- **6:43** — The Masoretic Text here reads 'Hilez' (with final zayin), whereas many Hebrew manuscripts and editions read 'Hilen' (cf. Holon in Joshua 21:15).
- **6:46** — The Hebrew text reads literally 'from the clan of the tribe, from the half-tribe, the half of Manasseh,' repeating 'half' with two synonyms (machatsit and chatsi) and omitting the names of Ephraim and Dan (cf. 6:51-55; Joshua 21:5).
