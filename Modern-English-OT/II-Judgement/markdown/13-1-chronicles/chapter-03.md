## Text

**3:1** And these were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelite; the second, Daniel, by Abigail the Carmelite;

**3:2** the third, Absalom the son of Maacah, the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur; the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith;

**3:3** the fifth, Shephatiah, by Abital; the sixth, Ithream, by Eglah his wife.

**3:4** Six were born to him in Hebron, and he reigned there seven years and six months; and he reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**3:5** And these were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bath-shua the daughter of Ammiel;

**3:6** and Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet,

**3:7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**3:8** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet—nine.

**3:9** All these were the sons of David, apart from the sons of concubines; and Tamar was their sister.

**3:10** Now the son of Solomon was Rehoboam, Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

**3:11** Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,

**3:12** Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,

**3:13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

**3:14** Amon his son, Josiah his son.

**3:15** And the sons of Josiah: the firstborn was Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum.

**3:16** And the sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son.

**3:17** And the sons of Jeconiah the captive: Shealtiel his son,

**3:18** and Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.

**3:19** And the sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei. And the sons of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith was their sister;

**3:20** and Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed—five.

**3:21** And the sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah; the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shecaniah.

**3:22** And the sons of Shecaniah: Shemaiah. And the sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat—six.

**3:23** And the sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hezekiah, and Azrikam—three.

**3:24** And the sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani—seven.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — The Hebrew prefixes the preposition le- to Absalom, functioning here as an emphatic or itemizing marker typical of Late Biblical Hebrew lists rather than a preposition of parentage.
- **3:17** — Masoretic accentuation connects assir with Jeconiah as an epithet, 'Jeconiah the captive'; alternatively, it may be read as a personal name, 'Assir, with Shealtiel his son.'
- **3:21** — The Masoretic text repeats the plural construct 'sons of' four times here, introducing four Davidic family lines without specifying their generational link to Hananiah.
- **3:22** — The Masoretic text gives the total 'six' while naming five sons of Shemaiah, either counting Shemaiah together with his five sons or reflecting a name not preserved in the list.
- **3:24** — Ketiv writes a variant orthography (hwdywhw), while Qere reads Hodavyahu ('Hodaviah').
