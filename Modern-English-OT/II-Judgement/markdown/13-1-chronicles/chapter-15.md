## Text

**15:1** He built houses for himself in the City of David, prepared a place for the ark of God, and pitched a tent for it.

**15:2** Then David said, "No one may carry the ark of God except the Levites, because the LORD chose them to carry the ark of the LORD and to minister to Him forever."

**15:3** David convened all Israel to Jerusalem to bring up the ark of the LORD to its place, which he had prepared for it.

**15:4** Then David gathered the sons of Aaron and the Levites:

**15:5** Of the sons of Kohath: Uriel the leader, and his brothers, one hundred twenty.

**15:6** Of the sons of Merari: Asaiah the leader, and his brothers, two hundred twenty.

**15:7** Of the sons of Gershom: Joel the leader, and his brothers, one hundred thirty.

**15:8** Of the sons of Elizaphan: Shemaiah the leader, and his brothers, two hundred.

**15:9** Of the sons of Hebron: Eliel the leader, and his brothers, eighty.

**15:10** Of the sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the leader, and his brothers, one hundred twelve.

**15:11** Then David summoned Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites: Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.

**15:12** He said to them, "You are the heads of the fathers' families of the Levites; consecrate yourselves, you and your brothers, and bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, to the place I have prepared for it.

**15:13** Because it was not you the first time, the LORD our God broke out against us, since we did not seek Him according to the ordinance."

**15:14** Then the priests and the Levites consecrated themselves in order to bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**15:15** The sons of the Levites carried the ark of God, just as Moses had commanded according to the word of the LORD, on their shoulders with the poles upon them.

**15:16** And David told the leaders of the Levites to appoint their brothers the singers with musical instruments—harps, lyres, and cymbals—sounding aloud to lift up sounds of joy.

**15:17** So the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel; from his brothers, Asaph son of Berechiah; and from the sons of Merari their brothers, Ethan son of Kushaiah.

**15:18** With them were their brothers of the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, the gatekeepers.

**15:19** The singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to sound aloud with bronze cymbals;

**15:20** Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play harps according to Alamoth;

**15:21** and Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to lead with lyres according to the Sheminith.

**15:22** Chenaniah, leader of the Levites in music, gave instruction in the music, because he was skilled.

**15:23** Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark.

**15:24** Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, were blowing the trumpets before the ark of God; and Obed-Edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the ark.

**15:25** So David, together with the elders of Israel and the commanders of thousands, went to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the house of Obed-Edom with joy.

**15:26** Now when God helped the Levites carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.

**15:27** David was wrapped in a robe of fine linen, along with all the Levites carrying the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah, leader of the music of the singers; and upon David was a linen ephod.

**15:28** Thus all Israel was bringing up the ark of the covenant of the LORD with shouting, with the sound of the ram's horn, with trumpets, and with cymbals, sounding aloud on harps and lyres.

**15:29** When the ark of the covenant of the LORD came to the City of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked down through the window; she saw King David dancing and making merry, and she despised him in her heart.

## Translator Notes

- **15:12** — Hebrew el-hakinoti lo attaches the definite article as a relative marker to the Hiphil perfect verb ("to [the place] that I have prepared for it"), an elliptical Late Biblical Hebrew construction omitting the antecedent noun "place."
- **15:13** — Hebrew lemabbarishonah is a rare compound preposition-adverb meaning "at the first time," followed by the elliptical clause lo attem ("not you," i.e., you were not the ones carrying the ark).
- **15:20** — Alamoth (literally "young women" or "maidens") and the Sheminith in 15:21 (literally "the eighth") are Hebrew musical terms of uncertain technical meaning, likely indicating pitch registers, tunings, or musical modes (compare the superscriptions of Psalms 6, 12, and 46).
- **15:22** — Hebrew massa ("lifting up") can denote either carrying the ark or lifting the voice in music/singing (compare 15:27, where it is paired with "the singers"); yasor is the Qal infinitive absolute of yasar, here used for instructing or directing.
- **15:24** — Ketiv reads the quadriliteral denominative participle mhtstsrim ("trumpeting," from chatsotserah, "trumpet"), whereas Qere reads the Hiphil participle machtserim from the shortened triliteral root chtsr; both mean "blowing trumpets."
