## Text

**4:1** The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.

**4:2** And Reaiah the son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the clans of the Zorathites.

**4:3** And these were of the father of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash; and the name of their sister was Hazzelelponi.

**4:4** And Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.

**4:5** And Ashhur, the father of Tekoa, had two wives: Helah and Naarah.

**4:6** And Naarah bore to him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.

**4:7** And the sons of Helah: Zereth, Zohar, and Ethnan.

**4:8** And Koz fathered Anub and Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel the son of Harum.

**4:9** And Jabez was more honored than his brothers, and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, "Because I gave birth in pain."

**4:10** And Jabez called out to the God of Israel, saying, "If only You would indeed bless me and increase my territory, and Your hand would be with me, and You would preserve me from harm so that it causes me no pain!" And God brought about what he asked.

**4:11** And Chelub, the brother of Shuhah, fathered Mehir; he was the father of Eshton.

**4:12** And Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah.

**4:13** And the sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah. And the sons of Othniel: Hathath.

**4:14** And Meonothai fathered Ophrah. And Seraiah fathered Joab, the father of the Valley of Craftsmen, for they were craftsmen.

**4:15** And the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah, and Naam. And the sons of Elah: Kenaz.

**4:16** And the sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.

**4:17** And the sons of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. And she conceived Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, the father of Eshtemoa.

**4:18** And his Judean wife bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. And these were the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered married.

**4:19** And the sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham: the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

**4:20** And the sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

**4:21** The sons of Shelah the son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the clans of the house of fine linen workers at Beth-ashbea;

**4:22** and Jokim, and the men of Cozeba, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled over Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. And these accounts are ancient.

**4:23** These were the potters and those who lived in Netaim and Gederah; they lived there with the king in his work.

**4:24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul;

**4:25** Shallum was his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

**4:26** And the sons of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.

**4:27** Now Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers did not have many sons, and their entire clan did not multiply as much as the sons of Judah.

**4:28** And they lived in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual,

**4:29** in Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,

**4:30** in Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag,

**4:31** in Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. These were their cities until David reigned.

**4:32** And their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan, five cities,

**4:33** and all their villages that were around these cities as far as Baal. These were their settlements, and they had their own genealogical record.

**4:34** Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah the son of Amaziah,

**4:35** Joel, Jehu the son of Joshibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,

**4:36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,

**4:37** and Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah.

**4:38** These who are listed by name were leaders in their clans, and their fathers' houses expanded greatly.

**4:39** And they went to the entrance of Gedor, as far as the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.

**4:40** And they found rich and good pasture, and the land was wide-open, tranquil, and peaceful; for those who had lived there formerly were from Ham.

**4:41** And these who were recorded by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and struck their tents and the Meunites who were found there, and devoted them to destruction to this day, and lived in their place, because there was pasture for their flocks there.

**4:42** And some of them, from the sons of Simeon, five hundred men, went to Mount Seir, with Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, at their head.

**4:43** And they struck down the remnant of the survivors of Amalek, and they have lived there to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The Masoretic text literally reads 'and these, the father of Etam' (avi Etam), keeping the singular title 'father of' rather than 'sons of.'
- **4:7** — Ketiv writes ytzhr ('Izhar' or 'Jezohar') without a conjunction, while Qere reads ve-Tzohar ('and Zohar').
- **4:10** — Hebrew im introduces an optative wish ('If only'), ve-asita me-ra'ah is an elliptical idiom literally meaning 'and You would act away from harm,' and otzbi ('cause me pain') plays on the name Jabez and the noun 'pain' in 4:9.
- **4:17** — The feminine verb 'and she conceived' (va-tahar) refers to an unnamed wife in this verse who is identified at the end of 4:18 as Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh.
- **4:20** — Ketiv writes wtwlwn ('and Tolon'), while Qere reads ve-Tilon ('and Tilon').
- **4:41** — Ketiv writes hm'ynym with yod, while Qere reads ha-Me'unim ('the Meunites').
