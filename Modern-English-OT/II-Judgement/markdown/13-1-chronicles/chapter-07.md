## Text

**7:1** Now as for the sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, four.

**7:2** And the sons of Tola: Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel, heads of their fathers' houses, of Tola, valiant warriors by their generations; their number in the days of David was twenty-two thousand six hundred.

**7:3** And the sons of Uzzi: Izrahiah. And the sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah, five; all of them were heads.

**7:4** And along with them, by their generations, according to their fathers' houses, were troops of an army for war, thirty-six thousand, because they had numerous wives and sons.

**7:5** And their relatives across all the clans of Issachar were valiant warriors, eighty-seven thousand by their genealogical enrollment in total.

**7:6** Benjamin: Bela, Becher, and Jediael, three.

**7:7** And the sons of Bela: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri, five, heads of fathers' houses, valiant warriors; and their genealogical enrollment was twenty-two thousand thirty-four.

**7:8** And the sons of Becher: Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All of these were sons of Becher.

**7:9** And their genealogical enrollment by their generations, heads of their fathers' houses, valiant warriors, was twenty thousand two hundred.

**7:10** And the sons of Jediael: Bilhan. And the sons of Bilhan: Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

**7:11** All of these were sons of Jediael, according to the ancestral heads, valiant warriors, seventeen thousand two hundred going out in the army for war.

**7:12** And Shuppim and Huppim were sons of Ir; Hushim, the sons of Aher.

**7:13** The sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum, sons of Bilhah.

**7:14** The sons of Manasseh: Asriel, whom she bore; his Aramean concubine bore Machir, the father of Gilead.

**7:15** And Machir took a wife for Huppim and for Shuppim, and his sister's name was Maacah; and the second one's name was Zelophehad, and Zelophehad had daughters.

**7:16** And Maacah, Machir's wife, bore a son, and she called his name Peresh; his brother's name was Sheresh, and his sons were Ulam and Rakem.

**7:17** And the sons of Ulam: Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh.

**7:18** And his sister Hammolecheth bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah.

**7:19** Now the sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

**7:20** And the sons of Ephraim: Shuthelah, and Bered his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, Tahath his son,

**7:21** and Zabad his son, Shuthelah his son, and Ezer and Elead. And the men of Gath born in the land killed them, for they had gone down to take their livestock.

**7:22** And Ephraim their father mourned for many days, and his brothers came to console him.

**7:23** And he went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son; and he called his name Beriah, for calamity was in his house.

**7:24** And his daughter was Sheerah, and she built Beth-horon the Lower and the Upper, and Uzzen-sheerah.

**7:25** And Rephah was his son, and Resheph, and Telah his son, and Tahan his son,

**7:26** Ladan his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son,

**7:27** Non his son, Joshua his son.

**7:28** And their possession and their dwelling places were Bethel and its villages, and toward the east Naaran, and toward the west Gezer and its villages, and Shechem and its villages as far as Ayyah and its villages;

**7:29** and alongside the sons of Manasseh were Beth-shean and its villages, Taanach and its villages, Megiddo and its villages, and Dor and its villages. In these the sons of Joseph, son of Israel, lived.

**7:30** The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah, and their sister Serah.

**7:31** And the sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel; he was the father of Birzaith.

**7:32** And Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer, and Hotham, and Shua their sister.

**7:33** And the sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were sons of Japhlet.

**7:34** And the sons of Shemer: Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram.

**7:35** And the sons of Helem his brother: Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.

**7:36** The sons of Zophah: Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah,

**7:37** Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera.

**7:38** And the sons of Jether: Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.

**7:39** And the sons of Ulla: Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia.

**7:40** All of these were sons of Asher, heads of the fathers' houses, chosen, valiant warriors, heads of the leaders; and as for their genealogical enrollment in the army for war, their number was twenty-six thousand men.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Following the qere Jashub; the ketiv writes Jashib.
- **7:10** — Following the qere Jeush; the ketiv writes Jeish.
- **7:14** — The Masoretic pausal accent divides the verse after 'whom she bore,' leaving Asriel's mother unnamed before introducing Manasseh's Aramean concubine as the mother of Machir; some take the concubine as the subject of both verbs.
- **7:23** — The name Beriah plays on the Hebrew phrase bera'ah, 'in calamity,' where the preposition 'in' is retained before the noun for the sake of the wordplay.
- **7:31** — Following the qere Birzaith; the ketiv writes Birzoth.
- **7:34** — Following the qere forms Rohgah and Hubbah; the ketiv spells Rohgah with a vowel letter waw and writes Jehubbah without the conjunction 'and.'
- **7:35** — Hebrew literally reads the singular 'son of Helem' before listing four sons, likely used collectively or as an abbreviated form of 'sons of.'
