## Text

**16:1** They brought the ark of God in and set it inside the tent that David had pitched for it, and they presented burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**16:2** David finished offering up the burnt offering and the peace offerings, and he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.

**16:3** He distributed to every Israelite, both man and woman, to each person a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a raisin cake.

**16:4** He appointed some of the Levites as ministers before the ark of the LORD—to commemorate, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel:

**16:5** Asaph was the head, and second to him was Zechariah, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, with harps and lyres, while Asaph sounded aloud with cymbals;

**16:6** and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests were continually with trumpets before the ark of the covenant of God.

**16:7** On that day David first committed the giving of thanks to the LORD into the hand of Asaph and his brothers:

**16:8** Give thanks to the LORD, call on His name, and make His deeds known among the peoples.

**16:9** Sing to Him, make music to Him; speak of all His wondrous deeds.

**16:10** Boast in His holy name; let the heart of those seeking the LORD rejoice.

**16:11** Seek the LORD and His strength; earnestly seek His face continually.

**16:12** Remember His wondrous deeds that He has done, His marvels, and the judgments of His mouth,

**16:13** offspring of Israel, His servant, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**16:14** He is the LORD our God; throughout all the earth are His judgments.

**16:15** Remember forever His covenant, the word He commanded for a thousand generations,

**16:16** the one He made with Abraham, and His oath to Isaac;

**16:17** and He established it for Jacob as a statute, for Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**16:18** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan, the allotted portion of your inheritance."

**16:19** When you were few in number, very few and sojourning in it,

**16:20** they roamed from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.

**16:21** He let no man oppress them, and He reproved kings for their sake:

**16:22** "Do not touch My anointed ones, and to My prophets do no harm."

**16:23** Sing to the LORD, all the earth! Proclaim the good news of His salvation from day to day.

**16:25** For the LORD is great and exceedingly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods.

**16:26** For all the gods of the peoples are empty idols, yet the LORD made the heavens.

**16:27** Majesty and splendor are before Him; strength and gladness are in His place.

**16:28** Ascribe to the LORD, you clans of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength!

**16:29** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name; bring an offering and come before Him; bow down to the LORD in holy splendor.

**16:30** Tremble before Him, all the earth! Surely the world is established; it will not be shaken.

**16:31** Let the heavens rejoice and the earth exult; let them say among the nations, "The LORD reigns!"

**16:32** Let the sea thunder and all its fullness; let the field be jubilant and all that is in it.

**16:33** Then the trees of the forest will shout for joy before the LORD, because He comes to judge the earth.

**16:34** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; for His faithful love endures forever.

**16:35** And say, "Save us, God of our salvation; gather us and rescue us from the nations, to give thanks to Your holy name and to glory in Your praise."

**16:36** Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting! And all the people said, "Amen," and praised the LORD.

**16:37** So he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark continually, according to each day's requirement;

**16:38** also Obed-Edom and their brothers, sixty-eight, with Obed-Edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah as gatekeepers;

**16:39** and Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests before the tabernacle of the LORD at the high place that was in Gibeon,

**16:40** to offer up burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, and according to everything written in the law of the LORD, which He commanded Israel.

**16:41** With them were Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest of the selected ones who were designated by name to give thanks to the LORD, for His faithful love endures forever.

**16:42** With them—Heman and Jeduthun—were trumpets and cymbals for those sounding aloud, and instruments for the song of God; and the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.

**16:43** Then all the people went away, each to his house, and David turned back to bless his household.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The exact meaning of the rare Hebrew noun eshpar (occurring only here and in 2 Samuel 6:19) is uncertain; it is traditionally rendered "a portion of meat," though some scholars propose "a date cake" in parallel with "raisin cake."
- **16:15** — The Masoretic text of 1 Chronicles 16:15 reads the Qal imperative zikru ("Remember!"), whereas the parallel in Psalm 105:8 reads the Qal perfect zakar ("He remembers").
- **16:19** — The Masoretic text reads the second-person plural suffix bihyotekem ("When you were"), shifting to third-person plural in 16:20; the parallel in Psalm 105:12 reads third-person plural ("When they were").
- **16:36** — Hebrew wehallel is the Piel infinitive absolute of halal continuing the preceding finite verb ("and praised the LORD"), a characteristic Late Biblical Hebrew construction.
- **16:42** — The Masoretic text places "Heman and Jeduthun" in apposition to the pronoun suffix on "with them" ("with them—Heman and Jeduthun—were trumpets and cymbals..."), rather than omitting the two names as in the Septuagint.
