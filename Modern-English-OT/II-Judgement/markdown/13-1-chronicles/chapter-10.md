## Text

**10:1** Now the Philistines fought against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before the Philistines and fell dead on Mount Gilboa.

**10:2** And the Philistines pressed hard after Saul and after his sons, and the Philistines struck down Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchi-shua, the sons of Saul.

**10:3** And the battle grew heavy against Saul, and those shooting with the bow found him, and he writhed in pain from the archers.

**10:4** Then Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me through with it, or else these uncircumcised men will come and treat me cruelly." But his armor-bearer was unwilling, because he was deeply afraid. So Saul took the sword and fell upon it.

**10:5** When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he too fell upon the sword and died.

**10:6** So Saul and his three sons died, and all his household died together.

**10:7** When all the men of Israel who were in the valley saw that they had fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities and fled; and the Philistines came and settled in them.

**10:8** Now on the next day, the Philistines came to strip the dead, and they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

**10:9** And they stripped him and took his head and his armor, and sent word all around the land of the Philistines to proclaim the good news to their idols and to the people.

**10:10** And they placed his armor in the house of their gods, and they fastened his skull in the house of Dagon.

**10:11** And all Jabesh-gilead heard everything that the Philistines had done to Saul.

**10:12** So all the valiant men arose and took away the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons and brought them to Jabesh. And they buried their bones under the terebinth in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

**10:13** So Saul died for his unfaithfulness, in that he acted unfaithfully against the LORD concerning the word of the LORD which he did not keep, and also for consulting a medium to make inquiry,

**10:14** and he did not inquire of the LORD. So He put him to death and turned the kingship to David son of Jesse.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — Hebrew wayyachel is a Qal wayyiqtol of chul/chil ('writhe in pain' or 'tremble in anguish'); in context the anguish may imply that he was wounded.
- **10:12** — Hebrew elah denotes a terebinth tree rather than the tamarisk in the parallel account of 1 Samuel 31:13, and gufah ('body' or 'corpse') appears in Biblical Hebrew only in this verse.
- **10:13** — The infinitive lishol ('to consult' or 'ask') plays on the name Saul (Shaul) from the same root, while lidrosh ('to make inquiry') contrasts directly with v. 14 ('did not inquire of the LORD').
