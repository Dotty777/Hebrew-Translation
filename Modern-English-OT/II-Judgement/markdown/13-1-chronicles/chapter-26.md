## Text

**26:1** For the divisions of the gatekeepers: of the Korahites, Meshelemiah the son of Kore, from the sons of Asaph.

**26:2** And Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,

**26:3** Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, and Eliehoenai the seventh.

**26:4** And Obed-edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sacar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

**26:5** Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peullethai the eighth, because God blessed him.

**26:6** And to his son Shemaiah sons were born who were rulers over their father's house, because they were mighty men of ability.

**26:7** The sons of Shemaiah were Othni, Rephael, Obed, Elzabad, and his brothers, men of ability: Elihu and Semachiah.

**26:8** All these were from the sons of Obed-edom; they and their sons and their brothers were men of ability with strength for the service, sixty-two belonging to Obed-edom.

**26:9** And Meshelemiah had sons and brothers, men of ability, eighteen.

**26:10** And Hosah, from the sons of Merari, had sons: Shimri was the head; though he was not the firstborn, his father made him head.

**26:11** Hilkiah was the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. All the sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.

**26:12** To these divisions of the gatekeepers, to the head men, belonged duties corresponding to their brothers, to minister in the house of the LORD.

**26:13** And they cast lots, the small just like the great, according to their fathers' houses, for each gate.

**26:14** The lot to the east fell to Shelemiah. And for his son Zechariah, a counselor with insight, they cast lots, and his lot came out to the north.

**26:15** For Obed-edom, to the south; and for his sons, the house of the storehouses.

**26:16** For Shuppim and for Hosah, to the west, with the Gate of Shallecheth on the ascending highway, watch corresponding to watch.

**26:17** On the east there were six Levites, on the north four each day, on the south four each day, and at the storehouses two by two.

**26:18** For the Parbar on the west, four were at the highway and two at the Parbar.

**26:19** These were the divisions of the gatekeepers of the sons of the Korahites and of the sons of Merari.

**26:20** As for the Levites, Ahijah was over the treasuries of the house of God and over the treasuries of the dedicated things.

**26:21** The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonites of Ladan, the heads of the fathers' houses of Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.

**26:22** The sons of Jehieli: Zetham and Joel his brother were over the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

**26:23** Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:

**26:24** Shebuel the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was ruler over the treasuries.

**26:25** And his brothers through Eliezer were Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.

**26:26** This Shelomoth and his brothers were over all the treasuries of the dedicated things that King David, the heads of the fathers' houses, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the commanders of the army had dedicated.

**26:27** From the wars and from the plunder they dedicated them to maintain the house of the LORD.

**26:28** And all that Samuel the seer, Saul the son of Kish, Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Zeruiah had dedicated—whatever anyone had dedicated was under the charge of Shelomith and his brothers.

**26:29** Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were for the outside work over Israel, as officers and judges.

**26:30** Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brothers, one thousand seven hundred men of ability, were over the administration of Israel west of the Jordan, for all the work of the LORD and for the service of the king.

**26:31** Of the Hebronites, Jerijah was the head of the Hebronites according to their generations by fathers' houses. In the fortieth year of David's reign they were sought out, and mighty men of ability were found among them at Jazer in Gilead.

**26:32** And his brothers, men of ability, were two thousand seven hundred, heads of fathers' houses; and King David appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of the Manassites, for every matter of God and every matter of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **26:6** — 'Who were rulers' renders the plural of the noun mimshal ('dominion' or 'rule'), used concretely here for rulers.
- **26:18** — 'The Parbar' transliterates the rare Hebrew term happarbar (likely a loanword), referring to a colonnade, annex, or western precinct of the temple.
- **26:20** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads the personal name 'Ahijah' after the topical heading 'As for the Levites,' rather than 'their brothers' as in the Septuagint.
- **26:25** — Ketiv reads 'Shelomoth' (matching the spelling in v. 26), while the translated Qere reads 'Shelomith' (matching v. 28).
- **26:28** — 'All that ... had dedicated' uses the definite article ha- as a relative pronoun before the Hiphil perfect verb hiqdish (a Late Biblical Hebrew feature), and 'whatever anyone had dedicated' renders the Hiphil participle hammaqdish used elliptically.
