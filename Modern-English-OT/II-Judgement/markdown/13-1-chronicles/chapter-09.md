## Text

**9:1** Now all Israel was registered by genealogy, and look, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel; and Judah was exiled to Babylon because of their unfaithfulness.

**9:2** And the first inhabitants who were in their possession in their cities were Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the temple servants.

**9:3** And in Jerusalem lived some of the sons of Judah, some of the sons of Benjamin, and some of the sons of Ephraim and Manasseh:

**9:4** Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, from the sons of Perez son of Judah.

**9:5** And from the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.

**9:6** And from the sons of Zerah: Jeuel, and their brothers, six hundred and ninety.

**9:7** And from the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah;

**9:8** and Ibneiah son of Jeroham, and Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri, and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah.

**9:9** And their brothers, according to their generations, were nine hundred and fifty-six. All these men were heads of fathers' houses for their fathers' households.

**9:10** And from the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin;

**9:11** and Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, the ruler of the house of God;

**9:12** and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer;

**9:13** and their brothers, heads of their fathers' houses, one thousand seven hundred and sixty, mighty men of ability for the work of the service of the house of God.

**9:14** And from the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, from the sons of Merari;

**9:15** and Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph;

**9:16** and Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of the Netophathites.

**9:17** Now the gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, and Ahiman; and their brother Shallum was the chief.

**9:18** And until now, at the king's gate on the east, they were the gatekeepers for the camps of the sons of Levi.

**9:19** Now Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his brothers of his father's house, the Korahites, were over the work of the service, keepers of the thresholds of the tent; and their fathers had been over the camp of the LORD, keepers of the entrance.

**9:20** And Phinehas son of Eleazar was ruler over them in former times; the LORD was with him.

**9:21** Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the doorway of the tent of meeting.

**9:22** All those selected as gatekeepers at the thresholds numbered two hundred and twelve. They were registered by genealogy in their villages; David and Samuel the seer established them in their position of trust.

**9:23** And they and their sons were over the gates of the house of the LORD, the house of the tent, by watches.

**9:24** The gatekeepers were on the four sides: east, west, north, and south.

**9:25** And their brothers in their villages were to come in every seven days, from time to time, to be with these;

**9:26** for the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were in a position of trust, and they were over the chambers and over the treasuries of the house of God.

**9:27** And they spent the night around the house of God, because the guard duty was upon them, and they were in charge of the key, morning by morning.

**9:28** And some of them were over the vessels of the service, for they brought them in by count and took them out by count.

**9:29** And others of them were appointed over the vessels and over all the holy vessels, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices.

**9:30** And some of the sons of the priests compounded the mixture of the spices.

**9:31** And Mattithiah, one of the Levites, who was the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, was in a position of trust over the making of the griddle cakes.

**9:32** And some of the sons of the Kohathites, from among their brothers, were over the rows of bread, to prepare it Sabbath by Sabbath.

**9:33** Now these were the singers, heads of fathers' houses of the Levites, who were in the chambers, exempt from other duties, because day and night they were on duty in the work.

**9:34** These were the heads of the fathers' houses of the Levites according to their generations, chief men; these lived in Jerusalem.

**9:35** And in Gibeon lived the father of Gibeon, Jeiel, and the name of his wife was Maacah.

**9:36** And his firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,

**9:37** Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth.

**9:38** And Mikloth fathered Shimeam. And they also lived opposite their brothers in Jerusalem, with their brothers.

**9:39** And Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.

**9:40** And the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal, and Meri-baal fathered Micah.

**9:41** And the sons of Micah were Pithon, Melech, and Tahrea.

**9:42** And Ahaz fathered Jarah, and Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Moza.

**9:43** And Moza fathered Binea, and Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

**9:44** And Azel had six sons, and these are their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — Translates the qere min-benei, meaning 'from the sons of'; the ketiv has transposed consonants spelling Binyamin ('Benjamin') before Perez.
- **9:17** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes achihem as singular ('their brother') and joins it by accentuation to Shallum, though some understand it collectively as 'their relatives; Shallum was the chief.'
- **9:22** — Hebrew emunah literally means 'faithfulness' and is used here and in vv. 26 and 31 for a permanent position of trust or entrusted charge.
- **9:33** — Translates the qere peturim, the Qal passive participle of patar ('exempt' or 'released'); the ketiv writes the variant form petirim with the same sense.
- **9:35** — Translates the qere Yeiel ('Jeiel'); the ketiv writes Yeuel ('Jeuel').
- **9:41** — The Masoretic Text lists three sons of Micah here without naming Ahaz, who introduces the next generation in v. 42.
