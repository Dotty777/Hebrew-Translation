## Text

**21:1** Then Satan rose up against Israel and incited David to count Israel.

**21:2** So David said to Joab and to the leaders of the people, "Go, count Israel from Beersheba even to Dan, and bring me back word so that I may know their number."

**21:3** Joab replied, "May the LORD add to His people a hundred times as many as they are! My lord the king, are they not all servants of my lord? Why does my lord seek this thing? Why should he become a source of guilt for Israel?"

**21:4** Yet the word of the king prevailed against Joab. So Joab went out and traveled throughout all Israel, and came to Jerusalem.

**21:5** Joab gave the number of the enrollment of the people to David. In all Israel there were one million one hundred thousand men who drew the sword, and in Judah there were four hundred seventy thousand men who drew the sword.

**21:6** But he did not enroll Levi and Benjamin among them, because the word of the king was abhorrent to Joab.

**21:7** This thing was evil in the eyes of God, and He struck Israel.

**21:8** David said to God, "I have sinned greatly in doing this thing. Now, please remove the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly."

**21:9** Then the LORD spoke to Gad, David's seer, saying,

**21:10** "Go and speak to David, saying, 'This is what the LORD says: I am holding out three things before you; choose one of them for yourself, and I will do it to you.'"

**21:11** So Gad came to David and said to him, "This is what the LORD says: 'Take for yourself,

**21:12** either three years of famine, or three months of being swept away before your foes while the sword of your enemies overtakes you, or three days of the sword of the LORD and pestilence in the land, with the angel of the LORD bringing destruction throughout all the territory of Israel.' Now see what word I should return to Him who sent me."

**21:13** David said to Gad, "I am in severe distress. Please, let me fall into the hand of the LORD, because His mercies are exceedingly great, and into the hand of man let me not fall."

**21:14** Then the LORD sent pestilence upon Israel, and seventy thousand men from Israel fell.

**21:15** And God sent an angel to Jerusalem to destroy it; but as he was destroying, the LORD looked and relented over the calamity, and He said to the destroying angel, "Enough! Now let your hand drop." Now the angel of the LORD was standing beside the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**21:16** David lifted up his eyes and saw the angel of the LORD standing between the earth and the heavens, with his drawn sword in his hand stretched out over Jerusalem. Then David and the elders, covered in sackcloth, fell down on their faces.

**21:17** David said to God, "Was it not I who ordered the people to be counted? Indeed, I am the one who has sinned and done great evil. But these sheep, what have they done? LORD my God, please let Your hand be against me and against my father's house, and not against Your people to bring a plague."

**21:18** Then the angel of the LORD instructed Gad to tell David that David should go up to erect an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**21:19** So David went up at Gad's word, which he had spoken in the name of the LORD.

**21:20** Now Ornan turned around and saw the angel, and his four sons with him hid themselves, while Ornan was threshing wheat.

**21:21** When David came up to Ornan, Ornan looked out and saw David, and he went out from the threshing floor and bowed down to David with his face to the ground.

**21:22** Then David said to Ornan, "Give me the place of the threshing floor so that I may build an altar to the LORD on it. Give it to me for the full price, so that the plague may be restrained from the people."

**21:23** Ornan said to David, "Take it for yourself, and let my lord the king do what is good in his eyes. Look, I give the oxen for the burnt offerings, the threshing sledges for the wood, and the wheat for the grain offering; I give it all."

**21:24** But King David said to Ornan, "No, for I will surely buy it for the full price. I will not take what belongs to you for the LORD, nor offer up a burnt offering without cost."

**21:25** So David gave Ornan six hundred shekels of gold by weight for the place.

**21:26** David built an altar to the LORD there and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings. And he called to the LORD, and He answered him with fire from the heavens upon the altar of burnt offering.

**21:27** Then the LORD commanded the angel, and he returned his sword to its sheath.

**21:28** At that time, when David saw that the LORD had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he sacrificed there.

**21:29** For the tabernacle of the LORD, which Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offering were at that time at the high place in Gibeon.

**21:30** Yet David was unable to go before it to inquire of God, because he was terrified by the sword of the angel of the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — Hebrew satan appears here without the definite article and may be understood either as the proper name "Satan" or as the common noun "an adversary."
- **21:12** — The form le-masseghet combines the preposition le- with a Hiphil feminine singular participle of nasag ("to overtake"), a Late Biblical Hebrew predicative construction agreeing with the feminine noun "sword."
- **21:17** — The paired forms hare'a hare'oti combine the Hiphil infinitive absolute and Hiphil perfect of ra'a' for intensive emphasis ("done great evil").
- **21:30** — Hebrew lephanaw ("before it") refers back to the tabernacle and altar of burnt offering at Gibeon mentioned in 21:29.
