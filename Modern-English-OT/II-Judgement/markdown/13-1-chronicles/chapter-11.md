## Text

**11:1** Then all Israel assembled to David at Hebron, saying, "Look, we are your bone and your flesh.

**11:2** Even in former times, even while Saul was king, you were the one leading Israel out and bringing them in. And the LORD your God said to you, 'You will shepherd My people Israel, and you will be ruler over My people Israel.'"

**11:3** So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the LORD. Then they anointed David as king over Israel, according to the word of the LORD by the hand of Samuel.

**11:4** Then David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus, and there were the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land.

**11:5** The inhabitants of Jebus said to David, "You will not come here." But David seized the stronghold of Zion, which is the city of David.

**11:6** And David said, "Whoever strikes down a Jebusite first will be chief and commander." Joab son of Zeruiah went up first, and he became chief.

**11:7** And David lived in the stronghold; therefore they named it the city of David.

**11:8** And he built the city all around, from the Millo to the surrounding circuit, while Joab restored the remainder of the city.

**11:9** And David grew steadily greater, and the LORD of hosts was with him.

**11:10** Now these are the chiefs of the mighty warriors who belonged to David, who stood strong with him in his kingdom, along with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel.

**11:11** And this is the number of the mighty warriors who belonged to David: Jashobeam son of Hachmoni, chief of the captains; he raised his spear against three hundred and killed them on a single occasion.

**11:12** After him was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite; he was among the three mighty warriors.

**11:13** He was with David at Pas-dammim when the Philistines had assembled there for battle, and a plot of the field was full of barley, and the people fled from the Philistines.

**11:14** Yet they took their stand in the middle of the plot, rescued it, and struck down the Philistines; and the LORD brought about a great deliverance.

**11:15** Then three of the thirty chiefs went down to the rock to David, to the cave of Adullam, while the camp of the Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.

**11:16** David was in the stronghold at that time, and a garrison of the Philistines was in Bethlehem at that time.

**11:17** Then David felt a longing and said, "Who will give me water to drink from the cistern of Bethlehem that is by the gate?"

**11:18** Then the three broke through the camp of the Philistines, drew water from the cistern of Bethlehem that was by the gate, carried it, and brought it to David. Yet David was unwilling to drink it, and he poured it out to the LORD.

**11:19** And he said, "May it be far from me before my God to do this! Should I drink the blood of these men with their lives? For at the risk of their lives they brought it." So he was unwilling to drink it. These things the three mighty warriors did.

**11:20** Now Abishai, the brother of Joab, was chief of the three; and he raised his spear against three hundred and killed them, and he had a name among the three.

**11:21** Of the three, he was doubly honored, and he became their commander; yet he did not attain to the three.

**11:22** Benaiah son of Jehoiada, son of a valiant man, great in deeds, from Kabzeel—he struck down the two lion-like heroes of Moab, and he went down and struck down a lion inside a pit on a day of snow.

**11:23** And he struck down an Egyptian, a man of stature, five cubits tall. In the Egyptian's hand was a spear like a weaver's beam, yet he went down to him with a staff, wrested the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear.

**11:24** These things Benaiah son of Jehoiada did, and he had a name among the three mighty warriors.

**11:25** Indeed, he was honored above the thirty, yet he did not attain to the three; and David placed him over his bodyguard.

## Translator Notes

- **11:8** — Hebrew yechayyeh is the Piel of chayah ("keep alive" or "revive"), used idiomatically here for restoring or rebuilding a ruined city.
- **11:11** — Following the Qere ("the captains" or "the officers," hash-shalishim, plural of shalish); the Ketiv reads "the thirty" (hash-sheloshim).
- **11:20** — Following the Qere ("and he had a name"); the Ketiv writes the negative particle ("and not"). The Masoretic Text reads "the three" in both clauses of this verse.
- **11:21** — Hebrew bash-shenayim ("doubly") literally reads "by the two" or "among the two," a compressed expression distinguishing Abishai's rank from the premier Three.
- **11:22** — Hebrew shene ari'el mo'av literally reads "two Ariels of Moab" (from ari, "lion," and el, "God" or "mighty"), likely denoting two famed lion-like champions of Moab.
