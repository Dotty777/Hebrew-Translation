## Text

**13:1** Then David took counsel with the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, with every leader.

**13:2** And David said to all the assembly of Israel, "If it seems good to you, and if it is from the LORD our God, let us send word far and wide to our kinsmen who remain in all the lands of Israel, and with them to the priests and the Levites in their cities with pasturelands, so that they may gather to us,

**13:3** and let us bring back the ark of our God to ourselves, for we did not inquire of it in the days of Saul."

**13:4** Then all the assembly declared that they would do so, for the matter was right in the eyes of all the people.

**13:5** So David assembled all Israel from the Shihor of Egypt to Lebo-hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim.

**13:6** And David and all Israel went up to Baalah, that is, to Kiriath-jearim, which belongs to Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God, the LORD who sits enthroned above the cherubim, which is called by the Name.

**13:7** And they mounted the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Abinadab, and Uzza and Ahio were driving the cart.

**13:8** And David and all Israel were celebrating before God with all strength, with songs, lyres, harps, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

**13:9** When they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, Uzza reached out his hand to take hold of the ark, for the oxen stumbled.

**13:10** And the anger of the LORD burned against Uzza, and He struck him down because he had reached out his hand to the ark; and he died there before God.

**13:11** And David grew angry because the LORD had broken out in an outbreak against Uzza; and he called that place Perez-uzza, as it is to this day.

**13:12** And David feared God that day, saying, "How can I bring the ark of God to myself?"

**13:13** So David did not move the ark to himself into the city of David, but turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

**13:14** Thus the ark of God remained with the household of Obed-edom in his house for three months, and the LORD blessed the household of Obed-edom and all that belonged to him.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Hebrew nifretsah nishlechah joins two Qal cohortatives asyndetically ("let us break out, let us send") to express sending word far and wide; the root p-r-ts ("break out") recurs as a motif in 13:11 and 14:11.
- **13:6** — The Masoretic clause asher-niqra shem ("which is called by the Name," Niphal perfect 3ms of qara) is elliptical, lacking a pronominal suffix or preposition (unlike the fuller parallel in 2 Sam 6:2).
- **13:12** — The interrogative hekh ("How?") is a late Biblical Hebrew form of ekh attested only here and in Daniel 10:17.
