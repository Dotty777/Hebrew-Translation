## Text

**9:1** Now it happened, when Solomon had finished building the house of the LORD, the house of the king, and every desire of Solomon that he delighted to do,

**9:2** that the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, just as He had appeared to him at Gibeon.

**9:3** The LORD said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea for favor that you pleaded before Me. I have consecrated this house that you built, to set My name there forever, and My eyes and My heart will be there all the days.

**9:4** Now as for you, if you walk before Me as David your father walked, in wholeness of heart and in uprightness, to do according to everything that I have commanded you, and you keep My statutes and My ordinances,

**9:5** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, just as I spoke to David your father, saying, 'You will not have a man cut off from the throne of Israel.'

**9:6** If you indeed turn back from following Me, you and your sons, and do not keep My commandments and My statutes that I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**9:7** then I will cut off Israel from the face of the land that I have given them, and the house that I have consecrated for My name I will send away from My presence, and Israel will become a proverb and a taunt among all the peoples.

**9:8** And as for this house, exalted though it may be, everyone passing by it will be appalled and will hiss, and they will say, 'Why has the LORD done like this to this land and to this house?'

**9:9** And they will say, 'Because they abandoned the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and took hold of other gods and bowed down to them and served them; therefore the LORD has brought upon them all this calamity.'" Once Pharaoh's daughter went up from the city of David to her house that he had built for her, then he built the Millo.

**9:10** Now it happened at the end of twenty years, during which Solomon had built the two houses, the house of the LORD and the house of the king,

**9:11** Hiram king of Tyre having supplied Solomon with cedar timber and cypress timber and with gold according to all his desire, that King Solomon then gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.

**9:12** Hiram went out from Tyre to see the cities that Solomon had given him, and they were not pleasing in his eyes.

**9:13** He said, "What cities are these that you gave me, my brother?" And he called them the land of Cabul, to this day.

**9:14** And Hiram sent to the king one hundred twenty talents of gold.

**9:26** Now King Solomon made a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber, which is beside Eloth on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom.

**9:27** Hiram sent his servants with the fleet, seamen who knew the sea, together with the servants of Solomon.

**9:28** They came to Ophir, took from there four hundred twenty talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "will be exalted" (yihyeh elyon), understood concessively here ("exalted though it may be"; compare 2 Chronicles 7:21). Some ancient versions read "a heap of ruins."
- **9:9** — The final sentence ("Once Pharaoh's daughter... the Millo") is Masoretic 9:24, carried here following the Saints (Greek) verse arrangement. The qere reads the plural "and bowed down," while the ketiv has a singular or defective spelling.
- **9:11** — The Piel verb nissa here means "supplied" or "assisted" with gifts (rather than "lifted up"). After the adverb az ("then"), the imperfect verb yitten has past narrative force ("then ... gave").
