## Text

**18:1** Now it happened after many days that the word of the LORD came to Elijah in the third year, saying, "Go, present yourself to Ahab, and I will give rain on the face of the ground."

**18:2** So Elijah went to present himself to Ahab. Now the famine was severe in Samaria.

**18:3** And Ahab called for Obadiah, who was over the household. Now Obadiah feared the LORD greatly.

**18:4** For when Jezebel was cutting off the prophets of the LORD, Obadiah took a hundred prophets and hid them, fifty men to a cave, and sustained them with bread and water.

**18:5** Then Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs of water and to all the wadis; perhaps we will find grass and keep horses and mules alive, and not cut off any of the livestock."

**18:6** So they divided the land between themselves to pass through it: Ahab went in one direction by himself, and Obadiah went in another direction by himself.

**18:7** Now as Obadiah was on the way, look, Elijah came to meet him. And he recognized him, fell on his face, and said, "Is this really you, my lord Elijah?"

**18:8** And he said to him, "It is I. Go, tell your lord, 'Here is Elijah.'"

**18:9** And he said, "What sin have I committed, that you are giving your servant into the hand of Ahab to put me to death?

**18:10** As the LORD your God lives, there is not a nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to search for you; and when they said, 'He is not here,' he put the kingdom and the nation under oath that they could not find you.

**18:11** And now you are saying, 'Go, tell your lord, "Here is Elijah."'

**18:12** And it will happen that as soon as I leave you, the Spirit of the LORD will carry you away to where I do not know; so when I come to tell Ahab and he cannot find you, he will kill me, although your servant has feared the LORD from my youth.

**18:13** Has it not been told to my lord what I did when Jezebel killed the prophets of the LORD, how I hid a hundred men of the prophets of the LORD, fifty men each in a cave, and sustained them with bread and water?

**18:14** And now you are saying, 'Go, tell your lord, "Here is Elijah"'; and he will kill me."

**18:15** Then Elijah said, "As the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, I will surely present myself to him today."

**18:16** So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him; and Ahab went to meet Elijah.

**18:17** And it happened when Ahab saw Elijah that Ahab said to him, "Is this really you, troubler of Israel?"

**18:18** He said, "I have not troubled Israel; rather, you and your father's house have, because you have forsaken the commandments of the LORD and you have gone after the Baals.

**18:19** So now send and gather all Israel to me at Mount Carmel, along with the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of Asherah, who eat at Jezebel's table."

**18:20** So Ahab sent throughout all the sons of Israel and gathered the prophets together at Mount Carmel.

**18:21** Then Elijah drew near to all the people and said, "How long will you keep limping between two divided opinions? If the LORD is God, go after Him; but if Baal, go after him." Yet the people did not answer him a word.

**18:22** Then Elijah said to the people, "I alone remain as a prophet of the LORD, while Baal's prophets are four hundred and fifty men.

**18:23** Now let them give us two bulls, and let them choose one bull for themselves, cut it into pieces, and lay it on the wood, yet set no fire; and I will prepare the other bull and lay it on the wood, and I will set no fire.

**18:24** Then you will call on the name of your god, and I will call on the name of the LORD; and the God who answers by fire, He is God." And all the people answered and said, "The word is good."

**18:25** Then Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "Choose one bull for yourselves and prepare it first, since you are the many, and call on the name of your god, yet set no fire."

**18:26** So they took the bull that was given to them and prepared it, and they called on the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, "Baal, answer us!" Yet there was no voice and no one answering. And they limped around the altar that had been made.

**18:27** Then it happened at noon that Elijah mocked them and said, "Call out in a loud voice, since he is a god! Maybe he is deep in thought, or he has stepped aside, or he is on a journey; perhaps he is asleep and must wake up."

**18:28** So they called out in a loud voice and gashed themselves according to their custom with swords and lances, until blood poured out over them.

**18:29** And when noon passed, they raved until the time of presenting the grain offering; yet there was no voice, no one answering, and no one paying attention.

**18:30** Then Elijah said to all the people, "Come near to me." So all the people came near to him. And he repaired the altar of the LORD that had been torn down.

**18:31** And Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of the LORD had come, saying, "Israel will be your name."

**18:32** With the stones he built an altar in the name of the LORD, and around the altar he made a trench with the capacity of about two seahs of seed.

**18:33** Then he arranged the wood, cut the bull into pieces, and laid it on the wood.

**18:34** He said, "Fill four jars with water and pour it onto the burnt offering and onto the wood." Then he said, "Do it a second time," and they did it a second time. He said, "Do it a third time," and they did it a third time.

**18:35** And the water flowed around the altar, and he also filled the trench with water.

**18:36** And it happened at the time of presenting the grain offering that Elijah the prophet drew near and said, "LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known today that You are God in Israel, and that I am Your servant, and that at Your word I have done all these things.

**18:37** Answer me, LORD, answer me, so that this people may know that You, LORD, are God, and that You Yourself have turned their heart back."

**18:38** Then the fire of the LORD fell and consumed the burnt offering, the wood, the stones, and the dust, and it licked up the water that was in the trench.

**18:39** And all the people saw this, fell on their faces, and said, "The LORD, He is God! The LORD, He is God!"

**18:40** Then Elijah said to them, "Seize the prophets of Baal; let not one man among them escape." So they seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the Wadi Kishon and slaughtered them there.

**18:41** Then Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, eat and drink, for there is the sound of the roar of rain."

**18:42** So Ahab went up to eat and drink, while Elijah went up to the summit of Carmel; and he crouched down to the ground and placed his face between his knees.

**18:43** He said to his servant, "Please go up and look toward the sea." So he went up, looked, and said, "There is nothing." And he said, "Go back seven times."

**18:44** And it happened on the seventh time that he said, "Look, a small cloud like a man's palm is rising from the sea." Then he said, "Go up, tell Ahab, 'Harness up and go down, so that the rain does not detain you.'"

**18:45** In a little while the sky grew dark with clouds and wind, and there was a heavy rain. And Ahab rode off and went to Jezreel.

**18:46** Then the hand of the LORD came upon Elijah, and he girded up his waist and ran ahead of Ahab all the way to the entrance of Jezreel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:5** — "Cut off" translates the Hiphil of karat ("cut off/destroy"), deliberately echoing the same verb used in verse 4 for Jezebel cutting off the LORD's prophets.
- **18:21** — "Limping" translates the Qal participle of pasach ("limp/hop"), echoed in the Piel in verse 26 for the ritual limping of Baal's prophets; "divided opinions" renders a rare plural noun (seippim) related to "branches" or "divided thoughts."
- **18:26** — Both "was given" and "had been made" translate Masoretic Qal active third-person masculine singular verbs (natan and asah), rendered here as impersonal passives.
- **18:27** — "Stepped aside" translates a hapax legomenon (sig, "turning aside/withdrawal"), widely understood as a sarcastic euphemism for relieving oneself.
- **18:36** — Ketiv reads "at Your words" (plural), while the Qere followed here reads "at Your word" (singular).
