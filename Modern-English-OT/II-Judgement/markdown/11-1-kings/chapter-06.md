## Text

**6:1** And the king gave the command, and they quarried large stones, costly stones, to lay the foundation of the house with hewn stones. Now it happened in the four hundred and eightieth year after the sons of Israel went out from the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv—that is, the second month—that he began building the house for the LORD. In the fourth year the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid, in the month of Ziv. And in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul—that is, the eighth month—the house was finished in all its details and in all its specifications; thus he built it over seven years.

**6:2** Now the house that King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits in length, twenty in width, and thirty cubits in height.

**6:3** And the portico in front of the main hall of the house was twenty cubits in length, across the width of the house, and ten cubits in width in front of the house.

**6:4** And for the house he made framed, recessed windows.

**6:5** And against the wall of the house he built a side structure all around, along the walls of the house all around both the main hall and the inner sanctuary, and he made side chambers all around.

**6:6** The lowest story was five cubits in width, the middle one six cubits in width, and the third seven cubits in width; for he put offsets on the house all around on the outside, so as not to fasten into the walls of the house.

**6:7** Now when the house was being built, it was built of stone completed at the quarry; neither hammers nor axe nor any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.

**6:8** The entrance to the middle side chamber was on the right side of the house, and by winding stairs they would go up to the middle story, and from the middle story to the third.

**6:9** So he built the house and finished it, and he roofed the house with beams and planks of cedar.

**6:10** And he built the side structure against the entire house, five cubits in height, and it fastened onto the house with cedar timbers.

**6:15** And he built the walls of the house on the inside with boards of cedar; from the floor of the house up to the walls of the ceiling he overlaid them with wood on the inside, and he overlaid the floor of the house with boards of cypress.

**6:16** And he built twenty cubits at the rear of the house with boards of cedar from the floor up to the walls; he built it on the inside as an inner sanctuary, as the Holy of Holies.

**6:17** And the house—that is, the main hall in front—measured forty cubits.

**6:19** And he prepared an inner sanctuary inside the house, in the innermost part, in order to place the ark of the covenant of the LORD there.

**6:20** Inside the inner sanctuary it was twenty cubits in length, twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits in height; he overlaid it with pure gold, and he overlaid an altar with cedar.

**6:21** And Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold; he drew chains of gold across in front of the inner sanctuary, and he overlaid it with gold.

**6:22** The entire house he overlaid with gold until all the house was finished, and the entire altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary he overlaid with gold.

**6:23** And in the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, each ten cubits in height.

**6:24** One wing of the cherub was five cubits, and the other wing of the cherub was five cubits; it was ten cubits from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other.

**6:25** The second cherub also measured ten cubits; the two cherubim had one measurement and one shape.

**6:26** The height of one cherub was ten cubits, and likewise the second cherub.

**6:27** And he set the cherubim inside the inner house. The wings of the cherubim were outstretched so that the wing of the first touched one wall and the wing of the second cherub was touching the other wall, while their wings toward the center of the house were touching wing to wing.

**6:28** And he overlaid the cherubim with gold.

**6:29** And all around the walls of the house he carved engraved reliefs of cherubim, palm trees, and open blossoms, in both the inner and the outer rooms.

**6:30** And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold, in both the inner and the outer rooms.

**6:31** And for the entrance of the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel and doorposts were a fifth part.

**6:32** And as for the two doors of olive wood, he carved on them reliefs of cherubim, palm trees, and open blossoms, and overlaid them with gold; he hammered the gold onto the cherubim and onto the palm trees.

**6:33** And in the same way he made for the entrance of the main hall doorposts of olive wood, of a fourth part,

**6:34** and two doors of cypress wood; the two leaves of the one door were folding, and the two leaves of the second door were folding.

**6:35** And he carved cherubim, palm trees, and open blossoms, and overlaid them with gold smoothed evenly over the engraved work.

**6:36** And he built the inner court with three courses of hewn stone and a course of cut cedar beams.

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The architectural phrase challone shequfim atumim ('framed, recessed windows') uses two rare terms of uncertain design, likely denoting fixed windows set within beveled or recessed frames.
- **6:5** — Here and in 6:6 and 6:10, the qere reads yatsia ('side structure' or 'story'), distinguishing the architectural term from the ketiv yatsua (elsewhere 'bed' or 'couch').
- **6:8** — The Hebrew text explicitly reads hattikhonah ('the middle') for the entrance chamber, even though stairs ascend from it to 'the middle' story; lulim ('winding stairs') is a hapax legomenon whose exact construction is uncertain.
- **6:20** — Hebrew lifne haddevir literally means 'in front of the inner sanctuary,' though the cubical dimensions refer to the interior of the inner sanctuary itself. The final clause ('he overlaid an altar with cedar') may alternatively mean 'he overlaid the cedar altar' (with gold, cf. 6:22).
- **6:31** — The architectural expressions chamishit ('a fifth part') here and me'et revi'it ('of a fourth part') in 6:33 are obscure; they are variously understood as one-fifth and one-fourth of the wall's width or as five-sided and four-sided doorway frames.
