## Text

**3:2** However, the people were sacrificing at the high places, because a house had not been built for the name of the LORD until those days.

**3:3** And Solomon loved the LORD, walking in the statutes of David his father, except that at the high places he was sacrificing and burning incense.

**3:4** Then the king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, because that was the great high place; Solomon would offer up a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.

**3:5** At Gibeon the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream during the night, and God said, "Ask what I should give to you."

**3:6** And Solomon said, "You have shown great loyal love to Your servant David my father, just as he walked before You in truth, in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with You; and You have kept for him this great loyal love, and have given him a son sitting on his throne as at this day.

**3:7** And now, LORD my God, You have made Your servant king in place of David my father, yet I am a young boy; I do not know how to go out and come in.

**3:8** And Your servant is in the midst of Your people whom You have chosen, a numerous people who cannot be numbered or counted for their multitude.

**3:9** Therefore give to Your servant a listening heart to judge Your people, to discern between good and evil; for who is able to judge this weighty people of Yours?"

**3:10** And the matter was good in the eyes of the Lord, that Solomon had asked for this thing.

**3:11** And God said to him, "Because you have asked for this thing, and have not asked for yourself many days, and have not asked for yourself wealth, and have not asked for the life of your enemies, but have asked for yourself discernment to hear justice,

**3:12** look, I have acted according to your words; see, I have given to you a heart that is wise and discerning, so that no one like you existed before you, and after you no one like you will arise.

**3:13** Moreover, what you did not ask for I have also given to you, both wealth and honor, so that no man among the kings will be like you all your days.

**3:14** And if you walk in My ways, to keep My statutes and My commandments, just as David your father walked, then I will prolong your days."

**3:15** Then Solomon woke up, and look, it was a dream. So he came to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and he offered up burnt offerings, prepared peace offerings, and prepared a banquet for all his servants.

**3:16** At that time two women, prostitutes, came to the king and stood before him.

**3:17** And the one woman said, "Please, my lord, this woman and I live in one house, and I gave birth while with her in the house.

**3:18** And it happened on the third day after I gave birth that this woman also gave birth. We were together; no outsider was with us in the house, apart from the two of us in the house.

**3:19** And the son of this woman died during the night, because she lay upon him.

**3:20** Then she got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while your maidservant was asleep; she laid him in her bosom, and her dead son she laid in my bosom.

**3:21** When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, look, he was dead! Then I examined him closely in the morning, and see, he was not my son whom I had borne."

**3:22** Then the other woman said, "No! Rather, my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one." Yet the first kept saying, "No! Rather, your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one." So they spoke before the king.

**3:23** Then the king said, "This one says, 'This is my son, the living one, and your son is the dead one,' while the other says, 'No! Rather, your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.'"

**3:24** And the king said, "Fetch me a sword." So they brought the sword before the king.

**3:25** Then the king said, "Cut the living child in two, and give half to one and half to the other."

**3:26** Then the woman whose son was the living one spoke to the king, because her compassion was deeply stirred over her son, and she said, "Please, my lord, give her the living newborn, and certainly do not put him to death!" But the other was saying, "Neither mine nor yours shall he be; cut him!"

**3:27** Then the king replied and said, "Give the living newborn to her, and you must certainly not put him to death; she is his mother."

**3:28** And all Israel heard the judgment that the king had passed, and they were in awe before the king, for they saw that the wisdom of God was within him to do justice.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — The verb ya'aleh ("would offer up") is a Hiphil imperfect of alah, cognate with olot ("burnt offerings"), expressing either customary practice at Gibeon or prolonged action on that occasion.
- **3:9** — Literally "a listening heart" (lev shomea, Qal active participle of shama), denoting a heart attentive to God and discerning in judicial hearings (compare "to hear justice" in 3:11). The adjective kaved ("weighty") conveys both the vastness of the nation and the heavy burden of judging it.
- **3:10** — Here and in 3:15 the Masoretic Hebrew reads Adonai ("the Lord") rather than the Tetragrammaton ("the LORD").
- **3:13** — The Hebrew uses the Qal perfect verb hayah ("was" or "has been") with the forward-looking phrase "all your days," conveying the assured outcome of God's promise ("will be").
- **3:26** — The idiom nikhmeru rachameha (Niphal perfect of kamar with rachamim) literally means "her compassion grew warm," expressing intense maternal yearning. Here and in 3:27 the Hebrew shifts from yeled ("child" in 3:25) to the Qal passive participle yalud ("born one" or "newborn").
