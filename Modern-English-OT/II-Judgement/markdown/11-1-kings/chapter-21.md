## Text

**21:1** Now Ben-hadad, king of Aram, assembled his entire army, and thirty-two kings were with him, as well as horses and chariots. He went up, laid siege to Samaria, and fought against it.

**21:2** He sent messengers into the city to Ahab, king of Israel.

**21:3** And he said to him, "This is what Ben-hadad says: 'Your silver and your gold belong to me, and the finest of your wives and your sons belong to me.'"

**21:4** The king of Israel answered and said, "According to your word, my lord the king: I belong to you, together with everything that is mine."

**21:5** The messengers returned and said, "This is what Ben-hadad says: 'Indeed, I sent to you, saying, "You must give to me your silver and your gold, your wives and your sons,"

**21:6** nevertheless, about this time tomorrow I will send my servants to you, and they will search through your house and the houses of your servants; and everything precious to your eyes they will put in their hands and take away.'"

**21:7** Then the king of Israel summoned all the elders of the land and said, "Please take note and see how this man is seeking harm; for he sent to me for my wives and my sons, and for my silver and my gold, and I did not hold them back from him."

**21:8** All the elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen, and do not agree."

**21:9** So he said to the messengers of Ben-hadad, "Tell my lord the king, 'Everything for which you sent to your servant at first I will do, but this matter I cannot do.'" The messengers went off and brought back word to him.

**21:10** Then Ben-hadad sent to him and said, "May the gods deal thus with me, and add still more, if the dust of Samaria proves enough for handfuls for all the people who follow at my feet."

**21:11** The king of Israel answered and said, "Tell him, 'The one who buckles on his armor should not boast like the one who unbuckles it.'"

**21:12** When he heard this word, while he was drinking, he and the kings in the shelters, he said to his servants, "Position yourselves!" And they positioned themselves against the city.

**21:13** Now a certain prophet drew near to Ahab, king of Israel, and said, "This is what the LORD says: 'Have you seen this entire great multitude? Look, I am giving it into your hand today, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**21:14** Ahab asked, "By whom?" He said, "This is what the LORD says: 'By the young men of the officials of the provinces.'" Then he asked, "Who will open the battle?" And he said, "You."

**21:15** Then he mustered the young men of the officials of the provinces, and there were two hundred thirty-two; and after them he mustered all the people, all the sons of Israel, seven thousand.

**21:16** They went out at midday, while Ben-hadad was drinking to drunkenness in the shelters, he and the kings, the thirty-two kings who were aiding him.

**21:17** The young men of the officials of the provinces went out first. Ben-hadad sent out, and they reported to him, saying, "Men have come out from Samaria."

**21:18** He said, "If they have come out for peace, capture them alive; and if they have come out for war, capture them alive."

**21:19** So these went out from the city: the young men of the officials of the provinces, and the army that was behind them.

**21:20** They struck down each one his man, and the Arameans fled as Israel pursued them; and Ben-hadad, king of Aram, escaped on a horse along with horsemen.

**21:21** Then the king of Israel went out and struck down the horses and the chariots, and inflicted a great blow on the Arameans.

**21:22** Then the prophet drew near to the king of Israel and said to him, "Go, strengthen yourself, and know and see what you should do; for at the return of the year the king of Aram is coming up against you."

**21:23** Now the servants of the king of Aram said to him, "Their God is a God of the mountains; for that reason they were stronger than we were. However, let us fight with them on the plain, and certainly we will be stronger than they are.

**21:24** And do this thing: remove the kings, each one from his position, and set governors in their place.

**21:25** And as for you, number for yourself an army like the army that fell from you, horse for horse and chariot for chariot; then let us fight with them on the plain, and certainly we will be stronger than they are." So he listened to their voice and did so.

**21:26** At the return of the year, Ben-hadad mustered the Arameans and marched up to Aphek for battle with Israel.

**21:27** The sons of Israel were also mustered and provisioned, and they went to meet them; the sons of Israel encamped opposite them like two small flocks of goats, while the Arameans filled the land.

**21:28** Then the man of God drew near, spoke to the king of Israel, and said, "This is what the LORD says: 'Because the Arameans have said, "The LORD is a God of the mountains, and He is not a God of the valleys," I will give this entire great multitude into your hand, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**21:29** They encamped facing one another for seven days. On the seventh day the battle commenced, and the sons of Israel struck down the Arameans, one hundred thousand foot soldiers in a single day.

**21:30** Those who remained fled to Aphek, into the city, and the wall fell on twenty-seven thousand of the remaining men. Ben-hadad also fled and went into the city, into a chamber within a chamber.

**21:31** His servants said to him, "Look now, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel are kings of mercy. Please let us put sackcloth around our waists and ropes on our heads, and go out to the king of Israel; perhaps he will preserve your life."

**21:32** So they bound sackcloth around their waists, with ropes on their heads, came to the king of Israel, and said, "Your servant Ben-hadad says, 'Please let me keep my life.'" And he said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother."

**21:33** Now the men were watching for an omen, and they hurried to confirm whether it came from him, and said, "Your brother Ben-hadad!" He said, "Go, bring him." Then Ben-hadad came out to him, and he brought him up onto the chariot.

**21:34** He said to him, "The cities that my father took from your father I will return, and you may establish streets for yourself in Damascus, just as my father established in Samaria." "And as for me, under this covenant I will release you." So he made a covenant with him and released him.

**21:35** Now one man from the sons of the prophets said to his companion by the word of the LORD, "Please strike me." The man, however, refused to strike him.

**21:36** Then he said to him, "Because you have not heeded the voice of the LORD, look: as you go away from me, a lion will strike you down." And he departed from beside him, and a lion found him and struck him down.

**21:37** Then he found another man and said, "Please strike me." So the man struck him hard, wounding him.

**21:38** Then the prophet went and stood waiting for the king along the road, and disguised himself with a bandage across his eyes.

**21:39** While the king was passing by, he cried out to the king and said, "Your servant went out into the midst of the battle; and look, a man turned aside and brought a man to me, and said, 'Guard this man. If he turns up missing at all, then your life will be in place of his life, or you will weigh out a talent of silver.'

**21:40** Yet while your servant was occupied here and there, he was no longer there." The king of Israel said to him, "That is your verdict; you yourself have determined it."

**21:41** Then he hurried and removed the bandage from over his eyes, and the king of Israel recognized him, that he was one of the prophets.

**21:42** He said to him, "This is what the LORD says: 'Because you have released from your hand the man devoted by Me to destruction, your life will be in place of his life, and your people in place of his people.'"

**21:43** So the king of Israel went to his house, resentful and angry, and came to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **21:11** — Hebrew reads literally "one who girds on" (Qal participle of חגר) and "one who unfastens" (Piel participle of פתח), with armor or battle gear implied.
- **21:23** — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים ("God/gods") is grammatically plural in form and here lacks a singular verb or pronoun, so the Aramean servants' remark can also be rendered "Their gods are gods of the mountains." Verse 28, however, explicitly reports their claim in the singular with "the LORD" and "He" (הוּא).
- **21:33** — The rare verb וַיַּחְלְטוּ (from חלט, occurring only here in Biblical Hebrew, meaning "confirm/decide/seize upon") followed by הֲמִמֶּנּוּ ("whether from him") follows the Masoretic vocalization and word division. Many commentators redivide the consonants as וַיַּחְלְטוּהָ מִמֶּנּוּ ("they snatched it [the word] from him").
- **21:34** — Hebrew חֻצוֹת ("streets") here refers to designated commercial quarters or trading bazaars. The Hebrew text marks no explicit speaker change before "And as for me," where Ahab replies to Ben-hadad's terms.
- **21:41** — Ketiv (written) reads מעל ("from over/off"); Qere (read) reads מֵעֲלֵי, the poetic or expanded form of the same preposition with identical meaning. The rare noun אֲפֵר (occurring only here and in v. 38) denotes a headband or bandage.
