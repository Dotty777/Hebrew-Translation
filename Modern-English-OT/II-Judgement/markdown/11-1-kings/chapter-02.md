## Text

**2:1** Now the days of David approached for him to die, and he commanded his son Solomon, saying,

**2:2** "I am going in the way of all the earth; therefore be strong and be a man.

**2:3** And keep the requirement of the LORD your God, to walk in His ways, to keep His statutes, His commandments, His judgments, and His testimonies, as written in the law of Moses, so that you may prosper in everything you do and everywhere you turn,

**2:4** in order that the LORD may establish His word that He spoke concerning me, saying, 'If your sons keep their way to walk before Me in truth with all their heart and with all their soul,' saying, 'a man will not be cut off from you from the throne of Israel.'

**2:5** Moreover, you yourself know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, to Abner son of Ner and to Amasa son of Jether: he killed them and shed the blood of war in peace, and he put the blood of war on his belt that was around his waist and on his sandals that were on his feet.

**2:6** Act according to your wisdom, then, and do not bring down his gray hair in peace to Sheol.

**2:7** But to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite show loyal love, and let them be among those who eat at your table, for in this way they came near to me when I fled from before Absalom your brother.

**2:8** And look, with you is Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim; he cursed me with a severe curse on the day I went to Mahanaim, yet he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the LORD, saying, 'I will surely not put you to death by the sword.'

**2:9** So now do not acquit him, for you are a wise man, and you will know what you are to do to him, and you shall bring down his gray hair with blood to Sheol."

**2:10** Then David lay down with his fathers and was buried in the city of David.

**2:11** Now the days that David reigned over Israel were forty years: in Hebron he reigned seven years, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three years.

**2:12** And Solomon sat on the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was established very firmly.

**2:13** Then Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon. She said, "Is your coming in peace?" And he said, "In peace."

**2:14** And he said, "I have a word for you." And she said, "Speak."

**2:15** And he said, "You yourself know that the kingship was mine, and toward me all Israel set their faces for me to reign. However, the kingship turned around and became my brother's, for from the LORD it became his.

**2:16** And now I am asking one request from you; do not turn away my face." And she said to him, "Speak."

**2:17** And he said, "Please speak to Solomon the king, for he will not turn away your face, that he may give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife."

**2:18** And Bathsheba said, "Good; I myself will speak concerning you to the king."

**2:19** So Bathsheba came to King Solomon to speak to him concerning Adonijah. And the king rose to meet her and bowed down to her; then he sat on his throne and had a throne placed for the mother of the king, and she sat at his right hand.

**2:20** Then she said, "I am asking one small request from you; do not turn away my face." And the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not turn away your face."

**2:21** And she said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to your brother Adonijah as a wife."

**2:22** Then King Solomon answered and said to his mother, "And why are you asking for Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for him the kingship as well, for he is my older brother—for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab son of Zeruiah!"

**2:23** Then King Solomon swore by the LORD, saying, "Thus may God do to me, and so may He add: surely Adonijah has spoken this word at the cost of his own life!

**2:24** And now, as the LORD lives, who has established me and seated me on the throne of my father David, and who has made a house for me just as He spoke, surely today Adonijah shall be put to death."

**2:25** So King Solomon sent by the hand of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he struck him, and he died.

**2:26** And to Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go to Anathoth, to your fields, for you are a man deserving death; yet on this day I will not put you to death, because you carried the ark of the Lord GOD before my father David, and because you were afflicted in everything in which my father was afflicted."

**2:27** Thus Solomon expelled Abiathar from being priest to the LORD, to fulfill the word of the LORD that He had spoken concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

**2:28** Now the report reached Joab—for Joab had turned after Adonijah, though he had not turned after Absalom—and Joab fled to the tent of the LORD and grasped the horns of the altar.

**2:29** And it was reported to King Solomon that Joab had fled to the tent of the LORD, and there he was beside the altar. Then Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him."

**2:30** So Benaiah came to the tent of the LORD and said to him, "This is what the king says, 'Come out!'" But he said, "No, rather I will die here." Then Benaiah brought back word to the king, saying, "This is what Joab spoke, and this is how he answered me."

**2:31** And the king said to him, "Do just as he has spoken: strike him and bury him, and so remove from me and from my father's house the blood that Joab shed without cause.

**2:32** And the LORD will bring back his blood upon his own head, because he struck two men more righteous and better than himself and killed them with the sword, while my father David did not know—Abner son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.

**2:33** Thus their blood shall come back upon the head of Joab and upon the head of his seed forever; but for David and for his seed and for his house and for his throne, there shall be peace forever from the LORD."

**2:34** Then Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up, struck him, and put him to death; and he was buried in his house in the wilderness.

**2:35** And the king appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the army in his place, and Zadok the priest the king appointed in place of Abiathar. These were the chief deputies who were over the work for Solomon, five hundred fifty, who ruled over the people doing the work. And three times a year Solomon would offer up burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar that he built for the LORD, and he would burn incense with it, which was before the LORD; and he completed the house.

**2:36** Then the king sent and summoned Shimei and said to him, "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem and live there, and do not go out from there to this place or that.

**2:37** And it will come about that on the day you go out and cross the Kidron brook, you shall know for certain that you will surely die; your blood will be on your own head."

**2:38** And Shimei said to the king, "The word is good; just as my lord the king has spoken, so your servant will do." So Shimei lived in Jerusalem many days.

**2:39** Now it happened at the end of three years that two of Shimei's servants fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. And they told Shimei, saying, "Look, your servants are in Gath."

**2:40** Then Shimei got up, saddled his donkey, and went to Gath to Achish to search for his servants; and Shimei went and brought his servants from Gath.

**2:41** And it was reported to Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had returned.

**2:42** So the king sent and summoned Shimei and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by the LORD and solemnly warn you, saying, 'On the day you go out and walk to this place or that, know for certain that you will surely die'? And you said to me, 'The word is good; I have heard.'

**2:43** Why then have you not kept the oath of the LORD and the command that I commanded you?"

**2:44** And the king said to Shimei, "You yourself know all the evil that your own heart knows, which you did to my father David; therefore the LORD will bring back your evil upon your own head.

**2:45** Yet King Solomon is blessed, and the throne of David will remain established before the LORD forever."

**2:46** Then the king commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck him, and he died. Thus the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon. Judah and Israel were numerous, like the sand that is by the sea in abundance, eating and drinking and rejoicing. And Solomon was ruling over all the kingdoms from the River to the land of the Philistines and as far as the border of Egypt; they presented tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life. And Judah and Israel lived in security, each man beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, from Dan as far as Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **2:22** — The Hebrew ends elliptically with "for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab son of Zeruiah" (resuming "ask for him"), implying that Abiathar and Joab stood behind Adonijah's bid for the throne.
- **2:23** — Following the self-imprecatory oath formula ("Thus may God do to me, and so may He add"), Hebrew ki introduces an emphatic sworn assertion ("surely"), and the preposition be- with "his own life" functions as a beth of price ("at the cost of his own life").
- **2:24** — Ketiv/Qere: the qere reads the standard Hiphil wayyiqtol with 1cs suffix "and seated me" (wayyoshivani), whereas the ketiv displays an orthographic variant (wywshybyny) with the same meaning.
- **2:35** — The last two sentences are Masoretic 9:23 (the 550 chief officers over the work) and 9:25 (Solomon's offerings three times a year), carried in this verse following the Saints (Greek) verse arrangement. In the final sentence, the Hiphil infinitive absolute "burn incense" continues the preceding frequentative verb, and "with it, which was before the LORD" is a compressed Hebrew reference to burning incense at the altar before the LORD.
- **2:46** — From "Judah and Israel were numerous" onward, this verse carries the Masoretic summary verses 4:20, 5:1, and 5:5 (traditional English 4:20-21, 25), following the Saints (Greek) verse arrangement.
