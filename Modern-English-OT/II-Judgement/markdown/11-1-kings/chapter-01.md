## Text

**1:1** Now King David was old, advanced in days, and they covered him with garments, yet he could not grow warm.

**1:2** So his servants said to him, "Let them search for a young virgin woman for my lord the king, and let her stand before the king and be a caretaker for him; let her lie in your embrace, so that my lord the king may grow warm."

**1:3** They searched for a beautiful young woman throughout all the territory of Israel, found Abishag the Shunammite, and brought her to the king.

**1:4** Now the young woman was exceedingly beautiful; she became a caretaker for the king and waited on him, but the king did not know her.

**1:5** Now Adonijah son of Haggith was exalting himself, saying, "I will reign." So he provided chariots and horsemen for himself, along with fifty men running ahead of him.

**1:6** And his father had never grieved him in all his days by asking, "Why have you acted like this?" He was also very handsome in appearance, and he was born after Absalom.

**1:7** He held discussions with Joab son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, and they sided with Adonijah and supported him.

**1:8** Yet Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and the mighty warriors who belonged to David were not with Adonijah.

**1:9** Then Adonijah sacrificed sheep, cattle, and fattened calves by the Stone of Zoheleth, which is next to En-rogel; he invited all his brothers, the sons of the king, along with all the men of Judah, the servants of the king.

**1:10** But Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty warriors, and his brother Solomon he did not invite.

**1:11** Then Nathan spoke to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother, saying, "Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, while our lord David does not know?

**1:12** Now come, please let me offer you counsel, and rescue your life and the life of your son Solomon.

**1:13** Go and come in to King David, and say to him, 'Did you not yourself, my lord the king, swear to your female servant, saying, "Surely your son Solomon will reign after me, and he himself will sit on my throne"? Why then has Adonijah become king?'

**1:14** Look, while you are still speaking there with the king, I myself will enter after you and corroborate your words."

**1:15** So Bathsheba went in to the king in the inner chamber; now the king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was waiting on the king.

**1:16** Bathsheba bowed low and prostrated herself before the king. And the king said, "What do you want?"

**1:17** She said to him, "My lord, you yourself swore by the LORD your God to your female servant, 'Surely your son Solomon will reign after me, and he himself will sit on my throne.'

**1:18** And now, look, Adonijah has become king, and now, my lord the king, you do not know it.

**1:19** He has sacrificed oxen, fattened calves, and sheep in abundance, and he has invited all the king's sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the army, yet your servant Solomon he did not invite.

**1:20** As for you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are upon you to declare to them who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.

**1:21** Otherwise it will happen, as soon as my lord the king lies down with his fathers, that I and my son Solomon will be regarded as offenders."

**1:22** Now look, while she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived.

**1:23** They informed the king, saying, "Here is Nathan the prophet." He entered before the king and prostrated himself to the king, with his face to the ground.

**1:24** Then Nathan said, "My lord the king, have you yourself said, 'Adonijah will reign after me, and he himself will sit on my throne'?

**1:25** For he went down today and sacrificed oxen, fattened calves, and sheep in abundance, and invited all the king's sons, the commanders of the army, and Abiathar the priest. Look, they are eating and drinking before him, and they are saying, 'Long live King Adonijah!'

**1:26** But me, even me your servant, and Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and your servant Solomon he has not invited.

**1:27** Has this matter come about from my lord the king, and you have not made known to your servant who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?"

**1:28** Then King David answered and said, "Call Bathsheba for me." So she came before the king and stood before the king.

**1:29** Then the king swore and said, "As the LORD lives, who redeemed my life from every distress,

**1:30** surely just as I swore to you by the LORD, the God of Israel, saying, 'Surely your son Solomon will reign after me, and he himself will sit on my throne in my place,' so indeed I will do this day."

**1:31** Then Bathsheba bowed low with her face to the earth, prostrated herself to the king, and said, "May my lord King David live forever!"

**1:32** King David said, "Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada for me." So they came in before the king.

**1:33** The king said to them, "Take the servants of your lord with you, set my son Solomon to ride on the mule that belongs to me, and take him down to Gihon.

**1:34** There let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him king over Israel; then blow the ram's horn and say, 'Long live King Solomon!'

**1:35** Then come up after him, and he will enter and sit on my throne, and he himself will reign in my place; and he is the one I have commissioned to be ruler over Israel and over Judah."

**1:36** Then Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king and said, "Amen! So may the LORD, the God of my lord the king, say.

**1:37** Just as the LORD was with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and may He make his throne greater than the throne of my lord King David."

**1:38** So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down, set Solomon to ride on King David's mule, and led him to Gihon.

**1:39** Then Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon; and they blew the ram's horn, and all the people said, "Long live King Solomon!"

**1:40** And all the people went up after him, with the people playing flutes and rejoicing with great joy, so that the earth split open at their sound.

**1:41** Now Adonijah and all the invited guests who were with him heard it as they finished eating. When Joab heard the sound of the ram's horn, he said, "Why is there the sound of the city in an uproar?"

**1:42** While he was still speaking, look, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said, "Come in, for you are a man of worth and you bring good news."

**1:43** Jonathan answered and said to Adonijah, "On the contrary, our lord King David has made Solomon king!

**1:44** The king has sent with him Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and they set him to ride on the king's mule.

**1:45** Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him as king at Gihon, and they came up from there rejoicing, so that the city is in an uproar; that is the sound you heard.

**1:46** Moreover, Solomon is seated on the throne of the kingdom.

**1:47** Furthermore, the servants of the king came to bless our lord King David, saying, 'May God make Solomon's name better than your name, and may He make his throne greater than your throne.' And the king bowed down on the bed.

**1:48** And the king also spoke as follows: 'Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who today has given one sitting on my throne, while my eyes see it.'"

**1:49** Then all the invited guests belonging to Adonijah trembled and rose up, and they went, each man on his own way.

**1:50** Now Adonijah was afraid of Solomon, so he got up, went, and grasped the horns of the altar.

**1:51** Then it was reported to Solomon, saying, "Look, Adonijah fears King Solomon, and look, he has taken hold of the horns of the altar, saying, 'Let King Solomon swear to me first that he will not put his servant to death by the sword.'"

**1:52** And Solomon said, "If he proves to be a man of worth, not a hair of his will fall to the ground; but if evil is found in him, he will die."

**1:53** Then King Solomon sent, and they took him down from the altar. He came and bowed down to King Solomon, and Solomon told him, "Go to your house."

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — Literally "and she bore him after Absalom," using a Qal perfect 3fs verb without an explicit subject in this verse, either referring back to Haggith in v. 5 or serving as an idiomatic impersonal construction for "he was born."
- **1:18** — The Masoretic Text reads "and now" a second time here rather than "and you," the reading of some Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions that differs by a single consonant.
- **1:27** — Following the Masoretic Qere (singular, "your servant"); the written Ketiv reads the plural ("your servants").
- **1:37** — Following the Masoretic Qere, which reads the full Qal imperfect form (used optatively in non-initial position after "so," parallel to the following volitive verb); the written Ketiv has the short Qal jussive form ("may He be").
- **1:47** — Following the Masoretic Qere ("God"); the written Ketiv reads "your God."
