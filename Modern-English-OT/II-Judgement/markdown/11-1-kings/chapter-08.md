## Text

**8:1** Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel, all the heads of the tribes, the chiefs of the ancestral houses of the sons of Israel, to King Solomon in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the City of David, that is, Zion.

**8:2** And all the men of Israel assembled to King Solomon in the month of Ethanim, during the festival—that is, the seventh month.

**8:3** And all the elders of Israel came, and the priests lifted up the ark.

**8:4** They brought up the ark of the LORD, and the tent of meeting, and all the holy articles that were in the tent; and the priests and the Levites brought them up.

**8:5** And King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel who had gathered to him were with him before the ark, sacrificing sheep and cattle that could not be counted or numbered because of their abundance.

**8:6** Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Holy of Holies, beneath the wings of the cherubim.

**8:7** For the cherubim were spreading their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered over the ark and over its poles from above.

**8:8** Now the poles were so long that the tips of the poles were visible from the Holy Place in front of the inner sanctuary, yet they were not visible outside; and they have been there to this day.

**8:9** Nothing was in the ark except the two stone tablets that Moses placed there at Horeb, where the LORD made a covenant with the sons of Israel when they came out from the land of Egypt.

**8:10** And it happened, as the priests came out from the Holy Place, that the cloud filled the house of the LORD.

**8:11** And the priests were unable to stand to minister on account of the cloud, because the glory of the LORD had filled the house of the LORD.

**8:14** Then the king turned his face around and blessed the entire assembly of Israel, while the entire assembly of Israel was standing.

**8:15** And he said, "Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth to David my father and with His hand has fulfilled it, saying,

**8:16** 'From the day that I brought out My people Israel from Egypt, I did not choose a city from among all the tribes of Israel to build a house for My name to be there, yet I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**8:17** And David my father had it in his heart to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**8:18** And the LORD said to David my father, 'Since you had it in your heart to build a house for My name, you have done well that it was in your heart.

**8:19** However, you shall not build the house; rather, your son who comes forth from your loins, he will build the house for My name.'

**8:20** And the LORD has established His word that He spoke, and I have risen in the place of David my father and sit on the throne of Israel, just as the LORD spoke, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**8:21** And I have appointed a place there for the ark, in which is the covenant of the LORD that He made with our fathers when He brought them out from the land of Egypt."

**8:22** And Solomon stood before the altar of the LORD in the presence of the entire assembly of Israel, and he spread out his palms toward heaven.

**8:23** And he said, "LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven above or on the earth beneath, keeping the covenant and loyal love for Your servants who walk before You with all their heart,

**8:24** You who have kept for Your servant David my father what You spoke to him; and You spoke with Your mouth, and with Your hand You have fulfilled it, as on this day.

**8:25** And now, LORD, God of Israel, keep for Your servant David my father what You spoke to him, saying, 'There shall not be cut off for you a man from before Me sitting on the throne of Israel, only if your sons keep their way, to walk before Me just as you have walked before Me.'

**8:26** And now, God of Israel, please let Your word that You spoke to Your servant David my father prove true.

**8:27** Yet will God truly dwell on the earth? Look, the heavens and the heaven of heavens cannot contain You—how much less this house that I have built!

**8:28** Yet turn toward the prayer of Your servant and toward his supplication, LORD my God, to listen to the outcry and to the prayer that Your servant is praying before You today,

**8:29** so that Your eyes may be open toward this house night and day, toward the place about which You said, 'My name will be there,' to listen to the prayer that Your servant prays toward this place.

**8:30** And listen to the supplication of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward this place. May You hear in the place of Your dwelling, in heaven; hear and forgive.

**8:31** If a man sins against his neighbor, and an oath is laid upon him to make him swear, and he comes and swears before Your altar in this house,

**8:32** then may You hear in heaven, and act and judge Your servants, condemning the wicked one by bringing his way upon his head, and vindicating the righteous one by giving to him according to his righteousness.

**8:33** When Your people Israel are routed before an enemy because they have sinned against You, and they return to You and acknowledge Your name, and pray and make supplication to You in this house,

**8:34** then may You hear in heaven, and forgive the sin of Your people Israel, and return them to the land that You gave to their fathers.

**8:35** When heaven is shut and there is no rain because they have sinned against You, and they pray toward this place and acknowledge Your name, and turn back from their sin when You humble them,

**8:36** then may You hear in heaven, and forgive the sin of Your servants and of Your people Israel, for You teach them the good way in which they are to walk, and give rain upon Your land that You gave to Your people as an inheritance.

**8:37** When there is famine in the land, when there is pestilence, when there is blight, mildew, locust, or caterpillar, when their enemy besieges them in the land of their gates, any affliction, any disease,

**8:38** whatever prayer, whatever supplication comes from any person, from all Your people Israel, as each knows the affliction of his heart and spreads out his palms toward this house,

**8:39** then may You hear in heaven, Your established dwelling place, and forgive and act, and give to each man according to all his ways, whose heart You know, for You alone know the heart of all the sons of mankind,

**8:40** so that they may fear You all the days that they live upon the face of the land that You gave to our fathers.

**8:41** And also concerning the foreigner who is not from Your people Israel, yet comes from a distant land for the sake of Your name—

**8:42** for they will hear of Your great name and Your strong hand and Your outstretched arm—when he comes and prays toward this house,

**8:43** may You hear in heaven, Your established dwelling place, and do according to everything for which the foreigner calls to You, so that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name, to fear You just as Your people Israel do, and to know that Your name is called upon this house that I have built.

**8:44** When Your people go out to war against their enemy, along the way that You send them, and they pray to the LORD toward the city that You have chosen and the house that I have built for Your name,

**8:45** then hear their prayer and their supplication in heaven, and uphold their cause.

**8:46** When they sin against You—for there is no person who does not sin—and You are angry with them and give them over before an enemy, and their captors take them captive to the land of the enemy, distant or near,

**8:47** yet they take it to heart in the land where they were taken captive, and turn back and make supplication to You in the land of their captors, saying, 'We have sinned and acted perversely; we have done wickedly,'

**8:48** and return to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their enemies who took them captive, and pray to You in the direction of their land that You gave to their fathers, the city that You chose, and the house that I have built for Your name,

**8:49** then hear in heaven, Your established dwelling place, their prayer and their supplication, and uphold their cause,

**8:50** and forgive Your people who have sinned against You, and all their rebellions by which they have rebelled against You, and grant them compassion before their captors, so that they will show them compassion.

**8:51** For they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out from Egypt, from the midst of the iron furnace.

**8:52** May Your eyes be open toward the supplication of Your servant and toward the supplication of Your people Israel, to listen to them in all their calling to You.

**8:53** For You set them apart for Yourself as an inheritance from among all the peoples of the earth, just as You spoke by the hand of Moses Your servant when You brought out our fathers from Egypt, Lord GOD."

**8:54** And it happened, when Solomon had finished praying all this prayer and supplication to the LORD, that he rose from before the altar of the LORD, from kneeling upon his knees with his palms spread out toward heaven.

**8:55** Then he stood and blessed the entire assembly of Israel in a loud voice, saying,

**8:56** "Blessed is the LORD, who has given rest to His people Israel according to everything He spoke. Not a single word has failed from all His good word that He spoke by the hand of Moses His servant.

**8:57** May the LORD our God be with us, just as He was with our fathers; may He neither forsake us nor abandon us,

**8:58** to incline our heart toward Himself, to walk in all His ways and to keep His commandments and His statutes and His judgments that He commanded our fathers.

**8:59** And may these words of mine, by which I have made supplication before the LORD, be close to the LORD our God day and night, so that He may uphold the cause of His servant and the cause of His people Israel according to the need of each day,

**8:60** so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the LORD, He is God; there is no other.

**8:61** And let your heart be whole with the LORD our God, to walk in His statutes and to keep His commandments, as on this day."

**8:62** And the king and all Israel with him were offering sacrifice before the LORD.

**8:63** And Solomon sacrificed the sacrifice of peace offerings that he sacrificed to the LORD: twenty-two thousand cattle and one hundred twenty thousand sheep. Thus the king and all the sons of Israel dedicated the house of the LORD.

**8:64** On that day the king sanctified the center of the courtyard that was before the house of the LORD, because he offered there the burnt offering, and the grain offering, and the fat portions of the peace offerings, for the bronze altar that was before the LORD was too small to contain the burnt offering, and the grain offering, and the fat portions of the peace offerings.

**8:65** And at that time Solomon celebrated the festival, and all Israel with him, a great assembly from Lebo-hamath to the Wadi of Egypt, before the LORD our God, seven days and seven days—fourteen days.

**8:66** On the eighth day he dismissed the people, and they blessed the king and went to their tents, rejoicing and glad of heart over all the goodness that the LORD had done for David His servant and for Israel His people.

## Translator Notes

- **8:9** — Elliptical Hebrew: MT literally reads "where the LORD cut with the sons of Israel," omitting the usual object berit ("covenant") after the Qal verb karat ("cut, make [a covenant]").
- **8:26** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv is plural ("Your words"), while the read qere translated here is singular ("Your word"), agreeing with the singular Niphal verb ye'amen ("prove true").
- **8:30** — MT uses the directional preposition 'el ("to") before both "the place of Your dwelling" and "heaven," either functioning locatively ("in") or depicting prayers ascending to God's heavenly dwelling place.
- **8:31** — MT opens the conditional clause with 'et 'asher ("if/when") and spells the Qal verb "laid" with shin (nasha') rather than sin (nasa'). The phrase uba' 'alah is terse, literally either "and he comes [and] swears [an oath]" or "and an oath comes."
- **8:35** — The verb ta'anem ("You humble them") is parsed as Hiphil of 'anah II ("afflict, humble"), fitting the drought context; the form is homographic with the Qal of 'anah I ("answer").
- **8:48** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv omits the final yod (bnyt), while the pointed qere translated here is first-person singular baniti ("I have built"), matching 8:43 and 8:44.
- **8:59** — The Hebrew idiom debar-yom beyomo (literally "the matter of a day in its day") denotes each day's ongoing need or requirement.
- **8:65** — Hebrew levo Chamath is a northern geographical place name ("Lebo-hamath," literally "the entrance of Hamath").
