## Text

**11:1** Now King Solomon loved many foreign women, as well as the daughter of Pharaoh: Moabite, Ammonite, Edomite, Sidonian, and Hittite women.

**11:2** From the nations concerning which the LORD had said to the sons of Israel, "You shall not go among them, and they shall not come among you; surely they will turn your heart after their gods"—to these Solomon clung in love.

**11:4** And it happened at the time of Solomon's old age that his wives turned his heart after other gods, and his heart was not whole with the LORD his God like the heart of his father David.

**11:5** Then Solomon built a high place for Chemosh, the detestable thing of Moab, on the mountain facing Jerusalem, and for Molech, the detestable thing of the sons of Ammon.

**11:6** And Solomon went after Ashtoreth, the goddess of the Sidonians, and after Milcom, the detestable thing of the Ammonites.

**11:7** And so he did for all his foreign wives, who burned incense and sacrificed to their gods.

**11:8** And Solomon did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, and he did not follow the LORD fully like David his father.

**11:9** So the LORD grew angry with Solomon, because his heart had turned away from the LORD, the God of Israel, who had appeared to him twice.

**11:10** And He had commanded him concerning this matter, not to go after other gods, yet he did not keep what the LORD had commanded.

**11:11** Then the LORD said to Solomon, "Because this has been with you, and you have not kept My covenant and My statutes that I commanded you, I will certainly tear the kingdom away from you and give it to your servant.

**11:12** However, I will not do it in your days, for the sake of David your father; out of the hand of your son I will tear it.

**11:13** Yet I will not tear away the whole kingdom; I will give one tribe to your son for the sake of David My servant and for the sake of Jerusalem, which I have chosen."

**11:14** Then the LORD raised up an adversary against Solomon, Hadad the Edomite; he was of the king's offspring in Edom. And God raised up an adversary against him, Rezon the son of Eliada, who had fled from his master Hadadezer, king of Zobah. He gathered men to himself and became commander of a raiding band when David killed them; and they went to Damascus and settled in it, and they reigned in Damascus.

**11:15** Now it happened when David was in Edom, as Joab the commander of the army went up to bury the fallen, that he struck down every male in Edom.

**11:16** For Joab and all Israel stayed there six months, until he had cut off every male in Edom.

**11:17** But Hadad fled, he and some Edomite men from his father's servants with him, to go to Egypt, while Hadad was a young boy.

**11:18** They arose from Midian and came to Paran; they took men with them from Paran and came to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt, who gave him a house, ordered food for him, and gave him land.

**11:19** And Hadad found great favor in the eyes of Pharaoh, so that he gave him as a wife the sister of his own wife, the sister of Tahpenes the queen.

**11:20** And the sister of Tahpenes bore him his son Genubath, whom Tahpenes weaned within Pharaoh's house; and Genubath was in Pharaoh's house among the sons of Pharaoh.

**11:21** Now Hadad heard in Egypt that David had slept with his fathers and that Joab the commander of the army had died; so Hadad said to Pharaoh, "Send me away, so that I may go to my land."

**11:22** Then Pharaoh said to him, "What are you lacking with me, that now you are seeking to go to your land?" And he said, "Nothing; rather, be sure to send me away."

**11:25** And he was an adversary to Israel all the days of Solomon, along with the harm that Hadad did; and he loathed Israel and reigned over Aram.

**11:26** Now Jeroboam the son of Nebat, an Ephraimite from Zeredah—his mother's name was Zeruah, a widow—was a servant of Solomon, and he lifted up a hand against the king.

**11:27** And this was the reason he lifted up a hand against the king: Solomon built the Millo and closed up the breach of the city of David his father.

**11:28** Now the man Jeroboam was a mighty man of strength; and Solomon saw that the young man worked diligently, so he appointed him over all the labor burden of the house of Joseph.

**11:29** And it happened at that time, when Jeroboam went out from Jerusalem, that Ahijah the Shilonite, the prophet, found him on the road. Now he was wrapped in a new cloak, and the two of them were alone in the field.

**11:30** Then Ahijah seized the new cloak that was on him and tore it into twelve pieces.

**11:31** And he said to Jeroboam, "Take for yourself ten pieces, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Look, I am about to tear the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and I will give you the ten tribes,

**11:32** while the one tribe shall belong to him, for the sake of My servant David and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel,

**11:33** because they have forsaken Me and bowed down to Ashtoreth, the goddess of the Sidonians, to Chemosh, the god of Moab, and to Milcom, the god of the sons of Ammon, and have not walked in My ways, to do what is right in My eyes and My statutes and My ordinances, as David his father did.

**11:34** Yet I will not take the whole kingdom out of his hand, but I will appoint him as prince all the days of his life, for the sake of David My servant whom I chose, who kept My commandments and My statutes.

**11:35** But I will take the kingship out of the hand of his son and give it to you—the ten tribes.

**11:36** And to his son I will give one tribe, so that David My servant may have a lamp before Me always in Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen for Myself to set My name there.

**11:37** And as for you, I will take you, and you will reign over everything that your soul desires, and you will be king over Israel.

**11:38** And it shall be that if you listen to all that I command you, and walk in My ways, and do what is right in My eyes by keeping My statutes and My commandments, as David My servant did, then I will be with you and build for you a steadfast house, just as I built for David, and I will give Israel to you.'"

**11:40** So Solomon sought to put Jeroboam to death, but Jeroboam arose and fled to Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, and he was in Egypt until the death of Solomon.

**11:41** As for the rest of the deeds of Solomon, and all that he did, and his wisdom, are they not written in the book of the deeds of Solomon?

**11:42** And the days that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel were forty years.

**11:43** And Solomon slept with his fathers and was buried in the city of David his father, and his son Rehoboam reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **11:11** — The Hebrew idiom literally reads "this has been with you," expressing a settled course of conduct or mindset harbored by Solomon.
- **11:14** — The sentences about Rezon son of Eliada are Masoretic 11:23-24, carried in this verse following the Saints (Greek) verse arrangement.
- **11:25** — The clause "along with the harm that Hadad did" translates an elliptical Hebrew construction (lit. "and with the evil which Hadad") where the verb is left implied.
- **11:33** — The Hebrew text uses third-person plural verbs ("they have forsaken Me ... bowed down ... have not walked") before concluding with the singular phrase "David his father," broadening Solomon's apostasy to implicate the nation as a whole.
