## Text

**5:1** And these governors sustained King Solomon and everyone who came near to the table of King Solomon, each during his month; they did not let anything go missing. And they brought the barley and the straw for the horses and for the steeds to the place where he would be, each according to his assigned duty.

**5:2** Now Solomon's food for one day was thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of meal,

**5:3** ten fat cattle, twenty pasture-fed cattle, and a hundred sheep, apart from deer, gazelles, fallow deer, and fattened fowl.

**5:4** For he ruled over the entire region beyond the River, from Tiphsah as far as Gaza, over all the kings beyond the River, and he had peace on every side all around.

**5:9** And God gave wisdom to Solomon and exceedingly great understanding, and a breadth of heart like the sand that lies on the shore of the sea.

**5:10** And the wisdom of Solomon was greater than the wisdom of all the sons of the east and more than all the wisdom of Egypt.

**5:11** He was wiser than all humanity—than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol—and his renown was among all the nations all around.

**5:12** And he spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered one thousand and five.

**5:13** And he spoke about the trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon as far as the hyssop that comes out of the wall; he also spoke about beasts, about birds, about crawling creatures, and about fish.

**5:14** And Solomon formed a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt; he took Pharaoh's daughter and brought her to the city of David until he finished building his own house, the house of the LORD, and the wall of Jerusalem all around. And they came from all the peoples to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom. Pharaoh king of Egypt had gone up, captured Gezer, and burned it with fire; he killed the Canaanites living in the city and gave it as a dowry to his daughter, Solomon's wife.

**5:15** Now Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon, because he heard that they had anointed him as king in place of his father; for Hiram had been a friend to David continually.

**5:16** Then Solomon sent word to Hiram, saying,

**5:17** "You know my father David, that he could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God on account of the warfare that surrounded him, until the LORD placed them beneath the soles of my feet.

**5:18** And now the LORD my God has granted me rest all around; there is no adversary and no evil occurrence.

**5:19** Now look, I propose to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, just as the LORD spoke to my father David, saying, 'Your son, whom I will put in your place on your throne—he will build the house for My name.'

**5:20** So now, give the command that they cut down cedars for me from Lebanon; my servants will be with your servants, and I will give you the wages of your servants according to all that you say, for you know that there is not a man among us who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**5:21** And when Hiram heard Solomon's words, he rejoiced greatly and said, "Blessed be the LORD today, who has given David a wise son over this numerous people."

**5:22** Then Hiram sent word to Solomon, saying, "I have heard what you sent to me; I will carry out all your desire concerning cedar timber and cypress timber.

**5:23** My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea, and I will make them into rafts on the sea as far as the place about which you send word to me; I will break them apart there, and you will carry them away. And you will carry out my desire by giving food for my household."

**5:24** So Hiram kept giving Solomon cedar timber and cypress timber, all that he desired.

**5:25** And Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat as provisions for his household and twenty kors of beaten oil; in this way Solomon gave to Hiram year after year.

**5:26** Now the LORD gave Solomon wisdom, just as He had spoken to him. And there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and both of them made a covenant.

**5:27** Then King Solomon raised forced labor from all Israel, and the forced labor amounted to thirty thousand men.

**5:28** And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand per month in rotations: they would be one month in Lebanon and two months each at home, and Adoniram was over the forced labor.

**5:29** Solomon also had seventy thousand carriers of burdens and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hill country,

**5:30** apart from Solomon's chief deputies who were over the work—three thousand three hundred who ruled over the people doing the work.

**5:32** So Solomon's builders, Hiram's builders, and the Gebalites shaped and prepared the timber and the stones to build the house.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — This verse translates Masoretic 5:7-8 (traditional English 4:27-28), following the Saints (Greek) verse arrangement; Masoretic 5:1 and 5:5 are carried in 2:46, and 5:6 in 10:26.
- **5:14** — The first sentence is Masoretic 3:1 and the last sentence is Masoretic 9:16 (Pharaoh's capture of Gezer), carried in this verse following the Saints (Greek) verse arrangement.
- **5:17** — The qere reads raglay ('my feet'), whereas the ketiv reads rglo ('his foot' or 'his feet', referring to David). Although 'warfare' (hammilchamah) is singular, the following verb ('surrounded him') and object pronoun ('them') are plural ad sensum, referring to the hostile forces.
- **5:23** — The noun doberot ('rafts') is a hapax legomenon in the Hebrew Bible; the Piel verb nippastim ('break them apart') describes dismantling the lashed timber rafts upon arrival.
- **5:24** — Here and in 5:32 the Hebrew text spells the Tyrian king's name Hirom, an alternate vocalization of Hiram.
