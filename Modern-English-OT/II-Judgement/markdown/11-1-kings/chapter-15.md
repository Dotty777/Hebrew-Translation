## Text

**15:1** And in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abijam began to reign over Judah.

**15:2** He reigned three years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Maacah, daughter of Abishalom.

**15:3** He walked in all the sins of his father that he had done before him, and his heart was not wholly with the LORD his God, like the heart of David his father.

**15:4** Yet for David's sake the LORD his God gave him a lamp in Jerusalem, to raise up his son after him and to make Jerusalem stand firm,

**15:5** because David did what was right in the eyes of the LORD and did not turn aside from anything that He commanded him all the days of his life, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.

**15:7** And the rest of the deeds of Abijam and all that he did, are they not written in the scroll of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? And there was war between Abijam and Jeroboam.

**15:8** And Abijam lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David, and his son Asa began to reign in his place.

**15:9** And in the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa began to reign as king of Judah.

**15:10** And he reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Maacah, daughter of Abishalom.

**15:11** And Asa did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, like David his father.

**15:12** He banished the male cult prostitutes from the land and removed all the idols that his fathers had made.

**15:13** Moreover, he removed Maacah his mother from being queen mother, because she had made a repulsive image for Asherah; and Asa cut down her repulsive image and burned it in the Kidron Valley.

**15:14** But the high places were not removed; nevertheless, Asa's heart was wholly with the LORD all his days.

**15:15** He brought in the dedicated things of his father and the dedicated things of the house of the LORD, silver and gold and vessels.

**15:16** And there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.

**15:17** Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah and built Ramah, so as not to let anyone go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.

**15:18** Then Asa took all the silver and the gold remaining in the treasuries of the house of the LORD and the treasuries of the king's house, and he put them into the hand of his servants. And King Asa sent them to Ben-hadad son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion, king of Aram, who resided in Damascus, saying,

**15:19** "Let there be a treaty between me and you, as between my father and your father. Look, I have sent you a gift of silver and gold; go, break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will withdraw from me."

**15:20** Ben-hadad listened to King Asa and sent the commanders of his armies against the cities of Israel, and he struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and all Chinneroth, along with all the land of Naphtali.

**15:21** When Baasha heard of it, he stopped building Ramah and remained in Tirzah.

**15:22** Then King Asa issued a proclamation to all Judah, with no one exempt, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and its timber with which Baasha had been building; and with them King Asa built Geba of Benjamin and Mizpah.

**15:23** And the rest of all the deeds of Asa, and all his might, and all that he did, and the cities that he built, are they not written in the scroll of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? Yet in the time of his old age he became diseased in his feet.

**15:24** And Asa lay down with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father, and his son Jehoshaphat began to reign in his place.

**15:25** And Nadab son of Jeroboam began to reign over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah, and he reigned over Israel two years.

**15:26** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and walked in the way of his father and in his sin by which he caused Israel to sin.

**15:27** Then Baasha son of Ahijah, of the house of Issachar, conspired against him; and Baasha struck him down at Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines, while Nadab and all Israel were laying siege to Gibbethon.

**15:28** So Baasha put him to death in the third year of Asa king of Judah, and began to reign in his place.

**15:29** As soon as he became king, he struck down the entire house of Jeroboam; he did not leave to Jeroboam anyone who breathed, until he had destroyed it, according to the word of the LORD that He spoke by the hand of His servant Ahijah the Shilonite,

**15:30** because of the sins of Jeroboam that he sinned and by which he caused Israel to sin, because of his provocation by which he provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger.

**15:31** And the rest of the deeds of Nadab and all that he did, are they not written in the scroll of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**15:33** In the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah became king over all Israel in Tirzah for twenty-four years.

**15:34** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and walked in the way of Jeroboam and in his sin by which he caused Israel to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:4** — Hebrew nir, a by-form of ner ('lamp'), is used metaphorically for an enduring Davidic dynastic line (cf. 1 Kgs 11:36), though some scholars compare Akkadian niru ('yoke, dominion').
- **15:13** — The rare noun miphletset (from a root meaning 'to shudder') denotes an abominable or repulsive image made for Asherah; gebirah ('queen mother', lit. 'great lady') was an official royal title in Judah.
- **15:15** — Following the Qere (construct plural, 'and the dedicated things of', linked to 'the house of the LORD'); the Ketiv reads 'and his dedicated thing(s)', making 'the house of the LORD' the destination ('into the house of the LORD', cf. 2 Chr 15:18).
- **15:23** — Hebrew chalah et-raglav literally reads 'he became sick as to his feet', using the particle et to mark the affected body part (accusative of specification).
