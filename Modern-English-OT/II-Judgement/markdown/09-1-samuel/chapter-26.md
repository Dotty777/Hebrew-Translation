## Text

**26:1** Now the Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah, saying, "Is David not hiding himself on the hill of Hachilah, facing Jeshimon?"

**26:2** Then Saul rose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, and three thousand picked men of Israel were with him, to search for David in the wilderness of Ziph.

**26:3** Saul camped on the hill of Hachilah, which faces Jeshimon, along the road. Meanwhile David was staying in the wilderness, and he saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness.

**26:4** David sent out scouts and learned that Saul had come for certain.

**26:5** Then David rose and came to the place where Saul had camped. David saw the place where Saul lay, with Abner son of Ner, the commander of his army; Saul was lying within the encampment, and the people were camping all around him.

**26:6** Then David spoke up and said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, "Who will go down with me to Saul, to the camp?" Abishai replied, "I will go down with you."

**26:7** So David and Abishai came to the people by night, and look, Saul was lying asleep within the encampment, with his spear thrust into the ground at his head, and Abner and the people were lying all around him.

**26:8** Then Abishai said to David, "God has handed your enemy over into your hand today. Now then, please let me strike him to the ground with the spear in one stroke, and I will not do it to him a second time."

**26:9** David told Abishai, "Do not destroy him! For who has reached out his hand against the LORD's anointed and gone unpunished?"

**26:10** And David said, "As the LORD lives, the LORD will surely strike him down, or his day will arrive and he will die, or he will go down into battle and be swept away.

**26:11** Far be it from me before the LORD to reach out my hand against the LORD's anointed! Now then, please take the spear that is at his head and the flask of water, and let us go our way."

**26:12** So David took the spear and the flask of water from by Saul's head, and they went their way. No one saw, no one knew, and no one woke up, for they were all asleep, because a deep sleep from the LORD had fallen upon them.

**26:13** Then David crossed to the other side and stood on the top of the mountain at a distance, with a wide space between them.

**26:14** David called out to the people and to Abner son of Ner, saying, "Will you not answer, Abner?" Abner responded and said, "Who are you, calling out to the king?"

**26:15** David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? And who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not kept guard over your lord the king? For one of the people came to destroy the king, your lord.

**26:16** This thing you have done is not good. As the LORD lives, you certainly deserve to die, because you have not kept guard over your lord, over the LORD's anointed. Now look where the king's spear is, and the flask of water that was at his head!"

**26:17** Saul recognized the voice of David and said, "Is that your voice, my son David?" David replied, "It is my voice, my lord the king."

**26:18** He went on to say, "Why then is my lord pursuing after his servant? For what have I done, and what evil is in my hand?

**26:19** Now then, please let my lord the king hear the words of his servant. If the LORD has incited you against me, may He smell an offering; but if the sons of men have, cursed are they before the LORD, because today they have driven me out from attaching myself to the inheritance of the LORD, saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'

**26:20** Now therefore, do not let my blood fall to the ground away from the presence of the LORD; for the king of Israel has come out to search for a single flea, just as one pursues a partridge in the mountains."

**26:21** Saul replied, "I have sinned. Come back, my son David, for I will not harm you anymore, because my life was precious in your eyes this day. Look, I have acted foolishly and have gone very far astray."

**26:22** David answered and said, "Here is the king's spear! Let one of the young men cross over and take it.

**26:23** The LORD will repay each man for his righteousness and his faithfulness; for the LORD gave you into my hand today, yet I was unwilling to reach out my hand against the LORD's anointed.

**26:24** Now look, just as your life was greatly valued in my eyes this day, so may my life be greatly valued in the eyes of the LORD, and may He rescue me from every distress."

**26:25** Then Saul said to David, "Blessed are you, my son David! You will both surely act and surely prevail." So David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.

## Translator Notes

- **26:16** — The phrase "deserve to die" translates the Hebrew idiom literally "sons of death," denoting those liable to capital punishment.
- **26:19** — "May He smell an offering" renders the Hiphil jussive of the verb meaning "to smell," the biblical cultic idiom for God accepting a sacrifice.
- **26:22** — The qere reads "the king's spear" in standard construct form without an article on "spear," whereas the ketiv prefixes the definite article to the construct noun.
- **26:23** — The Masoretic Text literally reads "into hand" without a possessive suffix, understood from context as "into my hand."
- **26:25** — "Surely act" and "surely prevail" render two paired Qal infinitive absolute constructions, the first using the verb "do/act" absolutely in the sense of acting effectively.
