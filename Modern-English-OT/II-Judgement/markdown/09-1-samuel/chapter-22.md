## Text

**22:1** So David departed from there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's house heard, they went down to him there.

**22:2** Every man in distress, every man who had a creditor, and every man bitter of soul gathered to him, and he became commander over them. There were about four hundred men with him.

**22:3** David went from there to Mizpeh of Moab and said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and my mother come out and be with you, until I know what God will do for me."

**22:4** So he brought them before the king of Moab, and they stayed with him all the days that David was in the stronghold.

**22:5** Then Gad the prophet said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold; go and enter the land of Judah." So David departed and came into the forest of Hereth.

**22:6** Then Saul heard that David and the men who were with him had been discovered. Now Saul was sitting in Gibeah under the tamarisk tree on the height, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him.

**22:7** Saul said to his servants who were standing around him, "Listen now, Benjaminites! Will the son of Jesse give fields and vineyards to all of you as well? Will he appoint all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds,

**22:8** that all of you have conspired against me, and there is no one who reveals to my ear when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse, and there is no one among you who grieves for me and reveals to my ear that my son has raised up my servant against me to lie in ambush, as at this day?"

**22:9** Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing beside Saul's servants, answered and said, "I saw the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Ahimelech son of Ahitub.

**22:10** He inquired of the LORD for him, gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

**22:11** Then the king sent to summon Ahimelech son of Ahitub the priest, along with all his father's house, the priests who were in Nob, and all of them came to the king.

**22:12** Saul said, "Listen now, son of Ahitub." And he said, "Here I am, my lord."

**22:13** Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, in giving him bread and a sword and inquiring of God for him, that he should rise against me to lie in ambush, as at this day?"

**22:14** Then Ahimelech answered the king and said, "And who among all your servants is as faithful as David, the king's son-in-law, who turns to your bidding and is honored in your house?

**22:15** Did I begin today to inquire of God for him? Far be it from me! Let not the king lay anything against his servant or against all my father's house, for your servant knew nothing of all this, small or great."

**22:16** Then the king said, "You shall surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father's house."

**22:17** And the king said to the guards who were standing around him, "Turn and put to death the priests of the LORD, because their hand also is with David, and because they knew that he was fleeing and did not reveal it to my ear." But the king's servants were not willing to stretch out their hand to fall upon the priests of the LORD.

**22:18** Then the king said to Doeg, "You turn and fall upon the priests." So Doeg the Edomite turned and fell upon the priests himself, and he put to death on that day eighty-five men who wore a linen ephod.

**22:19** And Nob, the city of the priests, he struck with the edge of the sword, both man and woman, child and nursing infant, and ox, donkey, and sheep, with the edge of the sword.

**22:20** But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escaped, and his name was Abiathar, and he fled after David.

**22:21** And Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the LORD.

**22:22** Then David said to Abiathar, "I knew on that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul. I myself have brought this upon every life of your father's house.

**22:23** Stay with me; do not be afraid, for the one who seeks my life seeks your life, for with me you are in safekeeping."

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — The Masoretic Text reads a Qal jussive of ytza ("come out") with "with you," a pregnant construction meaning "come out and be with you."
- **22:14** — The phrase "who turns to your bidding" translates the Masoretic Hebrew Qal participle of sur ("turn aside") with mishma'at ("obedience, bidding"), referring to attentive compliance with the king's orders.
- **22:17** — Qere reads "my ear" (ozni), fitting Saul's first-person speech; ketiv has "his ear" (ozno).
- **22:18** — Here and in 22:22, the qere spells the name "Doeg" with aleph (do'eg), whereas the ketiv spells it with yod (doyeg).
- **22:22** — The verb "I myself have brought this upon" translates sabbothi (Qal perfect of sbb, "turn around" or "bring about"), used uniquely here in the sense of being the cause or occasion of the disaster.
