## Text

**12:1** And Samuel said to all Israel, "Look, I have listened to your voice regarding all that you said to me, and I have made a king reign over you.

**12:2** And now, see, the king is walking before you, while I have grown old and gray-headed, and here are my sons with you; and I have walked before you from my youth to this day.

**12:3** Here I am. Testify against me in the presence of the LORD and in the presence of His anointed one: whose ox have I taken, or whose donkey have I taken? Whom have I exploited, or whom have I crushed? And from whose hand have I taken a bribe to hide my eyes with it? Then I will restore it to you."

**12:4** And they said, "You have not exploited us, and you have not crushed us, nor have you taken anything from anyone's hand."

**12:5** Then he told them, "The LORD is a witness against you, and His anointed one is a witness this day, that you have found nothing in my hand." And they said, "He is a witness."

**12:6** Samuel said to the people, "The LORD is the one who appointed Moses and Aaron, and who brought up your fathers from the land of Egypt.

**12:7** And now, take your stand, and let me enter into judgment with you before the LORD regarding all the righteous deeds of the LORD that He did for you and for your fathers.

**12:8** When Jacob came into Egypt, your fathers cried out to the LORD, and the LORD sent Moses and Aaron; and they brought your fathers out from Egypt and caused them to dwell in this place.

**12:9** Yet they forgot the LORD their God; so He sold them into the hand of Sisera, commander of Hazor's army, and into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the king of Moab, and they battled against them.

**12:10** Then they cried out to the LORD and said, 'We have sinned, for we have abandoned the LORD and served the Baals and the Ashtaroth. And now, rescue us from the hand of our enemies, and we will serve You.'

**12:11** So the LORD sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel, and rescued you from the hand of your enemies all around, and you dwelt in safety.

**12:12** Yet when you saw that Nahash, the king of the sons of Ammon, was coming against you, you said to me, 'No! Instead, a king will reign over us,' even though the LORD your God was your king.

**12:13** And now, here is the king whom you chose, whom you asked for; and look, the LORD has placed a king over you.

**12:14** If you fear the LORD and serve Him, and listen to His voice and do not defy the command of the LORD, then both you and the king who reigns over you will follow after the LORD your God.

**12:15** But if you do not listen to the voice of the LORD, and you defy the command of the LORD, then the hand of the LORD will be against you and against your fathers.

**12:16** Now too, take your stand and see this great thing that the LORD is about to do before your eyes.

**12:17** Is today not the wheat harvest? I will call to the LORD, and He will send thunder and rain; so know and see that your evil is great, which you have done in the eyes of the LORD in asking for a king for yourselves."

**12:18** Then Samuel called to the LORD, and the LORD sent thunder and rain on that day; and all the people feared the LORD and Samuel very much.

**12:19** And all the people said to Samuel, "Pray on behalf of your servants to the LORD your God, so that we do not die, for to all our sins we have added evil in asking for a king for ourselves."

**12:20** Then Samuel said to the people, "Do not be afraid. You yourselves have done all this evil; only do not turn away from following the LORD, but serve the LORD with your whole heart.

**12:21** And do not turn away, for that would mean going after empty things, which cannot benefit and cannot rescue, because they are empty.

**12:22** For the LORD will not cast off His people for the sake of His great name, since the LORD has resolved to make you a people for Himself.

**12:23** As for me as well, far be it from me to sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray on your behalf; instead, I will teach you in the good and upright way.

**12:24** Only fear the LORD and serve Him in truth with your whole heart; for see what great things He has done with you.

**12:25** Yet if you indeed do evil, both you and your king will be swept away."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**13:1** *(after `12:25`)* Saul was one year old when he became king, and he reigned two years over Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **12:5** — Lit., 'and he said, "Witness."' The MT uses a singular verb here, understood collectively of the assembly of Israel speaking with one voice.
- **12:10** — Qere reads the plural 'and they said' (translated here), whereas ketiv writes the singular 'and he said,' taking Israel collectively.
- **12:11** — MT reads 'Bedan,' a deliverer not otherwise named in the book of Judges (some ancient versions read 'Barak').
- **12:14** — The final clause ('then both you and the king... will follow after the LORD') is rendered here as the result clause; some grammarians read it as continuing the conditional clause with an implied conclusion such as 'it will be well.'
- **12:15** — MT reads literally 'against you and against your fathers,' often understood elliptically as a comparison ('as against your fathers').
- **13:1 *(additional)*** — The Hebrew text literally reads "one year old" and "two years," widely recognized as an ancient loss of the original numerals for Saul's age and the length of his reign.
