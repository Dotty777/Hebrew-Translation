## Text

**3:1** Now the boy Samuel was serving the LORD before Eli. The word of the LORD was rare in those days, and no vision was widespread.

**3:2** It happened on that day that Eli was lying down in his place. His eyes had begun to grow dim, and he could not see.

**3:3** The lamp of God had not yet burned out, and Samuel was lying down inside the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was.

**3:4** Then the LORD called to Samuel, and he said, "Here I am."

**3:5** And he ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, because you called me." But he said, "I did not call; go back and lie down." So he went and lay down.

**3:6** Then the LORD called once more, "Samuel!" And Samuel got up and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, because you called me." But he said, "I did not call, my son; go back and lie down."

**3:7** Now Samuel did not know the LORD yet, and the word of the LORD had not been revealed to him yet.

**3:8** Then the LORD called Samuel again, a third time. He got up and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, because you called me." Then Eli understood that the LORD was calling the boy.

**3:9** Eli said to Samuel, "Go and lie down; if He calls to you, you are to say, 'Speak, LORD, because Your servant is listening.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**3:10** Then the LORD came and took His stand, and He called as at the previous times, "Samuel! Samuel!" Samuel said, "Speak, because Your servant is listening."

**3:11** The LORD said to Samuel, "Look, I am about to do something in Israel such that for everyone who hears it, both of his ears will ring.

**3:12** On that day I will bring about against Eli everything that I have spoken about his house, from start to finish.

**3:13** And I declare to him that I am judging his house forever for the iniquity he knew of, because his sons were calling a curse upon themselves, and he did not restrain them.

**3:14** And therefore I have sworn to Eli's house that the iniquity of Eli's house will not be atoned for with sacrifice or grain offering forever."

**3:15** Samuel lay down until the morning and opened the doors of the house of the LORD. But Samuel was afraid to tell Eli the vision.

**3:16** Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son." He replied, "Here I am."

**3:17** He said, "What is the word He spoke to you? Please do not conceal it from me. May God do so to you, and may He add even more, if you conceal from me a single thing from the entire word He spoke to you."

**3:18** So Samuel told him all the words and concealed nothing from him. And he said, "It is the LORD; may He do what is good in His eyes."

**3:19** Samuel grew up, and the LORD was with him, and He did not let any of his words fall to the ground.

**3:20** So all Israel, from Dan even to Beersheba, came to know that Samuel was confirmed as a prophet of the LORD.

**3:21** And the LORD continued to appear in Shiloh, because the LORD revealed Himself to Samuel in Shiloh through the word of the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **3:12** — Hebrew closes with two infinitives absolute, the Hiphil of halal ("beginning") and the Piel of kalah ("finishing"), functioning adverbially to mean "from start to finish."
- **3:13** — The Masoretic Text reads "for themselves" (lahem), which Jewish tradition identifies as a scribal correction (tiqqun soferim) for "God" (reflected in the Septuagint). The Piel participle of qalal with this pronoun yields "calling a curse upon themselves."
- **3:14** — Hebrew uses the conditional particle im ("if") after the Niphal verb "I have sworn" as an emphatic negative oath ("will not"), followed by the Hithpael of kaphar in a passive sense ("be atoned for").
