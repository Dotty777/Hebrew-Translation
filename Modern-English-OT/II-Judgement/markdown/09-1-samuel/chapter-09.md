## Text

**9:1** There was a man from Benjamin, and his name was Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of Aphiah, the son of a Benjaminite man, a mighty man of wealth.

**9:2** And he had a son, and his name was Saul, a choice young man and handsome; there was not a man among the sons of Israel more handsome than he. From his shoulders and upward he was taller than all the people.

**9:3** Now the female donkeys of Kish, Saul's father, became lost. So Kish said to his son Saul, "Please take one of the attendants with you, get up, go, and search for the female donkeys."

**9:4** He passed through the hill country of Ephraim and passed through the land of Shalishah, yet they did not find them. Then they passed through the land of Shaalim, and they were not there. Then he passed through the land of the Benjaminites, yet they did not find them.

**9:5** When they came to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his attendant who was with him, "Come, let us go back, or else my father will stop caring about the female donkeys and become anxious about us."

**9:6** He said to him, "Look now, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is held in honor; everything that he speaks surely comes to pass. Now let us go there; perhaps he will tell us about our journey on which we have gone."

**9:7** Then Saul said to his attendant, "Look, if we go, what shall we bring to the man? For the bread is gone from our packs, and there is no gift to bring to the man of God. What do we have with us?"

**9:8** The attendant answered Saul again and said, "Look, a quarter of a shekel of silver is found in my hand; I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."

**9:9** Formerly in Israel, this is what a man would say when going to inquire of God: "Come, let us go to the seer," for today's prophet was formerly called the seer.

**9:10** Then Saul said to his attendant, "Your word is good; come, let us go." So they went to the city where the man of God was.

**9:11** As they were going up the ascent of the city, they came upon young women going out to draw water, and they said to them, "Is the seer here?"

**9:12** They answered them and said, "He is; look, he is just ahead of you. Hurry now, for he has come to the city today, because the people have a sacrifice today at the high place.

**9:13** As soon as you enter the city, right then you will find him before he goes up to the high place to eat, for the people will not eat until he comes, because he blesses the sacrifice; afterward those who are invited will eat. Now go up, for at this very time you will find him."

**9:14** So they went up to the city. As they were coming into the midst of the city, there was Samuel coming out toward them to go up to the high place.

**9:15** Now the LORD had uncovered Samuel's ear one day before Saul came, saying,

**9:16** "About this time tomorrow I will send to you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him as ruler over My people Israel; and he will save My people from the hand of the Philistines. For I have seen My people, because their outcry has come to Me."

**9:17** When Samuel saw Saul, the LORD answered him, "Here is the man of whom I spoke to you! This one will govern My people."

**9:18** Then Saul approached Samuel in the middle of the gate and said, "Please tell me where the seer's house is."

**9:19** Samuel answered Saul and said, "I am the seer. Go up ahead of me to the high place, and you will eat with me today; in the morning I will send you off, and I will tell you everything that is in your heart.

**9:20** As for your female donkeys that became lost three days ago, do not set your heart on them, for they have been found. And for whom is all the desire of Israel? Is it not for you and for all your father's house?"

**9:21** Saul answered and said, "Am I not a Benjaminite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and is not my clan the least of all the clans of the tribes of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me in this manner?"

**9:22** Then Samuel took Saul and his attendant and brought them into the hall, and gave them a place at the head of those who were invited, who were about thirty men.

**9:23** Samuel said to the cook, "Bring the portion that I gave to you, about which I said to you, 'Set it aside with you.'"

**9:24** So the cook lifted up the thigh and what was on it and set it before Saul. And he said, "Look, what was reserved is set before you. Eat, for it has been kept for you for the appointed time, since I said, 'I have invited the people.'" So Saul ate with Samuel on that day.

**9:25** Then they went down from the high place into the city, and he spoke with Saul on the roof.

**9:26** They rose early, and at the rising of dawn Samuel called up to Saul on the roof, saying, "Get up, so that I may send you off." So Saul got up, and both of them, he and Samuel, went outside.

**9:27** As they were going down at the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the attendant to pass on ahead of us"—and he passed on—"but you, stand still right now, so that I may let you hear the word of God."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Ketiv reads מבן ימין ('from Ben-yamin', written as two words); Qere reads מִבִּנְיָמִין ('from Benjamin', one word). The phrase גִּבּוֹר חָיִל literally means 'mighty man of strength/substance,' denoting wealth and social standing in this context.
- **9:17** — Hebrew יַעְצֹר בְּ (Qal imperfect of עצר, literally 'restrain' or 'hold in check') is used idiomatically with the preposition בְּ to mean exercising governing authority over.
- **9:21** — Masoretic Hebrew reads שִׁבְטֵי בִנְיָמִן (plural construct, 'tribes of Benjamin'), likely referring to tribal subdivisions within Benjamin.
- **9:24** — Hebrew וְהֶעָלֶיהָ ('and what was on it') uses the definite article as a relative before a prepositional phrase. The clause לֵאמֹר הָעָם קָרָאתִי literally reads 'saying, "I have invited the people,"' indicating the time since Samuel announced the feast.
- **9:26** — Ketiv reads הגג ('the roof'); Qere reads הַגָּגָה ('to the roof', with directional he).
