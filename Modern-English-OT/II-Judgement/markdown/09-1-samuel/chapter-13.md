## Text

**13:2** And Saul chose for himself three thousand from Israel: two thousand were with Saul at Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel, and a thousand were with Jonathan at Gibeah of Benjamin; as for the rest of the people, he sent them away, each man to his tents.

**13:3** Then Jonathan struck down the garrison of the Philistines that was in Geba, and the Philistines heard about it; and Saul sounded the ram's horn throughout all the land, saying, "Let the Hebrews hear!"

**13:4** And all Israel heard it said, "Saul has struck down the garrison of the Philistines, and Israel has also become a stench to the Philistines." Then the people were summoned to follow Saul to Gilgal.

**13:5** Now the Philistines gathered together to fight with Israel: thirty thousand chariots, and six thousand horsemen, and troops in abundance like the sand on the shore of the sea. And they went up and encamped in Michmash, east of Beth-aven.

**13:6** When the men of Israel saw that they were in distress, because the people were hard-pressed, the people hid themselves in caves, in thickets, in crags, in vaults, and in cisterns.

**13:7** And some Hebrews crossed the Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead, while Saul was still at Gilgal, and all the people followed after him in trembling.

**13:8** So he waited seven days for the appointed time fixed by Samuel, yet Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the people scattered away from him.

**13:9** Then Saul said, "Bring the burnt offering and the peace offerings near to me." And he offered up the burnt offering.

**13:10** Now just as he finished offering up the burnt offering, there was Samuel coming; and Saul went out to meet him to greet him.

**13:11** And Samuel said, "What have you done?" Saul said, "Because I saw that the people had scattered away from me, and you did not come at the appointed time of the days, while the Philistines were gathering at Michmash,

**13:12** and I said, 'Now the Philistines will come down against me to Gilgal, and I have not sought favor from the LORD.' So I constrained myself and offered up the burnt offering."

**13:13** Then Samuel said to Saul, "You have behaved foolishly. You did not keep the command of the LORD your God that He commanded you; for by now the LORD would have established your kingdom over Israel forever.

**13:14** And now your kingdom will not stand. The LORD has sought for Himself a man according to His own heart, and the LORD has commissioned him as ruler over His people, because you did not keep what the LORD commanded you."

**13:15** Then Samuel rose and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. And Saul mustered the people who were found with him, about six hundred men.

**13:16** And Saul, his son Jonathan, and the people who were found with them were staying at Geba of Benjamin, whereas the Philistines had encamped in Michmash.

**13:17** And the raiding force went out from the camp of the Philistines in three columns: one column turned toward the road to Ophrah, to the land of Shual,

**13:18** and another column turned toward the road to Beth-horon, and another column turned toward the road to the border that overlooks the Valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness.

**13:19** Now no smith was to be found in the whole land of Israel, because the Philistines said, "Or else the Hebrews will make a sword or a spear."

**13:20** And all Israel went down to the Philistines, each man to sharpen his plowshare, his mattock, his axe, and his plow-blade.

**13:21** And the fee was a pim for the plowshares, for the mattocks, for a three-pronged fork, for the axes, and for setting the oxgoad.

**13:22** And it came about on the day of battle that no sword or spear was found in the hand of any of the people who were with Saul and Jonathan, yet they were found with Saul and with Jonathan his son.

**13:23** Then the outpost of the Philistines went out to the pass of Michmash.

## Translator Notes

- **13:8** — Qere reads the Hiphil wayyiqtol 'and he waited,' whereas ketiv reflects a Piel or Niphal spelling of the same root; 'for the appointed time fixed by Samuel' renders an elliptical Hebrew phrase literally reading 'to the appointed time which Samuel.'
- **13:15** — MT moves directly from Samuel's ascent to Gibeah of Benjamin to Saul's muster of the six hundred men, without the longer narrative bridge found in the Septuagint.
- **13:19** — Qere reads the plural verb 'said' with 'the Philistines,' whereas ketiv writes the singular verb.
- **13:20** — MT lists two distinct agricultural tools derived from the Hebrew root meaning 'to plow' (translated 'plowshare' and 'plow-blade'), rather than 'sickle' as in some ancient versions.
- **13:21** — The rare noun rendered 'fee' denotes the sharpening charge, and a 'pim' is an ancient Hebrew weight attested on inscribed stones at about two-thirds of a shekel; 'a three-pronged fork' translates the Hebrew literally.
