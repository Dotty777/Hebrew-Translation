## Text

**21:1** Then he rose and departed, and Jonathan went into the city.

**21:2** Then David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest, and Ahimelech trembled on going to meet David and said to him, "Why are you alone, and no man is with you?"

**21:3** And David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king charged me with a matter and said to me, 'Let no man know anything about the matter on which I am sending you and which I have commanded you.' And I have directed the young men to such and such a place.

**21:4** Now then, what is under your hand? Give five loaves of bread into my hand, or whatever is found."

**21:5** The priest answered David and said, "There is no ordinary bread under my hand, but there is holy bread, if the young men have kept themselves at least from women."

**21:6** David answered the priest and said to him, "Truly women have been kept back from us as before when I set out, and the vessels of the young men are holy, even though it is an ordinary journey; how much more today will it be holy in the vessel!"

**21:7** So the priest gave him what was holy, for there was no bread there except the bread of the Presence, which had been removed from before the LORD to set out hot bread on the day it was taken away.

**21:8** Now a man from Saul's servants was there on that day, detained before the LORD; his name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of the herdsmen who belonged to Saul.

**21:9** And David said to Ahimelech, "Is there not here under your hand a spear or a sword? For I took neither my sword nor my weapons in my hand, because the king's matter was urgent."

**21:10** Then the priest said, "The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck down in the Valley of Elah, here it is, wrapped in a garment behind the ephod. If you would take that for yourself, take it, for there is no other except it here." And David said, "There is none like it; give it to me."

**21:11** Then David rose and fled that day from before Saul, and came to Achish king of Gath.

**21:12** And the servants of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, king of the land? Did they not sing to one another about this man in the dances, saying, 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

**21:13** David laid these words in his heart, and he was greatly afraid of Achish king of Gath.

**21:14** So he changed his demeanor before their eyes and acted like a madman in their hands; he scratched marks on the doors of the gate and let his saliva run down onto his beard.

**21:15** Then Achish said to his servants, "Look, you see a man acting madly; why are you bringing him to me?

**21:16** Do I lack madmen, that you have brought this fellow to act madly in my presence? Shall this one come into my house?"

## Translator Notes

- **21:3** — The verb translated "I have directed" is an unusual Poel (or variant Hiphil) form of the root yd' ("know"), occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible with the sense of appointing or directing someone to a place.
- **21:6** — The idiom translated "as before" literally means "as yesterday and the day before," which may also mean "for about three days" since David set out. The final clause ("will it be holy in the vessel") is terse; "vessels" (keli) may refer to the young men's gear or euphemistically to their bodies.
- **21:9** — The Masoretic Text has the unusual form ve'in (pointed with hireq) before yesh ("there is"), functioning as an interrogative ("Is there not...?") equivalent to ha'en.
- **21:14** — The verb "he changed" (Piel of shnh) carries a proleptic third-person masculine singular object suffix anticipating "his demeanor." The verb "he scratched marks" (Piel of tvh) follows the Masoretic Hebrew rather than the Septuagint's "drummed."
