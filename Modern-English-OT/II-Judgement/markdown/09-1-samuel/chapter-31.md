## Text

**31:1** Meanwhile, the Philistines were fighting against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before the Philistines and fell dead on Mount Gilboa.

**31:2** And the Philistines overtook Saul and his sons, and the Philistines struck down Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.

**31:3** The battle grew heavy against Saul, and the archers, men with the bow, found him; and he was in great anguish from the archers.

**31:4** Then Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me with it, or else these uncircumcised men will come and pierce me and mistreat me." But his armor-bearer was not willing, because he was deeply afraid. So Saul took the sword and fell on it.

**31:5** And his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, and he too fell on his sword and died with him.

**31:6** So Saul died, and his three sons and his armor-bearer, and all his men as well, together on that day.

**31:7** When the men of Israel who were across the valley and those who were across the Jordan saw that the men of Israel had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned the cities and fled; and the Philistines came and lived in them.

**31:8** Now it happened on the next day that the Philistines came to strip the dead, and they found Saul and his three sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

**31:9** They cut off his head and stripped off his armor, and sent word all around the land of the Philistines to proclaim the good news in the house of their idols and to the people.

**31:10** They placed his armor in the house of Ashtaroth, and they fastened his body to the wall of Beth-shan.

**31:11** Now the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard about him, what the Philistines had done to Saul.

**31:12** So all the valiant men arose and walked all night, and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Beth-shan; and they came to Jabesh and burned them there.

**31:13** Then they took their bones and buried them under the tamarisk tree in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

## Translator Notes

- **31:3** — Masoretic vayyachel is pointed as a Qal wayyiqtol of chil/chul ('to writhe, be in anguish'); ancient versions often rendered it 'was wounded' as if from chalal ('to pierce, wound').
- **31:9** — Hebrew levasser (Piel infinitive construct of basar) means 'to proclaim good news'; bet atsabehem ('house of their idols') stands parallel to et-ha'am ('the people') and may be understood as either 'in the house of their idols' or 'to the house of their idols.'
- **31:11** — Hebrew elav ('about him' or 'concerning it') is proleptic, anticipating the following object clause 'what the Philistines had done to Saul.'
