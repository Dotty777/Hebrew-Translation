## Text

**2:1** And Hannah prayed and said, "My heart exults in the LORD; my horn is raised high in the LORD. My mouth opens wide against my enemies, for I am glad in Your salvation.

**2:2** No one is holy like the LORD, for there is none apart from You, and there is no rock like our God.

**2:3** Do not keep speaking so very proudly, nor let insolence come forth from your mouth; for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by Him deeds are weighed.

**2:4** The bows of mighty men are shattered, while those who stumble gird themselves with strength.

**2:5** Those who were sated hire themselves out for bread, while the hungry have ceased from hunger. Even the barren woman has borne seven, while she who has many sons grows faint.

**2:6** The LORD puts to death and makes alive; He brings down to Sheol and brings up.

**2:7** The LORD impoverishes and enriches; He brings low, and He also lifts high.

**2:8** He raises the lowly from the dust; from the ash heap He lifts up the needy, to seat them with nobles and have them inherit a throne of honor. For the pillars of the earth belong to the LORD, and upon them He has set the world.

**2:9** He guards the feet of His faithful ones, while the wicked are put to silence in darkness; for not by strength does a man prevail.

**2:10** Those who strive against the LORD will be shattered; in the heavens He will thunder against them. The LORD will judge the ends of the earth, and He will give might to His king and raise high the horn of His anointed."

**2:11** Then Elkanah went to Ramah, to his house, while the boy was ministering to the LORD before Eli the priest.

**2:12** Now the sons of Eli were sons of worthlessness; they did not know the LORD.

**2:13** And this was the practice of the priests with the people: whenever any man offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come while the meat was boiling, with a three-pronged fork in his hand.

**2:14** He would thrust it into the basin, or the kettle, or the cauldron, or the pot; everything that the fork brought up, the priest would take with it. This is what they would do to all Israel who came there in Shiloh.

**2:15** Even before they burned the fat, the priest's servant would come and say to the man offering the sacrifice, "Give meat to roast for the priest, for he will not take boiled meat from you, but only raw."

**2:16** And if the man said to him, "Let them surely burn the fat first, and then take for yourself whatever your soul desires," he would reply, "No, you shall give it right now; and if not, I will take it by force."

**2:17** So the sin of the young men was exceedingly great in the presence of the LORD, because the men spurned the offering of the LORD.

**2:18** Meanwhile Samuel was ministering in the presence of the LORD, a boy girded with a linen ephod.

**2:19** And his mother would make a small robe for him and bring it up to him from year to year, whenever she went up with her husband to offer the annual sacrifice.

**2:20** And Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, "May the LORD grant you offspring by this woman in place of the request that was asked for the LORD." Then they would go to his home.

**2:21** For the LORD visited Hannah, and she conceived and gave birth to three sons and two daughters. And the boy Samuel grew up with the LORD.

**2:22** Now Eli was very old, and he kept hearing everything that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they were lying with the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**2:23** And he said to them, "Why are you doing things like these? For I keep hearing of your evil deeds from all these people.

**2:24** No, my sons! For the report that I hear is not good; the people of the LORD are passing it around.

**2:25** If a man sins against another man, God will arbitrate for him; but if a man sins against the LORD, who will intercede for him?" Yet they would not listen to their father's voice, because the LORD was pleased to put them to death.

**2:26** Now the boy Samuel kept growing and gaining favor both with the LORD and with people.

**2:27** Then a man of God came to Eli and said to him, "This is what the LORD says: Did I not clearly reveal Myself to your father's house when they were in Egypt, subject to Pharaoh's house?

**2:28** And did I not choose him from all the tribes of Israel to be My priest, to go up to My altar, to burn incense, and to bear an ephod before Me? And I gave to your father's house all the fire offerings of the sons of Israel.

**2:29** Why do you kick at My sacrifice and My offering that I commanded in My dwelling, and honor your sons more than Me, by fattening yourselves on the choicest of every offering of Israel, My people?

**2:30** Therefore, declares the LORD, the God of Israel, I did indeed say, 'Your house and your father's house will walk before Me forever,' yet now, declares the LORD, far be it from Me! For those who honor Me I will honor, while those who despise Me will be held in low esteem.

**2:31** Look, days are coming when I will cut off your arm and the arm of your father's house, so that there will be no old man in your house.

**2:32** And you will look upon distress in My dwelling, amid all the good that will be done for Israel; and there will not be an old man in your house for all time.

**2:33** Yet one man of yours I will not cut off from My altar, to wear out your eyes and to make your soul languish; and all the increase of your house will die in manhood.

**2:34** And this will be the sign for you, what will come upon your two sons, upon Hophni and Phinehas: on a single day both of them will die.

**2:35** And I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest; according to what is in My heart and in My soul he will act. And I will build a sure house for him, and he will walk before My anointed for all time.

**2:36** And it will happen that everyone who remains in your house will come to bow down to him for a piece of silver and a loaf of bread, and will say, 'Please attach me to one of the priestly offices, so that I may eat a morsel of bread.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — Ketiv reads ולא ('and not'); Qere (translated here) reads וְלוֹ ('and by Him'), marking the agent of the Niphal verb נִתְכְּנוּ ('are weighed').
- **2:16** — Ketiv reads לו ('to him'); Qere (translated here) reads לֹא ('No').
- **2:20** — The Masoretic text has the 3ms Qal active שָׁאַל ('one asked', translated passively) rather than a 3fs verb, and the 3ms suffix לִמְקוֹמוֹ ('to his home/place').
- **2:24** — The clause מַעֲבִרִים עַם־יְהֹוָה is syntactically ambiguous: the Hiphil participle of עבר can mean 'passing along/circulating' with 'the people of the LORD' as subject, or 'causing to transgress' with Eli's sons as implied subject.
- **2:32** — Masoretic וְהִבַּטְתָּ צַר מָעוֹן is difficult: צַר with מָעוֹן ('dwelling', as in v. 29) may mean either 'distress in My dwelling' or 'a rival in My dwelling'; יֵיטִיב is an indefinite 3ms Hiphil ('what will be done for good').
