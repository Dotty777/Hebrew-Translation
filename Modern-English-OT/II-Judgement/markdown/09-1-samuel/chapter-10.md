## Text

**10:1** Then Samuel took the flask of oil, poured it on his head, kissed him, and said, "Has not the LORD anointed you as ruler over His inheritance?

**10:2** When you leave me today, you will find two men near Rachel's tomb in the territory of Benjamin at Zelzah, and they will say to you, 'The female donkeys that you went to search for have been found, and look, your father has left off the matter of the female donkeys and is anxious about you, saying, "What shall I do about my son?"'

**10:3** Then you will pass on farther from there and come as far as the oak of Tabor, and three men going up to God at Bethel will find you there: one carrying three young goats, and one carrying three loaves of bread, and one carrying a jar of wine.

**10:4** They will ask about your welfare and give you two loaves of bread, which you shall take from their hand.

**10:5** After that you will come to Gibeah of God, where the garrisons of the Philistines are; and when you arrive at the city there, you will encounter a band of prophets coming down from the high place, with a harp, a tambourine, a flute, and a lyre ahead of them, and they will be prophesying.

**10:6** Then the Spirit of the LORD will rush upon you, and you will prophesy with them and be turned into another man.

**10:7** And when these signs come to you, do for yourself whatever your hand finds to do, for God is with you.

**10:8** And you shall go down ahead of me to Gilgal; and look, I will go down to you to offer up burnt offerings and to sacrifice peace offerings. Seven days you shall wait until I come to you and make known to you what you shall do."

**10:9** Then it happened, as he turned his shoulder to depart from Samuel, that God changed his heart into another heart; and all these signs came to pass on that day.

**10:10** When they came there to Gibeah, look, a band of prophets was coming to meet him; and the Spirit of God rushed upon him, and he prophesied in their midst.

**10:11** Now when all who knew him previously saw him, and there he was prophesying with prophets, the people said to one another, "What is this that has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:12** A man from there answered and said, "And who is their father?" Therefore it became a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:13** When he had finished prophesying, he came to the high place.

**10:14** Now Saul's uncle said to him and to his attendant, "Where did you go?" And he said, "To search for the female donkeys; and when we saw that they were not there, we went to Samuel."

**10:15** Saul's uncle said, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

**10:16** So Saul said to his uncle, "He plainly told us that the female donkeys had been found." Yet he did not tell him about the matter of the kingdom, of which Samuel had spoken.

**10:17** Then Samuel summoned the people together to the LORD at Mizpah.

**10:18** And he said to the sons of Israel, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'I brought Israel up from Egypt, and I rescued you from the hand of Egypt and from the hand of all the kingdoms that were oppressing you.'

**10:19** Yet today you have rejected your God, who Himself saves you from all your calamities and your distresses, and you have said to Him, 'Set a king over us!' Now therefore, present yourselves before the LORD by your tribes and by your thousands."

**10:20** So Samuel brought all the tribes of Israel near, and the tribe of Benjamin was taken.

**10:21** Then he brought the tribe of Benjamin near by its clans, and the clan of the Matrites was taken. And Saul the son of Kish was taken; yet they searched for him, and he was not found.

**10:22** So they inquired again of the LORD, "Has a man come here yet?" And the LORD said, "Look, he has hidden himself among the baggage."

**10:23** So they ran and took him from there; and when he took his stand in the midst of the people, he was taller than all the people from his shoulders and upward.

**10:24** Samuel said to all the people, "Do you see the one whom the LORD has chosen? Surely there is no one like him among all the people." Then all the people shouted and said, "May the king live!"

**10:25** Then Samuel spoke to the people the ordinance of the kingdom, and wrote it in the scroll and placed it before the LORD. And Samuel sent all the people away, each man to his own house.

**10:26** Saul also went to his house at Gibeah, and with him went the men of valor whose hearts God had touched.

**10:27** But worthless men said, "How can this one save us?" So they despised him and brought him no gift. Yet he was like one who keeps silent.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — 'Gibeah of God' literally translates 'the hill of God.' Hebrew נְצִבֵי פְלִשְׁתִּים uses the masculine plural construct ('garrisons' or 'outposts of the Philistines').
- **10:7** — Ketiv reads תבאינה; Qere reads the standard Qal imperfect 3fp form תָבֹאנָה ('they come'). The idiom 'whatever your hand finds to do' means to act as the occasion requires.
- **10:19** — Masoretic Hebrew reads וַתֹּאמְרוּ לוֹ ('and you have said to Him') followed by recitative or emphatic כִּי, rather than the variant 'No' (לֹא) reflected in some ancient versions.
- **10:22** — Masoretic Hebrew reads the indefinite אִישׁ ('a man') rather than 'the man,' and הֲבָא can be parsed as either Qal perfect ('has come') or Qal active participle ('is coming').
