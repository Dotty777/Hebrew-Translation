## Text

**29:1** Now the Philistines gathered all their camps to Aphek, while Israel was encamped by the spring that is in Jezreel.

**29:2** And the lords of the Philistines were passing along by hundreds and by thousands, while David and his men were passing along in the rear with Achish.

**29:3** Then the commanders of the Philistines said, "What are these Hebrews?" And Achish said to the commanders of the Philistines, "Is this not David, the servant of Saul king of Israel, who has been with me these days or these years, and I have not found anything in him from the day he defected until this day?"

**29:4** But the commanders of the Philistines were angry with him, and the commanders of the Philistines said to him, "Send the man back, so that he may return to his place where you appointed him, and let him not go down with us into the battle, so that he does not become an adversary to us in the battle. For with what could this man reconcile himself to his master? Would it not be with the heads of those men?

**29:5** Is this not David, of whom they used to sing in the dances, saying, 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

**29:6** Then Achish called David and said to him, "As the LORD lives, you are upright, and your going out and your coming in with me in the camp is good in my eyes, for I have found no evil in you from the day of your coming to me until this day. Yet in the eyes of the lords you are not good.

**29:7** Now then, return and go in peace, so that you do not do what is evil in the eyes of the lords of the Philistines."

**29:8** And David said to Achish, "But what have I done, and what have you found in your servant from the day I have been before you until this day, that I should not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"

**29:9** Then Achish answered and said to David, "I know that you are good in my eyes, like an angel of God; nevertheless, the commanders of the Philistines have said, 'He must not go up with us into the battle.'

**29:10** So now rise early in the morning, with the servants of your master who came with you; rise early in the morning, and as soon as it is light for you, go."

**29:11** So David rose early, he and his men, to go in the morning to return to the land of the Philistines. And the Philistines went up to Jezreel.

## Translator Notes

- **29:2** — Hebrew sarne (lords) is the distinctive Philistine title for their city-state rulers, in contrast to sare (commanders, vv. 3, 4, 9), the Hebrew term for military officers or officials.
- **29:3** — Hebrew noflo (Qal infinitive construct of nafal, 'to fall') literally means 'his falling,' used idiomatically in military contexts for defecting to another side.
- **29:4** — Hebrew yitratseh (Hithpael imperfect of ratsah, 'to be pleased with') literally means 'make himself acceptable' or 'reconcile himself.'
