## Text

**23:1** Then they told David, saying, "Look, the Philistines are fighting against Keilah, and they are plundering the threshing floors."

**23:2** So David inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall I go and strike these Philistines?" And the LORD said to David, "Go and strike the Philistines, and deliver Keilah."

**23:3** But David's men said to him, "Look, we are afraid here in Judah; how much more if we go to Keilah against the battle lines of the Philistines!"

**23:4** So David inquired of the LORD yet again, and the LORD answered him and said, "Rise, go down to Keilah, for I am giving the Philistines into your hand."

**23:5** So David and his men went to Keilah and fought against the Philistines; he drove off their livestock and struck them with a great blow. Thus David delivered the inhabitants of Keilah.

**23:6** Now it happened, when Abiathar son of Ahimelech fled to David at Keilah, that an ephod came down in his hand.

**23:7** When it was told to Saul that David had come to Keilah, Saul said, "God has handed him over into my hand, for he has shut himself in by entering a city with doors and a bar."

**23:8** So Saul summoned all the people to war, to go down to Keilah to besiege David and his men.

**23:9** Now David learned that Saul was plotting evil against him, so he said to Abiathar the priest, "Bring the ephod near."

**23:10** Then David said, "LORD, God of Israel, Your servant has surely heard that Saul is seeking to come to Keilah to destroy the city on my account.

**23:11** Will the citizens of Keilah surrender me into his hand? Will Saul come down, just as Your servant has heard? LORD, God of Israel, please tell Your servant." And the LORD said, "He will come down."

**23:13** Then David and his men, about six hundred men, rose and went out from Keilah, and they moved about wherever they could go. When it was told to Saul that David had escaped from Keilah, he refrained from going out.

**23:14** David stayed in the wilderness in the strongholds, and stayed in the hill country in the wilderness of Ziph. Saul sought him every day, but God did not give him into his hand.

**23:15** Now David saw that Saul had gone out to seek his life, while David was in the wilderness of Ziph at Horesh.

**23:16** Then Jonathan son of Saul rose and went to David at Horesh, and he strengthened his hand in God.

**23:17** He said to him, "Do not be afraid, for the hand of my father Saul will not find you; you will reign over Israel, and I will be second to you, and my father Saul knows this as well."

**23:18** So the two of them made a covenant before the LORD; David stayed at Horesh, while Jonathan went to his house.

**23:19** Then Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah, saying, "Is David not hiding himself among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hachilah, which is south of Jeshimon?

**23:20** And now, king, according to all the desire of your soul to come down, come down; and it will be our part to surrender him into the hand of the king."

**23:21** And Saul said, "May you be blessed by the LORD, for you have had compassion on me.

**23:22** Go now, make even more certain, and know and see his place where his foot is, and who has seen him there; for it has been told to me that he is very crafty.

**23:23** Therefore see and learn of all the hiding places where he hides himself, and return to me with certainty, and I will go with you. And if he is in the land, then I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah."

**23:24** So they rose and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. Now David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the Arabah to the south of Jeshimon.

**23:25** Then Saul and his men went to search, and they told David; so he went down to the rock and stayed in the wilderness of Maon. When Saul heard of it, he pursued after David into the wilderness of Maon.

**23:26** Saul went along one side of the mountain, and David and his men along the other side of the mountain. David was hurrying to get away from Saul, while Saul and his men were encircling David and his men to capture them.

**23:27** Then a messenger came to Saul, saying, "Hurry and come, for the Philistines have made a raid on the land!"

**23:28** So Saul turned back from pursuing after David and went to meet the Philistines. Therefore they called that place the Rock of Divisions.

## Translator Notes

- **23:6** — The Masoretic Text makes "an ephod" the grammatical subject of the Qal perfect verb yarad ("came down"), literally "an ephod came down in his hand."
- **23:7** — The verb "has handed him over" translates nikkar (Piel perfect of nkr, "treat as foreign" or "alienate"), used here in the sense of delivering someone into another's hand.
- **23:9** — The participle translated "plotting" (maharish) is a Hiphil participle of hrsh ("plow, engrave, devise"), which elsewhere carries the sense "devise" or "plot" in the Qal stem.
- **23:15** — Horesh (hachorshah, with the definite article and -ah ending) is either a place name in the wilderness of Ziph or a common noun meaning "wooded region" or "thicket."
- **23:28** — Hebrew Sela-hammahlekoth, literally "the Rock of the Divisions" (from hlq, "divide," referring to the parting of Saul and David, or possibly "the Rock of Slipping Away/Escape" from the homonymous root meaning "be smooth").
