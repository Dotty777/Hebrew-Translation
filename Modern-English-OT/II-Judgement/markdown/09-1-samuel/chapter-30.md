## Text

**30:1** Now it happened when David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, that the Amalekites had made a raid against the Negev and against Ziklag; they had struck Ziklag and burned it with fire.

**30:2** And they had taken captive the women who were in it, from small to great; they had not put anyone to death, but had driven them off and gone on their way.

**30:3** And David and his men came to the city, and look, it was burned with fire, and their wives and their sons and their daughters had been taken captive.

**30:4** Then David and the people who were with him lifted up their voices and wept until there was no strength in them to weep.

**30:5** And David's two wives had been taken captive: Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

**30:6** And David was in great distress, for the people talked of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was bitter, each man over his sons and over his daughters. Yet David strengthened himself in the LORD his God.

**30:7** Then David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, "Please bring the ephod near to me." So Abiathar brought the ephod near to David.

**30:8** And David inquired of the LORD, saying, "Should I pursue this raiding band? Will I overtake it?" And He said to him, "Pursue, for you will certainly overtake and you will certainly rescue."

**30:9** So David went, he and the six hundred men who were with him, and they came to the Brook Besor, where those remaining behind stayed.

**30:10** And David pursued, he and four hundred men, while two hundred men stayed behind, who were too exhausted to cross the Brook Besor.

**30:11** Now they found an Egyptian man in the field and took him to David; and they gave him bread and he ate, and they gave him water to drink.

**30:12** They also gave him a piece of fig cake and two clusters of raisins, and he ate, and his spirit returned to him; for he had not eaten bread or drunk water for three days and three nights.

**30:13** Then David said to him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" And he said, "I am an Egyptian young man, a slave of an Amalekite man; and my master abandoned me because I fell sick three days ago.

**30:14** We made a raid against the Negev of the Cherethites, and against what belongs to Judah, and against the Negev of Caleb; and we burned Ziklag with fire."

**30:15** Then David said to him, "Will you bring me down to this raiding band?" And he said, "Swear to me by God that you will neither put me to death nor hand me over into the hand of my master, and I will bring you down to this raiding band."

**30:16** So he brought him down, and look, they were spread out over the face of all the land, eating and drinking and celebrating over all the great plunder that they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.

**30:17** And David struck them down from twilight until the evening of the next day, and not a man of them escaped, except four hundred young men who rode on the camels and fled.

**30:18** So David rescued all that Amalek had taken, and David rescued his two wives.

**30:19** And nothing of theirs was missing, from the small to the great, including sons and daughters, or from the plunder, even all that they had taken for themselves; David brought back everything.

**30:20** And David took all the flocks and the herds; they drove them ahead of that livestock and said, "This is David's plunder."

**30:21** Then David came to the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow David, whom they had left to stay at the Brook Besor; and they went out to meet David and to meet the people who were with him. And David approached the people and asked about their welfare.

**30:22** Then every wicked and worthless man among the men who had gone with David answered and said, "Because they did not go with me, we will not give them any of the plunder that we recovered, except to each man his wife and his children, that they may lead them away and go."

**30:23** But David said, "You must not do so, my brothers, with what the LORD has given us; He has guarded us and has given the raiding band that came against us into our hand.

**30:24** And who will listen to you in this matter? For as is the share of the one who goes down into the battle, so will be the share of the one who stays by the baggage; they will share alike."

**30:25** And it came about from that day forward that he made it a statute and an ordinance for Israel until this day.

**30:26** When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the plunder to the elders of Judah, to his friends, saying, "Here is a blessing for you from the plunder of the enemies of the LORD."

**30:27** To those in Bethel, and to those in Ramoth of the Negev, and to those in Jattir;

**30:28** and to those in Aroer, and to those in Siphmoth, and to those in Eshtemoa;

**30:29** and to those in Racal, and to those in the cities of the Jerahmeelites, and to those in the cities of the Kenites;

**30:30** and to those in Hormah, and to those in Bor-ashan, and to those in Athach;

**30:31** and to those in Hebron, and to all the places where David had moved about, he and his men.

## Translator Notes

- **30:10** — Hebrew piggeru (Piel perfect of pagar) occurs only here and in v. 21 in the Hebrew Bible and denotes being too faint or exhausted to go on.
- **30:13** — Hebrew hayyom sheloshah literally reads 'today three,' an idiomatic expression meaning 'three days ago.'
- **30:17** — Hebrew neshef denotes twilight (either dusk or dawn), and lemochoratam literally reads 'to their next day,' an unusual suffixed form meaning 'of the next day.'
- **30:20** — The asyndetic Hebrew clause nahagu lifne hammiqneh hahu ('they drove [them] ahead of that livestock') refers to the newly captured Amalekite herds being driven in front of the recaptured Ziklag livestock.
- **30:22** — The Hebrew shifts from singular immi ('with me'), reflecting the speech of each individual objector, to plural nitten ('we will give') and hitsalnu ('we recovered').
