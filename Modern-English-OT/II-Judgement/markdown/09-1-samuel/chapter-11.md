## Text

**11:1** Then Nahash the Ammonite went up and encamped against Jabesh-gilead; and all the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, "Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you."

**11:2** Nahash the Ammonite said to them, "On this condition I will make a covenant with you: by gouging out every right eye of yours, and I will place it as a reproach upon all Israel."

**11:3** The elders of Jabesh said to him, "Grant us seven days' respite, so that we may send messengers throughout all the territory of Israel; and if there is no one to save us, we will come out to you."

**11:4** Then the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul and spoke the words in the hearing of the people; and all the people lifted up their voices and wept.

**11:5** Now look, Saul was coming from the field behind the oxen, and Saul said, "What is the matter with the people, that they are weeping?" So they recounted to him the words of the men of Jabesh.

**11:6** Then the Spirit of God rushed upon Saul when he heard these words, and his anger burned intensely.

**11:7** He took a yoke of oxen, cut them into pieces, and sent them throughout all the territory of Israel by the hand of the messengers, saying, "Whoever does not come out after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done to his oxen!" Then the dread of the LORD fell upon the people, and they came out as one man.

**11:8** He mustered them at Bezek; and the sons of Israel were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand.

**11:9** They said to the messengers who had come, "This is what you shall say to the men of Jabesh-gilead: 'Tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot, you will have deliverance.'" So the messengers came and told the men of Jabesh, and they rejoiced.

**11:10** Then the men of Jabesh said, "Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you may do to us according to all that is good in your eyes."

**11:11** On the next day, Saul arranged the people into three divisions, and they came into the midst of the camp during the morning watch and struck down Ammon until the heat of the day. And those who were left scattered, so that no two of them were left together.

**11:12** Then the people said to Samuel, "Who is the one who said, 'Shall Saul reign over us?' Hand over the men, so that we may put them to death!"

**11:13** But Saul said, "Not a man shall be put to death on this day, for today the LORD has accomplished deliverance in Israel."

**11:14** Then Samuel said to the people, "Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingdom there."

**11:15** So all the people went to Gilgal, and there they made Saul king before the LORD in Gilgal. There they also sacrificed peace offerings before the LORD, and there Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly.

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — Ketiv reads בשמעו ('at/in his hearing', with preposition בְּ); Qere reads כְּשׇׁמְעוֹ ('when he heard', with temporal preposition כְּ).
- **11:9** — Ketiv reads בחם ('in the heat of', with preposition בְּ); Qere reads כְּחֹם ('about the heat of,' i.e., 'by the time the sun is hot', with preposition כְּ).
- **11:12** — Hebrew שָׁאוּל יִמְלֹךְ עָלֵינוּ lacks an interrogative particle, expressing an incredulous rhetorical question by intonation ('Shall Saul reign over us?').
