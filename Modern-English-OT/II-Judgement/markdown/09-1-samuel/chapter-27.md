## Text

**27:1** Then David said in his heart, "Now I will be swept away one day by the hand of Saul. There is nothing better for me than that I surely escape to the land of the Philistines; then Saul will despair of searching for me anymore within all the territory of Israel, and I will escape from his hand."

**27:2** Then David rose and crossed over - he and the six hundred men who were with him - to Achish son of Maoch, the king of Gath.

**27:3** And David lived with Achish in Gath, he and his men, each man with his household, David with his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail of Carmel, Nabal's wife.

**27:4** Now it was reported to Saul that David had fled to Gath, so he did not continue to search for him anymore.

**27:5** Then David said to Achish, "If now I have found favor in your eyes, let them give me a place in one of the towns of the countryside so that I may live there; for why should your servant live in the royal city with you?"

**27:6** So on that day Achish gave Ziklag to him; for this reason Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah until this day.

**27:7** Now the number of days that David lived in the countryside of the Philistines was a year and four months.

**27:8** Now David and his men went up and made raids against the Geshurites, the Gizrites, and the Amalekites; for they were the inhabitants of the land from ancient times, as you go toward Shur and as far as the land of Egypt.

**27:9** And David would strike the land and would leave neither man nor woman alive, and he would take sheep and cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothing; then he would return and come to Achish.

**27:10** And Achish would say, "Did you not make a raid today?" And David would say, "Against the Negev of Judah, against the Negev of the Jerahmeelites, and against the Negev of the Kenites."

**27:11** David would leave neither man nor woman alive to bring to Gath, thinking, "Otherwise they might report against us, saying, 'This is what David did.'" And such was his practice all the days that he lived in the countryside of the Philistines.

**27:12** So Achish trusted David, thinking, "He has utterly made himself a stench among his people, in Israel, and he will be my servant forever."

## Translator Notes

- **27:4** — The qere reads the Qal perfect ("he did not continue"), whereas the ketiv reflects an imperfect form with the same sense.
- **27:7** — "A year" renders Hebrew yamim, literally "days," a standard biblical idiom for a full annual cycle.
- **27:8** — The qere reads "the Gizrites" (associated with Gezer), whereas the ketiv reads "the Girzites."
- **27:10** — The Masoretic Text has the negative particle al before a perfect verb, an unusual construction translated here as a negative question ("Did you not make a raid?"), whereas ancient versions reflect "Where" or "Against whom did you raid?"
