## Text

**14:1** Now it came about one day that Jonathan son of Saul said to the young man bearing his armor, "Come, and let us cross over to the outpost of the Philistines that is on the far side." Yet he did not tell his father.

**14:2** Saul was staying at the edge of Gibeah beneath the pomegranate tree that was in Migron, and the people who were with him were about six hundred men.

**14:3** And Ahijah son of Ahitub, the brother of Ichabod, son of Phinehas, son of Eli, priest of the LORD in Shiloh, was bearing an ephod. Now the people did not know that Jonathan had gone.

**14:4** Now between the passes through which Jonathan sought to cross over to the outpost of the Philistines, there was a crag of rock on one side and a crag of rock on the other side; the name of the one was Bozez, and the name of the other was Seneh.

**14:5** The one crag was a steep pillar on the north, opposite Michmash, and the other was on the south, opposite Geba.

**14:6** Then Jonathan said to the young man bearing his armor, "Come, and let us cross over to the outpost of these uncircumcised men. Perhaps the LORD will act for us, for there is no hindrance for the LORD to save by many or by few."

**14:7** And his armor-bearer said to him, "Do all that is in your heart. Turn your way; here I am with you according to your heart."

**14:8** Then Jonathan said, "Look, we are crossing over to the men, and we will reveal ourselves to them.

**14:9** If they say to us like this, 'Stay still until we reach you,' then we will stand in our place and will not go up to them.

**14:10** But if they say like this, 'Come up to us,' then we will go up, for the LORD has given them into our hand; and this will be the sign for us."

**14:11** So both of them revealed themselves to the outpost of the Philistines, and the Philistines said, "Look, Hebrews are coming out of the holes where they hid themselves!"

**14:12** Then the men of the outpost called out to Jonathan and his armor-bearer and said, "Come up to us, and we will let you know something." And Jonathan said to his armor-bearer, "Come up behind me, for the LORD has given them into the hand of Israel."

**14:13** So Jonathan climbed up on his hands and his feet, with his armor-bearer behind him. And they fell before Jonathan, while his armor-bearer was putting them to death behind him.

**14:14** And the first blow that Jonathan and his armor-bearer struck was about twenty men, within roughly half a furrow of a yoke of field.

**14:15** And there was trembling in the camp, in the field, and among all the people; the outpost and the raiders trembled too. The earth quaked, and it became a trembling from God.

**14:16** And Saul's watchmen in Gibeah of Benjamin looked, and look, the multitude was melting away and moving this way and that.

**14:17** Then Saul said to the people who were with him, "Count now and see who has gone from us." And they counted, and look, Jonathan and his armor-bearer were not there.

**14:18** Then Saul said to Ahijah, "Bring the ark of God near." For the ark of God was with the sons of Israel on that day.

**14:19** And it happened while Saul was speaking to the priest that the tumult in the camp of the Philistines kept growing greater and greater; so Saul said to the priest, "Withdraw your hand."

**14:20** Then Saul and all the people who were with him rallied together and came to the battle; and look, every man's sword was against his companion, a very great confusion.

**14:21** Now the Hebrews who had been with the Philistines previously, who had gone up with them into the camp all around, even they came to be with Israel who were with Saul and Jonathan.

**14:22** And all the men of Israel who were hiding themselves in the hill country of Ephraim heard that the Philistines had fled, and they also pursued closely after them in the battle.

**14:23** So the LORD saved Israel on that day, and the battle passed beyond Beth-aven.

**14:24** Now the men of Israel were hard-pressed on that day, for Saul had bound the people under an oath, saying, "Cursed is the man who eats food until evening and until I have avenged myself on my enemies." So none of the people tasted food.

**14:25** And all the people of the land came into the forest, and there was honey on the surface of the field.

**14:26** When the people came to the forest, look, there was a flow of honey; yet no one brought his hand to his mouth, for the people feared the oath.

**14:27** But Jonathan had not heard when his father charged the people with the oath; so he stretched out the tip of the staff that was in his hand, dipped it in the honeycomb, and brought his hand back to his mouth, and his eyes brightened.

**14:28** Then a man from among the people spoke up and said, "Your father strictly charged the people with an oath, saying, 'Cursed is the man who eats food today.'" And the people were faint.

**14:29** And Jonathan said, "My father has troubled the land. See now how my eyes have brightened because I tasted a little of this honey.

**14:30** How much more if the people had eaten freely today from the plunder of their enemies that they found! For then would not the blow against the Philistines have been greater?"

**14:31** And they struck down the Philistines on that day from Michmash to Aijalon, and the people were very faint.

**14:32** Then the people pounced upon the plunder, and they took sheep, cattle, and calves and slaughtered them on the ground; and the people ate them with the blood.

**14:33** Then they told Saul, saying, "Look, the people are sinning against the LORD by eating with the blood." And he said, "You have broken faith! Roll a large stone to me today."

**14:34** Then Saul said, "Spread out among the people and say to them, 'Bring to me, each man his ox and each man his sheep, and slaughter them here and eat, and do not sin against the LORD by eating with the blood.'" So all the people brought, each man his ox in his hand that night, and slaughtered them there.

**14:35** And Saul built an altar to the LORD; that one was the first altar he built to the LORD.

**14:36** Then Saul said, "Let us go down after the Philistines by night and plunder among them until the light of morning, and let us not leave a man among them." And they said, "Do all that is good in your eyes." But the priest said, "Let us draw near to God here."

**14:37** So Saul inquired of God, "Shall I go down after the Philistines? Will You give them into the hand of Israel?" Yet He did not answer him on that day.

**14:38** Then Saul said, "Come near here, all you chiefs of the people, and know and see how this sin has happened today.

**14:39** For as the LORD lives, who saves Israel, even if it is in Jonathan my son, he will surely die!" Yet no one from all the people answered him.

**14:40** Then he said to all Israel, "You will be on one side, and I and Jonathan my son will be on the other side." And the people said to Saul, "Do what is good in your eyes."

**14:41** Then Saul said to the LORD, the God of Israel, "Give a true decision." And Jonathan and Saul were taken, while the people went free.

**14:42** And Saul said, "Cast lots between me and Jonathan my son." And Jonathan was taken.

**14:43** Then Saul said to Jonathan, "Tell me what you have done." So Jonathan told him and said, "I did indeed taste a little honey with the tip of the staff that was in my hand. Here I am; I will die."

**14:44** And Saul said, "May God do so and add even more, for you will surely die, Jonathan!"

**14:45** But the people said to Saul, "Shall Jonathan die, who worked this great salvation in Israel? Far from it! As the LORD lives, not a hair of his head will fall to the ground, for he has worked with God on this day." So the people ransomed Jonathan, and he did not die.

**14:46** Then Saul went up from pursuing the Philistines, and the Philistines went to their own place.

**14:47** Now Saul took the kingship over Israel, and he fought on every side against all his enemies: against Moab, against the sons of Ammon, against Edom, against the kings of Zobah, and against the Philistines; and wherever he turned, he dealt punishment.

**14:48** And he acted valiantly, struck down Amalek, and delivered Israel from the hand of those who plundered them.

**14:49** Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchi-shua; and the names of his two daughters were these: the name of the firstborn was Merab, and the name of the younger was Michal.

**14:50** And the name of Saul's wife was Ahinoam daughter of Ahimaaz, and the name of the commander of his army was Abner son of Ner, Saul's uncle.

**14:51** Now Kish was the father of Saul, and Ner, the father of Abner, was the son of Abiel.

**14:52** Now the war against the Philistines was fierce all the days of Saul; and whenever Saul saw any mighty man or any man of valor, he gathered him to himself.

## Translator Notes

- **14:14** — Hebrew kebaḥăṣî maʿănāh ṣemed śādeh is literally "within about half a furrow of a yoke of field," referring to roughly half the furrow-length of the area a yoke of oxen could plow in a day.
- **14:18** — The Masoretic Text reads "the ark of God" (unlike the Septuagint's "the ephod") and closes with ûḇĕnê yiśrāʾēl, literally "and the sons of Israel," understood here as a comitative waw meaning "with the sons of Israel."
- **14:27** — Ketiv reads wtrʾnh ("and saw," from rʾh), while the translated Qere reads wattāʾōrnāh ("and brightened," Qal wayyiqtol from ʾwr), matching ʾōrû ("have brightened") in 14:29.
- **14:32** — Two Ketiv/Qere variants occur here: Ketiv wyʿś ("and did") against the translated Qere wayyaʿaṭ ("and pounced," Qal wayyiqtol from ʿyṭ), and Ketiv šll ("plunder") without the definite article against the translated Qere haššālāl ("the plunder").
- **14:41** — The Masoretic Text reads simply hāḇāh tāmîm, literally "give what is complete/true" (a true lot decision), without the longer Septuagint expansion mentioning Urim and Thummim.
- **14:47** — Hebrew yaršîaʿ (Hiphil imperfect of ršʿ, "condemn / declare guilty") is used here in the military sense of inflicting punishment or putting foes to the worse, whereas the Septuagint reads "he was saved."
