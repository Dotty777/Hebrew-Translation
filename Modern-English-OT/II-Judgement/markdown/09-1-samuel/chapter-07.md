## Text

**7:1** And the men of Kiriath-jearim came, brought up the ark of the LORD, and brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill; and they consecrated Eleazar his son to guard the ark of the LORD.

**7:2** From the day the ark stayed in Kiriath-jearim, the days grew many and became twenty years, and the whole house of Israel lamented after the LORD.

**7:3** Then Samuel spoke to the whole house of Israel, saying, "If you are returning to the LORD with all your heart, remove the foreign gods from among you and the Ashtaroth, and set your heart firmly toward the LORD and serve Him alone; and He will rescue you from the hand of the Philistines."

**7:4** So the sons of Israel removed the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and served the LORD alone.

**7:5** Then Samuel said, "Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray on your behalf to the LORD."

**7:6** So they gathered together to Mizpah, drew water and poured it out before the LORD, and fasted on that day; and they said there, "We have sinned against the LORD." And Samuel judged the sons of Israel at Mizpah.

**7:7** When the Philistines heard that the sons of Israel had gathered themselves to Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel. And when the sons of Israel heard of it, they were afraid of the Philistines.

**7:8** And the sons of Israel said to Samuel, "Do not stop crying out to the LORD our God for us, so that He may save us from the hand of the Philistines."

**7:9** Then Samuel took one nursing lamb and offered it up as a whole burnt offering to the LORD; and Samuel cried out to the LORD on behalf of Israel, and the LORD answered him.

**7:10** Now while Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near for battle against Israel. But the LORD thundered with a great sound on that day against the Philistines and threw them into panic, and they were defeated before Israel.

**7:11** And the men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the Philistines, and struck them down as far as below Beth-car.

**7:12** Then Samuel took one stone and set it between Mizpah and Shen, and called its name Ebenezer; and he said, "Up to now the LORD has helped us."

**7:13** So the Philistines were subdued, and they did not come into the territory of Israel again; and the hand of the LORD was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.

**7:14** And the cities that the Philistines had taken from Israel returned to Israel, from Ekron to Gath, and Israel rescued their territory from the hand of the Philistines. There was also peace between Israel and the Amorites.

**7:15** And Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.

**7:16** And he would go from year to year, making a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, and would judge Israel in all these places.

**7:17** Then his return was to Ramah, because his home was there, and there he judged Israel; and he built an altar to the LORD there.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The verb "lamented" translates a rare Niphal form of nhh (occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible), expressing mournful lamentation or earnest yearning in turning back toward the LORD.
- **7:9** — For "offered it up" (Hiphil of alah), the ketiv reads wy'lh without the final waw of the object suffix, while the qere vocalizes wayya'alehu with the explicit 3rd person masculine singular suffix.
- **7:12** — "Ebenezer" renders Hebrew Eben ha-Ezer ("Stone of Help"), playing on the verb "helped" (azar) in Samuel's words; "Shen" (ha-Shen) literally means "the Tooth" or "the Crag."
- **7:16** — Hebrew places the particle et before "all these places" as well as before "Israel," functioning either as an accusative of location or as the preposition "at/in."
