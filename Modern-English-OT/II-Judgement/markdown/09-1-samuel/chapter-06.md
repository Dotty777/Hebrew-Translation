## Text

**6:1** Now the ark of the LORD was in the territory of the Philistines for seven months.

**6:2** And the Philistines summoned the priests and the diviners, saying, "What shall we do with the ark of the LORD? Let us know with what we should send it to its place."

**6:3** And they said, "If you are sending away the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it away empty-handed, but you must certainly return a guilt offering to Him. Then you will be healed, and it will become known to you why His hand does not turn away from you."

**6:4** Then they said, "What is the guilt offering that we should return to Him?" And they said, "According to the number of the lords of the Philistines: five golden tumors and five golden mice, for one plague was on all of them and on your lords.

**6:5** So you shall make images of your tumors and images of your mice that are ravaging the land, and give glory to the God of Israel; perhaps He will lighten His hand from upon you, and from upon your gods, and from upon your land.

**6:6** Why then should you harden your hearts, as Egypt and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? After He had dealt ruthlessly with them, did they not send them away, and they departed?

**6:7** Now then, make one new cart and take two nursing cows on which no yoke has come, and hitch the cows to the cart, and bring their calves back home from following them.

**6:8** Then take the ark of the LORD and place it on the cart, and put the golden articles that you are returning to Him as a guilt offering in a box at its side; then send it away, and let it go.

**6:9** And watch: if it goes up by the way of its own territory toward Beth-shemesh, then it is He who has done this great harm to us; but if not, then we will know that it was not His hand that struck us; it happened to us by chance."

**6:10** So the men did so: they took two nursing cows and hitched them to the cart, and shut up their calves at home.

**6:11** And they placed the ark of the LORD on the cart, and the box, and the golden mice, and the images of their tumors.

**6:12** And the cows went straight along the way toward the road to Beth-shemesh; they kept going on one highway, lowing as they went, and did not turn aside to the right or to the left. And the lords of the Philistines walked after them as far as the border of Beth-shemesh.

**6:13** Now the people of Beth-shemesh were reaping the wheat harvest in the valley, and they lifted up their eyes and saw the ark, and rejoiced to see it.

**6:14** And the cart came into the field of Joshua the Beth-shemite and stopped there, where there was a large stone. Then they split the wood of the cart and offered up the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**6:15** And the Levites took down the ark of the LORD and the box that was with it, in which were the golden articles, and placed them on the large stone; and the men of Beth-shemesh offered up burnt offerings and made sacrifices on that day to the LORD.

**6:16** And the five lords of the Philistines saw it, and returned to Ekron on that day.

**6:17** Now these are the golden tumors that the Philistines returned as a guilt offering to the LORD: one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, one for Ekron.

**6:18** And the golden mice were according to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords, from fortified city to unwalled village, and as far as Great Abel, on which they set down the ark of the LORD, which is to this day in the field of Joshua the Beth-shemite.

**6:19** And He struck some of the men of Beth-shemesh because they had looked into the ark of the LORD; He struck down among the people seventy men, fifty thousand men. And the people mourned because the LORD had struck the people with a great slaughter.

**6:20** Then the men of Beth-shemesh said, "Who can stand before the LORD, this holy God? And to whom will He go up away from us?"

**6:21** So they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, "The Philistines have returned the ark of the LORD. Come down and bring it up to you."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — For "tumors" (here and in 6:5), the ketiv uses opelim ("swellings/tumors") while the qere substitutes the euphemism tehorim ("tumors/hemorrhoids"). The Masoretic Text also reads "all of them" (3rd person plural suffix) before shifting to "your lords."
- **6:6** — Hebrew plays on the root kbd ("be heavy/honored") across 6:5-6—"give glory" (kabod) and "harden" (Piel, lit. "make heavy")—contrasted with the Hiphil of qll ("lighten") in 6:5.
- **6:18** — The Masoretic Text reads "as far as Great Abel" (lit. "the great meadow/watercourse"), whereas the parallel wording in 6:14-15 and some ancient versions read "the great stone" (eben instead of abel).
- **6:19** — The Masoretic Text juxtaposes "seventy men, fifty thousand men" without a conjunction, a celebrated numerical crux variously explained as "seventy men out of fifty thousand" or as a composite figure.
