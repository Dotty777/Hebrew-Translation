## Text

**28:1** Now it happened in those days that the Philistines gathered their armies for war, to fight against Israel. And Achish said to David, "Know for certain that you will go out with me in the army, you and your men."

**28:2** David said to Achish, "Then you yourself will know what your servant will do." And Achish said to David, "Then I will appoint you guard for my head for all time."

**28:3** Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had lamented for him and buried him in Ramah, in his own city. And Saul had removed the mediums and the spiritists from the land.

**28:4** The Philistines assembled, came, and camped at Shunem, while Saul gathered all Israel, and they camped at Gilboa.

**28:5** Saul saw the Philistine camp; he was frightened, and his heart shuddered greatly.

**28:6** So Saul inquired of the LORD, but the LORD did not answer him, whether by dreams, by Urim, or by the prophets.

**28:7** Then Saul said to his servants, "Seek out for me a woman who is a medium, that I may go to her and inquire through her." And his servants said to him, "Look, there is a woman who is a medium at Endor."

**28:8** So Saul disguised himself and put on other clothes, and he went, he and two men with him, and they came to the woman by night. He said, "Please divine for me by a spirit of the dead, and bring up for me the one I tell you."

**28:9** But the woman said to him, "Look, you yourself know what Saul has done, how he has cut off the mediums and the spiritists from the land. Why then are you laying a snare for my life, to put me to death?"

**28:10** Then Saul swore to her by the LORD, saying, "As the LORD lives, no punishment will come upon you for this matter."

**28:11** The woman asked, "Whom should I bring up for you?" And he answered, "Bring Samuel up for me."

**28:12** And the woman saw Samuel and cried out in a loud voice, and the woman spoke to Saul, saying, "Why did you deceive me, when you are Saul?"

**28:13** The king said to her, "Do not be afraid! What have you seen?" And the woman said to Saul, "I have seen a god coming up from the earth."

**28:14** He asked her, "What is his appearance?" And she answered, "An old man is coming up, and he is wrapped in a robe." Then Saul knew that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and prostrated himself.

**28:15** Then Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?" And Saul answered, "I am in great distress, for the Philistines are fighting against me, and God has departed from me and answers me no more, either through the prophets or by dreams. So I called for you to make known to me what I should do."

**28:16** Samuel said, "Why then do you ask me, since the LORD has departed from you and has become your adversary?

**28:17** The LORD has done for Himself just as He spoke through me; the LORD has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.

**28:18** Because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD and did not execute His burning anger against Amalek, the LORD has therefore done this thing to you this day.

**28:19** And the LORD will deliver Israel as well, along with you, into the hand of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. The camp of Israel as well the LORD will deliver into the hand of the Philistines."

**28:20** At once Saul fell his full length to the ground and was greatly afraid because of the words of Samuel. There was also no strength in him, because he had not eaten bread all day and all night.

**28:21** Then the woman came to Saul and saw that he was deeply terrified, and she said to him, "Look, your maidservant listened to your voice; I put my life in my hand and listened to your words that you spoke to me.

**28:22** Now then, you also please listen to the voice of your maidservant, and let me place a piece of bread before you; eat, so that you may have strength when you go on your way."

**28:23** But he refused and said, "I will not eat." Yet his servants, and the woman as well, pressed him hard, so he listened to their voice. Then he got up from the ground and sat down on the bed.

**28:24** Now the woman had a fattened calf in the house, and she hurried and slaughtered it; then she took flour, kneaded it, and baked it into unleavened bread.

**28:25** She set it before Saul and before his servants, and they ate; then they got up and departed that night.

## Translator Notes

- **28:7** — "A woman who is a medium" renders Hebrew eshet ba'alat-ov, literally "a woman, mistress of a spirit of the dead"; in v. 8, the same noun ov refers to divining by such a spirit.
- **28:8** — The qere reads the Qal feminine singular imperative "divine" (qasomi), whereas the ketiv writes a plene spelling with waw (qswmy) reflecting the o-vowel.
- **28:13** — "A god" renders Hebrew elohim, here denoting a spirit or shade rising from the underworld; it takes the plural participle "coming up" in grammatical concord even though v. 14 describes a single figure.
- **28:16** — "Your adversary" translates the rare Hebrew noun ar ("enemy, adversary") attested in the Masoretic Text.
- **28:17** — The Masoretic Text reads "for Himself" (lo), referring reflexively to the LORD, whereas the Septuagint and Vulgate read "to you."
