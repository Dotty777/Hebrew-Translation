## Text

**21:1** Then the heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel.

**21:2** They spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, "The LORD commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to live in, and their pasturelands for our livestock."

**21:3** So the sons of Israel gave to the Levites from their inheritance, according to the command of the LORD, these cities and their pasturelands.

**21:4** The lot came out for the clans of the Kohathites, and to the sons of Aaron the priest, from among the Levites, went thirteen cities by lot from the tribe of Judah, from the tribe of the Simeonites, and from the tribe of Benjamin.

**21:5** And to the remaining sons of Kohath went ten cities by lot from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, from the tribe of Dan, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**21:6** And to the sons of Gershon went thirteen cities by lot from the clans of the tribe of Issachar, from the tribe of Asher, from the tribe of Naphtali, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.

**21:7** To the sons of Merari, according to their clans, went twelve cities from the tribe of Reuben, from the tribe of Gad, and from the tribe of Zebulun.

**21:8** Thus the sons of Israel gave to the Levites these cities and their pasturelands by lot, just as the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses.

**21:9** They gave from the tribe of the sons of Judah and from the tribe of the sons of Simeon these cities that are called by name.

**21:10** These went to the sons of Aaron, from the clans of the Kohathites, from the sons of Levi, because the lot came to them first.

**21:11** They gave them Kiriath-arba, father of Anak—that is, Hebron—in the hill country of Judah, and its pastureland around it.

**21:12** Yet they gave the city's fields and its villages to Caleb son of Jephunneh for his possession.

**21:13** So to the sons of Aaron the priest they gave the city of refuge for the killer, Hebron, and its pastureland; Libnah and its pastureland;

**21:14** Jattir and its pastureland; Eshtemoa and its pastureland;

**21:15** Holon and its pastureland; Debir and its pastureland;

**21:16** Ain and its pastureland; Juttah and its pastureland; and Beth-shemesh and its pastureland—nine cities from these two tribes.

**21:17** And from the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon and its pastureland; Geba and its pastureland;

**21:18** Anathoth and its pastureland; and Almon and its pastureland—four cities.

**21:19** All the cities of the sons of Aaron, the priests: thirteen cities and their pasturelands.

**21:20** Now as for the clans of the sons of Kohath, the Levites remaining from the sons of Kohath, the cities of their lot came from the tribe of Ephraim.

**21:21** They gave them the city of refuge for the killer, Shechem, and its pastureland in the hill country of Ephraim; Gezer and its pastureland;

**21:22** Kibzaim and its pastureland; and Beth-horon and its pastureland—four cities.

**21:23** And from the tribe of Dan: Elteke and its pastureland; Gibbethon and its pastureland;

**21:24** Aijalon and its pastureland; and Gath-rimmon and its pastureland—four cities.

**21:25** And from the half-tribe of Manasseh: Taanach and its pastureland; and Gath-rimmon and its pastureland—two cities.

**21:26** Ten cities in all, and their pasturelands, belonged to the clans of the remaining sons of Kohath.

**21:27** And to the sons of Gershon, from the clans of the Levites, from the half-tribe of Manasseh: the city of refuge for the killer, Golan in Bashan, and its pastureland; and Be-eshterah and its pastureland—two cities.

**21:28** And from the tribe of Issachar: Kishion and its pastureland; Daberath and its pastureland;

**21:29** Jarmuth and its pastureland; and En-gannim and its pastureland—four cities.

**21:30** And from the tribe of Asher: Mishal and its pastureland; Abdon and its pastureland;

**21:31** Helkath and its pastureland; and Rehob and its pastureland—four cities.

**21:32** And from the tribe of Naphtali: the city of refuge for the killer, Kedesh in Galilee, and its pastureland; Hammoth-dor and its pastureland; and Kartan and its pastureland—three cities.

**21:33** All the cities of the Gershonites according to their clans: thirteen cities and their pasturelands.

**21:34** And to the clans of the sons of Merari, the remaining Levites, from the tribe of Zebulun: Jokneam and its pastureland; Kartah and its pastureland;

**21:35** Dimnah and its pastureland; and Nahalal and its pastureland—four cities.

**21:36** ⟦And from the tribe of Reuben: Bezer and its pastureland; Jahaz and its pastureland;⟧

**21:37** ⟦Kedemoth and its pastureland; and Mephaath and its pastureland—four cities.⟧

**21:38** And from the tribe of Gad: the city of refuge for the killer, Ramoth in Gilead, and its pastureland; Mahanaim and its pastureland;

**21:39** Heshbon and its pastureland; and Jazer and its pastureland—four cities in all.

**21:40** All the cities for the sons of Merari according to their clans, the ones remaining from the clans of the Levites—their lot was twelve cities.

**21:41** All the cities of the Levites within the possession of the sons of Israel: forty-eight cities and their pasturelands.

**21:42** These cities were each a city with its pasturelands around it; so it was for all these cities.

**21:43** So the LORD gave to Israel the entire land that He had sworn to give to their fathers; they took possession of it and lived in it.

**21:44** And the LORD granted them rest all around, according to everything He had sworn to their fathers. Not one man of all their enemies stood before them; all their enemies the LORD gave into their hand.

**21:45** Not one word fell short of all the good words that the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel; everything came about.

## Translator Notes

- **21:9** — The Hebrew uses an impersonal third-person masculine singular Qal imperfect (yiqra, literally 'which one calls by name') with a feminine plural direct object referring to the cities, rendered passively in English.
- **21:25** — The Masoretic Text reads 'Gath-rimmon' here (the same name as the Danite city in v. 24), whereas the parallel list in 1 Chronicles 6:70 (MT 6:55) reads Bileam (Ibleam).
- **21:27** — Following the Qere (read form), 'Golan' is translated here; the Ketiv (written form) transposes the two medial consonants (glwn, i.e., Golon or Galon).
- **21:36** — Verses 36–37 are enclosed in double brackets ⟦ ⟧ because they are absent from the Aleppo-based Miqra al pi ha-Mesorah (MAM), the original body text of Codex Leningradensis (B19A, where a later hand added them in the margin), and Letteris (1852), though they appear in WLC (L) and Ben Chayyim (1525) and are required by the city totals in vv. 7, 40, and 41 (cf. 1 Chr 6:78–79).
- **21:45** — Literally 'not a word fell from all the good word that the LORD spoke,' combining the idiom of a word 'falling' (failing) with a threefold repetition of the Hebrew root for 'word/speak' (d-b-r).
