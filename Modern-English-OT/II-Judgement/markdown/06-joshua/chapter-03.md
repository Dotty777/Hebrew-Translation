## Text

**3:1** So Joshua got up early in the morning, and they set out from Shittim and came as far as the Jordan, he and all the sons of Israel, and they spent the night there before they crossed over.

**3:2** It happened at the end of three days that the officers passed through the middle of the camp.

**3:3** And they commanded the people, saying, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the Levitical priests carrying it, you yourselves shall set out from your place and walk after it.

**3:4** Yet there must be a distance between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure. Do not approach it, so that you may know the way in which you are to go, for you have not passed along this way previously."

**3:5** And Joshua said to the people, "Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will perform wondrous deeds in your midst."

**3:6** Joshua also spoke to the priests, saying, "Lift up the ark of the covenant and cross ahead of the people." So they lifted up the ark of the covenant and walked ahead of the people.

**3:7** Then the LORD said to Joshua, "This day I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel, so that they may know that as I was with Moses, I will be with you.

**3:8** And as for you, you shall command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant, saying, 'When you come to the edge of the waters of the Jordan, in the Jordan you shall stand.'"

**3:9** So Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "Draw near here and listen to the words of the LORD your God."

**3:10** And Joshua said, "By this you will know that the living God is in your midst, and that He will surely dispossess from before you the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, and the Jebusites.

**3:11** Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing before you into the Jordan.

**3:12** So now, take for yourselves twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man per tribe.

**3:13** And it will happen that when the soles of the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth, rest in the waters of the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan will be cut off—the waters flowing down from above—and they will stand in a single heap."

**3:14** It happened, when the people set out from their tents to cross over the Jordan, with the priests carrying the ark of the covenant ahead of the people,

**3:15** and when those carrying the ark came to the Jordan, and the feet of the priests carrying the ark were dipped into the edge of the waters—now the Jordan runs full over all its banks all the days of harvest—

**3:16** that the waters flowing down from above stood still and rose up in a single heap, very far away, from Adam, the city that is beside Zarethan; and those flowing down toward the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, were completely cut off. So the people crossed over opposite Jericho.

**3:17** And the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firmly on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel was crossing over on dry ground, until the entire nation had finished crossing over the Jordan.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — Hebrew מִתְּמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם ("previously") is a standard idiom literally meaning "from yesterday and the day before yesterday."
- **3:11** — The Masoretic Text places the definite article on הַבְּרִית within the chain אֲרוֹן הַבְּרִית אֲדוֹן כׇּל־הָאָרֶץ, treating "ark of the covenant" as a fixed compound governing "the Lord of all the earth" (similar to definite הָאָרוֹן in construct position in 3:14 and 3:17).
- **3:16** — Ketiv reads באדם ("at/in Adam"), while the translated Qere reads מֵאָדָם ("from Adam"), marking the upstream point from which the dammed waters rose. Also, תַּמּוּ נִכְרָתוּ pairs Qal תָּמַם ("finished") with Niphal כָּרַת ("were cut off") in verbal hendiadys ("were completely cut off").
