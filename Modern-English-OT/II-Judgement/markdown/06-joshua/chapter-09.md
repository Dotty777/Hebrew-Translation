## Text

**9:1** Now it happened when all the kings who were beyond the Jordan—in the hill country, in the lowland, and along all the coast of the Great Sea facing Lebanon, the Hittites and the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites—heard,

**9:2** they assembled together to fight against Joshua and against Israel with one voice.

**9:3** Now the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai,

**9:4** and they too acted with cunning; they went out and posed as envoys, and took worn-out sacks for their donkeys and worn-out wineskins, split open and tied up,

**9:5** with worn-out and patched sandals on their feet and worn-out garments on them; and all the bread of their provisions had dried out and turned to crumbs.

**9:6** They went to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, "We have come from a distant land; so now, make a covenant with us."

**9:7** The men of Israel said to the Hivites, "Perhaps you live among us; how then can we make a covenant with you?"

**9:8** And they said to Joshua, "We are your servants." Then Joshua said to them, "Who are you, and where do you come from?"

**9:9** They said to him, "From a very distant land your servants have come because of the name of the LORD your God, for we have heard the report of Him and all that He did in Egypt,

**9:10** and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon king of Heshbon and to Og king of Bashan, who was in Ashtaroth.

**9:11** So our elders and all the inhabitants of our land spoke to us, saying, 'Take provisions in your hand for the journey, and go to meet them and say to them, "We are your servants; so now, make a covenant with us."'

**9:12** This bread of ours, we packed it hot as provisions from our houses on the day we set out to come to you; and now, look, it is dry and has turned to crumbs.

**9:13** And these wineskins that we filled were new, and look, they have burst open; and these garments and sandals of ours have worn out because of the exceedingly long journey."

**9:14** So the men took some of their provisions, but they did not inquire of the mouth of the LORD.

**9:15** And Joshua made peace with them and made a covenant with them to let them live, and the leaders of the congregation swore to them.

**9:16** And it happened at the end of three days after they had made a covenant with them, that they heard that they were near to them and were living in their midst.

**9:17** Then the sons of Israel set out and arrived at their cities on the third day. Now their cities were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim.

**9:18** Yet the sons of Israel did not strike them down, because the leaders of the congregation had sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel. And the whole congregation grumbled against the leaders.

**9:19** But all the leaders said to the whole congregation, "We ourselves have sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel, and now we cannot touch them.

**9:20** This is what we will do to them: we will let them live, so that wrath will not come upon us because of the oath that we swore to them."

**9:21** And the leaders said to them, "Let them live." So they became cutters of wood and drawers of water for the entire congregation, just as the leaders had spoken to them.

**9:22** Then Joshua summoned them and spoke to them, saying, "Why have you deceived us, saying, 'We are very far from you,' when you are living in our midst?

**9:23** Now therefore, you are cursed, and there shall never fail to be from among you slaves—cutters of wood and drawers of water for the house of my God."

**9:24** So they answered Joshua and said, "Because it was surely told to your servants what the LORD your God had commanded His servant Moses: to give you all the land and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land from before you. Therefore we feared greatly for our lives because of you, and we have done this thing.

**9:25** Now here we are in your hand; do as is good and right in your eyes to do to us."

**9:26** So he did this for them: he rescued them from the hand of the sons of Israel, and they did not kill them.

**9:27** And on that day Joshua appointed them cutters of wood and drawers of water for the congregation and for the altar of the LORD, to this day, at the place that He would choose.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — "With one voice" literally renders "one mouth" (peh echad).
- **9:4** — The verb rendered "posed as envoys" is the Hithpael wayyiqtol of tsyr (wayyitstayyaru), related to tsir ("envoy, messenger"), contrasting with the similar-looking Hithpael of tsyd ("packed as provisions") in 9:12.
- **9:7** — The written ketiv reads plural "they said" (wy'mrw), whereas the read qere followed here has singular "said" (wayyomer) agreeing grammatically with the collective singular "man of Israel" (ish-yisrael); the verse also has a minor orthographic ketiv/qere on "make" ('krwt / ekhrat-), and the direct speech uses collective first-person singular pronouns in Hebrew.
- **9:20** — The clause "we will let them live" translates a Hiphil infinitive absolute of chayah (hachayeh) continuing the finite verb "we will do," a classical Hebrew construction for coordinated future or modal action.
