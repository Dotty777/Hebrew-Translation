## Text

**12:1** Now these are the kings of the land whom the sons of Israel struck down, and whose land they took possession of, across the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the valley of the Arnon to Mount Hermon, and all the Arabah on the east:

**12:2** Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, ruling from Aroer, which is on the rim of the valley of the Arnon, and the middle of the valley, and half of Gilead, as far as the Jabbok valley, the border of the sons of Ammon,

**12:3** and the Arabah to the Sea of Chinneroth on the east, and to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, on the east, in the direction of Beth-jeshimoth, and southward below the slopes of Pisgah;

**12:4** and the territory of Og, king of Bashan, from the remnant of the Rephaim, who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,

**12:5** and ruling over Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all of Bashan up to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and over half of Gilead to the border of Sihon, king of Heshbon.

**12:6** Moses, the servant of the LORD, and the sons of Israel struck them down; and Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave it as a possession to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**12:7** And these are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the sons of Israel struck down across the Jordan westward, from Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon to Mount Halak, which ascends toward Seir; and Joshua gave it as a possession to the tribes of Israel according to their divisions,

**12:8** in the hill country, in the lowland, in the Arabah, on the slopes, in the wilderness, and in the Negev—the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites:

**12:9** the king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, which is next to Bethel, one;

**12:10** the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of Hebron, one;

**12:11** the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one;

**12:12** the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one;

**12:13** the king of Debir, one; the king of Geder, one;

**12:14** the king of Hormah, one; the king of Arad, one;

**12:15** the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adullam, one;

**12:16** the king of Makkedah, one; the king of Bethel, one;

**12:17** the king of Tappuah, one; the king of Hepher, one;

**12:18** the king of Aphek, one; the king of Lasharon, one;

**12:19** the king of Madon, one; the king of Hazor, one;

**12:20** the king of Shimron-meron, one; the king of Achshaph, one;

**12:21** the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one;

**12:22** the king of Kedesh, one; the king of Jokneam in Carmel, one;

**12:23** the king of Dor in the upland of Dor, one; the king of Goiim in Gilgal, one;

**12:24** the king of Tirzah, one; thirty-one kings altogether.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Hebrew mizrachah hashemesh attaches the directional ending -ah directly to the construct noun mizrach ('rising') before 'the sun,' an uncommon construction denoting eastward direction.
- **12:4** — The Masoretic Text literally reads 'and the border/territory of Og' (ugevul Og), even though the following masculine singular participles ('who lived' and 'ruling' in 12:5) describe Og himself.
- **12:18** — The Masoretic pointing lasharon consists of the preposition le-, the definite article, and Sharon ('belonging to the Sharon'), though it is taken here as the place name Lasharon since the list counts him among the thirty-one kings.
- **12:23** — Hebrew goyim literally means 'nations' and is here treated as the proper name Goiim; the Masoretic Text reads 'in Gilgal' (legilgal), and 'in the upland of Dor' uses the singular construct naphath (compare plural naphoth in 11:2).
