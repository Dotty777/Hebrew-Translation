## Text

**18:1** Then the entire congregation of the sons of Israel gathered at Shiloh and set up the tent of meeting there; and the land had been subdued before them.

**18:2** However, seven tribes were left among the sons of Israel who had not divided up their inheritance.

**18:3** Then Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "How long will you be slack about entering to possess the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you?

**18:4** Provide for yourselves three men for each tribe, that I may send them; and they will rise, walk about through the land, record it in writing according to their inheritance, and come to me.

**18:5** They will divide it among themselves into seven divisions: Judah will remain within its boundary on the south, and the house of Joseph will remain within their boundary on the north.

**18:6** And you yourselves will record the land in writing in seven divisions and bring it here to me, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God.

**18:7** For the Levites have no share in your midst, since the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance; and Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have taken their inheritance across the Jordan to the east, which Moses the servant of the LORD gave them."

**18:8** So the men rose and set out, and Joshua commanded those going to record the land in writing, saying, "Go, walk about through the land, record it in writing, and return to me; and here I will cast lots for you before the LORD in Shiloh."

**18:9** The men went, passed through the land, and recorded it in writing by cities in seven divisions on a scroll; then they came to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh.

**18:10** Then Joshua cast lots for them at Shiloh before the LORD, and Joshua apportioned the land there for the sons of Israel by their divisions.

**18:11** Now the lot of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin came up by their clans, and the boundary of their lot came out between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph.

**18:12** Their boundary on the north side was from the Jordan; the boundary ascended to the slope of Jericho on the north and ascended into the hill country westward, and its outer limits were at the wilderness of Beth-aven.

**18:13** From there the boundary passed over to Luz, to the southern slope of Luz—that is, Bethel—and the boundary descended to Ataroth-addar, by the mountain that lies south of Lower Beth-horon.

**18:14** Then the boundary curved and turned on the west side southward, from the mountain that faces Beth-horon on the south, and its outer limits were at Kiriath-baal—that is, Kiriath-jearim—a city of the sons of Judah. This is the west side.

**18:15** And the south side was from the edge of Kiriath-jearim; the boundary went out westward and went out to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

**18:16** Then the boundary descended to the edge of the mountain that faces the Valley of Ben-hinnom, which is in the Valley of Rephaim on the north; and it descended to the Valley of Hinnom, to the southern slope of the Jebusites, and descended to En-rogel.

**18:17** Then it curved northward and went out to En-shemesh, and went out to Geliloth, which lies opposite the ascent of Adummim, and descended to the Stone of Bohan, son of Reuben.

**18:18** It passed on to the slope facing the Arabah on the north and descended into the Arabah.

**18:19** Then the boundary passed over to the slope of Beth-hoglah on the north, and the outer limits of the boundary were at the northern bay of the Salt Sea, at the southern end of the Jordan. This is the southern boundary.

**18:20** And the Jordan formed its boundary on the east side. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to its boundaries all around, by their clans.

**18:21** Now the cities belonging to the tribe of the sons of Benjamin by their clans were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz,

**18:22** Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,

**18:23** Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,

**18:24** Chephar-ammonah, Ophni, and Geba: twelve cities and their villages.

**18:25** Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,

**18:26** Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah,

**18:27** Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,

**18:28** Zelah, Haeleph, the Jebusite—that is, Jerusalem—Gibeath, and Kiriath: fourteen cities and their villages. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin by their clans.

## Translator Notes

- **18:4** — "Record it in writing" (here and in verses 6, 8, and 9) renders the Hebrew verb "to write" with the land as its direct object, meaning to survey and document the territory.
- **18:12** — Here and in verses 14 and 19, the written text (Ketiv) has the singular verb "was," while the read text (Qere) has the plural "were" to agree with the plural subject "outer limits." In verses 12, 13, 16, 18, and 19, "slope" literally renders "shoulder."
- **18:15** — The Masoretic Hebrew text reads "westward," though the overall southern line runs eastward from Kiriath-jearim toward Nephtoah and Jerusalem, perhaps reflecting a brief westward bend or a difficult geographic reading.
- **18:19** — In addition to the plural verb Qere (see note on verse 12), the written text (Ketiv) reads "its outer limits," while the read text (Qere) reads the construct form "the outer limits of the boundary." The word "bay" literally reads "tongue."
- **18:24** — Following the read text (Qere), this name is rendered "Chephar-ammonah"; the written text (Ketiv) reads "Chephar-ammoni."
- **18:28** — In the Masoretic text, "Gibeath" and "Kiriath" stand as separate names, bringing the list in verses 25-28 to the stated total of fourteen cities.
