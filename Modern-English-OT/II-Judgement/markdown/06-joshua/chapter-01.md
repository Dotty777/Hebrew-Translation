## Text

**1:1** It happened after the death of Moses, the servant of the LORD, that the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun, the attendant of Moses, saying,

**1:2** "Moses My servant has died. So now, rise and cross over this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving to them, to the sons of Israel.

**1:3** Every place where the sole of your foot will tread, to you I have given it, as I spoke to Moses.

**1:4** From the wilderness and this Lebanon to the great river, the Euphrates River—all the land of the Hittites—and to the Great Sea at the setting of the sun will be your territory.

**1:5** No man will stand firm before you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, I will be with you; I will not let go of you, and I will not forsake you.

**1:6** Be strong and resolute, for it is you who will bring this people to inherit the land that I swore to their fathers to give to them.

**1:7** Only be very strong and resolute, taking care to act in accordance with all the law that Moses My servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from it to the right or the left, so that you may succeed everywhere you go.

**1:8** This book of the law must not depart from your mouth; rather, you must meditate on it day and night, so that you may take care to act in accordance with everything written in it. For then you will make your ways prosperous, and then you will succeed.

**1:9** Have I not commanded you? Be strong and resolute! Do not be terrified, and do not be dismayed, for the LORD your God is with you everywhere you go."

**1:10** So Joshua commanded the officers of the people, saying,

**1:11** "Pass through the middle of the camp and command the people, saying, 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, for within three days you are crossing over this Jordan to enter and possess the land that the LORD your God is giving to you to possess it.'"

**1:12** And to the Reubenites, to the Gadites, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joshua spoke, saying,

**1:13** "Remember the word that Moses, the servant of the LORD, commanded you, saying, 'The LORD your God is granting you rest and will give you this land.'

**1:14** Your wives, your little ones, and your livestock shall stay in the land that Moses gave you beyond the Jordan, while you yourselves shall cross over armed ahead of your brothers—all the mighty warriors—and help them,

**1:15** until the LORD grants rest to your brothers just as to you, and they too possess the land that the LORD your God is giving to them. Then you shall return to the land of your possession and possess that which Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave you beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun."

**1:16** And they answered Joshua, saying, "Everything that you have commanded us we will do, and everywhere you send us we will go.

**1:17** In every respect as we obeyed Moses, so we will obey you. Only may the LORD your God be with you, just as He was with Moses.

**1:18** Every man who rebels against your command and does not heed your words in everything you command him shall be put to death. Only be strong and resolute!"

## Translator Notes

- **1:7** — The Hiphil verb תַּשְׂכִּיל (from שׂכל) combines prudent conduct with resulting success (paired with Hiphil תַּצְלִיחַ in 1:8). Also, מִמֶּנּוּ ("from it") is masculine singular, referring ad sensum to the feminine noun הַתּוֹרָה ("the law") as a body of instruction or book.
- **1:8** — Hebrew דְּרָכֶךָ ("your ways") is vocalized as a plural stem written defectively without the yod (-ֶךָ for -ֶיךָ), rather than singular דַּרְכֶּךָ.
- **1:14** — Hebrew חֲמֻשִׁים ("armed") is a Qal passive participle of debated etymology (either equipped for combat or arrayed in battle divisions), describing warriors ready for battle.
