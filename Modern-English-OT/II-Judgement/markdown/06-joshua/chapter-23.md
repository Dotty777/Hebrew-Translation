## Text

**23:1** Now it came about, many days after the LORD had given rest to Israel from all their enemies on every side, that Joshua was old, advanced in days.

**23:2** So Joshua summoned all Israel, its elders, its heads, its judges, and its officers, and said to them, "I have grown old; I am advanced in days.

**23:3** And you yourselves have seen everything that the LORD your God has done to all these nations on your account, for the LORD your God, He is the One who fights for you.

**23:4** Look, I have allotted to you these remaining nations as an inheritance for your tribes, from the Jordan—along with all the nations that I have cut off—to the Great Sea toward the setting of the sun.

**23:5** And the LORD your God Himself will thrust them back before you and drive them out from before you, and you will take possession of their land, just as the LORD your God spoke to you.

**23:6** Therefore be very strong to keep and to carry out everything written in the book of the law of Moses, not turning aside from it to the right or to the left,

**23:7** so that you do not mingle with these nations, these that remain with you; and you shall not mention the name of their gods, nor make anyone swear by them, nor serve them, nor bow down to them.

**23:8** Rather, you shall cling to the LORD your God, just as you have done until this day.

**23:9** For the LORD has driven out great and mighty nations from before you; and as for you, no man has stood before you until this day.

**23:10** One man from among you chases a thousand, for the LORD your God, He is the One who fights for you, just as He spoke to you.

**23:11** Therefore watch closely over your souls, to love the LORD your God.

**23:12** For if you do turn back and cling to the remnant of these nations, these that remain with you, and intermarry with them, and mingle with them and they with you,

**23:13** know for certain that the LORD your God will not continue to drive out these nations from before you; and they will become a snare and a trap for you, and a whip on your sides, and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that the LORD your God has given you.

**23:14** Now look, today I am going the way of all the earth, and you know with your whole heart and with your whole soul that not a single word has fallen short of all the good words that the LORD your God spoke concerning you. All of it has come about for you; not a single word of it has fallen short.

**23:15** And it will happen that just as every good thing that the LORD your God spoke to you has come upon you, so the LORD will bring upon you every harmful thing, until He has destroyed you from this good land that the LORD your God has given you.

**23:16** When you transgress the covenant of the LORD your God, which He commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them, then the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and you will perish quickly from the good land that He has given you."

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — Hebrew הִפַּלְתִּי (Hiphil of נָפַל, literally 'I have caused to fall') is an idiom for apportioning territory by lot. The geographic span מִן־הַיַּרְדֵּן ... וְהַיָּם הַגָּדוֹל omits a second preposition ('to') before 'the Great Sea.'
- **23:7** — Hebrew תַשְׁבִּיעוּ is Hiphil of שָׁבַע ('cause to swear / put under oath'), rather than Niphal ('swear'), forbidding Israelites from imposing oaths in the name of foreign gods.
- **23:13** — Hebrew שֹׁטֵט ('whip / scourge') is a hapax legomenon related to שׁוֹט ('whip'), varying the parallel imagery of Numbers 33:55.
