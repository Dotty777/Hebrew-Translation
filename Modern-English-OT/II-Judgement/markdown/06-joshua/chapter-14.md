## Text

**14:1** Now these are the portions that the sons of Israel inherited in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel assigned to them,

**14:2** by the lot of their inheritance, just as the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes and the half-tribe.

**14:3** For Moses had given the inheritance of the two tribes and the half-tribe beyond the Jordan, yet he gave the Levites no inheritance in their midst.

**14:4** For the sons of Joseph formed two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, and they gave the Levites no share in the land, except cities to live in, along with their pasturelands for their livestock and for their possessions.

**14:5** Just as the LORD had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they divided the land.

**14:6** Then the sons of Judah approached Joshua at Gilgal, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, "You yourself know the word that the LORD spoke to Moses the man of God concerning me and concerning you at Kadesh-barnea.

**14:7** I was forty years of age when Moses the servant of the LORD sent me from Kadesh-barnea to scout out the land, and I brought him back a report according to what was in my heart.

**14:8** Yet my brothers who went up with me caused the heart of the people to melt, whereas I completely followed the LORD my God.

**14:9** And Moses swore on that day, saying, 'Surely the land on which your foot has stepped will belong to you as an inheritance, and to your sons forever, because you have completely followed the LORD my God.'

**14:10** And now, look, the LORD has kept me alive, just as He spoke, these forty-five years from the time the LORD spoke this word to Moses, during which Israel walked in the wilderness; and now, here I am today, eighty-five years of age.

**14:11** Today I am still as strong as on the day Moses sent me; just as my strength was then, so is my strength now, for war, to go out and to come in.

**14:12** So now, give me this hill country about which the LORD spoke on that day, for you yourself heard on that day that Anakim are there, along with large fortified cities. Perhaps the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, just as the LORD spoke."

**14:13** And Joshua blessed him, and gave Hebron to Caleb the son of Jephunneh as an inheritance.

**14:14** For this reason Hebron has belonged to Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as an inheritance until this day, because he completely followed the LORD, the God of Israel.

**14:15** Now the name of Hebron previously was Kiriath-arba; he was the greatest man among the Anakim. And the land rested from war.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — Hebrew contrasts two stems of the verb nahal: the Qal ("inherited") for Israel's reception of the land and the Piel ("assigned") for the leaders' distribution of it as an inheritance. "Heads of the ancestral houses" renders the idiomatic shorthand "heads of the fathers."
- **14:8** — "Caused ... to melt" translates the rare Aramaizing Hiphil form himsiw (from masas/masa). "Completely followed" renders the Piel idiom mille ahare, literally "filled after" (also in vv. 9, 14).
- **14:12** — "With me" renders oti, here a Masoretic orthographic variant of the preposition itti ("with me") rather than the direct object marker.
- **14:15** — "He" (hu) refers to Arba, the namesake of Kiriath-arba ("City of Arba"). The definite adjective with be- ("the great man among the Anakim") expresses the Hebrew superlative ("the greatest man among the Anakim").
