## Text

**24:1** Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, and he summoned the elders of Israel, its heads, its judges, and its officers; and they took their stand before God.

**24:2** And Joshua said to all the people, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Beyond the River your fathers lived from ancient times—Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor—and they served other gods.

**24:3** And I took your father Abraham from beyond the River, and I led him throughout the entire land of Canaan, multiplied his offspring, and gave him Isaac.

**24:4** And I gave Jacob and Esau to Isaac. I gave Mount Seir to Esau to take possession of it, while Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt.

**24:5** And I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt with what I did in its midst; and after that I brought you out.

**24:6** So I brought your fathers out from Egypt, and you came to the sea, and the Egyptians pursued after your fathers with chariots and with horsemen to the Red Sea.

**24:7** And they cried out to the LORD, and He set deep darkness between you and the Egyptians, and He brought the sea over them and covered them. Your eyes saw what I did in Egypt, and you lived in the wilderness many days.

**24:8** And I brought you into the land of the Amorites who were living beyond the Jordan, and they fought against you. I gave them into your hand, and you took possession of their land, and I destroyed them from before you.

**24:9** And Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, rose up and fought against Israel; he sent and called for Balaam son of Beor to curse you.

**24:10** Yet I was not willing to listen to Balaam; so he indeed blessed you, and I rescued you from his hand.

**24:11** And you crossed over the Jordan and came to Jericho, and the citizens of Jericho fought against you, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and I gave them into your hand.

**24:12** And I sent the hornet ahead of you, and it drove them out from before you, the two kings of the Amorites—not by your sword and not by your bow.

**24:13** And I gave you a land on which you did not toil and cities that you did not build, and you live in them; you are eating from vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant.'

**24:14** So now, fear the LORD and serve Him in integrity and in truth, and remove the gods that your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve the LORD.

**24:15** And if it is evil in your sight to serve the LORD, today choose for yourselves whom you will serve—whether the gods that your fathers served, those from beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you live. But I and my house—we will serve the LORD."

**24:16** And the people answered and said, "Far be it from us to abandon the LORD to serve other gods,

**24:17** for the LORD our God, He is the One who brought us and our fathers up from the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves, and who did these great signs before our eyes, and guarded us along the entire way in which we walked and among all the peoples whose midst we passed through.

**24:18** And the LORD drove out all the peoples and the Amorites living in the land from before us. We too will serve the LORD, for He is our God."

**24:19** And Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve the LORD, because He is a holy God; He is a jealous God. He will not forgive your rebellion or your sins.

**24:20** If you abandon the LORD and serve foreign gods, He will turn and do harm to you and bring you to an end, after He has done good for you."

**24:21** And the people said to Joshua, "No, rather we will serve the LORD."

**24:22** Then Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that it is you who have chosen for yourselves the LORD, to serve Him." And they said, "We are witnesses."

**24:23** "So now, remove the foreign gods that are in your midst, and incline your heart toward the LORD, the God of Israel."

**24:24** And the people said to Joshua, "It is the LORD our God we will serve, and His voice we will heed."

**24:25** And Joshua made a covenant with the people on that day, and he set a statute and an ordinance for them at Shechem.

**24:26** Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of God; then he took a great stone and erected it there beneath the oak that was in the sanctuary of the LORD.

**24:27** And Joshua said to all the people, "Look, this stone will be a witness against us, because it has heard all the words of the LORD that He spoke with us; and it will be a witness against you, so that you do not deal falsely with your God."

**24:28** Then Joshua sent the people away, each man to his inheritance.

**24:29** And Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who lived on after Joshua, and who had known all the work of the LORD that He had done for Israel.

**24:30** And it happened after these things that Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died at one hundred and ten years of age.

**24:31** They buried him within the territory of his inheritance at Timnath-serah, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, to the north of Mount Gaash.

**24:32** And the bones of Joseph, which the sons of Israel had brought up out of Egypt, they buried at Shechem, in the plot of the field that Jacob had purchased from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred pieces of money; and it became an inheritance for the sons of Joseph.

**24:33** Now Eleazar son of Aaron died, and they buried him at Gibeah of his son Phinehas, which had been given to him in the hill country of Ephraim.

## Translator Notes

- **24:3** — Ketiv writes a shortened consonantal form lacking the final he, while qere supplies the full Hiphil wayyiqtol 1st-person singular form of rabah ("and I multiplied"), translated here.
- **24:7** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts within the LORD's speech from first person to third person ("He set ... He brought ... covered") and back to first person ("what I did"), using singular collective suffixes ("over them ... covered them") referring to Egypt.
- **24:8** — Ketiv reads a form with a final paragogic he and without yod, while qere reads the standard Hiphil wayyiqtol 1st-person singular of bo ("and I brought"), which fits the direct object ("you") and is translated here.
- **24:15** — Ketiv reads the preposition be-ever ("beyond"), matching vv. 2 and 14, whereas qere reads me-ever ("from beyond" or "on the other side of"), translated here.
- **24:19** — Hebrew pairs the plural adjective qedoshim ("holy") with the plural noun elohim ("God") as a plural of majesty, while the singular pronoun hu ("He") and singular el-qanno ("a jealous God") confirm singular reference to the LORD.
