## Text

**7:1** But the sons of Israel committed an act of unfaithfulness concerning what was devoted to destruction, for Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of what was devoted to destruction, so the LORD's anger burned against the sons of Israel.

**7:2** Now Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is beside Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and he said to them, "Go up and scout out the land." So the men went up and scouted out Ai.

**7:3** They returned to Joshua and said to him, "Do not let all the people go up; let about two thousand men or about three thousand men go up and strike Ai. Do not wear out all the people going there, for they are few."

**7:4** So about three thousand men from the people went up there, but they fled before the men of Ai.

**7:5** The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six men from among them, and they pursued them from before the gate as far as Shebarim and struck them down on the descent. Then the heart of the people melted and turned to water.

**7:6** Then Joshua tore his garments and fell on his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD until the evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they cast dust on their heads.

**7:7** Joshua said, "Alas, Lord GOD, why did You bring this people across the Jordan at all, to give us into the hand of the Amorites to destroy us? If only we had been content to stay on the other side of the Jordan!

**7:8** Please, Lord, what can I say after Israel has turned its back before its enemies?

**7:9** The Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear, and they will surround us and cut off our name from the earth. Then what will You do for Your great name?"

**7:10** Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Get up! Why is it that you have fallen on your face?

**7:11** Israel has sinned; they have also transgressed My covenant that I commanded them. They have also taken some of what was devoted to destruction, they have also stolen, they have also acted deceptively, and they have also placed it among their belongings.

**7:12** Therefore the sons of Israel cannot stand before their enemies; they turn their backs before their enemies because they have become devoted to destruction. I will no longer be with you unless you destroy what is devoted to destruction from your midst.

**7:13** Get up, consecrate the people, and say, 'Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: "Something devoted to destruction is in your midst, Israel. You cannot stand before your enemies until you remove what is devoted to destruction from your midst."

**7:14** In the morning you shall be brought forward by your tribes. The tribe that the LORD takes shall come forward by clans, and the clan that the LORD takes shall come forward by households, and the household that the LORD takes shall come forward man by man.

**7:15** And the one who is taken with what is devoted to destruction shall be burned with fire, he and all that belongs to him, because he has transgressed the covenant of the LORD and because he has committed a disgraceful act in Israel.'"

**7:16** So Joshua rose early in the morning and brought Israel forward by its tribes, and the tribe of Judah was taken.

**7:17** He brought the clans of Judah forward, and he took the clan of the Zerahites; then he brought the clan of the Zerahites forward man by man, and Zabdi was taken.

**7:18** He brought his household forward man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.

**7:19** Then Joshua said to Achan, "My son, please ascribe glory to the LORD, the God of Israel, and give confession to Him. Tell me now what you have done; do not conceal it from me."

**7:20** So Achan answered Joshua and said, "Indeed, I myself have sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel, and this is what I have done:

**7:21** I saw among the plunder one fine cloak from Shinar, two hundred shekels of silver, and an ingot of gold weighing fifty shekels; then I coveted them and took them. Now they are hidden in the ground inside my tent, with the silver underneath it."

**7:22** So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent; and there it was, hidden in his tent, with the silver underneath it.

**7:23** They took them out from inside the tent, brought them to Joshua and to all the sons of Israel, and poured them out before the LORD.

**7:24** Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak, the ingot of gold, his sons, his daughters, his ox, his donkey, his flock, his tent, and all that belonged to him, and they brought them up to the Valley of Achor.

**7:25** Joshua said, "Why have you brought trouble on us? The LORD will bring trouble on you today!" Then all Israel stoned him with stones; and they burned them with fire and stoned them with stones.

**7:26** They raised a large heap of stones over him, which remains to this day; and the LORD turned from His burning anger. Therefore the name of that place has been called the Valley of Achor to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — The Hebrew noun cherem denotes persons or property irrevocably dedicated to the LORD for destruction; taking from it brought Israel itself under the same status in 7:12.
- **7:5** — Hebrew ha-Shebarim carries the definite article and may denote either a proper place name, Shebarim, or a topographic feature meaning the quarries or the clefts.
- **7:17** — Unlike the Niphal passive vayyilaked in 7:16, 7:17b, and 7:18, the verb here is Qal active vayyilkod with an object marker, referring to Joshua taking the clan by lot or functioning impersonally. Hebrew also uses the singular mishpachat collectively for the clans of Judah.
- **7:21** — Ketiv reads the unapocopated Qal wayyiqtol va-er'eh, while Qere reads the standard apocopated form va-ere; both mean I saw. Hebrew leshon literally means tongue, denoting a tongue-shaped bar or ingot of gold.
- **7:25** — Joshua plays on the root akar, meaning to bring trouble on, foreshadowing the place name Valley of Achor in 7:26. The Masoretic text uses two distinct verbs for stoning, ragam with singular him and saqal with plural them after burning.
