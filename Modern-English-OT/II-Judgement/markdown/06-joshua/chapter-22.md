## Text

**22:1** Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**22:2** He said to them, "You have kept everything that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, and you have heeded my voice in everything I commanded you.

**22:3** You have not deserted your brothers during these many days until this day, and you have kept the charge of the command of the LORD your God.

**22:4** And now the LORD your God has given rest to your brothers, just as He spoke to them. Therefore now turn and go to your tents, to the land of your possession that Moses the servant of the LORD gave you across the Jordan.

**22:5** Only take great care to carry out the command and the law that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you: to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commands, to cling to Him, and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul."

**22:6** Then Joshua blessed them and sent them off, and they went to their tents.

**22:7** Now to one half of the tribe of Manasseh Moses had given land in Bashan, and to its other half Joshua had given land with their brothers across the Jordan to the west. And furthermore, when Joshua sent them off to their tents, he blessed them.

**22:8** He spoke to them, saying, "Return to your tents with great wealth and with very much livestock, with silver and gold, with bronze and iron, and with very many garments. Divide the plunder of your enemies with your brothers."

**22:9** So the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh turned back and departed from the sons of Israel, from Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan, to go to the land of Gilead, to the land of their possession, of which they had taken possession at the command of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**22:10** When they came to the districts of the Jordan that are in the land of Canaan, the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there by the Jordan, an altar great in appearance.

**22:11** Then the sons of Israel heard it said, "Look, the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built the altar facing the land of Canaan, at the districts of the Jordan, on the side of the sons of Israel."

**22:12** When the sons of Israel heard it, the whole congregation of the sons of Israel assembled at Shiloh to go up against them for war.

**22:13** Then the sons of Israel sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the sons of Reuben, to the sons of Gad, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead.

**22:14** With him were ten leaders, one leader for each father's house from all the tribes of Israel; each of them was head of his father's house among the thousands of Israel.

**22:15** They came to the sons of Reuben, to the sons of Gad, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead, and they spoke with them, saying:

**22:16** "This is what the whole congregation of the LORD says: 'What is this treachery that you have committed against the God of Israel, turning back today from following the LORD by building an altar for yourselves, to rebel today against the LORD?

**22:17** Is the iniquity of Peor too little for us, from which we have not cleansed ourselves until this day, though the plague came upon the congregation of the LORD,

**22:18** that you are turning back today from following the LORD? And it will happen that if you rebel today against the LORD, tomorrow He will be angry with the whole congregation of Israel.

**22:19** However, if the land of your possession is unclean, cross over to the land of the LORD's possession, where the tabernacle of the LORD dwells, and take possession among us. But do not rebel against the LORD, and do not rebel against us by building an altar for yourselves apart from the altar of the LORD our God.

**22:20** Did not Achan son of Zerah commit treachery regarding what was devoted to destruction, and wrath came upon the whole congregation of Israel? And he was not the only man to perish in his iniquity.'"

**22:21** Then the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered and spoke to the heads of the thousands of Israel:

**22:22** "God of gods, the LORD! God of gods, the LORD! He knows, and may Israel itself know! If it was in rebellion or in treachery against the LORD—do not save us this day—

**22:23** in building an altar for ourselves to turn back from following the LORD, or if it was to offer up burnt offering and grain offering on it, or to present sacrifices of peace offerings on it, may the LORD Himself require it.

**22:24** Rather, we truly did this out of anxiety over a matter, thinking that in time to come your sons might say to our sons, 'What do you have to do with the LORD, the God of Israel?

**22:25** For the LORD has set the Jordan as a boundary between us and you, you sons of Reuben and sons of Gad; you have no portion in the LORD.' So your sons might make our sons stop fearing the LORD.

**22:26** Therefore we said, 'Let us now take action for ourselves to build the altar, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice,

**22:27** rather, it shall be a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we are to perform the service of the LORD before Him with our burnt offerings, with our sacrifices, and with our peace offerings,' so that your sons will not say in time to come to our sons, 'You have no portion in the LORD.'

**22:28** And we thought that if it should happen that they speak to us or to our generations in time to come, then we would say, 'Look at the replica of the altar of the LORD that our fathers made, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice; rather, it is a witness between us and you.'

**22:29** Far be it from us indeed to rebel against the LORD and to turn back today from following the LORD by building an altar for burnt offering, for grain offering, and for sacrifice, besides the altar of the LORD our God that is before His tabernacle."

**22:30** When Phinehas the priest, the leaders of the congregation, and the heads of the thousands of Israel who were with him heard the words that the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the sons of Manasseh spoke, it was good in their sight.

**22:31** Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the sons of Reuben, to the sons of Gad, and to the sons of Manasseh, "Today we know that the LORD is in our midst, because you have not committed this treachery against the LORD. Now you have delivered the sons of Israel from the hand of the LORD."

**22:32** Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from the sons of Reuben and from the sons of Gad, from the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the sons of Israel, and brought back word to them.

**22:33** The report was good in the sight of the sons of Israel, and the sons of Israel blessed God and did not speak of going up against them for war to destroy the land in which the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad were living.

**22:34** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad named the altar, "It is a witness between us that the LORD is God."

## Translator Notes

- **22:7** — The ketiv reads מעבר ('from across'), while the qere followed here reads בְּעֵבֶר ('across / on the side of'), specifying the western side of the Jordan.
- **22:14** — Hebrew אַלְפֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל (literally 'thousands of Israel') refers to traditional tribal military subdivisions or clans.
- **22:19** — Hebrew וְאֹתָנוּ אַל־תִּמְרֹדוּ uses the Qal verb מָרַד ('rebel') with the direct-object pronoun אֹתָנוּ ('us'), meaning 'do not rebel against us,' parallel to 'do not rebel against the LORD.'
- **22:22** — The solemn invocation אֵל אֱלֹהִים יְהוָה may be construed as a construct chain ('God of gods, the LORD') or as three appositional titles ('The Mighty One, God, the LORD'). The parenthetical Hiphil jussive אַל־תּוֹשִׁיעֵנוּ ('do not save us') briefly addresses God directly in the second person.
- **22:34** — The Masoretic Text has no separate name word before recitative כִּי, so the clause itself functions as the altar's name; some later Hebrew manuscripts and the Syriac supply עֵד ('Witness').
