## Text

**20:1** Then the LORD spoke to Joshua, saying,

**20:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Designate for yourselves the cities of refuge about which I spoke to you through Moses,

**20:3** so that a killer who strikes down a person unintentionally, without knowledge, may flee there; and they shall be for you a refuge from the avenger of blood.'"

**20:7** So they consecrated Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, and Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah.

**20:8** And beyond the Jordan east of Jericho, they designated Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau, from the tribe of Reuben, and Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.

**20:9** These were the appointed cities for all the sons of Israel and for the sojourner who resides in their midst, so that anyone who strikes down a person unintentionally might flee there and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood until he stood before the congregation.

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — The Hebrew term translated 'killer' (Qal active participle of the verb for killing) covers both intentional and accidental homicide, and is here qualified as unintentional and without knowledge.
- **20:7** — The verb 'consecrated' renders the Hiphil of the root for holiness, marking the cities of refuge as set apart for sacred asylum, in contrast to 'designated' (Qal of 'give') in vv. 2 and 8.
- **20:8** — Ketiv writes Golon/Galon; qere reads Golan.
- **20:9** — 'Appointed' renders a unique biblical feminine form derived from the verb 'to appoint,' denoting officially designated cities of refuge.
