## Text

**19:1** Then the second lot came out for Simeon, for the tribe of the sons of Simeon according to their clans; and their inheritance was in the midst of the inheritance of the sons of Judah.

**19:2** They had in their inheritance Beersheba, Sheba, and Moladah;

**19:3** Hazar-shual, Balah, and Ezem;

**19:4** Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah;

**19:5** Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah;

**19:6** and Beth-lebaoth and Sharuhen: thirteen cities and their villages.

**19:7** Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan: four cities and their villages,

**19:8** and all the villages that were around these cities as far as Baalath-beer, Ramah of the Negev. This was the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon according to their clans.

**19:9** Out of the allotment of the sons of Judah came the inheritance of the sons of Simeon, for the portion of the sons of Judah was too large for them; so the sons of Simeon received an inheritance in the midst of their inheritance.

**19:10** Then the third lot came up for the sons of Zebulun according to their clans. And the border of their inheritance extended as far as Sarid.

**19:11** Then their border went up westward and to Maralah, and reached Dabbesheth, and reached to the brook that is opposite Jokneam.

**19:12** From Sarid it turned eastward toward the sunrise to the border of Chisloth-tabor; then it went out to Daberath and went up to Japhia.

**19:13** From there it passed on eastward toward the sunrise to Gath-hepher, to Eth-kazin; and it went out to Rimmon, curving toward Neah.

**19:14** Then the border circled around it on the north to Hannathon, and its endpoints were at the Valley of Iphtahel.

**19:15** And Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem: twelve cities and their villages.

**19:16** This was the inheritance of the sons of Zebulun according to their clans, these cities and their villages.

**19:17** For Issachar the fourth lot came out, for the sons of Issachar according to their clans.

**19:18** Their territory was Jezreel, Chesulloth, and Shunem;

**19:19** Hapharaim, Shion, and Anaharath;

**19:20** Rabbith, Kishion, and Ebez;

**19:21** and Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez.

**19:22** The border also reached Tabor, Shahazimah, and Beth-shemesh, and the endpoints of their border were at the Jordan: sixteen cities and their villages.

**19:23** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Issachar according to their clans, the cities and their villages.

**19:24** Then the fifth lot came out for the tribe of the sons of Asher according to their clans.

**19:25** Their territory was Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph;

**19:26** and Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal; and it reached Carmel on the west and Shihor-libnath.

**19:27** Then it turned toward the sunrise to Beth-dagon, and reached Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtahel northward to Beth-emek and Neiel; then it went out to Cabul on the north,

**19:28** and Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as Great Sidon.

**19:29** Then the border turned to Ramah and as far as the fortified city of Tyre; then the border turned to Hosah, and its endpoints were at the sea, from the region of Achzib.

**19:30** And Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob: twenty-two cities and their villages.

**19:31** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Asher according to their clans, these cities and their villages.

**19:32** For the sons of Naphtali the sixth lot came out, for the sons of Naphtali according to their clans.

**19:33** Their border ran from Heleph, from the oak in Zaanannim, and Adami-nekeb, and Jabneel, as far as Lakkum, and its endpoints were at the Jordan.

**19:34** Then the border turned westward to Aznoth-tabor and went out from there to Hukkok; it reached Zebulun on the south, and reached Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan toward the sunrise.

**19:35** The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth;

**19:36** Adamah, Ramah, and Hazor;

**19:37** Kedesh, Edrei, and En-hazor;

**19:38** and Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh: nineteen cities and their villages.

**19:39** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali according to their clans, the cities and their villages.

**19:40** For the tribe of the sons of Dan according to their clans, the seventh lot came out.

**19:41** The territory of their inheritance was Zorah, Eshtaol, and Ir-shemesh;

**19:42** Shaalabbin, Aijalon, and Ithlah;

**19:43** Elon, Timnah, and Ekron;

**19:44** Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath;

**19:45** Jehud, Bene-berak, and Gath-rimmon;

**19:46** and Me-jarkon and Rakkon, with the territory opposite Joppa.

**19:47** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Dan according to their clans, these cities and their villages.

**19:48** Now the territory of the sons of Dan slipped away from them; so the sons of Dan went up and fought against Leshem and captured it, and struck it with the edge of the sword, and took possession of it and settled in it; and they called Leshem Dan, after the name of Dan their father.

**19:49** When they finished apportioning the land as an inheritance according to its borders, the sons of Israel gave an inheritance to Joshua son of Nun in their midst.

**19:50** At the command of the LORD they gave him the city that he asked for, Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim; and he built the city and settled in it.

**19:51** These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel apportioned by lot in Shiloh before the LORD, at the entrance of the tent of meeting. So they finished dividing the land.

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — The Masoretic Text reads 'Beersheba and Sheba,' yielding fourteen town names before the thirteen-city total in v. 6; the conjunction may be epexegetical ('that is, Sheba') or reflect a double name.
- **19:13** — The Pual participle translated 'curving' (from a root meaning 'to trace or mark out' a boundary, cf. Josh 15:9; 18:14) describes the border bending toward Neah, though some take it as part of a compound name Rimmon-methoar.
- **19:22** — Ketiv writes Shahazumah; qere reads Shahazimah.
- **19:29** — Ketiv writes a prefix-conjugation form with waw, while qere reads the weqatal form ('and ... were') matching the surrounding boundary verbs and vv. 14 and 22. The phrase 'from the region of Achzib' translates the Masoretic wording directly.
- **19:34** — The Masoretic Text includes 'and Judah' at the Jordan on the east, even though Judah lay far to the south; some connect this to Judean-affiliated clans in Transjordan (cf. 1 Chr 2:21-22), while the Septuagint omits 'Judah.'
