## Text

**6:1** Now Jericho was closed and tightly shut up because of the sons of Israel; no one was going out and no one was coming in.

**6:2** And the LORD said to Joshua, "Look, I have given into your hand Jericho and its king, the mighty warriors.

**6:3** You shall march around the city, all the men of war, circling the city one time. So you shall do for six days.

**6:5** And it shall be that when they sound a long blast on the ram's horn, as soon as you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout a great shout; then the wall of the city will fall down in its place, and the people will go up, each man straight ahead of him."

**6:6** So Joshua son of Nun called to the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven ram's-horn trumpets ahead of the ark of the LORD."

**6:7** And he said to the people, "Move forward and march around the city, and let the armed men pass on ahead of the ark of the LORD."

**6:8** And it happened, when Joshua had spoken to the people, that the seven priests carrying the seven ram's-horn trumpets before the LORD moved forward and blew the trumpets, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD went along behind them.

**6:9** The armed men went ahead of the priests who were blowing the trumpets, and the rear guard went behind the ark, while the trumpets kept blowing as they went.

**6:10** Now Joshua commanded the people, saying, "You are not to shout, nor make your voice heard, nor let any word come out from your mouth until the day I say to you, 'Shout!' Then you shall shout."

**6:11** So he had the ark of the LORD go around the city, circling it one time; then they came into the camp and lodged in the camp.

**6:12** Joshua got up early in the morning, and the priests lifted up the ark of the LORD.

**6:13** The seven priests carrying the seven ram's-horn trumpets ahead of the ark of the LORD marched on continually and blew the trumpets; the armed men went ahead of them, and the rear guard went behind the ark of the LORD, while the trumpets kept blowing as they went.

**6:14** On the second day they marched around the city one time and returned to the camp. That is what they did for six days.

**6:15** Then on the seventh day they got up early at the rising of dawn and marched around the city in this same manner seven times; only on that day they marched around the city seven times.

**6:16** And on the seventh time, as the priests blew the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, "Shout, for the LORD has given the city to you!

**6:17** The city shall be devoted to destruction, both it and everything in it, for the LORD; only Rahab the prostitute shall stay alive, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers whom we sent.

**6:18** Only you yourselves, keep away from what is devoted to destruction, so that you do not devote yourselves to destruction by taking from what is devoted to destruction, and turn the camp of Israel into something devoted to destruction and bring trouble on it.

**6:19** Yet all silver and gold, and vessels of bronze and iron, are holy to the LORD; into the LORD's treasury they shall go."

**6:20** So the people shouted, and they blew the trumpets. And it happened, as soon as the people heard the sound of the trumpet, that the people shouted a great shout, and the wall fell down in its place; then the people went up into the city, each man straight ahead of him, and they captured the city.

**6:21** And they devoted to destruction all that was in the city, from man to woman, from young to old, and even ox, sheep, and donkey, with the edge of the sword.

**6:22** But to the two men who had spied out the land Joshua said, "Enter the house of the prostitute woman, and bring out from there the woman and all that belongs to her, just as you swore to her."

**6:23** So the young men who were spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father, her mother, her brothers, and all that belonged to her; they also brought out all her families, and they settled them outside the camp of Israel.

**6:24** Then they burned the city with fire, and everything that was in it; only the silver and the gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, they placed into the treasury of the house of the LORD.

**6:25** Yet Joshua kept alive Rahab the prostitute, her father's household, and all that belonged to her; and she has lived in the midst of Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua had sent to spy out Jericho.

**6:26** At that time Joshua imposed an oath, saying, "Cursed is the man before the LORD who rises up and builds this city, Jericho: at the price of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the price of his youngest he shall set up its gates."

**6:27** And the LORD was with Joshua, and his renown was throughout all the land.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — "Closed and tightly shut up" renders the paired Qal active participle and Pual passive participle of sagar ("shut"), emphasizing total enclosure.
- **6:7** — Ketiv reads plural "and they said" (wayyomeru), while the Qere followed here reads singular "and he said" (wayyomer), referring to Joshua.
- **6:9** — Ketiv reads the Qal perfect "they blew" (taqeu), while the Qere followed here reads the Qal active participle construct "who were blowing" (toqee).
- **6:13** — Ketiv reads the Qal active participle (holek), while the Qere followed here reads the Qal infinitive absolute (halok), matching the paired infinitive absolute wetaqoa ("going on and blowing").
- **6:18** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads taharimu, Hiphil imperfect of haram ("devote to destruction"), understood here reflexively as "devote yourselves to destruction" rather than emending to the Septuagint's "covet."
- **6:26** — The verb wayyashba is Hiphil of shaba ("imposed an oath"), and the preposition b- on "his firstborn" and "his youngest" is the beth of price ("at the price of").
