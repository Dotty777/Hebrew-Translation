## Text

**8:1** Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Take all the fighting men with you, and get up and go up to Ai. See, I have given the king of Ai, his people, his city, and his land into your hand.

**8:2** You shall do to Ai and its king just as you did to Jericho and its king; only its plunder and its livestock you may take as booty for yourselves. Set an ambush for the city behind it."

**8:3** So Joshua and all the fighting men rose to go up to Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand men, valiant warriors, and sent them out by night.

**8:4** He commanded them, saying, "See, you are to lie in ambush against the city, behind the city. Do not go very far from the city, and let all of you be ready.

**8:5** Then I and all the people who are with me will approach the city. When they come out to meet us as at the first, we will flee before them.

**8:6** They will come out after us until we have drawn them away from the city, for they will say, 'They are fleeing before us as at the first.' So we will flee before them.

**8:7** Then you shall rise up from the ambush and take possession of the city, for the LORD your God will give it into your hand.

**8:8** And when you have seized the city, you shall set the city on fire; you shall act according to the word of the LORD. See, I have commanded you."

**8:9** So Joshua sent them out, and they went to the place of ambush and stayed between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai; but Joshua spent that night among the people.

**8:10** Joshua rose early in the morning and mustered the people; then he and the elders of Israel went up before the people to Ai.

**8:11** All the fighting men who were with him went up, drew near, and arrived opposite the city; and they encamped north of Ai, with the ravine between him and Ai.

**8:12** He took about five thousand men and set them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city.

**8:14** When the king of Ai saw it, the men of the city hurried, rose early, and went out to meet Israel for battle, he and all his people, to the appointed place facing the Arabah; for he did not know that there was an ambush against him behind the city.

**8:15** Joshua and all Israel allowed themselves to be beaten before them, and they fled by way of the wilderness.

**8:16** All the people who were in Ai were summoned to pursue them, and they pursued Joshua and were drawn away from the city.

**8:17** Not a man was left in Ai or Bethel who did not go out after Israel; they left the city open and pursued Israel.

**8:18** Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Stretch out the javelin that is in your hand toward Ai, for I will give it into your hand." So Joshua stretched out the javelin that was in his hand toward the city.

**8:19** The ambush party rose quickly from its place, and as soon as he stretched out his hand they ran, entered the city, and captured it; and they hurried and set the city on fire.

**8:20** When the men of Ai turned around behind them and looked, there was the smoke of the city rising to the sky, and they had no power to flee this way or that, for the people fleeing to the wilderness turned back against the pursuers.

**8:21** When Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush party had captured the city and that the smoke of the city was rising, they turned back and struck down the men of Ai.

**8:22** The others came out from the city to meet them, so they were caught in the middle of Israel, some on one side and some on the other; and they struck them down until they left them neither survivor nor escapee.

**8:23** But they seized the king of Ai alive and brought him before Joshua.

**8:24** When Israel had finished killing all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness where they had pursued them, and all of them had fallen by the edge of the sword to the very last one, then all Israel returned to Ai and struck it with the edge of the sword.

**8:25** All who fell that day, both men and women, totaled twelve thousand, all the people of Ai.

**8:27** Only the livestock and the plunder of that city Israel took as booty for themselves, according to the word of the LORD that He had commanded Joshua.

**8:28** So Joshua burned Ai and made it a permanent mound of ruins, a desolation to this day.

**8:29** And he hanged the king of Ai on a tree until evening time; then at sunset Joshua gave a command, and they took his corpse down from the tree, threw it at the entrance of the city gate, and raised a large heap of stones over him, which remains to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **8:14** — Hebrew la-mo'ed can mean either at the appointed place or at the appointed time; followed by facing the Arabah, it likely designates a prearranged battle position.
- **8:15** — The verb vayyinnage'u is the sole Niphal occurrence of naga in the Hebrew Bible, used here in a tolerative sense meaning allowed themselves to be struck or beaten.
- **8:16** — Ketiv reads ba-ir, in the city, while Qere reads ba-Ai, in Ai; this translation follows the Qere.
- **8:20** — Hebrew lo hayah bahem yadayim literally reads there were no hands in them, an idiom for having no power or room to escape.
- **8:13 *(additional)*** — Hebrew aqevo literally means its heel, used militarily for the rear guard or ambush force positioned west of the city. The text reads Qal vayyelek, went, rather than spent the night.
- **8:26 *(additional)*** — The Hebrew term rendered "javelin" may also refer to a curved sword or scimitar.
- **8:30 *(additional)*** — The Septuagint places the account of the altar on Mount Ebal and the reading of the law (8:30-35) after 9:2.
