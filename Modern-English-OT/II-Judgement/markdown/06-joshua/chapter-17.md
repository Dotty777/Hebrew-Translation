## Text

**17:1** Then the lot was for the tribe of Manasseh, because he was the firstborn of Joseph. As for Machir, the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead, since he was a man of war, Gilead and Bashan belonged to him.

**17:2** There was also a lot for the remaining sons of Manasseh by their clans: for the sons of Abiezer, the sons of Helek, the sons of Asriel, the sons of Shechem, the sons of Hepher, and the sons of Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh, son of Joseph, by their clans.

**17:3** Now Zelophehad, son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, had no sons, but only daughters. These were the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**17:4** They came before Eleazar the priest, before Joshua son of Nun, and before the leaders, saying, "The LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our kinsmen." So according to the command of the LORD, he gave them an inheritance among the brothers of their father.

**17:5** So ten portions fell to Manasseh, apart from the land of Gilead and Bashan, which lies across the Jordan.

**17:6** For the daughters of Manasseh obtained an inheritance among his sons, and the land of Gilead belonged to the remaining sons of Manasseh.

**17:7** Now the boundary of Manasseh was from Asher to Michmethath, which faces Shechem, and the boundary went southward to the inhabitants of En-tappuah.

**17:8** To Manasseh belonged the land of Tappuah, while Tappuah at the boundary of Manasseh belonged to the sons of Ephraim.

**17:9** Then the boundary descended to the wadi of Kanah, south of the wadi. These cities belonged to Ephraim among the cities of Manasseh, while the boundary of Manasseh was north of the wadi, and its outer limits were at the sea.

**17:10** To the south it belonged to Ephraim, and to the north to Manasseh, and the sea was its boundary; and they reached Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.

**17:11** Also in Issachar and in Asher, Manasseh had Beth-shean and its surrounding towns, Ibleam and its surrounding towns, the inhabitants of Dor and its surrounding towns, the inhabitants of En-dor and its surrounding towns, the inhabitants of Taanach and its surrounding towns, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its surrounding towns, the three heights.

**17:12** Yet the sons of Manasseh were unable to dispossess these cities, and the Canaanites were determined to live in this land.

**17:13** Then, when the sons of Israel grew strong, they subjected the Canaanites to forced labor, yet they did not completely dispossess them.

**17:14** The sons of Joseph spoke with Joshua, saying, "Why have you given me one lot and one portion as an inheritance, when I am a numerous people, since the LORD has blessed me thus far?"

**17:15** Joshua said to them, "If you are a numerous people, go up into the forest and clear ground for yourself there in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, because the hill country of Ephraim is too cramped for you."

**17:16** And the sons of Joseph said, "The hill country will not suffice for us, and all the Canaanites living in the land of the valley have iron chariots, both those in Beth-shean and its surrounding towns and those in the Valley of Jezreel."

**17:17** Then Joshua spoke to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and to Manasseh, saying, "You are a numerous people, and great strength is yours; you will not have just one lot,

**17:18** but the hill country will be yours. Though it is a forest, you will clear it, and its outer limits will be yours; for you will dispossess the Canaanites, even though they have iron chariots and even though they are strong."

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — "According to the command of the LORD" literally reads "to the mouth of the LORD," an idiom for divine command. The verb "he gave" is masculine singular in the Hebrew text, referring to Joshua.
- **17:11** — Here and in verse 16, "its surrounding towns" literally reads "its daughters," the Hebrew idiom for dependent towns around a main city. "The three heights" translates a difficult Hebrew phrase literally meaning "three of the height/region," referring to three upland districts (the phrase may also be construed as a place name, "Napheth").
- **17:14** — "Since the LORD has blessed me thus far" renders a stacked Hebrew idiom literally meaning "to the extent that up to here the LORD has blessed me."
- **17:15** — The verb "clear" (here and in verse 18) is the Piel stem of a root meaning "to cut down" or "clear forest," distinct from the Qal verb "to create."
- **17:18** — The Hebrew conjunction "ki" appears five times in this verse with shifting adversative ("but"), concessive ("though" / "even though"), and causal ("for") senses.
