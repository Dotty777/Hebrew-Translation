## Text

**16:1** Now the lot came out for the sons of Joseph, from the Jordan by Jericho, at the waters of Jericho on the east, into the wilderness, ascending from Jericho into the hill country to Bethel.

**16:2** It went out from Bethel to Luz and passed over to the boundary of the Archites at Ataroth.

**16:3** Then it descended westward to the boundary of the Japhletites, as far as the boundary of Lower Beth-horon and as far as Gezer, and its outer limits were at the sea.

**16:4** And the sons of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, took their inheritance.

**16:5** Now the boundary of the sons of Ephraim by their clans was this: the boundary of their inheritance on the east was Ataroth-addar as far as Upper Beth-horon.

**16:6** Then the boundary went out toward the sea, at Michmethath on the north, and the boundary turned eastward to Taanath-shiloh and passed by it on the east to Janoah.

**16:7** It descended from Janoah to Ataroth and to Naarah, reached Jericho, and went out at the Jordan.

**16:8** From Tappuah the boundary ran westward to the wadi of Kanah, and its outer limits were at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim by their clans,

**16:9** along with the cities set apart for the sons of Ephraim within the inheritance of the sons of Manasseh, all the cities and their villages.

**16:10** Yet they did not dispossess the Canaanites living in Gezer, so the Canaanites have lived in the midst of Ephraim to this day, and they became subject to forced labor.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The phrase "into the wilderness, ascending" renders the abrupt Hebrew construction "the wilderness, going up" (masculine singular Qal participle), taking "the wilderness" as an adverbial accusative of direction for the boundary.
- **16:9** — The phrase "set apart" translates a rare feminine plural noun derived from the root "to separate," standing in apposition to "the cities."
- **16:10** — The phrase "forced labor" translates a Hebrew expression literally meaning "a serving levy" (corvee labor).
