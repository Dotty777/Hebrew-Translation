## Text

**11:1** Now it happened, when Jabin, king of Hazor, heard this, that he sent to Jobab, king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph,

**11:2** and to the kings who were in the north, in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinneroth, in the lowland, and in the uplands of Dor on the west;

**11:3** the Canaanites on the east and on the west, and the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, and the Jebusites in the hill country, and the Hivites below Hermon in the land of Mizpah.

**11:4** Then they went out, they and all their armies with them, many people, like the sand that is on the seashore in multitude, and very many horses and chariots.

**11:5** All these kings assembled, and they came and encamped together at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.

**11:6** And the LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them, for about this time tomorrow I am giving all of them over dead before Israel. You are to hamstring their horses, and you are to burn their chariots with fire."

**11:7** Then Joshua and all the fighting men with him came upon them suddenly at the waters of Merom, and they fell upon them.

**11:8** The LORD gave them into the hand of Israel, and they struck them down and pursued them as far as Great Sidon, and as far as Misrephoth-maim, and as far as the valley of Mizpeh toward the east; and they struck them until they left them no survivor.

**11:9** Then Joshua did to them as the LORD had said to him: he hamstrung their horses, and he burned their chariots with fire.

**11:10** At that time Joshua turned back and captured Hazor, and he struck its king down with the sword, for previously Hazor had been the head of all these kingdoms.

**11:11** They struck down every person who was in it with the edge of the sword, devoting them to destruction; not one who breathed remained. And he burned Hazor with fire.

**11:12** And Joshua captured all the cities of these kings and all their kings, and he struck them down with the edge of the sword; he devoted them to destruction, just as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded.

**11:13** Yet Israel burned none of the cities standing on their mounds, apart from Hazor alone, which Joshua burned.

**11:14** The sons of Israel took for themselves as plunder all the spoil of these cities and the livestock; however, they struck down every human being with the edge of the sword until they had destroyed them; they left not one who breathed.

**11:15** As the LORD had commanded His servant Moses, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did; he did not set aside a single thing from all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

**11:16** So Joshua took all this land: the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowland, the Arabah, as well as the hill country of Israel and its lowland,

**11:17** from Mount Halak, which ascends toward Seir, all the way to Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon below Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings, and he struck them down and put them to death.

**11:18** For many days Joshua waged war with all these kings.

**11:19** Not a single city made peace with the sons of Israel except the Hivites, the residents of Gibeon; they took all of them in battle.

**11:20** For it was from the LORD to harden their hearts so that they would face Israel in battle, in order to devote them to destruction, so that there would be no mercy for them, but rather to destroy them, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**11:21** At that time Joshua came and cut off the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, from all the hill country of Judah, and from all the hill country of Israel; together with their cities, Joshua devoted them to destruction.

**11:22** No Anakim remained in the land of the sons of Israel; only in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod were some left.

**11:23** Thus Joshua took all the land, in accordance with everything that the LORD had spoken to Moses, and Joshua gave it to Israel as an inheritance according to their divisions by their tribes. And the land rested from war.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Hebrew naphoth dor (feminine plural construct of naphah, 'height' or 'upland') denotes the elevated coastal territory around Dor, sometimes transliterated Naphoth-dor; compare the singular construct in 12:23.
- **11:11** — Hebrew hacharem is the Hiphil infinitive absolute of charam, used adverbially to express the solemn ban of devoting persons or cities to complete destruction; it is distinct from the Hiphil of shamad ('destroy') in 11:14 and 11:20.
- **11:13** — Literally 'on their mound' (singular tel with a plural suffix used distributively), referring to fortified cities built atop tells, the artificial hills formed by accumulated layers of earlier settlements.
- **11:20** — Hebrew lechazzeq is the Piel infinitive construct of chazaq (literally 'to strengthen' or 'to make firm,' idiomatically 'to harden'); the conjunction ki following the negative clause lebhilti functions adversatively ('but rather').
