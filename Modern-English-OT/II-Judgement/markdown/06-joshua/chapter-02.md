## Text

**2:1** Now Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men from Shittim as spies, saying, "Go, look over the land and Jericho." So they went and came to the house of a prostitute whose name was Rahab, and they stayed the night there.

**2:2** And it was reported to the king of Jericho, saying, "Look, men from the sons of Israel have come here tonight to scout out the land."

**2:3** So the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who came to you, who entered your house, for they have come to scout out the whole land."

**2:4** Now the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. And she said, "Yes, the men came to me, though I did not know where they were from.

**2:5** As the gate was to be shut in the dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went; quickly pursue them, for you will overtake them."

**2:6** Now she had brought them up onto the roof and concealed them among the flax stalks that she had arranged on the roof.

**2:7** So the men pursued them along the road to the Jordan up to the fords, and they shut the gate right after those pursuing them went out.

**2:8** And before they had lain down, she went up to them on the roof.

**2:9** And she said to the men, "I know that the LORD has given the land to you, that the dread of you has fallen on us, and that all who dwell in the land have melted away before you.

**2:10** For we heard how the LORD dried up the waters of the Red Sea before you when you came out from Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan—to Sihon and to Og—whom you devoted to total destruction.

**2:11** When we heard, our hearts melted, and no spirit endured any longer in any man because of you; for the LORD your God—He is God in the heavens above and on the earth below.

**2:12** Now then, please swear to me by the LORD, since I have acted with kindness toward you, that you too will act with kindness toward my father's household, and give me a sign of faithfulness,

**2:13** and that you will keep alive my father and my mother, my brothers and my sisters, and all that belongs to them, and rescue our lives from death."

**2:14** The men said to her, "Our lives in place of yours, even to death, if you do not disclose this matter of ours! Then, when the LORD gives us the land, we will act with kindness and faithfulness toward you."

**2:15** Then she lowered them with a rope through the window, for her house was in the side of the city wall, and it was in the wall that she lived.

**2:16** And she said to them, "Go to the hills, so that the pursuers do not come upon you, and hide yourselves there for three days until the pursuers return; afterward you may go on your way."

**2:17** Then the men said to her, "We will be free of this oath of yours that you have made us swear.

**2:18** Look, when we enter the land, you must tie this cord of scarlet thread in the window through which you lowered us, and gather your father, your mother, your brothers, and all your father's household to yourself into the house.

**2:19** And it shall be that anyone who goes out from the doors of your house into the open, his blood will be on his own head, and we will be free of blame; but as for anyone who is with you in the house, his blood will be on our head if a hand is against him.

**2:20** And if you disclose this matter of ours, then we will be free of your oath that you have made us swear."

**2:21** And she said, "According to your words, so it is." She then sent them off, and they left; and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

**2:22** So they went and came to the hills, and stayed there for three days until the pursuers returned. Now the pursuers had searched along the entire road, yet did not find them.

**2:23** Then the two men turned back, descended from the hills, crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun, and they recounted to him everything that had happened to them.

**2:24** And they said to Joshua, "Indeed, the LORD has given all the land into our hand; moreover, all who dwell in the land have melted away before us."

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — The Masoretic Text reads וַתִּצְפְּנוֹ (literally "and hid him"), using a masculine singular object suffix commonly understood distributively as "hid each of them."
- **2:13** — Ketiv אחותי could be read as singular ("my sister") or a defective plural; the translated Qere אַחְיוֹתַי explicitly specifies the plural "my sisters."
- **2:17** — The Masoretic Text pairs the feminine noun שְׁבוּעָה ("oath") with the masculine demonstrative הַזֶּה ("this"), a grammatical gender incongruence preserved in the text.
- **2:18** — Hebrew תִּקְוָה here denotes a "cord" or "line" (from a root meaning to twist or bind, homonymous with תִּקְוָה, "hope"), reinforced by חוּט ("thread").
