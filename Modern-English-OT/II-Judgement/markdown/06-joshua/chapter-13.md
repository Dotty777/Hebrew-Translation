## Text

**13:1** Now Joshua was old, far along in years, and the LORD said to him, "You have grown old, far along in years, yet a very great amount of the land remains to be possessed.

**13:2** This is the remaining land: all the districts of the Philistines and all the Geshurites,

**13:3** from the Shihor, which lies east of Egypt, as far as the border of Ekron to the north—it is reckoned as belonging to the Canaanites—the five rulers of the Philistines: the Gazites, the Ashdodites, the Ashkelonites, the Gittites, and the Ekronites; also the Avvim,

**13:4** from the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, as far as the border of the Amorites,

**13:5** and the land of the Gebalites, and all Lebanon toward the sunrise, from Baal-gad beneath Mount Hermon as far as Lebo-hamath.

**13:6** All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon as far as Misrephoth-maim, all the Sidonians—I Myself will drive them out before the sons of Israel. Only allot it to Israel as an inheritance, just as I commanded you.

**13:7** So now, divide this land as an inheritance for the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh."

**13:8** With him, the Reubenites and the Gadites received their inheritance, which Moses gave them beyond the Jordan to the east, just as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them:

**13:9** from Aroer, which is on the rim of the valley of the Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the valley, and all the plateau of Medeba as far as Dibon;

**13:10** and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the sons of Ammon;

**13:11** and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah;

**13:12** all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and in Edrei. He remained from the remnant of the Rephaim, and Moses struck them down and drove them out.

**13:13** However, the sons of Israel did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites, and Geshur and Maacath have lived in the midst of Israel until this day.

**13:14** Only to the tribe of Levi he gave no inheritance; the fire offerings of the LORD, the God of Israel, are his inheritance, just as He spoke to him.

**13:15** And Moses gave to the tribe of the sons of Reuben according to their clans.

**13:16** Their territory was from Aroer, which is on the rim of the valley of the Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the valley, and all the plateau by Medeba;

**13:17** Heshbon and all its cities that are on the plateau: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,

**13:18** Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,

**13:19** Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill of the valley,

**13:20** Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth,

**13:21** and all the cities of the plateau, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, whom Moses struck down along with the leaders of Midian—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the princes of Sihon, inhabitants of the land.

**13:22** The sons of Israel also killed Balaam the son of Beor, the diviner, with the sword, in addition to those they struck down.

**13:23** The border of the sons of Reuben was the Jordan and its borderland. This was the inheritance of the sons of Reuben according to their clans, the cities and their villages.

**13:24** Moses also gave to the tribe of Gad, to the sons of Gad, according to their clans.

**13:25** Their territory was Jazer, and all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the sons of Ammon, as far as Aroer, which lies east of Rabbah;

**13:26** and from Heshbon as far as Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim as far as the border of Debir;

**13:27** and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, with the Jordan and its borderland, as far as the end of the Sea of Chinnereth, beyond the Jordan to the east.

**13:28** This was the inheritance of the sons of Gad according to their clans, the cities and their villages.

**13:29** Moses also gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh; and it belonged to the half-tribe of the sons of Manasseh according to their clans.

**13:30** Their territory was from Mahanaim, all Bashan, all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all Havvoth-jair, which is in Bashan, sixty cities;

**13:31** and half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan, belonged to the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh, to half of the sons of Machir according to their clans.

**13:32** These are the portions that Moses assigned as an inheritance in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan by Jericho, to the east.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — The Masoretic verse division places "from the south" (mitteman) at the start of v. 4 with "all the land of the Canaanites," though some interpreters connect it to the Avvim at the end of v. 3.
- **13:6** — "Allot it" renders a Hiphil imperative of napal ("cause to fall"), an idiom for assigning territory by the casting of lots.
- **13:8** — "With him" (Hebrew immo) refers to the eastern half of the tribe of Manasseh, in contrast to the half-tribe mentioned at the end of v. 7.
- **13:14** — In the first clause "he" refers to Moses, whereas in the final clause "He" refers to the LORD. Although "the fire offerings" is plural, the Hebrew resumptive pronoun hu is masculine singular, serving as the copula.
- **13:26** — "Of Debir" follows the Masoretic vocalization of lidbir as the preposition le- plus the place name Debir; alternatively, the consonants are sometimes read as a single Transjordanian place name, Lidebir (cf. Lo-debar in 2 Sam 9:4).
