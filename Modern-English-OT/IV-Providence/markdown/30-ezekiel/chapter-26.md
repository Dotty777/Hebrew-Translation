## Text

**26:1** Now it happened in the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**26:2** "Son of man, because Tyre has said concerning Jerusalem, 'Aha! The gates of the peoples are broken; it has swung open to me; I shall be filled—she has been laid waste,'

**26:3** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, just as the sea brings up its waves.

**26:4** They will destroy the walls of Tyre and tear down her towers; and I will scrape her dust away from her and make her a bare rock.

**26:5** She will be a place for spreading nets in the midst of the sea, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD; and she will become plunder for the nations.

**26:6** Also her daughters who are in the open country will be killed by the sword, and they will know that I am the LORD.

**26:7** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am bringing against Tyre Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the north, king of kings, with horses, chariots, horsemen, and an assembly and many people.

**26:8** He will kill your daughters in the open country with the sword; he will set a siege wall against you, heap up a ramp against you, and raise up large shields against you.

**26:9** He will aim the blow of his battering ram against your walls, and he will demolish your towers with his axes.

**26:10** Because of the multitude of his horses, their dust will cover you; at the noise of horsemen, wheels, and chariots your walls will shake when he enters your gates, like the entry into a breached city.

**26:11** With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets; he will kill your people with the sword, and the pillars of your strength will come down to the ground.

**26:12** They will loot your wealth and plunder your merchandise; they will tear down your walls and demolish your prized houses; and your stones, your timber, and your dust they will put in the midst of the water.

**26:13** I will cause the noise of your songs to cease, and the sound of your lyres will be heard no more.

**26:14** And I will make you a bare rock; you will be a place for spreading nets; you will not be built again, for I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord GOD."

**26:15** This is what the Lord GOD says to Tyre: "At the sound of your downfall, when the wounded groan, when slaughter takes place in your midst, will not the coastlands shake?

**26:16** Then all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones, remove their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments. They will clothe themselves with trembling; they will sit on the ground, tremble every moment, and be appalled over you.

**26:17** And they will raise a lament over you and say to you, 'How you have perished, inhabited from the seas, the renowned city, which was mighty on the sea, she and her inhabitants, who imposed their terror on all her inhabitants!

**26:18** Now the coastlands tremble on the day of your downfall, and the islands that are in the sea are dismayed at your departure.'"

**26:19** For this is what the Lord GOD says: "When I make you a city laid waste, like the cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep over you and the great waters cover you,

**26:20** then I will bring you down with those who go down to the pit, to the people of long ago, and I will make you dwell in the lower parts of the earth, like the ruins of long ago, with those who go down to the pit, so that you will not be inhabited; and I will set glory in the land of the living.

**26:21** I will make you a horror, and you will be no more; you will be sought, yet you will not be found again forever, declares the Lord GOD."

## Translator Notes

- **26:7** — The Hebrew spells the Babylonian king's name Nevukhadretstsar (closer to Akkadian Nabu-kudurri-usur), rendered here with the standard English spelling Nebuchadnezzar.
- **26:9** — The phrase mechi qavollo ("the blow of his battering ram") consists of two hapax legomena from roots meaning "strike" and "be opposite/in front." "His axes" renders charvotav, literally "his swords" or "iron blades," here iron demolition tools (cf. Exod 20:25).
- **26:17** — MT reads the Niphal participle noshevet miyyammim ("inhabited from the seas," i.e., populated by seafaring peoples) and repeats yosheveha ("her inhabitants") at the close of the verse, referring to the inhabitants of the surrounding maritime region or Tyre's dependencies.
- **26:20** — MT reads venatatti tsevi be'erets chayyim ("and I will set glory in the land of the living"), contrasting Tyre's descent to the pit with God's establishment of splendor among the living.
