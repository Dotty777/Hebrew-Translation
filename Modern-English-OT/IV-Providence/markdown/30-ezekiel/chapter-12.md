## Text

**12:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**12:2** "Son of man, you are living in the midst of the house of rebellion, who have eyes to see yet do not see, ears to hear yet do not hear, for they are a house of rebellion.

**12:3** And you, son of man, make ready for yourself belongings for exile, and go into exile by day before their eyes; you shall go into exile from your place to another place before their eyes. Perhaps they will see, though they are a house of rebellion.

**12:4** And you shall bring out your belongings like belongings for exile by day before their eyes; and you yourself shall go out in the evening before their eyes, like those who go out into exile.

**12:5** Before their eyes, dig through the wall for yourself, and bring them out through it.

**12:6** Before their eyes you shall carry them on your shoulder and bring them out in the dark; you shall cover your face so that you do not see the land, for I have appointed you a sign to the house of Israel."

**12:7** And I did so, just as I was commanded. I brought out my belongings by day like belongings for exile, and in the evening I dug through the wall for myself by hand; in the dark I brought them out and carried them on my shoulder before their eyes.

**12:8** And the word of the LORD came to me in the morning, saying,

**12:9** "Son of man, did not the house of Israel, the house of rebellion, say to you, 'What are you doing?'

**12:10** Say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: This burden is about the prince in Jerusalem and all the house of Israel who are in their midst.'

**12:11** Say, 'I am your sign. Just as I have done, so it will be done to them: they will go into exile, into captivity.

**12:12** And the prince who is in their midst will carry a load on his shoulder in the dark and go out. They will dig through the wall to bring him out through it. He will cover his face so that he himself will not see the land with his eyes.

**12:13** And I will spread out My net over him, and he will be captured in My snare; I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, yet he will not see it, and there he will die.

**12:14** And all who are around him, his helpers and all his troops, I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.

**12:15** And they will know that I am the LORD when I disperse them among the nations and scatter them through the lands.

**12:16** Yet I will spare a small number of men among them from sword, from famine, and from pestilence, so that they may recount all their abominations among the nations where they go; and they will know that I am the LORD.'"

**12:17** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**12:18** "Son of man, you shall eat your bread with trembling, and you shall drink your water with agitation and with anxiety.

**12:19** And you shall say to the people of the land, 'This is what the Lord GOD says concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem on the soil of Israel: They will eat their bread with anxiety and drink their water with horror, so that her land will be desolated of all its fullness because of the violence of all who live in it.

**12:20** And the inhabited cities will fall into ruin, and the land will become a desolation; then you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**12:21** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**12:22** "Son of man, what is this proverb you have on the soil of Israel, saying, 'The days stretch out, and every vision perishes'?

**12:23** Therefore say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to this proverb, and they will no longer repeat it as a proverb in Israel.' Instead, speak to them, 'The days have drawn near, and the word of every vision.

**12:24** For there will no longer be any empty vision or flattering divination in the midst of the house of Israel.

**12:25** For I, the LORD, will speak, and whatever word I speak will be done; it will not be postponed any longer. For in your days, house of rebellion, I will speak a word and do it, declares the Lord GOD.'"

**12:26** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**12:27** "Son of man, look, the house of Israel is saying, 'The vision that he sees is for many days ahead, and for distant times he is prophesying.'

**12:28** Therefore say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Not one of My words will be postponed any longer; whatever word I speak will be done, declares the Lord GOD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **12:10** — The Hebrew verbless clause fronts "the prince" (הַנָּשִׂיא) without a preposition before "this burden" (הַמַּשָּׂא הַזֶּה), creating a wordplay between prophetic "burden/oracle" (מַשָּׂא) and "carry" (נָשָׂא, vv. 6-7, 12). The final suffix in בְתוֹכָם is literally 3rd-person masculine plural ("in their midst").
- **12:12** — The conjunction יַעַן אֲשֶׁר (normally causal, "because") functions here with purpose or result force ("so that"), accompanied by the emphatic pronoun הוּא ("he himself") and לַעַיִן (lit. "to/with the eye").
- **12:19** — Verses 19-20 weave a threefold wordplay on the root שׁמם: drinking "with horror" (בְּשִׁמָּמוֹן), "her land" (Jerusalem's) "will be desolated" (תֵּשַׁם), and the land becoming "a desolation" (שְׁמָמָה, v. 20).
- **12:25** — The Niphal imperfect תִמָּשֵׁךְ ("be drawn out / postponed") is 3rd-person feminine singular, used impersonally here and again in v. 28 with the masculine plural subject כׇּל־דְּבָרָי ("all My words").
