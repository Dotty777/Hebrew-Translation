## Text

**22:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**22:2** "And you, son of man, will you judge, will you judge the city of bloodshed? Then make known to her all her abominations,

**22:3** and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: A city shedding blood in her midst so that her time will come, and making idols against herself to become unclean!

**22:4** By your blood that you have shed you have become guilty, and by your idols that you have made you have become unclean; you have brought your days near and have come to your years. Therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations and a mockery to all the lands.

**22:5** Those near and those far from you will mock you, you of unclean name, full of turmoil!

**22:6** Look, the princes of Israel, each according to his might, have been in you in order to shed blood.

**22:7** Father and mother they have treated with contempt in you; toward the sojourner they have dealt with oppression in your midst; the fatherless and the widow they have wronged in you.

**22:8** My holy things you have despised, and My Sabbaths you have profaned.

**22:9** Men of slander have been in you in order to shed blood; upon the mountains they have eaten in you; they have committed lewdness in your midst.

**22:10** In you a father's nakedness has been uncovered; in you they have violated a woman unclean in her menstruation.

**22:11** One man has committed an abomination with his neighbor's wife, another has defiled his daughter-in-law in lewdness, and another in you has violated his sister, his father's daughter.

**22:12** In you they have taken bribes in order to shed blood; you have taken interest and increase, and you have made dishonest gain from your neighbors by oppression, and Me you have forgotten, declares the Lord GOD.

**22:13** And look, I have struck My palm at your dishonest gain that you have made, and at your bloodshed that has been in your midst.

**22:14** Will your heart stand firm, or will your hands remain strong, in the days when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will act.

**22:15** And I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you through the lands, and I will put an end to your uncleanness from you.

**22:16** And you will be profaned in yourself before the eyes of the nations, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**22:17** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**22:18** "Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to Me. All of them are bronze, tin, iron, and lead in the midst of a furnace; they have become the dross of silver.

**22:19** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Because all of you have become dross, therefore, look, I am gathering you into the midst of Jerusalem.

**22:20** As a gathering of silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin into the midst of a furnace, to blow fire upon it to melt it, so I will gather you in My anger and in My wrath, and I will place you there and melt you.

**22:21** And I will collect you and blow upon you with the fire of My fury, and you will be melted in the midst of it.

**22:22** As the melting of silver in the midst of a furnace, so you will be melted in the midst of it; and you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out My wrath upon you."

**22:23** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**22:24** "Son of man, say to her, 'You are a land that is not cleansed, not rained upon in the day of indignation.'

**22:25** There is a conspiracy of her prophets in her midst; like a roaring lion tearing prey, they have devoured lives, they take treasure and precious things, and they have multiplied her widows in her midst.

**22:26** Her priests have done violence to My law and have profaned My holy things; they have made no distinction between the holy and the common, nor have they made known the difference between the unclean and the clean; they have hidden their eyes from My Sabbaths, so that I am profaned in their midst.

**22:27** Her officials within her are like wolves tearing prey, shedding blood and destroying lives in order to get dishonest gain.

**22:28** And her prophets have plastered whitewash for them, seeing false visions and divining lies for them, saying, 'This is what the Lord GOD says,' when the LORD has not spoken.

**22:29** The people of the land have practiced oppression and committed robbery; they have wronged the afflicted and the needy, and they have oppressed the sojourner without justice.

**22:30** And I looked among them for a man who would build up a wall and stand in the breach before Me on behalf of the land, so that I might not destroy it, yet I did not find one.

**22:31** Therefore I have poured out My indignation upon them; with the fire of My fury I have consumed them. Their own way I have brought upon their heads, declares the Lord GOD."

## Translator Notes

- **22:16** — Hebrew wenichalt is vocalized as the Niphal of chalal ("you will be profaned"), meaning Jerusalem will suffer desecration in herself before the nations, rather than the Qal of nachal ("inherit").
- **22:18** — Ketiv reads lesug while Qere reads lesig ("for dross"), matching the plural sigim ("dross") later in this verse and in verse 19; the Qere is translated here.
- **22:24** — Hebrew gushmah is an unusual Masoretic form combining a passive verbal vocalization of gasham ("rained upon") with a final mappiq-he (as if a suffixed noun, "her rain"); the sense in context is that the land receives no rain in the day of indignation.
- **22:25** — The Masoretic Text reads qesher nevi'eha ("a conspiracy of her prophets"), which is followed here rather than the Septuagint's "her princes."
