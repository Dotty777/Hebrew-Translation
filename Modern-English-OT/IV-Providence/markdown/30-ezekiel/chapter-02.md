## Text

**2:1** Then He said to me, "Son of man, stand on your feet, so that I may speak with you."

**2:2** And a spirit came into me as He spoke to me, and stood me on my feet; and I heard the One speaking to me.

**2:3** Then He said to me, "Son of man, I am sending you to the sons of Israel, to rebellious nations who have rebelled against Me; they and their fathers have transgressed against Me to this very day.

**2:4** And the children are defiant of face and hard of heart; I am sending you to them, and you shall say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says.'

**2:5** And as for them, whether they listen or refrain—for they are a house of rebellion—they will know that a prophet has been in their midst.

**2:6** And you, son of man, do not fear them, and do not fear their words, though briers and thorns are with you and you sit among scorpions. Do not fear their words and do not be dismayed at their presence, for they are a house of rebellion.

**2:7** And you shall speak My words to them, whether they listen or refrain, for they are rebellious.

**2:8** And you, son of man, listen to what I am speaking to you; do not be rebellious like the house of rebellion. Open your mouth wide and eat what I am giving you."

**2:9** Then I looked, and see, a hand was extended toward me; and look, in it was a written scroll.

**2:10** And He spread it out before me, and it was written on front and back; and written on it were laments, and groaning, and woe.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The noun ruach ("spirit") lacks the definite article here (as also in 3:12, 14, 24; 8:3; 11:1, 24; 43:5) and can denote "a spirit," "the Spirit," or "wind." The participle middabber ("the One speaking") is a rare Hithpael form of dabar with assimilated taw.
- **2:6** — Sarabim ('briers') is a hapax legomenon, traditionally understood alongside sallonim ('thorns') as thorny briers or stubborn resisters.
- **2:10** — Hi ('woe') occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible, likely a shortened form of nehi ('wailing') or an exclamation of grief.
