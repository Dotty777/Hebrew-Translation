## Text

**11:1** Then the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD, which faces eastward. And look, at the entrance of the gate were twenty-five men, and among them I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, officials of the people.

**11:2** And He said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and give wicked counsel in this city,

**11:3** who say, 'Building houses is not near; it is the pot, and we are the meat.'

**11:4** Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, son of man!"

**11:5** Then the Spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and He said to me, "Say, 'This is what the LORD says: So you have spoken, house of Israel, and the things that rise in your spirit—I know them.

**11:6** You have multiplied those you have killed in this city, and you have filled its streets with the dead.

**11:7** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Those you have killed, whom you have laid in its midst—they are the meat, and it is the pot; but you will be brought out from its midst.

**11:8** You have feared a sword, and a sword I will bring upon you, declares the Lord GOD.

**11:9** And I will bring you out from its midst, and I will give you into the hand of foreigners, and I will execute judgments against you.

**11:10** By the sword you will fall; at the border of Israel I will judge you, and you will know that I am the LORD.

**11:11** It will not be a pot for you, nor will you be meat in its midst; at the border of Israel I will judge you.

**11:12** And you will know that I am the LORD, for you have not walked in My statutes nor carried out My ordinances, but you have acted according to the ordinances of the nations that are around you.'"

**11:13** Now it happened, as I was prophesying, that Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice and said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Are You making a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"

**11:14** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**11:15** "Son of man, your brothers, your brothers, the men of your kinship, and all the house of Israel, the whole of it—they are the ones to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, 'Stay far away from the LORD; to us this land has been given as a possession.'

**11:16** Therefore say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Though I have sent them far away among the nations and though I have scattered them through the lands, yet I have become a sanctuary for them in small measure in the lands where they have gone.'

**11:17** Therefore say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the lands in which you have been scattered, and I will give to you the land of Israel.

**11:18** And they will come there and remove all its detestable things and all its abominations from it.

**11:19** I will give to them a single heart, and I will place a new spirit within you; I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give to them a heart of flesh,

**11:20** so that they may walk in My statutes and observe My ordinances and carry them out; and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them.

**11:21** But as for those whose heart goes after the heart of their detestable things and their abominations, I will bring their way upon their own head, declares the Lord GOD.'"

**11:22** Then the cherubim lifted up their wings, with the wheels alongside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them from above.

**11:23** And the glory of the LORD went up from over the midst of the city and stood upon the mountain that is east of the city.

**11:24** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to Chaldea, to the exiles, in the vision by the Spirit of God. Then the vision that I had seen went up from me.

**11:25** Then I spoke to the exiles all the words of the LORD that He had shown me.

## Translator Notes

- **11:7** — MT reads the Hiphil third-person masculine singular hotsiʾ (used impersonally, "one will bring out," hence "you will be brought out"), contrasting with the first-person "I will bring you out" in v. 9.
- **11:15** — Heb. ʾanshe geʾullatekha is literally "the men of your redemption," referring to relatives bound by the obligation of kinsman-redemption (compare Lev 25:25).
- **11:16** — Heb. miqdash meʿat can mean either "a sanctuary in small measure" or temporally "a sanctuary for a little while" during the exile.
- **11:19** — MT abruptly shifts to a second-person plural suffix in beqirbekhem ("within you") amid the surrounding third-person plural pronouns ("them," "their"), continuing the alternation of address from vv. 16-18.
- **11:21** — MT reads weʾel-lev shiqqutsehem ("toward the heart of their detestable things"), an unusual expression pairing the people's "heart" with the "heart" (core or desire) of their detestable practices.
