## Text

**39:1** "And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**39:2** I will turn you around and lead you forward, and I will bring you up from the far reaches of the north and bring you onto the mountains of Israel.

**39:3** I will knock your bow out of your left hand and cause your arrows to fall from your right hand.

**39:4** On the mountains of Israel you will fall, you and all your troops and the peoples who are with you; I will give you as food to birds of prey of every wing and to the beasts of the field.

**39:5** Upon the open field you will fall, for I Myself have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

**39:6** And I will send fire into Magog and among those who live securely in the coastlands, and they will know that I am the LORD.

**39:7** My holy name I will make known in the midst of My people Israel, and I will no longer let My holy name be profaned; and the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel.

**39:8** Look, it is coming and it will be brought about, declares the Lord GOD; that is the day of which I have spoken.

**39:9** Then the inhabitants of the cities of Israel will go out and set fire to and burn the weapons—small shields and large shields, bows and arrows, hand clubs and spears—and they will make fires with them for seven years.

**39:10** They will not carry wood from the field nor cut any from the forests, for they will make fires with the weapons; and they will despoil those who despoiled them and plunder those who plundered them, declares the Lord GOD.

**39:11** It will happen on that day that I will give Gog a burial place there in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the sea, and it will block the travelers; there they will bury Gog and all his multitude, and they will call it the Valley of Hamon-gog.

**39:12** And the house of Israel will bury them for seven months so that they may cleanse the land.

**39:13** All the people of the land will bury them, and it will bring them renown on the day I am glorified, declares the Lord GOD.

**39:14** They will set apart men on continual duty to pass through the land, and with those passing through, men burying those left on the face of the land to cleanse it; at the end of seven months they will make a search.

**39:15** As those passing through go through the land, whenever anyone sees a human bone, he will set up a marker beside it until the buriers have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-gog.

**39:16** And the name of a city will also be Hamonah. Thus they will cleanse the land.'

**39:17** And you, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: Speak to the birds of every wing and to every beast of the field, 'Assemble and come; gather from all around to My sacrifice that I am sacrificing for you, a great sacrifice on the mountains of Israel, and you will eat flesh and drink blood.

**39:18** You will eat the flesh of mighty men and drink the blood of the princes of the earth—rams, lambs, and male goats, bulls, all of them fattened animals of Bashan.

**39:19** You will eat fat to fullness and drink blood to drunkenness, from My sacrifice that I have sacrificed for you.

**39:20** You will be filled at My table with horses and charioteers, with mighty men and every man of war, declares the Lord GOD.'

**39:21** I will place My glory among the nations, and all the nations will see My judgment that I have carried out and My hand that I have put upon them.

**39:22** Then the house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God from that day onward.

**39:23** And the nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile on account of their iniquity, because they acted unfaithfully against Me; so I hid My face from them and delivered them into the hand of their adversaries, and all of them fell by the sword.

**39:24** I dealt with them according to their impurity and their transgressions, and I hid My face from them.

**39:25** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Now I will restore the fortunes of Jacob and have compassion on the entire house of Israel, and I will be jealous for My holy name.

**39:26** They will bear their disgrace and all the unfaithfulness that they committed against Me, when they dwell securely on their land with no one to make them afraid.

**39:27** When I bring them back from the peoples and gather them from the lands of their enemies, I will show Myself holy through them before the eyes of the many nations.

**39:28** Then they will know that I am the LORD their God, because I sent them into exile among the nations and then gathered them to their land, and I will not leave any of them there anymore.

**39:29** And I will never again hide My face from them, since I have poured out My Spirit upon the house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD."

## Translator Notes

- **39:2** — The Piel verb shishetikha is a hapax legomenon of uncertain meaning; in this sequence of verbs it is understood as "lead you forward" or "drive you on"; a derivation from shesh ("six"), "leave a sixth part of you," has also been proposed.
- **39:11** — Masoretic meqom-sham qever literally reads "a place there of a grave" (i.e., a burial place there). The name Hamon-gog means "Multitude of Gog," anticipating the city name Hamonah ("Multitude") in v. 16.
- **39:14** — In meqabberim et-ha'overim et-hannotarim, the first et is the preposition "with" (joining the buriers with those passing through, as described in v. 15), while the second et marks the direct object "those left."
- **39:25** — Ketiv reads shevit ("captivity"), while Qere reads shevut ("fortunes" or "restoration," in the idiom "restore the fortunes of"); the translation follows the Qere.
- **39:26** — Masoretic venasu (pointed with sin and spelled without aleph) is the Qal of nasa ("bear"), matching Ezekiel's recurring idiom "bear disgrace" (cf. 16:52, 54); some emend to shin from nashah ("forget").
