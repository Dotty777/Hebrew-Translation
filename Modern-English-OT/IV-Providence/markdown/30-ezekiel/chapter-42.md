## Text

**42:1** Then he led me out to the outer court by the way toward the north, and he brought me to the chamber that was opposite the separated space and opposite the building toward the north.

**42:2** Facing the length of a hundred cubits was the north entrance, and the width was fifty cubits.

**42:3** Opposite the twenty cubits belonging to the inner court and opposite the pavement belonging to the outer court was gallery facing gallery in three stories.

**42:4** In front of the chambers was a walkway ten cubits wide toward the inside, a passage of one cubit, and their entrances were toward the north.

**42:5** Now the upper chambers were shorter, for galleries took space away from them more than from the lower and the middle ones of the building.

**42:6** For they were in three stories and did not have pillars like the pillars of the courts; therefore the upper ones were set back more than the lower and the middle ones from the ground.

**42:7** And there was a wall outside, parallel to the chambers, toward the outer court in front of the chambers; its length was fifty cubits.

**42:8** For the length of the chambers belonging to the outer court was fifty cubits, and look, those facing the temple hall were a hundred cubits.

**42:9** And below these chambers was the entryway from the east, as one enters them from the outer court.

**42:10** In the width of the wall of the court toward the east, in front of the separated space and in front of the building, there were chambers.

**42:11** And there was a passage in front of them, like the appearance of the chambers that were toward the north, matching both their length and their width, and all their exits, according to their arrangements and their entrances.

**42:12** And corresponding to the entrances of the chambers that were toward the south was an entrance at the head of the passage, the passage in front of the matching wall toward the east, as one enters them.

**42:13** Then he said to me, "The north chambers and the south chambers that are in front of the separated space are the holy chambers, where the priests who are near to the LORD shall eat the most holy things. There they shall place the most holy things: the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering, for the place is holy.

**42:14** When the priests enter, they shall not go out from the sanctuary into the outer court, but there they shall place their garments in which they minister, for they are holy; and they shall put on other garments, and then draw near to what belongs to the people."

**42:15** Now when he had finished the measurements of the inner house, he led me out by way of the gate whose front was toward the east, and he measured it all around.

**42:16** He measured the east side with the measuring reed: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed all around.

**42:17** He measured the north side: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed all around.

**42:18** He measured the south side: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed.

**42:19** He turned to the west side and measured five hundred reeds by the measuring reed.

**42:20** He measured it on the four sides; it had a wall all around, five hundred in length and five hundred in width, to separate between the holy and the common.

## Translator Notes

- **42:4** — The Masoretic Text reads "a passage of one cubit" (derekh ammah achat), whereas the Septuagint reflects "a length of a hundred cubits."
- **42:9** — Two Ketiv/Qere variants occur here: the Qere redivides the first phrase as "and below these chambers" and reads the Hiphil participle "the entryway" (hammevi) where the Ketiv has the noun "the entrance" (hammavo).
- **42:10** — The Masoretic Text reads "toward the east" (derekh haqqadim), whereas the Septuagint reads "toward the south," anticipating the south chambers in 42:12.
- **42:12** — The rare word haginah ("matching") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; its exact sense is uncertain, though cognates suggest "corresponding," "suitable," or "directly opposite."
- **42:16** — The Qere reads "reeds" (qanim), aligning with 42:17-19, whereas the Ketiv reads "cubits" (ammot), which agrees with the Septuagint and the scale of chapters 40-41.
