## Text

**35:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**35:2** "Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir and prophesy against it,

**35:3** and say to it, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Mount Seir; I will stretch out My hand against you and make you a desolation and a devastation.

**35:4** I will lay your cities in ruins, and you yourself will become a desolation; then you will know that I am the LORD.

**35:5** Because you have held a perpetual enmity and handed the sons of Israel over to the power of the sword at the time of their disaster, at the time of final punishment,

**35:6** therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will appoint you for bloodshed, and bloodshed will pursue you; since you did not hate bloodshed, bloodshed will pursue you.

**35:7** I will make Mount Seir a wasteland and a desolation, and I will cut off from it anyone who passes through and returns.

**35:8** I will fill its mountains with its dead; your hills, your valleys, and all your streambeds—those killed by the sword will fall in them.

**35:9** I will make you perpetual desolations, and your cities will not be restored. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**35:10** Because you have said, "The two nations and the two lands will be mine, and we will possess it," though the LORD was there,

**35:11** therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will act according to your anger and according to your envy, with which you acted out of your hatred against them; and I will make Myself known among them when I judge you.

**35:12** Then you will know that I, the LORD, have heard all your insults that you spoke against the mountains of Israel, saying, "They have become desolate; they have been given to us for food."

**35:13** You spoke arrogantly against Me with your mouth and multiplied your words against Me; I Myself have heard it.

**35:14** This is what the Lord GOD says: While the whole earth rejoices, I will make you a desolation.

**35:15** Just as you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it became desolate, so I will do to you. You will become a desolation, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it. Then they will know that I am the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:5** — Literally 'poured out the sons of Israel onto the hands of the sword,' an idiom for delivering them over to slaughter; 'final punishment' translates 'avon qets, literally 'iniquity/punishment of the end.'
- **35:6** — The Hebrew clause 'im-lo' dam sane'ta (literally 'if you did not hate blood') is grammatically difficult and is taken causally here ('since you did not hate bloodshed'), contrasting with the affirmative oath particle 'im-lo' elsewhere.
- **35:9** — Following the Qere tashovnah ('will not be restored/return', Qal imperfect of shuv); the Ketiv teyshavnah (from yashav) reads 'will not be inhabited.'
- **35:12** — Following the Qere shamemu ('they have become desolate', 3rd-person plural Qal perfect of shamem), agreeing with 'the mountains of Israel'; the Ketiv reads the singular shamemah ('it has become desolate' or 'desolation').
