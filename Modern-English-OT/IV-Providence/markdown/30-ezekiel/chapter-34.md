## Text

**34:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**34:2** "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy and say to them, to the shepherds, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who have been tending themselves! Should not the shepherds tend the flock?

**34:3** You eat the fat and wear the wool; you slaughter the fattened ones, yet you do not tend the flock.

**34:4** You have not strengthened the weak, you have not healed the sick, you have not bandaged the broken, you have not brought back the strayed, and you have not sought the lost; rather, you have ruled over them with force and with harshness.

**34:5** So they were scattered for lack of a shepherd, and they became food for every wild animal of the field, and they were scattered.

**34:6** My flock wandered through all the mountains and on every high hill. Over the whole face of the earth My flock was scattered, and there was no one searching and no one seeking.

**34:7** Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the LORD:

**34:8** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, surely because My flock became plunder, and My flock became food for every wild animal of the field because there was no shepherd, and My shepherds did not search for My flock, but the shepherds tended themselves and did not tend My flock,

**34:9** therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the LORD:

**34:10** This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against the shepherds, and I will demand My flock from their hand and stop them from tending the flock, so the shepherds will no longer tend themselves. I will rescue My flock from their mouth, and they will not be food for them.

**34:11** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I Myself will search for My flock and look after them.

**34:12** Just as a shepherd looks after his flock on the day he is among his scattered sheep, so I will look after My flock; and I will rescue them from all the places where they were scattered on a day of cloud and dense darkness.

**34:13** I will bring them out from the peoples and gather them from the countries, and I will bring them into their own land. I will tend them on the mountains of Israel, in the streambeds, and in all the dwelling places of the land.

**34:14** In good pasture I will tend them, and their grazing ground will be on the mountain heights of Israel. There they will lie down in a good grazing ground, and they will graze on rich pasture on the mountains of Israel.

**34:15** I Myself will tend My flock, and I Myself will make them lie down, declares the Lord GOD.

**34:16** I will seek the lost, I will bring back the strayed, I will bandage the broken, and I will strengthen the sick; but the fat and the strong I will destroy. I will tend them with justice.

**34:17** And as for you, My flock, this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am about to judge between sheep and sheep—the rams and the male goats.

**34:18** Is it too little for you that you graze on the good pasture, yet trample the rest of your pastures with your feet? And that you drink clear water, yet muddy what remains with your feet?

**34:19** And My flock must graze on what your feet have trampled and drink what your feet have muddied.

**34:20** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says to them: Look, I Myself will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep.

**34:21** Because you shove with flank and shoulder and butt all the weak with your horns until you have scattered them abroad,

**34:22** I will save My flock, and they will no longer be plunder; and I will judge between sheep and sheep.

**34:23** And I will raise up over them one shepherd, My servant David, and he will tend them. He will tend them, and he will be their shepherd.

**34:24** And I, the LORD, will be their God, and My servant David will be prince among them. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**34:25** I will make a covenant of peace with them and banish dangerous animals from the land, so that they will dwell securely in the wilderness and sleep in the forests.

**34:26** I will make them and the surroundings of My hill a blessing, and I will send down the rain in its season; they will be rains of blessing.

**34:27** The trees of the field will yield their fruit, and the earth will yield its produce, and they will be secure on their own land. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I break the bars of their yoke and rescue them from the hand of those who enslaved them.

**34:28** They will no longer be plunder for the nations, and the wild animals of the land will not devour them; they will dwell securely, with no one to frighten them.

**34:29** I will raise up for them a planting of renown, and they will no longer be swept away by famine in the land, nor bear the disgrace of the nations any longer.

**34:30** Then they will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them, and that they are My people, the house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.

**34:31** And as for you, My flock, the flock of My pasture, you are human beings; I am your God, declares the Lord GOD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — The Hebrew verb ra'ah ('tend, shepherd, feed') creates wordplay with ro'im ('shepherds'): instead of tending the flock, Israel's leaders have been tending and feeding themselves (v. 3).
- **34:12** — The noun baqqarah ('looking after, inspection') occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible, and niphrashot ('scattered') is the sole Niphal occurrence of parash.
- **34:16** — The Masoretic Text reads 'I will destroy' (Hiphil imperfect of shamad), contrasting judgment on the oppressive sheep with care for the vulnerable; several ancient versions (including the Septuagint) instead reflect 'I will guard' (from shamar).
- **34:18** — The noun mishqa' ('clear water', literally 'settled water', from shaqa', 'sink/settle') and the noun mirpas in v. 19 ('what is muddied', from raphas) each appear only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **34:29** — Literally 'a planting for a name,' meaning a renowned planting; the Masoretic Text reads leshem ('for renown'), whereas the Septuagint reflects 'of peace.'
