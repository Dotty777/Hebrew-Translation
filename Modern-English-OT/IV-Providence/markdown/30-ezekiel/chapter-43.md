## Text

**43:1** And he led me to the gate, a gate that faces toward the east.

**43:2** And look, the glory of the God of Israel was coming from the way of the east; His voice was like the sound of many waters, and the earth grew bright from His glory.

**43:3** And it was like the appearance of the vision that I saw, like the vision that I saw when I came to destroy the city, and the visions were like the vision that I saw by the Chebar River; and I fell on my face.

**43:4** And the glory of the LORD came into the house by way of the gate whose front is toward the east.

**43:5** Then a spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner courtyard; and look, the glory of the LORD filled the house.

**43:6** And I heard someone speaking to me from out of the house, while a man was standing next to me.

**43:7** And He said to me, "Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet, where I will dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel forever. And the house of Israel will no longer defile My holy name—neither they nor their kings—by their prostitution and by the corpses of their kings at their high places.

**43:8** When they set their threshold beside My threshold and their doorpost next to My doorpost, with the wall between Me and them, they defiled My holy name by their abominations that they committed, so I consumed them in My anger.

**43:9** Now let them put their prostitution and the corpses of their kings far away from Me, and I will dwell in their midst forever.

**43:10** As for you, son of man, describe the house to the house of Israel, so that they may be ashamed of their iniquities; and let them measure the pattern.

**43:11** And if they are ashamed of all that they have done, make known to them the design of the house, its arrangement, its exits, its entrances, all its designs, all its statutes, all its designs, and all its laws, and write it before their eyes, so that they may keep its entire design and all its statutes and do them.

**43:12** This is the law of the house: on the top of the mountain, its entire territory all around shall be most holy. Look, this is the law of the house."

**43:13** And these are the measurements of the altar in cubits—a cubit is a cubit and a handbreadth: the base is one cubit, and it is one cubit wide, and its border along its edge all around is one span; and this is the pedestal of the altar.

**43:14** And from the base on the ground to the lower ledge is two cubits, with a width of one cubit; and from the smaller ledge to the larger ledge is four cubits, with a width of one cubit.

**43:15** And the altar hearth is four cubits, and from the altar hearth upward are four horns.

**43:16** And the altar hearth is twelve cubits long by twelve cubits wide, square on its four sides.

**43:17** And the ledge is fourteen cubits long by fourteen cubits wide on its four sides, and the border around it is half a cubit, and its base is one cubit all around; and its steps face east.

**43:18** And He said to me, "Son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: These are the statutes of the altar on the day it is made, to offer up a burnt offering on it and to splash blood on it.

**43:19** You shall give to the Levitical priests who are from the offspring of Zadok, who draw near to Me, declares the Lord GOD, to minister to Me, a bull from the herd for a sin offering.

**43:20** And you shall take some of its blood and put it on its four horns, and on the four corners of the ledge, and on the border all around; thus you shall purify it and make atonement for it.

**43:21** You shall also take the bull for the sin offering, and it shall be burned in the appointed place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

**43:22** And on the second day you shall present a male goat without blemish for a sin offering, and they shall purify the altar just as they purified it with the bull.

**43:23** When you have finished purifying it, you shall present a bull from the herd without blemish and a ram from the flock without blemish.

**43:24** You shall present them before the LORD, and the priests shall cast salt on them and offer them up as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**43:25** For seven days you shall offer a male goat for a sin offering each day; and they shall offer a bull from the herd and a ram from the flock, without blemish.

**43:26** For seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it, and so consecrate it.

**43:27** When they have completed the days, it shall be that on the eighth day and onward, the priests shall offer on the altar your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, and I will accept you, declares the Lord GOD."

## Translator Notes

- **43:3** — MT reads first-person bebo'i ("when I came"), expressing Ezekiel's prophetic participation in the vision of Jerusalem's destruction (cf. ch. 9), whereas some Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions read third-person "when He came."
- **43:7** — The direct object marker 'et precedes "the place of My throne" without a governing verb, functioning presentatively ("this is"). At the end of the verse, MT vocalizes bamotam ("their high places," used locatively or denoting royal funerary shrines), whereas repointing the consonants to bemotam yields "at their death."
- **43:13** — Hebrew cheq (lit. "bosom" or "hollow") denotes the recessed base or drainage gutter around the altar. MT gav ("back," "mound," or "pedestal") is retained here rather than emending to "height" (govah) with the Septuagint.
- **43:15** — Hebrew uses two related terms for the altar hearth: har'el and Qere 'ari'el (also in 43:16), where the Ketiv transposes the yod and 'alef ('r'yl) while the Qere reads 'ry'l.
- **43:26** — Hebrew umille'u yadav (lit. "and they shall fill its hands," with defective suffix spelling) applies the standard priestly consecration idiom ("fill the hand," cf. Exod 29:9) uniquely to the altar.
