## Text

**6:1** Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**6:2** "Son of man, turn your face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them,

**6:3** and say, 'Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD! This is what the Lord GOD says to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys: "Look, I Myself am bringing a sword against you, and I will destroy your high places.

**6:4** And your altars will become desolate, and your incense altars will be shattered, and I will throw down those of you who are killed before your idols.

**6:5** And I will place the corpses of the sons of Israel in front of their idols, and scatter your bones all around your altars.

**6:6** In all your dwelling places, the cities will be laid waste and the high places will become desolate, so that your altars will be laid waste and bear guilt, your idols will be shattered and come to an end, your incense altars will be hewn down, and your works will be wiped away.

**6:7** And those killed will fall in your midst, and you will know that I am the LORD.

**6:8** Yet I will leave some remaining, in that you will have survivors of the sword among the nations when you are scattered through the lands.

**6:9** Then your survivors will remember Me among the nations where they were taken captive, how I have been broken by their heart that prostituted itself, which departed from Me, and by their eyes that prostituted themselves after their idols; and they will be disgusted with themselves in their own sight because of the evil things they have done, for all their abominations.

**6:10** And they will know that I am the LORD; I did not speak in vain about bringing this calamity upon them."'"

**6:11** This is what the Lord GOD says, "Strike with your hand and stomp with your foot, and say, 'Alas!' over all the wicked abominations of the house of Israel; for by the sword, by famine, and by plague they will fall.

**6:12** The one who is far away will die by plague, and the one who is near will fall by the sword, and the one who remains and is besieged will die by famine; and I will bring My wrath to an end upon them.

**6:13** Then you will know that I am the LORD, when those of them who are killed lie in the midst of their idols, all around their altars, on every high hill, on all the tops of the mountains, under every lush tree, and under every thick-branched terebinth, the place where they presented a pleasing aroma to all their idols.

**6:14** And I will stretch out My hand against them and make the land a desolation and a wasteland, from the wilderness to Diblah, in all their dwelling places; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — Ketiv/Qere: Ketiv reads ולגיאות, while Qere reads וְלַגֵּיאָיוֹת ("and to the valleys"), a variant plural spelling of גַּיְא ("valley").
- **6:6** — Hebrew וְיֶאְשְׁמוּ (Qal imperfect of אשׁם, "bear guilt" or "suffer the penalty of guilt") forms a soundplay with תִּישָׁמְנָה (Qal imperfect of ישׁם, "become desolate") earlier in the verse.
- **6:9** — Hebrew נִשְׁבַּרְתִּי (Niphal perfect 1cs of שׁבר, "I have been broken") is unusually followed by the marker אֶת before "their heart" and "their eyes," expressing the cause or respect in which the LORD was broken.
- **6:14** — Hebrew מִמִּדְבַּר דִּבְלָתָה combines the construct noun מִדְבַּר with directional דִּבְלָתָה ("from the wilderness to Diblah," or "more than the wilderness toward Diblah"). Diblah is otherwise unknown and is often compared to Riblah in northern Syria due to the graphic similarity of ד and ר.
