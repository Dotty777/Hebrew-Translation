## Text

**47:1** Then he brought me back to the entrance of the house, and look, water was coming out from beneath the threshold of the house toward the east, for the front of the house faced east; and the water was flowing down from beneath the right side of the house, south of the altar.

**47:2** He brought me out by way of the north gate and led me around on the outside to the outer gate that faces east; and look, water was trickling out from the right side.

**47:3** As the man went out eastward with a measuring line in his hand, he measured a thousand cubits and led me through the water, water up to the ankles.

**47:4** Again he measured a thousand and led me through the water, water up to the knees. Again he measured a thousand and led me through, water up to the waist.

**47:5** Again he measured a thousand, and it was a river that I could not cross, for the water had risen, water deep enough to swim in, a river that could not be crossed.

**47:6** And he said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man?" Then he led me and brought me back to the bank of the river.

**47:7** When I returned, look, along the bank of the river were very many trees on this side and on that side.

**47:8** And he said to me, "These waters flow out toward the eastern region and go down into the Arabah; then they enter the sea, being led out into the sea, and the waters will be healed.

**47:9** And every living creature that swarms, wherever the double river goes, will live; and there will be very many fish, because these waters go there so that the waters are healed, and everything will live wherever the river goes.

**47:10** Fishermen will stand beside it from En-gedi to En-eglaim; these will be places for spreading nets. Their fish will be according to their kinds, like the fish of the Great Sea, very many.

**47:11** But its swamps and its marshes will not be healed; they will be given over to salt.

**47:12** Along the river, on its bank on this side and on that side, every kind of tree for food will grow. Its leaf will not wither and its fruit will not fail; month by month it will bear fresh fruit, because its waters flow out from the sanctuary. Its fruit will be for food, and its leaf for healing."

**47:13** This is what the Lord GOD says: "This is the boundary by which you shall divide the land as an inheritance for the twelve tribes of Israel: Joseph shall have two portions.

**47:14** You shall inherit it, each man like his brother, for I lifted My hand to give it to your fathers; and this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

**47:15** This shall be the boundary of the land: on the north side, from the Great Sea by the way of Hethlon to the entrance of Zedad,

**47:16** Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which lies between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath, and Hazer-hatticon, which is on the border of Hauran.

**47:17** Thus the boundary shall run from the sea to Hazar-enon, at the border of Damascus, and on the north, northward, is the border of Hamath. This is the north side.

**47:18** On the east side, between Hauran and Damascus, and between Gilead and the land of Israel along the Jordan, you shall measure from the boundary to the eastern sea. This is the east side.

**47:19** On the south side, southward, the boundary shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meriboth-kadesh, then along the brook to the Great Sea. This is the south side, southward.

**47:20** On the west side, the Great Sea shall be the boundary from the boundary up to a point opposite the entrance of Hamath. This is the west side.

**47:21** So you shall divide this land among yourselves according to the tribes of Israel.

**47:22** You shall divide it by lot as an inheritance for yourselves and for the sojourners who sojourn in your midst, who have fathered children in your midst. They shall be to you like the native-born among the sons of Israel; with you they shall receive an allotment by lot as an inheritance among the tribes of Israel.

**47:23** In whatever tribe the sojourner sojourns, there you shall assign his inheritance," declares the Lord GOD.

## Translator Notes

- **47:8** — The Hophal participle "hammutsa'im" (from "yatsa", literally "those brought out / led forth") describes the temple waters discharging into the sea so that its waters are healed.
- **47:9** — The Hebrew uses the dual form "nachalayim" (literally "two rivers" or "double river") with a singular verb, emphasizing the great volume of the stream.
- **47:10** — The qere reads the Qal perfect "amedu" following "vehayah" ("will stand"), whereas the ketiv reads the Qal imperfect "ya'amdu".
- **47:12** — The qere reads the singular Qal perfect "vehayah" (agreeing with singular "its fruit"), whereas the ketiv reads the plural "vehayu".
- **47:13** — The demonstrative "geh" ("this") occurs only here in the Masoretic Text as a variant of "zeh" (cf. verse 15). "Two portions" renders the elliptic Hebrew "Yosef chavalim" (literally "Joseph, portions"), reflecting Ephraim and Manasseh.
