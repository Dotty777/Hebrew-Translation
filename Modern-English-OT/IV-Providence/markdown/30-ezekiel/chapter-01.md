## Text

**1:1** Now it happened in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, while I was in the midst of the exiles by the Chebar River, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.

**1:2** On the fifth day of the month—it was the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin—

**1:3** the word of the LORD indeed came to Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans by the Chebar River, and the hand of the LORD came upon him there.

**1:4** And I looked, and look, a tempestuous wind was coming from the north, a great cloud with fire flaring back and forth and brightness all around it; and from its midst was something like the gleam of electrum, from the midst of the fire.

**1:5** And from its midst came the likeness of four living creatures. Now this was their appearance: they had the likeness of a man.

**1:6** Each one had four faces, and each one of them had four wings.

**1:7** Their legs were straight, and the soles of their feet were like the sole of a calf's foot, and they sparkled like the gleam of burnished bronze.

**1:8** Human hands were under their wings on their four sides, and the four of them had their faces and their wings.

**1:9** Their wings were joined one to another. They did not turn as they went; each one went in the direction it faced.

**1:10** And the likeness of their faces was the face of a man; and the four of them had the face of a lion on the right side, and the four of them had the face of an ox on the left side, and the four of them had the face of an eagle.

**1:11** Such were their faces. And their wings were spread apart above: each had two that joined another, and two that covered their bodies.

**1:12** And each one went in the direction it faced; wherever the spirit was to go, they went; they did not turn as they went.

**1:13** As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like the appearance of torches; it was moving back and forth among the living creatures, and the fire had brightness, and lightning was coming out from the fire.

**1:15** And I looked at the living creatures, and look, there was one wheel on the earth beside the living creatures, for its four faces.

**1:16** The appearance of the wheels and their construction was like the gleam of beryl, and the four of them had one likeness; and their appearance and their construction were as if a wheel were inside a wheel.

**1:17** Toward any of their four sides they would go as they went; they did not turn as they went.

**1:18** As for their rims, they were high and awe-inspiring, and their rims were full of eyes all around on the four of them.

**1:19** And when the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.

**1:20** Wherever the spirit was to go, they went, there where the spirit was to go; and the wheels were lifted up alongside them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

**1:21** When those went, these went; and when those stood still, these stood still; and when they were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up alongside them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

**1:22** And over the heads of the living creatures was the likeness of an expanse, like the gleam of awesome crystal, stretched out over their heads above.

**1:23** And under the expanse their wings were straight, one toward another; each had two covering them, and each had two covering their bodies.

**1:24** And I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of many waters, like the voice of the Almighty, when they went—a sound of tumult like the sound of an army; when they stood still, they lowered their wings.

**1:25** And a voice came from above the expanse that was over their heads; when they stood still, they lowered their wings.

**1:26** And above the expanse that was over their heads was something like the appearance of a sapphire stone, the likeness of a throne; and upon the likeness of the throne was a likeness with the appearance of a man, above upon it.

**1:27** And I saw something like the gleam of electrum, like the appearance of fire enclosed within it all around, from the appearance of His waist and upward; and from the appearance of His waist and downward I saw something like the appearance of fire, and there was brightness all around Him.

**1:28** Like the appearance of the rainbow that is in the cloud on a day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all around. That was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD. And I saw it, and I fell on my face, and I heard a voice speaking.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The rare noun chashmal (occurring only in Ezek 1:4, 27; 8:2) likely denotes a luminous metal alloy such as electrum (cf. Akkadian elmeshu). The construct noun 'en (lit. 'eye') is used throughout chapter 1 for 'gleam' or 'luster,' and the Hithpael participle mithlaqqachat conveys fire continuously flaring back and forth.
- **1:5** — Throughout Ezekiel 1, masculine and feminine plural pronouns and suffixes alternate freely when referring to the four living creatures (chayyoth, grammatically feminine) and their parts.
- **1:8** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere (read form) is widey ('and hands of'), whereas the Ketiv (written form) is wydw ('and his hand').
- **1:15** — Literally 'for its four faces'; the masculine singular suffix on panaw apparently refers distributively to each four-faced creature (or to the four fronts of the group).
- **1:23** — The Hebrew text repeats the clause 'each had two covering them,' likely emphasizing the paired wings covering each creature's body on both sides.
