## Text

**30:1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**30:2** "Son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Wail, "Alas for the day!"

**30:3** For a day is near, indeed a day of the LORD is near; a day of clouds, it will be a time for the nations.

**30:4** A sword will come upon Egypt, and anguish will be in Cush when the mortally wounded fall in Egypt; and they will carry away its wealth, and its foundations will be torn down.

**30:5** Cush, Put, Lud, all the mixed peoples, Cub, and the sons of the land of the covenant will fall with them by the sword.

**30:6** This is what the LORD says: Those who uphold Egypt will fall, and the pride of its strength will come down; from Migdol to Syene they will fall in it by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.

**30:7** And they will be desolated in the midst of desolated lands, and its cities will be in the midst of ruined cities.

**30:8** And they will know that I am the LORD, when I set fire to Egypt and all its helpers are broken.

**30:9** On that day messengers will go out from before Me in ships to terrify secure Cush, and anguish will be upon them on the day of Egypt; for look, it is coming!

**30:10** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to the multitude of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

**30:11** He and his people with him, the most ruthless of nations, will be brought to destroy the land; and they will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the mortally wounded.

**30:12** And I will make the Nile streams dry ground, and sell the land into the hand of wicked men; and I will make the land and its fullness desolate by the hand of foreigners. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**30:13** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will destroy the idols and put an end to the worthless gods from Noph; and there will no longer be a prince from the land of Egypt, and I will put fear in the land of Egypt.

**30:14** I will make Pathros desolate, set fire to Zoan, and execute judgments on No.

**30:15** I will pour out My fury on Sin, the stronghold of Egypt, and I will cut off the multitude of No.

**30:16** I will set fire to Egypt; Sin will writhe in great anguish, No will be breached open, and Noph will have adversaries by day.

**30:17** The young men of Aven and of Pi-beseth will fall by the sword, and those cities will go into captivity.

**30:18** And in Tahpanhes the day will grow dark when I break there the yoke bars of Egypt, and the pride of its strength will come to an end in it; as for it, a cloud will cover it, and its daughters will go into captivity.

**30:19** Thus I will execute judgments on Egypt, and they will know that I am the LORD.'"

**30:20** Now in the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**30:21** "Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and look, it has not been bound up to apply remedies, or to put on a bandage to bind it up so that it might grow strong to grasp the sword.

**30:22** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I will break his arms, both the strong one and the broken one, and I will make the sword fall from his hand.

**30:23** And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the lands.

**30:24** I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put My sword in his hand; and I will break the arms of Pharaoh, and he will groan before him with the groans of a mortally wounded man.

**30:25** I will uphold the arms of the king of Babylon, while the arms of Pharaoh will fall. And they will know that I am the LORD when I put My sword into the hand of the king of Babylon and he stretches it out against the land of Egypt.

**30:26** And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the lands; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **30:13** — The Masoretic Hebrew names of the Egyptian cities in 30:13-18 are retained directly: Noph is Memphis, No is Thebes, Sin is Pelusium, and Aven is On or Heliopolis, vocalized as aven, wickedness, as a deliberate wordplay.
- **30:16** — Follows the qere tachul, will writhe, Qal imperfect 3fs of chul, rather than the ketiv spelling tachil. The closing phrase tsare yomam is an elliptical construct-before-adverb construction literally meaning adversaries by day.
- **30:17** — Hebrew wehennah is a feminine plural pronoun referring back to the cities Aven and Pi-beseth, since cities are grammatically feminine in Hebrew.
- **30:18** — The Masoretic text writes chasak with sin instead of chashak with shin, grow dark, an orthographic variant supported by the following reference to a covering cloud. Hebrew motot, yoke bars, follows the Masoretic vocalization rather than the Septuagint reading scepters.
