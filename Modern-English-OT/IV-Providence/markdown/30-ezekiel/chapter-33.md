## Text

**33:1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**33:2** "Son of man, speak to the sons of your people and say to them, 'When I bring a sword upon a land, and the people of the land take one man from among their number and appoint him for themselves as a watchman,

**33:3** and he sees the sword coming upon the land, and blows the trumpet and warns the people,

**33:4** then if anyone hears the sound of the trumpet and does not take warning, and a sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head.

**33:5** He heard the sound of the trumpet and did not take warning; his blood will be upon himself. Yet if he had taken warning, he would have rescued his life.

**33:6** But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the trumpet, so that the people are not warned, and a sword comes and takes a person from among them, that person is taken away in his iniquity, yet his blood I will require from the hand of the watchman.'

**33:7** Now as for you, son of man, I have appointed you as a watchman for the house of Israel; whenever you hear a word from My mouth, you must warn them from Me.

**33:8** When I say to the wicked, 'Wicked man, you will surely die,' and you do not speak to warn the wicked away from his way, that wicked man will die in his iniquity, yet his blood I will demand from your hand.

**33:9** But if you warn a wicked man away from his way to turn from it, and he does not turn from his way, he will die in his iniquity, while you have delivered your own life.

**33:10** Now as for you, son of man, say to the house of Israel, 'This is what you have spoken, saying: Surely our transgressions and our sins are upon us, and in them we are wasting away; how then can we live?'

**33:11** Say to them, 'As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I do not delight in the death of the wicked, but rather in the wicked turning from his way and living. Turn, turn from your evil ways! Why should you die, house of Israel?'

**33:12** And you, son of man, say to the sons of your people, 'The righteousness of the righteous will not deliver him on the day of his transgression; and as for the wickedness of the wicked, he will not stumble over it on the day he turns from his wickedness; nor will a righteous man be able to live by his righteousness on the day he sins.

**33:13** When I say to the righteous that he will surely live, and he trusts in his righteousness and commits injustice, none of his righteous deeds will be remembered; rather, for the injustice that he has committed, by it he will die.

**33:14** And when I say to the wicked, You will surely die, yet he turns from his sin and practices justice and righteousness,

**33:15** if the wicked restores a pledge, repays what was taken by robbery, and walks in the statutes of life so as not to commit injustice, he will surely live; he will not die.

**33:16** None of his sins that he has committed will be remembered against him; he has practiced justice and righteousness; he will surely live.'

**33:17** Yet the sons of your people say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair,' when as for them, it is their own way that is not fair.

**33:18** When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and commits injustice, he will die because of it.

**33:19** And when a wicked man turns from his wickedness and practices justice and righteousness, on account of them he will live.

**33:20** Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair.' I will judge you, each man according to his ways, house of Israel."

**33:21** It happened in the twelfth year of our exile, in the tenth month, on the fifth of the month, that the fugitive from Jerusalem came to me, saying, "The city has been struck down!"

**33:22** Now the hand of the LORD had been upon me in the evening before the fugitive arrived, and He opened my mouth by the time he came to me in the morning; so my mouth was opened, and I was no longer mute.

**33:23** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**33:24** "Son of man, the inhabitants of these ruins on the soil of Israel are speaking, saying, 'Abraham was one man, yet he possessed the land; but we are many, so to us the land has been given as a possession.'

**33:25** Therefore say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: You eat with the blood, lift up your eyes to your idols, and shed blood; should you then possess the land?'

**33:27** Say this to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: As I live, surely those who are in the ruins will fall by the sword, whoever is in the open field I will give to the wild beasts to devour him, and those who are in the strongholds and in the caves will die by pestilence.

**33:28** I will make the land a desolation and a waste, and the pride of her strength will come to an end; the mountains of Israel will be desolate, with no one passing through.

**33:29** Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I make the land a desolation and a waste because of all their abominations that they have committed.'

**33:30** Now as for you, son of man, the sons of your people are talking about you beside the walls and in the doorways of the houses; one speaks to another, each man to his brother, saying, 'Come now and hear what the word is that goes forth from the LORD.'

**33:31** They come to you as people come, and My people sit before you, and hear your words, yet they do not do them; for with their mouth they make a show of passionate love, while their heart goes after their dishonest gain.

**33:32** And look, you are to them like a song of passionate love, of one with a beautiful voice who plays well on an instrument; so they hear your words, yet they do not do them.

**33:33** And when it comes to pass—look, it is coming—then they will know that a prophet has been in their midst."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**33:26** *(after `33:25`)* You have depended on your sword, you have committed abomination, and each one of you has defiled his neighbor's wife; should you then possess the land?

## Translator Notes

- **33:12** — Hebrew bah ("by it") is feminine singular, referring back unambiguously to feminine tsedeqah ("righteousness") rather than the nearer masculine resha ("wickedness").
- **33:25** — Hebrew al-haddam ("with the blood") is the cultic and dietary idiom for eating meat without draining its blood, prohibited in Leviticus 19:26.
- **33:30** — Hebrew chad ("one") is an Aramaicized form of standard Hebrew echad, occurring only here in the Hebrew text of the Old Testament.
- **33:32** — Masoretic vocalization reads shir agavim ("a song of passionate love," echoing agavim in v. 31), followed by personal qualifiers describing a skilled singer and musician.
- **33:26 *(additional)*** — Hebrew literally reads "stood upon your sword" and uses a feminine plural verb form for "you have committed."
