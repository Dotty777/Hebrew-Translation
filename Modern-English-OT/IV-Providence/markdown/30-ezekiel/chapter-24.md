## Text

**24:1** Now the word of the LORD came to me in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, saying,

**24:2** "Son of man, write down for yourself the name of the day, this very day. The king of Babylon has closed in against Jerusalem on this very day.

**24:3** Speak a parable to the rebellious house and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Put the pot on, put it on, and pour water into it as well.

**24:4** Gather its pieces into it, every good piece, the thigh and the shoulder; fill it with the choicest bones.

**24:5** Take the choicest of the flock, and also pile the bones beneath it; bring it to a vigorous boil, so that even its bones are cooked within it.

**24:6** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the city of bloodshed, the pot whose corrosion is in it, and whose corrosion has not come out of it! Empty it out piece by piece; no lot has fallen upon it.

**24:7** For her blood was in her midst; she set it upon a bare rock; she did not pour it out on the ground to cover it with dust.

**24:8** To bring up wrath, to exact vengeance, I have put her blood on a bare rock, so that it would not be covered.

**24:9** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the city of bloodshed! I Myself will also make the pyre great.

**24:10** Heap up the wood, kindle the fire, cook the flesh completely, mix in the spice blend, and let the bones be charred.

**24:11** Then set it empty on its coals, so that its bronze may heat up and glow hot, and its impurity may be melted within it, and its corrosion may be consumed.

**24:12** She has exhausted efforts, yet her thick corrosion does not come out of her; into the fire with her corrosion!

**24:13** In your impurity is lewdness. Because I sought to cleanse you, yet you did not become clean, you will not be clean from your impurity anymore until I cause My wrath to rest upon you.

**24:14** I, the LORD, have spoken; it is coming, and I will act. I will not hold back, nor will I spare, nor will I relent. According to your ways and according to your deeds they will judge you, declares the Lord GOD.'"

**24:15** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**24:16** "Son of man, look, I am about to take from you the delight of your eyes with a stroke; yet you shall not mourn, and you shall not weep, nor shall your tears flow.

**24:17** Groan in silence; do not observe mourning for the dead. Wrap your turban on yourself and place your sandals on your feet; you shall not cover your upper lip, and you shall not eat the bread of men."

**24:18** So in the morning I spoke to the people, and my wife died in the evening; and in the morning I did just as I had been commanded.

**24:19** Then the people said to me, "Will you not tell us what these things mean to us, that you are doing them?"

**24:20** So I said to them, "The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**24:21** 'Say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am about to profane My sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the delight of your eyes, and the affection of your soul; and your sons and your daughters whom you left behind will fall by the sword.

**24:22** And you will do just as I have done: you will not cover your upper lip, and you will not eat the bread of men.

**24:23** Your turbans will stay on your heads and your sandals on your feet; you will not mourn and you will not weep, but you will waste away in your iniquities and groan to one another.

**24:24** So Ezekiel will be a sign to you; according to all that he has done, you will do. When this comes about, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.'"

**24:25** "And as for you, son of man, will it not be on the day I take from them their stronghold, the joy of their splendor, the delight of their eyes, and the longing of their soul, along with their sons and their daughters—

**24:26** that on that day the fugitive will come to you to bring the news to your ears?

**24:27** On that day your mouth will be opened to the fugitive, and you will speak and no longer be mute. So you will be a sign to them, and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **24:2** — Ketiv/Qere orthographic variant: the ketiv is spelled plene (ktwb) and the qere defectively (ktb) before the maqqef; both represent the Qal masculine singular imperative of katav ("write").
- **24:5** — MT reads "the bones" (ha-atsamim), not "the wood" (ha-etsim), maintaining the stark imagery of the pot; the preceding laqoach ("take") is a Qal infinitive absolute used imperatively.
- **24:12** — The opening clause te'unim hel'at ("she has exhausted efforts") is notably terse and preserves the archaic third-person feminine singular Hiphil ending -at on la'ah ("weary, exhaust"). The closing verbless clause be'esh chel'atah ("into the fire with her corrosion!") may alternatively indicate that her corrosion will not come out even by fire.
- **24:14** — MT reads the third-person plural Qal perfect shefatukh ("they will judge you," an indefinite plural or reference to the Babylonian invaders), rather than first-person singular "I will judge you."
- **24:22** — Within Ezekiel's recounting of the LORD's message, the voice briefly shifts in verses 22-23 into Ezekiel's own first-person perspective ("as I have done") before returning to third-person reference to Ezekiel in verse 24. "The bread of men" (also in v. 17) refers to consolation food customarily brought to mourners.
