## Text

**16:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**16:2** "Son of man, make Jerusalem know her abominations,

**16:3** and say, This is what the Lord GOD says to Jerusalem: Your origins and your birth are from the land of the Canaanite; your father was an Amorite, and your mother was a Hittite.

**16:4** And as for your birth, on the day you were born, your umbilical cord was not cut, you were not washed in water for cleansing, you were not rubbed with salt at all, and you were not swaddled at all.

**16:5** No eye took pity on you to do any one of these things for you, to show you compassion; instead, you were thrown out onto the open field in loathing of your life on the day you were born.

**16:6** Then I passed by you and saw you thrashing in your blood, and I said to you, 'In your blood, live!' And I said to you, 'In your blood, live!'

**16:7** I made you a myriad, like the growth of the field; you increased and grew up and came into the finest of ornaments. Your breasts became firm and your hair grew out, though you were naked and bare.

**16:8** Then I passed by you and saw you, and look, your time was the time for love. So I spread the edge of My garment over you and covered your nakedness; I swore an oath to you and entered a covenant with you, declares the Lord GOD, and you became Mine.

**16:9** Then I washed you in water, rinsed your blood off you, and anointed you with oil.

**16:10** I dressed you in embroidered cloth and put fine leather sandals on you; I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk.

**16:11** I adorned you with jewelry, and I placed bracelets on your wrists and a chain around your neck.

**16:12** I also set a ring on your nose, earrings on your ears, and a crown of splendor on your head.

**16:13** So you were adorned with gold and silver, and your attire was fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. You ate choice flour, honey, and oil, and you became exceedingly beautiful and rose to royalty.

**16:14** Your renown went out among the nations on account of your beauty, for it was complete through My majesty that I had placed on you, declares the Lord GOD.

**16:15** Yet you trusted in your beauty and prostituted yourself because of your renown, and you poured out your acts of prostitution on every passerby; his it became.

**16:16** You took some of your garments, made for yourself multicolored high places, and prostituted yourself on them; such things have never come about and never will be.

**16:17** You also took your jewels of splendor, of My gold and My silver that I had given you, and made for yourself male images and prostituted yourself with them.

**16:18** You took your embroidered garments and covered them, and you placed My oil and My incense before them.

**16:19** And My bread that I gave you, choice flour, oil, and honey that I fed you, you set before them as a soothing aroma; and so it was, declares the Lord GOD.

**16:20** You took your sons and your daughters whom you had borne to Me, and you sacrificed them to them to be devoured. Were your acts of prostitution too small a matter,

**16:21** that you slaughtered My children and gave them up by causing them to pass over to them?

**16:22** And with all your abominations and your acts of prostitution, you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare, when you were thrashing in your blood.

**16:23** And it happened after all your wickedness—woe, woe to you, declares the Lord GOD—

**16:24** that you built yourself a vaulted mound and made yourself a lofty platform in every open square.

**16:25** At the head of every road you built your lofty platform, made your beauty an abomination, spread your legs to every passerby, and multiplied your acts of prostitution.

**16:26** You also prostituted yourself with the sons of Egypt, your neighbors, large of flesh, and you multiplied your prostitution to provoke Me to anger.

**16:27** So look, I stretched out My hand against you, reduced your ration, and gave you over to the appetite of those who hated you, the daughters of the Philistines, who were ashamed of your lewd way.

**16:28** Then you prostituted yourself with the sons of Assyria because you were unsatisfied; you prostituted yourself with them, and even then you were not satisfied.

**16:29** And you multiplied your prostitution toward the land of commerce, to Chaldea, and even with this you were not satisfied.

**16:30** How feeble is your heart, declares the Lord GOD, when you do all these things, the deeds of a domineering prostitute!

**16:31** When you built your vaulted mound at the head of every road and made your lofty platform in every open square, you were not like a prostitute, for you scoffed at payment.

**16:32** Adulterous wife! In place of her husband, she takes strangers!

**16:33** To all prostitutes they give a gift, yet you gave your gifts to all your lovers and bribed them to come to you from all around for your acts of prostitution.

**16:34** So in you it was the reverse of other women in your acts of prostitution: no one went after you for prostitution, and because you gave payment while no payment was given to you, you became the reverse.

**16:35** Therefore, prostitute, hear the word of the LORD.

**16:36** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because your lust was poured out and your nakedness was exposed in your acts of prostitution with your lovers and with all the idols of your abominations, and because of the blood of your children whom you gave to them,

**16:37** therefore look, I am about to gather all your lovers to whom you were pleasing, all whom you loved along with all whom you hated. I will gather them against you from all around and expose your nakedness to them, and they will see all your nakedness.

**16:38** I will judge you with the judgments for women who commit adultery and who shed blood, and I will give you the bloodshed of fury and jealousy.

**16:39** I will deliver you into their hand, and they will tear down your vaulted mound and demolish your lofty platforms; they will strip you of your garments, take your jewels of splendor, and leave you naked and bare.

**16:40** They will bring up an assembly against you, stone you with stones, and hack you to pieces with their swords.

**16:41** They will burn your houses with fire and carry out judgments against you before the eyes of many women. I will cause you to cease from being a prostitute, and you will also no longer give payment.

**16:42** Then I will lay My fury to rest upon you, and My jealousy will turn away from you; I will grow quiet and will not be angry anymore.

**16:43** Because you did not remember the days of your youth, but enraged Me with all these things, look, I too have brought your way upon your head, declares the Lord GOD. Did you not commit this lewdness on top of all your abominations?

**16:44** Look, everyone who speaks proverbs will speak this proverb about you, saying, 'Like mother, like daughter.'

**16:45** You are the daughter of your mother, who abhors her husband and her children, and you are the sister of your sisters, who abhorred their husbands and their children. Your mother was a Hittite, and your father was an Amorite.

**16:46** And your older sister is Samaria, she and her daughters, who dwells at your left; and your sister younger than you, who dwells on your right, is Sodom and her daughters.

**16:47** You did not merely walk in their ways and act according to their abominations; as if that were a very small thing, you acted more corruptly than they did in all your ways.

**16:48** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, Sodom your sister, she and her daughters, did not act as you and your daughters have acted.

**16:49** Look, this was the iniquity of Sodom your sister: pride, fullness of bread, and tranquil ease belonged to her and her daughters, yet she did not strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.

**16:50** They grew haughty and committed an abomination before Me, so I took them away when I saw it.

**16:51** And Samaria did not sin half of your sins; you multiplied your abominations more than they and made your sisters seem righteous through all your abominations that you have done.

**16:52** You too, bear your disgrace, you who have judged in favor of your sisters; because of your sins in which you acted more abominably than they, they are more righteous than you. So you too, be ashamed and bear your disgrace, in making your sisters seem righteous.

**16:53** Yet I will restore their fortunes, the fortunes of Sodom and her daughters and the fortunes of Samaria and her daughters, and the restoration of your own fortunes in their midst,

**16:54** so that you may bear your disgrace and be humiliated for all that you have done, in comforting them.

**16:55** Your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, will return to their former condition, and Samaria and her daughters will return to their former condition, and you and your daughters will return to your former condition.

**16:56** Was Sodom your sister not a byword in your mouth on the day of your pride,

**16:57** before your wickedness was exposed, as at the time of the reproach of the daughters of Aram and all those around her, the daughters of the Philistines, who despise you from all around?

**16:58** Your lewdness and your abominations, you yourself bear them, declares the LORD.

**16:59** For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will indeed deal with you just as you have done, because you despised the oath by violating the covenant.

**16:60** Yet I Myself will remember My covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish an eternal covenant for you.

**16:61** Then you will remember your ways and be humiliated when you receive your sisters, both those older than you and those younger than you; and I will give them to you as daughters, though not on account of your covenant.

**16:62** And I Myself will establish My covenant with you, and you will know that I am the LORD,

**16:63** in order that you may remember and be ashamed, and no longer open your mouth because of your disgrace, when I make atonement for you for all that you have done, declares the Lord GOD."

## Translator Notes

- **16:4** — The noun translated "for cleansing" is a Hebrew hapax legomenon whose exact etymology is uncertain, generally understood from cognate roots as cleansing or rubbing smooth. The final two clauses pair a Hophal infinitive absolute with a passive verb (Hophal of malach, Pual of chatal) for emphatic negation.
- **16:13** — Following the qere ("fine linen" and "you ate"); the ketiv writes "my fine linen" (or a variant spelling) and an archaic second-person feminine singular ending (-ti) on the verb, a ketiv/qere pattern that recurs on the verbs in vv. 18, 22, and 31.
- **16:16** — The closing Hebrew clause is notably terse, literally "not coming things and it will not be," expressing unprecedented outrage.
- **16:20** — Following the qere plural ("your acts of prostitution"); the ketiv is singular ("your prostitution"), a variant that recurs in v. 25. In v. 21, the Hiphil of avar ("causing them to pass over") is the technical Hebrew idiom for child sacrifice by fire, though "fire" is unstated here.
- **16:29** — Hebrew eretz kenaan literally means "land of Canaan," playing on v. 3 while using kenaan in its secondary sense of "commerce" or "merchants" for Chaldea (compare 17:4).
- **16:36** — The noun translated "lust" (nechoshet) is uncertain in meaning, elsewhere denoting "bronze," and is commonly understood here from Akkadian cognates as sexual passion or lavishly spent wealth.
- **16:43** — Following the qere on both verbs (standard 2fs forms where the ketiv writes the archaic -ti ending, as also in vv. 47 and 51). The final negative clause (velo asit) is rendered as an unmarked rhetorical question matching the Qal perfect verb.
- **16:51** — Following the qere plural ("your sisters") where the ketiv consonantal spelling could be read singular; the verse also has the archaic -ti ketiv ending on the final verb.
- **16:53** — The idiom "restore the fortunes" pairs the verb shuv with shevit/shevut, where the qere reads shevut three times against the ketiv shevit. The closing phrase combines both nouns (shevut shevitayikh) emphatically.
- **16:59** — Following the qere 1cs verb ("I will indeed deal"); the ketiv omits the final yod. In v. 57, the Hebrew text reads "Aram" (Syria), translated literally rather than emended to "Edom."
