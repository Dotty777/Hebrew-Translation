## Text

**25:1** Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**25:2** "Son of man, turn your face toward the sons of Ammon and prophesy against them,

**25:3** and say to the sons of Ammon, 'Hear the word of the Lord GOD! This is what the Lord GOD says: Because you said, "Aha!" over My sanctuary because it was profaned, and over the land of Israel because it became desolate, and over the house of Judah because they went into exile,

**25:4** therefore, look, I am giving you to the sons of the east as a possession, and they will set up their encampments in you and make their dwellings in you; they themselves will eat your fruit, and they themselves will drink your milk.

**25:5** And I will turn Rabbah into a grazing ground for camels and the sons of Ammon into a resting place for flocks; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**25:6** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you clapped your hands and stamped with your foot, and rejoiced with all the contempt of your soul against the land of Israel,

**25:7** therefore, look, I have stretched out My hand against you, and I will hand you over as plunder to the nations; I will cut you off from the peoples and cause you to perish from the lands. I will destroy you, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**25:8** This is what the Lord GOD says: "Because Moab and Seir say, 'Look, the house of Judah is like all the nations,'

**25:9** therefore, look, I am about to lay open the flank of Moab from the cities, from his frontier cities, the glory of the land—Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim—

**25:10** to the sons of the east along with the sons of Ammon, and I will give it as a possession, so that the sons of Ammon will not be remembered among the nations.

**25:11** And upon Moab I will execute judgments, and they will know that I am the LORD."

**25:12** This is what the Lord GOD says: "Because Edom acted in taking vengeance against the house of Judah, and incurred grievous guilt and avenged themselves upon them,

**25:13** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will stretch out My hand against Edom and cut off from it both man and beast, and I will make it a ruin; from Teman as far as Dedan they will fall by the sword.

**25:14** And I will lay My vengeance upon Edom by the hand of My people Israel, and they will act against Edom according to My anger and according to My wrath; thus they will know My vengeance, declares the Lord GOD."

**25:15** This is what the Lord GOD says: "Because the Philistines acted in vengeance and avenged themselves with contempt of soul to bring destruction with everlasting enmity,

**25:16** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am stretching out My hand against the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethites and cause the remnant of the seacoast to perish.

**25:17** I will execute great acts of vengeance against them with wrathful rebukes; and they will know that I am the LORD when I lay My vengeance upon them."

## Translator Notes

- **25:7** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the qere reads levaz ("as plunder," matching 26:5), whereas the ketiv reads levag (either a rare loanword for "food/portion" or a scribal confusion of gimel and zayin).
- **25:9** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the qere reads the dual place-name ve-Qiryataymah ("and Kiriathaim," with final -ah), whereas the ketiv wqrytmh omits the yod.
- **25:16** — The Hebrew employs a wordplay between the Hiphil verb vehikhratti ("and I will cut off," root krt) and the gentilic name Keretim ("Cherethites").
