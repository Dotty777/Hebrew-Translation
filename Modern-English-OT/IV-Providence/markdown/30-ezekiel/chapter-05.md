## Text

**5:1** "Now you, son of man, take for yourself a sharp sword; as a barber's razor you shall take it for yourself and pass it over your head and over your beard. Then take for yourself weighing scales and divide the hair.

**5:2** A third you shall burn in the fire in the middle of the city when the days of the siege are fulfilled. And you shall take a third and strike it with the sword all around it, and a third you shall scatter to the wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.

**5:3** And you shall take from there a few in number and tie them in the corners of your garment.

**5:4** And from these you shall take some again, throw them into the middle of the fire, and burn them in the fire; from it fire will go out to the whole house of Israel."

**5:5** This is what the Lord GOD says, "This is Jerusalem; I have placed her in the midst of the nations, with lands all around her.

**5:6** Yet she has rebelled against My ordinances in wickedness beyond the nations, and against My statutes beyond the lands around her; for My ordinances they have rejected, and in My statutes they have not walked."

**5:7** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says, "Because your tumult is greater than that of the nations around you, and you have not walked in My statutes, nor carried out My ordinances, nor acted according to the ordinances of the nations around you—"

**5:8** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says, "Look, I Myself also am against you, and I will carry out judgments in your midst before the eyes of the nations.

**5:9** And I will do among you what I have not done, and anything like it I will never do again, because of all your abominations.

**5:10** Therefore fathers will eat sons in your midst, and sons will eat their fathers; and I will carry out judgments upon you and scatter all your remnant to every wind.

**5:11** Therefore, as I live," declares the Lord GOD, "indeed, because you have defiled My sanctuary with all your loathsome things and with all your abominations, I too will withdraw, and My eye will not look with pity, and I too will have no compassion.

**5:12** A third of you will die by plague and perish by famine in your midst; a third will fall by the sword all around you; and a third I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.

**5:13** And My anger will come to an end, and I will let My wrath rest upon them and find relief; and they will know that I, the LORD, have spoken in My zeal when I bring My wrath to an end on them.

**5:14** And I will make you a ruin and a reproach among the nations that are around you, before the eyes of every passerby.

**5:15** And she will become a reproach and a taunt, an admonition and a horror, to the nations that are around you, when I carry out judgments upon you in anger and in wrath and in rebukes of wrath. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**5:16** When I send the evil arrows of famine against them, which are for destruction, which I will send to destroy you, then I will increase famine upon you and break for you the staff of bread.

**5:17** And I will send famine and vicious beasts upon you, and they will bereave you; plague and bloodshed will pass through you, and I will bring a sword upon you. I, the LORD, have spoken."

## Translator Notes

- **5:7** — Hebrew הֲמָנְכֶם (from הָמוֹן/המה, "tumult, commotion, turbulence") refers to unruly commotion; also, the Masoretic Text includes לֹא ("not") in the final clause, implying Israel failed to live up even to the surrounding nations' standards (contrast Ezek 11:12).
- **5:11** — Hebrew אֶגְרַע (Qal imperfect of גרע, "diminish, withdraw," or "shear/clip off" as in Isa 15:2) appears here without an explicit object, hence rendered "withdraw."
- **5:13** — Hebrew וְהִנֶּחָמְתִּי is a Hithpael weqatal of נחם with assimilated taw, conveying the sense of relieving oneself of anger or finding satisfaction.
- **5:15** — The Masoretic Text uses the 3rd-person feminine singular verb וְהָיְתָה ("and she will become," referring to Jerusalem) before resuming 2nd-person feminine singular address (סְבִיבוֹתָיִךְ, "around you"); similarly, 5:16 shifts from 3rd-person plural בָּהֶם ("against them") to 2nd-person plural לְשַׁחֶתְכֶם ("to destroy you").
