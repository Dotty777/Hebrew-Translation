## Text

**20:1** Now it happened in the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth of the month, that men from the elders of Israel came to inquire of the LORD, and they sat down before me.

**20:2** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**20:3** "Son of man, speak with the elders of Israel and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Is it to inquire of Me that you are coming? As I live, I will certainly not let Myself be inquired of by you, declares the Lord GOD.'

**20:4** Will you judge them? Will you judge, son of man? Make known to them the abominations of their fathers.

**20:5** And say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I chose Israel, I lifted My hand to the seed of the house of Jacob and made Myself known to them in the land of Egypt, and I lifted My hand to them, saying, "I am the LORD your God."

**20:6** On that day I lifted My hand to them to bring them out from the land of Egypt into a land that I had scouted out for them, flowing with milk and honey; it is the splendor of all the lands.

**20:7** And I said to them, "Each of you, throw away the detestable things of his eyes, and do not defile yourselves with the idols of Egypt; I am the LORD your God."

**20:8** But they rebelled against Me and were unwilling to listen to Me; not one of them threw away the detestable things of their eyes, and they did not abandon the idols of Egypt. Then I thought to pour out My wrath upon them, to exhaust My anger against them in the midst of the land of Egypt.

**20:9** Yet I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the eyes of the nations in whose midst they were, before whose eyes I had made Myself known to them in bringing them out from the land of Egypt.

**20:10** So I brought them out from the land of Egypt and brought them into the wilderness.

**20:11** And I gave them My statutes and made My ordinances known to them, by which a person will live if he does them.

**20:12** I also gave them My Sabbaths to serve as a sign between Me and them, so that they might know that I am the LORD who sanctifies them.

**20:13** But the house of Israel rebelled against Me in the wilderness. They did not walk in My statutes, and they rejected My ordinances, by which a person will live if he does them, and they greatly profaned My Sabbaths. Then I thought to pour out My wrath upon them in the wilderness to bring an end to them.

**20:14** Yet I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the eyes of the nations, before whose eyes I had brought them out.

**20:15** Moreover, I also lifted My hand to them in the wilderness that I would not bring them into the land that I had given, flowing with milk and honey—it is the splendor of all the lands—

**20:16** because they rejected My ordinances, and did not walk in My statutes, and profaned My Sabbaths; for their heart was going after their idols.

**20:17** Yet My eye had pity on them so as not to destroy them, and I did not make a complete end of them in the wilderness.

**20:18** And I said to their children in the wilderness, "Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers, and do not keep their ordinances, and do not defile yourselves with their idols.

**20:19** I am the LORD your God; walk in My statutes, and keep My ordinances and do them.

**20:20** And sanctify My Sabbaths, and they will serve as a sign between Me and you, so that you may know that I am the LORD your God."

**20:21** But the children rebelled against Me. They did not walk in My statutes, and they did not keep My ordinances to do them, by which a person will live if he does them; they profaned My Sabbaths. Then I thought to pour out My wrath upon them, to exhaust My anger against them in the wilderness.

**20:22** Yet I drew back My hand and acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the eyes of the nations, before whose eyes I had brought them out.

**20:23** Moreover, I lifted My hand to them in the wilderness to scatter them among the nations and to disperse them across the lands,

**20:24** because they did not do My ordinances, and they rejected My statutes, and they profaned My Sabbaths, and their eyes were after the idols of their fathers.

**20:25** And I also gave them statutes that were not good, and ordinances by which they would not live.

**20:26** And I defiled them through their gifts, as they caused everything that opens the womb to pass over, so that I might appall them, in order that they would know that I am the LORD.'

**20:27** Therefore, speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: In this too your fathers blasphemed Me, by committing treachery against Me.

**20:28** When I brought them into the land that I lifted My hand to give to them, they saw every high hill and every leafy tree, and there they sacrificed their sacrifices, and there they gave the provocation of their offering, and there they placed their soothing fragrances, and there they poured out their drink offerings.

**20:29** Then I said to them, "What is the high place to which you are going?" So its name is called Bamah to this day.'

**20:30** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Are you defiling yourselves in the way of your fathers, and prostituting yourselves after their detestable things?

**20:31** And when you lift up your gifts, in making your children pass through the fire, you are defiling yourselves with all your idols to this day. And should I let Myself be inquired of by you, house of Israel? As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will certainly not let Myself be inquired of by you.

**20:32** And what rises in your spirit will certainly never happen, when you say, "We will be like the nations, like the families of the lands, ministering to wood and stone."

**20:33** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, surely with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with wrath poured out, I will reign over you.

**20:34** And I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you have been scattered, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with wrath poured out.

**20:35** And I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and I will contend in judgment with you there, face to face.

**20:36** Just as I contended in judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will contend in judgment with you, declares the Lord GOD.

**20:37** And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you within the bond of the covenant.

**20:38** And I will purge from among you the rebels and those who transgress against Me; from the land of their sojourning I will bring them out, yet none of them will enter the soil of Israel. And you will know that I am the LORD.

**20:39** As for you, house of Israel, this is what the Lord GOD says: Go, serve each one his idols, and afterward, if you will not listen to Me; yet My holy name you must not profane anymore with your gifts and with your idols.

**20:40** For on My holy mountain, on the mountain height of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, there all the house of Israel, all of it in the land, will serve Me. There I will accept them, and there I will require your contributions and the finest of your tribute offerings, along with all your holy gifts.

**20:41** As a soothing fragrance I will accept you when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you have been scattered; and I will show Myself holy among you before the eyes of the nations.

**20:42** And you will know that I am the LORD when I bring you into the soil of Israel, into the land that I lifted My hand to give to your fathers.

**20:43** And there you will remember your ways and all your deeds by which you defiled yourselves, and you will feel loathing for yourselves in your own sight because of all your evils that you have done.

**20:44** And you will know that I am the LORD when I deal with you for the sake of My name, not according to your evil ways nor according to your corrupt deeds, house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:5** — Hebrew "lifted My hand" (nasa yad) is a gesture and idiom for swearing a solemn oath, recurring throughout the chapter (20:6, 15, 23, 28, 42).
- **20:26** — The Hebrew phrase "cause everything that opens the womb to pass over" uses the Hiphil of abar (echoing Exod 13:12) and implicitly refers to child sacrifice by fire (explicit in 20:31). The Hiphil verb ashimmem (from shamem) can mean either "appall" or "devastate."
- **20:29** — The Hebrew contains a wordplay linking bamah ("high place," transliterated Bamah) with mah ("what") and baim ("going"), mocking the illegitimate cultic shrines.
- **20:37** — The noun masoret ("bond") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and is generally derived from asar ("to bind"), denoting the binding obligation of the covenant.
- **20:39** — The Hebrew syntax of the middle clause is terse and elliptical: literally "and afterward, if you are not listening to Me," which can also be read as an oath-like assertion meaning "and afterward you will surely listen to Me."
