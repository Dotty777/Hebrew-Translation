## Text

**7:1** Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**7:2** "And you, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says to the soil of Israel: 'An end! The end has come upon the four edges of the land.

**7:3** An end has come; the end has come! It has awakened against you; look, it is coming!

**7:4** The doom has come to you, inhabitant of the land! The time has come, the day is near—tumult, and not joyful shouting of the mountains.

**7:5** Now shortly I will pour out My fury upon you, and exhaust My anger against you, and judge you according to your ways, and lay all your abominations upon you.

**7:6** And My eye will not spare, nor will I have compassion; according to your ways I will repay you, and your abominations will be in your midst; and you will know that I, the LORD, am the one who strikes.

**7:7** Now the end is upon you, and I will send My anger against you, and judge you according to your ways, and lay all your abominations upon you.

**7:8** And My eye will not spare you, nor will I have compassion; for I will lay your ways upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst; and you will know that I am the LORD.'

**7:10** This is what the Lord GOD says: 'Disaster, a singular disaster! Look, it is coming! Look, the day! Look, it is coming! The doom has gone out; the rod has bloomed, arrogance has sprouted.

**7:11** Violence has risen up into a rod of wickedness; none of them, none of their multitude, none of their abundance, nor any eminence among them.

**7:12** The time has come, the day has arrived; let the buyer not rejoice, and let the seller not mourn, for burning anger is upon all her multitude.

**7:13** For the seller will not return to what was sold, even while their life is still among the living; for the vision against all her multitude will not turn back, and in his iniquity none of them will sustain his life.

**7:14** They have sounded the horn and prepared everything, yet no one is going to the battle, for My burning anger is upon all her multitude.

**7:15** The sword is outside, and the plague and the famine are inside; the one who is in the field will die by the sword, and the one who is in the city—famine and plague will devour him.

**7:16** And their survivors will escape and be upon the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them groaning, each man because of his iniquity.

**7:17** All hands will go limp, and all knees will run with water.

**7:18** And they will wrap themselves in sackcloth, and shuddering will cover them; on every face will be shame, and on all their heads baldness.

**7:19** They will throw their silver into the streets, and their gold will become an impure thing; their silver and their gold will not be able to rescue them on the day of the wrath of the LORD. They will not satisfy their appetite, nor will they fill their stomachs, for it has been the stumbling block of their iniquity.

**7:20** And as for the beauty of its ornament, they turned it into pride, and the images of their abominations—their detestable things—they made with it; therefore I have made it an impure thing to them.

**7:21** And I will give it into the hand of the foreigners for plunder, and to the wicked of the earth for spoil, and they will profane it.

**7:22** And I will turn My face away from them, and they will profane My hidden place; and violent men will come into it and profane it.

**7:23** Make the chain, for the land is filled with crimes of bloodshed, and the city is filled with violence.

**7:24** And I will bring the most wicked of nations, and they will take possession of their houses; I will end the pride of the mighty, and their sanctuaries will be profaned.

**7:25** Anguish has come, and they will seek peace, yet there will be none.

**7:26** Calamity upon calamity will come, and there will be report upon report; they will seek a vision from a prophet, yet instruction will perish from a priest, and counsel from elders.

**7:27** The king will mourn, and a prince will clothe himself with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land will shake with terror. According to their way I will deal with them, and by their own judgments I will judge them; and they will know that I am the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — Ketiv ארבעת (feminine construct) vs. Qere אַרְבַּע (masculine construct, standard before feminine כַּנְפוֹת, literally 'wings' or 'edges').
- **7:4** — הַצְּפִירָה ('the doom', also in 7:10) is a rare noun meaning 'cycle/turn of fate' or 'doom' (compare 'crown/wreath' in Isa 28:5); הֵד is a hapax legomenon related to הֵידָד ('joyful shouting').
- **7:10** — This edition's verse arrangement differs from the Hebrew here: Hebrew 7:6-9 appear at 7:3-6 and Hebrew 7:3-4 at 7:7-8, there is no separate 7:9, and Hebrew 7:5 ('This is what the Lord GOD says: Disaster, a singular disaster! Look, it is coming!') is combined with 7:10 here.
- **7:11** — Contains two hapax legomena of uncertain sense in elliptical verbless clauses: מֶהֱמֵהֶם ('of their abundance/wealth', or 'tumult') and נֹהַּ ('eminence/preeminence', or possibly 'wailing' from נהה).
- **7:21** — Ketiv וחללוה (third-person feminine singular suffix) vs. Qere וְחִלְּלוּהוּ (third-person masculine singular suffix, referring back to the masculine 'beauty of its ornament' in 7:20).
