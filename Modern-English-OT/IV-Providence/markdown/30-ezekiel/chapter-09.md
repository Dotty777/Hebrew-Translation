## Text

**9:1** Then He cried out in my ears with a loud voice, saying, "The appointed executioners of the city have drawn near, each man with his weapon of destruction in his hand."

**9:2** And look, six men were coming from the direction of the upper gate that is turned northward, each man with his smashing weapon in his hand; and one man in their midst was clothed in linen, with a scribe's writing kit at his waist. And they came in and stood beside the altar of bronze.

**9:3** Now the glory of the God of Israel rose up from above the cherub on which it had been to the threshold of the house; and He called to the man clothed in linen who had the scribe's writing kit at his waist.

**9:4** And the LORD said to him, "Pass through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a mark upon the foreheads of the men who are sighing and groaning over all the abominations that are being done in its midst."

**9:5** And to these He said in my hearing, "Pass through the city after him and strike; do not let your eye spare, and do not have compassion.

**9:6** Kill to destruction old men, young men and virgins, little children and women, but do not come near any man on whom is the mark; and begin from My sanctuary." So they began with the elder men who were in front of the house.

**9:7** And He said to them, "Defile the house, and fill the courtyards with the dead. Go out!" So they went out and struck throughout the city.

**9:8** And it happened as they were striking, and I was left behind, that I fell upon my face and cried out and said, "Alas, Lord GOD! Are You destroying all the remnant of Israel as You pour out Your fury upon Jerusalem?"

**9:9** Then He said to me, "The iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah is great in the extreme; the land has been filled with bloodshed, and the city is full of injustice; for they have said, 'The LORD has forsaken the land, and the LORD does not see.'

**9:10** And as for Me too, My eye will not spare, nor will I have compassion; I have laid their way upon their heads."

**9:11** And look, the man clothed in linen, who had the writing kit at his waist, brought back word, saying, "I have done according to all that You commanded me."

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — קָרְבוּ is vocalized as a Qal perfect ('have drawn near'), though with different vocalization (קִרְבוּ or קַרְּבוּ) it is often read as an imperative ('Draw near' or 'Bring near'); פְּקֻדּוֹת ('visitations/punishments') is used concretely for the appointed executioners.
- **9:5** — Two Ketiv/Qere variants: Ketiv על ('upon' or orthographic variant) vs. Qere אַל ('do not'), and Ketiv עיניכם (plural 'your eyes') vs. Qere עֵינְכֶם (singular 'your eye', matching the feminine singular verb תָּחֹס).
- **9:8** — וְנֵאשְׁאַר is an anomalous conflate Masoretic form blending the first-person singular imperfect אֶשָּׁאֵר and the Niphal participle נִשְׁאָר ('I was left').
- **9:9** — מֻטֶּה is a hapax legomenon from the root נטה ('bend, turn aside'), denoting 'perverted justice' or 'injustice'.
- **9:11** — Ketiv כאשר ('just as') vs. Qere כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר ('according to all that').
