## Text

**27:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**27:2** "Now you, son of man, raise a lamentation over Tyre,

**27:3** and say to Tyre, who sits at the entrances of the sea, trader of the peoples to many coastlands, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Tyre, you have said, "I am the perfection of beauty."

**27:4** Your borders are in the heart of the seas; your builders have perfected your beauty.

**27:5** With cypresses from Senir they built all the planks for you; they took a cedar from Lebanon to make a mast upon you.

**27:6** With oaks from Bashan they made your oars; your deck they made of ivory in boxwood from the coastlands of Kittim.

**27:7** Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt was your sail, to serve as a banner for you; blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah was your covering.

**27:8** The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were rowers for you; your wise men, Tyre, were in you—they were your helmsmen.

**27:9** The elders of Gebal and her wise men were in you, repairing your seams; all the ships of the sea and their sailors were in you to exchange your merchandise.

**27:10** Persia, Lud, and Put were in your army, your men of war; they hung shield and helmet in you; they bestowed your splendor.

**27:11** The sons of Arvad and your army were upon your walls all around, and Gammadim were in your towers; they hung their shields upon your walls all around; they perfected your beauty.

**27:12** Tarshish was your merchant because of the abundance of all wealth; with silver, iron, tin, and lead they bartered for your wares.

**27:13** Javan, Tubal, and Meshech—they were your traders; with human lives and bronze vessels they bartered for your merchandise.

**27:14** From Beth-togarmah they bartered horses, riding horses, and mules for your wares.

**27:15** The sons of Dedan were your traders; many coastlands were the market of your hand; ivory tusks and ebony they brought back as your payment.

**27:16** Aram was your merchant because of the abundance of your products; with turquoise, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral, and rubies they bartered for your wares.

**27:17** Judah and the land of Israel—they were your traders; with wheat of Minnith, fine meal, honey, oil, and balm they bartered for your merchandise.

**27:18** Damascus was your merchant in the abundance of your products, because of the abundance of all wealth, with wine of Helbon and white wool.

**27:19** Vedan and Javan from Uzal bartered for your wares; forged iron, cassia, and calamus were in your merchandise.

**27:20** Dedan was your trader in saddle blankets for riding.

**27:21** Arabia and all the princes of Kedar—they were the merchants of your hand; with lambs, rams, and male goats, in these they were your merchants.

**27:22** The traders of Sheba and Raamah—they were your traders; with the choicest of all spices, and with every precious stone and gold, they bartered for your wares.

**27:23** Haran, Canneh, and Eden, the traders of Sheba, Asshur, and Kilmad were your traders.

**27:24** They were your traders in splendid garments, in cloaks of blue and embroidered work, and in chests of variegated fabric, bound with cords and tightly secured, in your marketplace.

**27:25** The ships of Tarshish were your caravans for your merchandise; so you were filled and became very heavy in the heart of the seas.

**27:26** Those who rowed you brought you into great waters; the east wind has shattered you in the heart of the seas.

**27:27** Your wealth, your wares, your merchandise, your sailors and your helmsmen, those who repair your seams and those who exchange your merchandise, and all your men of war who are in you, along with all your company that is in your midst, will fall into the heart of the seas on the day of your downfall.

**27:28** At the sound of the outcry of your helmsmen, the countryside will shake.

**27:29** And all who grasp the oar, the sailors, and all the helmsmen of the sea will come down from their ships; they will stand on the land.

**27:30** And they will make their voice heard over you and cry out bitterly; they will cast dust upon their heads and roll themselves in ashes.

**27:32** And in their wailing they will raise a lamentation over you and chant a lament over you: "Who is like Tyre, like one silenced in the midst of the sea?

**27:33** When your wares went out from the seas, you satisfied many peoples; with the abundance of your wealth and your merchandise you enriched the kings of the earth.

**27:34** At the time you are shattered by the seas in the depths of the waters, your merchandise and all your company have fallen in your midst.

**27:35** All the inhabitants of the coastlands are appalled at you, and their kings shudder with horror; their faces are convulsed.

**27:36** The merchants among the peoples hiss at you; you have become a terror, and you will be no more forever."'"

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — Ketiv hyšbty preserves an archaic feminine participle ending (-i), while Qere hayyōšebet is the standard Qal active participle feminine singular ("who sits"); the translation follows the Qere.
- **27:6** — MT bat-ʾăšurîm (literally "daughter of boxwoods") is widely understood as a word-division variant for bĕtaʾăšurîm ("in boxwood," cf. Isa 41:19; 60:13).
- **27:15** — Ketiv whwbnm and Qere wĕhobnîm ("and ebony") are orthographic variants of the same plural noun; the translation follows the Qere.
- **27:17** — Hebrew pannag is a hapax legomenon of uncertain meaning, likely referring to a type of fine meal or baked confection (cf. Akkadian pannigu).
- **27:19** — MT wĕdān may be read either as the Arabian place name "Vedan" or as "and Dan," and mĕʾuzzāl either as "from Uzal" (cf. Gen 10:27) or as a Pual participle meaning "spun yarn."
