## Text

**4:1** "Now you, son of man, take for yourself a brick, set it in front of you, and inscribe on it a city, Jerusalem.

**4:2** Lay siege against it, build a siege wall against it, heap up a siege ramp against it, set camps against it, and position battering rams against it all around.

**4:3** And you, take for yourself an iron griddle and set it as an iron wall between you and the city, and direct your face toward it; it shall be under siege, and you shall besiege it. It is a sign to the house of Israel.

**4:4** And you, lie down on your left side and put the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it. For the number of days that you lie on it, you shall bear their iniquity.

**4:5** And I have appointed for you the years of their iniquity as a number of days, three hundred and ninety days; so you shall bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.

**4:6** When you have finished these, you shall lie down on your right side a second time and bear the iniquity of the house of Judah for forty days; a day for each year, a day for each year I have appointed it for you.

**4:7** And toward the siege of Jerusalem you shall direct your face, with your arm bared, and prophesy against it.

**4:8** Now look, I have placed cords upon you, so that you will not turn from side to side until you have finished the days of your siege.

**4:9** And you, take for yourself wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt, and place them in one vessel and make them into bread for yourself. For the number of days that you lie on your side, three hundred and ninety days, you shall eat it.

**4:10** And the food that you eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels a day; from set time to set time you shall eat it.

**4:11** And water you shall drink by measure, a sixth of a hin; from set time to set time you shall drink.

**4:12** And as a barley cake you shall eat it; you shall bake it on dung of human excrement before their eyes."

**4:13** Then the LORD said, "In this way the sons of Israel will eat their bread unclean among the nations to which I will drive them."

**4:14** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Look, my soul has not been defiled; from my youth until now I have not eaten an animal that died naturally or was torn by beasts, nor has foul meat entered my mouth."

**4:15** Then He said to me, "See, I have given you cattle dung in place of human dung, and you shall make your bread over it."

**4:16** And He said to me, "Son of man, look, I am breaking the staff of bread in Jerusalem; they will eat bread by weight and with anxiety, and they will drink water by measure and in horror,

**4:17** so that they will lack bread and water, and be horrified, each man and his brother, and waste away in their iniquity."

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — Ketiv/Qere: Ketiv reads הימיני (hayyemini), while Qere reads הַיְמָנִי (hayyemani); both are spelling variants of the adjective meaning "right."
- **4:10** — The Hebrew idiom מֵעֵת עַד־עֵת literally means "from time to time," denoting fixed daily intervals (repeated in 4:11).
- **4:12** — The verb תְּעֻגֶנָה (Qal imperfect 2ms of the rare denominative root עוג with a 3fs suffix) forms a wordplay with עֻגַת ("cake"), meaning "you shall bake it as a cake."
- **4:15** — Ketiv/Qere: Ketiv reads צפועי, while Qere reads צְפִיעֵי, both spelling variants of a rare noun denoting cattle dung.
