## Text

**14:1** Now men from among the elders of Israel came to me and sat before me.

**14:2** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**14:3** "Son of man, these men have brought up their idols upon their heart and have placed the stumbling block of their iniquity directly in front of their faces. Should I truly allow Myself to be inquired of by them?

**14:4** Therefore speak with them and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Any man from the house of Israel who brings up his idols into his heart and sets the stumbling block of his iniquity directly in front of his face, and comes to the prophet, I, the LORD, will respond to him who comes with the abundance of his idols,

**14:5** in order to seize the house of Israel in their heart, because all of them have become estranged from Me through their idols.'

**14:6** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Turn back and turn away from your idols, and from all your abominations turn your faces away.

**14:7** For any man from the house of Israel, or from the sojourner who resides in Israel, who separates himself from following Me and brings up his idols into his heart, and sets the stumbling block of his iniquity directly in front of his face, and comes to the prophet to inquire of Me for himself, I, the LORD, will respond to him by Myself.

**14:8** And I will set My face against that man and turn him into a sign and bywords, and cut him off from the midst of My people; then you will know that I am the LORD.

**14:9** And as for the prophet, if he is enticed and speaks a word, I, the LORD, have enticed that prophet; and I will stretch out My hand against him and destroy him from the midst of My people Israel.

**14:10** And they will bear their iniquity; as the iniquity of the inquirer is, so will the iniquity of the prophet be,

**14:11** so that the house of Israel may not wander astray from following Me anymore, nor defile themselves anymore with all their transgressions, but may be My people, and I may be their God, declares the Lord GOD.'"

**14:12** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**14:13** "Son of man, when a land sins against Me by committing treachery, and I stretch out My hand against it, and break its staff of bread, and send famine into it, and cut off from it man and beast,

**14:14** even if these three men—Noah, Daniel, and Job—were in its midst, they by their righteousness would rescue their own lives, declares the Lord GOD.

**14:15** If I cause vicious beasts to pass through the land and they bereave it, so that it becomes a desolation with no one passing through because of the beasts,

**14:16** though these three men were in its midst, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they would rescue neither sons nor daughters; they alone would be rescued, while the land would become a desolation.

**14:17** Or if I bring a sword upon that land and say, 'Let a sword pass through the land,' and I cut off from it man and beast,

**14:18** even though these three men were in its midst, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they would not rescue sons or daughters, but they alone would be rescued.

**14:19** Or if I send pestilence to that land and pour out My fury on it in blood, in order to cut off from it man and beast,

**14:20** even though Noah, Daniel, and Job were in its midst, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they would rescue neither son nor daughter; they by their righteousness would rescue their own lives.

**14:21** For this is what the Lord GOD says: How much more when I send My four disastrous judgments—sword, famine, vicious beasts, and pestilence—against Jerusalem, to cut off from it man and beast!

**14:22** Yet now, survivors will be left in it who will be brought out, sons and daughters; look, they are coming out to you, and you will see their way and their deeds, and you will be consoled over the disaster that I have brought upon Jerusalem—all that I have brought upon it.

**14:23** And they will console you when you see their way and their deeds; and you will know that not without reason have I done all that I have done in it, declares the Lord GOD."

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — The phrase "brought up their idols upon their heart" reflects the Hiphil of alah (lit. "cause to ascend"), portraying deliberate devotion to idolatry. The verb form ha-iddarosh iddaresh pairs a rare Niphal infinitive absolute (with prosthetic aleph assimilated from the following verb) with a tolerative Niphal imperfect of darash, emphasizing "Should I truly allow Myself to be inquired of?"
- **14:4** — Ketiv reads bh ("by it" or "in the matter"), whereas Qere reads ba ("coming," Qal participle of bo), translated here as "who comes." Both v. 4 and v. 7 use the Niphal of anah ("answer, respond") to stress that the LORD Himself will respond directly.
- **14:8** — The unusual verb form vahashimotihu combines the sin consonant of sim ("make, set") with the geminate Hiphil vocalization of shamem ("appall, devastate"), followed by the idiom "into a sign and bywords" (plural meshalim).
- **14:14** — Ketiv writes the name defectively as dn'l ("Danel") here and in v. 20, while Qere reads Daniyyel ("Daniel"). The verb yenatselu ("would rescue") is a rare Piel of natsal, contrasting with the standard Hiphil yatsilu in vv. 16, 18, and 20.
- **14:17** — Because cherev ("sword") is feminine singular, the 3fs verb taavor is translated as a jussive statement, "Let a sword pass through the land," rather than a second-person direct address.
