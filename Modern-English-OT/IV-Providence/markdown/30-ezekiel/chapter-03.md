## Text

**3:1** And He said to me, "Son of man, what you find, eat—eat this scroll, and go speak to the house of Israel."

**3:2** And I opened my mouth, and He made me eat this scroll.

**3:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, feed your stomach and fill your inward parts with this scroll that I am giving you." So I ate it, and it was in my mouth like honey for sweetness.

**3:4** Then He said to me, "Son of man, go, go in to the house of Israel and speak with My words to them.

**3:5** For you are not being sent to a people deep of speech and heavy of tongue, but to the house of Israel—

**3:6** not to many peoples deep of speech and heavy of tongue, whose words you cannot understand. Surely, if I sent you to them, they would listen to you.

**3:7** Yet the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you, because they are not willing to listen to Me; for all the house of Israel are hard of forehead and stubborn of heart.

**3:8** Look, I have made your face hard against their faces, and your forehead hard against their foreheads.

**3:9** Like diamond, harder than flint, I have made your forehead. Do not fear them, and do not be dismayed at their presence, for they are a house of rebellion."

**3:10** And He said to me, "Son of man, all My words that I will speak to you, take into your heart and hear with your ears.

**3:11** And go, go in to the exiles, to the sons of your people, and speak to them and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says,' whether they listen or refrain."

**3:12** Then a spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great rumbling: "Blessed be the glory of the LORD from His place!"

**3:13** And there was the sound of the wings of the living creatures brushing one against another, and the sound of the wheels alongside them, and the sound of a great rumbling.

**3:14** So a spirit lifted me up and took me away; and I went bitter in the fury of my spirit, while the hand of the LORD pressed hard upon me.

**3:15** Then I came to the exiles at Tel Abib, who were dwelling by the Chebar River, and I dwelt where they were dwelling; and I sat there seven days, stunned, in their midst.

**3:16** And it happened at the end of seven days that the word of the LORD came to me, saying:

**3:17** "Son of man, I have appointed you a watchman for the house of Israel; and you shall hear a word from My mouth and warn them from Me.

**3:18** When I say to the wicked man, 'You shall surely die,' and you do not warn him and do not speak to warn the wicked man from his wicked way to keep him alive, that wicked man shall die in his iniquity, but I will require his blood from your hand.

**3:19** Yet if you warn the wicked man, and he does not turn from his wickedness and from his wicked way, he shall die in his iniquity, but you have delivered your life.

**3:20** And when a righteous man turns from his righteousness and commits injustice, and I place a stumbling block before him, he shall die; because you did not warn him, he shall die in his sin, and his righteous deeds that he has done shall not be remembered, but I will require his blood from your hand.

**3:21** Yet if you warn the righteous man that the righteous man should not sin, and he does not sin, he shall surely live because he took warning, and you have delivered your life."

**3:22** And the hand of the LORD came upon me there, and He said to me, "Get up, go out to the valley, and I will speak with you there."

**3:23** So I got up and went out to the valley, and look, the glory of the LORD was standing there, like the glory that I had seen by the Chebar River, and I fell on my face.

**3:24** Then a spirit came into me and stood me on my feet, and He spoke with me and said to me, "Go, shut yourself inside your house.

**3:25** And as for you, son of man, look, they will put cords on you and bind you with them, and you will not go out among them.

**3:26** And I will make your tongue cling to the roof of your mouth, and you will become mute and will not be to them a man who reproves, for they are a house of rebellion.

**3:27** Yet when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Let the one who listens listen, and let the one who refrains refrain,' for they are a house of rebellion."

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — Literally 'deep of lip and heavy of tongue,' a Hebrew idiom for people who speak an obscure, foreign language (cf. Isa 33:19).
- **3:6** — The particle 'im-lo' ('surely') introduces an emphatic statement with implied conditional force: if Ezekiel were sent to foreign nations, they would listen.
- **3:12** — The Masoretic Text reads barukh ('blessed be'), preserved here without textual emendation.
- **3:15** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere (read form) is wa'eshev ('and I dwelt/sat'), translated here as 'and I dwelt where they were dwelling,' whereas the Ketiv (written form) is wa'asher ('and where').
