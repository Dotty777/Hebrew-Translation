## Text

**29:1** In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**29:2** "Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all of Egypt.

**29:3** Speak and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great monster crouching in the midst of his Nile streams, who has said, "Mine is my Nile, and I made it for myself."

**29:4** I will put hooks in your jaws and make the fish of your Nile streams cling to your scales, and I will bring you up from the midst of your Nile streams along with all the fish of your Nile streams that cling to your scales.

**29:5** I will cast you out into the wilderness, you and all the fish of your Nile streams. You will fall on the open field; you will not be gathered and will not be collected. To the beasts of the earth and to the birds of the sky I have given you for food.

**29:6** Then all the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am the LORD, because they have been a reed staff to the house of Israel.

**29:7** When they grasped you with the hand, you splintered and tore open all their shoulders; and when they leaned on you, you broke and brought all their loins to a standstill.

**29:8** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am bringing a sword upon you, and I will cut off from you man and beast.

**29:9** And the land of Egypt will become a desolation and a ruin, and they will know that I am the LORD. Because he said, "The Nile is mine, and I made it,"

**29:10** therefore, look, I am against you and against your Nile streams, and I will make the land of Egypt into ruins of waste and desolation, from Migdol to Syene and as far as the border of Cush.

**29:11** No foot of man will pass through it, and no foot of beast will pass through it, and it will not be inhabited for forty years.

**29:12** I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the midst of desolated lands, and its cities, in the midst of ruined cities, will be a desolation for forty years. And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the lands.

**29:13** For this is what the Lord GOD says: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples where they were scattered.

**29:14** And I will restore the fortunes of Egypt and bring them back to the land of Pathros, to the land of their origin, and there they will be a lowly kingdom.

**29:15** It will be the lowliest of the kingdoms and will never again exalt itself above the nations. And I will diminish them so that they do not rule over the nations.

**29:16** And it will never again be a source of trust for the house of Israel, bringing iniquity to remembrance when they turn after them. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.'"

**29:17** Now in the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**29:18** "Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made his army perform a great labor against Tyre; every head was made bald and every shoulder was rubbed bare, yet there were no wages for him or for his army from Tyre for the labor that he performed against it.

**29:19** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am giving the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will carry off its wealth and seize its spoil and plunder its booty, and it will be wages for his army.

**29:20** As his wages, for which he labored against it, I have given him the land of Egypt, because they acted for Me, declares the Lord GOD.

**29:21** On that day I will cause a horn to sprout for the house of Israel, and to you I will grant an opening of the mouth in their midst; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **29:3** — Hebrew tannim is a singular spelling variant of tannin, referring to a great aquatic monster or chaos dragon. The verb asitini, Qal perfect 1cs with a 1cs suffix, is translated here as a dative suffix meaning made it for myself, parallel to 29:9, though it can also be read reflexively as I made myself.
- **29:4** — Follows the qere hachim, hooks, rather than the ketiv spelling hachayim.
- **29:7** — Follows the qere bakkaf, with the hand, rather than the ketiv bekappekha, by your hand. The final verb wehaamadta is a Hiphil of amad, literally bring to a standstill; many scholars see metathesis for the root maad, cause to shake, as in Psalm 69:24.
- **29:10** — Migdol lay near Egypt's northeastern frontier and Syene, Hebrew Seveneh with a directional suffix meaning to Syene, lay at its southern frontier bordering Cush.
- **29:19** — Hebrew hamon carries both the sense of wealth or abundance, as here and in 30:4, and teeming multitude or horde, as in 30:10, 30:15, 31:2, and 31:18.
