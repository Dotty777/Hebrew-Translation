## Text

**38:1** Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**38:2** "Son of man, direct your face toward Gog, of the land of Magog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and prophesy against him,

**38:3** and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**38:4** I will turn you around and put hooks into your jaws, and I will bring you out along with all your army—horses and horsemen, all of them clothed in splendor, a vast company with large shields and small shields, all of them wielding swords.

**38:5** With them are Persia, Cush, and Put, every one with shield and helmet.

**38:6** Gomer and all its troops, the house of Togarmah of the far reaches of the north and all its troops—many peoples are with you.

**38:7** Be ready and prepare yourself, you and all your company assembled around you, and serve as a guard for them.

**38:8** After many days you will be summoned; in the latter years you will enter a land brought back from the sword, gathered from many peoples, upon the mountains of Israel, which had been a continual ruin; yet it was brought out from the peoples, and all of them dwell securely.

**38:9** You will go up; like a storm you will come; you will be like a cloud to cover the land, you and all your troops, and many peoples with you.

**38:10** This is what the Lord GOD says: It will happen on that day that thoughts will rise in your heart, and you will devise an evil scheme,

**38:11** and you will say, "I will go up against a land of open villages; I will come upon those who are at rest, dwelling securely—all of them dwelling without a wall, and having neither bar nor gates,"

**38:12** to capture spoil and to seize plunder, to turn your hand against ruins now inhabited and against a people gathered from the nations, acquiring livestock and possessions, who dwell at the center of the earth.

**38:13** Sheba and Dedan and the merchants of Tarshish and all its young lions will say to you, "Have you come to capture spoil? Have you assembled your company to seize plunder, to carry off silver and gold, to take livestock and possessions, to capture great spoil?"'

**38:14** Therefore prophesy, son of man, and tell Gog, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: In that day, when My people Israel dwell securely, will you not know?

**38:15** You will come from your place, from the far reaches of the north—you and many peoples with you, every one riding on horses, a great company and a vast army.

**38:16** You will go up against My people Israel like a cloud to cover the land. It will happen in the latter days that I will bring you against My land, so that the nations may know Me when I show Myself holy through you before their eyes, Gog.

**38:17** This is what the Lord GOD says: Are you the one about whom I spoke in earlier days by My servants, the prophets of Israel, who prophesied for years in those days that I would bring you against them?

**38:18** It will happen on that day, the day when Gog comes against the land of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, that My fury will rise in My anger.

**38:19** And in My jealousy, in the fire of My wrath, I have spoken: Surely on that day there will be a great earthquake upon the land of Israel.

**38:20** The fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the beasts of the field, every creeping thing that creeps on the ground, and every human being on the face of the ground will quake at My presence; the mountains will be torn down, the cliffs will fall, and every wall will fall to the earth.

**38:21** I will summon a sword against him throughout all My mountains, declares the Lord GOD; the sword of each man will be against his brother.

**38:22** I will enter into judgment with him by pestilence and bloodshed, and I will rain down torrential rain and hailstones, fire and sulfur, upon him and upon his troops and upon the many peoples who are with him.

**38:23** And I will show Myself great and show Myself holy, and I will make Myself known before the eyes of many nations; then they will know that I am the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — Hebrew nesi rosh (literally "prince of the head") is rendered here as "chief prince," with Meshech and Tubal paired as in 27:13 and 32:26; alternatively, taking rosh as a proper noun yields "prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal."
- **38:12** — Hebrew tabbur literally means "navel," an idiom denoting the center or highest point of the land or earth (cf. Judges 9:37). The 2nd-person masculine singular suffix on yadekha ("your hand") shows that Gog's direct speech ends at v. 11.
- **38:13** — Hebrew kefireha literally means "its young lions," a recurring metaphor in Ezekiel for proud rulers or warriors (cf. 19:3; 32:2).
- **38:18** — Hebrew be'appi literally means "in My nose" or "in My nostrils," a vivid anatomical idiom for burning anger.
