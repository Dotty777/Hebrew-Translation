## Text

**32:1** It happened in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**32:2** "Son of man, raise a lament over Pharaoh king of Egypt and say to him, 'Young lion of the nations, you are undone! You were like a monster in the seas; you burst forth in your rivers, muddied the waters with your feet, and trampled their rivers.

**32:3** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will spread My net over you with an assembly of many peoples, and they will bring you up in My dragnet.

**32:4** I will leave you on the land; on the open field I will hurl you. I will cause all the birds of the sky to settle on you, and I will satisfy the wild beasts of all the earth from you.

**32:5** I will place your flesh upon the mountains and fill the valleys with your towering bulk.

**32:6** I will drench the land with your outflow, with your blood, up to the mountains, and the streambeds will be filled with you.

**32:7** When I extinguish you, I will cover the heavens and darken their stars; I will cover the sun with a cloud, and the moon will not shine its light.

**32:8** All the shining lights in the heavens I will darken over you, and I will set darkness over your land, declares the Lord GOD.

**32:9** I will also trouble the heart of many peoples when I bring your downfall among the nations, to lands that you have not known.

**32:10** I will make many peoples appalled at you, and their kings will shudder in horror over you when I brandish My sword before their faces; they will tremble every moment, each man for his own life, on the day of your fall.

**32:11** For this is what the Lord GOD says: The sword of the king of Babylon will come upon you.

**32:12** By the swords of mighty warriors I will cause your multitude to fall, all of them the most ruthless of the nations; they will ravage the pride of Egypt, and all her multitude will be destroyed.

**32:13** I will also wipe out all her livestock from beside many waters; no human foot will muddy them anymore, nor will the hooves of livestock muddy them.

**32:14** Then I will cause their waters to settle and make their rivers run like oil, declares the Lord GOD.

**32:15** When I make the land of Egypt a desolation, and the land is made desolate of its fullness, when I strike down all who dwell in it, then they will know that I am the LORD.'

**32:16** This is a lament, and they will chant it; the daughters of the nations will chant it. Over Egypt and over all her multitude they will chant it, declares the Lord GOD."

**32:17** It happened in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**32:18** "Son of man, wail over the multitude of Egypt and bring it down, both her and the daughters of mighty nations, to the land below, with those who go down to the pit.

**32:20** In the midst of those killed by the sword they will fall. She is given over to the sword; drag her away and all her multitudes!

**32:21** The leaders of the mighty warriors will speak to him from the midst of Sheol, together with his helpers: 'They have gone down, they lie still, the uncircumcised, killed by the sword.'

**32:22** Assyria is there and all her assembly; her graves are all around her. All of them are killed, those who fell by the sword,

**32:23** whose graves are set in the recesses of the pit, and her assembly is around her grave; all of them are killed, fallen by the sword, who spread terror in the land of the living.

**32:24** Elam is there and all her multitude around her grave; all of them are killed, those who fell by the sword, who went down uncircumcised to the land below, who spread their terror in the land of the living and bore their disgrace with those who go down to the pit.

**32:25** In the midst of those killed they have set a bed for her with all her multitude, with her graves all around it; all of them are uncircumcised, killed by the sword, for their terror was spread in the land of the living, and they bore their disgrace with those who go down to the pit; it is placed in the midst of those killed.

**32:26** Meshech, Tubal, and all their multitude are there, with their graves all around them; all of them are uncircumcised, pierced by the sword, for they spread their terror in the land of the living.

**32:27** And they do not lie with the mighty warriors who fell from among the uncircumcised, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war and placed their swords under their heads, while their iniquities were upon their bones, for the terror of mighty warriors was in the land of the living.

**32:28** And as for you, in the midst of the uncircumcised you will be broken, and you will lie with those killed by the sword.

**32:29** Edom is there, her kings and all her princes, who for all their might were placed with those killed by the sword; they lie with the uncircumcised and with those who go down to the pit.

**32:30** The rulers of the north are there, all of them, and all the Sidonians, who went down with those killed, put to shame despite the terror of their might; they lay down uncircumcised with those killed by the sword and bore their disgrace with those who go down to the pit.

**32:31** Pharaoh will see them and be comforted over all his multitude, Pharaoh and all his army, killed by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.

**32:32** For I have spread My terror in the land of the living; yet he will be laid down in the midst of the uncircumcised with those killed by the sword, Pharaoh and all his multitude, declares the Lord GOD."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**32:19** *(after `32:18`)* Whom do you surpass in loveliness? Go down and be made to lie with the uncircumcised.

## Translator Notes

- **32:2** — The verb nidmeta is taken as Niphal of damah II ("be ruined/undone"), with "young lion of the nations" as vocative, since damah I ("resemble") is unattested elsewhere in Niphal and normally takes a preposition; alternatively, from damah I, "You have likened yourself to a young lion of the nations."
- **32:5** — Hebrew ramuteka is a hapax legomenon from the root rum ("be high"), literally "your height," denoting the monster's towering bulk or massive carcass.
- **32:6** — Hebrew tsafateka is a hapax legomenon from tsuf ("flow/overflow"), construed here as a second object ("your outflow") clarified by "with your blood"; alternatively, a construct phrase, "the land of your swimming."
- **32:27** — Translates the Masoretic Hebrew directly: me'arelim ("from among the uncircumcised") and avonotam ("their iniquities," a plural noun with a preceding 3fs verb).
- **32:32** — Translates the Masoretic Qere chittiti ("My terror"); the written Ketiv reads chittito ("his terror").
