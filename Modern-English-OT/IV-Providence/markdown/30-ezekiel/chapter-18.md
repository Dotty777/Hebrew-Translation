## Text

**18:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**18:2** "What is it to you that you quote this proverb concerning the land of Israel, saying, 'Fathers eat sour grapes, and the teeth of the children are set on edge'?

**18:3** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, you will surely no longer have occasion to quote this proverb in Israel.

**18:4** See, all souls belong to Me; like the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son belongs to Me. The soul that sins is the one that will die.

**18:5** Now if a man is righteous and practices justice and righteousness:

**18:6** if he does not eat at the mountains or lift up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, does not defile his neighbor's wife, and does not approach a woman in her menstrual impurity;

**18:7** if he does not oppress anyone, returns the pledge for a debt, commits no robbery, gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with a garment;

**18:8** if he does not lend at interest or take any increase, holds back his hand from injustice, and executes true justice between one man and another;

**18:9** if he walks in My statutes and keeps My ordinances to act in truth, he is righteous; he will surely live, declares the Lord GOD.

**18:10** Now if he fathers a violent son who sheds blood and who does any one of these things to a brother,

**18:11** though he himself has done none of those things, but has even eaten at the mountains and defiled his neighbor's wife,

**18:12** has oppressed the poor and needy, has committed robberies, does not return a pledge, has lifted up his eyes to the idols, has committed abomination,

**18:13** has lent at interest and taken increase -- will he then live? He will not live! He has committed all these abominations; he will surely be put to death. His blood will be upon him.

**18:14** Now look, if he fathers a son who sees all the sins that his father committed, and he considers them and does not act like them:

**18:15** if he does not eat on the mountains or lift up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, does not defile his neighbor's wife,

**18:16** does not oppress anyone, exacts no pledge, commits no robbery, gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with a garment;

**18:17** holds back his hand from the poor, takes neither interest nor increase, carries out My ordinances, and walks in My statutes -- he will not die for the iniquity of his father; he will surely live.

**18:18** As for his father, because he practiced extortion, committed robbery against a brother, and did what is not good among his people, look, he will die for his own iniquity.

**18:19** Yet you say, 'Why does the son not bear the iniquity of the father?' Because the son has practiced justice and righteousness and has kept all My statutes and carried them out, he will surely live.

**18:20** The soul that sins is the one that will die. A son will not bear the iniquity of the father, and a father will not bear the iniquity of the son; the righteousness of the righteous one will be on him, and the wickedness of the wicked one will be on him.

**18:21** But if the wicked one turns back from all his sins that he has committed and keeps all My statutes and practices justice and righteousness, he will surely live; he will not die.

**18:22** None of the transgressions that he has committed will be remembered against him; by the righteousness that he has practiced, he will live.

**18:23** Do I delight at all in the death of a wicked one, declares the Lord GOD, and not rather that he turn back from his ways and live?

**18:24** But when a righteous one turns away from his righteousness and commits injustice, doing according to all the abominations that the wicked one has committed -- will he then live? None of the righteous deeds that he has done will be remembered; for his treachery that he has committed and for his sin that he has sinned, in them he will die.

**18:25** Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair.' Hear now, house of Israel: is My way not fair? Is it not your ways that are not fair?

**18:26** When a righteous one turns away from his righteousness and commits injustice and dies because of them, it is for his injustice that he has committed that he will die.

**18:27** Again, when a wicked one turns away from his wickedness that he has committed and practices justice and righteousness, he himself will keep his soul alive.

**18:28** Because he considers and turns back from all his transgressions that he has committed, he will surely live; he will not die.

**18:29** Yet the house of Israel says, 'The way of the Lord is not fair.' Are My ways not fair, house of Israel? Is it not your ways that are not fair?

**18:30** Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel, each person according to his ways, declares the Lord GOD. Turn back and turn away from all your transgressions, so that iniquity will not become a stumbling block to you.

**18:31** Throw off from yourselves all the transgressions by which you have transgressed, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit! And why will you die, house of Israel?

**18:32** For I take no delight in the death of anyone who dies, declares the Lord GOD. So turn back and live!"

## Translator Notes

- **18:10** — MT וְעָשָׂה אָח מֵאַחַד מֵאֵלֶּה is syntactically compressed, literally "and does [to] a brother from one of these" (compare אָח in v. 18); some interpreters alternatively take אָח as meaning "the like of" any one of these offenses.
- **18:14** — וַיִּרְאֶה is an unapocopated Qal wayyiqtol of ראה ("see, consider"), appearing again in v. 28; some ancient versions instead read a form of ירא ("fear").
- **18:17** — MT reads מֵעָנִי ("from the poor," i.e., refraining from harming the poor), in contrast to מֵעָוֶל ("from injustice") in v. 8.
- **18:20** — Qere reads definite הָרָשָׁע ("the wicked one," matching definite הַצַּדִּיק, "the righteous one"), whereas ketiv writes anarthrous רשע.
- **18:28** — Qere reads the standard apocopated Qal wayyiqtol וַיָּשָׁב ("and turns back"), whereas ketiv writes the long form וישוב.
