## Text

**36:1** Now you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel and say, "Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the LORD.

**36:2** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because the enemy has said about you, 'Aha! Even the ancient heights have become a possession for us,'"

**36:3** therefore prophesy and say, "This is what the Lord GOD says: Because, yes because, they have made you desolate and crushed you from every side, so that you became a possession for the remnant of the nations, and you were taken up on the lips of talkers and in the slander of the people,

**36:4** therefore, mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD! This is what the Lord GOD says to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys, and to the desolate ruins and to the abandoned cities, which have become plunder and derision for the remnant of the nations that are all around—

**36:5** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Surely in the fire of My jealousy I have spoken against the remnant of the nations and against all Edom, who assigned My land to themselves as a possession with wholehearted gladness and with scorn of soul, so that its open pastureland would become plunder."

**36:6** Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys, "This is what the Lord GOD says: See, in My jealousy and in My wrath I have spoken, because you have borne the disgrace of the nations.

**36:7** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I Myself have lifted up My hand: surely the nations that are all around you—they themselves will bear their own disgrace.

**36:8** But you, mountains of Israel, will put forth your branches and bear your fruit for My people Israel, for they are near to coming.

**36:9** For look, I am for you, and I will turn to you, and you will be cultivated and sown.

**36:10** And I will multiply people upon you, the whole house of Israel, all of it; and the cities will be inhabited, and the ruins will be rebuilt.

**36:11** And I will multiply people and livestock upon you, and they will grow numerous and be fruitful; and I will cause you to be inhabited as in your former times, and I will do better for you than at your beginnings. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**36:12** And I will cause people—My people Israel—to walk upon you, and they will possess you, and you will become an inheritance for them, and you will never again bereave them of children."

**36:13** This is what the Lord GOD says: "Because they say to you, 'You are a devourer of people, and you have been one who bereaves your nations,'

**36:14** therefore you will no longer devour people, and you will no longer bereave your nations, declares the Lord GOD.

**36:15** And I will no longer let you hear the disgrace of the nations, and you will no longer bear the reproach of peoples, and you will no longer cause your nations to stumble, declares the Lord GOD."

**36:16** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**36:17** "Son of man, when the house of Israel were living on their own land, they defiled it by their way and by their deeds; their way before Me was like the impurity of menstrual separation.

**36:18** So I poured out My wrath upon them for the blood that they had poured out upon the land, and because they defiled it with their idols.

**36:19** And I scattered them among the nations, and they were dispersed through the lands; according to their way and according to their deeds I judged them.

**36:20** And when they came to the nations where they came, they profaned My holy name, in that it was said about them, 'These are the people of the LORD, yet they have gone out from His land.'

**36:21** But I had concern for My holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned among the nations where they had come.

**36:22** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: It is not for your sake that I am acting, house of Israel, but rather for My holy name, which you have profaned among the nations where you came.

**36:23** And I will sanctify My great name, which has been profaned among the nations, which you have profaned in their midst. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, declares the Lord GOD, when I show Myself holy through you before their eyes.

**36:24** And I will take you from the nations, and I will gather you from all the countries, and I will bring you to your own soil.

**36:25** And I will sprinkle clean water upon you, and you will become clean; from all your impurities and from all your idols I will cleanse you.

**36:26** And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will place inside you; I will take away the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh.

**36:27** And My Spirit I will place inside you, and I will make you walk in My statutes, and you will keep My ordinances and do them.

**36:28** And you will dwell in the land that I gave to your fathers; you will be a people to Me, and I Myself will be God to you.

**36:29** And I will deliver you from all your impurities; and I will call to the grain and multiply it, and I will not lay famine upon you.

**36:30** And I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the produce of the field, so that you will never again receive the reproach of famine among the nations.

**36:31** Then you will remember your evil ways and your deeds that were not good, and you will loathe yourselves in your own sight because of your iniquities and because of your abominations.

**36:32** It is not for your sake that I am acting, declares the Lord GOD—let it be known to you. Be ashamed and disgraced because of your ways, house of Israel.'"

**36:33** This is what the Lord GOD says: "On the day that I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will cause the cities to be inhabited, and the ruins will be rebuilt.

**36:34** And the desolate land will be cultivated, instead of being a desolation in the sight of everyone who passed by.

**36:35** And they will say, 'This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden, and the ruined, desolate, and demolished cities are fortified and inhabited.'

**36:36** Then the nations that remain all around you will know that I, the LORD, have rebuilt the demolished places, have planted the desolate place; I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it."

**36:37** This is what the Lord GOD says: "This also I will let Myself be inquired of by the house of Israel to do for them: I will multiply them with people like a flock.

**36:38** Like the flock for holy offerings, like the flock of Jerusalem during her appointed feasts, so the ruined cities will be full of flocks of people. Then they will know that I am the LORD."

## Translator Notes

- **36:3** — The forms shammot (an unusual infinitive construct of shamam used transitively, making desolate) and sha'oph (Qal infinitive absolute of sha'aph, crushing or trampling) function together with an indefinite subject; literally "upon the lip of a tongue" is a Hebrew idiom for gossip or slanderous talk.
- **36:5** — Literally "in order that its open pastureland for plunder," with an implied verb; the Masoretic vocalization migrashah points the word as the noun migrash (open or pastureland) with a feminine suffix, though some construe it as an Aramaizing infinitive of garash (to expel or empty).
- **36:13** — Here and in vv. 14-15, the Qere reads plural goyayikh ("your nations"), while the Ketiv reads singular goyekh ("your nation"); in v. 13 the Qere also reads standard feminine singular at ("you") for the archaic Ketiv spelling atti.
- **36:14** — The Qere reads teshakkeli (Piel of shakhal, "bereave"), matching vv. 12-13, whereas the Ketiv reads takhshili (from kashal, "cause to stumble"), matching the Hiphil verb takhshili in v. 15.
