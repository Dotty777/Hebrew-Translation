## Text

**41:1** Then he brought me to the temple hall and measured the jambs, six cubits wide on one side and six cubits wide on the other side, the width of the tent.

**41:2** The width of the entrance was ten cubits, and the side walls of the entrance were five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other side. He also measured its length, forty cubits, and the width, twenty cubits.

**41:3** Then he went inside and measured the jamb of the entrance, two cubits, and the entrance, six cubits, and the width of the entrance, seven cubits.

**41:4** He measured its length, twenty cubits, and the width, twenty cubits, before the temple hall; and he said to me, "This is the Most Holy Place."

**41:5** Then he measured the wall of the house, six cubits, and the width of the side room, four cubits, all around the house on every side.

**41:6** Now the side rooms were side room over side room, thirty-three times; and they extended into the wall that belonged to the house for the side rooms all around so as to be fastened, yet they were not fastened in the wall of the house.

**41:7** It broadened and wound around higher and higher for the side rooms, for the surrounding structure of the house went higher and higher all around the house; therefore the house had greater width upward, and so one went up from the lowest story to the highest by way of the middle.

**41:8** And I saw an elevation for the house all around; the foundations of the side rooms were a full reed, six cubits to the joint.

**41:9** The width of the wall belonging to the side room on the outside was five cubits, and the open space was beside the side-room structure belonging to the house.

**41:10** And between the chambers was a width of twenty cubits around the house on every side.

**41:11** The entrance of the side room was toward the open space, one entrance toward the north and one entrance toward the south; and the width of the area of the open space was five cubits all around.

**41:12** Now the building that was in front of the separated space on the side toward the west was seventy cubits wide; the wall of the building was five cubits wide all around, and its length was ninety cubits.

**41:13** Then he measured the house, a hundred cubits in length; and the separated space and the building and its walls were a hundred cubits in length.

**41:14** And the width of the front of the house and of the separated space toward the east was a hundred cubits.

**41:15** He measured the length of the building in front of the separated space that was behind it, together with its galleries on one side and on the other side, a hundred cubits. Now the inner temple hall and the porticoes of the court,

**41:16** the thresholds, the narrowed windows, and the galleries around all three of them, opposite the threshold, were paneled with wood all around, from the floor up to the windows—and the windows were covered—

**41:17** to the space above the entrance, and even to the inner house and on the outside, and on the entire wall all around, inside and outside, by measurements.

**41:18** It was made with cherubim and palm trees, a palm tree between cherub and cherub, and each cherub had two faces.

**41:19** The face of a man was toward the palm tree on one side, and the face of a young lion was toward the palm tree on the other side; it was made on the entire house all around.

**41:20** From the floor up to above the entrance, the cherubim and the palm trees were made, and on the wall of the temple hall.

**41:21** The temple hall had a square doorpost, and as for the front of the sanctuary, the appearance was the same.

**41:22** The altar was of wood, three cubits high, and its length was two cubits; its corners belonged to it, and its length and its walls were of wood. And he spoke to me, "This is the table that is before the LORD."

**41:23** The temple hall and the sanctuary each had two doors.

**41:24** And the doors had two leaves each, two swinging leaves: two for one door and two leaves for the other.

**41:25** And on them, on the doors of the temple hall, cherubim and palm trees were made, just as they were made on the walls; and there was a wooden overhang in front of the portico on the outside.

**41:26** And there were narrowed windows and palm trees on one side and on the other side, on the side walls of the portico, as well as the side rooms of the house and the overhangs.

## Translator Notes

- **41:1** — The Masoretic Text literally reads "the width of the tent," an unusual phrase echoing tabernacle language; the Septuagint diverges here.
- **41:6** — Hebrew literally reads "three and thirty times"; in light of the three stories in 41:7 this is often understood as three stories of thirty side rooms each.
- **41:8** — The Qere reads "foundations" (musdot), whereas the Ketiv spells the word with yod instead of waw. The rare term atsilah literally means "to the joint," often understood as six long cubits or up to the joining point of the side rooms.
- **41:15** — The Qere reads "and its galleries" (we-attiqeha, spelled with yod to match 41:16), whereas the Ketiv spells the stem with waw.
- **41:20** — The Masoretic Text places extraordinary scribal dots (puncta extraordinaria) over the final word "the temple hall" (hahekal), marking textual uncertainty, likely because 41:21 immediately begins with the same word.
