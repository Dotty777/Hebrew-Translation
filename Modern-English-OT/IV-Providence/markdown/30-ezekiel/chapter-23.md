## Text

**23:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**23:2** "Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother.

**23:3** They prostituted themselves in Egypt; in their youth they prostituted themselves. There their breasts were squeezed, and there their virgin bosoms were fondled.

**23:4** Their names were Oholah the older and Oholibah her sister. They became Mine, and they bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem is Oholibah.

**23:5** Oholah prostituted herself while under My authority, and she lusted for her lovers, for the Assyrians who were nearby,

**23:6** dressed in blue, governors and officials, desirable young men, all of them, horsemen riding on horses.

**23:7** She bestowed her acts of prostitution upon them, the choicest of the sons of Assyria, all of them; and with everyone for whom she lusted, she defiled herself with all their idols.

**23:8** Nor did she abandon her acts of prostitution from Egypt, for they had lain with her in her youth, and they had fondled her virgin bosoms and poured out their prostitution upon her.

**23:9** Therefore I gave her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the sons of Assyria for whom she lusted.

**23:10** They uncovered her nakedness; they took her sons and her daughters, and they killed her with the sword. So she became a byword among women, and they carried out judgments against her.

**23:11** Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet she corrupted her lust more than she did, and her acts of prostitution more than the prostitution of her sister.

**23:12** She lusted for the sons of Assyria, governors and officials who were nearby, dressed in full splendor, horsemen riding on horses, desirable young men, all of them.

**23:13** And I saw that she had defiled herself; there was one way for both of them.

**23:14** Yet she added to her acts of prostitution: she saw men portrayed on the wall, images of Chaldeans drawn in vermilion,

**23:15** belted with sashes around their waists, with flowing turbans on their heads, all of them having the appearance of officers—the likeness of the sons of Babylon, Chaldea being the land of their birth.

**23:16** She lusted for them at the sight of her eyes, and she sent messengers to them, to Chaldea.

**23:17** So the sons of Babylon came to her to the bed of love, and they defiled her with their prostitution; she became defiled by them, and then her soul grew estranged from them.

**23:18** She uncovered her acts of prostitution and uncovered her nakedness; so My soul grew estranged from her, just as My soul had grown estranged from her sister.

**23:19** Yet she multiplied her acts of prostitution, recalling the days of her youth, when she prostituted herself in the land of Egypt.

**23:20** And she lusted for their paramours, whose flesh was the flesh of donkeys and whose emission was the emission of horses.

**23:21** So you yearned for the lewdness of your youth, when those from Egypt fondled your bosoms on account of your youthful breasts.

**23:22** Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord GOD says: 'Look, I am stirring up your lovers against you, those from whom your soul grew estranged, and I will bring them against you from every side:

**23:23** the sons of Babylon and all the Chaldeans, Pekod and Shoa and Koa, all the sons of Assyria with them—desirable young men, governors and officials, all of them, officers and men of renown, riding on horses, all of them.

**23:24** They will come against you with weapons, chariots, and wagons, and with an assembly of peoples. They will array large shield, small shield, and helmet against you all around; and I will place judgment before them, and they will judge you according to their judgments.

**23:25** I will set My jealousy against you, and they will deal with you in fury. They will remove your nose and your ears, and your remnant will fall by the sword. They will take your sons and your daughters, and your remnant will be consumed by fire.

**23:26** They will also strip you of your garments and take your ornaments of splendor.

**23:27** So I will make your lewdness cease from you, along with your prostitution from the land of Egypt; you will not lift up your eyes to them, nor will you remember Egypt anymore.'

**23:28** For this is what the Lord GOD says: 'Look, I am giving you into the hand of those whom you hate, into the hand of those from whom your soul grew estranged.

**23:29** They will deal with you in hatred, take all you have labored for, and leave you naked and bare. And the nakedness of your prostitution will be uncovered, along with your lewdness and your acts of prostitution.

**23:30** These things will be done to you for prostituting yourself after the nations, because you defiled yourself with their idols.

**23:31** You have walked in the way of your sister; therefore I will place her cup into your hand.'

**23:32** This is what the Lord GOD says: 'You will drink your sister's cup, the deep and wide one; you will become an object of laughter and derision—so much does it hold.

**23:33** With drunkenness and grief you will be filled—a cup of horror and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria.

**23:34** You will drink it and drain it dry, and you will gnaw its potsherds and tear your breasts; for I have spoken,' declares the Lord GOD.

**23:35** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: 'Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back, you too bear your lewdness and your acts of prostitution.'"

**23:36** Then the LORD said to me, "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their abominations.

**23:37** For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. With their idols they have committed adultery, and even their sons whom they bore to Me they caused to pass over to them as food.

**23:38** Moreover, they did this to Me: they defiled My sanctuary on that very day, and they profaned My Sabbaths.

**23:39** When they had slaughtered their sons for their idols, they came into My sanctuary on that very day to profane it. And look, this is what they did in the midst of My house.

**23:40** Furthermore, they even sent for men coming from afar, to whom a messenger was sent, and look, they came! For them you bathed, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with ornaments.

**23:41** You sat on a stately couch with a table set before it, and you placed My incense and My oil on it.

**23:42** The sound of a carefree crowd was with her; and in addition to men from the masses of people, Sabeans were brought from the wilderness, and they put bracelets on their hands and a crown of splendor on their heads.

**23:43** Then I said concerning the one worn out by adulteries, 'Now they will practice her prostitution—even she!'

**23:44** So men went in to her, as one goes in to a prostitute; in the same way they went in to Oholah and to Oholibah, the women of lewdness.

**23:45** But righteous men—they will judge them with the judgment of adulteresses and the judgment of women who shed blood, because they are adulteresses and blood is on their hands.

**23:46** For this is what the Lord GOD says: 'Bring up an assembly against them, and give them over to terror and plunder.

**23:47** The assembly will stone them with stones and cut them down with their swords; they will kill their sons and their daughters, and burn their houses with fire.

**23:48** So I will make lewdness cease from the land, and all the women will take warning and will not act according to your lewdness.

**23:49** They will lay your lewdness upon you, and you will bear the sins of your idols; and you will know that I am the Lord GOD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — MT qerovim (also v. 12) is the plural of qarov ("near, nearby"), referring to Assyrian officials stationed in neighboring territories; some scholars instead link it to Aramaic or Akkadian roots meaning "warriors." Hebrew tachtai literally means "under Me," an idiom for being under a husband's authority.
- **23:21** — Follows the Masoretic text directly: ba'asot (Qal infinitive construct of 'asah II, "handle, fondle," cf. Piel in vv. 3, 8) with partitive mi-Mitsrayim ("those from Egypt") and lema'an ("on account of").
- **23:24** — Hebrew hotsen occurs only here in the Bible and is traditionally understood as "weapons" or "armaments."
- **23:42** — Follows the Qere ("Sabeans"); the Ketiv reads sove'im (Qal participle of sava', "drunkards").
- **23:43** — Follows the Qere 'attah yiznu ("now they will practice prostitution," Qal imperfect 3mp of zanah); the Ketiv reads 'et ("time") and yizneh (3ms). The terse final word vehi literally reads "and she."
