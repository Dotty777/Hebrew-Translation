## Text

**19:1** "And you, raise a lamentation over the princes of Israel,

**19:2** and say, 'What a lioness was your mother! Among lions she lay down; in the midst of young lions she raised her cubs.

**19:3** She brought up one of her cubs, and he became a young lion. He learned to tear prey; he devoured people.

**19:4** Then nations heard about him; in their pit he was caught, and they brought him with hooks to the land of Egypt.

**19:5** When she saw that she had waited in vain, that her hope had perished, she took another of her cubs and made him a young lion.

**19:6** He walked about among lions; he became a young lion. He learned to tear prey; he devoured people.

**19:7** He violated their widows and laid waste their cities, and the land and its fullness became desolate at the sound of his roaring.

**19:8** Then nations set themselves against him from the provinces all around; they spread their net over him, and in their pit he was caught.

**19:9** With hooks they put him into a cage and brought him to the king of Babylon; they brought him into strongholds, so that his voice would no longer be heard on the mountains of Israel.

**19:10** Your mother was like a vine in your blood, planted beside waters; she became fruitful and full of branches because of abundant waters.

**19:11** And she had strong branches for scepters of rulers; its stature grew high among the thick boughs, and it stood out in its height with the abundance of its branches.

**19:12** But she was uprooted in fury and thrown down to the ground, and the east wind dried up her fruit; her strong branch was broken off and withered, and fire consumed it.

**19:13** And now she is planted in the wilderness, in a land of drought and thirst.

**19:14** And fire has gone out from a branch of her shoots and consumed her fruit, so that no strong branch remains in her, no scepter for ruling.' This is a lamentation, and it has become a lamentation."

## Translator Notes

- **19:5** — נוֹחֲלָה is a Niphal perfect 3fs of יחל ("wait, hope"), denoting prolonged waiting in vain, parallel to אָבְדָה תִּקְוָתָהּ ("her hope had perished").
- **19:7** — MT וַיֵּדַע אַלְמְנוֹתָיו literally reads "he knew [i.e., violated] his/their widows" (with 3ms collective suffix referring to אָדָם in v. 6); many compare אַלְמְנוֹתָיו to the l/r by-form of אַרְמְנוֹתָיו ("citadels, fortresses") in Isa 13:22, parallel to "their cities."
- **19:9** — סוּגַר ("cage") is a hapax legomenon, cognate with Akkadian shigaru, denoting a wooden neck-stock, collar, or cage used to confine wild beasts or royal captives.
- **19:10** — MT reads בְּדָמְךָ ("in your blood," possibly meaning "in your bloodline" or "in your vigor"); a conjectural emendation reads בְּכַרְמְךָ ("in your vineyard").
