## Text

**48:1** Now these are the names of the tribes: From the northern end, alongside the road of Hethlon to Lebo-hamath, Hazar-enan, the boundary of Damascus northward alongside Hamath, the eastern side and the west shall belong to him: Dan, one share.

**48:2** And along the boundary of Dan, from the eastern side to the western side: Asher, one share.

**48:3** And along the boundary of Asher, from the eastern side to the western side: Naphtali, one share.

**48:4** And along the boundary of Naphtali, from the eastern side to the western side: Manasseh, one share.

**48:5** And along the boundary of Manasseh, from the eastern side to the western side: Ephraim, one share.

**48:6** And along the boundary of Ephraim, from the eastern side to the western side: Reuben, one share.

**48:7** And along the boundary of Reuben, from the eastern side to the western side: Judah, one share.

**48:8** And along the boundary of Judah, from the eastern side to the western side, shall be the contribution that you are to set apart: twenty-five thousand in width, and in length like one of the shares, from the eastern side to the western side; and the sanctuary shall be in the middle of it.

**48:9** The contribution that you are to set apart to the LORD shall be twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width.

**48:10** And to these shall belong the holy contribution, to the priests: on the north twenty-five thousand, and on the west ten thousand in width, and on the east ten thousand in width, and on the south twenty-five thousand in length; and the sanctuary of the LORD shall be in the middle of it.

**48:11** This shall belong to the consecrated priests from the sons of Zadok, who kept My charge, who did not stray when the sons of Israel strayed, just as the Levites strayed.

**48:12** And it shall belong to them as a dedicated contribution from the contribution of the land, most holy, next to the boundary of the Levites.

**48:13** And parallel to the boundary of the priests, the Levites shall have twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width; the entire length shall be twenty-five thousand, and the width ten thousand.

**48:14** And they shall not sell any of it, nor shall anyone exchange it, nor shall anyone transfer the best of the land, for it is holy to the LORD.

**48:15** And the five thousand remaining in width, fronting the twenty-five thousand, shall be unconsecrated, for the city, for dwellings and for open land; and the city shall be in the middle of it.

**48:16** And these are its measurements: the northern side, four thousand five hundred; and the southern side, four thousand five hundred; and on the eastern side, four thousand five hundred; and the western side, four thousand five hundred.

**48:17** And the city shall have open land: on the north, two hundred fifty; on the south, two hundred fifty; on the east, two hundred fifty; and on the west, two hundred fifty.

**48:18** And what remains in length, parallel to the holy contribution, shall be ten thousand to the east and ten thousand to the west, and it shall be parallel to the holy contribution; and its produce shall be for food for those who serve the city.

**48:19** And those from all the tribes of Israel who serve the city shall work it.

**48:20** The entire contribution shall be twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand; as a square you shall set apart the holy contribution along with the possession of the city.

**48:21** And what remains shall belong to the prince, on both sides of the holy contribution and of the possession of the city, fronting the twenty-five thousand of the contribution as far as the eastern boundary, and on the west fronting the twenty-five thousand to the western boundary, parallel to the shares; it shall belong to the prince. And the holy contribution and the sanctuary of the house shall be in the middle of it.

**48:22** And apart from the possession of the Levites and the possession of the city, which shall be in the middle of what belongs to the prince, what lies between the boundary of Judah and the boundary of Benjamin shall belong to the prince.

**48:23** And the remaining tribes: from the eastern side to the western side, Benjamin, one share.

**48:24** And along the boundary of Benjamin, from the eastern side to the western side: Simeon, one share.

**48:25** And along the boundary of Simeon, from the eastern side to the western side: Issachar, one share.

**48:26** And along the boundary of Issachar, from the eastern side to the western side: Zebulun, one share.

**48:27** And along the boundary of Zebulun, from the eastern side to the western side: Gad, one share.

**48:28** And along the boundary of Gad, on the southern side southward, the boundary shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, to the brook, to the Great Sea.

**48:29** This is the land that you shall assign by lot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these are their divisions, declares the Lord GOD.

**48:30** And these are the exits of the city: on the northern side, four thousand five hundred in measurement.

**48:31** And the gates of the city shall bear the names of the tribes of Israel, three gates on the north: the gate of Reuben, one; the gate of Judah, one; the gate of Levi, one.

**48:32** And on the eastern side, four thousand five hundred, and three gates: the gate of Joseph, one; the gate of Benjamin, one; the gate of Dan, one.

**48:33** And on the southern side, four thousand five hundred in measurement, and three gates: the gate of Simeon, one; the gate of Issachar, one; the gate of Zebulun, one.

**48:34** On the western side, four thousand five hundred; their gates are three: the gate of Gad, one; the gate of Asher, one; the gate of Naphtali, one.

**48:35** All around, it shall be eighteen thousand; and from that day onward the name of the city shall be "The LORD Is There."

## Translator Notes

- **48:8** — The Hebrew text omits the unit of measurement throughout chapter 48; the figures are traditionally understood as cubits.
- **48:11** — Hebrew uses a collective singular Pual participle, literally 'the consecrated one,' in apposition to the plural 'priests.'
- **48:14** — Translates the qere (Hiphil imperfect, 'transfer'); the ketiv reads the Qal imperfect ('pass over'), making 'the best of the land' the grammatical subject.
- **48:16** — The Masoretic Text contains a written dittography of 'five' in the southern measurement that is marked as ketiv velo qere (written, not read) and therefore omitted in translation.
- **48:28** — The Hebrew form rendered 'to the brook' is pointed like the noun 'inheritance,' though in this southern border formula it represents 'brook/wadi' with a directional ending, referring to the Wadi of Egypt.
