## Text

**28:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**28:2** "Son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Because your heart has grown lofty and you have said, "I am a god; I sit in the seat of the gods in the heart of the seas," yet you are a man and not a god, though you set your heart like the heart of a god—

**28:3** look, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret thing is hidden from you.

**28:4** By your wisdom and your understanding you have gained wealth for yourself, and you have gained gold and silver in your treasuries.

**28:5** By the abundance of your wisdom in your trade you have multiplied your wealth, and your heart has grown lofty because of your wealth.

**28:6** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have set your heart like the heart of a god,

**28:7** therefore, look, I am bringing foreigners against you, the most ruthless of the nations; they will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and defile your radiance.

**28:8** They will bring you down to the pit, and you will die the death of those pierced through in the heart of the seas.

**28:9** Will you really say, "I am a god," in the presence of the one who kills you? Yet you are a man and not a god in the hand of those who pierce you.

**28:10** You will die the death of the uncircumcised by the hand of foreigners, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.'"

**28:11** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**28:12** "Son of man, raise a lamentation over the king of Tyre, and say to him, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: You set the seal on perfection, full of wisdom and complete in beauty.

**28:13** You were in Eden, the garden of God; every precious stone was your covering: carnelian, topaz, and diamond, beryl, onyx, and jasper, lapis lazuli, turquoise, and emerald, and gold. The craftsmanship of your tambourines and your pipes was in you; on the day you were created they were prepared.

**28:14** You were an anointed, overshadowing cherub, and I appointed you; you were on the holy mountain of God; you walked about in the midst of stones of fire.

**28:15** You were blameless in your ways from the day you were created, until wrongdoing was found in you.

**28:16** In the abundance of your trade your midst was filled with violence, and you sinned; so I drove you out as profane from the mountain of God, and I destroyed you, overshadowing cherub, from the midst of stones of fire.

**28:17** Your heart grew lofty because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom on account of your radiance. I hurled you to the ground; I set you before kings so that they might gaze upon you.

**28:18** By the abundance of your iniquities, in the wrongdoing of your trade, you defiled your sanctuaries. So I brought fire out from your midst; it consumed you, and I turned you to ashes upon the earth before the eyes of all who see you.

**28:19** All who know you among the peoples are appalled at you; you have become a terror, and you will be no more forever.'"

**28:20** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**28:21** "Son of man, set your face toward Sidon and prophesy against her,

**28:22** and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Sidon, and I will be glorified in your midst. Then they will know that I am the LORD when I execute judgments in her and show Myself holy in her.

**28:23** And I will send pestilence into her and blood into her streets, and the pierced will fall in her midst by the sword against her on every side; and they will know that I am the LORD.

**28:24** And for the house of Israel there will no longer be a stinging brier or a painful thorn from any of those around them who treat them with contempt; and they will know that I am the Lord GOD.'"

**28:25** This is what the Lord GOD says: "When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they were scattered, and show Myself holy in them before the eyes of the nations, then they will dwell on their own land which I gave to My servant Jacob.

**28:26** And they will dwell on it securely; they will build houses and plant vineyards, and they will dwell securely when I execute judgments upon all those around them who treat them with contempt; and they will know that I am the LORD their God."

## Translator Notes

- **28:3** — Ketiv mdnʾl spells the name defectively ("Danel"), while Qere middānîyyēʾl reads the standard form "Daniel"; the translation follows the Qere.
- **28:12** — MT ḥōtēm tāknît uses the Qal active participle of ḥātam ("sealing up") with tāknît ("proportion, measurement," cf. 43:10), conveying "one who sets the seal on perfection"; many also take ḥōtēm as a construct form of ḥōtām ("seal/signet of perfection").
- **28:13** — The phrase tuppêkā ûnĕqābêkā literally refers to "your tambourines" (tōp) and "your pipes" or "perforations" (neqeb, a hapax common noun from nāqab, "to pierce"), though in proximity to gold and gemstones it is often interpreted as jewelry "settings and sockets."
- **28:14** — MT reads ʾatt-kĕrûb mimšaḥ hassōkēk ("You were an anointed, overshadowing cherub"), using the defective spelling ʾatt for the masculine singular pronoun "you" (cf. Num 11:15; Deut 5:27) rather than the preposition "with" (ʾēt); the hapax noun mimšaḥ is usually derived from māšaḥ ("anoint"), though some connect it to an Aramaic cognate meaning "outstretched."
- **28:16** — MT wāʾabbedkā is a Piel first-person singular wayyiqtol of ʾābad ("and I destroyed you") with contracted initial ʾālep, addressing the cherub directly.
