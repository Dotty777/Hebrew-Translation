## Text

**45:1** "And when you divide the land by lot for an inheritance, you shall set apart a contribution to the LORD, a holy portion of the land: its length shall be twenty-five thousand, and its width ten thousand; it shall be holy throughout its entire border all around.

**45:2** Of this there shall be for the sanctuary five hundred by five hundred, square all around, with fifty cubits of open space for it all around.

**45:3** From this measured area you shall measure a length of twenty-five thousand and a width of ten thousand, and within it shall be the sanctuary, the most holy place.

**45:4** It shall be a holy portion of the land for the priests, the ministers of the sanctuary, who draw near to minister to the LORD; it shall serve them as a place for houses and as a sacred site for the sanctuary.

**45:5** And twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width shall belong to the Levites, the ministers of the house, for themselves as a possession, twenty chambers.

**45:6** As the possession of the city you shall assign five thousand in width and twenty-five thousand in length, alongside the sacred contribution; it shall belong to the entire house of Israel.

**45:7** And the prince shall have land on this side and on that side of the sacred contribution and of the possession of the city, fronting the sacred contribution and fronting the possession of the city, from the western side westward and from the eastern side eastward, with a length corresponding to one of the portions, from the western border to the eastern border.

**45:8** This land shall be his as a possession in Israel; and My princes shall no longer oppress My people, but they shall give the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes."

**45:9** This is what the Lord GOD says: "Enough for you, princes of Israel! Put away violence and devastation, and practice justice and righteousness. Lift your evictions off My people," declares the Lord GOD.

**45:10** "You shall have honest scales, an honest ephah, and an honest bath.

**45:11** The ephah and the bath shall have a single standard measure, so that the bath holds a tenth of a homer and the ephah a tenth of a homer; its standard measure shall be regulated by the homer.

**45:12** The shekel shall be twenty gerahs; twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels, and fifteen shekels shall be your mina.

**45:13** This is the contribution that you shall set apart: a sixth of an ephah from a homer of wheat, and you shall give a sixth of an ephah from a homer of barley.

**45:14** And the prescribed portion of oil, by the bath of oil, shall be a tenth of a bath from each kor; ten baths are a homer, for ten baths make a homer.

**45:15** And one sheep from the flock out of every two hundred, from the watered pastures of Israel, shall be for a grain offering, a burnt offering, and peace offerings, to make atonement for them," declares the Lord GOD.

**45:16** "All the people of the land shall share in this contribution for the prince in Israel.

**45:17** And the prince shall be responsible for the burnt offerings, the grain offering, and the drink offering at the festivals, on the new moons, and on the Sabbaths, at all the appointed times of the house of Israel; he himself shall prepare the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make atonement on behalf of the house of Israel."

**45:18** This is what the Lord GOD says: "In the first month, on the first day of the month, you shall take a young bull without blemish and purify the sanctuary.

**45:19** The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the house, on the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and on the posts of the gate of the inner court.

**45:20** You shall do the same on the seventh day of the month on account of anyone who errs unintentionally or through ignorance; thus you shall make atonement for the house.

**45:21** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, you shall have the Passover, a festival of seven days; unleavened bread shall be eaten.

**45:22** On that day the prince shall prepare for himself and for all the people of the land a bull as a sin offering.

**45:23** During the seven days of the festival he shall prepare as a burnt offering to the LORD seven bulls and seven rams without blemish daily for the seven days, and a male goat daily as a sin offering.

**45:24** And he shall prepare as a grain offering an ephah for each bull and an ephah for each ram, along with a hin of oil for each ephah.

**45:25** In the seventh month, on the fifteenth day of the month, during the festival, he shall prepare the same for the seven days: the same sin offering, burnt offering, grain offering, and oil."

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — The unit of length is omitted in the Hebrew text here and in verses 3, 5, and 6 (likely cubits by comparison with 45:2); the noun "length" is also repeated resumptively around the numeral twenty-five thousand.
- **45:3** — The qere reads the masculine numeral "chamishah" ("five", matching the standard construction with "thousand"), whereas the ketiv writes the feminine form "chamesh".
- **45:5** — The qere reads the waw-consecutive Qal perfect "vehayah" ("shall belong"), whereas the ketiv reads the imperfect "yihyeh". The Masoretic Text explicitly reads "twenty chambers" ("esrim leshakot").
- **45:12** — The Masoretic Text lists three sub-weights totaling sixty shekels (20 + 25 + 15) for the mina, reflecting a sexagesimal weight standard.
- **45:21** — The Hebrew reads "shevu'ot yamim" (vocalized as plural of "shavua", literally "weeks of days"), functioning here in context as a seven-day period, matching "the seven days of the festival" in verse 23.
