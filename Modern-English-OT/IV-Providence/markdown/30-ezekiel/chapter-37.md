## Text

**37:1** The hand of the LORD came upon me, and He led me out by the Spirit of the LORD and placed me in the midst of the valley, and it was full of bones.

**37:2** And He caused me to pass among them round and round, and look, there were very many on the surface of the valley, and look, they were very dry.

**37:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, will these bones live?" And I said, "Lord GOD, You know."

**37:4** Then He said to me, "Prophesy over these bones and say to them, 'Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD.

**37:5** This is what the Lord GOD says to these bones: Look, I am about to bring breath into you, and you will live.

**37:6** And I will lay sinews upon you, and bring up flesh upon you, and spread skin over you, and put breath in you, and you will live; and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

**37:7** So I prophesied just as I had been commanded; and as I was prophesying, there was a sound, and look, a rattling, and the bones drew near, bone to its bone.

**37:8** And I looked, and see, sinews were on them, and flesh had come up, and skin had spread over them on top, yet there was no breath in them.

**37:9** Then He said to me, "Prophesy to the breath; prophesy, son of man, and say to the breath, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: From the four winds come, breath, and breathe into these who were killed, so that they may live.'"

**37:10** So I prophesied just as He commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they came to life and stood on their feet, a very, very great army.

**37:11** Then He said to me, "Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel. Look, they say, 'Our bones have dried up, and our hope has perished; we are completely cut off.'

**37:12** Therefore prophesy and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am about to open your graves and bring you up out of your graves, My people, and I will bring you to the land of Israel.

**37:13** And you will know that I am the LORD when I open your graves and when I bring you up out of your graves, My people.

**37:14** And I will place My Spirit in you, and you will live, and I will settle you upon your own soil. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken and have done it, declares the LORD.'"

**37:15** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**37:16** "Now you, son of man, take for yourself one stick and write on it, 'For Judah and for the sons of Israel, his companions'; then take one stick and write on it, 'For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and all the house of Israel, his companions.'

**37:17** And join them together, one to another, for yourself into one stick, so that they become one in your hand.

**37:18** And when the sons of your people speak to you, saying, 'Will you not tell us what these mean to you?'

**37:19** speak to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am about to take the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel, his companions, and I will place them with it, with the stick of Judah, and make them into one stick, and they will be one in My hand.'

**37:20** And the sticks on which you write will be in your hand before their eyes.

**37:21** And speak to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am about to take the sons of Israel from among the nations where they have gone, and I will gather them from every side and bring them to their own soil.

**37:22** And I will make them into one nation in the land, on the mountains of Israel; and one king will be king over all of them; and they will no longer be two nations, and they will no longer be divided into two kingdoms ever again.

**37:23** And they will no longer defile themselves with their idols, or with their detestable things, or with any of their transgressions; but I will deliver them from all their dwelling places in which they have sinned, and I will cleanse them. And they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them.

**37:24** And My servant David will be king over them, and there will be one shepherd for all of them; and they will walk in My ordinances, and they will keep My statutes and do them.

**37:25** And they will dwell on the land that I gave to My servant Jacob, in which your fathers dwelt; and they will dwell upon it—they and their sons and the sons of their sons—forever, and David My servant will be their prince forever.

**37:26** And I will make a covenant of peace with them; it will be an everlasting covenant with them. And I will establish them and multiply them, and I will set My sanctuary in their midst forever.

**37:27** And My dwelling place will be over them; and I will be God to them, and they will be a people to Me.

**37:28** And the nations will know that I am the LORD who sanctifies Israel, when My sanctuary is in their midst forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **37:9** — The Hebrew noun ruach carries a deliberate triple wordplay across vv. 1-14, shifting between "Spirit" (vv. 1, 14), "breath" (vv. 5-6, 8-10), and "winds" (arba' ruchot, "four winds," v. 9).
- **37:17** — The form la'achadim is the rare plural of echad ("one"), literally "into ones," expressing complete unity; qarav is an unusual transitive imperative ("join together" or "bring near").
- **37:22** — The Qere reads plural yihyu ("they will be"), matching the plural verbs in the verse, while the Ketiv reads singular yihyeh ("it will be"), agreeing with the collective "one nation."
- **37:23** — The Masoretic text reads moshvoteihem ("their dwelling places"); the Septuagint reflects a different reading, meshuvoteihem ("their apostasies" or "backslidings").
