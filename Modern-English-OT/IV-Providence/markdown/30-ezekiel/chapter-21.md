## Text

**21:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21:2** "Son of man, set your face toward the south, drop words toward the southland, and prophesy against the forest of the field in the Negev.

**21:3** And say to the forest of the Negev, 'Hear the word of the LORD! This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am kindling a fire in you, and it will devour every green tree and every dry tree in you. The blazing flame will not be extinguished, and all faces from south to north will be scorched by it.

**21:4** And all flesh will see that I, the LORD, have set it ablaze; it will not be extinguished.'"

**21:5** Then I said, "Alas, Lord GOD! They are saying of me, 'Is he not a speaker of parables?'"

**21:6** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21:7** "Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem, drop words toward the sanctuaries, and prophesy against the land of Israel.

**21:8** And say to the land of Israel, 'This is what the LORD says: Look, I am against you, and I will draw My sword out of its sheath and cut off from you the righteous and the wicked.

**21:9** Because I will cut off from you the righteous and the wicked, therefore My sword will go out from its sheath against all flesh from south to north.

**21:10** And all flesh will know that I, the LORD, have drawn My sword out of its sheath; it will not return again.'

**21:11** As for you, son of man, groan! With breaking loins and with bitterness you shall groan before their eyes.

**21:12** And when they say to you, 'Why are you groaning?' you shall say, 'Because of the report, for it is coming! Every heart will melt, all hands will go limp, every spirit will grow faint, and all knees will run with water. Look, it is coming and it will happen, declares the Lord GOD.'"

**21:13** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21:14** "Son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord says!' Say, 'A sword, a sword is sharpened and also polished!

**21:15** To make a slaughter it is sharpened; to have a flash of lightning it is polished. Or shall we rejoice? The scepter of My son despises every tree.

**21:16** And it was given to be polished, to be grasped in the palm; this sword is sharpened and it is polished, to give it into the hand of a killer.'

**21:17** Cry out and wail, son of man, for it is against My people; it is against all the princes of Israel. They have been cast to the sword along with My people; therefore strike your thigh.

**21:18** For there is a testing; and what if even the despising scepter will be no more?" declares the Lord GOD.

**21:19** "And you, son of man, prophesy and strike palm against palm! Let the sword be doubled a third time, the sword of the fatally wounded; it is the sword of the great fatally wounded one, which surrounds them.

**21:20** So that hearts may melt and stumblings multiply, at all their gates I have set the threat of the sword. Ah! It is made for a flash of lightning, wrapped for slaughter.

**21:21** Gather yourself, turn right! Set yourself, turn left, wherever your face is directed!

**21:22** I also will strike My palm against My palm, and I will put My wrath to rest. I, the LORD, have spoken."

**21:23** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21:24** "And you, son of man, lay out for yourself two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to come; both of them shall go out from one land. And carve a signpost; at the head of the road to a city, carve it.

**21:25** You shall lay out a road for the sword to come to Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and to Judah in fortified Jerusalem.

**21:26** For the king of Babylon stood at the fork in the road, at the head of the two roads, to practice divination; he shook the arrows, he consulted the teraphim, he looked at the liver.

**21:27** Into his right hand came the divination for Jerusalem: to set battering rams, to open the mouth for killing, to raise the voice with a battle cry, to set battering rams against the gates, to cast up a siege ramp, and to build a siege wall.

**21:28** And to them it will be like a false divination in their eyes, bound as they are by solemn oaths; yet he brings iniquity to remembrance so that they may be seized.

**21:29** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have brought your iniquity to remembrance, in that your transgressions are uncovered so that your sins appear in all your deeds—because you have been brought to remembrance, you will be seized by the hand.

**21:30** And as for you, fatally wounded wicked one, prince of Israel, whose day has come at the time of final iniquity:

**21:31** This is what the Lord GOD says: Remove the turban and lift off the crown! This will not remain what it is: lift up what is low, and bring low what is high.

**21:32** A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it! This also will not be, until he comes to whom the right belongs, and I will give it to him.

**21:33** And you, son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says concerning the sons of Ammon and concerning their reproach.' And say, 'A sword, a sword is drawn for slaughter, polished to the utmost, for a flash of lightning,

**21:34** while they see false visions for you, while they divine lies for you, to place you upon the necks of the fatally wounded wicked, whose day has come at the time of final iniquity.

**21:35** Return it to its sheath! In the place where you were created, in the land of your origin, I will judge you.

**21:36** And I will pour out My indignation upon you; with the fire of My fury I will blow upon you, and I will give you into the hand of brutish men, craftsmen of destruction.

**21:37** You will become fuel for the fire; your blood will be in the midst of the land; you will not be remembered, for I, the LORD, have spoken.'"

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The verse uses three Hebrew terms for south (Teman, Darom, and Negev); "drop words" translates the Hiphil imperative of nataph ("drip"), an idiom for prophetic utterance.
- **21:15** — The Hebrew of the second half ("Or shall we rejoice? The scepter of My son despises every tree") is famously cryptic, with the feminine participle mo'eset ("despising") linking either to "scepter" or to the feminine "sword"; the same phrase recurs in verse 18.
- **21:20** — Hebrew ibchat ("threat" or "slaughter") is a hapax legomenon of uncertain meaning, and me'uttah is the Pual participle of 'atah ("wrapped"), sometimes understood as a variant for "polished."
- **21:28** — Ketiv reads kqswm (plene spelling) while Qere reads kiqsam ("like divining" in construct with shav', "falsehood"), which is translated here. The phrase "bound as they are by solemn oaths" translates the rare Qal passive participle construct shevu'ei followed by shevu'ot ("oaths").
- **21:33** — Hebrew lehakhil is the Hiphil infinitive construct of kul (literally "to contain" or "to hold"), used idiomatically for "to the utmost" and often understood by ancient versions as "to consume."
