## Text

**40:1** In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was struck down, on that very day the hand of the LORD was upon me, and He brought me there.

**40:2** In visions of God He brought me to the land of Israel and set me down on a very high mountain, and on it was something like the structure of a city to the south.

**40:3** He brought me there, and look, there was a man whose appearance was like the appearance of bronze, with a linen cord and a measuring reed in his hand, and he was standing in the gate.

**40:4** The man spoke to me, "Son of man, see with your eyes and hear with your ears, and set your heart on everything that I am showing you, for you have been brought here in order that I might show it to you. Declare everything that you see to the house of Israel."

**40:5** And look, there was a wall outside the house all around, and in the man's hand was the measuring reed, six cubits, each a cubit and a handbreadth. He measured the width of the structure, one reed, and the height, one reed.

**40:6** Then he came to a gate that faced toward the east and went up its steps. He measured the threshold of the gate, one reed in width, and the other threshold, one reed in width.

**40:7** Each guardroom was one reed in length and one reed in width, and between the guardrooms was five cubits; and the threshold of the gate beside the vestibule of the gate toward the house was one reed.

**40:8** He measured the vestibule of the gate toward the house, one reed.

**40:9** Then he measured the vestibule of the gate, eight cubits, and its jambs, two cubits; and the vestibule of the gate was toward the house.

**40:10** The guardrooms of the gate toward the east were three on this side and three on that side; the three of them had one measurement, and the jambs on this side and on that side had one measurement.

**40:11** He measured the width of the entrance of the gate, ten cubits, and the length of the gate, thirteen cubits.

**40:12** There was a border in front of the guardrooms of one cubit, and a border of one cubit on the other side; and each guardroom was six cubits on this side and six cubits on that side.

**40:13** He measured the gate from the roof of one guardroom to the opposite roof, a width of twenty-five cubits, entrance opposite entrance.

**40:14** He also made the jambs sixty cubits, and up to the jamb of the court was the gate all around.

**40:15** From the front of the entrance gate to the front of the inner vestibule of the gate was fifty cubits.

**40:16** There were narrowed windows for the guardrooms and for their jambs inside the gate all around, and likewise for the vestibules; windows were all around on the inside, and on each jamb were palm ornaments.

**40:17** Then he brought me into the outer court, and look, there were chambers and a pavement made for the court all around; thirty chambers were along the pavement.

**40:18** The pavement was along the side of the gates, corresponding to the length of the gates; this was the lower pavement.

**40:19** Then he measured the width from the front of the lower gate to the front of the inner court on the outside, one hundred cubits, on the east and on the north.

**40:20** As for the gate of the outer court that faced toward the north, he measured its length and its width.

**40:21** Its guardrooms were three on this side and three on that side, and its jambs and its vestibule were according to the measurement of the first gate: its length was fifty cubits, and the width was twenty-five cubits.

**40:22** Its windows, its vestibule, and its palm ornaments were according to the measurement of the gate that faced toward the east; they would go up to it by seven steps, and its vestibule was in front of them.

**40:23** There was a gate to the inner court opposite the gate on the north and on the east, and he measured from gate to gate, one hundred cubits.

**40:24** Then he led me toward the south, and look, there was a gate toward the south; and he measured its jambs and its vestibule according to these measurements.

**40:25** It and its vestibule had windows all around, like these windows; the length was fifty cubits, and the width was twenty-five cubits.

**40:26** There were seven steps going up to it, and its vestibule was in front of them; and it had palm ornaments, one on this side and one on that side, on its jambs.

**40:27** There was a gate to the inner court toward the south, and he measured from gate to gate toward the south, one hundred cubits.

**40:28** Then he brought me into the inner court by the south gate, and he measured the south gate according to these measurements.

**40:29** Its guardrooms, its jambs, and its vestibule were according to these measurements. It and its vestibule had windows all around; the length was fifty cubits, and the width was twenty-five cubits.

**40:31** Its vestibule was toward the outer court, palm ornaments were on its jambs, and its ascent had eight steps.

**40:32** Then he brought me into the inner court toward the east, and he measured the gate according to these measurements.

**40:33** Its guardrooms, its jambs, and its vestibule were according to these measurements. It and its vestibule had windows all around; the length was fifty cubits, and the width was twenty-five cubits.

**40:34** Its vestibule was toward the outer court, palm ornaments were on its jambs on this side and on that side, and its ascent had eight steps.

**40:35** Then he brought me to the north gate, and he measured according to these measurements:

**40:36** its guardrooms, its jambs, and its vestibule; and it had windows all around. The length was fifty cubits, and the width was twenty-five cubits.

**40:37** Its jambs were toward the outer court, palm ornaments were on its jambs on this side and on that side, and its ascent had eight steps.

**40:38** There was a chamber with its entrance by the jambs of the gates; there they would rinse the burnt offering.

**40:39** In the vestibule of the gate were two tables on this side and two tables on that side, on which to slaughter the burnt offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering.

**40:40** On the outer side, as one goes up to the entrance of the north gate, were two tables, and on the other side, belonging to the vestibule of the gate, were two tables.

**40:41** Four tables were on this side and four tables on that side, at the side of the gate, eight tables on which they would slaughter.

**40:42** There were also four tables of hewn stone for the burnt offering, one and a half cubits in length, one and a half cubits in width, and one cubit in height; on them they would lay the instruments with which they would slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice.

**40:43** And double hooks, one handbreadth long, were fastened inside all around; and on the tables was the flesh of the offering.

**40:44** Outside the inner gate were chambers for singers in the inner court, at the side of the north gate, facing toward the south; one was at the side of the east gate, facing toward the north.

**40:45** He spoke to me, "This chamber, which faces toward the south, is for the priests who keep the charge of the house;

**40:46** and the chamber that faces toward the north is for the priests who keep the charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, who from among the sons of Levi draw near to the LORD to minister to Him."

**40:47** He measured the court: a length of one hundred cubits and a width of one hundred cubits, square; and the altar was in front of the house.

**40:48** Then he brought me to the vestibule of the house and measured the jamb of the vestibule, five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side; and the width of the gate was three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side.

**40:49** The length of the vestibule was twenty cubits, and the width was eleven cubits; and there were steps by which they would go up to it, and pillars beside the jambs, one on this side and one on that side.

## Translator Notes

- **40:8** — The Masoretic Text preserves this measurement of the vestibule toward the house (one reed) alongside the eight-cubit measurement in v. 9. The phrase mehabbayit (literally 'from the house,' vv. 7-9) indicates the inner side facing the temple house.
- **40:14** — The Hebrew syntax is compressed and difficult: wayya'as ('he made,' Qal of 'asah) appears to mean 'he reckoned' or 'measured out,' and the second clause literally reads 'and to the jamb of the court, the gate all around.'
- **40:15** — Translated from the qere ha'iton ('the entrance,' a rare noun from 'atah, 'to come'); the ketiv hy'twn is a variant consonantal spelling.
- **40:43** — The rare dual noun shefattayim is uncertain in meaning, referring either to double-pronged hooks or to ledges fastened around the inside of the slaughtering area.
- **40:44** — The Masoretic Text literally reads 'chambers for singers' (lishkot sharim) and 'the east gate' (sha'ar haqqadim), whereas the Septuagint reads 'two chambers' and 'the south gate.'
