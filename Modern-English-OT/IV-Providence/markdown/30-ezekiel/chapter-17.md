## Text

**17:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**17:2** "Son of man, pose a riddle and speak a parable to the house of Israel,

**17:3** and say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: The great eagle with great wings and long pinions, full of plumage with variegated colors, came to Lebanon and took the top of the cedar.

**17:4** He plucked off the topmost of its young shoots and brought it to a land of trade; he set it in a city of merchants.

**17:5** He also took from the seed of the land and put it in a field for sowing; he took it beside abundant waters and set it like a willow.

**17:6** And it sprouted and became a spreading vine, low in stature, so that its branches turned toward him while its roots remained under it. Thus it became a vine, produced shoots, and sent out boughs.

**17:7** Now there was another great eagle with great wings and abundant plumage. And look, this vine bent its roots toward him and sent out its branches to him, so that he might water it from the beds of its planting.

**17:8** In a good field beside abundant waters it was planted, to produce branches and bear fruit, to become a majestic vine.'

**17:9** Say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Will it prosper? Will he not tear out its roots and strip off its fruit so that it withers? All the fresh leaves of its growth will wither, and it will not take a strong arm or many people to pull it up by its roots.

**17:10** And look, though it is planted, will it prosper? Will it not utterly wither when the east wind strikes it? On the beds where it sprouted it will wither.'"

**17:11** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**17:12** "Say now to the rebellious house, 'Do you not know what these things mean?' Say, 'Look, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took its king and its officials, and brought them to himself in Babylon.

**17:13** He also took one from the royal seed and made a covenant with him, bringing him under an oath, and he took away the mighty men of the land,

**17:14** so that the kingdom would be lowly and not lift itself up, keeping his covenant in order that it might stand.

**17:15** But he rebelled against him by sending his messengers to Egypt, that they might give him horses and many people. Will he prosper? Will the one who does these things escape? Can he break a covenant and still escape?

**17:16** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, surely in the place of the king who made him king, whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke, with him in the midst of Babylon he will die.

**17:17** Nor will Pharaoh with a great army and a numerous company act for him in the war, when they cast up a siege mound and build a siege wall to cut off many lives.

**17:18** For he despised an oath by breaking a covenant, and look, he had given his hand, yet he did all these things; he will not escape.

**17:19** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: As I live, surely My oath that he despised and My covenant that he broke, I will bring it upon his own head.

**17:20** I will spread My net over him, and he will be caught in My snare. And I will bring him to Babylon and enter into judgment with him there for his treachery by which he acted treacherously against Me.

**17:21** And all his fugitives among all his troops will fall by the sword, and those who remain will be scattered to every wind. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken.'"

**17:22** This is what the Lord GOD says: "I Myself will also take from the lofty top of the cedar and set it out; from the topmost of its young shoots I will pluck off a tender one, and I Myself will plant it on a high and towering mountain.

**17:23** On the mountain height of Israel I will plant it, so that it will bear branches and produce fruit and become a majestic cedar. Every bird of every wing will dwell beneath it; in the shade of its branches they will dwell.

**17:24** And all the trees of the field will know that I, the LORD, have brought low the high tree and raised high the low tree, have dried up the green tree and made the dry tree blossom. I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it."

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — Hebrew אֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן ("land of Canaan") functions here as a wordplay on the sense of כְּנַעַן as "merchant" or "trade," parallel to עִיר רֹכְלִים ("city of merchants"), referring to Babylon.
- **17:5** — קָח is a rare form of לקח ("took") lacking the initial lamed; צַפְצָפָה ("willow") is a hapax legomenon, identified via Semitic cognates (cf. Arabic safsaf) as a water-loving willow.
- **17:9** — יְקוֹסֵס ("strip off") is a hapax Poel imperfect of the geminate root קסס, and לְמַשְׂאוֹת ("to pull it up") is a rare Aramaizing Qal infinitive construct of נשׂא with a mem prefix.
- **17:21** — MT מִבְרָחָו ("his fugitives," from ברח, "flee") shows a defective spelling of the plural suffix -āw and is preceded by the marker אֵת before the grammatical subject.
