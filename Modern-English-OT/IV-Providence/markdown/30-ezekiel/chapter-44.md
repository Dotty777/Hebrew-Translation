## Text

**44:1** Then he brought me back by way of the outer gate of the sanctuary, which faces east, and it was shut.

**44:2** And the LORD said to me, "This gate shall remain shut; it shall not be opened, and no one shall enter through it, because the LORD, the God of Israel, has entered through it; therefore it shall remain shut.

**44:3** As for the prince, since he is the prince, he may sit in it to eat bread before the LORD; he shall enter by way of the porch of the gate and go out by that same way."

**44:4** Then he brought me by way of the north gate to the front of the house; and I saw, and look, the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD, and I fell on my face.

**44:5** And the LORD said to me, "Son of man, pay careful attention, see with your eyes, and hear with your ears all that I am speaking to you concerning all the statutes of the house of the LORD and all its laws; and pay careful attention to the entrance of the house, along with all the exits of the sanctuary.

**44:6** And you shall say to the rebellious ones, to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Enough of all your abominations, house of Israel,

**44:7** in bringing in foreigners, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in My sanctuary to profane My house, when you present My food, fat and blood; and they broke My covenant in addition to all your abominations.

**44:8** And you have not kept the charge of My holy things, but you appointed them as keepers of My charge in My sanctuary for yourselves.

**44:9** This is what the Lord GOD says: No foreigner, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter My sanctuary, not any foreigner who is in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**44:10** Rather, the Levites who went far away from Me when Israel went astray, who went astray from Me after their idols, they shall bear their iniquity.

**44:11** They shall be ministers in My sanctuary, having oversight at the gates of the house and ministering to the house; they shall slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they shall stand before them to minister to them.

**44:12** Because they ministered to them before their idols and became a stumbling block of iniquity to the house of Israel, therefore I have lifted up My hand against them, declares the Lord GOD, and they shall bear their iniquity.

**44:13** And they shall not approach Me to serve as priests to Me, nor approach any of My holy things, to the most holy things; but they shall bear their disgrace and their abominations that they have committed.

**44:14** Yet I will appoint them as keepers of the charge of the house, for all its service and for everything that is to be done in it.

**44:15** But the Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge of My sanctuary when the sons of Israel went astray from Me, they shall draw near to Me to minister to Me, and they shall stand before Me to present to Me fat and blood, declares the Lord GOD.

**44:16** They shall enter My sanctuary, and they shall draw near to My table to minister to Me, and they shall keep My charge.

**44:17** And it shall be that when they enter the gates of the inner courtyard, they shall wear linen garments; and no wool shall come upon them while they minister at the gates of the inner courtyard and inside the house.

**44:18** Linen turbans shall be on their heads, and linen undergarments shall be on their loins; they shall not bind themselves with anything that causes sweat.

**44:19** And when they go out to the outer courtyard, to the outer courtyard to the people, they shall take off their garments in which they minister and lay them in the holy chambers; and they shall put on other garments, so that they will not make the people holy with their garments.

**44:20** And they shall not shave their heads, nor let their hair grow long; they shall certainly trim the hair on their heads.

**44:21** And no priest shall drink wine when entering the inner courtyard.

**44:22** And they shall not take a widow or a divorced woman for themselves as wives, but only virgins from the seed of the house of Israel, or they may take a widow who is the widow of a priest.

**44:23** And they shall teach My people to discern between the holy and the common, and make them know the distinction between the unclean and the clean.

**44:24** And in a dispute they shall stand for judgment; by My judgments they shall judge it. And they shall keep My laws and My statutes in all My appointed festivals, and they shall keep My Sabbaths holy.

**44:25** And none of them shall come near a dead person to become defiled; however, for a father or mother, for a son or daughter, for a brother, or for a sister who has had no husband, they may defile themselves.

**44:26** And after his cleansing, they shall count seven days for him.

**44:27** And on the day he enters the holy place, into the inner courtyard, to minister in the holy place, he shall present his sin offering, declares the Lord GOD.

**44:28** And this shall be an inheritance for them: I am their inheritance; and you shall not give them a possession in Israel: I am their possession.

**44:29** The grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering—they shall eat them; and every devoted thing in Israel shall belong to them.

**44:30** And the first of all the firstfruits of every kind, and every contribution of every kind from all your contributions, shall belong to the priests; you shall also give the first of your dough to the priest, to cause a blessing to rest on your house.

**44:31** The priests shall not eat anything that dies of itself or is torn, whether of bird or of animal.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:3** — The initial direct object marker 'et introduces "the prince" as a fronted topic ("As for the prince"). The Qere le'ekhol- binds "to eat" with maqqef to "bread" in defective spelling, whereas the Ketiv writes the Qal infinitive construct plene (l'kwl).
- **44:7** — MT reads third-person plural wayyaferu ("and they broke," Hiphil wayyiqtol of parar), shifting from second-person plural address; some ancient versions harmonize to second-person "you broke."
- **44:18** — Hebrew bayyaza' (lit. "in/with sweat," a hapax legomenon) refers to garments or belts that induce perspiration during priestly service.
- **44:24** — Two Ketiv/Qere variants occur here: Qere lemishpat ("for judgment") vs. Ketiv lshpt ("to judge," Qal infinitive construct), and Qere yishpetuhu ("they shall judge it," Qal imperfect) vs. Ketiv wshpthw ("and they shall judge it," Qal weqatal).
- **44:28** — The feminine singular verb wehayetah ("and it/this shall be") has no explicit subject, either functioning cataphorically to introduce "I am their inheritance" or agreeing by attraction with the feminine predicate noun nachalah ("inheritance").
