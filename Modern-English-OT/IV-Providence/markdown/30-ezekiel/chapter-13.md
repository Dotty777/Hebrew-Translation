## Text

**13:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**13:2** "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are prophesying, and say to those who are prophets from their own hearts, 'Hear the word of the LORD!

**13:3** This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe upon the foolish prophets who walk after their own spirit and have seen nothing!

**13:4** Like foxes in the ruins have your prophets been, Israel.

**13:5** You did not go up into the breaches, nor did you build up a stone wall around the house of Israel to stand in the battle on the day of the LORD.

**13:6** They have seen emptiness and lying divination, those who say, "The LORD declares," when the LORD has not sent them; yet they wait for the word to be confirmed.

**13:7** Have you not seen an empty vision and spoken a lying divination, saying, "The LORD declares," though I have not spoken?

**13:8** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have spoken emptiness and seen lies, therefore, look, I am against you, declares the Lord GOD.

**13:9** And My hand will be against the prophets who see emptiness and who divine lies. They will not be in the council of My people, nor will they be written in the register of the house of Israel, nor will they come to the soil of Israel; and you will know that I am the Lord GOD.

**13:10** Because, yes, because they have misled My people, saying, "Peace," when there is no peace, and when one builds a flimsy wall, look, they plaster it with whitewash.'

**13:11** Say to those who plaster with whitewash that it will fall: 'A flooding rain will come, and you, hailstones, will fall, and a stormy wind will break out.

**13:12** And look, the wall has fallen! Will it not be said to you, "Where is the plaster with which you plastered it?"

**13:13** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: I will cause a stormy wind to break out in My fury, and a flooding rain will come in My anger, and hailstones in fury for complete destruction.

**13:14** And I will demolish the wall that you plastered with whitewash and bring it down to the ground, and its foundation will be uncovered. And it will fall, and you will be consumed in its midst; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**13:15** And I will exhaust My fury against the wall and against those who plastered it with whitewash, and I will say to you, "The wall is no more, nor are those who plastered it—

**13:16** the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem and who see for her a vision of peace when there is no peace," declares the Lord GOD.'

**13:17** As for you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy from their own hearts, and prophesy against them.

**13:18** And say, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the women who sew bands onto all wrists and make veils for the head of every stature to hunt souls! Will you hunt the souls of My people, and keep souls alive for yourselves?

**13:19** And you have profaned Me before My people for handfuls of barley and for morsels of bread, to put to death souls that ought not to die and to keep alive souls that ought not to live, by telling lies to My people, who listen to lies.

**13:20** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against your bands with which you hunt souls like birds; I will tear them off your arms, and I will set free the souls that you are hunting, even those souls, like birds.

**13:21** And I will tear away your veils and rescue My people from your hand, and they will no longer be prey in your hand; then you will know that I am the LORD.

**13:22** Because you have disheartened the heart of the righteous with falsehood, though I Myself had not caused him pain, and have strengthened the hands of the wicked so that he would not turn back from his evil way to preserve his life,

**13:23** therefore, you will not see emptiness, nor will you practice divination any longer; and I will rescue My people from your hand, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The negative particle לְבִלְתִּי, ordinarily used with an infinitive construct, governs the finite Qal perfect verb רָאוּ ("and have seen nothing"), a rare biblical construction.
- **13:11** — The Masoretic Text reads the 2nd-person feminine plural pronoun וְאַתֵּנָה ("and you, hailstones, will fall"), addressing the hailstones (אַבְנֵי אֶלְגָּבִישׁ) directly in apostrophe rather than reading a 1st-person verb ("I will send") as in some ancient versions.
- **13:14** — After masculine singular suffixes referring to the wall (וְהִגַּעְתִּיהוּ, יְסֹדוֹ), the Hebrew shifts abruptly to feminine singular in וְנָפְלָה ("she/it will fall") and בְּתוֹכָהּ ("in her/its midst"), pointing through the metaphor to the city of Jerusalem (cf. v. 16).
- **13:18** — The rare nouns כְּסָתוֹת ("bands," also v. 20) and מִסְפָּחוֹת ("veils," also v. 21) refer to arm bindings and head coverings used in divinatory rituals. MT's אַצִּילֵי יָדַי literally reads "joints of my hands," generally understood as a shortened dual for "joints of hands" (wrists) parallel to "the head of every stature."
- **13:20** — The Qal feminine plural participle לְפֹרְחוֹת ("like birds") reflects the late Hebrew and Aramaic sense of פרח ("fly"). The resumptive object אֶת־נְפָשִׁים preserves an unusual masculine-ending plural of נֶפֶשׁ (elsewhere נְפָשׁוֹת).
