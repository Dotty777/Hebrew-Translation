## Text

**46:1** This is what the Lord GOD says: "The gate of the inner court that faces east shall remain shut during the six working days, but on the Sabbath day it shall be opened, and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.

**46:2** The prince shall enter by way of the vestibule of the gate from outside and stand beside the post of the gate. Then the priests shall offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall bow down in worship at the threshold of the gate and go out; yet the gate shall not be shut until evening.

**46:3** The people of the land shall also bow down in worship at the entrance of that gate before the LORD on the Sabbaths and on the new moons.

**46:4** The burnt offering that the prince presents to the LORD on the Sabbath day shall be six lambs without blemish and a ram without blemish,

**46:5** with a grain offering of an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs a grain offering of whatever his hand gives, along with a hin of oil for each ephah.

**46:6** On the day of the new moon it shall be a young bull without blemish, and six lambs and a ram; they shall be without blemish.

**46:7** He shall prepare as a grain offering an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs as much as his hand can afford, along with a hin of oil for each ephah.

**46:8** When the prince enters, he shall enter by way of the vestibule of the gate, and by the same way he shall go out.

**46:9** But when the people of the land come before the LORD at the appointed times, whoever enters by way of the north gate to bow down in worship shall go out by way of the south gate, and whoever enters by way of the south gate shall go out by way of the north gate; he shall not return by way of the gate through which he entered, but shall go out straight ahead.

**46:10** As for the prince, he shall be in their midst: when they enter, he shall enter, and when they go out, they shall go out.

**46:11** At the festivals and at the appointed times, the grain offering shall be an ephah for each bull and an ephah for each ram, and for the lambs whatever his hand gives, along with a hin of oil for each ephah.

**46:12** When the prince prepares a freewill offering, either a burnt offering or peace offerings as a freewill offering to the LORD, the gate that faces east shall be opened for him, and he shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings just as he does on the Sabbath day. Then he shall go out, and the gate shall be shut after he goes out.

**46:13** You shall also prepare a year-old lamb without blemish as a burnt offering daily to the LORD; morning by morning you shall prepare it.

**46:14** And with it you shall prepare a grain offering morning by morning: a sixth of an ephah, and a third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour, a grain offering to the LORD by perpetual statutes continually.

**46:15** They shall prepare the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil morning by morning as a continual burnt offering."

**46:16** This is what the Lord GOD says: "If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is his inheritance; it shall belong to his sons; it is their possession by inheritance.

**46:17** But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his until the year of liberty, and then it shall return to the prince; only his inheritance belongs to his sons; it shall be theirs.

**46:18** The prince shall not take any of the people's inheritance, oppressing them out of their possession; from his own possession he shall give an inheritance to his sons, so that My people will not be scattered, each man from his possession."

**46:19** Then he brought me through the entrance that was at the side of the gate into the holy chambers for the priests, which faced north; and look, a place was there at the extreme rear toward the west.

**46:20** And he said to me, "This is the place where the priests shall boil the guilt offering and the sin offering, and where they shall bake the grain offering, so as not to bring them out into the outer court and consecrate the people."

**46:21** Then he brought me out into the outer court and led me over to the four corners of the court; and look, in every corner of the court there was a court.

**46:22** In the four corners of the court were enclosed courts, forty in length and thirty in width; these four corner courts had one measurement.

**46:23** There was a masonry row all around inside them, around all four of them, and boiling hearths were built beneath the rows all around.

**46:24** And he said to me, "These are the cooking houses where the ministers of the house shall boil the sacrifice of the people."

## Translator Notes

- **46:9** — The qere reads the singular Qal imperfect "yetse" ("he shall go out", agreeing with the singular subject), whereas the ketiv reads the plural "yets'u" ("they shall go out").
- **46:15** — The qere reads the Qal imperfect "ya'asu" ("they shall prepare"), whereas the ketiv reads the waw-consecutive Qal perfect "ve'asu" ("and they shall prepare").
- **46:19** — The qere reads the dual "bayyarkatayim" ("at the extreme rear"), whereas the ketiv reads "beyarkatam" (either "at their far end" or a defective dual spelling).
- **46:22** — The Qal passive participle "qeturot" is a biblical hapax legomenon, traditionally understood as "enclosed" or "unroofed" courts. The final word "mehuqtsa'ot" (Hophal participle, "set in the corners") carries Masoretic extraordinary points (puncta extraordinaria) above its letters.
