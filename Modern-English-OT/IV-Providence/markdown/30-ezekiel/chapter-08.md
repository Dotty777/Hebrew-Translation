## Text

**8:1** Now it happened in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth of the month, while I was sitting in my house and the elders of Judah were sitting before me, that the hand of the Lord GOD fell upon me there.

**8:2** Then I looked, and look, a likeness like the appearance of fire: from the appearance of His waist and downward was fire, and from His waist and upward was like the appearance of radiance, like the sheen of glowing metal.

**8:3** And He stretched out the form of a hand and took hold of me by a lock of my head; and a spirit lifted me up between the earth and the heavens and brought me to Jerusalem in visions of God, to the entrance of the inner gate that faces toward the north, where the seat of the statue of jealousy was, which provokes to jealousy.

**8:4** And look, there was the glory of the God of Israel, like the appearance that I had seen in the plain.

**8:5** Then He said to me, "Son of man, lift your eyes now northward." So I lifted my eyes northward, and look, to the north of the gate of the altar was this statue of jealousy in the entryway.

**8:6** And He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what they are doing—the great abominations that the house of Israel is doing here, so that I should go far away from My sanctuary? Yet again you will see great abominations."

**8:7** Then He brought me to the entrance of the courtyard; and I looked, and look, there was a hole in the wall.

**8:8** And He said to me, "Son of man, dig through the wall now." So I dug through the wall, and look, an entrance!

**8:9** And He said to me, "Go in and see the evil abominations that they are doing here."

**8:10** So I went in and looked, and look, every form of creeping creature and detestable beast, and all the idols of the house of Israel, was carved upon the wall all the way around.

**8:11** And seventy men from the elders of the house of Israel—with Jaazaniah the son of Shaphan standing in their midst—were standing before them, each man with his censer in his hand, and the aroma of the cloud of incense was ascending.

**8:12** Then He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man, what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each man in the chambers of his carved image? For they say, 'The LORD does not see us; the LORD has forsaken the land.'"

**8:13** And He said to me, "Yet again you will see great abominations that they are doing."

**8:14** Then He brought me to the entrance of the gate of the house of the LORD that is toward the north, and look, women were sitting there, weeping for Tammuz.

**8:15** And He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man? Yet again you will see abominations greater than these."

**8:16** Then He brought me into the inner courtyard of the house of the LORD; and look, at the entrance of the temple of the LORD, between the portico and the altar, were about twenty-five men, with their backs toward the temple of the LORD and their faces eastward, and they were bowing down eastward to the sun.

**8:17** And He said to me, "Have you seen, son of man? Is it too light a thing for the house of Judah to do the abominations that they have done here, that they have filled the land with violence and have provoked Me to anger yet again? And look, they are extending the branch to their nose!

**8:18** And I too will act in fury; My eye will not spare, nor will I have compassion. And they will cry out in My ears with a loud voice, yet I will not listen to them."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads אֵשׁ ('fire', compare 1:27), whereas the Septuagint reflects אִישׁ ('a man'); הַחַשְׁמַלָה ('glowing metal', with paragogic -ah) is a rare term confined to Ezekiel (1:4, 27; 8:2) denoting glowing metal, electrum, or amber.
- **8:3** — Hebrew ruach lacks the definite article here and may be read as "a spirit," "the Spirit," or "wind" (cf. 2:2).
- **8:6** — Ketiv מהם (written as one word) vs. Qere מָה הֵם ('what they'); the Qal infinitive construct לְרׇחֳקָה ('to go far away') implies causing God to depart far from His sanctuary.
- **8:11** — עֲתַר is a hapax legomenon, understood either as 'aroma/fragrance' or as a 'thick, billowing mass' of incense smoke.
- **8:16** — מִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתֶם is an anomalous conflate Masoretic form combining the Hishtaphel participle מִשְׁתַּחֲוִים ('bowing down') with a second-person plural ending -tem.
- **8:17** — 'Extending the branch to their nose' describes an obscure gesture of cultic provocation; Jewish scribal tradition lists אַפָּם ('their nose') among the tiqqunei soferim ('emendations of the scribes') for an original 'My nose'.
