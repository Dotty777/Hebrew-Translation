## Text

**3:1** And afterward it will happen that I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh, and your sons and your daughters will prophesy; your old men will dream dreams, your young men will see visions.

**3:2** And even on the male servants and on the female servants, in those days I will pour out My Spirit.

**3:3** And I will display portents in the heavens and on the earth: blood and fire and columns of smoke.

**3:4** The sun will be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the coming of the day of the LORD, great and awesome.

**3:5** And it will happen that everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be rescued; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be deliverance, just as the LORD has said, and among the survivors whom the LORD calls.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Hebrew Masoretic numbering begins chapter 3 here (corresponding to 2:28-32 in most English Bibles); unlike the quotation in Acts 2:17, the Hebrew text reads simply achare-khen ("afterward") and et-ruchi ("My Spirit").
- **3:5** — The noun peletah ("deliverance," or concretely "those who escape") echoes 2:3 ("no escape"), and the final prepositional phrase u-vas-seridim ("and among the survivors") parallels "on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem" as the sphere where deliverance is found.
