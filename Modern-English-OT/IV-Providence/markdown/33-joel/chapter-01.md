## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Joel son of Pethuel.

**1:2** Listen to this, elders, and give ear, all inhabitants of the land. Has this happened in your days, or in the days of your fathers?

**1:3** Recount it to your children, and let your children recount it to their children, and their children to a following generation.

**1:4** What remained from the shearing locust, the swarming locust ate; and what remained from the swarming locust, the crawling locust ate; and what remained from the crawling locust, the consuming locust ate.

**1:5** Wake up, drunkards, and weep, and wail, all drinkers of wine, over the sweet wine, for it has been cut off from your mouths.

**1:6** For a nation has come up against My land, mighty and innumerable; its teeth are the teeth of a lion, and the fangs of a lioness belong to it.

**1:7** It has turned My vine into a desolation and My fig tree into splinters; it has stripped it completely bare and thrown it down; its branches have turned white.

**1:8** Lament like a virgin wrapped in sackcloth over the husband of her youth.

**1:9** Grain offering and drink offering have been cut off from the house of the LORD; the priests, the LORD's ministers, are in mourning.

**1:10** The field is devastated, the ground mourns; for the grain is devastated, the new wine has dried up, the fresh oil languishes.

**1:11** Be ashamed, farmers; wail, vineyard keepers, over the wheat and over the barley, for the harvest of the field has perished.

**1:12** The vine has dried up, and the fig tree languishes; the pomegranate, the palm also, and the apple tree—all the trees of the field have dried up; indeed, joy has dried up away from the sons of mankind.

**1:13** Put on sackcloth and mourn, priests; wail, ministers of the altar! Come, spend the night in sackcloth, ministers of my God, because grain offering and drink offering have been withheld from the house of your God.

**1:14** Sanctify a fast; proclaim a solemn assembly. Gather the elders, all inhabitants of the land, to the house of the LORD your God, and cry out to the LORD.

**1:15** Alas for the day! For the day of the LORD is near, and it comes like devastation from the Almighty.

**1:16** Has not food been cut off before our eyes, and from the house of our God, gladness and rejoicing?

**1:17** The seed grains have shriveled beneath their clods; the storehouses stand desolate, the granaries have been torn down, for the grain has dried up.

**1:18** How the animals groan! The herds of cattle are bewildered because there is no pasture for them; even the flocks of sheep suffer punishment.

**1:19** To You, LORD, I call out, because fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and a flame has scorched all the trees of the field.

**1:20** Even the animals of the field long for You, because the channels of water have dried up, and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The four Hebrew terms gazam, arbeh, yeleq, and chasil (repeated in a different order in 2:25) denote either distinct varieties or successive developmental stages of locusts; their exact zoological distinctions remain uncertain.
- **1:6** — "My land" (together with "My vine" and "My fig tree" in v. 7) reflects the LORD claiming Judah as His covenant possession (cf. 2:1, 18), though some understand the first-person pronouns as the prophet speaking on behalf of the community.
- **1:11** — Hebrew plays on near-identical Hiphil forms of yavesh ("dry up," vv. 10, 12, 17) and bosh ("be ashamed" or "fail," here as a Hiphil imperative and again in v. 12 with "joy").
- **1:15** — Hebrew creates a striking sound play in ke-shod mi-shaddai ("like devastation from the Almighty"), echoing the Pual verb shuddad ("is devastated") in v. 10.
- **1:17** — Several terms in this verse occur only here in the Hebrew Bible: aveshu ("have shriveled"), ferudot ("seed grains"), megrefotehem ("their clods," or possibly "their shovels"), and mammegurot ("granaries," cognate with megurah in Hag 2:19).
