## Text

**2:1** Blow a ram's horn in Zion, and raise an alarm on My holy mountain! Let all inhabitants of the land tremble, for the day of the LORD is coming, for it is near.

**2:2** A day of darkness and deep gloom, a day of cloud and heavy darkness! Like dawn spread out over the mountains—a vast and mighty people; its like has never occurred from ages past, nor will it happen again after it through the years of generation after generation.

**2:3** Before it fire devours, and behind it a flame scorches. The land is like the garden of Eden before it, yet behind it a wilderness of desolation; and truly there is no escape from it.

**2:4** Its appearance is like the appearance of horses, and like horsemen, so they run.

**2:5** Like the sound of chariots they bound upon the tops of the mountains, like the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble, like a mighty people arrayed for battle.

**2:6** Before it peoples writhe in anguish; all faces lose their color.

**2:7** Like mighty warriors they run; like men of war they climb a wall. Each one marches in his own ways, and they do not veer from their paths.

**2:8** Not one crowds another; each marches on his own course. Even when they plunge through the weapons, they do not break off.

**2:9** They rush through the city, they run along the wall, they climb up the houses; through the windows they enter like a thief.

**2:10** Before it the earth trembles, the heavens shake; sun and moon grow dark, and stars withdraw their radiance.

**2:11** And the LORD gives forth His voice before His army, for His camp is very vast, for mighty is the one who executes His word. For great is the day of the LORD and exceedingly awesome; and who can bear it?

**2:12** "And even now," declares the LORD, "turn back to Me with your whole heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning."

**2:13** Tear your heart and not your garments, and turn back to the LORD your God, for He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abundant in loyal love, and relenting over calamity.

**2:14** Who knows? He may turn back and relent, and leave a blessing behind Him—grain offering and drink offering for the LORD your God.

**2:15** Blow a ram's horn in Zion; sanctify a fast; proclaim a solemn assembly.

**2:16** Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the elders; gather the little ones and those nursing at the breast. Let the bridegroom go out from his chamber and the bride from her bridal canopy.

**2:17** Between the portico and the altar let the priests, the LORD's ministers, weep, and let them say, "Spare Your people, LORD, and do not give Your heritage over to reproach, for nations to rule over them. Why should they say among the peoples, 'Where is their God?'"

**2:18** Then the LORD grew zealous for His land and took pity on His people.

**2:19** And the LORD answered and said to His people, "Look, I am sending you the grain, the new wine, and the fresh oil, and you will be satisfied with it; and I will no longer make you a reproach among the nations.

**2:20** And the northern one I will remove far from you, and I will banish it into a dry and desolate land—its front into the eastern sea and its rear into the western sea; and its stench will rise, and its foul odor will go up, for it has done great things.

**2:21** Do not be afraid, ground; rejoice and be glad, for the LORD has done great things.

**2:22** Do not be afraid, animals of the field, for the pastures of the wilderness have sprouted green; for the tree has borne its fruit; fig tree and vine have yielded their strength.

**2:23** And children of Zion, rejoice and be glad in the LORD your God, for He has given you the early rain in righteousness, and He has sent down for you rain—early rain and late rain—at the first.

**2:24** And the threshing floors will be filled with grain, and the vats will overflow with new wine and fresh oil.

**2:25** And I will repay you for the years that the swarming locust has eaten—the crawling locust, the consuming locust, and the shearing locust—My great army that I sent among you.

**2:26** And you will eat abundantly and be satisfied, and you will praise the name of the LORD your God, who has acted wondrously with you; and My people will never be ashamed.

**2:27** And you will know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the LORD your God and there is no one else; and My people will never be ashamed."

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — Following the Masoretic vocalization, ke-shachar means "like dawn," picturing the swarm sweeping swiftly across the mountain ridges; some modern versions emend the vowels to ke-shechor ("like blackness").
- **2:6** — The rare idiom qibbetsu fa'rur (also in Nah 2:10, MT 2:11) is understood either as "withdraw their flush/glow" (losing color, from a root meaning "glow/beauty") or as "gather blackness" (from a noun for a soot-blackened cooking pot).
- **2:17** — The phrase li-meshol-bam goyim uses the idiom mashal be- ("rule over"), yielding "for nations to rule over them"; an alternative reading derives the verb from a homonymous root meaning "make a byword/taunt of."
- **2:18** — The Masoretic Text shifts here to wayyiqtol narrative past verbs ("grew zealous... took pity... answered... said" in vv. 18-19), presenting the LORD's merciful intervention as an accomplished turning point rather than a conditional future.
- **2:23** — The noun ha-moreh means "the early rain" (matching moreh u-malqosh later in the verse), though some ancient traditions read it as "the teacher" ("teacher of righteousness"); li-tsedaqah ("in righteousness") may convey "in right measure" or "for vindication," and ba-ri'shon ("at the first") can also mean "in the first month" (whereas the Septuagint reads "as before").
