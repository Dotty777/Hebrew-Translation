## Text

**13:1** The pronouncement concerning Babylon that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw.

**13:2** On a bare mountain lift up a banner; raise your voice to them; wave a hand, so that they may enter the gates of the nobles.

**13:3** I Myself have commanded My consecrated ones; I have also summoned My mighty warriors for My anger, those who exult in My majesty.

**13:4** The sound of a tumult on the mountains, the likeness of a vast people! The sound of an uproar of kingdoms, of nations assembling together! The LORD of hosts is marshaling an army for war.

**13:5** They are coming from a distant land, from the end of the heavens, the LORD and the instruments of His indignation, to ruin all the earth.

**13:6** Wail, for the day of the LORD is near; like devastation from the Almighty it will come.

**13:7** Therefore all hands will go limp, and the heart of every mortal will melt.

**13:8** And they will be terrified; pangs and birth pains will grip them; like a woman giving birth they will writhe. Each will stare aghast at his companion; their faces will be faces of flames.

**13:9** Look, the day of the LORD is coming, cruel, with wrath and burning anger, to make the earth a desolation, and He will wipe out its sinners from it.

**13:10** For the stars of the heavens and their constellations will not shine out their light; the sun will grow dark as it rises, and the moon will not cause its light to shine.

**13:11** And I will call the world to account for evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; I will make the arrogance of the insolent cease, and the pride of the ruthless I will bring low.

**13:12** I will make mortals scarcer than refined gold, and human beings than the choice gold of Ophir.

**13:13** Therefore I will make the heavens tremble, and the earth will quake from its place at the wrath of the LORD of hosts and on the day of His burning anger.

**13:14** And it will be that like a chased gazelle, and like a flock with no one gathering it, each will turn to his own people, and each will flee to his own land.

**13:15** Everyone who is found will be pierced through, and everyone who is caught will fall by the sword.

**13:16** And their little children will be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses will be looted, and their wives will be violated.

**13:17** Look, I am rousing the Medes against them, who do not value silver, and as for gold, they take no pleasure in it.

**13:18** And bows will dash young men to pieces; they will have no compassion on the fruit of the womb; their eye will not spare children.

**13:19** And Babylon, the splendor of kingdoms, the beauty of the pride of the Chaldeans, will be as in God's overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah.

**13:20** It will never be inhabited, nor will it be dwelt in from generation to generation; no Arab will pitch a tent there, and no shepherds will let their flocks lie down there.

**13:21** But desert animals will lie down there, and their houses will be filled with howling creatures; ostriches will dwell there, and wild goats will leap about there.

**13:22** Hyenas will howl in its citadels, and jackals in palaces of luxury. Its time is near to come, and its days will not be drawn out.

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — The Hebrew creates a wordplay between shod ("devastation") and Shaddai ("the Almighty").
- **13:16** — Ketiv/Qere: The Qere substitutes the euphemistic Niphal form tishshakhavnah ("be lain with," rendered "be violated") for the blunter Ketiv verb shagal ("be ravished").
- **13:21** — Several desert fauna terms in 13:21-22 (tsiyyim, ochim, benot ya'anah, se'irim, iyyim, tannim) are partly uncertain; se'irim literally means "hairy ones" and can denote either wild goats or goat-like demons.
- **13:22** — The MT spells "its citadels" as almenotav (with lamed rather than the usual resh in armenotav), either a phonetic variant or a wordplay on almanah ("widow"), suggesting widowed palaces.
