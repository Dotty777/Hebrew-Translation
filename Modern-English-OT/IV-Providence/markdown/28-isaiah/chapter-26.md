## Text

**26:1** On that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: "We have a strong city; He appoints salvation as walls and a rampart.

**26:2** Open the gates so that a righteous nation may enter, one that keeps faithfulness.

**26:3** You will guard a steadfast mind in complete peace, because it trusts in You.

**26:4** Trust in the LORD forever and ever, because the LORD, the LORD, is an eternal Rock.

**26:5** For He has humbled those who dwell on high, the exalted city; He brings it low, He brings it low to the ground; He brings it down to the dust.

**26:6** The foot tramples it down, the feet of the afflicted, the footsteps of the poor."

**26:7** The way of the righteous is level; Upright One, You make even the course of the righteous.

**26:8** Yes, in the way of Your judgments, LORD, we have waited for You; the longing of the soul is for Your name and for Your remembrance.

**26:9** With my soul I long for You in the night; yes, with my spirit within me I seek You eagerly; for when Your judgments are on the earth, those who dwell in the world learn righteousness.

**26:10** Even if grace is shown to a wicked person, he does not learn righteousness; in a land of uprightness he commits injustice and does not see the majesty of the LORD.

**26:11** LORD, Your hand is raised high, yet they do not see it. Let them see Your zeal for the people and be put to shame; indeed, let the fire for Your adversaries devour them.

**26:12** LORD, You will ordain peace for us, because You have also accomplished all our deeds for us.

**26:13** LORD our God, masters besides You have ruled over us, yet by You alone we call Your name to remembrance.

**26:14** Dead, they will not live; departed spirits, they will not rise; therefore You have punished them and destroyed them, and blotted out every remembrance of them.

**26:15** You have increased the nation, LORD, You have increased the nation; You have shown Yourself glorious; You have pushed far out all the boundaries of the land.

**26:16** LORD, in distress they sought You out; they poured out a whisper when Your chastening was on them.

**26:17** As a pregnant woman nears the time to give birth, writhing and crying out in her labor pangs, so have we been in Your presence, LORD.

**26:18** We conceived, we writhed in labor, and it was as if we gave birth to wind; we have not achieved deliverance for the earth, nor have those who dwell in the world fallen.

**26:19** Your dead will live; my corpses will rise! Awake and ring out in joy, you who dwell in the dust! For Your dew is a dew of lights, and the earth will bring forth the departed spirits.

**26:20** Go, my people, enter your inner rooms and shut your door behind you; hide yourself for a brief moment until indignation passes by.

**26:21** For look, the LORD is coming out from His place to punish the inhabitant of the earth for his iniquity; and the earth will uncover its bloodshed and will no longer cover over those killed upon it.

## Translator Notes

- **26:4** — The Hebrew pairs the shortened poetic form of the divine name with the full divine name and uses a beth essentiae ("the LORD, the LORD, is an eternal Rock").
- **26:16** — Hebrew tsaqun is a rare Qal perfect third-person plural of tsuq ("pour out") with a paragogic nun.
- **26:18** — Hebrew yipplu (Qal imperfect of naphal) literally means "fall"; some understand it in the sense of "be born," continuing the childbirth imagery of vv. 17-18 and anticipating the Hiphil tappil ("bring forth") in v. 19.
- **26:19** — Masoretic nevelati literally reads "my corpse," used collectively with the plural verb yequmun ("will rise") to express the speaker's solidarity with God's dead; Hebrew orot ("lights") is also taken by some as "herbs."
- **26:20** — Following the qere delatekha (singular, "your door"); the ketiv reads delateikha (plural, "your doors").
