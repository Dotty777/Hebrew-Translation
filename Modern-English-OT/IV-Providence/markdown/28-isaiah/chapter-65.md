## Text

**65:1** I let Myself be consulted by those who did not ask; I let Myself be found by those who did not seek Me. I said, "Here I am, here I am," to a nation that was not called by My name.

**65:2** I have spread out My hands all day long to a stubborn people, who walk on a way that is not good, after their own thoughts.

**65:3** The people who continually provoke Me before My face, sacrificing in the gardens and burning incense on the bricks,

**65:4** who sit in the tombs and stay the night in hidden places, who eat the flesh of swine, while broth of foul meat is in their vessels,

**65:5** who say, "Stay by yourself; do not approach me, for I am holier than you"—these are smoke in My nostrils, a fire burning all day long.

**65:6** Look, it is written before Me: I will not remain silent, but I will repay—and I will repay onto their lap—

**65:7** your iniquities and the iniquities of your fathers together, says the LORD, who burned incense on the mountains and reviled Me on the hills; so I will first measure out their wage into their lap.

**65:8** This is what the LORD says: "Just as new wine is found in the cluster and someone says, 'Do not destroy it, because a blessing is in it,' so will I act for the sake of My servants, so as not to destroy all of it.

**65:9** I will bring out seed from Jacob, and from Judah an inheritor of My mountains; My chosen ones will inherit it, and My servants will reside there.

**65:10** Sharon will become a grazing land for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a resting place for cattle, for My people who have sought Me.

**65:11** But as for you who abandon the LORD, who forget My holy mountain, who prepare a table for Fortune and fill up mixed wine for Destiny,

**65:12** I will destine you for the sword, and all of you will kneel down to the slaughter, because I called and you did not answer, I spoke and you did not listen; instead, you did evil in My eyes and chose that in which I took no pleasure."

**65:13** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: "Look, My servants will eat, but you will go hungry; look, My servants will drink, but you will go thirsty; look, My servants will rejoice, but you will be shamed.

**65:14** Look, My servants will shout for joy from gladness of heart, but you will cry out from anguish of heart, and from a broken spirit you will wail.

**65:15** You will leave your name behind as an oath-curse for My chosen ones, and the Lord GOD will put you to death, while He will call His servants by a different name,

**65:16** so that whoever blesses himself in the land will bless himself by the God of truth, and whoever swears in the land will swear by the God of truth, because the former distresses have been forgotten, and because they have been hidden from My eyes.

**65:17** For look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth; the former things will not be remembered, nor will they arise in the heart.

**65:18** Instead, be joyful and exult forever and ever in what I am creating; for look, I am creating Jerusalem as exultation and her people as joy.

**65:19** I will exult in Jerusalem and be joyful in My people; never again will the sound of weeping or the sound of outcry be heard in her.

**65:20** There will no longer be an infant there of a few days, nor an elderly man who does not complete his days; for the youth will die at a hundred years old, and the sinner at a hundred years old will be cursed.

**65:21** They will build houses and live in them, and they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

**65:22** They will not build and another live there; they will not plant and another eat; for like the days of a tree will be the days of My people, and My chosen ones will use to the full the work of their hands.

**65:23** They will not toil in vain, nor bear children for sudden disaster, because they are the seed of the LORD's blessed ones, and their descendants are with them.

**65:24** And it will happen that before they call, I will answer, and while they are still speaking, I will listen.

**65:25** Wolf and lamb will graze together, and the lion will eat straw like cattle; as for the serpent, dust will be its food. They will neither harm nor destroy on all My holy mountain," says the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **65:1** — The Masoretic vocalization qora is a Pual perfect passive ("was not called by My name"), rather than the Qal active qara ("did not call on My name") reflected in the Septuagint and Vulgate.
- **65:4** — Ketiv/Qere: the Ketiv reads wprq ("piece" or "broth"), while the Qere followed here reads umeraq ("and broth of").
- **65:5** — The Qal perfect with direct object suffix qedashtika ("I am holier than you") uses a comparative construction, or may express infectious taboo holiness ("I would transmit holiness to you" by contact).
- **65:7** — Ketiv/Qere: the Ketiv reads al ("upon"), while the Qere followed here reads el ("into their lap"), contrasting slightly with al-heqam ("onto their lap") in 65:6.
- **65:11** — "Fortune" and "Destiny" translate the definite divine names ha-Gad and ha-Meni; the latter creates a wordplay with umaniti ("I will destine," from mnh) at the beginning of 65:12.
