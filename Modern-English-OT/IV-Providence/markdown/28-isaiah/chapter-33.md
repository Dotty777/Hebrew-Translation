## Text

**33:1** Woe, ravager, though you have not been ravaged, and treacherous one, though they have not acted treacherously against him! When you finish ravaging, you will be ravaged; when you cease acting treacherously, they will act treacherously against you.

**33:2** LORD, show grace to us; for You we have waited. Be their arm every morning, indeed our salvation in a time of distress.

**33:3** At the sound of uproar, peoples flee; at Your exaltation, nations scatter.

**33:4** And your spoil will be gathered like the gathering of the caterpillar; like the rushing of locusts, one rushes upon it.

**33:5** Exalted is the LORD, for He dwells in the height; He has filled Zion with justice and righteousness.

**33:6** And He will be the steadiness of your times, a store of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge; the fear of the LORD, that is his treasure.

**33:7** Look, their valiant ones cry aloud outside; the messengers of peace weep bitterly.

**33:8** The highways stand desolate; the wayfarer has ceased. He has violated the covenant, he has scorned cities, he regards no mortal.

**33:9** The land mourns and droops; Lebanon is disgraced and withers; Sharon has become like an arid plain, and Bashan and Carmel shake themselves bare.

**33:10** "Now I will rise," says the LORD, "now I will exalt Myself, now I will be lifted up.

**33:11** You conceive dry grass, you bear stubble; your breath is a fire that will devour you.

**33:12** And peoples will become burnings of lime, cut thorns that will burn in the fire.

**33:13** Hear, you who are distant, what I have done; and know, you who are near, My might!"

**33:14** Sinners in Zion are in dread; shuddering has gripped the godless: "Who of us can reside with a devouring fire? Who of us can reside with eternal burnings?"

**33:15** The one who walks in righteous deeds and speaks with uprightness, who scorns profit from oppressions, who shakes his hands away from grasping a bribe, who closes his ear against hearing of bloodshed, and shuts his eyes against looking at evil;

**33:16** he himself will dwell on the heights; strongholds of cliffs will be his high refuge; his bread is given, his water unfailing.

**33:17** Your eyes will gaze upon a king in his beauty; they will see a land of far distances.

**33:18** Your heart will ponder terror: "Where is the one who counted? Where is the one who weighed? Where is the one who counted the towers?"

**33:19** You will not see a fierce people, a people too deep in speech to understand, stammering in tongue with no comprehension.

**33:20** Gaze upon Zion, the city of our appointed festival! Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a tranquil dwelling, a tent that will not be moved; its stakes will never be pulled out, and none of its cords will be snapped.

**33:21** Instead, there the LORD will be majestic for us, a place of rivers and broad streams; no vessel with oars will travel on it, and no majestic ship will cross it.

**33:22** Because the LORD is our judge, the LORD is our lawmaker, the LORD is our king; He Himself will save us.

**33:23** Your cords hang slack; they cannot keep the base of their mast secure; they cannot spread a sail. Then abundant booty and spoil will be divided; the lame will plunder the loot.

**33:24** And no dweller will say, "I am ill"; the people living in it will have their iniquity forgiven.

## Translator Notes

- **33:1** — The phrase 'when you cease' translates the rare MT form kanneloteka, traditionally derived from a hapax root nlh parallel in sense to tmm ('finish') in the preceding clause.
- **33:2** — The MT reads 'their arm' with a third-person plural suffix, shifting briefly from the first-person plural petitions ('us,' 'our') surrounding it.
- **33:7** — The hapax legomenon 'er'ellam ('their valiant ones') is traditionally linked to 'ari'el ('hero, lion-like warrior'; cf. 2 Sam 23:20) with a third-person plural suffix.
- **33:23** — The MT shifts abruptly to feminine singular ('your cords') and plural ('their mast') nautical imagery; 'booty' renders the rare noun 'ad (cf. Gen 49:27; Zeph 3:8) paired with shalal ('spoil').
