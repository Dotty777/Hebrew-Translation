## Text

**16:1** Send a lamb for the ruler of the land, from Sela across the wilderness to the mountain of Daughter Zion.

**16:2** And it will be that like a fleeing bird, a nest driven out, so the daughters of Moab will be at the fords of the Arnon.

**16:3** Bring counsel, execute judgment; set your shade like the night in the middle of noon; hide the outcasts, do not expose the fugitive.

**16:4** Let My outcasts sojourn with you, Moab; be a shelter for them from the ravager. For the oppressor has ceased, ravaging has come to an end, and those who trample are gone from the land.

**16:5** And a throne will be established in loyal love, and in truth a judge will sit upon it in the tent of David, seeking justice and swift in righteousness.

**16:6** We have heard of Moab's pride, how exceedingly proud it is—of its arrogance, its pride, and its insolence; its boasts are not true.

**16:7** Therefore Moab will wail for Moab; all of it will wail. For the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth you will groan, utterly crushed.

**16:8** For the fields of Heshbon wither, as does the vine of Sibmah; the lords of the nations have struck down its choice vines—they reached as far as Jazer, they strayed into the wilderness; its shoots spread out, they crossed the sea.

**16:9** Therefore I will weep with Jazer's weeping for the vine of Sibmah; I will soak you with My tears, Heshbon and Elealeh, for upon your summer fruit and upon your harvest the shouting has fallen.

**16:10** And gladness and joy are withdrawn from the orchard; in the vineyards there will be no singing for joy and no cheering; no treader will tread wine in the winepresses; I have made the shouting cease.

**16:11** Therefore My inward parts groan like a lyre for Moab, and My inner being for Kir-heres.

**16:12** And it will happen that when Moab appears, when it wears itself out on the high place and comes to its sanctuary to pray, it will not prevail.

**16:13** This is the word that the LORD spoke about Moab long ago.

**16:14** And now the LORD has spoken, saying, "In three years, as the years of a hired laborer, the glory of Moab will be dishonored, along with all the great multitude; and the remnant will be very small and meager, not mighty."

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — Ketiv reads Hiphil masculine plural imperative havi'u ("Bring," pl.); Qere reads feminine singular havi'i ("Bring," fem. sg.), matching the following feminine singular imperatives and suffixes.
- **16:4** — The Masoretic Text points niddahay ("My outcasts," Niphal participle with first-person suffix) followed by a disjunctive accent before vocative "Moab"; some ancient versions read construct niddahei mo'av ("the outcasts of Moab").
- **16:7** — Hebrew 'ashishei ("raisin cakes") fits the vineyard imagery of vv. 8-10; some older interpreters render it "foundations" from a different root, and the parallel in Jer 48:31 reads "men."
- **16:8** — In "the lords of the nations have struck down its choice vines," standard Hebrew word order treats "the lords of the nations" as subject of halemu (Qal of halam); alternatively, "its choice vines" is subject ("struck down [intoxicated] the lords of the nations").
- **16:9** — Hebrew 'arayyawek ("I will soak you") is an anomalous Piel imperfect of rawah with a second-person feminine singular suffix. Hebrew hedad ("the shouting") in vv. 9-10 carries a deliberate double sense, evoking both enemy battle cries falling on the harvest and joyous vintage shouts now silenced.
