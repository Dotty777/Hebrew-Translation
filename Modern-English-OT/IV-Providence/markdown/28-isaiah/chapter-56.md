## Text

**56:1** This is what the LORD says: "Keep justice and practice righteousness, for My salvation is near to come, and My righteousness to be revealed."

**56:2** Blessed is the mortal who does this, and the son of man who holds fast to it, who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it, and who keeps his hand from doing anything evil.

**56:3** And do not let the foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say, "The LORD will certainly separate me from His people," and do not let the eunuch say, "Look, I am a dry tree."

**56:4** For this is what the LORD says: "To the eunuchs who keep My Sabbaths, and choose what I delight in, and hold fast to My covenant,

**56:5** to them I will give in My house and within My walls a monument and a name better than sons and daughters; I will give each one an eternal name that will not be cut off.

**56:6** And the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD to minister to Him, and to love the name of the LORD, to be servants to Him, everyone who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it, and those who hold fast to My covenant,

**56:7** I will bring them to My holy mountain and gladden them in My house of prayer; their burnt offerings and their sacrifices will find acceptance on My altar, because My house will be called a house of prayer for all the peoples."

**56:8** The Lord GOD, who gathers the banished of Israel, declares: "I will gather still others to him, in addition to his gathered ones."

**56:9** All you wild animals of the field, come to eat, all you wild animals in the forest!

**56:10** His watchmen are blind; they all know nothing. They are all mute dogs that cannot bark, dreaming, lying down, loving to doze.

**56:11** And the dogs are fierce in appetite; they do not know satisfaction. And they are shepherds who do not know how to understand; all of them have turned to their own way, each man for his dishonest gain, without exception.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**56:12** *(after `56:11`)* "Come," they say, "I will get wine, and we will drink deeply of strong drink. And tomorrow will be like this day—vastly greater."

## Translator Notes

- **56:5** — Hebrew yad literally means 'hand' and idiomatically denotes a memorial monument or standing place. The final clause shifts in the Masoretic Text to the distributive singular lo (literally 'to him'), rendered 'each one.'
- **56:10** — Hebrew hozim (Qal participle of hazah) occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes dreaming or drowsing incoherently. Masoretic tzofaw is a defective spelling of tzofayw ('his watchmen,' referring to Israel).
- **56:11** — Hebrew miqqatzehu literally means 'from its end/extremity,' an idiom denoting totality ('without exception').
- **56:12 *(additional)*** — "They say" is supplied to introduce the abrupt Hebrew quotation, and the closing phrase literally reads "great, exceedingly much."
