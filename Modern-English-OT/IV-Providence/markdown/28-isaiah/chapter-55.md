## Text

**55:1** Come, every thirsty one, come to the waters, and whoever has no silver, come, purchase and eat; and come, purchase wine and milk without silver and without payment.

**55:2** Why do you weigh out silver for what is not bread, and your labor for what brings no satisfaction? Listen intently to Me, and eat what is good, and let your soul delight itself in rich fare.

**55:3** Turn your ear and come to Me; listen, and your soul will live; and I will make an eternal covenant for you, the sure acts of loyal love to David.

**55:4** Look, I have appointed him as a witness to peoples, a ruler and commander of peoples.

**55:5** Look, you will summon a nation you do not know, and a nation that has not known you will run to you, for the sake of the LORD your God, and for the Holy One of Israel, because He has glorified you.

**55:6** Seek the LORD while He can be found; call out to Him while He is near.

**55:7** Let the wicked abandon his way, and the man of iniquity his thoughts; and let him return to the LORD, that He may show him compassion, and to our God, for He will forgive abundantly.

**55:8** For My thoughts are not your thoughts, and your ways are not My ways, declares the LORD.

**55:9** For just as the heavens are higher than the earth, so My ways stand higher than your ways, and My thoughts than your thoughts.

**55:10** For just as the rain and the snow descend from the heavens and do not return there, but instead saturate the earth, causing it to bring forth and sprout, and giving seed to the one who sows and bread to the one who eats,

**55:11** so My word will be that goes out from My mouth: it will not return to Me empty, but instead it will carry out what I delight in, and it will succeed in that for which I sent it.

**55:12** For with joy you will go out, and in peace you will be led along; the mountains and the hills will burst into joyful shouting before you, and all the trees of the field will clap their hands.

**55:13** Instead of the thornbush a cypress will grow up, and instead of the brier a myrtle will grow up; and it will become a name for the LORD, an eternal sign that will not be cut off.

## Translator Notes

- **55:1** — Hebrew hoy here functions as an urgent call of summons rather than a lament of woe, and shivru (Qal imperative of shavar II) specifically means to purchase grain or provisions.
- **55:3** — Hebrew chasdei Dawid hanne'emanim (literally 'the proven/sure acts of loyal love of David') uses the Niphal participle of aman in apposition to 'eternal covenant,' referring to the enduring acts of loyal love promised to David.
- **55:13** — Translates the Qere 'and instead of' (wetachat); the Ketiv (tachat) lacks the conjunction. The noun sirpad occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and denotes a prickly weed such as a brier or nettle.
