## Text

**4:1** And seven women will grasp one man on that day, saying, "Our own bread we will eat, and our own garments we will wear; only let your name be called over us; remove our disgrace."

**4:2** On that day the branch of the LORD will be beauty and glory, and the fruit of the land will be majesty and splendor for the survivors of Israel.

**4:3** And it will be that whoever is left in Zion and whoever remains in Jerusalem will be called holy, everyone written down for life in Jerusalem,

**4:4** once the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and rinsed the bloodstains of Jerusalem from her midst with a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.

**4:5** Then the LORD will create over the entire place of Mount Zion and over her assemblies a cloud by day and smoke, and the radiance of a blazing fire by night; indeed, over all the glory there will be a canopy.

**4:6** And there will be a shelter for shade by day from the heat, and as a refuge and cover from downpour and from rain.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The noun צֶמַח literally means "sprout", "shoot", or "growth" (the same noun used of a Davidic figure in Jer 23:5; 33:15; Zech 3:8; 6:12), standing here in parallelism with פְרִי הָאָרֶץ ("the fruit of the land").
- **4:4** — The conjunction אִם ("if") with a perfect (רָחַץ) and imperfect (יָדִיחַ) functions temporally ("once/when"); the Piel infinitive בָּעֵר carries the dual sense of "burning" and "purging away".
