## Text

**40:1** "Comfort, comfort My people," your God says.

**40:2** "Speak to the heart of Jerusalem and call out to her that her hard service has been fulfilled, that her iniquity has been paid off, that she has received from the hand of the LORD double for all her sins."

**40:3** A voice calls out: "In the wilderness clear the way of the LORD; level in the desert plain a highway for our God.

**40:4** Every valley will be raised up, and every mountain and hill will become low; the uneven ground will become a level plain, and the rugged ridges a broad valley.

**40:5** The glory of the LORD will be unveiled, and all flesh together will see it, because the mouth of the LORD has spoken."

**40:6** A voice says, "Call out!" And he said, "What shall I call out?" "All flesh is grass, and all its loyal love is like a blossom of the field.

**40:7** The grass dries up, the blossom wilts, when the breath of the LORD blows across it; indeed, the people are grass.

**40:8** The grass dries up, the blossom wilts, yet the word of our God stands forever."

**40:9** Go up onto a high mountain, Zion, messenger of good news; raise your voice with strength, Jerusalem, messenger of good news; raise it up, do not be afraid! Say to the cities of Judah, "Here is your God!"

**40:10** See, the Lord GOD is coming with strength, and His arm rules for Him; look, His reward is with Him, and His recompense is ahead of Him.

**40:11** Like a shepherd He tends His flock; with His arm He gathers the lambs and carries them against His chest; the nursing ewes He gently guides.

**40:12** Who has measured waters in His cupped hand, and gauged the heavens with a span, and held the dust of the earth in a measure, and weighed mountains with a balance and hills on scales?

**40:13** Who has gauged the Spirit of the LORD, or as His counselor instructed Him?

**40:14** Whom did He consult to give Him understanding, to teach Him in the path of justice, to teach Him knowledge, and to make known to Him the way of understanding?

**40:15** Look, the nations are like a drop from a bucket, and like fine dust on the scales they are reckoned; look, He lifts the coastlands like a speck of dust.

**40:16** Lebanon is not enough for burning, nor are its wild animals enough for a burnt offering.

**40:17** All the nations are like nothing in His presence; as less than nothing and emptiness they are reckoned by Him.

**40:18** To whom then will you liken God, and what likeness will you set alongside Him?

**40:19** As for an idol, a craftsman casts it, and a metalworker plates it with gold and casts chains of silver.

**40:20** The one too poor for an offering picks wood that will not decay; he searches for a skilled craftsman to set up an idol that will not topple.

**40:21** Do you not know? Do you not hear? Was it not declared to you from the beginning? Have you not understood the foundations of the earth?

**40:22** He is the One who sits enthroned above the circle of the earth, and its dwellers are like grasshoppers; who stretches out the heavens like a thin veil and spreads them like a tent to dwell in.

**40:23** He is the One who reduces princes to nothing, and makes the judges of the earth like emptiness.

**40:24** Barely have they been planted, barely have they been sown, barely has their stem taken root in the earth, when He blows upon them and they dry up, and a stormwind carries them away like stubble.

**40:25** "To whom then will you liken Me, that I might be equal?" says the Holy One.

**40:26** Raise your eyes to the height and see: who created these? He is the One who brings out their host by number; He calls all of them by name. Because of the abundance of His vigor, and because He is mighty in strength, not a single one is missing.

**40:27** Why do you say, Jacob, and speak, Israel, "My way is concealed from the LORD, and my justice passes away from my God"?

**40:28** Have you not known, or have you not heard? The eternal God, the LORD, Creator of the ends of the earth, does not grow weary and does not become exhausted; there is no searching out His understanding.

**40:29** He gives strength to the weary, and to the one without vigor He multiplies power.

**40:30** Even youths grow weary and become exhausted, and young men utterly stumble;

**40:31** but those who wait for the LORD will gain fresh strength; they will ascend on wings like eagles; they will run and not become exhausted; they will walk and not grow weary.

## Translator Notes

- **40:3** — Both the Masoretic accentuation (zaqeph qaton on qore) and the poetic parallelism ("in the wilderness" // "in the desert plain") link "in the wilderness" with "clear the way" rather than with "a voice calls out."
- **40:6** — The Masoretic Text reads third-person va'amar ("and he said") rather than first-person ("and I said") found in some ancient witnesses; chasdo ("its loyal love," from chesed) describes how short-lived human constancy is (cf. Hos 6:4), though it may also suggest loveliness.
- **40:9** — The construct phrase mebasseret tsiyyon (and parallel mebasseret yerushalayim) can be read either appositionally ("Zion, messenger of good news," matching the feminine singular imperatives addressed to the city) or objectively ("messenger of good news to Zion").
- **40:20** — The Masoretic Text points hamesukkan as a Pual participle ("the one too poor for an offering," cf. misken in Eccl 4:13); some lexicographers connect the consonants to Akkadian musukkannu, a durable hardwood.
- **40:21** — The Masoretic Text has no preposition "from" before mosdot ha'arets, allowing it to be read directly as the object of "understood" ("understood the foundations of the earth"), though some carry over "from" from the previous clause.
