## Text

**42:1** Here is My servant, whom I support, My chosen one, in whom My soul takes pleasure. I have placed My Spirit upon him; he will bring forth justice for the nations.

**42:2** He will not shout, nor raise his voice, nor cause his voice to be heard in the street.

**42:3** He will not break a crushed reed, nor extinguish a dim wick; in truth he will bring forth justice.

**42:4** He will not grow dim nor be crushed until he establishes justice on the earth; and the coastlands wait in hope for his instruction.

**42:5** This is what God, the LORD, says, the Creator of the heavens who stretches them out, the One who spreads out the earth and what springs from it, who gives breath to the people upon it and spirit to those who walk in it:

**42:6** "I, the LORD, have summoned you in righteousness; I will take hold of your hand, I will guard you, and I will give you as a covenant for the people, as a light to the nations,

**42:7** to open blind eyes, to bring a prisoner out of the dungeon, and from the prison house those who dwell in darkness.

**42:8** I am the LORD; that is My name. My glory I will not give to another, nor My praise to carved images.

**42:9** Look, the former things have come about, and new things I am declaring; before they sprout forth, I announce them to you."

**42:10** Sing a new song to the LORD, His praise from the end of the earth, you who go down to the sea and its fullness, you coastlands and their inhabitants.

**42:11** Let the wilderness and its cities raise their voice, the settlements where Kedar dwells; let the residents of Sela sing for joy; from the top of the mountains let them shout aloud.

**42:12** Let them ascribe glory to the LORD, and in the coastlands declare His praise.

**42:13** The LORD will go out like a mighty warrior; like a man of battles He will stir up zeal. He will shout a war cry, indeed He will roar aloud; against His enemies He will prove Himself mighty.

**42:14** From long ago I have kept silent; I remained quiet and restrained Myself. Like a woman giving birth I will cry out; I will gasp and pant together.

**42:15** I will make mountains and hills desolate, and all their vegetation I will dry up; I will turn rivers into islands, and pools I will dry up.

**42:16** And I will lead the blind along a road they have not known; on paths they have not known I will guide them. I will make the darkness before them into light, and crooked places into level ground. These are the things: I will do them, and I will not abandon them.

**42:17** They are turned back, they will be put to utter shame, those who trust in a carved image, who say to a cast image, "You are our gods."

**42:18** You deaf ones, hear! And you blind ones, look so that you may see!

**42:19** Who is blind except My servant, or deaf like My messenger whom I send? Who is blind like the one at peace, or blind like the servant of the LORD?

**42:20** Seeing many things, yet you do not observe them; opening the ears, yet he does not hear.

**42:21** The LORD was pleased, for the sake of His righteousness, to make instruction great and glorious.

**42:22** Yet it is a people plundered and despoiled; all of them are ensnared in holes, and in prison houses they have been hidden away. They have become plunder with no one to deliver, and spoil with no one saying, "Give it back!"

**42:23** Which of you will give ear to this, pay attention and hear for the time to come?

**42:24** Who gave Jacob over as spoil, and Israel to plunderers? Was it not the LORD, the One against whom we have sinned, in whose ways they were unwilling to walk, and to whose instruction they did not listen?

**42:25** So He poured out upon him the fury of His anger and the might of war; it set him ablaze on every side, yet he did not realize it; it burned him, yet he would not lay it to heart.

## Translator Notes

- **42:4** — The verbs yikheh (Qal imperfect of kahah, "grow dim") and yaruts (Qal imperfect of ratsats formed on the analogy of hollow verbs, "be crushed") deliberately echo the adjective kehah ("dim") and Qal passive participle ratsuts ("crushed") in 42:3.
- **42:19** — Meshullam is the Pual participle of shalem ("be complete, at peace"), variously understood as "one at peace," "covenant partner," or "dedicated one." The Masoretic Text repeats iwwer ("blind") in both halves of the second question.
- **42:20** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere reads ra'ot (a Qal infinitive absolute form of ra'ah, "seeing," paralleling the Qal infinitive absolute paqoah, "opening"), whereas the Ketiv represents the Qal perfect 2ms ra'ita ("you have seen"). The Masoretic Text also shifts from second-person "you observe" to third-person "he hears."
- **42:22** — Hapeah is a Hiphil infinitive absolute of puah (denominative from pah, "snare"), functioning here with passive force ("ensnared"), and bahurim is vocalized as the preposition be with the article plus hurim ("in holes"), paralleling "in prison houses."
- **42:24** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere reads limshissah ("as spoil/plunder," matching meshissah in 42:22), whereas the Ketiv could be read either as an orthographic variant of the same noun or as a Poel participle ("to the despoiler"). The Masoretic Text shifts from first-person plural "we have sinned" to third-person plural "they were unwilling... they did not listen."
