## Text

**29:1** Woe to Ariel, Ariel, the city where David encamped! Add year to year; let the festivals complete their cycle.

**29:2** Yet I will bring distress to Ariel, and there will be moaning and mourning, and she will be to Me like Ariel.

**29:3** And I will encamp in a circle against you, and I will besiege you with a garrison, and I will raise up siegeworks against you.

**29:4** Then you will be brought low; from the ground you will speak, and from the dust your utterance will sink low. Your voice will be like a ghost from the ground, and from the dust your utterance will whisper.

**29:5** Yet the throng of your foreign foes will become like fine dust, and the throng of the ruthless like passing chaff; and it will happen in an instant, suddenly.

**29:6** By the LORD of hosts she will be visited, with thunder and earthquake and great noise, whirlwind and tempest, and flame of devouring fire.

**29:7** And the throng of all the nations that wage war against Ariel—all who wage war against her and her stronghold, and those who distress her—will be like a dream, a vision of the night.

**29:8** And it will be just as when a hungry man dreams, and look, he is eating, but he wakes up and his soul is empty; and as when a thirsty man dreams, and look, he is drinking, but he wakes up, and look, he is faint and his soul is craving—so will be the throng of all the nations that wage war against Mount Zion.

**29:9** Delay and be astounded; blind yourselves and be blind! They are drunk, but not with wine; they stagger, but not with strong drink.

**29:10** For the LORD has poured out upon you a spirit of deep sleep; He has tightly closed your eyes—the prophets—and covered your heads—the seers.

**29:11** And the vision of all this has become to you like the words of a sealed scroll, which they give to one who knows how to read, saying, "Please read this," and he says, "I cannot, for it is sealed."

**29:12** Then the scroll is given to one who does not know how to read, saying, "Please read this," and he says, "I do not know how to read."

**29:13** And the Lord said, "Because this people draws near—with their mouth and with their lips they honor Me—yet they have kept their heart far from Me, and their fear of Me has become a taught commandment of men,

**29:14** therefore, look, I will once more deal wondrously with this people, wondrously and with wonder; and the wisdom of their wise men will perish, and the discernment of their discerning men will conceal itself."

**29:15** Woe to those who go deep to hide a plan from the LORD, and their deeds are in a dark place, and they say, "Who sees us, and who knows us?"

**29:16** What perversity of yours! Shall the potter be reckoned like clay, so that what is made would say of its maker, "He did not make me," or what is formed say of the one who formed it, "He does not understand"?

**29:17** Is it not yet a very little while before Lebanon will turn into an orchard, and the orchard will be reckoned as a forest?

**29:18** On that day the deaf will hear the words of a scroll, and out of gloom and darkness the eyes of the blind will see.

**29:19** The humble will increase joy in the LORD, and the needy among mankind will exult in the Holy One of Israel.

**29:20** For the ruthless one will be no more, and the scoffer will come to an end, and all who keep watch for iniquity will be cut off—

**29:21** those who make a person guilty by a word, and lay a snare for the one who reproves at the gate, and turn aside the righteous with emptiness.

**29:22** Therefore, this is what the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, says to the house of Jacob: "Jacob will not now be ashamed, nor will his face now grow pale.

**29:23** For when he sees his children, the work of My hands, in his midst, they will sanctify My name; they will sanctify the Holy One of Jacob and stand in awe of the God of Israel.

**29:24** And those who stray in spirit will know understanding, and those who grumble will learn instruction."

## Translator Notes

- **29:2** — "Ariel" plays on its dual resonance as "lion of God" and "altar hearth" (compare Ezek 43:15-16), following the assonant pair ta'aniyyah wa-'aniyyah ("moaning and mourning").
- **29:6** — The Niphal imperfect tippaqed is 3rd feminine singular ("she will be visited"), referring to Ariel and shifting from the 2nd feminine singular address of vv. 3-5 to the 3rd feminine singular pronouns of v. 7.
- **29:9** — The Hithpalpel imperative hitmahmehu ("delay/linger," from mhh) plays on the sound of the Qal imperative temahu ("be astounded," from tmh), and the second half shifts to Qal perfects ("they are drunk ... they stagger").
- **29:11** — Ketiv reads hassefer ("the scroll/writing") with the definite article; the qere omits the article (sefer, matching v. 12) in the idiom yodea sefer (literally "one who knows writing," that is, "knows how to read").
- **29:13** — Masoretic Hebrew points richaq as a Piel perfect ("they have kept far"), and the Pual participle melummadah ("taught/learned") modifies "commandment of men."
