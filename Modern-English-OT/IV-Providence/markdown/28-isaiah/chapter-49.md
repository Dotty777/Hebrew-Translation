## Text

**49:1** Hear me, coastlands, and give heed, peoples from afar: the LORD called me from the womb; from my mother's body He made mention of my name.

**49:2** He has made my mouth like a sharp sword; in the shadow of His hand He hid me. He set me as a polished arrow; in His quiver He concealed me.

**49:3** And He said to me, "You are My servant, Israel, in whom I will display My splendor."

**49:4** But I said, "For nothing I have toiled; for emptiness and vapor I have used up my strength. Yet certainly my justice is with the LORD, and my reward is with my God."

**49:5** And now the LORD says, the One who formed me from the womb to be a servant to Him, to bring Jacob back to Him and for Israel to be gathered to Him—and I am honored in the eyes of the LORD, and my God has become my strength—

**49:6** He says, "It is too light a matter for you to be My servant to raise up the tribes of Jacob and to bring back the preserved ones of Israel; I will also give you as a light to the nations, so that My salvation may extend to the end of the earth."

**49:7** This is what the LORD, the Redeemer of Israel and its Holy One, says to one despised in soul, to one loathed by a nation, to a servant of rulers: "Kings will see and rise up, princes also will bow low, for the sake of the LORD, who is faithful, the Holy One of Israel, and He has chosen you."

**49:8** This is what the LORD says: "In a time of favor I answered you, and on a day of salvation I helped you; I will preserve you and give you as a covenant of the people, to raise up the land, to allot the desolate inheritances,

**49:9** saying to the prisoners, 'Go out,' and to those in darkness, 'Reveal yourselves.' Along the roads they will graze, and on all bare heights will be their pasture.

**49:10** They will not be hungry and will not be thirsty, nor will scorching heat or sun strike them; for the One who has compassion on them will lead them, and beside springs of water He will guide them.

**49:11** And I will make all My mountains into a road, and My highways will be lifted high.

**49:12** Look, these will come from afar; and see, these from the north and from the west, and these from the land of Sinim."

**49:13** Ring out with joy, heavens, and exult, earth, and burst forth, mountains, into a joyful shout! For the LORD has comforted His people and will show compassion to His afflicted ones.

**49:14** Yet Zion said, "The LORD has abandoned me, and the Lord has forgotten me."

**49:15** Can a woman forget her nursing infant, without having compassion on the son of her womb? Even these may forget, yet I Myself will not forget you.

**49:16** See, on My palms I have engraved you; your walls are constantly before Me.

**49:17** Your sons hurry; those who demolished you and those who laid you waste will go out from you.

**49:18** Raise your eyes all around and see: they all gather together, they come to you. "As I live," declares the LORD, "you will surely wear all of them like an ornament, and you will bind them on like a bride."

**49:19** For your waste places and your desolate places and your demolished land—now indeed you will be too cramped for inhabitants, and those who swallowed you up will be far off.

**49:20** The children of your bereavement will still say in your ears, "The place is too cramped for me; give me space so that I may dwell."

**49:21** And you will say in your heart, "Who has borne these for me? For I was bereaved and barren, exiled and cast aside—and these, who brought them up? Look, I was left by myself; where were these?"

**49:22** This is what the Lord GOD says: "See, I will lift My hand to the nations, and to the peoples I will raise My banner; and they will bring your sons in their arms, and your daughters will be carried on their shoulders.

**49:23** Kings will become your guardians, and their princesses your nursing mothers. With their faces to the earth they will bow low to you and lick the dust of your feet. And you will know that I am the LORD; those who wait for Me will not be ashamed."

**49:24** Can plunder be taken from a mighty warrior, or can the captives of a righteous one be rescued?

**49:25** For this is what the LORD says: "Even the captives of a mighty warrior will be taken, and the plunder of a tyrant will be rescued; with the one who contends against you I Myself will contend, and your children I Myself will save.

**49:26** And I will cause your oppressors to eat their own flesh, and as with sweet wine they will grow drunk on their own blood; and all flesh will know that I am the LORD your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob."

## Translator Notes

- **49:5** — Following the Qere (read form) "to Him"; the Ketiv (written form) reads "not," which would yield "though Israel is not gathered."
- **49:6** — Following the Qere (read form), a Qal passive participle meaning "the preserved ones of"; the Ketiv (written form) is a related adjectival spelling with the same sense.
- **49:13** — Following the Qere (read form), a Qal imperative with the conjunction ("and burst forth"); the Ketiv (written form) lacks the conjunction and is an imperfect ("they will burst forth").
- **49:17** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes the noun as "your sons"; an alternative ancient tradition vocalizes the same consonants as "your builders."
- **49:24** — The Masoretic Text reads "a righteous one" (sometimes understood as a rightful captor), in contrast to "a tyrant" in verse 25.
