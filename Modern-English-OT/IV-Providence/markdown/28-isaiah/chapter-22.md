## Text

**22:1** A pronouncement concerning the valley of vision: What is wrong with you now, that you have all gone up to the rooftops,

**22:2** you who are full of clamor, noisy city, exultant town? Your fallen are not fallen by the sword, nor dead in battle.

**22:3** All your commanders have fled together; without a bow they were taken prisoner. All of your people who were found were taken prisoner together, though they had escaped to a distance.

**22:4** Therefore I said, "Look away from me; let me weep bitterly. Do not press to comfort me over the devastation of the daughter of my people."

**22:5** For the Lord GOD of hosts has a day of turmoil and trampling and perplexity in the valley of vision, a tearing down of the wall and a cry for help to the mountain.

**22:6** Elam took up the quiver, with chariots of men and horsemen, and Kir uncovered the shield.

**22:7** Your finest valleys became filled with chariots, and the horsemen firmly stationed themselves at the gate.

**22:8** He stripped away the covering of Judah, and on that day you looked to the weaponry of the House of the Forest.

**22:9** And you saw that the breaches of the city of David were numerous, and you gathered the waters of the lower pool.

**22:10** And you numbered the houses of Jerusalem, and you demolished the houses to fortify the wall.

**22:11** You also made a reservoir between the two walls for the waters of the old pool; yet you did not look to Him who made it, nor did you see Him who fashioned it long ago.

**22:12** On that day the Lord GOD of hosts called for weeping and lamentation, for baldness and for binding on sackcloth.

**22:13** Yet look, jubilation and gladness, killing cattle and slaughtering sheep, eating meat and drinking wine: "Eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!"

**22:14** And the LORD of hosts revealed Himself in my ears: "This iniquity will certainly not be atoned for you until you die," said the Lord GOD of hosts.

**22:15** This is what the Lord GOD of hosts says: "Go, approach this steward, to Shebna, who is over the household:

**22:16** 'What do you have here, and whom do you have here, that you have hewn out a tomb for yourself here, hewing out his tomb on high, chiseling a dwelling for himself in the rock?

**22:17** Look, the LORD is about to hurl you violently away, strong man, and He will wrap you up completely.

**22:18** He will surely wind you up tightly like a ball into a broad land. There you will die, and there will be the chariots of your glory, you disgrace of the house of your master.

**22:19** And I will push you out of your post, and from your station He will pull you down.

**22:20** And it will happen on that day that I will call for My servant Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah,

**22:21** and I will dress him in your tunic, and fasten your sash securely on him, and put your authority into his hand; and he will be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.

**22:22** And I will put the key of the house of David on his shoulder; he will open, and no one will close; he will close, and no one will open.

**22:23** I will drive him as a peg into a secure place, and he will become a throne of glory for the house of his father.

**22:24** And they will hang upon him all the glory of the house of his father, the offspring and the offshoots, every small vessel, from the bowls all the way to all the jars.'"

**22:25** "On that day," declares the LORD of hosts, "the peg driven into a secure place will come loose; it will be sheared off and fall, and the load that was on it will be cut off," for the LORD has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **22:5** — The phrase מְקַרְקַר קִר combines the Pilpel participle of קרר/קור ("tearing down") with the defective spelling קִר ("wall"), creating wordplay that also anticipates the proper name Kir (קִיר) in v. 6.
- **22:16** — The participles חֹצְבִי ("hewing out") and חֹקְקִי ("chiseling") carry the poetic binding vowel (hireq compaginis) rather than a first-person suffix, while the verse shifts abruptly to third-person suffixes ("his tomb," "for himself").
- **22:17** — The Qal participle and infinitive absolute עֹטְךָ עָטֹה (from עטה) mean "wrap you up completely" (preparing for the ball-winding image of v. 18), though some lexicons render the root here as "grasp firmly."
- **22:19** — The Masoretic Text shifts within the verse from first person (וַהֲדַפְתִּיךָ, "I will push you out") to third person (יֶהֶרְסֶךָ, "He will pull you down"), both referring to the LORD.
- **22:24** — Hebrew כְּבוֹד ("glory") also carries its root sense of "weight," linking the "throne of glory" (v. 23) with the "load" hanging on the peg (v. 25).
