## Text

**62:1** For the sake of Zion I will not remain silent, and for the sake of Jerusalem I will not be still, until her righteousness goes out like radiance, and her salvation like a torch that blazes.

**62:2** And nations will see your righteousness, and all kings your glory; and you will be called a new name, which the mouth of the LORD will specify.

**62:3** And you will be a crown of splendor in the hand of the LORD, and a royal turban in the palm of your God.

**62:4** No longer will it be said of you, "Forsaken," and of your land it will no longer be said, "Desolate"; instead, you will be called "My Delight Is in Her," and your land "Married," for the LORD delights in you, and your land will be married.

**62:5** For as a young man marries a virgin, your sons will marry you; and with the joy of a bridegroom over a bride, your God will rejoice over you.

**62:6** On your walls, Jerusalem, I have stationed watchmen; all day and all night, continually, they will not remain silent. You who remind the LORD, let there be no rest for you,

**62:7** and do not give Him rest until He firmly establishes and until He makes Jerusalem a praise in the earth.

**62:8** The LORD has sworn by His right hand and by the arm of His strength: "Surely I will no longer give your grain as food to your enemies, nor will foreigners drink your new wine for which you have toiled;

**62:9** rather, those who harvest it will eat it and praise the LORD, and those who gather it will drink it in My holy courts."

**62:10** Pass through, pass through the gates; clear the way of the people; build up, build up the highway, rid it of stones; raise a banner over the peoples.

**62:11** Look, the LORD has announced to the end of the earth: "Say to the daughter of Zion, 'See, your salvation is coming! Look, His reward is with Him, and His recompense is before Him.'"

**62:12** And they will call them "The Holy People, the Redeemed of the LORD," and you will be called "Sought After, a City Not Forsaken."

## Translator Notes

- **62:3** — Following the qere tseniph ('turban' or 'diadem'); the written ketiv reads tsenoph, a variant noun form with the same meaning.
- **62:4** — The symbolic names 'My Delight Is in Her' (Hebrew Hephzibah) and 'Married' (Hebrew Beulah) play directly on the verbs chaphets ('delights') and tiba'el ('will be married') at the end of the verse.
- **62:5** — The Masoretic text reads banayikh ('your sons'), picturing the restored inhabitants taking Zion as a bride; the same Hebrew consonants without vowels could also be vocalized bonayikh ('your Builder').
