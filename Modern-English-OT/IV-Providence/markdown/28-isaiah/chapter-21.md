## Text

**21:1** A pronouncement concerning the wilderness of the sea: Like stormwinds in the Negev sweeping by, it comes out of the wilderness, from a fearsome land.

**21:2** A severe vision has been declared to me: the betrayer betrays, and the devastator devastates. "Go up, Elam! Lay siege, Media! I have put an end to all her groaning."

**21:3** Therefore my loins are filled with writhing pain; pangs have gripped me like the pangs of a woman giving birth. I am doubled over, unable to hear; I am dismayed, unable to see.

**21:4** My heart reels; shuddering has terrified me. He has turned the twilight of my desire into trembling for me.

**21:5** Set the table, spread the rugs, eat, drink! Rise up, commanders, oil the shield!

**21:6** For the Lord spoke to me in this way: "Go, station the lookout; whatever he sees, let him declare.

**21:7** When he sees chariotry, a pair of horsemen, riders on donkeys, riders on camels, let him listen intently, with utmost attentiveness."

**21:8** Then he cried out like a lion, "On the watchtower, Lord, I stand continually by day, and at my guard post I am stationed all the nights.

**21:9** And look, here comes a chariot of men, a pair of horsemen!" Then he answered and said, "Babylon has fallen, has fallen! And all the carved images of her gods He has smashed to the ground."

**21:10** My threshed one and son of my threshing floor, what I have heard from the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, I have declared to you.

**21:11** A pronouncement concerning Dumah: Someone is calling to me from Seir, "Watchman, what is left of the night? Watchman, what is left of the night?"

**21:12** The watchman said, "Morning has come, and night as well. If you wish to inquire, inquire; return, come!"

**21:13** A pronouncement concerning Arabia: In the thicket in Arabia you will spend the night, caravans of Dedanites.

**21:14** Bring water to meet the thirsty, inhabitants of the land of Tema; greet the fugitive with his bread.

**21:15** For they have fled from swords, from the unsheathed sword, from the strung bow, and from the heaviness of battle.

**21:16** For this is what the Lord said to me: "Within one more year, like the years of a hired laborer, all the glory of Kedar will come to an end,

**21:17** and the remainder of the number of bows of the mighty men of the sons of Kedar will be few," for the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Hebrew אַנְחָתָה lacks a mappiq in the final ה, traditionally understood as "her groaning" (an unpointed 3fs suffix referring to the groaning caused by Babylon) or simply "groaning."
- **21:5** — The phrase צָפֹה הַצָּפִית pairs a Qal infinitive absolute with the hapax legomenon צָפִית, meaning either "spread the rugs" (from צפה II, "spread out") or "keep the watch" (from צפה I, "watch").
- **21:8** — The Masoretic Text reads וַיִּקְרָא אַרְיֵה, literally "and he cried out, a lion" (taken comparatively as "like a lion"); this translation follows the MT rather than emending אַרְיֵה to הָרֹאֶה ("the seer/lookout").
- **21:12** — The verbs אָתָה / אֵתָיוּ ("come") and תִּבְעָיוּן / בְּעָיוּ ("inquire") employ Aramaizing roots and forms fitting this pronouncement concerning Seir and Dumah.
