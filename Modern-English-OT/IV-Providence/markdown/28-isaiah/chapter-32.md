## Text

**32:1** See, a king will reign according to righteousness, and officials will rule according to justice.

**32:2** And each one will be like a refuge from the wind and a shelter from a downpour, like channels of water in a dry place, like the shade of a massive crag in a weary land.

**32:3** Then the eyes of those who see will not be shut, and the ears of those who hear will pay close attention.

**32:4** And the heart of the hasty will understand knowledge, and the tongue of stammerers will hasten to speak plainly.

**32:5** No longer will a fool be called noble, nor a scoundrel be spoken of as honorable.

**32:6** For a fool speaks folly, and his heart works iniquity, to practice godlessness and to speak error against the LORD, to leave the appetite of the hungry empty and to withhold drink from the thirsty.

**32:7** As for a scoundrel, his devices are evil; he devises wicked plots to ruin the poor with words of falsehood, even when the needy speaks what is just.

**32:8** But a noble man devises noble things, and upon noble things he stands.

**32:9** Women at ease, rise up, listen to my voice; self-assured daughters, give ear to my speech!

**32:10** In a year and some days you will tremble, you self-assured ones; for the vintage is finished, and the harvest gathering will not arrive.

**32:11** Shudder, you women at ease; tremble, you self-assured ones! Strip off and bare yourselves, and wrap your waists!

**32:12** They beat their breasts in mourning for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine.

**32:13** Upon the soil of my people thorns and briers will grow up, yes, upon all the houses of joy in the jubilant city.

**32:14** For the palace has been abandoned, the throng of the city has been deserted; the citadel and watchtower have become caves forever, a joy for wild donkeys, a pasture for flocks,

**32:15** until the Spirit is poured out on us from on high, and the wilderness turns into an orchard, and the orchard is reckoned as a forest.

**32:16** Then justice will dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness will abide in the orchard.

**32:17** And the work of righteousness will be peace, and the labor of righteousness will be calm and security forever.

**32:18** And my people will dwell in a home of peace, and in dwellings of security, and in tranquil resting places.

**32:19** Yet it will hail when the forest comes down, and in lowliness the city will be brought low.

**32:20** Blessed are you who sow by all waters, who send out the foot of the ox and the donkey.

## Translator Notes

- **32:7** — Translated per the Qere aniyyim ("the poor/afflicted") rather than the Ketiv anawim ("the humble"). Hebrew also plays on kelay ("scoundrel") and kelaw ("his devices").
- **32:11** — The four imperatives regazah, peshotah, orah, and hagorah take an unusual -ah form for feminine plural addressees, and "wrap your waists" (hagor al-halatsayim) elliptically omits sackcloth.
- **32:15** — Translated per the Qere wehakkarmel ("and the orchard," with the definite article) rather than the Ketiv wekarmel ("and an orchard," without the article).
- **32:19** — The hapax verb ubarad ("it will hail") plays on the sound of beredet ("when ... comes down"), followed by the cognate emphasis ubashshiflah tishpal ("in lowliness ... will be brought low").
