## Text

**59:1** Look, the hand of the LORD is not too short to save, nor is His ear too heavy to hear.

**59:2** Rather, your iniquities have been making a separation between you and your God, and your sins have concealed His face from you so that He does not hear.

**59:3** For your palms are defiled with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken falsehood, your tongue mutters injustice.

**59:4** No one brings suit in righteousness, and no one pleads a case in faithfulness; they trust in emptiness and speak lies; they conceive trouble and give birth to wrongdoing.

**59:5** They hatch adder's eggs and weave spider's webs; the one who eats from their eggs dies, and what is crushed splits open into a viper.

**59:6** Their webs will not become clothing, nor will they cover themselves with their works; their works are works of wrongdoing, and an act of violence is in their palms.

**59:7** Their feet run to evil, and they hurry to shed innocent blood; their thoughts are thoughts of wrongdoing; devastation and shattering are on their highways.

**59:8** They have not known the way of peace, and there is no justice in their tracks; they have twisted their paths for themselves; whoever treads on them knows no peace.

**59:9** Therefore justice is far from us, and righteousness does not reach us; we wait for light, and look, darkness; for brightness, yet we walk about in deep gloom.

**59:10** We grope for a wall like the blind, and like those without eyes we grope; we stumble at midday as in twilight; among the vigorous we are like the dead.

**59:11** All of us growl like bears, and like doves we moan continually; we wait for justice, and there is none; for salvation, yet it is far from us.

**59:12** For our rebellions have multiplied before You, and our sins testify against us; for our rebellions are with us, and as for our iniquities, we know them:

**59:13** rebelling against and denying the LORD, and turning back from following our God, speaking oppression and revolt, conceiving and muttering from the heart words of falsehood.

**59:14** And justice is turned backward, and righteousness stands far off; for truth has stumbled in the public square, and uprightness cannot enter.

**59:15** Thus truth is missing, and the one who turns aside from evil becomes prey. And the LORD saw it, and it was displeasing in His eyes that there was no justice.

**59:16** He saw that there was no man, and He was appalled that there was no one to intervene; then His own arm brought salvation for Him, and His righteousness itself upheld Him.

**59:17** He put on righteousness like a coat of mail, with a helmet of salvation on His head; He put on garments of vengeance as attire, and wrapped Himself in zeal like a cloak.

**59:18** According to deeds, accordingly He will repay: wrath to His adversaries, recompense to His enemies; to the coastlands He will repay recompense.

**59:19** And they will fear the name of the LORD from the west, and His glory from the rising of the sun; for He will come like a pent-up river that the breath of the LORD drives forward.

**59:20** And a Redeemer will come to Zion, and to those in Jacob who turn from rebellion, declares the LORD.

**59:21** "And as for Me, this is My covenant with them," says the LORD: "My Spirit who is upon you, and My words that I have placed in your mouth, will not depart from your mouth, nor from the mouth of your descendants, nor from the mouth of your descendants' descendants," says the LORD, "from this moment and forever."

## Translator Notes

- **59:3** — The verb nego'alu ("are defiled", from ga'al II) is a rare mixed form blending the Niphal prefix with a Pual vowel pattern.
- **59:10** — The noun ashmannim is a hapax legomenon of uncertain meaning, here derived from the root shaman ("stout, vigorous") to yield "among the vigorous."
- **59:18** — The rare double preposition ke'al ... ke'al ("according to ... accordingly") pairs with a wordplay between gemulot ("deeds") and gemul ("recompense").
- **59:19** — Taking tsar as "pent-up" and nosesah as the Polel of nus ("drive forward") yields a river driven by the LORD's breath; an alternative parsing takes tsar as "an adversary" whom the Spirit of the LORD drives back or raises a banner against.
