## Text

**25:1** LORD, You are my God; I will exalt You and give thanks to Your name, because You have done marvelous deeds, plans from long ago, in faithfulness and sureness.

**25:2** For You have turned a city into a heap, a fortified town into a ruin; the citadel of foreigners is no longer a city, and forever it will not be rebuilt.

**25:3** Therefore a strong people will honor You; the city of ruthless nations will fear You.

**25:4** For You have been a fortress for the poor, a fortress for the needy in his distress, a refuge from the rainstorm, and shade from the heat; for the blast of the ruthless is like a rainstorm against a wall.

**25:5** Like heat in a parched land, You subdue the clamor of foreigners; as heat by the shade of a cloud, the song of the ruthless is brought low.

**25:6** And the LORD of hosts will prepare for all the peoples on this mountain a banquet of rich fare, a banquet of aged wine: rich fare full of marrow, aged wine thoroughly refined.

**25:7** And He will swallow up on this mountain the surface of the shroud that enshrouds all the peoples, and the veil that is spread over all the nations.

**25:8** He will swallow up death forever, and the Lord GOD will wipe tears from every face; and the reproach of His people He will remove from the entire earth, because the LORD has spoken.

**25:9** And it will be said on that day, "Look, this is our God; we waited for Him so that He would save us. This is the LORD; we waited for Him; let us exult and be glad in His salvation!"

**25:10** For the hand of the LORD will rest on this mountain, while Moab will be trampled down in his own place, just as straw is trampled down in a dung pit.

**25:11** And he will spread out his hands inside it just as a swimmer spreads out his hands to swim, but He will bring down his pride along with the craftiness of his hands.

**25:12** And the lofty fortification of your walls He will humble, bring low, and bring down to the ground, to the very dust.

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — Hebrew ya'aneh is the Qal imperfect of anah ("be bowed down, humbled"), conveying that the song of the ruthless is brought low just as scorching heat is subdued by a cloud's shade.
- **25:10** — Following the qere bemo (poetic form of the preposition "in") for "in a dung pit"; the ketiv reads bemei ("in the waters of").
- **25:11** — Hebrew orbot yadav ("craftiness of his hands") uses a hapax legomenon from the root arab ("lie in wait, scheme"), denoting the artifice or cunning skill of Moab's hands.
