## Text

**23:1** A pronouncement concerning Tyre: Wail, ships of Tarshish, for it has been devastated, with no house and no entry! From the land of Kittim it has been revealed to them.

**23:2** Be silent, inhabitants of the coastland, whom the merchants of Sidon, crossing the sea, have filled.

**23:3** And across many waters the grain of Shihor, the harvest of the Nile, was her revenue; and she became the marketplace of nations.

**23:4** Be ashamed, Sidon, for the sea has spoken, the stronghold of the sea, saying, "I have not been in labor, and I have not given birth; I have not raised young men, nor reared virgins."

**23:5** When the news comes to Egypt, they will writhe in anguish at the news of Tyre.

**23:6** Cross over to Tarshish! Wail, inhabitants of the coastland!

**23:7** Is this your exultant one, whose antiquity is from ancient days, whose feet used to carry her far off to sojourn?

**23:8** Who has purposed this against Tyre, who bestows crowns, whose merchants are princes, whose traders are the esteemed of the earth?

**23:9** The LORD of hosts has purposed it, to profane the pride of all splendor, to bring into contempt all the esteemed of the earth.

**23:10** Overflow your land like the Nile, daughter of Tarshish; there is no longer any restraint.

**23:11** He has stretched out His hand over the sea; He has caused kingdoms to tremble. The LORD has commanded concerning Canaan to destroy her strongholds.

**23:12** And He said, "You will exult no longer, oppressed virgin daughter of Sidon. Rise up, cross over to Kittim; even there you will have no rest."

**23:13** Look at the land of the Chaldeans! This is the people that is no more; Assyria appointed her for desert creatures. They raised up their siege towers, stripped bare her citadels; it made her a ruin.

**23:14** Wail, ships of Tarshish, for your stronghold has been devastated.

**23:15** And it will come about on that day that Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, like the days of one king. At the end of seventy years, it will be with Tyre as in the song of the prostitute:

**23:16** "Take a lyre, walk around the city, forgotten prostitute! Play skillfully, sing many songs, so that you may be remembered."

**23:17** And it will come about at the end of seventy years that the LORD will visit Tyre, and she will return to her hire and prostitute herself with all the kingdoms of the earth upon the face of the ground.

**23:18** Yet her merchandise and her hire will be holy to the LORD; it will not be stored up nor hoarded, for her merchandise will belong to those who dwell before the LORD, for eating to satisfaction and for stately clothing.

## Translator Notes

- **23:1** — Hebrew מִבַּיִת מִבּוֹא uses privative מִן twice, literally "from a house, from entering," indicating Tyre is devastated so that neither house nor entry/harbor remains.
- **23:10** — The Masoretic Text reads עִבְרִי ("overflow/pass over," Qal imperative of עבר) rather than the Septuagint's "till"; the rare noun מֵזַח denotes a "belt" or "girdle" (cf. Ps 109:19), used figuratively here for "restraint."
- **23:12** — Ketiv reads כתיים and Qere reads כִּתִּים, an orthographic variant for the same place name, "Kittim" (Cyprus).
- **23:13** — Qere reads בַחוּנָיו ("its/their siege towers," with 3ms suffix referring to Assyria) where Ketiv reads בחיניו; the clause זֶה הָעָם לֹא הָיָה is literally "this is the people that was not," expressing that it is no more.
