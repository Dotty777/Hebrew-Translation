## Text

**1:1** The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz that he saw regarding Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.

**1:2** Listen, heavens, and give ear, earth, for the LORD has spoken: "I have raised sons and exalted them, yet they have rebelled against Me.

**1:3** An ox knows its owner, and a donkey its master's feeding trough; Israel does not know, My people do not discern."

**1:4** Woe, sinful nation, a people heavy with iniquity, seed of evildoers, sons who act corruptly! They have abandoned the LORD, they have spurned the Holy One of Israel, they have turned backward in estrangement.

**1:5** Why will you still be struck? You keep adding defection! The entire head is sick, and the entire heart is ailing.

**1:6** From the sole of the foot to the head there is no soundness in it: gashes, bruises, and fresh wounds; they have not been pressed out, nor bound up, nor softened with oil.

**1:7** Your land is a desolation; your cities are burned with fire; your soil—in your presence foreigners are devouring it, and it is a desolation like an overthrow by foreigners.

**1:8** And Daughter Zion is left behind like a booth in a vineyard, like a lodging hut in a cucumber field, like a city under siege.

**1:9** Had the LORD of hosts not left behind a few survivors for us, we would have become like Sodom; we would have resembled Gomorrah.

**1:10** Listen to the word of the LORD, leaders of Sodom; give ear to the instruction of our God, people of Gomorrah.

**1:11** "What is the abundance of your sacrifices to Me?" says the LORD. "I have had My fill of burnt offerings of rams and the fat of fattened beasts, and I take no delight in the blood of bulls, lambs, and male goats.

**1:12** When you come to appear in My presence, who has required this from your hand, to trample My courts?

**1:13** Do not keep bringing futile offerings; incense is an abomination to Me. New moon and Sabbath, the summoning of a convocation—I cannot bear iniquity and a solemn gathering.

**1:14** My soul hates your new moons and your appointed festivals; they have become a burden upon Me; I have grown weary of carrying them.

**1:15** And when you spread out your palms, I will hide My eyes from you; even though you multiply prayer, I am not listening; your hands are filled with bloodshed.

**1:16** Wash yourselves, purify yourselves; remove the evil of your practices from before My eyes; stop doing evil.

**1:17** Learn to do good; seek justice, correct the oppressor; vindicate the fatherless, plead the case of the widow."

**1:18** "Come now, and let us settle the matter," says the LORD. "Even if your sins are like scarlet, they will turn white like snow; even if they are red like crimson, they will become like wool.

**1:19** If you are willing and obey, you will eat the good things of the land;

**1:20** but if you refuse and rebel, by the sword you will be devoured," for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.

**1:21** How the faithful city has become a prostitute—she who was full of justice! Righteousness used to lodge in her, yet now murderers.

**1:22** Your silver has turned into dross; your choice drink is diluted with water.

**1:23** Your officials are rebels and associates of thieves; every one of them loves a bribe and pursues payoffs. They do not vindicate the fatherless, and the case of the widow does not come to them.

**1:24** Therefore the Lord, the LORD of hosts, the Mighty One of Israel, declares: "Ah, I will gain relief from My adversaries and avenge Myself against My enemies.

**1:25** And I will turn My hand back against you; I will refine away your dross as with lye, and remove all your alloy.

**1:26** And I will restore your judges as at first, and your advisers as at the outset; after this you will be called City of Righteousness, Faithful City."

**1:27** Zion will be redeemed through justice, and those in her who turn back, through righteousness.

**1:28** Yet shattering awaits rebels and sinners together, and those who abandon the LORD will perish.

**1:29** For they will be ashamed because of the terebinths that you desired, and you will be disgraced because of the gardens that you chose.

**1:30** For you will become like a terebinth whose foliage is withering, and like a garden that has no water.

**1:31** And the mighty one will become tinder, and his work a spark; both of them will burn together, with no one to extinguish them.

## Translator Notes

- **1:7** — The Masoretic Text repeats zarim ("foreigners") in the final clause ("an overthrow by foreigners"), matching the preceding line; the noun mahpekah ("overthrow") is also the term commonly associated with the destruction of Sodom (cf. v. 9).
- **1:12** — The Masoretic vocalization points lera'ot as a Niphal infinitive construct of ra'ah ("to appear") with panai ("My face/presence") as an adverbial accusative, the traditional pious pointing of the idiom for appearing at the sanctuary.
- **1:17** — The Piel imperative ashru ("set straight/correct") governs the rare noun chamos, which is understood either as an active agent ("oppressor/ruthless one") or passively as "the oppressed" (yielding "relieve the oppressed").
- **1:20** — Hebrew creates a sharp wordplay with v. 19 using the same verb akal ("eat/devour"): Qal active to'kelu ("you will eat") in v. 19 is answered by Pual passive te'ukkelu ("you will be devoured") with instrumental cherev ("by the sword") in v. 20.
- **1:29** — The Masoretic Text shifts mid-sentence from third-person plural ("they will be ashamed") to second-person plural ("you desired ... you will be disgraced ... you chose"), a classic prophetic shift of person retained here.
