## Text

**58:1** Call out with full throat, do not hold back; raise your voice like a ram's horn, and announce to My people their rebellion, and to the house of Jacob their sins.

**58:2** Yet day by day they seek Me, and take pleasure in knowing My ways, like a nation that has done righteousness and has not abandoned the justice of its God; they ask Me for righteous judgments; they take pleasure in the nearness of God.

**58:3** "Why have we fasted, and You have not seen? We have afflicted our souls, and You do not take notice." Look, on the day of your fast you find pleasure, and you drive all your workers hard.

**58:4** See, you fast for dispute and strife, and to strike with a fist of wickedness. You do not fast as on this day to cause your voice to be heard on high.

**58:5** Is a fast that I choose to be like this, a day for a man to afflict his soul? Is it to bow down his head like a reed, and spread out sackcloth and ashes? Will you call this a fast, and a day of favor to the LORD?

**58:6** Is this not the fast that I choose: to untie the fetters of wickedness, to undo the straps of the yoke, and to send the crushed away free, and that you snap every yoke?

**58:7** Is it not to break your bread for the hungry, and bring the homeless poor into the house; when you see someone naked, to cover him, and not to hide yourself from your own flesh?

**58:8** Then your light will burst open like the dawn, and your healing will sprout swiftly; your righteousness will walk ahead of you; the glory of the LORD will serve as your rear guard.

**58:9** Then you will call, and the LORD will answer; you will cry for help, and He will say, "Here I am." If you remove the yoke from your midst, the pointing of the finger, and speaking wrongdoing,

**58:10** and extend your soul to the hungry, and satisfy the afflicted soul, then your light will rise in the darkness, and your gloom will be like midday.

**58:11** And the LORD will lead you continually, and satisfy your soul in scorched places, and invigorate your bones; you will become like a well-watered garden, and like a source of water whose waters never fail.

**58:12** Those from among you will rebuild the ancient ruins; you will raise up the foundations of generation upon generation; and you will be called Mender of the Breach, Restorer of Paths to Dwell In.

**58:13** If you turn your foot back from the Sabbath, from doing your own pleasures on My holy day, and call the Sabbath a delight, the holy day of the LORD honorable, and honor it by not doing your own ways, not finding your own pleasure, or speaking mere words,

**58:14** then you will take delight in the LORD, and I will make you ride upon the heights of the earth, and I will feed you with the inheritance of Jacob your father; for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **58:8** — The Qal verb ya'asfekha (from asaf, "gather") is used idiomatically for gathering in stragglers or serving as a military rear guard.
- **58:10** — The Masoretic Text reads nafshekha ("your soul"), idiomatically expressing offering oneself or what one's own appetite desires to the hungry.
- **58:13** — The privative sense of turning back from the Sabbath carries over to the infinitive asot ("from doing"); the concluding idiom dabber davar (lit. "speaking a word") denotes everyday talk or business chatter.
- **58:14** — Ketiv bmvty (plene spelling with waw) vs. Qere bamotei (construct vocalization without waw); both denote "the heights of" or "high places of."
