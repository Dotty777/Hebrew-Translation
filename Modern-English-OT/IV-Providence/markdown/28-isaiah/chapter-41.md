## Text

**41:1** Keep silent before Me, coastlands, and let the peoples regain their strength. Let them step forward, then let them speak; together let us draw near for judgment.

**41:2** Who has roused one from the east, whom He calls in righteousness to follow Him? He hands over nations before him and subdues kings; He makes them like dust with his sword, like blown stubble with his bow.

**41:3** He pursues them and passes on in safety, along a path he has not gone with his feet.

**41:4** Who has acted and done this, summoning the generations from the beginning? I, the LORD, am the first, and with the last ones, I am He.

**41:5** The coastlands saw and became afraid; the ends of the earth shuddered; they drew near and came.

**41:6** Each man helps his neighbor, and to his brother he says, "Be strong!"

**41:7** The artisan strengthens the metalworker, and the one who smooths with the hammer strengthens the one who strikes the anvil, saying about the soldering, "It is good"; and he secures it with nails so that it will not topple.

**41:8** Yet you, Israel, My servant, Jacob whom I chose, offspring of Abraham, My friend,

**41:9** you whom I took hold of from the ends of the earth and summoned from its remote corners, and I said to you, "You are My servant; I chose you and did not reject you."

**41:10** Do not be afraid, for I am with you; do not look about in alarm, for I am your God. I will make you strong; indeed, I will help you; indeed, I will support you with the right hand of My righteousness.

**41:11** Look, all who are furious with you will be ashamed and disgraced; the men who contend with you will be like nothing and will perish.

**41:12** You will search for them and not find them, the men who quarrel with you; the men who wage war against you will be like nothing and like nonexistence.

**41:13** For I am the LORD your God, who grasps your right hand, who says to you, "Do not be afraid; I Myself will help you."

**41:14** Do not be afraid, worm of Jacob, men of Israel; I Myself will help you, declares the LORD, and your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel.

**41:15** Look, I have made you into a sharp new threshing sledge with many teeth; you will thresh mountains and crush them fine, and the hills you will make like chaff.

**41:16** You will winnow them, and a wind will lift them away, and a whirlwind will scatter them. Yet you will exult in the LORD; in the Holy One of Israel you will boast.

**41:17** The afflicted and the needy search for water, and there is none; their tongue is dried up with thirst. I, the LORD, will answer them; I, the God of Israel, will not abandon them.

**41:18** I will open rivers on bare heights and fountains in the middle of valleys; I will turn the wilderness into a pool of water and parched land into wellsprings of water.

**41:19** I will place in the wilderness the cedar, the acacia, the myrtle, and the oil tree; I will set in the desert plain the cypress, the elm, and the box tree together,

**41:20** so that they may see and know, and lay it to heart and perceive together, that the hand of the LORD has done this, and the Holy One of Israel has created it.

**41:21** "Bring near your case," says the LORD. "Present your strong arguments," says the King of Jacob.

**41:22** Let them bring them forward and declare to us what will happen. As for the former things, what are they? Declare them, so that we may set our minds on them and know their outcome; or let us hear the things to come.

**41:23** Declare the things that are coming afterward, so that we may know that you are gods; indeed, do good or do harm, so that we may be alarmed and look on together.

**41:24** Look, you are less than nothing, and your work is less than nothingness; an abomination is the one who chooses you.

**41:25** I have roused one from the north, and he has come; from the rising of the sun he will call on My name. He will come upon governors as upon mortar, just as a potter tramples clay.

**41:26** Who declared this from the beginning, so that we might know, and from earlier times, so that we might say, "He is right"? Indeed, there is no one who declares; indeed, there is no one who proclaims; indeed, there is no one who hears your words.

**41:27** I first said to Zion, "Look, here they are!" and to Jerusalem I will give one who brings good news.

**41:28** I look, and there is no one; and among these there is no counselor who, when I question them, can return a reply.

**41:29** Look, all of them are futility; their deeds are nothing; their cast images are wind and emptiness.

## Translator Notes

- **41:2** — The Hebrew clause "righteousness calls/meets him at his foot" is syntactically ambiguous: it may take tsedeq adverbially with God as subject ("whom He calls in righteousness to follow Him," cf. 42:6; 45:13) or as subject ("whom righteousness meets at every step"). The verb yard is an apocopated form of radah ("subdue, have dominion over").
- **41:10** — The verb tishta is the Hithpael jussive of sha'ah ("look about in alarm" or "gaze around anxiously"; the same stem recurs in 41:23). "The right hand of My righteousness" preserves the literal Hebrew construct chain.
- **41:23** — Ketiv/Qere: the Qere reads wenir'eh (Qal imperfect 1cp of ra'ah, "and we will see/look"), which is followed here; the Ketiv lacks the final he and could be read either as a shortened spelling of ra'ah or as defective wenira from yare ("and we will fear").
- **41:24** — The noun epha ("nothingness") is a hapax legomenon parallel to ayin ("nothing"), generally understood as a poetic variant of ephes ("nothingness").
- **41:27** — The opening clause in the Masoretic Text is elliptically compressed (literally, "First to Zion: 'Look, here they are!'"), leaving the verb of speaking implied.
