## Text

**46:1** Bel has bent down, Nebo stoops; their idols are put on beasts and on livestock; what you carry is loaded up, a burden for the weary animal.

**46:2** They stooped, they bent down together; they were unable to rescue the burden, and they themselves have gone off into captivity.

**46:3** Listen to Me, house of Jacob, and all the remnant of the house of Israel, who have been borne from birth, who have been carried from the womb:

**46:4** And until old age I am He, and until gray hair I Myself will bear you; I made you, and I will carry you; I Myself will bear you and rescue you.

**46:5** To whom will you liken Me and make Me equal, and compare Me, so that we should be alike?

**46:6** Those who pour out gold from a bag and weigh out silver on the scale hire a metalworker, and he makes it into a god; they fall down, yes, they bow in worship.

**46:7** They lift it onto a shoulder, they bear it, and they set it down in its place, and it stands there; from its place it does not move. Even if one cries out to it, it does not answer; from his distress it does not save him.

**46:8** Remember this and be resolute; bring it back to heart, you rebels.

**46:9** Remember the former things from long ago, because I am God, and there is no other—God, and there is none like Me,

**46:10** declaring the outcome from the beginning, and from earliest times things that have not been done, saying, "My counsel will stand, and all My delight I will carry out,"

**46:11** calling a bird of prey from the east, from a distant land the man of My counsel. Indeed I have spoken, indeed I will bring it about; I have formed it, indeed I will do it.

**46:12** Listen to Me, you stubborn of heart, who are far from righteousness:

**46:13** I have brought My righteousness near; it will not be far off, and My salvation will not delay. And I will grant salvation in Zion, for Israel My splendor.

## Translator Notes

- **46:8** — The Hithpolel imperative הִתְאֹשָׁשׁוּ ("be resolute") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible; its sense is generally connected with standing firm or taking courage.
- **46:11** — Qere עֲצָתִי ("My counsel") is translated, matching עֲצָתִי in 46:10; Ketiv עצתו reads "his counsel."
- **46:12** — Hebrew אַבִּירֵי לֵב literally means "strong of heart," used here idiomatically for obstinacy ("stubborn of heart").
