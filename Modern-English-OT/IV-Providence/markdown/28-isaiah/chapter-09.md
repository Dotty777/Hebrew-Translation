## Text

**9:1** The people walking in darkness have seen a great light; on those living in a land of death's shadow, light has shone.

**9:2** You have multiplied the nation; for it You have made the joy great. They rejoice before You like the joy in the harvest, just as they exult when they divide plunder.

**9:3** For You have shattered the yoke of its burden, and the staff across its shoulder, the rod of the one oppressing it, just as on the day of Midian.

**9:4** For every boot of one tramping in tumult, and every cloak rolled in blood, will be for burning, food for fire.

**9:5** For a child has been born to us, a son has been given to us; and the dominion will be on his shoulder, and his name will be called Wonder-Counselor, Mighty God, Father of Eternity, Prince of Peace.

**9:6** Of the increase of the dominion and of peace there will be no end, upon the throne of David and over his kingdom, to establish it and to sustain it with justice and with righteousness, from now on and forever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.

**9:7** The Lord sent a word against Jacob, and it has fallen upon Israel.

**9:8** And the whole people will know it—Ephraim and those dwelling in Samaria—saying in pride and haughtiness of heart,

**9:9** "Bricks have fallen, yet we will build with hewn stone; sycamores have been felled, yet we will substitute cedars for them."

**9:10** So the LORD raised high the adversaries of Rezin against him and spurred on his enemies.

**9:11** Aram from the east and the Philistines from the west—they devoured Israel with wide-open mouth. Despite all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still outstretched.

**9:12** Yet the people have not turned back to Him who struck them, and they have not sought the LORD of hosts.

**9:13** So the LORD cut off from Israel head and tail, palm frond and bulrush, in a single day.

**9:14** The elder and the distinguished man is the head, and the prophet teaching falsehood is the tail.

**9:15** And those guiding this people have become ones who lead astray, and those guided by them are swallowed up.

**9:16** Therefore the Lord will not rejoice over their young men, nor will He show compassion to their orphans and their widows; for each of them is godless and practices evil, and every mouth utters folly. Despite all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still outstretched.

**9:17** For wickedness has burned like fire; it devours briers and thorns; it ignites among the thickets of the forest, and they billow upward in a swelling of smoke.

**9:18** Through the fury of the LORD of hosts, the land has been scorched, and the people have become like food for fire; no one spares his brother.

**9:19** One slices off on the right and remains hungry, and eats on the left, yet they are not filled; each person consumes the flesh of his own arm.

**9:20** Manasseh consumes Ephraim, and Ephraim consumes Manasseh, and both together are against Judah. Despite all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still outstretched.

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — Ketiv reads לא ("not"), while Qere, translated here, reads לוֹ ("for it"), referring to the nation.
- **9:4** — סְאוֹן סֹאֵן ("boot of one tramping") is a cognate noun and Qal participle occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible, understood via Akkadian and Aramaic cognates as a marching boot or sandal.
- **9:5** — וַיִּקְרָא שְׁמוֹ uses a Qal active verb ("and one will call his name" or "and He will call his name"), often rendered passively; the titles פֶּלֶא יוֹעֵץ (lit. "Wonder of a Counselor") and אֲבִי־עַד (lit. "Father of Eternity") pair nouns in apposition or construct.
- **9:6** — Ketiv preserves an anomalous final closed mem (ם) in the middle of the word, read by Qere as לְמַרְבֵּה ("of the increase"), derived from the root רבה.
- **9:18** — נֶעְתַּם ("has been scorched") is a Niphal perfect of עתם occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible; depending on Semitic cognates it is interpreted either as "scorched" or "darkened."
