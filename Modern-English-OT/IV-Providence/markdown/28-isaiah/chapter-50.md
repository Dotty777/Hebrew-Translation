## Text

**50:1** This is what the LORD says: "Where is your mother's document of divorce with which I sent her away? Or who among My creditors is it to whom I sold you? Look, because of your iniquities you were sold, and because of your transgressions your mother was sent away.

**50:2** Why did I come and no one was there? Why did I call and no one answered? Has My hand truly grown too short to ransom, or is there no strength in Me to deliver? See, with My rebuke I dry up the sea; I turn rivers into a wilderness; their fish stink because there is no water, and they die from thirst.

**50:3** I clothe the heavens in darkness, and sackcloth I set as their covering."

**50:4** The Lord GOD has given me the tongue of disciples, so that I may know how to sustain the weary with a word. He awakens morning by morning; He awakens my ear to hear like disciples.

**50:5** The Lord GOD opened my ear, and I did not rebel; I did not draw back.

**50:6** My back I gave to those who strike, and my cheeks to those who pluck the beard; my face I did not hide from humiliation and spitting.

**50:7** Yet the Lord GOD helps me; for this reason I have not been humiliated; for this reason I have made my face like flint, and I know that I will not be ashamed.

**50:8** Near is the One who vindicates me. Who will contend with me? Let us stand together. Who is my adversary at law? Let him approach me.

**50:9** Look, the Lord GOD helps me; who is the one who will declare me guilty? See, all of them will wear out like a garment; a moth will devour them.

**50:10** Who among you fears the LORD, heeding the voice of His servant? Whoever walks in darkness and has no brightness, let him trust in the name of the LORD and lean upon his God.

**50:11** Look, all of you who kindle a fire, who arm yourselves with firebrands: walk in the flame of your fire and among the firebrands you have set burning! From My hand this will happen to you: in anguish you will lie down.

## Translator Notes

- **50:4** — The Hebrew infinitive rendered "to sustain" occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible and is understood from comparative Semitic evidence and context to mean "to support, help, sustain."
- **50:8** — Literally "the master of my legal case," a Hebrew forensic idiom for an adversary or opponent in a lawsuit.
- **50:11** — The Piel participle literally means "girding yourselves with" or "equipping yourselves with" firebrands, picturing hostile adversaries arming themselves with flaming weapons.
