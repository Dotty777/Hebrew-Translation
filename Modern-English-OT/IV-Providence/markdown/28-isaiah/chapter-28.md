## Text

**28:1** Woe to the crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim, and to the fading flower, the beauty of its splendor, which is at the head of the rich valley of those overcome with wine!

**28:2** Look, the Lord has a strong and mighty one; like a downpour of hail, a storm of destruction, like a downpour of massive sweeping waters, he casts it down to the ground by hand.

**28:3** Underfoot it will be trampled, the crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim.

**28:4** And the fading flower, the beauty of its splendor, which is at the head of the rich valley, will be like a first-ripe fig before summer, which the observer sees, and while it is still in his palm he swallows it.

**28:5** On that day the LORD of hosts will be a crown of beauty and a diadem of splendor for the remnant of His people,

**28:6** and a spirit of justice for the one who sits in judgment, and strength for those who turn back the battle to the gate.

**28:7** And these also reel with wine and stagger with strong drink: priest and prophet reel with strong drink, they are swallowed up by wine, they stagger from strong drink; they reel in vision, they stumble in judgment.

**28:8** For all tables are full of vomit and filth; there is no space left.

**28:9** "To whom will he teach knowledge, and to whom will he explain the message? Those weaned from milk, those taken away from the breasts?

**28:10** For it is command after command, command after command, line after line, line after line, a little there, a little there."

**28:11** For with stammering speech and with another tongue He will speak to this people,

**28:12** to whom He said, "This is the place of rest; give rest to the weary, and this is repose," yet they were unwilling to listen.

**28:13** So the word of the LORD will become to them, "Command after command, command after command, line after line, line after line, a little there, a little there," so that they may go and stumble backward, and be broken, ensnared, and captured.

**28:14** Therefore, hear the word of the LORD, men of scoffing, rulers of this people who are in Jerusalem.

**28:15** For you have said, "We have made a covenant with death, and with Sheol we have made an agreement; when the sweeping scourge passes through, it will not reach us, for we have made falsehood our refuge, and in deceit we have hidden ourselves."

**28:16** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says: "Look, I have laid a stone in Zion, a proven stone, a precious cornerstone, a firmly laid foundation: the one who trusts will not act in haste.

**28:17** And I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the plumb line; hail will sweep away the refuge of falsehood, and waters will overflow the hiding place.

**28:18** Then your covenant with death will be annulled, and your agreement with Sheol will not stand; when the sweeping scourge passes through, you will be trampled down by it.

**28:19** As often as it passes through, it will seize you; for morning after morning it will pass through, by day and by night, and it will be nothing but terror to understand the message.

**28:20** For the bed is too short to stretch out on, and the covering is too narrow when one wraps up in it."

**28:21** For the LORD will rise up as on Mount Perazim; He will rage as in the valley at Gibeon, to do His deed—strange is His deed—and to perform His work—alien is His work.

**28:22** And now, do not scoff, or your bonds will grow stronger; for I have heard of a complete and decreed destruction from the Lord GOD of hosts upon the whole earth.

**28:23** Give ear and hear my voice; pay attention and hear my speech.

**28:24** Does the plowman plow all day long to sow, opening and harrowing his ground?

**28:25** When he has leveled its surface, does he not scatter black cumin and broadcast cumin, and plant wheat in rows, barley in its marked-off place, and spelt along its border?

**28:26** For He instructs him in the proper way; his God teaches him.

**28:27** For black cumin is not threshed with a threshing sledge, nor is a cartwheel rolled over cumin; rather, black cumin is beaten out with a stick, and cumin with a rod.

**28:28** Is bread grain crushed? Indeed, he does not keep threshing it forever; when he drives the wheel of his cart and his horses, he does not crush it.

**28:29** This also comes from the LORD of hosts; He makes counsel wonderful and makes sound wisdom great.

## Translator Notes

- **28:10** — The Hebrew tsav la-tsav, tsav la-tsav, qav la-qav, qav la-qav uses staccato monosyllabic wordplay ("command after command ... line after line") that mocks the prophet's teaching while mimicking childish or drunken repetition.
- **28:15** — Two ketiv/qere variants follow the qere: qere shot ("scourge," matching v. 18) for ketiv shayit, and qere Qal imperfect ya'avor ("passes through") for ketiv Qal perfect avar.
- **28:16** — Masoretic Hebrew points the verb as a Piel perfect yissad ("has laid") after hineni ("look, I"). The final clause lo yachish literally means "will not hasten," that is, will not act or flee in panic.
- **28:28** — Opening with an unmarked rhetorical question, the verse repeats the root dqq ("crush": Hophal imperfect yudaq and Qal imperfect yeduqqennu) and uses the unusual infinitive absolute spelling adosh (for dosh) to express continuous threshing.
