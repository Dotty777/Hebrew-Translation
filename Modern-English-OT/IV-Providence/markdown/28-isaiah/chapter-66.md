## Text

**66:1** This is what the LORD says: "The heavens are My throne, and the earth is the footstool for My feet. Where is a house that you would build for Me, and where is a place of My rest?

**66:2** My hand made all these things, and all these things came into existence," declares the LORD. "Yet to this person I will look: to one who is humble and stricken in spirit, and who trembles at My word.

**66:3** One who slaughters an ox is like one who strikes down a man; one who sacrifices a lamb, like one who snaps a dog's neck; one who offers up a grain offering, like offering swine's blood; one who offers a memorial of frankincense, like one who blesses an idol. Just as they have chosen their own ways, and their soul takes pleasure in their detestable things,

**66:4** so I too will choose their afflictions and bring the things they fear upon them; because I called and there was no one answering, I spoke and they did not listen; instead, they did evil in My eyes and chose that in which I took no pleasure."

**66:5** Listen to the word of the LORD, you who tremble at His word: "Your brothers who hate you, who cast you out for the sake of My name, have said, 'May the LORD show His glory, so that we may witness your joy!' Yet they themselves will be shamed."

**66:6** A sound of tumult from the city, a voice from the temple—the voice of the LORD repaying retribution to His enemies!

**66:7** Before she writhed in labor, she gave birth; before a birth pang came to her, she delivered a male child.

**66:8** Who has heard of something like this? Who has seen things like these? Can a land be brought forth in a single day, or can a nation be born at one stroke? For as soon as Zion writhed in labor, she also gave birth to her sons.

**66:9** "Would I bring to the moment of birth and not cause delivery?" says the LORD. "Or would I, the One who causes delivery, close the womb?" says your God.

**66:10** Rejoice with Jerusalem and exult in her, all who love her; be joyful with her in joy, all who mourn over her,

**66:11** so that you may nurse and be filled from the breast of her comfort, so that you may drink deeply and take delight from the abundance of her glory.

**66:12** For this is what the LORD says: "Look, I am extending peace toward her like a river, and the glory of nations like a rushing stream; and you will nurse, you will be carried at the hip, and you will be bounced upon the knees.

**66:13** Like a man whose mother comforts him, so I Myself will comfort you, and in Jerusalem you will be comforted.

**66:14** You will see, and your heart will be joyful, and your bones will sprout like tender grass; the hand of the LORD will become known among His servants, and He will express indignation against His enemies."

**66:15** For look, the LORD is coming in fire, and His chariots are like the whirlwind, to discharge His anger in fury and His rebuke in flames of fire.

**66:16** For by fire the LORD is entering into judgment, and by His sword with all flesh; and many will be those struck down by the LORD.

**66:17** "Those who consecrate themselves and purify themselves for the gardens, behind one in the middle, eating the flesh of swine and the detestable thing and the mouse—together they will come to an end," declares the LORD.

**66:18** "And I know their deeds and their thoughts; the time is coming to gather all the nations and languages, and they will come and see My glory.

**66:19** I will place a sign among them and send out survivors from them to the nations—to Tarshish, Pul, and Lud, who draw the bow, to Tubal and Javan, to the distant coastlands that have not heard of My fame and have not seen My glory—and they will proclaim My glory among the nations.

**66:20** And they will bring all your brothers out of all the nations as an offering to the LORD—on horses, in chariots, in covered wagons, on mules, and on dromedaries—to My holy mountain Jerusalem," says the LORD, "just as the sons of Israel bring the offering in a clean vessel to the house of the LORD.

**66:21** And from among them as well I will take some as priests and as Levites," says the LORD.

**66:22** "For just as the new heavens and the new earth that I am making will stand before Me," declares the LORD, "so your seed and your name will stand.

**66:23** And it will come about that from one new moon to another, and from one Sabbath to another, all flesh will come to bow down before Me," says the LORD.

**66:24** "And they will go out and look upon the corpses of the men who rebelled against Me; for their worm will not die, and their fire will not go out, and they will be a horror to all flesh."

## Translator Notes

- **66:3** — The four opening clauses use terse verbless juxtapositions without a comparative particle (literally "one who slaughters an ox—one who strikes down a man"), equating hypocritical sacrifices with pagan abominations; awen ("idol") literally means "iniquity" or "wickedness."
- **66:11** — The verb tamotzu ("drink deeply," Qal of mtz) is a hapax legomenon, and the rare noun ziz ("abundance") is cognate with Akkadian zizu ("teat, breast"), paralleling shod ("breast") in the first half of the verse.
- **66:17** — Ketiv/Qere: the Ketiv reads the masculine ehad ("one"), while the Qere followed here reads the feminine ahat ("one"), likely referring to a female cult leader or goddess figure in the center of the garden rite.
- **66:18** — The opening clause we'anoki ma'asehem umahshevotehem ba'ah is highly elliptic in the Masoretic Text, omitting a finite verb after "And I" (supplied as "know") and using the feminine singular Qal participle ba'ah impersonally for "the time is coming."
- **66:20** — The noun kirkarot ("dromedaries") is a hapax legomenon denoting swift riding camels, and tzabbim ("covered wagons") matches Numbers 7:3.
