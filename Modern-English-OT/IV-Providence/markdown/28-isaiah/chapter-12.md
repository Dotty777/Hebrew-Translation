## Text

**12:1** And on that day you will say, "I will thank You, LORD, for although You were angry with me, Your anger has turned back and You comfort me.

**12:2** Look, God is my salvation; I will trust and will not be terrified, for the LORD, the LORD, is my strength and song, and He has become salvation to me."

**12:3** And you will draw water with gladness from the springs of salvation.

**12:4** And on that day you will say, "Give thanks to the LORD; call on His name; make His deeds known among the peoples; declare that His name is exalted.

**12:5** Sing praises to the LORD, for He has done majestic things; let this be made known in all the earth.

**12:6** Cry aloud and sing out, inhabitant of Zion, for the Holy One of Israel is great in your midst."

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The verb yashov ("has turned back") is a short prefix form (jussive/preterite) of shuv paired with the Piel imperfect utenachameni ("and You comfort me"), expressing either fulfilled deliverance or a petition ("may Your anger turn back").
- **12:2** — The Hebrew pairs the shortened poetic form of the divine name (Yah) directly with the full Tetragrammaton (YHWH), rendered here as "the LORD, the LORD," echoing Exodus 15:2.
- **12:5** — Ketiv/Qere: The Qere reads the Hophal feminine participle muda'at ("made known"), while the unvocalized Ketiv spells the word with a yod (myd't, likely a Pual participle with the same passive sense).
