## Text

**60:1** Rise up, shine, for your light has come, and the glory of the LORD has dawned upon you.

**60:2** For look, darkness will cover the earth, and thick gloom the peoples; yet upon you the LORD will dawn, and His glory will appear over you.

**60:3** And nations will walk to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn.

**60:4** Lift your eyes all around and see: they have all gathered together, they come to you; your sons will come from afar, and your daughters will be carried at the hip.

**60:5** Then you will see and be radiant, and your heart will throb and expand; because the abundance of the sea will be turned toward you, the wealth of the nations will come to you.

**60:6** A throng of camels will cover you, the young camels of Midian and Ephah; all those from Sheba will come; they will carry gold and frankincense, and herald the praises of the LORD.

**60:7** All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered to you; the rams of Nebaioth will minister to you; they will go up with acceptance onto My altar, and I will beautify the house of My splendor.

**60:8** Who are these that fly like a cloud, and like doves to their windows?

**60:9** Surely the coastlands wait for Me, and the ships of Tarshish first, to bring your sons from afar, their silver and their gold with them, for the name of the LORD your God, and for the Holy One of Israel, because He has glorified you.

**60:10** And foreigners will build your walls, and their kings will minister to you; for in My wrath I struck you, yet in My favor I have had compassion on you.

**60:11** Your gates will stand open continually; day and night they will not be shut, to bring to you the wealth of the nations, with their kings led along.

**60:12** For the nation and the kingdom that do not serve you will perish, and those nations will be utterly laid waste.

**60:13** The glory of Lebanon will come to you, the cypress, the plane tree, and the boxwood together, to beautify the place of My sanctuary; and I will make glorious the place of My feet.

**60:14** The sons of those who afflicted you will walk to you bowing low, and all who despised you will bow down at the soles of your feet; and they will call you the City of the LORD, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**60:15** Instead of your being forsaken and hated, with no one passing through, I will make you an everlasting majesty, a joy from generation to generation.

**60:16** You will nurse on the milk of nations, and you will nurse at the breast of kings; and you will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

**60:17** Instead of bronze I will bring gold, and instead of iron I will bring silver, and instead of wood, bronze, and instead of stones, iron. And I will make your oversight peace, and your taskmasters righteousness.

**60:18** Violence will no longer be heard in your land, nor devastation and shattering within your borders; but you will call your walls Salvation, and your gates Praise.

**60:19** The sun will no longer serve as your light by day, nor for brightness will the moon give light to you; rather, the LORD will be an everlasting light for you, and your God your splendor.

**60:20** Your sun will no longer go down, nor will your moon be withdrawn; for the LORD will be an everlasting light for you, and the days of your mourning will be completed.

**60:21** Your people will all be righteous; they will possess the land forever, the shoot of My plantings, the work of My hands, that I may display My splendor.

**60:22** The small one will become a thousand, and the least one a mighty nation. I, the LORD, will hasten it at its time.

## Translator Notes

- **60:7** — In ya'alu al-ratson mizbechi, the preposition al governs ratson ("with acceptance"), while mizbechi ("onto My altar") functions as an adverbial accusative of destination.
- **60:11** — The verb ufittechu is vocalized as an intransitive Piel ("stand open") rather than a passive Pual, emphasizing that the gates remain continuously open.
- **60:19** — Unlike the Septuagint, the Masoretic Text does not include "by night" in the second clause; the translation follows the MT strictly.
- **60:21** — Ketiv mt'v ("His planting", 3ms singular) vs. Qere matta'ai ("My plantings", 1cs plural); the Qere is translated, aligning with 1cs "My hands" in the next clause.
