## Text

**43:1** But now, this is what the LORD says—He who created you, Jacob, and He who formed you, Israel: "Do not be afraid, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you belong to Me.

**43:2** When you cross through the waters, I am with you, and through the rivers, they will not flood over you; when you walk through fire, you will not be scorched, and flame will not burn against you.

**43:3** For I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior. I have given Egypt as a ransom for you, Cush and Seba instead of you.

**43:4** Because you are precious in My eyes, you are honored, and I love you, I will give people instead of you and nations in place of your life.

**43:5** Do not be afraid, for I am with you; from the east I will bring your offspring, and from the west I will gather you.

**43:6** I will say to the north, 'Give them up,' and to the south, 'Do not hold back! Bring My sons from afar and My daughters from the end of the earth,

**43:7** everyone who is called by My name, whom I created for My glory, whom I formed—indeed, whom I made.'"

**43:8** "Bring out the people who are blind, though they have eyes, and the deaf, though they have ears.

**43:9** Let all the nations gather together, and let the peoples assemble. Who among them can declare this and announce the former things to us? Let them present their witnesses that they may be proved right, and let them hear and say, 'It is true.'

**43:10** You are My witnesses," declares the LORD, "and My servant whom I chose, in order that you may know, trust Me, and discern that I am He. Before Me no god was formed, and after Me none will exist.

**43:11** I—I am the LORD, and there is no savior apart from Me.

**43:12** I Myself declared, saved, and proclaimed, when there was no foreign god in your midst; so you are My witnesses," declares the LORD, "and I am God.

**43:13** Even from the first day I am He, and no one can rescue from My hand; I act, and who can turn it back?"

**43:14** This is what the LORD, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, says: "For your sake I have sent to Babylon, and I will bring all of them down as fugitives, even the Chaldeans, in the ships of their jubilation.

**43:15** I am the LORD, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, your King."

**43:16** This is what the LORD says, who sets a way in the sea and a path in mighty waters,

**43:17** who leads out chariot and horse, army and mighty force—they lie down together, they will not rise; they are snuffed out, quenched like a wick:

**43:18** "Do not recall the former things, and do not reflect upon the things of long ago.

**43:19** Look, I am doing something new; now it sprouts forth—do you not recognize it? Indeed, I will set a way in the wilderness and rivers in the wasteland.

**43:20** The wild beasts of the field will honor Me, the jackals and the ostriches, because I have given water in the wilderness and rivers in the wasteland, to give drink to My people, My chosen one,

**43:21** the people whom I formed for Myself; they will recount My praise.

**43:22** Yet you did not call upon Me, Jacob; instead, you have grown weary of Me, Israel.

**43:23** You have not brought Me the sheep of your burnt offerings, nor have you honored Me with your sacrifices; I have not burdened you with a grain offering, nor have I wearied you with frankincense.

**43:24** You have not bought Me sweet cane with silver, nor have you satisfied Me with the fat of your sacrifices. Instead, you have burdened Me with your sins; you have wearied Me with your iniquities.

**43:25** I—I am He who wipes out your transgressions for My own sake, and your sins I will not remember.

**43:26** Remind Me; let us contend in judgment together. Recount your case yourself, so that you may be vindicated.

**43:27** Your first father sinned, and your mediators rebelled against Me.

**43:28** Therefore I will profane the officials of the sanctuary, and I will give Jacob to utter destruction and Israel to reviling."

## Translator Notes

- **43:8** — The opening verb has the Masoretic pointing of a Hiphil perfect or infinitive construct rather than the standard imperative form, but in this courtroom summons it functions as an imperative ('Bring out').
- **43:9** — The verb 'gather together' has the form of a Niphal perfect ('have gathered'), functioning here as a summons parallel to the following Niphal jussive ('let the peoples assemble').
- **43:13** — 'From the first day' renders a terse Hebrew phrase literally meaning 'from day,' idiomatically denoting 'from the beginning of time' or 'from eternity.'
- **43:14** — The Masoretic pointing reads 'fugitives' (whereas a different vowel pattern of the same consonants means 'bars'); the closing phrase literally reads 'in the ships of their shouting' or 'jubilation.'
