## Text

**44:1** "But now listen, Jacob My servant, and Israel, whom I have chosen."

**44:2** This is what the LORD says, who made you and formed you from the womb, who will help you: "Do not be afraid, My servant Jacob, and Jeshurun, whom I have chosen.

**44:3** For I will pour out water upon thirsty ground and flowing streams upon the dry land; I will pour out My Spirit upon your seed and My blessing upon your descendants.

**44:4** They will sprout among the grass, like willows along channels of water.

**44:5** One will say, 'I belong to the LORD,' and another will call himself by the name of Jacob; still another will write on his hand, 'Belonging to the LORD,' and title himself by the name of Israel."

**44:6** This is what the LORD, the King of Israel and his Redeemer, the LORD of hosts, says: "I am first and I am last, and apart from Me there is no God.

**44:7** Who is like Me? Let him proclaim it, declare it, and set it in order before Me, from the time I established an ancient people; and let them declare to them the things to come and what will arrive.

**44:8** Do not tremble and do not be terrified. Have I not announced to you from long ago and declared it? You are My witnesses! Is there a God apart from Me? There is no Rock; I know of none."

**44:9** All who form an idol are emptiness, and their prized things cannot profit; and their witnesses themselves neither see nor know, so that they will be put to shame.

**44:10** Who has formed a god or cast an idol that is of no profit?

**44:11** Look, all his companions will be put to shame, for the craftsmen are merely human. Let them all assemble and take their stand; they will tremble and be put to shame together.

**44:12** The ironsmith works a cutting tool over the coals; he forms it with hammers and works it with his strong arm. Yet he becomes hungry and has no strength; he drinks no water and grows faint.

**44:13** The woodworker stretches out a line; he outlines it with a stylus, shapes it with planes, and outlines it with a compass. He makes it in the form of a man, with the beauty of a human being, to dwell in a house.

**44:14** He cuts down cedars for himself, and takes a cypress and an oak, and lets it grow strong for himself among the trees of the forest. He plants a pine, and the rain makes it grow.

**44:15** Then it becomes something for a man to burn; he takes some of it and warms himself. He also kindles a fire and bakes bread; he also fashions a god and bows down to it—he makes it an idol and falls prostrate before it.

**44:16** Half of it he burns in the fire; over half of it he eats meat—he roasts a roast and is satisfied. He also warms himself and says, "Aha, I am warm! I have seen the fire!"

**44:17** The rest of it he makes into a god, his idol; he falls prostrate before it and bows down, and he prays to it and says, "Rescue me, for you are my god!"

**44:18** They do not know and they do not understand, for their eyes have been plastered shut from seeing, and their hearts from comprehending.

**44:19** He does not take it to heart, nor is there knowledge or discernment to say, "Half of it I burned in the fire; I also baked bread over its coals, roasted meat, and ate it. Shall I make the rest of it into an abomination? Shall I fall prostrate before a block of wood?"

**44:20** He feeds on ashes; a deluded heart has led him astray, so that he cannot rescue his own life, nor say, "Is there not a lie in my right hand?"

**44:21** "Remember these things, Jacob, and Israel, because you are My servant; I formed you, you are a servant to Me—Israel, you will not be forgotten by Me.

**44:22** Like a thick cloud I have wiped out your transgressions, and like a cloud your sins; turn back to Me, for I have redeemed you."

**44:23** Sing for joy, heavens, for the LORD has acted! Shout aloud, depths of the earth! Break forth into joyful song, mountains, forest, and every tree in it! For the LORD has redeemed Jacob, and in Israel He will glorify Himself.

**44:24** This is what the LORD, your Redeemer and He who formed you from the womb, says: "I am the LORD, who makes all things, who stretches out the heavens alone, who spreads out the earth by Myself;

**44:25** who nullifies the signs of liars and turns diviners into fools, who sends the wise backward and renders their knowledge foolish;

**44:26** who confirms the word of His servant and fulfills the counsel of His messengers; who says of Jerusalem, 'She will be inhabited,' and of the cities of Judah, 'They will be built,' and I will raise up her ruins;

**44:27** who says to the deep, 'Be dry, and I will dry up your rivers';

**44:28** who says of Cyrus, 'He is My shepherd, and he will fulfill all My desire,' and saying of Jerusalem, 'She will be built,' and of the temple, 'Its foundation will be laid.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:9** — In the Masoretic Text, the pronoun rendered 'themselves' carries ancient scribal dots (puncta extraordinaria) marking an early textual query, while remaining in the text.
- **44:12** — The opening clause is syntactically elliptical in Hebrew (literally 'craftsman of iron, a cutting tool, and he works over the coals'), with the tool serving as the object or instrument of the following verb.
- **44:18** — 'Have been plastered shut' translates a third-person masculine singular Qal active verb (literally 'he has plastered over'), functioning either as an impersonal passive or with God as the implied subject.
- **44:21** — 'You will not be forgotten by Me' renders a rare Niphal imperfect carrying a first-person pronominal suffix (literally 'you will not be forgotten to/by Me'), though some construe it actively as 'do not forget Me.'
- **44:24** — Translated following the Qere ('by Myself,' literally 'from with Me'); the Ketiv divides the consonants into two words meaning 'who was with Me?'
