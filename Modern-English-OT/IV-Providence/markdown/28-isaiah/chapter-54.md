## Text

**54:1** "Ring out in joy, barren woman who has not given birth; burst into joyful song and cry aloud, you who have not been in labor! For more numerous are the children of the desolate woman than the children of the married woman," says the LORD.

**54:2** "Widen the space of your tent, and let them stretch out the curtains of your dwellings; do not hold back! Lengthen your cords, and strengthen your pegs.

**54:3** For to the right and to the left you will spread out, and your offspring will possess nations and populate desolate cities.

**54:4** Do not be afraid, for you will not be ashamed; and do not be humiliated, for you will not be disgraced; for you will forget the shame of your youth, and you will no longer remember the reproach of your widowhood.

**54:5** For your husband is your Maker—the LORD of hosts is His name—and your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel; the God of all the earth He will be called.

**54:6** For as a wife abandoned and sorrowful in spirit the LORD has called you, and as a wife of youth when she is rejected," says your God.

**54:7** "For a short moment I abandoned you, yet with great compassion I will gather you.

**54:8** In a surge of anger I hid My face from you for a moment, but with everlasting loyal love I will have compassion on you," says your Redeemer, the LORD.

**54:9** "For this is to Me like the waters of Noah: just as I swore that the waters of Noah would not pass over the earth again, so I have sworn not to be angry with you and not to rebuke you.

**54:10** For though the mountains move away and the hills totter, My loyal love will not move away from you, and the covenant of My peace will not totter," says the LORD, who shows you compassion.

**54:11** "Afflicted one, tossed by storms, not comforted: look, I am setting your stones in antimony, and I will lay your foundation with sapphires.

**54:12** And I will make your pinnacles of rubies, your gates of sparkling stones, and all your border of precious stones.

**54:13** And all your children will be disciples of the LORD, and abundant will be the peace of your children.

**54:14** By righteousness you will be established; be far from oppression, for you will not be afraid, and from terror, for it will not approach you.

**54:15** Look, if anyone indeed attacks, it is not from Me; whoever attacks you will fall because of you.

**54:16** See, I have created the smith who blows on the fire of charcoal and brings out a weapon for its task, and I have created the destroyer to ravage.

**54:17** No weapon crafted against you will succeed, and every tongue that rises against you in judgment you will convict. This is the inheritance of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is from Me," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **54:5** — Both בֹעֲלַיִךְ ('your husband') and עֹשַׂיִךְ ('your Maker') are masculine plural Qal participles used as plurals of majesty referring to the one God (compare Job 35:10; Ps 149:2).
- **54:8** — Hebrew בְּשֶׁצֶף קֶצֶף pairs the hapax legomenon שֶׁצֶף ('surge, outburst, flood,' related to שׁטף) with the rhyming noun קֶצֶף ('anger, wrath') for rhetorical emphasis.
- **54:9** — The Masoretic Text divides כִּי־מֵי נֹחַ as two words ('for ... the waters of Noah,' matching מֵי־נֹחַ later in the verse), whereas some manuscripts and ancient versions divide it as כִּימֵי נֹחַ ('like the days of Noah').
- **54:15** — Hebrew גּוֹר יָגוּר (Qal infinitive absolute plus imperfect 3ms of גור III, 'stir up strife, attack') expresses emphatic attack; אֶפֶס מֵאוֹתִי ('not from Me') uses the rare spelling מֵאוֹתִי for מֵאִתִּי (compare v. 17).
- **54:16** — Ketiv reads הן (הֵן, 'look, see'); the Qere הִנֵּה ('look, see,' rendered 'See') carries the same deictic force.
