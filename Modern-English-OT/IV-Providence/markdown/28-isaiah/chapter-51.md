## Text

**51:1** Listen to Me, you who pursue righteousness, who seek the LORD; look to the rock from which you were carved out, and to the hollow of the pit from which you were dug out.

**51:2** Look to Abraham your father, and to Sarah who gave birth to you; for while he was just one I called him, and I blessed him and multiplied him.

**51:3** For the LORD has comforted Zion; He has comforted all her ruins, and He has made her wilderness like Eden and her desert plain like the garden of the LORD; joy and gladness will be found in her, thanksgiving and the sound of song.

**51:4** Attend to Me, My people, and give ear to Me, My nation; for instruction will go out from Me, and I will establish My justice as a light to the peoples.

**51:5** Near is My righteousness; My salvation has gone out, and My arms will judge the peoples; for Me the coastlands will wait, and for My arm they will hope.

**51:6** Lift up your eyes to the heavens, and look at the earth below; for the heavens will dissipate like smoke, and the earth will wear out like a garment, and its inhabitants will die in the same way. But My salvation will be forever, and My righteousness will not be shattered.

**51:7** Listen to Me, you who know righteousness, a people with My instruction in their heart: do not be afraid of the reproach of mortal man, and do not be dismayed at their insults.

**51:8** For the moth will eat them like a garment, and the grub will eat them like wool; but My righteousness will be forever, and My salvation through generation after generation.

**51:9** Awake, awake, put on strength, arm of the LORD! Awake as in ancient days, the generations of ages past! Are You not the One who hacked Rahab apart, who pierced the sea monster?

**51:10** Are You not the One who dried up the sea, the waters of the great deep, who made the depths of the sea a road for the redeemed to cross over?

**51:11** And the ransomed of the LORD will return and come to Zion with ringing song, with everlasting gladness on their heads; they will attain joy and gladness; grief and sighing will flee.

**51:12** I, I Myself am He who comforts you. Who are you that you should fear mortal man who will die, and a son of man who will be made like grass,

**51:13** and have forgotten the LORD, your Maker, who stretches out the heavens and lays the foundation of the earth, and dread continually all day long before the fury of the oppressor when he makes ready to destroy? And where is the fury of the oppressor?

**51:14** The one bowed down will swiftly be loosed; he will not die in the pit, and his bread will not fail.

**51:15** And I am the LORD your God, who stirs the sea so that its waves roar—the LORD of hosts is His name.

**51:16** And I have placed My words in your mouth and covered you in the shadow of My hand, to plant the heavens and lay the foundation of the earth, and to say to Zion, "You are My people."

**51:17** Rouse yourself, rouse yourself! Arise, Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the LORD the cup of His fury; the goblet of the cup of staggering you have drunk, you have drained it dry.

**51:18** There is no one to guide her from all the sons she has borne, and no one holding her by the hand from all the sons she has raised.

**51:19** These two things have come upon you—who will grieve over you?—devastation and ruin, and famine and sword; how can I comfort you?

**51:20** Your sons have fainted; they lie at the head of all the streets like an antelope in a net, full of the fury of the LORD, the rebuke of your God.

**51:21** Therefore, hear this now, afflicted one, you who are drunk, yet not from wine:

**51:22** This is what your Lord, the LORD, and your God who pleads the case of His people says: "Look, I have taken from your hand the cup of staggering; the goblet of the cup of My fury you will never drink again.

**51:23** And I will place it in the hand of your tormentors, who said to your soul, 'Bow down so that we may pass over,' and you set your back like the ground, and like the street for those passing over."

## Translator Notes

- **51:4** — Hebrew אַרְגִּיעַ (Hiphil imperfect 1cs of רגע II, 'settle, establish, give rest') is used transitively with מִשְׁפָּטִי ('My justice') to express establishing justice firmly as a light to the peoples.
- **51:6** — Hebrew כְּמוֹ־כֵן literally means 'like so, in the same way,' though some lexicons take כֵן as a singular related to כִּנִּים ('gnats'), rendering 'like gnats.' Hebrew נִמְלָחוּ is a Niphal perfect 3cp of מלח II ('dissipate, disperse, be torn to shreds').
- **51:14** — Hebrew צֹעֶה (Qal active participle ms of צעה, 'stoop, bend over') depicts a captive bowed down in chains, while לְהִפָּתֵחַ (Niphal infinitive construct of פתח) means 'to be loosed' or 'released.'
- **51:16** — The Masoretic Text reads לִנְטֹעַ (Qal infinitive construct of נטע, 'to plant,' used of pitching tents in Dan 11:45), rather than לִנְטוֹת ('to stretch out') as in v. 13.
- **51:19** — The Masoretic Text has the 1cs Piel imperfect אֲנַחֲמֵךְ ('I comfort you') with interrogative מִי used idiomatically in the sense 'how?' (compare Amos 7:2, 5), rather than a 3ms verb ('who will comfort you?').
