## Text

**38:1** In those days Hezekiah became sick to the point of death, and Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet, came to him and said to him, "This is what the LORD says: 'Give instructions for your household, for you are dying and will not live.'"

**38:2** Hezekiah turned his face toward the wall and prayed to the LORD.

**38:3** He said, "Please, LORD, remember now how I have walked before You in faithfulness and with a complete heart, and have done what is good in Your eyes." Then Hezekiah wept with great weeping.

**38:4** Now the word of the LORD came to Isaiah, saying,

**38:5** "Go and speak to Hezekiah: 'This is what the LORD, the God of David your father, says: I have heard your prayer; I have seen your tears. Look, I am adding fifteen years to your days.

**38:6** And from the grasp of the king of Assyria I will rescue you and this city, and I will shield this city.

**38:7** And this is the sign for you from the LORD that the LORD will carry out this matter that He has spoken:

**38:8** Look, I am making the shadow of the steps, which has gone down on the steps of Ahaz with the sun, go back ten steps.'" So the sun went back ten steps on the steps that it had gone down.

**38:9** A writing of Hezekiah, king of Judah, when he fell sick and recovered from his sickness:

**38:10** I said, "At the midpoint of my days I must pass through the gates of Sheol; I have been deprived of the remainder of my years."

**38:11** I said, "I will not see the LORD, the LORD in the land of the living; I will gaze on humanity no more among the dwellers of the fleeting world."

**38:12** My dwelling has been pulled up and carried away from me like a shepherd's tent; I have rolled up my life like a weaver; from the loom He severs me. From day until night You put an end to me.

**38:13** I calmed myself until morning; like a lion, so He shatters all my bones. From day until night You put an end to me.

**38:14** Like a swift or a crane, so I twitter; I moan like a dove. My eyes grow dim toward the height. Lord, distress is upon me; stand as my surety!

**38:15** What can I speak? He has spoken to me, and He Himself has acted. I will tread slowly all my years on account of the bitterness of my soul.

**38:16** Lord, by these things people live, and wholly in them is the life of my spirit; restore me to health and make me live!

**38:17** Look, it was for peace that bitterness upon bitterness came to me; yet You have lovingly kept my soul from the pit of nothingness, for You have thrown all my sins behind Your back.

**38:18** For Sheol does not give You thanks, nor does death praise You; those who descend to the pit do not hope for Your faithfulness.

**38:19** The living, the living, he is the one who gives You thanks, just as I do today; a father makes Your faithfulness known to children.

**38:20** The LORD is ready to save me; so we will play my stringed music all the days of our lives at the house of the LORD.

**38:21** And Isaiah said, "Let them take a cake of figs and rub it on the boil, so that he may recover."

**38:22** And Hezekiah said, "What sign is there that I will go up to the house of the LORD?"

## Translator Notes

- **38:8** — Hebrew ma'alot literally means "steps" or "stairway," possibly a stepped structure serving as a sundial; the feminine verb yaredah ("has gone down") agrees grammatically with feminine shemesh ("sun").
- **38:10** — Hebrew bidmi is rare and may mean either "at the midpoint" or "in the stillness/cutting off" of his days; puqqadti (Pual of paqad) means "I have been deprived of."
- **38:11** — Hebrew doubles the shortened divine name Yah ("the LORD, the LORD"). At the end of the verse, the Masoretic Text reads chadel ("cessation" or "transience," understood here as the transient world), often compared to cheled ("world").
- **38:16** — The Hebrew is terse: "by these things" (alehem) refers back to God's word and deeds in v. 15, and the Hiphil verb tachalimeni (from chalam II) means "restore to health."
- **38:17** — The Masoretic Text reads chashaqta (Qal of chashaq, "loved/clung to"), used in a pregnant construction with min to mean "lovingly kept back from."
