## Text

**10:1** Woe to those who enact statutes of iniquity, and to scribes who write out oppression,

**10:2** to turn the poor away from justice and to rob the afflicted of My people of their legal right, so that widows become their plunder and they may loot the orphans.

**10:3** And what will you do for the day of reckoning, and at the devastation arriving from far away? To whom will you flee for aid, and where will you leave your glory?

**10:4** There is nothing left except to bow down beneath the captives or to fall beneath the slaughtered. Despite all this, His anger has not turned back, and His hand is still outstretched.

**10:5** Woe, Assyria, rod of My anger—and the staff in their hand is My indignation!

**10:6** I send him forth against a godless nation, and against the people of My fury I charge him, to plunder spoil and to loot booty, and to make them a trampling place like mud of the streets.

**10:7** Yet he does not intend it this way, and his heart does not think this way; rather, it is in his heart to destroy and to cut off not a few nations.

**10:8** For he says, "Are not my princes kings altogether?

**10:9** Is Calno not like Carchemish? Is Hamath not like Arpad? Is Samaria not like Damascus?

**10:10** Just as my hand found the kingdoms of the idols, whose carved images surpassed those of Jerusalem and Samaria,

**10:11** just as I have done to Samaria and to her idols, will I not do the same to Jerusalem and to her images?"

**10:12** And it will happen, when the Lord has finished all His work on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, that I will punish the fruit of the king of Assyria's haughty heart and the boastfulness of his lofty eyes.

**10:13** For he said, "By the strength of my hand I have acted, and by my wisdom, for I am discerning; I removed the borders of peoples, and their stored treasures I pillaged, and like a mighty one I brought down the inhabitants.

**10:14** And like a nest my hand found the wealth of the peoples; just as someone gathers abandoned eggs, I myself gathered the whole earth, and there was not one flapping a wing, or opening a mouth, or chirping."

**10:15** Will the axe boast over the one who chops with it, or will the saw exalt itself over the one who swings it? As though a rod should swing those who lift it, or as though a staff should lift someone who is not wood!

**10:16** Therefore the Lord, the LORD of hosts, will send a wasting illness among his stout ones, and beneath his glory a burning will blaze like the blaze of fire.

**10:17** And the Light of Israel will turn into a fire, and its Holy One into a flame; it will blaze and consume his thorns and his briers in a single day.

**10:18** And He will bring to an end the glory of his forest and his fruitful orchard, from soul even to flesh, and it will be like a sick man melting away.

**10:19** And the remnant of the trees in his forest will be few in number, and a boy could write them down.

**10:20** And it will happen in that day that the remnant of Israel and the surviving ones of the house of Jacob will no longer lean on the one who struck them, but will lean in truth on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

**10:21** A remnant will return—the remnant of Jacob—to Mighty God.

**10:22** For even though your people Israel should be like the sand of the sea, a remnant among them will return; annihilation is decreed, flooding with righteousness.

**10:23** For the Lord GOD of hosts is carrying out a complete and decreed destruction in the midst of the entire earth.

**10:24** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD of hosts says: "Do not fear Assyria, My people dwelling in Zion, when he strikes you with the rod and raises his staff against you in the manner of Egypt.

**10:25** For in just a very short while, indignation will be over, and My anger will turn to their destruction."

**10:26** And the LORD of hosts will rouse a whip against him, like the striking of Midian at the rock of Oreb; and His staff will be over the sea, and He will raise it in the manner of Egypt.

**10:27** And it will happen in that day that his burden will lift from your shoulder, and his yoke from your neck, and the yoke will be destroyed because of fatness.

**10:28** He has come upon Aiath; he has passed through Migron; at Michmash he deposits his supplies.

**10:29** They have crossed the pass; at Geba they took their night's lodging; Ramah quakes; Gibeah of Saul has fled.

**10:30** Cry shrilly with your voice, daughter of Gallim! Listen closely, Laishah! Afflicted Anathoth!

**10:31** Madmenah flees away; the residents of Gebim have taken refuge.

**10:32** Even today he will stop at Nob; he shakes his hand against the mountain of Daughter Zion, the hill of Jerusalem.

**10:33** Look, the Lord, the LORD of hosts, is lopping off the boughs with terrifying force; the tall in stature are felled, and the high ones will sink low.

**10:34** And He will chop down the thickets of the forest with iron, and Lebanon will fall by a Mighty One.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — בִּלְתִּי כָרַע (lit. "except bowing down") is an elliptical construction answering the rhetorical questions of 10:3, meaning nothing remains except to crouch beneath the captives or fall beneath the slaughtered.
- **10:18** — כִּמְסֹס נֹסֵס ("like a sick man melting away") combines the Qal infinitive construct of מסס ("melt/waste away") with the Qal participle נֹסֵס, understood from Semitic cognates as a sick or fainting person.
- **10:27** — וְחֻבַּל עֹל מִפְּנֵי־שָׁמֶן (lit. "and a yoke will be destroyed because of fatness/oil") is a noted Hebrew crux; following the Masoretic text, it depicts the yoke broken by fatness (or metaphorically by anointing oil).
- **10:29** — לָנוּ is vocalized identically to both the Qal perfect 3cp of לוּן ("they spent the night/lodged") and "for us"; taking it as the verb parallels the preceding cognate pair עָבְרוּ מַעְבָּרָה ("they crossed the pass") with מָלוֹן לָנוּ ("they took their night's lodging").
- **10:32** — Ketiv reads בית ("house of"), whereas Qere, translated here, reads בַּת ("daughter of"), yielding "Daughter Zion."
