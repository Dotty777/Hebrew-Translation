## Text

**45:1** This is what the LORD says to His anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I have taken hold of to subdue nations before him, and I will unfasten the loins of kings, to open double doors before him, and gates will not be shut:

**45:2** "I Myself will go before you and make the rugged terrain level; doors of bronze I will shatter, and bars of iron I will hew through.

**45:3** And I will give you treasures of darkness and hidden stores of secret places, so that you may know that I am the LORD, who calls you by your name, the God of Israel.

**45:4** For the sake of My servant Jacob, and Israel My chosen one, I called you by your name; I designate you by an honored title, even though you do not know Me.

**45:5** I am the LORD, and there is no other; apart from Me there is no God. I gird you, even though you do not know Me,

**45:6** so that they may know from the rising of the sun and from the west that there is none apart from Me; I am the LORD, and there is no other,

**45:7** forming light and creating darkness, making peace and creating disaster; I, the LORD, am the One who does all of these.

**45:8** Shower down, heavens, from above, and let the skies pour out righteousness; let the earth open up so that they bear salvation as fruit, and let it cause righteousness to sprout together; I, the LORD, have created it."

**45:9** Woe to the one who quarrels with his Maker—a potsherd among the potsherds of the ground! Will clay say to the one who forms it, "What are you making?" or, "Your work has no hands"?

**45:10** Woe to the one who says to a father, "What are you fathering?" or to a woman, "With what are you in labor?"

**45:11** This is what the LORD, the Holy One of Israel and his Maker, says: "Ask Me about the things to come; concerning My sons and concerning the work of My hands, will you command Me?

**45:12** It is I who made the earth, and I created mankind upon it; My own hands stretched out the heavens, and all their host I commanded.

**45:13** It is I who stirred him up in righteousness, and all his ways I will make level; he will build My city and release My exiles, not for a price and not for a bribe," says the LORD of hosts.

**45:14** This is what the LORD says: "The earnings of Egypt and the trade of Cush, and the Sabeans, men of stature, will pass over to you and will be yours; behind you they will walk, in chains they will pass over, and to you they will bow down; to you they will plead, 'Surely God is in you, and there is no other; there is no god besides.'"

**45:15** Indeed, You are a God who hides Himself, God of Israel, Savior.

**45:16** They have been put to shame and also humiliated, all of them; together they have gone off in humiliation, the craftsmen of idols.

**45:17** Israel has been saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation; you will not be put to shame and you will not be humiliated for all eternity.

**45:18** For this is what the LORD says, Creator of the heavens—He is God—who formed the earth and made it; He established it; He did not create it a wasteland, He formed it to be inhabited: "I am the LORD, and there is no other.

**45:19** I have not spoken in secret, in a place of a land of darkness; I did not say to the offspring of Jacob, 'Seek Me in vain.' I, the LORD, speak righteousness, declaring things that are upright.

**45:20** Gather yourselves and come, draw near together, survivors of the nations! They have no knowledge, those who carry the wood of their carved image and pray to a god that does not save.

**45:21** Declare and bring it forward; indeed, let them take counsel together! Who proclaimed this from ancient times, declared it from long ago? Was it not I, the LORD? And there is no other God besides Me, a righteous God and a Savior; there is none apart from Me.

**45:22** Turn toward Me and be saved, all the farthest bounds of the earth, because I am God, and there is no other.

**45:23** By Myself I have sworn; a word has gone out from My mouth in righteousness and it will not turn back: that to Me every knee will bend, every tongue will swear.

**45:24** 'Only in the LORD,' one will say of Me, 'are righteousness and strength.'" To Him they will come and be put to shame, all who are incensed against Him.

**45:25** In the LORD all the offspring of Israel will be vindicated and will glory.

## Translator Notes

- **45:2** — Qere אֲיַשֵּׁר (Piel, "I will make level") is translated; Ketiv אושר represents the Hiphil of the same root ישר with the same sense. The rare plural הֲדוּרִים ("rugged terrain") literally denotes swollen or uneven ground.
- **45:7** — Hebrew רָע ("disaster"), paired directly with שָׁלוֹם ("peace"), denotes calamity or adversity rather than moral evil.
- **45:9** — The clause וּפׇעׇלְךָ אֵין־יָדַיִם לוֹ is syntactically compressed and may be read either as part of the clay's speech ("Your work has no hands/handles") or as an elliptical question ("or will your work say, 'He has no hands'?").
- **45:11** — Following the Masoretic vocalization and accents, שְׁאָלוּנִי ("Ask Me") is a Qal imperative, while the Piel imperfect תְּצַוֻּנִי ("will you command Me?") functions as an unmarked rhetorical question governing both prepositional phrases that precede it.
- **45:19** — Hebrew תֹּהוּ ("in vain," literally "emptiness") echoes תֹהוּ ("wasteland") in 45:18, linking God's purposeful creation of the earth with His clear self-revelation to Israel.
