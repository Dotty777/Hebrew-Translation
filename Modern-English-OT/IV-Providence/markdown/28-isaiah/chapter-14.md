## Text

**14:1** For the LORD will show compassion to Jacob and will choose Israel once more, and settle them on their own land; and the sojourner will join himself to them, and they will attach themselves to the house of Jacob.

**14:2** And peoples will take them and bring them to their place, and the house of Israel will take them as an inheritance on the land of the LORD, as male servants and female servants; and they will be captors of their captors and rule over their oppressors.

**14:3** And it will happen on the day the LORD gives you rest from your pain and your agitation, and from the hard labor you were made to serve,

**14:4** that you will take up this taunt against the king of Babylon and say, "How the oppressor has ceased! The exaction of gold has ceased!

**14:5** The LORD has broken the staff of the wicked, the rod of the rulers,

**14:6** that struck peoples in wrath with a blow without ceasing, that ruled nations in anger with persecution without restraint.

**14:7** All the earth rests and is still; they burst forth into a shout of joy.

**14:8** Even the cypresses rejoice over you, the cedars of Lebanon: 'Since you have lain down, no woodcutter comes up against us.'

**14:9** Sheol below is agitated for you to meet your coming; it rouses the shades for you, all the leaders of the earth; it raises up from their thrones all the kings of the nations.

**14:10** All of them will answer and say to you, 'You also have been made weak like us; you have become just like us.'

**14:11** Your pomp has been brought down to Sheol, the music of your harps; beneath you maggots are spread out, and worms are your coverings.

**14:12** How you have fallen from the heavens, Shining One, son of the dawn! You have been hewn down to the earth, you who laid nations prostrate!

**14:13** Yet you said in your heart, 'To the heavens I will ascend; above the stars of God I will lift up my throne, and I will sit on the mountain of assembly, in the farthest recesses of the north;

**14:14** I will ascend above the cloud heights; I will become like the Most High.'

**14:15** Instead, you will be brought down to Sheol, to the farthest recesses of the Pit.

**14:16** Those who see you will gaze intently at you; they will consider you closely: 'Is this the man who made the earth tremble, who made kingdoms quake,

**14:17** who made the world like the wilderness and demolished its cities, who did not release his prisoners to go home?'

**14:18** All the kings of the nations, all of them, lie in honor, each in his own house.

**14:19** But you have been thrown out away from your grave like a detested branch, clothed with those killed, pierced by the sword, who go down to the stones of the Pit, like a corpse trampled underfoot.

**14:20** You will not be united with them in burial, for you have ruined your land, you have killed your people. The offspring of evildoers will never be named.

**14:21** Prepare a slaughter place for his sons because of the iniquity of their fathers, so that they will not rise up and possess the earth and fill the surface of the world with cities."

**14:22** "And I will rise up against them," declares the LORD of hosts, "and I will cut off from Babylon name and remnant, offspring and descendants," declares the LORD.

**14:23** "And I will turn it into a possession for the hedgehog and marshes of water, and I will sweep it away with the broom of destruction," declares the LORD of hosts.

**14:24** The LORD of hosts has sworn, saying, "Surely, just as I have intended, so it will happen, and just as I have planned, that will stand:

**14:25** to break Assyria in My land, and on My mountains I will trample him underfoot; and his yoke will depart from them, and his burden will depart from his shoulder."

**14:26** This is the plan that has been planned over all the earth, and this is the hand stretched out over all the nations.

**14:27** For the LORD of hosts has planned, and who will thwart it? And His outstretched hand—who will turn it back?

**14:28** In the year of King Ahaz's death, this pronouncement came:

**14:29** Do not rejoice, Philistia, all of you, that the rod of the one who struck you has been broken; for out of the snake's root a viper will come forth, and its fruit will be a flying fiery serpent.

**14:30** And the firstborn of the poor will graze, and the needy will lie down in security; but I will put your root to death by famine, and he will kill your remnant.

**14:31** Wail, gate; cry out, city; melt away, Philistia, all of you! For smoke is coming from the north, and no one straggles in his ranks.

**14:32** And what will one reply to the messengers of the nation? "The LORD has founded Zion, and in it the afflicted of His people will take refuge."

## Translator Notes

- **14:4** — The MT reads the hapax legomenon madhevah, traditionally derived from Aramaic dehav ("gold") and rendered "exaction of gold"; the Dead Sea Scroll 1QIsa-a and ancient versions point instead to marhevah ("insolent fury").
- **14:9** — Hebrew repha'im denotes the shades or departed spirits in Sheol, and attudey arets ("leaders of the earth") literally means "he-goats of the earth," a metaphor for rulers.
- **14:12** — Hebrew heylel ("Shining One," from halal, "to shine") designates the bright morning star (Venus); the Latin Vulgate rendered it as Lucifer ("light-bearer").
- **14:19** — The phrase levush harugim meto'aney charev literally depicts the king as "clothed with those killed, pierced by the sword," using the hapax Pual participle meto'aney ("pierced").
- **14:30** — "Firstborn of the poor" (bekhorey dallim) is a Hebrew superlative idiom for the poorest of the poor; in the final clause, the MT shifts from first-person singular ("I will put ... to death") to third-person singular yaharog ("he will kill"), referring to the coming northern invader.
