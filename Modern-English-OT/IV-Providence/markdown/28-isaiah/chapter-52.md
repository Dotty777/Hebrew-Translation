## Text

**52:1** Awake, awake, put on your strength, Zion! Put on your garments of splendor, Jerusalem, the holy city; for never again will the uncircumcised and the unclean enter into you.

**52:2** Shake yourself from the dust; arise, take your seat, Jerusalem; loose from yourself the bonds of your neck, captive daughter of Zion.

**52:3** For this is what the LORD says: "For nothing you were sold, and not with money will you be redeemed."

**52:4** For this is what the Lord GOD says: "To Egypt My people went down at first to sojourn there, and Assyria oppressed them without cause.

**52:5** And now, what do I have here," declares the LORD, "since My people have been taken away for nothing? Those who rule over them howl," declares the LORD, "and continually all day long My name is treated with contempt.

**52:6** Therefore My people will know My name; therefore on that day they will know that I am He who speaks—here I am!"

**52:7** How lovely upon the mountains are the feet of a herald of good news, who proclaims peace, who heralds what is good, who proclaims salvation, who says to Zion, "Your God reigns!"

**52:8** The voice of your watchmen! They raise their voice; together they shout for joy, for eye to eye they will see when the LORD returns to Zion.

**52:9** Burst out, shout for joy together, ruins of Jerusalem! For the LORD has comforted His people; He has redeemed Jerusalem.

**52:10** The LORD has laid bare His holy arm before the eyes of all the nations, and all the ends of the earth will see the salvation of our God.

**52:11** Depart, depart, come out from there! Do not touch anything unclean; come out from her midst, make yourselves pure, you who carry the vessels of the LORD.

**52:12** For not in haste will you go out, and not in flight will you go; for the LORD is going before you, and your rear guard is the God of Israel.

**52:13** Look, My servant will act wisely; he will be exalted and lifted up, and will be exceedingly high.

**52:14** Just as many were appalled at you—so disfigured was his appearance beyond that of a man, and his form beyond the sons of men—

**52:15** so he will sprinkle many nations; kings will close their mouths because of him, for what had not been recounted to them they will see, and what they had not heard they will comprehend.

## Translator Notes

- **52:2** — Ketiv reads התפתחו (3cp Hithpael perfect 'they have loosed themselves' or 2mp imperative); the Qere הִתְפַּתְּחִי (2fs Hithpael imperative of פתח, 'loose from yourself') is translated here to match the surrounding feminine singular imperatives addressed to Jerusalem.
- **52:5** — Hebrew יְהֵילִילוּ (Hiphil imperfect 3mp of ילל, unusually retaining the Hiphil ה) normally means 'howl' or 'wail,' here expressing the rulers' mocking or boastful cries. Hebrew מִנֹּאָץ is a Hithpoel participle of נאץ with assimilated ת, meaning 'continually treated with contempt.'
- **52:13** — Hebrew יַשְׂכִּיל (Hiphil imperfect 3ms of שׂכל) combines the senses of acting with wisdom or insight and achieving success or prosperity.
- **52:14** — The Masoretic Text shifts abruptly from 2ms עָלֶיךָ ('at you') to 3ms מַרְאֵהוּ ('his appearance') and וְתֹאֲרוֹ ('and his form'). Hebrew מִשְׁחַת is a rare construct noun from שׁחת ('disfigurement, marring'), introducing an explanatory aside before the correlative כֵּן ('so') resumes in v. 15.
- **52:15** — Hebrew יַזֶּה (Hiphil imperfect 3ms of נזה) elsewhere consistently means 'sprinkle' (typically in ritual purification), though some scholars derive it from a homonymous root meaning 'startle.'
