## Text

**57:1** The righteous one perishes, and no one takes it to heart; and men of loyal love are taken away, with no one understanding that the righteous one is taken away from calamity.

**57:2** He comes into peace; they rest on their beds, whoever walks in his uprightness.

**57:3** But you, come close here, sons of a sorceress, offspring of an adulterer and of her who commits prostitution!

**57:4** At whom are you mocking? At whom do you open wide the mouth and stretch out the tongue? Are you not children of rebellion, offspring of falsehood,

**57:5** you who are inflamed with passion among the oaks, beneath every lush tree, who slaughter the children in the ravines, beneath the clefts of the rocks?

**57:6** Your portion is among the smooth stones of the ravine; they, yes they, are your lot. Even to them you have poured out a drink offering; you have offered up a grain offering. Should I be appeased over these things?

**57:7** On a high and elevated mountain you placed your bed; even there you went up to offer a sacrifice.

**57:8** Behind the door and the doorpost you placed your memorial; for away from Me you uncovered yourself and climbed up, you widened your bed and struck a bargain for yourself with them; you loved their bed, you gazed at nakedness.

**57:9** You traveled to the king with oil and multiplied your perfumes; you sent out your envoys far away, and brought yourself low down to Sheol.

**57:10** From the length of your way you grew weary, yet you did not say, "It is hopeless." You found revival of your strength; therefore you did not weaken.

**57:11** And whom did you dread and fear, that you dealt falsely, and did not remember Me, nor take it to your heart? Have I not been keeping silent, even from long ago, so that you do not fear Me?

**57:12** I Myself will announce your righteousness and your works, and they will not benefit you.

**57:13** When you cry out, let your gathered throngs rescue you! But a wind will carry them all off; a breath will snatch them away. Yet the one who takes refuge in Me will inherit the land and will possess My holy mountain.

**57:14** And it will be said, "Build up, build up, clear a way! Remove the stumbling block from the way of My people!"

**57:15** For this is what the One who is high and exalted, who dwells in eternity, and whose name is Holy says: "I dwell in a high and holy place, and with the one who is crushed and lowly in spirit, to revive the spirit of the lowly, and to revive the heart of the crushed.

**57:16** For I will not strive forever, and I will not be wrathful perpetually; for the spirit would fail before Me, and the breaths of life that I Myself have made.

**57:17** Because of the iniquity of his dishonest gain I was wrathful and struck him; I hid Myself and was wrathful, yet he kept going waywardly in the way of his heart.

**57:18** I have seen his ways, yet I will heal him; I will guide him and restore comforts to him and to his mourners,

**57:19** creating the fruit of the lips. Peace, peace to the one who is far and to the one who is near," says the LORD, "and I will heal him."

**57:20** But the wicked are like the churning sea, for it cannot be still, and its waters churn up mire and mud.

**57:21** "There is no peace," my God says, "for the wicked."

## Translator Notes

- **57:3** — Hebrew mena'ef wattizneh literally pairs a masculine Piel participle ('an adulterer') with a third-person feminine singular waw-consecutive Qal imperfect verb ('and she commits prostitution'), continuing the construct phrase idiomatically.
- **57:6** — Hebrew challaqei-nachal chelqekh creates a wordplay between chalaq ('smooth [stones]') and cheleq ('portion').
- **57:8** — Hebrew yad literally means 'hand' and is used here as a euphemism for male nakedness (or possibly a pagan monument/sign, cf. 56:5).
- **57:13** — Hebrew qibbutzayikh (a plural noun occurring only here, from qavatz 'gather') literally means 'your gatherings/collections,' an ironic reference to Judah's assembled idols or foreign allies.
- **57:19** — Translates the Qere niv ('fruit/produce') rather than the Ketiv nov, an alternate spelling of the same noun.
