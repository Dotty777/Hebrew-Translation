## Text

**30:1** "Woe to rebellious sons," declares the LORD, "who form a plan, yet not from Me, and seal an alliance, yet not by My Spirit, in order to add sin upon sin;

**30:2** who set out to go down to Egypt without inquiring of My mouth, to seek shelter in Pharaoh's stronghold and to take refuge in the shadow of Egypt.

**30:3** So Pharaoh's stronghold will become shame for you, and the refuge in Egypt's shadow, disgrace.

**30:4** For his officials are at Zoan, and his messengers reach Hanes.

**30:5** Everyone comes to shame because of a people who cannot profit them, bringing neither help nor profit, but shame and also reproach."

**30:6** A pronouncement concerning the beasts of the Negev: Through a land of distress and anguish, from which come the lioness and the lion, the viper and the flying fiery serpent, they carry their wealth on the shoulders of young donkeys and their treasures on the humps of camels to a people who cannot profit them.

**30:7** Indeed, Egypt helps in futility and emptiness; therefore I have called this one, "Rahab: they sit idle."

**30:8** Now go, write it on a tablet before them and inscribe it on a scroll, so that it may remain for a future day, forever and ever.

**30:9** For this is a rebellious people, deceitful sons, sons unwilling to listen to the instruction of the LORD,

**30:10** who say to the seers, "Do not see," and to the visionaries, "Do not envision right things for us; speak smooth things to us, envision illusions;

**30:11** turn aside from the way, veer off from the path; remove the Holy One of Israel from before us."

**30:12** Therefore, this is what the Holy One of Israel says: "Because you have rejected this word and trusted in oppression and crookedness and leaned upon it,

**30:13** therefore this iniquity will be to you like a breach ready to fall, bulging out in a lofty wall, whose collapse comes suddenly, in an instant,

**30:14** and it will be shattered like the shattering of a potter's jar, crushed without sparing, so that among its crushed pieces no shard will be found to scoop fire from a hearth or to dip water from a cistern."

**30:15** For this is what the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, says: "By returning and rest you will be saved; in calm and in confidence your strength will be." Yet you were unwilling,

**30:16** and you said, "No, rather on horses we will flee"—therefore you will flee!—"and on swift steeds we will ride"—therefore those who pursue you will be swift!

**30:17** One thousand will flee at the threat of one; at the threat of five you will flee, until you are left like a flagpole on the summit of a mountain and like a banner on a hill.

**30:18** Therefore the LORD waits to show you grace, and therefore He rises on high to have compassion on you. For the LORD is a God of justice; blessed are all who wait for Him.

**30:19** For a people will dwell in Zion, in Jerusalem; you will surely weep no more. He will certainly show you grace at the sound of your outcry; the moment He hears it, He will answer you.

**30:20** And though the Lord gives you bread of distress and water of oppression, your Teacher will no longer hide Himself, and your eyes will continually see your Teacher.

**30:21** And your ears will hear a word behind you, saying, "This is the way; walk in it," whenever you turn to the right or turn to the left.

**30:22** And you will defile the plating of your silver carved images and the sheathing of your gold cast image. You will scatter them like a menstrual cloth; "Get out!" you will say to it.

**30:23** Then He will give rain for your seed with which you sow the ground, and bread from the produce of the ground, and it will be rich and plentiful. In that day your livestock will graze in a broad pasture.

**30:24** And the oxen and the young donkeys that work the ground will eat seasoned fodder that has been winnowed with shovel and winnowing fork.

**30:25** And on every high mountain and on every elevated hill there will be streams and channels of water, on the day of great slaughter, when towers fall.

**30:26** And the light of the moon will be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be sevenfold, like the light of seven days, on the day the LORD binds up the fracture of His people and heals the bruise of their wound.

**30:27** Look, the name of the LORD comes from afar, His anger burning, with heavy rising smoke; His lips are filled with indignation, and His tongue is like a devouring fire.

**30:28** And His breath is like a surging torrent reaching up to the neck, to sift nations in a sieve of ruin, with a bridle that leads astray upon the jaws of the peoples.

**30:29** You will have a song as on the night a festival is consecrated, and gladness of heart like one who walks with a flute to come to the mountain of the LORD, to the Rock of Israel.

**30:30** And the LORD will make the majesty of His voice heard and will show the descent of His arm in furious anger and a flame of devouring fire, with cloudburst, downpour, and hailstones.

**30:31** For at the voice of the LORD Assyria will be terror-stricken; with the rod He will strike.

**30:32** And every stroke of the appointed staff that the LORD brings down upon him will be with tambourines and lyres; and in battles of brandishing He will fight against them.

**30:33** For Topheth has been set in order from long ago; indeed, it has been prepared for the king. He has made it deep and wide; its pyre has fire and wood in abundance. The breath of the LORD, like a torrent of sulfur, sets it ablaze.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — "Seal an alliance" translates Hebrew linsok massekah (Qal infinitive construct of nsk plus cognate noun), literally "to pour out a libation" or "to weave a covering," an idiom for ratifying a treaty.
- **30:7** — The difficult Hebrew clause rahab hem shabet is literally "Rahab, they [are] sitting/inaction," using the Qal infinitive construct shabet (from yshb "sit" or shbt "cease") as an ironic nickname mocking Egypt's boastful power.
- **30:8** — Masoretic vocalization reads la'ad ("forever"), whereas the unvocalized consonants can be read le'ed ("as a witness") as in several ancient versions; translated strictly according to the Masoretic Text.
- **30:20** — Hebrew morekha is morphologically plural ("your teachers"), yet its verb yikkanef (Niphal imperfect 3ms of knf, hapax legomenon, "hide oneself") is singular, indicating an honorific singular ("your Teacher").
- **30:32** — Translated per the Qere bam ("against them") rather than the Ketiv bh ("against her"). Hebrew matteh musadah literally means "staff of appointment" or "staff of foundation."
- **30:33** — Translated per the Qere hi (feminine singular, matching the feminine suffixes later in the verse) rather than the Ketiv hu (masculine singular); both refer to Topheth ("it").
