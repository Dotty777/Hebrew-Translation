## Text

**19:1** A pronouncement concerning Egypt. Look, the LORD is riding on a swift cloud and coming into Egypt; the idols of Egypt will shake before Him, and the heart of Egypt will melt within it.

**19:2** "And I will incite Egypt against Egypt, and they will fight, each against his brother and each against his neighbor, city against city, kingdom against kingdom.

**19:3** And the spirit of Egypt will be emptied out within it, and I will confound its counsel; they will consult the idols and the ghosts, the mediums and the spiritists.

**19:4** And I will deliver Egypt into the hand of a harsh master, and a fierce king will rule over them," declares the Lord, the LORD of hosts.

**19:5** And the waters will fail from the sea, and the river will be parched and dry up.

**19:6** The rivers will grow foul; the channels of Egypt will dwindle and be parched; reed and rush will wither away.

**19:7** There will be bare places along the Nile, at the mouth of the Nile, and all the sown land of the Nile will dry up, be blown away, and be no more.

**19:8** The fishermen will lament, and all who cast a hook into the Nile will mourn, and those who spread a net upon the surface of the waters will languish.

**19:9** Those who work with combed flax and those who weave white cloth will be put to shame.

**19:10** Its pillars will be crushed; all who work for wages will be grieved in soul.

**19:11** The officials of Zoan are sheer fools; the wisest of Pharaoh's counselors give senseless counsel. How can you say to Pharaoh, "I am a son of the wise, a son of kings of old"?

**19:12** Where, then, are your wise men? Let them tell you now, and let them know what the LORD of hosts has planned against Egypt.

**19:13** The officials of Zoan have become fools; the officials of Memphis are deceived; the cornerstones of its tribes have led Egypt astray.

**19:14** The LORD has mixed a spirit of bewilderment within it, and they have caused Egypt to reel in all its work, like a drunkard reeling in his vomit.

**19:15** And there will be no work for Egypt that head or tail, palm branch or reed can do.

**19:16** In that day Egypt will be like women, and it will tremble and be terrified before the brandishing of the hand of the LORD of hosts that He brandishes against it.

**19:17** And the land of Judah will become a terror to Egypt; everyone to whom it is mentioned will be terrified, because of the plan of the LORD of hosts that He is planning against it.

**19:18** In that day there will be five cities in the land of Egypt speaking the language of Canaan and swearing loyalty to the LORD of hosts; one will be called City of Destruction.

**19:19** In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the middle of the land of Egypt, and a pillar beside its border to the LORD.

**19:20** It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of oppressors, He will send them a savior and a champion, and will rescue them.

**19:21** And the LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and the Egyptians will know the LORD in that day; they will worship with sacrifice and offering, and they will make a vow to the LORD and fulfill it.

**19:22** And the LORD will strike Egypt, striking and healing; they will return to the LORD, and He will respond to their plea and heal them.

**19:23** In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria; Assyria will come into Egypt and Egypt into Assyria, and the Egyptians will worship with the Assyrians.

**19:24** In that day Israel will be a third alongside Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth,

**19:25** whom the LORD of hosts has blessed, saying, "Blessed be My people Egypt, and the work of My hands, Assyria, and My inheritance, Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **19:6** — "Will grow foul" renders an unusual double-prefixed Hiphil form (he'eznichu) of zanach ("stink/grow foul"). "Egypt" in "channels of Egypt" translates Matsor, a singular poetic form of the Hebrew name Mitsrayim.
- **19:10** — "Its pillars" translates shatoteha (literally "its foundations/pillars," cf. Ps 11:3, used figuratively of leading citizens), though some derive it from a weaving term ("its weavers"). "All who work for wages" translates the Masoretic Text (osey seker) literally.
- **19:12** — The Masoretic Text reads a Qal jussive (veyed'u, "and let them know"), rather than a causative Hiphil ("make known").
- **19:18** — The Masoretic Text reads Ir ha-Heres ("City of Destruction"); the Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsa-a) and some Hebrew manuscripts read Ir ha-Cheres ("City of the Sun," i.e., Heliopolis).
- **19:23** — The particle et before "Assyria" is taken here as the preposition "with," continuing the cultic sense of "worship" (avad) from 19:21, though grammatically et could also mark a direct object ("serve Assyria").
