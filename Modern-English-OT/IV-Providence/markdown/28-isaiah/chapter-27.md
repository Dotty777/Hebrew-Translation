## Text

**27:1** On that day the LORD, with His severe, great, and strong sword, will punish Leviathan the fleeing serpent and Leviathan the coiled serpent, and He will kill the dragon that is in the sea.

**27:2** On that day: A vineyard of wine! Sing about it:

**27:3** "I, the LORD, am its keeper; moment by moment I water it. So that no one harms it, night and day I guard it.

**27:4** There is no wrath in Me. If only briers and thorns faced Me in battle! I would stride against them; I would set them ablaze altogether.

**27:5** Or else let him take hold of My stronghold; let him make peace with Me, let him make peace with Me."

**27:6** In the days to come Jacob will take root; Israel will blossom and bud, and they will fill the face of the world with fruit.

**27:7** Has He struck him like the striking of his striker? Or has he been killed like the slaughter of those killed by Him?

**27:8** Measure by measure, by sending her away, You contended with her; He removed her with His severe wind on the day of the east wind.

**27:9** Therefore, by this Jacob's iniquity will be atoned for, and this is all the fruit of taking away his sin: when he makes all the altar stones like shattered chalkstones, Asherah poles and incense altars will not stand.

**27:10** For the fortified city is solitary, a dwelling place deserted and forsaken like the wilderness; there a calf grazes, and there it lies down and consumes its branches.

**27:11** When its boughs grow dry, they are broken off; women come and kindle fires with them. For they are not a people of understanding; therefore their Maker will not have compassion on them, and the One who formed them will not be gracious to them.

**27:12** And it will happen on that day that the LORD will thresh from the channel of the River to the Brook of Egypt, and you will be gleaned one by one, sons of Israel.

**27:13** And it will happen on that day that a great ram's horn will be sounded, and those who were perishing in the land of Assyria and the outcasts in the land of Egypt will come, and they will bow down to the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — Masoretic Hebrew reads chemer ("wine"), yielding "a vineyard of wine" (some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint read chemed, "delight/pleasantness"). The verb is a Piel imperative of anah ("sing").
- **27:4** — The phrase mi-yitteneni (literally "who will give me") is a Hebrew wish idiom ("if only"). The feminine singular suffixes translated "them" refer collectively to the briers and thorns.
- **27:7** — Masoretic Hebrew has the Qal passive participle harugav ("those killed by Him," or "his killed ones") rather than an active participle ("his killers"), pairing three forms of nkh ("strike") with three forms of hrg ("kill/slaughter").
- **27:8** — The rare form bese'se'ah is commonly analyzed as a reduplication of se'ah (a dry measure), hence "measure by measure," though some understand it as "by driving away." The Qal perfect hagah here means "removed" or "drove away."
- **27:12** — "The River" refers to the Euphrates, and shibbolet ("channel/flowing stream") plays on its homonym meaning "ear of grain," echoing the harvest verb chavat ("beat out grain/thresh").
