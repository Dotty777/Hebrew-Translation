## Text

**24:1** Look, the LORD is emptying the earth and stripping it bare; He warps its surface and disperses its inhabitants.

**24:2** And it will be: like the people, so the priest; like the servant, so his master; like the female servant, so her mistress; like the buyer, so the seller; like the lender, so the borrower; like the creditor, so the one who owes him.

**24:3** The earth will be entirely emptied and entirely plundered, because the LORD has spoken this word.

**24:4** The earth mourns and fades; the world languishes and fades; the lofty ones of the people of the earth languish.

**24:5** And the earth is defiled beneath its inhabitants, because they have transgressed laws, altered the statute, and broken the eternal covenant.

**24:6** Therefore a curse devours the earth, and those who dwell in it bear guilt; therefore the inhabitants of the earth are scorched, and few mortals remain.

**24:7** The new wine mourns, the vine languishes, and all who are glad of heart groan.

**24:8** The joy of tambourines has ceased, the clamor of the revelers has ended, the joy of the lyre has ceased.

**24:9** With singing they do not drink wine; strong drink turns bitter to those who drink it.

**24:10** The city of chaos is shattered; every house is shut up so that no one may come in.

**24:11** An outcry for wine is in the streets; all gladness has darkened; the joy of the earth has gone into exile.

**24:12** Desolation remains in the city, and the gate is smashed into ruin.

**24:13** For so it will be in the midst of the earth, among the peoples: like the beating of an olive tree, like the gleanings when the grape harvest has ended.

**24:14** They lift up their voice; they sing for joy; over the majesty of the LORD they cry out from the sea.

**24:15** Therefore in the east honor the LORD; in the coastlands of the sea, the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**24:16** From the edge of the earth we have heard songs: "Splendor to the Righteous One!" But I said, "I am wasting away! I am wasting away! Woe to me! The treacherous have acted treacherously; with treachery the treacherous have acted treacherously."

**24:17** Terror, pit, and snare are upon you, inhabitant of the earth!

**24:18** And it will happen that whoever flees from the sound of the terror will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs up from the midst of the pit will be caught in the snare; for the windows from on high are opened, and the foundations of the earth quake.

**24:19** The earth is completely shattered; the earth is thoroughly split apart; the earth totters violently.

**24:20** The earth staggers wildly like a drunkard and sways to and fro like a shelter; its transgression weighs heavily upon it, and it falls and will not rise again.

**24:21** And it will come about on that day that the LORD will punish the host of the height on high, and the kings of the earth on the earth.

**24:22** They will be gathered together as prisoners into a pit, and they will be shut away in prison; after many days they will be punished.

**24:23** Then the moon will be abashed and the sun put to shame, because the LORD of hosts reigns on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and before His elders there will be glory.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — Hebrew charu is the Qal perfect of charar ("be scorched, burned"), matching the consuming curse earlier in the verse.
- **24:15** — Hebrew ba'urim literally means "in the lights" or "in the fires," widely understood as the regions of sunrise in the east, balancing "the coastlands of the sea" in the west.
- **24:16** — Hebrew razi-li literally means "wasting away to me" (from razah, "grow lean, waste away"), paralleling "Woe to me!" rather than referring to a secret.
