## Text

**2:1** The word that Isaiah son of Amoz saw regarding Judah and Jerusalem.

**2:2** And it will happen in the latter days that the mountain of the house of the LORD will be firmly established at the summit of the mountains and lifted up above the hills, and all the nations will stream to it.

**2:3** And many peoples will go and say, "Come, let us ascend to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob, so that He may instruct us about His ways and we may walk in His paths." For from Zion instruction will go out, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

**2:4** And He will judge between the nations and arbitrate for many peoples; they will pound their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning knives; nation will not raise a sword against nation, and they will not learn warfare anymore.

**2:5** House of Jacob, come, and let us walk in the light of the LORD.

**2:6** For You have forsaken Your people, the house of Jacob, because they are filled with what comes from the east, and are diviners like the Philistines, and clasp hands with the children of foreigners.

**2:7** And their land is filled with silver and gold, and their treasuries have no end; their land is filled with horses, and their chariots have no end.

**2:8** And their land is filled with idols; to the work of their hands they bow down, to what their fingers have made.

**2:9** So mankind is bowed down, and each man is brought low; and do not forgive them.

**2:10** Go into the rock and hide yourself in the dust, away from the dread of the LORD and from the splendor of His grandeur.

**2:11** The haughty eyes of mankind will be brought low, and the loftiness of men will be bowed down; and the LORD alone will be exalted on that day.

**2:12** For a day belongs to the LORD of hosts against everything proud and lofty, and against everything lifted up, so that it will be brought low;

**2:13** and against all the lofty and lifted-up cedars of Lebanon, and against all the oaks of Bashan;

**2:14** and against all the lofty mountains, and against all the hills that are lifted up;

**2:15** and against every tall tower, and against every fortified wall;

**2:16** and against all the ships of Tarshish, and against all the prized vessels.

**2:17** And the haughtiness of mankind will be bowed down, and the loftiness of men will be brought low; and the LORD alone will be exalted on that day.

**2:18** And the idols will altogether vanish.

**2:19** And people will go into caves in the rocks and into holes in the dust, away from the dread of the LORD and from the splendor of His grandeur, when He arises to strike terror into the earth.

**2:20** On that day people will fling away their idols of silver and their idols of gold, which they made for themselves to bow down to, to the moles and to the bats,

**2:21** to go into the clefts of the rocks and into the crevices of the cliffs, away from the dread of the LORD and from the splendor of His grandeur, when He arises to strike terror into the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **2:6** — The phrase mal'u miqqedem literally reads "they are filled from the east" without naming the imported practices; yaspiqu (Hiphil of sapaq) denotes slapping or clasping hands to seal a bargain or pact, though some relate it to an Aramaic sense "abound with."
- **2:12** — The final word weshapel is read here as a Qal consecutive perfect verb ("so that it will be brought low"), matching the verbal usage of shapel in vv. 11 and 17 and the list of lofty objects in vv. 13-16, rather than as an adjective ("and low").
- **2:16** — The rare plural construct sekiyyot ("vessels") appears only here in the Hebrew Bible; parallel to "ships of Tarshish," it is widely linked to an Egyptian and Ugaritic term for seagoing craft, though older interpreters derived it from a root meaning "to look at" ("desirable sights" or "works of art").
- **2:20** — The Masoretic Text divides lachpor perot into two words, widely understood as a split spelling of the reduplicated animal name chaparparot ("moles" or "shrews," literally "diggers"), paired here with "bats."
