## Text

**5:1** Let me sing now for my dear friend the song of my beloved concerning His vineyard: My dear friend had a vineyard on a fertile ridge.

**5:2** He hoed it and cleared its stones, and planted it with choice vines. He built a tower inside it and also carved a wine vat in it. Then He hoped for it to produce grapes, but it produced foul grapes.

**5:3** And now, residents of Jerusalem and men of Judah, please judge between Me and My vineyard.

**5:4** What else was to be done for My vineyard that I did not do in it? Why, when I hoped for it to produce grapes, did it produce foul grapes?

**5:5** And now let Me make known to you what I am about to do to My vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it will be consumed; I will breach its wall, and it will be trampled underfoot.

**5:6** And I will make it a desolation; it will neither be pruned nor hoed, and thorns and briers will spring up; and I will command the clouds not to rain any rain on it.

**5:7** For the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah are the planting of His delight. He hoped for justice, yet look, bloodshed; for righteousness, yet look, an outcry!

**5:8** Woe to those who connect house to house, bringing field next to field, until no space remains and you are made to live alone in the middle of the land.

**5:9** In my ears the LORD of hosts has spoken: "Surely many houses will turn to desolation, large and fine ones, with no one dwelling in them.

**5:10** For ten acres of vineyard will produce a single bath, and a homer of seed will produce an ephah."

**5:11** Woe to those who rise early in the morning, chasing after strong drink, who linger into the evening while wine inflames them!

**5:12** Their feasts are lyre and harp, tambourine and flute and wine, yet they do not look at the deed of the LORD, nor do they see the work of His hands.

**5:13** Therefore My people have gone into exile for lack of knowledge; their honored ones are men of hunger, and their throng is parched from thirst.

**5:14** Therefore Sheol has widened its throat and opened its mouth without limit, and down descend her splendor, her throng, her uproar, and whoever exults in her.

**5:15** So mankind is bowed down and each man is brought low, and the eyes of the lofty will be brought low.

**5:16** But the LORD of hosts is lifted high by justice, and the holy God proves Himself holy by righteousness.

**5:17** Then lambs will graze as in their own pasture, and sojourners will feed on the ruins of the wealthy.

**5:18** Woe to those who haul iniquity with cords of deceit, and sin as with wagon ropes,

**5:19** who say, "Let Him hurry, let Him speed up His work so that we may see it; let the plan of the Holy One of Israel approach and arrive so that we may know it!"

**5:20** Woe to those who pronounce evil good and good evil, who set darkness as light and light as darkness, who set bitter as sweet and sweet as bitter!

**5:21** Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes and discerning before their own faces!

**5:22** Woe to those who are champions at drinking wine and men of valor at mixing strong drink,

**5:23** who declare the wicked righteous for a bribe, yet take away the righteousness of the righteous from each of them!

**5:24** Therefore, as a tongue of fire eats up stubble and dry grass sinks into flame, so their root will become like decay and their blossom will rise like dust; because they have rejected the instruction of the LORD of hosts and have spurned the word of the Holy One of Israel.

**5:25** For this reason the anger of the LORD flared against His people, and He stretched out His hand against them and struck them. The mountains quaked, and their corpses were like refuse in the middle of the streets. Despite all this, His anger has not turned back, and still His hand is stretched out.

**5:26** He will raise a banner for the nations far away and whistle for them from the end of the earth; and look, swiftly and rapidly they come!

**5:27** Not one among them is weary, and not one stumbles; not one dozes and not one sleeps; no belt on their waists is loosened, nor is any strap of their sandals snapped.

**5:28** Their arrows are sharpened, and all their bows are strung; the hooves of their horses are reckoned like flint, and their wheels like a storm wind.

**5:29** Their roar is like a lion; they roar like young lions. They growl and seize prey, and carry it away, with no one to rescue.

**5:30** And on that day they will roar over it like the roaring of the sea. And if one looks to the land, look, darkness and distress, and the light has grown dark in its clouds.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The idiom בְּקֶרֶן בֶּן־שָׁמֶן literally reads "on a horn, a son of fatness/oil", denoting a fertile hillside or ridge.
- **5:7** — The Hebrew employs striking wordplay pairing near-homophones: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat, "justice") with מִשְׂפָּח (mispach, "bloodshed/oppression"), and צְדָקָה (tsedaqah, "righteousness") with צְעָקָה (tse'aqah, "outcry").
- **5:10** — A צֶמֶד ("yoke") is the area a pair of oxen could plow in a day (roughly an acre); a בַּת ("bath") is a liquid measure of about 22 liters, and a חֹמֶר ("homer") equals ten אֵיפָה ("ephahs"), indicating a severe crop failure.
- **5:17** — The Masoretic text vocalizes מֵחִים ("fat ones", figuratively the wealthy) and גָּרִים (Qal participle of גור, "sojourners/strangers"), whereas the Septuagint reads "lambs".
- **5:29** — Ketiv reads ושאג (Qal weqatal, "and it will roar"), while Qere reads יִשְׁאַג (Qal imperfect without the conjunction, "they roar"), which is followed here; throughout 5:26b-30 the Hebrew uses collective singular forms for the invading nations named in 5:26a.
