## Text

**11:1** Yet a shoot will come out from the stump of Jesse, and a sprout from his roots will bear fruit.

**11:2** And the Spirit of the LORD will rest on him—the Spirit of wisdom and discernment, the Spirit of counsel and power, the Spirit of knowledge and the fear of the LORD.

**11:3** And his delight will be in the fear of the LORD; he will not judge according to the sight of his eyes, nor decide according to the hearing of his ears.

**11:4** Rather, he will judge the poor in righteousness, and he will decide in fairness for the humble of the earth; with the rod of his mouth he will strike the earth, and with the breath of his lips he will put the wicked to death.

**11:5** And righteousness will be the sash around his waist, and faithfulness the sash around his loins.

**11:6** And a wolf will dwell with a lamb, and a leopard will lie down with a young goat; a calf and a young lion and a fatling will be together, and a small boy will lead them.

**11:7** A cow and a bear will graze; together their young will lie down, and a lion, like the ox, will eat straw.

**11:8** And a nursing infant will play over a cobra's hole, and a weaned child will stretch out his hand over a viper's den.

**11:9** They will do no harm and cause no destruction throughout all My holy mountain, for the earth will be filled with knowledge of the LORD, just as the waters cover the sea.

**11:10** And it will happen in that day that the root of Jesse, who stands as a banner for peoples—nations will seek him out, and his place of rest will be glorious.

**11:11** And it will happen in that day that the Lord will once more reach out His hand a second time to reclaim the remnant of His people that is left from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea.

**11:12** And He will raise a banner for the nations, and He will gather the outcasts of Israel and assemble the scattered ones of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

**11:13** And Ephraim's envy will pass away, and the adversaries of Judah will be cut off; Ephraim will not envy Judah, and Judah will not show hostility toward Ephraim.

**11:14** And they will swoop down on the slope of the Philistines toward the west; together they will loot the sons of the east. Against Edom and Moab they will stretch out their hand, and the sons of Ammon will be obedient to them.

**11:15** And the LORD will completely destroy the tongue of the Sea of Egypt; He will wave His hand over the River with the scorching heat of His wind, and He will strike it into seven streams and make people walk across in sandals.

**11:16** And there will be a highway for the remnant of His people that is left from Assyria, just as there was for Israel on the day they came up from the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — וַהֲרִיחוֹ ("and his delight") is a Hiphil infinitive construct of רוח ("smell") with a 3ms suffix, literally "his smelling," an idiom for taking pleasure or delight in the fear of the LORD.
- **11:8** — Both the noun מְאוּרַת ("den") and the Qal perfect verb הָדָה ("stretch out") occur only here in the Hebrew Bible, with their senses inferred from context and parallelism.
- **11:13** — צֹרְרֵי יְהוּדָה (Qal participle construct of צרר + "Judah") can be read objectively as "the adversaries of Judah" or subjectively as "the hostile ones of Judah," paralleling the final clause of the verse.
- **11:15** — The Masoretic text reads וְהֶחֱרִים (Hiphil of חרם, "completely destroy") rather than "dry up" (חרב) reflected in some ancient versions. בַּעְיָם ("with the scorching heat") is a unique form whose exact etymology is uncertain, traditionally understood as glowing heat or fierce strength.
