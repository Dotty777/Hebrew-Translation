## Text

**8:1** Then the LORD said to me, "Take for yourself a large tablet and write on it with a human stylus, 'Belonging to Maher-shalal-hash-baz.'

**8:2** And I will summon for Myself trustworthy witnesses—Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah."

**8:3** Then I approached the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. And the LORD said to me, "Call his name Maher-shalal-hash-baz;

**8:4** for before the boy knows how to call out, 'My father' and 'My mother,' the wealth of Damascus and the plunder of Samaria will be carried off before the king of Assyria."

**8:5** And the LORD spoke to me yet again, saying,

**8:6** "Because this people has rejected the waters of Shiloah that flow gently, and rejoices with Rezin and the son of Remaliah,

**8:7** therefore, look, the Lord is bringing up over them the mighty and abundant waters of the River—the king of Assyria and all his glory; and it will rise over all its channels and flow over all its banks.

**8:8** It will sweep on into Judah, overflowing and passing through; it will reach up to the neck, and the spread of its wings will fill the width of your land, Immanuel."

**8:9** Break to pieces, peoples, and be shattered, and give ear, all far reaches of the earth! Gird yourselves, and be shattered; gird yourselves, and be shattered!

**8:10** Devise a plan, and it will be frustrated; speak a word, and it will not stand, for God is with us.

**8:11** For this is how the LORD spoke to me with the force of His hand, and admonished me not to walk in the way of this people, saying,

**8:12** "You must not call 'conspiracy' all that this people calls 'conspiracy,' and what it fears you must not fear, nor regard with dread.

**8:13** The LORD of hosts—Him you must regard as holy; He is to be your fear, and He is to be your dread.

**8:14** And He will become a sanctuary, and a stone of striking and a rock of stumbling to the two houses of Israel, a trap and a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**8:15** And many will stumble over them; they will fall and be broken, and be snared and captured."

**8:16** Bind up the testimony; seal the instruction among my disciples.

**8:17** And I will wait for the LORD, who conceals His face from the house of Jacob, and I will hope for Him.

**8:18** Look, I and the children whom the LORD has given me are for signs and portents in Israel from the LORD of hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.

**8:19** And when they say to you, "Inquire of the mediums and the spiritists who chirp and mutter," should not a people inquire of its God? Should they inquire of the dead on behalf of the living?

**8:20** To the instruction and to the testimony! If they do not speak in accordance with this word, it is because there is no dawn for them.

**8:21** Each one will pass through the land, hard-pressed and hungry; and it will happen that when he is hungry, he will grow enraged and curse his king and his God, and turn upward.

**8:22** Then he will look toward the earth, and see: distress and darkness, the gloom of anguish; and he will be thrust into thick darkness.

**8:23** Yet there will be no gloom for her who was in anguish. In the earlier time He brought dishonor upon the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, but in the later time He will bring honor to the way of the sea, the region across the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Hebrew becheret enosh (literally "with a human stylus") refers to ordinary, legible script; the inscribed name Maher-shalal-hash-baz means "Swift to the plunder, speedy to the prey," prefixed with lamed ("Belonging to").
- **8:6** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads umesos ("and rejoicing") with Rezin and the son of Remaliah, an unusual nominal construction continuing the preceding clause; it is translated as written rather than emended to a verb meaning "melt in fear."
- **8:9** — Hebrew ro'u is the Qal imperative of r'' ("break" or "shatter"), pairing with wechottu ("and be shattered"); some ancient versions instead reflect a reading from yd' ("know") or r'h ("band together").
- **8:16** — Taken here as Isaiah's instruction following the end of the LORD's speech in verse 15 ("my disciples"); some interpreters extend the LORD's speech through verse 16, yielding "My disciples."
- **8:23** — In Masoretic versification this verse concludes chapter 8 (corresponding to 9:1 in most English Bibles); it contrasts two Hiphil perfects of opposite roots: heqal (from qll, literally "made light" or "dishonored") and hikbid (from kbd, literally "made weighty" or "honored").
