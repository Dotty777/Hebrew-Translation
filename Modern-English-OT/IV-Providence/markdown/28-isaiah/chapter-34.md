## Text

**34:1** Come near, nations, to hear, and pay close attention, peoples! Let the earth hear, and its fullness, the world and everything that comes forth from it.

**34:2** For the LORD has wrath against all the nations, and fury against all their host; He has doomed them to destruction, He has given them to the slaughter.

**34:3** And their fallen will be thrown out, and from their corpses their stench will rise, and mountains will dissolve with their blood.

**34:4** And all the host of the heavens will decay, and the heavens will be rolled together like a scroll; and all their host will wither, like the withering of a leaf from a vine and like what withers from a fig tree.

**34:5** For My sword has been drenched in the heavens; look, it will descend upon Edom, and upon the people I have doomed to destruction, for judgment.

**34:6** The LORD has a sword, full of blood, made rich with fat, with the blood of lambs and male goats, with the fat of rams' kidneys; for the LORD holds a sacrifice in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of Edom.

**34:7** And wild oxen will go down with them, and young bulls with mighty ones; their land will be drenched with blood, and their dust will be made rich with fat.

**34:8** For a day of vengeance belongs to the LORD, a year of retribution for Zion's cause.

**34:9** And its streams will be turned to pitch, and its dust to sulfur, and its land will become blazing pitch.

**34:10** Night and day it will not go out; forever its smoke will ascend. From generation to generation it will remain waste; for all eternity no one will pass through it.

**34:11** The desert owl and the hedgehog will possess it, and the great owl and the raven will dwell in it; and He will stretch out over it the measuring line of formlessness and the plumb stones of emptiness.

**34:12** As for its nobles, there is no one there to proclaim a kingdom, and all its officials will come to nothing.

**34:13** And thorns will spring up in its citadels, nettles and brambles in its fortresses; it will become a lair of jackals, an enclosure for ostriches.

**34:14** And desert beasts will meet with hyenas, and a wild goat will call to its companion; indeed, there the night creature will settle down and find a resting place for herself.

**34:15** There the arrow snake will make a nest and lay eggs, and hatch and brood in its shade; indeed, there the kites will be gathered, each with her companion.

**34:16** Search from the scroll of the LORD and read: not one of them will be missing; none will lack her companion. For it is My mouth that has commanded, and it is His Spirit who has gathered them.

**34:17** And He Himself has cast a lot for them, and His hand has apportioned it to them with the measuring line; forever they will possess it, from generation to generation they will dwell in it.

## Translator Notes

- **34:6** — The verb 'made rich with fat' renders the rare Hothpaal passive-reflexive stem of dshn (huddashnah), echoed by the Pual form of the same root in v. 7.
- **34:11** — The exact species denoted by several desert animals in vv. 11 and 14-15 are uncertain; 'measuring line of formlessness and the plumb stones of emptiness' echoes tohu wabohu in Gen 1:2.
- **34:12** — The MT syntax in the first clause is highly compressed, literally reading 'its nobles--and there is none there--a kingdom they will proclaim,' meaning no noble remains there to proclaim kingship.
- **34:14** — The hapax noun lilit ('night creature,' often transliterated 'Lilith') refers to a nocturnal bird or night demon in ancient Near Eastern lore.
- **34:16** — The MT shifts directly from first person ('My mouth,' pi) to third person ('His Spirit,' rucho), with emphatic independent pronouns reinforcing each subject.
