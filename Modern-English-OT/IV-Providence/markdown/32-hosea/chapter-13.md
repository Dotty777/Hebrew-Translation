## Text

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he lifted himself up in Israel, yet he became guilty through Baal and died.

**13:2** And now they go on sinning and make for themselves a cast image from their silver, idols according to their skill, all of it the work of craftsmen. About them they say, "Those who sacrifice humans kiss calves."

**13:3** Therefore they will be like a morning cloud and like the dew that departs early, like chaff whirled from a threshing floor and like smoke from a window.

**13:4** Yet I am the LORD your God from the land of Egypt; you are to know no God besides Me, and there is no savior apart from Me.

**13:5** I Myself knew you in the wilderness, in a land of drought.

**13:6** According to their pasture they were satisfied; they were satisfied, and their heart grew lofty; for that reason they forgot Me.

**13:7** So I have become to them like a lion; like a leopard along the road I will keep watch.

**13:8** I will confront them like a bear bereaved of her cubs, and I will tear open the enclosure of their heart; and I will devour them there like a lioness; a wild beast of the field will rip them open.

**13:9** It has ruined you, Israel, because you are against Me, against your helper.

**13:10** Where then is your king, that he may save you in all your cities, and your judges, about whom you said, "Give me a king and officials"?

**13:11** I give you a king in My anger, and I take him away in My fury.

**13:12** Ephraim's iniquity is bound fast; his sin is stored away.

**13:13** The pangs of a woman in labor come upon him; he is an unwise son, for when it is time he does not take his stand at the mouth of the womb.

**13:14** Should I ransom them from the hand of Sheol? Should I redeem them from death? Where are your plagues, Death? Where is your destruction, Sheol? Compassion will be hidden from My eyes.

**13:15** Though he flourishes among brothers, an east wind will come, the wind of the LORD rising from the wilderness; and his fountain will dry up, and his spring will be parched. It will plunder the storehouse of every prized article.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The final clause is grammatically difficult; literally "sacrificers of mankind kiss calves," it may also be understood as a partitive construction, "let those among mankind who sacrifice kiss calves."
- **13:9** — The Hebrew is extremely terse, literally "It has ruined you, Israel, for against Me, against your help." The third-person singular Piel verb takes Israel's rebellion as its implied subject, with the repeated preposition marking apposition ("against Me, your helper").
- **13:14** — The opening clauses lack an interrogative marker in Hebrew and may alternatively be read declaratively ("I will ransom them... I will redeem them..."). The rare form rendered "Where" here and in v. 10 functions as an interrogative adverb rather than the verb "I will be," and the noun rendered "destruction" denotes deadly blight or plague.
- **13:15** — The verb "flourishes" is a Hiphil form of the root for bearing fruit spelled with an aleph, forming a wordplay on the name Ephraim. The masculine pronoun "It" resumes the east wind of the LORD, though it may also allude to the invading power or to the LORD Himself.
