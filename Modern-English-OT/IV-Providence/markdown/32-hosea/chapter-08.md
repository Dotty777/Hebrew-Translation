## Text

**8:1** Put the horn to your mouth! Like an eagle over the house of the LORD, because they have violated My covenant, and against My law they have rebelled.

**8:2** To Me they cry out, "My God, we, Israel, know You!"

**8:3** Israel has spurned what is good; an enemy will pursue him.

**8:4** They have installed kings, yet not from Me; they have appointed officials, yet I did not know. With their silver and their gold they have made idols for themselves, so that it may be cut off.

**8:5** He has spurned your calf, Samaria! My anger blazes against them. How long will they be unable to attain innocence?

**8:6** For it too is from Israel; a craftsman made it, and it is not God. Surely the calf of Samaria will become splinters.

**8:7** For wind they sow, and a whirlwind they will harvest. It has no standing grain; the sprout yields no flour; even if it should yield, foreigners will swallow it up.

**8:8** Israel has been swallowed up; now they are among the nations like a vessel in which there is no pleasure.

**8:9** For they themselves have gone up to Assyria, a solitary wild donkey by itself; Ephraim has hired lovers.

**8:10** Even if they hire among the nations, now I will gather them together, and they will start to grow few under the burden of the king of officials.

**8:11** Because Ephraim has multiplied altars for sinning, they have become to him altars for sinning.

**8:12** Though I write for him the many things of My law, they are counted as something foreign.

**8:13** As for the sacrifices of My offerings, they sacrifice meat and eat it, yet the LORD does not accept them. Now He will remember their guilt and punish their sins; they themselves will return to Egypt.

**8:14** Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces, and Judah has multiplied fortified cities; so I will send fire into his cities, and it will devour her citadels.

## Translator Notes

- **8:5** — Hebrew zanach is a 3ms Qal perfect ('He has spurned'), deliberately echoing Israel's rejection of what is good in v. 3; others construe it passively ('Your calf is spurned') or link it to a homonymous root meaning 'be foul'.
- **8:10** — Hebrew vayyachellu meat is a difficult Masoretic phrase: vayyachellu is pointed as a Hiphil of chalal ('begin', here with meat, 'few', yielding 'start to grow few'), though some relate it to chul ('writhe') with meat as 'soon'. Hebrew melekh sarim literally reads 'king of officials', an allusion to the Assyrian monarch.
- **8:12** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text (ketiv) reads ekhtov (plene spelling) and ribbo ('ten thousand' or 'a myriad'), while the read text (qere) followed here has ekhtov- (defective spelling) and rubbe ('multitudes' or 'many things'), agreeing with the plural Niphal verb nechshavu ('they are counted').
- **8:13** — Hebrew havhavay ('My offerings') is a hapax legomenon, a reduplicated noun from yahav ('give') with a 1cs suffix.
