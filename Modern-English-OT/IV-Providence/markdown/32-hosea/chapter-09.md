## Text

**9:1** Do not rejoice, Israel, to the point of exultation like the peoples, for you have prostituted yourself, forsaking your God; you have loved a prostitute's wage on all threshing floors of grain.

**9:2** Threshing floor and winepress will not feed them, and new wine will fail her.

**9:3** They will not dwell in the land of the LORD, but Ephraim will return to Egypt, and in Assyria they will eat what is unclean.

**9:4** They will not pour out wine to the LORD, nor will they be pleasing to Him; their sacrifices will be to them like bread of mourning. All who eat it will become unclean, for their bread is for their own appetite; it will not enter the house of the LORD.

**9:5** What will you do for the day of the appointed assembly, and for the day of the LORD's feast?

**9:6** For look, they are gone because of destruction; Egypt will gather them, Memphis will bury them. As for the precious things of their silver, nettles will take possession of them; thorns will be in their tents.

**9:7** The days of reckoning have come, the days of repayment have come; Israel will know! The prophet is a fool; the man of the spirit is mad, because of the greatness of your guilt, and great is the hostility.

**9:8** The watchman over Ephraim is with my God; as for the prophet, a fowler's snare is on all his ways, and hostility is in the house of his God.

**9:9** They have sunk deep in corruption, as in the days of Gibeah; He will remember their guilt; He will punish their sins.

**9:10** Like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel; like an early fig on a fig tree in its first season I saw your fathers. Yet they came to Baal-peor and consecrated themselves to the shameful thing, and they became detestable things like what they loved.

**9:11** Ephraim—like a bird their glory will fly away: no childbirth, no womb, and no conception.

**9:12** Even if they raise their children, I will bereave them until not a person is left; yes, woe to them as well when I depart from them!

**9:13** Ephraim, just as I saw concerning Tyre, is planted in a meadow; yet Ephraim must bring out his children to the killer.

**9:14** Give them, LORD—what will You give? Give them a miscarrying womb and dried-up breasts.

**9:15** All their wickedness is in Gilgal, for there I hated them. Because of the evil of their deeds, from My house I will drive them out. I will no longer love them; all their officials are rebels.

**9:16** Ephraim is struck down; their root has dried up; they will yield no fruit. Even if they give birth, I will put to death the precious ones of their womb.

**9:17** My God will reject them, for they have not listened to Him, and they will become wanderers among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Masoretic Text reads el-gil ('to exultation'), whereas the Septuagint reflects a negative jussive al-tagel ('do not exult').
- **9:2** — The Masoretic Text has a 3fs suffix bah ('her'), referring to Israel personified as an unfaithful wife (v. 1), though several ancient versions harmonize to plural 'them'.
- **9:8** — The opening clause tzofeh Efrayim im-elohay is terse: tzofeh is a Qal active participle ('watchman' or 'watching over') taking Ephraim as its object; some instead render 'Ephraim is a watchman with my God'.
- **9:13** — A famously difficult Masoretic verse: kaasher-raiti leTzor ('just as I saw concerning Tyre') uses le- as a marker of reference, and lehotzi is a Hiphil infinitive construct with le- expressing impending obligation ('must bring out').
- **9:16** — Ketiv/Qere: the written text (ketiv) reads the poetic negative beli, while the read text (qere) followed here has bal; both mean 'not'.
