## Text

**7:1** Whenever I would heal Israel, the guilt of Ephraim is laid bare, and the wicked acts of Samaria, for they practice falsehood; a thief comes in, and a troop raids outside.

**7:2** And they do not say to their heart that I remember all their wickedness. Now their deeds surround them; they are before My face.

**7:3** With their wickedness they make a king glad, and with their lies, officials.

**7:4** All of them are adulterers, like an oven set burning by a baker, who stops stoking from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.

**7:5** On the day of our king, the officials grew sick with heat from wine; he stretched out his hand with mockers.

**7:6** For they have brought their heart near like an oven in their ambush; all night their baker sleeps; in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire.

**7:7** All of them grow hot like an oven and devour their judges. All their kings have fallen; not one among them calls to Me.

**7:8** Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples; Ephraim has become a flat cake not turned over.

**7:9** Foreigners have devoured his strength, and he does not know; gray hair as well is sprinkled over him, and he does not know.

**7:10** And Israel's pride testifies to his face, yet they have not returned to the LORD their God, nor have they sought Him despite all this.

**7:11** And Ephraim has become like a gullible dove, devoid of sense; to Egypt they call, to Assyria they go.

**7:12** As they go, I will spread My net over them; like the birds of the sky I will bring them down. I will discipline them according to the announcement to their assembly.

**7:13** Woe to them, for they have wandered from Me! Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against Me! I would redeem them, yet they have spoken lies against Me.

**7:14** And they do not cry out to Me with their heart, but instead they wail on their beds; for grain and new wine they gather together; they turn against Me.

**7:15** I Myself trained and strengthened their arms, yet against Me they plot evil.

**7:16** They turn, but not upward; they are like a treacherous bow. Their officials will fall by the sword because of the rage of their tongue; this will be their mockery in the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **7:5** — Hebrew hehelu is a Hiphil perfect of chalah, taken here in an inchoative sense ('grew sick'), though some parse it transitively ('they made the officials sick'). The construct phrase chamat miyyayin ('heat from wine') places a prepositional phrase directly after a construct noun.
- **7:6** — The Masoretic Text reads qerevu (Piel perfect of qarav, 'brought near') and ofehem ('their baker', continuing the oven metaphor of v. 4); some ancient versions reflect vocalizations meaning 'their inward parts' and 'their anger'.
- **7:14** — Masoretic yitgoraru is a Hithpolel imperfect of gur ('gather together' or 'assemble'). A graphic variant reflected in the Septuagint and some Hebrew manuscripts reads yitgodadu from gadad ('gash themselves').
- **7:16** — Hebrew lo al literally means 'not upward' (or 'not to the height'), often understood as 'not to the Most High'.
