## Text

**12:1** Ephraim has surrounded Me with deception, and the house of Israel with deceit; Judah still roams unruly against God, against the faithful Holy One.

**12:2** Ephraim herds the wind and pursues the east wind; all day long he multiplies falsehood and devastation. They make a covenant with Assyria, and oil is carried to Egypt.

**12:3** The LORD also holds a dispute with Judah, and He will call Jacob to account according to his ways; according to his deeds He will pay him back.

**12:4** In the womb he grasped his brother by the heel, and in his vigor he struggled with God.

**12:5** He struggled with an angel and prevailed; he wept and pleaded with Him for favor. At Bethel He found him, and there He spoke with us,

**12:6** Even the LORD, the God of hosts—the LORD is His renown.

**12:7** So as for you, return to your God; keep loyal love and justice, and wait always for your God.

**12:8** A trader—in his hand are scales of deceit; he loves to defraud.

**12:9** And Ephraim said, "Indeed, I have grown rich; I have found wealth for myself. In all my toil they will not find in me any iniquity that is sin."

**12:10** Yet I am the LORD your God from the land of Egypt; once more I will cause you to dwell in tents, like the days of the appointed festival.

**12:11** And I spoke to the prophets; I Myself multiplied visions, and by the hand of the prophets I delivered parables.

**12:12** If Gilead is iniquity, surely they have become nothingness. In Gilgal they have sacrificed bulls; their altars too will be like stone heaps along the furrows of the field.

**12:13** Now Jacob fled to the country of Aram; Israel worked for a wife, and for a wife he kept watch.

**12:14** And by a prophet the LORD brought Israel up out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was watched over.

**12:15** Ephraim has provoked bitter anger; therefore He will leave his bloodguilt upon him, and his Lord will pay back his reproach to him.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Hebrew rad (Qal perfect of rud, 'roam restlessly/be unruly') with im-qedoshim ne'eman (plural of majesty 'the Holy One' plus Niphal participle 'faithful') is read here negatively in line with 12:3; others take the clause positively as 'still rules/walks with God and is faithful to the Holy One.'
- **12:5** — MT reads immanu ('with us'), identifying Hosea's generation with Jacob at Bethel; yimtsa'ennu ('He found him') takes God as subject consistently with the following verb, though some read 'he [Jacob] found Him.'
- **12:8** — Hebrew kena'an ('Canaan') is a biting wordplay meaning both 'trader/merchant' and the Canaanite nation.
- **12:13** — Hebrew shamar (Qal perfect of shamar, 'keep/guard/keep watch') has no expressed object here (referring to tending Laban's flocks) and pairs directly with Niphal nishmar ('was watched over/kept') in 12:14.
