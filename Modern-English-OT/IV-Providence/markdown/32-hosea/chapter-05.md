## Text

**5:1** Hear this, priests; pay close attention, house of Israel; and house of the king, give ear! For the judgment is against you, because you have been a snare for Mizpah and a net spread out over Tabor.

**5:2** And the rebels have plunged deep into slaughter, yet I am a discipline for all of them.

**5:3** I know Ephraim, and Israel is not concealed from Me; for now you have engaged in prostitution, Ephraim; Israel is defiled.

**5:4** Their deeds do not let them return to their God, because a spirit of prostitution is within them, and they do not know the LORD.

**5:5** Israel's pride testifies to his face; Israel and Ephraim will stumble in their iniquity; Judah too has stumbled with them.

**5:6** With their flocks and with their herds they will go to seek the LORD, yet they will not find Him; He has drawn away from them.

**5:7** They have betrayed the LORD, for they have borne alien children; now a new moon will devour them together with their allotments.

**5:8** Blow the ram's horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah! Sound the alarm in Beth-aven! Behind you, Benjamin!

**5:9** Ephraim will become a wasteland on the day of rebuke; among the tribes of Israel I have made known what is certain.

**5:10** The officials of Judah have become like those who shift a boundary; upon them I will pour out My fury like water.

**5:11** Ephraim is oppressed, crushed by judgment, because he resolved to walk after a precept.

**5:12** So I am like a moth to Ephraim, and like decay to the house of Judah.

**5:13** When Ephraim saw his illness and Judah his wound, Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to King Jareb; yet he is not able to heal you, nor will he cure your wound.

**5:14** For I am like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to the house of Judah. I, yes I, will tear apart and go away; I will carry off, and there is no one to rescue.

**5:15** I will go and return to My place until they admit their guilt and seek My face; in their distress they will diligently seek Me.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Masoretic Text reads shachatah setim he'miqu ("the rebels have plunged deep into slaughter"), using a Hiphil perfect of amaq with setim ("rebels, apostates"); "I am a discipline for all of them" preserves the literal nominal clause using musar ("discipline, correction").
- **5:7** — Hebrew chodesh means either "new moon" (referring to the New Moon festival) or "a month," and chelqehem denotes their allotted portions or tracts of land.
- **5:11** — The rare Hebrew noun tsav (from tsavah, "command") means "precept" or "command," likely referring to unauthorized human or idolatrous decrees; the Septuagint instead reads "vanity."
- **5:13** — The Masoretic Text divides the title as melekh yarev ("King Jareb"), either a symbolic epithet meaning "contending king" (from riv) or a wordplay on the Assyrian royal title "the great king."
