## Text

**4:1** Hear the word of the LORD, sons of Israel, for the LORD has a dispute with the residents of the land, because there is no truth, no loyal love, and no knowledge of God in the land.

**4:2** Cursing, lying, murdering, stealing, and committing adultery—they break out, and bloodshed touches bloodshed.

**4:3** Therefore the land mourns, and everyone dwelling in it wastes away, together with the wild animals of the field and the birds of the sky, and even the fish of the sea are swept away.

**4:4** Yet let no one dispute, and let no one rebuke, for your people are like those who dispute with a priest.

**4:5** You will stumble by day, and a prophet will also stumble with you by night; and I will bring your mother to ruin.

**4:6** My people are brought to ruin for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I will reject you from serving as priest to Me; and since you have forgotten the instruction of your God, I too will forget your children.

**4:7** As they grew numerous, so they sinned against Me; I will exchange their glory for disgrace.

**4:8** They eat the sin of My people, and each one sets his appetite on their iniquity.

**4:9** And it will be the same for the people as for the priest; I will call them to account for their ways and bring their deeds back upon them.

**4:10** They will eat, yet not be satisfied; they will engage in prostitution, yet not increase, because they have abandoned heeding the LORD.

**4:11** Prostitution, wine, and new wine take away discernment.

**4:12** My people inquire of their piece of wood, and their staff declares to them; for a spirit of prostitution has led them astray, and they have engaged in prostitution, departing from under their God.

**4:13** On the summits of the mountains they offer sacrifices, and on the hills they burn incense, under oak, poplar, and terebinth, because their shade is good. Therefore your daughters engage in prostitution, and your daughters-in-law commit adultery.

**4:14** I will not call your daughters to account when they engage in prostitution, nor your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery, for they themselves go aside with prostitutes and offer sacrifices with shrine prostitutes; and a people who do not understand will be thrown down.

**4:15** Though you engage in prostitution, Israel, let Judah not incur guilt; do not come to Gilgal, do not go up to Beth-aven, and do not swear, "As the LORD lives."

**4:16** For like a stubborn heifer, Israel has been stubborn; will the LORD now shepherd them like a lamb in an open pasture?

**4:17** Ephraim is bound to idols; let him be.

**4:18** Once their liquor is gone, they continually engage in prostitution; her rulers deeply love disgrace.

**4:19** A wind has bound her up in its wings, and they will be put to shame because of their sacrifices.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Masoretic Text reads we'ammekha kimrivei khohen ("for your people are like those who dispute with a priest"), using a Hiphil masculine plural construct participle of riv; some modern versions emend the text to read "my dispute is with you, priest."
- **4:6** — The written Ketiv reads w'm's'k with an extra aleph before the pronominal suffix; the translation follows the read Qere we'em'asekha ("and I will reject you"), the standard Qal imperfect 1cs form of ma'as with a 2ms suffix.
- **4:8** — The Hebrew noun chatta't means both "sin" and "sin offering," creating a wordplay on priests eating sacrificial sin offerings while feeding on the people's wrongdoing; the singular suffix on nafsho ("his appetite") is distributive.
- **4:10** — The Masoretic verse division places the infinitive lishmor ("to heed") at the end of verse 10 rather than joining it to the opening nouns of verse 11.
- **4:18** — The clause rendered "her rulers deeply love disgrace" translates the difficult Masoretic wording ahavu hevu qalon maginneha, taking ahavu hevu as an emphatic doubling of "love" (though hevu is sometimes read as the imperative "Give!") and maginneha (literally "her shields") as a metaphor for rulers.
