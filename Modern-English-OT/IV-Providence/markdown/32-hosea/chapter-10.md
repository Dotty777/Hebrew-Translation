## Text

**10:1** Israel is a spreading vine; he puts forth fruit for himself. According to the abundance of his fruit, he multiplied altars; according to the goodness of his land, they made fine standing stones.

**10:2** Their heart is deceitful; now they will bear guilt. He Himself will demolish their altars; He will devastate their standing stones.

**10:3** For now they will say, "There is no king for us, because we did not fear the LORD; and the king, what would he do for us?"

**10:4** They speak words, swearing false oaths, making covenants; so judgment blossoms like poison-weed along the furrows of the field.

**10:5** The inhabitants of Samaria will be in dread over the calves of Beth-aven; for its people mourn over it, and its idol-priests wail over it, over its glory, because it has gone into exile from it.

**10:6** It too will be carried to Assyria as tribute to King Jareb; Ephraim will receive disgrace, and Israel will be ashamed of his counsel.

**10:7** As for Samaria, her king is cut off like a splinter on the surface of the water.

**10:8** And the high places of Aven, the sin of Israel, will be destroyed; thorn and thistle will spring up over their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, "Cover us!" and to the hills, "Fall upon us!"

**10:9** From the days of Gibeah you have sinned, Israel; there they have remained. Will war against the sons of unrighteousness not overtake them at Gibeah?

**10:10** At My desire I will discipline them, and peoples will be gathered against them when they are bound for their two iniquities.

**10:11** Ephraim is a trained heifer loving to thresh, and I passed over her fine neck; I will hitch up Ephraim, Judah will plow, Jacob will harrow for himself.

**10:12** Sow for yourselves toward righteousness; reap according to the measure of loyal love. Break up unplowed ground for yourselves, for it is time to seek the LORD, until He comes and showers righteousness upon you.

**10:13** You have plowed wickedness; you have reaped unrighteousness; you have eaten the fruit of deception, for you trusted in your way, in the abundance of your mighty men.

**10:14** So an uproar will arise among your people, and all your fortresses will be devastated, just as Shalman devastated Beth-arbel on the day of battle, when a mother was dashed to pieces along with children.

**10:15** Thus Bethel will do to you because of the evil of your wickedness; at dawn the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — Hebrew boqeq (Qal participle of baqaq) is understood here as 'spreading/luxuriant' from the context of abundant fruit, though the homonymous root means 'emptying.'
- **10:2** — Hebrew chalaq (Qal perfect) is stative 'is smooth/deceitful' (chalaq II), though some render it 'is divided' (chalaq I); ya'arof (Qal imperfect of araf) literally means 'will break the neck of.'
- **10:5** — MT uses the feminine plural eglot ('calves/heifers') alongside masculine singular suffixes referring to the idol of Beth-aven; yagilu (Qal imperfect of gil) usually means 'rejoice' and is taken here as 'wail/tremble' in grief or ironically as former exultation.
- **10:6** — MT reads le-melekh yarev ('to King Jareb,' perhaps an epithet meaning 'contending king'); some redivide the consonants as malki rav, 'the great king.'
- **10:10** — Translates the Qere onotam ('their iniquities,' contracted plural of awon) rather than the Ketiv eynotam (resembling 'their eyes' or 'their springs'); Hebrew also pairs essorem (Qal imperfect of yasar, 'discipline') with be-osram (Qal infinitive construct of asar, 'bind').
