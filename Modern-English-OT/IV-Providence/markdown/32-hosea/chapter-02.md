## Text

**2:1** Yet the number of the sons of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured and cannot be numbered. And it will happen that in the place where it is said to them, "You are not My people," it will be said to them, "Sons of the living God."

**2:2** And the sons of Judah and the sons of Israel will gather together, and they will set one head for themselves and go up from the land, for the day of Jezreel will be great.

**2:3** Call your brothers "Ammi" and your sisters "Ruhamah."

**2:4** Contend against your mother, contend, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband; let her remove her prostitution from her face and her adultery from between her breasts,

**2:5** or else I will strip her naked and expose her as on the day of her birth, and I will make her like the wilderness, set her like a parched land, and put her to death with thirst.

**2:6** And to her children I will not show compassion, for they are children of prostitution.

**2:7** For their mother has prostituted herself; the one who conceived them has behaved shamefully. For she said, "I will go after my lovers, the givers of my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drinks."

**2:8** Therefore, look, I am about to hedge up your way with thorns, and I will erect a wall against her so that she will not find her paths.

**2:9** She will eagerly pursue her lovers, yet she will not catch up with them; she will seek them, yet she will not find them. Then she will say, "I will go and return to my first husband, for it was better for me then than it is now."

**2:10** Yet she did not know that I Myself gave her the grain, the new wine, and the fresh oil, and multiplied silver and gold for her, which they used for Baal.

**2:11** Therefore I will return and take My grain in its time and My new wine at its appointed season, and I will snatch away My wool and My flax meant to cover her nakedness.

**2:12** And now I will expose her indecency before the eyes of her lovers, and no one will rescue her from My hand.

**2:13** And I will bring all her rejoicing to an end: her feast, her new moon, her Sabbath, and every appointed festival of hers.

**2:14** And I will devastate her vine and her fig tree, of which she said, "They are my wages that my lovers gave me." I will turn them into a forest, and the wild animals of the field will devour them.

**2:15** And I will call her to account for the days of the Baals, when she would burn incense to them, adorned herself with her ring and her jewelry, and went after her lovers, yet she forgot Me, declares the LORD.

**2:16** Therefore, look, I am going to allure her, lead her into the wilderness, and speak to her heart.

**2:17** And I will give her her vineyards from there, and the Valley of Achor as a doorway of hope; and she will answer there as in the days of her youth, and as on the day she went up from the land of Egypt.

**2:18** And it will happen on that day, declares the LORD, that you will call out, "My husband," and you will no longer call Me "My Baal."

**2:19** And I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and they will no longer be remembered by their name.

**2:20** And on that day I will make a covenant for them with the wild animals of the field, and with the birds of the heavens and the creeping things of the ground; I will break bow, sword, and war from the land, and I will cause them to lie down in safety.

**2:21** And I will betroth you to Me forever; I will betroth you to Me with righteousness and with justice, with loyal love and with compassion.

**2:22** And I will betroth you to Me with faithfulness, and you will know the LORD.

**2:23** And it will happen on that day that I will answer, declares the LORD; I will answer the heavens, and they will answer the earth;

**2:24** and the earth will answer the grain, the new wine, and the fresh oil, and they will answer Jezreel.

**2:25** And I will sow her for Myself in the land, and I will show compassion to Lo-ruhamah; and I will say to Lo-ammi, "You are My people," and he will say, "My God."

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — Ammi ("My people") and Ruhamah ("She has been shown compassion") reverse the names Lo-ammi and Lo-ruhamah from chapter 1.
- **2:8** — The Masoretic Hebrew shifts abruptly from second-person feminine singular ("your way," darkek) to third-person feminine singular ("her wall ... her paths ... she will not find"); "erect a wall against her" renders a cognate construction literally "wall up her wall."
- **2:17** — Achor means "trouble" (cf. Joshua 7:26). The Qal verb anetah ("she will answer") links to the fivefold "answer" in 2:23-24, though the same root can also mean "sing" (as in Exodus 15:21).
- **2:18** — Hebrew baali means both "my master/husband" and "my Baal," creating a deliberate link to "the names of the Baals" in 2:19 in contrast to ishi ("my husband").
- **2:21** — The preposition be- ("with") with the Piel verb aras ("betroth") idiomatically marks the bride-price paid by the groom (cf. 2 Samuel 3:14); "loyal love" renders Hebrew hesed.
