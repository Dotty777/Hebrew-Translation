## Text

**7:1** In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel saw a dream and visions in his head while on his bed. Then he wrote down the dream and told the summary of the matter.

**7:2** Daniel spoke up and said, "I was looking in my vision by night, and look, the four winds of heaven were churning up the great sea.

**7:3** And four great beasts were coming up out of the sea, different from one another.

**7:4** The first was like a lion and had wings of an eagle. I kept looking until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted up from the ground and made to stand on two feet like a man, and the heart of a man was given to it.

**7:5** And look, another beast, a second one, resembling a bear. It was raised up on one side, with three ribs in its mouth between its teeth, and they were saying to it, 'Rise up, devour much flesh!'

**7:6** After this I kept looking, and look, another one, like a leopard, and it had four wings of a bird on its back; the beast also had four heads, and dominion was given to it.

**7:7** After this I kept looking in the night visions, and look, a fourth beast, terrifying and dreadful and exceedingly strong, and it had great iron teeth; it was devouring and crushing, and trampling down the rest with its feet. It was different from all the beasts that were before it, and it had ten horns.

**7:8** While I was closely observing the horns, look, another horn, a small one, came up among them, and three of the first horns were uprooted before it; and look, in this horn were eyes like the eyes of a man, and a mouth speaking great things.

**7:9** I kept looking until thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days took His seat; His clothing was white like snow, and the hair of His head was like pure wool; His throne was flames of fire, and its wheels were blazing fire.

**7:10** A river of fire was flowing and coming out from before Him; a thousand thousands were attending Him, and ten thousand times ten thousand were standing before Him. The court sat in session, and books were opened.

**7:11** Then I kept looking because of the sound of the great words that the horn was speaking; I kept looking until the beast was killed, and its body was destroyed and it was given over to the burning fire.

**7:12** As for the rest of the beasts, their dominion was taken away, yet a prolongation of life was given to them for a time and a season.

**7:13** I kept looking in the night visions, and look, with the clouds of heaven one like a son of man was coming; he came up to the Ancient of Days, and they brought him near before Him.

**7:14** And to him were given dominion, honor, and a kingdom, that all the peoples, nations, and languages should serve him; his dominion is an everlasting dominion that will not pass away, and his kingdom is one that will not be destroyed.

**7:15** As for me, Daniel, my spirit was distressed within its sheath, and the visions in my head kept alarming me.

**7:16** I approached one of those standing there and asked him the truth about all this. So he spoke to me and made known to me the interpretation of these matters:

**7:17** 'These great beasts, which are four, are four kings who will arise from the earth.

**7:18** But the holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom and possess the kingdom forever, even forever and ever.'

**7:19** Then I wanted to know the exact truth about the fourth beast, which was different from all the rest, exceedingly terrifying, with its teeth of iron and its claws of bronze, devouring, crushing, and trampling down the remainder with its feet;

**7:20** and about the ten horns that were on its head, and the other horn that came up, before which three fell, that horn which had eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and whose appearance was greater than its companions.

**7:21** I kept looking, and that horn was waging war with the holy ones and prevailing over them,

**7:22** until the Ancient of Days came, and judgment was given for the holy ones of the Most High, and the time arrived when the holy ones took possession of the kingdom.

**7:23** This is what he said: 'As for the fourth beast, there will be a fourth kingdom on the earth that will be different from all the kingdoms, and it will devour the whole earth, tread it down, and crush it.

**7:24** As for the ten horns, out of this kingdom ten kings will arise; and another will arise after them, and he will be different from the former ones and will bring low three kings.

**7:25** He will speak words against the Most High and wear down the holy ones of the Most High, and he will intend to alter times and law; and they will be given into his hand for a time, times, and half a time.

**7:26** But the court will sit in session, and his dominion will be taken away, to be annihilated and destroyed completely.

**7:27** Then the kingdom, the dominion, and the greatness of the kingdoms under the whole heaven will be given to the people of the holy ones of the Most High; His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions will serve and obey Him.'

**7:28** Here is the end of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts greatly alarmed me and my complexion changed, yet I kept the matter in my heart."

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — Here and in 7:5-8, 19-20, the Qere regularly uses the assimilated third-feminine-singular suffix -ah on plural nouns and prepositions where the Ketiv preserves the consonantal yod (-yh); in 7:6, the Qere reads singular "its back" (gabbah) against Ketiv "its backs/sides."
- **7:9** — Aramaic remiw (Peil passive of rmh, "throw, cast") here carries the idiomatic sense "were set in place," referring to the placing of thrones for judgment.
- **7:13** — Aramaic kevar enash literally means "like a son of man," an idiom for a human figure in contrast to the preceding beasts.
- **7:15** — Aramaic nidneh is a Persian loanword meaning "sheath," used metaphorically here for the human body containing the spirit.
- **7:27** — Aramaic elyonin ("the Most High," also in 7:18, 22, 25) is a plural of majesty; the singular suffix on "His kingdom" and "Him" refers either to the Most High or to the collective singular noun "people."
