## Text

**4:4** I, Nebuchadnezzar, was untroubled in my house and thriving in my palace.

**4:5** I saw a dream, and it terrified me, and the imaginings on my bed and the visions of my head alarmed me.

**4:10** And as for the visions of my head on my bed, I was watching, and look, a tree in the middle of the earth, and its height was immense.

**4:11** The tree grew great and strong, and its height reached to the heavens, and the sight of it reached to the end of all the earth.

**4:12** Its foliage was lovely and its fruit was abundant, and in it was nourishment for all; beneath it the animals of the field found shade, and in its branches the birds of the heavens dwelt, and from it all flesh was nourished.

**4:13** I was watching in the visions of my head on my bed, and look, a watcher and holy one was descending from heaven.

**4:14** He called out loudly and spoke in this way: "Cut down the tree and sever its branches; strip off its foliage and scatter its fruit. Let the animals flee from beneath it and the birds from its branches.

**4:15** However, leave the stump of its roots in the earth, with a band of iron and bronze, in the young grass of the field; and let him be soaked with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the animals in the grass of the earth.

**4:16** Let his heart be altered from that of a human, and let the heart of an animal be given to him, and let seven spans of time pass over him.

**4:17** The verdict is by the decree of the watchers, and the ruling is the word of the holy ones, so that the living may know that the Most High is sovereign over the kingdom of mankind and gives it to whomever He desires, and appoints the lowest of people over it."

**4:18** This dream I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw. Now you, Belteshazzar, declare the interpretation, because none of the wise men of my kingdom are able to make the interpretation known to me, whereas you are capable, for a spirit of the holy gods is in you.

**4:19** Then Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, was stunned for a short while, and his thoughts alarmed him. The king responded and said, "Belteshazzar, do not let the dream or the interpretation alarm you." Belteshazzar answered and said, "My lord, may the dream be for those who hate you, and its interpretation for your foes.

**4:20** The tree that you saw, which grew great and strong, whose height reached to the heavens and the sight of it to all the earth,

**4:21** and whose foliage was lovely and its fruit was abundant, and in it was nourishment for all, beneath which the animals of the field dwelt and in whose branches the birds of the heavens settled,

**4:22** it is you, King, who have grown great and strong, and your greatness has grown and reached to the heavens, and your dominion to the end of the earth.

**4:23** And as for the king seeing a watcher and holy one descending from heaven and saying, 'Cut down the tree and destroy it; however, leave the stump of its roots in the earth, with a band of iron and bronze, in the young grass of the field, and let him be soaked with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the animals of the field until seven spans of time pass over him':

**4:24** this is the interpretation, King, and it is a decree of the Most High that has come upon my lord the king:

**4:25** You will be driven away from mankind, and your dwelling will be with the animals of the field, and you will be fed grass like oxen, and you will be soaked with the dew of heaven, and seven spans of time will pass over you, until you know that the Most High is sovereign over the kingdom of mankind and gives it to whomever He desires.

**4:26** And as for their saying to leave the stump of the roots of the tree, your kingdom will stand firm for you from the time you know that Heaven is sovereign.

**4:27** Therefore, King, may my counsel please you: break off your sins through righteousness, and your iniquities by showing mercy to the poor; perhaps there may be an extension of your tranquility."

**4:28** All of it came upon Nebuchadnezzar the king.

**4:29** At the end of twelve months, he was walking atop the royal palace of Babylon.

**4:30** The king answered and said, "Is this not great Babylon, which I myself built as a royal house by the strength of my power and for the honor of my majesty?"

**4:31** While the word was still in the king's mouth, a voice fell from heaven: "To you it is declared, King Nebuchadnezzar: the kingdom has passed from you,

**4:32** and you will be driven away from mankind, and your dwelling will be with the animals of the field; you will be fed grass like oxen, and seven spans of time will pass over you, until you know that the Most High is sovereign over the kingdom of mankind and gives it to whomever He desires."

**4:33** At that very moment the word was fulfilled upon Nebuchadnezzar, and he was driven away from mankind, and he ate grass like oxen, and his body was soaked with the dew of heaven, until his hair grew like the feathers of eagles and his nails like the claws of birds.

**4:34** And at the close of the days, I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted up my eyes to heaven, and my understanding came back to me; and I blessed the Most High, and I praised and glorified the One who lives forever, because His dominion is an eternal dominion, and His kingdom lasts from generation to generation.

**4:36** At that very time my understanding came back to me, and for the honor of my kingdom, my majesty and my splendor came back to me; and my counselors and my nobles sought me out, and I was reinstated over my kingdom, and extraordinary greatness was added to me.

**4:37** King Nebuchadnezzar to all the peoples, nations, and languages who live in all the earth: May your peace multiply! It has pleased me to declare the signs and wonders that the Most High God has performed for me. His signs, how great they are, and His wonders, how powerful they are! His kingdom is an eternal kingdom, and His dominion lasts from generation to generation. Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and exalt and glorify the King of heaven, because all His deeds are truth and His ways are justice, and those who walk in pride He is able to bring low.

## Translator Notes

- **4:13** — "watcher and holy one": Aramaic ir weqaddish uses an epexegetical conjunction (confirmed by the singular participle nachit, "descending") to designate a single vigilant heavenly messenger.
- **4:19** — "those who hate you ... your foes": The Ketiv writes the plural 2ms suffix -ayk (lsn'yk, l'ryk), while the Qere reads -ak (lesane'ak, le'arak), a Tiberian Aramaic suffix variant that coincides with the singular form (similarly on plural nouns in 4:27 and 5:23). "a short while": Aramaic sha'ah denotes a brief moment or interval rather than a sixty-minute hour (cf. 4:33; 5:5).
- **4:26** — "Heaven is sovereign": Aramaic shemayya ("the heavens," grammatically plural with the plural adjective shallitin) serves here as a reverent metonymy for God.
- **4:27** — "my counsel ... break off": Aramaic milki ("my counsel") plays on malka ("King"), and the Peal imperative peruq ("break off" or "redeem") conveys either casting away sins or redeeming them through righteous conduct.
- **4:37** — Following the Saints verse arrangement, this verse carries Nebuchadnezzar's greeting and opening doxology (Masoretic 3:31-33; traditional English 4:1-3) before his closing praise (Masoretic 4:34; traditional English 4:37).
- **4:6 *(additional)*** — Alternatively, "as for the visions of my dream that I saw, tell me its interpretation."
- **4:32 *(additional)*** — Literally "strike against His hand," an Aramaic idiom for restraining or halting someone's action.
