## Text

**11:1** "And as for me, in the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to strengthen him and be a stronghold for him.

**11:2** Now I will tell you the truth. Look, three more kings will arise for Persia, and the fourth will acquire greater wealth than all of them; and when he grows strong through his wealth, he will stir up everyone against the kingdom of Greece.

**11:3** Then a mighty king will arise; he will rule with a vast dominion and do according to his will.

**11:4** Yet as soon as he has arisen, his kingdom will be broken and divided toward the four winds of heaven, not to his descendants, nor with the dominion by which he ruled, for his kingdom will be uprooted and pass to others besides these.

**11:5** Then the king of the south will grow strong, yet one of his princes will grow stronger than he and will rule; his dominion will be a vast dominion.

**11:6** At the end of some years they will form an alliance, and the daughter of the king of the south will come to the king of the north to make an equitable agreement. Yet she will not retain the strength of her arm, nor will he stand, nor his arm; she will be given over, along with those who brought her, the one who fathered her, and the one who supported her in those times.

**11:7** Then a shoot from her roots will arise in his place; he will advance against the army and enter the fortress of the king of the north, and he will deal with them and prevail.

**11:8** He will also carry off their gods into captivity to Egypt, together with their cast images and their precious vessels of silver and gold; and for some years he will hold back from the king of the north.

**11:9** Then he will come into the kingdom of the king of the south, yet he will return to his own land.

**11:10** His sons will mobilize for war and gather a multitude of great forces; and he will surely come, overflow, and pass through, and he will return and press the war all the way to his fortress.

**11:11** Then the king of the south will be enraged and will march out and fight with him, with the king of the north; he will marshal a vast multitude, yet the multitude will be given into his hand.

**11:12** When the multitude is carried off, his heart will be lifted up; he will bring down tens of thousands, yet he will not prevail.

**11:13** Then the king of the north will again marshal a multitude greater than the first; and at the end of the times, some years later, he will surely come with a great army and abundant supplies.

**11:14** In those times many will rise up against the king of the south, and violent men among your people will lift themselves up to establish a vision, but they will stumble.

**11:15** Then the king of the north will come, heap up a siege ramp, and capture a city of fortifications. The forces of the south will not stand, nor even his choicest troops, for there will be no strength to stand.

**11:16** The one who comes against him will do according to his will, and no one will stand before him; he will take his stand in the land of splendor, with destruction in his hand.

**11:17** He will set his face to come with the might of his whole kingdom, and he will make equitable terms with him; he will also give him the daughter of women to ruin it, yet it will not stand, nor will it be for him.

**11:18** Then he will set his face toward the coastlands and capture many of them. Yet a commander will put an end to his reproach against him; moreover, he will turn his reproach back upon him.

**11:19** Then he will turn his face back toward the fortresses of his own land, yet he will stumble and fall and will not be found.

**11:20** Then in his place one will arise who sends an exactor through the splendor of the kingdom; yet within a few days he will be broken, neither in anger nor in battle.

**11:21** In his place a despised person will arise, upon whom royal majesty has not been bestowed; he will come in a time of tranquility and seize the kingdom through flatteries.

**11:22** The sweeping forces will be swept away from before him and broken, as well as the prince of the covenant.

**11:23** From the time an alliance is made with him, he will practice deceit; he will rise up and become mighty with a small nation.

**11:24** In a time of tranquility he will enter the richest parts of the province, and he will do what neither his fathers nor his fathers' fathers did: he will scatter plunder, spoil, and goods among them. He will also devise his schemes against fortified places, yet for a time.

**11:25** He will stir up his strength and his heart against the king of the south with a great army, and the king of the south will mobilize for battle with an exceedingly great and mighty army; yet he will not stand, for they will devise schemes against him.

**11:26** Those who eat his royal food will break him; his army will be swept away, and many will fall mortally wounded.

**11:27** As for both kings, their hearts will be bent on doing harm, and at one table they will speak falsehood; yet it will not succeed, for the end is still for the appointed time.

**11:28** Then he will return to his land with great wealth, with his heart set against the holy covenant; he will take action and return to his own land.

**11:29** At the appointed time he will return and come into the south, yet this latter time will not turn out like the former.

**11:30** For ships of Kittim will come against him, and he will be disheartened; he will turn back and rage against the holy covenant and take action. He will turn back and pay regard to those who forsake the holy covenant.

**11:31** Forces from him will arise and profane the sanctuary, the fortress; they will remove the regular offering and set up the desolating abomination.

**11:32** He will corrupt through flattery those who act wickedly against the covenant, yet the people who know their God will prove strong and act.

**11:33** Those with insight among the people will bring understanding to the many; yet they will stumble by sword and by flame, by captivity and by plunder, for days.

**11:34** When they stumble, they will be helped with a little help, and many will join themselves to them with flatteries.

**11:35** Some of those with insight will stumble, in order to refine them, purify them, and make them white until the time of the end, for it is still for the appointed time.

**11:36** Then the king will do according to his will; he will lift himself up and magnify himself above every god, and against the God of gods he will speak astounding things. He will succeed until the indignation comes to an end, for what is determined will be carried out.

**11:37** He will pay no regard to the gods of his fathers; neither to the desire of women nor to any god will he pay regard, for he will magnify himself above all.

**11:38** Instead, he will honor a god of fortresses; a god whom his fathers did not know he will honor with gold and silver, with costly stones and precious things.

**11:39** He will take action against the strongest fortresses with a foreign god; for those whom he acknowledges he will increase honor, he will make them rule over the many, and he will apportion land for a price.

**11:40** At the time of the end, the king of the south will clash with him, and the king of the north will storm against him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships; he will enter countries, overflow, and pass through.

**11:41** He will also enter the land of splendor, and many will stumble; yet these will escape from his hand: Edom, Moab, and the foremost of the sons of Ammon.

**11:42** He will stretch out his hand against the countries, and the land of Egypt will not escape.

**11:43** He will rule over the hidden treasures of gold and silver and over all the precious things of Egypt, and Libyans and Cushites will be at his steps.

**11:44** Yet reports from the east and from the north will alarm him, and he will march out in great fury to exterminate and utterly destroy many.

**11:45** He will pitch the tents of his royal pavilion between the seas and the holy mountain of splendor; yet he will come to his end, and there will be no one to help him."

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — Masoretic vocalization reads ha-yoldah (Qal active participle with 3fs suffix), "the one who fathered her," rather than "her child"; likewise "his arm" translates the MT text (uzero'o) rather than an emendation to "his seed" (zar'o).
- **11:8** — Hebrew ya'amod min literally means "stand away from" (hold back from); alternatively, the preposition may be taken comparatively as "outlast the king of the north."
- **11:10** — Following the Qere (veyitgareh, 3ms Hithpael imperfect, "he will press the war"); the Ketiv (vyitgaru) reads a 3mp plural ("they will wage war").
- **11:12** — Following the Qere (veram, waw + Qal perfect); the Ketiv (yarum) reads a Qal imperfect with essentially the same meaning ("his heart will be lifted up").
- **11:17** — The feminine singular suffixes in "to ruin it" and "it will not stand, nor will it be for him" may refer either to the southern kingdom/scheme or to the daughter herself ("she will not stand, nor will she be for him").
- **11:18** — Following the Qere (veyasem, "he will set [his face]"); the Ketiv (vyashev) reads "he will turn [his face]" (as in v. 19). Hebrew bilti here carries an emphatic adversative sense ("moreover, indeed").
- **11:39** — Following the Qere (yakkir, Hiphil imperfect, "whom he acknowledges"); the Ketiv (hikkir) reads a Hiphil perfect ("whom he acknowledged"). The clause may also be understood as "those who acknowledge him."
