## Text

**12:1** "And at that time Michael will stand up, the great prince who stands watch over the sons of your people. And there will be a time of distress such as has not happened since a nation came to be until that time; yet at that time your people will be delivered, each one found written in the book.

**12:2** And many of those who sleep in the ground of dust will awaken, these to eternal life, and those to disgrace, to eternal abhorrence.

**12:3** And those with insight will shine like the radiance of the expanse, and those who bring the many to righteousness, like the stars forever and ever.

**12:4** And as for you, Daniel, shut away the words and seal the book until the time of the end. Many will roam back and forth, and knowledge will multiply."

**12:5** Then I, Daniel, looked, and here were two others standing, one on this side at the bank of the river, and one on that side at the bank of the river.

**12:6** And one said to the man wearing linen, who was above the waters of the river, "How long until the end of the wonders?"

**12:7** And I heard the man wearing linen, who was above the waters of the river, as he lifted his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and swore by the One who lives forever, "It will be for an appointed time, appointed times, and a half; and as soon as the shattering of the power of the holy people is finished, all these things will be finished."

**12:8** And I heard, yet I did not understand, so I said, "My lord, what is the outcome of these things?"

**12:9** And he said, "Go, Daniel, for the words are shut away and sealed until the time of the end.

**12:10** Many will be purified, whitened, and refined, while the wicked will behave wickedly; and not one of the wicked will understand, but those with insight will understand.

**12:11** And from the time the regular offering is removed and the desolating abomination is set in place, there will be one thousand two hundred ninety days.

**12:12** Blessed is the one who keeps waiting and reaches one thousand three hundred thirty-five days.

**12:13** But as for you, go to the end; and you will rest, and you will rise to your allotted share at the end of the days."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Hebrew admat-afar is a construct phrase meaning literally "ground of dust" (an image for the grave). The rare noun diraon ("abhorrence") occurs elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible only in Isaiah 66:24.
- **12:7** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes moadim as plural ("appointed times"), often understood as two times (parallel to Aramaic Dan 7:25). Hebrew pairs the Piel infinitive construct of kalah ("finishing" the shattering of the "hand," i.e., power) with its Qal imperfect ("will be finished").
- **12:10** — The first two verbs (from barar and laban) are Hithpael forms paired with a Niphal passive (from tsaraph); in Late Biblical Hebrew the Hithpael frequently carries a passive sense ("be purified, whitened"), though a reflexive nuance ("purify themselves") is also possible.
- **12:11** — Hebrew hatamid ("the continual") is used substantively in Daniel for the daily temple offering. The phrase shiquts shomem combines "abomination/detestable thing" with the Qal participle of shamem ("desolating" or "appalling").
- **12:13** — Hebrew hayamin ("the days") uses the Aramaic masculine plural ending -in rather than the standard Hebrew -im, a notable Aramaism in the final word of the book. The noun goral ("lot") denotes an allotted share or inheritance.
