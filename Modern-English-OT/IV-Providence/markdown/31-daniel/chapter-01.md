## Text

**1:1** In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and laid siege to it.

**1:2** And the Lord gave into his hand Jehoiakim king of Judah and some of the articles of the house of God. He brought them to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and the articles he brought into the treasure house of his god.

**1:3** Then the king told Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs, to bring some of the sons of Israel, both from the royal offspring and from the nobles,

**1:4** youths without any blemish, good in appearance, insightful in all wisdom, possessing knowledge, discerning in learning, and having the ability to stand in the king's palace—and to teach them the writings and language of the Chaldeans.

**1:5** The king assigned them a daily allotment from the king's fine food and from the wine he drank; they were to be trained for three years, and at the end of those years they were to stand before the king.

**1:6** And among them were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, from the sons of Judah.

**1:7** The commander of the eunuchs gave them names: Daniel he named Belteshazzar, Hananiah Shadrach, Mishael Meshach, and Azariah Abednego.

**1:8** But Daniel determined in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king's fine food or with the wine he drank; so he requested of the commander of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.

**1:9** And God granted Daniel favor and mercy before the commander of the eunuchs.

**1:10** The commander of the eunuchs said to Daniel, "I fear my lord the king, who assigned your food and your drink. For why should he see your faces looking more haggard than the youths of your own age? Then you would make my head forfeit to the king."

**1:11** Then Daniel spoke to the attendant whom the commander of the eunuchs had assigned over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:

**1:12** "Please test your servants for ten days, and let them give us vegetables to eat and water to drink.

**1:13** Then let our appearance and the appearance of the youths who eat the king's fine food be observed before you, and deal with your servants according to what you see."

**1:14** He listened to them regarding this matter, and he tested them for ten days.

**1:15** At the end of ten days, their appearance looked better and they were fuller in flesh than all the youths who ate the king's fine food.

**1:16** So the attendant continued taking away their fine food and the wine for their drinking, and giving them vegetables.

**1:17** As for these four youths, God gave them learning and insight in all writings and wisdom, and Daniel had understanding in every vision and in dreams.

**1:18** At the end of the days when the king had commanded to bring them in, the commander of the eunuchs brought them before Nebuchadnezzar.

**1:19** The king spoke with them, and out of all of them no one was found like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; so they stood before the king.

**1:20** And in every matter of wise understanding that the king sought from them, he found them ten times superior to all the magicians, the enchanters, who were in all his kingdom.

**1:21** So Daniel remained until the first year of Cyrus the king.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Hebrew sarisim can denote either literal eunuchs or high royal court officials (cf. Gen 39:1).
- **1:10** — The Piel verb rendered 'make ... forfeit' occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible, reflecting an Aramaic legal term meaning 'render guilty or liable.'
- **1:11** — Hebrew hammeltsar carries the definite article and is an official title (from Akkadian massaru, 'guardian' or 'attendant'), not a personal name.
- **1:20** — Literally 'ten hands,' a Hebrew idiom expressing a tenfold measure or degree (cf. Gen 43:34).
