## Text

**8:1** In the third year of King Belshazzar's reign, a vision appeared to me, Daniel, after the one that appeared to me at the beginning.

**8:2** I looked in the vision, and it came about while I was looking that I was in Susa the citadel, which is in the province of Elam; and I looked in the vision, and I was by the Ulai canal.

**8:3** Then I lifted my eyes and looked, and there was one ram standing in front of the canal, and it had two horns. The two horns were tall, yet one was taller than the other, and the taller one came up last.

**8:4** I saw the ram butting westward, northward, and southward, and no beasts could stand before it, nor was there anyone to rescue from its grasp; it acted according to its will and magnified itself.

**8:5** While I was pondering, look, a male goat was coming from the west across the face of the whole earth without touching the ground; and the goat had a prominent horn between its eyes.

**8:6** It came up to the ram with the two horns, which I had seen standing in front of the canal, and rushed at it in the fury of its strength.

**8:7** I saw it reach the side of the ram, and it raged furiously against it; it struck the ram and shattered its two horns, and the ram had no strength to stand before it. It hurled it down to the ground and trampled it, and there was no one to rescue the ram from its grasp.

**8:8** Then the male goat magnified itself exceedingly, yet when it became mighty, the great horn was broken; and in its place four prominent ones came up toward the four winds of heaven.

**8:9** From one of them came forth a single small horn, and it grew exceedingly great toward the south, toward the east, and toward the Splendor.

**8:10** It grew up as far as the host of heaven, and it brought down to the earth some of the host and some of the stars and trampled them.

**8:11** It even magnified itself up to the Prince of the host; from Him the regular offering was removed, and the foundation of His sanctuary was thrown down.

**8:12** And a host was given over along with the regular offering through transgression; it threw truth down to the ground, and it acted and prospered.

**8:13** Then I heard a holy one speaking, and another holy one said to that certain one who was speaking, "How long is the vision of the regular offering and the transgression that brings desolation, giving over both the sanctuary and the host to trampling?"

**8:14** He said to me, "For two thousand three hundred evenings and mornings; then the sanctuary will be vindicated."

**8:15** Now when I, Daniel, had seen the vision and was seeking understanding, look, standing before me was one with the appearance of a man.

**8:16** And I heard a human voice from the midst of the Ulai, and it called out and said, "Gabriel, make this one understand the vision."

**8:17** So he came near to where I was standing, and as he approached, I was terrified and fell upon my face. Then he said to me, "Understand, son of man, for the vision is for the time of the end."

**8:18** Now while he was speaking with me, I sank into a deep sleep with my face to the ground; yet he touched me and set me upright where I had been standing.

**8:19** He said, "Look, I am about to make known to you what will happen in the latter period of the indignation, for it is for the appointed time of the end.

**8:20** The ram that you saw with the two horns—they are the kings of Media and Persia.

**8:21** And the shaggy male goat is the king of Greece, and the great horn that is between its eyes is the first king.

**8:22** As for the one that was broken, with four arising in its place, four kingdoms will arise from the nation, yet not with his strength.

**8:23** In the latter period of their kingdom, when the transgressors have reached their fullness, a fierce-faced king who understands riddles will arise.

**8:24** His strength will become mighty, yet not by his own strength; he will destroy to an astonishing degree, and he will prosper and take action, destroying mighty ones and the people of the holy ones.

**8:25** Through his shrewdness he will cause deceit to prosper by his hand; in his heart he will magnify himself, and amid security he will destroy many. He will even stand against the Prince of princes, yet without a hand he will be broken.

**8:26** And the vision of the evening and the morning that was spoken is true; but as for you, seal up the vision, for it concerns many days."

**8:27** Then I, Daniel, was exhausted and lay sick for days. Afterward I got up and performed the king's work, yet I was appalled at the vision, and there was no one who understood.

## Translator Notes

- **8:9** — Hebrew "mitztze'irah" literally means "from smallness" or "rather small." "The Splendor" translates Hebrew "hatztzevi," an epithet for the land of Israel (cf. Dan 11:16, 41).
- **8:11** — Ketiv reads Hiphil active "herim" ("it removed"), while Qere (translated here) reads Hophal passive "huram" ("was removed"). Hebrew "hattamid" ("the continual") refers to the regular daily burnt offering.
- **8:12** — The compressed Hebrew clause "vetzava tinnaten al-hattamid befasha" treats "tzava" ("host") as feminine and can be construed either as the host being handed over along with the regular offering because of transgression, or as a hostile force being set over the regular offering in rebellion.
- **8:14** — Hebrew "venitzdaq" is the sole Niphal occurrence of the root "tzadaq" in the Hebrew Bible, denoting being set right, vindicated, or restored to its rightful state. The Masoretic text reads "to me" ("elay") rather than "to him."
