## Text

**1:1** And the word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying,

**1:2** "Rise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim against it, because their evil has come up before Me."

**1:3** Yet Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish from the presence of the LORD; he went down to Joppa and found a ship heading to Tarshish, paid its fare, and went down into it to go with them to Tarshish from the presence of the LORD.

**1:4** But the LORD hurled a great wind onto the sea, and a great storm arose on the sea, and the ship was on the verge of breaking apart.

**1:5** Then the sailors were afraid, and each man cried out to his god, and they hurled the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten the load on them. Jonah, however, had gone down into the recesses of the vessel, lain down, and fallen into a deep sleep.

**1:6** So the captain approached him and said to him, "What is the matter with you, fast asleep? Rise, call to your god! Perhaps God will think of us, so that we do not perish."

**1:7** And they said, each to his companion, "Come, let us cast lots so that we may know on whose account this calamity is upon us." They cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

**1:8** So they said to him, "Please tell us on whose account this calamity is upon us. What is your work, and where are you coming from? What is your land, and from what people are you?"

**1:9** And he said to them, "I am a Hebrew, and I fear the LORD, God of the heavens, who made the sea and the dry ground."

**1:10** Then the men feared with great fear and said to him, "What is this you have done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, since he had told them.

**1:11** So they said to him, "What should we do to you so that the sea may grow calm for us?" For the sea was growing ever more stormy.

**1:12** And he said to them, "Lift me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will grow calm for you, for I know that on my account this great storm is upon you."

**1:13** But the men rowed hard to turn back to the dry ground, yet they could not, for the sea was growing ever more stormy against them.

**1:14** Then they called to the LORD and said, "Please, LORD, do not let us perish for the life of this man, and do not lay innocent blood upon us; for You, LORD, have done just as You desired."

**1:15** So they lifted Jonah up and hurled him into the sea, and the sea ceased its fury.

**1:16** Then the men feared the LORD with great fear, and they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and vowed vows.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Literally "and the ship thought to be broken" (Piel of chashav plus Niphal infinitive of shavar), a vivid personification meaning it was on the verge of breaking apart.
- **1:5** — Hebrew sefinah ("vessel") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible, distinct from oniyyah ("ship") in verses 3-5, and probably denotes a decked craft with a lower hold.
- **1:6** — Hebrew yit'ashet (Hithpael of ashat, occurring only here in Biblical Hebrew) relates to Aramaic ashat ("think, plan") and means "take thought for" or "show concern for." Hebrew ha-Elohim could also be rendered "that god," referring back to "your god."
- **1:7** — Hebrew beshellemi ("on whose account") uses the shorter relative prefix she- (matched by beshelli, "on my account," in v. 12), equivalent to the fuller ba'asher lemi in v. 8.
