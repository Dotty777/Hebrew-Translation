## Text

**6:1** Listen now to what the LORD is saying: "Stand up, argue the case before the mountains, and may the hills hear your voice."

**6:2** Listen, mountains, to the LORD's lawsuit, and you steadfast foundations of the earth; for the LORD has a lawsuit with His people, and He will argue His case with Israel.

**6:3** "My people, what have I done to you, and in what way have I wearied you? Testify against Me.

**6:4** For I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and from the house of slaves I ransomed you; and I sent ahead of you Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

**6:5** My people, remember now what Balak king of Moab planned and what Balaam son of Beor answered him, from Shittim to Gilgal, in order to know the righteous deeds of the LORD."

**6:6** "With what shall I approach the LORD, bow low before God on high? Shall I approach Him with burnt offerings, with year-old calves?

**6:7** Will the LORD take pleasure in thousands of rams, in tens of thousands of streams of oil? Shall I give my firstborn for my rebellion, the fruit of my body for my soul's sin?"

**6:8** He has declared to you, mortal, what is good; and what is the LORD requiring from you except to practice justice, to love steadfast kindness, and to walk in humility with your God?

**6:9** The voice of the LORD calls out to the city, and sound wisdom perceives Your name: "Heed the rod and the One who has appointed it!

**6:10** Are there still in the house of the wicked treasures of wickedness, and a scant ephah that is cursed?

**6:11** Can I be pure with scales of wickedness and with a pouch of deceptive stone weights?

**6:12** For her wealthy people are full of violence, her residents speak falsehood, and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.

**6:13** So I also have made you sick by striking you, leaving you desolate because of your sins.

**6:14** You will eat, yet you will not be satisfied, and your emptiness will remain inside you; you will carry off, yet you will not rescue, and whatever you do rescue I will hand over to the sword.

**6:15** You will sow, yet you will not harvest; you will tread olives, yet you will not rub oil on yourself, and tread new wine, yet you will not drink wine.

**6:16** For the statutes of Omri are kept, along with every practice of the house of Ahab, and you walked in their counsels, so that I make you a desolation and her residents an object of hissing; and you will bear the disgrace of My people."

## Translator Notes

- **6:9** — The MT vocalizes the verb in the middle clause as Qal imperfect of ra'ah ("perceives" or "sees"), though many interpreters understand it as a variant form of the root meaning "to fear" ("it is sound wisdom to fear Your name"). The Hebrew noun translated "rod" can also mean "tribe."
- **6:10** — The interrogative form translated "Are there" uses a rare Hebrew variant (ish) of the standard particle of existence (yesh), as in 2 Samuel 14:19. An "ephah" was a dry grain measure.
- **6:11** — The MT vocalizes the verb as Qal imperfect ("Can I be pure?"), expressing either a rhetorical question from the merchant's standpoint or the LORD's refusal to remain blameless while tolerating fraud; it can also be repointed as Piel ("Can I acquit?").
- **6:13** — The MT vocalizes the verb as Hiphil perfect of chalah ("make sick" or "inflict a grievous blow"), whereas several ancient versions read a similar root meaning "begin."
- **6:14** — The noun translated "emptiness" is a Hebrew hapax legomenon widely understood as gnawing hunger or emptiness. The following Hiphil verb literally means "move away" or "remove."
- **6:16** — The MT opens with a passive Hithpael verb ("are kept"), shifts to a third person feminine singular suffix ("her residents," referring back to the city of 6:9 and 6:12), and reads "My people" rather than the Septuagint's "the peoples."
