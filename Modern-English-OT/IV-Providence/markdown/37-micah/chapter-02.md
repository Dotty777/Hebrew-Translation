## Text

**2:1** Woe to those who devise iniquity and contrive evil on their beds! By the light of morning they carry it out, because it lies in the power of their hand.

**2:2** They covet fields and snatch them away, and houses, and carry them off; they defraud a man and his household, and a man and his inheritance.

**2:3** Therefore this is what the LORD says: "Look, against this family I am devising a calamity from which you will not remove your necks, and you will not walk loftily, for it is a time of calamity."

**2:4** In that day one will lift up a taunt against you and wail a grievous lament, saying, "We are totally devastated! He exchanges the portion of my people; how He removes it from me! To an apostate He apportions our fields."

**2:5** Therefore you will have no one who casts a measuring cord by lot in the assembly of the LORD.

**2:6** "Do not preach!" they preach. They will not preach about these things; disgrace will not turn back.

**2:7** Should it be said, house of Jacob, "Has the Spirit of the LORD grown impatient? Are these His deeds?" Do My words not do good to the one who walks uprightly?

**2:8** Yet recently My people have risen up as an enemy; from over the garment you strip the mantle, from those passing by in safety, those averse to war.

**2:9** You drive out the women of My people, each from her house of delight; from her little children you take My majesty forever.

**2:10** Get up and go, for this is not the resting place; because it is defiled, it brings destruction, even a severe destruction.

**2:11** If a man walking in wind and falsehood should lie, "I will preach to you about wine and strong drink," he would be the preacher of this people.

**2:12** I will surely assemble all of you, Jacob; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel. I will place them together like sheep in a fold, like a flock in the midst of its pasture; they will clamor with people.

**2:13** The one who breaks through goes up ahead of them; they break through and pass the gate and go out by it; their king passes ahead of them, and the LORD is at their head.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — נִהְיָה is difficult; it may intensify נְהִי ("a grievous lament") or be Niphal perfect of הָיָה ("It is done!"). שׁוֹבֵב denotes a rebellious or apostate person, here apparently the foreign invader.
- **2:6** — Hiphil נטף literally means "drip," used idiomatically for prophetic preaching (also v. 11). יִסַּג is Qal imperfect of נסג ("turn back/depart"); some connect it with נשׂג ("overtake"), reading the second half as part of the opponents' quote ("disgrace will not overtake us").
- **2:10** — MT vocalizes טָמְאָה as Qal perfect 3fs ("it is defiled") rather than the noun טֻמְאָה ("uncleanness"), followed by Piel תְּחַבֵּל ("it brings destruction") and cognate חֶבֶל נִמְרָץ ("a severe destruction").
- **2:12** — בָּצְרָה (pointed botsrah) means "enclosure/fold," paralleling דֹּבֶר ("pasture"), though some take it as the place name Bozrah. MT הַדָּבְרוֹ unusually combines the definite article with a 3ms possessive suffix ("its pasture").
