## Text

**3:1** Then I said, "Please hear, heads of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel! Is it not yours to know justice?

**3:2** You who hate good and love evil, who tear their skin away from them and their flesh off their bones,

**3:3** and who eat the flesh of my people, strip their skin off them, shatter their bones, and chop them up like what is in the pot, like meat in the middle of a cauldron.

**3:4** Then they will cry out to the LORD, yet He will not answer them, and He will conceal His face from them in that time, because they have made their deeds evil."

**3:5** This is what the LORD says regarding the prophets who lead my people astray, who bite with their teeth and call out, "Peace," yet against anyone who puts nothing into their mouths, they consecrate war:

**3:6** "Therefore it will be night for you, without vision, and it will grow dark for you, without divining; the sun will set on the prophets, and the day will turn black over them.

**3:7** The seers will be put to shame, and the diviners will be disgraced; all of them will cover their upper lip, because there is no answer from God."

**3:8** Yet I myself am full of power, by the Spirit of the LORD, and of justice and might, to tell Jacob his transgression and Israel his sin.

**3:9** Please hear this, heads of the house of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel, who loathe justice and pervert all that is straight,

**3:10** building Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with injustice.

**3:11** Her heads judge for a bribe, her priests give instruction for a price, and her prophets divine for silver; yet they lean on the LORD, saying, "Is the LORD not in our midst? No calamity will come upon us."

**3:12** Therefore, on account of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become ruin heaps, and the mountain of the house will become high places of a forest.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Translates the Qere רָע (masculine "evil," matching טוֹב "good"); the Ketiv רעה is the feminine form רָעָה.
- **3:3** — פִּצֵּחוּ (Piel of פצח, "shatter/break in pieces") occurs only here in this sense. MT reads כַּאֲשֶׁר בַּסִּיר ("like what is in the pot") rather than "like flesh in the pot" as in the Septuagint.
- **3:6** — MT vocalizes וְחָשְׁכָה as a Qal weqatal 3fs verb ("and it will grow dark") rather than a noun ("darkness"), followed by the infinitive מִקְּסֹם ("without divining").
- **3:8** — אֶת־רוּחַ יְהֹוָה is uniquely prefixed with אֵת among the listed qualities, functioning as the preposition "with/by" to mark the Spirit of the LORD as the source of Micah's power.
- **3:12** — עִיִּין ("ruin heaps") uses the plural ending -in (cf. עִיִּים in Jer 26:18); "the mountain of the house" (הַר הַבַּיִת) refers to the temple mount.
