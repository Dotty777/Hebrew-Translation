## Text

**5:1** And you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, too small to be among the thousands of Judah, from you one will come out for Me to be ruler in Israel, and his origins are from antiquity, from days of long ago.

**5:2** Therefore He will hand them over until the time a woman in labor has given birth, and the remainder of his brothers will return to the sons of Israel.

**5:3** And he will stand and shepherd in the might of the LORD, in the grandeur of the name of the LORD his God. And they will remain, for now he will grow great as far as the ends of the earth.

**5:4** And this one will be peace. When Assyria enters our land and when he treads in our citadels, we will raise up against him seven shepherds and eight princes of humankind.

**5:5** And they will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod within its gateways; and he will rescue us from Assyria when he enters our land and when he treads within our border.

**5:6** And the remnant of Jacob will be in the midst of many peoples like dew from the LORD, like rain showers upon grass, which does not wait for a man nor linger for the sons of humankind.

**5:7** And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the animals of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep, which, if it passes through, tramples and tears apart, with no one to rescue.

**5:8** May your hand be raised high over your adversaries, and may all your enemies be cut off.

**5:9** "And it will happen on that day," declares the LORD, "that I will cut off your horses from your midst and I will destroy your chariots.

**5:10** And I will cut off the cities of your land and demolish all your fortresses.

**5:11** And I will cut off sorceries from your hand, and you will have no fortune-tellers.

**5:12** And I will cut off your carved images and your standing stones from your midst, and you will never again bow down to the work of your hands.

**5:13** And I will uproot your Asherah poles from your midst and wipe out your cities.

**5:14** And in anger and fury I will carry out vengeance against the nations that did not listen."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The term "thousands" denotes tribal and military clans of Judah. Literally "origins" is "goings out" (from the same root as "come out"), and "days of long ago" can also mean "days of eternity."
- **5:5** — The verb "shepherd" continues the shepherd imagery of 5:3-4 in an ironic military sense. The phrase "within its gateways" renders the MT literally (plural of the noun for entrance or doorway with a feminine singular suffix referring to the land of Nimrod).
- **5:13** — The MT reads "your cities" (as in 5:10), though the identical Hebrew form is occasionally understood as a homonym meaning "your adversaries" (cf. Ps 139:20).
