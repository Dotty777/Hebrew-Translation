## Text

**4:1** And it will happen at the end of the days that the mountain of the house of the LORD will be firmly established at the head of the mountains, and it will be raised higher than the hills, and peoples will stream to it.

**4:2** And many nations will go and say, "Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD and to the house of the God of Jacob, so that He may instruct us concerning His ways and we may walk in His paths." For from Zion instruction will go out, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

**4:3** And He will judge between many peoples and arbitrate for strong nations far away; and they will hammer their swords into plow blades and their spears into pruning knives. Nation will not raise a sword against nation, and they will no longer learn war.

**4:4** And they will sit, each person beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, and there will be no one to frighten them; for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.

**4:5** For all the peoples walk, each person in the name of his god, yet we ourselves will walk in the name of the LORD our God forever and ever.

**4:6** "On that day," declares the LORD, "I will gather the limping and assemble the outcast, and those whom I have afflicted.

**4:7** And I will make the limping into a remnant and the one driven far away into a strong nation; and the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion from this time on and forever."

**4:8** And you, tower of the flock, fortified hill of daughter Zion, to you it will come, and the former dominion will arrive, the kingdom belonging to daughter Jerusalem.

**4:9** Now why do you raise an outcry? Is there no king within you, or has your counselor perished, that writhing pain has taken hold of you like a woman giving birth?

**4:10** Writhe and push forth, daughter Zion, like a woman giving birth; for now you will go out from the city and dwell in the open field, and you will come as far as Babylon. There you will be rescued; there the LORD will redeem you from the grasp of your enemies.

**4:11** And now many nations have gathered together against you, saying, "Let her be desecrated, and let our eyes gloat over Zion."

**4:12** Yet they do not know the designs of the LORD, nor have they understood His counsel, that He has assembled them like sheaves to the threshing floor.

**4:13** Rise up and thresh, daughter Zion, for I will make your horn iron and I will make your hooves bronze; and you will crush many peoples, and I will devote their unjust gain to the LORD and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

**4:14** Now gather yourself in troops, daughter of troops; a siege has been set against us; with a rod they will strike the judge of Israel on the cheek.

## Translator Notes

- **4:7** — The word translated "the one driven far away" is a Hebrew hapax legomenon, a Niphal feminine singular participle of a denominative verb derived from the adverb meaning "farther" or "beyond."
- **4:13** — The MT reads the Hiphil verb as first person singular ("I will devote," with the LORD speaking of Himself in the third person, as in 4:7); some grammarians analyze the -ti ending as an archaic second person feminine singular ("you will devote"), matching the preceding verb.
- **4:14** — The Hithpolel verb translated "gather yourself in troops" plays on the noun "troops" (gedud) and can alternatively mean "gash yourself" in mourning, reflecting two senses of the Hebrew root gadad.
