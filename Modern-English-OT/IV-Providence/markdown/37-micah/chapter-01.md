## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Morashtite during the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem.

**1:2** Hear, all you peoples; pay attention, earth and its fullness; and may the Lord GOD be a witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple.

**1:3** For see, the LORD is going out from His place, and He will descend and tread upon the high places of the earth.

**1:4** And the mountains will melt beneath Him, and the valleys will split apart, like wax before the fire, like water poured down a slope.

**1:5** All this is because of Jacob's transgression and because of the sins of the house of Israel. What is Jacob's transgression? Is it not Samaria? And what are the high places of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

**1:6** And I will make Samaria a ruin heap of the field, planting places for a vineyard; and I will pour down her stones into the valley and lay bare her foundations.

**1:7** All her carved images will be smashed to pieces, all her wages will be burned with fire, and I will make all her idols a desolation; for from the wages of a prostitute she gathered them, and to the wages of a prostitute they will return.

**1:8** Over this I will lament and wail; I will walk barefoot and naked; I will make a lamentation like the jackals and a mourning like the ostriches.

**1:9** For her wound is beyond cure, for it has come as far as Judah; it has reached up to the gate of my people, all the way to Jerusalem.

**1:10** Do not tell it in Gath; do not weep at all; in Beth-le-aphrah, roll yourself in the dust.

**1:11** Pass on your way, inhabitant of Shaphir, in nakedness and shame; the inhabitant of Zaanan has not gone out; the lamentation of Beth-ezel will take from you its standing place.

**1:12** For the inhabitant of Maroth is in anguish for good, because calamity has come down from the LORD to the gate of Jerusalem.

**1:13** Hitch the chariot to the swift steeds, inhabitant of Lachish; she was the beginning of sin for the daughter of Zion, because the transgressions of Israel were found in you.

**1:14** Therefore you will give farewell gifts for Moresheth-gath; the houses of Achzib will be a deception to the kings of Israel.

**1:15** Once more I will bring the conqueror to you, inhabitant of Mareshah; the glory of Israel will come as far as Adullam.

**1:16** Make yourself bald and shear your hair over the children of your delight; widen your baldness like the vulture, for they have gone into exile from you.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — MT reads plural בָּמוֹת ("high places"), retained here rather than emending to "sin" with some ancient versions.
- **1:8** — Translates the Qere שׁוֹלָל ("barefoot," lit. "stripped"); the Ketiv שילל is a rare variant spelling of the same word.
- **1:9** — Hebrew construes the plural noun מַכּוֹתֶיהָ ("her wounds") with a feminine singular predicate and verb, then shifts to masculine singular נָגַע ("it has reached").
- **1:10** — Translates the Qere הִתְפַּלָּשִׁי ("roll yourself," Hithpael imperative 2fs); the Ketiv התפלשתי is 1cs perfect ("I have rolled myself"). Beth-le-aphrah ("house of dust") plays on עָפָר ("dust"), initiating a chain of town-name wordplays through v. 15.
- **1:11** — The final clause is notoriously obscure in MT; עֶמְדָּתוֹ is a hapax legomenon from עמד ("to stand"), literally "its standing place" or "support."
