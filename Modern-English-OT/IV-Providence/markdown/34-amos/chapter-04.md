## Text

**4:1** Listen to this word, cows of Bashan who are on Mount Samaria—oppressing the poor, crushing the needy, saying to their masters, "Bring, and let us drink!"

**4:2** The Lord GOD has sworn by His holiness: "Look, days are coming upon you, and they will lift you away with hooks, and the remainder of you with fishhooks.

**4:3** And you will go out through breaches, each woman straight ahead of her, and you will be flung toward Harmon," declares the LORD.

**4:4** "Come to Bethel and transgress; at Gilgal, multiply transgression! And bring your sacrifices each morning, your tithes every three days.

**4:5** And offer up in smoke a thank offering of leavened bread, and proclaim freewill offerings—announce them aloud! For you love it this way, sons of Israel," declares the Lord GOD.

**4:6** "And I, too, gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities and a shortage of bread in all your places; yet you did not turn back to Me," declares the LORD.

**4:7** "And I, too, withheld the rain from you when three months still remained until the harvest; I would send rain on one city, and on another city I would not send rain. One plot would be rained on, while the plot on which it did not rain would dry up.

**4:8** And two or three cities would stagger to one city to drink water, and they would not be satisfied; yet you did not turn back to Me," declares the LORD.

**4:9** "I struck you with blight and mildew; the cutting locust kept devouring your many gardens and your vineyards, your fig trees and your olive trees; yet you did not turn back to Me," declares the LORD.

**4:10** "I sent pestilence among you in the way of Egypt; I killed your young men by the sword, along with the capture of your horses, and I caused the stench of your camps to rise even into your nostrils; yet you did not turn back to Me," declares the LORD.

**4:11** "I overthrew some among you, like God's overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, and you became like a burning stick rescued from a fire; yet you did not turn back to Me," declares the LORD.

**4:12** "Therefore this is what I will do to you, Israel; since I will do this to you, make yourself ready to meet your God, Israel!"

**4:13** For look—He who forms mountains and creates wind, and tells a person what his thought is, who makes dawn into darkness and treads on the heights of the earth—the LORD, God of hosts, is His name!

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The phrase 'their masters' (adōnehem, literally 'their lords/masters,' using a masculine plural suffix for the women) here refers to their husbands, whom each woman addresses with the singular Hiphil imperative 'Bring!'
- **4:3** — The MT vocalizes the verb (we-hishlakhtenah) as an active Hiphil ('you will cast'), though in context it is usually understood passively ('you will be flung') or reflexively ('cast yourselves'). The meaning and location of the rare term 'Harmon' (ha-harmōnah) are uncertain.
- **4:7** — The clause 'on which it did not rain' uses an impersonal third-person feminine singular Hiphil form (tamtir), contrasting with the preceding Niphal passive ('would be rained on,' timmater).
- **4:9** — The word 'many' renders the Hiphil infinitive construct harbōt ('the multiplying/abundance of'), which can also be read adverbially as 'repeatedly.' The noun gazam denotes a cutting or shearing locust.
- **4:13** — The third-person masculine singular suffix on 'his thought' (secho, a rare by-form of siach, 'thought/concern') naturally refers to the human person, though some take it as referring to God's own thought.
