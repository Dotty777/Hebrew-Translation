## Text

**6:1** Woe to the complacent in Zion and to those who are confident on Mount Samaria, the renowned men of the foremost of the nations, to whom the house of Israel comes!

**6:2** Cross over to Calneh and see, and go from there to great Hamath, and go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than these kingdoms, or is their territory larger than your territory?

**6:3** You who thrust away the evil day and bring close the seat of violence!

**6:4** Those who lie on ivory beds and sprawl on their couches, eating lambs from a flock and calves from inside a fattening stall,

**6:5** who improvise to the tune of the harp, and like David devise instruments of song for themselves,

**6:6** who drink from bowls of wine and anoint themselves with the choicest of oils, yet they are not distressed over the shattering of Joseph!

**6:7** Therefore now they will go into exile at the head of the exiles, and the revelry of those who sprawl will cease.

**6:8** The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself, declares the LORD, God of hosts: "I detest the pride of Jacob, and I hate his citadels; and I will hand over the city and all that fills it."

**6:9** And it will happen that if ten men remain in one house, they will die.

**6:10** And when his uncle—the one who burns him—lifts him up to bring bones out of the house and says to the one in the deep recesses of the house, "Is anyone still with you?" and he says, "No one," then he will say, "Hush! For one must not invoke the name of the LORD."

**6:11** For look, the LORD is commanding, and He will strike the great house into fragments and the small house into fissures.

**6:12** Do horses run on a rocky crag, or does anyone plow it with oxen? For you turned justice into poison, and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood—

**6:13** you who rejoice in what is nothing, who say, "Did we not by our own strength take horns for ourselves?"

**6:14** "For look, I am raising up against you, house of Israel," declares the LORD, the God of hosts, "a nation—and they will oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the Wadi of the Arabah."

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — The verb 'improvise' renders the hapax Qal participle hap-pōretim (from parat), understood as strumming or singing idle, improvised tunes to the harp.
- **6:7** — The noun mirzach ('revelry') denotes an elite drinking feast or banqueting association, attested elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible only in Jeremiah 16:5.
- **6:10** — The Piel participle u-mesarefo ('the one who burns him') is spelled with samekh for sin (from saraf, 'burn'), referring to a relative burning the corpse or funeral spices during the calamity.
- **6:12** — The MT literally asks 'or does one plow with oxen?' (using the rare plural beqarim, 'oxen'), with the rocky crag of the first clause understood as the implied ground being plowed.
- **6:13** — The MT vocalizes 'what is nothing' (le-lō davar) and 'horns' (qarnayim, emblems of military strength) as a double wordplay on two Transjordanian towns recaptured by Israel: Lo-debar and Karnaim.
