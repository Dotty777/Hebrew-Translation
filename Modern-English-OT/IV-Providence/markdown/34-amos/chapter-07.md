## Text

**7:1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: look, He was fashioning a swarm of locusts as the late crop was starting to come up, and look, it was the late crop after the king's mowings.

**7:2** And it came about, when it finished eating the green plants of the land, that I said, "Lord GOD, forgive, please! How will Jacob stand, for he is small?"

**7:3** The LORD relented over this. "It will not happen," the LORD said.

**7:4** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: look, the Lord GOD was calling to contend by fire, and it consumed the great deep and was consuming the territory.

**7:5** Then I said, "Lord GOD, stop, please! How will Jacob stand, for he is small?"

**7:6** The LORD relented over this. "This also will not happen," the Lord GOD said.

**7:7** This is what He showed me: look, the Lord was standing beside a wall set with a plumb line, and in His hand was a plumb line.

**7:8** And the LORD said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "Look, I am setting a plumb line in the midst of My people Israel; I will not pass over them again.

**7:9** The high places of Isaac will become desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel will lie in ruins, and I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

**7:10** Then Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent word to Jeroboam, king of Israel, saying, "Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land cannot bear all his words.

**7:11** For this is what Amos has said: 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will surely go into exile away from its land.'"

**7:12** Then Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer, go, flee to the land of Judah; eat bread there, and there prophesy.

**7:13** But at Bethel do not prophesy anymore, for it is a king's sanctuary, and it is a house of the kingdom."

**7:14** Then Amos answered and said to Amaziah, "I am not a prophet, nor am I a son of a prophet; rather, I am a herdsman and a tender of sycamore figs.

**7:15** Yet the LORD took me from behind the flock, and the LORD said to me, 'Go, prophesy to My people Israel.'

**7:16** And now, hear the word of the LORD! You are saying, 'You must not prophesy against Israel, and you must not preach against the house of Isaac.'

**7:17** Therefore, this is what the LORD says: 'Your wife will act as a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword; your land will be apportioned by a measuring line, and you yourself will die on an unclean land, and Israel will surely go into exile away from its land.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Hebrew idiom mi yaqum ya'aqov (lit. "Who will Jacob stand?") uses mi ("who") adverbially to mean "How will Jacob stand?" (repeated in 7:5).
- **7:4** — The phrase lariv ba'esh uses the root riv ("contend" or "bring a legal dispute"), portraying the Lord GOD summoning judgment by fire; hacheleq ("the territory") literally denotes an allotted portion of land.
- **7:7** — The Hebrew noun anak is traditionally understood as a "plumb line" (a lead or tin weight on a cord for testing a wall), though its Akkadian cognate anaku means "tin," leading some scholars to read a "wall of tin."
- **7:8** — The idiom "I will not pass over them again" (lit. "I will not add again to pass by for him") means the Lord will no longer overlook Israel's offenses or spare them from judgment (repeated in 8:2).
- **7:14** — The verbless Hebrew clauses carry no explicit tense and may be rendered in the present ("I am not a prophet...") or past ("I was not a prophet..."); boles ("tender") is a hapax legomenon denoting one who pinches or punctures sycamore figs to help them ripen.
