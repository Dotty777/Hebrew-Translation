## Text

**3:1** Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, sons of Israel, against the whole family that I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying:

**3:2** "You alone have I known out of all the families of the earth; for this reason I will bring punishment upon you for all your iniquities."

**3:3** Do two walk together unless they have met by appointment?

**3:4** Does a lion roar in the forest if it has no prey? Does a young lion give forth its voice from its lair unless it has captured something?

**3:5** Does a bird fall into a snare on the earth when there is no bait for it? Does a snare spring up from the ground without capturing anything at all?

**3:6** If a ram's horn is blown in a city, will the people not tremble? If disaster occurs in a city, has the LORD not done it?

**3:7** Indeed, the Lord GOD does not do a thing unless He has disclosed His confidential counsel to His servants the prophets.

**3:8** A lion has roared; who will not be afraid? The Lord GOD has spoken; who will not prophesy?

**3:9** Proclaim to the citadels in Ashdod and to the citadels in the land of Egypt, and say, "Gather on the mountains of Samaria, and see the many tumults in its midst and the oppressions within it."

**3:10** "And they do not know how to do what is upright," declares the LORD, "those who hoard violence and plunder in their citadels."

**3:11** Therefore, this is what the Lord GOD says, "An adversary, all around the land! And he will bring down your strength from you, and your citadels will be plundered."

**3:12** This is what the LORD says, "Just as the shepherd rescues from the mouth of the lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so the sons of Israel who dwell in Samaria will be rescued, with the corner of a bed and the damask of a couch."

**3:13** "Hear and bear witness against the house of Jacob," declares the Lord GOD, the God of hosts.

**3:14** "For on the day I bring punishment upon Israel for its transgressions, I will also bring punishment upon the altars of Bethel; and the horns of the altar will be hacked off and fall to the ground.

**3:15** And I will strike down the winter house together with the summer house; the ivory houses will perish, and many houses will vanish," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Masoretic Text shifts abruptly within the sentence from third person ('the LORD has spoken') to first person ('I brought up'), merging the prophet's voice with God's.
- **3:5** — The noun rendered 'bait' denotes the lure or trigger mechanism that springs a snare; the final clause uses an emphatic Qal infinitive absolute with imperfect ('capturing, it does not capture').
- **3:11** — The Masoretic Text uses an abrupt verbless exclamation ('An adversary, and around the land!') rather than a finite verb 'will surround.'
- **3:12** — The Hebrew word rendered 'damask' occurs only here in the Bible and is uncertain in meaning, likely denoting luxury fabric or a cushion/part of a couch; the preposition 'with' may alternatively be read 'on,' modifying 'who dwell.'
- **3:15** — The Hebrew adjective rendered 'many' can also mean 'great' or 'large' in parallel with the winter, summer, and ivory houses.
