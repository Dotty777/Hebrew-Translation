## Text

**9:1** I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and He said, "Strike the capital so that the thresholds shake, and shatter them upon the heads of all of them; and the last of them I will kill with the sword. Not one fugitive among them will flee, and not one survivor among them will escape.

**9:2** If they dig into Sheol, from there My hand will take them; and if they go up to the heavens, from there I will bring them down.

**9:3** And if they hide on the summit of Carmel, from there I will search and take them; and if they conceal themselves from before My eyes at the bottom of the sea, from there I will command the serpent, and it will bite them.

**9:4** And if they go into captivity before their enemies, from there I will command the sword, and it will kill them; and I will set My eye upon them for harm and not for good."

**9:5** The Lord GOD of hosts is the One who touches the earth and it melts, and all its inhabitants mourn, and the whole of it rises like the Nile and sinks down like the Nile of Egypt,

**9:6** the One who builds His upper chambers in the heavens and has founded His vault on the earth, who summons the waters of the sea and pours them out over the face of the earth—the LORD is His name.

**9:7** "Are you not like the sons of the Cushites to Me, sons of Israel?" declares the LORD. "Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Caphtor, and Aram from Kir?"

**9:8** "Look, the eyes of the Lord GOD are on the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from the face of the ground; yet I will not completely destroy the house of Jacob," declares the LORD.

**9:9** "For look, I am giving a command, and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations, just as something is shaken in a sieve, and not one pebble will fall to the ground.

**9:10** By the sword all the sinners of My people will die, those who say, 'The calamity will not draw near or confront us.'"

**9:11** "In that day I will raise up David's fallen booth; I will wall up its breaches, raise up its ruins, and rebuild it as in ancient days,

**9:12** so that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the nations over whom My name has been called," declares the LORD, the One who does this.

**9:13** "Look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when the plowman will catch up to the reaper, and the treader of grapes to the one who scatters the seed; and the mountains will drip sweet wine, and all the hills will melt.

**9:14** And I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel; they will build up desolate cities and dwell in them, and plant vineyards and drink their wine, and make gardens and eat their fruit.

**9:15** And I will plant them on their land, and they will not be uprooted again away from their land that I have given to them," says the LORD your God.

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — Hebrew ma'alotav ("His upper chambers") literally means "His steps/ascents" (heaven's upper chambers, cf. Ps 104:3), and aguddato ("His vault") literally denotes a "band" or "bound structure," depicting the vaulted sky founded on the earth.
- **9:9** — The Niphal verb yinnoa ("is shaken") is impersonal with no explicit subject noun for what is sifted in the sieve (kevarah), and tseror literally denotes a "pebble" (though some interpret it as a "grain/kernel").
- **9:11** — The Hebrew pronominal suffixes shift across the verse—3fp "their breaches," 3ms "his [David's] ruins," and 3fs "rebuild it [the booth]"—smoothed here to "its/it" in English.
- **9:12** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads yirshu ("they may possess") and Edom ("Edom"), whereas the Septuagint (reflected in Acts 15:17) reads the visually similar consonants yidrshu ("seek") and adam ("mankind").
- **9:14** — The Hebrew idiom shuv shevut ("restore the fortunes") is also frequently connected to the root shavah ("take captive"), meaning "bring back the captivity/exiles."
