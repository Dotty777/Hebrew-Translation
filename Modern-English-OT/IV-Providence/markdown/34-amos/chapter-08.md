## Text

**8:1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: look, a basket of summer fruit.

**8:2** And He said, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will not pass over them again.

**8:3** And the songs of the temple will wail in that day," declares the Lord GOD. "Many are the corpses! In every place they are cast out. Hush!"

**8:4** Hear this, you who trample the needy, to put an end to the poor of the land,

**8:5** saying, "When will the new moon pass so that we may sell grain, and the Sabbath so that we may open the wheat, making the ephah small and making the shekel large, and falsifying scales of deceit,

**8:6** buying the poor for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals? And we will sell the sweepings of the wheat!"

**8:7** The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob, "I will certainly never forget any of their deeds."

**8:8** Will the land not tremble on account of this, and everyone who dwells in it mourn, and all of it rise like the Nile, and be churned up and sink down like the Nile of Egypt?

**8:9** "And it will come about in that day," declares the Lord GOD, "that I will make the sun go down at noon and darken the earth on a day of light.

**8:10** And I will turn your festivals into mourning and all your songs into a lament. I will bring sackcloth upon all loins and baldness upon every head, and I will make it like mourning for an only son, and its end like a bitter day."

**8:11** "Look, days are coming," declares the Lord GOD, "when I will send a famine into the land—not a famine for bread and not a thirst for water, but for hearing the words of the LORD.

**8:12** And they will stagger from sea to sea, and from north to east; they will roam about to seek the word of the LORD, yet they will not find it.

**8:13** In that day the beautiful young women and the young men will grow faint with thirst.

**8:14** Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria and say, 'As your god lives, Dan,' and, 'As the way of Beersheba lives'—they will fall and will not rise again."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The vision turns on a Hebrew wordplay between qayits ("summer fruit," harvested at the end of the season) and qets ("end").
- **8:3** — Hebrew shirot is pointed as "songs" (personified as wailing), though some read "female singers"; the Hiphil verb hishlik ("one casts out") is impersonal and rendered passively, followed by the abrupt interjection has ("Hush!").
- **8:4** — Ketiv reads anve ("the humble/meek of"), while Qere reads aniyye ("the poor/afflicted of"), translated here.
- **8:8** — Ketiv reads venishqah (apparently "and be drenched" or a spelling variant), while Qere reads venishqe'ah ("and sink down," Niphal of shaqa, matching the Qal in 9:5), translated here; ka'or is a shortened spelling of kay'or ("like the Nile," cf. 9:5).
- **8:14** — "The guilt of Samaria" (ashmat Shomron) likely mocks Samaria's idolatrous shrine or plays on the deity name Ashimah (2 Kgs 17:30); "the way of Beersheba" (derekh Be'er-Sheva) refers either to its pilgrimage road or, by comparison with Ugaritic drkt, its patron power.
