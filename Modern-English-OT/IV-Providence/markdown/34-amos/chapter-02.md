## Text

**2:1** This is what the LORD says, "On account of three transgressions of Moab, and on account of four, I will not turn it back, because he burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime.

**2:2** So I will send fire upon Moab, and it will devour the citadels of Kerioth; and Moab will die amid uproar, with a war cry, with the sound of a ram's horn.

**2:3** And I will cut off the judge from its midst, and I will kill all its officials with him," says the LORD.

**2:4** This is what the LORD says, "On account of three transgressions of Judah, and on account of four, I will not turn it back, because they rejected the law of the LORD and did not keep His statutes, and their lies, after which their fathers walked, caused them to stray.

**2:5** So I will send fire upon Judah, and it will devour the citadels of Jerusalem."

**2:6** This is what the LORD says, "On account of three transgressions of Israel, and on account of four, I will not turn it back, because they sell a righteous person for silver and a needy one for a pair of sandals,

**2:7** those who trample the head of the weak into the dust of the earth and turn aside the way of the afflicted; and a man and his father go to the same young woman, so as to profane My holy name.

**2:8** And on garments taken in pledge they stretch themselves out beside every altar, and the wine of those who were fined they drink in the house of their God.

**2:9** Yet I destroyed the Amorite from before them, whose height was like the height of cedars, and he was sturdy as the oaks; and I destroyed his fruit from above and his roots from below.

**2:10** And I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and I led you in the wilderness forty years to take possession of the land of the Amorite.

**2:11** And I raised up some of your sons as prophets and some of your young men as Nazirites. Is it not indeed so, sons of Israel?" declares the LORD.

**2:12** "Yet you gave the Nazirites wine to drink, and you commanded the prophets, saying, 'You must not prophesy.'

**2:13** Look, I am pressing down beneath you, just as a cart presses down that is full of cut grain.

**2:14** And flight will perish from the swift, and the strong will not maintain his strength, and the warrior will not rescue his life.

**2:15** And the one wielding the bow will not stand, and the one swift on his feet will not escape, and the one riding the horse will not rescue his life.

**2:16** And the strongest of heart among the warriors will flee naked on that day," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — The Qal participle rendered 'trample' (cf. 8:4) can also mean 'pant/gasp after,' yielding 'those who pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the weak.'
- **2:8** — The Hiphil verb 'stretch themselves out' uses the same Hebrew root and stem as 'turn aside' in 2:7, forming an ironic wordplay.
- **2:13** — The Hiphil verb rendered 'pressing down' comes from a rare root occurring only in this verse, meaning either 'press/weigh down' or 'groan/creak under weight'; 'beneath you' may also mean 'in your place.'
