## Text

**3:1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, set to Shigionoth.

**3:2** LORD, I have heard the report of You; I fear. LORD, in the midst of the years revive Your deed; in the midst of the years make it known; in wrath, remember to show compassion.

**3:3** From Teman God came, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. His majesty covered the heavens, and His praise filled the earth.

**3:4** And radiance was like the light; twin rays came from His hand, and there was the concealment of His power.

**3:5** Pestilence went ahead of Him, and plague came forth at His feet.

**3:6** He stood and measured out the earth; He looked and made the nations tremble. The perpetual mountains broke apart; the everlasting hills sank down. His pathways are everlasting.

**3:7** Under affliction I saw the tents of Cushan; the curtains of the land of Midian were trembling.

**3:8** Were You angry with the rivers, LORD? Was Your anger against the rivers, or Your fury against the sea, that You rode on Your horses, Your chariots of salvation?

**3:9** Your bow was laid completely bare; sworn were the shafts of command. Selah. With rivers You split open the earth.

**3:10** The mountains saw You; they writhed. A downpour of waters passed through; the deep uttered its voice; on high it raised its hands.

**3:11** Sun and moon stood still in their lofty dwelling at the light of Your arrows as they darted, at the radiance of the flash of Your spear.

**3:12** In indignation You strode across the earth; in anger You threshed the nations.

**3:13** You came forth for the salvation of Your people, for the salvation of Your anointed one. You shattered the head from the house of the wicked, stripping bare the foundation up to the neck. Selah.

**3:14** With his own shafts You pierced the head of his warriors; they stormed on to scatter me, their exultation like devouring the afflicted in secret.

**3:15** You trod through the sea with Your horses, the churning of many waters.

**3:16** I heard, and my belly trembled; at the voice my lips quivered. Decay entered into my bones, and where I stood I trembled, because I will wait quietly for the day of distress to come up against the people who attack us.

**3:17** Even if the fig tree does not bloom and there is no yield on the vines, though the olive's produce fails and the terraced fields yield no food, though the flock is cut off from the pen and no herd is in the stalls,

**3:18** yet as for me, in the LORD I will exult; I will be joyful in the God of my salvation.

**3:19** GOD the Lord is my strength; He makes my feet like those of deer, and on my high places He causes me to tread. For the music director, with my stringed instruments.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Shigionoth transliterates the plural Hebrew musical or liturgical heading shigyonot (singular shiggayon in Ps 7:1), whose exact technical meaning is uncertain.
- **3:9** — Sworn were the shafts of command translates the notoriously terse Masoretic Hebrew shevu'ot mattot 'omer (literally oaths, rods/shafts/tribes, word) without conjectural emendation.
- **3:13** — For the salvation of Your anointed one reflects the unusual construction leyesha' 'et-meshihekha, where the noun yesha' (salvation) governs the direct object marker 'et- (or alternatively, 'et- functions as the preposition with).
- **3:16** — To come up against the people who attack us renders the terse clause la'alot le'am yegudennu; the syntax can alternatively be read with 'am as the subject (for an invading people to come up against us).
- **3:19** — GOD the Lord reflects the Masoretic order of the divine name followed by 'adonay (the reverse of the usual 'adonay plus the divine name); the divine name is pointed here with the vowels of 'elohim.
