## Text

**1:1** The pronouncement that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

**1:2** How long, LORD, have I cried for help, and You do not listen? I shout to You, "Violence!" and You do not save.

**1:3** Why do You show me iniquity and look at trouble? Devastation and violence are in front of me; there is strife, and conflict rises up.

**1:4** Therefore the law grows numb, and justice never comes forth; for the wicked hem in the righteous, therefore justice comes forth twisted.

**1:5** Look among the nations and watch; be astounded, be amazed! For I am doing a deed in your days that you would not believe if it were recounted.

**1:6** For look, I am raising up the Chaldeans, that bitter and impetuous nation, who march across the broad expanses of the earth to take possession of dwellings that are not their own.

**1:7** They are terrifying and fearsome; from themselves come forth their justice and their dignity.

**1:8** Their horses are faster than leopards and keener than evening wolves. Their horsemen gallop forward, and their horsemen arrive from afar; they fly like an eagle rushing to devour.

**1:9** Every one of them comes for violence; the surge of their faces is forward, and like sand they gather captives.

**1:10** They deride kings, and rulers are a laughingstock to them. They laugh at every stronghold; they heap up earth and capture it.

**1:11** Then they sweep on like a wind and pass through, and incur guilt—these whose strength is their god.

**1:12** Are You not from of old, LORD, my God, my Holy One? We will not die. LORD, You have appointed them for judgment, and, Rock, You have founded them for reproof.

**1:13** You are too pure of eyes to see evil, and You cannot look at trouble. Why do You look at the treacherous, and stay silent while a wicked man swallows up one more righteous than himself?

**1:14** And You have made humanity like the fish of the sea, like creeping creatures with no ruler over them.

**1:15** All of them he draws up with a hook; he drags them in his dragnet and gathers them in his seine. Therefore he is glad and exults.

**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his dragnet and burns incense to his seine, because by them his portion is rich and his food is choice.

**1:17** Will he therefore keep emptying his dragnet, and continually kill nations without pity?

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Pronouncement renders Hebrew massa, literally a lifting up or burden, used as a technical term for a weighty prophetic pronouncement or oracle.
- **1:5** — Be astounded, be amazed pairs the Hithpael and Qal imperatives of the same root (tamah) for strong emphasis. The Qal active participle po'el (doing) lacks an explicit pronoun in Hebrew and is confirmed as first-person (I am doing) by the LORD's words in v. 6.
- **1:9** — The surge of their faces is forward translates the rare hapax noun megammat (meaning uncertain: surge, thrust, set, or horde) with qadimah (forward or eastward).
- **1:11** — Sweep on like a wind renders terse Masoretic Hebrew (halaph ruah) taking ruah as an adverbial comparison; alternatively, his spirit changes.
- **1:12** — We will not die follows the Masoretic Hebrew (lo namut); rabbinic tradition lists it among the tiqqune sopherim (scribal corrections), holding that it replaced an original "You do not die."
