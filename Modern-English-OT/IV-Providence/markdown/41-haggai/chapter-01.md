## Text

**1:1** In the second year of King Darius, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest:

**1:2** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "This people has said, 'The time has not arrived, the time for the house of the LORD to be built.'"

**1:3** And the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet, saying,

**1:4** "Is it time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses while this house is in ruins?"

**1:5** And now, this is what the LORD of hosts says: "Take your ways to heart.

**1:6** You have sown much, yet brought in little; you eat, yet not enough to be satisfied; you drink, yet not enough to drink your fill; you dress yourselves, yet no one gets warm; and the one who earns wages earns them into a pierced pouch."

**1:7** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Take your ways to heart.

**1:8** Go up to the hill country, bring timber, and build the house, so that I may take delight in it and be glorified," says the LORD.

**1:9** "You looked for much, and see, it amounted to little; and when you brought it home, I blew it away. Because of what?" declares the LORD of hosts. "Because of My house, which is in ruins, while you run, each one to his own house.

**1:10** Therefore, because of you the heavens have held back dew, and the earth has held back its yield.

**1:11** And I summoned a drought on the land and on the mountains, on the grain, on the new wine, on the fresh oil, on what the ground produces, on people, on livestock, and on all the labor of the hands."

**1:12** So Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the entire remnant of the people heeded the voice of the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, just as the LORD their God had sent him; and the people were in fear before the LORD.

**1:13** Then Haggai, the LORD's messenger, spoke to the people under the LORD's commission, saying, "I am with you," declares the LORD.

**1:14** And the LORD roused the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of the entire remnant of the people; so they came and did work on the house of the LORD of hosts, their God,

**1:15** on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — The Hebrew phrase "The time has not arrived" (lo et-bo) is syntactically terse, literally "not a time of coming," joining "time" in construct to the Qal infinitive construct of "come," followed in apposition by "the time for the house of the LORD to be built" (Niphal infinitive construct).
- **1:5** — Literally "set your heart upon your ways," a Hebrew idiom calling for serious reflection that recurs in 1:7, 2:15, and 2:18.
- **1:8** — The Qere (read form) is a Niphal cohortative ("so that I may be glorified") with the paragogic ending, whereas the Ketiv (written form) omits the final letter and reads as a standard Niphal imperfect.
- **1:11** — Hebrew chorev ("drought") forms a deliberate wordplay with charev ("in ruins / desolate"), used of the LORD's house in 1:4 and 1:9.
