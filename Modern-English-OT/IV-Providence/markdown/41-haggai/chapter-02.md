## Text

**2:1** In the seventh month, on the twenty-first of the month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:2** "Please speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying,

**2:3** 'Who among you remains who saw this house in its former glory? And what do you see it as now? Is it not just like nothing in your eyes?

**2:4** Now be strong, Zerubbabel, declares the LORD, and be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and be strong, all the people of the land, declares the LORD, and work—for I am with you, declares the LORD of hosts,

**2:5** in keeping with the word that I covenanted with you when you came out of Egypt, and My Spirit stands in your midst. Do not be afraid.

**2:6** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: Once more—in a brief while—I am about to shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry ground.

**2:7** And I will shake all the nations, and the precious things of all the nations will come, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of hosts.

**2:8** Mine is the silver, and Mine is the gold, declares the LORD of hosts.

**2:9** Greater will be the latter glory of this house than the former, says the LORD of hosts; and in this place I will grant peace, declares the LORD of hosts.'"

**2:10** On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2:11** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Please ask the priests for a ruling, saying,

**2:12** 'If a man carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and with that fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any food, will it become holy?'" And the priests answered and said, "No."

**2:13** Then Haggai said, "If someone unclean from a dead body touches any of these, will it become unclean?" And the priests answered and said, "It will become unclean."

**2:14** Then Haggai responded and said, "So is this people, and so is this nation before Me, declares the LORD, and so is all the work of their hands; and whatever they offer there is unclean.

**2:15** And now, please take this to heart from this day onward, before a stone was placed on a stone in the temple of the LORD,

**2:16** from the time those things were: one came to a heap of twenty measures, and there were ten; one came to the wine vat to draw fifty measures from the press, and there were twenty.

**2:17** I struck you with blight, with mildew, and with hail—all the work of your hands—yet you did not turn back to Me, declares the LORD.

**2:18** Please take this to heart from this day onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day the temple of the LORD was founded—take it to heart.

**2:19** Is the seed still in the granary? And as yet the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not borne fruit. From this day I will bless."

**2:20** And the word of the LORD came to Haggai a second time on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying,

**2:21** "Tell Zerubbabel, governor of Judah: 'I am about to shake the heavens and the earth.

**2:22** And I will overturn the throne of kingdoms and destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations; and I will overturn chariots and their riders, and horses and their riders will go down, each one by the sword of his brother.

**2:23** On that day, declares the LORD of hosts, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, My servant, declares the LORD, and set you like a signet ring, for it is you I have chosen, declares the LORD of hosts.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — The opening Hebrew clause (et-haddavar, literally the direct-object marker plus "the word") lacks an explicit governing verb and is rendered adverbially ("in keeping with the word") linking back to 2:4; the verb karat ("cut/covenant"), ordinarily paired with "covenant," takes "word" as its object here.
- **2:7** — Hebrew chemdat ("desire / precious things") is feminine singular in construct with "all the nations," while the verb ("will come") is plural; in view of the silver and gold in 2:8, chemdat is taken collectively as "precious things," though some render it "the desire of all the nations" or "they will come with the treasures of all the nations."
- **2:13** — Hebrew teme-nefesh, literally "unclean of a person/soul," is the standard priestly legal expression for ritual impurity contracted from a corpse (cf. Lev 22:4; Num 9:10).
- **2:15** — The phrase "from this day onward" (literally "from this day and upward") is identical in 2:15 and 2:18; some interpreters take it in 2:15 as looking backward in view of the following clause ("before a stone was placed on a stone").
- **2:16** — Hebrew mihyotam (literally "from their being") is a terse temporal expression meaning "from the time those things were"; purah elsewhere means "winepress" (Isa 63:3) and here denotes measures drawn from the press.
