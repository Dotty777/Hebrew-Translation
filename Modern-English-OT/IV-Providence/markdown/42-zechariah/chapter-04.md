## Text

**4:1** Then the angel who spoke with me returned and awakened me, like a man who is awakened from his sleep.

**4:2** He said to me, "What do you see?" And I said, "I have looked, and here is a lampstand all of gold, with its bowl on its top, and its seven lamps on it—seven and seven pipes for the lamps that are on its top.

**4:3** And two olive trees are beside it, one on the right of the bowl and one on its left."

**4:4** Then I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**4:5** The angel who spoke with me answered and said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

**4:6** Then he answered and said to me, "This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: 'Not by force and not by strength, but by My Spirit,' says the LORD of hosts.

**4:7** Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become level ground! And he will bring out the chief stone to shouts of 'Grace, grace to it!'"

**4:8** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**4:9** "Zerubbabel's hands have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands will complete it; and you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you.

**4:10** For who has despised the day of small things? Yet these seven will rejoice when they see the plumb weight in Zerubbabel's hand—they are the eyes of the LORD, roaming across the whole earth."

**4:11** Then I answered and said to him, "What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on its left?"

**4:12** And I answered a second time and said to him, "What are the two branches of the olive trees that are beside the two gold conduits pouring out the gold from them?"

**4:13** He said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

**4:14** And he said, "These are the two sons of fresh oil who stand beside the Lord of all the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Translated from the Qere וָאֹמַר ("and I said," 1cs); the Ketiv reads ויאמר ("and he said," 3ms). The distributive phrase שִׁבְעָה וְשִׁבְעָה מוּצָקוֹת ("seven and seven pipes") likely denotes seven pipes for each of the seven lamps, or fourteen pipes in total.
- **4:7** — הָאֶבֶן הָרֹאשָׁה uses the rare feminine form רֹאשָׁה (from רֹאשׁ, "head/first/top"), literally "the stone, the head/chief one," referring either to the crowning capstone that completes the temple or to its premier stone.
- **4:10** — הָאֶבֶן הַבְּדִיל is literally "the stone, the tin," denoting a tin plumb weight or plummet (or a ceremonial metal stone). Following the Masoretic accentuation, שִׁבְעָה־אֵלֶּה ("these seven") serves as the subject of וְשָׂמְחוּ וְרָאוּ ("will rejoice when they see").
- **4:12** — צַנְתְּרוֹת is a biblical hapax legomenon denoting conduits or pipes, and הַזָּהָב is literally "the gold," referring poetically to golden olive oil.
- **4:14** — בְּנֵי־הַיִּצְהָר is literally "sons of the fresh oil," a Hebrew idiom for figures consecrated with or characterized by fresh olive oil, commonly understood as anointed ones.
