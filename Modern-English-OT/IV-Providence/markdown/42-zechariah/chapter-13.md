## Text

**13:1** On that day there will be a spring opened for the house of David and for the residents of Jerusalem, for sin and for impurity.

**13:2** "And it will happen on that day," declares the LORD of hosts, "that I will cut off the names of the idols from the land, and they will not be remembered again; and I will also take away the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness from the land."

**13:3** And it will happen that if a man still prophesies, his father and his mother who brought him forth will say to him, "You must not live, because you have spoken falsehood in the name of the LORD." And his father and his mother who brought him forth will pierce him through while he prophesies.

**13:4** And it will happen on that day that the prophets will be ashamed, each man of his vision when he prophesies, and they will not wear a cloak of hair so as to deceive.

**13:5** And he will say, "I am not a prophet; I am a man who works the ground, for a man sold me from my youth."

**13:6** And someone will say to him, "What are these wounds between your hands?" Then he will say, "Those with which I was struck in the house of those who love me."

**13:7** "Awake, sword, against My shepherd, and against the man, My companion," declares the LORD of hosts. "Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will scatter, and I will turn back My hand against the little ones.

**13:8** And it will happen in all the land," declares the LORD, "that two-thirds in it will be cut off and perish, and the third part will be left in it.

**13:9** And I will bring the third part into the fire, and I will refine them as one refines silver, and I will test them as one tests gold. They will call on My name, and I Myself will answer them. I will say, 'They are My people,' and they in turn will say, 'The LORD is my God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — Hebrew hiqnani is a unique Hiphil perfect of qanah ("acquire, buy"), here understood causatively as "sold me" (transferred ownership of me), though some render it "acquired me" or "made me possess [land]."
- **13:6** — Hebrew ben yadekha literally means "between your hands," an idiom likely referring to the chest or upper back; me'ahavay is a Piel participle of ahav ("those who love me").
- **13:8** — Hebrew pi-shenayim literally means "a portion of two," here denoting two-thirds in contrast to hashelishit ("the third part").
