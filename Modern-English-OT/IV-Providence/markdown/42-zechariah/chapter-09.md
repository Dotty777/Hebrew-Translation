## Text

**9:1** A pronouncement: the word of the LORD is against the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is its resting place, for the eye of mankind and of all the tribes of Israel is toward the LORD,

**9:2** and also Hamath, which borders on it, and Tyre and Sidon, though she is very wise.

**9:3** Tyre built a fortification for herself and piled up silver like dust, and fine gold like the mire of the streets.

**9:4** Look, the Lord will dispossess her and strike down her wealth in the sea, and she herself will be devoured by fire.

**9:5** Ashkelon will see it and be afraid; Gaza too, and she will writhe greatly; and Ekron as well, for her expectation has been put to shame. A king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon will not be inhabited.

**9:6** A mixed population will dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.

**9:7** I will remove its blood from its mouth and its detestable things from between its teeth. It too will remain for our God; it will become like a clan in Judah, and Ekron like a Jebusite.

**9:8** Then I will encamp around My house as a guard against anyone passing through or returning, and no oppressor will pass over them again, for now I have seen with My own eyes.

**9:9** Exult greatly, daughter of Zion! Shout out, daughter of Jerusalem! Look, your king is coming to you; righteous and victorious is he, humble and riding on a donkey, even on a colt, the foal of a female donkey.

**9:10** And I will cut off chariotry from Ephraim and horses from Jerusalem, and the war bow will be cut off. He will speak peace to the nations; his rule will extend from sea to sea, and from the River to the farthest bounds of the earth.

**9:11** As for you as well, because of the blood of your covenant, I have released your prisoners from a pit that has no water in it.

**9:12** Return to a stronghold, you prisoners of hope! Today as well I declare that I will repay double to you.

**9:13** For I have bent Judah for Myself as a bow and filled it with Ephraim; I will rouse your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and make you like the sword of a mighty warrior.

**9:14** Then the LORD will appear over them, and His arrow will go forth like lightning; the Lord GOD will sound the ram's horn and march in the storms of the south.

**9:15** The LORD of hosts will shield them; they will devour and trample down sling stones, and they will drink and clamor as with wine, and be filled like the sacrificial bowl, like the corners of an altar.

**9:16** On that day the LORD their God will save them as the flock of His people, for they are jewels of a crown gleaming upon His land.

**9:17** For how great His goodness is, and how great His beauty! Grain will cause the young men to flourish, and new wine the young women.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Masoretic Hebrew literally reads "for to/toward the LORD is the eye of mankind and all the tribes of Israel," indicating either that humanity and Israel look toward the LORD or that His watchful attention encompasses them.
- **9:8** — The Hebrew mitstsava (a rare form related to matstsava, "garrison/guard") is rendered "as a guard"; some revocalize it as min-tsava ("against an army/host").
- **9:9** — The word rendered "victorious" is a Niphal participle (nosha, from yasha), literally "saved" or "delivered," indicating that the king is granted deliverance and victory by God.
- **9:15** — Following the Masoretic Hebrew literally ("sling stones" and "as with wine"); the closing simile evokes the mizraq, the temple basin used for dashing sacrificial blood onto the corners of the altar.
- **9:17** — The third-person masculine singular suffixes on "goodness" and "beauty" may refer to the LORD (compare Jeremiah 31:12, where the LORD's goodness yields grain and new wine) or collectively to His people in verse 16.
