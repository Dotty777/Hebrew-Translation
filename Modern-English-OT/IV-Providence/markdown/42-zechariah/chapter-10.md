## Text

**10:1** Ask the LORD for rain in the time of the late rain. The LORD makes thunderclouds, and He will give them heavy rain showers, vegetation in the field for each one.

**10:2** For the household idols speak wickedness, and the diviners see falsehood; they tell empty dreams and comfort with vanity. Therefore they wander away like a flock; they suffer affliction because there is no shepherd.

**10:3** "My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will call the male goats to account; for the LORD of hosts has attended to His flock, the house of Judah, and will make them like His majestic steed in battle.

**10:4** From them will come the cornerstone, from them the tent peg, from them the war bow; from them every commander will go forth, all together.

**10:5** They will be like mighty warriors trampling in the mud of the streets in battle; and they will fight because the LORD is with them, and those who ride horses will be brought to shame.

**10:6** I will make the house of Judah strong, and the house of Joseph I will save; I will restore them, because I have compassion on them, and they will be as if I had not cast them off. For I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

**10:7** Ephraim will become like a mighty warrior, and their heart will be glad as from wine; their children will see and be glad, and their heart will exult in the LORD.

**10:8** I will whistle for them and gather them in, because I have redeemed them; and they will multiply as they once multiplied.

**10:9** I will sow them among the peoples, and in distant places they will remember Me; they will live with their children and will return.

**10:10** I will bring them back from the land of Egypt and gather them from Assyria; I will bring them to the land of Gilead and Lebanon, and enough room will not be found for them.

**10:11** He will pass through the sea of affliction and strike the waves in the sea, and all the depths of the Nile will dry up; Assyria's pride will be brought down, and Egypt's scepter will depart.

**10:12** And I will make them strong in the LORD, and in His name they will walk," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — Hebrew uses the same verb paqad in two opposite senses: with the preposition al ("call to account" or "punish") and with the direct object marker et ("attend to" or "care for"). "Male goats" (attudim) is a Hebrew metaphor for flock leaders.
- **10:4** — Hebrew uses the masculine singular pronoun mimmennu ("from it" or "from him"), referring collectively to the house of Judah in v. 3. The Qal participle noges normally means "taskmaster" or "oppressor," but here denotes an authoritative commander or ruler.
- **10:6** — The verb wehoshbotim is a rare mixed Hebrew form blending the Hiphil of yashav ("settle" or "cause to dwell") and the Hiphil of shuv ("bring back" or "restore," cf. v. 10).
- **10:11** — Hebrew bayyam tsarah literally reads "through the sea, affliction," an appositional construction meaning "the sea of affliction" or "distress." The third-person masculine singular verbs ("He will pass... strike") have the LORD as subject.
