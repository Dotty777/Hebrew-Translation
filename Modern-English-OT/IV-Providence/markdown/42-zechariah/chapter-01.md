## Text

**1:1** In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying:

**1:2** "The LORD was fiercely angry with your fathers.

**1:3** And you shall say to them, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: Return to Me, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will return to you, says the LORD of hosts.'

**1:4** Do not be like your fathers, to whom the earlier prophets called out, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: Turn back now from your evil ways and your evil deeds.' Yet they did not listen and did not pay attention to Me, declares the LORD.

**1:5** Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, will they live forever?

**1:6** Yet My words and My statutes, which I commanded My servants the prophets, did they not overtake your fathers? So they turned back and said, 'Just as the LORD of hosts planned to do to us according to our ways and according to our deeds, so He has done with us.'"

**1:7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying:

**1:8** I saw during the night, and look, a man was riding on a red horse, and he was standing among the myrtle trees that were in the hollow; and behind him were red, sorrel, and white horses.

**1:9** Then I said, "What are these, my lord?" And the angel who spoke with me said to me, "I will show you what these are."

**1:10** And the man standing among the myrtle trees answered and said, "These are the ones whom the LORD has sent to walk about through the earth."

**1:11** So they answered the angel of the LORD standing among the myrtle trees and said, "We have walked about through the earth, and look, all the earth is settled and quiet."

**1:12** Then the angel of the LORD responded and said, "LORD of hosts, how long will You not show compassion to Jerusalem and to the cities of Judah, against which You have shown indignation these seventy years?"

**1:13** And the LORD answered the angel who spoke with me with good words, words of comfort.

**1:14** Then the angel who spoke with me said to me, "Proclaim, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with great jealousy.

**1:15** And with great anger I am angry against the nations that are at ease; for I was angry only a little, yet they aided the calamity.

**1:16** Therefore this is what the LORD says: I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion; My house will be built in it, declares the LORD of hosts, and a measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem.'

**1:17** Proclaim again, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: My cities will again overflow with good things, and the LORD will again comfort Zion and will again choose Jerusalem.'"

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — The ketiv reads a longer orthographic form with an extra yod (um'alileikhem); the qere reads uma'alleleikhem ("and your deeds"), the regular plural of ma'alal with a 2mp suffix, which is translated here.
- **1:8** — Hebrew metsulah normally refers to a depth (often of water) and here designates a low-lying hollow or glen; seruqqim ("sorrel") occurs only here as a horse color and denotes a reddish-brown or chestnut hue.
- **1:15** — Literally "they helped for evil/calamity" (azeru lera'ah), an idiom meaning that the nations exceeded God's intended discipline and intensified Judah's disaster.
- **1:16** — The ketiv reads the older spelling qaweh (cf. 1 Kgs 7:23; Jer 31:39); the qere reads the standard form qaw ("a measuring line"), which is translated here.
