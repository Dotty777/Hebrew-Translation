## Text

**12:1** The oracle of the word of the LORD concerning Israel—the declaration of the LORD, who stretches out the heavens, founds the earth, and forms the spirit of man within him:

**12:2** "Look, I am about to make Jerusalem a bowl of staggering for all the peoples around; and it will also be against Judah during the siege against Jerusalem.

**12:3** And it will happen on that day that I will make Jerusalem a heavy stone for all the peoples; all who lift it will be severely gashed. And all the nations of the earth will be gathered against it.

**12:4** On that day," declares the LORD, "I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness. Upon the house of Judah I will open My eyes, while I strike every horse of the peoples with blindness.

**12:5** Then the chiefs of Judah will say in their heart, 'The inhabitants of Jerusalem are a strength to me in the LORD of hosts, their God.'

**12:6** On that day I will make the chiefs of Judah like a brazier of fire among wood and like a torch of fire among sheaves; they will devour all the surrounding peoples to the right and to the left, while Jerusalem will again dwell in its own place, in Jerusalem.

**12:7** And the LORD will save the tents of Judah first, so that the splendor of the house of David and the splendor of the inhabitants of Jerusalem will not surpass that of Judah.

**12:8** On that day the LORD will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem; the one who stumbles among them on that day will become like David, and the house of David like God, like the angel of the LORD before them.

**12:9** And it will happen on that day that I will seek to wipe out all the nations that come against Jerusalem.

**12:10** And I will pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and supplications. They will look to Me, whom they have pierced, and they will mourn over Him as in mourning for an only son, and grieve bitterly over Him as in bitter grief for a firstborn.

**12:11** On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will grow great, like the mourning of Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.

**12:12** And the land will mourn, each clan apart: the clan of the house of David apart, and their wives apart; the clan of the house of Nathan apart, and their wives apart;

**12:13** the clan of the house of Levi apart, and their wives apart; the clan of the Shimeites apart, and their wives apart;

**12:14** all the clans that remain, each clan apart, and their wives apart."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Hebrew clause literally reads "and also upon Judah it will be in the siege against Jerusalem," which may mean either that Judah too will be caught in the siege or that Judah will be forced into the siege against Jerusalem.
- **12:5** — Hebrew allufe ("chiefs" or "leaders"; also in v. 6) can alternatively be rendered "clans." The singular pronoun li ("to me") is distributive, representing each leader's inner thought.
- **12:10** — The Masoretic Text reads elay ("to Me") with et asher-daqaru ("whom they have pierced") and then shifts to the third-person pronoun alav ("over Him"). The form wehamer is a Hiphil infinitive absolute of marar ("grieve bitterly") continuing the preceding finite verb.
- **12:11** — The construct phrase "mourning of Hadad-rimmon" may refer either to lamentation at a place named Hadad-rimmon (often linked to King Josiah's death, 2 Chr 35:22-25) or to ritual mourning for a deity of that name. Hebrew spells Megiddo here as Megiddon.
