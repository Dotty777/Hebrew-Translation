## Text

**7:1** Now it happened in the fourth year of King Darius that the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, in Kislev.

**7:2** And Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-melech and his men to implore the favor of the LORD,

**7:3** to speak to the priests who belonged to the house of the LORD of hosts and to the prophets, saying, "Should I weep in the fifth month, abstaining as I have done these many years?"

**7:4** And the word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying,

**7:5** "Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests, saying, 'When you fasted and lamented in the fifth and in the seventh months for these seventy years, did you truly fast for Me, even Me?

**7:6** And when you eat and when you drink, is it not you who are eating and you who are drinking?

**7:7** Should you not hear the words that the LORD proclaimed through the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and at ease, along with its cities around it, and when the Negev and the lowland were inhabited?'"

**7:8** Then the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, saying:

**7:9** This is what the LORD of hosts has said, saying, "Judge with true justice, and practice loyal love and compassion, each man with his brother.

**7:10** Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the resident alien or the poor, and do not devise evil in your hearts, each man against his brother."

**7:11** Yet they refused to pay attention; they turned a rebellious shoulder and made their ears heavy so as not to hear.

**7:12** They made their hearts like diamond so as not to hear the law and the words that the LORD of hosts had sent by His Spirit through the former prophets. Therefore great wrath came from the LORD of hosts.

**7:13** And it happened that just as He called and they did not listen, "so they would call and I would not listen," said the LORD of hosts,

**7:14** "and I scattered them as in a storm among all the nations that they had not known." So the land became desolate behind them, with no one passing through or returning; thus they turned a desirable land into a desolation.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — In the Masoretic accentuation, Bethel is the subject ("Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-melech and his men"), though some construe Bethel-sharezer as a single Babylonian personal name or read Bethel as the destination. The idiom translated "implore the favor of" literally means "appease/soften the face of" (Piel of chalah).
- **7:7** — The Hebrew begins with the interrogative particle and direct-object marker (halo et-haddevarim) without an expressed verb, implying a verb such as "hear" or "heed"; alternatively, et may function as an emphatic subject marker ("Are these not the words...").
- **7:14** — The Hebrew verb rendered "I scattered them as in a storm" is an unusual imperfect form from sa'ar ("whirl/storm away"), used here in context for the past exile that left the land desolate.
