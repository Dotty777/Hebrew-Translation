## Text

**6:1** Again I lifted up my eyes and looked, and there were four chariots coming out from between the two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**6:2** The first chariot had red horses, and the second chariot had black horses;

**6:3** and the third chariot had white horses, and the fourth chariot had dappled, strong horses.

**6:4** Then I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**6:5** The angel answered and said to me, "These are the four winds of the heavens, going out after presenting themselves before the Lord of all the earth.

**6:6** The one with the black horses is going out to the land of the north, and the white ones have gone out after them, and the dappled ones have gone out to the land of the south."

**6:7** And the strong ones went out and sought to go and range through the earth. Then He said, "Go, range through the earth." So they ranged through the earth.

**6:8** Then He called out to me and spoke to me, saying, "Look, those going out to the land of the north have given rest to My Spirit in the land of the north."

**6:9** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**6:10** "Take from the exiles, from Heldai, from Tobijah, and from Jedaiah; and you yourself go on that day and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, where they have come from Babylon.

**6:11** And take silver and gold and make crowns, and set them on the head of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

**6:12** Then speak to him, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: Look, a man—Branch is his name—and from his own place he will branch out, and he will build the temple of the LORD.

**6:13** And he himself will build the temple of the LORD, and he himself will bear majesty, and he will sit and rule on his throne; and a priest will be on his throne, and a counsel of peace will be between the two of them.'

**6:14** And the crowns will be for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen the son of Zephaniah as a memorial in the temple of the LORD.

**6:15** And those who are far away will come and build on the temple of the LORD, and you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you. And this will happen if you diligently listen to the voice of the LORD your God."

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — אֲמֻצִּים (also in 6:7) derives from the root אמץ ("be strong") yet follows the Hebrew color-adjective pattern (like אֲדֻמִּים "red" and בְּרֻדִּים "dappled"), leading some scholars to understand it as a horse color such as "bay" or "sorrel."
- **6:5** — Hebrew רוּחוֹת can mean either "winds" (matching the four compass directions, cf. Zech 2:6) or "spirits" (matching their standing in the heavenly court before God, cf. Ps 104:4).
- **6:12** — צֶמַח ("Branch") and יִצְמָח ("will branch out") play on the root צמח, literally "sprout/shoot" and "sprout up"; מִתַּחְתָּיו is literally "from beneath him," an idiom for "from his own place."
- **6:13** — וְהָיָה כֹהֵן עַל־כִּסְאוֹ is syntactically ambiguous in Hebrew and may be read either as "a priest will be on his throne" (a second figure beside the royal Branch, matching "between the two of them") or as "he will be a priest on his throne" (uniting royal and priestly roles in one figure).
- **6:14** — Plural הָעֲטָרֹת ("the crowns," as in 6:11) takes a feminine singular verb תִּהְיֶה, indicating either a single composite crown or collective crowns. חֵלֶם ("Helem") and חֵן ("Hen," also possibly the common noun "graciousness") correspond to Heldai and Josiah in 6:10.
