## Text

**2:1** Then I raised my eyes and saw, and look, there were four horns.

**2:2** And I said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem."

**2:3** And the LORD showed me four artisans.

**2:4** And I said, "What are these coming to do?" And he spoke, saying, "These are the horns that scattered Judah to such an extent that no one raised his head; and these have come to terrify them, to cast down the horns of the nations that raised a horn against the land of Judah to scatter it."

**2:5** Then I raised my eyes and saw, and look, there was a man, and in his hand was a measuring cord.

**2:6** So I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see what its width is and what its length is."

**2:7** And look, the angel who spoke with me was going out, and another angel was going out to meet him.

**2:8** And he said to him, "Run, speak to that young man, saying, 'Jerusalem will be inhabited as unwalled villages because of the abundance of people and livestock in its midst.

**2:9** And I will be to it, declares the LORD, a wall of fire all around, and I will be a glory in its midst.'"

**2:10** "Come! Come, and flee from the land of the north, declares the LORD; for I have spread you out like the four winds of the heavens, declares the LORD.

**2:11** Come, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon."

**2:12** For this is what the LORD of hosts says—after glory He has sent me to the nations that plunder you—"Indeed, the one who touches you touches the pupil of His eye.

**2:13** For look, I am waving My hand over them, and they will become plunder for their servants." And you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me.

**2:14** "Shout for joy and rejoice, daughter of Zion! For look, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst, declares the LORD.

**2:15** And many nations will join themselves to the LORD on that day, and they will become My people, and I will dwell in your midst." And you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you.

**2:16** And the LORD will inherit Judah as His portion on the holy ground, and will again choose Jerusalem.

**2:17** Keep silent, all flesh, in the presence of the LORD, for He has stirred Himself from His holy dwelling.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — The clause kefi-ish lo-nasa rosho is idiomatic (literally "according to the measure that no man raised his head"); leyaddot ("to cast down") is the Piel infinitive construct of yadah II ("throw/cast").
- **2:10** — The interjection hoy (doubled here and repeated in 2:11) functions as an urgent summons to attention and flight rather than a lament; the preposition ke literally means "like" ("like the four winds of the heavens").
- **2:12** — The elliptical phrase achar kavod literally means "after glory" (variously understood as "for the sake of glory," "in pursuit of glory," or "after the glory"). The hapax legomenon bavah ("pupil") denotes the delicate center of the eye; Masoretic tradition lists eino ("His eye") among the scribal emendations (Tiqqune Sopherim) for "My eye," and the speaker alternates between the sent messenger (here and at the ends of 2:13, 2:15) and the LORD.
- **2:17** — The verb ne'or is the Niphal perfect of ur ("to awake/rouse oneself"), portraying the LORD stirring Himself to act from His heavenly dwelling.
