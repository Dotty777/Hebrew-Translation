## Text

**3:1** "Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will clear a way before Me. And suddenly the Lord whom you are seeking will come to His temple; and the messenger of the covenant in whom you delight—look, He is coming," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:2** But who can endure the day of His coming, and who can stand at His appearing? For He is like the fire of a refiner and like launderers' soap.

**3:3** And He will sit refining and purifying silver, and He will purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and like silver, and they will be for the LORD ones who present an offering in righteousness.

**3:4** Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasant to the LORD, as in ancient days and as in earlier years.

**3:5** "Then I will draw near to you for judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against those who swear falsely, and against those who oppress the hired laborer in his wages, the widow, and the orphan, and those who turn aside the resident alien, and do not fear Me," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:6** "For I, the LORD, have not changed; and you, sons of Jacob, have not come to an end.

**3:7** Ever since the days of your fathers you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept them. Turn back to Me, and I will turn back to you," says the LORD of hosts. "Yet you say, 'In what way shall we turn back?'

**3:8** Will a human being rob God? Yet you are robbing Me! Still you say, 'In what way have we robbed You?' In the tithe and the contribution!

**3:9** With a curse you are cursed, for you are robbing Me—the entire nation!

**3:10** Bring the entire tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house, and please test Me in this," says the LORD of hosts, "see if I will not open for you the windows of the heavens and pour out for you a blessing until there is no more room.

**3:11** And I will rebuke the devourer for your sake, so that it will not destroy for you the fruit of the soil, nor will the vine in the field drop its fruit for you," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:12** "Then all the nations will call you blessed, for you yourselves will be a land of delight," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:13** "Your words have been harsh against Me," says the LORD. "Yet you say, 'What have we spoken together against You?'

**3:14** You have said, 'It is futile to serve God; and what gain is it that we have kept His charge, and that we have walked mournfully before the LORD of hosts?

**3:15** And now we ourselves call the arrogant blessed; not only are those who practice wickedness built up, they even test God and escape!'"

**3:16** Then those who feared the LORD spoke together, each to his companion, and the LORD paid attention and heard it; and a scroll of remembrance was written before Him for those who fear the LORD and who value His name.

**3:17** "And they will be Mine," says the LORD of hosts, "on the day when I act, as a treasured possession; and I will have compassion on them, just as a man has compassion on his son who serves him.

**3:18** Then you will see once more the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between the one who serves God and the one who has not served Him.

**3:19** For look, the day is coming, burning like an oven, when all the arrogant and everyone who practices wickedness will become stubble; and the coming day will set them on fire," says the LORD of hosts, "so that it will leave them neither root nor branch.

**3:20** Yet for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will shine forth with healing in its wings; and you will go out and leap like calves from the stall.

**3:21** And you will trample the wicked, for they will be ashes beneath the soles of your feet on the day when I act," says the LORD of hosts.

**3:22** "Look, I am sending Elijah the prophet to you before the coming of the day of the LORD, great and awesome.

**3:23** And he will turn back the heart of fathers to children, and the heart of children to their fathers, so that I will not come and strike the land with utter destruction.

**3:24** Remember the law of Moses My servant, which I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel—statutes and judgments."

## Translator Notes

- **3:8** — The rare Hebrew verb qaba ('rob, defraud') occurs in the Hebrew Bible only here, in 3:9, and in Proverbs 22:23.
- **3:10** — The phrase 'until there is no more room' renders Hebrew ad-beli-day, literally 'until without sufficiency,' an idiom for overflowing abundance beyond capacity.
- **3:13** — The Niphal stem of dabar ('speak') here and in 3:16 carries reciprocal force, denoting people conversing together among themselves.
- **3:17** — Hebrew segullah ('treasured possession') can modify 'they will be Mine' with oseh ('when I act') used absolutely as in 3:21, or serve as the direct object of oseh ('on the day when I prepare a treasured possession').
- **3:24** — In the Hebrew text this command is 3:22 and stands before the Elijah oracle (Hebrew 3:23-24); the verse order here follows this edition's Greek-based numbering, which, like the Septuagint, places it last. In 3:23, 'utter destruction' renders Hebrew cherem, the covenant ban of total destruction.
