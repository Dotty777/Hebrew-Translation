## Text

**1:1** The pronouncement of the word of the LORD to Israel by the hand of Malachi.

**1:2** "I have loved you," says the LORD. "Yet you say, 'In what way have You loved us?' Was Esau not a brother to Jacob?" declares the LORD. "Yet I loved Jacob,

**1:3** and Esau I hated; and I made his mountains a desolation and gave his inheritance to the jackals of the wilderness."

**1:4** Though Edom says, "We have been demolished, yet we will return and build up the ruins," this is what the LORD of armies says: "They may build, but I will tear down; and people will call them 'Territory of Wickedness,' and 'The People against whom the LORD is indignant forever.'

**1:5** And your own eyes will see it, and you yourselves will say, 'The LORD is great beyond the territory of Israel!'"

**1:6** "A son honors a father, and a servant his master. If then I am a father, where is My honor? And if I am a master, where is the reverence due Me?" says the LORD of armies to you, the priests who despise My name. "Yet you say, 'In what way have we despised Your name?'

**1:7** You are presenting defiled food on My altar! Yet you say, 'In what way have we defiled You?' By saying, 'The table of the LORD is despised.'

**1:8** And when you present a blind animal to sacrifice, is there no evil in that? And when you present a lame or sick one, is there no evil in that? Offer it now to your governor! Would he be pleased with you, or would he grant you favor?" says the LORD of armies.

**1:9** And now, plead for the favor of God, that He may be gracious to us! "With this coming from your own hand, will He grant favor to any of you?" says the LORD of armies.

**1:10** "Who is there even among you who would shut the doors, so that you would not kindle fire on My altar for nothing? I have no delight in you," says the LORD of armies, "and I will not accept an offering from your hand.

**1:11** For from the sun's rising to its setting, My name is great among the nations, and in every place incense is presented to My name, along with a pure offering; for My name is great among the nations," says the LORD of armies.

**1:12** "But you are profaning it when you say, 'The table of the Lord is defiled, and its produce, its food, is despised.'

**1:13** You also say, 'Look, what a weariness!' and you sniff at it," says the LORD of armies. "And you bring what was seized by force, and the lame, and the sick; and thus you bring the offering! Should I accept it from your hand?" says the LORD.

**1:14** "And cursed is the deceiver who has a male in his flock and vows it, yet sacrifices what is blemished to the Lord! For I am a great King," says the LORD of armies, "and My name is feared among the nations."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Hebrew massa literally means 'burden,' used as a technical term for a prophetic pronouncement. The prophet's name Malachi literally means 'My messenger,' anticipating the same Hebrew form in 3:1.
- **1:3** — Hebrew tannot is a rare feminine plural of tan ('jackals'), occurring only here in the Masoretic Text.
- **1:8** — The idiom 'grant favor' renders Hebrew nasa panim, literally 'lift up the face,' which recurs in 1:9 and carries the negative sense of 'showing partiality' in 2:9.
- **1:11** — The verbless clause 'My name is great' and the Hophal participles 'incense is presented' (muqtar muggash) are rendered in the present tense to match the nominal Hebrew syntax, though they can also carry futuristic force.
- **1:13** — Masoretic Hebrew reads 'sniff at it' (oto), referring to the Lord's table or service; rabbinic scribal tradition (tiqqun sopherim) notes an older reading 'at Me.'
