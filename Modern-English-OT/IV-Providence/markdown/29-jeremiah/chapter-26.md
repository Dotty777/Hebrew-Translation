## Text

**26:2** Concerning Egypt: regarding the army of Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, which was by the Euphrates River at Carchemish, which Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, struck down in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.

**26:3** Ready the small shield and the large shield, and step forward for battle.

**26:4** Harness the horses and mount up, horsemen; take your stand in helmets, polish the lances, put on the coats of armor.

**26:5** Why do I see them dismayed, retreating backward? Their mighty men are crushed, and they flee in full flight and do not look back; terror is on every side, declares the LORD.

**26:6** Let the swift not flee, nor the mighty warrior escape; northward, alongside the Euphrates River, they have stumbled and fallen.

**26:7** Who is this, rising like the Nile, whose waters churn like the rivers?

**26:8** Egypt rises like the Nile, and waters churn like the rivers; and he says, "I will rise up, I will cover the earth; I will destroy the city and those who dwell in it."

**26:9** Charge, horses, and rage wildly, chariots! Let the mighty warriors go out: Cush and Put, who handle the shield, and the Ludim, who handle and bend the bow.

**26:10** Yet that day belongs to the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance to avenge Himself on His adversaries. The sword will devour and have its fill, and be drenched with their blood; for there is a sacrifice for the Lord GOD of hosts in the land of the north, at the Euphrates River.

**26:11** Go up to Gilead and take balm, virgin daughter of Egypt. In vain you have multiplied remedies; there is no healing for you.

**26:12** Nations have heard of your disgrace, and your outcry has filled the earth; for warrior has stumbled against warrior, and both of them have fallen together.

**26:13** The word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the coming of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, to strike the land of Egypt.

**26:14** Declare in Egypt and proclaim in Migdol, and proclaim in Noph and in Tahpanhes. Say, "Take your stand and prepare yourself, for the sword has devoured all around you."

**26:15** Why has your mighty one been swept away? He did not stand, for the LORD thrust him down.

**26:16** He has made many stumble; indeed, a man has fallen against his companion, and they said, "Arise, and let us return to our people and to the land of our birth, away from the oppressing sword."

**26:17** They cried out there, "Pharaoh, king of Egypt, is a loud noise; he has let the appointed time pass by."

**26:18** As I live, declares the King—the LORD of hosts is His name—surely like Tabor among the mountains and like Carmel by the sea, he will come.

**26:19** Make ready for yourself baggage for exile, inhabitant, daughter of Egypt; for Noph will become a desolation, laid in ruins, without an inhabitant.

**26:20** Egypt is a very beautiful heifer; a gadfly from the north has come—it has come!

**26:21** Her mercenaries in her midst are also like fattened calves; for they too have turned back, they have fled together, they did not stand, because the day of their calamity has come upon them, the time of their punishment.

**26:22** Her sound moves like that of a serpent, for they march in force, and with axes they have come against her like cutters of wood.

**26:23** They have cut down her forest, declares the LORD, though it cannot be searched out; for they are more numerous than locusts, and they are without number.

**26:24** The daughter of Egypt has been put to shame; she has been given into the hand of a northern people.

**26:25** The LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, has said, "Look, I am about to punish Amon of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, and her gods, and her kings—even Pharaoh and those who trust in him."

**26:27** But you, do not fear, My servant Jacob, and do not be dismayed, Israel; for look, I am about to save you from afar, and your offspring from the land of their captivity. Jacob will return and be quiet and at ease, and no one will frighten him.

**26:28** As for you, do not fear, My servant Jacob, declares the LORD, for I am with you. For I will make a complete end of all the nations to which I have banished you, yet of you I will not make a complete end; I will discipline you in justice, and I will by no means let you go unpunished.

## Translator Notes

- **26:11** — Ketiv writes the verb with an archaic 2fs ending (hrbyty), while the qere standardizes it to the Hiphil 2fs perfect hirbet, "you have multiplied."
- **26:15** — The noun "your mighty one" (abbireka) is morphologically plural, yet all three accompanying verbs and suffixes ("has been swept away," "did not stand," "thrust him down") are masculine singular, suggesting a collective or majestic plural referring to Pharaoh or Egypt's champion.
- **26:20** — The rare noun qeres ("gadfly") occurs only here in the Hebrew Bible, from a root meaning "to nip or pinch," fitting the heifer metaphor. The verb "has come" (ba') is doubled in the Masoretic text for urgency.
- **26:25** — Amon was the principal deity of No (Egyptian Thebes); similarly, Noph in 26:14 and 26:19 is the Hebrew name for Memphis.
