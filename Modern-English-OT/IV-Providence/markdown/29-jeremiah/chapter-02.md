## Text

**2:2** "Go and proclaim in the ears of Jerusalem, saying, 'This is what the LORD says: I remember for you the loyal love of your youth, the love of your bridal days, your walking after Me in the wilderness, in a land not sown.

**2:3** Holy was Israel to the LORD, the firstfruits of His produce; all who devoured him became guilty; disaster came upon them, declares the LORD.'"

**2:4** Listen to the word of the LORD, house of Jacob, and all the clans of the house of Israel.

**2:5** This is what the LORD says: "What wrong did your fathers find in Me, that they withdrew far from Me, and walked after futility and became futile?

**2:6** And they did not say, 'Where is the LORD, who brought us up from the land of Egypt, who led us in the wilderness, in a land of desert plain and pits, in a land of drought and deep gloom, in a land through which no man passed, and where no human dwelt?'

**2:7** And I brought you into the fertile land to eat its fruit and its bounty. Yet you came in and defiled My land, and My inheritance you turned into an abomination.

**2:8** The priests did not say, 'Where is the LORD?' And those who hold the law did not know Me, and the shepherds rebelled against Me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal and walked after things that do not benefit.

**2:9** Therefore I will contend with you further, declares the LORD, and with the children of your children I will contend.

**2:10** For cross over to the coastlands of Kittim and see, and send to Kedar and examine closely, and see whether anything like this has happened.

**2:11** Has a nation exchanged gods, even though they are not gods? Yet My people have exchanged their glory for what does not benefit.

**2:12** Be appalled at this, heavens, and shudder, be completely desolate, declares the LORD.

**2:13** For My people have done two evils: Me they have forsaken, the spring of living water, to carve out cisterns for themselves, broken cisterns that cannot contain water.

**2:14** Is Israel a slave, or is he one born in the household? Why has he become plunder?

**2:15** Young lions have roared against him; they have lifted up their voice. And they have turned his land into a desolation; his cities have been burned, with no inhabitant.

**2:16** Also the sons of Noph and Tahpanhes have grazed bare the crown of your head.

**2:17** Has not your forsaking the LORD your God done this to you, at the time He was leading you along the way?

**2:18** And now, what business do you have on the way to Egypt, to drink the waters of Shihor? And what business do you have on the way to Assyria, to drink the waters of the River?

**2:19** Your wickedness will discipline you, and your apostasies will rebuke you. Know and see that your forsaking the LORD your God is evil and bitter, and that the dread of Me is not in you, declares the Lord GOD of hosts.

**2:20** For long ago I broke your yoke and snapped your bonds, and you said, 'I will not transgress'; yet upon every high hill and under every luxuriant tree you lie down as a prostitute.

**2:21** Yet I planted you as a choice vine, all of it genuine seed. How then have you turned before Me into degenerate shoots of a foreign vine?

**2:22** For even if you wash with lye and multiply soap for yourself, your iniquity remains stained before Me, declares the Lord GOD.

**2:23** How can you say, 'I have not been defiled; I have not walked after the Baals'? Look at your way in the valley; know what you have done: a swift young female camel interlacing her ways,

**2:24** a wild donkey accustomed to the wilderness; in the craving of her soul she sniffs the wind. Her mating urge, who can turn it back? All who seek her will not grow weary; in her month they will find her.

**2:25** Hold back your foot from going barefoot and your throat from thirst. Yet you said, 'It is hopeless! No! For I love strangers, and after them I will go.'

**2:26** Like the shame of a thief when he is found out, so the house of Israel is put to shame: they, their kings, their officials, their priests, and their prophets,

**2:27** saying to a tree, 'You are my father,' and to a stone, 'You gave birth to us.' For they have turned the back of their neck to Me, and not their face; yet in the time of their disaster they will say, 'Arise and save us!'

**2:28** And where are your gods that you made for yourself? Let them arise, if they can save you in the time of your disaster; for your gods have become as many as the number of your cities, Judah.

**2:29** Why do you contend against Me? All of you have rebelled against Me, declares the LORD.

**2:30** In vain I struck your children; they did not accept discipline. Your sword devoured your prophets like a destroying lion.

**2:31** You, this generation, look at the word of the LORD! Have I been a wilderness to Israel, or a land of deepest darkness? Why have My people said, 'We roam freely; we will not come to You again'?

**2:32** Does a virgin forget her jewelry, or a bride her sashes? Yet My people have forgotten Me for days beyond number.

**2:33** How skillfully you set your way to seek love! Therefore you have taught your ways even to wicked women.

**2:34** Moreover, on the hems of your garments is found the blood of the lives of the innocent needy; you did not catch them breaking in. Yet despite all these things,

**2:35** you say, 'Indeed I am innocent; surely His anger has turned back from me.' Look, I am entering into judgment with you for your saying, 'I have not sinned.'

**2:36** How greatly you go about to alter your way! By Egypt as well you will be shamed, just as you were shamed by Assyria.

**2:37** From this one also you will go out with your hands upon your head, for the LORD has rejected the objects of your trust, and you will not succeed through them."

## Translator Notes

- **2:15** — Ketiv reads 3fs נצתה while qere reads 3cp נִצְּתוּ (Niphal perfect of יָצַת, "be burned"), agreeing in number with plural עָרָיו ("his cities").
- **2:16** — Noph is the Hebrew name for Egyptian Memphis, and qere תַחְפַּנְחֵס (ketiv תחפנס) is Tahpanhes; יִרְעוּךְ is Qal imperfect of רָעָה ("graze"), used figuratively for stripping bare the scalp.
- **2:20** — Ketiv reads אעבוד ("I will not serve"), whereas qere reads אֶעֱבוֹר ("I will not transgress," translated here); MT points שָׁבַרְתִּי and נִתַּקְתִּי as 1cs ("I broke... I snapped"), though some read them as archaic 2fs forms ("you broke... you snapped").
- **2:27** — Ketiv reads ילדתני ("you gave birth to me"), while qere reads יְלִדְתָּנוּ ("you gave birth to us," translated here, matching "save us" at the end of the verse).
- **2:33** — Ketiv reads למדתי (archaic 2fs ending), while qere reads standard Piel perfect 2fs לִמַּדְתְּ ("you have taught"); הָרָעוֹת may denote either "wicked women" (as translated) or "wicked deeds."
- **2:34** — מְצָאתִים can be parsed as 2fs ("you caught/found them," since 2fs perfect takes -tî- before suffixes) or 1cs ("I found them"); כִּי עַל־כׇּל־אֵלֶּה (lit. "indeed upon all these") is syntactically terse and leads into 2:35.
