## Text

**9:1** If only I had in the wilderness a lodging place for travelers, so that I might abandon my people and depart from them! For all of them are adulterers, a gathering of traitors.

**9:2** "They bend their tongue as their bow for falsehood, and not for faithfulness have they grown mighty in the land. For they go out from evil to evil, and they do not know Me," declares the LORD.

**9:3** "Let each one be on guard against his neighbor, and do not trust in any brother; for every brother surely supplants, and every neighbor goes about as a slanderer.

**9:4** Each person deceives his neighbor, and truth they do not speak; they have taught their tongue to speak falsehood, and they wear themselves out acting perversely.

**9:5** You dwell amid deceit; by deceit they refuse to know Me," declares the LORD.

**9:6** Therefore, this is what the LORD of hosts says: "Look, I am refining them and will test them; for how else shall I act on account of the daughter of My people?

**9:7** Their tongue is a sharpened arrow; it speaks deceit. With his mouth a person speaks peace with his neighbor, yet within himself he sets his ambush.

**9:8** Shall I not punish them for these things?" declares the LORD. "On a nation like this, shall My soul not avenge itself?"

**9:9** "Over the mountains I will lift up weeping and wailing, and over the pastures of the wilderness a dirge, because they have been scorched so that no one passes through, and no one hears the sound of livestock. From the birds of the sky to the beasts, they have fled, they are gone.

**9:10** And I will turn Jerusalem into heaps of ruins, a lair of jackals, and the cities of Judah I will make a desolation, without an inhabitant."

**9:11** Who is the wise man who can understand this, and to whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, that he may declare it? Why has the land perished and been scorched like the wilderness, without anyone passing through?

**9:12** And the LORD said, "Because they have abandoned My law which I set before them, and have not listened to My voice nor walked in it,

**9:13** but have walked after the stubbornness of their heart and after the Baals, which their fathers taught them."

**9:14** Therefore, this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I am about to feed this people wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink.

**9:15** And I will scatter them among the nations whom neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send the sword after them until I have consumed them."

**9:16** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Consider carefully, and summon the mourning women, that they may come; send for the skilled women, that they may come!"

**9:17** Let them hurry and raise up wailing over us, so that our eyes may run down with tears and our eyelids flow with water.

**9:18** For a sound of wailing is heard from Zion: "How we are devastated! We are deeply ashamed, because we have abandoned the land, because they have cast down our dwellings."

**9:19** Hear now, women, the word of the LORD, and let your ear receive the word of His mouth; teach your daughters wailing, and each woman her neighbor a dirge:

**9:20** "For death has climbed up through our windows; it has entered our citadels, to cut off the child from the street, the young men from the public squares."

**9:21** Speak, "This is what the LORD declares: 'Human corpses will fall like dung upon the surface of the field, and like cut grain behind the harvester, with no one gathering them.'"

**9:22** This is what the LORD says: "Do not let a wise man boast in his wisdom, and do not let the mighty man boast in his might; do not let a wealthy man boast in his wealth.

**9:23** Rather, let the one who boasts boast in this: having insight and knowing Me, that I am the LORD who carries out loyal love, justice, and righteousness on the earth; for in these things I take delight," declares the LORD.

**9:24** "Look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will bring punishment upon everyone circumcised yet in uncircumcision:

**9:25** upon Egypt, upon Judah, upon Edom, upon the sons of Ammon, upon Moab, and upon all those who clip the edges of their hair who dwell in the wilderness; for all the nations are uncircumcised, and the whole house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart."

## Translator Notes

- **9:3** — The Hebrew Qal infinitive absolute plus imperfect ("surely supplants") forms a wordplay on the name Jacob (cf. Gen 27:36).
- **9:7** — Qere reads the Qal passive participle "sharpened" (or "beaten/drawn," cf. 1 Kgs 10:16); Ketiv reads the Qal active participle "slaughtering" (i.e., "deadly").
- **9:18** — The indefinite plural Hiphil verb ("they have cast down our dwellings") is also sometimes construed with "our dwellings" as subject ("our dwellings have cast us out").
- **9:24** — Literally "every circumcised one in uncircumcision," referring to those physically circumcised yet uncircumcised in heart (v. 25).
