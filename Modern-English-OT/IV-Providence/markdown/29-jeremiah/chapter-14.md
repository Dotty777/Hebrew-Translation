## Text

**14:1** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the droughts.

**14:2** Judah mourns, and her gates languish; they sink to the ground in gloom, and the outcry of Jerusalem rises up.

**14:3** Their nobles send their attendants for water; they come to the cisterns and find no water. They return with their vessels empty; they are ashamed and humiliated, and they cover their heads.

**14:4** Because the ground is cracked, for there has been no rain in the land, the farmers are ashamed; they cover their heads.

**14:5** For even the doe in the field gives birth and forsakes her young, because there is no fresh grass.

**14:6** The wild donkeys stand on the bare heights; they pant for air like jackals. Their eyes fail, because there is no vegetation.

**14:7** Though our iniquities testify against us, LORD, act for the sake of Your name! Indeed, our apostasies are many; against You we have sinned.

**14:8** Hope of Israel, its Savior in time of distress, why should You be like a sojourner in the land, and like a traveler who turns aside to lodge for the night?

**14:9** Why should You be like a stunned man, like a mighty warrior who cannot save? Yet You are in our midst, LORD, and Your name is called over us; do not leave us!

**14:10** This is what the LORD says concerning this people: "This is how they have loved to wander; they have not held back their feet. And the LORD does not accept them; now He will remember their iniquity and call their sins to account."

**14:11** Then the LORD said to me, "Do not pray on behalf of this people for their good.

**14:12** When they fast, I will not listen to their outcry; and when they offer up burnt offering and grain offering, I will not accept them. Rather, by sword, famine, and pestilence I am going to consume them."

**14:13** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Look, the prophets are saying to them, 'You will not see the sword, nor will you have famine; rather, I will give you true peace in this place.'"

**14:14** And the LORD said to me, "The prophets are prophesying falsehood in My name. I did not send them, nor did I command them, nor did I speak to them. They are prophesying to you a vision of falsehood, divination, futility, and the deceit of their own heart.

**14:15** Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning the prophets who prophesy in My name, though I did not send them, yet they say, 'Sword and famine will not be in this land': By the sword and by famine those very prophets will perish.

**14:16** And the people to whom they are prophesying will be flung out into the streets of Jerusalem because of the famine and the sword, with no one to bury them—them, their wives, their sons, and their daughters. Thus I will pour out their own evil upon them.

**14:17** And you shall speak this word to them: 'Let my eyes stream with tears night and day, and let them not cease; for the virgin daughter of my people has been shattered with a great shattering, with a most grievous wound.

**14:18** If I go out into the field, look, those pierced by the sword! And if I enter the city, look, the sicknesses of famine! For both prophet and priest go wandering through the land, and they do not understand.'"

**14:19** Have You altogether rejected Judah? Does Your soul abhor Zion? Why have You struck us, with no healing for us? We hoped for peace, yet there is no good; and for a time of healing, yet look, terror!

**14:20** We acknowledge our wickedness, LORD, and the iniquity of our fathers, for we have sinned against You.

**14:21** Do not spurn us, for the sake of Your name; do not dishonor the throne of Your glory. Remember; do not break Your covenant with us.

**14:22** Are there any among the worthless idols of the nations who can bring rain? Or can the heavens give showers? Are You not the One, LORD our God? Therefore we hope in You, for it is You who have made all these things.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — Qere reads "tse'ireihem" ("their lowly ones / attendants," contrasting with "their nobles"), whereas Ketiv displays the orthographic variant "ts'wryhm."
- **14:4** — Hebrew "hattah" literally means "is shattered" or "is dismayed," used here of earth cracked by severe drought.
- **14:9** — Hebrew "nidham" is a hapax legomenon (Niphal participle of "daham") meaning "stunned," "astonished," or "taken aback."
- **14:14** — Two Ketiv/Qere morphological variants occur here: Qere reads "we'elil" ("and futility/worthlessness") and "wetarmit" ("and deceit") for Ketiv "w'lwl" and "wtrmwt."
- **14:18** — The closing clause "saharu el-erets welo yada'u" is syntactically compressed and may mean either "go wandering / ply their trade through the land and do not understand" or "wander into a land that they do not know."
