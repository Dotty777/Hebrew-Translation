## Text

**27:1** The word that the LORD spoke concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, through Jeremiah the prophet.

**27:2** Declare among the nations and proclaim, and lift up a banner; proclaim, do not conceal it! Say, "Babylon has been captured, Bel has been put to shame, Merodach has been shattered; her images have been put to shame, her idols have been shattered."

**27:3** For a nation has come up against her from the north; it will make her land a desolation, and there will be no inhabitant in it. From human to beast, they have fled, they have gone.

**27:4** In those days and at that time, declares the LORD, the sons of Israel will come, they and the sons of Judah together; walking along and weeping as they go, they will seek the LORD their God.

**27:5** They will ask the way to Zion, with their faces turned in this direction: "Come and join yourselves to the LORD in an everlasting covenant that will not be forgotten."

**27:6** My people have become lost sheep. Their shepherds have led them astray; they have turned them aside on the mountains. They have gone from mountain to hill; they have forgotten their resting place.

**27:7** All who found them devoured them, and their adversaries said, "We are not guilty, because they have sinned against the LORD, the pasture of righteousness and the hope of their fathers—the LORD."

**27:8** Flee from the midst of Babylon, and go out from the land of the Chaldeans; be like male goats in front of the flock.

**27:9** For look, I am stirring up and bringing against Babylon an assembly of great nations from the land of the north. They will draw up in battle array against her; from there she will be captured. Its arrows are like a skilled warrior who does not return empty-handed.

**27:10** Chaldea will become plunder; all her plunderers will be satisfied, declares the LORD.

**27:11** Though you are glad, though you exult, pillagers of My inheritance, though you prance like a threshing heifer and neigh like stallions,

**27:12** your mother will be deeply ashamed; she who bore you will be disgraced. Look, she will be the last of the nations, a wilderness, a dry land, and a desert.

**27:13** Because of the wrath of the LORD she will not be inhabited, but all of her will become a desolation. Everyone who passes by Babylon will be appalled and hiss at all her wounds.

**27:14** Draw up in battle array against Babylon on every side, all you who bend the bow! Shoot at her, spare no arrow, for she has sinned against the LORD.

**27:15** Raise a battle shout against her on every side! She has surrendered; her bulwarks have fallen, her walls have been torn down. For this is the vengeance of the LORD: take vengeance on her; as she has done, do to her.

**27:16** Cut off the sower from Babylon, and the one who handles the sickle at harvest time. Because of the oppressing sword, each one will turn to his people, and each one will flee to his land.

**27:17** Israel is a scattered sheep; lions have driven him away. The first one, the king of Assyria, devoured him, and this last one, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, has gnawed his bones.

**27:18** Therefore, this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I am about to punish the king of Babylon and his land, just as I punished the king of Assyria.

**27:19** And I will bring Israel back to his pasture, and he will graze on Carmel and Bashan; on the hill country of Ephraim and in Gilead his soul will be satisfied.

**27:20** In those days and at that time, declares the LORD, the iniquity of Israel will be sought, and there will be none, and the sins of Judah, and they will not be found; for I will pardon those whom I leave remaining."

**27:21** Against the land of Merathaim—go up against it, and against the inhabitants of Pekod! Kill and devote to destruction in pursuit of them, declares the LORD, and do according to all that I have commanded you.

**27:22** The sound of battle is in the land, and great shattering!

**27:23** How the hammer of the whole earth has been cut down and shattered! How Babylon has become a desolation among the nations!

**27:24** I set a snare for you and you were indeed captured, Babylon, though you did not know it; you were found and also seized, because you contended against the LORD.

**27:25** The LORD has opened His storehouse and brought out the weapons of His indignation; for the Lord GOD of hosts has a work in the land of the Chaldeans.

**27:26** Come against her from the farthest border; open up her granaries, pile her up like heaps, and devote her to destruction; let there be no remnant for her.

**27:27** Put all her bulls to the sword; let them go down to the slaughter. Woe to them, for their day has come, the time of their punishment.

**27:28** The sound of fugitives and escapees from the land of Babylon—to declare in Zion the vengeance of the LORD our God, the vengeance for His temple!

**27:29** Summon archers against Babylon, all who bend the bow! Encamp against her on every side; let there be no escape for her. Repay her according to her work; according to all that she has done, do to her, for she has acted arrogantly against the LORD, against the Holy One of Israel.

**27:30** Therefore her young men will fall in her open squares, and all her men of war will be silenced on that day, declares the LORD.

**27:31** Look, I am against you, Arrogant One, declares the Lord GOD of hosts, for your day has come, the time when I punish you.

**27:32** The arrogant one will stumble and fall, with no one to raise him up; and I will kindle a fire in his cities, and it will devour all that surrounds him.

**27:33** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "The sons of Israel and the sons of Judah are oppressed together; all who took them captive have held fast to them; they have refused to let them go.

**27:34** Their Redeemer is strong; the LORD of hosts is His name. He will vigorously plead their case, in order to give rest to the earth, but bring unrest to the inhabitants of Babylon."

**27:35** A sword against the Chaldeans, declares the LORD, and against the inhabitants of Babylon, and against her officials, and against her wise men!

**27:36** A sword against the diviners, and they will become fools! A sword against her mighty warriors, and they will be dismayed!

**27:37** A sword against his horses and against his chariots, and against all the mixed troops who are in her midst, and they will become like women! A sword against her treasuries, and they will be looted!

**27:38** A drought against her waters, and they will dry up! For it is a land of carved images, and over terrifying idols they go mad.

**27:39** Therefore desert creatures will dwell with hyenas, and ostriches will dwell in her. Never again will she be inhabited, nor will she be settled from generation to generation.

**27:40** As in God's overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighboring towns, declares the LORD, no man will dwell there, nor will any son of man reside in her.

**27:41** Look, a people is coming from the north, and a great nation and many kings are being stirred up from the farthest parts of the earth.

**27:42** They grasp bow and javelin; they are cruel and have no compassion. Their voice roars like the sea, and they ride on horses, arrayed like a man for battle against you, daughter of Babylon.

**27:43** The king of Babylon has heard the report of them, and his hands have gone limp; distress has gripped him, anguish like a woman in labor.

**27:44** Look, like a lion he will come up from the thicket of the Jordan against a perennial pasture; for in an instant I will make them run away from her, and whoever is chosen I will appoint over her. For who is like Me, and who will summon Me? And who is this shepherd who can stand before Me?

**27:45** Therefore hear the plan of the LORD that He has planned against Babylon, and His purposes that He has purposed against the land of the Chaldeans: surely they will drag them off, the little ones of the flock; surely the pasture will be appalled over them!

**27:46** At the cry, "Babylon has been seized!" the earth quakes, and an outcry is heard among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **27:5** — The verb "join yourselves" (nilwu) is a rare Niphal 2mp imperative retaining the n- prefix (parallel to "Come," Qal 2mp imperative), rather than a 1cp cohortative.
- **27:6** — Two ketiv/qere variants occur here: ketiv has singular hayah ("became") while qere reads plural hayu to match collective "My people," and ketiv has the adjective sobabim ("straying") while qere reads the Polel 3cp perfect with suffix sobebum ("they have turned them aside").
- **27:11** — Four verbs in this verse show ketiv 2fs forms addressing Babylon directly, whereas the qere reads 2mp forms ("you are glad," "you exult," "you prance," "and neigh") to agree with the plural vocative "pillagers of My inheritance" and 27:12 (MT 50:12).
- **27:21** — Merathaim (a Babylonian region) plays on the Hebrew root for "double rebellion," and Pekod (a tribe in Babylonia) plays on the Hebrew root paqad, "to punish/visit."
- **27:29** — The prepositional phrase "for her" (lah) is a qere w'lo ketiv (read in the Masoretic tradition though not written in the consonantal text); "archers" translates rabbim from the root rabab, "to shoot."
- **27:44** — Ketiv writes 'rwsm while qere vocalizes the Hiphil 1cs imperfect with suffix 'arisem ("I will make them run away"); the adverbial Hiphil form 'argi'ah means "in an instant."
