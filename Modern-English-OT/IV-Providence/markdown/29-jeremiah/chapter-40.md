## Text

**40:1** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still detained in the courtyard of the guard, saying,

**40:2** "This is what the LORD says who made it, the LORD who forms it to establish it—the LORD is His name:

**40:3** Call to Me, and I will answer you, and I will declare to you great and inaccessible things that you have not known.

**40:4** For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says concerning the houses of this city and concerning the houses of the kings of Judah that are torn down against the siege ramps and against the sword:

**40:5** They are coming to fight against the Chaldeans and to fill them with the corpses of the people whom I have struck down in My anger and in My wrath, for I have hidden My face from this city because of all their wickedness.

**40:6** Look, I will bring to it recovery and healing, and I will heal them; and I will reveal to them an abundance of peace and truth.

**40:7** And I will restore the fortunes of Judah and the fortunes of Israel, and I will rebuild them as at the first.

**40:8** And I will cleanse them from all their iniquity by which they sinned against Me, and I will forgive all their iniquities by which they sinned against Me and by which they rebelled against Me.

**40:9** And it will be to Me a name of joy, a praise, and a splendor before all the nations of the earth, who will hear of all the good that I am doing for them; and they will fear and tremble because of all the good and because of all the peace that I am providing for it."

**40:10** This is what the LORD says: "In this place of which you are saying, 'It is a waste, without human and without animal'—in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem that are desolate, without human and without inhabitant and without animal—there will once more be heard

**40:11** the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the bridegroom and the sound of the bride, the sound of those saying, 'Give thanks to the LORD of hosts, for the LORD is good, for His loyal love is forever,' as they bring a thank offering to the house of the LORD. For I will restore the fortunes of the land as at the first," says the LORD.

**40:12** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "In this place that is a waste, without human or even animal, and in all its cities, there will once more be a pasture for shepherds making flocks lie down.

**40:13** In the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the lowland, in the cities of the Negev, in the land of Benjamin, in the surroundings of Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, flocks will once more pass under the hands of the one who counts them," says the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **40:2** — The Hebrew uses three feminine singular object suffixes ("it") with no explicit antecedent in the Masoretic text, variously understood as the earth, Jerusalem, or the LORD's decree.
- **40:3** — The Qal passive participle betsurot literally means "fortified" or "walled off," used metaphorically for inaccessible things beyond human reach.
- **40:5** — The terse Hebrew connects directly to 40:4: the third-person plural suffix on the Piel infinitive construct lemal'am ("to fill them") refers back to the demolished houses of Jerusalem.
- **40:6** — The noun ateret ("abundance") is a biblical hapax legomenon, related to the root atar ("be rich, abundant").
- **40:8** — Ketiv and Qere are purely orthographic (plene versus defective) spelling variants of the same form, "for all."
- **33:16 *(additional)*** — MT uses the feminine pronoun here, applying this title directly to the city of Jerusalem.
