## Text

**50:1** Now when Jeremiah had finished speaking to all the people all the words of the LORD their God, with which the LORD their God had sent him to them, even all these words,

**50:2** Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the arrogant men spoke, saying to Jeremiah, "You are speaking falsehood! The LORD our God has not sent you to say, 'You must not enter Egypt to reside there.'

**50:3** Rather, Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us in order to give us into the hand of the Chaldeans, to put us to death and to exile us to Babylon."

**50:4** So Johanan son of Kareah, all the commanders of the forces, and all the people did not obey the voice of the LORD to stay in the land of Judah.

**50:5** Rather, Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces took all the remnant of Judah who had returned from all the nations to which they had been driven, to reside in the land of Judah—

**50:6** the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters, and every person whom Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, and Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah.

**50:7** And they entered the land of Egypt, for they did not obey the voice of the LORD; and they came as far as Tahpanhes.

**50:8** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,

**50:9** "Take large stones in your hand and hide them in the mortar in the brick pavement that is at the entrance of the house of Pharaoh in Tahpanhes, in the sight of Jewish men.

**50:10** And say to them, 'This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I am sending and will fetch Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and I will set his throne above these stones that I have hidden, and he will spread his royal canopy over them.

**50:11** And he will come and strike the land of Egypt: whoever is for death, to death; whoever is for captivity, to captivity; and whoever is for the sword, to the sword.

**50:12** And I will kindle a fire in the temples of the gods of Egypt, and he will burn them and take them captive; and he will wrap himself in the land of Egypt just as a shepherd wraps himself in his garment, and he will go out from there in peace.

**50:13** And he will shatter the pillars of Beth-shemesh, which is in the land of Egypt, and he will burn the temples of the gods of Egypt with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **50:9** — The noun melet ("mortar") is a hapax legomenon, and malben (from levenah, "brick") here denotes a brick pavement or terrace at the palace entrance.
- **50:10** — The Ketiv reads shprwrw while the Qere reads shafrirw, a hapax legomenon meaning "his royal canopy" or "pavilion." The 1cs Qal perfect tamanti ("that I have hidden") attributes Jeremiah's prophetic sign-act directly to the LORD, and the Hebrew spells the king's name Nebuchadrezzar.
- **50:11** — The Ketiv reads wb'h while the Qere reads uva (Qal perfect 3ms with vav-consecutive of bo, "and he will come").
- **50:12** — The MT shifts directly from the 1cs Hiphil wehitstati ("I will kindle," spoken by the LORD) to 3ms verbs referring to Nebuchadnezzar. The Qal verb atah literally means "wrap oneself in" or "put on," portraying Nebuchadnezzar's effortless domination of Egypt.
- **50:13** — The "pillars" (matstsevot) of Beth-shemesh ("House of the Sun") refer to the monumental obelisks of the Egyptian sun-temple city of Heliopolis.
