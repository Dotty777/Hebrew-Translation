## Text

**7:2** "Stand in the gate of the house of the LORD and proclaim this word there, and say, 'Hear the word of the LORD, all Judah, who enter through these gates to bow down to the LORD.'"

**7:3** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Make your ways and your actions good, and I will cause you to dwell in this place.

**7:4** Do not trust in words of falsehood, saying, 'The temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD are these.'

**7:5** For if you truly make your ways and your actions good, if you truly practice justice between a man and his neighbor,

**7:6** if you do not oppress the sojourner, the orphan, and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not walk after other gods to your own harm,

**7:7** then I will cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers from ancient times and forever.

**7:8** Look, you are trusting in words of falsehood to no profit.

**7:9** Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely, burn incense to Baal, and walk after other gods whom you have not known,

**7:10** and then come and stand before Me in this house over which My name is called, and say, 'We have been rescued,' in order to do all these abominations?

**7:11** Has this house, over which My name is called, become a cave of robbers in your eyes? Look, I Myself have seen it too," declares the LORD.

**7:12** "Indeed, go now to My place that was in Shiloh, where I caused My name to dwell at first, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of My people Israel.

**7:13** And now, because you have done all these deeds," declares the LORD, "and I spoke to you, rising early and speaking, yet you did not listen, and I called you, yet you did not answer,

**7:14** I will do to the house over which My name is called, in which you are trusting, and to the place that I gave to you and to your fathers, just as I did to Shiloh.

**7:15** And I will cast you away from My presence, just as I cast away all your brothers, all the offspring of Ephraim."

**7:16** "As for you, do not pray for this people, and do not lift up a cry or a prayer on their behalf, and do not plead with Me, for I will not listen to you.

**7:17** Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**7:18** The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead dough to make cakes for the queen of heaven, and they pour out drink offerings to other gods in order to provoke Me to anger.

**7:19** Is it Me whom they are provoking to anger?" declares the LORD. "Is it not themselves, to the shame of their own faces?"

**7:20** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: "Look, My anger and My wrath are being poured out on this place, on man and on beast, on the trees of the field and on the fruit of the ground; and it will burn and will not be extinguished."

**7:21** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices and eat meat.

**7:22** For I did not speak with your fathers, nor did I command them on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, concerning matters of burnt offering and sacrifice.

**7:23** Rather, this word I commanded them, saying, 'Listen to My voice, and I will be God to you, and you will be a people to Me; and walk in all the way that I command you, so that it may go well with you.'

**7:24** Yet they did not listen and did not incline their ear, but walked in counsels, in the stubbornness of their evil heart, and moved backward and not forward.

**7:25** From the day your fathers came out of the land of Egypt until this day, I have sent to you all My servants the prophets, daily, rising early and sending them.

**7:26** Yet they did not listen to Me and did not incline their ear, but stiffened their neck; they acted more wickedly than their fathers."

**7:27** "And you shall say to them, 'This is the nation that has not listened to the voice of the LORD its God and has not accepted discipline. Truth has perished and has been cut off from their mouth.'"

**7:29** "Cut off your consecrated hair and throw it away, and lift up a lament on the barren heights; for the LORD has rejected and abandoned the generation of His wrath.

**7:30** For the sons of Judah have done what is evil in My sight," declares the LORD. "They have set their detestable things in the house over which My name is called, to defile it.

**7:31** And they have built the high places of Topheth, which is in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, which I did not command, nor did it arise in My heart.

**7:32** Therefore, look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when it will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, but rather the Valley of Slaughter; and they will bury in Topheth for lack of room.

**7:33** And the corpses of this people will become food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth, and no one will frighten them away.

**7:34** And I will cause to cease from the cities of Judah and from the streets of Jerusalem the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, for the land will become a wasteland."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**7:27** *(after `7:26`)* And you will speak all these words to them, yet they will not listen to you; and you will call to them, yet they will not answer you.

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — The closing pronoun hemma is masculine plural ("these"), pointing either to the buildings and courts of the temple complex or to the repeated slogan itself.
- **7:13** — The phrase "rising early and speaking" renders the Hiphil infinitive absolute of shakam ("rise early") paired with daber ("speak"), a Hebrew idiom for persistent, earnest action (compare 7:25).
- **7:18** — The Masoretic Text vocalizes the title "queen of heaven" as melekhet rather than malkat ("queen"), resembling the word for "workmanship" and likely reflecting a deliberate vocalic alteration to avoid honoring a pagan deity.
- **7:22** — Following the Qere (read form) hotsi'i ("I brought out," Hiphil infinitive construct with first-person singular suffix); the Ketiv (written form) hotsi lacks the suffix ("bringing out").
- **7:29** — The Hebrew noun nezer denotes consecration or a crown, here referring to uncut hair worn as a sign of consecration and shaved off in mourning.
