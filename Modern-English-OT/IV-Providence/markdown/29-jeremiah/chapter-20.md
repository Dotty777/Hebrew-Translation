## Text

**20:1** Now Pashhur the son of Immer, the priest, who was chief officer in the house of the LORD, heard Jeremiah prophesying these words.

**20:2** Then Pashhur struck Jeremiah the prophet and put him in the stocks that were at the Upper Gate of Benjamin, which was in the house of the LORD.

**20:3** On the next day, when Pashhur brought Jeremiah out of the stocks, Jeremiah said to him, "The LORD has not called your name Pashhur, but rather Terror All Around.

**20:4** For this is what the LORD says: 'Look, I am making you a terror to yourself and to all your friends, and they will fall by the sword of their enemies while your eyes look on. And I will give all Judah into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will carry them into exile to Babylon and strike them down with the sword.

**20:5** And I will give all the wealth of this city, and all the produce of its labor, and all its precious things; and all the treasures of the kings of Judah I will give into the hand of their enemies, and they will plunder them and take them and bring them to Babylon.

**20:6** And you, Pashhur, and all who live in your house will go into captivity; you will go to Babylon, and there you will die and there you will be buried, you and all your friends to whom you have prophesied falsely.'"

**20:7** You enticed me, LORD, and I was enticed; You overpowered me and prevailed. I have become a laughingstock all day long; everyone mocks me.

**20:8** For whenever I speak, I cry out; I shout, "Violence and devastation!" For the word of the LORD has become scorn and ridicule to me all day long.

**20:9** If I say, "I will not remember Him and I will not speak anymore in His name," then in my heart it becomes like a burning fire, shut up in my bones; I grow weary of holding it in, and I cannot.

**20:10** For I have heard the whispering of many: "Terror all around! Denounce him! Let us denounce him!" All my close friends are watching for my stumbling: "Perhaps he will be enticed, and we can prevail against him and take our vengeance on him."

**20:11** But the LORD is with me like a fearsome warrior; therefore my persecutors will stumble and will not prevail. They will be utterly put to shame, for they have not succeeded; everlasting dishonor will not be forgotten.

**20:12** Yet, LORD of hosts, You who test the righteous, who see the inmost being and the heart, let me see Your vengeance on them, for to You I have laid open my case.

**20:13** Sing to the LORD! Praise the LORD! For He has delivered the life of the needy from the hand of evildoers.

**20:14** Cursed be the day on which I was born! Let the day when my mother gave birth to me not be blessed!

**20:15** Cursed be the man who brought the news to my father, saying, "A male child has been born to you!" making him very glad.

**20:16** Let that man be like the cities that the LORD overthrew and did not relent; let him hear an outcry in the morning and a battle shout at noontime,

**20:17** because he did not put me to death in the womb, so that my mother would have been my grave, and her womb pregnant forever.

**20:18** Why is it that I came out from the womb to see trouble and sorrow, and for my days to end in shame?

## Translator Notes

- **20:3** — "Terror All Around" translates the symbolic Hebrew name Magor-missabib, which is immediately explained in v. 4 ("making you a terror") and echoed verbatim as a taunt in v. 10.
- **20:7** — Pittitani (Piel) and va-eppat (Niphal) come from the verb patah ("entice / persuade / deceive"), echoed in the Pual yefutteh in v. 10. Chazaqtani uses Qal of chazaq with a direct object suffix to mean "overpower / prove stronger than."
- **20:12** — "Inmost being and the heart" translates Hebrew kelayot va-lev (literally "kidneys and heart"), the biblical idioms for the seat of emotions and thought. Gilliti (Piel of galah, "uncover / lay open") pairs with rivi ("my legal case").
- **20:17** — Motetani (Polel of mut, "put to death") has a masculine singular subject referring back to the messenger of vv. 15-16, and merachem literally reads "from the womb" (i.e., while in the womb).
