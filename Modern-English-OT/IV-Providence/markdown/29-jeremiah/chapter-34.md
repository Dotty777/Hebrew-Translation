## Text

**34:2** This is what the LORD said to me: "Make straps and yoke bars for yourself, and place them on your neck,

**34:3** and send them to the king of Edom, to the king of Moab, to the king of the sons of Ammon, to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon by the hand of the messengers coming to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.

**34:4** And command them for their masters, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Thus you shall say to your masters:

**34:5** I Myself made the earth, mankind, and the animals that are upon the face of the earth, by My great strength and by My outstretched arm, and I give it to the one who is right in My eyes.

**34:6** And now, I Myself have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and the wild beasts of the field as well I have given to him to serve him.

**34:8** And it will be that the nation and the kingdom that will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and that will not place its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon—with the sword, with famine, and with pestilence I will punish that nation, declares the LORD, until I have consumed them by his hand.

**34:9** As for you, do not listen to your prophets, to your diviners, to your dreams, to your fortune-tellers, or to your sorcerers, who are speaking to you, saying, "You will not serve the king of Babylon."

**34:10** For a lie is what they are prophesying to you, so as to remove you far away from your own land, and I will banish you, and you will perish.

**34:11** But the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him—I will leave it upon its own land, declares the LORD, and it will cultivate it and live in it.'"

**34:12** And to Zedekiah king of Judah I spoke according to all these words, saying, "Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, serve him and his people, and live.

**34:14** And do not listen to the words of the prophets who are telling you, 'You will not serve the king of Babylon,' for a lie is what they are prophesying to you.

**34:15** For I did not send them, declares the LORD, yet they are prophesying falsely in My name, so that I will banish you and you will perish, you and the prophets who prophesy to you."

**34:16** And to the priests and to all this people I spoke, saying, "This is what the LORD says: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you, saying, 'Look, the vessels of the house of the LORD are now about to be brought back swiftly from Babylon,' for a lie is what they are prophesying to you.

**34:18** And if they are prophets, and if the word of the LORD is with them, let them now plead with the LORD of hosts so that the vessels remaining in the house of the LORD, and in the house of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem will not go to Babylon.

**34:19** For this is what the LORD of hosts says concerning the pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the stands, and concerning the rest of the vessels remaining in this city,

**34:20** which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he exiled Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, from Jerusalem to Babylon, together with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem:

**34:22** To Babylon they will be brought, and there they will stay until the day I attend to them, declares the LORD; then I will bring them up and restore them to this place."

## Translator Notes

- **34:8** — 'until I have consumed them' translates the Qal infinitive construct of tamam with a 1st-person suffix and direct object ('my bringing an end to them'), a rare transitive use of a normally intransitive verb. The verb paqad ('punish' here with the preposition al) recurs in v. 22 with a direct object in the gracious sense 'attend to.'
- **34:9** — The Hebrew text explicitly reads 'your dreams' (chalomotheikhem) rather than 'your dreamers,' even though it appears in a list of human practitioners.
- **34:11** — Note the wordplay on the verb avad ('serve/work'): the nation that 'serves' the king of Babylon will be left on its land to 'cultivate' (literally 'work') it.
- **34:18** — The negation lebhilti ('so that ... not'), which normally governs an infinitive construct, is followed here by the finite 3rd-person plural perfect verb bo'u ('go').
- **34:20** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the written ketiv spells the king's name plene with a waw, while the read qere has the defective spelling without the waw (Jeconiah).
