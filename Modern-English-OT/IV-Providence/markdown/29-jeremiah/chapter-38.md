## Text

**38:1** "At that time," declares the LORD, "I will be God to all the clans of Israel, and they will be a people to Me."

**38:2** This is what the LORD says: "The people, survivors of the sword, found favor in the wilderness, as Israel went to find his rest."

**38:3** From afar the LORD appeared to me: "With an eternal love I have loved you; therefore I have drawn you with steadfast love.

**38:4** Again I will build you, and you will be built, virgin Israel! Again you will adorn yourself with your tambourines and go out in the dance of those who celebrate.

**38:5** Again you will plant vineyards on the mountains of Samaria; the planters will plant and enjoy the harvest.

**38:6** For there will be a day when watchmen call out in the hill country of Ephraim, 'Rise, and let us go up to Zion, to the LORD our God.'"

**38:7** For this is what the LORD says: "Shout joyfully for Jacob with gladness, and cry out for the foremost of the nations; announce it, offer praise, and say, 'Save Your people, LORD, the remnant of Israel!'

**38:8** Look, I am bringing them from the northern land, and I will gather them from the farthest reaches of the earth—among them the blind and the lame, the pregnant woman and the one in labor together; as a great assembly they will return here.

**38:9** With weeping they will come, and with pleas for mercy I will lead them; I will guide them to streams of water, on a level path where they will not stumble, for I have become a father to Israel, and Ephraim is My firstborn."

**38:10** Hear the word of the LORD, nations, and announce it in the distant coastlands; say, "He who scattered Israel will gather him and watch over him like a shepherd over his flock."

**38:11** For the LORD has ransomed Jacob and has redeemed him from the hand of one mightier than he.

**38:12** They will come and shout joyfully on the height of Zion, and they will stream to the goodness of the LORD—for grain, for new wine, and for fresh oil, and for the young of the flock and the herd; their soul will be like a well-watered garden, and they will not languish anymore.

**38:13** Then the virgin will be glad in the dance, and the young men and the old together. "I will turn their mourning into joy; I will comfort them and make them glad after their sorrow.

**38:14** I will saturate the soul of the priests with richness, and My people will be satisfied with My goodness," declares the LORD.

**38:15** This is what the LORD says: "A voice is heard in Ramah—wailing, bitter weeping. Rachel is weeping over her children; she refuses to be comforted over her children, because they are gone."

**38:16** This is what the LORD says: "Hold back your voice from weeping and your eyes from tears, for there is a reward for your labor," declares the LORD, "and they will return from the land of the enemy.

**38:17** And there is hope for your future," declares the LORD, "and children will return to their territory."

**38:18** "I have indeed heard Ephraim grieving: 'You disciplined me, and I was disciplined, like an untrained calf. Bring me back, and I will return, for You are the LORD my God.

**38:19** For after I turned, I felt remorse; and after I was instructed, I struck my thigh. I was ashamed and also humiliated, because I bore the disgrace of my youth.'

**38:20** Is Ephraim a precious son to Me? Is he a child of delight? For whenever I speak against him, I certainly remember him still. Therefore My inward parts yearn for him; I will surely show him compassion," declares the LORD.

**38:21** Set up road markers for yourself; place guideposts for yourself. Set your heart on the highway, the road you went. Return, virgin Israel; return to these cities of yours.

**38:22** How long will you waver back and forth, wayward daughter? For the LORD has created a new thing on the earth: a woman will surround a man.

**38:23** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Once again they will speak this word in the land of Judah and in its cities when I restore their fortunes: 'May the LORD bless you, dwelling of righteousness, holy mountain!'

**38:24** And Judah and all its cities will dwell in it together—farmers and those who travel about with the flock.

**38:25** For I have refreshed the weary soul, and every languishing soul I have filled."

**38:26** Upon this I woke up and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me.

**38:27** "Look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "and I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of mankind and the seed of livestock.

**38:28** And it will be that just as I kept watch over them to uproot and to tear down, to demolish, to destroy, and to bring harm, so I will keep watch over them to build and to plant," declares the LORD.

**38:29** In those days they will no longer say, "Fathers ate unripe grapes, and the teeth of children are set on edge."

**38:30** Rather, each one will die for his own iniquity; every person who eats the unripe grapes—his own teeth will be set on edge.

**38:31** "Look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "and I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah,

**38:32** not like the covenant that I made with their ancestors on the day I grasped their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt—My covenant that they themselves violated, even though I was a husband to them," declares the LORD.

**38:33** "Rather, this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel after those days," declares the LORD: "I will place My law within them, and upon their heart I will inscribe it; I will be God to them, and they will be a people to Me.

**38:34** And no longer will each one teach his neighbor, nor each one his brother, saying, 'Know the LORD,' for all of them will know Me, from the smallest of them to the greatest of them," declares the LORD, "for I will pardon their iniquity, and their sin I will no longer remember."

**38:35** This is what the LORD says: "If the heavens above can be measured and the foundations of the earth below can be searched out, then I too will reject all the offspring of Israel for everything they have done," declares the LORD.

**38:36** This is what the LORD says, who sets the sun for light by day and the statutes of the moon and stars for light by night, who agitates the sea so that its waves roar—the LORD of hosts is His name:

**38:37** "If these statutes depart from before Me," declares the LORD, "then the offspring of Israel will also cease from being a nation before Me for all time."

**38:38** "Look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "and the city will be built for the LORD from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.

**38:39** The measuring line will go out further straight ahead to the hill of Gareb, and then it will turn toward Goah.

**38:40** And the whole valley of the corpses and the ashes, and all the terraced fields as far as the Brook Kidron, to the corner of the Horse Gate toward the east, will be holy to the LORD. It will not be uprooted or demolished again forever."

## Translator Notes

- **38:21** — Ketiv/qere variant: the written ketiv reflects an archaic second-person feminine singular spelling (homographic with the first-person singular), while the read qere is the standard second-person feminine singular Qal perfect of halakh ("you went").
- **38:22** — The Hebrew literally reads "a female surrounds a man" (Polel imperfect of savav, "encircle, encompass, protect"), an enigmatic proverb often understood as a surprising reversal of roles in which a woman protects or courts a warrior.
- **38:32** — The Hebrew Qal perfect ba'alti bam literally means "even though I was a husband/master to them," contrasting with Israel's breach of the covenant; the Septuagint reads "and I disregarded them" (cited in Hebrews 8:9).
- **38:38** — The word "coming" (Qal participle of bo') is a Masoretic qere without ketiv, supplied in the reading tradition though absent from the written consonantal text, matching the formula in 38:27 and 38:31.
- **38:40** — Ketiv/qere variant: the written ketiv (hasheremot) is an obscure, otherwise unattested form, while the read qere (hash-shedemot) means "the terraced fields" (compare 2 Kings 23:4).
