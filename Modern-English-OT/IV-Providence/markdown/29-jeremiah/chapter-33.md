## Text

**33:1** At the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came from the LORD, saying,

**33:2** "This is what the LORD says: Stand in the courtyard of the house of the LORD, and speak to all the cities of Judah who come to worship at the house of the LORD all the words that I have commanded you to speak to them; do not leave out a word.

**33:3** Perhaps they will listen and turn back, each person from his evil way, and I will relent concerning the disaster that I am planning to do to them because of the evil of their deeds.

**33:4** And you shall say to them, 'This is what the LORD says: If you do not listen to Me, to walk in My law that I have placed before you,

**33:5** to listen to the words of My servants the prophets, whom I am sending to you, rising early and sending, though you have not listened,

**33:6** then I will make this house like Shiloh, and this city I will turn into a curse for all the nations of the earth.'"

**33:7** And the priests, the prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the LORD.

**33:8** Now it happened, when Jeremiah finished speaking everything that the LORD had commanded him to speak to all the people, that the priests, the prophets, and all the people seized him, saying, "You shall surely die!

**33:9** Why have you prophesied in the name of the LORD, saying, 'This house will become like Shiloh, and this city will lie in ruins, with no inhabitant'?" Then all the people assembled around Jeremiah in the house of the LORD.

**33:10** And the officials of Judah heard of these matters, and they went up from the house of the king to the house of the LORD and sat at the entrance of the New Gate of the LORD.

**33:11** Then the priests and the prophets spoke to the officials and to all the people, saying, "A judgment of death is due this man, for he has prophesied against this city, just as you have heard with your own ears."

**33:12** And Jeremiah addressed all the officials and all the people, saying, "It was the LORD who sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that you have heard.

**33:13** So now, make your ways and your deeds good, and listen to the voice of the LORD your God, and the LORD will relent concerning the disaster that He has spoken against you.

**33:14** As for me, here I am in your hand; do to me according to what is good and right in your eyes.

**33:15** Only be completely sure that if you put me to death, you are placing innocent blood upon yourselves, and upon this city, and upon its inhabitants; for in truth the LORD has sent me to you to speak all these words in your ears."

**33:16** Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and to the prophets, "No judgment of death is due this man, for in the name of the LORD our God he has spoken to us."

**33:17** And some men from among the elders of the land stood up and spoke to the entire assembly of the people, saying,

**33:18** "Micah the Morashtite was prophesying in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and he spoke to all the people of Judah, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: Zion will be plowed into a field, and Jerusalem will become heaps of ruins, and the mountain of the house will become high places of a forest.'

**33:19** Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah actually put him to death? Did he not fear the LORD and seek the favor of the LORD, and the LORD relented concerning the disaster that He had spoken against them? Yet we are doing great harm against our own lives."

**33:20** There was also a man prophesying in the name of the LORD, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim, and he prophesied against this city and against this land according to all the words of Jeremiah.

**33:21** And King Jehoiakim, along with all his mighty men and all the officials, heard his words, and the king sought to put him to death; when Uriah heard of it, he became afraid and fled, and he came to Egypt.

**33:22** So King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt, Elnathan son of Achbor and men with him into Egypt.

**33:23** And they brought Uriah out of Egypt and took him to King Jehoiakim, who struck him down with the sword and threw his corpse into the graves of the common people.

**33:24** However, the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that he would not be given into the hand of the people to put him to death.

## Translator Notes

- **33:3** — Note the threefold wordplay on the Hebrew root for evil or disaster (ra'ah/ro'a): 'evil way,' 'the disaster,' and 'the evil of their deeds.' The Niphal of nacham ('relent') is used consistently here and in vv. 13 and 19 for God withholding announced judgment.
- **33:5** — 'rising early and sending' renders two paired infinitives absolute (Hiphil hashkem and Qal shaloach), a recurring Jeremianic idiom expressing persistent, tireless action.
- **33:6** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the written ketiv has the extended feminine demonstrative form hazothah ('this'), while the read qere has the standard feminine demonstrative hazzoth ('this').
- **33:18** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the written ketiv reads 'Micaiah' (the longer theophoric spelling), whereas the read qere has the shorter form 'Micah,' followed here.
- **27:1 *(additional)*** — MT reads "Jehoiakim" here, though the ensuing oracle is set in the reign of Zedekiah.
