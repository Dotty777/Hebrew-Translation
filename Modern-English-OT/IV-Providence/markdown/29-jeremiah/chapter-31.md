## Text

**31:1** Concerning Moab. This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Woe upon Nebo, for it is devastated! Kiriathaim is put to shame, captured; the high fortress is put to shame and dismayed.

**31:2** The praise of Moab is no more; in Heshbon they have plotted disaster against her: 'Come, let us cut her off from being a nation!' You also, Madmen, will be silenced; a sword will follow behind you.

**31:3** A sound of outcry from Horonaim: devastation and great ruin!

**31:4** Moab is shattered; her little ones have made an outcry heard.

**31:5** For on the ascent of Luhith weeping goes up with weeping; for on the descent of Horonaim they have heard the anguished outcry of ruin.

**31:6** Flee, rescue your lives, and become like a shrub in the wilderness!

**31:7** For because of your trust in your works and in your treasuries, you also will be captured; and Chemosh will go out into exile, his priests and his officials together.

**31:8** A devastator will come against every city, and no city will escape; the valley will perish and the plateau will be destroyed, as the LORD has spoken.

**31:9** Give wings to Moab, for in flight she will depart; and her cities will become a desolation, with no one dwelling in them.

**31:10** Cursed is the one who does the work of the LORD with negligence, and cursed is the one who holds back his sword from blood!

**31:11** Moab has been undisturbed from his youth, and he has rested on his dregs; he has not been emptied from vessel to vessel, nor has he gone into exile. Therefore his flavor has remained in him, and his aroma has not changed.

**31:12** Therefore look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will send tilters against him, and they will tip him over; they will empty his vessels and smash their jars.

**31:13** And Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, just as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their reliance.

**31:14** How can you say, 'We are warriors, and men of valor for battle'?

**31:15** Moab is devastated, and one has gone up against her cities; and the choicest of his young men have gone down to the slaughter," declares the King—the LORD of hosts is His name.

**31:16** "The calamity of Moab is close to coming, and his disaster hurries very quickly.

**31:17** Mourn for him, all you around him, and all you who know his name; say, 'How the scepter of strength is broken, the staff of splendor!'

**31:18** Come down from glory and sit in thirst, inhabitant of daughter Dibon; for the devastator of Moab has come up against you, he has ruined your fortresses.

**31:19** Stand by the road and keep watch, inhabitant of Aroer; ask the man who flees and the woman who escapes, say, 'What has happened?'

**31:20** Moab is put to shame, for it is dismayed. Wail and cry out! Declare by the Arnon that Moab is devastated.

**31:21** Judgment has also come upon the land of the plateau: upon Holon, upon Jahzah, upon Mephaath,

**31:22** upon Dibon, upon Nebo, upon Beth-diblathaim,

**31:23** upon Kiriathaim, upon Beth-gamul, upon Beth-meon,

**31:24** upon Kerioth, upon Bozrah, and upon all the cities of the land of Moab, far and near.

**31:25** The horn of Moab has been hacked off, and his arm is broken," declares the LORD.

**31:26** "Make him drunk, for he has exalted himself against the LORD; so Moab will wallow in his vomit, and he too will become a laughingstock.

**31:27** Was not Israel a laughingstock to you? Was he found among thieves, that as often as you speak about him you shake your head?

**31:28** Abandon the cities and dwell in the cliffs, inhabitants of Moab, and be like a dove that nests along the sides of the mouth of a pit.

**31:29** We have heard of the pride of Moab—exceedingly proud is he—his loftiness, his pride, his arrogance, and the haughtiness of his heart.

**31:30** I know," declares the LORD, "his insolence, yet it is empty; his boasts have achieved nothing.

**31:31** Therefore I will wail over Moab, and for all of Moab I will cry out; for the men of Kir-heres there will be moaning.

**31:32** More than the weeping for Jazer I will weep for you, vine of Sibmah! Your tendrils crossed over the sea; they reached to the sea of Jazer. Upon your summer fruit and upon your grape harvest a devastator has fallen.

**31:33** Gladness and joy have been taken away from the orchard and from the land of Moab; I have caused wine to cease from the winepresses. No one will tread with the harvest shout; the shouting will not be a harvest shout.

**31:34** From the outcry of Heshbon as far as Elealeh, even to Jahaz they have raised their voice, from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah; for even the waters of Nimrim will become desolations.

**31:35** And I will bring to an end in Moab," declares the LORD, "the one who offers on a high place and the one who burns incense to his gods.

**31:36** Therefore My heart moans for Moab like flutes, and My heart moans like flutes for the men of Kir-heres; therefore the wealth he acquired has perished.

**31:37** For every head is shaved bald and every beard is clipped; on all hands are gashes, and on the waist is sackcloth.

**31:38** On all the rooftops of Moab and in her squares, everywhere is lamentation; for I have shattered Moab like a vessel in which there is no delight," declares the LORD.

**31:39** "How it is dismayed! Wail! How Moab has turned his back in shame! So Moab will become a laughingstock and an object of terror to all around him."

**31:40** For this is what the LORD says: "Look, like an eagle he will swoop down and spread out his wings against Moab.

**31:41** The towns are captured, and the strongholds are seized; and the heart of the warriors of Moab on that day will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

**31:42** Moab will be destroyed from being a people, because he has exalted himself against the LORD.

**31:43** Terror and pit and snare are upon you, inhabitant of Moab," declares the LORD.

**31:44** "The one who flees from the terror will fall into the pit, and the one who climbs out of the pit will be caught in the snare; for I will bring upon her, upon Moab, the year of their punishment," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **31:2** — Hebrew wordplay pairs the town Heshbon with the verb hashebu ('they have plotted') and Madmen with tiddommi ('you will be silenced').
- **31:9** — Hebrew tsits elsewhere means 'blossom' or 'plate,' but here (with cognate support and the following verb) is widely understood as 'wings'; the verb phrase natso tetse pairs an unusual infinitive absolute from natsah ('fly') with a finite verb from yatsa ('go out').
- **31:15** — The clause weareha alah ('and one has gone up against her cities') pairs the feminine plural noun 'her cities' with a masculine singular Qal perfect verb alah ('has gone up'), rendered here with an impersonal subject, contrasting with yaredu ('have gone down') in the next clause.
- **31:20** — Translates the qere masculine plural imperatives helilu uzeaqu ('Wail and cry out'), matching the plural 'Declare' that follows; the ketiv reads feminine singular imperatives addressed to the city or land.
- **31:41** — Hebrew haqqeriyyot has the definite article and may denote either 'the towns' (paralleling 'the strongholds') or the Moabite city 'Kerioth' (cf. 31:24); both feminine plural subjects take singular feminine Niphal verbs (nilkedah, nitpasah).
