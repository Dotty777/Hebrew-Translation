## Text

**39:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah, king of Judah—that was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar.

**39:2** Now at that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was confined in the courtyard of the guard that was in the house of the king of Judah,

**39:3** where Zedekiah, king of Judah, had confined him, saying, "Why do you prophesy, saying, 'This is what the LORD says: Look, I am giving this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it,

**39:4** and Zedekiah, king of Judah, will not escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, for he will certainly be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and his mouth will speak with his mouth, and his eyes will see his eyes;

**39:5** and he will lead Zedekiah to Babylon, and there he will stay until I visit him, declares the LORD. Even if you fight against the Chaldeans, you will not succeed'?"

**39:6** And Jeremiah said, "The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**39:7** 'Look, Hanamel, the son of Shallum your uncle, is coming to you, saying, "Buy for yourself my field that is in Anathoth, for the right of redemption is yours to buy it."'

**39:8** Then Hanamel, my uncle's son, came to me at the courtyard of the guard according to the word of the LORD, and he said to me, 'Please buy my field that is in Anathoth, which is in the land of Benjamin, for the right of inheritance is yours, and the redemption is yours; buy it for yourself.' Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD.

**39:9** So I bought the field that was in Anathoth from Hanamel, my uncle's son, and I weighed out the silver to him, seventeen shekels of silver.

**39:10** I wrote in the deed and sealed it, and called witnesses, and weighed out the silver on scales.

**39:11** Then I took the deed of purchase, both the sealed copy—the terms and stipulations—and the open copy.

**39:12** And I gave the deed of purchase to Baruch, the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, in the sight of Hanamel my uncle, and in the sight of the witnesses who wrote in the deed of purchase, in the sight of all the Jews who were sitting in the courtyard of the guard.

**39:13** And I commanded Baruch in their sight, saying,

**39:14** 'This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Take these deeds—this deed of purchase, the sealed one, and this open deed—and place them in a clay vessel, so that they may endure for many days.

**39:15** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Once more houses, fields, and vineyards will be bought in this land.'

**39:16** Then I prayed to the LORD after giving the deed of purchase to Baruch, the son of Neriah, saying,

**39:17** 'Ah, Lord GOD! Look, You Yourself made the heavens and the earth by Your great strength and by Your outstretched arm; not a single thing is too difficult for You.

**39:18** You show loyal love to thousands, yet repay the iniquity of fathers into the lap of their children after them—the great, mighty God; the LORD of hosts is His name—

**39:19** great in counsel and abundant in deed, whose eyes are open upon all the ways of the children of mankind, to give to each person according to his ways and according to the fruit of his deeds,

**39:20** who set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, and to this day both in Israel and among mankind, and made for Yourself a name, as it is this day.

**39:21** And You brought Your people Israel out from the land of Egypt with signs and with wonders, with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm, and with great terror.

**39:22** You gave them this land, which You swore to their fathers to give to them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**39:23** They entered and took possession of it, yet they did not listen to Your voice and did not walk in Your law; all that You commanded them to do, they did not do. Therefore You have caused all this disaster to come upon them.

**39:24** Look, the siege ramps have come up to the city to capture it, and the city has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword, the famine, and the pestilence. What You spoke has come to pass, and look, You see it.

**39:25** Yet You Yourself have said to me, Lord GOD, "Buy the field for yourself with silver and call witnesses," though the city has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans.'"

**39:26** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying,

**39:27** "Look, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh. Is anything at all too difficult for Me?

**39:28** Therefore this is what the LORD says: Look, I am giving this city into the hand of the Chaldeans and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and he will capture it.

**39:29** And the Chaldeans who are fighting against this city will enter and set this city on fire and burn it, together with the houses on whose rooftops they burned incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, so as to provoke Me to anger.

**39:30** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have been doing only evil in My sight from their youth; indeed, the sons of Israel have been doing nothing but provoking Me to anger with the work of their hands," declares the LORD.

**39:31** "For this city has been to Me a provocation of My anger and My wrath from the day they built it right up to this day, so that I will remove it from before My face,

**39:32** because of all the evil of the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah that they have done to provoke Me to anger—they, their kings, their officials, their priests, and their prophets, along with the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**39:33** They turned their back to Me and not their face; and though I taught them, rising early and teaching, yet they would not listen to receive discipline.

**39:34** Instead, they set their detestable things in the house over which My name is called, to defile it.

**39:35** And they built the high places of Baal that are in the Valley of Ben-hinnom, to cause their sons and their daughters to pass over to Molech—something I never commanded them, nor did it ever enter My heart that they should do this abomination—so as to cause Judah to sin.

**39:36** Now therefore, this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says concerning this city of which you are saying, 'It has been given into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence':

**39:37** Look, I am about to gather them from all the lands to which I drove them in My anger, in My wrath, and in great fury; and I will bring them back to this place and make them dwell in safety.

**39:38** And they will be My people, and I Myself will be their God.

**39:39** I will give them one heart and one way, so that they may fear Me all the days, for their good and for the good of their children after them.

**39:40** And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, that I will not turn back from following them to do them good; and I will put the fear of Me into their hearts, so that they will not turn aside from Me.

**39:41** And I will delight over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, with all My heart and with all My soul.

**39:42** For this is what the LORD says: Just as I have brought all this great disaster upon this people, so I am bringing upon them all the good that I am declaring concerning them.

**39:43** Fields will be bought in this land of which you are saying, 'It is a desolation, without human or animal; it has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans.'

**39:44** People will buy fields with silver, and write in deeds, and seal them, and call witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, in the surroundings of Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, in the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the lowland, and in the cities of the Negev; for I will restore their fortunes," declares the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — Ketiv reads the construct bishnat ("in the year of") before the definite ordinal, whereas Qere reads the absolute bashanah ("in the tenth year"); both convey the same date.
- **39:9** — Literally "seven shekels and ten of the silver," an additive Hebrew numeral construction meaning seventeen shekels of silver.
- **39:12** — MT reads dodi ("my uncle") here without ben ("son of"), unlike 39:7-9, where Hanamel is the son of Jeremiah's uncle; dod can also denote a kinsman more broadly.
- **39:23** — Ketiv reads the plural ("Your laws"), whereas Qere reads the singular ("Your law"); the Hiphil wayyiqtol wattaqre ("You caused to befall") is spelled with final aleph from the root qara II (equivalent to qarah, "happen, befall").
- **39:35** — The Hiphil infinitive construct leha'avir literally means "to cause to pass over" (without "through the fire" in the Hebrew text here), an idiom for child sacrifice to Molech.
