## Text

**22:1** This is what the LORD says: "Go down to the house of the king of Judah and speak this word there,

**22:2** and say, 'Hear the word of the LORD, king of Judah who sits on the throne of David—you, your servants, and your people who enter by these gates.

**22:3** This is what the LORD says: Practice justice and righteousness, and rescue the one who has been robbed from the hand of the oppressor. Do not wrong or deal violently with the sojourner, the orphan, and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place.

**22:4** For if you indeed do this thing, then kings who sit for David on his throne will enter through the gates of this house, riding in chariots and on horses—he, his servants, and his people.

**22:5** But if you do not heed these words, I swear by Myself, declares the LORD, that this house will become a ruin.'"

**22:6** For this is what the LORD says concerning the house of the king of Judah: "You are Gilead to Me, the summit of Lebanon; yet I will surely make you a wilderness, cities not inhabited.

**22:7** And I will consecrate destroyers against you, each with his weapons; and they will cut down your finest cedars and cast them onto the fire.

**22:8** And many nations will pass by this city, and each one will say to his neighbor, 'Why has the LORD done so to this great city?'

**22:9** And they will say, 'Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD their God and bowed down to other gods and served them.'"

**22:10** Do not weep for the dead, and do not grieve for him; weep deeply for the one who is going away, for he will not return again and see the land of his birth.

**22:11** For this is what the LORD says concerning Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who reigned in place of Josiah his father, who has gone out from this place: "He will not return there again;

**22:12** but in the place where they exiled him, there he will die, and this land he will not see again."

**22:13** "Woe to the one who builds his house without righteousness and his upper chambers without justice, who makes his neighbor work for nothing and does not give him his wages,

**22:14** who says, 'I will build for myself a large house and airy upper chambers,' and cuts open windows for himself, paneled with cedar and coated with vermilion.

**22:15** Do you reign because you vie in cedar? Your father—did he not eat and drink, and practice justice and righteousness? Then it went well with him.

**22:16** He judged the case of the afflicted and needy; then it went well. Is that not knowing Me? declares the LORD.

**22:17** Yet your eyes and your heart are on nothing but your unjust gain, and on shedding the blood of the innocent, and on practicing oppression and violence."

**22:18** Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: "They will not lament for him, 'Alas, my brother!' or 'Alas, sister!' They will not lament for him, 'Alas, lord!' or 'Alas, his majesty!'

**22:19** With the burial of a donkey he will be buried—dragged away and thrown beyond the gates of Jerusalem."

**22:20** "Go up to Lebanon and cry out, and in Bashan lift up your voice, and cry out from Abarim, for all your lovers have been shattered.

**22:21** I spoke to you in your security; you said, 'I will not listen.' This has been your way since your youth, for you have not listened to My voice.

**22:22** The wind will shepherd all your shepherds, and your lovers will go into captivity; for then you will be ashamed and disgraced because of all your wickedness.

**22:23** You who inhabit Lebanon, who are nested in the cedars, how pitiable you will be when labor pains come upon you, anguish like a woman giving birth!"

**22:24** "As I live, declares the LORD, even if Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were a signet ring on My right hand, yet from there I would tear you off.

**22:25** And I will give you into the hand of those who seek your life, and into the hand of those whom you dread, and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of the Chaldeans.

**22:26** And I will hurl you and your mother who gave birth to you into another land where you were not born, and there you will die.

**22:27** But to the land to which they lift up their soul to return, there they will not return."

**22:28** Is this man Coniah a despised, shattered pot, or a vessel in which there is no delight? Why have he and his offspring been hurled away and thrown into a land that they did not know?

**22:29** Land, land, land, hear the word of the LORD!

**22:30** This is what the LORD says: "Register this man as childless, a man who will not succeed in his days; for no man from his offspring will succeed, sitting on the throne of David and ruling again in Judah."

## Translator Notes

- **22:4** — The Hebrew shifts from plural kings to a distributive singular, he, his servants, and his people, viewing each succeeding Davidic king individually.
- **22:6** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv reads a third-person feminine singular Niphal perfect treating cities collectively, whereas the read qere translated here reads the third-person plural Niphal perfect agreeing directly with plural cities.
- **22:14** — The unusual ending on hallonay, windows, likely reflects an archaic plural or dual form; paneled translates a Qal passive participle and coated translates a Qal infinitive absolute continuing the preceding finite verb.
- **22:23** — Ketiv/Qere: on both inhabit, a Qal participle, and nested, a Pual participle, the written ketiv preserves an archaic final i ending that the read qere omits. How pitiable you will be translates the Masoretic Niphal of hanan, to be pitied, though ancient versions read how you will groan from anah.
- **22:24** — The Hebrew shifts abruptly from third-person reference to Coniah to direct second-person singular address in tear you off, continuing into verses 25 and 26.
