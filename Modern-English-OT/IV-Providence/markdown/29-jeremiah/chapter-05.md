## Text

**5:1** Go back and forth through the streets of Jerusalem, and look now and know, and search in her plazas; if you find a man, if there is one who practices justice, who seeks faithfulness, then I will forgive her.

**5:2** Even though they say, "As the LORD lives," still they swear falsely.

**5:3** LORD, are Your eyes not on faithfulness? You struck them, yet they felt no pain; You consumed them, yet they refused to accept discipline. They hardened their faces more than rock; they refused to turn back.

**5:4** Then I said, "These are only the poor; they have acted foolishly, for they do not know the way of the LORD, the justice of their God.

**5:5** I will go to the great ones and speak with them, for they know the way of the LORD, the justice of their God." Yet they all together have broken the yoke; they have snapped the bonds.

**5:6** Therefore a lion from the forest will strike them, a wolf of the desert plains will devastate them, a leopard is keeping watch over their cities; everyone who goes out from them will be torn to pieces, because their transgressions are many, their apostasies are numerous.

**5:7** "How can I forgive you for this? Your sons have forsaken Me and sworn by those who are not gods. When I fed them to the full, they committed adultery, and they thronged to a prostitute's house.

**5:8** They have become well-fed, lusty stallions; each one neighs after his neighbor's wife.

**5:9** Shall I not punish for these things?" declares the LORD. "And on a nation such as this, shall My soul not take vengeance?

**5:10** Go up through her vine rows and destroy, yet do not make a complete end; strip away her tendrils, for they do not belong to the LORD.

**5:11** For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have dealt utterly treacherously with Me," declares the LORD.

**5:12** They have denied the LORD and said, "It is not He; no disaster will come upon us, nor will we see sword or famine.

**5:13** And the prophets will become wind, and the word is not in them; thus it shall be done to them."

**5:14** Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of hosts, says: "Because you have spoken this word, look, I am making My words in your mouth fire, and this people wood, and it will consume them.

**5:15** Look, I am bringing a nation upon you from afar, house of Israel," declares the LORD. "It is an enduring nation, it is an ancient nation, a nation whose language you do not know, nor can you understand what it says.

**5:16** Its quiver is like an open grave; all of them are warriors.

**5:17** It will devour your harvest and your bread; they will devour your sons and your daughters; it will devour your flocks and your herds; it will devour your vines and your fig trees; it will demolish with the sword your fortified cities in which you trust.

**5:18** Yet even in those days," declares the LORD, "I will not make a complete end of you.

**5:19** And it will happen, when you say, 'Why has the LORD our God done all these things to us?' then you shall say to them, 'Just as you have forsaken Me and served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve strangers in a land that is not yours.'"

**5:20** Declare this in the house of Jacob, and proclaim it in Judah, saying:

**5:21** "Hear this now, foolish people without understanding: they have eyes, yet they do not see; they have ears, yet they do not hear.

**5:22** Do you not fear Me?" declares the LORD. "Will you not tremble in My presence, I who set the sand as a boundary for the sea, a perpetual decree that it cannot cross? Even when its waves surge, they cannot prevail; even when they roar, they cannot cross over it.

**5:23** Yet this people has an obstinate and rebellious heart; they have turned aside and walked away.

**5:24** They do not say in their heart, 'Let us now fear the LORD our God, who gives rain, early rain and late rain in its season, who preserves for us the fixed weeks of the harvest.'

**5:25** Your iniquities have turned these things away, and your sins have held back what is good from you.

**5:26** For wicked men are found among My people; they lie in wait as fowlers crouch; they set a trap; it is men they catch.

**5:27** Just as a cage is full of birds, so their houses are full of deceit; therefore they have grown great and become wealthy.

**5:28** They have grown fat, they have become sleek; they also go beyond all bounds in evil deeds. They do not judge the cause, the cause of the orphan, so that they may prosper, and they do not uphold the right of the needy.

**5:29** Shall I not punish for these things?" declares the LORD. "On a nation such as this, shall My soul not take vengeance?

**5:30** A shocking and ghastly thing has taken place in the land:

**5:31** the prophets prophesy with falsehood, and the priests govern at their direction, and My people love it that way. Yet what will you do at its end?"

## Translator Notes

- **5:7** — The Qere reads 'eslach (standard Qal imperfect of salach, "forgive"), while the Ketiv spells it 'slwch. The Hithpolel verb yitgodadu (from gadad II, "band together") means "throng in troops."
- **5:8** — The Pual participle meyuzzanim ("well-fed," from zun) and the hapax legomenon mashkim ("lusty" or "roaming") are both rare terms describing the horses' unrestrained appetite.
- **5:17** — The second verb (yo'kelu, "they will devour") shifts to the plural, referring to the enemy troops; alternatively, the clause may be read as an asyndetic relative modifying "harvest and bread" ("which your sons and daughters were to eat").
- **5:24** — The Qere reads yoreh ("early rain") without the conjunction, whereas the Ketiv has wyrh ("and early rain").
- **5:26** — The phrase yashur keshak yequshim literally reads "he watches like the crouching of fowlers," using the Qal infinitive construct shak (from shakak, "stoop/crouch") with a distributive singular verb.
