## Text

**37:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying:

**37:2** "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken, saying: Write for yourself in a scroll all the words that I have spoken to you.

**37:3** For look, days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel and Judah, says the LORD, and I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it."

**37:4** Now these are the words that the LORD spoke concerning Israel and concerning Judah:

**37:5** For this is what the LORD says: "We have heard a sound of trembling: dread, and there is no peace.

**37:6** Ask now and see whether a male gives birth. Why have I seen every strong man with his hands on his loins like a woman giving birth, and all faces turned to paleness?

**37:7** Alas! For great is that day, with none like it; and it is a time of trouble for Jacob, yet from it he will be saved.

**37:8** And it will happen in that day, declares the LORD of hosts, that I will break his yoke from off your neck and snap your bonds, and foreigners will no longer enslave him;

**37:9** instead they will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them."

**37:12** For this is what the LORD says: "Incurable is your fracture, and severe is your wound.

**37:13** There is no one to defend your case; for the sore, there are no healing remedies for you.

**37:14** All your lovers have forgotten you; they do not seek after you. For I have struck you with the blow of an enemy, with the discipline of a cruel one, because of the abundance of your iniquity, for your sins have grown numerous.

**37:16** Therefore all who devour you will be devoured, and all your foes, all of them, will go into captivity; those who despoil you will become spoil, and all who plunder you I will give over as plunder.

**37:17** For I will bring healing to you, and of your wounds I will heal you, declares the LORD, because they have called you an outcast: 'She is Zion; there is no one who seeks after her.'"

**37:18** This is what the LORD says: "Look, I am about to restore the fortunes of the tents of Jacob, and I will have compassion on his dwelling places; the city will be rebuilt on its mound, and the citadel will stand on its proper site.

**37:19** And from them will go forth thanksgiving and the sound of those rejoicing; I will multiply them, and they will not decrease; and I will honor them, and they will not be lowly.

**37:20** And his sons will be as in former days, and his assembly will be established before Me, and I will punish all his oppressors.

**37:21** And his noble one will be from himself, and his ruler will come forth from his midst; I will bring him near, and he will approach Me, for who is this who would pledge his heart to approach Me? declares the LORD."

**37:23** Look, the tempest of the LORD! Fury has gone forth, a sweeping tempest; upon the head of the wicked it will whirl down.

**37:24** The burning anger of the LORD will not turn back until He has done and until He has established the purposes of His heart; in the latter days you will understand it.

## Translator Notes

- **37:8** — Hebrew shifts abruptly from third person ('his yoke') to second person ('your neck... your bonds') and back to third person ('enslave him'), common in prophetic poetry; the idiom avad be- ('impose service on / enslave') contrasts with avad et- ('serve') in 37:9.
- **37:13** — The Masoretic pause (athnach) stands after lemazor ('for the sore,' traditionally linked to the first clause as 'for binding up'), though mazor ('sore/ulcer') pairs naturally with the medical phrase refuot te'alah ('remedies of healing').
- **37:21** — Hebrew arav et-libbo literally means 'pledged his heart' (Qal of arav, 'stand surety / give as a pledge'), an idiom for risking one's life or daring on one's own authority.
- **37:23** — Mitgorer is a Hithpolel participle (from garar, 'sweep/drag away,' hence 'a sweeping tempest'), contrasting with mitkholel ('whirling') in the parallel oracle at Jeremiah 23:19.
