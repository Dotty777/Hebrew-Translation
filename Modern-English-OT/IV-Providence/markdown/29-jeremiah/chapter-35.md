## Text

**35:1** Now it happened in that same year, at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month, that Hananiah son of Azzur, the prophet who was from Gibeon, spoke to me in the house of the LORD in the sight of the priests and all the people, saying,

**35:2** "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, has spoken, saying: 'I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.

**35:3** Within two full years I am bringing back to this place all the vessels of the house of the LORD, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took from this place and brought to Babylon.

**35:4** And Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, together with all the exiles of Judah who went to Babylon, I am bringing back to this place,' declares the LORD, 'for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.'"

**35:5** Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Hananiah the prophet in the sight of the priests and in the sight of all the people who were standing in the house of the LORD.

**35:6** And Jeremiah the prophet said, "Amen! So may the LORD do! May the LORD establish your words that you have prophesied, to bring back the vessels of the house of the LORD and all the exiles from Babylon to this place.

**35:7** Only listen now to this word that I am speaking in your ears and in the ears of all the people:

**35:8** The prophets who were before me and before you from long ago prophesied against many lands and against great kingdoms, concerning war, disaster, and pestilence.

**35:9** As for the prophet who prophesies concerning peace, when the word of the prophet comes about, that prophet will be known as one whom the LORD has sent in truth."

**35:10** Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke bar off the neck of Jeremiah the prophet and broke it.

**35:11** And Hananiah spoke in the sight of all the people, saying, "This is what the LORD says: Like this I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, within two full years, off the neck of all the nations." And Jeremiah the prophet went on his way.

**35:12** And the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, after Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke bar off the neck of Jeremiah the prophet, saying,

**35:13** "Go and tell Hananiah, saying, 'This is what the LORD says: Yoke bars of wood you have broken, and in their place you have made yoke bars of iron.

**35:14** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: A yoke of iron I have placed upon the neck of all these nations to serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him; and even the wild beasts of the field I have given to him.'"

**35:15** Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Hananiah the prophet: "Hear now, Hananiah! The LORD has not sent you, yet you have caused this people to trust in a lie.

**35:16** Therefore, this is what the LORD says: Look, I am sending you off from the face of the earth; this year you will die, because you have spoken rebellion against the LORD."

**35:17** And Hananiah the prophet died in that year, in the seventh month.

## Translator Notes

- **35:1** — Ketiv/Qere variant: the written ketiv has the construct form bishnath ('in the year of'), while the read qere has the definite absolute form bashanah ('in the [fourth] year'), agreeing with the definite adjective 'the fourth.'
- **35:3** — 'two full years' translates the Hebrew idiom shenatayim yamim (literally 'two years of days'; also in v. 11).
- **35:8** — The Hebrew text reads ra'ah ('disaster/calamity') rather than ra'abh ('famine'), which appears in the similar triad in 34:8 (MT 27:8).
- **35:13** — The Hebrew verb is 2nd-person masculine singular with penultimate stress (we'asitha, 'and you have made'), attributing the resulting iron yoke bars to Hananiah's own act of breaking the wooden bars, in contrast to God's 1st-person declaration in v. 14.
- **35:16** — 'sending you off' (Piel participle of shalach) creates an ironic wordplay with v. 15, 'The LORD has not sent you' (Qal perfect of the same root).
