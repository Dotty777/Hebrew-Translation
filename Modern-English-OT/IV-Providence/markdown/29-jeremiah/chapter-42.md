## Text

**42:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

**42:2** "Go to the house of the Rechabites and speak with them, and bring them into the house of the LORD, into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink."

**42:3** So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah, and his brothers and all his sons, and the whole house of the Rechabites.

**42:4** And I brought them into the house of the LORD, into the chamber of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God, which was beside the chamber of the officials, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold.

**42:5** Then I set before the sons of the house of the Rechabites pitchers full of wine and cups, and I said to them, "Drink wine."

**42:6** But they said, "We will not drink wine, for Jonadab son of Rechab, our father, commanded us, saying, 'You shall not drink wine, you or your sons, forever.

**42:7** You shall not build a house, nor sow seed, nor plant a vineyard, nor shall you have any; rather, you shall dwell in tents all your days, so that you may live many days on the face of the land where you sojourn.'

**42:8** And we have obeyed the voice of Jehonadab son of Rechab, our father, in all that he commanded us, not to drink wine all our days—we, our wives, our sons, and our daughters—

**42:9** and not to build houses for us to dwell in; nor do we have a vineyard or field or seed.

**42:10** We have dwelt in tents, and we have obeyed and done according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.

**42:11** But when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against the land, we said, 'Come, and let us go into Jerusalem away from the army of the Chaldeans and away from the army of Aram.' So we have dwelt in Jerusalem."

**42:12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying:

**42:13** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Go and say to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, 'Will you not receive instruction to listen to My words?' declares the LORD.

**42:14** 'The words of Jehonadab son of Rechab, which he commanded his sons not to drink wine, have been upheld; and they have not drunk to this day, for they obeyed the command of their father. Yet I Myself have spoken to you, rising early and speaking, and you have not listened to Me.

**42:15** And I sent to you all My servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, "Turn back now, each man from his evil way, and make your deeds good, and do not go after other gods to serve them; then you will dwell in the land that I gave to you and to your fathers." Yet you did not incline your ear, nor did you listen to Me.

**42:16** Indeed, the sons of Jehonadab son of Rechab have upheld the command of their father that he commanded them, but this people has not listened to Me.'"

**42:17** Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I am bringing upon Judah and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the disaster that I have spoken against them, because I spoke to them and they did not listen, and I called to them and they did not answer."

**42:18** Then Jeremiah said to the house of the Rechabites, "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Because you have obeyed the command of Jehonadab your father, and kept all his commands, and done according to all that he commanded you,

**42:19** therefore this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: A man to stand before Me shall not be cut off from Jonadab son of Rechab all the days.'"

## Translator Notes

- **42:6** — The Hebrew text alternates between the shorter spelling Jonadab (42:6, 10, 19) and the longer spelling Jehonadab (42:8, 14, 16, 18) for the same Rechabite ancestor.
- **42:14** — The Hophal singular verb huqam ("have been upheld") governs a plural logical subject introduced by the object marker et, an impersonal passive construction in Biblical Hebrew. The paired infinitives absolute hashkem wedabber ("rising early and speaking," and similarly hashkem weshaloaḥ in 42:15) are a characteristic Jeremianic idiom for earnest, persistent action.
- **42:15** — Hebrew contrasts two similar-sounding Qal imperatives: shuvu ("turn back," from shuv) at the start of the prophetic message and ushevu ("then you will dwell," from yashav), an imperative expressing the promised consequence of repentance.
- **42:17** — Unlike 42:13, 18, and 19 ("the LORD of hosts"), the Hebrew here reads the fuller title "the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel."
