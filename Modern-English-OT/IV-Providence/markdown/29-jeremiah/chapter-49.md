## Text

**49:1** Then all the commanders of the forces, Johanan son of Kareah, Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and all the people from the least to the greatest drew near.

**49:2** And they said to Jeremiah the prophet, "Please let our petition fall before you, and pray on our behalf to the LORD your God, on behalf of all this remnant; for we remain only a few out of many, just as your eyes see us.

**49:3** And may the LORD your God tell us the way in which we should walk and the thing that we should do."

**49:4** Then Jeremiah the prophet said to them, "I have heard. Look, I am going to pray to the LORD your God according to your words, and every word that the LORD answers you I will tell you; I will not withhold a word from you."

**49:5** And they said to Jeremiah, "May the LORD be a true and faithful witness against us if we do not act according to every word with which the LORD your God sends you to us.

**49:6** Whether good or bad, we will obey the voice of the LORD our God, to whom we are sending you, so that it may go well with us when we obey the voice of the LORD our God."

**49:7** Now at the end of ten days, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah.

**49:8** Then he summoned Johanan son of Kareah, all the commanders of the forces who were with him, and all the people from the least to the greatest.

**49:9** And he said to them, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says, to whom you sent me to let your petition fall before Him:

**49:10** 'If you will indeed stay in this land, then I will build you up and not tear down, and I will plant you and not uproot; for I have relented concerning the disaster that I have done to you.

**49:11** Do not be afraid of the king of Babylon, of whom you are afraid; do not be afraid of him,' declares the LORD, 'for I am with you to save you and to rescue you from his hand.

**49:12** And I will grant you compassion, so that he will have compassion on you and bring you back to your own soil.'

**49:13** But if you say, 'We will not stay in this land,' so as not to obey the voice of the LORD your God,

**49:14** saying, 'No, rather we will enter the land of Egypt, where we will not see war, nor hear the sound of a trumpet, nor go hungry for bread, and there we will stay,'

**49:15** then now therefore hear the word of the LORD, remnant of Judah. This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'If you indeed set your faces to enter Egypt and go to reside there,

**49:16** then the sword of which you are afraid will overtake you there in the land of Egypt, and the famine about which you are anxious will cling closely after you there in Egypt, and there you will die.

**49:17** So it will be with all the men who set their faces to enter Egypt to reside there: they will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence; and they will have no survivor or escapee from the disaster that I am bringing upon them.'

**49:18** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Just as My anger and My wrath were poured out upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so My wrath will be poured out upon you when you enter Egypt. And you will become an execration, a horror, a curse, and a reproach, and you will never see this place again.'

**49:19** The LORD has spoken concerning you, remnant of Judah: 'Do not enter Egypt!' Know for certain that I have solemnly warned you today.

**49:20** For you have practiced deceit at the cost of your own lives, since you yourselves sent me to the LORD your God, saying, 'Pray on our behalf to the LORD our God, and according to everything that the LORD our God says, so tell us and we will do it.'

**49:21** And I have told you today, yet you have not obeyed the voice of the LORD your God or anything for which He has sent me to you.

**49:22** Now therefore know for certain that you will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence in the place where you desire to go to reside."

## Translator Notes

- **49:6** — The Ketiv reads the unique biblical form anw while the Qere standardizes it to classical anahnu, both meaning "we."
- **49:10** — The construction shov teshvu pairs an abbreviated infinitive absolute (resembling shuv, "return," but serving here for yashav, "stay") with the Qal imperfect of yashav for emphasis ("if you will indeed stay").
- **49:20** — The Ketiv reads ht'tym while the Qere reads hit'etem (Hiphil perfect 2mp of ta'ah, "lead astray / practice deceit"), combined with benafshoteikhem ("at the cost of your own lives," using the preposition be of price).
