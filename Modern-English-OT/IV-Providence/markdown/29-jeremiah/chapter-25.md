## Text

**25:1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah—that was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon—

**25:2** which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying:

**25:3** "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, even to this day—these twenty-three years—the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking, yet you have not listened.

**25:4** And the LORD sent to you all His servants the prophets, rising early and sending, yet you have not listened, nor have you inclined your ear to hear,

**25:5** saying, 'Turn back now, each one from his evil way and from the evil of your deeds, and dwell on the soil that the LORD gave to you and to your fathers from everlasting to everlasting.

**25:6** And do not walk after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands; then I will do no harm to you.'"

**25:7** "Yet you have not listened to Me," declares the LORD, "so as to provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands, to your own harm."

**25:8** Therefore this is what the LORD of hosts says: "Because you have not listened to My words,

**25:9** look, I am sending and will take all the clans of the north," declares the LORD, "and will send to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and bring them against this land, against its inhabitants, and against all these nations all around; I will devote them to destruction and make them a desolation, an object of hissing, and everlasting ruins.

**25:10** And I will eliminate from them the sound of rejoicing and the sound of gladness, the voice of a bridegroom and the voice of a bride, the sound of millstones and the light of a lamp.

**25:11** This whole land will become a ruin and a desolation, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.

**25:12** Then it will happen, when seventy years are fulfilled, that I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation," declares the LORD, "for their iniquity, along with the land of the Chaldeans, and I will make it everlasting desolations.

**25:13** And I will bring upon that land all My words that I have spoken against it—everything written in this scroll, which Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations."

**25:14** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam, at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, saying:

**25:15** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Look, I am about to break the bow of Elam, the foremost of their might.

**25:16** And I will bring upon Elam four winds from the four ends of the heavens, and I will scatter them toward all these winds; and there will be no nation where the outcasts of Elam do not come.

**25:17** I will terrify Elam before their enemies and before those who seek their life; and I will bring disaster upon them, My fierce anger," declares the LORD, "and I will send the sword after them until I have consumed them.

**25:18** I will set My throne in Elam, and I will destroy king and officials from there," declares the LORD.

**25:19** "Yet it will happen in the latter days that I will restore the fortunes of Elam," declares the LORD.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**25:14** *(after `25:13`)* For many nations and great kings will enslave them as well, and I will repay them according to their deeds and according to the work of their hands.

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — Hebrew ashkem is a variant spelling with initial aleph of the Hiphil infinitive absolute hashkem (as in v. 4); the idiom "rising early and..." expresses persistent, urgent action.
- **25:7** — Translates the qere hakhiseni (Hiphil infinitive construct with 1st-person suffix, "to provoke Me to anger"); the ketiv has a plural-like variant spelling.
- **25:13** — Translates the qere veheveti (Hiphil weqatal 1st-person singular of bo, "and I will bring"); the ketiv preserves an older variant spelling of the same form.
- **25:14** — In native Masoretic order, the oracle concerning Elam stands at Jeremiah 49:34–39 within the oracles against the nations (chapters 46–51); in the Saints.Bible parallel display, it is mapped to 25:14–19 immediately after 25:13.
- **25:16** — Translates the qere Elam ("Elam"); the ketiv reads olam ("forever/eternity"), an apparent scribal error for the nation's name.
- **25:19** — Translates both qere readings: ashiv (Hiphil of shuv, "I will restore") and shevut ("fortunes/restoration"); the ketiv reads Qal ashuv and shevit ("captivity").
