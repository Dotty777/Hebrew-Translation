## Text

**13:1** This is what the LORD said to me: "Go and buy yourself a linen waistcloth and put it around your waist, but do not put it into water."

**13:2** So I bought the waistcloth according to the word of the LORD, and I put it around my waist.

**13:3** Then the word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying,

**13:4** "Take the waistcloth that you bought, which is around your waist, and arise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a crevice of the rock."

**13:5** So I went and hid it by the Euphrates, just as the LORD had commanded me.

**13:6** And at the end of many days, the LORD said to me, "Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the waistcloth that I commanded you to hide there."

**13:7** So I went to the Euphrates and dug, and took the waistcloth from the place where I had hidden it; and look, the waistcloth was ruined; it was fit for nothing.

**13:8** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying:

**13:9** "This is what the LORD says: In this very way I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.

**13:10** This evil people, who refuse to listen to My words, who walk in the stubbornness of their heart, and have gone after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them, shall become just like this waistcloth, which is fit for nothing.

**13:11** For just as the waistcloth clings to a man's waist, so I caused the entire house of Israel and the entire house of Judah to cling to Me, declares the LORD, to be for Me a people, a name, a praise, and a splendor; yet they did not listen.

**13:12** And you shall speak this word to them: 'This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Every jar will be filled with wine.' And they will say to you, 'Do we not surely know that every jar will be filled with wine?'

**13:13** Then you shall say to them, 'This is what the LORD says: Look, I am about to fill all the inhabitants of this land—even the kings who sit for David on his throne, the priests, the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem—with drunkenness.

**13:14** And I will smash them one against another, both the fathers and the sons together, declares the LORD. I will not pity, nor spare, nor show compassion so as to keep from ruining them.'"

**13:15** Listen and give ear; do not be arrogant, for the LORD has spoken.

**13:16** Give glory to the LORD your God before He brings darkness, and before your feet stumble on the twilight mountains; while you wait for light, He turns it into deep shadow and makes it into thick gloom.

**13:17** But if you do not listen to it, my soul will weep in secret places because of pride; my eye will weep bitterly and stream with tears, because the flock of the LORD has been taken captive.

**13:18** Say to the king and to the queen mother, "Take a lowly seat, for your crown of splendor has come down from your heads."

**13:19** The cities of the Negev are shut up, with no one to open them; all of Judah has been carried into exile, carried wholly into exile.

**13:20** Lift up your eyes and see those who are coming from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you, the sheep of your splendor?

**13:21** What will you say when He appoints over you—since you yourself have trained them over you—close allies as head? Will not pangs seize you like a woman in labor?

**13:22** And if you say in your heart, "Why have these things happened to me?" It is because of the abundance of your iniquity that your skirts have been uncovered and your heels have suffered violence.

**13:23** Can a Cushite change his skin, or a leopard his spots? Then you as well could do good, you who are trained to do evil.

**13:24** So I will scatter them like drifting chaff before a wilderness wind.

**13:25** This is your lot, the portion measured out for you from Me, declares the LORD, because you have forgotten Me and have trusted in falsehood.

**13:26** So I Myself will strip back your skirts over your face, and your disgrace will be seen.

**13:27** Your adulteries and your neighings, the lewdness of your prostitution—on the hills in the field I have seen your detestable acts! Woe to you, Jerusalem! You will not become clean; how much longer still?

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — Hebrew "Perat" normally denotes the Euphrates River, though some scholars identify it here with Parah, a town northeast of Anathoth (Josh 18:23), as a wordplay on the Euphrates.
- **13:16** — Qere reads "weshit" ("and makes it," waw plus an infinitive form continuing the preceding verb), whereas Ketiv reads "yashit" ("He makes it," an imperfect without "and").
- **13:20** — Qere reads masculine plural imperatives ("se'u", "ure'u") agreeing with plural "your eyes," whereas Ketiv reads feminine singular imperatives ("se'i", "ure'i") anticipating the feminine singular address to Jerusalem in the second half of the verse.
- **13:21** — Hebrew "allufim" plays on the double meaning "close companions/allies" and "chiefs/rulers," while the middle clause functions as a parenthetical aside highlighting Judah's own culpability.
- **13:27** — "How much longer still?" renders the compressed Hebrew idiom "aharei matay od," literally "after when yet?"
