## Text

**36:1** Now these are the words of the letter that Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem to the remnant of the elders of the exiles, and to the priests, and to the prophets, and to all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had exiled from Jerusalem to Babylon,

**36:2** after Jeconiah the king and the queen mother, the court officials, the princes of Judah and Jerusalem, and the craftsmen and the smiths had gone out from Jerusalem,

**36:3** by the hand of Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, to Babylon, saying:

**36:4** "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says to all the exiles whom I have exiled from Jerusalem to Babylon:

**36:5** Build houses and dwell in them, and plant gardens and eat their fruit.

**36:6** Take wives and father sons and daughters; take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, that they may bear sons and daughters; and multiply there, and do not decrease.

**36:7** And seek the peace of the city to which I have exiled you, and pray to the LORD on its behalf, for in its peace you will have peace.

**36:8** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Do not let your prophets who are among you and your diviners deceive you, and do not listen to your dreams that you cause to be dreamed;

**36:9** for they are prophesying falsely to you in My name; I did not send them, declares the LORD.

**36:10** For this is what the LORD says: When seventy years have been completed for Babylon, I will attend to you, and I will establish My good word toward you, to bring you back to this place.

**36:11** For I Myself know the plans that I am planning concerning you, declares the LORD, plans of peace and not for disaster, to give you a future and hope.

**36:12** And you will call on Me, and go and pray to Me, and I will listen to you.

**36:13** And you will search for Me and find Me, when you seek Me with your whole heart.

**36:14** And I will be found by you, declares the LORD, and I will restore your fortunes and gather you from all the nations and from all the places to which I have driven you, declares the LORD, and I will bring you back to the place from which I exiled you.

**36:15** Because you have said, 'The LORD has raised up prophets for us in Babylon,'

**36:21** this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says concerning Ahab son of Kolaiah and concerning Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who are prophesying falsehood to you in My name: Look, I am giving them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will strike them down before your eyes.

**36:22** And from them a curse will be taken up by all the exiles of Judah who are in Babylon, saying, 'May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire,'

**36:23** because they have committed disgraceful folly in Israel, and have committed adultery with the wives of their neighbors, and have spoken a word falsely in My name that I did not command them; and I am the one who knows and am a witness, declares the LORD."

**36:24** And to Shemaiah the Nehelamite you shall speak, saying:

**36:25** "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, has spoken, saying: Because you sent letters in your own name to all the people who are in Jerusalem, and to Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest, and to all the priests, saying,

**36:26** 'The LORD has appointed you priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, that there should be overseers in the house of the LORD over every madman who acts as a prophet, so that you put him into the stocks and into the iron collar.

**36:27** So now, why have you not rebuked Jeremiah of Anathoth, who is acting as a prophet to you?

**36:28** For on this account he sent to us in Babylon, saying, "It will be long; build houses and dwell in them, and plant gardens and eat their fruit."'"

**36:29** And Zephaniah the priest read this letter in the hearing of Jeremiah the prophet.

**36:30** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying:

**36:31** "Send to all the exiles, saying, 'This is what the LORD says concerning Shemaiah the Nehelamite: Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you, though I did not send him, and has made you trust in falsehood,

**36:32** therefore this is what the LORD says: Look, I am about to punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his offspring; he shall not have a man dwelling among this people, and he shall not see the good that I am about to do for My people, declares the LORD, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD.'"

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**29:17** *(after `36:15`)* This is what the LORD of hosts says: Look, I am sending the sword, the famine, and the plague against them, and I will make them like disgusting figs that cannot be eaten because they are so bad.

**29:18** *(after `36:15`)* I will pursue them with the sword, the famine, and the plague, and I will make them an object of dread to all the kingdoms of the earth, to be a curse, a horror, a hissing, and a disgrace among all the nations where I have banished them,

**29:19** *(after `36:15`)* because they did not listen to My words, declares the LORD, when I sent My servants the prophets to them, sending them time and again—yet you did not listen, declares the LORD.

**29:20** *(after `36:15`)* As for you, listen to the word of the LORD, all you exiles whom I sent away from Jerusalem to Babylon.

## Translator Notes

- **36:8** — Hebrew machlemim is a Hiphil participle with the second-person plural subject 'you,' understood either causatively ('that you cause to be dreamed,' i.e., solicit from your prophets) or as an internal Hiphil ('that you dream').
- **36:14** — Ketiv reads shevitchem ('your captivity'), whereas Qere reads shevutchem ('your fortunes/restoration'), forming the idiomatic expression shuv shevut ('restore the fortunes of,' also in 37:3, 18).
- **36:21** — The Hebrew here spells the king's name Nebuchadrezzar (closer to Akkadian Nabu-kudurri-usur), contrasting with Nebuchadnezzar in 36:1 and 36:3; vv. 21-22 also feature wordplay across Kolaiah (Qolayah), 'curse' (qelalah), and 'roasted them' (qalam).
- **36:23** — Ketiv reads hwyde' (a scribal transposition of yod and vav), whereas Qere reads hayyodea' (Qal active participle of yada' with the definite article, 'the one who knows').
- **36:26** — Peqidim ('overseers') is plural despite the singular address to Zephaniah, either stating a general office ('that there should be overseers') or including his fellow priests; tsinoq ('iron collar') is a biblical Hebrew hapax legomenon for a restraining neck-iron or pillory.
- **29:19 *(additional)*** — MT shifts abruptly from third person ("they") to second person plural ("you").
