## Text

**32:15** For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, said to me: "Take this cup of the wine of wrath from My hand, and make all the nations to whom I am sending you drink it.

**32:16** They will drink and reel and go mad because of the sword that I am sending among them."

**32:17** So I took the cup from the hand of the LORD and made all the nations drink to whom the LORD had sent me:

**32:18** Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and its officials, to make them a ruin, a desolation, an object of hissing, and a curse, as it is this day;

**32:19** Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials, and all his people;

**32:20** and all the mixed peoples, all the kings of the land of Uz, and all the kings of the land of the Philistines, and Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;

**32:21** Edom, Moab, and the sons of Ammon;

**32:22** all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the coastland across the sea;

**32:23** Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who trim the edges of their hair;

**32:24** all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the mixed peoples who dwell in the wilderness;

**32:25** all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of Media;

**32:26** and all the kings of the north, near and far, one with another, and all the kingdoms of the earth that are on the face of the ground. And the king of Sheshach will drink after them.

**32:27** And you shall say to them, "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Drink, get drunk, and vomit; fall and do not rise, because of the sword that I am sending among you.'"

**32:28** And it shall be, if they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, that you shall say to them, "This is what the LORD of hosts says: 'You shall certainly drink!

**32:29** For look, in the city upon which My name is called I am beginning to bring harm, and shall you really go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am summoning a sword against all the inhabitants of the earth,' declares the LORD of hosts."

**32:30** And you shall prophesy against them all these words, and say to them: "The LORD will roar from on high, and from His holy dwelling He will utter His voice; He will roar mightily against His fold. He will shout a harvest cry like those who tread grapes against all the inhabitants of the earth.

**32:31** A clamor has come to the end of the earth, for the LORD has a dispute with the nations; He is entering into judgment with all flesh. As for the wicked, He has given them to the sword," declares the LORD.

**32:32** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Look, disaster is going forth from nation to nation, and a great tempest is being stirred up from the farthest reaches of the earth.

**32:33** And those killed by the LORD on that day will be from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth. They will not be lamented, nor gathered, nor buried; they will become dung on the face of the ground.

**32:34** Wail, shepherds, and cry out! Roll in ashes, lords of the flock! For your days for slaughter and your dispersions have been fulfilled, and you will fall like a precious vessel.

**32:35** Flight will perish from the shepherds, and escape from the lords of the flock.

**32:36** The sound of the outcry of the shepherds, and the wailing of the lords of the flock! For the LORD is devastating their pasture,

**32:37** and the peaceful folds are silenced because of the fierce anger of the LORD.

**32:38** Like a young lion He has left His lair, for their land has become a desolation because of the fierceness of the oppressor and because of His fierce anger."

## Translator Notes

- **32:26** — 'Sheshach' is a traditional Hebrew atbash cipher (reversing the alphabet) representing Babel (Babylon).
- **32:30** — Hebrew hedad ('harvest cry') refers to the rhythmic shout of grape-treaders in the winepress and is also used as a battle cry (cf. 31:33).
- **32:34** — Hebrew utephotekhem / utephotsotekhem ('and your dispersions') is a rare plural noun from the root puts ('scatter') with a second-person plural suffix, occurring only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **32:38** — Hebrew haron hayyonah literally means 'the fierceness of the oppressing [one],' using the feminine Qal active participle of yanah ('oppress'), which elsewhere in Jeremiah modifies the feminine noun 'sword.'
