## Text

**6:1** Flee for safety, sons of Benjamin, out of the midst of Jerusalem! In Tekoa blow the horn, and on Beth-hakkerem lift up a signal fire, for disaster looms from the north, and great destruction.

**6:2** The lovely and pampered one I will cut off, daughter of Zion.

**6:3** Shepherds and their flocks will come to her; they will pitch tents against her all around; each one will graze his own place.

**6:4** "Consecrate war against her! Rise up, and let us go up at noon!" "Woe to us, for the day has declined, for the shadows of evening stretch out!"

**6:5** "Rise up, and let us go up by night, and let us destroy her palaces!"

**6:6** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: "Cut down trees and heap up a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This is the city to be punished; all of her is oppression in her midst.

**6:7** Like a well keeping its waters fresh, so she keeps her wickedness fresh; violence and devastation are heard within her; before Me continually are sickness and wounds.

**6:8** Accept discipline, Jerusalem, or else My soul will be torn away from you, or else I will make you a desolation, an uninhabited land."

**6:9** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "They will thoroughly glean the remnant of Israel like a vine; pass your hand back like a grape-gatherer over the branches."

**6:10** To whom shall I speak and give warning, that they may listen? Look, their ear is uncircumcised, and they cannot pay attention. Look, the word of the LORD has become a reproach to them; they take no delight in it.

**6:11** But I am full of the wrath of the LORD; I am weary of holding it in. "Pour it out on the child in the street, and on the gathering of young men together; for both husband and wife will be captured, the aged along with the one full of days.

**6:12** Their houses will be turned over to others, fields and wives together, for I will stretch out My hand against the inhabitants of the land," declares the LORD.

**6:13** "For from the least of them to the greatest of them, every one of them is greedy for unjust gain; and from prophet to priest, every one of them practices falsehood.

**6:14** And they heal the fracture of My people lightly, saying, 'Peace, peace,' yet there is no peace.

**6:15** They acted shamefully, for they committed an abomination; yet they were not ashamed at all, nor did they know how to blush. Therefore they will fall among those who fall; at the time I punish them they will stumble," says the LORD.

**6:16** This is what the LORD says: "Take your stand along the roads and look, and ask about the ancient pathways, which is the good way, and walk in it, and find rest for your souls. Yet they said, 'We will not walk in it.'

**6:17** And I raised up watchmen over you: 'Pay attention to the sound of the horn!' Yet they said, 'We will not pay attention.'

**6:18** Therefore hear, you nations, and know, congregation, what is among them.

**6:19** Hear, earth! Look, I am bringing disaster upon this people, the fruit of their schemes, because they have not paid attention to My words, and as for My law, they have rejected it.

**6:20** What is it to Me that frankincense comes from Sheba, and sweet cane from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices are not pleasing to Me."

**6:21** Therefore this is what the LORD says: "Look, I am placing stumbling blocks before this people, and fathers and sons together will stumble over them, neighbor and his companion, and they will perish."

**6:22** This is what the LORD says: "Look, a people is coming from the land of the north, and a great nation is being roused from the farthest reaches of the earth.

**6:23** They grasp bow and javelin; they are cruel and show no compassion; their voice roars like the sea, and upon horses they ride, arrayed like a man for battle against you, daughter of Zion."

**6:24** We have heard the report of it; our hands have grown limp; anguish has gripped us, pain like a woman giving birth.

**6:25** Do not go out into the field, and do not walk along the road, for the enemy has a sword; terror is on every side.

**6:26** Daughter of my people, put on sackcloth and roll in ashes; make mourning for yourself as for an only son, a bitter lamentation, for suddenly the devastator will come upon us.

**6:27** I have made you a tester among My people, a fortress, so that you may know and test their way.

**6:28** All of them are the most stubborn of rebels, going about with slander; they are bronze and iron; all of them act corruptly.

**6:29** The bellows are scorched; the lead is consumed by the fire. In vain the refining continues, yet the wicked are not separated.

**6:30** "Rejected silver," people call them, because the LORD has rejected them.

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Qal perfect damiti comes from damah II ("cut off, destroy"; cf. Hos 4:5), rather than damah I ("liken," which is normally Piel when transitive).
- **6:7** — The Qere reads bayir (a rare variant of bor, "well/cistern," which is the Ketiv). The Hiphil forms haqir and heqerah derive from qur/qarar ("keep cool/fresh," or "cause to well up").
- **6:21** — The Qere reads we'avadu (Qal waw-consecutive perfect, "and they will perish"), linking "neighbor and his companion" with the preceding clause; the Ketiv has the imperfect yo'vedu ("will perish") without the conjunction.
- **6:25** — The Qere uses masculine plural forms (tetse'u, teleku) addressing the people as a whole, while the Ketiv has feminine singular forms (tetse'i, teleki) addressing personified Zion.
- **6:29** — The Qere divides the Ketiv's single word (m'shtm, perhaps "from their fire") into two words, me'esh tam ("by the fire [the lead] is consumed"). The opening verb nachar may be parsed as Niphal of charar ("is scorched") or Qal of nachar ("blows fiercely").
