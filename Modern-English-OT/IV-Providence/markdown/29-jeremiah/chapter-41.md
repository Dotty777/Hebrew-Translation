## Text

**41:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth under the dominion of his hand, and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and against all its cities, saying:

**41:2** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: "Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and say to him, 'This is what the LORD says: Look, I am giving this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.

**41:3** And you will not escape from his hand, for you will surely be captured and given into his hand; and your eyes will see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and his mouth will speak with your mouth, and you will go to Babylon.

**41:4** Yet hear the word of the LORD, Zedekiah king of Judah! This is what the LORD says concerning you: You will not die by the sword.

**41:5** In peace you will die; and as with the burnings for your fathers, the former kings who were before you, so they will make a burning for you, and they will lament for you, "Alas, lord!" For I Myself have spoken the word, declares the LORD.'"

**41:6** Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem.

**41:7** Now the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the remaining cities of Judah, against Lachish and against Azekah, for these remained among the cities of Judah as fortified cities.

**41:8** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after King Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people who were in Jerusalem to proclaim release to them,

**41:9** that each man should let his male slave and each man his female slave, the Hebrew man and the Hebrew woman, go free, so that no one should hold them in bondage—a Judean, his brother.

**41:10** And all the officials and all the people who had entered into the covenant obeyed, that each man should let his male slave and each man his female slave go free, so as not to hold them in bondage any longer; they obeyed and let them go.

**41:11** Yet afterward they turned back and brought back the male slaves and the female slaves whom they had let go free, and subdued them to be male slaves and female slaves.

**41:12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying:

**41:13** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: "I Myself made a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves, saying,

**41:14** 'At the end of seven years each of you shall let go his Hebrew brother who has been sold to you and has served you six years; you shall let him go free from you.' But your fathers did not listen to Me, nor did they incline their ear.

**41:15** And you turned today and did what is right in My eyes, proclaiming release, each man to his neighbor; and you made a covenant before Me in the house upon which My name is called.

**41:16** Then you turned back and profaned My name, and you brought back, each man his male slave and each man his female slave, whom you had let go free according to their own desire, and you subdued them to be male slaves and female slaves for you."

**41:17** Therefore this is what the LORD says: "You have not listened to Me to proclaim release, each man to his brother and each man to his neighbor. Look, I am proclaiming a release for you," declares the LORD, "to the sword, to the plague, and to the famine; and I will make you an object of horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**41:18** And I will make the men who have transgressed My covenant, who have not upheld the words of the covenant that they made before Me, like the calf that they cut in two and passed between its parts—

**41:19** the officials of Judah and the officials of Jerusalem, the eunuchs and the priests, and all the people of the land who passed between the parts of the calf—

**41:20** I will give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life; and their corpses will become food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth.

**41:21** And I will give Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life, and into the hand of the army of the king of Babylon, which is withdrawing from you.

**41:22** Look, I am commanding," declares the LORD, "and I will bring them back to this city; and they will fight against it and capture it and burn it with fire, and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation without an inhabitant."

## Translator Notes

- **41:3** — The clause "his mouth will speak with your mouth" renders the Hebrew idiom literally, using the preposition et ("with") to express a direct face-to-face encounter.
- **41:5** — The noun misrefot ("burnings") and verb yisrefu ("make a burning") refer to the royal funerary custom of burning aromatic spices in honor of a deceased king (cf. 2 Chr 16:14; 21:19).
- **41:11** — Ketiv reads a Hiphil form (wayyakhbishum, causative "made them subject"), whereas Qere reads the Qal wayyikhbeshum ("and subdued them"), matching the Qal verb in 41:16; the translation follows the Qere.
- **41:17** — Ketiv writes the noun with transposed middle consonants (lezo'ah/lezawa'ah), whereas Qere reads leza'awah ("an object of horror"); both convey the same sense, and the translation follows the Qere.
- **41:18** — Hebrew plays on two repeated verbs: karat ("cut") for both making a covenant and cutting the calf in two, and abar ("pass/transgress") for both transgressing the covenant and passing between the calf's parts. Literally "the calf" lacks a comparative preposition and functions as a double accusative with natan ("make [them like] the calf"), with the verb resumed in 41:20.
