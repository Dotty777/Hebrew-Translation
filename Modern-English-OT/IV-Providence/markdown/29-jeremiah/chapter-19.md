## Text

**19:1** This is what the LORD says: "Go and buy a potter's earthenware flask, and take some of the elders of the people and some of the elders of the priests,

**19:2** and go out to the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, which is at the entrance of the Potsherd Gate, and proclaim there the words that I speak to you.

**19:3** You shall say, 'Hear the word of the LORD, kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I am bringing calamity upon this place, such that the ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle,

**19:4** because they have forsaken Me and have alienated this place, and have burned incense in it to other gods whom neither they nor their fathers nor the kings of Judah have known, and because they have filled this place with the blood of the innocent,

**19:5** and have built the high places of Baal to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal, something I did not command or speak of, nor did it come into My heart.

**19:6** Therefore, look, days are coming, declares the LORD, when this place will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, but rather the Valley of Slaughter.

**19:7** And I will empty out the counsel of Judah and Jerusalem in this place, and I will cause them to fall by the sword before their enemies and by the hand of those who seek their life. And I will give their corpses as food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth.

**19:8** And I will make this city a desolation and an object of hissing; everyone who passes by it will be appalled and will hiss because of all its wounds.

**19:9** And I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters, and each one will eat the flesh of his neighbor in the siege and in the distress with which their enemies and those who seek their life will distress them.'

**19:10** Then you shall shatter the flask before the eyes of the men who go with you,

**19:11** and you shall say to them, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: In this way I will shatter this people and this city, just as one shatters a potter's vessel that cannot be mended again. And they will bury in Topheth for lack of a place to bury.

**19:12** Thus I will do to this place, declares the LORD, and to its inhabitants, even making this city like Topheth.

**19:13** And the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will become defiled like the place of Topheth, all the houses on whose rooftops they burned incense to all the host of heaven and poured out drink offerings to other gods.'"

**19:14** Then Jeremiah came from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the house of the LORD and said to all the people,

**19:15** "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I am bringing upon this city and upon all its towns all the calamity that I have spoken against it, because they have stiffened their necks so as not to listen to My words."

## Translator Notes

- **19:1** — The Hebrew omits a verb before "some of the elders" by ellipsis; "take" is supplied to complete the command.
- **19:2** — Ketiv reads hchrswt, while Qere reads hacharsit ("the Potsherd Gate," linked to chares, "earthenware," in v. 1).
- **19:7** — Uvaqqoti (Qal weqatal of baqaq, "empty out / make void") creates a wordplay on baqbuq ("flask") in vv. 1 and 10.
- **19:13** — Vehassekh is a Hiphil infinitive absolute of nasakh continuing the finite verb qitteru ("burned incense"), a standard Biblical Hebrew syntactic construction.
