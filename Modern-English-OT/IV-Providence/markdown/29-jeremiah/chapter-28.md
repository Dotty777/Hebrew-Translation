## Text

**28:1** This is what the LORD says: "Look, I am rousing a destroying wind against Babylon and against the residents of Leb-kamai.

**28:2** And I will send foreigners to Babylon, and they will winnow her and empty her land; for they will be against her from every side in the day of disaster.

**28:3** Do not let the archer bend his bow, nor let him lift himself up in his armor; and do not spare her young men; devote her entire army to destruction.

**28:4** And those mortally wounded will fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and those pierced through in her streets."

**28:5** For neither Israel nor Judah has been left widowed by his God, by the LORD of hosts, even though their land is filled with guilt against the Holy One of Israel.

**28:6** Flee from the midst of Babylon, and let each one rescue his own life! Do not be cut off in her iniquity, for it is a time of vengeance for the LORD; He is repaying recompense to her.

**28:7** Babylon was a cup of gold in the hand of the LORD, making the entire earth drunk; from her wine nations drank, therefore nations act madly.

**28:8** Suddenly Babylon fell and was shattered! Wail over her; get balm for her suffering, perhaps she will be healed.

**28:9** "We tried to heal Babylon, yet she was not healed. Abandon her, and let each of us go to his own land, for her judgment has reached to the heavens and has been lifted up to the clouds."

**28:10** "The LORD has brought forth our vindication; come, and let us tell in Zion the work of the LORD our God."

**28:11** Polish the arrows, fill the quivers! The LORD has roused the spirit of the kings of Media, for His purpose is against Babylon, to destroy her; for this is the vengeance of the LORD, the vengeance for His temple.

**28:12** Against the walls of Babylon raise a standard! Strengthen the watch, station watchmen, prepare the ambushers! For the LORD has both purposed and done what He spoke against the residents of Babylon.

**28:13** You who dwell beside many waters, abundant in treasures, your end has come, the measure of your cutting off.

**28:14** The LORD of hosts has sworn by Himself: "Surely I will fill you with men like locusts, and they will raise a battle shout over you."

**28:15** He is the One who made the earth by His strength, who set the world firm by His wisdom, and by His understanding He stretched out the heavens.

**28:16** When He gives His voice, there is a roaring of waters in the heavens, and He causes clouds to rise from the end of the earth; He makes lightning flashes for the rain and brings out wind from His storehouses.

**28:17** Every person is senseless, devoid of knowledge; every metalworker is disgraced by a carved image, for his cast image is a lie, and there is no breath in them.

**28:18** They are emptiness, a work of mockery; at the time of their reckoning they will perish.

**28:19** Not like these is the portion of Jacob, for He is the Former of all things, and the tribe of His inheritance; the LORD of hosts is His name.

**28:20** "You are a war club to Me, weapons of war; and with you I dash to pieces nations, and with you I destroy kingdoms;

**28:21** and with you I dash to pieces horse and its rider, and with you I dash to pieces chariot and its rider;

**28:22** and with you I dash to pieces man and woman, and with you I dash to pieces old man and boy, and with you I dash to pieces young man and virgin;

**28:23** and with you I dash to pieces shepherd and his flock, and with you I dash to pieces plowman and his team, and with you I dash to pieces governors and officials.

**28:24** And I will repay Babylon and all the residents of Chaldea for all their evil that they did in Zion, before your eyes," declares the LORD.

**28:25** "Look, I am against you, destroying mountain," declares the LORD, "who destroys all the earth; and I will stretch out My hand against you, roll you down from the cliffs, and make you into a mountain of burning.

**28:26** And they will not take from you a stone for a corner or a stone for foundations, for you will be everlasting desolations," declares the LORD.

**28:27** Raise a standard in the land, blow a ram's horn among the nations! Consecrate nations against her; summon the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz against her. Appoint a commander against her; send up horses like bristling locusts!

**28:28** Consecrate nations against her: the kings of Media, her governors and all her officials, and every land of his dominion.

**28:29** And the earth quakes and writhes, for the plans of the LORD stand firm against Babylon, to make the land of Babylon a desolation with no inhabitant.

**28:30** The mighty men of Babylon have stopped fighting; they stay in the strongholds; their might has dried up; they have become women; her dwellings are set ablaze; her bars are shattered.

**28:31** Runner runs to meet runner, and messenger to meet messenger, to report to the king of Babylon that his city has been captured from every quarter;

**28:32** and the crossings have been seized, the reed marshes are burned with fire, and the men of war are panicked.

**28:33** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Daughter Babylon is like a threshing floor at the time of trampling it; a little while longer, and the time of the harvest will come for her."

**28:34** "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has devoured me, he has crushed me; he has placed me as an empty vessel; he has swallowed me like a sea monster; he has filled his belly with my delicacies; he has flushed me away.

**28:35** May the violence against me and my flesh be upon Babylon," she who dwells in Zion will say; "and may my blood be against the residents of Chaldea," Jerusalem will say.

**28:36** Therefore this is what the LORD says: "Look, I am arguing your case and will take vengeance for you; and I will dry up her sea and parch her spring.

**28:37** And Babylon will become heaps of rubble, a lair of jackals, a desolation and an object of hissing, with no inhabitant.

**28:38** Together they roar like young lions; they growl like cubs of lions.

**28:39** While they are heated, I will set out their banquet and make them drunk, so that they revel, and then sleep an everlasting sleep and not wake up," declares the LORD.

**28:40** "I will bring them down like lambs to be slaughtered, like rams along with male goats."

**28:41** How Sheshach has been captured, and the praise of all the earth seized! How Babylon has become a desolation among the nations!

**28:42** The sea has risen over Babylon; she has been covered by the tumult of its waves.

**28:43** Her cities have become a desolation, a dry land and a desert plain, a land where no person lives and through which no human being passes.

**28:44** "And I will punish Bel in Babylon, and bring what he has swallowed out of his mouth; and nations will no longer stream to him. Even the wall of Babylon has fallen!"

**28:49** Babylon also must fall for the mortally wounded of Israel, just as on account of Babylon the mortally wounded of all the earth have fallen.

**28:50** Survivors from the sword, go, do not stop! Remember the LORD from a distance, and let Jerusalem come into your heart.

**28:51** "We are ashamed, for we have heard insult; disgrace has covered our faces, because foreigners have come into the sanctuaries of the house of the LORD."

**28:52** "Therefore, look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will punish her carved images, and across all her land the mortally wounded will groan.

**28:53** Even if Babylon rises to the heavens, and even if she fortifies the height of her strength, devastators will come to her from Me," declares the LORD.

**28:54** A sound of outcry from Babylon, and a great shattering from the land of the Chaldeans!

**28:55** For the LORD is devastating Babylon, and He will wipe out the loud voice from her; and their waves will roar like many waters; the uproar of their voice is sounded forth.

**28:56** For a devastator has come against her, against Babylon, and her mighty men are captured; their bows are broken, for the LORD is a God of recompenses; He will certainly repay.

**28:57** "And I will make her princes and her wise men drunk, her governors, her officials, and her mighty men; and they will sleep an everlasting sleep and not wake up," declares the King—the LORD of hosts is His name.

**28:58** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "The broad walls of Babylon will be utterly razed, and her lofty gates will burn with fire; so peoples will labor for emptiness, and nations for fire, and they will grow weary."

**28:59** The word that Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, when he went to Babylon with Zedekiah king of Judah in the fourth year of his reign; now Seraiah was quartermaster.

**28:60** And Jeremiah wrote in one scroll all the disaster that would come against Babylon, all these words that are written against Babylon.

**28:61** And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you arrive in Babylon, see to it that you read all these words,

**28:62** and say, 'LORD, You Yourself have spoken against this place to cut it off, so that no inhabitant remains in it, from human to beast, for it will be everlasting desolations.'

**28:63** And it will be that when you finish reading this scroll, you will bind a stone to it and cast it into the middle of the Euphrates,

**28:64** and say, 'In this way Babylon will sink and not rise because of the disaster that I am bringing upon her, and they will grow weary.'" To this point are the words of Jeremiah.

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — Leb-kamai literally means "the heart of those who rise against Me" and is a Hebrew Atbash cipher for Chaldea (Kasdim).
- **28:2** — Following the Masoretic vocalization, the noun means "foreigners," forming a wordplay with the following Piel verb "and they will winnow her" (repointed, the noun would read "winnowers").
- **28:3** — The written consonantal text (ketiv) duplicates the verb "bend," which the read tradition (qere) omits; the negative jussive particle is here vocalized with segol before the jussive verbs "bend" and the apocopated Hithpael "lift himself up."
- **28:13** — The ketiv preserves an archaic feminine singular participial ending, whereas the qere reads the standard feminine singular participle "you who dwell." The phrase "the measure of your cutting off" literally refers to the cubit-measure for cutting woven cloth from a loom, or alternatively "the measure of your unjust gain."
- **28:19** — Unlike the parallel in Jeremiah 10:16, the Hebrew text here omits "Israel" before "and of the tribe of His inheritance," connecting the phrase either to "the Former" or elliptically to Jacob.
- **28:34** — Five verbs in this verse have a ketiv/qere variant where the written text (ketiv) reads first-person plural suffixes ("us") and the read text (qere) reads first-person singular suffixes ("me"), aligning with the singular speaker Zion in verse 35. The final verb is vocalized from the Hiphil of the root for rinsing or flushing out rather than driving away.
- **28:41** — Sheshach is a Hebrew Atbash cipher for Babylon, parallel to Leb-kamai in verse 1.
- **28:49** — The Hebrew syntax is compressed ("also Babylon [is] to fall, the mortally wounded of Israel"), using the preposition plus infinitive construct to express inevitability ("must fall") in reciprocity for those killed because of Babylon.
- **28:64** — The Hebrew text retains "and they will grow weary," echoing the closing verb of verse 58 immediately before the editorial colophon concluding Jeremiah's words.
