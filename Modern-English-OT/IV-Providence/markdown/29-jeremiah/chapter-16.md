## Text

**16:1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**16:2** "You shall not take a wife for yourself, nor shall you have sons or daughters in this place.

**16:3** For this is what the LORD says concerning the sons and the daughters born in this place, and concerning their mothers who bear them, and concerning their fathers who father them in this land:

**16:4** They will die of fatal diseases; they will not be lamented, nor will they be buried; they will become dung on the surface of the ground. By the sword and by famine they will come to an end, and their corpses will become food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth.

**16:5** For this is what the LORD says: Do not enter a house of mourning, do not go to lament, and do not grieve for them; for I have withdrawn My peace from this people, declares the LORD—loyal love and compassion.

**16:6** Both great and small will die in this land; they will not be buried, nor will anyone lament for them, nor will anyone cut himself or shave his head bald for them.

**16:7** No one will break bread for them in mourning to comfort one over the dead, nor give them the cup of consolation to drink for his father or for his mother.

**16:8** And you shall not enter a house of feasting to sit with them to eat and to drink.

**16:9** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I am about to cause the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, to cease from this place, before your eyes and in your days.

**16:10** And it will be, when you declare to this people all these words, and they say to you, 'Why has the LORD pronounced all this great calamity against us? And what is our iniquity, and what is our sin that we have sinned against the LORD our God?'

**16:11** then you shall say to them, 'Because your fathers abandoned Me, declares the LORD, and went after other gods and served them and bowed down to them, while Me they abandoned and My law they did not keep.

**16:12** And you have done more evil than your fathers; and look, each of you walks after the stubbornness of his evil heart, so as not to listen to Me.

**16:13** Therefore I will hurl you from this land into a land that neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you shall serve other gods day and night, for I will grant you no favor.'

**16:14** Therefore, look, days are coming, declares the LORD, when it will no longer be said, 'As the LORD lives, who brought up the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt,'

**16:15** but rather, 'As the LORD lives, who brought up the sons of Israel from the land of the north and from all the lands to which He had banished them.' And I will bring them back to their own soil that I gave to their fathers.

**16:16** Look, I am sending for many fishermen, declares the LORD, and they will fish them out; and afterward I will send for many hunters, and they will hunt them from every mountain and from every hill and out of the clefts of the rocks.

**16:17** For My eyes are upon all their ways; they are not hidden from before Me, nor is their iniquity concealed from before My eyes.

**16:18** First, I will repay double for their iniquity and their sin, because they have profaned My land; with the carcasses of their detestable things and their abominations they have filled My inheritance."

**16:19** LORD, my strength and my stronghold, and my refuge in the day of distress, to You nations will come from the ends of the earth and say, "Our fathers have inherited nothing but falsehood, futility, and things in which there is no profit."

**16:20** Can man make gods for himself, when they are not gods?

**16:21** "Therefore, look, I am about to make them know; this once I will make them know My hand and My might, and they will know that My name is the LORD."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**17:1** *(after `16:21`)* The sin of Judah is written with an iron stylus, with a diamond point; it is engraved on the tablet of their heart and on the horns of your altars,

**17:2** *(after `16:21`)* while their children remember their altars and their Asherah poles beside a leafy tree, on the high hills.

**17:3** *(after `16:21`)* My mountain in the field, your wealth, all your treasures, I will give for plunder—your high places, because of sin throughout all your borders.

**17:4** *(after `16:21`)* And you will relinquish—by your own doing—your inheritance that I gave you, and I will make you serve your enemies in a land that you do not know, for you have kindled a fire in My anger; it will burn forever.

## Translator Notes

- **16:5** — Hebrew bet marzeah ("house of mourning") refers to a ritual funerary banquet held in honor of the dead.
- **16:7** — The Masoretic verb yifresu (Qal of paras, "break [bread]") omits an explicit object ("bread") and is followed by al-ebel ("in mourning"), which some ancient versions read as "bread."
- **16:16** — Translates the Qere dayyagim ("fishermen"), matching the pattern of tsayyadim ("hunters") later in the verse; the Ketiv reads dawagim (or dogim) with the same meaning.
- **17:1 *(additional)*** — MT shifts here from third-person "their heart" to second-person plural "your altars."
- **17:4 *(additional)*** — MT reads literally "and in you," indicating Judah forfeits its inheritance by its own doing.
