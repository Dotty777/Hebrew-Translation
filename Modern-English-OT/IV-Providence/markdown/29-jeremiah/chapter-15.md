## Text

**15:1** Then the LORD said to me, "Even if Moses and Samuel stood before Me, My soul would not be toward this people. Send them away from before My face, and let them go out.

**15:2** And it will be, when they say to you, 'Where shall we go out?' you shall say to them, 'This is what the LORD says: Whoever is for death, to death; and whoever is for the sword, to the sword; and whoever is for famine, to famine; and whoever is for captivity, to captivity.'

**15:3** I will appoint over them four kinds, declares the LORD: the sword to kill, and the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth to devour and to destroy.

**15:4** And I will make them an object of horror to all the kingdoms of the earth on account of Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, for what he did in Jerusalem.

**15:5** For who will have pity on you, Jerusalem, and who will grieve for you, and who will turn aside to ask about your welfare?

**15:6** You have abandoned Me, declares the LORD; you keep going backward. So I have stretched out My hand against you and destroyed you; I am weary of relenting.

**15:7** I winnowed them with a winnowing fork in the gates of the land; I have bereaved, I have destroyed My people; they did not turn back from their ways.

**15:8** Their widows have become more numerous to Me than the sand of the seas; I have brought upon them, against the mother of young men, a destroyer at noon; I have caused anguish and terrors to fall suddenly upon her.

**15:9** She who bore seven has languished; she has gasped out her life. Her sun has gone down while it is still day; she is shamed and disgraced. And their remnant I will give to the sword before their enemies, declares the LORD."

**15:10** Woe to me, my mother, that you bore me as a man of strife and a man of contention to the whole land! I have not lent, nor have they lent to me, yet every one of them curses me.

**15:11** The LORD said, "Surely I will set you free for good; surely I will cause the enemy to plead with you in a time of calamity and in a time of distress.

**15:12** Can iron shatter iron from the north and bronze?

**15:13** Your wealth and your treasures I will give for plunder, without a price, because of all your sins and throughout all your borders.

**15:14** And I will make you pass over with your enemies into a land you have not known, for a fire has kindled in My anger; against you it will burn."

**15:15** You know, LORD; remember me and attend to me, and avenge me on my pursuers. In Your slowness to anger do not take me away; know that for Your sake I bear reproach.

**15:16** Your words were found, and I ate them; and Your word became to me exultation and the gladness of my heart, for Your name is called over me, LORD, God of hosts.

**15:17** I did not sit in the company of revelers and rejoice; because of Your hand I sat alone, for You have filled me with indignation.

**15:18** Why has my pain become unending and my wound incurable, refusing to be healed? Will You truly be to me like a failing stream, waters that are not dependable?

**15:19** Therefore, this is what the LORD says: "If you turn back, then I will restore you; before Me you shall stand. And if you bring forth what is precious from what is worthless, you shall be as My mouth. They shall turn to you, but you shall not turn to them.

**15:20** And I will make you to this people a fortified wall of bronze; they will fight against you, but they will not prevail over you, for I am with you to save you and to deliver you, declares the LORD.

**15:21** I will deliver you from the hand of the wicked, and I will redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless."

## Translator Notes

- **15:8** — The noun rendered "anguish" (Hebrew ir, distinct from the homonym meaning "city") denotes sudden agitation or terror when paired with behalot ("terrors").
- **15:10** — The clause "every one of them curses me" translates a grammatically blended Masoretic form (mekalelawni, combining participial and verbal suffix elements from the Piel of qalal) following the collective kulloh ("all of it").
- **15:11** — Translates the Qere sheritika (Piel perfect of sharah, "set free, release"); the Ketiv reads shrwtek, variously understood as a verbal noun from the same root or a defective form meaning "your remnant."
- **15:14** — Follows the Masoretic Hiphil of abar ("cause to pass over") with et taken as the preposition "with" and an implied object ("you"); several ancient versions instead reflect the graphically similar root abad ("make to serve"), as in 17:4.
- **15:16** — Translates the Qere singular debarka ("Your word"), agreeing with the singular verb wayehi; the Ketiv reads the plural debareka ("Your words").
