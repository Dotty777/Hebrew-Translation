## Text

**46:1** In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army came against Jerusalem and besieged it.

**46:2** In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the city was breached.

**46:3** Then all the officials of the king of Babylon came in and sat at the Middle Gate: Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim the Rab-saris, Nergal-sharezer the Rab-mag, and all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon.

**46:14** And they sent and took Jeremiah from the courtyard of the guard, and handed him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, to bring him home. So he remained among the people.

**46:15** Now the word of the LORD had come to Jeremiah while he was confined in the courtyard of the guard, saying,

**46:16** "Go and speak to Ebed-melech the Cushite, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I am bringing My words against this city for harm and not for good, and they will come about before you on that day.

**46:17** But I will deliver you on that day, declares the LORD, and you will not be given into the hand of the men whom you dread.

**46:18** For I will certainly rescue you, and you will not fall by the sword; instead, your life will be yours as plunder, because you have trusted in Me, declares the LORD.'"

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — The Hebrew spelling here is נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר ("Nebuchadrezzar"), rendered with the standard modern English spelling "Nebuchadnezzar."
- **46:3** — "Rab-saris" (lit. "chief officer/eunuch") and "Rab-mag" are transliterated Babylonian court titles; the translation follows the MT word divisions סַמְגַּר־נְבוּ ("Samgar-nebo") and שַׂר־סְכִים ("Sarsechim").
- **39:4 *(additional)*** — MT shifts from plural ("they") to singular ("he"), focusing on King Zedekiah.
- **39:9 *(additional)*** — MT repeats "the rest of the people" in both clauses.
- **39:12 *(additional)*** — Ketiv-wela-Qere: the consonantal text writes the particle im ("if", enclosed in parentheses in the critical text), which is not read aloud in the Masoretic tradition; the translation follows the Qere (omitting the unread particle).
- **39:13 *(additional)*** — Rab-saris and Rab-mag are Babylonian court titles.
