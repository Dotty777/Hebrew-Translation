## Text

**21:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when King Zedekiah sent Pashhur son of Malchijah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah, the priest, to him, saying,

**21:2** "Please inquire of the LORD on our behalf, for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon is fighting against us. Perhaps the LORD will deal with us according to all His wondrous deeds, so that he will withdraw from us."

**21:3** Then Jeremiah said to them, "This is what you shall say to Zedekiah:

**21:4** 'This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Look, I am turning back the weapons of war that are in your hands, with which you are fighting against the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are laying siege against you outside the wall, and I will gather them into the middle of this city.

**21:5** And I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, and in anger, in fury, and in great indignation.

**21:6** And I will strike the inhabitants of this city, both human and animal; they will die by a great pestilence.

**21:7** And afterward, declares the LORD, I will give Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, the people, and those remaining in this city from the pestilence, from the sword, and from the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their lives. He will strike them down with the edge of the sword; he will not spare them, nor show pity, nor have compassion.'"

**21:8** And to this people you shall say, "This is what the LORD says: Look, I am placing before you the way of life and the way of death.

**21:9** Whoever remains in this city will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence; but whoever goes out and surrenders to the Chaldeans who are laying siege against you will live, and his life will be his as plunder.

**21:10** For I have set My face against this city for harm and not for good, declares the LORD. It will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire."

**21:11** And to the house of the king of Judah: Hear the word of the LORD!

**21:12** House of David, this is what the LORD says: "Execute justice in the morning, and rescue the one who has been robbed from the hand of the oppressor, or else My fury will go out like fire and burn with no one to extinguish it, because of the evil of your deeds.

**21:13** Look, I am against you, dweller of the valley, rock of the plain, declares the LORD—you who say, 'Who will come down against us, and who will enter our dwellings?'

**21:14** And I will punish you according to the fruit of your deeds, declares the LORD; and I will ignite a fire in her forest, and it will consume all her surroundings."

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Hebrew spelling here and elsewhere in Jeremiah is Nebuchadrezzar, closer to the Babylonian form, rendered here with the standard English spelling Nebuchadnezzar.
- **21:5** — The Hebrew reverses the traditional Exodus formula of strong hand and outstretched arm into outstretched hand and strong arm, underscoring that the LORD Himself now fights against Jerusalem.
- **21:9** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv reads the imperfect without waw, while the read qere translated here reads the weqatal form, meaning will live. Surrenders to renders the Hebrew idiom naphal al, literally falls upon or to, meaning to defect or surrender.
- **21:12** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv reads their deeds with a third-person plural suffix, whereas the read qere translated here reads your deeds with a second-person plural suffix, matching the direct address in verses 12 and 14.
