## Text

**4:1** "If you will return, Israel," declares the LORD, "then return to Me. And if you remove your detestable things from My presence and do not waver,

**4:2** and swear, 'As the LORD lives,' in truth, in justice, and in righteousness, then nations will bless themselves in Him, and in Him they will boast."

**4:3** For this is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and to Jerusalem: "Break up fallow ground for yourselves, and do not sow among thorns.

**4:4** Circumcise yourselves to the LORD and remove the foreskins of your heart, men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, or else My wrath will go out like fire and burn with no one to quench it, because of the evil of your deeds."

**4:5** "Declare in Judah and proclaim in Jerusalem, and say, 'Blow the trumpet in the land!' Cry out at full voice and say, 'Gather together, and let us go into the fortified cities!'

**4:6** Raise a signal toward Zion! Take refuge, do not stand still! For I am bringing disaster from the north, and great destruction."

**4:7** A lion has gone up from his thicket, and a destroyer of nations has set out; he has gone out from his place to make your land a desolation; your cities will fall into ruins, without an inhabitant.

**4:8** Because of this, put on sackcloth, lament and wail, for the fierce anger of the LORD has not turned back from us.

**4:9** "And it will be on that day," declares the LORD, "that the heart of the king and the heart of the officials will fail, the priests will be appalled, and the prophets will be astounded."

**4:10** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Surely You have utterly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, 'You will have peace,' whereas the sword has reached to the throat!"

**4:11** At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem, "A scorching wind from the bare heights in the wilderness toward the daughter of My people, not to winnow and not to cleanse,

**4:12** a wind too strong for these comes for Me. Now I too will pronounce judgments against them."

**4:13** Look! Like clouds he comes up, and like the whirlwind are his chariots; his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, for we are devastated!

**4:14** Wash your heart from evil, Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long will thoughts of your wickedness lodge within you?

**4:15** For a voice declares from Dan and proclaims calamity from the hill country of Ephraim.

**4:16** "Announce to the nations; look, proclaim against Jerusalem, 'Besiegers are coming from a distant land, and they raise their voice against the cities of Judah.'

**4:17** Like watchmen of a field they are against her all around, because she has rebelled against Me," declares the LORD.

**4:18** Your way and your deeds have done these things to you. This is your evil; truly it is bitter, truly it has reached to your heart.

**4:19** My inward parts, my inward parts! I writhe in pain! The walls of my heart! My heart is pounding within me; I cannot keep silent, for you have heard the sound of the trumpet, my soul, the alarm of war!

**4:20** Destruction upon destruction is reported, for the whole land is devastated; suddenly my tents are devastated, in an instant, my tent curtains.

**4:21** How long must I see the signal and hear the sound of the trumpet?

**4:22** "For My people are foolish; they do not know Me. They are senseless sons, and they are not discerning. They are wise to do evil, but they do not know how to do good."

**4:23** I looked at the earth, and see, it was unformed and empty; and to the heavens, and their light was gone.

**4:24** I looked at the mountains, and see, they were quaking, and all the hills shook to and fro.

**4:25** I looked, and see, there was no human being, and all the birds of the heavens had fled.

**4:26** I looked, and see, the fruitful land was a wilderness, and all its cities were torn down before the LORD, before His fierce anger.

**4:27** For this is what the LORD says, "The whole land will be a desolation, yet I will not make a complete end.

**4:28** Because of this the earth will mourn, and the heavens above will grow dark, because I have spoken, I have purposed, and I have not relented, nor will I turn back from it."

**4:29** At the sound of horsemen and archers, every city flees; they go into the thickets and climb up into the crags; every city is abandoned, and no one dwells in them.

**4:30** And you, devastated one, what will you do? Though you dress in scarlet, though you adorn yourself with ornaments of gold, though you enlarge your eyes with paint, in vain you beautify yourself. Lovers despise you; they seek your life.

**4:31** For I heard a cry like that of a woman in labor, anguish like that of a woman bearing her first child, the cry of the daughter of Zion, gasping for breath, stretching out her hands: "Woe to me now, for my soul faints before murderers!"

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — Following the Qere tiq'u ("Blow," omitting the conjunction) so that the imperative begins the quoted proclamation; the Ketiv reads we-tiq'u ("and blow").
- **4:10** — "To the throat" renders 'ad-hannefesh; Hebrew nefesh can mean both "throat/neck" and "life/soul," here conveying mortal danger.
- **4:16** — "Besiegers" translates notsrim (Qal participle of natsar, lit. "watchers"), referring to enemy troops keeping siege watch around a city and paralleling "watchmen of a field" in 4:17.
- **4:19** — Following the Qere 'ohilah ("I writhe in pain," Ketiv 'ahulah) and the Qere 2fs verb shama'at ("you have heard," addressing "my soul"); the Ketiv sham'ti preserves either an archaic 2fs ending or a 1cs form ("I have heard").
- **4:30** — Following the Qere we'att ("and you," standard 2fs pronoun; the Ketiv we'atti preserves an archaic 2fs form). The masculine singular Qal passive participle shadud ("devastated one") stands in unusual gender apposition to the feminine singular pronoun.
