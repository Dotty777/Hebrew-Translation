## Text

**1:1** The words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, from the priests who were in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin,

**1:2** to whom the word of the LORD came during the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.

**1:3** It also came during the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the completion of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, until Jerusalem went into exile in the fifth month.

**1:4** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**1:5** "Before I shaped you in the womb, I knew you, and before you came out from the womb, I set you apart as holy; a prophet to the nations I have appointed you."

**1:6** And I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Look, I do not know how to speak, for I am a youth."

**1:7** But the LORD said to me, "Do not say, 'I am a youth,' for to whomever I send you, you will go, and everything that I command you, you will speak.

**1:8** Do not be afraid because of them, for I am with you to rescue you, declares the LORD."

**1:9** Then the LORD reached out His hand and touched my mouth. And the LORD said to me, "Look, I have placed My words in your mouth.

**1:10** See, I have commissioned you this day over the nations and over the kingdoms, to uproot and to break down, to destroy and to demolish, to build and to plant."

**1:11** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "What are you seeing, Jeremiah?" And I said, "I see a branch of an almond tree."

**1:12** And the LORD said to me, "You have seen well, for I am keeping watch over My word to carry it out."

**1:13** And the word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying, "What are you seeing?" And I said, "I see a boiling pot, and its face is away from the north."

**1:14** And the LORD said to me, "From the north the disaster will break open upon all the inhabitants of the land.

**1:15** For look, I am summoning all the clans of the kingdoms of the north, declares the LORD. And they will come, and each one will place his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all her walls all around, and against all the cities of Judah.

**1:16** And I will speak My judgments against them for all their wickedness, because they have forsaken Me, and burned incense to other gods, and bowed down to the works of their hands.

**1:17** But as for you, gird your waist, and arise and speak to them everything that I command you. Do not be terrified because of them, or else I will terrify you before them.

**1:18** And as for Me, look, I have made you today a fortified city, and an iron pillar, and walls of bronze against the entire land: against the kings of Judah, against her officials, against her priests, and against the people of the land.

**1:19** And they will fight against you, yet they will not prevail over you, for I am with you, declares the LORD, to rescue you."

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — Ketiv reads אצורך while qere reads אֶצָּרְךָ, Qal imperfect 1cs of יָצַר ("shape, form") with an assimilated yod and 2ms suffix.
- **1:11** — Hebrew plays on the sound of שָׁקֵד ("almond tree") here and שֹׁקֵד (Qal active participle of שָׁקַד, "keeping watch") in 1:12.
- **1:13** — סִיר נָפוּחַ is literally "a pot blown upon" (Qal passive participle of נָפַח), meaning fanned by fire and boiling; וּפָנָיו מִפְּנֵי צָפוֹנָה ("and its face is away from the north") pictures the pot tilted southward toward Judah.
- **1:17** — Repeats the root חָתַת ("be shattered/terrified"): תֵּחַת paired with the causative Hiphil אֲחִתְּךָ ("terrify/shatter").
