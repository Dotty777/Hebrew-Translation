## Text

**3:1** Saying, "If a man sends away his wife, and she goes from him and becomes another man's, will he return to her again? Would not that land be utterly polluted? Yet you have played the prostitute with many lovers, and would you return to Me?" declares the LORD.

**3:2** "Lift up your eyes to the bare heights and see: where have you not been lain with? Along the roads you sat for them like an Arab in the wilderness, and you polluted the land with your acts of prostitution and with your evil.

**3:3** Therefore the showers were withheld, and there was no spring rain; yet you had the forehead of a prostitute; you refused to be ashamed.

**3:4** Have you not just now called to Me, 'My Father, You are the companion of my youth!

**3:5** Will He keep anger forever? Will He hold it to the end?' Look, you have spoken, yet you have done evil things and have had your way."

**3:6** Then the LORD said to me in the days of Josiah the king, "Have you seen what wayward Israel has done? She went up on every high mountain and under every lush tree, and she played the prostitute there.

**3:7** And I said, 'After she has done all these things, she will return to Me,' but she did not return. And her treacherous sister Judah saw it.

**3:8** And I saw that for all the occasions when wayward Israel committed adultery, I had sent her away and given her a certificate of her divorce; yet treacherous Judah, her sister, did not fear, but she went and played the prostitute as well.

**3:9** And it happened, because of the casualness of her prostitution, that she polluted the land and committed adultery with stone and with wood.

**3:10** Yet even in spite of all this, her treacherous sister Judah did not return to Me with all her heart, but only in falsehood," declares the LORD.

**3:11** And the LORD said to me, "Wayward Israel has proved herself more righteous than treacherous Judah.

**3:12** Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, 'Return, wayward Israel, declares the LORD; I will not look angrily on you, for I am faithful, declares the LORD; I will not keep anger forever.

**3:13** Only acknowledge your iniquity, that you have rebelled against the LORD your God and have scattered your ways to strangers under every lush tree, and you have not listened to My voice, declares the LORD.'"

**3:14** "Return, wayward sons," declares the LORD, "for I am a husband to you, and I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and bring you to Zion.

**3:15** And I will give you shepherds according to My own heart, and they will shepherd you with knowledge and insight.

**3:16** And it will be, when you multiply and are fruitful in the land in those days," declares the LORD, "that they will no longer say, 'The ark of the covenant of the LORD.' It will not come to mind, nor will they remember it, nor will they miss it, nor will it be made again.

**3:17** At that time they will call Jerusalem the throne of the LORD, and all the nations will be gathered to it, to the name of the LORD, to Jerusalem; and they will no longer walk after the stubbornness of their evil heart.

**3:18** In those days the house of Judah will go to the house of Israel, and they will come together from the land of the north to the land that I gave as an inheritance to your fathers."

**3:19** "I Myself said, 'How I would set you among the sons and give you a desirable land, the most splendid inheritance of the hosts of nations!' And I thought you would call Me 'My Father' and would not turn away from following Me.

**3:20** Yet as a woman treacherously departs from her companion, so you have acted treacherously against Me, house of Israel," declares the LORD.

**3:21** A voice is heard on the bare heights, the weeping pleadings of the sons of Israel, because they have perverted their way; they have forgotten the LORD their God.

**3:22** "Return, wayward sons; I will heal your wayward ways." "Here we are, we come to You, for You are the LORD our God.

**3:23** Truly, from the hills comes falsehood, the tumult of the mountains; truly, in the LORD our God is the salvation of Israel.

**3:24** But the shameful thing has devoured the labor of our fathers since our youth, their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.

**3:25** Let us lie down in our shame, and let our disgrace cover us; for we have sinned against the LORD our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even to this day, and we have not listened to the voice of the LORD our God."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Hebrew opens abruptly with the Qal infinitive construct le'mor ("saying"), introducing the divine speech continuing from chapter 2. At the end of the verse, the Qal infinitive absolute shov ("return") can be read either as an incredulous question ("and would you return to Me?") or as an imperative ("yet return to Me!").
- **3:2** — Following the Qere shukkavt ("you have been lain with," Pual of shakhav); the Ketiv uses a coarser Pual form of the verb shagal ("be violated" or "ravished").
- **3:9** — The Hebrew miqqol derives from the root qll ("lightness," i.e., treating her prostitution lightly or casually), though it is spelled identically to a defective form of qol ("voice" or "rumor").
- **3:12** — "Look angrily on you" renders the Hiphil idiom 'appil panay bakhem (lit. "cause My face to fall against you," denoting a frown of displeasure).
- **3:19** — Following the Qere feminine singular verbs tiqre'i ("you would call") and tashuvi ("you would turn away"), matching the feminine singular suffixes earlier in the verse; the Ketiv has masculine plural verbs (tiqre'u and tashuvu).
