## Text

**23:1** "Woe to shepherds who destroy and scatter the flock of My pasture!" declares the LORD.

**23:2** Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says against the shepherds who shepherd My people: "You yourselves have scattered My flock and driven them away, and have not cared for them. Look, I am about to hold you accountable for the evil of your deeds," declares the LORD.

**23:3** "And I Myself will gather the remnant of My flock out of all the lands where I have driven them, and I will bring them back to their pastureland, and they will be fruitful and multiply.

**23:4** I will also raise up shepherds over them who will tend them; and they will no longer be afraid, nor be terrified, nor will any be missing," declares the LORD.

**23:5** "Look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when I will raise up for David a righteous Branch; a king will reign and act with insight, and he will carry out justice and righteousness in the land.

**23:6** In his days Judah will be delivered, and Israel will dwell in safety; and this is his name by which he will be called: 'The LORD Is Our Righteousness.'"

**23:7** "Therefore look, days are coming," declares the LORD, "when they will no longer say, 'As the LORD lives, who brought the sons of Israel up out of the land of Egypt,'

**23:8** but rather, 'As the LORD lives, who brought up and who brought the seed of the house of Israel out of the northern land and out of all the lands where I had driven them.' Then they will dwell on their own soil."

**23:9** Concerning the prophets: My heart is broken within me; all my bones tremble. I have become like a drunk man, and like a man whom wine has overwhelmed, because of the LORD and because of His holy words.

**23:10** For the land is filled with adulterers; because of a curse the land mourns, and the pastures of the wilderness have dried up. Their course is evil, and their might is not right.

**23:11** "For both prophet and priest are godless; even in My own house I have found their evil," declares the LORD.

**23:12** "Therefore their way will be for them like slippery ground in deep darkness; they will be driven along and fall in it. For I will bring disaster upon them in the year of their reckoning," declares the LORD.

**23:13** "Among the prophets of Samaria I saw something unseemly: they prophesied by Baal and led My people Israel astray.

**23:14** But among the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen an appalling thing: committing adultery and walking in falsehood; and they strengthen the hands of evildoers so that no one turns back from his evil. All of them have become like Sodom to Me, and her inhabitants like Gomorrah."

**23:15** Therefore this is what the LORD of hosts says concerning the prophets: "Look, I am about to feed them wormwood and make them drink poisoned water; for from the prophets of Jerusalem godlessness has gone out into the whole land."

**23:16** This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you; they are filling you with emptiness. They speak a vision of their own heart, not from the mouth of the LORD.

**23:17** They keep on saying to those who spurn Me, 'The LORD has spoken: Peace will be yours'; and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his heart they say, 'Disaster will not come upon you.'

**23:18** For who has stood in the council of the LORD so as to see and hear His word? Who has paid close attention to His word and listened?

**23:19** Look, the tempest of the LORD—wrath has gone out, even a whirling storm; it will whirl down upon the head of the wicked.

**23:20** The anger of the LORD will not turn back until He has done and accomplished the purposes of His heart; in the latter days you will understand it thoroughly.

**23:21** I did not send the prophets, yet they themselves ran; I did not speak to them, yet they themselves prophesied.

**23:22** But if they had stood in My council, then they would have made My people hear My words, and would have turned them back from their evil way and from the evil of their deeds."

**23:23** "Am I a God nearby," declares the LORD, "and not a God at a distance?

**23:24** Can a man conceal himself in hidden places so that I will not see him?" declares the LORD. "Is it not the heavens and the earth that I fill?" declares the LORD.

**23:25** "I have heard what the prophets have said, those who prophesy falsehood in My name, saying, 'I dreamed, I dreamed!'

**23:26** How long? Is this to be in the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehood, even prophets of the deceit of their own heart,

**23:27** who scheme to make My people forget My name by their dreams that they recount, each to his neighbor, just as their fathers forgot My name because of Baal?

**23:28** Let the prophet who has a dream recount a dream, and let the one who has My word speak My word in truth. What does straw have to do with grain?" declares the LORD.

**23:29** "Is not My word like this—like fire," declares the LORD, "and like a hammer that shatters rock?

**23:30** Therefore look, I am against the prophets," declares the LORD, "who steal My words, each from his neighbor.

**23:31** Look, I am against the prophets," declares the LORD, "who take their own tongues and utter a declaration.

**23:32** Look, I am against those who prophesy dreams of falsehood," declares the LORD, "and recount them and lead My people astray by their lies and by their recklessness, when I Myself did not send them nor command them; and they bring no benefit at all to this people," declares the LORD.

**23:33** "Now when this people, or the prophet, or a priest asks you, saying, 'What is the burden of the LORD?' then you shall say to them, 'What burden? I will cast you off!'" declares the LORD.

**23:34** "As for the prophet, the priest, or the people who say, 'The burden of the LORD,' I will punish that man and his household."

**23:35** This is what you shall say, each to his neighbor and each to his brother: "What has the LORD answered?" and "What has the LORD spoken?"

**23:36** And you shall no longer mention "the burden of the LORD," for every man's own word will become the burden, and you have overturned the words of the living God, the LORD of hosts, our God.

**23:37** This is what you shall say to the prophet: "What has the LORD answered you?" and "What has the LORD spoken?"

**23:38** But if you say, "The burden of the LORD," therefore this is what the LORD says: "Because you have spoken this word, 'The burden of the LORD,' even though I sent to you, saying, 'You shall not say the burden of the LORD,'

**23:39** therefore look, I will completely forget you, and I will cast you off from My presence, along with the city that I gave to you and to your fathers.

**23:40** And I will put upon you an everlasting reproach and an everlasting humiliation that will never be forgotten."

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — Hebrew repeats the root paqad with contrasting senses across vv. 2 and 4: "cared for" (Qal), "hold accountable" (Qal with al, visit judgment upon), and in v. 4 "be missing" (Niphal).
- **23:18** — Translates the qere devaro ("His word"); the ketiv reads devari ("My word"). Hebrew sod denotes an intimate council or confidential assembly.
- **23:26** — The Masoretic clause "until when? Is there in the heart..." is syntactically elliptical, leaving the subject ("this" lying practice) implied.
- **23:31** — Hebrew vayyinamu neum uses the sole finite verb occurrence of naam in the Hebrew Bible together with its cognate noun neum ("declaration"), mocking the false prophets' imitation of the formula "declares the LORD."
- **23:33** — Hebrew massa carries a deliberate double sense throughout vv. 33-38, meaning both "prophetic oracle" and "burden." The Masoretic text reads et-mah-massa ("What burden?"), whereas the Septuagint and Vulgate redivide the consonants as attem hammassa ("You are the burden").
- **23:39** — The Masoretic vocalization reads Qal of nashah ("forget") with an infinitive absolute spelled with final aleph, creating an orthographic pun on nasa ("lift up") and massa ("burden"); the Septuagint and Vulgate read "lift up."
