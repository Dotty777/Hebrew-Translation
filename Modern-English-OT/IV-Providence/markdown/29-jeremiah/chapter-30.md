## Text

**30:1** Concerning Edom. This is what the LORD of hosts says: "Is wisdom no longer in Teman? Has counsel perished from the discerning? Has their wisdom decayed?

**30:2** Flee, turn back, go deep to dwell, inhabitants of Dedan, for I will bring the disaster of Esau upon him, the time when I punish him.

**30:3** If grape harvesters came to you, would they not leave gleanings? If thieves came by night, they would destroy what suffices for them.

**30:4** But I have laid Esau bare; I have uncovered his hiding places, and he is unable to hide himself. His offspring, his brothers, and his neighbors are devastated, and he is no more.

**30:5** Leave your fatherless children; I will preserve them alive, and let your widows trust in Me."

**30:6** For this is what the LORD says: "Look, those whose sentence was not to drink the cup must surely drink it, and are you the one to go completely unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for you will surely drink it!

**30:7** For I have sworn by Myself, declares the LORD, that Bozrah will become a horror, a reproach, a ruin, and a curse, and all her cities will become perpetual ruins."

**30:8** I have heard a report from the LORD, and an envoy has been sent among the nations: "Gather together and come against her, and rise up for battle!"

**30:9** "For look, I have made you small among the nations, despised among mankind.

**30:10** The dread you inspire has deceived you, the arrogance of your heart, you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, who occupy the height of the hill. Though you set your nest as high as the eagle, from there I will bring you down, declares the LORD.

**30:11** Edom will become a horror; everyone passing by her will be horrified and will hiss at all her wounds.

**30:12** As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, says the LORD, no one will dwell there, and no human being will sojourn in her.

**30:13** Look, like a lion one will come up from the thicket of the Jordan against an enduring pasture; for in a moment I will make him run away from her, and whoever is chosen I will appoint over her. For who is like Me, and who will summon Me? And who is the shepherd who can stand before Me?"

**30:14** Therefore hear the counsel of the LORD that He has devised against Edom, and His designs that He has conceived against the inhabitants of Teman: Surely the little ones of the flock will drag them away; surely their pasture will be appalled at them.

**30:15** At the sound of their fall the earth quakes; an outcry—its sound is heard at the Red Sea.

**30:16** Look, like an eagle he will soar up and swoop, and spread his wings over Bozrah; and the heart of the warriors of Edom in that day will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

**30:17** Concerning the sons of Ammon. This is what the LORD says: "Does Israel have no sons? Or has he no heir? Why then has Malcam taken possession of Gad, and his people dwelt in his cities?

**30:18** Therefore look, days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will cause the alarm of war to be heard against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon; she will become a mound of desolation, and her villages will be set on fire, and Israel will dispossess his dispossessors, says the LORD.

**30:19** Wail, Heshbon, for Ai is devastated! Cry out, daughters of Rabbah! Wrap yourselves in sackcloth, lament, and rush back and forth among the enclosures, for Malcam will go into exile, his priests and his officials together.

**30:20** Why do you boast in the valleys—your valley flows away—wayward daughter who trusts in her treasures: 'Who will come against me?'

**30:21** Look, I am bringing terror upon you, declares the Lord GOD of hosts, from all around you; and you will be driven out, each one straight ahead, and there will be no one to gather the fugitive."

**30:23** Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck. This is what the LORD says: "Rise, go up against Kedar, and devastate the sons of the east!

**30:24** They will take their tents and their flocks; they will carry off for themselves their tent curtains, all their equipment, and their camels, and they will cry out to them, 'Terror on every side!'

**30:25** Flee, wander far away, go deep to dwell, inhabitants of Hazor, declares the LORD, for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has devised a plan against you and conceived a design against you.

**30:26** Rise, go up against a nation at ease, dwelling in security, declares the LORD; it has neither gates nor bars; they live alone.

**30:27** Their camels will become plunder, and the multitude of their livestock spoil. I will scatter them to every wind, those who clip the edges of their hair, and from every side of it I will bring their disaster, declares the LORD.

**30:28** Hazor will become a den of jackals, a desolation forever; no one will dwell there, and no human being will sojourn in her."

**30:29** Concerning Damascus. Hamath and Arpad are put to shame, for they have heard a bad report; they have melted away. There is anxiety on the sea; it cannot be quiet.

**30:30** Damascus has grown feeble; she has turned to flee, and trembling has gripped her. Anguish and birth pangs have seized her like a woman giving birth.

**30:31** How has the city of praise not been abandoned, the town of my joy!

**30:32** "Therefore her young men will fall in her squares, and all the men of war will be silenced in that day, declares the LORD of hosts.

**30:33** And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus, and it will devour the citadels of Ben-hadad."

## Translator Notes

- **30:14** — The Hebrew clause can also be construed with "the little ones of the flock" as the object ("they will drag them away, even the little ones of the flock") and the Hiphil verb yashim as transitive ("he will lay waste their pasture over them").
- **30:17** — Malcam (Hebrew malkam) is a form of the Ammonite deity Milcom's name and is identical in vocalization to "their king"; the reference to his priests in 30:19 confirms the deity.
- **30:23** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv spells the king's name nbwkdr'swr (Nebuchadrezor), while the read qere followed here is Nebuchadrezzar, rendered with the standard English spelling Nebuchadnezzar.
- **30:25** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv reads "against them" ('alehem), while the read qere followed here is "against you" ('alekem), matching the second-person plural earlier in the verse.
- **30:29** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "on the sea" (bayyam), with masculine singular "it cannot" (yukal) referring back to the sea to portray restless agitation that cannot subside.
- **30:31** — Ketiv/Qere: the written ketiv is the standard feminine noun tehillah ("praise"), while the read qere followed here is tehillat, preserving an archaic feminine ending with the same meaning. The Masoretic Hebrew retains the negative "not" (lo) in this dramatic lament voiced for Damascus ("my joy").
