## Text

**51:1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Judeans who were living in the land of Egypt, those living at Migdol, at Tahpanhes, at Noph, and in the land of Pathros, saying:

**51:2** "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'You yourselves have seen all the disaster that I brought upon Jerusalem and upon all the cities of Judah; look, they are a ruin this day, and no one lives in them,

**51:3** because of their evil that they committed to provoke Me to anger, going to burn incense, to serve other gods whom neither they, nor you, nor your fathers had known.

**51:4** Yet I sent to you all My servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, "Please do not commit this abominable thing that I hate."

**51:5** But they did not listen nor incline their ear to turn back from their evil, so as not to burn incense to other gods.

**51:6** So My wrath and My anger poured out and burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, and they became a ruin, a desolation, as they are this day.'

**51:7** And now, this is what the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Why are you doing great harm against your own lives, to cut off from yourselves man and woman, child and nursing infant, from the midst of Judah, so as not to leave yourselves a remnant,

**51:8** provoking Me to anger with the works of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt, where you have come to sojourn, in order to cut yourselves off and so that you may become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

**51:9** Have you forgotten the evils of your fathers, the evils of the kings of Judah, the evils of their wives, your own evils, and the evils of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**51:10** They have not been humbled to this day, nor have they feared, nor have they walked in My law and in My statutes that I set before you and before your fathers.'

**51:11** Therefore, this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Look, I am setting My face against you for disaster, and to cut off all Judah.

**51:12** And I will take the remnant of Judah who have set their faces to enter the land of Egypt to sojourn there, and they will all be consumed; in the land of Egypt they will fall, by the sword and by famine they will be consumed. From the least to the greatest, by the sword and by famine they will die, and they will become an imprecation, a horror, a curse, and a reproach.

**51:13** And I will punish those who live in the land of Egypt, just as I punished Jerusalem, with the sword, with famine, and with pestilence.

**51:14** And there will be no fugitive or survivor for the remnant of Judah who have come to sojourn there in the land of Egypt, so as to return to the land of Judah, to which they are lifting up their soul to return to live there; for they will not return, except fugitives.'"

**51:15** Then all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense to other gods, and all the women standing by, a great assembly, and all the people living in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying:

**51:16** "As for the word that you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD, we will not listen to you.

**51:17** Instead, we will surely carry out every word that has gone out from our mouth, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her, just as we ourselves did, we and our fathers, our kings and our officials, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Then we were satisfied with bread, and we fared well, and we saw no disaster.

**51:18** But from the time we stopped burning incense to the queen of heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything, and by the sword and by famine we have been consumed.

**51:19** And when we were burning incense to the queen of heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, was it apart from our husbands that we made cakes for her to form her image and poured out drink offerings to her?"

**51:20** Then Jeremiah said to all the people, to the men, to the women, and to all the people who had given him an answer, saying:

**51:21** "As for the incense that you burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, you and your fathers, your kings and your officials, and the people of the land, did not the LORD remember them, and did it not come to His mind?

**51:22** So the LORD could no longer bear it, because of the evil of your deeds, because of the abominations that you committed; therefore your land became a ruin, a horror, and a curse, without an inhabitant, as it is this day.

**51:23** Because you burned incense and because you sinned against the LORD, and did not obey the voice of the LORD nor walk in His law, in His statutes, and in His testimonies, therefore this disaster has come upon you, as it is this day."

**51:24** Then Jeremiah said to all the people and to all the women, "Hear the word of the LORD, all Judah who are in the land of Egypt.

**51:25** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, has spoken, saying: 'You and your wives have spoken with your mouths and have fulfilled it with your hands, saying, "We will surely carry out our vows that we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her." Surely confirm your vows, and surely carry out your vows!'

**51:26** Therefore hear the word of the LORD, all Judah who live in the land of Egypt: 'Look, I have sworn by My great name,' says the LORD, 'that My name shall no longer be pronounced by the mouth of any man of Judah, saying, "As the Lord GOD lives," in all the land of Egypt.

**51:27** Look, I am watching over them for disaster and not for good, and all the men of Judah who are in the land of Egypt will be consumed by the sword and by famine until they come to an end.

**51:28** Yet fugitives from the sword will return from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah, few in number; and all the remnant of Judah who came to the land of Egypt to sojourn there will know whose word will stand, Mine or theirs.

**51:29** And this will be the sign to you,' declares the LORD, 'that I am going to punish you in this place, so that you may know that My words will surely stand against you for disaster.'

**51:30** This is what the LORD says: 'Look, I am giving Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies and into the hand of those who seek his life, just as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy and the one who sought his life.'"

**51:31** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words on a scroll from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

**51:32** "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says concerning you, Baruch:

**51:33** 'You have said, "Woe to me now, for the LORD has added grief to my pain; I am worn out from my groaning, and I have not found rest."'

**51:34** Thus you shall say to him: 'This is what the LORD says: "Look, what I have built I am tearing down, and what I have planted I am uprooting, and indeed it is the entire land.

**51:35** And as for you, are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them! For look, I am bringing disaster upon all flesh," declares the LORD, "yet I will give you your life as plunder in all the places where you go."'"

## Translator Notes

- **51:4** — The paired infinitives absolute (Hiphil of shkm and Qal of shlh, literally 'rising early and sending') form a recurring Jeremianic idiom for persistent, earnest action.
- **51:9** — Hebrew literally has a third-person masculine singular suffix on 'wives' ('his wives'), used distributively for each of the kings of Judah (or collectively referring back to Judah); it is smoothed here to 'their wives.'
- **51:19** — Hebrew leha'atsivah is a Hiphil infinitive construct of the verb 'tsb ('shape, form') with a third-person feminine singular suffix, referring to shaping sacrificial cakes in the likeness of the queen of heaven.
- **51:25** — The Hebrew alternates between second-person feminine plural verbs ('spoken,' 'confirm,' 'carry out') and masculine plural suffixes ('your mouths,' 'your hands,' 'your vows'), highlighting the joint participation of both the Judean men and their wives.
- **51:34** — The closing Hebrew clause is notably terse (literally 'and [object marker] all the land/earth it is'), specifying the full scope of the LORD's tearing down and uprooting; Hebrew erets can mean either 'the land' or 'the earth.'
