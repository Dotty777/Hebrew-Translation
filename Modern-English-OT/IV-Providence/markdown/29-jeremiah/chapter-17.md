## Text

**17:5** This is what the LORD says: "Cursed is the man who trusts in humanity and makes flesh his arm, while his heart turns aside from the LORD.

**17:6** He will be like a shrub in the desert plain and will not see when good comes; he will dwell in parched places in the wilderness, a salt land that is not inhabited.

**17:7** Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD, and the LORD is his confidence.

**17:8** He will be like a tree planted beside water, sending out its roots toward a stream; it does not see when heat comes, and its foliage stays green; in a year of drought it is not anxious, and it does not stop yielding fruit.

**17:9** The heart is more deceitful than anything else, and it is incurably sick; who can know it?

**17:10** I, the LORD, search the heart and test the inward parts, to give to each man according to his ways, according to the fruit of his deeds.

**17:11** Like a partridge brooding over what it did not lay is one who acquires wealth, yet not by justice; in the middle of his days it will forsake him, and at his end he will be a fool."

**17:12** A throne of glory, exalted from the beginning, is the place of our sanctuary.

**17:13** LORD, the hope of Israel, all who forsake You will be put to shame; and those who turn aside from Me will be written in the earth, because they have forsaken the spring of living water, the LORD.

**17:14** Heal me, LORD, and I shall be healed; save me, and I shall be saved, for You are my praise.

**17:15** Look, they keep saying to me, "Where is the word of the LORD? Let it come now!"

**17:16** Yet I have not hurried away from being a shepherd following You, nor have I longed for the woeful day; You Yourself know. What came out of my lips was right before Your face.

**17:17** Do not become a terror to me; You are my refuge in the day of calamity.

**17:18** Let my pursuers be put to shame, but let me not be put to shame; let them be terrified, but let me not be terrified. Bring upon them a day of calamity, and shatter them with double destruction.

**17:19** This is what the LORD said to me: "Go and stand in the Gate of the Sons of the People, through which the kings of Judah enter and through which they go out, and in all the gates of Jerusalem,

**17:20** and say to them, 'Hear the word of the LORD, kings of Judah, and all Judah, and all inhabitants of Jerusalem who enter through these gates:

**17:21** This is what the LORD says: Take care for your lives, and do not carry a burden on the Sabbath day or bring it in through the gates of Jerusalem.

**17:22** Do not carry a burden out of your houses on the Sabbath day, nor do any work, but keep the Sabbath day holy, just as I commanded your fathers.

**17:23** Yet they did not listen and did not incline their ear, but stiffened their neck so as not to listen and not to receive discipline.

**17:24** And it will be, if you diligently listen to Me, declares the LORD, by bringing no burden through the gates of this city on the Sabbath day, and by keeping the Sabbath day holy so as not to do any work on it,

**17:25** then kings and princes who sit on the throne of David will enter through the gates of this city, riding in chariots and on horses, they and their princes, the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and this city will be inhabited forever.

**17:26** And people will come from the cities of Judah, from the places around Jerusalem, from the land of Benjamin, from the lowland, from the hill country, and from the Negev, bringing burnt offerings and sacrifices, grain offerings and frankincense, and bringing thank offerings to the house of the LORD.

**17:27** But if you do not listen to Me, to keep the Sabbath day holy and not to carry a burden and enter through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day, then I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it will devour the citadels of Jerusalem and will not be quenched.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:8** — Translates the Qere yir'eh (Qal imperfect of ra'ah, "see"), echoing 17:6 ("will not see when good comes"); the Ketiv yr' is understood as yira' ("fear," from yare').
- **17:13** — Translates the Qere wesuray ("and those who turn aside from Me"), from sur with a first-person singular suffix marking an abrupt shift to the LORD's voice; the Ketiv reads yswry.
- **17:16** — Hebrew lo-atsti mero'eh ahareka (Qal perfect of uts, "hurry," plus Qal participle of ra'ah, "shepherd") literally means "I have not hurried away from being a shepherd after You," and yom anush ("woeful day") uses the same root (anash, "incurable") as 15:18 and 17:9.
- **17:19** — Translates the Qere ha'am ("the people," with the definite article) in "the Gate of the Sons of the People"; the Ketiv reads am without the article.
