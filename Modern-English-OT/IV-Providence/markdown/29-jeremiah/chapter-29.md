## Text

**29:1** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet regarding the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.

**29:2** This is what the LORD says: "Look, waters are rising from the north and will become a sweeping torrent; they will sweep over the land and its fullness, the city and those dwelling in it. People will cry out, and everyone dwelling in the land will wail.

**29:3** At the sound of the pounding hooves of his stallions, at the rumbling of his chariots, the tumult of his wheels, fathers do not turn back to their children from feebleness of hands,

**29:4** on account of the day that is coming to devastate all the Philistines, to cut off from Tyre and Sidon every surviving helper; for the LORD is devastating the Philistines, the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor.

**29:5** Baldness has come upon Gaza; Ashkelon is ruined. Remnant of their valley, how long will you gash yourself?"

**29:6** Alas, sword of the LORD! How long will you not be quiet? Withdraw into your sheath; rest and be silent!

**29:7** How can you be quiet when the LORD has commanded it? Against Ashkelon and against the shore of the sea—there He has appointed it.

## Translator Notes

- **29:3** — The Hebrew nouns rendered "pounding" (sha'atah) and "feebleness" (ripyon) occur only here in the Hebrew Bible.
- **29:5** — The Masoretic Hebrew reads "their valley" ('imqam), referring to the Philistine coastal lowland, rather than the proper name "Anakites" reflected in the Septuagint.
- **29:7** — The Hebrew shifts abruptly from second-person feminine singular ("you be quiet") to third-person feminine singular ("it") in addressing and speaking about the sword.
