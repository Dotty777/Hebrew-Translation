## Text

**8:1** "At that time," declares the LORD, "they will bring out the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of its officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem from their graves.

**8:2** And they will spread them out before the sun and the moon and all the host of heaven, whom they loved, and whom they served, and after whom they walked, and whom they sought, and to whom they bowed down. They will not be gathered and will not be buried; they will become dung on the surface of the ground.

**8:3** And death will be chosen rather than life by all the remnant who remain of this wicked family, in all the remaining places to which I have driven them," declares the LORD of hosts.

**8:4** And you shall say to them, "This is what the LORD says: Do they fall and not rise up? If one turns away, does he not turn back?

**8:5** Why has this people, Jerusalem, turned away in an unceasing turning away? They cling to deceit; they refuse to turn back.

**8:6** I have paid attention and listened: they do not speak what is right. No one feels remorse over his wickedness, saying, 'What have I done?' Every one of them turns into their course, like a horse surging into the battle.

**8:7** Even the stork in the sky knows her appointed times, and the turtledove, the swift, and the crane keep the time of their coming; yet My people do not know the ordinance of the LORD.

**8:8** How can you say, 'We are wise, and the law of the LORD is with us'? Indeed, look, the false pen of the scribes has turned it into falsehood.

**8:9** The wise are put to shame; they are dismayed and trapped. Look, they have rejected the word of the LORD, so what kind of wisdom do they have?

**8:10** Therefore I will give their wives to others, their fields to new possessors; for from the least to the greatest, every one of them is grasping for dishonest gain; from prophet to priest, every one of them practices falsehood."

**8:13** "I will surely sweep them away," declares the LORD. "There are no grapes on the vine, and there are no figs on the fig tree, and the leaf has withered; and what I gave them will pass away from them."

**8:14** "Why are we sitting still? Gather together, and let us enter the fortified cities and be silenced there; for the LORD our God has silenced us and has given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD.

**8:15** We waited for peace, yet there is no good; for a time of healing, and look, terror!"

**8:16** From Dan the snorting of his horses is heard; at the sound of the neighing of his stallions the whole land quakes. They come and devour the land and its fullness, the city and those who dwell in it.

**8:17** "For look, I am sending among you serpents, vipers for which there is no charm, and they will bite you," declares the LORD.

**8:18** When I seek relief from sorrow, within me my heart is faint.

**8:19** Look, the sound of the cry for help of the daughter of my people from a distant land: "Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her King not in her?" "Why have they provoked Me to anger with their carved images, with foreign vanities?"

**8:20** "The harvest has passed, the summer is over, and we have not been saved."

**8:21** Because of the shattering of the daughter of my people I am shattered; I mourn; dismay has taken hold of me.

**8:22** Is there no balm in Gilead? Is there no healer there? Why then has the healing of the daughter of my people not appeared?

**8:23** If only my head were waters, and my eyes a spring of tears, so that I might weep day and night for those killed of the daughter of my people!

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Following the Qere (read form) yotsi'u ("they will bring out," Hiphil imperfect without a prefixed conjunction); the Ketiv (written form) includes a prefixed waw ("and they will bring out").
- **8:6** — Following the Qere (read form) bimerutsatam (singular "into their course"); the Ketiv (written form) reads the plural ("into their courses").
- **8:7** — Following the Qere (read form) vesis ("and the swift"); the Ketiv (written form) reads vesus, an alternate spelling of the bird's name that is homographic with the word for "horse" (compare Isaiah 38:14).
- **8:13** — The opening words asof asifem pair the Qal infinitive absolute of asaf ("gather") with the Hiphil imperfect of suf ("bring to an end / sweep away"). The terse closing clause literally reads "and I gave to them, they will pass away from them," understood elliptically as "what I gave them will pass away from them."
- **8:18** — The opening word mabligiti is a unique Masoretic noun derived from the root balag ("brighten / find relief") with a first-person singular suffix, literally "my relief over sorrow," expressing a desperate search for comfort.
