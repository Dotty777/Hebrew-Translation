## Text

**2:1** Gather yourselves, indeed gather together, shameless nation,

**2:2** before the decree gives birth—like chaff the day passes by—before the fierce anger of the LORD comes upon you, before the day of the LORD's anger comes upon you.

**2:3** Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land, who have practiced His justice; seek righteousness, seek humility; perhaps you will be hidden on the day of the LORD's anger.

**2:4** For Gaza will be forsaken, and Ashkelon a desolation; at midday they will drive out Ashdod, and Ekron will be uprooted.

**2:5** Woe to the residents of the seacoast, nation of the Cherethites! The word of the LORD is against you, Canaan, land of the Philistines: "I will destroy you so that there is no resident."

**2:6** And the seacoast will become pastures, caves of shepherds, and pens for flocks.

**2:7** And the coast will belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; upon them they will graze. In the houses of Ashkelon they will lie down in the evening, for the LORD their God will attend to them and restore their fortunes.

**2:8** "I have heard the reproach of Moab and the insults of the sons of Ammon, with which they have reproached My people and acted arrogantly against their border.

**2:9** Therefore, as I live," declares the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, "surely Moab will become like Sodom and the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah: a territory of nettles and a pit of salt and a desolation forever. The remnant of My people will despoil them, and the rest of My nation will take possession of them."

**2:10** This is what they receive in return for their pride, because they reproached and acted arrogantly against the people of the LORD of hosts.

**2:11** The LORD will be fearsome against them, for He will cause all the gods of the earth to waste away, and to Him they will bow down, each one from his own place, all the coastlands of the nations.

**2:12** "You also, Cushites, will be struck down by My sword."

**2:13** And He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy Assyria; He will turn Nineveh into a desolation, dry like the wilderness.

**2:14** Herds will lie down in her midst, every kind of wild creature; both the desert owl and the screech owl will spend the night on her column capitals. A voice will sing in the window; devastation will be on the threshold, for He has stripped bare the cedarwork.

**2:15** This is the exultant city that lived in security, that said in her heart, "I am, and apart from me there is no other." How she has become a waste, a resting place for wild animals! Everyone who passes by her will hiss and wave his hand.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The imperative pair hitqosesu waqossu (Hithpolel and Qal imperatives of qasas, literally "gather stubble") intensifies the call to assemble; lo niksap (Niphal of kasap) is commonly understood via Aramaic and late Hebrew as "shameless," though in Biblical Hebrew kasap also means "long for" (hence "undesired").
- **2:4** — The verse employs two Hebrew sound-plays: azza azuba ("Gaza ... forsaken") and eqron teager ("Ekron will be uprooted," Niphal of aqar).
- **2:6** — Hebrew kerot is uncertain, derived either from kara ("dig," hence "caves/cisterns") or from kar ("meadow"), while echoing "Cherethites" (keretim) in verse 5.
- **2:7** — Ketiv reads sebutam and qere reads sebitam, two orthographic variants of the same noun in the idiom sub sebit/sebut, meaning "restore their fortunes" (literally "turn back their captivity/turning").
- **2:14** — Hebrew hayto-goy (literally "beast of a nation") denotes wild creatures of every kind; the exact species of qaat and qippod are uncertain, and the translation follows the Masoretic readings qol ("a voice") and horeb ("devastation") rather than the Septuagint.
