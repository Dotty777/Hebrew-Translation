## Text

**3:1** Woe to the rebellious and defiled one, the oppressing city!

**3:2** She has listened to no voice; she has accepted no correction. In the LORD she has not trusted; to her God she has not come near.

**3:3** Her officials in her midst are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves; they save nothing until morning.

**3:4** Her prophets are insolent, men of treachery; her priests have profaned what is sacred; they have treated the law with violence.

**3:5** The LORD in her midst is righteous; He will commit no injustice. Morning after morning He sets His justice in the light; He never fails. Yet the unjust one knows no shame.

**3:6** "I have cut off nations; their corner towers have become desolate. I have laid waste their streets without a passerby; their cities have been devastated, without a man, with no resident.

**3:7** I said, 'Surely you will fear Me, you will accept correction,' so that her dwelling would not be cut off in accordance with all that I had appointed against her. Yet indeed they rose early and corrupted all their deeds.

**3:8** Therefore wait for Me," declares the LORD, "for the day when I rise up for prey. For My judgment is to gather nations, to assemble kingdoms, to pour out upon them My indignation, all My fierce anger; for by the fire of My jealousy the entire earth will be consumed.

**3:9** For then I will turn the peoples to a purified speech, so that all of them may call upon the name of the LORD, to serve Him with one shoulder.

**3:10** From across the rivers of Cush, My suppliants, the daughter of My scattered ones, will bring My offering.

**3:11** On that day you will not be ashamed over all your deeds by which you transgressed against Me; for then I will remove from your midst those who exult in your pride, and you will no longer be haughty on My holy mountain.

**3:12** And I will leave in your midst an afflicted and lowly people, and they will take refuge in the name of the LORD.

**3:13** The remnant of Israel will commit no injustice and will speak no falsehood, nor will a tongue of deceit be found in their mouths; for they will graze and lie down, and there will be no one to terrify them."

**3:14** Sing for joy, daughter of Zion! Shout triumphantly, Israel! Be glad and exult with a whole heart, daughter of Jerusalem!

**3:15** The LORD has removed the judgments against you; He has turned away your enemy. The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst; you will fear calamity no longer.

**3:16** On that day it will be said to Jerusalem: "Do not fear; Zion, do not let your hands drop slack.

**3:17** The LORD your God is in your midst, a mighty warrior who saves; He will delight over you with gladness, He will be still in His love, He will rejoice over you with joyful singing."

**3:18** "I will gather those grieving away from the appointed festival; from you they came; reproach was a burden upon her.

**3:19** Look, at that time I am going to deal with all your oppressors; and I will save the lame and gather the outcast, and I will turn them into praise and renown in every land of their shame.

**3:20** At that time I will bring you in—yes, at the time when I gather you—for I will appoint you for renown and praise among all the peoples of the earth, when I restore your fortunes before your eyes," says the LORD.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — Hebrew lo garemu (Qal perfect of garam, related to gerem, "bone") literally means "they do not leave a bone" or "they save nothing" until morning.
- **3:8** — The Masoretic text reads lead ("for prey/plunder"), whereas the Septuagint reflects a different vocalization, leed ("as a witness").
- **3:9** — Hebrew ehpok el-ammim sapa berura literally reads "I will turn/change to peoples a purified lip," and sekem ehad ("one shoulder") is an idiom for united service.
- **3:17** — Following the Masoretic text, yaharis (Hiphil imperfect of haras) means "He will be still/silent" in deep love, whereas the Septuagint reflects yehaddes ("He will renew").
- **3:18** — The Masoretic text is terse and shifts grammatical person from second feminine singular (mimmek, "from you") to third feminine singular (aleha, "upon her"); nuge is the Niphal participle construct of yaga ("those grieving"), and asapti ("I will gather") functions as a prophetic perfect.
