## Text

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah, son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, during the days of Josiah, son of Amon, king of Judah.

**1:2** "I will thoroughly sweep away everything from upon the face of the ground," declares the LORD.

**1:3** "I will sweep away mankind and animals; I will sweep away the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea, and the stumbling blocks along with the wicked; and I will cut off mankind from upon the face of the ground," declares the LORD.

**1:4** "And I will stretch out My hand against Judah and against all the residents of Jerusalem, and I will cut off from this place the remnant of Baal, the name of the idolatrous priests together with the priests,

**1:5** and those who bow down on the rooftops to the host of the heavens, and those who bow down, who swear to the LORD and swear by Milcom,

**1:6** and those who turn away from following after the LORD, and those who have not sought the LORD and have not inquired of Him."

**1:7** Silence before the Lord GOD! For near is the day of the LORD; for the LORD has prepared a sacrifice, He has consecrated His invited guests.

**1:8** "And it will happen on the day of the LORD's sacrifice that I will punish the officials and the sons of the king, and all who wear foreign attire.

**1:9** And I will punish everyone who leaps over the threshold on that day, those who fill the house of their master with violence and deceit."

**1:10** "And on that day," declares the LORD, "there will be the sound of an outcry from the Fish Gate, and a wailing from the Second Quarter, and a great crash from the hills.

**1:11** Wail, residents of the Mortar, for all the people of Canaan have been silenced; all who are laden with silver have been cut off.

**1:12** And it will happen at that time that I will search Jerusalem with lamps, and I will punish the men who thicken on their dregs, those who say in their hearts, 'The LORD will do no good, nor will He do harm.'

**1:13** And their wealth will become plunder, and their houses a desolation; and they will build houses, yet they will not live in them, and they will plant vineyards, yet they will not drink their wine."

**1:14** Near is the great day of the LORD, near and hurrying very rapidly! The sound of the day of the LORD! There a mighty warrior cries aloud bitterly.

**1:15** That day is a day of wrath, a day of trouble and anguish, a day of devastation and waste, a day of darkness and blackness, a day of cloud and thick gloom.

**1:16** A day of ram's horn and war cry against the fortified cities and against the high corner towers.

**1:17** "And I will afflict mankind with distress, and they will walk like blind people, because against the LORD they have sinned; and their blood will be poured out like dust, and their entrails like dung."

**1:18** Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to rescue them on the day of the LORD's wrath, and by the fire of His jealousy the entire earth will be consumed; for He will make a complete, truly terrifying end of all the residents of the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Hebrew pairs the Qal infinitive absolute of asap ("gather/remove") with the Hiphil imperfect of sup ("bring to an end/sweep away"), creating an emphatic wordplay meaning "thoroughly sweep away."
- **1:3** — Hebrew hammakselot ("the stumbling blocks," from kasal, "stumble") can refer either to idolatrous objects that cause sin or to heaps of ruins.
- **1:5** — Masoretic bemalkam literally reads "by their king," widely understood as a vocalization referring to the Ammonite deity Milcom (also spelled Malcam).
- **1:11** — "The Mortar" translates hammaktes (Maktesh), a basin-shaped commercial district of Jerusalem; "people of Canaan" idiomatically denotes traders, paired with netile kasep (Qal passive participle of natal, "laden with silver" or "weighing out silver").
- **1:12** — "Thicken on their dregs" translates the Qal participle of qapa ("congeal/thicken") with semarim ("dregs/lees"), a winemaking image depicting undisturbed complacency.
