## Text

**1:1** The pronouncement concerning Nineveh: the scroll of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

**1:2** The LORD is a jealous God and an avenger; the LORD is an avenger and master of fury. The LORD exacts vengeance on His foes, and He retains wrath for His enemies.

**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in strength, yet He will surely not acquit the guilty. The LORD—His path is in whirlwind and tempest, and clouds are the dust of His feet.

**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up, and all the rivers He parches; Bashan and Carmel languish, and the blossom of Lebanon languishes.

**1:5** Mountains tremble because of Him, and the hills melt away; and the earth heaves at His presence, the world and all who live in it.

**1:6** Before His indignation, who can stand? And who can remain standing in the burning of His anger? His fury is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered because of Him.

**1:7** Good is the LORD, a stronghold on a day of distress, and He knows those who seek refuge in Him.

**1:8** But with a sweeping flood He will make a full end of her place, and He will chase His enemies into darkness.

**1:9** What are you devising against the LORD? He Himself will make a full end; distress will not arise twice.

**1:10** For though entangled like thorns and drunk as from their drink, they are devoured like thoroughly dry stubble.

**1:11** Out of you has come forth one who devises evil against the LORD, one who counsels wickedness.

**1:12** This is what the LORD says: "Even if they are intact and likewise numerous, even so they will be cut down and he will pass away. And though I have afflicted you, I will not afflict you again.

**1:13** And now I will break his yoke bar off you, and your bonds I will tear apart."

**1:14** And the LORD has commanded regarding you: "No longer will anything of your name be sown; from the house of your gods I will cut off carved image and molten image. I will make your grave, because you are despicable."

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Ketiv reads wgdwl (plene), while Qere reads ugedol-koach (defective construct form before maqqef, "great in strength"); both carry the same meaning. The Masoretic atnach divides the verse after yenaqqeh, placing the second occurrence of the divine name at the start of the second half as casus pendens ("The LORD—His path is...").
- **1:5** — The verb wattissa' ("heaves") is an unusual intransitive Qal of nasa' ("lift up, rise"), depicting the earth heaving upward before God's presence.
- **1:8** — The Masoretic text reads meqomah ("her place," referring to Nineveh). In the final clause, yeraddef-choshekh (Piel of radaph) can be read with the LORD as subject and "darkness" as adverbial goal ("He will chase His enemies into darkness") or with "darkness" as subject ("darkness will chase His enemies").
- **1:10** — The Hebrew syntax is unusually terse and elliptical, pairing 'ad-sirim sebukhim ("though entangled like thorns") with a wordplay on sava' in ukhesov'am sevu'im ("and drunk as from their drink").
- **1:12** — The Niphal perfect nagozzu (from gazaz, "shear, mow down") and the shift to third-person masculine singular we'avar ("and he will pass away," anticipating 3ms "his yoke bar" in 1:13) portray Assyria's collective might being cut down swiftly.
