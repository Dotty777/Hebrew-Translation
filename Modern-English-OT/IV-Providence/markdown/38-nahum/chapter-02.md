## Text

**2:1** Look! Upon the mountains are the feet of one bringing good news, announcing peace. Observe your feasts, Judah; pay your vows, because the wicked one will not pass through you again; he is entirely cut off.

**2:2** A scatterer has come up against your face! Guard the rampart, watch the road, brace your loins, fortify your strength greatly.

**2:3** For the LORD is restoring the grandeur of Jacob, like the grandeur of Israel, because those who lay waste have laid them waste and ruined their vine branches.

**2:4** The shield of his warriors is dyed red; the men of valor are dressed in crimson. With fiery steel are the chariots on the day of his preparation, and the cypresses are made to quiver.

**2:5** In the streets the chariots rage madly; they hurtle back and forth in the broad squares. Their appearance resembles torches; like lightning bolts they dart about.

**2:6** He recalls his nobles; they stumble in their advance. They rush to her wall, and the protective barrier is made ready.

**2:7** The gates of the rivers have been opened, and the palace dissolves.

**2:8** And it is determined: she is laid bare, she is carried away; and her maidservants moan like the voice of doves, drumming on their breasts.

**2:9** And Nineveh has been like a pool of water from the days she existed, yet they are fleeing. "Stand! Stand!" Yet no one turns around.

**2:10** Plunder silver! Plunder gold! And there is no end to the store, riches from every treasured article.

**2:11** Waste, and utter waste, and devastation! Melting heart, buckling knees, and writhing anguish in all loins; and the faces of all of them gather pallor.

**2:12** Where is the den of lions, and the feeding ground it was for the young lions, where lion, lioness, and lion cub walked, with no one to frighten them?

**2:13** A lion tore enough for his cubs and strangled for his lionesses; he filled his holes with prey and his dens with torn prey.

**2:14** "Look, I am against you," declares the LORD of hosts, "and I will burn her chariots in smoke, and a sword will devour your young lions; I will also cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers will not be heard again."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Ketiv reads l'bwr (plene), while Qere reads la'avor-bakh (defective infinitive construct with qamets hatuf before maqqef, "to pass through you"); the meaning is unchanged.
- **2:6** — Qere reads the singular bahalikhatam ("in their advance/march"), whereas Ketiv reads the plural bhlkwtm ("in their marches/courses"). Hassokhekh (Qal active participle of sakhakh, "cover, shield") denotes a movable protective siege barrier or mantelet.
- **2:8** — A well-known crux: wehutzav is here parsed as an impersonal Hophal perfect of natzav ("and it is determined/decreed"; some take it as a proper name, Huzzab), followed by Pual gulletah ("she is laid bare," or "exiled") and Hophal ho'alatah ("she is carried away").
- **2:9** — The Masoretic phrase mime hi' (literally "from the days of she") is an elliptical temporal idiom meaning "from the days she existed" or "throughout her days."
- **2:14** — The Masoretic text abruptly shifts to a third-person feminine singular suffix in rikhvah ("her chariots") amid second-person feminine singular address, and mal'akhekheh ("your messengers") displays a unique lengthened 2fs suffix.
