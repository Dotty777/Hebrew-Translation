2:1  HE: אַחַר הַדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה כְּשֹׁךְ חֲמַת הַמֶּלֶךְ אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁ זָכַר אֶת־וַשְׁתִּי וְאֵת אֲשֶׁר־עָשָׂתָה וְאֵת אֲשֶׁר־נִגְזַר עָלֶיהָ׃
     EN: [BHS 2:1] After these things, when King Ahasuerus’s wrath had subsided, he remembered Vashti, what she had done, and what had been decreed against her.

     EN: [BHS 2:2] Then the king’s young attendants who served him said, “Let young virgins lovely in appearance be sought for the king.

     EN: [BHS 2:3] Let the king appoint officers in all the provinces of his kingdom, and let them gather every young virgin lovely in appearance to Susa the citadel, to the women’s house, into the care of Hegai, the king’s eunuch who guards the women; and let their beauty treatments be given to them.

2:4  HE: וְהַנַּעֲרָה אֲשֶׁר תִּיטַב בְּעֵינֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ תִּמְלֹךְ תַּחַת וַשְׁתִּי וַיִּיטַב הַדָּבָר בְּעֵינֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ וַיַּעַשׂ כֵּן׃
     EN: [BHS 2:4] Let the young woman who is good in the king’s eyes reign as queen in Vashti’s place.” The proposal was good in the king’s eyes, and he did so.

2:5  HE: אִישׁ יְהוּדִי הָיָה בְּשׁוּשַׁן הַבִּירָה וּשְׁמוֹ מָרְדֳּכַי בֶּן יָאִיר בֶּן־שִׁמְעִי בֶּן־קִישׁ אִישׁ יְמִינִי׃
     EN: [BHS 2:5] There was a Jewish man in Susa the citadel named Mordecai son of Jair, son of Shimei, son of Kish, a Benjaminite,

2:6  HE: אֲשֶׁר הָגְלָה מִירוּשָׁלַיִם עִם־הַגֹּלָה אֲשֶׁר הָגְלְתָה עִם יְכָנְיָה מֶלֶךְ־יְהוּדָה אֲשֶׁר הֶגְלָה נְבוּכַדְנֶאצַּר מֶלֶךְ בָּבֶל׃
     EN: [BHS 2:6] who had been exiled from Jerusalem with the exiles carried away with Jeconiah king of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had exiled.
